NEC1 73-KICO-22

29 April 1965

6.0 contd.

MO can't locate Lopez Aguara until 1300. I could go to some address around 1430. The Dominicans can go by "Excelsior." That would be fine. Call MO - anyway at 1330. Mo is Eduardo (Deschamps).

VKG would like to talk to Eduardo, E. is at home sick in bed. VKG can visit him. ED's address is reforma 10-504. VKG will call before he goes by and will probably go today. VKG hopes that the "beast" (MO's wife) won't be there. No, she has gone to France. Then, there is no problem.

After 1100, 29 April

9.2 Out to 25-80-32. MI (Dominican probably) with WO, who he calls "Chinita." MI identifies himself as Tobias (7). Did Karon Grullo (ph) phone back again? No. But WO got the message that they should meet at 1200 at "Las Brasas"? Yes. Tell him, if he calls back, that they will be there at 1200. Fine.

9.3 Out to 31-06-06. VKG asks MO, who answers, for Sr. Miric. Not there now. Will be there around 1330 or 1400. Ask him to phone VAG urgently.

9.7 Out to 45-91-36. VKG with Tonia, who answers, for Luis Suárez. Went to see Sr. Angel Sanchez. Does she have his phone there? Yes, but they were meeting near Cine de las Americas. If he phones ink, ask him to phone VKG. In any case VKG will leave a message for him at "Siempre."

10.2 Out to 46-43-24. Busy.

10.5 Out to 33-24-28. VKG with WO, who answers at "Siempre." When Luis Suárez comes in, ask him to phone VAG at home.

11.0 Out to 35-36-32. Marcio Mejia kicardi asks WO, who answers at "Politics," for Sr. Gomez Lorenzo. Not there yet. Is he at home? She thinks so. Ask for his home phone. (During wait - MI tells Emilio to go take (some protest) to the Presidencia while MIM phones...) Gomez Lorenzo's phone is 39-39-39.

11.5 Out to 39-39-39. Marcio Mejía kicard with Gomez Lorenzo's daughter, who answers, for GL. Not there. They want to talk to GL. Should be in the office in about an hour. Advise him that a group of Santo Domingans - leaves address where it is to be. GL needs to know should invite someone who works at "Diccion de la Tarde" and should select someone who is "adequate" (sympathizer).

Sr. Jordan takes the phone. A1 tells him about the press conference they are planning for today at 1600...giving address...
Fine, will go.
