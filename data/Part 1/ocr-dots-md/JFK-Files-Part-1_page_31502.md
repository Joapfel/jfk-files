MENORANDUM FOR: Chief, IO Division

SUBJECT: Report of Ex-King Michael's Interest in Romanian Refugee Unity

1. Transmitted herewith is a report which will be of interest to the NCFB in its current negotiations with the Romanian refugees.

2. The views of the King with regard to the Iron Guards and others listed, are in agreement with those of this Division.

3. It is requested that arrangements be made for representatives of the NCFB to meet with personnel of this division to discuss the current negotiations and utilizing this recent information.

GRATIAN M. YATSEVITCH
Deputy for Policy Coordination, SE
