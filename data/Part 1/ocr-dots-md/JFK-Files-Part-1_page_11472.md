Organization of the Guatemalan Communist Party (PGT) in Mexico

ACQ: Mexico, Mexico City (11 June 1971)

FIELD NO.: EDF-12,647

SOURCE A member of the PGT who has good contacts with Guatemalan Communists in Mexico. Each of the information which he has collected has been confirmed by other sources.

1. The highest-ranking body of the Guatemalan Communist Party (PGT) organization in Mexico is the "Delegation of the Central Committee." This body consists of Ruberto Alvarado Arellano, Jose Alberto Cardona Aguilar, and Jose Luis Balcarcel Orellana. (Field Consult: CS-311/02978-70 (EDF-12,183), from the same source as this report, reported on the make-up of the PGT organization in Mexico as of April 1970. Cardona and Balcarcel made on the "Delegation of the Central Committee" at that time. FDCS-314/-71 (EGO- ), from a reliable source, provided the information that Alvarado, the Third Secretary of the PGT, planned to reside in Mexico for the immediate future.)

2. Balcarcel also heads the "Directive Committee" of the PGT organization in Mexico, which is responsible for determining what activation the local PGT organization will undertake and who will carry them out. The other members of the "Directive Committee" are Alfonso Solozaro Fernandez and Julio Rodriguez Aldama. (Source Consult: There has been no change in the membership of the "Directive Committee" since April 1970. Although Rodriguez occupies this leadership position, he has been relatively inactive in PGT affairs.)
