FOLLOWING FROM LIT-TIL-7, 25 MARCH: TERESA PROENZA VISITED PERUEN EMPASSY 24 MARCH WITH SISTER CACHITA. SAID SHE RETURNING TO HABANA SOON ON UNSPECIFIED DATE. HER PASSPORT MARKED "PARA DETENTRERSE" BY SU CARGO, BUT L-7 TELLS OF CABLE PRECEDING PROENZA ARRIVAL: "TERESAR A LIQUIDAR SUS ASINTOS. TENIAMOS INFORMADO. EMB STAFF WARNED NOT TO TELL PROENZA OF THE MENTION OF CABLE WHICH INDICATES SOMETHING DONE BEHIND HER BACK DESPITE WHAT SHE MAY HAVE BEEN TOLD. P SAID SHE NOW PREFERS WORK INSIDE PERUENK rather than in an EMS SINCE SHE CAN DO MORE GOOD THERE. L-7 BELIEVES THAT EVEN HABANA NOT SURE WHAT TO DO WITH PROENZA.
