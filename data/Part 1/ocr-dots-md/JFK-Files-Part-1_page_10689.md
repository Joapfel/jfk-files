**COUNTRY:** Haiti/Cuba

**SUBJECT:** Further Activities of Rolando Mauferrer Rojas

**DOI:** Late April 1966

**PUBA:** United States, Miami (27 April 1966)

**SOURCE:** A member of a group of Cuban Emigrés trained in the techniques of information collection. The group has provided useful reports since 1961.

1. According to Enrique Mauferrer Rojas, his brother Rolando Mauferrer Rojas, former Cuban senator who has been planning action against President François Duvalier of Haiti, left Miami, Florida, for New York on 25 April 1966 to settle several matters pending there. He planned to return to Miami afterward to wait for the right time to invade Haiti.
2. Enrique Mauferrer stated that his brother had found somebody who had agreed to transport arms am from the United States to a point where the invasion forces were to meet. Barraquiel and others met with Duvalier at a site in the north to plan an invasion. The operation was to be carried out by a special unit of paratroopers. This unit was to act as a last resort if the normal invasion effort is still pending. According to Enrique Mauferrer, the Haitian who was in Canada arranging various aspects of the operation, had traveled to the United States in the company of two former colonels of the Haitian Army who allegedly had the support of 2000 to 500 Haitian emigres. The Haitian emigrés were to meet at 5 p.m. on a site chosen when the time to initiate the plan came about. The former Haitian colonels have stated that if they succeed in establishing a beachhead in Haiti with the arms and men currently supporting them, Duvalier will leave the country within 24 hours.
