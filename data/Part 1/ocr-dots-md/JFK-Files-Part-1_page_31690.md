SECRET

MAR 27
1953

MEMORANDUM FOR: CHIEF TO DIVISION

SUBJECT: Proposal for establishing a Microfilm Library of Hungarian Reference Material

1. The attached paper outlines a project which may be of interest to your division. A Romanian emigré, interested in propaganda and cultural activities, prepared the paper after consulting with a number of refugee scholars.

2. Although the results of such a project would be of great value to our Romanian operations, implementation is considered beyond the scope of the activities of this Division. Further, it appears more practicable for a project of this type to be undertaken by a research or cultural organization, rather than an intelligence agency, in order that the results may be more widely used.

3. It is suggested that the NCF, which is concerned with worthwhile employment of refugee intellectuals, may be interested in this project for its Mid-European Studies Center. If the NCF is not in a position to finance such a project, it is possible that a privately endowed foundation would be interested. The emigré has discussed this project with Dr. Sergius Jacobson of the Library of Congress and with Professor Mosley of the Russian Institute of Columbia University. He has reported that both agencies appear interested.

4. Because of some doubt concerning the reliability of three of the individuals mentioned in section III of the attached report, the following data is submitted for your information:

a. Dr. Mircea Elia de was formerly editor of an Iron Guard (Romanian fascist group) newspaper and a doctrinaire of the Guard. He is reported to have disassociated himself from the Guard in the last year but this information cannot be confirmed.

b. Dr. Ionin Constantinescu reported as formerly pro-Iron Guardist in his sympathies.
