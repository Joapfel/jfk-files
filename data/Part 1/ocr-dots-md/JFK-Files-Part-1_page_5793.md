1. The following report was received from a source of unknown location.

*In America with the name of (first and last unknown)* COE arrived in Rome in August and stayed there until September 1961 extensively as a tourist. He had his hotel room booked beforehand by the party headquarters, (he lived at the new hotel "International,")

*During his stay there he had several conferences with the Communist Americans resident in Rome, George A., Walter and a CLA.A. C.U. is periodically a Danish man in the fifties with dark hair and rather smallish (about 165 cm.)*

*Regarding the recent B.A. defector to the U.S., Professor Charles COLE, who came to Rome with wife and child, everything was reportedly prepared before his arrival and his hotel room had been booked, his name, birthday, had a punch factor after his arrival.*

2. The CUI referred to above is presumably identical with Charles Joseph COE, the subject of your memorandum of August 21, 1961 from the Baltimore field office. George A., who is presumably identical with a George A., should have come May 22, 1948 in Tokyo, Washington. This may be identical with June 19, 1948, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Professor Charles JONES is presumably identical with the subject of your memorandum of November 13, 1961 captioned Charles Joseph Jone, Josephine Jone, Messrs. Research,
