## ARTICLE III. Overseas Allowance and Transportation Expenses.
When specifically authorized by the ADSO, the expense of travel and transportation of the Employee, his immediate family, household goods, and other allowances, will be paid by the Employee in accordance with the Foreign Service Act of 1948 and Regulations issued thereunder by the Department of State. When authorized by the ADSO, the Employee shall be paid a currier's allowance, cost of living allowance, or special foreign living allowance in accordance with the Foreign Service Act of 1948 and Regulations issued thereunder. The amount of currier's allowance, cost of living allowance, and special foreign living allowance is at fourth in order of the Budget Circular A-8, which it amended per diurnally to reflect adjustments in writing order. This circular was promulgated to explain and authorize this circular to all employees.

## ARTICLE IV. Annual and Sick Leave.
The employee will be entitled annual leave, sick leave, and leave of absence in accordance with Title II of Part D of the Foreign Service Act of 1948. Under such Act, the Employee may be granted not to exceed fifty calendar days Annual Leave of Absence with pay in each year. Annual leave which the Employee does receive and which is not used in any one year shall be accumulated for subsequent years until it totals 780 days. Sick leave with pay may be restored to the Employee at the rate of fifteen calendar days each calendar year and may be re Accumulated for successive years until it reaches 1,580 days.
