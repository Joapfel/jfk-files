SUBJECT: PLANS OF ROLANDO MASFERRER ROJAS, FORMER CUBAN SENATOR, FOR ACTION AGAINST HAITI FROM THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC

ACQ: UNITED STATES, MIAMI (16 JUNE 1956) UFG-8382

SOURCE: FORMER LATIN AMERICAN DIPLOMAT WHO MAINTAINS RELATIONS WITH OFFICIALS OF LATIN AMERICAN COUNTRIES. HE IS TRAINED REPORTER AND HIS INFORMATION HAS BEEN RELIABLE IN THE PAST.

1. ON 13 JUNE 1966 IN MIAMI, FORIDA, ROLANDO MASFERRER ROJAS, FORMER CUBAN SENATOR, SAID THAT HE WAD REVISED HIS PLANS FOR ACTION AGAINST PRESIDENT FRANCISCO DEVALIER OF HAITI TO INCLUDE THE COOPERATION OF SANTIAGO REY PEREA. REY WAD SAID THAT MASFERRER WOULD BE ALLOWED TO TAKE A VESSEL WITH SIX OR SEVEN MEN TO THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC AFTER JOAQUIN ZALAGUER IS INSTALLED AS PRESIDENT. Secrecy
