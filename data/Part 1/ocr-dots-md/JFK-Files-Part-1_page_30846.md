ADMINISTRATION

REF: MILLS-6919 DTD 22 JULY 72

1. JEREMY K. BENADUM REPORTS RECEIPT OF BANK DEPOSIT NOTICE COVERING GOVERNMENT CHECK IN AMOUNT $950.00. ONLY LARGE DEPOSIT BENADUM ANTICIPATES AT PRESENT TIME IS FIRST RETIREMENT ANNUITY CHECK.

2. REF INDICATED ESTIMATE FOR BENADUM ANNUITY $1,282 PER MONTH, WHICH HARD TO RECONCILE WITH $950 DEPOSIT. FACT THAT DEPOSIT FOR SUCH EVEN AMOUNT ALSO INDICATES IT MAY NOT BE FIRST ANNUITY PAYMENT.

3. BENADUM REQUESTS HEADQUARTERS IDENTIFY DEPOSIT IF POSSIBLE, AND PROVIDE EARNINGS AND DEDUCTIONS BREAKDOWN IF APPROPRIATE. BENADUM ALSO REQUESTS CROSS-DEDUCTIONS -NET BREAKDOWN OF ANNUITY PAYMENTS. SP-1 SECRET
