Operations Against ANGEL09 Representative in Canada

Reference: A. URAW 671, 1 December 1969
B. URAW 678, 1 December 1969

1. After his return to Ottawa from Montreal for the ANGEL'S/5 -ANCHORING meeting, BOLivar had a number of extended discussions with SHAROVE headquarters' officers on the general subject of operations against ANGEL09 in Montreal. They had one complaint about the ANGEL'S/5 meeting that we believe is logical and warranted on their part. At our request they had more or less reserved the services of their two surveillance teams in Montreal to cover the ANGEL'S/5 operation. This was based on our early request which in turn was based on the headquarters' called request that SHAROVE give as much coverage as possible to the meeting. When it turned out that it would be impossible to cover the meeting at all with surveillance, SHAROVE felt that they had "reserved" their team for nothing. As you may know, SHAROVE was especially pressed during that period. The Grey Cup football game - an event that compares to our Rose Bowl - was scheduled for the following weekend and they expected trouble from militant separatists, etc. In other words, they were not too happy that we asked for support and then did not use it.

2. Looking back over various records, we find that this situation is not entirely new. We do, however, want to avoid this in the future. In future cases we will have to reach a firm decision on what we want SHAROVE to do for us well before we go to them and ask for support. Once this support is offered, then we should follow through and use it. If not, SHAROVE will soon stop offering to help us in this way. In order to get an operation such as this organized and presented to SHAROVE, we suggest that headquarters make their views known well in advance. If a change is desired after the operation is launched, as happened in the case of the ANGEL'S/5-ANCHORING meeting in Montreal, our liaison relationship with SHAROVE will surely suffer.
