10. In October 1964, the Guatemalan authorities captured a number of tape recordings from the guerrillas in operations subsequent to a guerrilla attack on a garrison in Panzos, Alta Verapaz. Included in those tapes was one made by RICO Galan for delivery to the guerrillas. It consists mainly of an exhortation for unity (among the POT and the 13 November group) and counsel against letting doctrine replace action. The tape clearly reveals RICO to be at once a very leftist who also has his feet planted quite firmly on the ground. His approach is pragmatic, not theoretical, after The text of the tape indicates that it was made sometime between February and March 1964 before mid-October 1964.
