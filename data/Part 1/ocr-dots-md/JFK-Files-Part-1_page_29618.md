16 June 1964

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chairman, Career-Service Board, NPIC
THROUGH : Secretary, Career Service Board, NPIC
SUBJECT : Recommendation for Promotion

1. NAME: Maksymiee, Myroslaw Andrew

2. POSITION TITLE AND PRESENT ASSIGNMENT:
Photog (Gen) occupying Physical Scientist Slot, assigned to the Exploratory Development Laboratory Branch of the Plans and Development Staff.

3. PRESENT GRADE, TIME IN CIA:

GS-7/3, EOD Date November 1961

4. EDUCATION AND TRAINING: High School

5. EXPERIENCE:

Mr. Maksymiec retired as a Master Sergeant in July 1961, after 20 years of Army photographic experience. He worked in PSD/PIB from November 1961 until 15 September 1963. On this date he was assigned to the Exploratory Development Laboratory Branch of the Plans and Development Staff on a 30-day trial basis. This period was extended to January, 1964 at which time the assignment was made permanent.
