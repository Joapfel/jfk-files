NOV 19 1965

MIAMI, FLA., NOV. 19 (AP)-A SHARP DECLINE IN SHIPPING FROM NON-COMMUNIST COUNTRIES TO CUA WAS REPORTED TODAY.

ONLY SEVEN VESSELS FROM THESE AREAS TOUCHED CUBAN PORTS LAST MONTH, SAID REVOLUTIONARY UNITY (CU), AN EXILE ORGANIZATION WHICH WATCHES SUCH MOVEMENTS. UPON THIS IS THE SMALLEST HARMFUL SINCE IT BEGAN KEEPING COUNT FOUR YEARS AGO.

LISTED AS VISITING CUFA IN OCTOBER WERE THREE SHIPS FROM CYPRUS, TWO FROM GREAT BRITAIN AND TWO FROM GREECE.

THIS COMPARES WITH 17 SHIPS THE PREVIOUS MONTH. OTHER OCTOBER TOTALS: 21 IN 1965, 23 IN 1964, AND 26 IN 1963.

URSAID IT DID NOT KNOW WHAT CAUSED THE DROP.
PLAASAES
