GLASS

1. Shanghai Muni Police files, filed, 16-4-2-37, p 2

In Shanghai GLASS made the acquaintance of one Damaree C.B.II., American from Kansas City, born 1933. This was some time after April 1932.

Name O.P. GLASS Cecil Frank OLASS
Born 25 March 1901, Birmingham, England

Passport British passport, # 94153, issued 5 Jan 1931 in Pretoria, South Africa
Pol Affiliation Number of the CP
Overt Profession Journalist

Believed to have left England in the early 1920s for South Africa where he resided at Johannesburg for many years.

Arrived in Shanghai from Dartmoor May 1931

On 12 June 1931 made a trip through up the Yangtze to Shanghai with Harold ISMUS, American Communist and writer-for-the editor of the China Forum. A letter written by ISAACS to his fiancée, Miss Viola ROBINSON, during this trip contained information that ISAACS had decided to turn "Red" as a result of his conversation with GLASS.

In Sep. 1931, GLASS became a member of the "CENTRAL DEFENSE COMMITTEE" and two months later was appointed Shanghai Representative of the Anglo-Asiatic Telegraph Agency (Angasta) a news agency with HQ in Hainan, which position he held until 1933.

In Aug 1933, he was living in Robm 510, # 1276, North Suenchem Road, Shanghai.
