15 February 1969

The change had been made as the result of instructions which had just been received from the PGT in Guatemala. The change was two-fold. CARDOZA is a member of the central committee of the PGT whereas BALCARCEL is not, and BALCARCEL has been lax in his duties, once being unavailable when some important PGT members passed through Mexico. However, BALCARCEL will continue to act as chief of the exile group until CARDOZA is on his feet again since the latter had suffered another heart attack and was confined to his bed at home. Until this change CARDOZA had been the central committee representative in Mexico and had functioned as an advisor (15). Presumably BALCARCEL replaced GUTIERREZ and CARDOZA replace BALCARCEL although on 4 September 1968, CARDOZA was to take GUTIERREZ' place in Mexico (GUTIERREZ was killed in Guatemala in 1966) (15).
