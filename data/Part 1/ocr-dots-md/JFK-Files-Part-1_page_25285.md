10. An appointment was made for KIKOFORTS to see SANCHEZ at 12:00 on 24 September. KIKOFORTS also wanted to see Corandante OROZCO but the latter had not yet returned from Tanaulipas.

11. On 24 September KALLIN informed Ricardo RADOS Flores, then Army 0-2, of the proposal of the Ambassador 'for this meeting' on 29 September. RADOS Flores agreed to inform the appropriate persons.

12. On 27 September ROSCHIL took a message for RAIZOV that (lt.) Jesus RAIZO Reguero of Comision Inspectora had to go out of town and could not meet with RAIZOV that day.

13. On 28 September Yuriy Alekseyovich CHERNOV talked to Col. RADOS Flores and asked if (General) TOnas SAIGEZ Hernandez were coming to the reception at the Embassy the following day. However SAIGEZ had left for Toluca and Tenancingo, and Col. Eduardo SOBRETO Quintero, Chief of Staff Air Force, would attend alone, as his wife was ill. In all, four persons with wives would attend and SAIGEZ without his wife. RADOS Flores mentioned that (Col.) Rosendo ESPARZA Arias would be taking his place on the first of January, and asked if they would like for him to invite ESPARZA also. However, GELPANOV apparently did not understand, and they decided only on those mentioned previously.

14. On 29 September ROSCHIL contacted Alejandro LOSADA of the Second Section of the Navy and asked why they hadn't sent for any motion pictures. LOSADA answered that the projector had been out of order for about a month, and was being fixed at Casa Steele. ROSCHIL said that they had some new pictures, and that BALTARAZ should let them know when they wanted some.
