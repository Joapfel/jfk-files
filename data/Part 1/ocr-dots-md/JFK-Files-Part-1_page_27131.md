25 January 1974

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: DCD Contact with Frank Sturgis of the Watergate Plumbers Group

Frank Sturgis contacted DCD in Miami recently and requested a meeting. DCD, who apparently did not recognize the name at first, agreed to meet with him. The DCD meeting with Sturgis has been cleared with George Carey, CIA Legal Counsel, and was cleared with the DDO on 25 January 1974. No specific date for the meeting was given to WH, but it is assumed that the meeting will take place within the next few days.

Orville Bathe
