2. There have been no new developments in France on this matter. Our source has been given a facsimile of the PORORILIY letter, but there has been no report on its delivery to Antin HICZPORUK (see attachment).

3. We have received the following biographic information:

a. Daytro CHYP was born on 13 October 1898 at Maasycyna Rohatyn, Western Ukraine. He is married and the father of two sons, one of whom repatriated to the Ukraine in 1948 and the other of whom lives in France and is a member of the Ukrainian nationalist organization - Spilka Ukrainskoji Molodii (The Union of Ukrainian Youth in France). CHYP's education consists of four years of elementary schooling in the Ukraine. In 1918 he served as a non-commissioned officer in the Ukrainian Army in a formation called the Situschovi Strilci. CHYP emigrated to France in 1925. He has been a member of two Ukrainian communist organizations in France; in 1927 he joined the Soyus Ukrainskych hromadians u Pranoji (Union of Ukrainian Citizens in France), and from 1945 to 1947 he was a member of the Batkiwtechyna (Homeland). His address for the past two years has been 211 rue de Bercy, Paris VIIIe.
