MENORANDUM FOR: Chief, Information and Privacy Staff
SUBJECT: James G. Bruce
REFERENCE: Request No. 75-5742

This Directorate has no file on James G. Bruce.
Two Agency documents, however, were surfaced. These
documents are denied under (b)(1), as being properly
classified under F.O. 11652 and (b)(3) (sources and
methods) FOIA. Release of the portion concerning
Subject would probably identify the source to him.
Thus, these documents must be denied in full.

Charles A. Briggs
Chief, Services Staff
