## Mexican Communist Party and Communist Fronts

A significant amount of information was received from LIP AT during the past year concerning the Mexican Communist Party and Communist Party front organizations. The latter included information on the Círculo de estudios Mexicanos, the Mexican Peace Movement, the Society of Friends of China and various art fronts. This information has been of value in supplementing absent coverage of these organizations. The following reports are examples of this type of information:

## American Communists in Mexico

One of the most extensive and productive coverages initiated through LIP AT during the past year was directed against American Communists in Mexico. Most of those targets were covered at the request of ODEMY and the production was processed in part by ODEMY. A total of 342 reels of information were recorded on three separate cases. A fourth case, LCMAYPO-L, was mounted at the suggestion of ODEMY just 15½ hours after the station received the request from Headquarters. The Station has received three communications from Headquarters on the LIP AT coverage of this case and Edward P. SACHEN received a personal commendation from the DCI for his work on this operation. Over 90 reels of valuable information have been produced since the beginning of this operation on 9 February 1957.
