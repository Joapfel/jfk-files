29 April 1965

23.0 contd.

"France Presse," for Sr. Katz. His secretary is not in yet, but should be in soon - Sr. Alvarez. MO is Jorge Vasquez, an editor. Would like to invite any one of them to Dominican press conference. MMA explaining they are a group of exiles. The Dominican Embassy is also having a conference today (of press). Does MO know what the Amb. has said? No, nothing yet. They made no comments yesterday. What was the last cable from Santo Domingo? That the supporters of Bosch were attacking the airport, San Isidro. Things have changed greatly (in their favor) this morning. Gives MO the details about where the press conference is to be....

23.0 Out to 35-63-99. Marcio Mejia Ricart with MO, who answers, for Sr. Rosenberg. MO answers "Press Association" (AP). Rosenberg is on another phone. Best call back. Ask if they have had any news from Santo Domingo. Have not heard anything. Ask him to phone back in a few minutes.

23.5 Out to 48-91-33. Marcio Mejia Ricart with MO, who answers (indistinctly), for Head of Information. Wants to invite him to press conference on Dominican affairs...

MO takes the phone. MMA says there is a group of exiles here, among whom is the group of 14 de July, the Central Committee, here... They have a lot of information to give..invites him to press conference at 1600....Thanks.

24.2 Out to 45-44-40. Marcio Mejia Ricart asks MO, who answers at "Ovacionos", for Sr. Lopez Antunez (?ph).

LA takes the phone. Victor Rico Galan takes the phone.
Greetings. Asks if LA has any info on the Dominican activities.
The O.E.A. is now in meeting where the Americans are going to explain
why they demand disembarked there. The situation in Santo Domingo
appears that Bosch's troops continue in control. VRO was told that
there was a France Press cable that they were attacking San Isidro -
military and civil men. MO has not seen this yet.

The Dominican exiles here are giving a press conference this afternoon and VRO needs MO's help. This is at the house of Dr. Marcio Mejia Ricart, Ave. Valerio Trujano 325, Apt. H-3, Colonia Florida. MO will send a reporter.

When will they see one another? Does VRC still go to the café of...No, where does VRO go now? To none, He had coffee at home. MO will call VRO and come by to see him.
