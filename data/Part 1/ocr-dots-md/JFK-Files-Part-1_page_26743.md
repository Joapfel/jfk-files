**Aool 72-KICO-28**

3 May 1963

5.0 Out to 03. Time is 1210, 3 May.

5.2 Out to 11-07-75. said with MO, who answers, for Toribia... Not there...

5.3 Out to 24-92-11. said asks MO, who answers, for Anita. Anita takes the phone. said inside is Costa...About not seeing her yesterday...what they did...about changing jobs... After 1325, 3 May

8.0 In. MO asks said, who answers, for VKG. Inge lico takes the phone. VKG is not in.

8.2 In. MO, (identifying hi-self indistinctly) with Inge lico, who answers, for VKG. Not there. Will be home for lunch. MO will call back later.

8.5 In. Sra. lico asks said, who answers, for VKG. Inge. VKG takes the phone. About visiting Anita...children...

9.0 In. MO asks Inge lico, who answers, for VKG. Not there. MO wants to see him. Will call back around 1700.

9.2 In. Carmen with Inge lico, who answers. Asks for VKG. Not there. She had expected him for lunch (but did not come).

9.5 In. Irina with Inge lico, who answers. Greetings. Signuelito went out to play with his grandmother...Only Maria (other child) is there...VKG has been very busy.
What does Inna think about that situation (in Dominican Republic)? Terrible.
The speech (of Fidal) was very good. Inge agrees.

9.7 Out to 03. Time is 1608, 3 May.

10.0 In. MO (very faint) with Inge lico, who answers. VKG has not come back. MO saw him at the magazine but when MO returned, VKG had left. (MO is Luis.) VKG was having coffee with Luis Gutierrez and others of magazine when MO left...He went somewhere to eat but IN does not know. VKG still has not had any news from Dominican Republic... Ask VKG to call MO when he comes in. After 1710, 3 May

10.2 In. Long Distance (from Habana) is calling VKG. Call back in an hour.

10.5 In, from long distance. VKG is not there, but will be back in an hour. Inge lico will have his report in.

10.7 Oveig 19-93-28. marcue asks said, who answers, for KENNY Sra. Not there. Abaxar is at house of Sra. Aguilar. MAXIMILIAN MEXICO He has wrong number.

11.0 In. Alberto Domingo asks Inge lico, who answers, for VKG. Not there. AD wanted to ask VKG about the t.v. raffle..would like to know if it is going to take place or not. AD is leaving the house now. Was an appt. at 1800. Will phone back later.
