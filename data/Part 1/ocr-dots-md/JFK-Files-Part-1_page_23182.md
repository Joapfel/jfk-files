THIS IS UNVERIFIED INFORMATION:

(St. de): Cuban national, A 17 357 520, graduate medical doctor. Source: A specialist in psychiatry and served on the staff of Mazarron Hospital in Havana for over 20 years. He also had a large private practice. Source arrived in the U.S. via airlift on 14 April 66.

This report was developed by an representative assigns to the office of preparation./

At 6 of June 1966 rumors are present concerning Fidel Castro's mental illness and a U.S. wire service has reported that he is undergoing mental treatment in the USNR.

Although I have no first-hand knowledge, I do not believe it could be the case. There is still considerable psychological anxiety in Cuba, particularly Dr. Jose Angel Húmez who, as reported by Castro's secretary, says he is receiving electric shock treatments because it would be too soon since the recent release of treatments he received to have received in Cuba before.

Based on my long professional experience, I believe that a full course of mental diffusion following such a series of electro-shock treatments would be necessary to be handled by medication. There are a number of medications which could be used and which are available in Cuba.
