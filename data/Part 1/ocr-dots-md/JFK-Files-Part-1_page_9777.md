Subject: LIRING/4 Report of Meeting with Manuel Marcue Fardinas.

1. Rowton met with LIKING/4 for lunch at the Punto Blanco restaurant on Melchor Ocampo at 1300 on 27/4/68. Rowton had given up the safe-room in the Hotel Suites Marne earlier that morning since he felt it was not longer necessary.

2. LIRING/4 reported that Manuel Marcué Fardinas, the uncle of Carlos Persabal Marcué, had telephoned her the previous evening "frontier street" and arranged to see her privately at the Koala Restaurant (nowo Toleba hotel) later in the evening. According to LIKING/4, Marcué told her that through his appeal to Carrillo Flores the case of LIKING/3 and his nephew was now in diplomatic channels and he hoped that both prisoners could be simply deported after a summary trial. He could not, however, guarantee anything, especially the length of time involved, and asked LIKING/4 to refrain from any activity that might prejudice the case. Specifically he instructed LIKING/4 to break off all contact with the Guatemalan revolutionaries in Mexico, the LIRING establishment, and all communication with the Mexican Consul in Guatemala City. He specifically stressed that any false move on LIRING/4's part might cause Carrillo Flores to withdraw his intervention. Marcué promised to keep in touch with LIRING/4 so that she would not feel completely helpless. He added that the mother of Carlos Persabal Marcué (supposedly Manuel Marcué Fardinas' sister) would be traveling to Guatemala City in the next few days so that she could visit her son and follow the case at first hand.

3. LIRING/4 also reported to Rowton that Rodolfo Sandoval of the Ym Sosa group had promised to visit her at her apartment the previous evening at 2200 in the company of Sergio Valdez to discuss matters further. He had informed her on the phone in making the date that "El Gordo" had returned from Guatemala City - implying that the latter had news concerning the fate of Francisco Lara (whose arrest was not reported in the press, nor by Octavio Serrano, the Mexican Consul, to LIKING/4). LIRING/4 suggested that Francisco Lara had escaped arrest and his involvement with LIRING/3 was not known. She therefore agreed with Marcué that she should not see Sandoval and the other Guatemalans in public, and especially not at her apartment.
