10 March 1953

was one of the signers of an open letter to the Soviet Government and people expressing sympathy on the death of Stalin. The letter was published in the 30 March 1953 edition of La Hora (3).

18 March 1953

was a member of the greeting party at the Guatemala City Airport to meet Vicente LOPEZ TOLEDO and Henri JOUDEAUX (VETU directors who stopped on the way to CTAL Congress in Santiago) (3).

As early as April
1953

was a member of the Partido Guatemalteco del Trabajo (PGT) (2, 3). As of 18 June 1953, he was serving as a Congressional deputy to the National Congress from the Department of Guatemala, having been elected on the ticket of the PGT. His term was to expire in 1955 (2, 3).

18 June 1953

was regularly receiving the News Bulletin of the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City at the address of the Communist-dominated Sindicato de Artes Graficos (SAG) (3).

October 1953

was scheduled to act as Secretary General of the CGTG while Victor Manuel GUTIÉRREZ and four other CGTG members attended the World Federation of Trade Unions' Third World Labor Conference, which was to meet in Vienna, Austria, in October 1953, according to the Communist newspaper Tribuna Popular (5).

1954

was in Caracas and acting as Secretary General of CGTG. He denounced the U.S. proposal to put the question of Communist activity in the Western Hemisphere on the agenda of the Tenth International.

As of 20 January
1954

was a member of the presidium of the Second Guatemalan Syndical Congress (19).

28 January 1954

was among the members of the presidium at the Second Guatemalan Syndical Congress which was inaugurated in the Teatro America before a large audience (28).

February 1954

was a delegate to a mass meeting in Mexico City which was sponsored by the Society of Friends of Guatemala (5).

5 May 1954

met Obdulio BARTHE and family at the La Aurora Airport, Guatemala City and was accompanied by other Guatemalan Communist leaders and leftists (9).

29 June 1954

requested for himself and his family asylum in the Mexican Embassy in Guatemala.

14 September 1954

he and his family appeared before Licenciado Gustavo GARCIA Guerrero, Assistant Chief of the Immigration Department, Department of the Interior, in Mexico City for the purpose of clarifying the matter of his internment and stay in Mexico (20).

30 October 1954

was listed as an exile on whom extradition proceedings had been filed (4).
