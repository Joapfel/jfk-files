## Recruitment of FRIPPEL (1959-1963)

As a member of the American-British Commonwealth Section, NOSENKO in 1959 personally participated in the recruitment of the [American Express Company] representative in Moscow, Arsene FRIPPEL. He continued to be one of this agent's two KGB handlers, even after transferring to the American Department in 1960. [FRIPPEL] left Moscow in 1961 but returned on trips to the USSR in 1962 and 1963, when NOSENKO met him. The only recruited American whom NOSENKO ever claimed to handle, [FRIPPEL] confessed to his recruitment when questioned by the FBI on the basis of the NOSENKO lead. Although the [FRIPPEL] case only started during NOSENKO's first assignment to the Seventh Department (1955-1960), it is described in its entirety through 1963, for the convenience of the reader.

## NOSENKO'S INFORMATION

At his second meeting with CIA in Geneva on 11 June 1962, NOSENKO described the circumstances in which an unnamed foreigner was compromised by a KGB female agent, Klara Konstantinovna GORBACHEVA. He described the scene in detail including the attempt by a KGB technician to take motion pictures through a two-way mirror, and the confrontation of the foreigner by the militiamen, who broke into the room. NOSENKO identified that foreigner as [FRIPPEL] by name the following day while discussing KGB successes in Moscow:
