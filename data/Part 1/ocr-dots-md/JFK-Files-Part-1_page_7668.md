2. Contacts

A. Subject and KGB Case Officers

First contact by the Soviets on Subject's return to Mexico was effected by KGB case officer Aleksandr Vlastimirovich Irkhodko (201-20379) on 3 December in Subject's office for a few minutes. On 8 December, Volentin Sergueyevich Ioginov (201-2044), Subject's regular KGB case officer, called Subject down to the street outside Subject's office to present him with Bolshot Bullet tickets. On 19 December, Volimin visited Subject's office and spent no more than half an hour there. And on 20 or 21 December, Subject was paid a brief visit by the KGB officer (possible candidate for resident) (201-120123) at Subject's office.

B. Subject at Mexico City Station Case Officer

In the two months since Subject's return from Europe in mid-November, 1966, C/O, Assistant Chief, Police, has seen Subject six times for an approximately one hour's time.

The C/O met Subject at his office on 22 November for two hours.

In December, the C/O met Subject three times, on 8 December at his office for almost two hours, on 19 December at the Hotel Reforma for a half-hour, and on 22 December at Subject's office for an hour.

On 7 January 1967, the C/O and his wife took Subject to a city dinner at the Hotel Reforma. One afternoon, Subject went to Subject's home. On 12 January, the C/O met Subject again at the latter's office for one hour.
