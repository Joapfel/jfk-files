May 25, 1965

To whom it may concern:

I hereby accept to be employed by
the government of the Republic of the Congo or any compa-
ny or agency that the said Government may designate at a
basic salary of 31,000 for sixty hours of flying.

The date of my contract is May 25, 1965.

Any flight time in excess of sixty hours is considered
overtime. This is only a provisional statement and should
be consider cancelled as soon as I signed my contract.

Antonio Soto /
