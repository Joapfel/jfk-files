MENTIONED PARA TWO BUT CRITICS INTO SUBJECT SUPPLIED ON DOMINICAN LIVING IN CUBA AND CUBAN PERSONALITIES KNOWN TO HIM WHICH WILL BE FORWARDER SEPARATE CAUSE. BEFORE LEAVING DOMREP IN 1947, SUBJECT WORKED FOR ESSO STANDARD OIL AND WAS BEEN PRIVATE IN RIGHT COMPANY DOMINICAN AIR COMMANDED BY CAPTAIN SANTOS MELI DO MARTE PICHARDO. LEFT DOMREP IN LAUNCH (GOLETA) WHICH HE SAILLED TO DUTCH WENT INDIES (SUBJECT WAS A "CARVEY CH GENTLEMAN"). FROM THERE, SUBJECT WENT TO UNITED STATES WHERE HE WORKED FOR "F. RINALDI AND COMPANY", 116 BROAD STREET, NEW YORK CITY (SUBJECT WAS SOCIAL SECURITY CARD NO. 850-28-1639). IN 1951, SUBJECT RECEIVED CUBAN VISA IN NEW YORK ON "LETTER OF IDENTITY AND TRANSIT" GIVEN BY U.S. AUTHORITIES AND LEFT MIAMI FOR HAVANA 19 JULY 1951. SUBJECT WAS PHOTOGRAPHER IN CUBA AND WORKED FOR PUBLICITY AGENCY. SUBJECT FOUNDED WITH CASTRO IN THE ESCAMARRAY FRONT UNDER COMMAND OF ARMANDO FLEITAS DIAZ. IN 1961, WAS POLICE SERGEANT AND CHIEF OF WELFARE SECTION AND WAS ARMED WITH 16 APRIL OF THAT YEAR "LUN TO THE DAY OF PIGS LANDING" AND ACCUSED OF CONSPIRING AGAINST CASTRO. SUBJECT SENTENCED IN 1963 TO "SUSPENDED MINOR PENALTY" BUT LATER CAVE REVIEW AND SENTENCED TO THIRTY YEARS IN PRISON. AFTER SPENDING FIVE YEARS
