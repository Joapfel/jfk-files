BRAZIL.-

I.- Actual situation of the Delegation.

For the better understanding of the situation of the DXS in Brazil, we will first consider the situation of the DXE Delegation at Rio de Janeiro and then the labor of the Delegation at Sao Paulo.

As well as the Delegation in Caracas, the Delegation in Rio de Janeiro has succeeded in carrying, through a labor which is worthy of all praise.

In spite of the luctiosnerasy of the Brazilian people and the seriousness of the interior situation of the nation, we believe that the Delegation at Brazil follows the Delegation at Venezuela in best functionin. We could point out as main reasons for this success the following:

1.- The members of the Delegation are fully identified with the reality and problems of the Brazilian nation. All the members of the Delegation speak for these.

2.- As well as in Venezuela there is "team work" and unity in the accomplishment of labor.

Indubitably, the labor at Brazil is the hardest to be carried tona, followed by Chile. The luctiosnerasy of the Brazilian people, which are in general somewhat important about most serious problems the world is actually encountering, the powerful forces of extreme-left, the desperate economic situation product of the rapid depression which has more than once almost drawn the nation to civil war, are the reasons for which many times the Cuban problem has been placed in second level, generally giving it little importance even inside democratic sectors.

For approximately one year, the Delegation at Rio de Janeiro has been struggling against this real difficult situation, and the results obtained cannot be better.
