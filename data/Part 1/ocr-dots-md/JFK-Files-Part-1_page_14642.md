## Item # 6

A call from a friend in Paris. The last answer and says that the R.I.A. are leaving this afternoon for 15 days and won't be returning till the beginning of next month.

Report No. 1938 9 October 1969

## Item # 3

Dr. P. FELL C.U.RS. DEPT. P.R.I.C.

Hilda tells the doctor that yesterday she had to take Gudmundsson(Rúni) to the doctor. Dr. Perin asks if he is ill. Hilda says not really but he has liver trouble and is forcing him to go to the doctor.

## Item # 4

BOSTON, MA. 6. JGS R.J. PROJ. BRING T.S.

Here, - While Ruiz is waiting for the call he can be heard saying to Horacio "The doctor says my liver is inflamed and that I must take a rest. So, I am going to Lisbon--".

Report No.1939 9 October 1969

## Item # 3

CAPTAIN ROBERT ELLIS C.I.RS. CHS. ST. DA. IN PARIS, Q: "Yesterday the delegate was here and I sent C.I.R.I. to talk to him. He said he was going to London to wait for you and from there he is going to 'that country/'Paris/, and that within a week he was going to a turn and work with you here in Paris". Q. "I haven't seen him. I said that I would be in Milan, if he should happen to ask you". "We agreed to meet one night, but I had to go to Grenoble and he didn't come."
