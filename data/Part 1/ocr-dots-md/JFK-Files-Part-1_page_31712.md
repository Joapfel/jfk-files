10 February 1953

ATTACHMENT FOR: DEPUTY DIRECTOR (ACCIDENTATION)

VIA: General Counsel

SUBJECT: Redesignation of Mr. Francisco B. Stevens

1. It is requested that approval be given to relative Mr. Francis B. 'Levins' of the Department of State for a trip to France, New York. This trip was made at our request and with the approval of the DIP and CCI, to approach Mr. Levins to discuss a possible replacement for Mr. F. J. Folchero, head of the vision for Middle East matters of the National Committee for a Free Europe.

2. Mr. Stevens was asked to do this work because of his close personal friendship with Mr. Levis. r. Stevens is also acting in the capacity of a State Department contact with the National Councils for a time in Europe.

3. An accounting of Mr. Stevens' committee is attached. The airline status, to be attached to a weather report, will be kept in this office, pending approval of this request.
