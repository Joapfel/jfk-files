**From 2230, 1 May (Saturday) to 0845, 2 May 1965**

2.0 Out to 09. Victor Rico Galan (VKG) with long distance operator. VKG has been reporting in from the phone of a friend's house, but he is now at home. As soon as they have communication to Cuba, call him here. With Sr. Orlando Fundora ur with Sr. Vinas, radio Habana.

3.7 In, from long distance. They still don't have communication with Cuba. Will he continue to wait? Yes.

4.0 In, from long distance. Ask with whom VKG wants to talk to in Cuba? With Fundora or Vinas? Yes. VKG takes the phone. Says there is no one by the name of Fundora or Vinas in Radio Habana. Of course, they are the Director and Sub-Director of Radio Habana.

Finally gets through to Fundora. VKG says that they talked to Bosch last night - at 2330. VKG couldn't call him because line was out to Cuba. Bosch made sensational declarations. MO will record it.

They change phones. They asked Bosch what his opinion was of the O.E.A. Commission. VKG did not talk, but a Dominican friend talked to Bosch. However, they recorded the conversation. Bosch said some important things. This friend asked Bosch what he thought of OEA Commission. Bosch said that O.E.A. and nothing are the same thing. Who talked to Bosch. Marcio Mejia Ricart. They then asked Bosch if they / the U.S. marines had occupied the city. He said they had and they had gotten as far as Avenida Nella, near downtown area, but that the armed civilians forced them back until Avenue Maximo Gomez, about 3-1/2 kilometers back. Ave. Maximo Gomez is what one might call their combat line since it is the zone where the U.S. Embassy is. So, they made them return to their original pack positions. This took place yesterday afternoon. But the most important thing - they asked Bosch what political solution Bosch saw. He answered "textually - 'It appears that in Washington they are thinking of converting Santo Domingo into Pideicomiso.' Then he commented ironically, so "we get out of colonialism into independence." VKG says that this is of course very grave. As F. knows there are 2 or 3 left in the world - of Pideicomisos - in Southern Africa. This would be unprecedented case - that they convert an independent country into a country under Pideicomiso (Protectorates). When Mejia Ricart asked if they (U.S.) want to convert "us into a colony." Bosch replied that - Yes, into a colony but with 25,000 armed men. Civilians, of course. Bosch further said that "what the people had done was something extraordinary and that he had complete faith in the people." H. did not want to say anything to provoke the uprising of the people because he (Bosch) is out of the country "and from outside, this is a comfortable role. If I were inside I would take up the responsibilities."

VKG had to pay the call since Fundora did not call him. VKG will be ruined; this, so Fundora should have a call always in for VKG.

VKG will let him know when he has anything new.

F. asks what the situation is inside Dominican republic. There is nothing new. They are expecting a call from Dominican republic today. VKG may have something in 2 hours. In this case, VKG will call. F. will then call VKG back at 2300, Habana time, or 2200, Mexico time. (Or 0900, 1000 hours).
