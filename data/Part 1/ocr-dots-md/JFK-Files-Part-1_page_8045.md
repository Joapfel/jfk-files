CONFIDENTIAL
SECURITY

MEMORANDUM

January 28, 1952

To: The Ambassador

From: E. T. CRAIN

Subject: Dr. Fortell Villa wants Embassy that Octodoro left-wingers are trying to bring Communists into the party.

Dr. Fortell Villa called today at the Embassy and during the course of the conversation asked if his grave preoccupation regarding the efforts of Octobere left-wingers to bring Communists into the party.

He stated that Fardo Linda, Eduardo Corona, Vicentina Antufa, Max Kernik and others were trying to get Communists into the October party. He said that, specifically, they were trying to get the notorious Communist, Salvador Garcia Aguirre, not only into the October party but to run as an October candidate for senator. He said that a group of October approache him on the matter to learn his views and he told them he would resist such a move with all his strength.

Fortell Vila said he considered the matter so urgent that he at once wrote an article entitled "The Octoberites" which he was going to try to get into El Perú of January 20 instead of Wednesday the 30th which is the day of the week when his column usually appears. He said this would be an extension of his article published January 20 in El Perú.

I told Fortell Vila that while the Embassy maintained a strict neutral attitude in the matter of Cuban politics, it would have a very deep interest in anything pertaining to the Communists when we considered an enemy common to all free countries and free national political parties. I congratulated him on his efforts to keep Communists out of his own October party and wished him every success in this undertaking. I said I assumed that if the party announced notorious Communists as October candidates for office that the party would presumably lose a lot of support from persons who did not wish to be associated with Communists. I added that I hoped my assumption was not mere wishful thinking. He stated that it was definitely not wishful thinking. He stated that it was definitely not wishful thinking and that he exactly what would happen. He said that he would revert entirely opposite the efforts of Fardo Linda and company with all the strength at his command, regardless of whether an association with Communists won or lost votes for the October party.
