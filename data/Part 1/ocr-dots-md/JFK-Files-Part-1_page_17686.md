SECRET

3 July 1957

MECRADETUM FUP: Chief, EE/Czechoclovakia
SUBJECT: Project LIFETAT

1. The WH/Mexico Desk is now in the process of requesting the annual renewal of Project LIFETAT for the period 1 July 1957 - 30 June 1958.

2. During the past year a considerable amount of operational and intelligence information from the LIVINY phase of this project has been forwarded to your office. In view of the pending project renewal, it would be appreciated if you would forward to the WH/Mexico Desk at your earliest convenience a memorandum evaluating this project with regard to your area and sphere of interest.

J. C. KING
Chief, MHD
