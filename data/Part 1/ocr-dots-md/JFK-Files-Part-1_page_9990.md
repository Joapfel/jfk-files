**Cap:** A letter? This is very important. Can you tell me who this man is?

**L/3:** Yes. His name is Nédiés Crozco Teal, and he has lived many years in Mexico.

**Cap:** Ah, yes, I know this man. He was arrested recently by the Mexican police and released Friday. I have been informed of this by his family.

**L/3:** Then things are worst than I imagined. Who knows what he has revealed to the Mexicans about my business connections.

**Cap:** Please do not be too worried. I have been given to understand that Crozco, who is a valiant person, conducted himself in a commendable manner. I doubt that he revealed anything about you.
