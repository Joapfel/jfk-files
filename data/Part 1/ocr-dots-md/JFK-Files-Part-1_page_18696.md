RECONCILIATION F.O.B: Deputy Director for Jans

SUBJECT : Expression of Appreciation and Condolence for the Intelligence Watch.

1. I wish to express my appreciation and personal praise for the performance of the FI/Intelligence Watch during the height of the recent Middle East crisis. I am informed that the Arab-Israeli War has an aftermath were primary factors which resulted in the Watch process ring for the month of June the highest number of dispositions since its establishment. The Watch was particularly helpful to this Division in ensuring that the dissemination of the reporting on the Middle East situation was not only rapidly disseminated locally to the Intelligence Community but also promptly, reliably to interested overseas military commands and CINB field stations.

2. The judgment shown by Watch officers, often under trying circumstances, was invariably sound and careful. The worth of the Watch, particularly during the crisis situation, was once more demonstrated to all of us in HE Division.

JAMES H. CRITCHFORD
Chief, Near East and
South Asia Division
