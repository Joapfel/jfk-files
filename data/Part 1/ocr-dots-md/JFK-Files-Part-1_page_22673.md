OUT75968

TO MEXI INFO HAVA CITE DIR 14189

RE MEXI 3392 (IN 22960)*

1. ON BASIS HAVA AND ODEVY REPORTS ON HOGAN HAS PREFER NOT IMPROVE HIS CHANCES SECURING CUBAN ACCOUNT. ACCORDING ANNAIL, CUBAN OFFICIAL IN HOS AREA 20 FEBRUARY, AN ACCOUNT MAY GO TO EITHER OF TWO FIRMS IN PBPRIME WITH WHICH KUBARK HAS CONTACT AND EITHER OF WHICH WOULD SERVE ODYOKE INTERESTS BETTER THAN HOGAN.

2. HOWEVER, IN EVENT HOGAN BEATS OUT OTHER CONTENDERS IT COULD BECOME ADVANTAGEOUS HAVE KUBARKER IN HIS GROUP, DEPENDING ON NATURE OF ACCOUNT. THEREFORE/ REQUEST NOEMAYR ATTEMPT KEEP JOB OFFER OPEN WITHOUT COMMITTING HIMSELF TO HOGAN AND THEREBY STRENGTHENING LATTER'S HAND WITH CUBANS. USE CAUTION IN TOUCHING ON WEAK ASPECTS NOEMAYR COVER STORY WHICH COULD BE CHECKED ON BY HOGAN.

3. MICKEY IS GEORGE MICHAEL KAPPES, ABOUT 24, U.S. CITIZEN WHO HAS WORKED WITH HOGAN SINCE LATE 1958

(END OF MESSAGE)

WH Comment:

*Noemayr received letter dated 1 Feb from "Mickey" bounding out Noemayr for job with Don Hogan, who hopes to get major public relations contracts with Cubans.

WH/III/Mexico Paul Zientz for J.B. R. H. DAllitto
