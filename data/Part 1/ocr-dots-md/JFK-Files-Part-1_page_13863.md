January 1964

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

FROM : Lawrence F. Barker

SUBJECT : Meeting with LITAMIL-9, 6 January 1964

1. A meeting was held with L-9 from 2000 hours until 2320 hours 6 Jan -- pick-up and drop-off were made by car, the meeting took place at B's home with B's wife working the gate.

2. L-9 was unusually depressed -- had just received a letter from Spain indicating that his sister had been forced to leave her work as a result of excessive fatigue and the "never ending round of discussions and study circles" etc. She had not received much sympathy from the Cuban FOMIN and her request for 2 months leave in Spain was denied pending further study. L-9, who is quite fond of his sisters, took this news to heart and it ate away at him and compounded his usual miserable feeling. The first part of the meeting was taken up in a C/O delivered pop talk.

3. L-9 had the following to report:

a. At the evening reception of 2 Jan at the Embassy, the TASS representative remarked that he believed KHRUSHCHEV did not travel to Cuba to attend the anniversary celebrations because of the revelation of an exile plot to bomb Havana -- revelations which came about as the result of the arrest by the US Coast Guard of some war-like Cubans with bombs in their possession. The TASS man said that he believed that KHRUSHCHEV would show up someday in Havana quite unannounced as did CASTRO in the USSR.

b. Teresa PROENZA may be hired back by the Embassy -- APARICIO recently remarked to L-9, "Surely they will re-hire her." It is quite clear that APARICIO doesn't want her in his office again and he expressed the wish that if she were to be rehired she would work in the main embassy building.

c. In regard to the PROENZA firing, ROJAS of Cubana de Aviacion, who is married to Teresa's sister, said that he had written to FOMIN Raul ROA in her behalf but had received no reply.

d. Ambassador HERNANDEZ Armas sent a Christmas present to COROSTIZA of the Mexican Foreign Office this year -- it was really a New Year's present, sent 31 Dec. L-9 does not know what it was.

L-9 was asked what all the ruckus was at the airport on 5 Jan. This drew a blank. B then said he had heard that a phalanx of Embassy officers was out there apparently guarding someone who was on his way to Cuba. L-9 didn't know anything about it. B remarked that two Cuban officials were supposedly on the flight to Havana -- one from the Embassy in Montevideo. At this point, L-9 slapped his forehead and said, "My God, PEREZ! I forgot about him." He then related the details of a conversation he had had with Earl PEREZ Friman at the Embassy on 2 Jan -- details sent to HQS separately by cable. (See MEXI 7659 ). L-9 was not aware that PEREZ had been on the 5 Jan flight -- he described him perfectly. He was not made aware of KUBARK involvement in the alleged "assault" nor was he told anything about PEREZ' appearance at TPELIDE.

5. L-9 identified a number of persons appearing in the photos taken at the diplomatic reception held at the Embassy 2 Jan. Identifications were noted
