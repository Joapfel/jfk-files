## J. Cover

As a completely overt and legitimate local organization, with an avowed anti-DGYPSY program, the FUIERIAL group has natural cover. WFD support will be covered by channeling funds through the secure DTIAREAS mechanism. Martin J. COKENIAS is a Staff Agent under unofficial cover in addition to which his relationship with the FUIERIAL group will be carried out in his capacity as the local representative of DTIAREAS.

## K. Security

All indigenous personnel involved in this project are unvitting of ODYOKE interest. Funding, control, and guidance of the FUIERIAL group will be handled in such a way that ODYOKE interest will not be disclosed to indigenous or unauthorized persons. Attribution for the group's activities can and will be accepted by the group. The original source of funds will not be known by the group.
