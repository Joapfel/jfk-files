9 March 1963

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, PM

SUBJECT : Irving C. DEVUONO Award of Intelligence Star

REFERENCE : UFGS-1382 dated 18 May 1962

1. Reference is drawn to paragraph 2 of reference which states that when DEVUONO returns to Headquarters arrangements will be made for the Director to make the award to DEVUONO.

2. 10 months have passed since this award was granted and it is recommended that action be taken to send DEVUONO to Headquarters to receive his award.

Philip S. WALTUCK
