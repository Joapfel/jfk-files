13 January 1970

MEMORANDUM FOR: WH/SAO

ATTENTION : Mr. Charles Anderson

SUBJECT : JMROD Counterinsurgency Target List

1. Per your request, the following is an updated version of the target listing forwarded to you on 25 September 1967.

a. Herroberto CASTILLO Martinez (201-789870)
Subject is a pro-Cuban leader of the MLN -
National Liberation Movement. He reportedly was a leading figure in the student movement against the Mexican Government in the fall of 1963. He was president of the Mexican delegation to OLAS. Subject was arrested in May 1969 and an of August 1969 was still in jail.

b. Rafael ESTRADA Villa (201-263944)
A Marxist activist who favors Castro's violent route to revolution. Subject attended the OLAS Conference inHAVANA in August 1967 as one of Mexico's representatives. He is the former Federal Deputy leader of the now defunct CHAR - National Organization of Revolutionary Action. Subject wasarrested on April 1969.

c. Victor RICO Galan (201-336474)
A Spanish-Born Mexican Communist journalist, Subject is an agent of the Cuban Government known to have been sent by the Cubans to talk to Guatemalan guerrillas in 1963. He is presently in prison.

2. Those names on the 1967 target list which do not appear above should be deleted.

Ernest J. Tsikerdaos
Acting Chief, WH/1
