MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Luis Clemente POSADA-Carriles

1. The Department of Justice has been investigating one Lefty Rosenthal whom they believe to be tied in with key members of organised crime in the Miami area and who also they believe has been involved in seven recent bombings in the Miami area. According to the information the Department has gotten so far, Rosenthal went to a United States citizen named Norman Rothman in the Dominican Republic and through him directly or indirectly through a person known as Diego, Rosenthal was put in touch with Luis Clemente Posada-Carrilles, Allen Registration Number A-12419708, 263 S.W. 26th Street, Miami. Rosenthal understands that Posada-Carrillo is attached to CIA and claims that Posada-Carrillo supplied him with capes, primers and C-4 explosives. Rosenthal is reported to have offered to talk to Posada-Carrillo about the background of the bombings and who was involved and why, but Posada-Carrillo said he had no interest in the background.

2. Rosenthal is reported to be asking Posada-Carrillo to supply 240 pounds of C-4 explosives with capes and primers by Saturday and has told Posada-Carrillo that twenty more bombings are scheduled. The local police are investigating the seven bombings which have taken place and do not know this other background material.
