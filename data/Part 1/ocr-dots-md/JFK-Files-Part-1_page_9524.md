STANDARD FORM NO. 69

Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO : Mr. Baker

FROM : JDC

SUBJECT: Clearance status - Melvin J. Leaky

DATE: 2 March 1950

1. Regarding Provisional Clearance - Mr. Garland has informed me that the CIA indices check has revealed derogatory information (in part the derogatory information concerns the fact that OSS would not utilize services of subject). Mr. Garland stated Provisional Clearance would be considered only if there is a written statement concerning status of employment, that is: exactly how subject will be utilized, will Gov't or CIA interest be shown, with whom will he be working and with that person be under cover?

2. Regarding Operational Clearance - Mr. Garland advised that investigation of subject cannot be initiated at least for another month as subject is currently being investigated by FBI for Voice of America.
