The S. R. O. will pay my passage to any part of the world if I can produce definite proof - documents enabling me to enter a country for resettlement, they don't care which or where, provided it is friendly and well accept me. I must tell you something about this great and powerful organization - The International Resettlement Organization for Displaced People. S. R. O. for that is equipped & maintained for the support and resettle ment of foreigners who have fled their own country on account of aggression resulting from political difference. These people / all gentilest the jews have a better and richer organization (of their own) termed and classified as D. P. under S. R. O. are accorded every facility; they have a hostel of their own, received a living allowance every month for every member of the family, plus air & foodstuffs for milk etc. for children. Free hospitals - own, doctors & medicines. The organization supplies all chat and also do their best for them by securing a living visa in different countries for then resettlement. All these privileged people do is to sit back and occasionally get drunk to celebrate their good fortune. Most of them are resourced one way or the other now. There are only very few remaining. The S. R. O. started in 1946. When it first came across the name in 1950, I thought it was a Godsend as I also consider myself as a square peg in a round hole. Their region were still open then.
