7 February 1980

CONCLUSION OF CONFERENCE

SUBJECT: Proposed Berlin Conference for Cultural Freedom

DATE: 15 January, 1980
FROM: Matthew T. Christie memorandum dated 23 December 1980

1. A conference to discuss subject matter was held 6 Feb 1980 in the office of Mr. John F. Anker, Chief P-M-III, Present were:

Mr. Cornel Offle, SANC
Mr. John Elliott, Chief Staff II, PhD
Mr. James L. Deakin, Staff II, Area Branch 3
Mr. Joseph Bryan III, Chief PM-III, PhD
Mr. Fred F. Williams, PM-II
Mr. Gerald Droller, PM-II
Mr. Louis Blasor, PM-III
Mr. Baker

For the purpose of this conference, Mr. Elliott acted as the personal representative of GPF, and Mr. Baker as the personal representative of GCF.

2. Reference communications concerning subject matters were read and discussed. There was no binding agreement upon the following points:

a. That the project was feasible, jointly and partially valuable as a partial counter to the Communist German youth fully scheduled for 27 May in Berlin.

b. That it could not be started meaningfully for less than $40,000, but that an allocation of $50,000 should be made to insure fully adequate financial support;

c. That approval of the French and British High Commissioners for Germany should be obtained to ensure tri-partite support of the conference, and that French and British material aid should be forthcoming, at least to the extent of providing transportation for delegates from their countries.
