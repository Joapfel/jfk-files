30 July 1964

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Reported Marriage of AMBIDDY-1

SAMBORA has received conflicting reports concerning the possibility that AMBIDDY-1 has married Adelaïda PADRON. One of the reports stated that AMBIDDY-1 married Adelaïda in Miami during the first part of July. Another report originating from Sixto Mesa reported that AMBIDDY-1 was married in Costa Rica during the latter part of July.

Paul J. Hernandez
WH/SA/SO/HR
