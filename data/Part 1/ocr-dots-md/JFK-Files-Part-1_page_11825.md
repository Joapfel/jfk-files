Dear Sir,

In accordance with the requirement to the Freedom of
Information Act that took effect February 1, I request information
on any file the Central Intelligence Agency may have on
the Bay City files.

I understand your concern requires the CIA to respond
to all inquiries within ten days of receipt.

If requests for information on any file the CIA may
keep in active possession until the end of his term if he was unable
to get a bill from Congress to the CIA.
