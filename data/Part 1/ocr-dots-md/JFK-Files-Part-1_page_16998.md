MEMORANDUM FOR: DC/WH
SUBJECT: Case Officer Assignments in Mexico

1. US Staff has told Rio Station to remove their liaison case officer from handling their Number One clandestine operation.

2. Why should we not do this in the case of Moxico where Estancona is handling LIVESTOCK-2 (Station's most overt and dangerous agent) and LIFERAT (unilateral telephone tap)?

3. Incidentally, telephone taps in Mexico are limping along with about half the target coverage of a year ago.

CHARLES W. ANDERSON
WH/FI
