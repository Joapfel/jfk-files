7. Physical Penetration and Coverage of LIRING.

A. **LITAMIL-7.** LITAMIL-7 continued to report on the activities known to her of the LIRING personnel she comes in contact with and to provide information on PERUANE visitors and dependents of LIRING personnel. She furnished interesting information on the goings-on at Liringi as at the time of the APRIMAR crash including the presence of General Lazzaro CArOEMAS. Later the same month, she obtained from one of the Liringi officers propaganda records produced by the Cultural Ministry which she lent us for copying. She was the first to report that a former LIRING intelligence officer had gained a high level position in the PERUANE Ministry of Culture. Extract from HMMA 31777, 10 Apr 67
