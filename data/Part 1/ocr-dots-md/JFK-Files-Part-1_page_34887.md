January 25th, 1968

TO whom in my name,

In the name of the Mothers, Wives and Sons, Pig's Bay Prisoners' Convention, I beg you to make your biggest effort to get in touch with the Commission or Department in care, to confectionate the list of names in the possible trading between Presidento Castro of Bolivia andidel Castro of Cuba. In order they include the names of the 9 Prisioners-Pig's Bay Convention left in Cuba on the 1962 Trading because at that opportunity Castro did not want to trade them. As you know on this trading are involved prisoners belonging to the CO. Department and others that the Pentagon can include in the Trading List.

I know always the CIA Department, the Pentagon and the Government of the United States of America have their names in mind, of course they want to bring these nine Prisioners to freedom, in this opportunity there is a very good chance, because Castro agreed with President B. Trientos to change all Comandante Prisioners and the Body of Ernesto (Che) Guevara for the Prisioners he has in Cuba that will be stated on the Trading List.

We are sure you will do your best in order to include the nine Pig's Bay Prisioners left on Cuba on the 1962 in the Trading's List. Please let me know something about it. Also we are enclosing copy of the letter we are sending to Senator Robert F. Kennedy asking the same thing.

Thank you very much in advance for your attention in this matter,
I remain,

cc: Senator Robert F. Kennedy
