"Who Owns the Automobiles". (He got this from a 13-yr-old boy in Germany in
1947, and he and an Army man each put $20 of their own into having it xpr printed

about 60 of the photos with his own captions. Munson will then let

to leave me out).

Barnes has been OK. I said I would check how his clearance is coming.

from Nov. 2
