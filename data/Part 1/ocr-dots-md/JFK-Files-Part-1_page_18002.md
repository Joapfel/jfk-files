## e. Technical

The only technical problems encountered were those falling within the province of the telephone company, as noted in 1, b above, or the normal malfunctions encountered in the aging LIFEAT equipment. ## g. Personnel

David M. WILSTED (ps) departed Mexico City on 21 May 1960 for home leave and Headquarters consultation of approximately two months duration. His duties as outside Case Officer and principal technician have been undertaken by Ralph B. RAFANELLI (ps) and LIFEURD-S.
