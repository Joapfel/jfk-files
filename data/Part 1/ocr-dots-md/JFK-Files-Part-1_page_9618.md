SUBJECT: GENERAL - Agents and Informants
SPECIFIC - HYDROL - Operational Review

DATE: 12 April 1949

OPERATIONAL REVIEW

COUNTRY: YAMAO. PERIOD CONVED: October 1945 - March 1949.

1. Subject is no longer an active operative of this station. He was forced to discontinue his activities on our behalf because of the press of personal business. MELANCHOLY is now in contact with HYDROL and HYDROL I has been taken over by HYDROLCIC.

2. During the period of his connection with this station, HYDROL failed to submit any informational report which in itself merited transmission to the Home Office. None of his operations were clean in the sense that a U.S. Government influence had to be introduced into the actual activation of each of his sources. This coupled with the fact that he lacked follow-through has given a slightly sour aspect to all of his operations. He intend to discontinue our connections with Subject.

Grover J. Waring

G.J.Wemh
