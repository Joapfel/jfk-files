# Identity 1

EPI: A. DE 2/97
B. STH 1/97
C. EDL 5/97

1. **Objective:** In accordance with the request contained in Reference A, a classified evaluation of the potentialities of Identity 1 was forwarded in Reference B. This dispatch is prepared to record in greater detail the information forwarded in References B and to report the latest developments concerning Identity 1.

2. **Background:** Following the 17 June 1953 demonstrations, a group of East German refugees, who identified themselves as "strike leaders", was reported as forming Identity 1. From several sources, it was determined that this group was receiving training from local moral support, information and guidance from each person to (see C. Van Vin), Paul V. Beudermann and others who allegedly wished to create a real organization for West Berlin resistance-type groups. It was previously known that Van Vin was very active for over six months attempting to establish a central file for information concerning East Germany to which all West Berlin organizations would be required to submit information. Publicly, his reason for this was to simplify concentration activities among the Berlin groups, but it is known that his real reason was to establish a personal database of control over all of these organizations. In this past, his efforts had been unsuccessful due to, among other factors, the absence of a common "carrier" organization. The continuing efforts upon the part of Documents to regain this position he had prior to his chemical from DILDE are a matter of record.

3. **Current Status:** As appeared that the creation of Identity 1 as a separate entity would not add anything of constructive nature to the general "cold war" front, and further, that its existence under the behind-the-scenes guidance of the persons mentioned above would hardly complicate the already complicated West Berlin scene. The BDM position was therefore, to attempt to prevent the formation of Identity 1 until to result the creates available among the "strike leaders" upon an individual basis. Reference B contains a description of the activities of DILDE in this regard.
