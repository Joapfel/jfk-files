18. History of Employment.

a) Subject arrived in Mexico City 10 May 1961 (LIFIRE 15 May 61). along with 8 other young diplomats between (Excelsior 13 May 61) 19 and 25, accompanied by member of Cuban Foreign Office. Purpose was to make known Castro Revolution in Mexico, Chile, Colombia Argentina, Venezuela and Brazil. Subject was Third Secretary.

b) Assistant Press and Cultural Attache (LITALUS 11 Oct 61). Cuban Embassy, Mexico

c) Returned to Cuba 4 May 1962. Replaced by Manuel VEGA Perez (LITEMPO-1 May 62).

d) Arrived Mexico 3 September 1962. Orders from Cuba that he not be placed on Diplomatic List. Leaving for Cuba 28 Sept 1962. (LIFIRE 4 Sept 62)
(LITANIL-7 7 Sept 62)
(LIENVOY 27 Sept 62)

e) Unfortunately received a scholarship - not known what kind or where. (EMLA-21403 21 May 63) (LITANIL-9)

f) Arrived Mexico 6 July 1963. as second secretary (LIFIRE 9 July 63)
