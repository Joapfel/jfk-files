NEW YORK JANUARY 15--ONE OF HUNGARY'S LEADING ATHLETES AND FOUND IN THE FREEDOM REVOLT SAYS NORTH KOREAN, COMMUNIST CHINESE AND VIETNAMEST STUDENTS WERE AMONG THE SYMPATHIZERS IN THE STRUGGLE AGAINST SOVIET INTERVENTION LAST FALL.

IN AN INTERVIEW THIS TODAY, FORMER TENNIS CHAMPION THOMAS O'BRIEN RECALLED THAT "PRACTICALLY EVERYONE NOT INVOLVED IN THE ACTUAL FIGHTINGS" LEFT SUPPORT BEHIND THE SCENES TO THE FREEDOM FIGHTERS. HE COULD NOT REALL SPECIFIC DETAILS OF THIS "PASSIVE RESISTANCE" BECAUSE AT THE TIME HE WAS "TOO BEST FURNISHING OFF SOVIET TROOPS AND TAKEN WITH SMALL ARMS." BUT ACCORDING TO THE 30-YEAR OLD HUNGARIAN ATHLETE AND STUDENT, THE FIDE WAS DEFINITELY FOR FREEDOM --ONLY THE OVERPOWERING FORCE OF SOVIET ARMOUR AND ARTILLERY ENFORCES EMMAILED TO HOLD UP THE COMMUNIST PUPPET BEING.

WHEN THE FIRST Wave of the Revolt broke out in Budapest,
O'Brien was on leave from the university at an Olympic training camp
in Támási. He said he and other athletes at the camp heard the news
of the fighting over western radio broadcasts. It came just
before their scheduled return to Budapest. O'Brien and some of the
other athletes joined the Freedom Fighters as soon as their
OLYMPIC team arrived; but they returned to Budapest. The athletes were returned
to the capital to prepare for departure to the Australian
OLYMPIC Games.
