8 JAN 1967

MEMORANDUM FOR: Assistant Deputy Director for Plans

THROUGH: NTS/EO

SUBJECT: Project LIEUTY

1. This action seeks renewal of Project LIEUTY covering Mexico City Station's stationary surveillance basehouses aimed against the Soviet Embassy. The activity will cost $45,000 for the period 1 December 1966 to 30 November 1967. Approval is recommended.

2. Project LIEUTY has been in being for nine years. At present it supports three stationary basehouses near the Soviet Embassy and a mobile photographic basehouse. These are used to obtain photographic coverage and logs of Soviet movements and visitors to the Soviet Embassy. The mobile basehouse is used also to cover meeting sites of the local communist parties and targets of opportunity.

3. A principal agent and eight agents are used in the LIEUTY activity. The agents are variously employed as basehouse keepers, photo lab technicians and surveillance operators.

4. Security has been generally good although one incident occurred in mid 1966 which may have some impact on the future security of the operation. An approach was made to an agent of the Station, who owns a small store near the Soviet Embassy, to report on his neighbors among whom are some of the LIEMTY agents. One, LIEMTY-o, was mentioned by name. The Station is watching this development carefully.

5. The LIEUTY activity proved its worth during the past year when check was made on conflicting stories told by two Station agents. The LIEMTY surveillance reports proved that one of the agents was lying about his contacts with the Soviets. LIEMTY is well managed and continues to serve an important Mexico City station need.

ROBERT E. ANDERSEN
Acting Chief
Foreign Intelligence
