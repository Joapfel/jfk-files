IMORANDUM FOR THE FILES

SUBJECT: l.mst GOEBEL
DATE: 28 Juno 1966

1. I called Subject at his office on 28 June. He said that he hasn't seen or been able to do such lately because he has been travelling almost constantly. (CONFIDENTIAL; I tried to get him twice during the last two weeks and he was out of town both times).

2. However Subject has seen LOGAN with wife or child at the club two or three times. He was never with Antonio SARMENTO. He has not seen COILSYN.

3. I will call Subject again in mid-July and see him at that time.
