## 2. Fnu DJAPADZE (CHAPADZE)

Now lives in London. Born 1912. Married, has children. Was in close contact with CABACHVILI and PATARIDZE. Went to Georgia in 1951 and 1952. AEPADDLE gathered that DJAPADZE was the partner of BERESHVILI. BERESHVILI said that a clash occurred on the last trip involving politics. He did not mention DJAPADZE, but since they are of different parties, AEPADDLE has connected them. (It is not clear whether this clash occurred on the trip when BERESHVILI was wounded.)

While in London, and later in Paris, AEPADDLE met Irakli MENTETACHVILI, 2) Alexandria Rd., Wimbledon, London SW 13. MENTETACHVILI stated that he has contact in London with the police or intelligence service, and offered to be of service to AEPADDLE. AEPADDLE asked MENTETACHVILI about DJAPADZE. MENTETACHVILI had nothing good to say about him. Considered him a big bluff, who was not properly handled by the British. MENTETACHVILI said the 1953 work was not continued because of the poor quality of DJAPADZE. MENTETACHVILI said that DJAPADZE never actually crossed the border. AEPADDLE discounts some of this, saying that MENTETACHVILI is a very bitter man. CABACHVILI and PATARIDZE said that they have a good, courageous man for entry into Georgia, whom AEPADDLE assurem to be DJAPADZE. They said this man is in London working for British Intelligence.
