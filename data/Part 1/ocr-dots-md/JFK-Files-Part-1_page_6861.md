As a supplement to bits of information which only transmitted here is a summarized account on the movements and activities of

**HALFORD** Maurice, alias PASTOR Partial, born 3.3.1926, US citizen, economist, married to Edith; domiciled at Bellin 36-9, Mexico 7, D.F.

during his sojourn in Switzerland from 9 October to 10 November 1958:

On 15 October subject and his wife arrived by air from LISBON at the Hotel "Schweizerhof" in ZURICH. On 18 October he and his wife took the 1320 H. train to GENEVA, where they stayed at the Hotel "Bernina". On 19 October, 1700 - 1900 E., he visited

**EREGN** David, born 1.9.1904, citizen of ARGENTINE, Consellor with the ILO in GENEVA, domiciled Avenue André Gide 2, CHILE-SOUTHERNES/GENEVA.

On 20 October at GENEVA he inquired at a local travel agency for the possibilities of a flight from ZURICH to PRAGUE. On that day the HALFORD's traveled by train (leaving GENEVA at 1245 H) to ZURICH, where they occupied a room at the "Schweizerhof" again. At 1830 H. subject visited the information office of the Zurich train station to learn about train connections for PRAGUE and for LUBANO. On 21 October, in answer to a telephone call, he visited the Czech Consulate General in ZURICH, where he stayed from 0940 - 1115 H. At 1630 H. he want to consult the "Swissair" information desk at the train station. He stated that he possessed a flight-ticket Mexico-Lisbon-Zurich-Tel-Aviv, but had no definite booking so far and wanted his luggage to be stored at Milotan airport for the time being. He then mailed letters to

1) Mr. David C. HALFORD, 5411 South University Ave., CHICAGO/ Ill.;

2) Mr. Theodore HALFORD & family, 22, Sahler Ave., MILTON/ Mass.;

3) Rabbi & Mrs. Hiller CAMERAN, 4 de Kald Place, MORRISTOWN/ New Jersey;

4) Mrs. Annie FRISCH, 17 Beach Road, LYNN/ Massachusetts;

letters 1 - 3 are of similar contents. Photostat copies of Nr. 1 & 2 are attached.

At 1930 H. he inquired at the "Swissair" office for any communication that may have come in for him.

On 22 October Subject negotiated with the KLM airtravel office for cancelling of his 2 flight tickets to Tel-Aviv and refunding.

On 23 October at 0925 H. he inquired at the information office of the Federal Railways about train connections to PARIS and fares. At 1025 H. he entered the Czech Consulate General, but reappeared in the street 3 minutes later.
