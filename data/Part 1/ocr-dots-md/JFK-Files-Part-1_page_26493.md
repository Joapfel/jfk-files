### C. Mexico to Czechoslovakia

There is a good chance that Mexico may participate in the Fifth Industrial Fair in Brno, Czechoslovakia, in September 1963. The announcement was made by Dr Emilio Vara Blanco, president of the National Chamber of Industry of Mexico, upon his arrival in Mexico City from Europe on 30 September 1962. While in Europe, Dr Vara Blanco had interviews with European industrialists and government officials, among them the Assistant Trade Minister of Czechoslovakia, who showed great interest in Mexico. (Mexico City, La Prensa, 1 Oct 62)

### D. Mexico -- Contacts with Cuba

#### 1. Soviet Couriers to Cuba

Russian diplomatic couriers Sergey Kulikov and Ivan Pavitsov left Mexico City for Havana recently in a Cubana de Aviacion airline plane on a special and very secret mission. They were carrying several bulky briefcases, which the Russian diplomats marked "diplomatic pouch," and which weighed 120 pounds. Four special guards accompanied the couriers and their briefcases. The arrivals of Cuba de Aviacion airline planes in Mexico therefore have caused incidents, as their passengers tried to smuggle Communist propaganda which the Mexican federal agents confiscated. Among the other passengers in the flight to Havana, in addition to the Russians, were the Mexican Communists Elia Calderon, a music composer, and Victor Ricardo Galan, a journalist. (Mexico City, El Universal, 3 Oct 62)

#### 2. Cuban and Communist Propaganda Confiscated

Agents of the Federal Judicial Police at the airport in Mexico City, under the command of Naj Alberto Nacrigal Nieto, confiscated Communist propaganda pamphlets from three Argentine citizens who arrived in the city from Havana on their way to Uruguay.
