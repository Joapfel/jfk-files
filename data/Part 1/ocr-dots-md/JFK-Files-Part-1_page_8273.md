SUBJECT: First Sistone and Ferelem Cover JGB Outing with Consular Corps
Brief Initial Exposure to Soviets Loginov and G. Loukov

1. Terence D. Sistone and Ferelem, both under Consular cover, accompanied (with wives) Fradyer on an outing of the "Cuerpo Consular en Mexico", a social club open to all consular officers regardless of nationality) to new Ford factory in Cuautitlan, lunch and excursion of Span. col. Convent at Tepotzotlan from1000 to 1000hrs. on 17 July 1969. Consular Corps is creature of Amer. cit and ex-Consul Gen. of Greece, Leander F. Vourvoulina, who led excursion. In addition to helping the two new Sta. officers work into consular cover and make contacts, the purpose of the outing in part was to see at first hand the Sov. Bloc and Cubans who belong to the CC. Three Sovs showed, no identified bloc and no Cubans. I did not see any other particularly interesting cons. types out door opened to meet people at future CC meets.

2. Three Sovs who showed were Valentin Loginov, well-known to Sta. as KGB under cons. cover, and two men of Commercial Sec. of Sov. Emb. Both Sistone and Ferelem independently identified Lev M.却yevich Golozov as one of two commercial types from mug book—were unable identify the other, who was quiet and hardly talked as he tagged along. Golozov was also quiet but was engaged twice by Sistone in conversation. One one occasion said he had been in Mexico short time, was there as result recent Sov-Hex trade agreement, just happened to be around and invited with colleague to Ford outing. On other occasions he said Tpotzotlan/golf leaf and general art more elaborate and impressive than Byzantine church-monuments he familiar with in Moscow. spoke Span. with heavy accent and didn't talk much but was quite fluent nevertheless. Staline Sistone also talked briefly to Loginov and introduced by Fradyer as Fralyer replacement. However conversation is mucous and uninteresting compared to constant verbal sparring betw. Fradyer and Loginov which Sistone will defer to Fradyer to report if he sees fit.
