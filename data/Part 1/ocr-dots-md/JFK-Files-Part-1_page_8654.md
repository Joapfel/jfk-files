Contact Report
Subject: LJOVAN-1 (F-8903)
Date of Meeting: Sept 4 at Hoyo 19 Rest at 1340 hrs.

1. The meet was prearranged. Next meet: Sept 11 at the same place at 1330 hrs.

2. During the language class on Mon Sept 1 LIBACON told L-1 that he had a lunch with AEDIENARD on Aug 29 and on that occasion A accepted to come to LIBACON-L's house for dinner on Sept 15 about 1900 - 1930 hours. LIBACON-L added that Yuriy CHEMCHYSH (F-13,042) would ask also attend the dinner.

3. LIBACON-L told L-1 that he wanted to invite him to the same dinner L-1 with wife, Mrs. REX and Odil, but his wife refused to have more guests than the two Sov couples, because they did not have sufficient space and could not afford to spend the amount money the additional number of guests would cost. He went on to state that even this way he was in a quite embarrassing situation because he would not have money to buy more than a bottle of Castillo rum.

4. L-1 thanked LIBACON-L for thinking of him and added that he would not have been able to come for dinner that evening because he had to give a class which he was not able to cancel. LIBACON-L felt relieved, but he appeared to be very nervous. L-1 added, as zxxxxx an afterthought that he would bring to LIBACON-L a bottle of whisky, because his friend Octavio from Katanoros was scheduled to come to town before Sept 15 and he usually brings him a couple of bottles of whiskey; if not sooner L-1 was certain that Octavio would call on him Sept 14 or 15 the latest and in that case L-1 could hand the bottle to LIBACON-L on the evening of the party at 2100-2130 hrs. LIBACON-L felt that this would be great and that L-1 could remain with his guests for a drink.

5. L-1 commented that the above mentioned developments made it unnecessary for him to go through the crisis of asking to have his classes at LIBACON-L's place.
