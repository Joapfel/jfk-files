Archibald, brother, born 1916 in Bormond near Flirtis, architect, served in the French Army from 1920 until 1940, became Lt. in a motorized infantry unit and fell in North Africa, France and Germany; twice married, first wife Nov., see JORCAJIA, divorced, second wife, Jacqueline, see SYLVIANN, born 1935 in Gap; lives in Gap with second wife and two of the three children born of the first marriage.

Helena: Sister, born 1916 in Grossi, married Irakli LORIKPAHIDZE, born 1913, now believed divorced. Helena and her husband returned to the USSR in 1948, subsequent to which both were arrested, exiled to Siberia. She is now believed to be living in Moscow. He volunteered with the German Army in WW II.
