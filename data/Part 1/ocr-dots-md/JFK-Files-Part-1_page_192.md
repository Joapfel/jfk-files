Cooperated with the Interchange Foundation headed by Anna Lord Strauss and Mrs. Harvey Picker of CC (44) (Project JEFERVENT) in what proved an abortive effort under a joint CA-SR project to sponsor the U.S. visit of a group of prominent Soviet women.
