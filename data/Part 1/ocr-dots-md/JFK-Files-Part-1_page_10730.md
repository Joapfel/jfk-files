VISITING PANAMA CITY. MASFERRER IS A RESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES. IN PANAMA CITY HE STAYED IN ROOM 405 OF THE CONTINENTAL HOTEL. MERNANDEZ AND ORGALLEZ, WORKING AS A TEAM, LOCATED MASFERRER AT HIS HOTEL SHORTLY AFTER HIS ARRIVAL AND KEPT HIM UNDER CONSTANT SURVEILLANCE UNTIL HE DEPARTED PANAMA ON 21 MARCH. ACCORDING TO ORGALLEZ, MASFERRER WAS A WELL KNOWN CUBAN SUITAN AND AN EXILE TROUBLE MAKER. (SOURCE COMMENT: ORGALLEZ' DESCRIPTION OF MASFERRER IS NOT TRUE. HE IS THE SON OF A PROMinent MEMBER OF THE CUBAN EXILE COMMUNITY IN MIAMI.) (FIELD COMMENT: WEP-13,367, TDFI RDB-315/81842-75, FROM THE SAME SOURCE, REPORTED THAT IN JANUARY 1975 THE GN G-2 DETAINED A CUBAN EXILE IN COOPERATION WITH THE CUBAN EMBASSY.)
