Chief, Miami Field Office

Local OJI Inquiry Regarding Information on Mitchell L. Warbell

1. On 15 May 1969, Special Agent Dave Plouman, local OJI representative, USAF, visited the Miami Field Office to solicit any information we might have regarding Mitchell L. Warbell who apparently is undergoing a security clearance check as the result of presently being employed by a company with a government contract.

2. Warbell was an unsolicited contact of this office beginning in 1959 and he reached out to me personally in November 1967 as a result of his involvement in the Palomino Massacre trial in connection with the abortive Haitian invasion. John Ortangy, OJI was in Miami at the time to oversee Agency interests.

3. Plouman in contacting other US Government agencies locally including the US Attorney's Office somehow received an indication that CIA had information on Warbell; thus Plouman's visit to the Miami Field Office.

4. It was indicated to Plouman, who is known personally by several members of this staff, that we knew of Warbell and that on occasion he had reported unsolicited information. It was suggested, however, that Plouman direct his request officially through channels via OJI's agency liaison officer in Washington.

5. This is for background only and is being forwarded at the suggestion of local CIA Security.
