C. TRAINING RECORD

: Grayston Lynch

: SOD

**COURSE NO.:** 15 - 23 June 1970

The student is taught fundamental understanding of the photographic process, manipulation of typical 35mm cameras and film processing.

The student is instructed how to use two different types of 35mm camera copy systems for document photography - the LEXCA X-3 Range-finder, and the RUMMX single lens reflex camera. Documents are photographed under available light and artificial light with the camera hard-hard and other unconventional means. Students' results of various assignments demonstrated the following abilities.

* **A - OUTSTANDING**

* **B - PROFICIENT**
