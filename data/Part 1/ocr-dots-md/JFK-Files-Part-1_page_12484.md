1. MINISTER OF INTERIOR LORENZO FERNANDEZ DEPARTED VENEZUELA EVENING I SEPTEMBER FOR EUROPEAN VACATION. REPORTED "EL UNIVERSAL" 27 AUGUST.

2. CIFENCE-4 NOTIFIED 30 AUGUST HE TO ACCOMPANY INTERIOR MINISTER. TRIP ESTIMATED ONE MONTH TO SIX WEEKS, AND CIFENCE-4 MENTIONED SWEDEN, ENGLAND, GERMANY, FRANCE, SPAIN, PORTUGAL AND ITALY AS ON ITINERARY. WHILE TRIP OSTENSIBLY VACATION, IT WIDELY SPECULATED THAT FERNANDEZ TOUCHING BASE FOR POLITICAL REASONS PRIOR TO ANNOUNCING HIS PRESIDENTIAL CANDIDACY UPON RETURN. HE KNOWN TO BE FAVORED CANDIDATE OF PRESIDENT CALDERA.

3. CIFENCE-4 INSTRUCTED TO REPORT ITINERARY, PERSONS CONTACTED, AND TO EXTENT POSSIBLE SUBJECTS DISCUSSED.
