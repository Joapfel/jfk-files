Reel 73-K1CO-25

1 May 1963

## 5.3 contd.

VKG asks if Juan B. did not mention about the ones who have left (the group)? No, MO asked for companions and Juan B. said there were a lot of disappeared. Two or three members of the Committee have disappeared. They may be dead or are being taken away went to the hills (left or really went to the hills). The campaign of UP must be combatted. VKG will call Habana today. The civilian people are patrolling the streets. They are maintaining a order. There is combat, of course, but where there is no combat, everything is in order. The movement wants this campaign that they are using Czech arms to be clarified - that it is not true.

VKG and MO could get together tonight. Dominicans are having a meeting tonight around 2000. VKG and MO could get together a little before. About the matter MO took by to VKG. Yes, and other things which VKG wants to get MO help to establish. Then, about 1700? No, beat at 1800. MO will come by at 1800 - with his frienda. All right.

Marcio (Nejia Ricart) with VKG. What did Bosch say? Said there was complete calm and there was no doubt of control, that they were still disorientating distributing arms. MNA saw that Bosch was more interested in talking than 3 days ago when MNA talked to him. Bosch said that of the 30 tanks, there are 9 only 9 left. They (Bosch people) have 11 and 10 are out of combat. No one has published this, at least MNA has not seen this. Bosch said that yesterday morning they conquered Portales Isama where there were several hundreds of dead, but they had taken it, and there is a cable stating that everyone there was shot. They are still picking up political leaders in city. The National Police has not come over to Bosch yet. The U.S. troops are now entering into action. Of course, if they disembarked in San Isidro, it was to enter into action. Bosch said that things are being arranged (between them and U.S.) but the conditions right now contain immediate evacuation of the (U.S.) troops.
