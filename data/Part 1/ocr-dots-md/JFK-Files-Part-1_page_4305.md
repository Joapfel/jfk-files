TRANSLATION

MRR Letterhead

Miami
4 September 1965

Mr. Carl J. James
P. O. Box 9349
Arlington 22209, Virginia

Dear Friend:

I wish to submit for your approval this extraordinary budget in order to be able to reimburse the Movimiento for the expenses I incurred during my last trip, during which I believe I obtained very interesting reports.

- Pan Am ticket Miami-Managua (4 Aug) $ 98.00
- Stay in Managua (4-10 Aug) (I only paid for food since the MRR rents a house there) $ 25.00
- Extra expenses in Managua (Gas for the MRR station wagon, dinner with Major Perez and drinks with Lt. Corrales (aide of General Sonoma)) $ 20.00
- Pan Am ticket Managua-San Jose (10 Aug) $ 22.00
- Stay in San Jose (10-13 Aug) (Only food since I lived in the house of the MRR delegate) $ 12.00
- Extra expenses in San Jose (taxis, invitation for dinner to Guillermo Vargas of INVU and Roberto Madris, secretary to the President, invitation for drinks to the people of INVU, invitation for drinks to Colonel Lippa and people of the Resguardo Fiscal) $ 53.00
- TACA ticket San Jose-Panama-Managua $103.02
- Quarters in Panama, Hotel "La Siesta," 13-15 Aug $ 57.53
