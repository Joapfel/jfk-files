1. FEASIBILITY STUDY ON BOTH LOCAL AND WESTINGHOUSE PLUGS JUST COMPLETED. STUDY REVEALED LOCAL BAKELITE PLUGS NOT SUITABLE SINCE INSIDE PLUG TOO EASILY CHECKED BY ANY FJSTEALER WITH SCREWDRIVER.

2. WESTINGHOUSE MOLDED PLUGS ADAPTABLE ONLY FOR NON-SWITCHED, UNMASKED XMITTER. SINCE PLAIN LANGUAGE SIGNAL WOULD BE ON AIR CONSTANTLY, WE BELIEVE SUCH INSTALLATION WOULD BE RAPIDLY DISCOVERED BY FJSTEALERS. IN VIEW POSSIBLE POLITICAL FLAP, INCREASED FJSTEALER SECURITY, AND POSSIBLE JEPARDY TO OTHER GOING AND POTENTIAL AUDIO OPS IN SOUTH AMERICA WHICH COULD RESULT FROM EARLY COMPROMISE OF WESTINGHOUSE PLUG REGRET HQS CANNOT APPROVE ITS USE. OTHER FACTORS SUCH AS UNKNOWN TARGET AREAS WHERE MACHINES WILL BE INSTALLED AND POSSIBILITY THEY MAY BE LEFT UNPLUGGED FOR LONG PERIODS OF TIME ALSO ARGUE AGAINST OP.
