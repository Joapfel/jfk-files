CONFIDENTIAL

Report No: EER-1716, Page 3
Date 24 February 1958

Field Comments.

1. The confidential letter to the Cuban DI is considered to be an obvious attempt to put the major clues on Communist elements for recent anti-Government demonstrations as evidenced by the reference to painting on the walls of houses such as "Abajo Estamos" and "26 de Julio" as "Communist Inkstands". Recent reports also indicate that the FJ is opposed to the acceptance of Communist support in its demonstrations and refused permission to CABLE and other lists to participate in the 10 January event honoring III. It seems that the confidential letter are therefore evaluated as "unsubstantiated".

2. No information is available to confirm the reported presence in Cuba of Ernesto Yañez Ortega, Fidel Castro or Augusto Arango.
