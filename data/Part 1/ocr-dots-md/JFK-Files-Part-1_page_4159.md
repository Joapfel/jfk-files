AMBOLD Meeting in Panama City. 8 August 1964

SUBJECT: Parents of AMBIDY-1

In connection with his parents I told AMBIDY-1 that my HUS had investigated the possibility of intercmission with Mexico in their behalf. Our considered opinion was that this would not serve a useful purpose and might be counter-productive. The relations with Mexico are strained at present because of their adverse vote in the CM meeting and my people could see no way in which they could intercede without directly indicating an official U. S. Government interest in AMBIDY-1 himself. He agreed that this was probably the situation. He asked only if in my opinion we might be able to assist in expediting their entry into the United States as a refugee seeking asylum in case they were able to make their way to Mexico. I stated that in my opinion we would be able to assist in such a situation.

Carl R. Jenkins
