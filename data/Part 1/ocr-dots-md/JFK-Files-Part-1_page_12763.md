14. As Headquarters is already aware, WKSCARLET-3 passed his SOSWIRL with flying colors on 8 May. Based on the SOSWIRL and his earlier debriefing concerning his alleged involvement in narcotics, it seems that WKSCARLET-3 was only guilty of having the wrong kind of friends. LIONEL Caracas was advised on 15 May regarding our conclusions based upon the debriefing and SOSWIRL of WKSCARLET-3. LIONEL Caracas agreed with our conclusions and now regards the case as being closed. WKSCARLET-3 agreed, and we so advised LIONEL, that WKSCARLET-3 would help out with narcotics leads he might come across. We told LIONEL that such assistance would only be on an ad hoc basis subject to our other priorities as well as WKSCARLET-3's security.
