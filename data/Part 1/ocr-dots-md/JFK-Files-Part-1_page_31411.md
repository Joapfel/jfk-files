NO. 31 1952

MEMORANDUM WCR: Chief, Cover Division
Thru: FI Plans

SUBJECT: NCFE Employment of Mr. Erutus Coste

1. This is to advise you that, due to delay in our operations, the NCFE has been requested to provide interim employment for Mr. Erutus Conte.

2. We have been informed that the NCFE/MEEC has offered Mr. Conte a contract for the preparation of a study on integration of the Russian with the Soviet economy. However, in view of Subject's knowledge of the refugee political situation and interest in propaganda measures, a position utilizing this background would be more advantageous to the NCFE and this Division than the proposed economic study.

3. It is requested that the proposed employment of Subject be discussed with the NCFE and that we be informed of the action taken. This division is prepared to reimburse the NCFE for Subject's salary if such is desired. It is understood that Subject will continue, during the period of NCFE employment, to perform for this Division certain assignments which will not interfere with his NCFE work.

4. Your approval of the proposal for such employment is requested.

JOHN E. BAKER
Chief, SE
