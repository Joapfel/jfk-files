**Question:** What kind of weapons did you bring for assassinations?

**Lancerica:** We brought two .45mm revolvers with silencers, a .22-cal. pistol with holster, and an A-J with a silencer.

**Question:** What kind of ammunition did you bring for the .22-cal. pistol with silencer?

**Lancerica:** Part of the ammunition was conventional bullets and part of it was expanded-tipped bullets.

**Question:** The prepared the bullets with gunpowder?

**Lancerica:** I prepared them.

**Question:** What were these bullets prepared for?

**Lancerica:** These bullets were prepared over there in Miami. (Polo--phonetic) Associates, number of an organization called (191 Race) asked me to prepare them. In spite of the fact that I know nothing about chemistry, for attempts on lives, to make sure of the death of the individuals against whose lives attempts were to be made, but they were for specific attempts.

**Question:** What plans were made in the United States or among the counterrevolutionary circles, what is the main objective sought?

**Lancerica:** Well, the physical elimination of the leader of the Cuban revolution, Fidel Castro,

**Question:** The physical elimination of Mj. Fidel Castro?

**Lancerica:** Yes, sir.

**Question:** Those counterrevolutionary groups which operate from Florida, from Miami, do they have the approval of the North American Government for their actions?

**Lancerica:** Almost entirely. For example the most opportunist elements over there create small groups to obtain dollars from the US Government for their subsistence, but there are close links among all the organizations in exile with the Central Intelligence Agency. We have, for example, the case of (191 Race) which--through lies Posada, who is known by all as a CIA agent--belongs, that is all its activities of landing on Cuban soil, actions against merchant ships and so forth, are linked to the CIA.
