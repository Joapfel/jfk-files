1. Reference is made to your letter of 13 March 1975 requesting Consultant advice on the form of a contract for services to be provided by persons who are now Contract Type I employees.

2. This is to advise that the terms and conditions of the agreement are hereby modified for an term of three years, from 1 April 1976 to 30 June 1979. A new contract will then be drawn up between the parties, which will be available from 1 July 1979. All work in the same class of work shall be included in this contract.

3. In order to comply with the requirements of Directive 89/36/EEA, the following additional requirements are added to contract type 1 or 2. The contract must be worded in English and all payments must be made in English dollars.
