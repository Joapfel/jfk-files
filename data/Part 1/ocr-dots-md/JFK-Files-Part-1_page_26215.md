f. Roberto CASTILLO-Martinez - Acting head of the MLN and head of the Mexican delegation to the Tricontinental Conference held in Havana in 1966.

g. Rafael ESTABO-Villa - Politically estranged from Vicente LOPEZ-Teledano and the FCS - Now head of the newly named (August 1967) National Organization of Revolutionary Action, a FCS faction.
