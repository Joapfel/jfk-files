DD/S 65-3075

27 SEP 1985

MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Security
SUBJECT : Commendation

1. The Director was pleased with the attached letter from Mr. Rowley in which he expressed deep appreciation for the excellent and dedicated as-sistance rendered to the U. S. Secret Service during the past year. The Director wishes to continue this cooperation and assistance.

2. I appreciate very much this kind of performance which reflects credit to the U.S. Air Agency and I compliment you and your staff for another job well done.

Ad: Letter did 13 Sep 60 to DCI:
Mr. Rowley, re: Appreciation

Deputy Director
for Support
