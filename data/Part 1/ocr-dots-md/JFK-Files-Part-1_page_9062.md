SECRET

25 May 1967

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, DC

VIA : Chief, DC/CO

SUBJECT : Edward G. Tichborn (P)

1. The undersigned on 23 May 1957 reviewed in Will Division Tichborn's 201 file (4 folders) and related operational files. Particular attention was given in this review to Tichborn's security.

2. The files reflect that Tichborn is very security conscious. There is no evidence that Tichborn was ever suspected of being a WO/AC I operative.

3. Tichborn practiced law in California and was active in state and national politics. He was recruited as a political adviser on a part-time basis in 1959 by the CA Staff. In 1962, he was taken on full-time under contract by Will Division and assigned to Mexico City. His cover for this assignment was publishing and movie interests in Mexico and elsewhere in Latin America. In Mexico City he published a magazine, Decloros, aimed at left-wing intellectuals. The CI/OA staff reviewed it in 1965 stating that Tichborn's cover story was completely accepted by his friends and associates. The SCC/IRI of Tichborn in 1965 revealed that he had informed only his wife of his association with WOFACT.

4. The files reflect that Tichborn is very sensitive about security. No contact was made with a WOFACT officer in Mexico City, because a friend told Tichborn that the WOFACT officer not only was WOFACT but had openly admitted it to the friend. A proposed trip to Bolivia was aborted because Tichborn felt there was no suitable cover.

5. Tichborn has traveled extensively in Latin America on training and election assignments. Three countries in which he worked are Chile, Dominican Republic and Puerto Rico. On these assignments Tichborn used his legal, publishing, and movie cover. It is understood that he also usedRadio and disguises, although this was not noted in the quick review of the files. He can, of course, be debriefed in detail on this aspect.
