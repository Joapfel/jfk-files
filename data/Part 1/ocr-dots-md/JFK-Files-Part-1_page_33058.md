ALTHOUGH INSTRUCTED BY HIS CASE OFFICER TO DO SO, AMCHALK-5 HAS NOT YET PREPARED THE FIRST PART OF THAT HISTORY OF PUPOCK ACTIVITY AGAINST CUBA WHICH WAS A TASK ASSIGNED BY HIS CUIS CONTACT IN CANADA. IN DISCUSSING THIS ON 12 FEBRUARY, AMCHALK-5 REITERATED HIS RELUCTANCE TO PREPARE SUCH A DOCUMENT AND SAID THAT WHILE HE WOULD BE WILLING TO WRITE THE PAPER FOR HIS CASE OFFICER, HE COULD NOT DO SO IF IN THE COUPLE OF PREPARATION HE HAD IN MIND THAT IT WOULD BE PASSED TO THE CUIS. AMCHALK-5 CITED HIS PAST CLOSE RELATIONSHIPS WITH MANUEL PINEIRO AND PEPIN NARANJO AND SAID THAT ALTHOUGH HE WAS MUCH CLOSER TO THE LATTER, BOTH KNEW HIM WELL ENOUGH THAT THEY AND OTHERS WOULD PEGARD ANY SUCH DOCUMENT WITH COMPLETE DISTREUST.
