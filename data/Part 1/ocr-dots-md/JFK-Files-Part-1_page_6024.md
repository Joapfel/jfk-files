27 September 1963

Tip-Top restaurant, Coral Gables.

1600-1850 p.m.

Harold R. NORMAN

1. The meeting with AMT-2 was to clarify the continuing disunderstanding and disagreement over AMBARB funding. The original AMBARB policy meeting with AMT-2 was reviewed, and AMT-2 pointed out that it was not agreed that AMT should pay all AMBARB costs, but only the covers. Without going over the entire evolution of the AMBARB project funding, it was pointed up to AMT-2 that the project was a two-way arrangement, of great mutual convenience, and we must find a functional "middle-ground" upon which to operate, although it may not be entirely satisfactory to either of the parties. AMT-2 agreed, on the following ground rules were arrived at as a workable funding plan:

a. AMBARB's assigned as overt AMSPELL representatives will be funded by AMSPELL from Miami on all future assignments. Those now being funded by station will remain in that status quo.

b. Cover AMBARBS for station contact will be assigned by initial processing, both parties agreed, and station will fund and control.

c. Be the four pending cases where stations have requested funding as soon as possible - a meeting is to be held where each case will be considered individually and decided upon.

2. While this arrangement is not entirely acceptable, it is believed that upon the return of AMT-2 to the operational control, a better arrangement can be made. 01
