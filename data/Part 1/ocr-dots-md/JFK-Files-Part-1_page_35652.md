Under the terms of our agreement with you, we hereby wish to advise you that beginning 1 December 1964 we desire to reduce the number of the "Mexico Jated" broadcasts from six per week to three per week. Thank you very much for your cooperation in this matter. We look forward to continued collaboration with you in these matters of mutual interest.
