8 November 1960

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Contact Report on Meeting with AMBUSH-1 201-270613

1. On 5 November at 1130 hours, AMBUSH-1 and KRC-4 were met at the Washington Airport by undersigned. They were then taken by taxi to the Mayflower Hotel, where a room had been reserved ... for their use. After having lunch at the hotel, the party adjourned to the room, where AMBUSH-1 was given a short briefing on Francisco Castell, the -/T operator who was scheduled to meet AMBUSH-1 at 1400 hours.

2. At 1400 hours, the undersigned went to the lobby of the hotel where Francisco was waiting with [Key Garcia], his escort officer. [Garcia] was instructed to bring Francisco up to the room. It was agreed in advance of this that all principals would draw from the room to give AMBUSH-1 an opportunity to talk with Francisco alone. However, it was suggested that [Garcia] return a half hour before the interview was terminated to clear up any points as to the nature of Francisco's training, or any aspects that AMBUSH-1 might wish to clarify. The interview with Francisco was terminated about 1510 hours, as AMBUSH-1 stated he would be pleased to have Francisco assigned to him. All indications were that a happy rapport was established between the principals and a complete understanding was reached as to the future role of Francisco as the -/T operator for the AMYUM group in Cuba.

3. At approximately 1535 hours, [Andy Rogers] put in his appearance at the room and was introduced to AMBUSH-1 as Mr. Rodrick. Prior to this meeting, AMBUSH-1 was told that Mr. Rodrick was the coordinator for operations and would like to discuss some of the more specific aspects with him, prior to his return to Cuba. [Cecil Cartwright, who had escorted AMBUSH-1 to Washington, asked to sit in on this meeting along with Peters and Rogers] as he felt that MASH should be fully informed on whatever operational matters were discussed with AMBUSH-1 for more effective future coordination between Headquarters and MASH. Rogers indicated no objections to this and stated he was glad to have him sit in at this meeting.
