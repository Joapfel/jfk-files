1. Attached, par Headquarters instructions in reference B., are the required documents from Harold R. NORMAN applying for reimbursement for medical treatment required by his dependent daughter. These include Form 1126, receipts for payments made and a statement from the attending physician as to the treatment given. The physical examination statement merely indicates that he provided medical care and a biopsy, without delving into the nature of the illness, his diagnosis or specific treatment. Mrs. NORMAN was unable to obtain any further declaration from the physician and security considerations prevented her from becoming more demanding on this point.

2. If Headquarters requires a more elaborate statement, would it be permissible for NORMAN to certify as to the missing aspects from the physician's statement? Otherwise, no other document of this nature is available.
