During April 1965, the American Embassy Protection and Welfare officer advised the Station that he had received a call from the States from a Mr. James Green, Jr., concerning his son, James C. Green, Jr. and a friend of his sons, Grant H. Crayne. He said that according to Mr. Green, his son and Crayne had called Mr. Green and told him that they were going to the Soviet Union. The two men later visited the U. S. Embassy and were interviewed by a Station officer. The following day on 12 April, Subject reported that two Americans (who fitted the descriptions of Green and Crayne) had visited the Soviet Embassy that same day. This fact was subsequently confirmed by LIMITED LIEMERACE surveillance was placed on the two men and this eventually led to their arrest and deportation from Mexico.

Also during the reporting period, Subject reported an incident which the Station felt could be used to discredit the Soviet Embassy in the eyes of the local population. The story, which is factual, was placed in the newspaper La Prensa by LILIFETE during July 1965. An English language translation of the article follows :
