SOURCE

Nationalized US citizens of Haiti birth, official of a US import-export enterprise. Source makes occasional trips to Haiti and has may contracts facing Haitian business and political figures both in the US and in Haiti.

1. The Government of Haiti (COH) has issued claims that the invaders of TO May 6 have been wiped out and that total victory against them has been achieved. A special Thanksgiving message was celebrated recently in Port-au-Prince to commemorate and celebrate the event. On the surface, things are politically quiet.
2. The reality seen, however, to differ to a marked degree from the foregoing. Although the populace has remained quiet, it does not believe the COH claims of total victory. In point of fact, the invaders have not been wiped out and they have occupied in gaining control of the small town of Quartier Mors and of the Citodelle Lafarriere (both situated near Cap Haïtien). The COH has decided not to begin military operations against them, but to adopt a defensive attitude and wait and see what happens. The COH realizes that to dilute the rebels from the citadel, which is located in very rugged terrain, would be extremely difficult militarily and would be quite indefensible politically, because the citadel has strict emotional historical associations for Haitians. To bostard it into rubble, which would be the only feasible way to recapture it, would create reason/ism among the populace. Accordingly, the COH seems prepared to do nothing, in the hope that the rebels will die away. Such an eventuality seems unlikely, however, because the invaders have not only succeeded in gaining control of the area above mentioned places, but also have succeeded in relying on about 140 of the local populace to their side. Beports in the US Spanish-language press of 12 Jun 68 stated that the rebel force is now 300 strong and that 140 of these are local people who have rallied to them. My information conveys these figures. The populace in Port-au-Prince expects further action from the rebels. The latest rumor (and the word "runners" must be emphasized) in Port-au-Prince are those other invasion will be launched into southern Haiti this time, and that Cuba has infiltrated a number of illegal enclaves.
