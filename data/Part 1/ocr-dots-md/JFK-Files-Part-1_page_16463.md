1. AMG/1 HAS A STRONG INTEREST AND NEED TO BECOME A NATURALIZED AMERICAN CITIZEN. HE HAS MADE APPLICATION TO AVIATICA AIRLINE, WHICH IF ACCEPTED, WILL REQUIRE HIM TO TRAVEL TO COLOMBIA. HE HAS BEEN ASSURED THAT PVROCK WILL ATTENT ASSIST HIM IN OBTAINING HIS PAPERS.

2. AMG/1 HAS NOT FILED AN APPLICATION FOR NATURALIZATION LOCALLY BECAUSE HE IS AWARE THAT HE WILL BE REQUIRED TO ANSWER SPECIFIC QUESTIONS ABOUT HIS ENTRY INTO THE COUNTRY, ADDRESSES IN THE STATES, EMPLOYER REFERENCES, POLITICAL BACKGROUND, AND OTHER QUESTIONS IN DELICATE OPERATIONAL AREAS. HE REALIZES THAT HE CANNOT ANSWER THESE QUESTIONS WITHOUT PVROCK TITLES. WH/MIAMI HAS REACHED THIS SUBJECT, IN A GENERAL WAY, WITH LOCAL IMMIGRATION AND WE HAVE BEEN ADVISED THAT TO RESOLVE THE PROBLEM, WE WOULD HAVE TO HANDLE ON THE WASHINGTON LEVEL.

3. A DETAIL OF AMG/1'S HISTORY WILL BE COMING SOON. ONE OF THE THINGS THAT MAKES US DIFFERENT FROM OTHERS IS OUR COMMITMENT TO DO WHAT IS RIGHT FOR PEOPLE WHEN THEY NEED IT. WE ARE COMMITTED TO DO THE RIGHT THING WHENEVER WE CAN DO IT BY USING ALL THE POWER WE HAVE AND LEADING BY EXAMPLE. WE WANT TO BE THE MODEL FOR OTHERS TO FOLLOW. WE ARE COMMITTED TO DOING WHAT IS RIGHT FOR PEOPLE WHEN THEY NEED IT.
