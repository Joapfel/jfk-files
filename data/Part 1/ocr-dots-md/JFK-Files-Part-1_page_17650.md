MESSAGE FOR: L/OPS - Projects Branch
CRIMONTH IT MAT
AREA DIVISION Western Asia here
STATION Mexico City

HEAD QUARTERS CASE OFFICER:
Name Louis D. Jackett
Room 2112 Marte 511
Text 2555 or 302
Date 31 October 1957

1. It is requested that the LI/PAT project be amended to include authorization to open a bank account of $20,000 for Daniel K. BROMUND (Staff Agent). Project LIFAT was approved for renewal for the period 1 July 1957 to 30 June 1958.

2. Subsequent to the renewal of Project LIFAT, it was determined that the cover appropriate for Daniel K. BROMUND would be that of a _Rentista_. In order to obtain Rentista status from the Mexican Government, it is necessary to show documents which prove that the applicant has an income of approximately $350 per month for a period of five years. On approval of this amendment, it is planned to open an account for Daniel K. BROMUND for the amount of $20,000 against which the bank will issue him a letter of Credit which will permit him to draw against this account at the rate of $1,000 quarterly. This letter of Credit will then be used to obtain _entista_ status for BROND. This letter of Credit will be held by the Station and will be passed to BROMUND for use once every three months. BROMUND will use this letter of Credit, withdrawing his $1,000 quarterly, and will immediately deposit this money and the letter of Credit with the Finance Officer at the Station. These deposits will be accounted for in the station's accountings.

3. To protect the United States Government's interest, the necessary Declarations of Trust and/or Assignment of Interest as determined by the Finance Division will be executed and filed with the Monetary Branch, Finance Division.

ROBERT H. DAHLEES, C/M/III
CHARLES APPLÈ, S.E./FI
COLLINS ALMON, M/Staff/Chief
