23 January 1962

If this Headquarters and the Department of State approve,
the Chief of Station plans to give to Minister of Government
Gustavo DIAZ Ordaz:

a. A tape recording of a long conversation of one MOTA, a DFI agent, with Cuban Ambassador PORTUONDO, his secretary and Mrs. PORTUONDO. MOTA entered the Cuban Embassy on 18 January 1962, pretending to be a political student. The Cubans called MOTA a prevacateur and loudly protested their innocence, saying they had not intervened in Mexican internal affairs. (The Chief of Station has played this tape for DIAZ Ordaz, who remarked, "They protest too much.")

b. A tape recording of a conversation between a group of Colombian revolutionaries and Ambassador PORTUONDO, in which the former said an armed insurrection had already begun in practice in Colombia. They said the Colombian revolution was a part of the Cuban and Mexican revolutions and asked for solidarity and collaboration of all revolutionaries. PORTUONDO said he would send their report to Habana so that Cuba would have news of the situation and know who they are.

c. A fake copy of a page from the personal file of Angel BOAR of Prusa Latinia, who is now in Cuba.
