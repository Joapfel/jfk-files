SUBJECT : TRAVEL OF MAURICE HALPERIN AND WIFE, COMMUNISTS

1. ACCORDING TO A PERSON WITH ACCESS TO TRAVEL RECORDS, WHO IS A USUALLY RELIABLE SOURCE, MAURICE HALPERIN AND HIS WIFE, AMERICAN COMMUNISTS WHO ARE SUSPECTED OF BEING SOVIET AGENTS, LEFT MEXICO CITY VIA KLM FLIGHT 968 FOR CURACAO, AND LISBON VIA KLM FLIGHT 722 ON 13 OCTOBER.
