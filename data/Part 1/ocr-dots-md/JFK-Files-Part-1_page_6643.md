1. G-E reported that on 7 May 1950 a B-26 type bomber arrived in David, Panama, from Brownsville, Texas, due to a low gasoline supply. The plane was loaded for Tocumen Airport in Panama City, and carried as a crew the following according to G-E:

Frank ORGEL, U.S. Citizen, Pilot
O. MARTINEZ, U.S. Citizen, Co-Pilot
Steve MURRAY, U.S. Citizen, Mechanic
Mauricio CASTRO, Panamanian, Passenger

A tire blew out at David, and Mr. John LERCAS, Civil aeronautics technician at Tocumen Airport, was instrumental in getting a spare tire to them.

2. [Mr. LERCAS advised this office that he had not Steve MURRAY in Havana in April 1950 in company with a fellow named ZORRILLO, who claimed to have some connection with the Secret Police. In-Mexico, LERCAS described MURRAY as an adventurer who had been in the Philippines and the Far East and whom he suspected of gold smuggling activities. [LERCAS said that MURRAY was pilot of the plane and he thought it was unusual that so fast a plane as this and as expensive to operate was being used when it had comparatively little cargo space. In May 1950, when MURRAY was in Panama, he told LERCAS that he was buying orchid bults.]

3. On 16 June 1950 the following information was received from Civil Intelligence Section, Panama Canal, regarding this matter:

"It has been reported that very frequently a B-26 airplane (N5546N - Martin Manaudor) makes trips between Tocumen and David, R.P. It is piloted by Steve MURRAY, and at least two other Americans ride on the plane, which sometimes met at Tocumen Airport by either Tito, Harrodito or Toni ARIAS. On landing at the airport it goes directly to a hangar, and allegedly the only ones who visit it are the ARIAS brothers, PASTOR Ponce, Chief of the Airport, and one (fnu) LERDES, Chief of Customs at Tocumen.

"Informant stated that it was believed at first that the plane was engaged in arm contraband, but later it appeared more logical to assume that it was involved in another type of smuggling, possibly drugs.

Virgilio RAMIREZ, then Chief of the Control Tower, is said to have stated that the plane came and went without being registered at the airport under orders of POLICE Airport Manager; that the plane lands without signaling
