DESENSITIZED

1. COS AND GENERAL MONTIEL DISCUSSED REF PROBLEM 23 MAY.

DESPITE HIS REASSIGNMENT AS MINISTER TREASURY MONTIEL STILL INTERESTED THIS MATTER. BECAUSE PRESIDENT CHARGED HIM WITH CS SOLUTION. HE EXPLAINED QUANTITY TOO GREAT TO BE BROUGHT MANAGUA AND FORWARD PANAMA BY GON AS ORIGINALLY ENVISIONED.

THIS UP TO WOFACT ARRANGE TRANSPORTATION. SUGGESTED PLANE OR BOAT BE BROUGHT TO EL BLUFF. SAID PRESIDENT SOMOZA HAS MADE IT CLEAR MATERIAL TO BE REMOVED FROM NICARAGUA IN MANNER PRESCRIBED BY COS.

2. AIR LISTS REVEAL PRIMITIVO ABLANEDO HAS DEPARTED FOR MIAMI. ASSUME COLONEL BRENES GUARDING HOUSE WHERE ARMS STORED. COS AND TABOTH FEEL BEST MEANS REMOVE ARMS IS BRING SIZE CARGO PLANE INTO EL BLUFF AIRSTRIP, LOAD AT NIGHT, LEAVE NEXT MORNING. ALTERNATE MEANS, BRING BOAT TO DOCK IN FRONT HOME ROBERT BARTLETT, MANAGER BOOTH FISH PROCESSING PLANT. BARTLETT LIVES SECRET
