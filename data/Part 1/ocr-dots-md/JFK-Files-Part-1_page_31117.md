U.S. DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
WASHINGTON, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

October 07, 1960

Mr. Donald V. Iullesky
Chief, Procurement & Placement
Employees Division
Central Intelligence Agency
Washington 18, D.C.

Dear Mr. Iullesky:

Thank you for your letter (HD -3), dated 18 October 1960, which informed me that interest had been expressed in my qualifications.

I have completed the three copies of Form #34-1, and am returning them to you under separate cover by registered mail.

I shall plan to be in Washington on Wednesday and Thursday, November 8 and November 9, 1950, for the interviews which you suggested. I shall plan to report to Temporary L. Building on the south side of the reflecting pool adjacent to the Lincoln Memorial at 9:30 A.M. of Wednesday, November 8, 1950.

If it should be impossible for us to have interviews on November 8 and 9, please notify me by letter or telegram. I shall leave Brooklyn for Washington at about 9:45 A.M. on Tuesday, November 7.
