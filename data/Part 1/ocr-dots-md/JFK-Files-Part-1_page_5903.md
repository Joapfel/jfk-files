OCHAR was asked first to bring me up to date on what he and the others in his group have been doing and he reported that they were not doing much of anything except waiting. Waiting, first, for some funds which they are expecting to come primarily from the sale of the C-66; and, second, waiting for the establishment of some better communication with their contacts in Cuba.<br/>2. With regard to the aircraft, DCHM told me on this date that he believes he does now have a sale for the aircraft and he thinks the sale price will be between US$50 and US$55 thousand.<br/>3. OCHAR said I that [OBU] has communicated with someone (he wasn't sure of the name) in an attempt to solicit funds from people in Cuba. OCHAR presented this in the light that [OBU] was "authorizing" some people in Cuba to go ahead and solicit funds. In this connection, OCHAR mentioned that at one time in the past Julio LOBO (note: this name has never been mentioned in any previous conversations with this group) had considered donating US$100 thousand for the air force school which OCHAR was promoting at that time, but apparently became frightened of the deal and nothing along this line materialized. OCHAR said that he was hopeful that sometime he would be able to re-establish contact with LOBO. All his reference is entirely coincidental to my suggestions made to OCHAR that I might try to bring this group together with Julio LOBO. Having learned of this previous connection, I want to revive this idea and proceed with the plan to bring these two together.<br/>4. If and when they are able to accumulate some funds, it is their intention to go ahead with their plans for the establishment of a propaganda program which would be initially voice radio. OCHAR said that he had been trying to persuade OBIAS and [OBU] to a more narrow program within the limitations which I had cited for them in a previous meeting (See UIMA-11). He said he thinks now that this is the more realistic approach, [OBU] holds to the original thinking that they cannot commit themselves to such limitations. OCHAR informed me that [OBU] requests another meeting with me. He was told that I would be away for a few days but would contact him as soon as I return.<br/>5. I then questioned OCHAR about his knowledge and OBIAS' knowledge of the presence of Soviet Russians in Cuba. OCHAR stated that his only information had come to him through Douglas ROOF (this is the Canadian whom OCHAR referred to frequently in his earlier debriefing by SADELLY et al.). [Root] claimed to him to have seen the 05 Soviets in Santiago de Cuba and OCHAR said that furthermore he had obtained the names which he passed to Colonel WILKINS in the American Embassy in Havana. When asked if he thought these were the same Soviets that OBIAS had referred to in his testimony in Washington, OCHAR said he felt that they were, although he believed OBIAS' information was obtained independent of his, OCHAR's, source.<br/>6.
