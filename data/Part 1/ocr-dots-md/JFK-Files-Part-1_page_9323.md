27 June '90

Re: "STALIN'S PHOTO-BOOK.

Target: Stalin,

Basic思想: Stalin means H.I.,

For publication in: to be suitable for publication in any country.
France to start with.

Item: BS: "It should be so good that Stalin himself couldn't answer it. Captions to be as far as possible genuine quotes from Marx, Lenin, Stalin, Molotov, under photos showing undeniable facts such as the assassination of Frosky. Nothing phony. If we have a book that no one in the Arelin can pick holes in, it will be good for publication anywhere in the world."

Item: BS: Target is Stalin and his regime. "It should not contain a line that would offend any non-Communist Russian".

Item: BS: "Don't use any quotes from avoided anti-Stalinists such as Kravchenko."

Title: "Joseph Stalin", "This Man Wants 'Peace!', "This Man Talks Peace!", "Thirty-three Years of Peace", "The Trojan Dove".

BS: Don't let's worry about the title now.

Item: BS: It says there is a very striking contrast between photos of the Polituro leaders in the 20's and untouched photos of them now, e.g. in the official 20-minute news-reel of May Day 1949 in Red Square, bloated patents. BS: It looks (28 June) as though this news-reel, and stills from it, may be available. BJ: If it's technical, so much the better.

Item: BS: "Victorial History of Russia" by John Stuart Martine.
