Dear Mr. Tichborn:

Reference is made to your contract with the United States Government, as represented by the Contracting Officer, effective 19 May 1961, as amended.

Effective 30 September 1963, said contract, as amended, is further amended by adding the following paragraph after paragraph ten (10) of said contract:

"1. Health Insurance. You are herein authorized to apply for enrollment in a health insurance program for certain selected con- tract individuals of this organization, subject to all the terms and conditions of that program. If accepted, this organization will bear that portion of your total premium cost which it is authorized to bear for the original group of contract participants in said program. you will bear the remainder. Your financial contribution will be effected either by payroll deduction or by direct remittance at periodic inter- vals to be established by this organization.

All other terms and conditions of the contract, as amended, remain in full force and effect.

UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

BY
Contracting Officer
