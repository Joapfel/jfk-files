37 March 1968

RESEARCH FOR: CI/CA
Attention: Mr. Jack Lally

SUBJECT : Request for an Amendment of ARMY-117's
          Operational Approval (201-307537)

1. It is requested that Subject's Operational Approval be amended for his one-time use to assist in the easing and possible installation of a proposed audio operation under the cover of an air conditioner repairman against the Soviet Union in Buenos Aires.

2. We would appreciate expedient handling of this request because this operation is expected to get underway sometime in early April 1965, (per BUENOS AIRES-0341, IE-73483).

3. This request has been coordinated with Mr. David Phillips, W/L/COC.

Raymond A. Barros
