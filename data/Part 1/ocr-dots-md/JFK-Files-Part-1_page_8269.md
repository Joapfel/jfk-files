July 16, 1969

Contact Report
Subject: LIOAI-1 (p-8903)
Date of meet: July 17 at Hoyo 19 at 1430 hrs

1. Prearranged meet -which lasted 90 minutes.
   Next meet: August 17 at the same place.

2. On Friday July 11 LIBACON-1 gave a regular language class to L-1 and L-1 stated his interest to meet the Sov girl at LIBACON-1's house. And on the course of conv L-1 explained to LIBACON-1 by he believed this was important to him or in other words he gave him the cover story about which we spoke at our previous meet. LIBACON-1 assured L-1 that he would bring the Sov girl (Alla KIEFOVA) to his house.

3. On July 16 at 1000 hrs, i.e. at their next class LIBACON-1 told L-1 that he Background to the Sov Emb to visit LCHUOY and he found him this time. In the course of conv with LOG he (LIBACON-1) told him about meeting Alla and his plans to invite her to his home and show her around. LOG told him that Alla was his secretary and that he should not dare to get close to her.

4. I'll felt defected with this development, because he believed that he was on the way/developing something for us. I told L-1 that there was no reason of feeling bad about this fact, especially, because LOG was leaving relatively soon and the girl would remain here.

5. L-1 told me that on July 11 LIBACON-1 had taken an old friend from his office to see a movie and he asked L-1 how long he'd be there if he wanted it. Apparently, LIBACON-1 promised the magazine to L-1 and wanted to joke with the picture but LOG became very angry about the fact "that International Figure" are used for cheap porn. L-1 suspected that LOG might have asked from there if he saw it. LIBACON-1 got his name.

6. L-1 planned to talk to LOG on July 20th. The meeting took place as scheduled.
