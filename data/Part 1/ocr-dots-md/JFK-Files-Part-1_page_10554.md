EXCERPTS FROM: Memorandum for the Record

SUBJECT : Regular Weekly Meeting with Department of State, 6 November 1967

S. Mr. Broe noted that the Rolando Master trial is finally coming up in Miami. He left a document concerning the present status of the trial with Mr. Sayre for his personal and later return to the Agency. A similar memorandum of Mr. Sherman Kent's discussions with Diaz Orders was also given to Mr. Sayre for reading and return at a convenient time.
