# AMOUNT/2

12 January 1960

Subject: Ideo A

AMOUNT/2 was interviewed and tested on 6 January 1960 at a safe site in the Miami area. The interview was requested by Heuben A. HANNAHA and it was conducted in Spanish. ARSIB/1 served as interpreter.

AMOUNT/2 is being recruited as a potential infiltration team member.

RESULTS

AMOUNT/2 has never taken part in any activity which he regarded as communist. He was a member of the 26th of July segment but did not believe it to be communist at the time.

He has not cooperated with any Castro Intelligence or Security service since leaving DIEI in 1959. He has had no contact unknown to KUBARK with any Castro agents since 1960. (In this connection see WAVE 8248, dated 8 September 1962). He has no connection or agreement with the government of any country other than PERNICE.

He is not obligated to any of the Cuban Exile groups.

He has been essentially truthful in regard to his personal history and he does not believe that he is liable to blackmail. He has not used or trafficked inPORSCHEs.

Unless otherwise noted below, he has not told any unauthorized persons of his involvement with KUBARK.

DETAILS

AMOUNT/2 has been LOPUTTELED by ODIDEX.

AMOUNT/2 has told his wife that he is involved with KUBARK but he has not been specific regarding his assignment. He stated that Ideo A had also tried to join a HUNARIK unit so he certainly knows that AMOUNT/2 is involved. AMOUNT/2 stated that other friends probably suspect but he has said nothing to confirm their suspicions.
