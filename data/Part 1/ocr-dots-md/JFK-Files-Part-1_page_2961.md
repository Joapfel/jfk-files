HOFFMAN, F. Stephen 06

NOSENKO'S Information

An American Embassy official and a friend of the Ambassador's aide Robert ARMSTRONG (see above), HOFFMAN 06 is known to have engaged in homosexual acts with an Armenian in Yerevan. The KGB currently (in 1964) is planning to attempt to compromise and recruit HOFFMAN.

Comment: Interviewed on the basis of NOSENKO's allegation, HOFFMAN 06 in a State Department security interview denied homosexual activities. Informed that the results of a polygraph examination indicated he had practiced deception on that question, HOFFMAN 06 resigned from the Department of State on 31 July 1964. HOFFMAN 06 asserted that he had no contact with the KGB while assigned in Moscow, an assertion supported by the polygraph examination.

NOSENKO claims he was assigned to the Seventh Department at the time HOFFMAN 06 was the target of the Embassy Section of the American Department.
