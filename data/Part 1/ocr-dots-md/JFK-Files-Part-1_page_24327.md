ZIBET would appreciate receiving from JACOBSON any current material on the activities and contacts of the following, Africans who either reside in London or who visit there frequently. These targets have been selected according to the criteria outlined in paragraph 8 of the referenced dispatch.

### Ghana

William B E DOE-A N S A H - Sensitive Headquarters' sources have indicated that John K. T E T T E G A H is planning to return to West Africa. Since B E D O E-A N S A H is known to maintain contact with T E T T E G A H and was in fact responsible for T E T T E G A H's employment with a Japanese business firm in Cairo, we feel we may be involved in any new subversive moves undertaken by T E T T E G A H.

Kojo B OTS I O - There has been some indication through sensitive sources that B OTS I O has recently been travelling in Africa and that he too may be involved in T E T T E G A H's current activities.

(In addition we are still interested in the other targets mentioned in Reference C.)

### Nigeria

L. B A L O G U N - He is the purported author of the recently published Manifesto of Nigerian Young Revolutionaires Organization.

Alex C H I M A - He is an Ibo resident in London who is not only a self-described Marxist but is also a regular contributor to Advance the organ of the Marxist Nigerian Trade Union Confederation.

Sam I K O K U - Although I K O K U is the Commissioner for Economic Development and Reconstruction of the Nigerian East Central State and is resident in Europe, we understand that he occasionally visits London. We would appreciate being alerted to these visits and receiving information on his London contacts.
