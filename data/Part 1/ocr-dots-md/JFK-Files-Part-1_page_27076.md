On 14 June 1965, files of Headquarters, Office of Special Investigations, United States Air Force, were reviewed in the name of Antonio SOTO (VAZQUEZ), born 20 August 1934 at Marianas, Habana, Cuba, and reflected that OSI conducted a lie detector examination (File #72-90) on SUBJECT on 21 May 1963.

During the course of this examination, the SUBJECT reacted to basic questions concerning homosexuality, and as a result of this reaction, a re-examination was conducted during the morning of 12 June 1963 at Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama. The initial examination was predicated in that SUBJECT was a former member of 250th Cuban Brigade and was being processed for consideration for appointment as a Second Lieutenant in the United States Air Force.

Prior to re-examination, SUBJECT made an initial statement, the substance of which is as follows:

SUBJECT indicated he resided at 859 Second Avenue, 474, Santa Fe Beach, Banta, Cuba, from 1948 to 1959. He entered the Cuban Air Force as an Aviation Cadet in March 1953 and in August 1953, he began aviation training, which continued until August 1954. In August 1954, he was sent to the United States where he attended pilot training school until August 1955, at which time he graduated and was given a commission as a Second Lieutenant in the Cuban Air Force. During these periods of time, at various unrecalled intervals, SUBJECT stated he would return to his home at Santa Fe Beach. His homosexual acts at Santa Fe Beach occurred during 1949. This homosexual activity consisted of not more than five acts of anal intercourse. SUBJECT stated that he was the active or male partner in these acts. The passive partner was another Cuban boy who lived nearby. SUBJECT could not recall the name of the passive participant. During 1960, while at Managua, Nicaragua, as a Counter-Revolutionist preparing for invasion of Cuba, he became acquainted with a Cuban Catholic priest who was also a revolutionist. SUBJECT further stated that while attending a theatre, this priest made homosexual advances toward SUBJECT, which culminated in the SUBJECT being masturbated by the priest.
