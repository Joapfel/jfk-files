1. For the information of COB, Brussels: KUBAN and KUBAN are jointly engaged in the operational exploitation of the peculiar talents and contacts of a newly-acquired asset. STEBANT (Iden B) STEBANT has had a rather turbulent career as a leftist revolutionary agitator and propagandist over the past several years during which time he has come to know (and be known by) a number of significant personalities in the pre-Chinese fractions of several European Communist Parties. Among these personalities is Idem A. STEBANT has been denounced within those same circles as a KURANK agent for some time, though until January of this year KURANK was not in contact with him. It was only early June of this year that he actually signed a letter of agreement with an "OEVORON Service". Currently STEBANT is in Algiers seeking to re-establish himself in revolutionary circles as a prelude to a long-term penetration operation (at KURANK's behest). Targeted against the revolutionary cadres of Africa, Asia and Latin America which are apparently being given increasingly direct support by Peking.

2. STEBANT's primary accuser not only charges he is a KURANK agent but also an Idem A agent, which Idem A of course knows not to be the case. However, STEBANT is actually in the process of seeking to ingratiate himself with Idem A and of winning his confidence to such an extent that their personal relationship might serve as STEBANT's credentials for some time to come. At present the relationship shows signs of being off to a good start.
