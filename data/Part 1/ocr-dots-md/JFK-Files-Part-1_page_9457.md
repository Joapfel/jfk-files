* On the book matter, it would be wonderful if we could go ahead with our project of book exhibitions of all works of Congress authors. I have asked Pearl Kluger -- she can be reached at the above number in Freedom House -- to go ahead with a limited amount of orders from the publishers. I trust there will be money to cover it; although some of the bills will go out to be in Berlin.

* I am leaving tonight for New York Field. It was a big mistake not to get commercial air travel on my orders. No planes to London, none to Paris. First stop tomorrow -- Frankfurt! And there I turn around again. But then my contract calls for no breakdowns until the first of July. See you in the sanatorium at that time.

* Best regards to you and Marcia, and remember me to your children, who fascinated me.

"Ir's,

A/MAHIN J. LASEY'
