April 3, 1952

Dear Allen,

Thank you for your letter. I have no present intention of coming to Washington as for the past week I have been knocked out by this virus infection that has been going around. However, I look forward to seeing you whenever you find it convenient to drop in.

/s/ Frank

Mr. Allan W. Dulles
1308 - 29th Street, N.W.
Washington 7, D.C.
