23 June 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR: RECORD

SUBJECT: OPC Operations in Connection with Whitehead Committee Youth Demonstration in Berlin

1. At the request of the Departments of State and Defense, we had asked the Chief of the OPC German station to obtain a statement from Generalканwel. L. Taylor, U. S. Commissioner in Berlin, so as to the effectiveness of the subject operations.

2. The cablegrams from Lawrence to Louisville, Chief of OPC German Mission, dated 22 June, stated that General Taylor had asked Mr. De Leefville to convey his appreciation in the following terms:

a. "It has been of most-positive value to my head office to know that lines of action outlined by our propaganda authorities have been disseminated throughout the Soviet Zone by a group such as yours. The success of the OPC mission in distributing over one million pieces of propaganda material before and during the Deutschlandtreffen is an achievement upon which the organization should be congratulated."

b. "Your mission is a delicate one in that you are specifically charged with avoiding attribution of your activities to any U. S. Government source or agency. This you have evidently succeeded in doing and it has been a matter of satisfaction to me to know that various matters that it was in our interest to accomplish could be performed by OPC without disclosing American interest or activity."

c. "If judgment from Berlin is of value to your organization, let us say that OPC work has proved its value during the Deutschlandtreffen. We are relying on similar or increased help from OTC in the future."

3. The German mission has advised that the original communication from General Taylor was published on 27 June 1950.

LOUIS GLASER
JOHNSON & COMPANY Executive for Germany, III
