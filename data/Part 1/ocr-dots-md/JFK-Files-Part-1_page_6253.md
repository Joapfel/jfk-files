UNIVERSITIES STATUS

Before Communism

1.- There were 8 universities in Cuba.

2. In 1959 there were more than 30,000 students in the 8-universities.

3.- The Constitution of 1940 stipulated that 1% of the national budget would go to the University of Havana,

4.- The Great Charter of Cuba guaranteed autonomy to the Universities, freeing them from interference by the government.

5.- There was a free and open exchange of ideas in the classrooms.

6.- Every year the students elected their own representatives in free elections.

7.- The universities served as spokenmen for the wishes of the Cuban people.

8.- To enter the Universities it was necessary to be 17 years of age and to have graduated from a secondary school or to have taken rigorous tests to prove knowledge equivalent to a secondary school education.

9.- Ninety percent of the University professorate is in exile or has retire, unwilling to have communist doctrines emposed upon it.

10.- Hundreds of university students are held in communist prisons, only because of their love for liberty. The lists of cuban martyrs, murdered by firing squads or killed by its rebellion, had been increased by the names of Cuban universities students such as: Juanin Pereira, Virgilio Campaneria, Julio A. Yebra, Tapia Ruan, Porfirio Ramirez, and so many others that had preferred to die than to submit to communism.

With Communism

1.- There are three universities.

2. There are no more than 20,000 university students presently.

3.- The Constitution of 1940 has been abolished and the Communist regime has cut by 75% the funds destined for the University of Havana.

4.- The regime subjects the Universities requiring them to follow its dictates in all matters.

5. Marxism Leninism is required subject for all students and all other ideologies are forbidden.

6.- There have been no election since September 1959. At one point when an effort was made to have them held, the Communists arranged a so called "general assembly" where by "popular acclaims", those who have since held control, were "elected".

7. Today, they are servile instruments of the regime.

8.- No requisites concerning age or previous education are set. There have been cases of fourteen-year old with little previous education studying at the universities.

9.- It is no longer a requisite to be a college graduate in order to be able to teach at the university. Second year students teach freshmen, third year students teach second year ones, etc...

10.- While real students left their classes, are held in prison, went to exile or fight communism, the Red Government uses uneducated young people as university students, with the only purpose to serve communist International Scene.
