JUN 5 1965

MEMORANDUM FOR THE SECRETARY OF STATE

ATTENTION: Mr. Fisher Moore

Subject: Request for Clearance for Return of Mr. James H. Moore

Reference: a. Memorandum for Mr. Neal (upon this office, subject Mr. James Walton Moore, dated 5 December 1965)

1. This is to advise you that Mr. James H. Moore, CIA representative who is presently serving at Calcutta, India with the Foreign Service title of Vice Consul, is returning to the United States on or about 1 August 1969 for a permanent change of station.

2. It is requested that clearance be arranged for Mr. Moore's return to the United States and that an unclassified message, to include a travel order for Mr. Moore and his three dependents, be issued by the Department of State.
