PICO Paredón, Reinaldo

Parents: Infante and Maria de la Paz. DPOB: 27 July 33, Plegue, Havana. Married to Corren FJNC Herrera. Brothers: Hector, Sonja and Kiliegros. Address: 2867 CV 16th., Miami. Address in Cuba: Paredón 516, Apt. 2, Poparito Palatino, Corte. Arrived at the US on 29 Dec. 60 from Teiocigalpa, Honduras. Passport 35972. Source: Personal data recruitment form, 17 Jan 61.

26 years old. Married. Cuban. High School Education. Civil servant.
Secretary of the ACPR.
Source: MRR registration form.

Says he will not return with the rest of the GFFM prisoners
that came in a Commission to try to obtain an exchange of
prisoners.

Source: Memo C-300 (from Newspaper "El Popular", 29 July 61).

DPOB: 21 July 33, Plegue, Havens. Current address: 1067 CV 8th St. Miami. Joined the military commission on 1st Cent. Cl (transportation organization) Metropolitan buses. Does not receive pay.
Unemployed.
Sources: Form as member of the Revolutionary Organization, Military Commission, 16 March 62.

Born in Plegia, Havana on 21 July 33. Address: 1067 CV 8th St. Miami.
