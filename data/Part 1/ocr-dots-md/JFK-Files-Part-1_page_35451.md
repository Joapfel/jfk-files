## AMLASH/1 - Paris Station Aspect

Paris Station involvement with the AMLASH case began in 1962, after AMLASH/1 came to Paris after attending the Helsinki Youth Festival that year. In Helsinki, AMLASH/1 was contacted by UNRINDELE/? (an asset of the Paris Station who attended the Festival for purposes of disruption) and by other KUBARK assets. It is not clear from our files just when AMLASH/1 was "recruited" but it appears that he was recruited during or shortly after the Festival.

In Paris he was in contact with Peter J. KIMNOHAM (probably in alias although I know that AMLASH/1 was at KIMNOHAM's home upon occasion). In 1962 AMLASH/1 was given training in explosives by OHMEX (I believe) upon request of the Paris Station. He appears to have returned to FRANCEEN in late 1962. It is not known if other Paris Station personnel were in contact with AMLASH/1 during that period.

AMHASH/1 next arrived in Paris on 14 September 1963, coming from the International Games held in Puerto Alegre, Brazil, where he was in contact with TYPIC officers. At this point AMHASH/1 (now QUEPORT) was in contact with AMLASH/1 in Paris and AMHASH/1 was another course channel (the one being used primarily by Eqs.). The Headquarters TYPIC officer, ONTRICH (P), arrived in Paris on 3 October 1963 and picked up the contact with AMLASH/1 which was being maintained by AMPORT/1 and KIMNOHAM. The meetings with AMLASH/1 took place in KIMNOHAM's home as host I remember. These discussions concerned AMLASH/1's plans to assassinate AMHASH/1 and overthrow his regime.

ONTRICH returned to Paris in late October, after discussions at Headquarters on the case. ONTRICH and DAVIDID, who were there in Paris at the time, met with AMLASH/1 in late October or early November. DAVIDID's participation was due to AMLASH/1 strong desire to talk with some high-level OMYONE official concerning his plans and to obtain OMYONE blessing if possible. I am not aware of exactly what happened at these meetings but I received the impression from KIMNOHAM that such blessing was given. AMLASH/1 returned to FRANCEEN on 27 November 1963.
