25 March 1952

**HONOLULU:** TO: Assistant Director for Policy Coordination
(Department (Plans))
Deputy Director of Central Intelligence

SUBJECT: Attached Letter from Spencer Inexix

Attached is a letter from Spencer Inexix to Bob Lang in which Phoenix details the story of his efforts to reach agreement with the German authorities on the status of IGZ under new post-HOG government. I would judge that Spencer has done a wonderful job to date and that things are on the track, but I think you would be interested in reading his letter and the second attachment, which is the memorandum of understanding with the German Government on which he is now working.

Attachment

Ltr 3 Mar 52 to Bob Lang.
