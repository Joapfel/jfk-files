DEC 2 1 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR THE SECRETARY OF STATE

ATTENTION: Mr. Fisher Howe

Subject : Cancellation of Foreign Service Title
of Administrative Assistant at Bombay
for Miss Ann Lorene Goodpasture

1. This is to advise you that Miss Ann Lorene Goodpasture, CIA representative who has been serving at Bombay, India with the Foreign Service title of Administrative Assistant, has been transferred to Washington for a permanent change of station. It is requested, therefore, that the Foreign Service title for Miss Goodpasture be cancelled.
