SOURCE:

A member of a group of Cuban enigres trained in the techniques of information collection. The group has provided useful reports since 1961.

1. On 27 or 28 April 1966, in New York, Rolando Masferrer Rojas, former Cuban senator who has been involved in plans for action against President Francois Duvalier of Haiti, received a letter from the Haitian who is heading the operation against Haiti. The Haitian instructed Masferrer to send a trusted person to the office of Bill Harris, 7150 N.W. 27th Avenue, Miami, Florida, to talk with Vernon Johnson, a Negro. The Haitian described Johnson as a trustworthy person who would be in charge of getting arms. Under no circumstances was Masferrer's emisary to talk with anyone else.

2. Antonio Rojas Masferrer, Rolando Masferrer's cousin in Miami, contacted Johnson, who said that he had in the Miami area all the equipment and arms needed for the operation without waiting was only awaiting the orders release them.

3. Rojas and Enrique Masferrer Rojas, Rolando Masferrer's brother, asked a son of Cesar Caracho Coventi, who was Minister of Justice in Cuba under Fulgencio Batista Kilió Zaldivar, to investigate Johnson. Caracho's son, who conducts private investigations, reported later that Johnson is an arms runner who also deals in military equipment.
