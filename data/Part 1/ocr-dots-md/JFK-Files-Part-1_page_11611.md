CHILEAN CHIEF'S CHILEAN NEWSPAPER
NEWSPAPER'S TREATMENT OF KLEIN-SAKS MISSION

ACTION REQUESTED: As requested in paragraph 1.

1. Please advise CICATINE of the following, which has been received by Chief, AN Division, from the COs, Santiago, regarding CHCATINE's newspaper.

2. "...The newspaper owned by CHCATINE has been engaging in an all-out campaign against the Klein-Saks Mission and their economic program for Chile. While the newspaper in itself is not terribly importamt, the Embassy has become disturbed because of the newspaper being owned by an Americans. Recently the Ambassador called in the local editor of the newspaper and asked for an explanation. He recalled that he was himself in complete accord with the Klein-Saks program until that his hands were tied as he was acting upon instructions from his publisher. Personally, I think that that was probably not true but was simply a means of passing the buck to exonerate himself from a difficult situation. I do not believe that CHCATINE is very much at fault with the activities of the newspaper and that its policy is left pretty much in the hands of the local editor. Unfortunately, the Air Attache, who has been here for a considerable period, mentioned to the Ambassador that he thought I might be able to throw some light on the situation due to the fact that he, himself, knew HCATINE had some sort of connection with this matter. I stated if there was such a connection, I was not confident of it. Nevertheless, I think that the most circumspect thing that could be done would be to expunge HCATINE of what is going on in his newspaper, so that he could correct the situation if he so desires. I understand the Ambassador intends to write a very strong letter directly to CICATINE. A strong statement in reply could very well avoid embarrassment at a future date for Mr. E.".
