III.- PERSONALITY OF ROSA ALITRIEVA

A certain duality of personality is apparent in her behavior. In
her married life she shows herself to be intolerant and impetuous, arriving
at absolute lack of control on various occasions, bringing forth continual
marital quarrels.

On the contrary, in her social life, she shows herself as a very
decile, cheerful woman, being the center of attraction in almost all the
gatherings for her "lovely disposition" and gayety, not only among the men
but also their wives. (See section I).

Rosa Alekseevna ALFERIEVA is a woman brought up in the typical
soviet atmosphere, subjected since childhood to a rigid political indoctrination
in some juvenile organisation and in school. She is not sufficiently intelligent
to rid herself, even partially, of what she has been taught. She does not separate
her affective life from the obligations that she, presumes, every good communist
citizen should have. It seems that she does not realise the importance of
her husbands mission. She is not a woman who is attuned to her companion.
She is not on the same wave length, she does not understand him. She barbers
a concentrated and continuous reascuitment against him. Maybe this is due to
his ironic, and keen and wounding sense of humour.
