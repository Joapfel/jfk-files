17 January 1958

MEMORANDUM FOR: Mr. John Trawe
Department of State

SUBJECT: Memorandum of Conversation of Meeting at Mr. Barrett's Home on November 20, 1958 to discuss USIS and OIC Relationships.

1. Your memorandum of conversation of November 20, 1951, was mentioned to me by Mr. Dulles this morning with particular reference to paragraph 2 of the conclusions.

2. Mr. Dulles does not believe that the meeting was quite so definite on this point as you indicate, and I suggest, therefore, an amendment as follows:

*2. That possibilities for toning down the Crusade for Freedom to a somewhat less high-pressure and specific campaign be carefully explored by all concerned.*

THOMAS W. BRAeden
