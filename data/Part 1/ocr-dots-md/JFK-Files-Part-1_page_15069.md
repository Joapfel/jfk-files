COMMENTS:

CO dropped in to see Subject and found him all hopped up about his latest invention. He preferred not to discuss it with CO but says that he is so sure that it is a money maker that he intends to apply for a German patent as soon as he works out all the details. CO told him that he should hold off as long as possible but in any event he should remember that his name is Georg Sanders and that any business he conducts should by done using his Hohemarkstr. address. 145.

CO may be wrong but he feels that Subject is agreeing to immigrate to S.A. only in the hope that we will provide him with a stake of some kind.
