## 6. Counter-intelligence matters

Counter-intelligence matters constitute the major LI portions of this of this Station's related Mission Directive and MI -41. It has contributed more leads and general information on the Soviet aspect of this subject than all other Station sources combined. The following reports are cited as examples of many of this type of reports and information developed in this category:

a. MI -1958, MI -2266, describing contacts between Narciso DAS, JILS, leading Mexican pro-Communist, and Viktor F. GLOTOV, his Soviet case officer who appears in LIEMET under the name of "LOPEZ."

b. MI -2218, MI -2262 and MI -1336, dealing with the contacts between the leading Venezuelan Communist, Carlos Augusto Leon, and his Soviet case officer, Doris E. KOLOMYAKOV. A comparison of readings from LIEMET/LIEHICH with LIEMET coverage on the Venezuelan Communist Party Officer Eduardo AGOSTO Morales indicates that Leon is probably the intermediary between the Soviets and the Venezuelan exiles.

c. MI -2420, setting forth arrangements for clandestine contact between Colonel Jesus de LUCIANOFORAL, of the Mexican General Staff College, with his Soviet case officer.
