Bogotá, D. E., October 17, 1966

Exmo. Sr.
Subajador del Japon
En Colombia
E. S. D.

**Innovation:**

On account of the forthcoming Trade Seminar that will take place from the 12th to the 15th of this month in the City of Tokyo, with the participation of some Latin American countries, the CONFEDERATION OF WORKERS OF COLOMBIA, CTC, wishes to reiterate through you, to the Democratic Government of Japan, the request we have formulated in other occasions, reforent to the count of the permanent trade that Japan has established with the Cuban Commonwealth, considering that the currency obtained from the selling of products is used by the Government of Cuba to finance the subversion and terrorism in Latin America.

We formulate our request in the hope of saving the interests of the French Democratic workers and the institutional integrity of our country, since Colombia, the same as other American countries, form part of the in - filmation program that has been planned from Cuba by the International Communist Party, as was confirmed in the newspaper "El Tiempo" in one of its editions on October 12.

It consists in this petition because we consider that if Japan continues to trade with Communist Cuba, it would be the same as to contribute to the annihilation of a regime in America which has put an end to the democratic freedom, has promoted the mass murder and the imprisonment of thousands of demo crats, the leaders and workers. If this situation persists, we would be forced to terminate, together with other countries of America, the boycott to all Japanese products as we have done with the ships of those countries that are holding trade with Cuba.

Looking to hear from you soon, we remain,

CS COPY
