3 April 1962

Mr. H.F. Roper
Chief, Benefits and Services Division
Central Intelligence Agency
Washington 25, D.C.

Dear Mr. Roper:

Enclosed please find the signed Application for Leave as requested in your letter. Your attention and consideration of this matter is my behalf is greatly appreciated.

Please forward any further correspondence on this matter to my address at Andrews Air Force Base, Washington D.C.

Waiting for an early release from duty and return to the fold, I remain,

SCREEMED
