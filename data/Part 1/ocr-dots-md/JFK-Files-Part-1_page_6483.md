Provisional Guide No. 1. (Cost)
CRITERIA FOR POLOUTY ASSISTANCE UNDER ESCAPED REFUGEES

Sub paragraph E of unnumbered paragraph following paragraph five of Provisional Guide No. 1 is amended to read:

"E. Is recognized by the competent authorities of the country in which he resides or other country outside of the area of Soviet domination as having the rights and obligations which are attached to the possession of citizenship of that country, e.g., German refugees and exiles, persons of Turkish ethnic origin from Turkey and Bulgaria are excluded from assistance under this paragraph."
