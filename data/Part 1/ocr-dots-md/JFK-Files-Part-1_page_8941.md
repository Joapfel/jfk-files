19 March 1963

Paul O:

Tichborns original contract provided for travel on an actual expense basis. This was when he was working on a daily or job rate basis.

This contract was terminated and a new contract was written in May 1961. He was given $8,000 per year, and the reimbursement for operational travel section reads "This may include per diem in lieu of subsistence--."

None of Tichtorn's travel claims have been processed since the new contract has been effective. All travel claims submitted by him starting in June 1961 have been submitted on an actual expense basis.

Finance takes the view that the "This may include per diem---" as restricting Tichborn to per diem only plus of course operational expenses. Processing Tichborns old accounts on this basis would require him to refund several hundred dollars.

An amendment to the contract would take care of these old accounts as submitted.

Options:

1. Get refund from Tichborn

2. Amend contract to cover actual expenses to present date.

3. Amend contract to cover actual expenses past and future.

What are your wishes?

/ Currie
