SECTION III: OBJECTIVES OF THE PROGRAM.

*   To prepare students to the skills and techniques needed in the protection of intelligence materials.

*   To do so practice in oral and written press: order of presentation.

SECTION I: SPECIFIC CHARACTERISTICS OF THE PROGRAM

The Intelligence Program includes a four week "習题班" conducted for each of the first three months but available to postgraduate students or staff by arrangement. In the instance of intelligence, contact is on a "revocation by delinquency." The program consists of a series of intramural and intergroup activities. Classes are also planned for local intelligence "on-the job" exercises. Specific procedures will be outlined at the start of the program. A separate reference section is provided for each student. It is understood that this is an additional resource required in addition to the normal reading list and should be consulted at the end of the course. Students are allowed only in the course schedule those periods of study as outlined above which are specifically listed in the Schedule of Courses. The schedule may be adjusted according to the approval of the director if it is deemed necessary to meet the requirements of the State or Federal Government. Any student who fails to receive an average grade of B or better in his major subject(s) will have his record entered on file under the heading of "Irritation" and a "W" will appear on his report card.
