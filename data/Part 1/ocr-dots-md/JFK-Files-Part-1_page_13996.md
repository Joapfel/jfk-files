Convert to MarkdownAPRIL TO MAY NATIONAL ACADEMY OF MEDICINE INTERVIEWED -- HAVANA, MAY 15

Pedro Badza, Cuban delegate to the Centenary celebration of the National Academy of Mexico, was interviewed upon his return to Havana. The most important event at the celebration, he told reporters, was his meeting with US scientist Linus Pauling who wishes to visit Cuba.

BOLIVIAN DELEGATE TO LABOR DAY CELEBRATION LAUDS REVOLUTION -- HAVANA, MAY 20

Paulino Tito Calle, secretary for foreign affairs of the Agrarian Workers Union of Canton Cuyara, in the Province of Le Puz Department, Bolivia, praised the Cuban Revolution and branded as false the propaganda against it. He is a member of the Bolivian delegation to the Cuban celebration of Labor Day which included Alejandro Silva and Mario Cortes.

SECOND SECRETARY RETURNS TO MEXICO CITY POST -- HAVANA, MAY 17

Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez, second secretary in the Cuban Embassy in Mexico, left Havana by plane for Mexico City to resume his post.

AMERICAN TO UN DEPARTS FOR NEW YORK -- HAVANA, REVOCATION, MAY 19

Ambassador to the UN Carlos M. Lechuga, accompanied by his wife Gladys Lauderman and daughter Miriam, left Havana for New York City to resume his post on 18 May.

SINGER TOURS COMMUNIST CHINA CITIES -- HAVANA, REVOCATION, MAY 24

Cuban singer Miriam Acayedo, who sang in various cities in the Soviet Union, is currently appearing in various cities in Communist China such as Peiping, Nanking, and Shanghai. While in the latter city, she was visited by Cuban students.
