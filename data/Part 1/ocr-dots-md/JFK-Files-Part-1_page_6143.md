30 May 1975

**MEMORANDUM FOR:** Chairman, Information Review Committee

**ATTENTION:** Mr. H.G. Boon, Executive Secretary

**SUBJECT:** FOIA Appeal - Philip A. Singerman

**REFERENCES:**

a. Request from Mr. Singerman dated 1 April 1975
b. OPS/ITC Memo dated 14 May 1975
c. DO/83 #75-340 dated 16 May 1975
d. IRS 75-801 dated 30 May 1975
e. Appeal from Mr. Singerman dated 26 June 1975
f. OPS/ITC Memo dated 15 July 1975

1. The initial request and subsequent appeal by Mr. Singerman, the Agency's correspondence to him, and the documents that pertain to Mr. Singerman have been reviewed and the documents involved are as follows:

a. B-561, 11 August 1969

b. HDPA-37509, 10 July 1969

c. HDPA-37416, 23 June 1969

d. Headquarters 876, 24 June 1969

e. MEXICO CITY 329, 23 June 1969

f. MEXICO CITY 330, 24 June 1969

g. EX-3663, 24 October 1969

h. TH-55988, 26 August 1969

2. Items a., b., and c. from the list in paragraph 1. were the basis of the initial response and denial to Mr. Singerman (Reference d.). Items d. and e. do not mention Mr. Singerman by name but are relative and are included for background information. The documents f., g. and h. did not become available until after the initial response to Mr. Singerman and therefore were not considered in that reply.

3.- On the basis of our review, we believe items a., b. and c. were properly exempted in accord with (b)(1) and (b)(3) of the FOIA and 55(1) of E.O. 11632. This was reported to Mr. Singerman in Reference d. on 30 May 1975.
