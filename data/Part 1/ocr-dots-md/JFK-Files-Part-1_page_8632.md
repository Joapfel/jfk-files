Subject: AEDIEHARD (P-11,712) & Thoughts No possibility of Pitching Him

1. Assuming it was agreed that A should be pitched before his POS departure, because there are no indications that he would be more susceptible later to an approach later when he comes to a new post abroad in few years and, to the contrary, it is more likely that he would be still more daring, more selfconfident and more determined to advance and solidify his position within the KGB, I wish to add some views of mine to the list of possibilities as to how a pitch could be carried out.

2. My views and thoughts on this subject will be based on the possible use of existing assets and means. I am fully aware that there will be plenty of room for improvements, additions and modifications of what will be said in the following paragraphs.
