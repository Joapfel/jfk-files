Security Information

26 January 1953

FOR RELEASE: TOP SECRET

SUBJECT: Draft NOFF Policy Statement

1. Attached is a draft NOFF Policy Statement which has been reviewed and accepted by the Executive Committee of NOFF, but which was sent to us without the knowledge of Admiral Miller and therefore should probably not be commented on by you to any of our friends in New York unless you have also received a copy from another source. Admiral Miller probably intends to send us this statement after it has been approved by the Board of Directors.

2. You may wish to compare it with the old ice-box statement now in effect, also attached.

3. You may also wish to note Part I, paragraph 2 in which mention is made of government support. It is apparently the intent of NOFF to make this statement not an ice-box statement, but a public document and if so, I think you ought to make particularly certain that you approve this part of it.

4. Finally, I think you will want to note the general tenor of Part III. Perhaps I am quibbling, but it seems to me a trifle full-blown. I realize that NOFF is a separate government agency just under cabinet status, but this reminds a little bit as though it were putting on the trappings of national sovereignty.

THOMAS W. ERICKSON
Chief
International Organizations Division
