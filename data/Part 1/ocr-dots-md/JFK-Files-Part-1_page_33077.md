1. ANCHALK-5 WAS MET BY NEBECKER UNDER SECURE CONDITIONS IN NEW YORK CITY FROM 1600 TO 2230 ON 25 NOVEMBER FOLLOWING HIS MEETING WITH AN AMGHOST REPRESENTATIVE IN MONTREAL. THE FOLLOWING COVERS A-5'S NARRATIVE OF EVENTS. PREVIOUS CORRESPONDENCE COVERS THE PREPARATION FOR THE MEETING.

2. A-5 ARRIVED IN MONTREAL AS SCHEDULED AND PROCEED TO THE PLAZA MOTOR HOTEL AS INSTRUCTED BY CUIS LETTER. HE CONFIRMED RESERVATIONS UPON ARRIVAL AT MONTREAL AIRPORT. THE HOTEL WAS LOCATED IN A COMMERCIAL/RESIDENTIAL AREA REMOVED ABOUT FIFTEEN MINUTES DISTANCE FROM THE CENTER OF THE CITY. /THIS WAS CONFIRMED BY NEBECKER AND NOLTHAM./ ACCORDING TO A-5 NO SPANISH SPEAKERS APPEARED TO BE EMPLOYED BY THE HOTEL. FRENCH WAS DOMINANT LANGUAGE WITH LITTLE ENGLISH SPOKEN. A-5 CHECKED INTO THE HOTEL AT 1600 ON 24 NOVEMBER. AT 0645 ON 26 NOVEMBER HE WAS CALLED BY PERSON IDENTIFYING SELF AS FRIEND OF ALBERTO /SIGNER OF LETTER WHICH MADE CONTACT ARRANGEMENTS FOR MONTREAT/. CALLER IN CUBAN SPANISH SAID QUOTE CALLS ARE DIFFICULT TO MAKE. ALBERTO WILL CALL YOU BETWEEN SEVEN AND NINE UNQUOTE. AM OR PM WAS NOT SPECIFIED NOR DID A-5 HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO ASK.
