S NOV 1965

MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Director for Support

SUBJECT: Request for Exception to Provision of FR 45-105 — Terence S. CRIBANAC (P)

1. This memorandum contains a recommendation in paragraph 4 for the approval of the Deputy Director for Support.

2. Terence S. CRIBANAC (P) is a Contract Employee assigned to the Madrid area in Spain in a sensitive assignment since his conversion from a Staff Employee on 4 July 1963. CRIBANAC was a CS-15, step 7 ($19,880.00 per annum) and he is being paid at the same rate in his present contract status. His contract provides that he will be entitled to a living quarters allowance in conformance with regulations of this organization applicable to its appoiated personnel.

3. The basic living quarters allowance for Class II officers in Madrid is $3,000 per annum. The house which CRIBANAC has located, with a one year lease from 15 November 1965 to 14 November 1966, has an annual rent of $5,800.00 excluding utilities. Therefore, for rent alone, he will be $1,980.00 per year over the 160 percent maximum allowed under the provisions of FR 45-105. On a 75/25 percent sharing basis of the 60 percent over the quarters allowance, he would be out of pocket $2,095.00.

4. Due to the operational sensitivity of CRIBANAC's assignment, his request for relief on his housing costs was handled by Mr. Thomas Harnessing, ADDP. On 15 September 1965, Mr. Harnessing, ADDP, approved his being reimbursed on a 75/25 percent basis for the excess allowable costs up to, but not to exceed, 60 percent above his quarters allowance.
