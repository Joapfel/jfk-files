V. **OPERATIONAL OUTLINE**

A. <u>Area of Operation</u>

1. <u>Geographic area</u>

a) Chile

2. <u>Specific target groups</u>

a) Business and intellectual circles

b) Working class reader audience

B. <u>Operational Control</u>

1. <u>Control exercised by Headquarters</u>

WHD/KUGWNI will exercise operational control through DTHAREAS which will disburse funds to the FUNEBRIAL group in accordance with specific WED instructions. Control will be effected through the allotment of funds on the basis of budgeted need, through DTHAREAS, to the FUNEBRIAL group upon evidence of satisfactory performance of the missions assigned to this organization.

2. <u>Control exercised by the Field</u>

Chief of Mission, Santiago, will exercise indirect control through Staff Agent Martin J. COKERIAS. By means of independent checking and reporting procedures, Mission Santiago will keep headquarters informed of the FUNEBRIAL group's activities. Should the group fail to provide satisfactory performance, Mission Santiago will advise headquarters to withdraw financial and technical assistance.

C. <u>Duration of the Project</u>

It is anticipated that this project, if approved for FY 1954, will be continued in the future by means of a re-allotment of funds obtained through the yearly Operational Programs.
