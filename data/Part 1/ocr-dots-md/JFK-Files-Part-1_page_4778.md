In October 1974, the Bahamian authorities captured seven Cuban counterrevolutionaries who, on orders of the Alfa-66 organisation, were going to infiltrate Cuba through the northern coast of Oriente Province. They were to land Luis Lobaina and Aristides Marques armed with M-1 carbines and pistols with silencers. The objective of this mission was to assassinate Prime Minister Fidel Castro.
