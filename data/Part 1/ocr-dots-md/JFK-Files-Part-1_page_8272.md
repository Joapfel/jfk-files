3. GOLOVSKY was, as previously stated, more reserved. He stayed in the background and accompanied the unidentified Russian during most of the tour. FURILEY was introduced to him but little conversation passed between them. He appeared a little stiff, more the Russian stereotype of the reserved, social-ill-at-ease person. But this was just an impression as FURILEY had no chance for an indepth conversation. GOLOVSKY seemed to possess decent Spanish. He stated he was from the commercial section. Hopefully subject will attend other Corps meetings and FURILEY will have an opportunity to speak to him at greater length.

G A FURILEY
