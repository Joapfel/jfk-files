SUBJECT: SUAREZ, Jose C.I.R. Cuay Dec. 5 1963 Doc. No. 14

1. Source met Subject in late December 1957 in Mexico City, but had very little contact with him then. Source saw Subject again early in 1958 in a public office building in Havana. This was the last time he saw Subject. Source says that Subject was one who attacked the Cuartel Honcada on the 26 of July of 1953 and was arrested with Fidel CASTRO. Source believes the Subject is not a Communist. As far as source knows Subject does not hold any position in the Cuban government. Source does not know if Subject has ever traveled to any of the Soviet bloc countries or if he has any relatives outside Cuba.

2. Physical Description: PBOB: Circum 1926, probably Artemisa, Pinar del Rio. Race: white. Height: 5'6", Weight: 145 lbs. Hair: light. Eyes: light. Shape of face: long. Physique: medium. No visible scars.

3. JEWAVE Traces: One Jose SUAREZ, member of the 26th of July Movement, N.Y.C., resides at 610 W. 163rd Street, Apt. 16, N.Y. 112, N.Y. (DEW 97723, 29 Dec 61).
