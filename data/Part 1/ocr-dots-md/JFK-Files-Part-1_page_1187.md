### 1. SITUATION:

Daniel L. Carswell (Alfus), Roderic H. Durbrunt (Alfus) and Edward R. Taransky (Alfus) are TID Staff Employees who were apprehended by Cuban authorities in September 1960 and who are now serving ten year sentences. Efforts are being made to secure their release. Since their release may occur without advance notice out at a variety of places, ie. Havana, Miami, Mexico City, Switzerland, Israel, etc., it is imperative that a contingency plan be formulated to secure the secure receipt of these employees.

The controlling factor in all phases of the release procedure is that these employees were in Cuba under allies and last during the entire period since their capture. Their missions and other activities apparently have been maintained. Therefore it is essential that there shall be maintained until such time as the employees have returned to Agency control and then have these allies about them, lose disorderly as quickly as possible.
