Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO : Chief, Soviet Russia Division

FROM : Director of Security

SUBJECT: REASPIC - #174803

REPORTE: Memorandum from D/C, SR Division dated 29 September 1959

DATE: 6 October 1959

1. Action has been initiated with the Immigration and Naturalization Service to permit the subject to remain in the United States in a parole status for an additional six months' period. This is the normal length of time for which such paroles are granted. If necessary, at the expiration of six months, assuming the conduct of the subject so warrants, steps will then be taken to further extend his parole.

2. It is requested that this Office be provided with a complete physical description of the subject, along with front view and profile photographs. In addition, in order to satisfy the requirements of the IUS, it is felt that he should be presented to an appropriate official of the IUS for registration, which will include the taking of his fingerprints (for submission to the FBI). This registration procedure and other security aspects of his handling during his stay in the United States should be discussed with a representative of this Staff in the immediate future.

3. The request for a security approval for his utilization by TSS is currently being processed in the Security Support Division of this Office.

FOR THE DIRECTOR OF SECURITY:

William J. Citter
Chief, Alien Affairs Staff
