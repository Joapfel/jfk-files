## B. Purely Commercial Activities:

1. EISENBERG, Jacques, was contacted on 16 December 1955, and reportedly told the Soviets that the affair with the electrodes would have to be postponed until 10 or 15 January 1956, due to his (EISENBERG's) sickness. This electrode transaction was connected with the visit to Mexico of two Soviet engineering experts (Comments: See HMM-A-4139, pp. 2-4, 6 February 1956). 2. CONZALEZ, José R.,
Uruguay 55-218

CONZALEZ is a frequent contact of the Soviet Embassy Commercial Section, and their actual and proposed transactions cover a rather wide range.

### a. Rails and Pipes:

Early in December CONZALEZ and WAYEVSKI discussed the prospects for importing Soviet pipe and standard gauge railway rails into Mexico. It is reported that the market for rails in Mexico was not considered to be very hopeful, but the Soviets believed they might be able to arrange some sales in Central America.

### b. Petroleums:

CONZALEZ brought news that the Natarazzo industrial combine in Brazil had bought sufficient petroleum (from outside the Soviet orbit) to cover its needs for four months, but would need more when the above time expired. NATARAZZO asked for an estimate on about 15-20,000 tons of Soviet petroleum delivered in about 100,000 darts per boat.

### c. Cellulose:

GONZALEZ informed BOLSHEKOV that a US firm named PASSOIS & WEALTHY visited to know why GONZALEZ was selling cellulose to Brazil, and further wanted to know GONZALEZ' source of supply.
