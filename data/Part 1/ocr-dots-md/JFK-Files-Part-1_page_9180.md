17 May 1945

## PRIED SHUB

Lou Scherer (LE) phoned to say that he had to see Lavitas, editor of New Leader, in connection with a new project, and with several other matters. Could he please to see him for about an hour. CUP had suggested I ask Boris Shub to arrange the appointment.

I phoned Boris Shub, and phoned Scherer back that Shub would meet him at 18.50 a.m. tomorrow in the bookstore below (?) the IL office at 7 E. 15 St. Shub will introduce Scherer to Lavitas at 11.

Re PHOTO BOOK

Boris said there are 3 matters which "we can live without," but it
would distinctly strengthen the book if you could perhaps get them at the
Library of Congress."

(1) It is conceivable that there exist photos of crowds being fired upon in Nov.—Jan. 1917-1918, in connection with rioting on the dissolution of the Constituent Assembly. (See Shub's _Lemin_).

(2) Fighting in Moscow, Nov. 1917 (There was no fighting in Petersburg).

(3) Collectivization. Some think it is conceivable there exist photos of peasants being snipped north to Siberia during Collectivization.
Cf. CIA Graphics?
Cf. Library of Congress.

Re RUSSIANS AS ALLIES

Boris has just heard from a Russian friend, and has sent to London for confirmation —seen nothing in the papers about it here— that there has been formed in London a BRITISH SOCIETY FOR A FREE RUSSIA, with Major Wilton (author and ex-military attaché in Moscow) as president and the Duchess of Atholl as vice-president. Encouraging, he thought.

He also mentioned the FREEDOM UNION FOR GERMAN-PUSSIAN FRIENDSHIP reported in the Lay 11 NY Herald-Trib, with Layor Reutor and some Russians among the members.

Re PHOTO BOOK

LAY-CUT of the book received from Lamson in SP/MI today.
