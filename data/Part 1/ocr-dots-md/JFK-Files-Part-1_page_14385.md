Report No.350 (continued)

24 June 1970.

It seems that they would like to bring a professor from Havana, so that the children could study the same revolutionary methods as there, but Ruiz sees this as a very difficult thing to do. Because, one would not solve the situation of the different grades and ages, and they are not going to send four or five because they are needed there. He sees it as a better possibility to hire Spanish professors among the young capable people, with revolutionary leanings and who are sympathetic toward Cubans, and have them teach the methods, and from books that are brought from there. In this way they could have many professors or teachers for the various ages, and they could rent and furnish an apartment for this purpose.

Apparently Ruiz has no problems with the school his children attend. The Director of the school is a friend of theirs and visits the embassy. Her husband is a communist. The children that attend the school have the same political leanings as their parents. Neither religion nor politics are taught. Ruiz says that the two older ones eat at the school, but the little ones don't as they need help. Ruiz pays over $100 for L. The person who is with him has two children and pays $100 for the two.

The children of this new comrade that is talking to Ruiz arrive day after tomorrow. He has a little girl 11 years old and the other one is nine. Ruiz is advising the comrade to send his children to the same school where his children go. The name of the school is "ESPINO" or "EL PINO", in the Calle Serrano. He says he will be glad to speak to the Director. They have sports, and music, and the children can have lunch at the school.

Report No.351

25 June 1970.

He is talking with Mercedes, and they enter the office and continue the conversation. They are speaking in confidential voices, because Mercedes normally talks very loud, they are now talking in very low voices. Mercedes is trying to console him, saying that his work is very difficult and little understood. Ruiz says that they are "throwing him the bait." He is very depressed.

Tomorrow he has an appointment with Quiroga publishing company, who have had a meeting with DAVID PAX(phon) about the rights of the son: "Guantanamera," which was acknowledged in London by an American firm, and its author as being the Cuban Jose FERNANDEZ Diaz, at a reunion that was held there.
