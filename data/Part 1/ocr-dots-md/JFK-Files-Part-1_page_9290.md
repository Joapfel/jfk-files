8 Sept. 1950

ROBERT M. HEADLEY

is a contract agent; the effective date of
his contract with CIA is 1st Dec. 1949, for two years.

Fred Richards (CFD) says the agreement with him re royalties, etc., should be signed by Mr. Eccles, as contracting officer.

General Counsel's office informed of the
above today, on their inquiry.
