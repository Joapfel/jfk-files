Convert to MarkdownText of instructions issued to all field units December 1945:

> "Regardless of his country of origin, any Iron Curtain escapee who,
> during the period 1945 to the present time, was voluntarily or forcibly
> repatriated, but who proceeded no further than the Eastern Zones of
> Germany or Austria, and was detained in the Eastern Zones of Germany
> or Austria under the discipline of the Soviet occupation forces or was
> employed in an official, semi-official, or any other capacity, regard-
less of the nature of his employment, by or for the Soviet occupation
forces, and who subsequently defected, fled, escaped, or otherwise
returned to the West, for any reason, should be considered eligible
for USEP assistance, provided he is otherwise eligible."

Your attention is again directed to Section 7 of Guide §1 which authorises you to make exceptions in any individual case, regardless of eligibility criteria.
