## COURSE OBJECTIVES - CONTENT AND METHODS

This course aims to develop in the Clandestine Services officers and others recently returned from the field an on-to-date awareness of the role of CIA in the United States intelligence community, the relations of the DDI and DDR areas with the CS, and the support available from the offices of the DDS.

The course is presented by lectures given by CIA officials who are directly responsible for the missions, functions, programs, and services discussed. Each lecture is usually followed by a short question period. Approximately 10 per cent of the students' time is scheduled for reading pertinent on-to-date regulations and background summaries.
