# CZEO LOVAK AFFAIRS

Miss Augustine reported that the Council of Free Czechoslovakia and the National Committee of Free Czechoslovakia, composed of 17 and 13 persons respectively, had signed an agreement in New York City on January 15, 1952, which bear the signature of Modar. Modarich, Mileok, Letzarch, and Tenkl.

(Copy of this document is herewith attached).

Miss Augustine advised that Mr. Mileok had represented Mr. Paprock in signing the agreement. The agreement, Miss Augustine pointed out, sets up a provisional management of Czechoslovak affairs consisting of two administrators — one from each group. Mr. Modarich represents the 17 of the Council of Free Czechoslovakia, and Mr. Paprock will represent the 13 of the National Committee of Free Czechoslovakia. The provisional management also consists of a body of 26 persons — 12 from each side and which, Ms. Augustine reported, might be called a "chairholder body".

Ms. Augustine also presented another document which is a protest of the Council's 17 against the Committee's 13, insisting on the exclusion of Dr. Gousky from any major function.
