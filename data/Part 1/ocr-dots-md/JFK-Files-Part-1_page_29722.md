29 APR 1971

MEMORANDUM FOR: Registrar, Office of Medical Services

SUBJECT: Medical Report for Myroslaw A. Maksymiec

1. Mr. Myroslaw A. Maksymiec is applying for disability retirement.

2. In accordance with FPM Supplement 931-1, § 10-6, it is requested that a medical report be submitted to this office to be forwarded with other documents to the Bureau of Retirement, Insurance and Occupational Health, Civil Service Commission.

3. Mr. Maksymiec plans to remain on duty through 28 May 1971 pending a decision on his application for retirement.

4. Copies of the following documents are attached: Application for Retirement (Form 581), Statement, (2001-B).
