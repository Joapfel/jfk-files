According to A. M. Koltin, the sequence of events leading up to NKORO's certification with the Soviets was as follows:

- On the morning of 10 February by the Soviet Brusilovgrad Office, a note from the U.S. White House Department requesting an interview with NKORO, but no reply was given.

- In the early morning of 17 February, NKORO arrived in the United States.

- On 13 February, the American agreement to meet the Soviets was obtained, and initial arrangements as to the timing and place of the consultation were discussed with him and within the U.S. government.

- Final arrangements were agreed upon during the morning of 1. February; the Americans were then notified, and the consultations took place late in the afternoon of the same day.
