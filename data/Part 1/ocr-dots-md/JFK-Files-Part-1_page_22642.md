NOTE FOR THE RECORD:

NOEMAYR is a Career Agent in Mexico City on Project LIEDE. According to Charlotte Zehrung he is to depart Mexico City for Hqs upon the arrival of HEGARTY. HEGARTY is scheduled for a medical release about 23 Feb 60 and should arrive in Mexico about 20 March. After a few weeks overlap, NOEMAYR should depart for Hqs about 1 April 60, ready to go to work.

According to Charlotte he prefers to take no home leave.

Apparently Mr. Gomez has been working with the Cover Training people in an attempt to place him in that type of activity in a cover status. Per Col. King on 10 Feb Cover Training indicated that they had no interest in subject and Col. King requested the above information for use in his contact with Col. Baird in a further attempt to place NOEMAYR with OTR.
