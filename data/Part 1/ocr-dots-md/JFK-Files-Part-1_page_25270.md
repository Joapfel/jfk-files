## V. GOOD AND SERVICES:

A. On 3 October the wife of Leonard I. Pascoe, General Counselor, visited Dr. Walter Schachman, a dentist with offices at J. Moran 12, to have her teeth cleaned.

B. During October members of the Soviet Embassy were in contact with Mrs. Mercedes Perez of Tokyo 3, a seaplane.

C. On 22 October Albert Allarazet, attache, ordered a tank of oxygen from 11-31-74, United Mexico de Oxigeno. They promised to send him the note for renting the other tanks. (In the 1956 Mexico City telephone directory this number is listed under the name of Dr. Guillermo Olavoz, Plaza Calavalle 3-4, and also under Oxigeno Para Nuevos at the same address.)

D. Also on 22 October ALLARAZET contacted Justo Del Rodriguez, Extinguishers, Noctezuma 246, and asked if they sent mechanics to check and recharge the fifteen or sixteen extinguishers at the Embassy.

E. Aleksey D. KALYANBERG, Assistant Commercial Officer, contacted Dr. Jorge SAVIER Millan, well-known doctor with offices at artículo 123 1/16, on 25 October concerning some x-ray treatments for his heart.
