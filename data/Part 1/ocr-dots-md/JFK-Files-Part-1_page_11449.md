1. ON 9 MAR A MADAME BOYER AND UNIDEN MAN CALLED on DAILY PARROT, CURANAGAR CULTURAL COUNSELOR. BOYER TOLD APARICIO SHE RECEIVELY DATE FROM CURA, PLANNING 4 MONTH TRIP THRU ALL COUNTRIES LATIN AMERICA TO COMPILE INFO FOR BOOK SHE PLANNING PUBLISH. WANTED TO READ BOOKS ABOUT EACH COUNTRY BEFORE GOING AND ASKED APARICIO HELP OBTAINING THE. PLANNED RETURN CURA SOON WITH BOOKS. APARICIO NOT INFORMED THAT BOYER COVING NEXI BUT OFFERED ASSISTANCE INTRODUCED HER TO LITANIL-3 AND AMBASSADOR'S WIFE.

2. BOYER SAW SHE BORN UNITED STATES, MARRIED FRENCHMAN, NOW 11, BROUGHT BUT HAD FRENCH NATIONALITY. FAIDEN NAME HUNGARIAN "KISAN" (PHONETIC). MOTHER RUSSIAN, GRANDPARENTS CAME TO U.S. BEFORE WAR (SIC). BOYER LIVED PARIS AND 7 YEARS ITALY. SPEAKS SPANISH WITH ACCENT AND SOME DIFFICULTY. (SOURCE: DELAYED TRANSCRIPT).
