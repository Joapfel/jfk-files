RESULTS

Subject has never taken part in any communist activity and he has never worked for any Castro intelligence or security service. Apart from PDBIME he has no connection or agreement with the government of any other country.

Unless otherwise noted below Subject is not obligated to any Cuban exile group.

Subject has been essentially truthful in regard to his personal history and he does not feel he is liable to black-mail for any reason. Subject has never been involved in the use or sale of narcotics.

DETAILS

Subject is being recruited for use with WITHUSH. He has served there before. Like all of the men in the group who have been in the Iden B, Subject assumes that the operation is sponsored by KUBARK.

Subject joined AMWCOLD but was never called to go to the training campa.

In connection with blackmail, Subject mentioned that his father is in jail in Cuba. To date no attempts have been made by the Castro government to put any pressure on Subject.

Keith D. WINETT
