4 March 1954

MEMORANDUM FOR: Personnel Officer, FI

SUBJECT : Earl J. WILLIAMSON
Recommendation for Promotion

1. Mr. Williamson has been with the WH Division since June 1952 and at the Havana station since 17 January 1953. With approximately eight months' headquarter training he has worked into a position demanding extremely sensitive liaison work. His efforts have been highly successful and are marked by an excellent group of the problems involved. Mr. Williamson also acts as Chief of Station during any absence of the Chief. The variety of problems he encounters are met with considerable ability.

2. The promotion of Mr. Williamson to the next higher grade is recommended, in view of the excellent performance of his present responsibilities. He has demonstrated his capacity to perform fully and effectively in a field assignment.

J. C. KING
Chief, WH
