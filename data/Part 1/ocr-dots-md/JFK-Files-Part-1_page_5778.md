On 30 June 1971, DELASCIO returned STCIOY/1 with the 'Biomedical News'. At the same time, DELASCIO gave him the business address of Dr. S.C. SUNG, and convinced STCIOY/1 that he could try to write to Dr. SUNG, stating that STCIOY/1's office address will soon move to a new address and that STCIOY/1 welcomes SUNG for a visit because SUNG will surely enjoy the new tourist attractions in Los Angeles such as 'The Magic Mountain' and 'The Lion Safari'. STCIOY/1 still doubted that SUNG would spend so much money to make such a trip, but he promised to write this letter inviting him.
