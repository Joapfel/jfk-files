Convert to Markdown### III. Miscellaneous

1. The following is given as evidence of Soviet activity among Georgians in France:

a. The following have received telephone calls from Tiflis:

Leo SARDJVELADZE, from his daughter two phone calls on family matters. The daughter married a Ww II escapee who came out in 1943 or 1944 and went back in 1946 or 1947.

Shota KALAFOZI, from his mother. He also received several letters in which she said that everything is fine in Georgia but that Shota is old enough to make up his own mind about repatriation.

Shota TAKTAKICHVILI, from his mother who said she may visit him in Paris.

b. The following have received packages from Tiflis containing food and home made candy:

*   **Shota TAKTAKICHVILI**
    *   Mrs. Ketho ICHENKELI (CHENKELI)
    *   Georgi CHANCHIACHVILI (SHANSHASHVILI)

c. Nearly everyone received the Georgian language paper of the Mikhailov Committee in Berlin.

2. CABACHVILI, PATARIDZE and ZURABICHVILI all expressed interest in American support for their group. They stated that the support given to their group as a whole by Polish intelligence before WW II largely contributed toward the readiness of large numbers of Caucasians to fight against the USSR during the war. PATARIDZE said he would like to be sent to Turkey to organize contacts with Georgia. Says he can get necessary papers for trip to Turkey, and has man in mind for such mission (apparently DJAPADZE). PATARIDZE says this operation would cost no more than one million French francs.
