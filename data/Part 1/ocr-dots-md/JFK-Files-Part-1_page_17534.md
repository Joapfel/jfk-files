## G. Operational Leads and Support Provided Other Station Operations

During the past project year, LIFCAT continued to support Mexico City Station through the timely production of useful leads, notably in the field of Cuban operations, and provision of continuing support to other Station operations. The following is a selective list of the use made of leads provided by LIFCAT:

**LIFCAT/Cuban:** In this phase of the LIFCAT operation the most important operational function has been the use of LIFCAT leads in checking the veracity and activities of the KUBARK agents in the Cuban Embassy in Mexico. LIFCAT live-monitoring was employed in the case of Guillermo HERNANDEZ Vega to determine the success of this suspected Cuba penetration of the AMFON program in contacting Cuban officials or pro-Cuban Mexican officials while he was held in custody by Mexican authorities and Station assets. In another case LIFCAT provided leads which enabled Station assets to monitor a clandestine meeting between a member of the Cuban Embassy staff and one Manuel ROJAS Villa (aka James Bradford STEWART), a fugitive from ODYONE and a potential CASTRO agent, and ultimately return ROJAS to ODYONE jurisdiction. LIFCAT was also able to determine the whereabouts of another suspected CASTRO agent, Oscar RODRIGUEZ Moruna. LIFCAT leads and support provided increased security for Station assets involved during the defection of Jose PARRO Llada, CASTRO "propaganda" minister, and provided leads for KUCAGE play on the reaction to the defection within the Cuban Embassy in Mexico.

**LIFCAT/Polish:** LIFCAT provided vital support in the RECOW recruitment attempt which increased the security of the operation and gave a quick and secure means of evaluating the chances of success of the operation. In this effort, LIFCAT support included long periods of live-monitoring three separate telephone lines.
