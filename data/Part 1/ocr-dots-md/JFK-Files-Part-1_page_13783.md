RODRIGUEZ Lapez, Rogalio: (DIP PP-D-63/454)

RODRIGUEZ Lapez, Rogelio: Left Mexico on 15 May 61,
diplomatic visa. (Source: passenger list from "friends.")

Left Mexico on 7 May 62, diplomatic visa. (Source: passenger list from "friends.")

In Dec 62 he appears as Third Secretary in the Cuban Embassy
in Mexico City. Promoted to Second Secretary and stayed in the Embassy
in Mexico City. Was accredited to Mexico as Third Secretary. Was called
to Havana and on 5 Jul 63 he returned to Mexico as Second Secretary.
Dangerous. Reserved and competent young man. Works with interest.
Hard core Communist. (Source:

30 Jul 63.)
