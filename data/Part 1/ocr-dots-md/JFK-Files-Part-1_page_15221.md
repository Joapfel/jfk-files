6 October 1959

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Lockpicking Instruction for AELASPIC

Case Officer, Fred Kirkpatrick picked up George F. Muns, TSS/ASD and drove him to safehouse for purpose of instruction of basic fundamentals of lockpicking to AELASPIC. Instruction was from 1100 hours to 1230 hours. After lunch, Muns was driven back to town. Muns was introduced to a .ent as "George".

F. Kirkpatrick
