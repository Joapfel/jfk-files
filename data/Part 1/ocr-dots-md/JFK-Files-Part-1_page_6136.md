Dear Sir:

This is a request under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. 552, et seq., on behalf of A Quaker Action Group (QAG). We, the undersigned, were the authorized officers at the time of the dissolution of the organization.

We request that you provide us with any and all records, documents, memoranda, letters, or any other type of kind of information concerning our group that is currently on file, in possession of, or maintained by your agency.

The full name of the organization is A Quaker Action Group. It was also known by its initials, QAG. The organization was established in 1966 and existed until 1971. The main office was maintained at 20 South 12th Street in Philadelphia, PA.

If for any reason you determine not to forward to us any of the information that we have hereby requested, further request that you identify the nature of the information withheld and the reasons for your actions.

As you know, the amended Act permits you to reduce or waive the fees if that "is in the public interest because furnishing the information can be considered as primarily benefiting the public." We believe that this request plainly fits that category and ask you to waive any fees.

As provided for in the amended Act, we will expect to receive a reply within 10 days.
