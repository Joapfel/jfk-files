# PROJECT LIFETAT

## Background

Project LIFAT was initially approved on October 19, 1957. Actual operations began on 10 January 1951 and have run almost four Imbally since that time. The LIFAT operation was begun with the installation of one telephone line and has since expanded to an average of 23 telephone lines.

Project LIFAT is a multilateral operation. It is completely independent from any liaison arrangement; and it is staffed by LIBRT personnel, both staff and contract, together with a substantial number of local indigenous controlled agents.

During the time of its operation Project LIFAT has produced more than 50,000 pages of operational and intelligence information in direct support of the Mexico City station's objectives specifically and has contributed materially to the intelligence gathering activities connected with theino-Soviet bloc. In addition, Project LIFAT has been of considerable value and assistance in monitoring the activities of Americans residing in Mexico City in which OOVNY has an interest and also has, at the direction of Headquarters, installed specialaps on targets of opportunity.
