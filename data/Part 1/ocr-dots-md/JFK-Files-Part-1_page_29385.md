### A. COURSE OBJECTIVES

1. This course is designed primarily for either a civil officer who expects to switch and manage an audio surveillance operation, or for those who have related responsibilities, i.e., civil officer and physical security officers.

2. Although the course provides a basic identification with audio devices the primary emphasis is on the collection of target data, planning the operation, locating a listening post, supporting the entry, exploiting "the take", and the orderly termination of the operation once it has outlived its usefulness. In short, managing an audio operation from inception to termination.

3. The course provides a basic knowledge of "carry plants" devices to permit the exploitation of certain targets of opportunity. The same devices could be used and used for "carry in" devices.

4. Finally, the course provides instruction in the first selection maintenance of listening post equipment, so that an operation can continue without theitatent presence of an audio technician.
