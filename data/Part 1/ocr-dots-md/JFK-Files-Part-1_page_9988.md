9 August, 1947

Subject: LINDSAY'S Report of Contact with Ernesto Capoano.

1. Route: net with LINDSAY'S at 1000 at the Tascouteve apartment and re-
trained with him until about 1000. LINDSAY'S reported that he had tele-
phoned Ernesto Capoano at his office on 4/8/67, after having obtained
the phone number from César Tejeda Fornedoa, the husband of the daughter
of Ortizs Rubio ( ex-President). L/S stated that he did not identify him-
self to Capoano, but stated simply that he wanted to talk with him re-
garding a sensitive matter. According to L/S Capoano said quickly: "Don't
mention any names, but give me some idea of what it's all about". L/S
continued that he replied simply that it concerned "the medicine". At
the mention of this, Capoano requested that the caller telephone him again
at 1000 or 7/6/67 for an appointment, explaining that he had to leave town
over the week-end. L/S reported Capoano's number as 18-62-09.

2. LINDSAY'S continued to Rowton that when he called again on 7/8/67 as requested, still not identifying himself, Capoano asked to meet him in front of Cooperativa at 1200. Each gave a general description of the other for recognition. When, however, L/S appeared in front of Cooperativa at the time appointed, he saw no sign of anyone answering the description of Capoano (about 45 years old, wearing dark glasses, with green suit), nor was he approached by anyone. After waiting about 30 minutes in the vicinity L/S told Rowton that he left. L/S then stated that he planned to drop in to Capoano's home that same evening since obviously the phone call had not smoked him out. He gave the address of Capoano as Calle Bucareli 128, apt. C-7, which he stated he received from César Tejeda.

3. When Rowton met with L/S again at 1000 on 8 August, the latter reported that finally he had managed to contact Capoano at his home (and office). He reported that at about 1930 he rang the bell and was shown into a spacious and nice elegant apartment and asked to take a seat in what served as a waiting room. After about twenty minutes Capoano appeared, introduced himself, and led L/S into his office.

and LINDSAY'S, as recalled by Rowton from L/S's report:
