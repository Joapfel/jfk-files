AMORLOW MEETING IN NEW ORLEANS, 24 June 1964
SUBJECT: Adelaide PADRON y Cruz - Decision to move her to Nicaragua

1. Creating liberally upon data supplied in OPGA 16512, we reminded AMBIDDY-1 about our misgivings regarding his association with Adelaide PADRON y Cruz. He conceded awareness of some of the rumors circulating about her past private life and stated that he had satisfied himself that they were unfounded. We injected that we knew that Jose Mole Rosales has been asked to do some investigating and had given Adelaide Padron a clean bill of health. We also happened to know that KLE was aware of the true story and for reasons of his own had decided to withhold it from AMBIDDY-1. We inquired what we know about Jerona MILK and he commented that she had been a "bad woman". We observed that the "had been" was open to question.

3. AMBIDY-1 assured that he had listened to a telephone conversation between Adelaide and her former husband, Enger Jules Lavennale, in which the letter had admitted that the story about his having acted under higher orders when carrying Adelaide were unfounded. AMBIDY-1 also charged the ex-husband of having tried to justice his wife to participate in ergo, but that Adelaide had refused to do so.

J. As regards Adelaide's alleged illicit relationship with ex-President BATISTA, AMBIDDY-1 simply declared the charge to be unfounded. Adelaide did know Carlos CANTILLO Gomez who was an old friend of the family. She also had met ex-President JIMMIS, but nothing of a compromising nature had existed.

4. When asked whether it was true that Adelaide had introduced him to several prominent Venustians, he denied this and stated that introductions had been made by Silvia GARCIA, a trusted childhood friend of AMBIDY-1.
