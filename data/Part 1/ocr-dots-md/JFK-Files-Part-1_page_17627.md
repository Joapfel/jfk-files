PROJECT REVIEW - LIFEAT

# 1. Product of the Project

## The Soviet embassy -

### a. Soviet Commercial Activities:

During the past year reports submitted from screening the traffic of the two established Soviet Commercial Offices in Mexico have provided a great amount of detail concerning Soviet efforts to sell in the Latin America can market, both heavy industrial merchandise and movies and literature. Knowledge of their methods of transshipping and attempts at concealment of point of origin of products has been gleaned from this take. As a result of this knowledge, reports on the firms with whom they are doing business have been procured and provided for background information. Following are a list of the reports submitted as a result of intelligence procured from this source:

* uMM-2053 - Other Commercial Activities in Mexico
* HMM-3165 - Soviet Commercial Activities in Mexico
* HMM-3286 - Soviet Commercial Activities in Mexico, December 1957
* HMM-3253 - Soviet Commercial Contracts in Mexico - Mihanacene del Oecidento, S. de R.L.
* HMM-3264 - Soviet Commercial Contracts in Mexico - Servicio Agricola, S.A.
* HMM-3139 - Anilinas y Productos Químicos S. de R.L.
* HMM-3404 - Imports into Mexico from Soviet Union
* HMM-A-9519 - American Cyanid Co., Calco Chemical Division

### b. Soviet Diplomatic Activities:

This project has provided coverage and details of some of the diplomatic activities of the Soviets in Mexico. It has served to show the relationships maintained, culturally, socially and diplomatically, between the Soviets and the members of the Satellite establishments in Mexico.

* HMM-3215, Soviet Attendance at Yugoslav Reception
* HMM-3351 - Reception at Soviet Embassy.
* HMM-2992 - Diplomatic Activities of Soviet Embassy, Mexico City
* HMM-2849 - Diplomatic Activities of Soviet Embassy, Mexico City

### c. Soviet Cultural Activities:

Information produced by LIFEAT has given details of Soviet activities in connection with publications, cultural institutes, and related activities. Examples of such reports are:

* HMM-2870 - Soviet Embassy Reception for Mexican Musicians
* HMM-2847 - Cultural & Propaganda Activities of Soviet Embassy
* HMM-3080, Cultural & Propaganda Activities of Soviet Embassy
