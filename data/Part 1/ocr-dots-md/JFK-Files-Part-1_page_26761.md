18.7 Out to 24-07-15. VKG with MO, who answers, for Jose Luis Gonzalez. Not there yet. Is still waiting for that call? Yes. (-O is -ejia kicart). VKG will go there and take his recorder.

After 1915, 7 may

19.0 Out to NAXAAXIXEX 15-17-20. VKG (v) orders a cab. Wrong number.

19.2 Out to 15-50-20, twice. Busy. Out to 15-91-27. VKG (v) asks WO, who answers, for Sr. Elizondo. Not there now.

19.7 In. Antonio Gonzalez de Leon with VKG, who answers. Greetings...VKG is sick - This Ministry does not react. Did VKG expect it to react? VKG expected that its opinion would be at least Mexican (with respect to Dominican Republic). MO has to talk to VKG.... -O is almost inaudible...MO wants to ask about Gonzalez Martinez Quvales (??). VKG saw this, but was not impressed. Has VKG seen Gonzalez? And Of course. Recently? Yes. Where can MO locate him? Phone him at 24-88-84. Yes, but the phone does not answer. Yes, they do, that is his house. When will they see one another? When MO likes. Can have coffee together tomorrow? Call VKG tomorrow around 1000 at home (to arrange this). Fine.

20.7 In. MO with Inge kico, who answers. Greetings...Ask how -O is... -O went to see dentist...VKG is very drunk and is still drinking. He is out too now. VKG had lunch with Airic, came home for an appt., and told me he had to go out again and must be still in orbit.

21.0 In. No answer.

After 2250, 7 May
