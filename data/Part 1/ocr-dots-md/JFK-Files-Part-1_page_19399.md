William G. Tharp, Chief of Personnel
VIA James b. Opsata, Chief, Personnel Division
John L. Cronkrite, Acting Chief,
Classification and Personnel Utilisation Section
James W. Moore

In accordance with Personnel Branch Order No.1 dated
3 May 1946, we are forwarding for review the personnel action
for the appointment of James W. Moore as Intelligence Officer,
CAF-12, $5905.20, with PSRO-FRO at Shanghai, China.

The duties and responsibilities of the position to be
occupied by Mr. Moore were discussed with Mr. Vaughn and Mr.
Duggan and it was found that he is to serve as a Senior
Intelligence Officer with responsibility for directing all
intelligence activity within a specified area.

It is the recommendation of the Classification Section
that the position be approved at CAF-12.
