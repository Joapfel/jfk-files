5. Kolando Masferrer again mentioned that his ultimate objective was an attack on Curaçao using Haiti as his base.

Field Comments

1. Kolando Masferrer has been discussing plans for an invasion of Haiti for many months. TLCSDA-315/00658-66 reported the difficulties he was encountering. See also, UFG-0326- CC BL-712/01431-66.

2. Among earlier claims by Masferrer was one that he had already purchased the two aircraft. See TLCSDA-315/ 00289-66 and UFG-0326, FC BL-712/01431-66.

3. The Haitian referred to is probably Henri Vixamar, described earlier as a former Haitian and an official of the Republic of Chad. Vixamar left Kingston for Toronto on 12 April by air. There was a previous report on a telephone conversation between Kolando Masferrer, then in Miami, and "the Haitian in Canada" regarding "Operation Haiti." UFG-0215.
