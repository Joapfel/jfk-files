1. Harold R. NOEMAYR, a Career Agent, is incurring medical expenses from the required hospitalization and medical/surgical treatment of one of his minor dependents. He wishes to know if he is covered by insurance that includes the above type of expenses for his dependents.

2. It is noted that reference regulation mentions in general terms that Career Agents are usually entitled to the same benefits as Staff Employees. However, since the Station does not have a copy of NOEMAYR's contract, it is requested that Headquarters check his personnel file to determine if he and his dependents are all covered by some manner of hospitalization plan and, if so, what the exact terms are that this plan covers.
