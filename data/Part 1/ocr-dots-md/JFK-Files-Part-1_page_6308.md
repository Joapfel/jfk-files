MINUTES

In the City of Havana, on 30 June 1962, the members of the NATIONAL EXECUTIVE OF THE "UNTERRAIN DE RECUPERACION REVOLUCIONARIA" (N.R.R.), Rigoberto CASAS, Civilian National Coordinator, and Jose Antonio AGARANTE, Military National Coordinator, had a meeting and adopted the following agreements:

**NOTE:** That in view of the present situation of our movement and the general situation of the country, we have decided to change the organization of the movement, which will now be composed as follows: (A) One Civilian National Coordinator, and (B) One Military National Coordinator. All previous Secretaries and denominations are annulled.

**NOTE:** To give full autonomy, with regard to functioning, to the Civilian and Military Coordinations. Thus, each will function separately.

**NOTE:** To give full support and recognition as GENERAL DELIBATE in exile of our movement, to Dr. Jose Ignacio LAZAGA and to the executive appointed by him with the authorization of the previous National Coordination.

**NOTE:** As result of the SECOND agreement regarding autonomy in the functioning of the National Coordinations, we agree to accept the Hierarchy in the command of the full Executive of our Movement in exile, with the provision that the orders originating from this Executive will be complied with in accordance with our capabilities and taking into consideration that as we are in the National Territory we have better knowledge of the real situation which we are undergoing.

Signed by Rigoberto CASAS and Jose Antonio AGARANTE exclusively, as the rest of the members of the previous NATIONAL COORDINATION are in prison with the exception of Mr. DELIO, ex Coordinator of Civic Resistance who is in asylum.

Rigoberto CASAS
CIVILIAN NATIONAL COORDINATOR

Jose Antonio AGARANTE
MILITARY NATIONAL COORDINATOR
