ISAACS, Harold Robert

Born 13 Sept 1910,NYC
Nat'l American

DESCRIPTION

Height 5'9"
Build medium
Hair black,wavy,brushed back
Eyes brown

DOCUMENTATION

In 30s was traveling on passport # 177026

Arrived in China from Manila on 10 Dec 1930
Upon arrival joined the editorial staff of the Shanghai Evening Post and Mercury.

Was a secretary of the HOULENS Defense Committee and a member of the Society of Friends of the UCRB

On 12 June 1931, ISAACS and his friend GLASS took a trip up the Yangtze. During this trip ISAACS wrote his fiancée, Viola ROBINSON, that he had decided to stay "in China" as a result of conversations with GLASS.

The two returned to Shanghai in Aug 1931 and took up residence at # 128 Weihaiwei Road.

ISAACS later started and became the editor and publisher of the "China Forum" which at first was job-printed but from Oct 1932 on was printed by the Level Publishing Co., INC., # 33, Lane 478 Tiandong Road, Shanghai. ISAACS openly stated he was manager of the printing co. and owned 40% of its shares.

On 7 Aug 1933, ISAACS and his wife Viola ROBINSON when he had married in Sept 1932 left Shanghai for Japan on the SS Nagasaki Maru. On 16 Aug, they proceeded to Japan, returning to Shanghai on Aug 26th.

In addition to his other activities, ISAACS was also employed with the Havantime Agency, he covered connection with this agency around Dec 1933.

In Dec 1933, ISAACS was in Poochow to gather first hand info re the newly formed "People's Revolutionary Government of the Republic of China."

In the spring of 1934, ISAACS and his wife left for Tianjin en route to Peking at which city he resided at # 2 Tung Fu Chia Tao, East City. Peking mailing address was $ Mr. HUIMIANG, Grand Hotel..
