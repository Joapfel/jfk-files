STEIN announced the call on the number indicated. HALFRIE talked in Spanish, announcing himself as Professor Homarise HALFRIE from Mexico. He explained that he was a friend of LOUIS ARNOLD, with whom they (the partners on the phone) had accidentally met last year at the shop of Mr. PORTA ARENARDS. At present he (HALFRIE) was staying in LUGANO and waiting for the visa to go to PRAGUE, there he would like to find his friends, the STEIN family, when his partner should also know. He then wanted to know when LOUIS would arrive in PRAGUE from MEXICO?

On 29 October the HALFRIE's left LUGANO at 0904 H. by train for ZURICH. During their sojourn at LUGANO no contact of the couple with strangers were made; they want for walks at random in town or in the vicinity, bought papers and periodicals by the dozen, but did not receive any mail or telephone calls.

At 1253 H. of 29 October they arrived in ZURICH, went to the Hotel "Schwöizerhof" and later to the Czech Consulate General. At 1550 H. subject inquired at the KLM airline agency about refunding for the tickets to Tel Aviv, but the agency had not received directions from Amsterdam yet.

On 30 October the couple again went to the Czech Consulate General at 1035 H. and remained there for 75 minutes. At 1215 H. HALFRIE inquired at the Dutch Travel Agency about tourist visas to Pragoe and was given the necessary forms to fill in. These he returned to the agency at 1410 H. together with the passports.
