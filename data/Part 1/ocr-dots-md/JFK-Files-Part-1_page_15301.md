In 1944 Achilles married Nathela JORDANIA, a daughter of
the former President of the Republic of Georgia (1917-1921).

They are divorced now. Of their three daughters two, both born in Paris, are living with the father: Maria-Helene (14) and Irene (12). Catherine (6), born in Gap, is living with the mother, who, since 1959, is remarried. Her second husband is a physician, Dr. Louis ELET. The last name of this physician was formerly BORREAU. He changed it because of the bad meaning: bourreau = the hangman.

7. Cousin Mathé KERESSELIDZE, who had joined the Germans during WW II and was parachuted to USSR. He was captured there, sentenced to 15 years of hard labor (1943-58) and was then allowed to repatriate to France, where he arrived some six months to one year ago. Now he is in Stuttgart, Germany.
Before returning to France he has occasionally met in Moscow Helene who had told him of herself and of her father (see items 3 & 5).
