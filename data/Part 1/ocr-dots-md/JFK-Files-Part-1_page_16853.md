1. Upon reviewing Station's Obligation Status Reports from Headquarters, it has been noted that there apparently have been no credits entered for KDABBEY's net rent refunds on MEXI-03

2. MEXI-03 is owned by JKLAHCH, with KDABBEY as the ostensible owner. Each month the station gives the occupant of Units #11, 13, 15 and 17 of MEXI-03 their rent for the month in advance, which they then pay to KDABBEY's agent. Originally this agent was KDVAULT/2, who was sitting of the rental arrangement and neither received nor paid rent on Unit #11, which he occupied. Since his termination in April 71, Unit #11 has been occupied by KDVAULT/9, who is not sitting of the arrangement. The current occupancy and rent structure for MEXI-03 is:

| UNIT # | OCCUPANT | MONTHLY RENTAL | PROJECT CHARGED |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | KDVAULT/9 | 1,700.00MN | KOVAULT |
| 13 | KDVAULT/3 | 1,650.00MN | KOVAULT |
| 15 | KDFACTOR/2 | 1,430.00MN | KDFACTOR |
| 17 | KDVAULT/5 | 1,540.00MN | KOVAULT |

3. At the end of each quarter, KDABBEY submits to the Station an accounting recording the rents received on the four units of MEXI-03, and the expenses incurred for property taxes, rent receipt stamp taxes, municipal water charges, maintenance and repairs, and similar items during the quarter. The difference between the rents paid to and the expenses incurred by KDABBEY as ostensible owner of MEXI-03, is refunded to the Station by KDABBEY with the quarterly accounting.
