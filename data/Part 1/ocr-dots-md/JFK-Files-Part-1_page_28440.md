19 JUN 1957

MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Central Intelligence

THROUGH : Deputy Director for Plans

SUBJECT : Appointment of Mr. Tennent H. Bagley as Chief of Station, Brussels, Belgium

1. The appointment of Mr. Tennent H. Bagley as Chief of Station, Brussels, Belgium, effective on or about 15 June 1967, is recommended. Mr. Bagley would replace Mr. William D. O'Ryan.

2. Mr. Bagley has been an employee of the Agency since July 1950, and is presently assigned as an Operations Officer, Deputy Chief, Soviet Bloc Division, GS-16. A biographic profile, including information regarding his Agency experience and training, is attached.

Rolfe Kingsley
Chief
European Division
