This letter will serve as an official invitation and credential
as a delegate to the Congress for Cultural Freedom. The passport and
visa authorities know about the Congress and will extend all facilities
to delegates. When you have secured your passport, you can obtain your
Berlin visa from the Allied High Command Permit Office, 42 Broadway, New
York City. The American Express Company, through its foreign travel
section, will arrange for air-travel bookings. We will inform you when
your ticket is available and all further details of transportation.

We are enclosing a copy of our first New York release. It con-
tains a copy of the proposed program of the Congress and the names of
many of our international delegates and supporters. The details of the
program are now being worked out in Berlin. As soon as we receive them,
we shall be happy to send you a copy.

Copies of your books are being ordered and will be sent to Berlin where they will be on public display. We would appreciate your sending us a negative of your photograph for use in connection with this display.

If there is any other information you require, or any way we can
be of help to you, please feel free to call upon us at our New York office.
