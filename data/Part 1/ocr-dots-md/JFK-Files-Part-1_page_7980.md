On 26 August 1957, a source within SH, considered usually reliable, informed quarried concerning the reports from official sources of Orford–Marconi Committee collaboration and specifically concerning the past with the Communists involving Subject and others mentioned in paragraph 7A above, said that these reports were false (phantasia) and had been fabricated to designate the same active and hostile opponents of the BATISTA regime. Subject denied that the Orford–Marconi committee was connected with the Communists. He claimed that he has never met Charles Rafael RODRIGUEZ, for that matter, any of the top Communist leaders except President PÉRA, who was once pointed out to him in Maracaibo City.

3. A. An unverbalated report dated 16 July 1951 from a source whose reliability was described as fairly stated that Man RODRÍGUEZ is a Communist sympathizer and a very good friend of Liental FIO, editor of Varejóndia Cubana and Communist leader in the University of Habana, The report concerned RODRÍGUEZ's candidacy for the office of Secretary General of the United Orford–Marconi.

a. Subject, when asked if he knew Liential FIO, said that he knew him but that he did not share his views. It is noted that the same report mentioned that it was very doubtful if the Junta de Orford–Marconi was going to send a delegation to the Youth Congress (Communist) in Australia, a statement hard to reconcile with the presence of a Communist sympathizer as a candidate for Secretary General of that organisation.

b. A. An article in Revista Libro of 12 March 1954 reported declarations of the Cuban Committee for Guatemala against the aggression against that country. Subject's name was listed among the signers of the declaration as a member of the Committee.

c. Subject stated that he signed the declaration in the sinecure belief that Guatemala was a democratic country and that democratic forces had to defend that country against forces which would maintain it in economic subjection and political backwardness. He said that many non-Communists of like belief also signed the declaration. He added that when ARMIET went behind the Iron Curtain it became clear that Guatemala really had been in danger of becoming a Communist beachhead.
