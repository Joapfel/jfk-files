b. HU'JANOID, is an American citizen, was born in 1922 in DeKalb, Illinois. 5. Cover: Subject is established in Costa Rica on a purely personal basis, with no apparent connection with the San Jose station. Subjects activities in connection with HUJANOID-3 will be handled through safe house facilities to be acquired by the station. It is felt that the processing of the material acquired from HUJANOID-1 & 2, can be done in the office of the Chief of Station, because of the small quantity of this type of material. The cut-out and direction activities of HUJANOID-4, will be handled by HUJANOID in periodic meetings in cafes, bars, or coffee shops, logical meeting places in San Jose.

6. Contact and communications: Contact between HUJANOID and other personnel involved in this Project is described above under Cover.
