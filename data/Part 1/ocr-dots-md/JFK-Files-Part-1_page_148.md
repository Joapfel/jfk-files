MENORANDUM FOR THE CONCERN OF

FROM : Lt. Col. Lawrence D. Parker
SUBJECT : Meeting of the A.I.F. 1-9, 17 July 1943

1. An unexpected meeting was held with LT. COL. W. R. BARKER, who is a member of the 21st Battalion,國民軍陸軍第21787 1. The reason for this meeting is not known at present. It is believed to have been held at 21:03 on Thursday, July 16, 1943. It was attended by approximately 1,000 men and women from all over Australia. After the meeting, Mr. Barker left for home. Later, it was learned that Mr. Barker had spoken with his wife, Ms. Parker, on the phone just before he left the building. It is also stated that he spoke to his brother, Mr. Thomas Parker, of Melbourne, about an hour later, but no contact was made. It is presumed that Mr. Parker has received word that his brother has left the area, and that he will return as soon as possible.

2. It was revealed that Mr. Parker's concern about security since the war has increased and that there are such sharp people in the country that even the government cannot trust. The elements involved in a complete clandestine communication system were explained to him by the principal officers, Mr. and Mrs. George V. Parker of Melbourne. The event will take place at 11:00 pm on Thursday, July 16, 1943.

3. It was reported that the British Army is preparing to withdraw its troops from Cuba. The reason for this withdrawal is unknown, but it is assumed that it is related to the war in South America. It is also assumed that this withdrawal will be temporary and that they will return when the situation about 21 July is settled.

4. No report has been received of any additional developments:

(a) A big party was held on the 25th of July to celebrate the transfer of the C.S.S. "Avalon" from its base in Sydney to the port of Singapore, where it will take place in the flesh.

(b) Donations were made to the floatation tanks in Sydney to help support the Australian forces in the Pacific. Australia has sent Australia's main warship "RANSON" to the Pacific to help defend Australia against Japanese invasions. Thewarships are now in the Pacific and will be able to fight there.
