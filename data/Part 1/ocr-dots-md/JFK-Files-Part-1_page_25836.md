4 June 1957

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: LIPSTICK
Raymond H. GERENDE

1. On 28 May 1957, Harry Mexell (Safehouse Procurement Officer, X8185), William Lidenheimer (S.C.) and the undersigned met to obtain a safehouse for Raymond H. GERENDE (ps). The safehouse obtained was the following:

Apartment D-22, Madison
Presidential Gardens Apartment Hotel
Mount Vernon Avenue and Russell Road
Alexandria, Virginia
Telephone: Temple 6-4400

2. This apartment was tentatively approved orally by Mr. Lidenheimer from a security stanpoint. Registration was made by me in the name of Rufus KING, attorney, Southern Building, Washington, D. C. National 8-3115. GERENDE will be ostensibly a legal client of Mr. King and will be registered in true name upon his arrival. His name was not given upon obtaining the apartment nor was mine. Only Mr. King's name was used.

3. My cover story, if necessary to use one, was to be that of law clerk, Larry HERMAN, in Mr. King's office. Mr. Mexell has cleared this cover with Mr. King's office. Mr. King's office, according to Mr. Mexell, will pay for the apartment by check.

4. The apartment was rented at the rate of $155 a month from 8 June 1957 onward (to be through 13 July 1957).

5. Mr. Frazes of S.C. (SB/2, X547 and X3071) informed me telephonically on 31 May that the safehouse site had been approved.

Lawrence B. Sulc

SECRET.
