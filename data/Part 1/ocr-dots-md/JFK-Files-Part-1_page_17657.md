### h. Goods and Services Provided to the Soviet Embassy

Periodic reports of the persons and institutions providing goods and services to the Soviet embassy have been of considerable value in developing and mounting a local operation. This type of information is valuable in determining the identity of individuals having access to the Soviet embassy who can later be recruited by the station as controlled agents. For example, dated 27 February 1957, subject: "Goods and Services Provided to the Soviet Embassy, Mexico City, December 1956 - January 1957," is an example of the station's reporting of this type of operational information.

### i. Miscellaneous Activities of the Soviet Embassy

LITAT material relating to the Soviet embassy, which does not fit into any general category, is set forth each month in a series of reports on miscellaneous activities. These reports include such random information as banking transactions, landscaping activities, evidences of internal construction, etc. The following reports are cited as examples of this type of information:

* Mth-2375, dated 30 January 1957, "Miscellaneous Activities of the Soviet Embassy, Mexico City, December 1956."

* Mat-2148, dated 27 February 1957, "Miscellaneous Activities of the Soviet Embassy, Mexico City, January 1957."

## The Second Station

The information obtained on the (second) location during the past year is basically the same as the information obtained on the Soviet embassy; consequently, to avoid repetition in this review, only the highlights of each location coverage are mentioned.

### a. Data Pertaining to (Second Personnel)

The LITAT collection effort in this connection has been productive. Reports have reflected personality data, official functions and domestic matters pertaining to each staff member and information suitable for use in planning defection approaches.
