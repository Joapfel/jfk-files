1. THELA KINO, PRO-CUBAN DEPUTY TO THE PANAMA NATIONAL ASSEMBLY, WAS SCHEDULED TO LEAVE PANAMA ON 5 FEBRUARY FOR CURACAO, FROM WHERE SHE WAS TO GO TO THE SOVIET UNION VIA CUBA. (HEADQUARTERS COMMENT: TD SDB-3/659,136, OF 21 JANUARY, REPORTED THAT MSC. KINO WOULD LEAVE FOR THE SOVIET UNION AND CUBA ON 26 JANUARY. SHE WAS MAKING THE TRIP TO SEE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR PANAMA FROM MOSكو.)

2. OK 28 JANUARY MRS. KING MET "PRANKO", AND TOLD HER THAT VICTOR RICO GALAN HAD ASKED HER TO GO INSIDE TO CUBA AND THEN TO THE SOVIET UNION AND TO DROP HER PLANS TO GO TO CUBA. PRANKO GAVE MRS. KING $1,000 FOR HIS TRIP, AND THIS IS EXPECTED TO MEET HIM IN CURACAO. IF FIELD OFFICE: DRANKO
