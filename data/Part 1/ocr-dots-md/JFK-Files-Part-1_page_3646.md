19 October 1972

TO : Miami

FROM : CAS, Miami

SUBJECT: Traces on Sources of Information on Drugs

1. The following information has been extracted from our files on the 7 persons mentioned below who are narcotic sources of your office. Additional information on them will be forwarded to you as it becomes known to us.

As Bernardo G. de TORRES Alvarez: Also known as Bernardo CONZALZ de Torres Alvarez. TORRES entered the U.S. in January 1955, and began to play professional baseball. He gave up his studies in civil engineering (he began this career in 1948), and he obtained work with the Ford Motor Co. in Michigan and with the Aldor Co., Inc., for whom he installed garage doors that they manufactured. He went to Cuba in January 1959 to attempt to exfiltrate his father. Although he was unable to do so at that time, due to his father's illness, his father came out himself later. Subject stayed in Havana and worked against the CASTRO regime until November 1959, when he returned to the U.S. After his arrival he took a course given by the Miami Police Department for private detectives, and obtained a certificate allowing him to practice as a detective, which he did for the C.de Torres Detective Firm (C. de Torres is probably his brother, Carlos de Torres). He made trip trips to Cuba in early 1960 to discuss shipments of arms to Anti-Castro forces. Torres became the Chief of Intelligence of Brigade 2508. He was captured on 17 April 1961 at Playa Giron, Cuba, and was ransomed for $50,000, after which he was returned to the U.S. on a Pan American charter flight landing at Homestead Air Force Base on 24 December 1962. He later became the Assistant Secretary for Military Affairs and the Chief of Intelligence of the Association of Bay of Pigs veterans. His brother Carlos was the liaison between the Intelligence Department of the Association and the CIA, and provided the necessary means to guide Subject in his position.
