Concerning the impending trade negotiations with Spain, in addition to the economic problem the Cubans are now faced with another impediment which further delays the negotiations: the recent changes in the Spanish government. It is difficult at this time for the Cubans to approach any Spanish official because of the climate of confusion among them. Most Spanish officials have no idea as to whether they will remain in their posts or will be replaced. Therefore, it is futile for the Cubans to press them for action on the negotiations. The most they can do is wait patiently until the situation is normalized. If the negotiations are not held within the 30-day expiration period of the agreement, it is possible that the present agreement can be extended for two months. This would be advantageous to the Cubans because during the first two months of the year they will be able to acquire all the goods they will need for 1970.
