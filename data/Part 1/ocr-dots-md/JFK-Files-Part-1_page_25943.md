SUBJECT: POSSIBLE ATTEMPT ON THE LIFE OF A RELATIVE OF FORMER PRESIDENT SALVADOR ALLENDE

(SOURCE COMMENT: PASCAL'S EXACT CONNECTION WITH SALVADOR ALLENDE OR HIS CURRENT ACTIVITIES IN CARACAS ARE UNKNOWN.)

2. THE ATTEMPT AGAINST PASCAL IS TO BE CARRIED OUT BY

ROLANDO OTERO HERNANDEZ AND OTHER MEMBERS OF CUBAN EXILE GROUPS. ALTHOUGH IT IS UNCLEAR AS TO WHETHER OTERO WAS ACTUALLY ARRIVED IN CARACAS. HE IS EXPECTED TO BE IN CARACAS BY THE EVENING OF 17 FEBRUARY 1976.
