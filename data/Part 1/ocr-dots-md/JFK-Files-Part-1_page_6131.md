Dear Mr. Young,

Under the provisions of 5 USC 552, the Freedom of Information Act, request access to, and copies of all files and documents maintained by the CIA relating to myself.

The material I seek includes, but is not limited to, dossiers, card files, microfilm files, microfiche files, and all computerized data and references maintained by the CIA. This request also encompasses any photographs and fingerprint files which you may have.

If, perchance, you rule that portions of these files are "reasonably aggregable" I request copies of the remaining material and reserve your right to appeal any such deletions.

If, perchance, this request is denied in whole or in part, please inform me of the appropriate appeals procedure.

If deletions are made, please cite the statutory justification for each deleted item.

I am submitting my full name, date and place of birth and Social Security Number. I am prepared to pay a reasonable fee for the reproduction of this material.

As required by 5 USC 552, I expect to hear from you within 10 working days.
