31 March 1952

Dear Tim:

I should be glad to discuss a matter of some interest to us with Kenneth Walters. If agreeable to his and to you, we should like to have from you a few sheets of Radio Free Europe stationery to use for a letter, along the line of the attached draft, to an individual in Panama City who has a radio station useful to our purposes. The draft is, I believe, self-explanatory, although not necessarily in the final form the letter will take. In case you are willing to assist us as suggested, we would handle the money and accounting for it. You will note, however, that the mobility exists that the funds will be attributed to Kenneth's organization.

Please return with your reply the enclosed draft.
We will provide you with a copy of the letter which is sent, if the plan is carried out.
