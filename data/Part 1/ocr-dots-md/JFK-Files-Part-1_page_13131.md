Are any of your relatives members of the Communist Party?

Are any of your relatives working for PBRUMEN Intelligence?

This training lasted for a full year and occurred in 1962 to the best of Subject's memory. Subject was reasonably sure of this date because of the "Missile crisis" and President Kennedy's death which occurred later.
