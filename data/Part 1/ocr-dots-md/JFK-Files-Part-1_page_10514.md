1954:

Subject reportedly terminated his
employment with Sandwich Brothers
between June and July 1954, exact date not
known, and established his own firm
under the name of PARIS ASSOCIATED
ENTERPRISES.

The 1954 Japan Directory of Foreign
Firms lists SUNDERVICH BROTHARDS
as a United States firm, with offices
at 31 - Tamao-cho, 4-chome, Shiba,
Minato-ku, Tokyo, Japan. Telephone:
63-1809, 63-4369, 63-4287, and liste
E. OSTROVSKY as the Tokyo Representa-
tive.

E. OSTROVSKY, of course, is Emmanuel
Abraham OSTROVSKY, who is known to
have been the Tokyo Branch Manager of
the above firm, who resided in 1954 at
19 Kouncho-Mita, Minato-ku, Tokyo.
Home telephone: 63-6773.
