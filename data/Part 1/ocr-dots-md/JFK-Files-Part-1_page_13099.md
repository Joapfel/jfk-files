ATTACHMENT #3

SUBJECT: Jose (pat and mat unk.) Altas: "Bepe" DSE and CP member and Installer and repairman for "Area Special" of the Telephone Company.

SOURCE: Lazaro Armandi MIRANDETZ Perez DPOB: 17 December 1937, Plaza Velez, Pinar del Rio Prov. Alien registration BA17-405-510

1.- HERNANDEZ Let Jose during 1957 when they both started to work for the Cuban Telephone Company in Havana.

2.- Ua to circa 1962, Jose was employed as installer-repairman for "Area Forte" of the telephone company at 19 Street corner of B Street, Vedado, Havana.

3.- In circa 1962 Jose was assigned to work as repairman and installer of telephone equipment for "Area Special" at 19 Street corner of C Street, Vedado, and since then it has been known that Jose was a DSE agent, as every other "Area Special" employee.

4.- Jose has all other "Area Special" personnel brags a lot about being a DSE agent. He wears military uniform always, showing no rank and is always side armed.

5.- In early 1966, Jose was elected to become member of the Cuban Communist Party in an assembly to elect new CP members at his place of employment.

6.- In the performance of his job, Jose installs and repairs telephones' equipments at: Offices of ministers, DSE offices, Military Headquarters, COE personaltives'offices and homes, etc.

7.- HERNANDEZ saw Jose for the last time in early 1967 at "Area Forte" of the Telephone Company.

8.- PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION: DPOB: circa 1937, unk.; caucasian (brunette;avy black hair; dark eyes (wears sunglasses); round face; good teeth; 5'2" in height and 160lbs strong build. Married (mated un.). Resides at (address unk.) Havana. DSE and CP member, Installer-repairman of Telephone equipment for "Area Special" of the Telephone Company at 19 Street corner of C Street, Vedado, Havana.
