MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Director of Central Intelligence

THROUGH : Chief of Operations, DD/P
SUBJECT : Project LIFETAT (Amendment)

1. The attached project, originating in WH Division, is designed to determine Sino/Soviet/Satellite plans, activities, and connections with other Communist elements through independent audio-surveillance operations in Mexico. It is presented for your approval of two amendments; the first, to permit the utilization of Roland K. WINGSHSS (p) as an additional Staff Agent in the project, and the second, to establish for cover purposes a $20,000 U. S. bank account for Staff Agent Daniel A. BROMUND (p) in order that he may obtain Kantinta status in Mexico.

2. Authority is requested to obligate an additional $26,493 from the DD/P-WH Division budget for fiscal year 1958. Sufficient funds are available in the WH Division budget. If approved, these two amendments will raise the total amount authorized for the present project year, 1 July 1957 through 30 June 1958, to $156,647. Project LIFETAT was renewed by the DCI on 30 October 1957 in the amount of $130,154.

3. Attached is the LIFETAT Project Outline, which sets forth the important factors involved in the operation; a WH Division memorandum, dated 31 October 1957, which explains the proposal for securing Kantinta status for BRONAUD; and a WH Division memorandum, dated 23 November 1957, which requests authority to incorporate WINGSHSS into the project as a Staff Agent.

4. CCB/WH comments that according to Finance Division, establishing a bank account for BRONAUD is the only means of satisfying the requirements of the Mexican government. The Letter of Credit must be irrevocable and allow a stated withdrawal per period (as in this case, per quarter) in order that the full amount cannot be liquidated before the expiration of the Kantinta. CCB/WH recommends approval in this connection. In addition CCB/WH recommends approval of the proposed use of WINGSHSS, provided that his cover, which has not been determined as yet, be coordinated with CCB/WH sufficiently in advance to allow CCB/WH to state any objections deemed necessary and to take effective action toward establishing sound cover.
