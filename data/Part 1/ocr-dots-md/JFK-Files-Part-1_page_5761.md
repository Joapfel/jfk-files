Members of the Central Standing Committee of the Revolutionary Committee of the Kuomintang Kan Tzu-sen, Liu Chung-jung, Wu Mao-sen, Hou Ching-ju, Hsu Wen-lien and Chien Chang-chao, and members of the Central Committee of the Revolutionary Committee of the Kuomintang Wang Pao-chen, Li Ping-heng, Li Chun-lung, Li Cheng, Chen Li and Chiao Shih-chai;

Deputies to the National People's Congress, representatives of fine art circles, well-known personsages and Ho Hsiang-ning's relatives and friends Wang Chen, Wu Te-feng, Sha Chien-li, Chuang Ming-li, Huang Ting-chen, Tien Pu-ta, Wu Yuan-hsing, Chu Kiang-ya, Huang Chang-shui, Chang Kuo-chi, T'ie Ching-chang, Ho Lien-chih, Chang Jui-hwa, Yu Li-chun, Pu Ya, Hu Yuan, Li Ko-jan, Chiang Chao-ho, Chou Dai-ying, Lung Chien, Cheng Szu-yuan, Teng Kuang-yin and his wife, Ma Hai-te (George Haten), Su Pei (Frank Cee), Suuth Cee, Sol Adler, Pat Adler, Ichihei Sugiyama and his wife, Rewi Alley and Jack Chen.

More than 500 party and government functionaries and representatives of the masses attended.

After the ceremony, Ho Hsiang-ning's coffin was transported by special train to Nanking to be buried by the side of Mr. Liao Chung-kai's coffin in the same grave.

Yeh Chien-ying, Li Hsian-nien, Hsu Hsiang-chien, Kuo Mo-jo and leading members of departments concerned escorted the coffin to Peking railway station.

Liu Yu-fa, Teng Ying-chao, Hsieh Pu-min, Li Chin-te, Liu Fei, Chu Yun-shan, Chen Tzu-sheng and Kan Tzu-sen and Ho Hsiang-ning's relatives Liao Cheng-chih, Liao Meng-hsing and Ching Pu-chun escorted the coffin to Nanking.

Ho Hsiang-ning's coffin arrived in Nanking this afternoon and the funeral ceremony was held at the grave of Mr. Liao Chung-kai on the eastern outskirts of the city.

Present at the ceremony were Hsu Shih-yu, member of the Political Bureau of the CCP Central Committee, First secretary of the Jiangsu Provincial Committee of the CCP and chairman of the Jiangsu Provincial revolutionary Committee, and leading members of the Jiangsu Provincial and Nanking municipal party and revolutionary committees Tu Ping, Wu Ta-sheng, Yang Kuang-li, Hsiao Yung-yin, Chiang Ko, Fang Min, Liu Chi-chang and Pang Ming.

Liu Yu-fa and others and relatives of the deceased who had escorted the coffin to Nanking attended the ceremony.
