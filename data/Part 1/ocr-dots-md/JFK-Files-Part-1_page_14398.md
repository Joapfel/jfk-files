1. FOLLOWING ARE HIGHLIGHTS SAMKO/MAUTO-1 FOUR-HOUR MEETING 31 MAY.

D. AMAUTO-1 AND WIFE VISIBLY MOVED WHEN SAMKO ASKED DISCUSS HIS BUSINESS SUCCESSES AND THEY ASKED NUMBER OF QUESTIONS RELATING TO FINANCIAL ASPECTS OF HIS BUSINESS.
