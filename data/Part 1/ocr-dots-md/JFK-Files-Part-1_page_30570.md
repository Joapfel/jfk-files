Convert to Markdown31 October 1947

Subject: Personnel

To: DIRECTOR, Special Operations
Washington 25, D. C.

Attn:

1. For sometime now, we have had the papers in Washington for the reclassification of Mr. David E. West and Mr. Newton S. Miler.

2. Mr. David West is now stationed in Fairfax, and is ably performing the duties of a Junior Intelligence Officer. He is responsible for thearial net which he has very successfully taken over from Mr. Frank Bossao. In addition to this, he is also handling Colonad. It is hoped that his reclassification to a CAF-5 will be forthcoming.

3. Mr. Newton S. Miller, who is presently a CAJ-5, is now stationed in Mukden and is performing the duties of a Junior Intelligence Officer. He is handling some North Korean operations from the Mukden station and is rapidly gaining experience. It is hoped that his reclassification to a CAJ-7 will be acted upon.

4. It is requested that this headquarters be advised of any action taken by Washington regarding the reclassification of the above personnel.

Approved

RALPH T. WALKER
