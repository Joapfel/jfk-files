GRIFFITH VISIT

Conversation with Prof. William S. Griffith, London, 25/4/67.

Tadeusz Komko phoned my home to tell me that XXXXXX Griffith, who is spending a sabbatical year in Munich from MIT, wanted to see me. Later that evening, Leo Lebedz and Griffith arrived. Stella Joyce of Amnesty International and Matthew Kooma also arrived unexpectedly. It turned out that Lebedz and Joyce had often talked over the phone, but had never met.

Griffith told me that he had recently visited East Africa. In Dar es Salaam, he had met David Sikeko and Ahmed G. Morahim of the PAC (Pan Africanist Congress). He said his distinct impression was that PAC was on a strong pro-Chinese course. Griffith said he had written to hana Mahomo's old address in London in an effort to contact him, but had learned later that Mahomo was now at Kirk Kirkland House, Harvard University. He said he looked forward to talking with Mahomo when he went briefly to the USA in May.
