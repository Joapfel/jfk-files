SUBJECT: BESABER P-8777
DATE OF MEETING: 16 May 1968

1. I met Subject in his room in the Del Paseo Hotel from 1415-1630. The next meeting will be arranged by telephone.

2. <u>LOGINOV</u>. Subject reported that <u>LOGINOV</u> called him at his factory in Guernavaca on Friday, 3 May. <u>LOGINOV</u> said he had some visitors who would like to buy a few things to take back with them to Russia and asked if it would be convenient if he brought them down to Subject's factory on Saturday afternoon, 4 May. Subject said fine and invited <u>LOGINOV</u> and the visitors to come back to his house for a few drinks after they finished shopping. <u>LOGINOV</u> gratefully accepted and said they would be there at 1330. (Per LIENVOY--see attached transcript--<u>LOGINOV</u> called BEWEEP on 3 May to ask for Subject's telephone number.)

3. <u>LOGINOV</u> arrived (in 19 DA, a dark blue Chevy) at the factory at 1345 with three other persons:

(a) A big, fat, plain, peasant-looking woman by the name of "IGOROVA," "IGANOVA," or something similar. <u>LOGINOV</u> introduced her as a commercial representative, but did not elaborate.

(b) A second Russian woman whose name <u>Subject couldn't</u> remember. She looked to be 33-35 years old and wore glasses. <u>LOGINOV</u> did not mention her profession, and she seemed simply to be a friend of the first woman.
