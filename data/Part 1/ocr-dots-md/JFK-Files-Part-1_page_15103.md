REPORT ON CONTACT WITH AGENT OR DEFECTOR

SUBJECT: INVOICE
DATE: 19 June 1962
TIME: 1400
PLACE: Ostendstr 1/9

David F. Reinhold, Case Officer

COMMENTS:

CO visited Subj at his net and said him DM 300 subsistence (and deals) for the period 11 June through 30 June 1962.

Subj stated that the firm in Dortmund was not interested in his invention stating that they would have to sell it so cheaply they could not make any money on it.

Subj asked if there was anything new. CO informed him that the ways of life are often strange to understand but that there is evidence that something is being done since a telegram was received inquiring whether or not he had any preference as to where he may prefer to live. He stated that he did not care where he lived, the important thing being that he would like to work for us anywhere in the world. He even mentioned loss where he claimed he could be of some use.

CO informed Subj that for future planning he would like to know that in the event we would assist him in legalizing in S. America, but informed he that he would no longer have a job with us, whether he could get along on his own. He stated that though this would make him unhappy he would naturally have to make the best arrangements possible.

Subj then concerned about his future and indicating that he dislikes sitting around doing nothing nevertheless was not over bearing or demurring.
