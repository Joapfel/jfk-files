SUBJECT VICTOR RICO GALAN'S VIEWS ON THE REVOLUTIONARY MOVEMENT IN LATIN AMERICA AND ON FIDEL CASTRO

ACQ 31 JANUARY 1967

FIELD NO.

SOURCE LEFTIST JOURNALIST WHO TALKED WITH VICTOR RICO GALAN IN THE LEONQUEBRI PENITENTIARY IN MEXICO CITY. SOURCE IS A RELIABLE REPORTER.

1. (HEADQUARTERS COMMENT) VICTOR RICO GALAN, PRO-CUBAN JOURNALIST WAS ARRESTED ON 12 AUGUST 1966, TOGETHER WITH SOME FORTY OTHER MEMBERS OF THE POPULAR REVOLUTIONARY MOVEMENT (PRP). RICO GALAN WAS CHAPTED WITH INFLUENZ REBELLION, CONSPIRACY, AND ACCUMULATION OF ARMS. UNDER THESE CHANGES, HE IS NOT ELIGIBLE FOR RAIL.)

2. RICO GALAN BELIEVES THAT VIOLENT REVOLUTION THROUGHOUT LATIN AMERICA IS "INDISPENSABLE," SINCE THE PEACEFUL ROAD TO CHANGE IS SLOCRED EVERTHERE. REVOLUTIONARY MOVEMENTS ARE ON THE RISE EVERYWHERE IN LATIN AMERICA, ACCORDING TO RICO GALAN. IN HIS OPINION, THESE MOVEMENTS ARE MARKET, WITH EIRÁL TENORENCIES, AND ARE NOT AS REGD AS THE INTERNATIONAL COMMITTEE RESEMBLES. RICO GALAN STATED THAT CASTRO HAS THE DOMINANT INFUENCE AT WORK.
