## MEMORANDUM OF CONVERSATION

September 6, 1966

SUBJECT: Andrew St. George -- free lance photographer now working with CBS

Mr. St. George came to see me at his request. I have known him from the time that he went to Cuba in the late fifties to film the Castro movement.

He said he wanted to see me as an old friend to tell me that CBS is filming a story of gun-running from the U.S. to the D.R. for use against Haiti. He is working with the CBS crew.

Mr. St. George said that the CBS team had completed its filming in Miami and in the near future would be travelling to Santo Domingo to film the operation at that end. He claimed that in Miami, Joaquín Masserrer and Mitchell Warbel (phonetic) had permitted him to film what he described as a large arsenal in Masserrer's house.

In response to my question about the identity of Warbel, he said that he is a soldier-of-fortune - arms merchant type from Atlanta who is working with Masserrer in the acquisition of arms. St. George claimed that Warbel had been responsible for the seizure of Manuel Any's vessel "Venzia" in the Dominican Republic and the acquisition of the arms (mostly sub-machine guns) which were on board.

St. George stated that base in the Dominican Republic was on Beata Island. Arrangements in the DR are being handled through Santiago Rey with Martires Arana and others whom he did not name. St. George said that Belaguer had given his nod to the operation. I told him that I did not believe there was any truth to this latter point as President Zanquer has recently normalized diplomatic relations with Haiti and is not interested in foreign adventures of this type.
