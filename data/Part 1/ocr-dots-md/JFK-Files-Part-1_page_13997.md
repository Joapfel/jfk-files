            1. Reference your telephone request of 24 June 1969 requesting a photograph of <u>Eugelio RODRIGUEZ</u> which is better than the one appearing in the Cuban Mugbook, it is believed that Subject of your request is <u>Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez</u>, whose photograph is included in the Mugbook. There is no photograph of <u>Eugelio RODRIGUEZ</u>.

3. AMBEDEW-1 has been shown and has positively identified the attached photo as Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez, a DGI officer in Section III who was known to AMBEDEW-1 as "Rogelio." A-1 last saw Rogelio in Paris on 21 November 1968, at which time Rogelio was enroute to Conakry on what A-1 thought to be an important mission. It appeared that someone in Conakry could not leave until Rogelio arrived. A-1 understood that Rogelio had been in the UN Mission in New York, or in Canada, in 1964-65.

4. The above information may be passed to LNERGO.

Olive S. ECKHOLM
