TO DIR LCHARVEST

1. TIEBAR'S MEMO PASSED TO STATION 13 JAN. STATES USUA RELIABLE SOURCE (IDENTIFIED ORALLY BY THE AS REPORTED BY OFFICIA WITH HIGH LEVEL CONTACTS) REPORTS TWO NEW ORGANIZATIONS FOUND DURING COMMIE MEETING MOSCOW DEC 6. ONE IS OFFICE DEALING WITH COUNTRIES FORMERLY INCLUDED FRENCH COLONIAL SYSTEM. OTHER STUDIES AND COOPERATION WITH LATIN AMERICAN PARTIES IS "OFFICE FOR PERMANENT (PORTION MISSING BEYOND LATERN)." LATER TO BE ORGANIZED BY COMMIE PARTIES ARGENTINA, BRasil AND CUBA, LOCATED PEIPING. HEAD OFFICE TO BE DR. N HALPERIN, ADMINISTRATIVE DIRECTOR MRS. HARTA STERN. HALPERIN, FORMERLY OF PROF BOSTON, FORMERLY OSS EMPLOYEE, IS PRESUMED HAVE LEFT PORTNAME CO. STERN ALSO PRIME CITIZEN.

2. TIER STATED ONE MRS. STERN, FORMERLY CITIZEN, WAS IN SWEDEN IN EARLY 50. NO STATION TRACES STERN OR HALPERIN.

3. TIER ANXIOUS HAVE REPORT CONFIRMED AS TRUE LEADING SOURCE; WOULD LIKE HIS COMMENTS HALPERIN AND STERN INVOLVEMENT AND WHETHER LATTER WAS IN SWEDEN; BELIEVES IMPORTANT DETERMIN IF OFFICE ACTUALLY LOCATED PEIPING AND NOT MOST likely, FUN TO HIM THIS WOULD MEAN MAJOR ALLOCATION RESPONSIBILITY.
