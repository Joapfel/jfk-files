Dear Sirs,

Thank you very much for your assistance in this investigation. I have received your latest report dated August 30th, 1986 and I have compared it with the previous one dated August 19th, 1986. The main features of the economic situation are the same as last year's report. The main positive factors are the low interest rates and the strong consumer confidence index. There were some concerns about the high unemployment rate, however, and I expect that this situation will improve in the near future. I also see some concerns about the slow rate of economic development in the rest of the world. In particular, there are some concerns about the strength of the dollar and the prospect for inflation in the next few months. I therefore suggest that we continue to monitor the situation closely and update our reports accordingly. In the mean time, I would like to express my thanks for your help and your cooperation in this matter. I am sure that your experience with economic issues will be very useful to me in my own studies. I would also like to thank you for your help in preparing the report of the Economic Analysis Committee of the International Monetary Fund. I believe that this report will be very useful for all those who are interested in the global economic picture.

Sincerely yours,
