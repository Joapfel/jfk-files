REMITTANCE OF READING

DATE : 7 APRIL 1940
SUBJECT : SECRET and CONFIDENTIAL -> Not.
PRESENT : Hossrs. Glaser, Denty, Chester, Droller, Saitt.

1. Discussion of arrangements made for departure and cover for James Turnbam, Mr. Saitt will handle details.

2. It was agreed that a recommendation be made to CO' that two members of this committee, representing CO' and CII', respectively, be given authority to take emergency action on cables received out of normal hours of work requiring emergency handling; it being anticipated that such emergencies may arise in connection with projects PIPER and PDUICE over any of the weekends between the present date and 1 June.

3. FRDAY 113 (IN 21895) was considered in detail and all items in paragraph 1 and 2 were approved. With reference to item F of paragraph 2, project LOGAN MAN 111 (IN 21715), Mr. Denby assured the responsibility for clearing this matter with the State Department through Mr. Offis, and if a favorable response is received, a cable of concurrence will be sent to the field.

With reference to paragraph 3, it was proposed by Mr. Droller that Items A and I be combined. That is, that no scars be created with reference to butonic plaque or other serious diseases but that a campaign be instituted to the effect that the water of East Berlin is seriously contaminated and is causing dysentery, that earnings to issued and published in the press sympathetically urging the pop. In coo of East Berlin, including the MJ participants, not to drink the water.
