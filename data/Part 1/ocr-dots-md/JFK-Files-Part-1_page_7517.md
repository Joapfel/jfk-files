After her arrival in China, Viola RUBINSON ISAACS was employed at the DaH Cung Middle School, and by November 1933 was reported as a teacher at the Comparative Law School in Shanghai.

On 7 August 1933, the ISAACS left Shanghai for Japan, and on 16 August 1933 proceeded to Dalian, returning to Shanghai by rail on 26 August 1933. In December 1933 ISAACS left Shanghai for Peking to gather first hand information on the newly formed People's Revolutionary Government of the Republic of China.

In January 1934, ISAACS is reported to have visited Mr. and Mrs. NOULENS while they were on a hunger strike in prison. In the spring of 1934, ISAACS and his wife left for Peking as route to Peking at which city, they resided at 2. T'iang Pu Chia 'Tao', East City. His mailing address was 3 of one Mr. UREXANN; Grand Hotel, Peking.
