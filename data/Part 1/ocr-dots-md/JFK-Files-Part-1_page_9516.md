March 8, 1950

REICHSTAG PROCEEDINGS

(To be broadcast over worldwide radio network in all languages in collaboration with Radio Free Europe, RIAS, the various European networks, BBC, CBS-NBC and the Voice of America).

## I) Purpose

To re-assert the universal right for freedom in simple dramatic terms which men everywhere will understand, regardless of language.

## II) Session

Berlin

The opening session of the cultural Freedom Congress, now scheduled for late June 1950.

## III) Sponsor

The Congress. Its executive body should include sponsors for all leading countries. Most specifically, it should include at least two outstanding Russians of world reputation and no minor ex to grind. The Russian nobel prize winning novelist Ivan Samin (living in Paris, I believe), Igor Stravinsky, Sergei ISoussevitsky are names that suggest themselves. sponsors If the Congress itself includes a larger delegation from the leading European countries, then several Russian scholars and scientists now in U.S. or in DP camps should be included. sponsors All delegations, from countries on both sides of the Iron Curtain, should be made up entirely of men of unimpeachable moral stature and real reputation. Professional politicians, even when they're good corporate, should be restricted to the rock-bottom minimums and must be of the moral stature of Mayor Ernst Reuter of Berlin. Petty, self-seeking politics must be kept out. Would kill the whole show.
