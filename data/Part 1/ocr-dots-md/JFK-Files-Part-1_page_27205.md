DEC 22 1960

MEMORANDUM FOR THE SECRETARY OF STATE

ATTENTION: Mr. W. Park Armstrong, Jr.

Subject: Request for Appointment in the Foreign Service as Attaché at Manila for Mr. William Vincent Broe

Enclosures:
a. Biographical Sketch, William Vincent Broe
b. Forms DSP-3a,

1. It is requested that Mr. William Vincent Broe be appointed in the Foreign Service with the title of Attaché, FCS-3, $7,710.00, for duty in the American Embassy at Manila, Republic of the Philippines.

2. Mr. Broe received his B.A. Degree from Fordham College. He has had approximately two years and six months of experience as an Intelligence officer in Government service. It is believed that he possesses the professional qualifications for the duties planned for him, and, in addition, has the representative qualifications expected of an American official serving abroad. Mr. Broe will receive from CIA a basic salary of $7,600.00 per annum.

3. Mr. Broe will replace Mr. Frank M. Chapin, for when a request for clearance for return will be submitted at a later date.

4. There is transmitted herewith a biographical sketch on Mr. Broe which sets forth in detail pertinent information on him and on his special qualifications for the duties it is intended that he assume at Manila.

FOR THE DIRECTOR OF CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE:

ROBERT A. SCHOS
Assistant Director
