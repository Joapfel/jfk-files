SUSTENANCES AND RENTS

In Attachment-4 the various entries under this title are explained.

Entry #400 of 2 July 1960, check $22, was for the payment of rent
for the office of the DEMOCRATIC REVOLUTIONARY FRONT at #1001 Congress
Building, Miami.

Entry #402 of 27 July, check $64, in the name of Abel Varona was
used to give food, from 27 July to 2 August 1960, both dates inclusive,
to the following persons: Miguel Penton, Hugo Zamora, Martin Torres,
Giraldo Serafin Penton, Orlando Garcia, Claudio Orozco, Benigno Perez,
Humberto Cordero, and Felix Sosa.

As you may note the expenses under this title will necessarily
increase, therefore an ample estimate destined to cover future needs is
advisable.

Check #67 of 27 July 1960 in the name of the labor leader Pascasio
Linera was made void.

COMMUNICATIONS

entries are 500 and 501 in the amounts of $400.00 and $300.00 for postage

$23.15 appear in the "statement for reimbursement" for $834.20 which he
submitted for the month of July.
