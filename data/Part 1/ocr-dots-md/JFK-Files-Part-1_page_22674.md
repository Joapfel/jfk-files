MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, WH Division
ATTENTION : WH/III/Mexico (ed Ogden)
SUBJECT : Cover Review - Harold Noemayr (P)

1. In compliance with the regulative responsibility placed upon Central Cover Branch for the review of nonofficial cover arrangements, it is requested that the action herein prescribed be taken in the subject case.

2. The interested Area Branch and the field station, if applicable, should make a thorough assessment of this notional cover in relation to its operational suitability and report to you on the strength or weakness of the arrangement, and any recommendation they deem advisable to strengthen it. For guidance, the field station should consider such factors as the security consideration involved and the ability of the individual to live his cover. The report from the field station, along with the Area Branch comments should be forwarded to CCS.

3. The Area Branch Desk should also advise CCS of future plans for the Agent with particular regard to termination, change of operations, change of station, extension of the Agent's tour of duty, etc., in order that Central Cover Branch may take measures to ensure cover continuity and to protect the security of the arrangement.

4. After the above-requested review has been completed, action will be taken to solve any existing problems.

5. This case will be reviewed again six months after the completion of this review, or if the Agent is being rotated or terminated, 90 days prior to his termination or departure
