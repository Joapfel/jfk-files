Mexican woman, Sra. V. P. Mexico. Will call at that airport. Is very poor and single. Has $500 in her bank. She is a pooressa. Her husband is an engineer. Would like to know if they have a plane ticket to Madrid. I can get one. The problem is this: my plane starts in 12 hours from here and has no seat. Will take a train instead of a plane. Will call the airport when she gets there to find out if they can give her a ticket. I will be there on time. Will also ask them to let her smoke. Will contact them by telephone.

Then this correspondent wants to know the address of either a Mexican or the Mexican Consulate. Ruiz says that if it is to exchange for his Madrid-mexico ticket for a Madrid-Cuba one, go by Fuenlabarri 15, and ANGUSC, a Senior, or PACHECO, a Cuban, until make the change for her. It seems she wants to leave on Monday. She wants them to take care of her Mexican visa because she says that if she goes it will not be timely, after what happened with Humberto CARRILLO. She says that OJVARAS is new in charge. She is going to Cuba now and will go to Mexico in 15 days.

Nilla comes in and gives her the name and telephone number of the lady she is going to interview: 226-86-96.
