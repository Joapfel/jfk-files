MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief of Operations, CO/F
SUBJECT : LIEMPTY (Renewal)
WH - Mexico City

1. The attached project, originating in WH Division, is presented for renewal for the period 1 December 1962 through 30 November 1963. This project provides for physical and photographic surveillance operations in support of Mexico City station operations, particularly those directed against Soviet personnel and installations.

2. Authority is requested to obligate $45,500 from the CO/P-WH Division budget: $20,500 in fiscal year 1962, and $20,000 in fiscal year 1963 subject to the availability of funds. This estimate includes $30,500 for salaries of three contract agents and eleven indigenous agents; $5,500 for operational expenses, including QP vehicle operation and maintenance, new warehouse procurement, post office boxes, purchase of information, and rental of vehicles; $1,000 for repair, maintenance, and replacement of photographic equipment; and $5,000 for maintenance of three warehouses.

3. The attached request for renewal discusses project activities in the past period. Contract Agent Oliver G. SCANTLING (o), serving as an investigator, has continued to follow-up on leads developed from other operations; has been the primary source of information on residences of Soviet embassy personnel; has provided investigative information on several cases of CI interest; and has trained surveillance assets in Interrogation. He also handled the LEXICO surveillance team during the time Contract Agent Simon J. GLADETTI (p) was absent from Mexico. The project segment under the direction of Contract Agent Raymond H. GALLAGHER (q) continued to collect operational information on the Soviet Embassy through photographic surveillance; and to provide operational support
