Convert to MarkdownMORONE'S fellow code clerks and residents of America House have described him as a heavy drinker, a heavy gambler, and a ladies' man. Various reports indicate that MORONE was involved in a currency-speculation ring operated by the KGB agent mentioned by NOSEMKO, and MORONE has confirmed that, on at least one occasion, he arranged the introduction of MORONE and other code clerks to Soviet females. MORONE had relations with one of them in his apartment in the spring of 1961. MORONE was also said to be a close friend of the Embassy Identified by NOSEMKO as an agent of KOSOLAPOV, but not mentioned by him in connection with the MORONE operation. (MORONE admitted that along with other Americans, he made it a common practice to purchase rubles illegally from One report states that he asked a Marine guard to deliver blackmarket rubles to MORONE in the State Department code room, a restricted area to which he had no access. A number of reports indicate that MORONE was sexually intimate with Svetlana IVANOVA, DEMKIN's agent, and with Ella UMANETS, another KGB agent at America House identified by NOSEMKO but not mentioned by him in connection with MORONE. (MORONE denies having had relations with IVANOVA, but said that he told his friends that he had been intimate with her. He said, however, that he knew both IVANOVA and UMANETS well and that he had once asked IVANOVA to arrange dates for him and another code clerk with two Russian females. IVANOVA did, and sexual relations ensued. MORONE has also admitted sexual relations with a number of women from various Western embassies in Moscow.)
