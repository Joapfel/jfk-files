SUBJECT: Operational Meeting with LINCHM/I (P-1204)

DATE & TIME: 24 November 1967

I. The undersigned met with LINCHM/I on 24 November from 1200 hours to 1545 hours at an in-town safe house. From 1205 to 1245 the meeting was conducted in L/I's car inasmuch as the safe house was occupied during this period. The next meeting is scheduled for the same safe house on 8 December at 1500 hours.

2. The following meetings between L/I and the below named Soviets occurred during the months of October and November:

a. Classes of 3 October: BEDRIN (P-9037) and CONCHARENKO (P-9379) studied on this day from 1530 to 1700. KAZANTSEV (P-880) studied from 1700 to 1800.

b. Classes of 5 October: In the morning of this day, an unidentified Soviet phoned Mrs. ROCKET (L/I was out of the house) and requested her to inform L/I that there would be no classes that day. No reason was given, either then or subsequently, for this cancellation.

c. Classes of 10 October: BEDRIN & Concharenko studied from 1530 to 1700. KAZANTSEV came at 1610, interrupting class, to pick up several pocket books which he had previously asked L/I to procure for him. L/I selected them from his own library. KAZANTSEV stated that he did not care what subjects were chosen; he wanted them only to improve his reading ability. He did mention, however, that he preferred not to read books which had either violence or pornography as their central theme. L/I brought a book on science fiction (Kaz. was not particularly interested in this), a book on ballet, and a book on the FBI—which KAZANTSEV seemed very interested in. KAZANTSEV said that he would not be taking class today and that they would all discontinue classes after 12 Oct. KAZANTSEV stated that he would be taking his vacation from now and 24 November.
