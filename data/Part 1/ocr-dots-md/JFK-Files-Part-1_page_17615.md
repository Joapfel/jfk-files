Information on this installation closely parallels that obtained on the Czech Legation and the Soviet installations. The largest amount of information concerning the Polish installation has been procured from LIFAT. An example of reports from this source is 1961-3161, Leon KILARSKI, Polish Commercial Attache. Another is 1961-3130, Suspicious Contact of Edward RUUSKI, Josef Isak GENSCHAPT.

IBM-3193 - Martiso BASSOLE and Family;

American Communists in Mexico:

One of the most extensive and productive coverages maintained through LIFAT during this past year was directed against American communists in Mexico. Most of these targets have been covered at the request of JENYI and the production has been partially processed by them for the justification of one of their dots in their local establishment. A total of 361 reels have been the result of the coverage since 1 July 1957 until 25 April 1958.

Foreign Communists in Mexico and Mexican Communists:

LIFAT continued to provide partial coverage of Eduardo NACHADO Morales, Venezuelan Communist, until the time of his departure for return to Venezuela. LIFAT also provided some 50 reels of coverage on Diego RIVERA, well-known Communist Mexican painter, until his death in November 1957. From this coverage it was determined that RIVERA was sufficiently concerned over the departure of the former Soviet Cultural Attache, Yury PAPOROV, that he arose from his sick bed in order to make the trip to Venezuela by car to insure that PAPOROV's departure was without incident.
