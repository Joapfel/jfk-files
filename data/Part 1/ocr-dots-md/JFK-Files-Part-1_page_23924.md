(Sinet is French)

His real name is Maurice Sinet. He is French. He is in his thirties. Married, rather unhappily; has one child, which his wife claims is not his. He's more of an anarchist. He was in the French Communist Party but for a very brief period, I gather. He is very virulent. Before Revolution came out he put out his own little magazine, cartoon magazine called Sine Massacre which was the most virulent cartoon I've ever seen. or else they depicted things like the clergy, love - very, very sick type of cartoon, which contrasted greatly from what made him famous, Sine's cat. cats, the Sine cat. He made a fortune with his cat and he also had a tie-in with some promotional things, Key-chain and others. That's where of course he made a lot of money. He does a lot of commercial art work. He sells quite a bit here in England. He does work for British ad agencies.
