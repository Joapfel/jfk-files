SUBJECT: CONCEPCION Morales, Jose Antonio
aka: Tito

1. Source has known Subject all his life because they are distant relatives. Source gave Subject a position as laborer in the Ministerio de Obras Publicas in 1980, when source worked there. Prior to this, Subject had never worked, because of his youth. After working in the Ministerio Subject became a member of the Militia and a staunch supporter of the COC. Source claims that about 1982 Subject accused him of having removed a TV set from his (source's) home, which the source did because at that time he was arranging his exit permit to leave Cuba. Subject on several occasions received military instructions for periods of one to two months, but source is not able to say the exact nature of these courses or where the Subject received the training. Subject has not traveled outside Cuba, and has no close relatives outside Cuba.

2. Physical Description: DFOB: circa 1941, Santa Clara (probably), Las Villas Province. Race: white. 5'7", 130 lbs. Hair: blonde. Eyes: light. Shape of face: oval. Physique: slim.
No visible scars.

3. MAVEZ Traces: None.
