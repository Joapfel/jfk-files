She complained that L/3 kept her in the dark about many things and said she could not understand his willingness to risk his life and the happiness of his family "so precipitously". Then Rowton questioned her discreetly as to L/3's trip to Tapachula, she stated in uncontrollable sobs ( they appeared to Rowton quite genuine) that he suavenyl left the preceding week, saying he was going to Chiapas and disappeared for 2-4 days. She said she was so upset she could not remember anything but the feeling of abandonment. She then complained that although Rowton was the only person she could turn to in a crisis like the one she went through last week, she could not locate him anywhere. She called his telephone ( answering service) repeatedly and then in desperation called LIRING/7 in the home that he would know something ( L/7 implied that he did not know "which Harris" she was referring to). LIRING/4 then stated that she realized suavenyl that if L/3 were to disappear she would be subject to deportation to Cuba, a fate which she apparently genuinely dreaded. Rowton assured her that he would do everything possible to see that she was taken care of in this eventuality, but she protested that L/3 was the only person she could hope to find happiness with and Rowton could not bring him back if anything happened to him.

14. Rowton then stated that before L/3 could take such a trip with serious risks involved, he would have to study the situation thoroughly. He explained that at the moment he had serious doubts regarding the contacts that have brought L/3 to this point. L/4 replied that she herself had seen the latest contact ("Valentin") only once when he came to the house and gave her the message regarding the wounded person. When Rowton queried her again concerning the trip to Tapachula, she stated that L/3 had told her upon his return that he had asked someone else to make daily calls to Rowton's answering service, and upbraided her for calling. ( This would explain what appeared to be L/3's daily calls during the period 19-21 July, but it would not explain L/3's conversations with Berta Vigisser and Sergio Valdez on 20/7/67 and his remark to Berta Vigisser on 21/7/67 that he "did not go out of town after all".)

15. Rowton felt that, although his meeting with L/4 was not particularly productive, it served its purpose in advising L/3 indirectly that he did not accept L/3's story of the trip to Tapachula and his initial contact with "Valentín".
