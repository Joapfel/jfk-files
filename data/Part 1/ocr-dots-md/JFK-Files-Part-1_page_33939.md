3. Miller hopes that no decision has been made to put Admiral Kirk and his Committee for the liberation of the Russian People under Crusade for Freedom cover. He thinks this would be a great mistake because it would further stretch the already too thin cover of the Crusade and no further safe say if it has to be done, so cannot agree that the Crusade effort should be lessened according to the agreement with the State Department.

## Action

I understand that subsequently Admiral Miller talked to Mr. Bullos who made it clear that no decision had been made to put Admiral Kirk and his Committee under the Crusade but that we here in CIA would much appreciate it if H.Q. could see its way clear to doing so. Admiral Miller agreed that he would take the matter up with the Board of Directors and if they were favorably inclined, CIA would take up with State the possibility of arranging to some extent the decision to cut down on the Crusade in order to try to furnish better cover for this operation.

h. Admiral Miller said that Landowsky and Ranft were still in Greece, that the situation was impossible and that one of them must go. He said that his advice from Bill Lang was that the H.Q. man was the better of the two and therefore it should be Landowsky who must go.

## Action

The history of this problem is involved and long. Landowsky has been called home, however, and will not go back to Greece so that it no longer remains a problem. I have told Admiral Miller.

5. Admiral Miller has been talking to the Ford Foundation on the subject of theildebrand operation in Berlin. The board had taken a dim view on the accounting, a matter which he straightened out by promising better ones. Ford raised the question of whether or not HQ. wanted Ford to spend the additional $50,000 pledged after the payments of the present $50,000 eight months from now. C. D. Jackson was present at this meeting and told Ford he did not feel the operation was worth an additional $50,000. Miller reserved judgment, however. He wanted to know whether CIA wished him to hold Ford to its pledge of an additional $50,000 or not.

## Action

I have checked this with the E. Division, which has cabled the field and come to the view that this operation is still very important. They want to urge Admiral Miller to hold Ford to its agreement. I passed this on to Admiral Miller.
