G E R Y

June 17, 1950

Dear Doctor,

For your benefit given in your call of yesterday. I found when I got here confirmation of our room at Hotel de la Courville, 14, rue de la Courville, scheduled for July 15th. (The rue de la Courville is a short street off Ave. Francolies 1er.) We shall accept to see you there and then.

I enclose from yesterday's H-T an item that you may not have seen. Our man seems to be doing just right so far.

I had last night a lengthy telephone call from Phillipa Phillips (the editor of Partisan Review who was in Paris last June, and who was concerned in the plan for a French edition). Then Ms. Léon was here, Phillips was ill; and Ms. Léon invited Phillips (his co-editor) to go to Berlin, more or less as representative of Partisan Review. But later, turned the invitation down. An official introduction was never given to Phillips, but he had been reproached with information. I believe: Last night Phillips was concerned both with Berlin and (especially) with the French magazine, which would have a special relation to P.R. (He made a few points in confidence, about whom it issupposed to be concerned in this magazine - and I, for one, am not altogether easy in my mind on the matter.)

In any case, Phillips thinks that if a special PR connection is thought of, he probably ought to be involved in it. Specifically, if the French magazine affair is going ahead, she would like to be in Paris; and we could also place her in Berlin. He certainly thought that if there were officials invited to Berlin, then she could attend to Paris on the same day; and he suggested that if something were planned on, I could also work on it when in Paris in July.

I bring this to the attention of you and your friends: (I told Phillips that I would see in Berlin as soon as I got there how things stood. He said that he would be ready to leave at once if he were invited.)
