1. A pact has been completed between leaders of the Partido Socialista Popular (PSP, Cuban Communist Party) and certain leaders of the Ortodoxo Party for the purpose of combatting the Estrella regime. Other meetings were held in the law offices of Communist lawyers, located in the Plaza de Gómez, Hatoa, and in the locales of radio stations 8.0.C.0., Radio Progreso, and Union Radio. The slogan for all these meetings was "CONTRA ESTISTA."

2. Behind this pact in the desire of the Ortodoxo leaders involved to seize control of the leadership of the Ortodoxo Party and realization by the PSP that it needs to stir up its edifics with some strong action and to prove to the Cuban people that its charges against president-elect Fulgencio ETISTA have been legitimate ones. The pact calls for a two-phase attack on the Government, the first phase being the propaganda campaign against the proposed "Via Cuba" canal, to convince the masses that ETISTA is a traitor, and the second phase being one of apperceptive action. The latter action would have as its objective the saturation of the national economy and would be accomplished, mainly, by influence with the sugar harvesting (safra) and the shipping activities. Included in this action are setting fire to sugar plantations on a large scale, agitation among the sugar workers over salary and dimensional questions, a "forteo caicos" (eilt-coun strike) movement in the port workers sector over the question of bulk shipment of sugar, which would favor the way for the union of the port workers and sugar workers, and the intensification of strikes and the support of strikers' demands in all industries. The final step would be the launching of another propaganda campaign, begun by members of student, industrial workers, and professional men's organizations, prior to the assumption of the presidency by ETISTA.
