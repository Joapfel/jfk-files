LUSTRAN REPORT #231, Kudus

25 Jan 62

Subj: Contacts 71-74 Jembran.

1. Some Michel: Saw six four times at Hotel Aletti, discussed mostly "Atlas". As he to prepare six color covers in advance, first one to illustrate ancient Corbusier plan for face-lifting of Algeria which is now planned by Algerian Government. Claims African Revolution will be "absorbed" by Atlas (probably both under same editorship). Roberto told: phoned Josie Fagon from Tunis, he will be in Rome Saturday, will send ticket and visa for Congo from there. I am to leave soon as possible.

2. Louis Aletti, about sixty years, 220 pounds, 6'2", white hair, blue eyes, French, worked in State several years in 1961, speaks English well, friend of most diploma in city includes Chinese, is accepted by Algérians as being anti-OAS in spirit. He claims the East German movement has full recognition by the Algérians, cited Benito Juárez's attendance at national holiday of East Germans, though Scharfenberg is only Canadian Ambassador. Gave me Chinese a. assad address as 35 Rue Luciani, El Biar, phone 5-9-4F. Will have to come with East Germans whenever I wish.

3. Talb Janai, Hotel Aletti & the city. Janai had difficulty with reporters of Republique Algerie: nd -as in rare over "stupid communists". Again claimed Irving Brown was CIA asset in front of a French radio speakerine.

4. Dr. Nadi Jafary, Janai Hissi, Brahan Bouzar, at my home and at their homes.
Kothim of later at conversation.

✓5. Fru Belkam, police commissioner, plus two of his friends, at Rora. Nothing of interest except the three of them very idealistic, insisting everyone must work to maximum for minimum pay in order build Algeria. Seems to be typical attitude among young Africans.

✓6. Zerati, police commissioner, Le Feuilles for lunch. Extremely pro-American, talked of all his OIS friends from Morocco at Ben Buerir while he worked in Marrakech. He should be contacted by someone under official cover as being sent by his old OIS friends or some such play. He can be developed.

✓7. Abdulkader Mawery (phonetic), Secretry General of the Assembly, my home. Nothing of interest, but he is quite likeable and certainly of importance. Will see him again rather frequently.
