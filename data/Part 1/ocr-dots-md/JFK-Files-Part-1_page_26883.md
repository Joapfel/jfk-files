# SECRET

# 72514

## 28 April 1965

Subject: Iden A

Subject was interviewed and tested on 28 April 1965 at a safe site in the Miami area at the request of Edward W. LORENTZ. The interview was conducted in English.

## RESULTS

Subject has never taken part in any communist activity and he has never worked for any Castro intelligence or security service. Apart from PRIME, he has no connection or agreement with the government of any other country.

Unless otherwise noted below, Subject is not obligated to any Cuban exile group.

Subject has been essentially truthful in regard to his personal history and he does not feel he is liable to blackmail for any reason. Subject has never been involved in the use or sale of narcooperation.

## DETAILS

Subject is being recruited for use with WITHUSN. He has served there before. Like all of the men in the group who have been in the Iden-D, Subject assumes that the operation is sponsored by KUBARKA.

Subject joined AMICHAL but was never called to go to the training camp.

In connection with blackmail, Subject mentioned that his father is in jail in Cuba. To date no attempts have been made by the Castro government to put any pressure on Subject.

Keith D. WINSTY
