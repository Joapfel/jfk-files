SUBJECT: Telephone Conversation between AMBIDDY-1 and Martin Ferrer

DATE : 9 April 1964

1. A-l will obtain the address of the Tampa barge today and call our office. A-l is departing for Nicaragua on 10 April via Lamica at 0730.

2. Manuel Hernandez has gone to Panama and should have returned to Costa Rica by now. A-l will report on Hernandez' findings upon his arrival in Costa Rica.

3. A-l told not to forget the budget before his departure. The quarterly charter payment to Talaria for the C-47 is due this month.

4. Petrosky will send a letter to Carrillo stating he would like to charter a C-47. Carrillo will then write back saying that he has a C-47 to sub-let. A-l hopes that this exchange between Carrillo and Petrosky will culminate in the C-47 being based in Panama.

5. The undersigned asked A-l if he had his Q signals to be used for the operation. A-l stated he does have them.

6. A-l was requested to send the list of those expelled.

7. A-l should make certain that the Nicaraguan Consulate in New York is alerted to the heavy equipment shipment and the Consular stamped invoice.

8. After signing the Libermeer contract General Somosa should mail the contract to the Libermeer people.

9. A-l was advised that someone should now be working on the registration of the DOLORES.

10. We would appreciate A-l following up on his visa while in Nicaragua.

11. The following was passed to A-l concerning the village radio: Will arrive in Managua at 14:25 hours on 10 April, Pan Am flight no.2501. Air Bill number is Pan Am 026-4-674334. Consists of 27 packages, weighing 2,014. Has been prepaid $603.00. Consignee: Consolidated Electrical Equipment Co. It is identified as transceivers and batteries. No.20763 appears on each case along with the address.
