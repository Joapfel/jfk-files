PRIORITY WH/MIAMI INFO DIRECTOR GEORGETOWN
REF CARACAS 3859 (IN 70765)

1. CIWOOD INTELLIGENCE SECTION CHIEF F. HAS ASKED NIKOLAKO P. HART TO BE ASSIGNED IF THERE IS AN ARREST ORDER BY JAMES HART IN MIAMI AND, IF SO, FOR WHAT REASON? ON QUESTIONING, HE ADVISED HART THAT HAYCOCK MAY LEFT FOR MIAMI FROM NATURIN HIGHT OF 21 OCTOBER. WE ADVISING CIWOOD WE HAVE NO INFORMATION ABOUT AN ARREST ORDER SINCE HART APPARENTLY HAS BROKEN NO U.S. LAW BUT, DUE HIS KNOWN INVOLVEMENT IN JANUARY KUPUNURI INCIDENT, POSSIBLE FBI OR CUSTOMS MAY WISH QUESTION HIM IN MIAMI. REQUEST WH/MIAMI AGENCY ASK IF ARREST ORDER DOES EXIST OR IF ANY GOVERNMENT AGENCY PLANS TO INTERVIEW.

2. CIWOOD QUERY AND KNOWLEDGE HART'S ACTIVITIES STRONGLY INDICATES HE COOPERATING WITH CIWOOD. REF INFORMATION INDICATES HE IS BEING INVESTIGATED BY CIVISTA-5 GROUP WHO HAS SENT AGENCY-15 TO MIAMI TO INVESTIGATE ACTIVITIES OF RICHARD HART. COORDINATION BETWEEN VENEZUELAN SERVICES BEING WHAT IT IS, SITUATION LIKE THIS IS ENTIRELY POSSIBLE.

SECRET

23 OCT 1969
