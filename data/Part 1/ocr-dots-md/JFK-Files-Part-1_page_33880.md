# Bunu Mekan Tiyatrisine Fora Geri Parla

## Problem

To clarify the relationship of NIE to CIA and the respective responsibilities and authorities of each for the conduct of NIE operations.

## Background

There is no currently effective clear statement on to the respective responsibilities and authorities of NIE and CIA for the conduct of operations. This has resulted in a number of misunderstandings and problems with respect to the administration of this project by CIA.

## Discussion

In order to define the respective responsibilities and authority of NIE and CIA, it is proposed that an agreement be entered into between NIE and CIA which will in effect provide for:

a. Policy, program, budgetary, security control, and veto power by CIA,

b. Subject to such general controls direct administrative and operational control over its operations by NIE,

c. The working out of machinery for submitting annual operating programs and supporting budgets by NIE for a period by CIA (and major subsequent additions thereto),

d. Adequate progress, financial, and other reports by NIE to CIA,

An outline of the principal provisions of such an agreement is set forth in Attachment A.

It is the consensus of all concerned in CIA that something like the above is essential. There is one technical point of difference, however, between the DOD division and the Administrative Staff Special. This is the matter of a CIA regulation (Revisions for All Staff and Divisional Chiefs)
