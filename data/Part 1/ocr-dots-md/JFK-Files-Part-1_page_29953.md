MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Personnel

SUBJECT: Fitness Report - Mr. David E. Murphy

1. This memorandum In In lieu of the Fitness Report firs.
It covers Mr. Murphy's performance in the EE Division, Charles-
tine Services from 27 May 1960 to 31 March 1962.

2. Mr. Murphy has entered upon his new assignment as Chief
of the EE Division with vigor. There is every reason to believe
that Mr. Murphy will manage the EE Division in the same outstanding
manner with which he won the ungrudging respect of the entire com-
munity as the leader of American intelligence in that area. Be
continues to perform with the same competence as was reflected in
his previous Fitness Report.

Richard Holme
Deputy Director (Plants)
