1. Forwarded herewith is a Memorandum for the record concerning Paris/LCPILOT's investigation of the security of QREBONY/1's &/N communications system following an episode involving an 11 October 1965 message received from him by LCPILOT.

2. We request Headquarters' guidance concerning this situation, with special reference to Paragraph 5 of the attached Memorandum, in order that we may take all appropriate steps to resolve the problem of maintaining secure communications with QREBONY/1, especially in view of the plans to send QREBONY/1 on an extended visit to Cuba commencing sometime in mid-December 1965.

Harvey G. FARRIGAN
