Dear Sir,

Fellows, up our talk of last event or, I believe I am now in a position to allow you in your absence to comment International community in the Panama area. During the past few months, I could not but think of your experience with the French in England after you were that son, and of how you as a tourist have a personal interest in letting people in Panama know that they must be done to keep the Western world free.

Unfortunately, Radio Free Europe, in the very nature of its charter, cannot ensure it satisfying within this framework. But in the course of our interview, I have been approached by a group of persons, none of whom an international relations specialist, who would like to determine an officialliaison between films, which would like to invest itself in our neighbourhood free of the communist means.

I am, therefore, writing to this group to ask them if they can lend me the use of 1500.00 per month for an indefinite period, which I hope you will grant until arrangements of cartels are concluded. Effective in explanation given at the time of payment of fees. In the mean time. I wish also to run by a local organization which would act in my name.

There is one way when a local organization shall not get credit. It is through agreement, but, of course, if need arises there will be no objection to your explaining the source of the funds.

In order to selling it as above, the group will appoint receiving an individual accounting including station accounts, some lengths, trans. etc., at their own expense, and salaries of my staff.

Please let me understand, then, that I am not involved in any matter deriving by me individually, and not in behalf of Radio Free Europe with which I am affiliated.

By the way, I am sure you will agree with me. He has written to me several times.

Let me hear whether you will be able to take up his offer.

Your,
