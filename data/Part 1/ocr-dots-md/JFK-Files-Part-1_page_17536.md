### III. Planning for the Future

Following the suggestion provided by Headquarters in renewing Project LIFMAT for FY 1961 (FY and project year coincide), the Station is expending every effort to maintain LIFMAT as a secure, compartmented, unilateral operation, even though on the presently reduced scale, so as to have readily available for service this flexible support asset with its proven capacity for coverage of almost any target in Mexico City. Also in line with Headquarters suggestion LIFMAT will attempt to increase its coverage of those individuals suspected of maintaining clandestine contact with the AIS.

In the future, the unilateral LIFMAT operation, with the removal from its responsibility the coverage of the Soviet and Czech lines, will tend to be more flexible, with greater percentage of its capacity available for shifting to new targets as they are presented, and able to adjust readily to new situations. It is noted that this may result in a lower quantity of positive intelligence production, but will increase the CI/CE production which has always been basic to the LIFMAT project.

### IV. Performance of Agents

The overall performance of all agents employed in the LIFMAT complex continued to be good during the period of the project review, with security being stressed in all phases of the operation. No security problems were encountered which were not solved by the personnel involved. Since the success of the LIFMAT operation demands largely use the security of its personnel, Headquarters clearances and evaluation of new personnel will continue to be sought on those new additions who pass a strict field assessment.

### V. Expenditures

Approval for renewal of Project LIFMAT was granted to August 1960 for the FY 1961 in the amount of $88,501.00. The proposed budget for FY 1962 is broken down in the attached Field Project Outline, Part II, and totals $98,973.00.
