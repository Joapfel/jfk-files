Dear Bank,

Your detailed letter arrived in yesterday's mail. However, in order to process it I also need all receipts relative to those expenses. This would include all possible ticket stubs for all transportation, car rentals, hotel bills, doctor bills, telephone charges, and other supporting receipts.

The balance of your fees will be deposited after this accounting has been reconciled.

In the future, in any other communications directed to me, please use your true name in the return address. Continue to sign accounts directed to me as Reichborn.

Thanking you for your cooperation and hoping to hear from you soon, I remain,
