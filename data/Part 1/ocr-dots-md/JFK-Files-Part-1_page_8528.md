11 January 1968

MEMORANDUM FOR THE FILE

SUBJECT: Visit of Unidentified Woman to Soviet Embassy grounds

1. LICALLA reported that at 1203 hours on Tuesday 9 January 1968, an unidentified woman, described by LICALLA as a "Northamerican type" entered the Soviet Embassy. This woman entered the receiving room and came out into the courtyard of the Soviet Embassy grounds at 1206 hours. While in the garden (courtyard) she spoke to Yuriy S. CHERNYSH (P-13042), Vladislav Sergeyovich KORMUSHKIN (P-11707) and to Vladimir Agafonovich DOLGOV (P-9774). From 1215 hours until 1226 hours, when she again entered the reception room in the company of Valentin S. LOGINOV (P-11712), she spoke in the courtyard to DOLGOV. LICALLA reported that this unidentified woman departed the Soviet Embassy at 1252 hours.

2. Attached for Vladimir A. DOLGUYa (P-9774) file are LICALLA photos of this woman taken with DOLGOV. (FOR WOFIRM USE ONLY).

Grace H. FARMILANT
