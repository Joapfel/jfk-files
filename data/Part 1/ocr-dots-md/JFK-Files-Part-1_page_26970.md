The Need for MARTOC

THE POSSIBILITIES of MARTA is both good and bad: tremendous. On its good side the area needs a rapid and economical transportation system. The area also needs an construction project.

When MARTA was established it was apparent that the need it would bring was balanced by the tremendous possibilities for passenger and featherbedding. So a committee was established by the General Assembly to oversee MARTA and it was called MARTOC.

The area needs MARTA but it now needs MARTOC as never before.
