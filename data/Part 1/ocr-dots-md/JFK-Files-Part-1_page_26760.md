After 1325, 7 May

6.2 In. VKG (v) asks waid, who answers, for Sra. (Inge Kico). Ik takes phone. VKG is going to have lunch with Miric. All right. Will he be back early? Yes. Has anyone called? No. Did VKG take "Las 20 lineas"? No, will take them (later).

6.5 In. Sra. Kico (v) with Sra. Diener, who answers, for Inge Kico.

Ik takes phone, VKG is not at home. Sra. Kico called this morning, but hung up on her...Everyone is fine...Invites Sra. K. tomorrow...

6.7 In. NO asks Sra. Diener (v), who answers, for VKG. Not there. Won't be home for lunch.

After 1515, 7 May

9.0 Out to 20-07-77. aid asks NO. who answers, for Jose(fina). Josefine (aid) comes to phone...Greetings..how each in... Asks about whether Kona has come to town...

10.7 In. NO with Inge Kico (v), who answers, for Casta (aid). Casta takes the phone. About whether she is angry...seeing one another...

After 1715, 7 May

11.0 In. VKG (v) with Inge Kico (v), who answers. VKGis with Jovan (Miric) at a restaurant. Julian will go by there at 1730. VKG will go there (now) now, but may be late. Keep Julieu there - wait for him. Fine.

11.2 In. VKG (v) with Sra. Diener (v), who answers, for Quiche (pa (nickname for Inge kico).

Ik takes the phone. VKG is coming home now. This young man (Julian) is now here. Yes, but there are no taxis...Just as soon as he gets a cab, he will be home. In thinks he is not going to be able to wait. Tell him to wait on VKG...Julian says for him to come right away because Julian has another engagement.

12.2 In. NO asks VKG (v), who answers, for his sister, Casta Hernandez.

Aid takes phone. No is Antonio...about visiting her... about family....NO is going home to visit...
