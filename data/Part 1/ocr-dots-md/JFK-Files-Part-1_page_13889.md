1. DEFECTED CUBAN INTEL OFFICER VLADIMIR RODRIGUEZ LANERE GIVEN PRELIMINARY DENIERING BY LANGOSCH NICHT 23 APRIL. FOLLOWING FROM RODRIGUEZ INDICATES SCOPE HIS KNOWLEDGE:

A. BACKGROUND: 11 YEARS FORMAL SCHOOLING. IN 1957 RESACED IN CAM AND PROP ACTIVITIES AGAINST BATISTA ON BEHALF 26 JULY MOVEMENT. AT LEAST JULY 57. PRISONER UNTIL FEB 50. ON RELEASE JOINED COLUMN OF CUBAN MATOS IN SIERRA MAESTRA. FOUGHT AS PRIVATE UNDER CAPTAIN BATISTA UNTIL BATISTA FELL. THEN MADE LIEUTENANT AND ASSIGNED TO BOARD CHIEF IN HAPANA INVESTIGATION DEPT TO CONTROL GAMBLING ACTIVITIES OUTSIDE CADARETS. NO 59 BECAME DEPUTY CHIEF POLICE DIRECT 10. JAN 61 WAS ASSIGNED NATIONAL POLICE HOS ON STAND-UP TROTTING. COLLECTED PAY BUT WAS NO ASSIGNED DUTIES UNTIL TRANSFER TO BATTALION. SEPT 61 SENT TO STUDY AT CASTILLO DEL NORCO.
B. STUDENT UNTIL DECEMBER 61 WHEN GIVEN HOLIDAY LEAVE. HE COMMITTED RESIGNATION FROM ARMY IN WHICH HELD RANK FIRST LIEUTENANT UNTIL HOLDING ABOVE JOBS. HE DID NOT REPORT BACK TO DUTY. WAS CERTIFIED AS DESERTER. THIS MADE REEMPLOYMENT DIFFICULT. AT GOT JOB, MINISTRY OF EXTERIOR CONFERED 3 APRIL 64 WORKING IN PLAYING AND MET TACTICAL SECTION OF DEPARTMENT CONCERNED WITH AUTOMOBILE IMPORTS.
C. AT LEAST GOT JOB IN INTEL THROUGH FRIENDSHIP WITH SAUL CONOLLY, CHIEF OF DEPUTY CHIEF OF ILLEGAL SECTION B OF GENERAL INTELLIGENCE BUREAU RATE. THIS SECTION TRAINS AGENTS FOR ASSIGNMENTS IN LATIN AMERICA.

D. 1 THE ASSOCIATION: RODRIGUEZ FROM AUGUST 63 UNTIL DEFECITION ENDED IN SECTION M OF DIRECCIO: GENERAL DE INTELLIGENCIA WHERE A DEEM OFFICER FOR SALVADORIAN OPS. HE SUPERVISED ARRANGEMENTS SETTING SALVADORANS PICKED BY CP SALV TO CUPA FOR GUERRILLA GROUP. AND THEN SELECTING THOSE WITH APTITUDE AS AGENTS WHO WOULD BE INITIATED AND RETURNED TO WORK WITHOUT KNOWLEDGE OF CP. INSIDE TELLY TO O OPS, BECAME FAMILIAR WITH AGENTS PICKED FOR OPS IN OTHER MANS. C. BIECCION GENERAL DE INTELLICENCIA (GCI) IS HEADED BY PINIER AND COLLECTS INTEL AWARD. COMPLETELY SEPARATE FROM ADMINISTRATION TO BE SECURID DEL ESTADO (DES) WHICH Headed by P.M.S. ALL COORDINANTS COUNTER INTEL OPS. BOTH REPORT TO RAMIRO VALDEZ WHO IS CHIEF OF INTERIOR AND SUPREME CHIEF OF INTEL AND SECURITY.
