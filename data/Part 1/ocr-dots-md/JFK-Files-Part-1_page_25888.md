REF: ZURICH 1629, 17 MAR 1969, ET. AL. (NOT NEEDED BONN FRANKFURT)

1. INDORED HAS REPORTED THAT RICHARD CASE NAGELL BORN 5 AUGUST 1930, NEW YORK, US CIT, HAS AGAIN COME TO SWISS ATTENTION. IN MID APRIL 1970 HE REGISTERED AT ZURICH HOTEL. ON 15 APRIL 1970 REPORTED TO POLICE THAT HIS RAINCOAT HAD DISAPPEARED. CLAIMED THAT AN IMPORTANT DOCUMENT INTENDED FOR THE AMERICAN MILITARY AUTHORITIES IN BERLIN WAS IN THE POCKET. DOCUMENT WAS ENTITLED "FISCAL YEAR 70 (USAREUR)."

2. RAINCOAT WAS FOUND SHORT WHILE LATER IN HOTEL SANS ALLEGED DOCUMENT. HOTEL TURNED RAINCOAT OVER TO ZURICH POLICE WHERE SUBJ SUBSEQUENTLY PICKED IT UP.

3. WHEN QUESTIONED BY POLICE, SUBJ GAVE AS REFERENCES:

A. COL. FRANCIS H. DILLON, OFFICE OF COUNTERINTELLIGENCER AND SECURITY, DIA, WASHINGTON.

B. FRANCIS X. PLANT, SPECIAL ASSISTANT, OFFICE OF UNDER SECRETARY OF ARMY.
