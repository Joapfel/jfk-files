1. EAST GERMANY SHIPS AT VERACRUZ.

The HEINRICH WELTZ (II photos enclosed) arrived as expected on February 19, (see referenced airgram, pgh. 1) direct from La Havre, and left for Jamaica February 21 in ballast. The ship had planned to take a Conviction passenger (Alexander John BLANCHARD, ppt & BE-602204, issued Toronto, 12/19/64; age: 35, professor of physics, Lecroy, 225 Glen Polar Drive, Toronto 13), but he disappeared at the last minute. Blanchard planned to return to Mexico City from Cuba via the CMI flight, and then come back down to Veracruz to get his wife Harry Ann, who was to remain here. The Mexican tourist cards of Mr. & Mrs. Blanchard expired February 12, 1967. The local agent for the East German line stated that, recently, the honorary Consul of East Germany (a rival shipping agent) has been going aboard each East German ship to offer all possible assistance; on looking into this, it said the agent, he discovered that West Germany's representatives throughout the world, wherever East Germany is not represented, have been ordered to act discretely in represent-ation of East German interests as well as West German interests.
