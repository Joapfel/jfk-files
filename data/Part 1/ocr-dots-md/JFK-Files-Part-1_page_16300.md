SOURCE AND APPRAISAL AN OFFICIAL BRITISH SERVICE FROM A SOURCE DESCRIBED AS "WELL KNOWN, EXPERIENCED IN SECRET AND RELIABLE, FROM ALISTAIR WICKS."

- 1. MAJOR MIKE HOARE, COMMANDER OF 5 COMMANDO, CONGOOLE SE GOVT MERCENARY FORCE, TOGETHER WITH HIS SECOND-IN-COMMAND ALISTAIR WICKS, RETURNED TO STANLEYVILLE AREA OF CONGO TO RESUME OPERATIONS DURING THE THIRD WEEK OF JANUARY. THEIR RETURN FOLLOWED FURTHER DIRECT NEGOTIATIONS WITH PRESIDENT TSHOMBE DURING WHICH TSHOMBE ACCEPTED RECOMMENDATIONS PUT TO HIM IN STRONG TERMS BY HOARE AND ASSURED HOARE OF HIS CONFIDENCE IN HIM.

2. THE MERCENARY FORCE LEADERS WERE CONFIDENT THEY COULD NO LONGER WITH THE NORTHERN CONGO SITUATION PROVIDED THE 396 ADDITIONAL MERCENARIES, WHOSE ENGAGEMENT TSHOME HAS ACCEPTED, ARE BROUGHT INTO THE COUNTRY TO COME UNDER THE PROTECTION OF THE KINGDOM OF KINGDOM OF MOROCCO FROM THE DAY OF ARRIVAL IN BISSAU.

This special operation information being sent to the Red Cross by order of the President of the Republic of the Congo, Tshome, for use only in connection with the conservation and assistance of which it is known to be required, shall not be published by any means whatever.
