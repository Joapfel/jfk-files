23 April 1948

MEMORANDUM :

TO : John F. Hook
Chief, Admin. Section

THRU : Chief of Mission
Shanghai, China

INFO : Robert A. Koko
Chief, PI

1. It is requested that I be granted TOY duty in the United States for a period of approximately 60 days starting on or about 1 June 1948.

2. It is my understanding that in June 1918 the 18 months contract which I signed before leaving the United States in December 1945 will have expired. If this is the case I would like to take advantage of the expiration of the contract. However, even if, in June 1948, my contract has not expired, I request that I be returned to the United States on TOY duty.

3. It is further request that my wife, Nell Stine Miller, be allowed to accompany me and that we be permitted to fly to the United States.

4. Mrs. Miller's contract with the organization expired 30 November 1947 but she has remained in China since that time and she desires to return to China with me and continue working with the organization.

FORMED FOR APPROVAL : SECRET
