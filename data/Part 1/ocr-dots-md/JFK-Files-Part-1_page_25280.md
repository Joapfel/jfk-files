## 2. IICHR (Mexican-Russian Institute for Cultural Interchange)

a. KOLCHAKOV contacted the IICHR on 13 September and told Tomas CHAVEZ Morado that it would not be necessary for the Institute to send over an operator and projector as they had been able to obtain a 35mm copy of the films they were going to show.

b. On 25 September the president of the Sociedad de Guerreroense contacted the Soviet Embassy and requested some motion pictures to take on a cultural tour through his state of Guerrero. He was referred to the IICHR as the films were not kept at the Embassy..
