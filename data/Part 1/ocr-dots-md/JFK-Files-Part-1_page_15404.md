Dear Mr. McCone:

I am one of the Cuban pilots survivor of the Bay of Pigs ill-fated invasion to Cuba. I flew several missions and talked with the four American pilots who died in the morning of April 19th 1961 while flying as volunteers for our cause.

Contrary to the general opinion that the CIA was to lynch for that fiasco, I wish to express to you my admiration and gratitude for the excellent instruction received while training in Central America and for those who gave up their lives for the cause of Democracy. A wrong and regrettable decision by the Administration turned a positive victory into a harmful defeat for the Cubans and the United States.

I understand that certain organizations are receiving direct help from your Agency. Request to inform you that most of them are underfy of such help and not so long ago, their leaders were astro's followers.

Would like the opportunity of discussing this matter personally with you for the welfare of both our countries.

Please accept the testimony of my highest consideration.

Cajt. Edouard J. Whitehouse
