A-5 FOLLOWED THE SCENARIO NO PREVIOUSLY AGREED UPON IN MIAMI. HE CLEARLY EXPLAINED THAT CIRCUMSTANCES IN MIAMI AND FOR HIM PERSONALLY WERE MUCH DIFFERENT THAN WHEN THEY HAD MET IN SPAIN SEVERAL YEARS AGO. HE SAID THAT HE AND ALL HIS FRIENDS HAD BEEN TERMINATED BY BLACK, THAT HIS MOTHER WAS NOW DEAD AND THAT HE HAD PERSONALLY LOST HOPE THAT CUBA COULD BE LIBERATED. HE POINTED OUT HOWEVER THAT HE STILL OPPOSED FIDEL CASTRO AND NOW MORELY WANTED TO HELP NEGOTIATE THE RELEASE FROM PRISON OF HIS FRIENDS. GUILLERMO WAS NOT SURPRISED OR DISTURBED BY THIS APPROACH. GUILLERMO COUNTERED WITH THE STATEMENT THAT THE CUBAN REVOLUTION HAD SUCCEEDED AND THE ENTIRE MATTER OF RETAINING PRISONERS WAS UNDER CONSIDERATION IN HAVAN ANYWAY. AS CONVERSATION CONTINUED GUILLERMO SAID THAT WHILE HE NATURALLY WOULD HAVE TO DISCUSS THE OFFER OF COOPERATION OF A-5 WITH AMGHOST-I HE FELT QUITE CERTAIN THAT THE RELEASE OF FERNANDO DE ROSAS PENICHE, A RELATIVE OF A-5'S COULD BE ARRANGED EITHER IMMEDIATELY BEFORE OR SHORTLY AFTER CHRISTMAS, BUT THAT THE RELEASE OF PERSONS SUCH AS COMANDANTE GUIN WAS NOT CONVENIENT AT THIS TIME. TO THIS AGAIN KEEPING WITH THE PREARRANGED SCENARIO A-5 SAID THAT FIDEL WAS AN INGRATE SINCE GUIN HAD GIVEN FIDEL MUCH HELP IN MEXICO PRIOR TO THE INITIATION OF ACTION AGAINST BATISTA. GUILLERMO REPEATELY ERISTLED WHEN FIDEL WAS ATTACKED. ONLY WHEN FIDEL WAS ATTACKED DID THE MEETING TAKE ON A LESS THAN FRIENILY TIME.
