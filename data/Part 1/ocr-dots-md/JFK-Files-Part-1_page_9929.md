Subject: Wallace B. Rowton Meeting with LIRING/3.

1. Rowton met with LIRING/3 at 1700 on 31 July, 1967, at the safehouse. L/3 reported at once that he had moved his family into the safehouse and that L/4 and their child were out at the moment so that he and Rowton could talk privately. He explained that things were moving very rapidly and that Miosés Crozco Leal was in fact staying at his apartment (Tapachula) in preparation for the trip they were to take to Tapachula. He stated that he had met with Crozco as planned at 1200 on 29 July and, after a lengthy talk, Crozco had asked to be put up in L/3's apartment for the night, since it was late and it would be better in view of the trip that they planned to take together. L/3 commented that Crozco appeared quite nrevous and was particularly concerned about answering the door in the event someone knocked while L/3 was away. It was agreed that he would answer the door only in the event that there were three knocks, indicating that the person was LIRING/3 himself. According to L/3 Crozco stated that he had at all costs to get to Tapachula within the next two or three days to meet FAR contacts that were expecting him there. He expressed fear of traveling by plane or even train and welcomed the suggestion of L/3 that the latter's friend Raúl Moreno drive him and L/3 to Tapachula in Moreno's car. L/3 commented to Rowton that he was amazed at the knowledge Crozco had concerning him and his activities, including the dates of his (L/3's) trips to Guatemala and Cuba.

2. Rowton then realizing that L/3 had probably walked into a dangerous trap, queried him very carefully concerning the origin of his contact with Crozco. Rowton explained that he was certain that L/3's story of the trip to Tapachula and the meeting with the Guatemalan woman there was untrue and that he assumed that L/3 had a good reason for distorting the truth. Rowton continued that he had come to the conclusion that L/3 had had a domestic crisis again and had decided to disappear for a few days and had told his wife that he had to go out of town on business for Rowton. He was therefore forced to lie to Rowton also for fear that Rowt a might disclose the truth to L/4. Rowton stressed that it was essential that he know the true facts surrounding the first contact with Crozco, since, as L/3 would see later, it might be a matter of life and death for him.
