AB Nemo No. 9568
12 April 1968

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, MIA/Argentina

ATTENTION : Mr. Robert Fumbrini

SUBJECT : Transmittal of Nicaraguan Ordinary Passport in Alias for Use by AMSTALK-1 - AB Case No. 8037

REFERENCE : Will Memorandum dated 5 April 1968, same subject

1. Transmitted herewith for use as outlined in Reference are the following two documents issued in alias for AMSTALK-1: Nicaraguan Ordinary Passport No. S07S1 and U.S. Immigration and Naturalization Form I-9A (stapled in the passport, page 9). Also furnished herewith is a travel itinerary on which AMSTALK-1 should be briefed before he applies for the Argentine visa in New York.

2. In keeping with arrangements made by TSD/AB, the Medical Office is preparing, for direct delivery to W/4, the International Vaccination Certificate in alias to be used with the above documents. This certificate should be checked for accuracy on to name, place and date of issue. It is also understood that AMSTALK-1 will have received a base place smallpox vaccination prior to departing from the U.S.

3. TSD/AB prepared the above Nicaraguan passport on the strength of information that BIOGENESIS would instruct an Argentine Consular post in the U.S. to place a visa in the passport. The document was prepared in one week from date of request because of operational urgency. The requirement specified use of a Central American passport. To avoid placing a long, time-consuming simulated itinerary in the passport, it was given a recent issuance date, which necessitated issuance on the basis of exemplars issued nearly one year earlier (the most recent ones available). This short itinerary also would seem to make application at this time in the U.S. for an Argentine visa somewhat unusual. Since we are now informed that AMSTALK-1 is to obtain his Argentine visa without BIOGENESIS assistance we wish to make the limitations of the documentation provided for this operation, and agreed to by Mr. Fumbrini, a matter of record. This confirms discussion with Mr. Fumbrini. A telephone check with the Argentine Embassy by WII indicated that a Nicaraguan transient in the U.S. can obtain an Argentine visa in this country.
