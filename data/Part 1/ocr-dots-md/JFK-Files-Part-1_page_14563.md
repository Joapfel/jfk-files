TRAVEL : Following are the trips that Guillermo BUIZ made from October 1969 to February 1970:

To Havana to settle a "personal problem" on 20 October 1969 by Iberia ( first class), returning to Madrid on 29 October.

To London on 14 November 1969 for consultations with Raúl LEON Torras, returning two days later by air ( also first class).

To Bilbao, San Sebastian, and Burgos, by car, on 28 November 1969, returning two days later.

To Italy from Barcelona with his wife and two children on 29 December 1969. He went to pick up an Alfa Romeo, which he paid out of his own funds. They returned through Florence, Genoa, San Remo, and Barcelona, arriving in Madrid on 6 January 1970.
