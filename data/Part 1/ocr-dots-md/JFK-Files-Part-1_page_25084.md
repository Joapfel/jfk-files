On 22 Sep 1965 LIEUVOY reported that "Modesto BERMUDEZ, panamáno" called from inside the Soviet Embassy at 1128 hours to a local travel agency (T.( 1./Greyhound) and spoke with Luis LAESTIDA, asking her about the price of the ticket to Prague under the same system they had used to send the other people. LAESTIDA asked for one of the names in order to pull the card to see how it had been done, and "BERMUDEZ" gave her the name Guillermo AGUIRRE. LAESTIDA gave him various pos- sible flights and asked that he call back in the afternoon for more definite information. (Station traces show that Guillermo AGUIRRE, Rodriguez, 201-245711, is a Nicaraguan who was at the CPBU school in Moscow in 1965.) (LILYMIC reported that the man in the attached photograph, who is undoubtedly identical with Alejandro BERMUDEZ Alegria, visited the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City from 11:11 to 11:57 hours. There therefore seems no question that he is the "Modesto BERMUDEZ, panamáno" who has been reported by LIEUVOY in all the above conversations.)
