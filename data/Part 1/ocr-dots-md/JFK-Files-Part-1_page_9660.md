Convert to Markdown2. Regarding Lyle STUART, Dr. Baul OSEGUDA, Bartolome COSTA Amic, and Mario MONTEFORTE Toledo:

Dr. OSEGUDA apparently has had a secret logging to get something of his own printed in English. He seized upon the opportunity of commenting SKALS' introduction to Antikommunismo in order to write to STUART this spring.

STUART wrote very cordially back to OSEGURA, hoping to meet him on a trip to Mexico in June. When OSEGUDA confessed to me his correspondence with STUART, he sought to make me see it as an attempt to speak with STUART about the royalties STUART has never paid me.

Then Dr. OSEGURA finally did see STUART here in Mexico during the book congress in June, he only very tizidly and very, very briefly touched upon the royalty problems, and indeed went to a great deal of trouble to serve STUART by introducing us to OSEGURA, who - and also Mario MONTEFORTE Toledo. (STUART had expressed interest in getting in touch with some publishers and writers.) OSEGUDA hounded about, taking OSEGURA and MONTEFORTE Toledo to lunch with STUART, even managed to get the MONTEFORTE Toledos to invite STUART to dinner at their apartment. There were tentative discussions of collaborating in the distribution of COSTA Amic books in the United States and of publishing some book of MONTEFORTE Toledo's.

However, both COSTA Amic and the MONTEFORTE Toledos told me that they found STUART offensively vulgar and were not at all enthusiastic about having anything to do with him.

Two weeks ago, OSEGUDA sketched the outline of a book (contemporary history of U.S. and Latin American relations) he hopes STUART will want to publish, and sent the outline up to him.
