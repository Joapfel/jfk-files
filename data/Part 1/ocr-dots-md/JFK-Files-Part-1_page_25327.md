### C. Miscellaneous Commercial Activity:

Other transactions on which vague information has been received were:

1) **Dyes:** Confirmation received from Moscow on Letter of Credit 18 978 for Violet Dyes.

2) **Coffee:** BOLSHANGV ridiculed a newspaper story that a Latin American country (Colombia?), claimed the Soviet Union was offering dollars for that country's coffee.

3) **TV:** Vague information has been received to the effect that Soviet Export Film is interested in the TV market in Mexico.

Information on certain contacts is rather vague, but it was ascertained that the Soviet Commercial Office had discussions with the following in December 1955, the exact nature of which was not clear.

1) **CARCIN, Francisco**, a member of the "Comision de Vigilancia" (Part of Mexican Social Security).

2) **GARCIA Vidal, Raül**, was contacted by BOLSHANGV during the month of December 1955, with information that certain material was in readiness for GARCIA. It has not been ascertained exactly what GARCIA's relationship with the Soviet Commercial Office is.
