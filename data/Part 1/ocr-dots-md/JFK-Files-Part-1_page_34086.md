Matanzas Province: Malanga fields were covered with water which will rot most of the plant root. Complete loss of corn crops were observed in the Malanga area.

Las Villas Province: 'Viands' crops were destroyed.

Camaguey Province: 2% of the plantain, yucca and sweet potatoes crops were destroyed.

Oriente Province: In 'Mabay' 1,400 bags of sweet potatoes and 160 quintals of yucca were collected. All vegetable crops in the vicinity of Santiago de Cuba suffered serious damage.

## BEANS AND RICE

Pinar del Río Province: 15 'caballerías' of rice were damaged from the wind and the heavy rains in 'Patricio Lumumba' farm.

Havana Province: None.

Matanzas Province: None.

Las Villas Province: In the zone of Jatibonico to Sancti-Spiritus, serious damages in rice plantations. In El Megano, 8 'caballerías' of beans were destroyed.

Camaguey Province: 20% of beans crop were damaged.

Oriente Province: Rice fields near Yara had been leveled to the ground.
