MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: James P. O'Connell

1. In accordance with FR 20-9 b (2) this memorandum is written in lieu of submission of Form 45.

2. This memorandum has been attached to Okinawa Station since 24 July 1962. As Chief, Regional KUSOBA Staff, Okinawa, he has the responsibility of providing KUSOBA support to all FE and SEA Stations. Although as Chief of Station, Okinawa I do not have supervisory responsibility over the Regional KUSOBA Program, I am pleased to state that in my opinion he has carried out his work in a commendable manner.

3. In addition to carrying out his Regional responsibilities, [REDACTED] has been very helpful to Okinawa Station. He has established excellent rapport with the military and has highly assisted the Station in several delicate problems. In approaching any problem [REDACTED] is objective, tactful, and exercises good judgment.

4. It would be a pleasure to serve with him at any time.

/S/ George E. Meloon, COS

READ:

/S/ James P. O'Connell

Reviewer Comments: Subject has placed emphasis on his required responsibility as contrasted to Okinawa security problems. This emphasis plus his personality and professional reputation has enabled a definite contribution to the Regional Far East security function, in spite of the fact that there is an inherent tendency to bypass Okinawa on security matters since it is not in the command channel to all FE Stations.
