In the hospital NOSENKO decided he could not return to school as he was ashamed of the type of girls he had been with the night of the accident and could not stand the thought of being made an example of by the school KOMSO- MOL organization. He left the hospital in civilian clothes and stayed with a friend of his father. He obtained a state ment that he had attended the Naval Prep School, which was just a report of his courses and an average of his marks up to that time. As the son of a minister it was then easy for him to enter the Shipbuilding Tekh- nikum whefer, with special tutoring, he was able to complete the Tenth Class in the summer. (May)
