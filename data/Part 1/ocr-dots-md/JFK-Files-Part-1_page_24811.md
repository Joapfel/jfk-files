WH/6-509

15 February 1967

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, CI/OA

SUBJECT : Travel of Edward G. TICHBORN
(201-253203)

1. In accordance with your request for information on TICHBORN's foreign travel (CI/OA Memorandum dated 22 November 1966) the following is forwarded.

2. Edward G. TICHBORN left New York City for Mexico City on 9 January 1967, returning on 21 January. He travelled primarily on cover company business but extended his stay to consult with Station Mexico City on a possible research project of interest to the Station and Headquarters.

Paul E. Oberst
Chief, WH/6
