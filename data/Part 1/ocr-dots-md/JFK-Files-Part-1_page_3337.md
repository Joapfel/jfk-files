AMBOLED Meeting in Miami, 23 June 1964

SUBJECT: Photographs of Adelaida Padron

1. During a lull in the meeting I took AMBIDY-1 aside (as instructed) and told him it would be best for all concerned to take Adelaida out of PEPRIME as he had suggested. I mentioned to him there is a growing number of reports on Adelaida finding their way to Washington, making it increasingly difficult for us to protect his rear.

2. I then casually mentioned to him that we had received somelecene photographs which have caused us to do some fast talking with "some people in Washington" that are beginning to have some misgivings about AMBIDY-1 (actually this story is notional).

3. I noticed the remark concerning the photographs used AMBIDY-1. "Do you mean immoral pictures, Martin?" AMBIDY-1 inquired. I added and to avoid his asking further questions about the pictures, I reminded him of his promise that Lucia Cano, Hatacha d'Angelo (AMBIDY-1 denied having her) and Aida Perez Ponce would not follow Adelaida to Nicaragua.

4. During the rest of the meeting AMBIDY-1 appeared to be somewhat dejected and preoccupied. It is the opinion of the CO's attending the meeting that this was attributed to the remarks made concerning the existence of these photographs.

5. As the meeting was about to end, AMBIDY-1 asked so again, "Do you really have some immoral pictures of her (Adelaida), Kurtia?" Again I said "Yes." These photographs had come into our possession plus much more
