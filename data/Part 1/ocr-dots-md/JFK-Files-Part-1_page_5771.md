I. 3 Jan 72

A 21

P.C.

INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS

"The national liberation movements of the peoples of Asia, Africa and Latin America are continuing to surge forth vigorously. The revolutionary mass movements of the peoples of Europe, North America and Oceania are developing in depth. Countries want independence, nations want liberation and the people want revolution--this has become an irresistible historical trend. The truculent practice of power politics and hegemony by the superpowers has met with the strong resistance of an increasing number of small and medium countries; the superpowers are beset with internal and external difficulties and are having a tougher and tougher time."

The Chinese vice premier said: "In external affairs, our country has firmly carried out Chairman Mao's proletarian revolutionary line in foreign affairs and won great victories. The exchange of friendly visits between our people and the people of other countries, including the American people, is continuously expanding; and the number of countries which have established diplomatic relations with our country is ever increasing. The vain scheme of the superpowers to blockade and isolate China has gone totally bankrupt!"

He continued: "The domestic situation of our country is also excellent. Since the Ninth Congress and the Second Plenary Session of the Ninth Central Committee of our party, the whole party, the whole army and the whole nation, acting on our great leader Chairman Mao's instruction 'carry out education in ideology and political line', have unfolded a deep-going movement for criticizing revisionism and rectifying the style of work, further heightened their consciousness of class struggle, the struggle between the two lines and of continuing the revolution under the dictatorship of the proletariat, and have won great victories. The struggle-criticism-transformation campaign of the Great Proletarian Cultural Revolution is continuing to develop in depth. We have reaped bumer harvests for 10 years running. A new upsurge is emerging in industrial production. Prices are stable and the market [is] flourishing. The revolutionary spirit of our people is rising higher than ever. Our socialist motherland is prospering and thriving with each passing day."

Vice Premier Li Hsien-nien wished the experts success in their work and good health. In conclusion, he said: "Let us work together to better promote the mutual understanding and militant friendship between the Chinese people and the people of other countries and support and assist the world revolution!"

The banquet proceeded in a warm and friendly atmosphere. Hosts and guests talked cordially and proposed toasts to the daily consolidation and development of the revolutionary friendship and militant unity of the people of various countries.

Also attending the banquet were leading members of departments under the CCP Central Committee, Chinese Government departments, the Peking Municipal Revolutionary Committee, the Chinese People's Association for Friendship With Foreign Countries and other departments concerned Wang Liang-en, Wu Ching-tung, Ma Wen-po, Yang Ce-chung, Tu Wan-jung, Hsieh Hua, Chang Chi-chih, Liu Chien-kung, Chang Shih-chang, Lu Yung, Li Meng-fu, Liu Pu, Chiu Wei-kao, Liu Chuan-hsin, Wu Yin-hsien, Ting Hsi-lin, Li Ching-chuan, Wang Lei, Chu Kai-yin, Chu Chih-tung and Chiang Shou-li.
