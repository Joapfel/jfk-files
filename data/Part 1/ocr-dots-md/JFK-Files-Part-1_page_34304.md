Another Castroite affront to a Western European nation in which Calbe was instrumental!

Lastly, we wish to call your attention on the fact that the more decent and stable of Cuba's diplomatic representatives have shown that they cannot stem Castro's ruckery of diplomacy and international law.

As examples, we can cite the very recent defections of years, Lulio P. Alonso Ponsilván, Cuban Ambassador to Great Britain; Alberto Alcana-Vilar, Economic Counselor of the Cuban Embassy in Japan; José Ignacio Alveares de la Caridad, Economic Counselor of the Cuban Embassy in Spain; and Alberto Cabrera Infante, Economic Attaché of the Cuban Embassy in Spain.

Individually and collectively, the decent peoples of the world are disassociating themselves from the infamy of Castroian. Just now, the cream of Cuba's own population -professional men, businessmen, intelligent males, etc.- is about to join the second part of the greatest mass exodus recorded by History since World War II.

Let us assume that the fact that a given regime is audacious and totalitarian were not reason enough for the nation with the oldest democratic tradition to break all ties with it. By any standard, Calbe's preposterous behaviour at Thessaloniki is more than reasonable. And his continuing affronts to the Greek people give even more reason,
confident that this time, our advice, which is the advice of resolute and affection, will not go unheeded.
