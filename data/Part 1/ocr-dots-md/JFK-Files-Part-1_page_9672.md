I have discussed with Paul Burke, DC/CI/ICC, Mexico's request for amendment of OA to use LICOCKY-l against the Soviet official. We both are of the opinion that she can be used for development of the target to determine his susceptibility to recruitment, but that she should not be used to make the actual recruitment should the matter develop to that point, due to these considerations: (1) She is not a recruiter, never has made a recruitment. (2) Odds are she would be blown to the Soviets, since we must consider that the odds for successful recruitment of a Soviet official are not favorable. (3) Her relationship with LILLUCK in Mexico is fairly well known, so that there could be an unfavorable blow back on LILLUCK.

In authorizing her use in the developmental role, Burke would like for Station B to be informed that it should tell LILLUCK that Station's continuing investigation of LICOCKY-l discloses her association with Soviets; therefore, he should be very careful in his relationship with her.
