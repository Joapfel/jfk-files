CONFIDENTIAL

16 December 1948

TO: Special Funds

FROM: M. K. Haller

SUBJECT: James Walton Moore

1. Subject returned from Shanghai on Permanent Change of Station and reported to Washington Headquarters 10 December 1948. Personnel Action Form 37-1 transferring him to FBZ Washington has been prepared and will be forwarded to you in the near future.

2. Mr. Moore will be on annual leave until 20 January 1948 plus LTOP and will report back to Washington for duty. This will be reported on Standard Form 1130.

CONFIDENTIAL
