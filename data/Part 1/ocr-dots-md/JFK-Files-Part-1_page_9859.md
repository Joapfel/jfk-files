19 March, 1968

Subject: LILING/3 Report of Contact with Memebers of FAR, Guatemala.

1. At 0945 on 19/3/68 at the hobbins Restaurant, Calle Leibnitz and Helchor Ooampo, LILING/3 reported to Rowton that he had again met the FAR member whose sketch he had given Rowton -the previous day and whom he had met thru Carlos Persaval on 17/3/68. He explained that Persaval had finally contacted him by phone late Sunday afternoon (17/3/68) and introduced him to one of the two Guatemalans who wanted to return clandestinely to their country. L/3 described this man, who later showed himIDENTIFICATION PAPERS in the name of Miguel Angel Oliva, aged 33, as short, stocky, with black hair trimmed short and rather thick prominent eyebrows. L/3 estimated his age as 35 and said there was no doubt in his mind that he was a native-born Guatemalan. It added that this person wore a thin mustache. L/3 then drew Rowton a sketch of the person concerned which Rowton passed in to the Station the same day.

2. LIKING/3 continued that he conversed with the Guatemalan, Oliva, in the presence of Carlos Persaval and his wife Teresa Lozada de Persaval at the Miss restaurant on Reforma for about 45 minutes, taking the line that he was willing to help the cause as he had done in the past, but he wanted to be certain of the exact affiliations and bona fides of the persons with whom he was dealing. Then this apparently surprised and somewhat shocked Carlos Persaval and Teresa, L/3 explained that he had had some unpleasant experiences with such persons as Crozco and was aware that many FAR desertors were circulating around Mexico these days. The Guatemalan, Oliva, however reacted favorably saying that he understood L/3's position and did not blame him. He added that he would furnish proof of his FAR affiliations and indicated that there were two other comrades, one much more important than himself, who also needed help in returning. L/3 told Rowton that he stressed to all present the risks involved in providing false documentation and acting as a guide for them to cross the border illegally and his original commitment to help only two persons. In reply to L/3's query, the Guatemalan Oliva stated that their destination would be Guatemala City itself and they hoped that L/3 would be able to accompany them there since they were not familiar with the countryside between the border in the Comitán area and Guatemala City.
