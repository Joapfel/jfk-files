1.  MANUEL ARTIME WAS IN MANAGUA FROM 11 TO 20 MAY 1967. HE TOLD STATION OFFICERS HE HAD BEEN CONTACTED AGAIN BY HAITIAN EMIGRES INCLUDING CANTAVE, WHO SOMEHOW, HOUGHT HE COULD HELP THEM GET BASES AND ARMS. ARTIME ALSO SAID HE HAD RECEIVED A MESSAGE FROM DUVALIER ASKING HIS HELP IN GETTING GENERAL SOMOZA TO PUSH FOR SANCTIONS AGAINST CUBA IN ORDER TO KEEP PRESSURE OFF DUVALIER AT SUMMIT CONFERENCE.

2.  ARTIME HAS REPORTED CONTACTS BY HAITIAN EXILES OVER PAST TWO OR THREE YEARS, ASKING FOR HELP TO OVERTHROW DUVALIER, AND FROM DUVALIER REPS ASKING INTERCESSION WITH SOMOZA ON HIS BEHALF. ARTIME SAYS HE HAS LISTENED TO ALL. MADE NONCOMMITTAL REPLIES AND REFUSED TO INVOLVE SELF WITH EITHER GROUP.
