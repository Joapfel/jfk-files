26 February 1974

- 3 -

7. Since receiving the 27 February 1974 call from OSS, we have checked with Charles Haynes and Bob Perry of the FBI. Both are familiar with Van Cleve and his operations. Perry confirmed that Van Cleve had passed the information contained in Enclosure No. 4 to the FBI. Perry had a few other things to say about Van Cleve that probably do not need to be repeated in this memorandum.

8. Concerning the future, we intend to be hard to reach when Van Cleve calls; if necessary, talk to him on the phone a few times to see if he gets the idea; then if all fails simply tell him we have been told to stay out of this business.

A. B. COLLEEN
