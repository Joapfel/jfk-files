Keel 72-ICO-28

From 0620, 3 May (Monday) to 0620, 4 may 1965

2.0 Ingebord Rico (V) answers.
VKG comes to the phone. Call goes through with Pundora.
VKG still has nothing. Expects (he) may call him early today and
will call F. when he has something.

2.2 Out to 03. Time is 0821 (3 May).

2.5 Out to 25-80-32. VKG asks NO, who answers, for Dr. (Baillo)
Corder.
BC takes the phone. Asks if EC has any news. No. No one
phoned him. A friend of his was in contact with person in Santo
Domingo - that Miraval's (?) were killed. It is said that the
Commission does not know what to do. They are looking for Provisional
President...But apparently the masses are demanding Bosch. They are
still indecisive. There are now 14,000 men (armed) and VKG thinks
there are more...(14,000 marines?). BC says they have ordered another
4,000 some odd (to enter). Bosch has given the insurrection as finished
and that they should not fire mass against U.S. and that he (Bosch) did
not think that U.S. had bad intentions. There are about 40 hurt among
U.S. and about 4 or 5 dead...

VKG will call EC later from magazine office. VKG will also
leave office number at home in case Juan B. (Mejia) phones him.

3.5 Out to 23-02-91. VKG (V) orders a cab.

3.7 Out to 34-88-63, three times. No rings, complete. Cuesta
(maid) with MO, who answers, for MO's mother.
MO takes the phone...Maid says that Sra. does not give the
enough food...also Sra. (Dicner) is very demanding...will leave in
13 days...

4.2 Out to 18-27-27. Maid asks for Antoulo. Not there. Maid
is Cuesta...

4.5 Out to 24-92-11. Maid asks for Anita. She is out now.

4.7 Ina. Wrong number. Maid answers.

5.0 Ina. Wrong number. Maid answers.

5.2 Ina. Victor Rico Galan (VKG) asks maid, who answers, for
Sra. Out. Has anyone phoned him? No.
