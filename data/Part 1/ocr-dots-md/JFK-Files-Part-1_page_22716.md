6 August 1964.

MEMORANDUM FOR : Chief, CI/OA
SUBJECT : AMIASH-1 C-101080

1. Reference is made to your "Request for Information" dated 31 July 1964 concerning Subject.

2. The PRQ Part II on AMTRUNK-10 (201-142291) contains certain errors which are being struck: AMIASH-1 is not a Headquarters internal asset, nor is he running any internal group. The relationship between AMTRUNK-10 and AMIASH-1 is a personal one - they have been friends for many years. AMIASH-1 is not doing anything whatsoever at the present time inside Cuba which has anything to do with any KUBARK operation, plan, activity; in addition, he is not furnishing any Political or Foreign Intelligence. A FOI was requested on him in June of 1962 for recruitment as a possible defector. He returned to Cuba and has resumed his career as a surgeon. He is working with the cardiology department of the Manuel Fajardo Hospital.

3. Station JMWAVE is being advised of the above.

Samuel Halpern
ADC/MH/SA
