I returned to the United States on 25 October 1945, and completed my terminal leave on 19 December 1945.

During my term of service my superiors rendered reports on my fitness each ninety days. All of those reports were favorable, and many of them contained commendations. I was promoted to Major in August 1945, to rank from May 1943, and I hold a permanent commission as Major in the Reserve. By file number is 013458.
