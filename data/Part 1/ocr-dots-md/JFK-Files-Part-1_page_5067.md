Memorandum

TO POL/R, SANTIAGO

FROM : LEGAT ROBERT W. SCHERRER, BUENOS AIRES

SUBJECT: ORLANDO BOSCH AVILA; LETELIER ASSASSINATION

DATE: 5/27/77

The Chilean Department of Investigations advised that the International Police located a record of entry for PEDRO ANTONIO PENA RODRIGUEZ into Chile on S.'30/75 from Colombia as a tourist. PENA RODRIGUEZ was carrying Dominican Passport #OS6768, which disclosed his date of birth as 1/8/29. PENA RODRIGUEZ was admitted to Chile as a tourist. No record for PENA RODRIGUEZ's departure from Chile could be located.

The Chilean Department of Investigations advised that the Chilean Passport Office located a record for HECTOR EMILIO RAMON DAVANZO CINTOLESI, who was born on 3/15/27 in Santiago, the son of HECTOR DAVANZO and AMALIA ENRIQUETA CINTOLESI. This individual has Chilean Santiago Cedula de Identidad #2,357.4 and Chilean Passport #27,671, the latter being issued on 10/22/75. Chilean Passport Office records list DAVANZO CINTOLESI's address in Santiago as Calle Miguel Claro #1129.

Attached is a copy of a photograph of DAVANZO CINTOLESI, which was copied from his Cedula de Identidad.

The Chilean Department of Investigations advised that the International Police reported that DAVANZO CINTOLESI departed Santiago on 12/28/75 en route to Panama. According to International Police Records, DAVANZO CINTOLESI was carrying Chilean Passport #26,583, when he departed Chile on 4/26/75.
