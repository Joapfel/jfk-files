In such circumstances, FIONE had to do all the thinking, all the planning, all the casing for meeting sites, all the analyzing, all the preparing and pointing for the recruitment. It was pure joy to watch this man, after each meeting and again before the next one, go to work on the problem. It was know where he was, know where he wanted to go, know how to get there. When TELQUIST , during the critique at the end of the series of exercises said: "I did a beautiful job," FIONE had earned that accolade all by himself. 7. At this point, the instructor wishes to execute a low bow in the direction of TELQUIST . He played his agent role excellently well and made the exercise a meaningful one for FIONE. The three trips to Key Largo by TELQUIST must have been very taxing, particularly in view of his immediately upcoming MVI trip, but they were efforts well spent.

8. No man, of course, is perfect. Each has an Achilles heel, a weakness, or an all-too-human trait, at the very least. Our FIONE has his. He has a very quick mind. He knows it. And knowing it, he relies on it, counts on it. This, in turn, leads to a small failure in self-discipline. For example, told to write out his plan for the recruitment pitch, he came in with six words (bytes not that many) scratched on a 9 by 5 leaf torn copy of his pocket notebook. He then proceeded, from these few words, to run off his plan without a single pause for an hour, half an hour. It was as though he was reading a definition from a dictionary, so smoothly did his mind and thoughts run. He prepared everything in his mind, it appears, and does not like to write out that which he feels he should not have to write out. While it is great to have so facile a mind, a bit of discipline, to write out in full so important an act as a recruitment pitch, might one say save grief from an error would have been spotted had the entire plan been written out then looked over for possible flaws.

9. This small film, the instructor feels, accounts in considerable measure for FIONE's failure to bring in a written dead drop plan till the last days of the progress.
