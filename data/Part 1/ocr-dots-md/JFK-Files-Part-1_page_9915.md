Attachment A to EIA-12989 dated 6 September 1967

## AUDIO

1. Special CO operations No. 1 and No. 2 have functioned satisfactorily with calls for assistance.

2. LIXING-3, LIKES-50: A Tain-4000 has been installed for this operation in place of a severe. There was a slight problem at first when the LP operator recorded too loud and distorted the tape. The Lick battery and the quality has greatly improved by getting off the AC line. CONEWEET has come to the conclusion that Mexico City does not have a good earth ground. Consequently, great adjustments must be made with any equipment that needs on earth grounds...

3. DISPENSE: A separate report is being prepared on this operation. To date, we are not too happy with the results. There is an intermittent but which we cannot identify and the present switching arrangement makes it hard to felicit Subject from room to room. Some details will be included in a separate dispatch.

4. LISANPAH: We now feel that this operation is beginning to shape up, thanks to the expertise of Peter J. BENTUP. Thereafter so DIPREP will provide a technical report, and previously is still working on some of the problems, the details will not be repeated here. It is too soon to say, but we hope to be able to have the LP keepers move defective GHL-1's out and replace them with operating units whenever there is a failure. A problem might be having to adjust each unit on the target line. Time will tell.

5. Plans for LISANPAH are to activate the OCT of LISANPAH/BAKER early in September using LICARO as the LP. Then we have reliable switching on LISANPAH/DG, this, too, will be piped to LICARO via OCT from LISANPAH.

6. There were a few other miscellaneous ADDI charges. Three duplicates to tapes had to be made for the COs, three leave tape recorders had to be cleaned and repaired, and one DLR 4000-L was cleaned and repaired.
