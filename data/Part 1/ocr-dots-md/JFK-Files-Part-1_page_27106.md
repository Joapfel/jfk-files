Dear Mr. Wilson:

Before exercising my right to a freedom of information request for copies
of any and all available (in accordance with the declassification processes
initiated in Executive Order 11959 and its amendment of January 1974) files
of records and documents, both computerized and manual, except as to my
activation under my present and former names and reissues, I hereby request
the complete and final estimate of charges for search and duplication services.

In regards to the charges for these services, two matters may be worthy of
consideration. The first of these is this, given the proper access codes, the
execution of a computer search is neither difficult nor costly. And the second
is that in light of the controversial nature of my past, a valver or reduction
of charges may conclude to the furtherance of the public interest. I therefore
respectfully request that those factors be considered in the making of a decision
as to the cost of services.

In compliance with your request for identity verification (pursuant to
provisions of the 1974 Privacy Act, section 532, subsections b and d) I
herby state before a notary public as to my name and former names, my recent
address, my date as well as place of birth, and my citizenship. Thank you
for your cooperation, I am...
