# NEIL F. DOHERTY
## PERSONAL HISTORY STATEMENT

I, NEIL F. DOHERTY, do hereby make known that I was born on August 26th, 1957 and lived as a minor until the age of 18 years. During my early years I spent much time with some friends in the town of Doolan, County Meath, where I attended the High School there. After completing my secondary education at Doolan, I went to University College, Dublin, where I received my degree in Engineering, B.Eng., in 1983. Following my graduation I worked for a period of two years with a company called "Mars", in the development of a new type of engine. This work led to my being employed by another company, "The Motor Company", in Doolan, where I have since been working as a engineer in various capacities until the present day.
