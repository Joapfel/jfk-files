SUBJECT: General - Administrative - Personnel
         Specific - Recommendation for Promotion - Newton S. MILER

1. It is requested that Newton S. MILER be promoted from GS-12 to G3-13.

2. Subject served as the Chief, Chi/Ind Section until his departure for ZI on 3 December 1952. During the next 9 months and under his leave, the Section has grown from a 3 - 4 man section with 2 or 3 additional people in the field, to approximately 10 people at Headquarters and 4 to 5 in the field at all times. During this time, he has taken on the tremendous additional responsibility in his section of the primary handling of FRU relations with and support of STPROMIX, as well as the building on a sound basis of an enlarged FRU sub-station in Hong Kong, in addition to the overall direction of all FRU China Independent operations.

3. Subject requires little supervision or assistance in carrying out its job. He is mature and competent. He exercises a maximum of independent action, directing his section and at the same time giving what amount to on-the-job training to the Junior case officers assigned to him.

4. MILER supervises directly the 14 - 15 individuals mentioned above. As such he is responsible for all their work. He has discharged this responsibility, a particularly heavy one in view of the inexperience of his personnel, in an exemplary fashion.

5. Subject is a highly qualified operation officer with demonstrated executive ability and a liking for hard work. He has consistently made a major contribution to the proper functioning of FEU.

6. MILER is a definite, career-employee asset to the organization. It is, therefore, recommended that he be promoted from GS-12 to G3-13.

S. NEBRAN HORTON
