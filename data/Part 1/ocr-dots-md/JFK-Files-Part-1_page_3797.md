Dear Jim,

Pardon my lateness in writing to you but I have had to face
many problems and I wanted to be calm when I wrote to you.

I was at Luis Somosa's funeral and I think that I made a good
impression. I am at present more worried than I was before, but not
for my future, because Tacho is a very good friend of mine and Luis
was not. I am worried about Nicaragua's destiny. Let us not
forget that Nicaragua is the most solid pillar of Central America.
Lainoz grew closer to Tacho during the funeral. Lainoz took
practically all the load of the funeral arrangements upon himself.
Many of the good men who gave the Somosa's advice were intimate
friends of Luis and they are afraid of coming to Tacho in order to
give him advice.
