Leave Unit

2 April 1948

Staffs I and III, SI
Miss Ann L. Goodpasture

Miss Ann L. Goodpasture reported back to SI Washington from Shanghai on 1 April 1948. On the trip back, she developed a rather serious ear ailment, and it is feared that an operation may be necessary. Dr. Thompson of the Medical Division was going to attempt to have her admitted to a hospital Monday night for further attention and treatment. After release from the hospital she will go to her home in Tipton, Oklahoma.

In view of the above facts, we shall show Miss Goodpasture as being on sick leave (approximately one month) until her return to duty, at which time she will fill out the form 71 and any other necessary papers.

Livia M. Demian
