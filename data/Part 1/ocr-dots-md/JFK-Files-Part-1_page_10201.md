INFO. HER WORK CONSISTS MOSTLY UNCLASSIFIED PROTOCOL. NOTES MEXI

CONSULATE. SHE ALSO PAY CLERK LOCAL EMPLOYEES. SHE THUS FAR NOT
AGGRESSIVE "NATA HARI" TYPE AND PROVIDES ONLY INFO WHICH AVAILABLE
HER ROUTINE WORK. AT LEAST MTG 27 JULY PROVIDED FIRST TANGIBLE ITEM
WHEN SHE GAVE CO EXEMPLAR COURTESY VISA GIVEN ALL TRAVELERS HAVA
WHO NOT WISH HAVE VISA PASSPORTS. L-9 EXPLAINED IN DETAIL MANNER
FILLING OUT COURTESY VISA WHICH ISSUED ON SHEET CUBAN EMB PAPER.
