In Mexico LOGINOV replaced Oleg M. NECHIPORENKO, another KGB officer and took over all of NECHIPORENKO's intelligence agents and duties. In January 1966 he attempted to cultivate and recruit a chauffeur at the American Embassy in Mexico. LOGINOV is an unusually active and capable intelligence officer. Since he has almost no overt duties he is able to spend about 95% of his time on intelligence activity. We have known that he is a KGB officer since 1958.
