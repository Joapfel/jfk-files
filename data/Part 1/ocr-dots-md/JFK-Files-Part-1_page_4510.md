SUBJECT : Alicia CRUZ Bustillo

It is requested that the files of the Federal Bureau of Investigation and the Department of State be searched for any information they may contain regarding Alicia CRUZ Bustillo, and that information be forwarded to this Office. Available information indicates that Miss BUSTILLO, a Cuban ballet dancer, is about 30 years of age, and was one of the group of Cuban ballet dancers which recently made a tour of Europe. She reportedly has recently injured her foot and is not now dancing but is working as a secretary to Alicia ALONSO, the prime ballerina of Cuba.
