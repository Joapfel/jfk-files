18 February 1963

**MEMORANDUM FOR:** Director of Training

**ATTENTION** : Chief, Cover Training/Operations School
Chief, TSD Training

**SUBJECT** : Request for Cover Training of Luis ALBELLU Sonto

1. It is requested that full time cover training be provided at Mexico City, Mexico for approximately ten days beginning on or about 19 February 1963 for Luis ALBELLU Sonto.

2. The assignment of this training is in principle subject to the basic principles of clandestine tradecraft to better prepare him as an FI agent operating inside a denied area.

3. In addition to the basic tradecraft, training should emphasize reporting techniques and include appropriate secret writing instruction, as arranged by the case officer with TSD.

4. Subject is a PERPETUAL cultural attaché in the Embassy in Mexico City. A Provisional Operational Approval was requested on 26 July 1962 and its status is being checked. (C-101331; MRG: 1915, Uvieda, Spain)

5. Responsible Headquarters case officer for the training is H. E. Mulley, extension 6172.

OSCAR D. MCCULLUM
Training Officer 2/63

APPROVED:

Chief, Special Affairs Staff
