Since his precipitate meeting with the Director about 1730 hours on 16 May 1961, Lynch has not been heard from. Presumably he returned to his home in Fayetteville, North Carolina.

On 18 May 1961, CA/PME had calls from the office of both WH/4 and C/WH asking if we knew of Lynch's whereabouts, and whether we had a record of his Fayetteville address. The answer to both questions was negative. Subsequently his address was located in files and WH was informed.
