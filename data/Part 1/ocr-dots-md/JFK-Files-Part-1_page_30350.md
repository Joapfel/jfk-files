OCA, AMCONGEN
APO 757, US Forces

Dear Mr. Stewart

On the departure of Mr. James W. McCord I wanted you to know of OSI's appreciation for his constant support and cooperation during his tour. He has given us valuable assistance in the performance of our mission.

We appreciate also Mr. McCord's constant efforts to create unity of purpose and action among the many security and counterintelligence agencies in the area. Through his efforts new liaison avenues were opened to OSI which, on several occasions, facilitated the completion of our tasks. His efforts in contingency planning also were noteworthy.

OSI in USAFE will miss Mr. McCord. Please extend our good wishes to him and Mrs. McCord for continued success in their endeavors.
