## SECRET ROOM

In the left hand side of the page below are a series of statements that add up to one degree to one person. On the right hand side of the page are four major categories of descriptions. For each category fill in the appropriate box under the right column and mark off the corresponding statement with an X. Place an "X" in the first category where it applies, and place another "X" in the second category where it applies. Put a line through the category on the right which best fits your idea of this person. Mark the person on the left so clearly, and write his or her name on the right where he or she was found. Place an "X" in the "Not Applicable" column when you have no opinion as to whether a phrase applies to him or her, and an "X" in the "Does Not Apply" column when you have the definite intention to fire the person.
