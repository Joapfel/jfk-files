a. **Identification.** Base - New York City. Political Action Support.

b. **Objectives.** To support contract agent Edward G. TICHEBORN (P) who will be used as a covert action advisor and political action agent by C.M.D. in addition TICHEBORN will be available for election campaign research, special political surveys on selected countries and other allied research requirements which will utilize his political expertise and area knowledge.

c. **Background.** In late 1959, TICHEBORN, a bilingual Harvard-trained lawyer with considerable domestic political experience, was hired as a political action advisor for Agency-sponsored election operations throughout Latin America. In 1961 he became a full-time contract agent and was assigned to Mexico City, under personal cover, which permitted him to travel readily on third country political action assignments. He has participated in political operations in Cuba, El Salvador, Guatemala, Argentina, Chile and the Dominican Republic. While based in Mexico he was primarily a headquarters assist in addition did an excellent job for Mexico City station in first establishing and running a magazine aimed at Latin American intellectuals, and second reporting on the activities and attitudes of Mexican left-wing intellectuals. TICHEBORN has shown himself to be a very intelligent, outgoing Mexican-American with initiative and energy who has highly developed political sensibilities.

This project is being presented to cover the salary and operational expenses of Contract Agent Edward G. TICHEBORN.
