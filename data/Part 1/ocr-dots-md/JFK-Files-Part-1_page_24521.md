Convert to MarkdownIt seems I will have an interview with the president. Answers to questions will be in writing - guaranteed 100 percent. But I want a personal interview. And that is the whole business.

The situation is altogether normal. The humidity is terrible: 93 percent. I am wet all day long. A strange city: the skies are sullen all day long but there is not a drop of water, and they tell me there never is. Can you believe it, the annual rainfall is 1 cm, but darkness penetrates to your intestines. In comparison with Mexico this is a backward province and one can see this in everything. But it has much charm. Its colonial buildings are good. I cannot talk about the provinces, I have not seen them, but I have heard that in their pittresqueness they are unique. There are as few hotels as in Moscow. I live in a [illergible] for 8 dollars and kill cockroaches day and night and place patches on the scratches on my legs which I do not know where to put because of the restricted area. I am very happy that this one was built for the Americans. They deserve it.

Give me a call. It seems everything is in order [illergible].

H. Leonov
