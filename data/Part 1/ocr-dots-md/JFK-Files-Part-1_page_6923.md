TO DIR INFO CITE MEXI 2592

1. MAURICE HALPERIN CONTACTING ANN SEBORER RE KLM FLT TO LISBOA VIA SAN JOSE, CURACAO, CARACAS. INTERESTED IN KNOWING IF RESERVATIONS CAN BE OBTAINED WITH SHORT NOTICE. ANN SEBORER ALSO CHECKED FOR CANPAC WITHOUT STOP IN UNITED STATES FOR TRIP TO LISBOA. (LIFEAT 29 SEPT DOC).

2. ANN SEBORER TELLING FRIENDS OF FURNISHED APT ON CALLE MEDELLIN FOR RENT FOR 6 MONTHS-YEAR. FC: HALPERIN LIVES AT MEDELLIN 36 APT 9. ALSO TELLING OF GOOD BUY ON STUDEBAKER 50 VINTAGE AT 12,000 PESOS WHICH HAS HAD ONLY 1 DRIVER. FC: HALPERIN OWNS 50 STUDEBAKER. BOTH MAY BE PURE COINCIDENCE. SOURCE LIFEAT DOC.

3. HALPERIN SEEING ANN SEBORER PERSONALLY ON ARRANGEMENTS ALTHOUGH PHONING FOR APPOINTMENT WITH HER. LIFEAT B DOC.

4. HALPERIN STILL IN MEXI 8 OCT WHEN EDITH ASKED MGT MALTZ OVER FOR COFFEE. LIFEAT BDOC.

END OF MESSAGE
