COMMENTS:

CO dropped by to see Subject and found him working on his latest invention. He has given up working on the device originally proposed by CO since he does not have sufficient tools and because some parts are must be ordered from the US. CO encourages him as long as it keeps him busy and helps to pass the time.

Subject has an extremely inventive mind and has explained several of his ideas to CO. They would appear to have commercial value. Unfortunately Subject has neither the money to develop them nor is he in a position to get involved with patents or manufacturers at the present time.

He seems reasonably happy with his present status but CO anticipates a hairy situation if upon applying for immigration to a SA country, the processing period takes a long time.
