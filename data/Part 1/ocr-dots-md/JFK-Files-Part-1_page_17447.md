6. The summary of the case of Francisco DELGADO Garcia is authentic and is a case of LITEMPO/2's organisation handled.

7. Finally, LITEMPO, 2 was orally reminded of the material furnished (personally) to LITEMPO, 2 by Manuel VILLAFANA Martínez, Air Attache, Cuban Embassy, who defected. A copy of the interview between LITEMPO/2 and VILLAFANA Martínez was made—and the station still has a copy.

8. The Ambassador was allowed to believe that all of the above material is authentic.

9. The Ambassador hopes that this material which has been shown to LITENSOR by LITEMPO/2 will be helpful in convincing Mexico that they should break relations with Cuba. The Station feels that this cooperation in the effort to impress LITENSOR and LITEMPO/2 with these (which were said to be "some examples") illustrations of Cuban interference in the internal affairs of Mexico was good political action for OBYOKE and with the Chief, ODACID.

Willard C. Currid

Attachments (3)

22 January 1962
