Convert to MarkdownREVOLT WAS A QUICK LOOK AT THE BORDER. COMMUNISTS WERE A GO-ON AT THE UNIVERSITY, THE YOUNG DETECTIVE. THE COMMUNIST HAD FOUND "HUNT BLACK MARKERS" AGAINST HIM IN SEVEN-YEAR RECORD COVERING ALL PHASES OF HIS DAILY ACTIVITIES FROM TRIPS ABROAD WITH THE FENCING TEAM TO CONVERSATIONS IN TAURANTS.

IN YOUNG ORLEAT'S VIEW, IT WAS ONLY BECAUSE HE HAD WON THE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP AT CREMONA, ITALY IN 1934 AND BECAUSE HE WAS KEPT UP HIS STUDIES AS A BADLY NEEDED STUDENT OF PHARMACY. NO ACTION WAS NOT BEEN TAKEN AGAINST HIM.

AT THE TIME ORLEAT AND HIS FRIEND DECIDED TO MEET FOR THE BORDER, SAID SOME 60,000,000 CZECHUUNGARIANS ALREADY HAD FLED TO AUSTRIA. THOSE WHO HAD LIVED NEAR ZHOUZH, TO THE BORDER NEW THE LAND AND MADE HER WAY TO FREESOM WITH LITTLE OPPOSITION. BUT OTHERS HAD TO TAKE THEIR WAY FROM BUDAPEST.

ON NOVEMBER 10, THOMAS AND HIS FRIEND DRESSED IN WORKERS' CLOTHING AND MANAGED TO BOARD A TRAIN—ONE OF THE FEW STILL MOVING TO THE WEST. THEY TRAVELLED TO THE FIRST TOWN OUTSIDE BUDAPEST WITHOUT BEING STOPPED. THERE, THEY HITCHED A RIDE WITH TRUCK DRIVER WHO WAS HEADED FOR THE BORDER AND ASKED NO QUESTIONS. ALTHOUGH THEY WERE STOPPED "SIX OR SEVEN TIMES," BY THE SOVIET SOLDIERS THEY TRAVELLER WITH THE TRUCK TO WITHIN 90 MILES OF THE BORDER.

FOR THE FINAL STRETCH THE TWO ESCAPING STUDENTS WERE JOINED BY A YOUNG COUPLE. ALL FOUR OF THEM TRAVELLING BY FOOT FOR TWO DAYS TO COVER THEIR LAST 90 MILES.
