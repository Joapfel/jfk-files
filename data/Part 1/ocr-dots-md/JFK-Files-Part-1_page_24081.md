MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: ORPHONE/1 FINANCES

1. This memorandum outlines a new procedure which is to be allowed in the handling of the operational costs of field agent QRPHONE/1. This new procedure is being instituted in order to ensure the security and compartmentation of the handling of the administrative aspect of this project and parallels revisions in the handling of operational traffic. The new procedure which is assessed below is in accordance with accepted field procedures.

2. Currently QRPHONE/1 receives advances and accounts for those with detailed statements of expenses accompanied by true income receipts, etc. These accountings and the accompanying concepts have been maintained in the LCPIPIT files. This system can be changed. Henceforth monies for operational expenses should be given to QRPHONE/1 by his handler (Career Agent Rufus F. RODIGAST) and will be carried on RODIGAST's accountings as operational expenses supported by receipts from QRPHONE/1 acknowledging receipt of the funds. This RODIGAST accounting and the QRPHONE/1 receipt will be used for write-off of the funds against approved project QRPHONE. No detailed accountings and no receipts carrying QRPHONE/1's true name are to be maintained in the LCPIPIT/Admin files.

3. It will be the responsibility of Chief, LCPIPIT and the project case officer to ensure that QRPHONE/1 is supplied with funds necessary for the furtherance of the operation and to ensure that these amounts are carefully controlled to avoid overpayment. In discharging this responsibility the Chief, LCPIPIT may require individuals to itemize the various expenses covered by the payments.

APPROVED:

Chief, LCPIPIT
Thomas K. Halkom
Chief of Station, London
