CASE 52,872 - HUSH O. MCDONALD / WORLD ASSOCIATES INC.
REFSE WASH-83082 AND LANS-3977

WA-TWX WAS BASED ON INFORMATION OBTAINED FROM REPRESENTATIVE FROM ORD, WHO MAY HAVE OVERESTIMATED MACDONALD'S ENTHUSIASM TO BE OF SERVICE TO HIS FORMER BENEFACATORS. WE ARE FOLLOWING TOPORD COMPONENTS TO DETERMINE RESPONSE TO HIS OFFER TO COME TO WASHINGTON ON 8 MAY AS INDICATED IN SECOND REF. BELIEVE FIRST WE SHOULD HAVE MEETING OF MINDS ON EXTENT AND SCOPE TO WHICH MACDONALD COULD AGREE TO DISCUSS OR DEMONSTRATE HIS VOICE IDENT SYSTEM WITH AGENCY REPRESENTATIVES, WHO ARE QUALIFIED TO DISCUSS SUCH MATTERS FROM A SECURITY STANDPOINT. THE ITINERARY OF HIS WORLD TRIP IS IMPORTANT BUT NOT A PRESSING MATTER AT THIS TIME. IF THERE IS SUFFICIENT OPERATIONAL INTEREST, WE CAN SERVE REQUIREMENTS LATER WHEN MORE INFORMATION IS AVAILABLE REGARDING HIS SO CALLED WORLD TOUR IN THE FALL. SHOULD MACDONALD BE DISPOSED TO COME TO WASHINGTON D.C. WE WILL MAKE THE NECESSARY ARRANGEMENTS FOR THE APPOINTMENT AND INTRODUCTION TO TSD/ORD REPS...HIS POINT OF CONTACT WOULD BE DC$/OSS (MUSULIN)
