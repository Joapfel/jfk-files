## I. ORGANIZATION

The commercial office of the USSR is located at Calle General Leon 32, in Mexico City.

The phone number of the commercial office is 15-12-64. The commercial office was formerly located in the Soviet Embassy, main building at Tucubaya 204. Due to expansion of activities, additional personnel, and the resultant need for greater operating space, the office was moved in April 1955 to its present address.
