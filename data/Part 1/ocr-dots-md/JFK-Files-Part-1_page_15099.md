REPORT ON CONTACT WITH AGENT OR DEFECTOR

SUBJECT: SECRET

DATE: 28 June 1962

TIME:

PLACE: Paris

David R Reinefeld, Case Officer

COMMENTS:

CO visited. Subject at his apartment and learned that through the German firm in Düsseldorf is interested in his invention, it nevertheless he has not committed itself and he intends to study it while. Subject indicated to CO that he has no intentions to act until the Germans make up their minds. He was obviously discouraged by his inability to sell his invention and realization he is no longer needed by CRIME.

As the result of the above he commented that he is not anxious to go to South America or to any country where we can resist him locally. He even stated that since he has no funds from the sale of his invention he is not anxious to return to the Congo and say goodbye to his friends. He stated several times previously that the only reason he would return would be to see his friends, that he had no business to live here, and that he could handle the person-1 matters himself. We will.

CO informed him that he could inform Germany that subject is now really anxious to go to America. Subject then brought up the fact that the Düsseldorf firm which he visited was very interested in taking advantage of his Congo background and send him there as a business representative. If first he would do strictly as a private individual contract for a business firm, but that his situation would depend on some arrangement. Once the American Government would offer him one part of the field of the firm's activity in the Congo. He agreed that he have no real interest to return to the Congo for a German firm since such an act in his mind indicate that he no longer is available for us. He would therefore rather go to a place of our choosing and be available any time he was linked to serve by Uncle Sam. He commented however that in the event we delay his leaving Germany unduly (in his estimation) he may be forced to take the offer made by the German firm. He made it clear that he did not mean this as a threat, but a straight resettlement fact, induced by taking the job in the Congo he would at least have a job, wherever he going to South America he has no job in sight.
