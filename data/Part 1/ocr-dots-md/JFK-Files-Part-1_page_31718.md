5. A member of the National Peasant Party and supporter of Iliau MANTU, TIELEA broke away from that group around 1935 to join Alexander VAIDA-VOEVOD who founded the fascist ROMANIAN FRONT. VAIDA-VOEVOD, having aided CAROL in becoming dictator, became President of the NATIONAL RENAISSANCE FRONT, the totalitarian party set up by CAROL in 1936. VAIDA-VOEVOD was an advocate of the "numerus valahicus", an ultra-nationalist policy aimed at limiting to 5% the number of Jews allowed to practise professions or to attend universities. In 1938, according to this source, who is considered usually reliable, TIELEA became one of the mainstays of the CAROL dictatorship. Another source, in referring to this period, states that TIELEA who was then Under Secretary of State, acted as liaison between VAIDA-VOEVOD and the IRON GUARD.

6. A source evaluated as usually reliable stated that in late 1946 TIELEA wrote to Alexander CRETZIANU requesting loan of 30,000 pounds from the "resistance funds" in Switzerland which CRETZIANU controlled. Upon receiving CRETZIANU's refusal, TIELEA reportedly wrote to the latter that it would be necessary, since he had not received the treatment to which he believed he was entitled, "to revise his attitude (towards CRETZIANU) in future."
