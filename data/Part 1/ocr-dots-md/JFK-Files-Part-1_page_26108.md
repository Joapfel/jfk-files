Final One & Two

Feb 24 1974 15:21:27

Gus DIRECTOR

77510

To CAPTAIN, JONWAVE

HIGHWAY

REFERENCE: JONWAVE 1343 (EX 55099)*

1. HOS GUIDELINES FOR SMART MANGEMENTS BETWEEN AMSHADY-1 AND CIRASK SIMILAR AMSHADY-15 CASE. FIRST, SALARY NEGOTIATIONS BECOME AMSHADY-1 AND PROSPECTIVE EMPLOYER BE Carried Out WITHOUT NOTIFIED INVOLVEMENT. SECOND, NO FIRM SALARY BE DISCONTINUED UPON AMSHADY-1 ARRIVAL CARACAS. THREE, HOS WILL APPROVE SUPPLEMENT TO AMSHADY-1 CIRASK SALARY ONLY IF AMSHADY-1 BECOMES PRODUCIBLE AND IS STATION CARACAS.

2. HOS CONCERNING PASSAGE MAY BEST DESCRIBED IN REFERENCE CONTINGENT ON HOS VERIFICATION NAME WAS SURFACED BY ELAZ AND IS PROVIDED THROUGH ITALIAN CAYMAN.

END OF MESSAGE

In Content:

*Request of concurrently passageto AMSHADY-1 of names surfaced by HOS. In connection with Cuban involvement in material activities in Venezuela; agreed with Station Casing assignment of AMSHADY-1 utility in Caracas; assigned AMSHADY-1 initiate contact with individual who tentatively offered her

COUPLING OFFICE
