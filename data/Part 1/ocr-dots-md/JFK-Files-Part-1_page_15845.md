He stated he needed one to accomplish his mission. It was suggested to him that under the present circumstances in the Congo it would not be wise to consider such a purchase until he has discussed it fully with you and received your concurrence. If he did purchase a vehicle after receiving your approval, then he would have to do it from his own funds and not use funds made available to him to establish his cover business. Obviously he has violated the suggestions.</td>
