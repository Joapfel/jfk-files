   CITY. HE WAS GRANTED OA 6 JUNE 60 FOR ASSISTING IN COORDI-
   NATION OF ANTI-CASTRO ACTIVITIES IN MEXICO AS PART OF
   AWAIL OPERATION. BY 1963, HOWEVER, HIS OPERATIONAL
   CONTACTS HAD BECOME MARGINAL AND THEREAFTER HE WAS NOT
   USED BY STATION. THERE IS NO INFO AT HQS TO PRECLUDE YOUR
   PROPOSED USE OF HIM. WE PROCESSING POA.
