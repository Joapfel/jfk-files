UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT
Memorandum

TO: OP/Retirement Office

ATTN: Paul Sidel

FROM: C/WH/Contracts

SUBJECT: Jeremy K. BENADUM, Retirement

DATE: 15 March 1971

1. Per our telecon this morning, I have sent over to you a copy of the cable from Mexico City (IN 266625) which stated BENADUM had elected not to make the payment as described in our outgoing cable which your office coordinated with on Friday.

2. It is understood that necessary papers will be forwarded from your office to Miss Harrison in the Office of Finance today. I have called Miss Harrison and she advised that upon receipt of the papers from you there would be no difficulty in insuring that BENADUM's actual annuity is computed including the retroactive portion for January and February and included in the check for March.

Wm. Renehan
