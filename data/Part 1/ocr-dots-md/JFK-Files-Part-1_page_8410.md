SUBJECT: LIROBEINS/1 and 2.

DATE OF MEETING: 15 October 1968

1. The undersigned met with LIROBEINS/1 and 2 at their apartment on 15 October from 1600 to 1730 hours. The LIROBEINS were informed that REIDEMANN would be out of the country for the next two weeks and, accordingly, were requested to contact S.A. C., should they obtain information which they deem perishable and of urgency. The ROBBINS will contact S.A. C., either at MIMI the Station or at home via telephone.
A supplementary report on the activities of Pearl CONZALEZ is attached to this Contact Report as a Memorandum for the Record (separate).

2. LIROBEINS/2 reported that on 2 October Aleksandr PRIKHODO (P-2270) delivered an invitation to the LIROBEINS to attend a reception at the Soviet Embassy for the Soviet Olympic team. PRIKHODKO did not come in and visit with the LIROBEINS but merely made out an invitation for the LIROBEINS to attend. Both the LIROBEINS attended the function arriving at approximately 1930 and departing about 2130. The reception was rather poorly organized and there were few people in attendance from the Mexico City community. The LIROBEINS stated that they were the only American journalists and recognized no other guests with the exception of a Mexican movie star/director, Amilio FERNANDIZ ("El Indo") and wife. During the reception, LIROBEINS/2 saw GARIPOV (P-13125) who approached the LIROBEINS and apologized for the fact that he had not been able to hold his party. GARIPOV said that he had been extremely busy with the Olympics which occasioned him to go out to the airport every day as well as doing the work of others who he implied had been shirking their duties.
