### COURSE OBJECTIVES - CONTENT AND METHODOLOGY

This course aims to develop in the Clandestine Services officers, and others, recently returned from the field, an up-to-date awareness of the role of CIA in the United States intelligence community, the relations of the ID/I area with the CB and the support available from the offices of the DD/S. Recently this course has been designated as an alternate pre-requisite for CIA officers nominated for certain external training.

The course is presented by lectures given by CIA officials who are directly responsible for the missions, functions, programs, and services discussed. Each lecture is usually followed by a short question period. Approximately 10 per cent of the students' time is scheduled for reading pertinent up-to-date regulations and background summaries.
