Luis Clemente POMPA Carías (201-300/89)

MOSCUEY-3 (formerly ANGELINE-15, CIPFENUS-4)

EPCOS 15 February 1980, Cienfuegos, Cuba

U.S. Permanent Resident Allen (PRA) at least until 30 May 75. Possibly was lost status as result of allowing his reentry permit to expire, but this not clear. We protected his PNA status while he worked for us in Caracas--until termination June 74.

Also holds Venezuelan passport.

Entered U.S. as exile 20 February 1961.
Served U.S. Army as 2nd Lt. Fort Benning March 63 - March 64.
Member of JCSA to early 65.

April 65 - granted POA and recruited by JMMAVE as instructor working for Training Branch and as source of info on exile activities in Miami area. OA Oct 65.

Aug 66 - being used by JMMAVE CI Branch to report on activities of "Central Junta" being organised to govern activities of RECE, Comandos L, MRN, and AVBC. Subject associated with RECE.

June 67 - Subject known to FBI and Department of Justice as CIA employee. Cooperating with FBI/Miami in investigation of Frank "Lefty" Rosenthal.

July 67 - Annually terminated by JMMAVE. Continuing to cooperate with FBI.

Aug 67 - Mentioned as a CIA agent in broadcasts from LASO conference.

Oct 67 - Having obtained job in DIGEPOL, Caracas, Subject picked up by Caracas Station as unilateral penetration of DIGEPOL. Held several responsible positions--Chief of 4th Division, Explosives & Armaments, and Chief of CI section. Continued working for DISIP, successor to DIGEPOL, during COPEI administration and was used by DISIP Chief, Dr. Renéerto UZCATEGUI XXXXXXXXXXXX on various sensitive operations.

March 73 - Suspected by DEA of involvement in narcotics trafficking. (He was in Europe on DISIP business at the time.) May 73 "passed" polygraph on this subject.

March 74 - Dismissed from DISIP, along with many others, when AD administration inaugurated. Terminated 30 June 74 due to loss of access. Salary at time of termination $400 per month.

Contact with Subject continued because of outstanding problem re U.S. taxes, which was finally settled February 76. At that time Subject was informing Station re movements and activities of Orlando BOSCH. Occasional contact continued at least through June 76.

Agency protected Subject's PNA status from March 1970 until his termination June 1971. In January 1975, memo was sent to Alien Affairs Officer telling him that I&AI file could be "decentralized."
