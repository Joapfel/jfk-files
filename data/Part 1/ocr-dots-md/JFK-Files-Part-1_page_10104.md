    <td colspan="4">4. Future of the Operation: Although for all intents and purposes the LITAMIL-7 operation has come to an end, the Station intends to maintain a casual contact with her. It is very likely that she may know new personnel assigned to LIRING and may be able to furnish information on these individuals. There may also be developments in LIRING which, on the basis of her lengthy service in LIRING, she might be able to assess and clarify. Since LITAMIL-7 was not a salaried agent for the Station, the operation cost nothing more than the investment of Case Officer time, and an occasional gift of flowers.
