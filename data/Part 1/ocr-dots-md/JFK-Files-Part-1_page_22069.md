** CIA has conducted a series of tests designed to determine whether a substance such as metka was being used as a KGB control device For the purpose of these tests, articles of clothing worn by members of the U.S. Embassy and those of other selected Western embassies were sent back to CIA Headquarters The clothing in each case had been sent to Soviet dry-cleaning establishments or had been permitted to hang unattended in unrestricted areas of the Embassy or in the apartments of Embassy employees Special filter paper was applied to these articles of clothing The same paper was applied in Moscow to desk tops, safe drawers, and the like The paper was then subjected to ultra-violet, chemical, and microscopic analysis as well as to examination for traces of radioactivity Results have been consistently negative with a single exception One test, on one item of clothing, produced a sharply positive reaction This article of clothing belonged to a third national diplomat who was a CIA operational support agent in Moscow; he was also the only person known to CIA to have been a victim of radio- active isotope tagging in connection with an operational task (not letter-mailing) which he had performed for CIA.
