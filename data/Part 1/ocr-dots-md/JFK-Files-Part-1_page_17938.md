 - Marshall: Mexico City Telephone Directory

Reference: TNA-7337, 3 July 1957 (50-F-12/3)

1. Reference requested serialization of the Mexico City telephone directory by street address. The matter was discussed with the Headquarters units concerned with this type of work. Due to the time required to complete this job and because of the existing heavy work load, they are unable to undertake this requirement. The requirement could be filled by another government agency after the directory was reduced to abstracts based on a numerical coding system. The outside agency would then punch the related addresses and indices onto cards at a cost of $75 per thousand or $6,250 for 110,000 addresses. A full time person with a knowledge of Spanish would have to be assigned by WHD for this work.

2. The major part of the job would be the first step — creating which is an estimate of what it would require. For one's full time for eight to ten days, the second step—the key punching—would take about two months. The third step—a machine listing—would be a fairly rapid process.

3. Because of the cost and the shortage of personnel, Headquarters regrets that it is unable to undertake the serialization job at this time.

J. C. KING
Chief, WHD

17 July 1957
