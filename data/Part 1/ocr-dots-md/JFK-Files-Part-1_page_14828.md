Black GIs anti-war action reported in South Vietnam

(JANOI .LNS) - More than 100 GIs staged an anti-war protest July 20 in the port base of Qui Nhon, in Kind Hoa province, according to a report by the Liberation Press Agency of South Vietnam.

The report, as transmitted by Prensa Latina, said that the men marched from one end of the base to the other and also marched on the local airport. They reportedly "shouted slogans demanding peace, the end of the U.S. aggression in Vietnam and the immediate withdrawal of the U.S. troops."

At the airport, the black GIs battled with cops who tried to stop the demonstration.
