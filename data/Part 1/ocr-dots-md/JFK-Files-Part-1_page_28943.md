MEMORANDUM FOR: Chairman, CSCS

ATTENTION: Agent Panel

SUBJECT: Appointment of Career Agent
Arthur R. SCHNICKRATH

1. It is requested that Arthur R. SCHNICKRATH be approved for contract as Career Agent in furtherance of the long term objective of Project ZRJEL, this objective being to provide the Commanding Services with an improved standby paramilitary manpower capability to assist the Operating Divisions in emergency situations.

2. Mr. SCHNICKRATH has, over a long period of time, well demonstrated his operational value to this Agency, and thus is more than maply qualified under the terms of R 20-1000 which defines this category of personnel.
