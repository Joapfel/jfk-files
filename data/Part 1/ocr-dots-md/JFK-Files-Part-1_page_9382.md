3. Urgent problem facing the CONGRESS by September 1st in the working out of a legal or constitutional basis of the permanent CONGRESS organization. Who are "members"? How is the International Committee to be maintained; how are additions, subtractions, changes in this and the Executive Committee to be made? Who invites to the next Congress, and who "votes"? A number of proposals should be considered before a final Memorandum is presented. This should be done in conjunction with the man who will probably be General-Secretary and/or Executive Director of the CONGRESS.

4. It is suggested that the original title be kept and made permanent -- namely, CONGRESS FOR CULTURAL FREEDOM/KONGRESS POUR LA LIBERTÉ DE LA CULTURE. The use of the word "CONGRESS" in such connection has its parallel both with the CIO (Congress of Industrial Organizations) in the USA, and with the Congress Party in India.
