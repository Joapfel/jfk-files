26 JULY 1962

FIELD TRACES
LUIS ALBERU SOUTO

MEXICO STATION TRACES REVEAL THAT AMSTOKE-1, LITAMIL-1, LITAMIL-2, LITAMIL-3 AND LITAMIL-7 CONSIDER ALBERU TO BE ANTI-COMMUNIST. AN ARMY REPORT DATED 25 AUGUST 1959, STATED THAT COLONEL ARTURO CORONA, G-2 MEXICAN ARMY, BELIEVED ALBERU TO BE A COMMUNIST AND ONE OF THE MOST INFLUENTIAL MEMBERS OF THE CUBAN EMBASSY STAFF. THIS WAS REPORTED IN MEXI 1506, DATED 26 JULY 1962.

WILLIAM K. HARVEY
