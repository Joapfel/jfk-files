25 September 1967

**MEMORANDUM FOR:** WU/SAO

**ATTENTION:** Mr. Anderson

**SUBJECT:** JROD Counterinsurgency Target List

There follows a list of Mexican individuals who fall within the
Subject categories:

a. Arnoldo MATELLIER - First Secretary of the PGM Secretariat. He was the CP Mexico contact with the CP Panama in 1963. From 1954 to January 1956 he underwent training in the U.S.S.R. In 1956 he attended the Marx/Lenin Institute in Moscow for four months.

b. Manuel Jesus Tzavas-Guerrero - Newly elected Second Secretary of the PGM Secretariat. In 1966, he attended the Cadre School in Moscow for a year. He has made numerous trips to the Soviet Union, Communist China, and Cuba and is reportedly a graduate of a Havana school for agitation.

c. Jose Encarnacion Perez-Gaytan - Newly elected Third Secretary of the PGM Secretariat. He was arrested in 1959 for his part in the railroad strike of 1959 and was released in 1963. In 1965 he went to Moscow to attend the 6-month Cadre School.

d. Alejandro Menéndez Garcia - First Secretary of the Federal District Committee of the PGM. It is noteworthy that he functioned as liaison man between the PGM and the Cuban Embassy since 1962 after having been invited by CASTRO to visit Havana. He took a 2-year CP training course in the U.S.S.R. from 1950 to 1954.

e. Esther CHAPA-Tijerina. Prominent active Mexican Communist
who works incessantly in behalf of the Chinese Communists.

SECRET
