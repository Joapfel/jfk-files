Contact Report

Subject: LIBACON-1 (P-4305)
Date of meet: June 21 at the Cafeteria JET at 1100 hrs.

1. This meeting was held in lieu of the prearranged meeting for June 18 which L-1 cancelled by phone. Due to the peculiarities of L-1's working habits I was not able to reach him by phone until June 20 at 1600 hrs when this meeting was arranged.

Next meet: Wednesday July 2 at 1615 hrs at the previous pick up place.

2. On June 18 L-1 was without car and he felt obliged to cancel the meet. He stated that he felt happy to have made up with his wife and to be living again home. He told me about his arrangement to go to the Russian show with LIOVAL-1 and wife. He described me the developments as LIOVAL-1 reported them to me.

3. With regard to his & "contact" with LOGINOV which he hinted by phone, there was nothing to it. L-1 was at a party given by Andrea CATANO (P-13,545) on June 14 at her house and YURIY CHIRNISH (P-13,042) was there and they exchanged few words about LOGINOV (see the attachment). L-1 stated that he was not with LOGINOV since the encounter in Cuautitla and did not speak to him since the attempt to reschedule the lunch which was missed on May 9. Therefore, the story L-1 gave to LIOVAL-1 on June 15 about LOGINOV being disappointed with the fact that he had to return home, etc., has, quite likely, no connection with any recent developments. Further, it is quite obvious that L-1 did not know then about the fact that LOGINOV was postponed 3 - 4 months.

4. L-1 stated that A. CATANO and her mother told him that they had expected EOGATYREV (P-11,351) to attend their party, but he neither came nor excused himself by phone. L-1 did not know how it came to the point that EOGATYREV was invited to the party. L-1 considered it as a significant fact that CERMINISH came to that party with his wife. L-1 interpreted this fact to the effect that CERMINISH gave up the idea of having a romance with ANDREA CATANO, but wanted to preserve and maintain friendship with her and her family.
