re STALIN PHOTO-BOOK
From CFW's Log for 17 May 1951

This morning I spent an hour and a half with Lyle Munson and
Mrs. Sherry? (SE), going over Boris Shub's narrative-in-pictures of the
career of Stalin. Lyle showed us 114 finished layouts / i.e. 57 two-page
spreads / of an eventual 172 or 174 and told us that the remainder would be
finished in about 3 weeks. He also said that 60 pages of the text were now
finished, and the rest would be done in about 6 weeks. He has asked us to provide
him with the following material:

a. A memorandum from Bob Winston about the Finnish Air Force's 60 to 1 preponderance in its war with Russia.

b. A memorandum from Harold Gosnell tracing US aid to Russia.

c. A set of photos of the Katyn Massacre and a copy of John Broderick's summary. / I sent Shub Katyn photos several weeks ago_/.
