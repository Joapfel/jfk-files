* FARRIS AIRPORT

If you make your home or residence for a prolonged period abroad, you should begin at the beginning of each stay. The customs and laws may differ from those of your home country.

• INSURANCE BY NATIONALITY

You may issue an United States certificate of insurance by stating that, in case of accident, you want it to be issued in the name of the insured person, or by giving the name and address of the place where you wish it to be issued. It will then be issued in the name of the United States government, and so long as it remains current it will be good evidence of your insurance, or by making it part of your passport, you can secure it even if you leave the United States. For information, write to:

A. M. Best Insurance Company,
P. O. Box 1395, New York, N. Y.

• CIVILIAN APPLICATIONS FOR AID

If you are an applicant for aid who is not physically fit for the performance of his normal duties because of illness or injury, or in the event of any other physical or mental ailment, or when he has been disabled from performing his ordinary duties, whether medical or psychiatric, or when, after being disabled from performing his ordinary duties, he is unable to perform the same duties in the same manner, or when, after being disabled from performing his ordinary duties, he has become unable to perform the same duties, either in the same amount or in the same manner as before, his application for aid must be processed and approved by the Director General of Civilian Applications for Aid.

* IMMIGRATION INFORMATION OF VICTIMS

This passport applies also to its resident citizen. You must submit any application for the right to remain in the United States and to any other benefit to which you are entitled, whether on the basis of the law or otherwise, at the request of the Government of the United States, and by the same means that was used to obtain the visa. Only authorized representatives of the Government of the United States are authorized to receive applications for admission under this system.
