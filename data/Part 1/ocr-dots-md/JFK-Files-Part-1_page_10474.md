OPERATIONAL INFORMATION REPORT

This report has been prepared by the DOMESTIC CONTACT SERVICE, DDI. The operational information contained herein may not be disclosed to personnel outside of the CLANDESTINE SERVICE, CIA, without the prior permission of the Domestic Contact Service.

1. On 20 June 1973 the Miami Field Office responded to a call on the listed line from a man who identified himself as Rolando Masferrer. Mr. Masferrer requested to meet with Matt MacVane of the Miami Field Office. Mr. Masferrer said that he had some information concerning one Guillermo Iglesias (subject of OOA(3) 322/17013-73) that would prove to be of interest to the Central Intelligence Agency. Rolando Masferrer lives at 6080 W. Fiegl er Avenue, Miami, Florida. As a result of his request Matt MacVane and Luisessa-Soto of the Miami Field Office responded to the call and met with Rolando Masferrer that afternoon.

2. Mr. Masferrer related details of his relationship with one Guillermo Iglesias, age about 38, an individual on whom there are other Agency references. According to Masferrer, Iglesias approached him in late March 1973 and identified himself as working for the Central Intelligence Agency. Iglesias said that he was trying to raise a large sum of money to finance secret CIA operations. Iglesias said that CIA funds have been tied up as a result of the Watergate affair. He referred to these operations as black operations. Mr. Masferrer said that he knew several high ranking government officials in Colombia, Venezuela, and Guatemala. Masferrer contacted several of these friends in South America and asked them to aid the Central Intelligence Agency in raising funds for secret operations in South America and the Caribbean. Masferrer has contacted Manuel Vega, a retired Colombian army colonel and presently head of the Colombian Internal Police. He has also contacted one Alvaro Vargas, who is an advisor in arrangements to the Colombian National Police. Masferrer also passed Iglesias the name of Carlos Cabri, head of the Venezuela Internal Police. Iglesias showed a great interest in small arms of all types. Masferrer said that he personally turned over five M-26 fragmentationemi had grandes, one M-16 automatic rifle, one AR-15 semi-automatic rifle, and several bandolines 3d caliber type. Masferrer was led to believe that the weapons would be used in a secret CIA operation in Puerto Rico. This operation was described by Iglesias as a black operation.
