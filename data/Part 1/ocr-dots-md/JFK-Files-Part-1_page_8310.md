4. Subject had the following comments and observations to make as we went through the cards:

**SOLOMATIN (P-7452):** departing POS on 28 June 1969. He's suffering with an ulcer. He takes sleeping pills constantly and has been complaining about the great amount of work he has. Said his mother in the USSR (who neither smokes nor drinks) is very religious. She keeps a picture of Christ hidden at home and many of her women friends come to pray in front of it. SOLOMATIN did not come to Subject's house for dinner on 1 June as planned because he couldn't get permission. SOLOMATIN's wife will have 3 months off when they return to the USSR in order to rest up from her work here, but SOLOMATIN will have only 22 days off. He's going to take charge of some wood working shops with lathes and other machinery.

**PODI SHOY (P-1369):** able to speak very little Spanish.
**FUGITIV (F-9027):** speaks very good Spanish. Very aggressive and not very pleasant.
**VILLAR (F-820):** strikes Subject as offeninate.
**JUAN (F-1657):** shout to leave, replacement not known.
**ROCHELIX (F-1176):** his wife is a doctor.
**GONZAY (F-636):** nice guy; speaks good Spanish; warm diplomatic and positive type.
**HOLLY (F-562):** very smart guy. Makes o'clock features.
**LUCI (C-7141):** his wife is a member of the secret police.
For arrivals: on 19 May, two new officials come into the store. One speaking Russian, and one speaking English, and one speaking German.
