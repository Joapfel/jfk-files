22 JUL 1969

REORDER FOR: Chief, Contract Personnel Division
SUBJECT : Raymond H. CREDEDE, Contract Assistant

1. It is requested that the contract for Raymond H. CREDEDE, Independent Contractor be amended effective 1 October 1969 to extend the term for an additional year and to increase compensation from $6,500. to $7,000. per annum.

2. The Mexico Station recommendation, concurred with by WMD, was based on CREDEDE's initiative overall performance, and demonstrated capability to perform assigned duties.

John R. Borton
Acting Chief
Eastern Hemisphere Division
