MEMORANDUM

(On Americans of Central and East European Descent)

From Jerzy Lerski, Vice Chairman of the Polish Political Council's working Committee in the U.S., 612 4 Street, 9., 1pt. 15, Washington 3, D.C.

The last three decades of the past century and the first two of the present one, mark the period in which millions of the impoverished peasants of central and eastern Europe rose to the happy lives of free America. There are over at least twelve million U.S. citizens of Central and East European descent. This is, of course, a best careful estimate. Congressman Kenneth J.
