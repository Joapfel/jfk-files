### I. SENIOR OBJECTIVE:

To discover and/or develop such basic working principles and operating concepts as can be derived from the acquisitions and knowledge accumulated by the Chinese Services in operating against the Soviet Union, European Satellites, and Communist China.

### II. SPECIFIC CHARACTERISTICS OF COURSE:

The Senior reviewed the present operational situation and the operational future of the various Chinese Services' programs against the Golds. Emphasis was on the results achieved, present operational status, specific problems and recommendations. The Senior will hold half-days (9:00-12:30 hours) for two weeks (25 April-6 May), including, as well, two clean-up workshops (7-22 July, 1 June). The first week consisted of lectures on Orbit countries, surrounding the present operational situation; the second week was devoted entirely to Senior discussions conducted by Harry A. Roushke, Chief, Operations School.
