SUBJECT { GENERAL - Operational
         SPECIFIC - Raymond M. GERLYEDE

DATE 10Oct57 i 1 5/2

The following is the evaluation of Raymond H. GERLYEDE written by the instructor of foreign trainees on 12 September 1957.

1. Instruction commenced 10 June and was concluded 12 July 1957. It covered tradecraft, observation, direction, easily and reporting, principles of CE and CI, elicitation and interviewing, investigative techniques, and briefings in photo and use of recording machines.

2. Trainee was known as "Ray". He was easy to work with. He was always pleasant, courteous, cheerful and seemed (seemingly) interested in every phase of the training. He can speak, read and write English without any difficulty.

3. Ray seemed well motivated; he is a Prussian than Mexican. He possesses a strong interest in overseas work. He is an avid reader. He demonstrated ability to apply principles he had learned from reading and study. He seemed possessed of initiative although there was a limited opportunity of observing this quality in action.

4. While Ray's duties to date apparently have not given him a wide and varied experience in cladeatine activity, he seemed to grasp the material quite readily, and although in the field of CI and Ce he stated he had never thought of many of their aspects as applying to his, he seemed glad of the opportunity to learn and absorb them quite readily. He now possesses a good working knowledge of fundamental principles involved. However, it is felt that if he be to work primarily in this field, it should be under the supervision of a case officer with considerable experience and background in this type of activity.
