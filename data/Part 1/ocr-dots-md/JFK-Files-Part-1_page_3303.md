     A. Jose Narez "S.A.T." (Heary, left) served as the head of the Communist Party, an active member of the Communist League, and a revolutionary organization in Cuba. During his stay as a federal agent, he was involved in the Leftist Student Front, and transferred (to an organization called "Cuba Anti-Español") the name of his brother, Joaquin (Joan), the son of the late General Theodorico García García, Jose Narez's son-in-law. He reported that his brother was a Communist.

     Eloy Acuña, later of Bteh. Final "COMMITTEE," then "ATTORNEY WILLIAMS," called on Jose Narez, the last remaining leader of the Cuban Communist Party (Comité Central de la Revolución). He was the only one who managed to get into the cell where the dead agent was. It took him two years to finally bring him out, and then he was put in jail again for another six months. When he was released, Acuña, with others, organized the "Este Juzgo es el juzgo Púnible." (This states that about this time the Committee set up a special Committee of Experts.) In 1953 Acuña was a member of the National Medical Association which the Party had organized. In attempt to seize control of the leadership of the National Medical Association, in 1954 Acuña tried to connect himself to the party. He failed, but remained in close contact with it until his death in 1956.
