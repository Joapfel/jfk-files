13 May 1948

I certify that on 6 March 1948, I, Newton Scott Miller, U.S. citizen,
was married, in accordance with United States State Department regulations
governing foreign marriage of United States citizens, to Miss Nell Catherine
Stine in Mukden, Manchuria.

Mr. Nell Stine Miller, as of 6 March 1948, is my legal dependent and
is entitled to all my material possessions, including whatever sum of money
is deposited in my name in any bank, and including whatever sum of money is
deposited under a joint bank account by my legal allottee, my mother, Mrs.
Elizabeth C. Miller, in any bank, in the event of my decease.

Mrs. Nell Stine Miller is also the legal beneficiary of my National
Service Life Insurance policy number 16 256 563.

Witness

JOHN H. CLASSEN

date 13 may 1948

Witness

JOHN H. STILES

date 13 may 1948
