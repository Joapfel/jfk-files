Convert to Markdown# Victor Rico Gallegos membership in the following organizations

FII - members subscribe to an active campaign to out U.S. business interests from Mexico and all Latin America, and to fight against U.S. interests and influence in Mexico and throughout Latin America. Members support the Cuban revolution, the Mexican Government's policy of non-intervention and auto-determination and pledge themselves to participate in activities which will bring greater economic, personal and social freedom to the people of Mexico.

There is no indication (at least in public/official FII documents) that the FII or its branches have any allegiance to any foreign power. It has, however, until 1965, been coöperate and instrumental in the Mexican Communist Party which FII gives allegiance to the Soviet Union. The FII and the other Communist groups with which Rico Gallegos has been associated, support Soviet foreign policy objectives, usually without any reservations.

The Mexican Journalists Principles of Cuba and the Committee for Solidarity with Cuba follow this same lines as the FII - with emphasis placed on the distribution of propaganda clarifying the Cuban revolution, and advocating the Cuban Government as the ideal for all Latin American countries.

Mexican-Cuban Institute of Cultural Relations - emphasizes exchange of cultural and academic information, exhibits materials, artists, etc., always, of course, extolling glorious Cuba and urging Mexico to follow Cuba's lead.
