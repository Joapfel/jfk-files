~~Sep 4, 1981~~ - Hong Kong Parolee Chu (Lee) will be released on June 5.

(A-27-85-271)

REF : Chairman's Memorandum 7 June 81

1. The local office of the IANS requested the file of Mrs. Chu from the Houston Office. This revealed the previously unpublished information that Mr. Chu's husband died in Pori, Finland in 1958 where he had worked as Manager of the Pin Chong Food Company. This file further revealed that Mrs. Chu had come to the United States under the pseudonyms of the International Rescue Committee and was being sponsored by Morning Star of 5101 Elmendorph, Dallas, Texas.

2. She was contacted at 4321 Travis Street where she is living with her six children. She corroborated much of the information contained in her IANS file and advised that she was not in contact with anyone on the mainland of China but occasionally heard from friends in Hong Kong. She is looking for work and hopes to find a job as a waiting or possibly a filing clerk. She stated that she had left the country in 1949 and had no recent information on conditions in Communist China. Since her potential appeared extremely limited, she was not questioned on the checklist guides and it is suggested that this case be closed.

J. WALTER MUSE
