XXIV

1. ON MORNING 26 OCT, WHEN BECAME APPARENT LITEMPOS UNABLE PERSUADE GOVERNACION SWITCH PAPERS OF GONZALEZ FOR LATER ARREST, STATION PLACED LIEEMRACE SURVEILLANCE ON GONZALEZ AT HOTEL ASTORIA. (LIEEMRACE COVERAGE HAD BEEN WITHDRAWN WHEN LITEMPOS FOUND GONZALEZ, AS STATION DID NOT WANT LITEMPOS AND LIEMBRACES TRIPPING OVER EACH OTHER AT ASTORIA). LIEEMRACES DID NOT PICK UP GONZALEZ UNTIL MORNING 28 OCT WHEN HE DEPARTED FOR AIRPORT AND FLEW TO CUBA.

2. CLANDESTINE PHOTOS TAKEN OF GONZALEZ AT AIRPORT BY LIEMBRACES CLEARLY SHOW HIM IN COMPANY OF MANUEL VEGA AND ROGELIO RODRIGUEZ, KNOWN DOI OFFICERS. POUCHING COPIES.

3. INDEX ELICIO DARENTO GONZALEZ MORENO/
