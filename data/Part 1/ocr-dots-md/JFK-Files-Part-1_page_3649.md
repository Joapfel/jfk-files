MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Chief, Western Hemisphere Division
SUBJECT : Dr. "Manuel Artine Buesa

1. Dr. Manuel Artine Buesa has been a close friend of mine since we first met in Nicaragua in late 1966 or early 1967. It was as a result of Artine's revelations at that time that we recovered several tons of AMGOLD arms and munitions which had been secretly buried at El Bluff. I last saw Artine in Tegucigalpa in July or the first days of August 1972.

2. Artine's business activity has narrowed down pretty much to exporting meat from Central America and arranging its importation into the United States at Miami. His principal partner abroad is General Anastasio Sonoda Debayle of Nicaragua. I am not sure with whom he deals in the U.S. although he has mentioned on a couple of occasions a business connection with Edgard Buttarri (not sure of this snelling) who, in turn, is supposed to be tied in with "Bebe" Rofozo. I have also heard Artine mention the "United States Meat Company" or the "American Meat Company" (not sure which) as an importer of his Nicaraguan meats.

3. Artine was quite active during the past year trying to get a meat export business going in Honduras. Active in concert with Sonoda, and using to some extent the Honduran connections of Teofilé Omar Babun Franco (another close friend of mine), Artine was attempting to refurbish an idle meat packing facility at Puerto Arturo on the north coast of Honduras near Tela. The principal motivating force in this Honduran venture was to have been General Oswaldo Lopez Arellano. After repeated attempts to get the business rolling, Artine threw up his hands in disgust about July 1972 owing to Lopez' failure to carry through on the Honduran end.
