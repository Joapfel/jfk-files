FORM FORM NO. 10

UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT
Memorandum

TO : Record

FROM : DDAldrige

SUBJECT: Mr. Grimeson I. Lynch

DATE: 25 July 1971

Mr. Lynch telephoned today and advised that he did not want his termination bonus to be held for payment in Calendar Year 1972 as agreed upon, but would like to have it paid at the time of his retirement. I pointed out that this would result in a good tax bite, but Gray stated that he would need the money at the time of retirement and would just have to sustain the additional tax.

Mr. Lynch also asked if he could obtain an advance on his salary of about $500 as his salary checks were nalled to a bank in Florida and he had exhausted his supply of EMB checks because he had run up against he would have left the area by now. He was advised that this could be done but that it would take a mono from him to the Director of Finance. He stated that he would come to the office on 16 July to prepare the memo. He should be sent to C/SCD/R&F to initiate the memo and the action to obtain the advance on his salary.
