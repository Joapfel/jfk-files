The Partido Popular Socialista (PPS) has held infrequent meetings about twice a month in San Luis, Sonora recently, according to a reliable source. In February, they plan to hold meetings weekly in connection with stopped-up activities during the coming political campaign. The local committee from San Luis, Sonora, headed by LORETO HOLEA-VIIIA, recently were successful in forming a block or group of the PPS at Camp 57, Sonora. The source states this group has been organized strictly for political purposes and that the membership have not so far been undertrained as regular members of the PPS. (CONFIDENTIAL.)
