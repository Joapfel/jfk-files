# 10. Soviet Military Activities

Monthly reports are prepared from LIAT information on contacts between Soviet and Texan military personnel. They reflect the identities of the local military figures with whom the Soviets maintain contact and in most cases contain some data on the reasons for the contact. These reports are of particular interest to the U.S. service attaches in Mexico.

## 6. Movements of Soviet Officials

The arrivals and departures of Soviet officials have been reported in almost every instance during the past five years through LIAT coverage. Frequently the station has determined forthcoming departures long before they took place by careful study and interpretation of LIAT materials indicating Soviet contacts with local clothiers and other merchants selling luxury items - it is the habit of Soviets, prior to departure from Mexico, to stock up on items which are not available in the U.S.S.R. This type of coverage is of particular interest to DRESVO.

## f. Soviet Embassy Contacts

Monthly reports of contacts by members of the Soviet diplomatic corps are compiled and submitted to Headquarters. These reports are of considerable value in planning agent operations against the Soviets. They are also of operational assistance in identifying local and foreign Communists with whom the Soviets maintain regular contact. The following reports are examples of this type of coverage:

M-2376, dated 3) January 1957, "Contacts with Soviet Embassy, Mexico City, December 1956."

M-2459, dated 27 February 1957, "Contacts with Soviet Embassy, Mexico City, January 1957."

## c. Personality Reports on Soviet Officials

LIAT has been a very productive source for collecting personality information on Soviets. Such data as Soviet contacts, marital situations, official duties, etc., are of value in determining which officials may be susceptible to recruitment
