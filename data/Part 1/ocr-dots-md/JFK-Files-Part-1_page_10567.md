2. The author of this bulletin is Solando FASPER R Rojas, who uses it in some of the pseudonyms he formerly used in his articles in the paper, El Tiemno, of New York, such as "I, motus," "Miguel Peralta" and Criao.

3. 4. This bulletin follows the same line as the other bulletin put out by FASPER R under the name ^_Chora_, which was also called the "official organ of the municipality of Holguín." (See E-7314.)

5. In this bulletin FASPER R attacks the U.S. government and its departments and agencies. These attacks are underlined in red in the attached copy.
