6. GALICIA, Concha - Influential PGT member (6)

CARDOZA's wife lived at Concha GALICIA's home in February 1958 and reportedly the home served as a means of communications between Guatemala and Mexico (7). In 1957, she was the mistress of Florencio MENDEZ Orozco, (201-787943) who in 1965 was a businessman, owner of a drug store, in Guatemala.

8. *GUTIERREZ Garbini, Victor Manuel, Secretary-General of the Confederacion General de Trabajadores de Guatemala (CGTG) (7)*

During December 1961 to February 1962, relations between Victor Manuel GUTIERREZ and CARDOZA worsened considerably (7).
