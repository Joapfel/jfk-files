On 31 October the SALPETER'S moved from Hotel "Schweizerhof" to the smaller hotel "Linnæus". On this day they finally resolved to be requested refur'd by the FAA airline's office.

Sunday, 19 November the requested tourist vias were issued
to the SALPETER'S, valid for 6 days and including Hotel reservation
for 5 days at St. Petersburg. They immediately departed
by train and left Switzerland via Buchs/rail at 10:24 E. for Austria,
travelling on paid-class tickets. PRICE-FNAGEL returns.

In the period of 31 October to 18 November the couple did not make or receive any telephone calls and had no visitors, nor did they call on anybody.

19 November 1956.
