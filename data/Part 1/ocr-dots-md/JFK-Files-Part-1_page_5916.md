Transmitted herewith is the Memorandum of Oral Commitment for Mr. Alberto PONZOA y Alvarrez (aka "Junior").<br/>2. Subject was originally a contact of the Havana Station and in the summer of 1960, using his boat PISCIS, made the first of four successful sea rendezvous operations with MATUSA TME. He was subsequently forced to escape from Cuba, in PISCIS, in December 1960. Since this date Subject has sailed on many maritime operations in WASP. MATUSA TME and on several Cuban-crewed boats. On several occasions he has effected rubber-boat beach landings to meet reception parties under extremely hazardous circumstances.<br/>3. In Miami Subject was associated with the AMDEPs but has become disenchanted with this group because of constant political squabbles and their lack of ability to perform to his satisfaction. He now wishes to disassociate himself completely from any Cuban political group and desires to place himself under KUBARK control.<br/>4.
