SECTION 14

This section is provided as an aid in describing the individual. For description, see instructions. Each category is to be interpreted literally.

On the left hand side of the page below are a series of statements that apply only if at least one degree to each person. On the right hand side of the page are four major categories of descriptions. The scale within each category is divided into three small blocks; this is to allow you to make final judgments of you so desire. Look at the statement on the left - then check the category on the right which best fits how each the statement applies to the person you are rating. Placing an "X" in the "Not Observed" column means you have no opinion on whether a phrase applied to an individual. Placing an "X" in the "Does Not Apply" column means that you have the definite opinion that the description is not at all suited to the individual.
