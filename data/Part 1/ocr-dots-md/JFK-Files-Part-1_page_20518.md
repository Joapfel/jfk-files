1 June 1967

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Mr. Howard Hunt

Mr. Hunt, on instructions of the Deputy Director for Plans, and with approvals from the DCI and the DDCI, was converted to contract employment and assigned to Spain for a special undertaking in behalf of the DD/P. He left for Spain in July 1965 and returned to the U. S. in June 1966 having completed his assignment successfully. If an evaluative comment is needed to cover his work during this period of time he should be rated as "strong".
