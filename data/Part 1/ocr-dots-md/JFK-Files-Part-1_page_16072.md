The operation of the HUNTER and KILLER teams would be encouraged and prohibited from entering into any area of combat activity, whatsoever.

During the operational phase, (4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, and 9th months) no personnel would follow closely so closely as to hamper their own or others’ counterparts in performing their tasks. The evaluation of team activities during the entire operational phase continuing evaluation of the NCTTR Program would be carried on at some time between the 5th and 6th month(s) a mutual decision would be made with TRDC officials for recommendations to be made to the RTAF Supreme Command for the expansion of team activity and the inclusion of the two additional Special Operations Hunter/Killer Platoons into a Special Operations Hunter/Killer Company for further operations and protections for additional villages. These operations would be continued to include deep penetration operations as appropriate and permissible providing the RTAF obtains permission for across the border operations “in hot pursuit” of Communist terrorists. During the entire insertion and operational phase, it is to be understood that psychological operations will be conducted at all times in conjunction with Special Operations Hunter/Killer Team activity. The final three months of the 12 month NCTTR program schedule would be devoted to further expansion as necessary of Special Operations Hunter/Killer team activity and/or organization of further Special Operations Hunter/Killer Companies for employment throughout the border areas of Thailand.
