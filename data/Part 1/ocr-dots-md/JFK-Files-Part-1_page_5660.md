TO : Director, Domestic Contact Service
Attn : International Division (GAlyea)
FROM : Chief, Philadelphia Office

PHI-457-68
DATE: 24 December 1968

SUBJECT: Letters From Cleland Charles, Haiti, to US Officials and Businessmen

Ref : Phil 00-3-321/36501-68 dated 17 Dec 68

1. Attached for your information are letters from Cleland Joseph Charles addressed to Vice President Robert H. Humphrey and President-elect Richard Nixon. These letters were received in person from Charles, in Haiti, by Donald Z. Rowe, source of reference, and brought to the US by the latter. The letters have not been delivered as of this date. Rowe plans to ask the New Jersey Third District Member of the US House of Representatives to deliver them.

2. In addition to the correspondence referred to above, Rowe also received letters of introduction from Charles to:

* Howard L. Burris (believed to be a lawyer)
  Suite 101, RCA Building
  1725 K. Street, N.W.
  Washington, D.C. 20006

* Mr. A. E. Incleison, Vice President
  J. G. White Engineering Corp.
  80 Broad Street
  New York, N. Y. 10004

* Monsieur Albert J. Krieger (believed to be an accountant and business acquaintance of Charles)
  401 Broadway
  New York, N. Y. 10013

* Mr. Herman Albert Miller (an accountant)
  230 W. 41st Street
  New York, N. Y. 10036

* Mr. N. V. Rosello (an old friend and very reliable acquaintance of Charles)
  Export Credit Western Hemisphere Corp.
  477 Madison Avenue
  New York, N. Y. 10022

The addressees are individuals from whom Charles is seeking financial assistance.

JAY B. L. REEVES
