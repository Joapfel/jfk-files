MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
REGARDING MILITARY SERVICE OBLIGATION

I understand that in accepting a position with the Central Intelligence Agency while subject to Induction into the Armed Forces under the Selective Service Act, I cannot and will not expect deferment from such military service by virtue of my employment with the Central Intelligence Agency.

SIGNED Josephine Rieder

WITNESS George Reder

DATE 31 May 57
