ALIEN (SITUATION) FROM FERDIN
CASE 41,612 - JAROSlav KRUPNÍK

1. FROM CHAIR, STATEMENT TAKEN BY JANS (ORIGINAL NOT CL), USING INTERPRETER FOR GERMAN (NOT CZECH), SOME CERTIFICATE FACTS;

2. DR. JAROSlav KRUPNÍK, BORN 25 APRIL 1926 IN OSTRAVA,
CZECHORUVANIA; MIFE ZEFNA NEE PUROVEROVA; MARRIED 23 JULY 1955;
ONE DAUGHTER, 2-1/2 YEARS; FATHER (DENTIST), DR. LUDVIK
KRUPNÍK; MOTHER MARIE NEE KRUPEK; ALL RESIDING OSTRAWA,
ZOROVSKA 22. SISTER, MILITA KRUPLOVA, SAME ADDRESS.
NO RELATIVES IN FREE WORLD.

3. AT HOSPITAL OF STEEL AND IRON WORKS, VITROVICE, OSTRAVA,
WAS CHIEF ANESTHETIST, HAD TO STAFF. CANE INSTRUCTION WHEREVER
ANESTHESIA USED, WHETHER IN DEPARTMENT OBSTETRY, NESE AND

THERAPY, OR OTHER. ANESTHESIA NOT SO WELL DEVELOPED
CZECHORUVANIA AS U.S., OFTEN ADMINISTERED BY NURSES
RATHER THAN U.K. TONG STATE EXAMINATION IN SURGERY JUNE 1956; STATE
EXAMINATION IN ANESTHESIA FEBRUARY 1958. AFTER COMPLETING
DEGREE MEDICINE AT CHARLES UNIVERSITY 1951, WAS CITY HOSPITAL
OSTRAVA NO. 1 AS INTER 1951-52, CAME TO PLANT HOSPITAL 1952,
WHERE HE WAS SECRETARY, SURGEON OF FIRST DEGREES 1956-59; A
ASSISTANT AT SECOND DEGREE 1960.
