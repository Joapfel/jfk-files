5 Oct. '50

"STALENT" PHOTO-BOOK PROJECT (to be named KILLNOUT)

Is a project for preparation of a book of photographs-plus-text
on the career of Stalin.

Then formally written up, it will be titled KILLNOUT, and will
be a sub-project of SUTRI, requiring only the approval of IST.

Some photographs have been assembled, from CIA Graphics and from
agencies in N.Y., and a legal agreement has been drafted and signed,
CIA and
between/through Robert H. HEADLEY (preliminary draft for B.S.), who will prepare
the book for publication in the U.S. under his own name, retaining the copyright,
which (together with royalties) he will assign to CIA.

Mr. Headley is now working on the book. It is planned that, after
publication in the U.S., there will be editions (some perhaps subsidized)
in many foreign areas.

**ATTENTION**

I assume you have the details on this.

**CONFIDENTIAL**

I am listed as responsible for this, but know nothing about it. (I am
going to look at the file today). Ray Lillard and IST know about it.

"STALIN'S BIOGRAPHY"

This, I believe, is not technically a project. But I understand that
Bob H. FEADLEY, from contract agent, has instructions from IST, after finishing
the "Stalin" photo-book, to complete a biography of Stalin, for publication
in the U.S. under his own name; which the expectation of foreign editions to
follow.
