G U D E L I B A R G H O S E V I L L E S E C R I P T

MAY 24, 1959.

The White House said Tuesday that it had been informed that a Cuban man was missing in his car on State Street and that he might have fallen down the steep side of a cliff along with another passenger in the same car. The man's name was Adolfo Perez and the other one was the driver of the car. The two men were both 25 years old, about 6 feet seven inches tall and weighed approximately 150 pounds each. Their last known location was at the corner of Seventh and Eighth Streets in New York City.

Mr. Perez had been driving his car in New York for more than three months when he was last seen. He had a black mustache and a mustache ring on one hand. The car was a blue Buick with a red license plate. Mr. Perez left the city the day before and did not return. His last known place was at the foot of the steep hill where the car was lost.
