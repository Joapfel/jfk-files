**D. II, continued:**

1944 **Berlin** He was held for days, chained to a heater in solitary arrest, nobody caring for his hand. Then he was brought into a hospital.

From there he telephoned the Belgian girl, asking some civilian clothes sent to him. The girl promised to bring those. Instead of her came policemen and took him to hospital in Berlin- Teglitz jail.

For months he was daily driven to the Police House for interrogation.

Later he was held in a cellar of the Police HQ. His father came from Vienna and was allowed to see him (somewhere where the evidences of his forgery were displayed).

He wrote to "00008" to report an invention of his - to escape being liquidated. He was interviewed by specialists and asked to work on it. He, Katz and Allumes were transferred to —
Wolfenbüttel Jail, near Hannover, away from the approaching front line. He does not know of Asterée's faith.
