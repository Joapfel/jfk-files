Dear Headley ( pseudonym )

Dear Boris,

Re "The Choice". I hope the Overseas Press Club panel was a success.
Nothing in the "NY Times" about it, but they don't usually write up those
affairs. —I've seen nothing further in the "Post", etc since the mention in
the ed I sent you; will send you anything I see.

I have a complete file, "The Choice", here, with all the reviews, etc.
in it. I went fully into the question of ordering copies, and it is clear
that it is not policy anywhere to promote the distribution of it (or for that
matter, equally clearly, any other book). After all, if the head man himself
/ Bedell Smith / had written "The Choice", there is nothing in his official
charter that would authorize him to promote it in his official capacity; on
the contrary. Nothing anywhere against you personally or the book; it's just
that there is no policy to promote any book of that wide scope.

Therefore, as I imagine you gathered at lunch the other day / with IST/
work in connection with "The Choice" should be during weekends and other free
time.

I hope Winchell's mention of it, and the good "NY Times" review, etc., will
help sales, and that if it hasn't gotten off to a fast start, it will develop
as a "crooner". "Sleeper"
