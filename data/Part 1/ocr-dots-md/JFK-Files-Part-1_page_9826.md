19 February, 1960

Subject: LIVING/4 Report of Conversation with Echemendia ( Ismail Suarez de la Paz).

1. LIKING/4 reported to Rowton at 1030 on 19/2/68 at the Duca d'Este café that she had met with Echemendía for about one hour on 17 February at a small coffee shop off calle Pachuca in Colonia Condesa. She explained that she had telephoned Echemendía from the home of her friend Blanca Peña de Anand and arranged to meet him about 1730 at a nearby corner.

2. LIMING/4 stated that Echemendía was cordial as usual and told her that he hoped - looked forward- t. seeing her again whenever she felt she could go out with him for lunch or dinner. He asked her again about her trip to Havana and she informed him that she hoped to leave on the 23 February flight. Echemendía asked if she thought she might have difficulty returning to Mexico, to which L/4 replied in the negative, commenting that in the past neither the Mexicans nor the Cubans had given her any trouble.

3. According to L/4, Echemendía then queried bar in some detail about how LIVING/3 was doing, his business in Chiapas, where they were living, and what his plans were. She replied in as matter-a-fact way as possible, alleging that L/3 was selling his paintings and doing well in business.

4. Echemendía then commented that the LIVING office locally had commented re L/3 that although they appreciated the information he had passed to them they did not have the sources and details and hence had to view them with suspicion. Echemendía apologized to K&G LIVING/4 for having to say this (me da mucha pena) but he felt obliged to pass it along to her. He added that both Havana and LIVING are accustomed to get sources along with the reports turned in to them and always are uneasy when these are lacking. He agreed with L/4 that L/3 had acted in good faith, but also defended the position of the LIVING office which he said was surrounded by projections and infiltrations.

5. L/4 told Rowton that she did not tell L/3 about thisrendulous with Echemendía ( whose a tentions to L/4 in the past have made L/3 particularly jealous and annoyed) since she did not want to disturb him. LIVING/3, however, reported to Rowton at about 2100 the previous evening (16/2/68) in an obviously irritated tone that by pure chance he had seen Echemendía and LIVING/4 together at a coffee shop about 1800 on 17/2/68. When Rowton explained that he had known of and approved of the meeting beforehand, LIVING/3 calmed down and shrunken it off.
