# CONTACT REPORT

**AGENT**: AMMUG-1
**CASE OFFICER**: Eustace D. KLOOCK
**FUNDS PASSED**: None

**PLACE**: AMMUG-1's home
**DATE**: 1630-1700, 19 May 1971

1. At the time and place indicated above, I reviewed with AMMUG-1 Volumes II and III of the Cuban Mugbook, with the following results:

## Volume II

Armando U. Estrada Fernandez (201-809440) Photo #39

AMMUG-1 correctly identified the pseudo of Estrada as being "ULISES". AMMUG-1 added that he had last spoken with Estrada in February of 1964.

German Miguel Ramos (201-739084) Photo #42

AMMUG-1's only comment was that he wasn't sure if Ramos was his correct name.

Roque Dalton Garcia (201-215097) Photo #118

AMMUG-1 commented that he had spoken with Dalton in September of 1964

Manuel Carbonell Dique (201-739084) Photo #159

AMMUG-1 commented that he knew Carbonell from circa 1960, when Carbonell was a captain in the police.

## Volume III

AMMUG-1 did not recognize any of the photographs contained in Volume III of the Cuban Mugbook.

2. AMMUG-1 was also shown the photograph of Luis Martínez Menocal (201-769095) which was forwarded in UFAW-1118 of 7 May 1971. While AMMUG-1 thought the face familiar, he was unable to identity the photograph.

3. In addition to the above, AMMUG-1 was also shown the photographs of the following individuals with negative results: Jorge López García (201-838320) and Sergio López Otoña Valdes (201-754447).

Eustace D. KLOOCK
