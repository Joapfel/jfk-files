3 August, '67

three page type written letter and 27/10/67
1. LIAING/3 received/latter from René Cohna Firmers (Rivier) & 8/27x
but reached in Mexico City 2/2/67 by "N.C., Carrera P. Janeiro
100, Mexico 7, LF" after having been hand-carried from Havana by the
wife of a "corpanero de trabaje" of Cohoa. Copy being puched.

2. In letter Cohoa states he understands L/3's situation "since it is necessary to move ahead with one's own resources and to prove with deeds". He adds that he hoped to send detritus "concrete and complete news" but he still labeling under difficulties known to L/3. He assures L/3 that he has delivered L/3's last letter to Arnulfo the "corpanero" (Arnol Rodriguez) but could not talk with him privately because of the many visitors who had arrived that day. Arnol's wife Ruby told him that they had received L/3's letters and had answered them (L/3x told Rowton that he has never received these replies from Arnol). Ruby Rodriguez also informed Cohoa that the "organizaciones corres clientes" were going to invite L/3 to Havana. Cohoa promised L/3 that he would do whatever he could to expedite this trip (hacer nuevas gestiones).

3. Cohoa complains of the "realidades que se han tocado vivir acorde" but indicates that he has no intention "resign himself" to bureaucracy commenting that it is necessary "producir la ruptura, apudiendo una rascarte con las propias uñas", taking the initiative and not expecting anything from anyone. He adds that he has finally cut the ties which have bound "ain to Cuba and although he feels that he can do a great deal there, but it is "infinitely small in comparison with what he must do EX outside". He assures L/3 that he is serious and that it concerns a "planteamiento serio".
