It has been stated by friends of ISAACS even during the height of his Trot- skyist career, he never accepted money from any group; that he was open and above board; and that he never engaged in any conspiracies to overthrow established govern- ment. They are also reported to have stated that ISAACS was attracted to Communism by Khaneer idealism."

It has been further reported that immediately after Pearl Harbor, ISAACS broke with the Trotskyite group as he had broken with the Communists", and began writing propaganda for the United States Government.

Some time in the late 1930s, ISAACS returned to the United States.-By-the-fall until the fall of France, he was employed by the Hays News Agency. In October 1943, of 1943, he was working for Newweek Magazine in Washington,D.C. His address as that time was Friendship Heights, Newport Ave., 4938.

By late 1944 or early 1945, ISAACS was in Chungking as a representative of Hainan. During this period, he had a great deal of trouble about censorship, and reportedly was denied permission to visit Communist areas. It has been stated that actually he was merely unfortunate in that he applied just after the government used restored Isas ban on journalistic travel in that direction. At the time he had was denied permission, "because" WHITE of Time had just been allowed to go, and ISAACS felt that he was being disingenuous against it. It has been stated that WHITE, upon his return gave such a good account of administration in the Red areas that this, added to other similar experiences, solidified the government's determination no to let any more American newspapers go up and become Communist converts." In March 1945, ISAACS was reported as about to leave Chungking permanently.
