7. MIE was an applicant for CIA employment in early 1953 and was security approved subject to polygraph on 11 August 1953. He did not enter on duty and in September 1953 the office which had been interested in him was 'no longer interested'. On 13 November 1953, MIE was granted a CIA to permit his use as a contract employee on ACTIVE in New York City. His CIA was cancelled on 26 March 1955.

8. MIE was named as a friend of William Orville MUNELL, #61693-DL. During an investigation of the latter for possible CIA employment in 1952. MIE, according to MUNELL's landlady and social acquaintance in Washington, D.C. during the period of about a year which ended in April 1952, was the only person she knew by name with whom MUNELL was friendly. According to the informant, MIE was a student at Columbia University whose address was 423 Pennsylvania Hall. MUNELL was security disapproved for CIA employment in June 1952 because of his close association on a professional and social basis, while attending Denver University from 1956 to 1959, with persons of questionable loyalty. In November 1954, MUNELL was of interest to project FJALIVE with reference to assignment to Dhahran, Saudi Arabia; however, the interested officer withdrew their request on him, after Security advised of the derogatory information regarding him.

9. MIE from 1951 to 1954 attended Scarndale High School in Scarndale, New York, which was attacked by a Citizens Group, which alleged Communist influence in the school system. He had several questionable associates in addition to MUNELL.
