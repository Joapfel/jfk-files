MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, FI

SUBJECT : Project LIMPTY Amendment

HEADQUARTERS CASE OFFICER

Name: J. B. Brady
Room: 211A Jarton Hall
Ext.: 2535
Date: 23 June 1957

1. It is requested that approval be granted to amend Raymond H. GERENDE's contract to provide him with a termination settlement of one month's salary for each full year that he works for KUBANK and to raise his salary effective July 1959 from $4,080 to $4,560.00 per year. His last salary increase was granted on 1 September 1957. The date, 18 March 1955, has been suggested by the Station as the beginning date for the computation of GERENDE's termination benefits. The Station believes that this benefit will provide GERENDE with the necessary incentive to make KUBANK a career and at the same time will give the station an additional element of control.

2. As pointed out in the LIMPTY Project Approval, GERENDE, an indigenous agent, is one of the station's most valuable assets. He has a responsible full time job as the LIMPTY Principal Agent who handles the LIMITED, LICENCIA and LICALLA base houses, the LIENTRAP operation, the outside photographic processing and translation service and all the agents participating in these activities. He performs his duties in a conscientious and professional manner and his already excellent abilities are showing continued improvement.

3. Honey was already allocated in the LIMPTY project approval for the salary increase. With the effective date of 13 March 1955, for the termination clause, GEORGIAN will already have accrued $1,520 (his salary is $4,560 per year) if he should resign today. The LIMPTY project, as approved, has sufficient funds to cover this cost if it is necessary to obligate this amount during the present project year. Therefore, no additional funds are requested.
