SEB. 21. GEOLOGICAL HISTORY

I have never been employed permanently as I have
been in school all of the time. In the summer I
worked on my Grandmother's farm in Bellevue, Wisconsin.
The few jobs I have had are:

1. Stock boy in Super Market on Saturdays only.
   Apr. Roy. 8 mo. lew., Dec. 1943.
   Name of Employer and Supervisor unknown.
   42nd St. & University Ave., Per Moines, Iowa.

2. Clerk
    Jerrem's Men's Store
    Locust and 7th Streets
    Des Moines, Iowa
    Christmas Holidays only - 1943.
    Employer and Supervisor: Leslie H. Shaw

3. Helper to winces Dresser end display man.
   This was connected with my course of study at
   Tuscaloosa, Ala. High School.
   March, April, May 1941.
   Employer and Supervisor: R. Harlen
   Allen and Jemison Hardware Co., Tuscaloosa, Ala.

4. Paperboy.
   1936-1939
   Louisville Times.
   Supervisor: unknown.
