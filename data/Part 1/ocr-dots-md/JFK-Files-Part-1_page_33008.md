15 August 1972

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, CH/COS

FROM : Thomas H. Keogh, Jr.

SUBJECT : Proposal to Continue Attempt to Establish Channel/Dialogue With ANGHOST-1 (201-810126)

1. As a follow-up measure to our attempt to establish a channel to ANGHOST-1 by using ANCHALK-2 on 10 August in Montreal, I propose that we establish an operation with the same end; on this occasion, however, I propose that at this stage we use AMWOLF-1 at our secure channel to ANGHOST-1.

2. The dramatic performance in this operation are:
(a) ANCHALK-5 (201-307337), reliable, courageous asset who has been involved with and who knows ANGHOST-1; and (b)
AMWOLF-1, Deputy Chief of Center, Cuban Intelligence Services (CUNS), Madrid.

3. Timing of this operation would be for that period in September in which ANCHALK-5 visits Europe.

4. The place for the meeting between ANCHALK-5 and AROZ would be in Madrid, Spain, at a place mutually convenient to both. The meeting would be established either by telephone or by personal contact.

5. The purpose of this operation is to establish a channel of communications/dialogue with ANGHOST-1.

6. This operation involves ANCHALK-5's passing of a sealed envelope with a message in it for AMWOLF-1's unopened transmittal to ANGHOST-1. ANCHALK-5'S instructions would be to pass the sealed and unopened communication to AMWOLF-1. ANCHALK-5 would then tell AMWOLF-1 that the message must be transmitted directly without tampering or being opened to ANGHOST-1. ANCHALK-5 could add that ANGHOST-1 is waiting for this communication. (TTY: Director 148293, dated 13 June 1971, paragraph 2 noted that AMWOLF-1 was contact man in DGI effort recruit ANCHALK-5 in June 1967.)
