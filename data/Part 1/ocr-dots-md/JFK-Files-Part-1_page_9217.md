Feb. 26, 1951

Dear Boris,

Glad to hear from Leo-doe that you are still alive and welling. And, especially, that there is some prospect of your getting down this way. I sure hope you do.

Also glad to hear from our mutual friend William that he's seen you, and to get some news of you from him, and that you will have got some done in general from him.

How is your collaborator coming, and is he soured up with his bosses? What is your best current guess as to the date when it will be wrapped up?

Joe got generally overworked and came down with grippes, and left for two or three weeks or more to recuperate. A lot of people round here have been so. They laid up, I was for a couple of days and an still somewhat under the weather. However, even if I haven't written I've thought of you often.

Drop a line, and don't delay your visit to the Nation's Capital too long.
