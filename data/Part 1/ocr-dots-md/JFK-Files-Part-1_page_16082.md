(2) Ambush controls:

Snipers from II/A teams will be attached to IMA or LIA units and deployed with ambush controls. This technique has been the most successful method of employment. Two techniques will be used extensively:

(a) Snipers accompany a platoon ambush.

When employed with a platoon ambush, the snipers will remain close to the platoon command post. When an element of the platoon observes enemy activity, the snipers are alerted to that location. They then engage selectively at night with the starlight scope and II-A2 suppressors or with the HBT scope and TAN-A2 suppressor during daylight. If there is but one quiet long in the area, he is taken under fire by a sniper. In this way, the ambush position and location of automatic weapons are not compromised due to the quiet operation of the rifle. When a group of communists approach the killing zone, the snipers engage the leaders and radio operators while the platoon fires into the general kill zone. Another technique will be used in an ambush is that of leaving the snipers fire tracers to mark the flanks of an enemy force which has approached the position in an area other than the specified kill zone.
