TO: Director
Federal Bureau of Investigation
Attention: Mr. J. J. Papich

FROM: Deputy Director, Plans

SUBJECT: Sample POLARIZATION

1. Representatives of the FBI Laboratory have been previously briefed on the AMFOTEST which we believed had been used when working on the MOBILE letter and which established the presence of 3 in the latter.

2. We have now been advised by a field representative that a modified version of this process was actually used and it is possible that the 3 is detectable by this version of the test, but not by the regular test of which your Bureau has the details.

3. It should be noted that our work on known systems revealed two cases exhibiting the same type of floating detection as was noted on the POLARIZATION letter. This, of course, does not mean that the MOBILE letter is written in one of these systems, but merely that floating detections can occasionally be expected with this variation of the AMFOTEST.

4. Agency headquarters laboratory has not completed a comparison of the original and modified AMFOTEST procedures so as to confirm the finding of our field laboratory that the modified test is more sensitive. It is being provided to your Bureau at this time, however, since it is a technique which has been used successfully in a current case.
