NAME Raymond H. GERTZE DIVISION WA/71
SUBJECT Audio Briefing
DATE TRAINED 3 July 1957 (133 hours)

On 3 July 1957 an Audio Briefing was given to Raymond H. GERTZES. The major portion of the briefing was on the Revere T-700 tape recorder. When asked if he knew anything about recorders, GERTZE stated that he was quite familiar with the Revere T-700 as he had had some experience with this recorder when he was a disc jockey in a radio station. A step by step operation of the recorder was explained to him, the tape recorder head was explained also how the sound was recorded on the tape. The following were also explained to him: tape speeds, frequency response, footage indicator, dual tracks—how they can be used in the Revere T-700—, the mechanical section of the recorder and the amplifier.

The recorder was removed from the case by the Subject and the possible breakdown points were shown to him, the recorder and amplifier units were taken out of the case by him and the operation of the sections explained. The recorder was then put together by GEFEME.

GERENTE asked many questions relative to microphones and microphone installations. There were explained to him—carbon microphone, crystal microphone, dynamic and controlled reluctance.

He appeared to be intelligent and was interested in the subjects discussed.

EDWARD A. SIVIK
TSS/IRD/Instructor
