Career:

17 May 1962: Transited Mexico en route to the USSR for leave,
9 September 1963: Left Cuba to return to the USSR
September 1963:- July 1955: Officer in KGB Headquarters, Moscow.
3 August 1955: Arrived Mexico

In Mexico LOGNOV replaced Oleg M. NECHPORENGKO, another KGB officer and took over all of NECHPORENGKO's intelligence agents and duties. In January 1956 he attempted to cultivate and recruit a chauffeur at the American Embassy in Mexico. LOGNOV is an unusually active and capable intelligence officer. Since he has almost no overt duties he is able to spend about 95% of his time on intelligence activity. We have known that he is a KGB officer since 1953. His immediate KGB chief in Mexico is Rafael A. YATSKOV.
