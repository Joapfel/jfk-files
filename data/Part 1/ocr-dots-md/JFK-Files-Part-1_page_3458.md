Recent Travels of Naval Artists

Boards of the US Immigration Service show that Naval Artis-
has made several trips in and out of Miami between 5 and 13 May 64.
USBS records show the following:

4. On 20 April Artine was issued on I-512 type visa good for travel to Costa Rica, Nicaragua, and the Dominican Republic. He departed from Miami on 3 May 64 for Jamaica, Nicaragua on Latice Flight number 413. He returned to the US on 11 May 64. No flight information available on return trip.

b. An I-512 was issued in his name on 3 May 64 good for travel to
Costa Rica and the Dominican Republic. Artine left Miami on 11 May and
returned on 12 May 64.

c. Another I-512 was issued in his name on 11 May, and he left
Miami on 13 May on Pan American flight number 503 for Jamaica. His USBS
file does not presently show evidence that he has returned to Miami
from this trip. A reliable USBS official has orally confirmed to us
this date that Artine has not in fact returned from his latest trip
abroad.
