Chief, Washington Field Office

12 July 1972

Director, Domestic Contact Service (033)

Bolando Masferrer Jr., Military Armament Corporation, Powder Springs, Georgia

REF : Van Rapper/Masulin telecons

1. Attached per referent telephone conversations is copy of Don and Bradstreet report as requested on Military Armament Corporation, bad-in-charge, in Department G, please note that Chairman of the Board, is Mitchell Livingston Verbell, III. Verbell is a former Office of Strategic Services (OSS) officer known to this writer during assignments in China during WWII.

2. This Agency, as well as other government agencies, have considerable information on Verbell. He is an old and unsavory, unsolicited, contact of the Miami Field Office dating from 1959. He is of dubious character and reputation who had and has been involved in grandiose schemes and international arms deals. He has had perpetual financial difficulties.

3. Verbell was involved with Bolando Masferrer, Senior, in connection with the abortive Haitian invasion in 1967. This may throw a different light on possible source development of young Bolando Jr. Any approach to him should be executed with some degree of caution. We do not desire to get involved with Verbell. However, we cannot foresee any objection to planned debriefings of Masferrer Jr., following his trips to Latin America and other areas, in pursuit of FPI and possible information of operational significance.

4. If we can be of further assistance in this matter, please advise.

JAMES R. MURPHY
