SUBJECT: Mario DANIEL RIVADULLA y Carcedo

Comments: (Dr) Jose Antonio CuenILALR Aquilera arranged an introduction to RIVADULLA on 8 July, and he has since been seen by Palinger on several occasions.

The 5 January 1955 report was from AMPLECT (HMG-95L); the 6 November 1952 report from AMELU (HMG-27); both were appraised as 3. RIVADULLA, who was denied a visitor's visa in November, 1955 largely on the strength of those two reports from the Station, is still interested in obtaining the visa and willing to clarify his status vis-a-vis the JS and FP with the local OACID.

It is believed this report will assist in answering that portion of the latest periodic requirements list, forwarded under HMG-W-2379, - one of concerning RIVADULLA.
