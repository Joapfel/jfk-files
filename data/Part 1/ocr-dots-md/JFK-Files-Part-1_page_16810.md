ALTHOUGH STATEMENT AGAIN USED VAGUE AND AMBIGUOUS LANGUAGE TO AVOID CLEAR-CUT COMMITMENT TO DEFEND CUBA IN ALL CONTINGENCIES, IT HAS FURTHER ENGAGED SOVIET PRESTIGE IN ENSURING SURVIVAL OF CASTRO REGIME.
