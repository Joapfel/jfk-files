1. LIRING/3 reported to Houston at 1800 on 8/2/67 that he informed by Sergio Valdez that morning that Alberto Domingo of El siempre was taking plane to Havana that afternoon. L/3 stated that he took this opportunity write a best note to Arnol Rodriguez explaining recent developments in Boasés Crozco legal affair and asking Arnol to expedite new contact for L/3 with FAR either in Havana or Guatemala City. L/3 explained to Houston that in order put heat on Arnol and FAR he wrote that if he not invited to havana within next two weeks he will definitely be in Guatemala City staying at Hotel Florida from 21-24 August and that new FAR liaison should identify himself as "Jose Luis". He added that Arpol should cable him using code "La medicina esta en el estado" in event FAR wished him keep out of Crozco affair.

2. L/3 stated that Cerillo Valdez personally asked Alberto Domingo de-liver sealed envelope containing this letter to Arnol Rodriguez. Valdez feels that Domingo will not read letter, although it indicates mentions him as person who (unwittingly) originally referred Crozco to Valdez as LIRING/3.

3. L/3 also reported that he spoke with Ernesto Capoano by phone and has appointment to meet with him at 1000 on 7/9/67. Capoano cautioned L/3 that he did not identify himself by give some indication of nature of business. L/3 stated it concerned "the medicine" and it was then that Mr Capoano gave his appointment.
