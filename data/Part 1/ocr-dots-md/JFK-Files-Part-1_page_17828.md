    <td colspan="3">"Today at 09: local time the First Deputy Minister of the Council of Ministers USSR, A.I. MIKOIAN, and his escorting party flew out of the capital of Mexico on the Il-18 for the Fatherland. LINDSEYIN was seen off at the airport by the Minister of Foreign Affairs of Mexico, Manuel Torres; the Minister of Foreign Trade, Raúl SÁNCHEZ; the Minister of Education, TOGCU Bodet; and officials of the Mexican ministry. The Soviet Ambassador to Mexico also saw A.I. MIGLIAN off, V.I. MARKOV, and workers of the Sovietembassy. Before the flight out A. I. MIKOIAN made a short speech in which he thanked the President of Mexico, LUEZ Yateos, members of the Mexican Government, the whole Mexican people for the warm reception they received, was shown him and his escorting party in their country."</td>
