**William F. Heinlich**

Scheduled to be employed at a salary of $10,000 on December 1949 as a program adviser until March 1950. Worked under General Clay and under General Frank Howley.

Used to be with BIAS (Radio in American Sector). Said he would not be continued because up to the time (no date) records showed three official investigations on the following subjects:

1. When serving as Intelligence Officer, why instead of putting the Goebbels diaries as captured documents into official channels known to him, did he turn them over to private persons in a transaction from which he subsequently expected to profit, both in cash and in a future position with Double Day.

2. When serving as an Intelligence Officer, how did it happen that another captured document being the German appraisal of USAR Intelligence Service was changed in a foot locker sent by Heinlich to his home.

3. When serving as head of BIAS, entertaining at the expense of the station and ultimately, therefore, of the US government at private American parties, why did he accompany a German female singer during her appearance at a German vaudeville theater? In the latter case, the entire BIAS symphony orchestra appeared without payment at the German theater for a period of one week. There was an investigation into the existence of extensive payroll padding at BIAS when subject was informed that the State Department did not wish to inform him and he thereupon resigned.
