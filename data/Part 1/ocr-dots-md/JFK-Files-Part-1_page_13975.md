ELAGIN was shown two photographs (as furnished by your office) of the individual believed to be Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez, EXUN First Secretary and DGI officer, who was engaged in an intelligence activity in Queens on Saturday night, October 1969. ELAGIN identified the better of the two photographs as RODRIGUEZ; his identification was unambiguous. In questioning ELAGIN further to be sure that he was positively convinced that the individual in question was RODRIGUEZ, ELAGIN stated that RODRIGUEZ had recently had his hair cut and that it was now lying flatter on his head and that he was keeping it "slipped down" with water or oil. There was no doubt at all in ELAGIN's mind that the individual was RODRIGUEZ. ELAGIN has known RODRIGUEZ for several years and has seen him here this past month fairly frequently.

2. ELAGIN could not furnish any information as to where RODRIGUEZ might have been over the week-end of 4/5 October. He did not see him on either of those two days.

3. ELAGIN commented that RODRIGUEZ has been spending quite a bit of time lately with Maria de los Angeles FLORES Prida, an Alternate Representative of the Cuban UNCA Delegation. Their companionship is assumed by ELAGIN to be more in the social line than in the professional line. RODRIGUEZ is a bachelor; FLORES is a divorcee.
