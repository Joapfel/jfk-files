3 July 1957

MEMORANDUM FOR: CI/OPS

ATTENTION : Mr. Robert Brown
SUBJECT : Project LITEAT

1. The NH/Nexico Desk is now in the process of requesting the annual renewal of Project LITEAT for the period 1 July 1957 - 30 June 1958.

2. During the past year a considerable amount of operational and intelligence information from the LAMAYE'L phase of this project has been forwarded to your office. In view of the pending project renewal, it would be appreciated if you would forward to the NH/Nexico Desk at your earliest convenience a memorandum evaluating this project with regard to your area and sphere of interest.

J. C. KING
Chief, WHD
