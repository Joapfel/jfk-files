file "STALIN" PHOTO-BOOK

20 Feb. 1951

Re SHUB and BRAINWAVE

At request of Mr. Winston I. phoned & Shub (AC-2-0164) to ask about progress on the Photo-book, and what is the earliest date at which he may be thru with it. No answer.

RAW says Kintner is asking for him for BRAINWAVE. I suggested not interrupting him till he has finished the photo-book. RAW consulted LST, who said to say Shub might be made available on a part-time basis.
RAW understands he would do his work for BRAINWAVE in NY. I said this seemed OK, if it were to be only part-time.

21 Feb. 1951

I phoned Kintner re Shub. He has recently seen Shub in NY.
Shub said he had reached the stage in his work (on photo-book) for us where his collaborator wd hv to do most of the work for about a month.
Kintner said the work Shub wd do for him wd be in NY, and only part-time. I said this all sounded OK. /Shub not referred to by name over the phone/ .

WCS
