Memo for the Records

SUBJECT: First Sistone and Fernam Cover Job Outing with Consular Corps; Brief Initial Exposure to Soviets Leginov and G. Lou Rev

1. Terence D. Sistone and Fernam, both under Consular cover, accompanied (with wives) Frälyer on an outing of the "Cuerpo Consular en Mexico", a social club open to all consular officers regardless of nationality) to new Ford factory in Cuautitlan, lunch and excursion of Span. col. Convent at Tepotzotlan from 1100 to 1000hrs. on 17 July 1969. Consular Corps is creature of Amer. cit and ex-Consul Gen. of Greece, Leander F. Vourvoulins, who led excursion. In addition to helping the two new Sta. officers work into consular cover and make contacts, the purpose of the outing in part was to see at first hand the Sov, Bloc and Cubans who belong to the CC. Three Sovs showed, no identifiable Bloc and no Cubans. MIAMI No other particularly interesting cons. types but door opened to meet people at future CC meets.

2. Three Soviets who shod were Valentin Loginov, well-known to Sta. as KGB under cons. cover, and two men of Commercial Sec. of Sov. Emb. Both Sistone and Fernam independently identified Lev Mihayevich Golozov as one of two commercial types from mug book-were unable identify the other, who was quiet and hardly talked as he tagged along. Golozov was also quiet but was engaged twice by Sistone in conversation. On one occasion said he had been in Mexico short time, was there as result recent Sov-Mex trade agreement, just happened to be around and invited with colleague to Ford outing. On other occasion said Tepotzotlan/golf leaf and general art more ornate and in repressive than Byzantine church-monuments he familiar with in Moscow. spoke Span. with heavy accent and lit in'talk much but was quite fluent nevertheless. MIAMAI Sistone also talked briefly to Loginov and introduced by Frälyer as Frälyer replacement. However conversation is mucinous and uninteresting compared to constant verbal sparring betw. Frälyer and Loginov which Sistone will defer to Frälyer to report if he sees fit.
