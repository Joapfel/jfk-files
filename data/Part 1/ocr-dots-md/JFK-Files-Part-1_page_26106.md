1. ON 22 FEB 1968 AUNT OF AMPLOT-1 DENOUNCED AMPLOT-1 TO DIGEPOL VIA AMCLEVE-15 (WHO IS ACQUAINTANCE OF AUNT THROUGH CUBAN EXILE COMMUNITY CARACAS). AMCLEVE-15 REPORTED UNILATERALLY TO STAPON. DENUNCIATION WAS THAT AMPLOT-1 IS COMMUNIST WHO INVOLVED SUBVERSIVE ACTIVITIES IN VENEZUELA IN PAST AND WILL BE ENTERING VENEZUELA SOON FROM ITALY TRAVELING UNDER FALSE NAME.

2. WE ASSUME DIGEPOL WILL PICK UP AMPLOT-1 WHEN HE ARRIVES, PRESUMABLY THROUGH TIP FROM AUNT. WOULD BE ABLE APPROACH AMPLOT-1 THROUGH DIGEPOL OR AMCLEVE-15 SHOULD HOS SO DESIRE. AT VERY LEAST MIGHT BE ABLE GUIDE INTERPRETATION IF HE ARRESTED. AMCLEVE-15 DILLING RUN INVESTIGATIONS OR OPS THROUGH DIGEPOL FOR STATION BY GIVING DIGEPOL TIPS AND INFO "FROM CUBAN CONTACTS MIAMI" WHICH NEVER QUESTIONED.
