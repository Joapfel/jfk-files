SUBJECT: GENERAL. Operations.
SPECIFIC. Operational data - INDROPATHIC PART II

DATE: 4 November 1948

1. The subject of T2P-1-365 Part I is INDROPATHIC.

2. Subject is best qualified to serve as a cutout in handling informants. He has had experience along those lines in cultivating and collecting information from the following individuals:

**Government Oppositionists:**

Dr. José PEZET
Lic. Angel VILLAR Lendea
Eng. Luis A. CRUZ
Prof. Luis NARIAS

**Government Supporters:**

Lic. Díaz PARTIDA
Lic. Alberto MUISALLO -
Lic. Generoso SANCHEZ

As a result of the recent meeting between Elmer C. MERCEER and Grover J. WARDING, Subject has been recruited for the specific task of acting as a cutout in handling HYDROCYST I, who can be handled more profitably if transferred to the control of MERCEER's station. Subject to Home Office approval, no definite commitments have been made in this case.

3. Subject has a working knowledge of the following subjects:

a. Protocol

b. Liaison methods pertaining to intelligence in Latin American countries and inter-departmental liaison within our own government.

c. Communications systems. Subject transmits and receives about 12 - 16 words per minute in Morse code.

d. The political structure of Latin American governments.
