31 AUG 1970

Mr. James W. McCord, Jr.
7 Kinder Court
Rockville, Maryland 20850

Dear June,

As you bring to a close more than twenty-five
years of service to your country, I want to join
your friends and co-workers in wishing you well
and hoping that you find the years ahead filled with
enjoyment and satisfaction.

It takes the conscientious efforts of many
people to do the important work of this Agency. You
leave with the knowledge that you have personally
contributed notably to our success in carrying out
our mission. Your faithful and loyal support has
measured up to the high ideals and traditions of the
Federal service.

May I express to you my appreciation and
extend my best wishes for the years ahead.
