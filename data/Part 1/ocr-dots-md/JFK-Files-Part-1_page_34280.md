1. DEF DELEGATION UNABLE SEE TSIRIMOKOS DUE PRESS UN BUSINESS.

HOWEVER RECEIVED FOR HOUSE AND HALF 12 DEC BY MARKOS ECONOMIDES WHO DESCRIBED SELF AS GREEK DIPLOMAT WHO AIDE TO AND TRAVELING WITH TSIRIMOKOS.

2. DELEGATION RAISED TWO SUBJECTS;

A. GALBE

B. GREEK SHIPPING TO CUBA

3. RE 2A ECONOMIDES INDICATED HIS DISAPPROVAL OF GALBE AND LISTENED SYMPATHETICALLY TO AMSCROLL ARGUMENTS. MADE NO PROMISES BUT SAID WOULD GIVE AMSCROLL PREPARED PRESENTATION OF CASE TO TSIRIMOKOS. (THIS INCLUDED LATEST AMSCROLL LETTER DESCRIBED WAVE 7793.) ECONOMIDES DID NOT INDICATE ANY ACTION BEING TAKEN TO P.N.C. GALBE.
