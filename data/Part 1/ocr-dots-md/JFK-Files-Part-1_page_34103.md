MIAMI, FLA., NOV. 23 (AP)-A CUBAN DIPLOMAT WHO REFLECTS SAYS "Cuba's ECONOMY IS WIND ON THE ROAD TO REACHING A CLIMAX AFTER SUFFERING ONE SETBACK AFTER ANOTHER."

RAUL VOLTA, WHO LEFT THIS PRINCIPAL OFFICE IN THE CUBAN EMBASSY IN LONDON AND CANT TO THE UNITED STATES FOR ACYL", TOLD US WHEN LAST NIGHT THAT GENERAL FISCONTEIT LEIGNED IN CUBA, BECAUSE OF SCARCITIES AND ECONOMIC WISDOM.

WHEN HE LAST WALKED IN CUBA. IN JULY, VOLTA SAID, IT HURT HIM TO WITNESS THE "ECONOMIC CHAOS" THERE.

VOLTA, 36, WAS THE FOURTH PERSON TO REFECT FROM THE CUBAN EMBASSY IN LONDON SINCE FILIPPO CAZIER'S TAKEOVER IN 1989.
