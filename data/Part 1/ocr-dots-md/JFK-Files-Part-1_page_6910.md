DATE OF INFO : 21 OCTOBER 1968
SUBJECT : TRAVEL OF AMERICAN HUMAN HALF-BROTHER AND WIFE, AMERICAN COMMUNISTS
REFERENCE : OUR TELEPHONE MESSAGE OF 21 OCTOBER 1968

AN OFFICIAL SWISS SOURCE HAS REPORTED THAT AMERICAN HYDRO-POWER FISCAL YEAR 1967 AND HIS WIFE, EDITH FRISCH HALFTBROTHER, AMERICAN COMMUNISTS WHO ARE SUSPECTED OF SERVING SOVIET AGENTS, RETURNED TO ZURICH FROM GENEVA ON 21 OCTOBER 1968, SPENT AN HOUR AND A HALF AT THE GENEVA CONSULATE IN ZURICH, CANCERECTED THEIR RESERVATIONS TO TELAVIV, AND WERE SCHEDULED TO LEAVE ZURICH FOR PRAGUE VIA AUSTRIA BY RAIL ON 23 OCTOBER.
