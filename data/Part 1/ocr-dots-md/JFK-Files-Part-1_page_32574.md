SECRET

7 August, 1950

RE:KO. U.K., DU.

TO: Chief, Employee Division

FROM: Chief, Foreign Division T

SUBJECT: Earl J. WILLIAM'S CU:

1. It is requested that subject be processed at the grade of CS-11 for Slot No. 2, Intelligence Officer Operations, Lima, Peru, as a replacement for Mr. George P. Stone. Mr. Stone will be transferred to another station in Latin America in the next few months.

2. Although Mr. Williamsen is at present a C.S.-9, he has had a total of approximately 8 years in investigative work with C.I.C., and FCI believes that a rating of CSF-11 is thoroughly justified. Since his present salary in the Canal Zone is $5750 per annum, it is specifically requested that he be processed at the grade of CS-11, $5600 per annum.
