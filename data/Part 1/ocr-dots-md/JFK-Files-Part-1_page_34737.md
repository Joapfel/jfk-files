1. Biographic data concerning the parents of Subject (201-298852) has been received from the trustee, Raul SANTOVENIA, and is forwarded herewith as Attachment A.

2. A review has been made of JMWAVE, AMTIKI, and AMOT filos concerning continued justification for the payment of $225.00 per month to the trustee on behalf of the parents in Cuba. Significant points resulting from the review are as follows:

a. Subject was executed for a prior crime in Cuba and not because of participation at the Bay of Pigs. (Murder of a Cuban soldier during escape from Cuba in March 1960). See "Bohemia" clipping forwarded herewith as Attachment B.

b. The father of Subject, Pedro KIM, is probably identical to one Pedro KIM who is alleged by AMTIKI-1 to be an active communist in Cuba. A Pedro KIM is also described as an active communist in the following reports: DCE-15460, 16 August 1962; DCE-15946, 26 October 1962.

c. Although it appears that the trustee has efficiently handled the funds forwarded to him ($12,602.00 in bank account as of December 1965), there is no evidence that any form of assistance has been provided to the dependents in Cuba.

3. In view of the background of this case, as described in paragraph 3 above, it is recommended that all payments for this case be terminated.
