VAN CLEAVE TOLD US DURING ONE OF THE ADOPTIONED CONVERSATIONS THAT HE IS ACCORDED WITH MITCHELL WERFELL, PENDER SPRINGS, GEORGIA, AND THAT HE MADM BEEN "PRESCONED" INTO TAXING WERFELL TO ARGENTINA WITH HIM IN LATE JANUARY 1974 BY OFFICIALS IN THE COCA COLA COMPANY, CORAL GABLES, FLORENTIA, WITH WHICH VAN CLEAVE HAD (OMNIC) A CONSULTING CONTRACT IN CONNECTION WITH COCA COLA'S SECURITY SYSTEM IN ARGENTINA. VAN CLEAVE ADVISED US THAT HE NEVER AGAIN WOULD HAVE ANYTHING TO DO WITH WERFELL BECAUSE THE latter HAD ACTED SO BADLY DURING THIS TRIP. HE TOLD US THAT WERFELL HAD COMMENTED TO ARGEATINE OFFICIALS THAT HE (WERFELL) WAS SOMEHOW CONNECTED WITH THE CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE AGENCY, AND ON A FIRST NAME LAGIS WITH 'HILL' COLEY, DCI. VAN CLEAVE TOLD US THAT MATTERS HAD DELEGATED TO SUCH A DEGREE THAT HE RETURNED TO THE US WITHOUT WERFELL. (OUR MEMORY IS A FIT FUZZY ON THE CONTENTS OF THIS DISCUSSION, BUT WE RECALL SOMETHING TO THE EFFECT THAT WERFELL WAS INVOLVED WITH EWE, WOMEN, AND SONS, AND VAN CLEAVE HAD AFFAIR THAT WERFELL WOULD BE ASSESSED BY ARGENTINE POLICE BEFORE IT WAS OVERTAKED.) VAN CLEAVE TOLD US THAT HE PERSONALLY FEELN PANG SO MUCH AS IMPLIED A RELATIONSHIP WITH THE AGENCY TO ANY CLIGEES.
