Saturday, July 30th, 1966

Today at 4 o'clock in the afternoon, as Stelis K.O.V. came in at 1110 AM for a cup of "tea" (Tea) we started our talk. As you know by now, Mr. Chichay Don as every Saturday, since it starts at six, starts talking, and so it went on till ten, when I told him that in his country they have large museums where the people are showing their treasures, and all that sort of thing. He said, "I know, I know." Well, this started it, and then, after some time, he told me that his Spanish is not very good, and I explained it all to him, since his English is not very good either. And what he meant for me to understand was that the American always asks all difficulties, and thus his value would be increased many fold. These friends (Sovs) are very clever, and they suppose - and they will even stay 20-25 minutes if they have to - and they would not be discovered by their superiors. But what is more, they would not be discovered by their superiors, but that is not the case if one of them is none other than the second secretary, or the Ambassador.
