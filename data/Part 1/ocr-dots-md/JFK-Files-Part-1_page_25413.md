G C R E T 241038Z APR 69 CITE CONTACTS/LDS ANGELES 1-77
CONTACTS/WASHINGTON

TO GSS, MUSILLIN

SUBJ: CASE 52872

NICK C. MCDONALD, WORLD ASSOCIATES INC., INFORMS ME THAT SOMEONE PROVIDED TSD/ORD WITH INCORRECT INFORMATION. WE DID NOT OFFER TO DEMONSTRATE HIS VOICE IDENTIFICATION SYSTEM, WHICH HE REGARDS AS A HIGHLY SENSITIVE SYSTEM FROM A SECURITY STANDPOINT. WE ALSO DID NOT PLAN TO BE IN WASHINGTON WITHIN THE NEXT THREE WEEKS. HIS SO CALLED WORLD TOUR WILL BEGIN SOMETIME THIS FALL WITH A VISIT FIRST TO LATIN AMERICA, A RETURN TO US. A TRIP TO EUROPE. RETURN US AND A FINAL TRIP TO FAR EAST.

THE CHICAGO REPRESENTATIVE OF THE FIRM PLANS OR REPRESENTING THE FIRM AT ANY MAY MEETING WITH FINANCIAL GENERAL GROUP CO. IN WASHINGTON.

MCDONALD AGREES TO GOING HIMSELF AND TO MEETING WITH AGENCY PEOPLE IF YOU WILL PROVIDE US WITH NAMES, TELEPHONE NUMBERS, ETC. ADVISE SECRET
