5 April 1967

MEMORANDUM FOR: CI/OA

ATTENTION : 

SUBJECT : LITANIL-9
201-329609

1. LITANIL-9 returned to Mexico from Cuba in October 1966. On 8 November he was contacted and debriefed by Mexico City Station. Throughout the month of November he debated the question of whether or not he should return to Cuba. In the first week of December 1966, he decided to remain in Mexico.

2. Since it was believed that LITANIL-9 would have little access to LINSO or to persons of interest in Mexico, he was terminated and the balance of his escrow account was paid to him on 9 December 1966. However, it has since become apparent that from time to time it would be valuable to NOFACT to contact LITANIL-9 for information on PERUEN personalities. He is acquainted with a large number of persons who themselves are of interest to NOFACT or who could supply information on third persons on whom information is needed.

3. Therefore, provisional operational approval and subsequent operational approval for such occasional contact with LITANIL-9 is requested. It is not planned to pay him a salary or formalize an agent relationship. LITANIL-9 would only be contacted when specific needs for information arise and the possibility exists that he has or could obtain the information.

Chief, WH/1
