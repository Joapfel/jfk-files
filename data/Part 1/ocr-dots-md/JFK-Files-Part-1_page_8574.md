Contact Report

Subject: Jimmy SAEORI (P-13,850)

Date of Meeting: July 12, at 1:30 p.m. at Heliotrope Campo & Leibnitz Coffee Shop at 1500 hrs.

1. The meeting was arranged by phone. On July 17 S phoned to our office and requested that FERNMANN ring him up the next morning. After numerous fruitless attempts F finally reached S at his house at 1130 hrs. S told F that he was with a Chinese the previous day and would see him again on that afternoon at 1400 hrs. S concluded that on the barbe of his first encounter with the Chinese he prepared a report for his friend (LOCINOV) when he would see the same evening at 2000 hrs. F told S that he would want to see what he wrote before he gives it to LOC and a meeting was arranged at the above indicated location.

2. F asked S how did he arrange to see Mr. Chin-Hsiang Teng and where should he see him. S stated that Pierre-Louis helped him; F phoned to the Chinese Emb and said that S, a collaborator in the La Mission, would like to visit the Emb and talk to a person who would be able to give him some info for an article he was preparing for La Mission. On July 17 about 1300 hrs S went to the Emb and after saying that he was coming from Pierre Louis he was received by TAME, Counselor of the Emb. S stated that he was interested in preparing an article concerning China - Soviet ideological conflict, Mao's territorial claims and repeated border clashes; S would want have some views of Red China government on these problems.
TAME stated that he , nor his government, had any interest in the ideological conflict between Teikiyo and Moscow; which regard to Mao's territorial claims there was no doubt that they were legitimate: the fact that Mao was bringing up this claims tends to indicate that Mao's position was weak within the country and his ruling party, and therefore, Mao said to seek an issue which would unite the leaders and people around him: such a political play may lead to war. If claims that he asked BULL on which side -Red or White - Mao is in case of war between Red China and the USST and TAME told him that such a decision would be made by a special top level Department of their government.
