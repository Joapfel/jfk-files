SOURCE: NEW YORK TIMES, New York City,

October 22, 1938 - Page 12

EUROPE'S CRISIS: JAPAN PREPARABLES

By Hensu Chen

* There are numerous American journalists of different political color and shades of opinion who are "cooperating" in this way with Japanese imperialism, but whoever has followed the writings of Harold Isacksen, Alexander Buchman, Frank Glass and Wilbur Burton in The China Press, The China Weekly Review, Asia, Pacific Affairs, New International, etc., realizes that these American Troskyist journalists are the most vicious and most dangerous of the whole lot of reactionary journalists.

Widing behind a barrage of Marxist phraseology (which none of them has yet learned to manipulate with facility), these people are energetically conducting a campaign of lies against the Communist International, the Chinese Communist Party, the Soviet Union, the Chinese Soviets and the Chinese Red Army, a campaign which aims to undermine the confidence of the outside world in the Chinese Soviet movement and the Red Army.
