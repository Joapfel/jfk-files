PADULLO PAEZ VEREZ LEFT MEXI, 17 DEC FOR HAVAGA.

FOR YEAR MEXI AND TRANSFER NOT INDICATIVE ALTHOUGH POSSIBLE
AS SYSTEM INTRODUCED CURAN EMB AND THIS UNKNOWN TO HIM.
AS PREVIOUSLY REPORTED PAEZ REPLACED BY PEDRO LARA MUÑOZ WHO SPENT
YEAR MOSCAM.

3. ON 1º DEC 62, LITAMIL-7 REPORTED THAT ONLY RAMON CUENCA AND ANA MARIA BLANCO OF ABOVE GROUP HAVE BEEN "DROPPED" OFFICIALLY FROM CUBAN DIP LIST MEXI. OFFICIAL NOTIFICATION PAEZ EXPECTED SHORTLY. GARCIA AND LECHUGA STILL ON LIST. CO HAS SCHEDULED MEETING 21 DEC WITH LITAMIL-7 AT WHICH TIME EXPECTS HAVE REPORT ON NEWLY DRAWN UP CUBAN DIP LIST FOR PASSING FOREIGN OFFICE WHICH DUE FIRST OF YEAR.

L-7 OPINION THIS WILL REVEAL FUTURE STATUS DIPS IN QUESTION
PARTICULARY GARCIA LARA.

4. ON 18 DEC 62 ALBERTO MANUEL FERRER, CUBAN DIP PP 52/695 ARRIVED MEXI FROM HAVA. LITAMIL-9 BELIEVES HE MEXI INSPECT AND ACTIVITIES ASSOCIATES WITH RAMON SINOBAS CASADO, MANUEL VEGA AND PEDRO LARA ALMOST EXCLUSIVELY. ON 19 DEC L-7 REPORTED SEE INTRODUCED TO RODRIGUEZ FRU A MYSTERIOUS CHARACTER WHO LIVING AT EMBASSY. STATION BELIEVES RODRIGUEZ AND FERRER IDENTS. FERRER SCHEDULED RETURN HAVA ON 21 DEC VIA CUBANA.
