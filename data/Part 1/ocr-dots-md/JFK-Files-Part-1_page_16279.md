CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE AGENCY
# Intelligence Information Cable

CONFIDENTIAL

DATE OF
27 SEPTEMBER 1965

SITREP AS OF 0600 HOURS LOCAL TIME

PLACE & DATE AND AREA CODE, ALBERTVILLE (27 SEPTEMBER 1965)

SOURCE: REPORT FROM TWO SOONLIS IN ALBERTVILLE.

APPROVAL THIS IS AN ALBERTVILLE SITUATION REPORT PREPARED AT 0600 HOURS LOCAL TIME ON 28 SEPTEMBER 1965 BY CAS.

1. On 27 SEPT 1965 a column of the Fifth Commandos (5,000). REACHED LULIMBA AND WAS PREPARING TO MOVE TOWARDS LUFONOJA ON 26 SEPT. HIS COLUMN LID BY MAJOR ALISTAIR WICKES HAD 3 OF ITS MEN WOUNDED DURING THE MOVEMENT. FROM BENDERA TO LULIMBA (SOURCE: PILOT DECREEDING. CONSOLESE AIRCRAFT HAVE HAD THE ONLY CONTACT WITH THE FIFTH COE SINCE 26 SEPT).

2. ON 27 SEPT, CONSOLESE AIRCRAFT PILOTS OBSERVED A 5,000.

INSTRUCTION, WHICH LENS: 10-DEG. ENTERING BARAKA. THIS SAME

The essential information concerning the National Defence of the United States within the exception of the Envoys Delegated by the President, the Vice-Chairmen and the Vice-Treasurer, is not to be distributed without prior approval from the President.
