Convert to Markdown**From 0630, 4 May (Tuesday) to 0715, 5 May 1965**

2.0 In, from long distance. Habana Cuba is calling. Maid answers. Call back in an hour. Holds the line while waiting for a reply....Finally hang up.

2.5 In, from long distance. Maid answers. Habana is calling. Victor Rico Galan says for them to call back in an hour. After 0835, 4 May

3.0 In. WO with Inge Kico, who answers. -WO asks when they returned (from Cuba). In Holy Week...They have some cigars. WO says they (family) are leaving tomorrow. Perhaps they can visit them this afternoon for coffee. WO is not sure he can come by their house. Beat them visit WO's house. She will have VKG talk to WO about this. WO will be either at home or at Aspa (ph).

Ask if VKG can bring a copy of "Siempre" - the new issue to take on his trip. This issue is not out yet. It comes out on Wednesday so this is not possible.

4.2 In, from long distance. Habana is calling VKG. VKG can't take the call...Call back in an hour. (He is asleep.)

4.5 In. WO, sister-in-law, with maid, who answers, for Inge Kico. Not there. WO is Ross.

4.7 In. WO asks maid, who answers, for Sra. Kico. Not there. And VKG? He is asleep.

5.0 In. WO asks maid, who answers, for VKG. He is still asleep.

5.2 In. Carmen with Inge Kico (In), who answers, Greetings. VG! takes the phone. Asks if there is anything. Yes, when will they see one another? VKG has should say. At 1200 in "Siempre."

5.3 One extension off...

5.7 In. Sra. Kico with VHO, who answers. Greetings. VKG gets home very late yesterday...Anita is ill. Wants to ask VKG for some advice...

6.0 In. MO with VG, who answers. MO to Julian (Cuban). Puts on Marie with VG. Greetings. Music would like to see VHO. Marie is at Habana, VG will go by there in a little bit. Marie will wait for him. Marie has some books for VG - seat by Sr. Vilaa of Radio Habana, Wine.

6.3 In. MO, calling for Association of Students of Law School (of Miami, University), with VG, who answers. They have not set up case round tables (there) to explain the Caribbean situation to students. Would like to know if VG could participate in this on Tuesday or Thursday of next week. All right. Tuesday of next week. MO will cell VHO on Saturday to get exact time. She will participate in these? They had planned VG to participate with Alberto Domingo and with Eusi-que also. Is that ok? Please. Do you want me to come?
