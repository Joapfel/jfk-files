a) A letter dated 22 September 1959 FRENKEL acknowledges receipt of two letters, dated 31 August and 3 September, the subject of which is the formation of the State of Israel, obviously at the request of MALTZ.

b) A recently in answer to a request by letter dated 16 October from Subject, FRANKEL, in a letter dated 20 October, describes medical practices among the Druses. In a handwritten portrait the writer notes he will arrive in time, as Subject requested, to facilitate his stay in Israel, only however if the writer is given notice of HANSEN's arrival date well in advance.

c) A letter dated 9 October from FRENKEL acknowledges receipt of two cables from Subject and encloses a summary of security measures in Cyprus, Egypt, during the Aliyaoth era, presumably as background information for Subject's writings.

d) Three letters, dated 13, 15, and 19 October 1959, from Mr. Adia FRENKEL provide Subject with additional background information and research analysis. The letter dated 23 October includes photocopies of identity cards issued in 1946, 1947, and 1948.

e) In a letter dated 20 October 1959 Subject poses to FRENKEL a series of questions concerning the Hagannah leaders and Irgun members. Subject indicates that "Otto" (probably MENACHEM) was in Mexico the previous week, working with Subject on the story. "Otto" will be arriving at the Hotel Dan between November 15 and 18 for a five-week stay in Israel.

In view of information contained in these letters, as well as in other pieces of correspondence noted herein, it would appear that Subject is working on a screenplay for the movie to be based on Leon LUS' best seller, *Axanas*. Otto PREIDOR, well-known Hollywood movie director, is scheduled to direct the film version of *Axanas*.

12. Correspondence with Larry Edmonds Bookshop, 6659 Hollywood Boulevard, Hollywood, California. (Attachment 12)

a) A letter from the bookshop dated 23 September 1959 is signed "Milton." Milton sent Mr. AMAN "the Cassarole Cooking" the day he received MALTZ's letter but neglected to bill Subject. Books for Subject have been ordered from England.
