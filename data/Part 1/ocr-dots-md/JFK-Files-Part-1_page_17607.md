MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, W/III
SUBJECT: LIMUS Phase of LIFEAT Project
REFERENCE: W/III Memorandum dated 2 July 1958

1. Due to a period of dormancy, the LIMUS phase of the LIFEAT Project has furnished a relatively small amount of reporting, during the past year, concerning BDOX officials and their activities in Mexico. While small in quantity, the information furnished has been of considerable operational value because of the nature of the operation and the fact that it serves as a major source of data on the BDOX target. The LIMUS operation has furnished accurate travel data, information on the function and scope of activity engaged in by the BDOX personalities we are interested in and has helped us determine the number of contacts the BDOX installation maintains with the local citizenry in the course of its activities. In the last case, the LIMUS operation has linked a local citizen, long suspected of Soviet Bloc intelligence activity, with one known and one suspected BDOX intelligence officer serving under diplomatic cover in Mexico. This local citizen is now the subject of an extensive effort to prove his Soviet Bloc intelligence role and to determine the extent of his activity. Finally, the LIMUS operation has begun to furnish valuable operational data on a BDOX personality who has recently arrived to assume a consular post. This particular BDOX official was recommended to the field as a target of interest because of his past intelligence background. We feel confident that the LIMUS operation will aid considerably in our effort to monitor the activities of this individual.

2. Now that the LIMUS phase of the LIFEAT operation has once again, begun to function, we believe that the information obtained therefrom will show a steady increase in operational value during the coming year.

PETER K. HEDDAH
Chief, E/P
