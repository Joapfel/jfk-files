INFORMATION FOR: Chief, Contract Personnel Division

SUBJECT:bert C. TICHBURG (p), amendment of Contract

1. It is requested that the contract, as amended for Edward J. TICHEBURN, Contract Agent, be further amended effective 1 June 1962 as follows:

a. Increase basic compensation from $3,000 to $2,000 per annum.

b. provide additional compensation for extra-rate procurement at the rate of $3,000 per annum for self and dependents.

c. extend the period of the contract for an additional year.

d. all other provisions of the contract remain in effect.

2. The duty in compensation is transferred by the city to you, and from the City of Chicago to the Illinois Department of Public Health.
