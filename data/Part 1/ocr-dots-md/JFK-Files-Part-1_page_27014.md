SUBJECT: Memorandum of Visit to Mitchell Warbell III
*Luis Velazquez, Cira*
Coral Gables, Florida

DATE: 27 July 1959

1. In the evening of 24 July 1959 I received a telephone call from subject, who had been given my name and address by another agent of the intelligence community in connection with the possibility of his passing along late information concerning the probable impending flight of Batista to the US.

2. Warbell indicated that he spent a great amount of his time in the Dominican Republic and had effectively closed association with leaders of the government, and also with General Jone Pedrana. He claimed to have been a former member of the OSS with service in China in WWII. Warbell is a soldier-orElse type who wears a monobeveled, driven aJegegden-bone, sports a must attractive blonde girl friend, and is a Big Spender. He also volunteered that he has a wife and six children living in Georgia.

3. Warbell told me that his principal source of income as in dealing with Cuban pesos, and said that he is associated in the business with a John Lipko of Los Angeles, a jazz musician who formerly played with the Jimmy Dorsey and Jan Garver bands.

4. Lipko also claims to have been a member of OSS during WWII, serving in the Caucasian and Eastern European area. Lipko was initially engaged in making long-distance phone calls to Mexico City, Guatemala City, and Los Angeles during a part of the time I was with the group.

5. Warbell gave me some information on Batista's plans, which is basically supposition, and finished some other marginal information which is being forwarded, although in my opinion little evidence can be placed in anything he says. It is hard to evaluate what Warbell's motives are in connection with his desire to furnish information regarding Batista to us, but it appeared that he was also attempting to fish for information, throwing out a number of questions concerning the Wilkins Terrace Hotel, reportedly recently to have been purchased by Cuban interests with 9 million Cuban pesos. The penthouse reportedly has been completely redecorated, with heavy steel doors guarding the entrance. This penthouse suite has been moved to be Batista's intended headquarters once he arrives in the US. All of this has been written about in the gossip columns of the local papers.

6. In addition as Warbell has my name and phone number, probably little can be done to prevent him getting in touch with me from time to time, but contact will not be initiated by me except by direction. The basic purpose of this memo is for the record.
