Ukraine, USSR, where

- "My father was sent to Leningrad, to the Sudomekh plant, a small plant in Lenin-grad. He was at the plant for half a year and then became director. This was 1936-1937. Then he was sent to the Baltic Shipyard in Lenin-grad as Chief En-ginoer. He was there less than a year."
