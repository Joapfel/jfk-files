## 1 May 1963

### 14.0 contd.

a Dominican here yesterday and he is insisting on immediate withdrawal of (U.S.) troops (for negotiations). Juan B. also said that it is not true that the Americans have taken in medicine, etc. Absolute lie. VNG will call F. when he knows more news.

After 1935, 1 May 1963

### 17.2 In. Charo (ph) (woman) with Inge (Kico), who answers.
Greetings...about maids...Inge asks how Sandino is. Fine. No says they were with the Cardoso last night and Lila (Cardoso) had said that Inge was going to call her. Inge got a second maid yesterday.
No asks about Inge's visa. Nothing has done nothing. Has her time limit run out? Inge is not sure. She has to go see about this. Perhaps it is possible in October.
Invites them tomorrow afternoon - around 1600 - to have coffee. Fine.

### 18.2 Out to 25-20-32. VKG asks NO, who answers, for Sr. Cordero.
Not there.

### 18.3 In. Cordero with VKG, who answers. VKG has a family problem and has to go out. Leave their apt. for another day. VKG will call him. If Cordero hears anything, let VKG know. VKG has notified the other to Habana. There was a report that some parachute troops went in. Yes, but that was given out by news agencies. No, VKG is referring to direct calls. Cordero is going to call New York and also Washington tonight. If there is anything interesting, let VKG know. VKG will be at home tonight.

### 18.3 In. Josefina asks VKG, who answers, for Inge.
Inge kico takes phone. Asks how things went in Cuba. Fine. They were there 2 weeks. About how Josefina is...children...sickness...

### 19.2 In. Habana is calling VKG.
VKG comes to the phone. No asks what is now. Nothing. There is a call to go through to Bosch tonight and another one for Santo Domingo, but VKG will call when he has something new. No is Dikes. When will Pidel talk? Around 1900, Habana time. No asks VKG to repeat what he said this morning so she can record it. VKG repeats again - about Juan B. Mejia's call from Santo Domingo....Mejia could not give details on who was in the bills, etc. Could not give names.
