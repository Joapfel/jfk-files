SUBJ: Contact Report/ JCOZY-5

20 Apr, 1966, Subject's office

1. When the C.O. arrived at Subj's office, one of the male employees of Subj evidently advised him that "Mr. Ferrier" was there. Subj came from his inner office to advise the C.O. that (Leonid Andreevich) Mikhaforov was in his office making travel arrangements. The C.O. remained in the outer office, but with his back to the passageway and his face buried in a magazine.

Subsequently, Subj told the C.O. that Mikhaforov was leaving for vacation to the Soviet Union (on 21 Apr via Air France). He was due to return to Mexico on 24 May.

Subj also mentioned that the INTERIST delegation coming to the ANZ meeting in Guadalajara (27-30 Apr) had not arrived in Mexico as scheduled (on 19 Apr) because there had been a delay in their receiving Mexican visas. The delay was occasioned by Mexican demand for minimal biographical information in order to process the visas. The group of four has been re-scheduled to arrive in Mexico on 23 Apr via Sabena.

The C.O. now the exchange of telegrams re the information required for visa issuance, and noted that the following:

Viktor Bolchenko, born 21 July 1925 in Aktубинск; president of INTERIST; Passport D 01155 B, issued 6 Oct 1965.

Lev Akhmelov, born 11 May 1924 in Moscow; chief of the International Department; Passport C 065257, issued 25 Sept 1965.

Assan Ahmedov (sic), born 24 June 1924 in Tashkent; chief of the Uzbekistan Tourism Directorate; Passport 069757, issued 24 Dec 1965.

Iyudnila Yuz etova, born 5 Mar 1923 in Moscow; Latin American Department; Passport C 065784, issued 6 Oct 1965.

Subj mentioned that at the Copenhagen Congress on Congress Organizations that he attended 30 Mar to 2 Apr 1966, he met two Soviet "scientists" and the INTERIST rep for Denmark. The "representatives from the Academy of Sciences" were Gennadiy Andreyevich Koshaev and Timur T. Timofeev (cards attached).

2. The C.O. who had heard from Subj on 19 Apr by telephone that he had had his confrontation with Valentin Leginov last week requested a full account of that meeting. Subj reported that Leginov had called him on 15 Apr requesting a meet somewhere outside; Subj insisted that Leginov come to his office.
