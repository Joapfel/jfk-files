Biographical Data

David TZITZICHILI was born 20 December 1919, in Tbilisi, Georgia. He is 5' 7½" and of slight build. Subject has been assessed as of superior intelligence. He identifies himself with city life and although of Georgian birth and ancestry, has lived most of his life in France. Subject served in the French Army as a private (1939-1941). Upon demobilization, he worked in Germany as an electrician (1943-1945). He was liberated by the U.S. Army in 1945. Subject speaks French (native), Georgian, German and some English.
