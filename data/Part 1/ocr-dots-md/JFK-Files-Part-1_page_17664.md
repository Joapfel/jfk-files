PROJECT OUTLINE

COPYING LIFRAT

PART I

1. Area of Operations

This project will be run from the Mexico City Station. It will
be concerned with targets located in Mexico City.

2. Purpose

The purpose of this project is to determine Sino-Soviet plans,
activities and connections with other communist elements through
the use of technical surveillance. This purpose appears to be in
consonance with the Related Mission Directive for Mexico (dated
7 May 1957), Priority A, Objective One.

3. Background

Project LIFEAT was approved initially on 3 October 1950. Actual operations began on 18 January 1951 and have run continuously since that time. The LIFEAT operation was begun with the installation of one telephone tap and has expanded since that time to an average daily monitoring of 23 telephone lines and from one to four hidden microphone installations (the microphone operations were originally under a separate project-LINO).

Project LIFEAT is a unilateral operation. It is completely independent from any liaison arrangement and is staffed by Agency staff and contract employees together with a substantial number of local indigenous controlled events.

During the past six years Project LIFEAT has produced more than 40,000 pages of operational and intelligence information in direct support of the Mexico City Station's objectives specifically and has contributed materially to the intelligence gathering activities connected with the Sino-Soviet bloc. In addition, Project LIFEAT has been of considerable value and assistance in monitoring the activities of Americans residing in Mexico City in which OAMMY has major interest.
