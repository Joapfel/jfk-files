MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Final Arrangements for Edward G. Tichborn's (P) Contract Status in Mexico.

REFERENCE: Memorandum for the Record, dated 17 May 1961

1. When I talked with Tichborn (P) on Wednesday, 17 May, to arrange a meeting with him for Messrs. Collins and McCoy, he stated that he was most anxious to leave on Saturday for Mexico City and wanted to know if final details could be taken care of at our arranged meeting on the 19th in order that he could depart on Saturday, the 20th. As I told Tichborn, I was somewhat surprised that he planned to leave so soon although I did know he was anxious to get settled as soon as possible. I said I would do everything possible to make the final arrangements so that he could leave as he planned.

2. As a result, Mr. Charles Penney, WH/CA, Mr. Michael and the undersigned of CA/PAG, and Tichborn met Friday the 19th at the Roger Smith Hotel to talk about the terms of Subject's contract, to brief him on operational details, station contact, etc. Mr. Penney had also arranged that one of their agents connected with the Pan American Institute of the University of Florida meet Tichborn during the briefing.
