15. July 1975

MEMORANDUM FOR: Freedom of Information Officer, DDO
SUBJECT: Phillip A. Singerman
REFERENCE: IRS 75-801 (Appeal)
Request for Agency documents

1. Attached are the three Agency documents listed in our memorandum of 14 May 1975:

a. Agency dispatch IMMA-37416, dated 23 June 1969. This covering dispatch does not mention Subject by name; Subject's photograph was forwarded as an attachment to this dispatch. Two other attachments listing Subject's name on two flight manifests are not available here, but the reference to his travel from Mexico city to Cuba had been incorporated into a Special Channel memorandum to the FBI, B-561, dated 11 August 1969 (paragraph id of our 14 May memorandum).

b. Agency dispatch IMMA-37509, dated 10 July 1969 listed requestor as a traveler to Cuba and listed his passport number. It also forwarded a copy of his passport in an attachment which is not available here.

c. Special Channel Memorandum B-561, dated 11 August 1969 (see paragraph 1a above).

2. We believe that the above mentioned documents have been properly exempted in our 14 May memorandum under (b)(1) S(B)(1) (foreign liaison, in this case, two sections of Mexican liaison) and (b)(3). The material is not segregable for release, as the nature of the information and its format are such that the requester could determine that the information concerning him was obtained by the Agency through surreptitious access to airline manifests and clandestine photography of passports of U.S. citizens. Release would thus violate protection of sensitive sources and methods (Mexican liaison and a unilateral agent).
