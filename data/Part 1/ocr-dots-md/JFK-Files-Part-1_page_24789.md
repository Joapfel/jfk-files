## 6. Cover and Security.

a. Cover. TICHEORN has done an excellent job of building his cover with WUSALINE. His area knowledge and language ability have proven of real help to the cover company, and he has shown himself unusually well suited to the needs of that company. The cover company demands on his time, both on-the-job and for foreign and domestic travel, have not interfered with his operational utilization.

b. Security. While in Mexico, TICHEORN acted as an unofficial legal advisor for the Center of Studies and Documentation which was mentioned in the MIDOWEL flap as being funded by a CIA contract. However, there was no publicity in Mexico which linked him with the Center. Similarly, there was apparently no public exposure of the funding of the intellectual magazine with which he was connected, "Dialogos", by other CIA conducts. There is no available evidence that his security has been compromised by the MIDOWEL flap.
