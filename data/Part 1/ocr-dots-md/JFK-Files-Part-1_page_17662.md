## 3. Planning for the Future

During the coming year Station capabilities will be expanded to permit monitoring approximately 30 lines. Basic coverage of Soviet, Czech and Polish activities will be continued to the maximum extent possible and assets and capabilities will be main- yained for exploiting important targets of opportunity. At the present time the Station believes that 30 lines are about the maximum load the technical agents assigned to LI-FAT can properly handle and protect. The station is currently preparing plans for submission to headquarters which call for the establishment of a similar but separate and compartmented LI-FAT-type operation. Such a project would have new agents not previously associated with or knowledgeable about LI-FAT.
