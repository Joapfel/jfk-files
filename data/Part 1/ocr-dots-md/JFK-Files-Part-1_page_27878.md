### COURSE OBJECTIVES, CONTENT AND METHODS

The purpose of the COS Seminar is to assist newly designated Chiefs of Station, Deputy Chiefs of Station and Chiefs of Base to prepare themselves for their field assignment.

The Seminar is conducted as a series of open discussions with senior officers drawn from the offices, staffs and operating elements of all Directorates of the Agency. The intent is to provoke an exchange of views and sharing of experiences as well as to provide information on current policies, procedures, services and operational goals.

A folder of selected reading material is provided to each participant in advance of the Seminar, which is designated as related reading in preparation for specific sessions in the schedule.
