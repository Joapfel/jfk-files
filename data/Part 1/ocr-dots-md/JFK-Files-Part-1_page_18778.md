Chief of Station, Bombay, India
Chief, Administration and Services
Administrative

29 March 1963

APR 6

I. In reference to ZIB-85 and enclosure, you may inform Robert H. Biggs that he will be returned to the United States at the completion of his 18-month tour of duty. However, because of the fact that this tour of duty will not be completed until October, 1943, authorization for return will be granted at a future date. In the meantime, efforts will be made to recruit a replacement for the slot which Biggs is now occupying.

E. M. TERRELL
