REV. MEXI 139th 1942

1. ON MORNING 26 OCT, WHEN BECAME APPARENT LITEMPOS UNABLE PERSUADE GOBERNACION SWITCH PAPERS OF GONZALEZ FOR LATER ARREST, STATION PLACED LIEBRACE SURVEILLANCE ON GONZALEZ AT HOTEL ASTORIA. (LIEBRACE COVERAGE HAD BEEN WITHDRAWN WHEN LITEMPOS FOUND GONZALEZ, AS STATION DID NOT WANT LITEMPOS AND LIEMBRACES TRIPPING OVER EACH OTHER AT ASTORIA). LIEMBRACES DID NOT PICK UP GONZALEZ UNTIL MORNING 28 OCT WHEN HE DEPARTED FOR AIRPORT AND FLEW TO CUBA.

2. CLANDESTINE PHOTOS TAKEN OF GONZALEZ AT AIRPORT BY LIEMBRACES CLEARLY SHOW HIM IN COMPANY OF MANUEL VEGA AND ROGELIO RODRIGUEZ, KNOWN DOI OFFICERS. POUCHING COPIES.

3. INDEX ELICIO DARDINTO GONZALEZ NORENO/
