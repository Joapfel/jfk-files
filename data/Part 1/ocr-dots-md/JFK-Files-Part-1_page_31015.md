Training for Jeremy K. BENADUM

PRIORITY

REFERENCE: IMMA 24477

Action Required: Cable Reply to paragraph 3

1. Reference is made to paragraph 3 of IDSW 12349, 28 February 1964, which suggested that KUJARK training for BENADUM might be beneficial, and to page 8 paragraph 3 of IMMA 24477, the LITEMPO Project Renewal Request, which also indicated that a two-week intensive field training course would be valuable for BENADUM.

2. Headquarters has discussed this possibility with KUHOMR, and they are prepared to send a trainer to Mexico City for two weeks beginning 18 January 1965. Headquarters has suggested that the trainer be prepared to cover positive intelligence and operational reporting, requirements, and elicitation from his contacts and agents.

3. Please cable whether the dates 18 - 29 January are convenient and whether the subjects to be covered are those envisioned by the Station.

4. Headquarters added to the Project Renewal request $748.95 to cover the per diem and travel of the TDY trainer.

Chester D. DAINOLD
