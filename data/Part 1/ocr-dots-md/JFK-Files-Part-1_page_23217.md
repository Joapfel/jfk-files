Convert to MarkdownOn 1 May 1953, MR. CROZIER was in charge of an attempt to physically penetrate the home of Manuel NOHA Valverde, President of the Costa Rican Communist Party for the purpose of obtaining documents which could be used to prove the Communist party's control over the partido Progresista Independiente, a front which had been organized to participate in the 1953 elections. The sub-agent who was to actually perform the penetration failed to appear and realizing the urgency of the operation, CROZIER voluntarily made the penetration. At the time he entered the NOHA house, the entire family was known to be sleeping in the rear part of the house. Working quietly and quickly under conditions of great personal risk, Mr. CROZIER succeeded in carrying away approximately 85 percent of the legal PPI documentation and other records as well as a large amount of Communist Party documents such as minutes to meetings, code words, etc. A few days later a high level party penetration reported that the PPI may have been eliminated from the electoral scene due to the almost impossible task of reconstructing the legally required records. However, the PPI was able to reconstruct the records and registered by the deadline of 26 May.
