ATTN: INT-DIV (GELLER)

SUBJECT: MINI 10756 - PROPOSED INVASION CANCELLED

DOI: 25 NOV 66 AND EARLIER

ACQ: --25 NOV 66

REF: LA CIRL C-DCG-5.851

C&CS: RITCHIEL L. VERMELA

POWDER SPRINGS, GEORGIA

U.S. NATIONAL, SOLDIER OF FORTUNE TYPE WITH WIDE EXPERIENCE IN LATIN AMERICA. HE IS CLOSELY ASSOCIATED WITH ROLANDO MASFERRER.

(THIS REPORT WAS DEVELOPED BY A NAVY REPRESENTATIVE ASSIGNED TO THE OFFICE OF PREPARATION.)

1. DURING THE NIGHT OF 23 NOV 66 A DECISION WAS REACHED TO CALL OFF THE PROPOSED INVASION OF MAITI BY THE GROUP SPONSORED BY POLANDO (MASFERRER). THE INVASION IS OFF INDEFINITELY.

2. FATHER JEAN CAPTISTL (GEORGES) AND COLONEL RENE (LEOM) HAVE BEEN ARRIVING WITH MASFERRER WHICH HAS CAUSED CONFUSION AND DISORGANIZATION IN THE MOVEMENT. MANY CUSTODians SCHEDULED TO PARTICIPATE HAVE PULLED OUT AND MILITARY CHIEF NAPOLEON (VILLARDJOISE) (CIC), A CUBAN NATIONAL AND ALLEGED MILITARY EXPERT, HAS RESIGNED BECAUSE OF DISSENSION IN THE GROUP.

3. IT IS BELIEVED THAT U.S. GOVERNMENT AGENCIES HAVE INFILTRATED THE MOVEMENT AND THE LEADERS PLAN THAT ALL WEAPONS AND EQUIPMENT SHOULD BE CONFISCATED IF THEY FOUND THE INVASION PASSED OFF.
END OF REPORT.

4. C/S: BARTOLO CONZALE. PLEASE PREPARE HARD COPY IN HEADQUARTERS.
