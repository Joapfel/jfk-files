His wife is from a well-known German-Mexican family from Guadalajara, nee BAHNSEN. His father is a Dr. of chemistry from Stuttgart who recently BM came to Mexico. WIEGANDT owns er has a controlling interest in Barnices Aislantes, S.A., located at Avenida Tolteca 176. This company manufactures plastic foam insulating materials. He lives in Coyoacan in a house which he recently had built, Europa 26. Subject dislikes WIEGANDT whom he describes as a pushy, ambitious businessman. The implication is that he is unscrupulous and would do anything for a buck. Subject doesn't know anything about his politics. Subject will obtain more background information on him.
