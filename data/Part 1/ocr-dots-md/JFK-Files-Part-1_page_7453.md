Confidential

AMERICAN CONSULATR SERVICE.

American Consulate General,
Shanghai, China, December 10, 1931.

Subject: Harold H. Isacksen.

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHBIGHTON,

Sir:

I have the honor to report that an American citizen named Harold R. Isenes has been reported to this office by the police of the International Set-lement as a communist suspect. The facts regarding Mr. Isenes are briefly as follows:

Mr. Harold R. Isaaes called at this office in December, 1930, and stated that he would be for a few months in Shanghai; at that time he exhibited pass-port No. 177028, issued by the Department of State on March 17, 1930. He stated that he expected to leave Shanghai in June, 1931; that his home was in New York City and that his local address was in care of the American Express Company.

According to Police reports Isenes arrived in Shanghai from Manila as a first-class passenger on the S. S. PRESIDENT JEFFERSON on December 17, 1930. He was employed by the SHANGHAI EXPRESS CO., and later by the CHINA PRESS but apparently lost both positions.

H/W ATT'n To XA 2-5656
