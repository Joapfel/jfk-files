MEMORANDUM & THE RECORD

FROM: M. Berger, WH/D/V

SUBJECT: Naturalization of CIFENCE/1's Wife

1. John Rooney of OS/AMS informed the Desk on 19 January 1960 that he had been mistaken when he said that CIFENCE/1's wife could be naturalised two years after C/1 receives his naturalisation. C/1's wife will have to wait three years. It is planned to inform C/1 of this change soon (and the Station) when C/1 comes to the U.S. to process his application.
