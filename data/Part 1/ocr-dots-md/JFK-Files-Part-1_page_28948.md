19 March 1963

MEMORANDUM FOR: SOD/Personnel
SUBJECT: Promotion -
REFERENCE: Fitness Report dated 25 Feb. 1963

1. In view of present and past Fitness Reports, and by comparison with other ZRJEWEL personnel, I believe that the promotion of <u>~~GS-14 equivalent~~</u> to GS-14 is justified, and that it would be in order to convey this view to FE/Personnel. Technically <u>however, the administration of (and decision to promote)</u> is a responsibility of FE Division until <u>reverts to SOD under ZRJEWEL.</u>

2. I would strongly recommend against any move to request Army to advance <u>'s retired status from</u> Lt. Col. to Col.; although, for operational purposes while he is at Saigon it might be possible to give him notional or "assimilated" rank.

E. F. Fox
SOD/Plans
