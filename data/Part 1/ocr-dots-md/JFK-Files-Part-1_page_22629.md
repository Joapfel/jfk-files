1. The following "notional cover firm" was established in April 1957 and backstopped mail-cable-telephone checks:

Public Surveys Institute
General Polling Service

A few hundred business cards were printed up both in Washington and locally in Havana, Cuba for my use. It was agreed that stationary would be printed up and some correspondence should be initiated from Havana. The business cards were first used in May 1957 in Havana; renting an apartment, purchasing furniture, automobile, and among friends and acquaintances. It was later used as a developmental recruitment approach with known communists in Havana - details set forth with each case reviewed here.

2. Generally, it is believed that this cover held up well with Latinamericans in Cuba, Mexico, Costa Rica, Panama. It was received with some curiosity by some Americans living abroad in these countries, and was often quizzed "Well, what do you do?" Actually, university background in economics made it possible for me to answer and converse professionally, and only difficulties ever encountered with two persons who were in a position to appeal to my firm for services. This, of course, made it obvious that I had to be prepared at all times to actually render some service or proven a valid technical reason for not being able to do so. To my knowledge, this cover firm has never been compromised as such, and it has only been seriously challenged on one occasion. This was a newsmen in the Sierra Maestra in Cuba and will be set forth in detail.
