DEPARTMENT OF THE ARMY
OFFICE OF THE ASSISTANT CHIEF OF STAFF, G-2, INTELLIGENCE
WASHINGTON 25, D. C.

G2-AP

3 DEC 1954

SUBJECT: Letter of Appreciation

TO: Director of Central Intelligence
Washington 25, D. C.
ATTN: Deputy Director of Plans

1. I have received a letter from Major General Doniface Campbell, Commanding General, Army Intelligence Center, expressing appreciation for a presentation by Mr. [signature] at Fort Belair in October 1954. Mr. [signature] also arranged for presentations by two other speakers at the Army Intelligence Center. A copy of General Campbell's letter is attached.

2. The remarks contained in this letter are a source of real satisfaction to me, and I take this opportunity to add my appreciation to that expressed by General Campbell.

AKINGUS G. T.
Major Gen.
A. C. of L.
