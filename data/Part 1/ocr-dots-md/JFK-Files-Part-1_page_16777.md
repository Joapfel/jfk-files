2. FOIL SUMMARIZES BACKGROUND AND POLICY RE PAKACII PLAN:

A. THE POLISH PROPOSAL FOR CENTRAL EUROPEAN NUCLEAR-FREE TOE IS INTRODUCED AT 2 OCT '77 SESSION OF UN GENERAL ASSEMBLY BY POLISH FOREIGN MINISTER, ADAM RAPACIK. ACTING AS SPONSOR FOR POLISH GOVERNMENT IS FORMULATED PROPOSAL: SHOULD EAST AND WEST GERMANY AGREE TO BAN ON PRODUCTION AND STORAGE NUCLEAR WEAPONS ON THEIR TERRITORIES, POLAND WOULD SEULTRADESLY INTRODUCE IDENTICAL EAN ON HER TERRITORY. RAPACIK DID NOT LENTION CHECHUGLOVAKIA, BUT HEAD OF CZLICHOSLOVAK UN DELEGATION, SPEAKING AFTER RAPACIK, SUPPORTED PROPOSAL, DECLARING HIS COUNTRY WOULD JOINT AGREEMENT, IMPLIEING BAN OVER NEW TERRITORY, TOO. EAST GERMANS SUBSEQUENTLY MANALLY ENDORSED THIS PROPOSAL.

B. RAPACKI'S PROPOSAL ACCURATEY CLEARED AND CONSIDERED WITH SOVIET LEADERS PRIOR TO INTRODUCTION. MOSков PRIMELY REPORTED THIS PROPOSAL APPROVALLY AND SOVIET PRESS STRESSED AGREEMENT WOULD BE WELLCORED BY PEOPLES
