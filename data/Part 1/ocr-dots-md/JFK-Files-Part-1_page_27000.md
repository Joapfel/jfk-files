APPROVED FOR RELEASE 1994
CIA HISTORICAL REVIEW PROGRAM

2 March 1967

ATTN : John McConauley

Chief, Miami Field Office

Mitchell L. Norrell

1. You will recall that John Crenshaw of the Office of General Counsel was in Miami for several days in connection with the federal case pending against subject. Norrell was indicted on federal conspiracy charges growing out of the abortive invention attempt of Haiti a short time ago.

2. Following the indictment Norrell called us and indicated that he was to be interviewed on "nationwide TV through station WSB-TV in Atlanta." We called Harry McCrea and asked him to monitor local coverage, and we covered the ABC stations for a period of several days. Norrell did not appear on a national news program, but Harry McCrea informed us that he did appear on a two-minute tape segment on the WSB-TV, 3:25 A.M. telecast only. Norrell said he would, if he could, "allow a citizen's arrest of Delight Klemmboord, John McCrea, et al, because he did no more than they did." Harry said that he didn't believe that the station took his statement seriously.

3. Norrell was also interviewed by "The Atlanta Constitution", and we are enclosing a clipping from the "Constitution" of 28 Feb 67 in which Norrell is quoted concerning the indictment.

4. Would you please pass the above to John Crenshaw. You might inform him that the statement of an unauthorized federal official in Miami unreasonably refers to the customer agent in charge, with whom Norrell had a conversation prior to calling us. Norrell quoted him in the course following our conversation. He has had no further contact with Norrell.

J. F. GLADES
