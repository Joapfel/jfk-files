Mexico

To September 27,1969

1. Talking to various people, such as Guillermo Tardiff, no information has been circulated or has circulated regarding the fact that Carrillo Flores did not attend the interview of Raul Roa, foreign minister of Cuba and president Diaz Urdaz.
2. By going to the General Assembly of the United Nations, Inc. Carrillo Flores, secretary of Foreign Relations is out of the presidential race by constitutional reasons.

According to people who may be considered as knowing about politics Carrillo Flores never had a chance to be nominated because of his close associations with United States Bankers.

2. On the 18 of September I went to the national day celebration at the Chilean embassy, where I met Kazantsev who invited me to go to their embassy on the first of October for a farewell party for Valentin Longuinov, who is leaving soon. The new Chilecoslovenian ambassador was with Kazantsev. I arrived late and many people had already left. Wallace Stuart was there, the new Rau ambassador, the Ghana ambassador, the Paraguyan charge d'affairs, an several other diplomats of minor importance. The Chilean colony seems to be quite large.
