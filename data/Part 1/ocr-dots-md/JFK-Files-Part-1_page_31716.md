b. In the case of a suit brought before the Supreme Court, County of New York, in November 1948, by Mircea A. CROŞIU, formerly employed by MALAXA, against the latter for recovery of certain funds, the following was given as "Third Cause of Action":

"That on or about the 10th day of August 1948, at the special instance and request of the defendant, and upon defendant's promise to repay the plaintiff the sum of three thousand ($3,000.00) dollars, plaintiff paid to one Mr. V. V. TILFA, the sum of three thousand ($3,000.00) dollars.

"That no part of the said sum of three thousand ($3,000.00) dollars has been paid to plaintiff by defendant, although demand therefor has been duly made."

c. Another source is quoted as follows in March 1948:

"There is an important consideration which, in the minds of some Romanian political exiles, necessitates the greatest caution concerning collaboration with TILFA. It is a well-established fact that TILFA has close business connections with MALAXA and is working hand in hand with him. As TILFA'S collaboration is of no interest for MALAXA from a business point of view, it is clear that the strong ties which exist between them are of another nature. It is obvious to Romanians abroad that TILFA should not be included in a Rumanian National Committee."
