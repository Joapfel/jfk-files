1. I entered politics in Los Angeles in 1949.

2. Democratic nominee for Secretary of State of California, 1958.
   Elected president of Los Angeles Young Democrats, 1950-51.

3. I was chairman of Speakers Bureau for James Roosevelt,
   candidate for Governor of California, 1950

   I was chairman of Speakers Bureau for Helon Gahagan Douglas
   candidate for U.S. Senator, 1950

   Member of Minorities Committee for Adlai Stevenson for President
   1952 and 1956. Also on speakers bureau.

   Have participated in numerous other local campaigns.

4. Present activities:
   (a) Member, Democratic State Central Comm.

   (b) Member, Host Committee, Demo Nat'l Conv.

   (c) Board of Directors, Democratic Minority
   Conference

   (d) Executive Comm., 63rd Assembly Dist.

5. I have remained active. And it's quite possible that I
may run for Congress in 1960 or 1962, depending on the
political climate.
