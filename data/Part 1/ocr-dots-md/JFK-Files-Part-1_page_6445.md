1. In a recent conversation with Mr. Richard R. Brown, OFCEP, Frankfurt, he read several portions of a letter which he had just received from his Washington headquarters directing some fairly strong criticism to the German country unit in its handling of the program. Needless to say, Brown was considerably upset since it appears that such of the criticism originated from KURAREX. It was his impression that our conversations of last November and December and the remedial action taken by me at that time relative to the USEP German unit's handling of the Soviet program had indicated his willingness to do anything possible to correct deficiencies and to forego just such criticism. In paragraph 1 of the reference we indicated that satisfactory answers to our questions had been received. In this connection we enclose as Attachment A a copy of Brown's written answers to our specific questions. (Attachment B is a criteria for USEP assistance.)

2. In regard to the criticism levied at Brown and the German unit by the Komar's office, the following pertinent exceptions were passed to the undersigned:

"At the third meeting a great deal of stress was laid on the question of the special interest cases of other Government agents too. I was able to dwell at some length on the special handling that we have given special interest cases, and to cite chapter and verse of our report to the OOF. Nonetheless, it was felt that we should very greatly integrate our efforts in this respect. It is clearly considered that the disposal of special interest cases on a very high priority basis warrants unusual effort, procedure, and expense on the part of the Program. Both Army and (HOMSEC) attach very great importance to this, and any dissatisfaction was expressed with the manner in which the present procedures are being implemented by the Program. In Germany, specifically, it was felt that the Program has been reluctant to associate itself or its contractors with cases which might prevent irrevocable difficulties in connection with the program.
