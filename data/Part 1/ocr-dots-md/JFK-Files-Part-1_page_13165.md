IN ANY CASE, HIS ONLY COMMUNICATION WITH ROBERTO SINCE HIS DEPARTURE FROM CUBA IN FEB 1961 WERE TWO LETTERS SENT IN 1966, AT JMWAVE'S REQUEST, WHICH WERE NEVER ANSWERED.
