# keel 72-KICO-27

2 May 1963

4.2 In. AM with VKG, who answers. AM (very faint) with VKG, who answers. AM got a newspaper today from Cuba and there is a complete interview of VKG with Juan R. Mejia (as if he had). It is complete as VKG gave it to radio Habana. Also a lot of other items. AM may be able to bring it by to VKG and leave it. It would be good since VKG has more news. It must would be good if AM could come now. Will do. (AM is Sinova's?).

4.7 In. Carlos with VAG, who answers. Greetings. Told Carlos to phone VKG on Sunday about that article. VKG has been calling long distance and not been sleeping - since Radio Habana has commissioned him for news on Dominican republic. VKG is now waiting for an important call and VKG will have the complete info. VKG changed the idea of the article. What he can do is give Carlos a lot of data. He can come and will try not to take up too much of VKG's time. It is not important (his time). VKG is very interested in Carlos's publishing something on this (Dominican Republic) "there" (Italy?). Carlos wants to send his info tomorrow morning and have it come out day after tomorrow. VKG will call Carlos later on - when he has complete info. Will phone Carlos before 1900.

After 1450, 2 May

5.2 In. Irina with Ingeborg Aco, who answers. Greetings... Aksa if she can come by today. Yes. But come on time (for a class?). Fine.

5.5 In. Habana is calling VAG. Call goes through. AM (Pun-dora?) with VAG. AM is very faint. Aksa if VAG has anything new. Nothing new yet. The last one was what he gave AM. ...AM is inaudible to monitor...VAG says - That is right. Don't mention Mejia nor 14 de June (not July). Everything is (done by) Constitutionalism. They should follow the lines as get out in Fidel's speech.

6.0 In. Marcio (Mejia ricardi) with VKG, who answers. They would like to come by for a moment since they have the bulletin for the press ready. Fine.

6.2 Out to 23-29-63. VKG with Juanito, who answers, for Sr. Carlos Cocheli (ph). Just went out. CC went by VKG's house - about 3 minutes ago. Should be there soon. Juanito is now in the Escuela de Teatro...gets out late. That is why they have not seen one another. KAXX will get together one night.

After 1700, 3 May

6.7 Out to 30-07-63. Manon Emilio Colombo asks AM, who answers, for Sr. Anedo(?)?

Sr. Anedo takes the phone. ABC says that they are all at the house of VKG and asks if A. can come by. A. went by there and he did not see anyone or the car, then returned home. Will come by in 30 minutes because he has to go by the office (first). All right.

7.0 Out to 20-12-68. HI asks AM, who answers, for Sr. Miric. thinks he is in Apt. 4. Hang up.
