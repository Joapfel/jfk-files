1st June, Cont.

17.10 hours: Mark SCHLEIFER calls from the Embassy to Yepe's flat, speaks to Yepe's wife, Galina, says he, "Maria and the children" will spend the night with the Yepes. His wife and children are at present in Pablo's flat (Note: Both flats are in Park West).

17.30 hours: Mark SCHLEIFER called at Embassy by Richard GIBSON, who suggests their sharing a flat in ALGIERS. Both need to brush up their French before "the others pour in" to ALGIERS. Mark agrees to go to Richard's flat, 50 Stockleigh Hall, Prince Albert Road, to discuss various points. Mark mentions that his wife is an "Afro-American". Richard says that integration is much worse here than in the U.S.A.

2nd June

11.00 hours: Yepe calls the C.T.E., speaks to telex room, asks whether they can send a telex to Yepe's brother in Rumania. He explains that his (Yepe's) brother is Cuban Ambassador to Rumania and is due to go to Cuba today or tomorrow. He wants first to establish whether his brother is still in Rumania. If he is, Roberto wants the following message passed: "If you see Leo HUBBEVAN in Cuba, please pass this message to min - 'If you come to LONDON, Dennis PHOMBEAH is anxious to see you'. (Signed) Roberto Yepe".

3 June

12.30 hours: Pablo calls Cuban Embassy (believed PARIS)
(Obtained by direct calling.) Speaks to Esther, then asks
for Paco, who mentions that Guillermo has to return to Cuba,
because his daughter has a cerebral haemorrhage. Pablo says
he and Kamija are also "going to return". Paco asks "But
they haven't called you?" Pablo replies "No". Gives no
further explanation, but will write to Paco. Mentions that
Mark SCHLEIFER is coming to PARIS and will telephone Paco.
Guillermo, Paco remarks, has already caught his plane to
PRAGUE. Miriam is alone. Guillermo had been in touch with
FRANQUI, and ROA had agreed to his going. This leaves the
Commercial Attache.
