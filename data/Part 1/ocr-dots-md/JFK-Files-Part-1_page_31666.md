Convert to MarkdownWhen the contract ends, we will take care of all the rest. We are sure you won't want me to be a third party in this business, but I am so interested in your success that I can not stay away from it.

The contract with General Motors is still under discussion, and we're not allowed to talk about it right now. But we are making every effort possible to get the contract done and to make it a great one for both parties. The point is, if General Motors were to win the contract, I should lose my job and my income, which would really be very hard on me. And as far as I know, there is no chance of our winning it. So, you see, I am going to have to quit the company.

I personally agree that it would be a good thing for us to end this arrangement early, and I think that would be the best way to do it. So I am beginning work toward a contract with General Motors. I hope to get one by the time this one ends. And then, after a while, we'll see if we can get a better offer. It would be a very good thing for us to agree to let the company own the plant and the equipment used in the production of the cars, since that's where most of the money comes from. And also we could agree to let the company run the business. I think this would be a very good plan.

The decision to terminate the contract has been taken with full realization of the policy of Public Law 102 relative to private enterprise in the broadcasting industry. Now, naturally, I asked to terminate construction contracts for two large Government broadcast stations in this country.

This present action is not intended to prejudice any continues or increased participation on a private level in official broadcasting by these licenses. I shall cooperate to the extent possible.

This action is made with sincere regret particularly because you and your Company have devoted in many ways an enormous amount of time and money to the development of radio. Since that time, I have known you and seen you do a lot of very good things. In a highly patriotic spirit,

Sincerely yours,
Robert L. Johansson
Administrator
