1. Reference is made to your request dated 12 September 1969 concerning Subject.

2. On 22 December 1968, an article entitled "Russian Contimeo Passing Themselves Off As Journalists" appeared in La Prueba Libre, a newspaper published in San Jose, Costa Rica, which made the following statements concerning Subject. According to the article, Subject, a journalist, had shortly prior to the date of the article arrived in Chile to attend the Congress of the CUI (Central Worker's Union), In this respect Subject's name was linked with that of one known KORENSKOV and one Aleksandr OPEKUNOV, who were also to attend the Congress, and who, according to the article, on a previous visit to Colombia, had been accused of espionage activities by the Colombian authorities. La Prueba Libre article goes on to quote the Chilean magazine Politica. Economia, Cultura, as stating that Subject "does not work for any publication or information medium of the USAR which operates regularly. He predominantly has not engaged about doing it, because recently no less than 100 representatives of Soviet newspapers, radio, or information agencies who work abroad (Pravda, Investiga, TASS, Novosti, Radio Moscow) have been accused of doing work in the service of the KGB. In Latin America alone 15 Russian journalists have been declared persona non grata."

3.
