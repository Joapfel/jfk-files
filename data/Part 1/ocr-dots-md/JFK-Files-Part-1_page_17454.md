FOR GALBOND AND SAYRE FROM AMBASSADOR MANN:

1. AS YOU KNOW, SOME WEEKS AGO WE BECAME CONVINCED THAT ONLY CHANCE OF GETTING MEXICO TO BREAK RELATIONS WITH CUBA WOULD BE ON BASIS CUBAN INTERVENTION MEXICAN INTERNAL AFFAIRS. THIS OF COURSE WAS ON ASSUMPTION TWO THIRDS RIO TREATY VOTE COULD NOT BE OBTAINED AT PUNTA DEL ESTE.

2. WITH THIS OBJECTIVE IN MIND DEPT AND CAS AUTHORIZED ME TO TURN OVER MATERIALS DESCRIBED IN MEXI 9855 TO APPROPRIATE MEXICAN OFFICIAL. I CHOSE GUSTAVO DIAZ ORDAZ.

3. DIAZ ORDAZ HAS INFORMED CAS THAT HE DISCUSSED THIS EVIDENCE WITH LOPEZ MATEOS WHO EXPRESSED SURPRISE AND INTEREST.
