Dec. 10, 1953

Dear Boris,

Small!

I've phoned our friend and will be seeing him tomorrow morning.

I do hope something or other may bring us to New York —though
there's no immediate prospect of it— as it would be great fun to foregather.

Incidentally, I expect to be in Boston the 4 days after mine,
to get the benefit of the two Monday holidays.

Incidentally also, re sale on newsstands (which sounds fine)
did you notice that 368 are able to offer FDR, a Pictorial Biography,
which is 260 photos with captions and commentary, and is $3.95 in cloth,
at only $1½ in paper?

J much pleased too.
