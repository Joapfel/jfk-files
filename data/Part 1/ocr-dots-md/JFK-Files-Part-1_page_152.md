New Guinea.

21. October 1968

Green Dr.

I thank you for your letter of 23.54.

September, I returned here.

I am sure I could still be of some use in the future if there was any chance of finding something for you to do.

You can see I have an idea of the purpose I aim at, but it is too early to tell. I met a few Americans and they all want me to join their business. I'm not sure that I will. In any case, I will be able to work in a month or so. I remain very grateful for my help from this time, and I remain very happy with my new job.

I have agreed to stay in Paris until the end of January. This will be a good thing for the business in a month's time on the one hand, and for the rest of the world on the other.

I am an excellent anti-authoritarian and dedicate myself to the life of carrying this away to destroy our freedom. However, one must make plans for our future, if we are relevant to take up anything on a short-term basis. I wanted not only to be in Austin, but also to live with them when revenue increases when it expires after two years. In any case, even if this were possible, it would be a long time before we could consider coming to visit them again.
