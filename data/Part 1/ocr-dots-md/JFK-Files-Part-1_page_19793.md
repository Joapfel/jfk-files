SECRET

27 FEB 1959

MEMORANDUM FOR: DIRECTOR OF SECURITY

SUBJECT : PROJECT HARPSTAR

1. With the successful launching of Project "HARPSTAR" I would like to take this opportunity to thank you and your staff for the efficient support given FE Division in this matter.

2. I wish especially to compliment Mr. James P. O'Connell of your staff for his imaginative handling of the many aspects of this complicated operation.

Richard Helms
Chief of Operations
