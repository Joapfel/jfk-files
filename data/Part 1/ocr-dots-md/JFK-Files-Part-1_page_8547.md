Subject was fifteen minutes late for the meeting scheduled for 1600 hours, claiming a terrific hangover and continuation, having spent most of the previous night with REI, driving (\"suppose his eyes were very clear, but then he is quite young.\") I am quite sure that immediately upon his arrival and after making his explanation for being late he added that his car had in the shop for repairs, but at the end of the unproductive meeting he walked to his car parked only half a block away from the cafe. I walked with him and commented on his earlier remark regarding repairs but he denied saying anything about it.

2. Subject had not yet seen LUBAN but said that he planned to attend a party allegedly to be given by Ignatilla (not Sergio's wife,) at which time he will attempt to obtain info re LUBAN's recent trip. Incidentally, Subj. stated that Sergio OLVIDO had returned to Mexico 3 or 4 months ago. (It had been my understanding that Sergio was still in Moscow.) Subject had not seen either of the OLVIDOVs' for several months.

3. We chatted for great length about many innocuous topics, Russian literature, Mexican food, etc. At one point I attempted to have him work on LOYD'S book, but he bagged off, referring again to his excommunication, etc. However, we scheduled a 1000 hour meeting at the JEF cafe for November, 5 August, Subject wishing to be in good condition enabling him to tackle the Part at this time. (Personality, I am not in favor of doing this sort of work in a festooned man.)
