# REPORT OF PROJECTS

## 1. Mr. Julian HAYA

HAYA seems to be a good prospect. I gave a small cocktail party for mutual friends (both political and social!) so that I could observe HAYA's meetings to a variety of problems concerning Latin America. It would appear from his lengthy comments and reactions to comments from others that he would be helpful to us.

HAYA received his Ph.D. in political science at Harvard University, having done undergraduate work at Pomona College. He's traveled widely in Latin America, and spent ten days in Hawaii last year. In speeches in Latin America subsequently he characterized CÁPOLLA as a "paramoid segalomanic".

HAYA is articulate, bilingual and resourceful. Assistant Professor at San Francisco State College and lives in Mexico.

He has, of course, no link with my sale so do not mention it by name in any way if he's contacted. His name is in the valley directory (the brown one) of the Los Angeles area. It's a Mohon number.

## 2. Manuel ARAGON

Appears to be definitely anti-FUERA due to the Cuban invasion plans. He's a very able person, but I doubt that he would be interested in helping us.

## 3. Ralph WUZKAN

Having done blank to ARAGON, I arranged a casual meeting with FUZKAN to Latin American political matters. He's doing Ph.D. thesis at UCLA on Mexican American voters. Bright, resourceful, articulate and bilingual. I would judge that he might be willing to do some work for us.

He lives near UCLA campus and can be reached through graduate school(journalistic science division).
