**MEMORANDUM FOR:** Chief, Information Review Staff
**SUBJECT:** Phillip A. Singerman
**REFERENCE:** Request No. 75-801

1. This Directorate maintains a personality dossier on Phillip A. Singerman. A review of this file and other records of this Directorate reveal the following information pertinent to Subject:

a. A few FBI documents, which are excluded under 1980.3 (c)(4) of the Federal Register. The office that maintains the information on Subject is unable to further identify these documents.

b. Overt information provided by a joint Department of the Army and foreign liaison operation abroad. This material is considered exempt under 5B (1) of E.O. 11552 and 1980.3 (a)(4) and (3) of the Federal Register.

c. Two Agency dispatches based on information from unilateral and liaison sources abroad concerned Subject's travel to Cuba. This material is considered exempt under (b)(3) of FOIA and 5B (1) of E.O. 11652.

d. One memorandum to the FBI reported Subject's travel as mentioned in paragraph 1.c. above. This material is considered exempt for reason stated in paragraph 1.c. above.

i. Form 3535 is attached.

J. F. D. units
Charles A. Briggs
Chief, Services Staff
