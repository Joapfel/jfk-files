MEMORANDUM FOR: CHIEF, SPECIAL SECURITY DIVISION

THROUGH : FI/STC/SFB
SUBJECT : Request for Badge--David Allen PHILLIP

1. It is requested that a limited badge be issued to Mr. David A. Phillips, cover agent for PP/CT, working in the newspaper and lecture field. The limited badge should include access to the Admin. Building, 2210 "E" Street, I, J, K, and L Buildings.

2. Mr. Phillips' needs to have access to these buildings because of his frequent contact with various officials to better equip him for his future assignment. It is anticipated that he will be assigned to this Agency for one year.

3. For further information, contact W. F. Hawley, PP/Security Officer, room 1412 K Building, extension 3600.
