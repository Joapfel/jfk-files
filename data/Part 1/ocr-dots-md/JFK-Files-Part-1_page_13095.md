Subject: Lizarro Guzardo HERNANDEZ Perez
DCC: 17 December 1957, Argentina, Pinar del Rio Prov.
Allen Registration 8A17-405-510

9.- HERNANDEZ claims that he never belonged to any GOC organization.

10.- In April 1951, during the Bay of Pigs Invasion, HERNANDEZ was detained for 11 days at Principe prison in Havana, for not being pro-GOC. On 18 September 1952, HERNANDEZ and seven others were caught when they stole an IHT boat and attempted to escape from Ocoa by Sanako beach, Guanabacoa, Havana. This time, HERNANDEZ was detained (together with his companions) for a month and a half at DSE offices, Quinta corner of Cateierte street, Miramar, then they were released after they were given a lecture that the Revolution was sending them free because it was a general revolution. (Debriefer's note: By that time the GOC was not scattering to Prison terms persons who were caught escaping). During 1954 HERNANDEZ was detained for four days when he had a first fight with a CDR member in his neighborhood, this time HERNANDEZ was aided in getting out of jail by his sister, Andrea HERNANDEZ Perez, who is pro-GOC. In early 1955, HERNANDEZ was detained for two days accused of playing domino (betting).

11.- HERNANDEZ sister; Andrea HERNANDEZ Perez (DPOB: circa 1955, Argentina, Pinar del Rio Province), CDR-member and GOC transmitter. Works at "Salud Pública" with offices at 17 street (rearranged) between 2 and 4 streets, Vedado, employed as office clerk. During March 1957 was punished to work for "Flota de Bayas" at Havana docks, packing fish, when she refused to do voluntary work. During 1959 she was married to (Fno) CANCIO (mat. univ.) (DP05: circa 1927, Havana, Havana Province who is employed as Director Physical Fitness at Havana University, and who is attached GOC supporter and a militant. Andrea and his husband reside at Calle San Miguel (#. univ.) corner of Morques Consolero, Havana city.

d) Manel BELTRAN (mat. univ.); DSE and CP member and installer and repairman for "Area Especial" of the Telephone Company. Attachment #4
