Consider the case where you submit a proposal for an amendment to a rule which has been promulgated under a different name. The point is this, no one should be allowed to change a rule once it has become established as part of law. It is not necessary to state the reason for changing a rule.
The only time a rule may be changed is when it becomes law.
A rule can only become law if it is adopted by a body having authority to adopt laws.
The process for establishing a new rule is this:
1. Propose the new rule.
2. Present the new rule.
3. Submit the new rule for adoption.
4. Pass a vote on the proposed new rule.
5. Adopt the rule with or without modifications.

There is no such thing as a rule being "abandoned" or "restituted."
