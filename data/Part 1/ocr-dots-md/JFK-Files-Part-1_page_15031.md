10. Mr. Heegard stressed that each applicant is processed thoroughly to avoid sending an unqualified person to South America.

11. Basic Spanish, according to Mr. Heegard, can be learned by an applicant over a period of three months - at least three hours of instruction per week. This should be done prior to departure from Germany.

12. During the processing the applicant receives a pamphlet explaining the economic, social, political, etc. conditions of the country of his choice.

13. Mr. Heegard said that, in general, promotional opportunities are excellent in certain fields due to the lack of a qualified personnel.

14. The Special Program is the only one under which ICEM operates for foreign refugees desiring to emigrate from Europe to South America.

15. Professions and skills required in South America are: (COLUMBIA) Electrical machine construction (Elektromaschinenbau): engineer, technician (drafting and operating), draftsman, master, cutting-tool maker (Schneiwerkzeugmacher), matrix maker for plastic material. Metallurgy: Skilled foundry worker; moulder (metal foundry); rolling-mill worker and technician, casting mould matrix maker (Metallguss-form-Schabionenmacher). Miscellaneous professions: Repair mechanic, photo-engraver, offset-retoucher, technician for plastic material, papermaker.

16. Professions and skills not required in South America: Lawyers, office worker, specialist for commerce and sale, construction engineer, construction technician and construction worker, carpenter, leather manufacturer, waiter, servant, unskilled laborer.

17. Housing - ICEM does not find housing for this migrant but may give advice if needed.
