# COURSE OBJECTIVES - COMMENT AND METHODS

This course, designed for the staff employee who requires a basic knowledge of counterintelligence doctrine and methodology, aims to increase his understanding of this activity by (a) acquainting him with current counterintelligence concepts and objectives and the organization and functions of counterintelligence targets, (b) familiarizing him with skills and techniques employed in detecting, investigating, and operating against targets, and (c) acquainting him how to report, record, and disseminate counterintelligence information.

The student is instructed through the media of lectures, directed reading, tours, training films, examinations, class exercises, and discussions.
