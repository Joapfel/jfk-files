19 May 1964

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Ted Skule
Telephonic Conversation

1. Ted Skule called the undersigned at home shortly before 2300 on 18 May 1964 from Miami, Florida. I began the conversation by telling Skule that I had discussed the matter of a contact in the Miami area and that it was our opinion that we could best collaborate with him from Washington in ammasing as it was only here that the full story is known. Skule accepted this with the comment that he would naturally go along with our judgment in this matter. The essence of our general conversation follows:

a. *Skule meets with RAY in Caribbean.*

Toward the end of our conversation, Skule interjected the statement, "Oh, I guess I had better tell you because you will read it in the paper in the morning, that I have just come from a meeting with RAY." Skule gave the impression of being very satisfied with his trip and convinced that RAY was fully prepared for his venture. In answering a question that I had raised on 18 May, Skule informed me that he was convinced that there was no serious discussion within the ranks of the JUNX. It is my opinion that this is true, but that there are small frictions which are particularly inherent in Cuban organizations. I commented to Skule that if he had just seen RAY, then in all likelihood RAY would not be in Cuba by the 20th. Skule assured me that he would be in Cuba by the 20th. (Note: On 17 May I spoke with Reggio CISARENO, who informed me that he would have an important message for me in two or three days. I have a premise from RAY and CISARENO.)
