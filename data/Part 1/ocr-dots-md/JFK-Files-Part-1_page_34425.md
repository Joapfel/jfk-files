SECRET

1 May 1963

**TOBE: Coordination Outline - Donald E. Thomas, Bell Labs**

**FROM: New York Field Office**

Following is coordination outline for D. E. Thomas. Dr. W. O. Baker requested that Thomas be given defensive briefing by us before going behind certain. As you are already aware, Baker is Vice President at Bell Labs and member of President's Scientific Advisory Council and President's Intelligence Board.

1. **Name:** Donald Edward Thomas
2. **Date and Place of Birth:** 18 May 1907, Denver(Touchup), Pella.
3. **Address:** 48 Fairview Avenue, Madison, Evy Jersey.
4. **Place of Employment and position:** Bell Labs, Murray Hill, Evy Jersey; Electronics Engineer.
5. **Previous:** Subject leaves US 3 May for Moscow to attend Popov Society meeting, 5-14 May. Following Moscow meeting he will visit labs in Western Europe for approximately one week.
6. **Mission and reason for trip:** Subject was invited to give paper at Popov meeting by IBM Academy of Sciences via X.E.S.E. (International Electronics and Electrical Engineers).
7. **Security Status:** We hold favorable HAG completion on subject dated 1st Jan '99.
8. **Previous Intelligence Activities:** Subject was contacted by GCS 1958-59 concerning info on Soviet work in his field which he acquired from contacts with Soviet scientific counterparts attending International Meeting in US.
9. ** Associates or close friends in denied areas?** Yes.
10. **Contact of previous trips to denied areas?** No.
11. **Analysis of Subject's Competence:** Po. State College, B.A., B.S., 1949; Columbia University, M.A., 1952; US Army Signal Corps, 1949-54; USEW, 1944-45, on radio countermeasures, 1944-45; Retired Lt. Col. USEW, U.S. Airborne (Civilian) personnel on Electronic Countermeasures MAD Board, Page of Defense, 1948-51.
12. **Status of personal assessment:** During our limited relationship with subject, he has given every indication of being disorient, securityensems, cooperative, will activated, etc. He is will regarded in his particular field of competence.
13. **Future of subjective briefing proposed and requirements to be met?** No. Subject will not be briefed on operations of any kind. No photography or insurmounting note taking will be requested of him.
14. **Objective and realities briefings** Subject will receive routine defensive and fraternal realities briefings.
15. **Passing for trip?** Subject's trip will be funded by his agency.
16. **Briefing office and participants:** Briefing will be conducted by 8/19 New York Office personnel.
17. **Coordination Outline:** 8 May 1963.
