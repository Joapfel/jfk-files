1. The following is a quotation of a conversation held on 2 December 1959:

A man outside called the Soviet Embassy, introduced himself as Yevgeniy N. VOYEVODIN and asked Yelena Ivanovna FILIPOVA, secretary of the Commercial Office, if he could still send a cable off (that late) on that date. FILIPOVA promised to try to get the cable off. VOYEVODIN then said he wished the cable sent to - RAZNOEKSPORT, Moscow, 9019, 9021 and then dictated:

"Exposition will close on 15th of December. To deliver the souvenirs is too late.

VOYEVODIN."

2. It is requested that this pouch be routed via KUTUBE/D.

Willard C. CURTIS

20 Jan. 1960
