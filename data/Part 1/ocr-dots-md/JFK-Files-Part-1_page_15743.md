13 September 1960

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD.

SUBJECT: Contact Report

1. On 8 September 1970 the undersigned accompanied by S/3B case officers Perry Crookham and Fred Kirkpatrick met with David Titzschwill (201-233477) in a 50/DCH safe house. The purpose of this meeting was to assess David for possible use by JMA C. The subject was aare he was being interviewed as the same procedure had been used previously by a representative of the African Division.

2. The subject has led an exciting and diverse life. The SI Division brought him to the United States (black) to train for a mission which has subsequently been cancelled. At the present time the SI Division is making arrangements for the resettlement of the subject in Latin America, probably in Mexico. However, due to the training this agency has given the subject SI feels he should be utilized by the DD/P if possible.

3. Observing David for some two hours and listening to his story the undersigned feels there may be some use of him by JMAC. David is a smooth talker who has obviously told his story many times to the satisfaction of his listeners. He elaborates on the exciting and bizarre parts of his life and tends to gloss over those sections he feels that are common to everyone.

4. The subject has been in the United States for some time. This time has been spent in training for a specific mission which never came to pass. Recently he has been engaged in "make work" programs to pass the time. This could well explain his apparent impatience and desire to do something. In his own words he wishes to prove that he is capable of accomplishing a task assigned to him. He asks for a chance to surveil someone, to take clandestine pictures, even to assassinate someone. He very nearly succeeded to be given a pitch during this meeting.

5. The subject is a stateless person. He is in the United States black and could be completely unattributable if necessary. He desires citizenship and a chance to settle down in one spot. He prefers U.S. citizenship but is not adjacent on this. However the basic motivation appears to be love of excitement. The subject gives a very good appearance of the classical adventurer. He relates his past exploits in wartime Europe as motivated by the thrill of dueling the existing authority.
