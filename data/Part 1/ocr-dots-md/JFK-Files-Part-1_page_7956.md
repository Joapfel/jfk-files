30 NOV 1964

TO : Director
Federal Bureau of Investigation
Attention: R. S. J. Polich

FROM : Deputy Director for Plans

SUBJECT : Martin Luther King, Jr.

1. Reference is made to a communication from your Bureau dated 20 August 1964 concerning proposed travel to Western Europe of Martin Luther King, Jr. Reference noted that subject planned to visit France, Italy, on 18 and 19 September 1964.

2. This agency has received the following information on subject from a source who is considered unavailable. Dr. Martin Luther King, past with various leaders of the Italian Communist Party (PCI) and of the Italian socialist party (PSP), on 18 September 1964 in Rome. He discussed with these Italian politicians the program of demonstrations against suppression in the United States which are to be organized in Italy during the months immediately ahead. Dr. PCI reportedly conferred with PCI Senator Vello SPANO and Sen. CECILIA ROMA, with PCI senator Vello VITTORINI, and with Deputy Vice President of the Italian socialist party of Peasantist Unity (PIUP).

3. This Agency is unable to evaluate the above report.

4. The information in paragraph two has been made available to your Bureau's representative in New York.
