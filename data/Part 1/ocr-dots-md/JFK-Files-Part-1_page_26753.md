Reel 73-RICO-30

From 1325, 3 May (Wednesday) to 0625, 6 May 1965

2.0 In. Wrong number.

2.2 In. Sra. Rico with Victor Rico Galan (VKG), who answers.
Greetings...about how everyone is...

2.5 In. WO asks VKG, who answers, for Sra. Diener. Joyeria
Porra (?) is calling.

Inge Rico takes the phone...about price for repairing a
clock...

3.7 Out to 23-02-91. VKG (v) orders a cab.

3.0 In. WO with & Inge Rico, who answers, for VKG. Not there.
WO has the documents, the data, she offered to give VKG. I will tell
him. WO is Carman.

3.2 In. Sr. Valentin (Soviet) with Inge Rico, who answers, for
VKG. Not there.

3.5 In. Miric (v) with VKG, who answers. Greetings...
How does VKG see the problem? VKG doesn't think they (U.S.) will
leave there (Dominican republic). Miric thinks this is going to be
a long-term think. Will O.E.A. approve the force there? Yes, Miric
says, they have the majority of votes. Since Dominican Republic
does not vote..,Nothing can be done. VKG thinks that the Dominicans
are handling things well. Miric thinks that this Cease Fire is dan-
gerous. Things quiet down..a coup d'etat can take place....
Miric is going to be very busy with CVPAL meeting taking
place starting tomorrow. Miric will be out nearly all day. They
have inauguration tomorrow at Seguro Social and plenary sessions will
take place in theater of Banco debanco Nacional on Reforma. One
can get accredited to go into sessions. If he is not accredited, can
get materials. Perhaps Miric can inform him where this is tomorrow.
Where will VKG be at noontime tomorrow? At home. Miric will call
VKG after inauguration tomorrow morning at "Siempre." Fine.

Reel off at NT 4.7, 0625, 6 May 1965
