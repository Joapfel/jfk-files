SECURITY INFORMATION

2 April 1953

TO: DEPUTY COUNTERLYST (PLANS)

VIA: Chief, Political and Psychological Warfare

SUBJECT: Points raised by you in connection with Mr. Turke's conversation with Leland Stone on 11 March 1953 and Whitney Shepardson on 16 March 1953.

The following answers to Mr. Minner's comments on subject matter have been prepared by Mr. Turke:

1. "Mr. Lang does not intend to put Stone in official charge of Munich operations. His remark was intended to indicate that apparently with Lang's consent, Mr. Stone is becoming increasingly influential in the Munich operation. If Sykes' Information Service develops, so that it is writing stories and programming as he proposes, and if Stone's suggestions for dealing with the German problems are followed, he will be looked upon as having full support, he the determinant factor in Munich."

2. "The original 'ice-box' statement was indeed passed on by us. By reference was to our approval of an alternate 'ice-box' statement entitled, 'UKEE policy Statement', which Statement proposes to use in place of the original. I have not been able to determine whether this receiver's approval and, therefore, on 24 March, sent it through channels to the B/P and U. 2/4 for their official approval."

THOMAS W. BRADEN
Chief
International Organizations Division
