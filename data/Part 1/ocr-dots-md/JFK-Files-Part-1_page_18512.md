COMMISSIONER OF AUSTRALIA
AUSTRALIAN SECURITY INTELLIGENCE ORGANISATION

G.P.O. CORR. 5103BB.,
ACCOUNTANT

May, 1966.

My Liaison Officer in Home has told me of the tremendous assistance and co-operation that he and his section received at all times from Mr. W.K. Harvey.

I understand that Mr. Harvey has now returned to Washington and I would like to say how much I appreciate all that he has done to assist the work of my officers in home.

With kindest regards and best wishes,

Yours sincerely,
C.E.B. SPRY

The Honorable W.F. Ruborn,
Director,
Central Intelligence Agency,
WASHINGTON.
