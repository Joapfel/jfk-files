SECURITY INFORMATION

CRUSADE FOR FREEDOM

The Crusade is in full support of the Crusade for Freedom, a major peace-relief mechanism. From September 1, 1950 to date, the Crusade has been responsible for some $7 billion dollars in contributions to UNGF and RFF. Donations for the Crusade, over the same period, amounted to some 21⁄2 billion dollars. All monies raised, such as expenses, or a total of $1.3 billion, have been used exactly and solely on LDC's capital installations, i.e., banks, schools, receivers, surcharging equipment.

NOTES

In July 1950 employed 177, of whom 118 were Americans, 61 of whom were employed in Germany. At present, LIF has a total staff of 1770, of whom 252 are American. In New York, the striking and administrative headquarters, the staff number 195. In London, where for the programming headquarters, there are 1249 employees. In Lisbon, the technical relay headquarters, there are 133 scientifics. These figures include only staff employees on the regular payroll.
