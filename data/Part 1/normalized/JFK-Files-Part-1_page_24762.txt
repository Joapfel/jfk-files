Comments on Dispatch HPLW-5390 Prepared by WH/SAO

SUBJECT: The LEONOV Matter (Nikolay Sergeyevich LEONOV, (201-132138)

REF : HPLW-5390, Typed 29 August 1969

GENERAL OBSERVATIONS:

1. With one exception (HGGW-4035, 2 Dec 66), the personality and biographic report on LEONOV is based entirely on Mexico City reporting. Much of this appears to be quite sensitive if passage to Peru liaison is intended.

2. Since the report concerns a Soviet official, the fact that the report is based upon WH Division material exclusively appears to be self-limiting and, possibly, self-defeating.

3. Although the writer of the report refers to its acceptability to SB Division, there is no SB coordinator.

4. It would appear that, to achieve the desired result (and we don't really know what this is), it would not be necessary to go into such exacting detail. A more generalized report, following more or less an AEASSAULT format, might achieve the results desired by Station Lima without the pitfalls of utilizing the sensitive material incorporated in the present compilation.
