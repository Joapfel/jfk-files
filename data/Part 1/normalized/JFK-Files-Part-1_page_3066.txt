Forwarded under separate cover for your information in a copy of a BIO/CULAR report of 8 January describing a conversation between Ivan Gavrilovich ALFARAY and Boeoeor player Lev YABANIN (DPOB: 22 October 1969, Moscow, Passport 331432). YABANIN and the rest of the Moscow Iynama team had just arrived at Excela Airport on Varig Flight 845 from Rio and were departing for Santiago on BAD Flight 955 after a layover in Argentina of three and one half hours. The team was on a Latin American tour covering Chile, Peru, Colombia, and Ecuador. 2. During the conversation EOB officer ALFARAY and YABANIN, whom we believe is EOB, show that they are fairly close friends. They also mention mutual friends: Ivan Gavrilovich ALFARAY (201-731434), a suspect EOB officer formerly stationed in Argentina; and Vitaliy Borisovich BEBANIN, the former Consul/EOS officer in Chile.
