1. A representative of ELIJEP recently contacted subject, a U.S. manufacturers' representative, who lives in Mexico City. After ELIJEP made its standard explanation of KUBARK interests, the subject said that he felt that he should make it clear that he worked for KUBARK in Mexico. He qualified this statement by saying that his exclusive background and experience in Mexico enabled him to set up meeting places and front offices as well as other cover projects for KUBARK, MCACID and CDHWY.

2. Subject seemed quite candid and ELIJEP had no reason to believe that he was not telling the truth. Subject doubted that he could have KUBARK a great deal of information which was not available through official agencies operating in Mexico. However, he seems to be temporarily不可readable on the situation there and ELIJEP feels that he might be a source of some interesting information if their contacting him would not interfere with his arrangements which your Mission might have made with him.

3. Headquarters requests your comments regarding subject. Please advise as soon as possible, since ELIJEP would like to contact subject again when he returns to this country in July.
