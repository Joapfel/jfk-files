8 July 1964

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division

VIA : Deputy Chief, VI (Special Affairs)

SUBJECT : AMWORLD--Proposed Meeting between Chief, VI and AMBIDDY-1 in August 64

1. AMBIDDY-1 has expressed a desire to meet with Chief, Western Hemisphere Division after the meeting in August 64.

3. We have replied via AMJAVA-4 that Chief, VI will be glad to meet with AMBIDDY-1 at that time.

3. We intend to hold on to the Maryland safehouse until after the meeting has taken place. Preferably AMBIDDY-1 should be routed via San, Puerto Rico, to Baltimore Friendship Airport whence he would be picked up at a nearby hotel and taken to the safehouse.

Henry D. Hecksher WH/EA/SO/MH
