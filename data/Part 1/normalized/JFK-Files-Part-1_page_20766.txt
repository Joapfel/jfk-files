TECHNICAL SERVICES DIVISION -- TECHNICAL SCHOOL SHORT RANGE AGENT CONTACT SURVEY (A-105)

Training Report

Name : PHILLIPS, David A.

Office: WH/COG

Date : 6 June 1969

I. OBJECTIVES:

To provide a general knowledge in:

Selected gear used for clandestine short range agent contacts. Included are representative samples of:

One way RF radio devices; two way RF radio devices; two way carrier current devices; optical communications; and special telephone devices used for establishing agent contact.

The philosophy, purpose, considerations and limitations of short range agent contact systems, including message security, link security, reliability and feasibility of agent contact systems.

INSTRUCTOR: TSD/TECHNICAL SCHOOL
