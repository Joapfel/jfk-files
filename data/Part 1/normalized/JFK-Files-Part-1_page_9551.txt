MEMORANDUM TO: CPS II

SUBJECT: Meeting with Kenneth S. HALEY and Messrs. Williams, Droller, Glessner, and Smith re Berlin Conference

1. Direct action is imperative in Berlin.

Organize groups to beat the Communist opposition in street fighting.

Carry the counterattack into the Soviet Sector.

It is not desirable to have Soviet's fire on German citizens.

2. Fighting, against the background of the Peace Conference, with the general population and the conference participants in the streets, would have tremendous and desirable effect.

To be beaten in the streets would be defeat complete.

A fight is important. A successful fight is imperative. It would cause good publicity, instill confidence in the Berlin population and insure major victories for our side.

If no Communist fighting group plans to start the fight, create one and beat it up.

3. A head-on challenge — rioting and perhaps shooting — is inherent in the streets as it is now conceived.

4. HALEY thinks that the Soviet authorities plan control of the streets.

5. What would mass effect be of a background of American tanks?

If it is necessary to prevent defeat — do it.

It would be far better, however, to have force exercised by the Berlin population — with no American military force in sight...

CENTRAL OFFICE
