1. SDOM HAS INQUIRED WHETHER EDWARD O. TICHBORN COULD BE MADE AVAILABLE FOR USE IN DOMREP DURING THREE WEEKS PRECEDING ONE JUNE ELECTION. Although Recognised Problem due XIRGAL is not now in force AND ISSUES IN DOMREP MAY WOULD APPRECIATE NEXI COMMENTS ON TICHBORN AVAILABILITY AND ON ANY COVER OR OTHER PROBLEMS SUCH ASSIGNMENT MIGHT INVOLVE.

2. IF MEXI HAS NO OBJECTION SDOM WOULD LIKE HAVE TICHBORN COME FOR TWO WEEK TDY EARLY IN APRIL TO READ IN ON SITUATION, THEN RETURN DURING FIRST WEEK MAY FOR CONCENTRATED ELECTION EFFORT.
