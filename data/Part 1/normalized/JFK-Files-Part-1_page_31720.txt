AGENDA:

2. In your discussions with Henry Ford and D'A. Brophy Wednesday, I wonder whether you should make the following points:

The principal objectives of the AIF program are to provide cover for HCP-FCE and CFA through a multiplicity of ostensible sources of funds. Fund raising is a distinctly secondary objective.

For several reasons, cover fund-raising for the Committee for a Free Asia should be carried on outside the Crusade for Freedom activity. We may at some future date wish to transfer the fund-raising for the Committee for a Free Asia to some other organization. In any event, we do not want the Crusade name linked with the Committee for a Free Asia.

The previous Crusade operations have been inefficiently operated. It has cost a dollar to raise a dollar. We know that AIF will be run on a businesslike basis, as have AIF past operations.
