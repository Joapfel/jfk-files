CLEMAND JOSEPH CHARLES, WHO HAS STATED ON NUMEROUS OCCASIONS THAT WE WILL BE THE NEXT PRESIDENT OF HAITI AND WHO HAS GATHERED ABOUT HIMSELF A PERSONAL FOLLOWING OF INDIVIDUALS COVERTLY OPPOSED TO THE GOVERNMENT OF PRESIDENT DUVALIER /COLLECTOR'S NOTE: SEE-90-2 - 321/76913-69/, WAS NOW DECLARED THAT ACTION AGAINST HAITI'S PRESIDENT IS IMPRETABLE AND THAT DUVALIER'S OVERTHROW MUST AND CAN BE ACCOMPLISHED DURING THE MONTH OF JUNE 1969. WE HAS SET A 1 JULY 1969 DEADLINE FOR THE ACCOMPLISHMENT OF HIS GOAL. CHARLES HAS ALSO STATED THAT TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE BECAUSE OTHER OPPOSITION GROUPS /COLLECTOR'S NOTE: PREVIOUSLY, HE REFERS TO ONE OR MORE OF THOSE GROUPS LISTED IN OCR-321/769.3-69./ MAY TRY TO ACT BEFORE WE CAN. CHARLES LISTS NO REASON FOR THE SPECIFIC TIME LIMIT OF 1 JULY 1969.

2. CHARLES HAS REQUESTED THAT "THE PENTAGON" (SPECIFICALLY, HIS FORMER INTERMEDIATE, COLONEL HOWARD BURR) DISPATCH A SECRET ENVOY TO TALK TO HIM. HE LAYS DOWN NO MODELS OPERATIONAL FOR THE ENVOY AND PREVIOUSLY WILL TALK TO HIM IN PORT-AU-PRINCE. THIS REQUEST HAS BEEN LABELED "Urgent".
