Matt MacVane, DCD officer in Miami, met with Joseph R. MEROLA on 15 January 1974; at which time MEROLA identified the friend in San Salvador who had identified Mr. Gremillion in the American Embassy in San Salvador as a CIA representative. MEROLA also provided the names of two women employed in the Embassy who were involved in compromising situations with both Americans and local nationals. MEROLA identified the two women as Nancy SHALLOT and Delia OBESA. MEROLA was met again on 17 January 1974 at which time he provided the name of a third woman in the Embassy. Gloria, who has also been involved in compromising situations. Upon inquiring Headquarters personnel who had recently returned from San Salvador, the above identities were confirmed; all three are American citizens assigned to the Embassy in San Salvador.

Above is a note dated 24 January 1974 from patti Burke, WH/2/GS/IA, based on information received orally from DCD.
