OPERATIONAL ACTIVITY

A. Preparations were underway in early November to dispatch AMTRUNK-13 to Madrid in mid-November to cement the recruitment of AMTEBUNK-19. These plans fell through when the Madrid Station notified Headquarters and WAVE that their workload would not allow sufficient case officer time to handle AMTEBUNK-13 in Madrid at this time. WAVE agreed to postpone AMTEBUNK-13's trip to Madrid several weeks until Madrid Station's workload abated somewhat.
