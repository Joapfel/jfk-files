1. LIST YOUR MAJOR DUTIES IN APPROPRIATE ORDER OF IMPORTANCE, WITH A BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF EACH. EXIT MINOR DUTIES.

Present position is Chief, SR/3 (West Branch). Responsible to the Chief, SR Division for directing and planning SK operations in the western USNSE, BSSR and Ukrainian SSR. These operations cover the RI, 77 and PM missions of SR Division. Chief, SR/3 is also responsible for directing and coordinating operations directed at the above areas of the USSR mounted from Germany, Austria, Switzerland, the Low Countries, France, Italy and the Iberian Peninsula. To implementing the latter, coordination is effected with the appropriate area decks of the FE and WE Divisions. In addition to these above responsibilities in the operational field, Chief, SR/3 is responsible for directing counter-evasion operations arising from RIS action against enotre groups with which we are in contact and our positive operations.
