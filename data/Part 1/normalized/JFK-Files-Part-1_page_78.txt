MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Central Intelligence VIA : Deputy Director for Plans SUBJECT : Letter for Director from Sir Charles Spry (ABIO)

1. Attached is a sealed letter from Sir Charles Spry, Director General of the Australian Security Intelligence Organisation (who provided a copy of the letter to the Chief of Station, Melbourne), and a suggested reply for your signature.

2. Sir Charles' letter to you recommends against declassification of the Warren Commission document CD-871, which refers to our investigation of anonymous telephone calls to the Canberra Embassy before and after the ascension of President Kennedy.

3. I consider the points made by Sir Charles in his letter to be valid and accordingly recommend against the declassification of CD-871 in the foreseeable future. CI Staff concurs with this recommendation.

William K. Nelson Chief, Far East Division
