LISACK Progress Report, 1 July - 30 September 1969

Reference: LIEA-37451, 1 July 1969

Operations

Under this roof project, operations continue to be directed against the IED ARMY target and, on occasions, Bloc targets. Activities covered by the Project include providing information on IED ARMYHERS, their activities, personalities, visitors, and contacts (LISACK-2, -4, -6, and LICOML-1); serving as access agents (LICRAVE-1, -2, -3, LIROCKET-1, and BESABER); spotting, assessing and occasionally recruiting access agents (LIEMPTY-3); providing information on DETET activities (DEPEMTAL-1) and BEOOX activities (BESABER); and performing such other tasks as may be required on an ad hoc basis (LISACK-7 and all other assets of the Project).

Administration

As assets take on a permanent character in terms of expenditure or other continuing administration, they are transferred from SB/OCA to LISACK for orderly handling. Beginning with this reporting period, and as outlined in the LISACK Project Renewal, BESABER, DEPEMTAL, and LICOML-1 were transferred from SB/OCA to LISACK.
