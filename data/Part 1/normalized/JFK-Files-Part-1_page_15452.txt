FROM Chief of Station, Leopoldville

SUBJECT Major Peter DE GROEVE

ATTACHMENT

DATE 2 April 1962

RECEIVED APR 27 1962 11:00

1. The recent purchase of three DC-3s and a DC-4 by the Congolese government is having continuing repercussions. This deal has been reported in detail through both TELINE and OUNIT channels including the acquisitions of substantial graft which accompanied it. The most recent developments, however, have not yet, to the Station's knowledge, been reported by either of the above channels.

2. STAHl reported to HESTON in a meeting 28 February that WIRGUE is back with the Congolese Air Force. STAHl described him as an Austrian with a Belgian passport, formerly with the German commandos, who knows nothing about aviation. STAHl continued to say

Major Peter DE GROEVE (Advisor to Congolese Air Force ca. February 61 - March 62. Belgian. Involved in purchase of three DC-3 planes from Iran Airways at price $125,000 per aircraft. Competent authorities stated comparable aircraft could be purchased $50,000 to $60,000 and predicted trouble from purchases.)

left the Congo with 14,000 pounds which, supposedly, was his share of some 814,000 reputedly made on the above airplane purchases. According to STAHl, DE GROEVE bled someone at the Suruf who then let him cross safely to Brasssville.

3. On 30 March WIRGUE informed SALVACK that the CCS had put him in charge of investigating the above airplane purchases. His first real task is to arrest DE GROEVE and bring him back to the Congo. Congo (Brassa), however, refuses to cooperate and told WIRGUE when he arrested them that DE GROEVE had requested political refuge. Besides, WIRGUE felt, the Brassa side of the river would be very hostile to return a white man to the tender mercies of the Congolese in Leopoldville. DEROEVE is supposedly in Pointe Noire waiting for a French ship to take him to Marseille, a voyage of some 20 days. WIRGUE is currently toying with various ideas for possibly kidnapping DE GROEVE in Pointe Noire.

4. Both WIRGUE and STAHl said DE GROEVE is wanted for rape in Belgium. WIRGUE said he had also stolen some money there, though STAHl's version was that DE GROEVE had debts amounting to 400,000 Belgian francs. In either case, it is doubtful if DE GROEVE will return to Belgium.

Lester H. SALVACK

2 April 1962
