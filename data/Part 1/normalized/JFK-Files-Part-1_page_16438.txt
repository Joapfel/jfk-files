REF: WH/MIAMI 09383 (L-234350)

1. RE PARA I REF, ON 21 DECEMBER ROBERTO RODRIGUEZ LLONPART CALLED DIONISIO ARRANZ TREMOLS, CUBAN COMMERCIAL COUNSELOR IN LONDON, AND TOLD HIM HE LEAVING THAT DATE FOR LONDON. LLONPART, WHO TRAVELLING WITH EUGENIO GONZALEZ ALVEREZ, WAS TO BE MET BY ARRANZ AT AIRPORT.

2. SHORTLY AFTERWARDS, BLANQUITA (PROBABLY BLANCA CALVET, ADMINISTRATOR CUBAN COMMERCIAL OFFICE LONDON) CALLED RODRIGUEZ LLONPART AND SAID SHE LOOKING FORWARD TO HIS VISIT. RODRIGUEZ ASKED BLANCA NOT GIVE UP APARTMENT IN LONDON WHICH THEY SCHEDULED GIVE UP 1 JANUARY SO THAT HE MIGHT USE IT.

3. BOTH ARRANZ AND BLANQUITA ASKED RODRIGUEZ ABOUT HIS BROTHER'S EYESIGHT TO WHICH RODRIGUEZ REPLIED HE GETTING ALONG QUITE WELL.
