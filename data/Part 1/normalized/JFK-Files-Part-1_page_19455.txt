27 May 1977

MEMORANDUM IN LIEU OF FITNESS REPORT

SUBJECT: Moore, J. Walton

1. This is a reassignment report covering the period 1 January to 1 June 1977. It is prepared due to the reassignment of the rating officer.

2. During the period under review, Mr. Moore continued in his assignment as Chief of the Dallas Office. He has directed effectively the attention of the office to the collection of priority intelligence information which has resulted in the submission of an assortment of well-prepared reports on important intelligence topics. In managing the full range of collection and support functions, Mr. Moore's performance as Office Chief continues to be marked by exceptional proficiency.

Overall Rating: STRONG.

ELBERT H. WATTS Deputy Chief of Operations, DCD

I certify that I have seen this report.

J. Walton Moore
