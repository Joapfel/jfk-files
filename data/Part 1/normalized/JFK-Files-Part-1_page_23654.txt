Transmitted for approval herewith is the operational plan

for ANTRONIX VII. The ANTRONIX team is scheduled for infiltration on 7 February and for exfiltration on 17 February. A five-man team with Intermediate Craft will be infiltrated into northern Batemanas Province from which point the team leader, AMICE-14, and ANTRONIX-13 will proceed into Lavena to accomplish the MEAPRAN objectives of this operation. Approval to be requested at the earliest possible time.
