III.- JAPAN-LATIN AMERICA RELATIONS.

A.- Japan's economic and trade interests in Latin America.

1. Japan has 77% of its foreign investment placed in Latin America.

2. Proof of Japan's increasing interest in Latin America has been the visit of two Japanese Trade Missions to this Hemisphere in less than one year — (March and September 1965).
