Reel 30-841-6

3-June 1966

7.5 In. NO asks Olga (v), who answers, for Sr. Civeira. Civeira takes phone. Blain (?pn) called. Wanted to talk to C. Fine. They have to go by aeroplanes to Mexico before 1300 for tickets to Los Angeles. There is a flight leaving every day. martinez barroso called and there is no flight until day after tomorrow.

MI takes phone. There is no direct flight to Los Angeles. In any case they have to go to play for tickets. Have to go before 1330. MI will go by sc. if and only if can go by for tickets.

8.0 In. WO with acid, who answers...wrong number.

8.2 In. WO with acid, who answers. Asks if this is house of Ambassador. Yes, it is house of Sr. Martinez. But then says it is wrong number. Acid thinks Martinez B. Is Amb.

8.5 In. WO with Juanita, who answers, for Marqueta. Juanita comes to phone. About WO's work...sings...and...(Sra.) went downstairs at 1500. Aid just got back, Sra. went on to the embassy. About visiting Marqueta this Sunday. I think is not sure if she will be off on Sunday....WO's Mrs. is having guests on Saturday evening...

Reel off at RT 10.2, 2100, 3 June 1966
