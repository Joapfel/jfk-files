CONTINUATION OF DISPATCH SECRET REDA-23487

5. On 2 March 1968 LIOUENI-1 reported that she had decided to visit her brother in PARIS for two weeks or so, and will take advantage of the trip to renew her tourist documentation. She said that LIOUNI-1 has become extremely difficult to live with since REIBECKER was here, and that she must get away from him for a while. He objected to her leaving, but acquiesced when he became convinced that she intends to return to Mexico. LIOUNI-1 plans to leave on 4 March 1968, and will return on or about 20 March 1968.

William C. CURTIS
