His initial training in the intricacies of his duty assignment was marked by a high degree of application, interest, and achievement. Because of his fine record, Mr. Flores was chosen one of the three specialists for this section. He specializes in the Spanish-Portuguese area and his work is characterized by exceptional proficiency.

At present Mr. Flores is registered at American University as a sophomore. Inasmuch as he has exhibited both potentiality and strong working habits, I recommend that Mr. Flores be given the Professional Battery Test at the earliest opportunity.
