Dec. 6 : Reimbursement of the remaining of the check 771 (Rafael Miguel) that was extended in the amount of $400.00 for a Caribbean Conference

213.51

NOTE: This reimbursement correspond to an amount that, in excess of what corresponded to the expense for which the check was extended, was returned by Mr. Miguel and deposited in the account "Pau-la and Sosa". It represents real diminution in the expenses of the month of November, that is compensated when been considered as incomes in this month.
