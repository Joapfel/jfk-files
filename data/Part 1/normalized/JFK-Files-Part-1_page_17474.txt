3. The Chief of State Peraltan made a rather impassioned speech in which he covered the following points:

His own long experience in the OAS, in Cuba and in Central America;

His early warnings about Castro--which were disregarded;

The extent to which Guatemala has lived up to its obligations in the OAS; to which it has cooperated in efforts against Castro; and to which it has looked toward U.S. leadership;

The failure of the U.S. to take aggressive action against Cuba and the need for taking a hard line, including the use of force in support of military activity by the free Cubans and the other American countries;

His appreciation that the Soviets will not be willing to risk a nuclear war over Cuba;

Condemnation of Mexico for her dealings with Cuba, for failure to abide by her obligations in the OAS, and for permitting the Castroites to operate out of Mexico against other American countries;
