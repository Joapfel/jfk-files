SUBJECT Chief of Station, Caracas / S/1 Venezuelan Security Service Study on the Diamond Fields in the Venezuelan Guyana Territory

ACTION REQUIRED · REFERENCES Action: FYI

Attached is a study in Spanish language prepared by the Venezuelan Directorate of the Service of Intelligence and Prevention (DISIP) in the Ministry of Interior on a diamond field in the Venezuelan Guyana region which may be of interest to the Venezuelan and Guyana Desks. It was provided by CIFENCE-4, a unilateral security source, on 26 April 1972. According to CIFENCE-4, President Rafael Caldera was very interested in the study and excerpted large portions verbatim for one of his weekly press conferences.
