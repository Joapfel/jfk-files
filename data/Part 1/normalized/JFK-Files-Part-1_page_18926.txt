SECRE

JUN. 13 1951

TO : Chief, FDT

FROM : Chief, Investigative Techniques Section, TRD

SUBJECT: Counterintelligence Techniques Course

1. Ann Goodpasture was enrolled in the counter-intelligence techniques course for the period 29 May through 5 June 1951. He received instruction in the following elements:

Familiarisation with the techniques of long range photography (taking of telephoto pictures)

Instruction in methods of intensifying and reducing of negatives

Field expedient for the taking of extremely long range pictures

Simple technique for producing small images of documents for concealment purposes

Work with the Speed Graphic camera and accessories

Practice in the taking of photo-flash pictures

Work with the Photo-Record and Recordak cameras

Continued practice in darkroom techniques

Locking devices, their relative security, methods of picking safes and combination locks

Instruction and demonstration of microphones and wire-tapping and recording equipment

Surreptitious entry methods, opening of sealed documents

Fingerprints, possibilities, limitations, and laboratory practice

Scientific aids, identification of tool marks, string, paper, etc.

2. This student completed all phases of the training in a satisfactory manner.

3. Additional comments: Miss Goodpasture had to leave the course before the end of the second week.

R. L. MILBROO
