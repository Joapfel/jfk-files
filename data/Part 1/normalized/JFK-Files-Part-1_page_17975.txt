CIRCULATION

1. The following report is submitted in accordance with CSH-F 52-6-1, Section V, paragraph 2 (d)(1):

Disclosure of Leads

(1) LIFEAT/LIFEZE, with related lines, provided overall coverage of the local Cuban and exile activities, and was considered extremely valuable in support of Station operations in the Cuban area. LIFEAT coverage of the Cuban Embassy enabled the appropriate Case Officer to check the veracity and activities of the penetrations of that installation; particularly LITAT-3, LITATS-3, and LITAINT-5. It was also invaluable in the detriefing of LIFEZE-1. LIFEAT material was the basis of an effective KUBARK inspired press release concerning敏感 activities of Cuban Embassy personnel.

LIFETAT leads were of special value in two "one-time" operations. The first of these was the ability of LIFEAT to provide advance information on a meeting arranged between Major ROJAS Villa (aka James Bradford STEWART) and a Cuban Embassy official. As a result of the lead time given by LIFEAT, the Station was able to provide surveillance of the meeting and arrange for the detention of ROJAS. Ultimately, ROJAS (a fugitive from ODEMY) was returned to COYENE jurisdiction through Station action. In a second case LIFEAT provided follow-up information which, when provided to COYENE, led to the apprehension of Thomas Howard GRIEKS (aka Carl JOHNSON). He, too, had attempted to arrange a meeting with Cuban officials in Mexico, and was under warrant for abduction in Chicago, Illinois.

LIFETAT information was heavily drawn upon in the case of Guillermo HERNANDEZ Vega, persible Castro agent, who was suspected of penetrating the AMIRAH program. Through LIFEAT the Station was able to monitor HERNANDEZ's attempts to contact various Mexican and Cuban officials and to arrange for his return to MADD before any such contacts were made.

(2) LIFEAT/LITEUS continued to provide daily information on the official Polish installation in Mexico City which is described by the interested Case Officer as "invaluable and unique." During this reporting period, major contribution has been in providing the leads which determined that a local cafe is a front for much Polish intelligence activity in the area. It has been further indicated that this cafe is the Headquarters for a suspected Polish operation directed against the OBACD installation (previously fully reported to MIGD, KFCTT, and WUQST/D in HWA-19514, HWA-15005, and IDVA-16222. The telephone of this cafe has been added to the LIFEAT coverage with resulting increase of Station knowledge of Polish Intelligence activity.

Also of major interest was the LIFEAT support of a Station audio operation against the Polish Embassy in Mexico City. In this, LIFEAT provided the information on delivery date of a KUBARK "bugged" piece of equipment ordered by the Polish Embassy. As a result of the lead time provided, the Station was able to promptly initiate base house procurement and other support for this operation.
