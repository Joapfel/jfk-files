The French Media

The first issue of the new edition of La Revolution was entitled Revolution, this is how it will be published in Paris by S.A.P.L. Nouvelles Editions Internationalizes, 1920, rue Galand - Paris 5°. The first issue of La Revolution in September was published at 40, rue Franche-1er - Paris 8°, printed by "La Cité", Lausanne, Switzerland, but bound by Artigny, France then in the spring, the Directors, Jacques H. Yenais, had run into financial difficulties, he found cheaper premises for his editorial office at 52, rue Galande - Paris 5° and with no aim to change his printers to Offret-Press. It turns out that La Revolution was in financial difficulties for several months. The trouble began, apparently, when the French authorities noted copies of issue No.7 of the French edition of March 1964 because of an article it contained on French overseas territories. Creditors became alarmed, fearing that its publication might be banned, and demanded settlement of their debts which were then estimated to exceed £10,000.
