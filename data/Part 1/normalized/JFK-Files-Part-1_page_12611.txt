TO Chief, Western Hemisphere Division

FROM Chief of Station, Caracas

SUBJECT AMCLEVE-15 MOC and Security Clearance

As the Desk is aware, the last word from Headquarters was that AMCLEVE-15 was to be treated as a "hostile" and that full clearance was being withheld pending his SGSWIRL. He has now been SGSWIRLed, and to the Station's knowledge, the test was favorable although an official Headquarters report has not been received. A PRQ Part I was submitted on Subject on 29 May 1968. Therefore, it would be appreciated if the Desk would contact the appropriate Headquarters officials and advise the Station of AMCLEVE-15's official status. We assume that he will be fully cleared and that a POA will be issued followed by an QA after the Station submits PRQ Part II.

Headquarters recently inquired as to Station thoughts on including AMCLEVE-15 in the CIFLUTE Project rather than making AMCLEVE a separate project. The Station is completely in accord with this idea although it will probably necessitate funds being shifted from CIGUSTY to CIFLUTE. However, a separate dispatch will be prepared on this subject.

The Station realizes that AMCLEVE-15's clearance has been a thorny problem to the Desk and hopes that we can now clear it up once and for all. Perry G. DRAPKEN

ATTACHMENT MOC, h/w
