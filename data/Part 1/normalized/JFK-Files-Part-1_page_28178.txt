16 APR 1979

Dear Frederick,

Please accept my appreciation for an assignment well done as a member of the FY 1979 GS-13 Personnel Evaluation Board.

Your full-time service with the Board represents a major contribution to the Directorate's program of personnel assessment and management. Your recommendations for promotion, career development, and other matters have been very helpful.

I believe you will find on return to your regular duties that this time spent with the Board has broadened and sharpened your own focus on the personnel aspects of Agency management. I think it will make you appreciate more than ever our needs for carefully and candidly written appraisals of employees.

Sincerely,

John N. McMahon Deputy Director for Operations
