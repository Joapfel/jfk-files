1. Brief Biography Subject is a 40-year-old Georgian emigre living in Gap, Hautes Alpes, France. Together with his parents, brother and sister he moved from the disintegrating Georgian Republic in 1921 to France where he has lived ever since, except for about three years during World War II when he worked voluntarily in Germany as a semi-skilled laborer. He joined the French Foreign Legion in 1940 and was demobilized in 1941, having served for the most part in North Africa. While in Germany he developed a skill at forging documents which he used to help French officers to escape to France. He was arrested, convicted and jailed for about a year and a half. After the war he returned to Paris. In 1946 and 1947 Subject committed two armed robberies. In 1949 he robbed a bank in Paris, subsequent to which he was caught, convicted and sentenced to ten years, which was later reduced to five by the terms of a general amnesty. Since his release in 1955 Subject has lived in Gap where his brother lives and works as a moderately successful architect. Subject was married in 1947, divorced in 1950 while in prison. He has a daughter, born before his arrest, and a son, born while he was in prison.

3. Education, Languages and Cultural Standing Subject probably has a better-than-average formal education for aPremailer who did not and never planned to go on to a university. He has a fair lay knowledge of history but is ill informed on details. Subject is something of a scientific dilutant, interested in the atom, gadgets, electrical and mechanical, and probably the game of scientific and pseudo-scientific things and ideas. His knowledge is broad but not deep; in some things it is about as deep as popular magnetos, but in others he knows considerably more.
