SUBJECT KUDESK STEBOHY JAGUAR JAGUAR Officer's Comment on Lrnbw1/1 and Alis

ACTION REQUIRED

REFERENCES 1. In the course of an informal discussion on 8 June with the JAGUAR officer most familiar with the history of STEBOHY/1 (and our relationship with him), the JAGUAR officer commented that he was frankly surprised at the volume of sexual activity of the African Communists and far-leftists. His first reference was to types such as Jeanne PYNER, but he broadened the comment to include Rosalynde AISILIE and others, and of course there were the recipients of these girls' affections as well - Africans (usually black) such as Kenyatta and Babu. 2. The JAGUAR officer then added that S/1's wife was also known to have shared her favors with other black men than S/1 - to such an extent, at any rate, that her promiscuity had come to JAGUAR'S notice. (I mentioned that this must be some new news, since we had not heard of it before, but he indicated that this was old news, and apparently not of the type to be considered "derogatory.") The JAGUAR officer went on to say that S/1 himself has put in a certain amount of time in the beds of other white girls, and perhaps this is how he knows of the promising propensities of some of them. 3. While S/1 has not particularly affected prudery in our talks, neither has he professed himself to be a great and universal lover.

George H. KATZENBACH
