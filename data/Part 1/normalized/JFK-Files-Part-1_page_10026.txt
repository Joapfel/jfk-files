1. IN DISCUSSION THIS CASE WITH ESLYRIC-1, COS STRESSED DESIRABILITY OF ATTACHING DETECTIVE OROZCO AS HAS BEEN IN CASE OF "GALLO GIRL". PUBLIC DENUNCIATIONS OF FAR/LEADER/K CURANS BY OROZCO WOULD UNDOUBTEDLY HAVE GREAT IMPACT, PARTICULARLY AT THIS TIME WHEN INTERNAL DISSENSION RAMPANT IN GUATEMALAN COMMUNIST MOVEMENT. ESLYRIC-1 IN AGREEMENT AND STATED THAT IF OROZCO TURNED OVER TO GUATEMALA, THEN ATTEMPT TO SECURE HIS DESTRUCTION WILL BE PRIMARY EFFORT. MUCH WOULD DEPEND ON WILLINGNESS OF OROZCO TO COOPERATE, AND STATION, OF COURSE, CAN NOT GUARANTEE THAT HE MIGHT NOT BE EXECUTED.

2. STATION AGREES THAT OROZCO HAS PROBABLY BEEN NEUTRALIZED AS A FAR ASSET, BUT BELIEVE THAT IF HE WERE TO BE RELEASED LIKELY HE WILL TRY TO AFFILIATE WITH "FED GUARD" EXTREMISTS LED BY "GABRIEL SALAZAR", JOE MARIA (FORMIZ), JONES, AND RICARDO GRAMIREZ DE LEON.
