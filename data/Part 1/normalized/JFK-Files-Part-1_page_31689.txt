SECRET SECURITY INFORMATION

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, International Organizations Division

SUBJECT: Reported Financial Support of Dr. Dimitrov by Bulgarians in Germany

REFERENCE: Memorandum (10-3-682), dated 2 March 1953

1. With regard to reference memorandum, this Division has long suspected that some such arrangement existed. Any money which Dr. Dimitrov managed to extract from the Guard Company members is obtained by him as a result of his own personal sway exercised over this group of Bulgarians.

2. It has also come to our attention that Dr. Dimitrov is receiving money from miscellaneous Bulgarians in Canada and the United States in the name of a trust fund which he has set up. You are no doubt aware of this development.

3. We are most interested in getting from you, your estimate of Dr. Dimitrov's income from all sources as well as a listing of those sources of income about which you know.

CHRISTIAN M. FREER ACTING CHIEF, SE
