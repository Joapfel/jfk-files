BY THE STATEMENT THAT PRECEDING OF THE PARTY HAS GOOD, HE SAID IF AFTER HE HAD WRITTEN ARTICLES, MADE REPORTS, AND DELIVERED DOCUMENTS ON HIS AMERICAN ARCHITECTURAL THOUGHTS, IS SIMPLY DISCOVERED THAT HE HAS A NATIONAL TARGET FOR EXPULSION. HE SAID "I WAS DOWNAWAKED." I HAVE NO QUARREL WITH THE PARTY. I WAS SURE I WAS BEING SUBJECTED TO CHEAT AND DO NOT BELIEVE BECAUSE MY ARCHITECTURAL STORIES DID NOT FIT INTO THE NATIONAL CONSTRUCTION PROGRAM. MY PARTY WAS A DEEP AS THAT OF A CHILD BEING PUNISHED BY HIS MOTHER. MAY BECAUSE I TRIED OUR PARTY AS MUCH AS I WOULD TRIED MY MOTHER. THE PARTY, REMEMBER, WAS VERY PATIENT WITH ME. AFTER I CAL EXPLAINED OUT MY CASE, I ADDED MY MESSAGES."

HE SAID: "I WAS A PRODIGAL SON, AFTER THIS EDUCATION, I WILL AFTER MAKE OUR PARTY -- NOT EVEN A SEEP AWAY FROM HERE."
