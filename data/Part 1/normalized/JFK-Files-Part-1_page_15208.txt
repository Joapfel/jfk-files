Mrs. Elbet isaves' brother's first wif (friends)

Mr & Mrs Dr. Louis Elbet a TALLARD (Hautes Alpes) France

26 Oct 59

" Tired from a long journey " - signed the Pelican.

and now I am in Cha-cha land. Rio is a magnificent town

principal industries are pleasure and love. If I were a

Brazilian I would say here we make love. Maybe this name

is less poetic than Rio de Janerio but it is more poetic in the way

would

sense. Louis will make much money if he were to come here in

treating venereal disease cases. I think there will be here for

several months and after that I think I will come and see you.

Now I am in San Paolo which is more magnificant that Rio.

In the movies you can see Bart Lancaster and Brigitte Bardot

talking in fluent Portugese.

I am now learning new language, Portugese,which seems to

be very much like Spanish. Exactly! Everything now becomes mixed

in my head. And written in English, German, Russian, French, Georgian

Spanien. If I go to China I will be lost-if I have to learn that

language/

I think often of you. How are you and how are the girls.

Blico, Irene and Cathy I would like to have a letter from each

one. It has been almost a year since I have seen them. At the

same time I am writing to my brother.

How is everyone? My best regards to them.
