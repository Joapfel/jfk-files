TRANSLATION

Miami 7 May 1967

Mr.

The only paper that I have in my possession is my daughter's birth certificate. My marriage certificate is in Cuba. I cannot send anyone to get it because the person who xxixxx goes to get it would be persecuted because the name of my husband is on it.

Attached is the child's certificate. I would appreciate k your letting me know if this is sufficient since trying to obtain mine would be difficult.

/s/ Teresa SUAREZ
