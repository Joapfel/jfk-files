TO INFO FROM SUBJECT ACTION REQUESTED Chief of Staff, Monterrey Chief, AM Division Chief of Station, Mexico City • Jose Arturo DE LA PENA 9U-500-1 2 February 1962 X MARLBORO REPORT NO. 1 NOTING REQUEST INDICATION CAN BE ADDED BY QUADRED (x) ORK ONLY

REFERENCES I. LIENVOY (B) reported that Jose Arturo DE LA PENA had been trying to reach Rogelio • RODRIGUEZ Lopez, Cuban Third Secretary, on 20 January. Since he was unable to reach RODRIGUEZ, he left a message to the effect that he was leaving Mexico City and on his return he would give the address to which they should send the information Bulletin of the Cuban Embassy. He said the pressure is a "little strong here, since yesterday (19 January) they were picking up all that left the embassy - newspapers and all that" and for that reason he did not go in. He said that he was taking several copies of No. 4 of the Information Bulletin to Cuahulla, but that he also wanted No. 5 which contained information about scholarships.

2. Station would appreciate any information Monterrey may have on DE LA PENA.

Willard C. CURTIS

1 February 1962
