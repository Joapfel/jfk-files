Ref: AGENCY C08776, 23 January 1907.

1. as requested in reference, Station Fort au Prince is pouching to headquarters all of the LI/AL/LAF cards currently on file at the station. Please return to station copies of those cards containing sufficient information to be of value to station.

2. Station is in agreement with headquarters that a hard copy of the tapes should be sent directly from Station Mexico City to headquarters in order that headquarters may expedite the processing of the material. However, station suggests that in order to reduce the overall processing time and eliminate what could be duplication of effort, only headquarters receive a copy of the tapes. Each headquarters, when processing the original tapes, could release those Creole portions and send a composite tape to the station for translation.

3. Station currently has a backlog of the LI/AL/LAF tapes. Please advise whether these tapes also should be sent to headquarters for processing.

4. headquarters comments and suggestions are welcome.
