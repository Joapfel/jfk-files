RECIPIENT KEYWAY Payoh Jacoque VEROES & Richard T. CIBSON Expelled from Switzerland

ACTION REQUIRED REFERENCE: OELW - 20852 (9 December 1963)

1. LCBEFORE reported on 17 January 1964 that Subjects, both of whom are mentioned in reference dispatch concerning Revolution Africaine (CIBSON is a US citizen) and were working from that publication's Lausanne office, have left Switzerland at the request of the Swiss Government. According to LCBEFORE, VEROES departed Switzerland on 25 November 1963 for Latin America (possibly Brazil), and CIBSON departed on 15 December 1963.

2. LCBEFORE believes that in part it can take credit for those expulsions in view of the fact that LCBEFORE-inspired publicity about Revolution Africaine has appeared in the Swiss press and that this publicity helped to draw the matter to the Swiss authorities' attention, thus prompting them to excommunicate these two propagandists.

3. LCBEFORE also noted on 17 January 1964 that the November 1963 issue of Revolution Africaine lists Lausanne as a bureau (and Paris as the Main Office) whereas the October 1963 issue listed Lausanne as the Main Office and Paris as one of the bureaus.
