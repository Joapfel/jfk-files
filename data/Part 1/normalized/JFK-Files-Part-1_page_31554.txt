MEMORANDUM FOR: DIRECTOR OF CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE DEPUTY DIRECTOR (PLANS)

SUBJECT: Monseigneur John C. Kirk

1. You asked me by telephone for a report on subject, who is domestic prolate on leave from the Savannah-Atlanta diocese in Georgia.

2. Monseigneur Kirk was formerly secretary to Archbishop Gerald P. O'Hara, Bishop of the Savannah-Atlanta diocese, and also his secretary while Bishop O'Hara was Apostolic Nuncio to Romania from 1946 until O'Hara's official expulsion in July of 1950. (MSB has been reliably informed that Kirk remained behind in Romania disguised as a Swiss tourist. He visited many of the imprisoned hierarchy but eventually escaped. This report is without objective verification.)

3. Because no single member of the Romanian hierarchy escaped, Kirk was appointed as president of the Romanian Catholic Mission for Europe. The headquarters of the Mission is now Passegginta del Gianicolo 5, Rome. Monseigneur Kirk resides at Via Panama 110, Rome.

4. While in the United States in 1952 as personal delegate of Cardinal Tisserant to the convention of the association of Romanian Catholics in America, Kirk visited the office of MSBS. Members of the staff met with him on five occasions and made the following estimate:

As a prelate on leave in a southern diocese, Kirk can be classified as a religious liberal interested in the betterment of various classes and, particularly the Negro. In Romanian politics, he vigorously resisted the influence of the iron guard. Evidence of this is that in 1952 Kirk moved the headquarters of the Mission from Madrid, where it was located at Calle de Sarrano 30, because of continuing attempts of the iron guardists to use him for political purposes.
