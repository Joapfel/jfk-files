UN EVALUATION OF AERIAL TRANSPORTATION OF CONQUEST AIRCRAFT

COUNCIL A Royal Roocosta Air Force (Gulf) Reserve Officer who obtains information from reserve aircrews and other officials of air services. This provides mercurial or mercantaries recruited and sent to the Conquest area to provide needed medical care. He is probably a member of the Council. Jack Myles (JCL), head of Air Transportation.

AS OF 2 February, a total of eight mercenary flights had been completed to the Conquest since the first of this year, with personnel drawn largely from contracting who have been joined by small groups of roocastens from Salvador. Majorlistairlicks, former deputy commander of No. 5 Commando, is now acting as mercenary recruitment officer in Salvador. Each of the eight flights carried one by Air Transport Africa transported 70 mercantaries. One additional flight of approximately the same number is still to be undertaken.
