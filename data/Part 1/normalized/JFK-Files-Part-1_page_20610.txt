6 January 1965

MEMORANDUM TO: Benefits & Services Division SUBJECT: Cause of Illness - E. Howard Hunt

1. Having been requested to give an opinion regarding the origins of the duodenal ulcer for which I was hospitalized October 12-15, 1964, I believe it to have resulted from three years of work frustration and professional disappointments occasioned by (a) Agency failure to assign me to an appropriate post abroad following my participation in Project JOAVE, and (b) the passive, non-challenging nature of the domestic work I was given.

2. I should point out that before joining Project JOAVE in May, 1960, I requested in writing, and was granted by responsible senior officials, commitment to return me to an appropriate assignment abroad upon the termination of JOAVE. Despite continuing verbal assurances in the ensuing three years I was not sent abroad. Instead, I was retained in domestic duties which I felt were inconsistent with the heavier responsibilities discharged by so during a long and active operational career abroad.

3. The T/O position I have been occupying - Chief of Cover Action for DO Division - is the equivalent of my 1953-54 assignment as Chief of Cover Action for SS Division. Organisationally I am where I was 11-12 years ago, but with a smaller staff and many fewer responsibilities.

4. To see this represented a professional dead-end, without hope of promotion or foreign assignment, a situation which preoccupied my mind and in due course found physical reflection in duodenal hemorrhage.

5. For the record, the foregoing information is not volunteered, but provided in response to an official request.

EDWARD HUNT

JOAVE 1965
