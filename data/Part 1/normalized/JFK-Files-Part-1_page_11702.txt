SECRET SECURITY INFORMATION

PLANS Acting Chief, BMD FILLMEATR-1

26 November 1951

1. The FULMINATE Project, in which FULMINATE was used on several occasions, did not develop and was not formalized. FULMINATER-1 is therefore not active currently, but is being held in reserve status for future use in other projects.

2. In the future, there will be no charges made against FULMINATER or FULMINATER-1.

W. M. SHELER, JR.
