10 May 1951

Mr. James Walter McCord, Jr. 222 Pacific Avenue Alameda, California

Dear Mr. McCords

Thank you for your recent application for employment in our organization.

Enclosed you will find the forms which we require all prospective applicants to fill out in order that we may become more thoroughly acquainted with their back- ground and experience. If you will be good enough to fill them out and return them with three passport size photographs of yourself, we will be in a better position to review your qualifications and will then advise you further. You may retain the fourth copy of the Personal History Statement for your own records.

We sincerely appreciate your interest in our organization and look forward to hearing from you in the near future.

JOSEPH B. RAGAN Chief, Personnel Procurement
