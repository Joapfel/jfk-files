Cuba

Evaluation of Report of Revolutionary Preparations in Central America Against Cuban Government

21 January 1953.

Cuba, Habana.

16 Feb. 1953

1

-4-5692

1. No additional information concerning alleged plans of the Caribbean Legion to attack the BATISTA government have been received in Habana, Cuba. Reports received there in the past concerning the Caribbean Legion's attempts to enlist BATISTA's support for legion revolutionary activities have been given a low evaluation. To date, the Habana Mission has received no information which would warrant changing such an evaluation.

2. Indications in Cuba are that the BATISTA regime is too preoccupied with a possible revolt attempt originating within the country to permit planning for any offensive action against any other country in the Caribbean area. Recent friendly overtures by BATISTA, in the form of decorations bestowed on Doctor TRWILLO of the Dominican Republic and PEEZ Jimenez of Venezuela, also serve to indicate that the present Cuban government is not contemplating any action against those countries.

3. As to a possible attack on the BATISTA regime from outside Cuba, the Cuban armed forces and security agencies have long been alerted for a possible air attack on key installations in Habana as well as multiple attacks from the sea. Mexico, Costa Rica, and Guatemala have been mentioned as possible take-off points for planes to be used in the air attack.

4. There has been no information received which would confirm the allegations concerning the concentration of arms in the hands of Cuban youth leaders. Jose JOLESIAS, Max LESNICK, and Pedro QUIMI are well-known leaders of the Jovenal Ortodon (Orthodox Youth) and, as such have participated in export opposition activities against the BATISTA government, demanding the restoration of the Constitution of 1940 and the holding of general elections.
