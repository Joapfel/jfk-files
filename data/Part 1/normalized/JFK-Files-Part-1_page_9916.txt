DISPATCH

TO Chief of Station, Mexico City

FROM Chief, WH Division

PROJECT OPBURNIN/LERING-3

Headquarters notes that on 9 August 1967 LIRING-3 was in contact via LIFEAT with one Electa LMU. Given LIRING-3's artist background and the subject of conversation - the visit of Rodolfo Heisler, a Swiss artist, to Cuba - it is suggested that Electa LMU might be Electa ARENAL (201-173795), the mother-in-law of David ALFALO Siquieros the well-known Mexican Communist artist.

Andrew F. MERTON

Distribution: 2 - COS, Mexico City
