UNCLASSIFIED CITATION

Mr. William V. Broe is hereby awarded the Distinguished Intelligence Medal in recognition of his outstanding service to the Central Intelligence Agency for more than 25 years. Serving in senior positions both in Headquarters and abroad, Mr. Broe has earned the respect of his colleagues for his skill, judgment, and strong leadership in responding to numerous crises. In addition, he has been a valuable advisor and consultant to top policy makers of the United States Government as well as to several foreign leaders. Culminating his career as Inspector General, he has demonstrated a wise and enlightened approach to the human aspects of Agency affairs. Mr. Broe has made singular and lasting contributions to the national security effort of our country, reflecting the highest credit on him and the Federal service.
