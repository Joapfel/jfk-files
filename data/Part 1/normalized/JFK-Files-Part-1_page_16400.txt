Dominican Republic

Target Analysis Summary Report

AMEG/1 Operation

SUBJECT: Jaime DURAN Hernandez aka Jaime DURAN Hernandez aka "Jimmy"

I. Significance of Target

Connection with Cuban Intelligence Service

Subject is not a Cuban Intelligence Service agent, but did receive guerrilla training in Cuba.

Communist Party Affiliation

DURAN is Secretary-General of the pro-Castro Agrupación Política de Catorce Junio (APCJ - 11th of June Movement) in the Dominican Republic, and is a member of that faction of the APCJ favoring the training of its members to direct the future of a socialist government.

Subject is a close friend of Francisco Javier MELLA Fesa, an identified Cuban Intelligence Service Agent in the Dominican Republic. Subject was also used as an "unfitting" courier by a still unidentified Cuban IS agent "TIBURON" in the Dominican Republic. Subject unwittingly carried to Cuba for "TIBURON" in September 1965, a catch-book containing microfilms.

Utility - Penetration of the 11 June & plans & intentions of the Cubans vs D.R.

II. Current Location

Believed to be in Cuba. Has been there since September 1965. Last known Dominican Republic address: Capsilat #20, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic.

III. Vulnerabilities (for recruitment or defection)

None known. (Also see VII - Evaluation)

IV. Family and relatives

Wife - Gloria AGUIRRE de Duran, Dominican citizen (no information) No other relatives known.

No known relatives in the U.S.A.
