National Press Service Washington

JEB

16 June

Present at conference in office of Asst. Secretary of State McKnIGHT at conference today:

Measles: McKnigley Grundahl Kalorex Buttles Huddle Neilson Glaser

I have a complete, practically verbatim record of the meeting, but an setting down an extraot of what took place.

Mr. McHnigle stated that the meeting had been called to discuss ways and means of taking advantage of the Cultural Congress in Berlioz by widespread dissemination of the product of the Congress, speeches, declarations, etc. Why would it not be a good idea to have Buttles communicate with LABOUR and have latter send the material to Paris for publication in France, as well as here,

Mr. Neilson (ECA) said that speaking for ECA, they have a liberal view as to spending ECA funds and are anxious to cooperate with State, subject to limitations which are relevant to the present case, which are (a) expenditure to be approved on a project-basis (b) a finger to be kept on the progress of expenditure (c) within certain totals (a) keep away from the flamboyant and spectacular. (For instance, no large jobs of ECA money to send US symphony orchestras touring through Europe.) Said Mr. Neillson, the Cultural Congress would fall within the zone of probable acceptability and should be a cooperative enterprise with State, to be cleared with ECA financial and legal advisers, but do not anticipate difficulty because the Cultural Congress is clearly related to the approved ECA policy of countering the Soviet peace offensive.

Mr. Buttles suggested that the best thing to do with product of Congress was to get local people to publish it; we furnish translations, paper, ink, etc.
