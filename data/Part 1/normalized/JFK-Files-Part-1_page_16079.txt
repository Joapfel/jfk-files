I. Plan II - Organization and Training

Selection of Personnel

The JSOFC will, during the 4th and 5th week select a total of 80 highly qualified and motivated personnel. Students accepted into the sniper program will be selected from volunteers. Those who are selected must have 20/20 vision, have qualified on an expert rifleman and be well motivated soldiers. Experience in competitive marksmanship is preferred but is not mandatory.
