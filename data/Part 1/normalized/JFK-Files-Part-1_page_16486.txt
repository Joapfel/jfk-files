2. S-1 not seen SARK-1 (P-1) since c. 1964, thus unable provide updated assessment. S-1 does not know F-1's wife.

3. S-1 considers highly improbable F-1 would accept private meeting via phone. F-1 could be suspicious of S-1's motives and might round up own welcoming committee for confrontation.

4. S-1 confidant a direct street meeting -one on one- would offer best opportunity to communicate Agency offer to F-1.

5. S-1's second choice would be home approach, were it known F-1 was alone or only with wife.

6. S-1 is disciplined, discreet, devoted,ETY PROFESSIONAL. Can adapt to any Station Name/ States will go anywhere, anytime to cooperate. Prepared to take up to 5 days sick leave without pay to participate approach to F-1. Could leave Miami on Saturday, returning latest Sunday following week. S-1 is Amcit and has US passport. S-1 needs c. 1 weeks prior notice to prepare for CARACAS PET.

7. S-1 reviewed names of Cubans on latest Diplomatic List. Only positive was: Norberto HERNANDEZ Curbelo (the Ambassador) who former OGI colleague of S-1.

Henry S. Pachankis.
