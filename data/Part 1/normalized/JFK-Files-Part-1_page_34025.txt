SUBJECT TYPIC/MHSPAWN/AMSCROLL Study of Cuban/USSR Trade Relations

REFERENCES: A. SANTIAGO-3885 B. DIRECTOR-86882 C. JMWAVE-6430

1. JMWAVE forwards herewith a study entitled Economic Relations Between Cuba and the Soviet Union: How Much Dependency? Both Santiago and Headquarters are being provided with an original and one copy of the study in English and an original and one copy in Spanish. Two sets of the one table and the six charts that support the study are also being forwarded each addressee. This is an AMSCROLL prepared study; the author is AMFRAME-1 in collaboration with AMSHAKE-1. It was prepared in response to references A and B.

2. We have limited this study to an attempt to prove statistically the very rapid rise of Cuban economic dependency upon the Soviet Union. We have intentionally avoided editorializing as the facts speak for themselves; thus we have drawn no political conclusions, nor have we attempted to establish that this economic dependency carries with it an implicit policy control on the part of the Soviets (partly because policy control does not necessarily follow and, in any event, the reader is free to draw his own conclusions).
