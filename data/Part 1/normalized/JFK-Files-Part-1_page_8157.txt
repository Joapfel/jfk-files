Most of Latin America considers itself decades behind in the socio-economic de- velopment of the West, and the urge to move quickly and drastically is every- where evident.

Now a word about Mr. Dillen's expectations in the realm of private investments as part of the Alliance para el Progreso. According to reports in some of the business journals, prospective investors from Europe, Japan and the United States have certain misgivings about the program.

First of all, these businessmen feel that the Alliance offers only minimal encouragement for private investment in Latin America. Indeed, some are quick to point out that in his initial speech at Punta del Este, Secretary Dillen did not once mention private enterprise -- nor in any of his subsequent comments before the committee on labor.
