Memorandum

TO : Chief, Contact Division.

Attn : Support (Shirley Stetson)

FROM : Chief, New York Office

DATE: 26 June 1954

SECRET SENSITIVE

11-0930-1230 hours 14 June, Manfred K. Schroeder of Bell Labs received a strong defensive briefing in connection with his June-July trip to the USSR and Bulgaria (see our 7 May Coordination Outline for subject). During Schroeder's briefing, Nagle elicited from him that prior to a trip to the USSR in 1959 he had been contacted by an individual who represented himself as a West German Intelligence Officer. More important, we also learned that, at about the same time, RIS cultivation of Schroeder apparently began.

Copy and Forward.
