Box 130, Flesherton, Ontario; Financial Times of Montreal; Financial Post, Toronto; Canadian Chamber of Commerce, Montreal; Canadian Press, a news agency located in Toronto; and the Ministry of Trade and Commerce.

2. As part of the follow-up play Station of course would appreciate any reaction from the Canadian press or other sources. We will particularly appreciate being informed if it comes to the attention of Station Ottawa that the health authorities inspect, condemn, or otherwise take any action on these shipments.
