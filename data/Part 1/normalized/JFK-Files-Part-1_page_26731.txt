Reel 72-KICO-25

1 May 1963

After 2135, 1 May

23.7 Out to 25-80-32, text as WI Flmaks Cuban) asks WO, who answers for Sr. Cordero. Not there, then says one moment. But hangs up. WI holds phone.

24.0 Out to 25-80-32. WI asks WO, who answers, for Sr. Cordero. Not there. But he can be located at 39-07-51.

24.7 Out to 39-07-51. YKG asks WO, who answers, for Dr. Cordere, Emilio Cordero.

EC takes the phone. Did EC hear Fidel? Yes, YKG says they KENANUM are in the center of history. How did he like it? Wonderful. The first two hours were dedicated to Dominican situation. He made a correct analysis of Dominican situation, who Bosch is, etc. And at the end, he gave out his philosophy.

YKG talked to Radio Habana twice. EC was just heading over Radio Habana what Juan B. Mejia stated. And Habana had called Cordero, but there is no line now to Habana. In case EC talks to his wife or Marcio (Mejia Ricart) talks to Bosch, YKG would like to be there. WO thinks Marcio will talk to Bosch. Can YKG talk to him? Yes. And perhaps EC can inform YKG immediately about whatever EC's wife informs him - at whatever time it is. In case anyone calls EC, be it his wife, Juan B., or another member of the Party, he will let YKG know. In case he talks to Juan B., give him YKG's phone so they can phone YKG. Or give YKG's number to any other member (of Party) who calls because YKG is turning over information directly (to Habana). They are very interested.

Marcio Mejia Ricart takes the phone. MIT has not heard all Fidel's speech. Will AM talk to Bosch? Yes, at 2230, in an hour. Will call from where he is. Could YKG go there so VEG could be there - so MIT can ask his a couple of questions. Fine. Where is MIT?

He is at Zempoa la 533, between Pilsres and Cuicuilco. Explains where this is located. This is a house, not an apartment. YKG will go by there.

Reel off at 27.2, 2230, 1 May 1963
