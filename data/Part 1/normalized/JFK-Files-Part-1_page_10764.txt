TELEGRAM TO SECSTATE WASHD C IMMEDIATE LINDIS

SUBJECT: ALLEGED ARMS SMUGGLING TO GUATEMALA

FROM AMBASSADOR TO DEPT. ASST. SEC. ROBERT HURWITCH.

"1. AFTERNOON FEBRUARY 21 MEDEFENSE VASSAUX ASKED DOM AND Q&A STATION CHIEF TO HIS HOUSE AND SAID GOG URGENTLY NEED HELP OF U.S. IN STOPPING IMMINENT ILLEGAL, SHIPMENT OF ARMS FROM MIAMI TO GUATEMALA. ARMS DEALER ALLEGEDLY CUBAN EXILE RESIDENT MIAMI, CARLOS MONTIEL. VASSAUX HAD NO DETAILS ON ADDRESS. ARMS IN QUESTIONS ARE: 25 INGRAHAM .45 CALIBER SUB MACHINEGUNS EQUIPPED WITH SILENCERS; 125 MAGAZINES FOR THESE WEAPONS; 2 MAC COUNTER-SNIPER RIFLES, 7.62 CALIBER, WITH SILENCERS AND TELESCOPES; 2 MAC-12/22 SILENCED RIFLES WITH STRAIGHT STOCKS; AND-2 MAC-10/22 SILENCED RIFLES WITH FOLDING STOCK AND TELESCOPES. VASSAUX SAID HE HAD NO FURTHER INFORMATION BUT THAT GOG WOULD APPRECIATE ANY HELP WE COULD GIVE IN STOPPING THIS UNAUTHORIZED SHIPMENT."
