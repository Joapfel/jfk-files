2 June 1959

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, SR/DOB FROM : Training Staff, SR/DOB Billy Jack Johnson SUBJECT : ASENTINEL Training 25 - 30 May 1959 Map and Compass

1. The originator enjoyed the opportunity to instruct agents ASENTINEL 1 and 2 in Map and Compass.

2. Heavy stress was placed on Maps. Compass work had to be brought into the subject but in a very light degree.

3. The agents showed a very keen interest in the subjects and felt that they had really improved their working knowledge in Maps and Compass.

4. In Maps, the following subjects were covered.

What are maps and why they are needed.

Various types of maps.

Terrain and how indicated on maps.

Map orientation, Compass inspection, Graphic

True North, Mag North and Grid North.

Map declination

Map direction

Sand Table contours

Cardboard profiles

Map Symbols

Ridge lines and Valleys

Terrain Movement
