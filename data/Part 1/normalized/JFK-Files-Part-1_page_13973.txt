Subject: TYPE: DONOPS AMROSE - Debriefing of AMCLOCK/1 on Cuban UN Mission and Delegation Personalities ACTION REQUIRED: REFERENCES Action Required: Extract for appropriate 201 files Refs: a. UNDA-1675, 14 October 1969 b. UNDA-1676, 14 October 1969

1. Attached herewith for and COS/WH/MIAMI are two copies each of three memoranda concerning results of an 11 October 1969 debriefing of AMCLOCK/1 by Howard G. FIGALAN on CMUN and Delegation personalities. The meeting with AMCLOCK/1 was necessarily brief, and thus the results are not as extensive as those forwarded by the two referenced dispatches (from a 6 October meeting with AMCLOCK/1).

2. A copy of each of the memoranda has been passed by FIGALAN to the local LNERGO Cuban Squad, per our existing informal arrangement. "ELGIN" is the code name for AMCLOCK/1 as used between the Base and LNERGO here.

3. Items should be extracted for the appropriate 201 files.

Howard G. FIGALAN
