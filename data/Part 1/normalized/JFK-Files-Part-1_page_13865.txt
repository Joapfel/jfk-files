1. FOLLOWING REPORTED BY ERYTHROID/1 ON 13 AND-14 FEB:

IN LATE JAN NIC SECURITY SENT TWO AGENTS, TO COSTA RICA TO PENETRATE GROUP LED BY MARCIAL AGUILUZ WHICH PLOTTING TO KILL GEN. ANASTASIO SOMOZA. TWO AGENTS ARE IDEN A AND IDEN B. THEY WERE SUCCESSFUL AND IDEN A REPORTED ON 11 FEB THAT AGUILUZ HAD INSTRUCTED IDEN B GO TO CUBA FOR SPECIAL TRAINING. IDEN B TO GO MEXICO CITY ON 12 FEB, TO REGISTER AT HOTEL CONGRESO, CALLE ALLENDE, AND -- ON FOLLOWING DAY HE TO GO CALLE FRANCISCO MARQUEZ NUMBER-160 (POR TACUSA) WHERE HE TO ASK FOR SENOR VEGA OR SENOR ROGELIO. WHEN ASKED BY VEGA OR ROGELIO "DE PARTE DE QUIEN", HE TO ANSWER "DE PARTE DE SENOR CANEIRAS" WHO HAD SENT HIM TO ARRANGE HIS PAPERS. AGUILUZ TOLD IDEN B, THAT IF HE UNABLE LOCATE EITHER VEGA OR ROGELIO, HE TO CALL TELEPHONE NUMBER-14-13-26 REPEAT-14-13-26.

IDEN B ALSO TOLD THAT IF HE NEEDED SOMETHING (PRESUMABLY WHILE IN ALSO RCVD 1110 (CEBA) HE TO WRITE TO APARTADO 110, SAN JOSE, COSTA RICA. HE WOULD, HOWEVER, TO WRITE NOTHING OF SIGNIFICANCE. AGUILUZ TOLD US WE WOULD BE GIVEN A COMBO SYSTEM IN MEXICO AT THE CUBANEmbassy, SO HE COULD LET IDEN A KNOW WHEN HE PLANNING TO RETURN.

AGUILUZ EXPLAINED THAT IDEN B WOULD RETURN FROM CUBA CLANDESTINELY BY SUBMARINE.

2. THIS OPERATION BEING RUN BY ERYTHROID/1 WHO NOT REPEAT NOT AWARE STATION BEING INFORMED BY ERYTHROID/3. STATION OBTAINING ADDITIONAL DETAILS WHICH WILL BE REPORTED SOONEST.

3. FOR NEXI: E/3 REQUEST URGENT IDENTIFICATION REGISTRY OF MEXICO CITY TELEPHONE NUMBER-14-13-26. MUST HAVE REPLYP MORNING-15 FEB IF S/3 TO BE ABLE ADVISE HIS CONTACTS IN MEXICO CITY. IDEN B TO MAKE CONTACT VEGA OR ROGELIO -- OR BY PHONE, BY 17 FEB AT LAEST.
