23 APR 69 IN 47116

1. AS INDICATED IN REFERENCE AND DISCUSSED BY THE COS DURING HIS RECENT VISIT TO HEADQUARTERS, STATION PLANNED ASK JEREMY K. BENADUM, CAREER AGENT AND OUTSIDE CASE OFFICER FOR LITEMPO-PROJECT, TO RETIRE FROM GOVERNMENT SERVICE EFFECTIVE 30 JUNE-69. THIS WAS DONE IN WRITING ON 7 APRIL-69.

2. SENADUM HAS WRITTEN MEMORANDUM DATED-24 APRIL-69 IN WHICH HE INDICATES THAT HE DOES NOT DESIRE TO RETIRE AND THAT ANY ACTION ALONG THESE LINES SHOULD BE CONSIDERED "INVOLUNTARY" ON HIS PART. JXB ALSO INDICATED THAT WITH ACCUSED ANNUAL LEAVE HE WILL LACK ABOUT MONTH IN COMPLETING FULL-50 YEARS SERVICE AS OF 30 JUNE AND ASKED THAT HE BE KEPT ON BOARD UNTIL FULL-30 YEARS ARE COMPLETED.

3. JXB REQUESTS THAT HE BE ALLOWED TO TAKE HOME LEAVE AT GOVERNMENT EXPENSE. HE INDICATED HIS UNDERSTANDING WITH REGULATION ABOUT NO HOME LEAVE WITHIN TWO YEARS OF VOLUNTARY RETIREMENT BUT SAID THAT HE CANNOT AGREE WITH THIS WHEN RETIREMENT
