No. U.11-10

G December 19th

TO: Anton S. Miller --- SUBJECT: Emergency Transportation Orders.

1. You are authorized to ship personnel and household effects from Shanghai, China on or about 7 December 1940 to Houston for shipment, temporarily.

2. Stock of effects for duration of emergency and not to exceed 30 days. Longer is authorized.

3. This authorization is in accordance with Cable INRT-5240 (Oct 71009) dated 27 November 1940 which authorizes transportation and storage of effects due to emergency conditions in China.

DEPUTY I. GALLAGH Assistant Director Special Operations
