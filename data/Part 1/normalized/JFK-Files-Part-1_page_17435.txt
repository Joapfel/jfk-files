26 March 1963

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Task Force Washington

ATTENTION : John Caswell

SUBJECT : Feasibility of Effecting Release of Manuel URQUETIA, Ex-President of Cuba, Political State in Mexican Embassy, Havana

1. The Mexico City station has the capability of approaching LITERATOR (Iden A) and requesting him to attempt to arrange for a safe conduct pass for Subject. The approach to LITERATOR can be made through three different channels: LITREP-0/2, (Iden B), LIREGANT (Iden C), and LIRECO (Iden D). Also, a direct approach by the COS to LITERATOR could probably be arranged if no desire by Headquarters for operational security reasons.

2. No assessment on the success of an approach of this type can be made because of the Mexican interest and position re Cuba,

3. If a decision is made to attempt to obtain a safe conduct pass for Subject, it is recommended that the COS Mexico City be informed in detail of Headquarters minima and maximum goals and that he be requested to recommend the best channel to LITERATOR.

NDT F. HOLMAN Chief, WR/3
