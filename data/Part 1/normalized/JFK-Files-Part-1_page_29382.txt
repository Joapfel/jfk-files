TECHNICAL DIVISION -- (TECHNICAL SCHOOL)

10-MINUTE COURSE (H-101)

CARRYING EVALUATION

Name : Grayson Lynch Office : SOD Course Dates: 19 - 22 May 1970

COURSE DESCRIPTION

This course touches the student to become more aware of the value in proper initial observation, -- the method of monthly recording the observations -- and finally -- the mechanical manipulation of the identikit to provide a permanent record.

EVALUATION

LOW CLASS STANDARD AVERAGE LOW AVERAGE HIGH EXCELLENT

Student understands the principles of identification.

Student understands the manipulation of the identikit.

Ability to construct composites from photographs.

Ability to reconstruct composite from identification code.
