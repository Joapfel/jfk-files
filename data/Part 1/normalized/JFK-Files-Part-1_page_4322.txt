TO: WILLIAMSON SUBJ: COMMENTS ON TRIDENT FROM: CHACONAS DATE: 16 MAI-66

1. I DO NOT REGARD TRIDENT AS A PARTICULARLY USEFUL VEHICLE FOR PROP ACTIVITY IN CENTRAL AMERICA. THE TRIDENT IS CLOSELY IDENTIFIED WITH ARTIME AND AS SUCH IS LESS FLEXIBLE A CONVINCING PROP OUTLET THAN WOULD BE THE CASE OTHERWISE. I WOULD NOT THEREFORE FAVOR FINANCIAL SUPPORT (KUBARK) OF THE TRIDENT, ESPECIALLY SINCE KUBARK IS NOT IN POSITION CLOSELY TO MONITOR THE CONTENTS PRIOR TO PUBLICATION. ALSO, THE CIRCULATION IS EXTREMELY LIMITED TO WARRANT PLACING A CASE OFFICER IN CHARGE FOR THIS ACTIVITY, I.E. GUIDING THE PROP LINE OF TRIDENT.

2. WE HAVE NOT USED TRIDENT IN THE PAST (AS FAR AS I ANkuE) FOR PLANTING MATERIAL AND I DONT FOR THE MOMENT VISUALIZE USING TRIDENT FOR OUTLET IN THE NEAR FUTURE. HOWEVER, THE "MIG-21 PROJECT", CONCEIVABLE MIGHT FIND TRIDENT USEFUL - THAT IS, IF IT IS DESIRABLE TO ADD A QUASI-OFFICIAL AURA TO THE PROPOSAL.

CHACONAS
