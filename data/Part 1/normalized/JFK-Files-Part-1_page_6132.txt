MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Information and Privacy Staff SUBJECT: Documents Relating to the Venceremos Brigade

1. This Directorate is unable to locate or to acknowledge origin of the following reports contained in the files of the Office of Security:

The Venceremos Brigade - a Report - 31 May 1970.

List of names of People Who Traveled to Cuba to Cut Sugar Cane - dated 18 May 1963. We therefore decline to rule on the releaseability of these two documents on FOIA and PA requests, and ask that these documents not be referred to us for action.

2. From the portions forwarded in connection with specific requests, there would appear to be no reason for denying the information. We defer, however, to OS for a final judgement.

Frederick C. Randall - (signed) Frederick C. Randall DDO/PIC
