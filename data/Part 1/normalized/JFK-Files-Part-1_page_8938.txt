SUBJECT Tichborn Report on Caracas Travel

ACTION REQUIRED · REFERENCE REFERENCE: HMMW-V11415, 22 March 1963 5-134-5

1. Station Mexico regrets that, to date, it has been unable to obtain the Reference report from Tichborn. As Headquarters is aware, Tichborn, while brilliant and articulate, is not always dependable, especially when it comes to routine tasks. It is true, however, that Tichborn has been extremely busy traveling for KUBARK, and is at the moment on another of his frequent trips.

2. In any event, Tichborn has promised to provide the report as soon as he returns from his present trip. It will be forwarded when received from him.
