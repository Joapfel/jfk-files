3. (Field Comment: Until recently, the indemnifications and pensions problem had not emerged as a serious factor in the negotiations for a new trade agreement. There has been a gradual deterioration of Spanish-Cuban commercial relations since the conclusion in May of preparatory negotiations for a new agreement, but this deterioration ostensibly was the result of a Spanish Government decision to reduce Cuban sugar imports over the next three years. The Cuban Government apparently had not considered such decision in its analysis of Cuban-Spanish commercial relations, and it was quite upset when the decision was revealed during the preparatory negotiations. Some of the problems causing the strain in Spanish-Cuban relations were previously reported in TDCSDB-315/2470-69, TDCSDB-315/02182-69, TDCSDB-315/03509-69, CS-311/08659-69 and CSDB-312/02600-69.)
