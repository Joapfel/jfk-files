Date: 12-01-95, 30 December 1997

ATLAS BROWN, Name, for information only.

1. The station's request for the assignment of either a PS or TIT clerk-typist experienced in LOMP reporting has been discussed with the E.I. division, and we are and all divisions recognize the station's need for such an individual; however, it is at this time impossible to assign 1/8 an additional slot for the PS assignment of an employee and it is not believed feasible to assign individuals on an extended TIT basis.

2. Although the additional final training program proposed by the station would apparently initially be an excellent way to live the referred procedure, Headquarters believes that the long-term effect would not achieve the desired results. It is therefore being considered as a lack of a sufficient number of operators. Should we have a large number of operators available, we would then be able to put someone in the TIT position, thereby reducing the total slots normally available to that office - a troublesome problem under the present personnel ceiling.

3. The E.I. division was granted that they would be allowed to provide LOMP training to the most competent local employee scheduled for its assignment to the station. This, of course, will not increase the station's load; it will only add one employee from one TIT job to another. If you have any questions along these lines, please advise Headquarters.
