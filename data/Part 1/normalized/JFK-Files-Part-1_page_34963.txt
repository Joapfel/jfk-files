Miami, Fla., May 15, 1967

Dear Sir:

Your letter of May 2 was received by me, but as I do not have on hand, my son JULIO ANTONIO YEEA SUAREZ'S birth certificate I had to look for and AFFIDavit.

Enclosed photostatic copy of my Marriage Certificate, this is one of the few documents I could to receive from Cuba. I have asked many times for Julio's birth certificate but up to the present I have been unable for getting it. Enclosed is the affidavit signed by my close friend Dr. Martha A. Garcia Ochoa, attorney at Law, Member of the Cuban Bar Association, journalist, former Congresswoman and very honorable and very well known person. I have written again to one friend in Cuba, asking for Julio's birth certificate, if I would receive it, I will send it to you immediately.

Thanking you for your interest on my case, I remain,

590 S.W. 10 St. Miami, Fla. 33129.
