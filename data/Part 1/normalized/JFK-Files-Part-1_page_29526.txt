MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Policy Concerning Guests at Award Ceremonies

1. The Office of the Director has determined that when inviting guests to award ceremonies, the Agency will be as liberal as possible within the bounds of available space and security considerations since one of the purposes of an award ceremony is to let the family, friends, and associates of the recipient know that he has been honored.

2. In implementing this policy the points listed below will be guiding criteria:

Any Agency employee who is a friend of a recipient may come to the ceremony, up to the limit of space available, if the recipient asks that he be present.

Any Government employee (who is not employed by the Agency but who is witting of the recipient's employment) can come so long as there are no operational security reasons that would make his presence inappropriate.

Any friend who is not employed by the Government may be invited only if it is obviously desirable (Example-- A very close old friend who stood in the relationship of "family" to the recipient when subject had no immediate family). Friends who are not employed by the Government normally would be discouraged.

ROBERT M. GAYNOY Recorder, Honor and Merit Awards Board
