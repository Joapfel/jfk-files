January 187

SUBJECT: Progress Report/ E.C.27-5 (2 November 1966 - 15 January 1967)

REFERENCE: HUVA-3072?, 1 December 1966

J. The last Progress Report (see Reference) reported developments in the operation through 21 October 1966, but not included were Subject's activities at the INTURIST Conference in Moscow to which Subject traveled on 22 October 1966. These activities will be reported below.

Contacts:

A. Subject and KGB Case Officer

(201-305670)

First contact by the Soviets on Subject's return to Mexico was effected by KGB case officer Aleksandr Vlasimirovich Frirkache (201-202879) on 3 December in Subject's office for a few minutes. On 8 December, Valentin Surgeyevich Lginov (201-285412), Subject's regular KGB case officer, called Subject down to the street outside Subject's office to present him with Bolshoi Bulletin tickets. On 13 December, Lginov visited Subject's office and spent no more than half an hour there. And on 30 or 31 December, Subject was paid a brief visit by Valentin Surgeyevich, KGB officer (and possible candidate for president) (201-120129) at Subject's office.

B. Subject and Mexico City Station Case Officer

In the two months since Subject's return from Europe in mid-November 1966, the C/O, Prentice R. Karlineine, has seen Subject six times for an approximate total of nine hours.

The C/O met Subject at his office on 22 November for two hours.

In December, the C/O met Subject three times, on 6 December at his office for almost two hours, on 13 December at the Hotel Reforma for a half-hour, and on 22 December at Subject's office for an hour.

On 7 January 1967, the C/O and his wife took Subject and his wife-out for dinner at the Belvedere Room of the Total Continental Hotel, to reciprocate for a former invitation to Subject's name. On 12 January, the C/O met Subject at the latter's office for one hour.
