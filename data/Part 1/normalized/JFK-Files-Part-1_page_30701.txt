This section is provided as an aid in describing the individual. For description, see instructions. Each category is to be interpreted literally.

Placing an "X" in the "Not Observed" column means you have no opinion on whether a phrase applied to an individual. Placing an "X" in the "Does Not Apply" column means that you have the definite opinion that the description is not at all suited to the individual.
