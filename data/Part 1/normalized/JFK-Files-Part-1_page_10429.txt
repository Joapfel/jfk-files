SERI PROMPT: Activities of LIRING-3, LIRING-4 May through Mid-September 1968

ACTION REQUIRED - REFERENCES

RMP: EMA-346-82, 17 May 1968

Action Required: For your information.

1. Forwarded under separate cover are twelve reports prepared by Ballea D. ROSTON on the activities of LIRING-3, LIRING-4, and contracts of the letter.

...of the letter is described in detail, with copies to the appropriate agencies. A summary of results will be issued to obtain help in getting LIRING-3 out of jail in Guatemala as soon as possible. LIRING-4 is almost penultimate (ROSTON has made only token payments to her after 15 June) and has been moving from one apartment to a cheaper one living on the sales of paintings left by LIRING-3 and small loans from list contracts. In the hope that she may be able to do something for LIRING-3, LIRING-4 has resisted all of ROSTON's efforts to get her to undertake a classification mission to PROMPT, a mission, as headquarters knows, was planned long ago. As a foreigner, LIRING-4 cannot work in Mexico without taking the chance of being deported, if caught. Although she has not tried it, she would probably be referred a vice to G toGM to visit LIRING-3. She might also face difficulties in Guatemala including possible detention and interrogation if she succeeded in obtaining an entry permit to that country.

3. By mid-September 1968 LIRING-4 had received very little help from her Mexican leftist or underground Committee friends. In spite of promises to try to bribe the Guatemalan judge scheduled to try LIRING-3 all LIRING-4's "friends" succeeded in doing was to print several articles in the local press (some of them with LIRING-3's photo) claiming that LIRING-3 was unjustly imprisoned, that he was about to be executed, etc. If anything, these continued ... ...
