FIELD TRACES LUIS ALBERTO SOUTO

MEXICO STATION TRACES REVEAL THAT AMSTONE-1, LITAMIL-1, LITAMIL-2, LITAMIL-3 AND LITAMIL-7 CONSIDER ALBERT TO BE ANTI-COMMUNIST.

AN ARMY REPORT DATED AUGUST-25, 1957, STATED THAT COLONEL ARTURO CORONA, 0-2 MEXICAN ARMY, BELIEVED ALBERT TO BE A COMMUNIST AND ONE OF THE MOST INFLUENTIAL MEMBERS OF THE CUBAN EMBASSY STAFF. THIS WAS REPORTED IN MEXI 1506, DATED-26 JULY 1962.
