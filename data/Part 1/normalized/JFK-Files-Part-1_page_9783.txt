8 May,1968

Subject: LILING/4 Report Concerning sentencing of LIKING/3.

1. LIKING/4 reported to Rowton at the Puerto Blanco restaurant on El sectors Ocaampo at 1300 on 8/5/68 that Manuel Marcué Fardinas had informed her at 0100- that same day that he had just learned that LIKING/3 and his predecessor had been sentenced to prison, with a minimum term of six months. Marcué added that his sister and her daughter-in-law would be returning to Mexico City by the end of the week and should be able to supply details of the trial and prospects for the immediate future of having the prison term reduced. LIKING/4 explained to Rowton that she had accepted a dinner invitation at Sanborns extended to her by Aguirre Jerjes. Aguirre said that with respect to her study at the University of Havana, and arrived at the home of Gilberto and Blanca Amant (Blanca Peña de Amant), which she was staying, rather late and found Manuel Marcué waiting for her. After logging profusingly for his previous rude conduct with L/4, he reported the latest news concerning L/3 to her.

2. LIKING/4 agreed with Rowton that although the prison sentence was probably definite, she should check it out with the Mexican Control personally. She stated that fortunately she remembered a certain Josefina Rodríguez who worked in the large distillery service of Teléfonos de México whom she had originally met as a friend of LIKING/2. L/4 commented that Sra. Rodríguez whom she described as "ruy fea" was enoreded of LIKING/2 some years ago but stopped seeing him (to her knowledge) when she found out that he was married. L/4 continued that Sra. Rodriguez had already promised to put through long distance calls to Guatemala for L/4 without charging the calls, if they could be arranged during her shift at the switchboard.

3. LIKING/4 also reported that she had again visited the LIRING offices at 1345 on 7/5/68 and again about 1130 that same morning. At Rowton's request she again checked the phone in the third office. On the first visit the office had already been closed but she ran into LIKING/13 at the gate and accepted a ride home (she felt this fortunate since he dropped her at the Tacubaya apartment at her request). On this occasion during a brief exchange of comments with the gatekeeper and L/13, LIKING/4 learned that LIKING/2 will return from ABRUEN on 10/5/68. CS COPY
