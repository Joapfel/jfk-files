MEMORANDUM FOR: The Director of Intelligence and Security Department of State

SUBJECT: BGE, William Vincent Request for Appointment in the Foreign Service and Assignment at Tokyo, Japan

REMARKS: Annex F-3 to Memorandum dated 23 November 1951, Subject, Representation in Foreign Service Missions

1. It is requested that Mr. Eme, c/o-16, #15,515, be appointed in the Foreign Service with the title First Secretary, PRC-8, #15,255 for duty in the Political Section of the American Embassy at Tokyo, Japan. Mr. Eme will occupy position marker 1 of Annex F-3.

2. It is requested that Mr. Eme arrive at his destination on or about 1 July 1961.
