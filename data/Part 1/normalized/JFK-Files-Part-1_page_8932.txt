MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Contract Personnel Division

SUBJECT: Edward G. TICHBORN (p), Amendment of Contract

1. It is requested that the contract, as amended for Edward G. TICHBORN, Contract Agent, be further amended effective 1 June 1963 as follows:

Increase basic compensation from $8,000 to $9,000 per annum.

Provide additional compensation for quarters procurement at the rate of $3,000 per annum for self and dependents.

Extend the period of the contract for an additional year.

All other provisions of the contract remain in effect.

2. The increase in compensation as recommended by the Mexico City Station is based on the manner of TICHBORN's performance and the increased amount of time which he devotes to Agency requirements.

3. The WII Division concurs with the Station recommendation. TICHBORN has become an increasingly valuable and productive covert asset.

W. E. BROOKS Chief, WII Support
