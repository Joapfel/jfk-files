17 AUG 1965

MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Assistant Secretary for Far Eastern Affairs Department of State

SUBJECT : Detail of CIA Personnel to the Department of State

1. We concur in the detail to the Department of State of the four persons identified in your letter of August 9, 1965.

2. Arrangements are now being made between Mr. Wendt of the Department and representatives of our Far East Division for their assignment to Vietnam. In the absence of unforeseen difficulties, all of the persons named will proceed to Saigon as soon as possible. One of those named, Mr. Joseph E. Baker, is now serving in Europe, but this should not delay his assignment to Vietnam.

/s/ Richard Helms

Richard Helms Deputy Director
