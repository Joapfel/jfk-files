14 FEB 64 IN 19935

PBRUMEN

1. FOLLOWING REPORTED BY ERYTHROID/1 ON 13 AND-14 FEB: IN LATE JAN NIC SECURITY SENT TWO AGENTS, TO COSTA RICA TO PENETRATE GROUP LED BY MARCIAL AGUILUZ WHICH PLOTTING TO KILL GEN. ANASTASIO SOMOZA. TWO AGENTS ARE IDEN A AND IDEN B. THEY WERE SUCCESSFUL AND IDEN A REPORTED ON 11 FEB THAT AGUILUZ HAD INSTRUCTED IDEN B GO TO CUBA FOR SPECIAL TRAINING. IDEN B TO GO MEXICO CITY ON 12 FEB, TO REGISTER AT HOTEL CONGRESO, CALLE ALLENDE, AND -- ON FOLLOWING DAY HE TO GO CALLE FRANCISCO MARQUEZ NUMBER-160 (POR TACUBA) WHERE HE TO ASK FOR SEÑOR VEGA OR SEÑOR ROGELIO. WHEN ASKED BY VEGA OR ROGELIO "DE PARTE DE QUIEN", HE TO ANSWER "DE PARTE DE SENOR CANEIRAS" WHO HAD SENT HIM TO ARRANGE HIS PAPERS. AGUILUZ TOLD IDEN B, THAT IF HE UNABLE LOCATE EITHER VEGA OR ROGELIO, HE TO CALL TELEPHONE NUMBER-14-13-26 REPEAT-14-13-26. IDEN B ALSO TOLD THAT IF HE NEEDED SOMETHING PRESUMABLY WHILE IN ALSO RCVD 1110 (CBEA) HE TO WRITE TO APARTADO 110, SAN JOSE, COSTA RICA. HE MARKED, HOWEVER, TO WRITE NOTHING OF SIGNIFICANCE. AGUILUZ TOLD IDEN B HE WOULD BE GIVEN A COMMO SYSTEM IN MEXICO AT THE CUBAN ENBassy, SO HE COULD LET IDEN A KNOW WHEN HE PLANNING TO RETURN. AGUILUZ EXPLAINED THAT IDEN B WOULD RETURN FROM CUBA CLANDESTINELY BY SUBMARINE.

2. THIS OPERATION BEING RUN BY ERYTHROID/1 WHO NOT REPEAT NOT AWARE STATION BEING INFORMED BY ERYTHROID/8. STATION OBTAINING ADDITIONAL DETAILS WHICH WILL BE REPORTED SOONEST.

3. FOR MEN: E/S REQUEST URGENT IDENTIFICATION REGISTRY OF MEXICO CITY TELEPHONE NUMBER-14-13-26. MUST HAVE REPLY BY MORNING-15 FEB IF E/S TO BE ABLE ADVISE HIS CONTACTS IN MEXICO CITY. IDEN B TO MAKE CONTACT VEGA OR ROGELIO - OR BY PHONE, BY 17 FEB AT 11:00.
