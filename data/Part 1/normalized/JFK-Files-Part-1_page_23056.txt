32. Nephrology Institute. The Nephrology Institute is located on the fifth floor of the Joaquin Albarran Clinical-Surgical Hospital, Avenida 26 at Avenida Rancho Boyeros, Havana. It is headed by Dr. Abelardo Buch, M.D., an extraordinarily able researcher who was working on:

acute, chronic renal insufficiency,

microdeterminations of certain normal quantities of the renal apparatus, and

the experimental verification of modern theories that explain the physiopathology of shock and its treatment; his experiments were based on the research hypothesis of Dr. Richard Lillehei, chief of the Department of Surgery, Minnesota University.
