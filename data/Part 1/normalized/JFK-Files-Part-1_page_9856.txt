22 March, 1968

Subject: LIRING/3 Report of Contact with Guatemalans in Mexico City.

1. At a brief meeting with Rowton at 1700 on 22/3/68 at the Diana restaurant LIRING/3 reported that he was working on the fabrication of identity documents for the two FAR representatives ( Miguel Angel Oliva Castañeda and Rodolfo) through his serigraphic process and that he hoped to have the pictures of the persons concerned the following day. He asked Rowton to bring along his copying kit so that he could quickly copy the pictures just before LIRING/3 put them on the identity cards. Rowton prom sed to bring the kit to the Atoyac apartment at 1500 on 23/3/68.

2. LIRING/3 also reported that he had learned from the Guatemalans that a certain Esquenasi or Eskenazi, a Guatemalan who has resided for many years in Mexico and who owns or operates a factory called "Cronos de México", has been very helpful to the FAR members in Mexico. Likewise a doctor by the name of Jorge Flesch has been of service when needed.

3. In reply to Rowton's query LIRING/3 stated that all the seven Guatemalans he had recently met ( four of whom live at 54 Río赫nera) appeared to be FAR adherents, except one, who he had learned in passing was a follower of San Sosa. L/3 also stated that he had learned that the one who worked in the photo shop on San Juan Latrán street was an accountant rather than a photographer.

Jallace B. Rowton
