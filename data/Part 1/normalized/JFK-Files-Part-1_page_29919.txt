COMMENTS OF REVIEWING OFFICIAL

Under Mr. Murphy's leadership the Paris Station became very operationally oriented, concentrating on high priority targets, and much exceptionally fine intelligence and counterintelligence production resulted. I have some doubt that these operational successes - as outstanding as they were - really required the total number of personnel of all categories who were committed to them. Nevertheless, Mr. Murphy made Paris a shining example for all other large stations.
