CONTRACT REPORT II PROJECT LUSHERG

1. The record of KUZ'S dealings with Serge Melan and Roberto Holcman appear to be as follows:

Four (4) initial meetings with Melan on 17, 18, and 19 July. b. Two (2) meetings with Holcman on 25th and 26th July. It was obviously at these meetings that the trip to the Congo was arranged.

2. ETRI contacted Farnese during the afternoon of Saturday, June 10, about the possibility of a meeting with the Planeta on Sunday, June 11. Farnese discussed that day but in ETRI and Holcman, he should call back on Friday at 0900 the following day, Thursday and Friday, in order to discuss it further.
