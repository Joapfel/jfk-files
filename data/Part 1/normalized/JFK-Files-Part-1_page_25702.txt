14 July 1965

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Contract Personnel Division SUBJECT: Raymond H. GERENDE, Contract Amendment

1. It is requested that the contract, as amended for Raymond H. GERENDE, Contract Agent, be further amended as follows.

Increase compensation to $6500. per year effective 1 July 1965.

Extend period of contract for one year from date of expiration.

2. The recommendation of the Mexico City Station, concurred with by WHD, was based on his demonstrated technical ability and professional competence.

William V. Bruce Chief Western Hemisphere Division
