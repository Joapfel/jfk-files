3. MINSK INSTITUTE OF FOREIGN LANGUAGES WAS LOCATED ON ULITSA ZAKHAROVA, NEAR POBEDY SQUARE. IT HAD FOUR TEACHER TRAINING FACULTIES (ENGLISH, GERMAN, FRENCH AND SPANISH) AS WELL AS INTERPRETER FACULTY COMPRISED OF FOUR DEPTS REPRESENTING SAME LANGUAGES. SUBJ CLAIMS THAT ABOUT EIGHTY PER CENT OF STUDENTS, ESPECIALLY THOSE OF GERMAN AND SPANISH, WERE DRAWN FROM RURAL AREAS OF BELORUSSIA. AMONG THE OTHER STUDENTS WERE SOME CHILDREN OF BELORUSSIAN VIPS.

4. SUBJ KNEW SEVERAL GRADUATES OF INTERPRETER FACULTY OF HIS INSTITUTE WHO WERE PART OF A SMALL GROUP EVENTUALLY CIRCULATING ON FOREIGN ASSIGNMENTS. SUBJ STATED THAT THEY TRAVELLED ABROAD UNDER CONTRACTS WITH VARIOUS SOVIET MINISTRIES (THOUGH IN MOST CASES WORKING AS MILITARY TRANSLATORS/ INTERPRETERS). UPON RETURN TO USSR, THEIR CONTRACTS WERE TERMINATED AND THEY HAD TO SEEK OTHER JOBS WHILE AWAITING POSSIBLE NEW ASSIGNMENTS ABROAD. SUBJ WAS NOT INFORMED ON THE IR CP MEMBERSHIP AND BELIEVED THAT MOST OF THE TRANSLATORS KNOWN TO HIM DID NOT BELONG TO THE PARTY.
