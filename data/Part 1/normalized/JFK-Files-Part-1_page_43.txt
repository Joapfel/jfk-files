It is believed that the Counterintelligence Directorate, KGB (Kontrrazvedyvatelnoye upravleniya-KRU), of the KGB initiates operations against foreigners inside the USSR. When the operation leads outside the USSR into foreign countries, i.e., when the probable recruitments have been achieved, and the persons in question leave the USSR, the operation is then turned over to the Foreign Intelligence Directorate (Inostrannoye upravleniye-INU) which continues the operation until such time as it is terminated.
