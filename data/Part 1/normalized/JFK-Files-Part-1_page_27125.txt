2. JOSE TORRIENTE HAS TOLD AMCHALK-I THAT NEGOTIATIONS ARE WELL ALONG FOR OBTAINING TWO ISLANDS AS TRAINING AND OPERATIONAL BASES FOR EXILES. ONE IS THE ISLA BEATA OFF THE SOUTH COAST OF THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC. NEGOTIATIONS WITH UNIDENTIFIED DOMINICANS FOR USE OF THIS ISLAND ARE TAKING PLACE THROUGH THE ARGENTINE CONSUL GENERAL IN PUERTO RICO. TORRIENTE WAS IN PUERTO RICO LAST WEEK. THE OTHER ISLAND WHICH IS BEING NEGOTIATED FOR BY EX-PRESIDENT CARLOS PRIO SOCARRAS IS ILE A VACHE WHICH IS LOCATED OFF THE SOUTHERN HAITIAN PENINSULA. TRAINING ACTIVITIES AND RECRUITING FOR A MARITIME AND INFILTRATION COURSE IS CURRENTLY TAKING PLACE IN THE MIAMI AREA, HOWEVER THIS FAR ALL TRAINING HAS BEEN CONDUCTED UNDER CLASER ROOM CONDITIONS IN PRIVATE HOMES THROUGHOUT THE MIAMI AREA.
