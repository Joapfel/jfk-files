OPERATIONAL/TYPICAL AMBIDDY-1's Involvement in Haitian Affairs

1. On 18 October 1964 AMCARBON-1 accidentally met AMBIDDY-1 in the lobby of the hotel El Amajador in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. AMBIDDY-1 was obviously embarrassed by the fact that he had the chance meeting with AMCARBON-1 along he asked that AMCARBON-1 not tell anyone in Miami that he had seen AMBIDDY-1 in Santo Domingo. As an aftermath of this challenge meeting, AMCARBON-1 arranged for AMBIDDY-1 to talk with Mr. Badestrandt DIEDERICH, a freelance newspaperman residing in Santo Domingo. The purpose of this conversation was to enable Diederich to give AMBIDDY-1 a briefing on the Haitian scene and, as a follow-up, arrange a meeting between AMBIDDY-1 and certain Haitian exile leaders. The meeting with the Haitians took place at Diederich's house on 18 October 1964. The meeting was attended by AMCARBON-1, AMBIDDY-1, a Cuban known as Milo Jesus MESSER Pujol (MA-294655), Father BAJEZUK and Sylvia WADESTRANDT. After a preliminary series of discussions, AMBIDDY-1 adjourned to another room of the Diederich house to talk privately with Father Bajezuk and Sylvia Wadestrandt. At the end of this private meeting, AMCARBON-1 had an opportunity to talk with AMBIDDY-1. As a result, AMCARBON-1 learned that, as of 18 October 1964, it was AMBIDDY-1's intention to help the Haitians in their fight against Duvalier. AMBIDDY-1 believed that he could furnish the arms and the transportation for the invasion of Haiti by Haitian exiles. AMBIDDY-1 was also talking about committing some of his troops to such an invasion as he felt that, if he were successful in this venture, he could obtain significant base rights for his organization in Haiti. These bases would in turn be used by AMBIDDY-1's operations against PERUKEN.

2. On the morning of 19 October 1964, AMCARBON-1 had an opportunity to talk in Santo Domingo with a Cuban called Rafael Garcia who is AMBIDDY-1's representative in the Dominican Republic.
