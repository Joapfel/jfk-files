For Supervisors:

1. The office or division will maintain five and attendance records during the period of this attachment.

2. Overtime can be authorized only after supervisor has checked with the JOT Training Officer. CTR will be reimbursed by division or office for overtime payments. The TIA clerk should report to JOTP at the end of each pay period any overtime worked by a JOT.

3. Any security violations by this JOT will be handled in the manner that is appropriate to the division or staff. Chief/JOTP will be notified in this event.

4. Supervisors will execute any fitness reports covering the period of this attachment. Chief/JOTP will countersign all reports.

5. Supervisors will provide job performance data to the Chief/JOTP when the JOT presents a promotion zone of consideration.

6. JOTP is located in Form 743, Brothill Building, Extension 3261. Any questions should be addressed to Mr. JOHN GERRY.

7. It is requested that each supervisor submit a progress report after three months of attachment. In the event of rotational assignments within a division where supervisors change, a progress report should be submitted at the end of each phase of the rotation.

For JOT:

8. Any leave will be worked out with the supervisor and if approved must be communicated by telephone to the JOTP office. See paragraph (1) above.

9. The JOT will furnish Chief/JOTP with a progress report after the first month of attachment and every two months thereafter. Items desired include:

Description of activities

Plans for next reporting period

Names and titles of supervisors

Assessment of value of past experience

Any constructive suggestions

These reports should be sent to Chief/JOTP via the supervisor.

10. The JOT will advise this office of his record number and extension and name of his supervisor so it may be possible after attachment.
