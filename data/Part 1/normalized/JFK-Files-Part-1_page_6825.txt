2. This office has learned through a sensitive source of known reliability that, for many years, Maurice Hyman HALLIDAY has been associated with a Dr. Mario JADIA who had an office at 55bis rue de la Convention, Paris 15, France.

3. On 12 February 1960 we learned through a delicate source that he has provided reliable information in the past that Mark JADIA, M. N., a naturalized French citizen, born on 29 December 1926 in Paris, France, resides with his family at 55 bis rue de la Convention, Paris 15. His wife, Armelle HALLENE JADIA, born SITAN, was born in Paris on 30 January 1933. A son, Serge JADIA, was born on 7 October 1940 in Charenton-le-Orient, Saône, France. This source had no confidential information on JADIA.

4. According to our records, a Dr. H. JADIA, living at 55 bis rue de la Convention, Paris 15, was a member of the "Concorde d'Initiation" (representing committees) of IBAP (In Movement Centre le Décide, l'Antidraftisme et pour la Fois - Movement Agitiste's Environnent, Antidraftisme end for feme), a Communist front organization formed in 1949 to promote Christian anti-Jewish elements. Records, our records fail to show any continued record of activity in this organization on the part of JADIA.
