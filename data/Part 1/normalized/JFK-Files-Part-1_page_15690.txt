17 NOV 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Finance Division

ATTENTION : Monetary Branch

SUBJECT : Opening Bank Account for Georg Franz REINER

1. It is hereby requested that an account be opened and a sum of four thousand dollars be deposited in a bank in Switzerland under the name of Georg Franz REINER. Subsequently a letter of credit in the amount of 150,000 Belgium Congo Francs ($3,000.00) is to be provided REINER, in Switzerland.

2. To facilitate the transaction and expedite this request, Messrs. Daniel R. Fiesciur and Georg Franz Reiner will be in Bern on 28 November. Mr. Fiesciur will contact the station and will act as an intermediary between the Bern Station and REINER who is a contract agent.

3. Your assistance in this matter will be greatly appreciated.

Bronson Tweedy Chief, AF Division
