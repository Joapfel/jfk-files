REFERENCE(S) 1. Attached in a photograph of Soviet POHORIL'V, which we would like shown, without name, to appropriate unilateral source for possible identification.

2. FYI: POHORIL'V's name appears as the sender of a letter containing secret writing to Austin BICZYNSKIK in Paris, France. The letter was sent in care of Dnytro (CYP), another Ukrainian resident of Paris. CYP, in November 1960, asked AROGKA/I to deliver the letter to BICZYNSKIK who was confined to a mental sanatorium near Paris. CYP told AROGKA/I that he (CYP) and BICZYNSKIK had been involved with Visually Impaired (WIA) Soviet assigned to UNECO and the case officer of the arrested enigre Grigorii BEMGOECKE, and therefore CYP feared seeing to the attention of the French police by delivering the letters to BICZYN'SKIK. MIGRATZ analysis revealed the presence of the s/w in the letter from POHORIL'V, but failed in attempts to develop the s/w. CODECY and Farid Station are now investigating this case; therefore, in your reply please send an information copy to Paris.
