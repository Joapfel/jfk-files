REF: A. PALP 5033 (IN 96553) B. OFLA 3128 (18 November 1965)

1. We have discussed the substance of the question raised in Ref B through the regular Headquarters channel with the ODEWV Rep here who has acted as a point of liaison for GREGGNY material in the past. We passed on, also, the materials which you prepared for the ODEWV representative with whom you are in contact in Paris. We raised specifically the question of passing GREGGNY's identity to the Paris ODEWV representative, which you mentioned in Ref B, stating that, so far as KUBARK is concerned, we had no objections. The ODEWV representative here, however, thought that this might serve to confuse somewhat the lines of coordination for GREGGNY's information, for which ODEWV headquarters has sole responsibility. He therefore felt that we should continue the present arrangement by which, in effect, GREGGNY'S identity is held here at the point where his material within ODEWV originates. He appreciated, however, your initiative in filling in the Paris ODEWV representative on the targets which GREGGNY can service, thus alerting him to possible leads he might look into. He suggests also that we be kept aware of your liaison with ODEWV in the field to guard against false confirmation or other possible duplication which could lead to confusion here in those quarters of ODEWV responsible for processing the GREGGNY material.

2. With respect to Freedomways, discussed in Ref A, and Q's possible relationship with it, we will report separately.
