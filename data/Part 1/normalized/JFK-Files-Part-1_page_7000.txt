TO: Director Federal Bureau of Investigation

Attention: Mr. S. J. Rapideh

FROM: Deputy Director (Plane)

SUBJECT: Maurice Hyman HALPERIN

1. Reference is made to our C.C.I-3/772,459, Subject: "Contact with U.S. Defectors Abroad," dated 13 September 1962.

2. Sub-paragraph (e) of referenced C.C.I reported that at sometime during the period October 1960—February 1961 Maurice Halperin had stated several times to a CIA source in Moscow that "he would like to come back to the U.S., and once asked the source if he should accept a position in Cuba which he had been offered by Fauro CHICKEN."

3. This office has received the following additional report concerning HALPERIN from the CIA office which contributed the original report:

"On 27 December 1962 another source of this office advised that he received a 1962 Christmas card from Maurice Halperin on which the return address was given as 'Apt C-2, Calle 12, no 103, Miramar, Havana, Cuba.' The only message on the card was one of Christmas greeting. Halperin and the source have known each other for a number of years and they always exchange Christmas cards, but this is the first time Halperin's greeting has borne a Cuban address."

"The last time that the source saw Halperin was in Moscow when source was visiting in the USSR during August—September 1961 as a member of an official U. S. delegation."

There is no record of the Havana return address in the intelligence files of this office.
