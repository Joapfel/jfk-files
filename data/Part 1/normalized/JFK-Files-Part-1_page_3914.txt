SUBJECT: *GRAUPERA, Roberto

1. Source met Subject about April 1960 at which time source was appointed to the Personnel Department at the Distrito #3, Havana, in the Ministerio de Obras Publicas, at which time Subject was the Chief Engineer of the said Distrito #3, Havana. In early 1961 Subject was transferred to the main office of Obras Publicas at Plaza Civica and also at this time source believes Subject was appointed a professor at the University of Havana. At that time source became friendly with Subject. Source went to visit Subject at his home in Calle Santa Catalina, Reparto Santos Suarez, when source heard that Subject was leaving Cuba and coming to the US and source wanted to say goodbye. Source has had no contact with him since that time. Source has heard that Subject is living with his family in Miami, and has a position as an Engineer in Nassau, where he goes every week. Source believes Subject has never been a communist, has never traveled to any communist country, nor has received any military, political or intelligence training.

2. Physical Description: DPOS: circa 1920, place of birth unknown. Race: white. 6'1", 210 lbs. Hair: black. Eyes: black. Shape of face: oval. Physique: heavy. No visible scars.

JEWAVE Traces:

One Roberto GRAUPERA was employed as Cuban manager for IBM Dredging before the advent of the Castro regime (KIJUMP Homo, 18 September 1962).

Roberto J. GRAUPERA Graniela departed from Mexico 11 November 1960 (Travel Manifest)

Roberto J. GRAUPERA Graniela appeared on a list of the Technological Faculty, School of Engineering, Havana University (DOI: September 1962) (UFG 2714, 22 April 1963)
