We recognize all the difficulty, in the course of a project of this type, of shifting responsibility from one deputy to the other; and we realize that development must also lap over into the operational phase. We suggest having as a general rule (but granting that exceptions will arise) that when operation begins the responsibility for the operations should be the DD/P's, but that DD/R should retain his responsibility for seeing that the equipment he has developed continues to function properly.
