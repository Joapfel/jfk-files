18 February 1963

EDUCATION RDR: Member of Trading

SECRETARY: Chief, Court Trading/Operations School Chief, Staff Trading

CRRNT: Request for Court Trading of all ALMS funds

1. It is requested that full time court trading be provided at Barcelona City, Spain so that approximately ten days beginning on or about March 20th may be used for staff training purposes.

2. The objective of the training is to provide the subject with the basic principles of collective treatment to better prepare them as far as possible for inside a judicial career.

3. In addition to the basic treatment, trading should expose repeating tasks, cases and include appropriate source writing instructions, to be taught by the case officer till he is.

4. Subject is a FAREL official staff is the Revenue in Barcelona City. A Provisional Operational Appointments was requested on 25 May 1963 and the status is being checked. (C-1043) from 23rd, October, Spain)

5. Responsible Headquarters case officer for the trading to B. E. Mullen, external WFL
