MEMORANDUM FOR : Mr. N. Scott Miles

SUBJECT : Notification of Intent to Recommend Retirement

Personnel of the Deputy Director for Operations have been informed that we are in a surplus personnel situation. You have informed me that in these circumstances you are willing to be retired. I am grateful for your offer to assist in this matter. Accordingly, I intend to recommend your retirement which, if approved, will entitle you to an immediate annuity.

William E. Nelson Deputy Director for Operations
