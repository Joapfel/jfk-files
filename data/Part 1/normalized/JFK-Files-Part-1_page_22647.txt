VI. OPERATIONAL BACKGROUND

NOEMAYR was first recruited in Panama in November 1948 and transferred to Costa Rica under the HUHANOID PROJECT. He successfully directed and exploited a high-level penetration of the Costa Rican Communist Party. Among other accomplishments he recruited two mail intercept agents, processing the take himself, and recruited three additional agents. He also set up safe houses and was in charge of the monitoring post in a technical surveillance.

In early 1953 he was transferred to the PBSUCCESS Project with additional duties as cutout under Project ESCROW.

In 1954 he transferred to Cuba under Project AMPHODARCH. He went under cover as a student since he had one more year of academic work to finish in order to receive a B.A. in Economics. In Cuba he worked on penetration of the Communist targets at the University of Habana. During the same period he was also in contact with subversive oppositionist elements.

In May, 1957, Chief CI/ICD recommended that NOEMAYR be considered for spot assignments of making recruitment pitches throughout Latin America to CP targets. This recommendation was made after Mr. Paul Burke, Deputy Chief CI/ICD, had an informal discussion with NOEMAYR on 9 May on this subject. Mr. Burke believed NOEMAYR would be an effective recruiter and NOEMAYR expressed an interest in such assignments.
