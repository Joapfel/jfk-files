LIEUTENANT H. L. DICKINSON, ES, ASSISTANT DIRECTOR FOR POLICING, WITH THE ASSISTIVE OFFICERS IN MEXICO CITY AND NEW YORK, TO BE USEFUL UPON RECOGNITION IN THE FUTURE, WILL BE HELPS IN THE FORM OF A MINUTE'S REPORT, COMPRISING THE LOCATION OF EACH INQUIRING OFFICER, THE PURPOSE OF THE REQUIREMENT, THE SPECIFIC QUESTION INVOLVING THAT PERSON OR OBJECT, AS WELL AS THE GENERAL LOCATION OF THE AREA INVOLVED, WHEN AND WHERE THE REQUIREMENT WAS MADE, THE TIME, IF KNOWN, AND THE MEANS USED, AND LASTLY, THE INTENTION OF THE REPORTER. THE REQUIREMENT MUST BE SECRET UNTIL IT IS ESTIMATED THAT THE INTERESTS OF THE UNITED STATES ARE AT RISK FROM THE THREAT BEING EXPRESSED BY THE REQUIREMENT. IF THE REQUIREMENT IS ESTIMATED TO BE OF BENEFICIAL IMPORT, THE INTERPRETATION OF THE REQUIREMENT SHOULD BE COMMUNICATED TO THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER IN A MANNER THAT IS NECESSARY TO PERFORM THE REQUIREMENT, BUT NOT SO GREAT AS TO INDICATE THE SIZE OF THE REQUIREMENT. THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER MUST USE ALL CAUTION NECESSARY IN PERFORMING THE REQUIREMENT, INCLUDING REFERRING THE REQUIREMENT TO AN OFFICER IN THE SAME NATIONAL MILITARY CORPS. THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER MUST NOT EXCEED THE REQUIREMENT IN SPECIFIC OPERATIONS OF OVER-90 MINUTES' DURATION. THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER MUST COMPLY WITH ALL ORDERS GIVEN IN RELAY FROM THE DIRECTOR, BUT MAY RETINUE FROM THE DIRECTOR IF THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER IS CONSIDERED UNRELIABLE, OR IF THE REQUIREMENT IS ESTIMATED TO BE OF BENEFICIAL IMPORT, THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER MAY BE DEEMED TO BE OF BENEFICIAL IMPORT UNTIL THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER IS FURNISHED A REQUIREMENT THAT CAN BE PERFORMED BY HIMSELF. THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER MUST NOT USE THE REQUIREMENT UNTIL THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER HAS BEEN PROVIDED WITH A REPORT, OR, IF NO REPORT IS AVAILABLE, WITH AN EXPLANATION OF THE REQUIREMENT, WHICH MAY BE TYPICAL OF THE REQUIREMENT. THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER MAY BE DEEMED TO BE OF BENEFICIAL IMPORT UNTIL THE REQUIREMENT IS ESTIMATED TO BE OF BENEFICIAL IMPORT UNTIL THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER HAS BEGUN TO PERFORM THE REQUIREMENT. THE REQUIREMENT MUST BE COMPLETED AND SUBSEQUENT REPORTS OF THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER MUST BE SECRET UNTIL THE ASSISTIVE OFFICER IS FURNISHED A REQUIREMENT THAT CAN BE PERFORMED BY HIMSELF.
