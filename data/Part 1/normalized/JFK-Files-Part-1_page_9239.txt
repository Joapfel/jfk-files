1739 N St NW Nov. 13, 1950

C-O-P-Y

Dear Boris,

Many thanks for your excellent letter of the 13th, which I got this evening, and all the good news about "The Choice", which I'll share.

I'll try to clarify about our mutual friend / hinson / just as soon as I possibly can. Couldn't reach him all this evening. Maybe I can get him tomorrow morning. Will urge him to see you at the earliest and get it all clarified, as that will -as you say- be a great help. Meanwhile why not go ahead on an estimate of say 150 pp. Hope he can see you by Tuesday or at earliest thereafter.

Best regards hastily

F.S.

Good about "Lenin" too. And the Navy! And the dual-German edition! And how right Matthew XIII, 57 was about prophets.
