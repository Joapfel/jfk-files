15 May 1957

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Safehouse Branch, Office of Logistics TIRACOUGH : Chief, Central Cover Branch, BO/P REQUESTING OFFICE : WH/TIII/Mexico CASE OFFICER : Laurence B. Sule - Extension 2555 PROJECT : LIPSTICK Allotments: 7-3570-83-130

Reference: R 45-1025, 25 June 1956.

Type of safehouse requested: Furnished apartment (efficiency apart- ment or larger). The apartment should be large enough to show motion pictures to trainees.

Location and environment: Washington, D. C. Middle class or above type of environment.

Maximum Price Range: $10 per day.

Occupancy from: 8 June 1957 To: 14 July 1957

Intended Use: Safehouse will be occupied by one (1) agent for residence and training. The agent is a Mexican (completely bilingual in Spanish and English, was born in the U. S.), aged 23, a college graduate and purchasing agent for a Mexico City firm. He will occupy the safehouse most of the time, both day and night, for five weeks. Three or four other persons (from OTR and possibly the case officer) will be on site at the same time in the safehouse during the day. Sensit-ive material will be in the house during the day.

Cover Arrangements: Subject will be in the U. S. as a tourist from Mexico. He has American relatives in North Carolina. Wh Division's memorandum requesting training for Subject, and which included this cover plan, was approved by the FI and CI Staffs on 14 May 1957.
