1. REF BOMBING. WHICH WIDELY COVERED IN LOCAL PRSSS. OCCURRED ON 31 AUGUST IN T-E MEXICAN CONSULATE IN DOWNTOWN GUATEMALA CITY. THE CONSULATE SUFFERED SOME DAMAGE AND A RECEPTIONIST WAS SLIGHTLY INJURED. SINCE BOMB EXPLODED WITHIN THE MEXICAN CONSULATE OFFICES, WHICH LOCATED IN A LARGE BUILDING. IT IS LIKELY THAT THE CONSULATE WAS INDEED THE TARGET. NEITHER ESMICA-68 NOR ESCE--4 (WHO INVESTIGATED THE BOMBING) HAD ANY HARD INFORMATION ON THE IDENTITIES OR MOTIVES OF THE BOMBERS. ALTHOUGH SOME SUSPICION WAS ATTACHED TO THE GUARD WHO WAS HOMERück on the day of the bombing.
