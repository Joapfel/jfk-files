DIRECTOR INFO PRETORIA, LONDON

SECABOB

1. ON 3 MARCH 1967 SECAROB/I HAD MEETING WITH HASLANGER AND REPORTED FOLLOWING ABOUT HIS MEETING WITH VISTATIC/1.

A. SECAROB/I TRIED UNSUCCESSFULLY TO GET VISTATIC/I TO ATTUNE ON WHAT GDRC WAS GOING TO GIVE HIM FOR PHASING OUT THE COMMANDO AND FOR RECRUITING NEW MEN IN ENGLAND. BASED ON WISTATIC/I'S ATTITUDE THAT SECAROB/I WAS SOMETHING OF A CAD FOR BRINGING UP MONEY MATTERS, SECAROB/I TOLD VISTATIC/I HE HAD NO INTENTION OF PHASING OUT S COMMANDO UNLESS THIS FOLLOVED UP WITH RECRUITMENT OF 400 MEN FROM ENGLAND WITH EVENTUAL EXPANSION THIS FORCE TO 800 MEN, MOST OF WHOM WOULD BE ARTISANS AND PROFESSIONAL MEN; AND FINALLY SECAROB/I TO BE ALLOWED TO DEVELOP TOURISM INTO THE EASTERN CONGO. AS A RESULT OF THIS CONVERSATION SECAROB/I STATED THAT HE NOW HAS RESERVATIONS ABOUT BEING AX MAN FOR THE S COMMANDO BECAUSE HE HASBNO ASSURANCE THAT THE GDRC WILL PERMIT HIM TO CARRY OUT THE OTHER ASPECTS OF THIS PROGRAMME.
