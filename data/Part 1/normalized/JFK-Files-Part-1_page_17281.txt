An amendment to the original request for project approval was submitted before the original project was approved. This amendment requested approval to include Simon D. CLACKETT (ps) and Wilma J. FIORALISI (ps) under the LIMBTT Project effective 1 January 1959 and remove them from the LIMBTT Project. The funds designated in the original FPO for the undesignated Staff Agent were used to cover the expenses for CLACKETT and FIORALISI. A copy of the amendment request is being forwarded for your information and records.

Among the Staffs' comments on the LIMITY Project, the following recommendations were made. KODESK suggested that the Case Officer should work at devising ostensible sources of incomes for the agents which will stand the scrutiny of friends, relatives and less friendly forces.

An amendment to CLACKETT's contract has been requested to allow for a quarters allowance. The educational allowance requested in paragraph 11 of the project amendment was disapproved because CLACKETT was a Contract Agent. The Quarters Allowance of $2,500 was turned down because it was too high in relation to CLACKETT's salary. Therefore, a quarters allowance of $1,800 has been approved and will be written into CLACKETT's contract. If, however, operational and cover reasons require CLACKETT to rent an apartment larger than the one he would normally obtain under his allowance, the Chief of Station can authorize the extra rent as an operational expense which will not be considered as compensation for CLACKETT and therefore not subject to federal income tax.
