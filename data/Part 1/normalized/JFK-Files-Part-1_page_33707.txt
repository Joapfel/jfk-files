TRADE UNIONISM:

The AugustinianSWU (the Catholic Church), too, the situation of the Socialist Party and Trade Unions-in-Exile and the Christian Democratic parties of the Trade Unions-in-Exile, and ahead of the antiguities in this situation. She indicated that in this present situation:

There are no existences:

On the Communist side:

(1) The political-party organization: The Socialist Union of Central and Eastern Europe.

(2) The professional-trade union organization; The International Center of Free Trade Unions-in-Exile.

and that

On the Christian Democratic side there are:

(1) The political party organization; the Christian Democratic Union of Central Europe.

(2) The professional-trade union organization; the Central European Federation of Christian Trade Unions.

She pointed out that:

2. HCR gives financial support and "recognition" to the Socialists on the professional and trade union level through the International Center of Free Trade Unions in Exile and has done so since September 1951. HCR gives financial support and "recognition" to the Christian Democratic element on the political-party level through the Christian Democratic Union of Central Europe which it has supported since January, 1951.
