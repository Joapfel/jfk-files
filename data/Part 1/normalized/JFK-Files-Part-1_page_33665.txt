30 January 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR: SPECIAL ASSISTANT FOR INSPECTION AND REPORTS

SUBJECT: Radio Free Europe Broadcasts Regarding the Czech Currency Reform

REFERENCE: TD/P Memorandum to DCopy of 16 January 1952, Same Subject

1. At the risk of making this memorandum overly long, a review of the cable traffic on the above subject appears desirable. According to information available to this office, the campaign to take the psychological initiative in connection with the rumored reform of currency of Czechoslovakia was first proposed by FE Division on 30 December 1951 in cable to Frankfurt and Munich (OUT 96920) as follows:

Persistent rumors claim Czech currency reform imminent. Hungary allegedly printing new Czech currency. Long New Year holiday appears suitable for reform.

Request all information this subject forwarded to Fran anduni.

Believe general knowledge rumored reform to Czechs would harass government, cause excessive buying.

Suggest Czech operation out of Germany exploit theme конец в СТОРОВУ МЛASY and/or pamphlets or other media emphasizing:

(1) Economic and political dependency Czechs on Russians and consequent disadvantages.

(2) Instability Czech economy.

(3) Advisability population unload cash for goods.

If White Legion prepared suggest exploit them along lines paragraph d.

f.uni: Suggest you request Radio Free Europe also exploit them.
