Harold Robert ISAACS was born in New York City on 13 September 1910 of Amer- ican parents. His father has been reported as "decidedly of the commercial type," ISAACS has been described as intelligent, critical, deft, and nervously organized.

Little is known concerning the activities of ISAACS prior to his arrival in China from Manila on 10 December 1930, however, who visited ISAACS in Shanghai, is reported to have embarrassed his son in the presence of friends by "kidding" the latter about trying to make money on real estate while in Los Angeles before going to China.

The actual date when Cecil Frank GLASS and Harold ISAACS is not known, but it must have been shortly after the arrival of GLASS in Shanghai in May 1931, for in June 1931, ISAACS and GLASS made a trip up the Yangtze together.

It has been stated that ISAACS became interested in Communism with specific relation to China; it has also been stated that letters between ISAACS and his fiancée, Viola ROBINSON, indicated his intention to take his trip with GLASS, and he obtained approval to do so.

As you are aware, ISAACS and GLASS returned to Shanghai in August 1931 and took up residence at 128 Weihaiwei Road. In September 1931, both men are reported to have become members of the PULLES Defense Committee, ISAACS being the Secretary.
