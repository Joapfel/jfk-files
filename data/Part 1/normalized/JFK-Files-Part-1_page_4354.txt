DRAFT

The following are the highlights of operational meeting with AMBIDDY-1 on 3-4 May 1966 conducted in Room 1609 in the Hotel Everglades in Miami, Florida.

The first part of our discussion concerned reports which he had recently originated or been responsible for concerning possibility of agitation and disturbances in Costa Rica preceding and during the inauguration of President-elect Trejos 9 May 1966.

AMBIDDY-1 first spent considerable time in attempting to justify his efforts to establish relations with various Government leaders in Central America and why he had placed certain of his people in position to develop information on these Governments. He said that sometime ago it had been suggested upon him by other people with whom he had been in contact including agency personnel that it would be important to the future of his movement to be sure that he had the support of the Central American Governments. This he added was considered important in connection with the necessity of this support in establishing his training basis in Costa Rica and Nicaragua but also that the political support of these countries would be essential if and when the movement had any success in developing internal opposition within Cuba and, bring about circumstances for Castro's overthrow.

He said that all part of the efforts he has maintained either personally, or through one of his representatives or tried to maintain periodic personal contact with Government leaders in Central America, and that the recent source of information on activities in Costa Rica, through Luis Arrizurieta Sardinas, a man of his personal confidence and a member of the MRR, was placed as Secretary to Pres.
