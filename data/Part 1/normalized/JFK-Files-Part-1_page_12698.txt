30 January 1975

MEMORANDUM FOR: Alien Affairs Officer, OPS/LSN

SUBJECT : Luis Clemente POSADA Carriles

1. On 30 June 1974 Luis Clemente POSADA's employment with the Agency was terminated by mutual consent. At that time he was establishing a private investigative agency from which he expected to earn his livelihood. The Immigration and Naturalization Service file on POSADA, therefore, may now be decontrolled. As of 27 January 1975, POSADA is still located in Caracas, Venezuela.

2. On 12 June 1974 Caracas Station advised that POSADA had been informed of and fully understands the effect that termination of his Agency employment will have upon his resident alien status and eligibility for U.S. citizenship. We have not yet received termination records on POSADA, probably because the Station is still meeting with him regarding settlement of his outstanding U.S. tax liabilities.

Ramon E. Gonzalez LA/NW/V
