6. At Headquarters, the Soviet Bloc Division uses LIEMPTY photographs, particularly photographs of Soviet Embassy officials, for identification purposes in connection with defector cases. The LIEUPTY material also keeps the Station's Soviet section up-to-date on which Soviet officers have been in the Embassy, which ones have been away; who has used which car; who has been in contact with whom; and other similar information useful in the conduct of Soviet operations.
