SUBJECT ADMIN/CONTRACTS Sandra B. CAZAZUS (C/E) (132830) - Contract Entitlements

1. Subject is a contract employee who is required to perform her duties during various hours of the day (including a varying number of hours) in order to meet operational deadlines.

2. Even though CAZAZUS' contract allows her annual and sick leave benefits, she has not been reimbursed for any leave during the period 8 September 1967 to the present. In addition, the Station has not reimbursed her for any holidays, WOLADY or local, while other contract employees whose contracts call for a regularly scheduled tour of duty, have received compensation.

3. Since subject has worked an average of 30 hours per week, with headquarters concurrence, it is recommended that she be reimbursed six hours per day when in a leave status and for paid holidays retroactively to 8 September 1967, the effective date of her present contract.

For John A. HOGBERT
