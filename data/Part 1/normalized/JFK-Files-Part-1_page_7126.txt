1. The C.O. resumed acquaintance with Subject, and in the immediate and sizable vacuum left by Gerald F. Costotner's departure, tried to establish a friendly rapport with Subject. For this reason the C.O. gave Subject a more "friendly" name to know him by, Hel Berger- it being explained to Subject that Hel was the C.O.'s true first name. Subject thought the choice of Berger was good, because he knew several people of that name, and it would accustomed Subject to his office. Subject will continue to use his normal manner of telephoning in, and asking for Monroy.
