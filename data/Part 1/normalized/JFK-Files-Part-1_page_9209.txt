12 March 1953

CSP

G/P/OP!

To: ROBERT H. HEARDLEY

As lunch today Thomson said that HEARDLEY has finished his part of the work on the photo-book on the career of Stalin, he has completed the second part; his collaborator is "1000% better qualified to collect the photos and do the lay-out.

/ Fo security the collaborator's employees told Thomson "sincerely" finally that while the collaborator's name can appear on the book (along with HEARDLEY's) and it will be revealed that that magazine was the source of the pictures, and they may help "syndicate" it, if it is blown that they are making the photographs available free, the deal is off.

Thomson thinks the book will be a world-beater, but this is not

take till August to have it ready.

I said that we have $40,000 still untouched in EUPURY, which was appropriated for "admiral" photo-books at the discretion of CSPs, and suggested helping Swan out with some of that. I also said to him that money, that he thought CPC was if anything too ready to sit around spending the taxpayer's money that Swan's products should be able to pull their own weight. The book might have to sell for $3.50 or more in the US; in Hong Kong it would be a $1 or $2.

He will pay both HEARDLEY and the collaborator royalties. HEARDLEY'S

will be returned to Swan.

Tie off Signed by LOT JGB II
