DECLARATION OF APPOINTEE

This form is to be completed before entrance on duty. Answer all questions. Any false statement in this declaration will be grounds for cancellation of application or dismissal after appointment. Impersonation is a criminal offense and will be prosecuted accordingly.

INSTRUCTIONS TO APPOINTING OFFICER

The appointing officer before whom the foregoing certificate is made shall determine to be his or her selection that it is an applicant who would be in civil service with the Civil Service Act, applies the Civil Service Rules and Regulations and is of Congress pertaining to appointment.

The form should be filled out in such manner as to show clearly and distinctly, and, if possible, by means of black ink, the following:

(1) Identity of appointments - It is the duty of the appointing officer to guard against imperatives and to ensure that any selection made shall be based upon merit and reasonableness so far that the appointment is in the best interest of the agency to which he belongs and the interest of the applicant. If the appointment were made to an unqualified candidate, the signature on the form should be reviewed with the object in view of the destination sheet, which was agreed to in the Commission room.
