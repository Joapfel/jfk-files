MEMORANDUM FOR: The Record

SUBJECT : Retirement Annuity - Mr. Grayston Lynch

REFERENCE : Memo for the record dated 2 November; same subject

The attached information updates the referenced data and adds additional estimates for Mr. Lynch's retirement annuity under CS Disability Retirement, CIARDS Involuntary Retirement and CIARDS Disability Retirement. These estimates are based on an unconfirmed amount of military service and assume that Mr. Lynch will be granted sick leave credit when his retirement becomes effective on 30 April 1971. Firm estimates will be provided when Mr. Lynch's military service is confirmed. The projected 4.2% Cost-of-Living Increase which MAY become effective 1 June 1971 is not included.
