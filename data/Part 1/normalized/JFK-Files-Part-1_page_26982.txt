I. DURING THE AFTERNOON OF 21 JANUARY 1974 WE RECEIVED A TELEPHONE CALL FROM ALBELL TO ALLIES, SSN 177650, FROM FLORIDA, (A CONTACT OF THE MIAMI FIELD OFFICE) WHO ADVISED US THAT AN ACCOMMODANCE OF HIS IN ATLANTA, GEORGIA, STEVEN VAN CLEAVE, WANTED TO SPEAK WITH US IN CONNECTION WITH INTELLIGENCE MATTERS CONCERNING LATIN AMERICA. WE TOLD ALLIED THAT VAN CLEAVE SHOULD CONTACT OUR OFFICE IN ATLANTA; NON-VERS, ALLIES SAID THAT VAN CLEAVE DIDN'T WANT TO DEAL WITH "RECRUITERS", PATHER HE WANTED TO SPEAK WITH A CIA OFFICER "IN THE BUSINESS OF INTELLIGENCE COLLECTION". WE TOLD ALLIED THAT OUR ATLANTA OFFICE HAS THE SAME STATUTE AS THE MIAMI FIELD OFFICE, AND THAT WE SHOULD URGED VAN CLEAVE TO CONTACT OUR REPRESENTATIVE IN ATLANTA. ALLIED WOULDN'T ACCEPT THIS RESPONSE ON OUR PART AND INSISTED THAT WE AT LEAST HEAR WHAT VAN CLEAVE HAD TO SAY. FINALLY, WE AGREED TO ACCEPT A CALL FROM VAN CLEAVE, SO THAT WE COULD TELL YOU TO CONTACT OUR ATLANTA REPRESENTATIVE.

11-29-74
