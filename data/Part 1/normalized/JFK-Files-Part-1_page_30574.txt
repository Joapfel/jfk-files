MEMORANDUM

Subject: Promotion of Newton Scott Miller

To: MR. AMOS D. MOSKOP, Jr. Chief of Mission

1. It is requested that papers be initiated for the promotion of Newton Scott Miller to a CAF-7. Mr. Miller is at present a CAF-5 and has been in grade approximately one year. He arrived in China 3 January 1947.

2. Mr. Miller is at present performing the duties of a junior intelligence investigator at Mukden.

Robert A. Koke Chief, SI
