Central Intelligence Agency, 2630 E. Government, N.W. Washington, D.C.

Gentlemen:

I. In accordance with the policy of this agency, it is understood and agreed by me that I shall be required to serve a minimum term of two years from the date of my arrival at my overseas post of duty, unless terminated by the Government for its convenience. If the agreement is terminated at my request in less than forty-four months, the remaining term shall be extended.

(a) If I reside in less than twelve months from the date of my arrival at my overseas post of duty, all reimbursements (CIA for all travel expenses involved in the transportation of myself, my immediate family, household goods, and personal effects and equipment) to the foreign station, and pay all such expenses for return to the United States.

(b) If I reason between the twelfth and twenty-fourth month from the date of my arrival at my overseas post of duty, I shall pay all expenses for the travel and transportation of myself, my immediate family, household goods, and personal effects and equipment to the United States.

2. Part (a) so far shall not apply to employees who have served in a departmental position with CIA or who have served an overseas tour of duty with CIA, and in each case part (b) only shall apply, attended to read: "If I desire to terminate or return to the United States prior to the expiration of twenty-four months from the date of my arrival at my overseas post of duty, I shall pay all expenses for the travel and transportation of myself, my immediate family, household goods, and personal effects to the United States."

David E. Murphy

Kilpatrick
