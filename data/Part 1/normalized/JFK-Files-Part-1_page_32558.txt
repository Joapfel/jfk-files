Dear Mr. Stuart:

I hereby tender my resignation from the Foreign Service Staff Corps to accept an appointment as Foreign Service Reserve Officer, Class FSR-5. This resignation is tendered pursuant to the conditions contained in your letter of May 27, 1959, in which it is stated that my resignation from the Foreign Service Staff Corps will be effected without a break in service.

Wallace W. Stuart, Esquire Chief, Personnel Operations Division Department of State Washington, 25, D. C.
