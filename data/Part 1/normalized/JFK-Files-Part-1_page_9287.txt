After all, words alone can paint pictures, and evoke emotions, and as your basic aim is to evoke an emotional response, there's no need to cramp your style with a self-imposed restriction banning the use of words. I should think the test would be: If the reader wants to stop reading it in the middle, the caption, however true, is bad; if you've held his interest to a pitch where he really wants to read or, and is painting pictures in his own mind as he reads, then it's OK.
