The Soviet -Mexico -

Soviet Commercial Activities:

During the past year reports submitted from screening the traffic of the two established Soviet Commercial Offices in Mexico have provided a great amount of detail concerning Soviet efforts to sell in the Latin American market, both heavy industrial merchandise and movies and literature. Knowledge of their methods of transhipping and attempts at concealment of point of origin of products has been gleaned from this take. As a result of this knowledge, reports on the firms with whom they are doing business have been procured and provided for background information. Following are a list of the reports submitted as a result of intelligence procured from this source:

IBM-3085, Soviet Commercial Activities in Mexico IBM-3165, Soviet Commercial Activities in Mexico IBM-3286 - Soviet Commercial Activities in Mexico, December 1957 IBM-3253 - Soviet Commercial Contracts in Mexico - Michoacán del Pacífico, S. de R.L. IBM-3264 - Soviet Commercial Contracts in Mexico - Servicio Agrícola, S.A. IBM-3139 - Aniline and Products Chemicals S. de R.L. IBM-3404 - Imports into Mexico from Soviet Union IBM-1-9519 - American Cyanide Corp., Dalton Chemical Division

Soviet Diplomatic Activities:

This project has provided coverage and details of some of the diplomatic activities of the Soviets in Mexico. It has served to show the relationships maintained, culturally, socially and diplomatically, between the Soviets and the members of the Satellite establishments in Mexico.

IBM-3215, Soviet Attendance at Yugoslav Reception IBM-3351 - Reception at Soviet Embassy. IBM-2992 - Diplomatic Activities of Soviet Embassy, Mexico City IBM-2649 - Diplomatic Activities of Soviet Embassy, Mexico City
