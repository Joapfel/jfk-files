10 June 1965

In compliance with FR-20-5 para. b(1), the writer hereby recites his intent to marry Miss Virginia Lobo, a Cuban citizen, with the understanding that the Organization may not permit me to remain employed after marriage. Therefore this letter can be considered as a resignation notice, said resignation to become effective not later than 45 days after the date of marriage, if permission is not obtained and the marriage occurs.

Carl J. Williamson

SAR J. Williamson
