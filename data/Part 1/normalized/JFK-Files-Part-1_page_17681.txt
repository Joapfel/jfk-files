9 July 1957

MEMORANDUM FOR: WH/3 Mexico SUBJECT: Project LIFMAT REFERENCE: Memorandum for Chief, EE/Czechoslovakia from Chief, VI, subject as above, dated 3 July 1957

The LIVEST phase of Project LIFMAT continues to provide considerable biographic data on the members of the Czech Legion in Mexico City, which EE/Czechoslovakia finds most useful in the evaluation of these individuals as possible targets for recruitment, either in Mexico City or upon their assignment to another overseas post. For this reason EE/Czechoslovakia is interested in seeing Project LIFMAT renewed.

PHILIP DIRKES AC/II-C
