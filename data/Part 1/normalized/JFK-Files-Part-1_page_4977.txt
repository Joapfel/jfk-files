SUBJECT: COMMENTS OF A CUBAN EMBASSY OFFICIAL IN CARACAS ON RECENT ACTS OF TERRORISM AGAINST THE CURANS

ACQ : VENEZUELA, CARACAS (11 OCTOBER 1976) FIELD NO. HVC-7513

SOURCE : A FOREIGN DIPLOMAT WITH A WIDE VARIETY OF CONTACTS, HIS RELIABILITY HAS NOT BEEN ESTABLISHED.

1.
