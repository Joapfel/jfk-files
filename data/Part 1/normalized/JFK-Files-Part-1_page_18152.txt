SUBJECT LIEMRACE/LIEMPTY - Transfer of Responsibility for the LIEBTMAP Vehicle

References : MEXICO CITY-2600 MEXICO CITY-2694

1. In July 1967 the responsibility for the LIEMPTY Project passed from the Station Support Section to the REETOP Section, where it more reasonably belongs. However, the LIEBTMAP segment of the LIEMPTY Project has little to do with REETOP activities and more rightly belongs in the Support Section. The Station, therefore, decided to transfer the LIEBTMAP vehicle, LIEMPTY-10 and LIEMPTY-11 to the LIEMRACE Project with LIEMRACE-3 to be the Principal Agent. This decision was based on both security and operational efficiency considerations. The formal change of command was made on 10 August 1967 in a meeting between GENEDES, LIEMPTY-10 and LIEMRACE-3.

2. Certain members of the LIEMRACE surveillance team were already knowledgeable of the existence of the LIEBTMAP vehicle and its operations from past operations and also from the old LIPSTICK Project. Conversely, LIEMPTY-10 was knowledgeable of the existence of the surveillance team and knew some of the members of the team (LIEBTIMATE-4 is the stepson of LIEMPTY-10). By placing the vehicle under the command of LIEMRACE-3, there was no breakdown of compartmentation.
