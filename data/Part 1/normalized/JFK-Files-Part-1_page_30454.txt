26 MAR 1975

MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Central Intelligence FROM : Deputy Director for Operations SUBJECT : Consultant Status for - James Angleton Raymond G. Rocca William J. Hood Newton S. Miler

REFERENCE : HR 20-52 and IIIB-20-8

Action Requested:

This memorandum requests the Director to approve the use of Messrs Angleton, Rocca, Hood and Miler as Agency Consultants for the period 1 April - 30 September 1975.

Background:

All four officers retired under the CIARDS system 31 December 1974 and are currently contract employees, contracts to expire 30 March 1975.

It is proposed that Subjects' services be retained under consultant status on an in-termittent, as-needed basis as determined by Acting Chief, CI Operations, Mr. George T. Kalaris. In view of the long and unique experience of these officers in counter-intelligence, not duplicated in any other personnel, the new Acting Chief, CI Operations will want to have available the benefit of their advice and counsel during the next few months.

CONFIDENTIAL.
