Monroe N. Roidemann took over the handling of LIROCKET-I from Keith B. Lavenderis on 28 July 1967. There has been no change in the objectives of the operation, and meetings with LIROCKET-I have continued to be devoted largely to debriefing him on his sessions with the Soviets. Forwarded herewith is Roidemann's progress report for the period 1 August through 8 December 1967.
