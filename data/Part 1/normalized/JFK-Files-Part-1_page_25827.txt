Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO : Chief, W-1 Division Attention : Mr. Lawrence B. Sule, WH/III/Mexico Room 211, Barton Hall FROM : Deputy Director of Security (Investigations and Support)

SUBJECT: COVERT SITE Apartment D-22 The President Madison Presidential Gardens Alexandria, Virginia #122877

DATE: 8 AUG 1967

1. Reference is made to your request (Requisition No:57-51) for safehouse facilities. Further reference is made to your subsequent request that a survey be scheduled of the Subject covert site and that you be advised of the results thereof.

2. It is the understanding of this office that the Subject covert site was utilized for the training and housing of an agent of interest to your office in connection with Project LIPSTICK. It is further understood that motion pictures would be used as part of the training program at the site. The Subject apartment was leased in the name of Rufus KING, cleared attorney.

3. This confirms previous verbal advice that a security survey was conducted at the Subject premises with favorable results. You were previously advised of security approval for the use of the Subject premises for the above purposes.

4. In view of the fact that you have completed your use of the Subject covert site, copies of the report of the survey of the site are being retained in this office. Safehouse Branch and the Central Cover Branch are being advised of the confirmation of the security approval for the use of the site by copies of this memorandum.

FOR THE DIRECTOR OF SECURITY:

cc: Chief, Safehouse Branch Central Cover Branch
