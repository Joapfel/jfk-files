Office of Chief, Intelligence

Report No: NIX-850

Office of Chief, Intelligence

Subject: AFFIDAV OF CONT. MINISTR. IN

Classification: TOP SECRET (UFOREIGN ACTIVITY)

Reference:

Approximately four days ago, one of the U. S. Agencies in Kunming received a copy of a document obtained by it from a Communist party source. It was the first time, as far as we could learn, that this paper had appeared. Nothing is known about where it is printed or how it is distributed.

The month is marked but no date. Although it arrives quite regularly. During this past ten days three different editions have been picked up. These editions have been taken to the Intelligence Division of the OI and there translated.

UNCLASSIFIED LAD: This office is making every endeavor, in cooperation with the Office of War Information in Kunming, to locate the source of this paper and also its backers. Further information will be reported as soon as had.

Evaluation: Distribution: 2-Washington (1 Copy of translations attached) 1-Det. 202 1-New Delhi 1-Kunming 1-P-MEX

Approved by: BH/1015 Officer in Charge
