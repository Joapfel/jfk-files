RECOMMENDS AWARD OF

UNCLASSIFIED CITATION Mr. Earl J. Williamson is hereby awarded the Certificate of Distinction in recognition of his sustained superior performance throughout his Agency career. Since 1952 he has served in a variety of important positions in Headquarters and overseas in which the superior quality of his performance was sustained by his skillful leadership and dedication. In each assignment he has shown unwerving dedication to duty, good judgment and the ability to respond quickly in demanding situations. Mr. Williamson's overall contributions to the mission of the Agency reflect credit on him and the Federal Service.
