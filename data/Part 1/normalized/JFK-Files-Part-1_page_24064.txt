Općina Bolaica Zamenji Odjel (Firale) Spre Yugoslavia 13 August 1968.

Dear Mr. Randall,

unexpectedly. I found myself in the hospital in Split, Yugoslavia, on the night of August, confined to the isolation ward with a fairly rough case of infectious hepatitis, undoubtedly picked up in East Africa. (They told me the incubation period is about 3 weeks.) In any case, I am hoping to be released from here in about 10 or 15 days at the most. The treatment is good and seems thorough. My biggest problem was originally linguistic. I studied abroad with the doctors, who knew no English, in German or Italian, but they know - beside of Sarbo-Germanian - has increased steadily with the injections, blood testing and intravenous injections of glucosealdehyde solution, etc.

The reason behind this note is, though, gone, to inform the first of my condition. I hope to return to London - without many, if any, days on the boat - as planned, but I can't tell you that I would restrict my activities physically for another week.
