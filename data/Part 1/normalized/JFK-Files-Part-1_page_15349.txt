Jun 10th 1965

Col. J. oh Anderson Chief, Air Operations Louisville Camp.

Dear Jack:

Many times attached a copy of my letter. The job was done April 20th '65. The original and other copies of W. E.-Coy's and all other orders will be received by Order for 1965. I will see that the work is done in a way that the camp will be able to continue to do so in the future. The above order means one camp connection for U.S. Air men. We must make this one our standard. I am sure you will success with it. Good luck. -Jimmy

POOR QUALITY DOCUMENT

c: W. H. Ball J. G.
