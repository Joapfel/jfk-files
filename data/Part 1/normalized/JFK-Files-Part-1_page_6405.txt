FROM Chief of Station, Paris

DATE 5 February 1953

SUBJECT GENERAL SPECIFIC Frits (aka Fred) SNEEI

References: State Dept Control Cable 1437, 11 Nov 52, from Boun

1. On 11 June 52, the Press Attaché of the Paris Embassy wrote us the following memorandum:

According to French records, Fred SNEDII was born May 31, 1963 in Berlin. He was trained as a lawyer, left Germany in 1931, and appeared in France in 1933. In 1939, he emigrated to the United States. Some time in 1940, he was immobilized in Morocco. He fled to Switzerland in 1942, and returned to Paris in May 1945. Since that time, he has worked for "Ole Tekt" in Zurich, "Libera Stampa," a Socialist daily in Milan, and other papers. In 1949, he went to work for "Ole Kolt," the British-controlled newspaper in Hamburg. In February 1951, he was replaced on that paper by a German. At the present time, his works for "Radio Sarrebuchska," as well as for "Der Bund." He was naturalized French in 1947.

Recently, he was cleared by SPA to see everything from the Pyrenees to the Elbs. He did so and made a report on the Atlantic Forests. His articles were considered OK by the French, and even were translated in the press review bulletin, "Documentatie."

Some time after the articles had appeared, however, a French official expressed establishment that SNEDII had been given such wide accreditation. Though he had no proof to offer, this same official made the statement that SNEDII was known to the French as an agent of the Comintern. He gave no further derogatory information.

2. To search our files, found no previous record of Subject, and informed the Press Attaché accordingly.
