MEMORANDUM FOR: Chairman, Information Review Committee (J) ATTENTION : Mr. R. G. Pean, Assistant for Information (J) SUBJECT : TOIA Appeal - Mr. Carl Oglesby (F 75-1161) (U) REFERENCES : A. Original Request dated 11 April 1975 (U) B. IPS Response dated 19 February 1976 (U) C. Appeal Letter dated 24 February 1976 (U)

(U) Summary of Recommendations:

Release additional information from six DDO documents initially released in segregated versions.

Release portions of eight DDO documents initially denied in toto.

Sustain the denial in toto of 13 DDO documents.

Release in toto nine news items and seven DDO documents discovered during the appeals review.

Deny in toto 15 DDO documents discovered during the appeals review.

Release segregated versions of two DDO documents discovered during the appeals review.

(U) Background:

Mr. Carl Oglesby on 11 April 1975 requested "any material or files the CIA may have collected and kent on any activities." The original DDO search surfaced 27 DDO documents, 29 FBI files, one FBI cable and three other-agency documents. IPS responded on 19 February 1976.
