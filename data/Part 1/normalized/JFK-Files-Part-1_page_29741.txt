JACK W. WOOD Brig., Gen., USAF Air Atache

(2) TO: Master Sergeant Myroskey A. Maksymiee Office of the Army Atache American Embassy London, England

1. The Army Atache has informed me of the outstanding manner in which you have performed your duties and has commended your services to me as deserving special recognition. Therefore, as you complete this tour of duty in the Army Atache System I wish to express to you my appreciation for the commendable manner in which you performed your duties.

2. Since your assignment to the London office in August 1950, you have performed all assigned duties in a superior manner. The nature of your duties often required that you work on many occasions at night and on normal non-working days, which you cheerfully did with utmost willingness. This continued demonstration of loyalty to duty reflects creditably upon yourself and the Atache System.
