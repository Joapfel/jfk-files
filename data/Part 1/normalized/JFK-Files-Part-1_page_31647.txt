1 July 1953

MEMORANDUM FOR: DIRECTOR OF CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE SUBJECT: Comments on Arthur Page's letter of 29 June 1953

1. I think a budget presentation before a mixed audience of PRC members and directors or members of the Executive Committee of NCFC plus supposedly members of the NCFC staff would be extremely confusing, whether it were held here or in New York. Not all the members of the PRC have detailed knowledge of NCFC operations and most of the members of the Executive Committee do not have such knowledge. I see a meeting such as Arthur Page suggests going about as follows:

IO Division presents the budget with cuts which we plan to recommend.

Members of the Executive Committee of NCFC oppose the cuts.

PRC is placed in a position of turning down its own staff before the assembled outsiders with whom the staff is working.

2. I should suggest instead that we follow the following procedure:

IO Division presents the budget with the suggested cuts in same.

PRC considers these cuts carefully.

PRC calls in expert members of the staff of NCFC, i.e. Spencer Phenix, Bob Lang, Bernie Yarrow etc., if it desires to do so, and questions them on any matter within their respective responsibilities and in particular about the suggested cuts.

The experts leave the room.

PRC makes up its mind whether to accept the cut proposed or to make bigger cuts, or to add to the budget, as it may desire to do.
