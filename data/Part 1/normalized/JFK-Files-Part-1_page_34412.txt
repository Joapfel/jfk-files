Attacked HIB Penetration of Bell Telephone Laboratories - M-10612

1. Attached are two memoranda, dated 26 June 1963, from our New York Field Office concerning apparent HIB interest in Manhattan R. Schroeder and Donald E. Thomas, Bell Telephone Laboratories, Murray Hill, New Jersey. We believe this information should be passed to the FHI immediately. We would appreciate being advised the date this information is forwarded to the Department.

2. For your information, permission to brief Dr. Schroeder prior to his trip to the USMI was requested by our New York Office but refused by FI Staff. In view of his employment, however, and previous CD/CO contacts with him, FI recommended he be provided with a strong defensive and realities briefing.

K. M. ASBECRAFT
