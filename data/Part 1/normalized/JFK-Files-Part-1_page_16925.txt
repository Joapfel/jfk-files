LICALLA

5. During this period LIEMPTY-9, operator of the LICALLA basehouse, provided 1112 frames of operational photographs covering the patio and garden area of FJSTEAL. All photographs provided by LIEMPTY-9 were accompanied by fixed-surveillance reports, sighting reports and special notes. LIEMPTY-9 is a perceptive individual and his special notes and comments are considered to be of great value by the interested section in the Station. LICALLA coverage was suspended during the period 19 through 25 March while LIEMPTY-9 was taking a well-deserved vacation. The cover of the LICALLA basehouse appears to remain good, with no undue attention devoted to it by FJSTEAL personnel.

LILYRIC

6. This basehouse, which is staffed by LIEMPTY-13, -14 and -27, produced 2553 frames of operational photographs during the review period. LIEMPTY-13 is employed full-time and most of the coverage is provided by LIEMPTY-14, and -27, although during this period, LIEMPTY-14 carried the largest share of the workload. In March the hours in which LIEMPTY-27, a medical student, attended school were changed from the afternoon to the morning. This schedule change will remain in effect until the end of June 1967. LIEMPTY-14 does not have the mental composure to remain on the job for the hours needed to provide full-time coverage and her homekeeping duties often interfered with her working hours. In order to alleviate this problem, the working hours of LIEMPTY-6/LIMITED were increased and those of LILYRIC/LIEMPTY-14 decreased. No serious loss of coverage resulted from this temporary change in working hours. The salary of LIEMPTY-27 was cut in half for the period of time in which he will not be providing continual assistance to the Project.

7. LILYRIC remains as the main source of information on WOLADY and other foreign visitors to FJSTEAL.
