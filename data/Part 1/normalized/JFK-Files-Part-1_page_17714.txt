SFCPE T 132130Z CITE MEXICO CITY-99 DIRECTOR FEDCOAT

1. LIFFAT REPORTS THAT MAN GIVING HIS NAME AS DON LOWPY (REDACTED) IS CITING AMERICAN, WAS IN CONTACT WITH SOVIET PRESS ATTACHE LEONOV-221-132139 ON 9 JAN AND ALSO WITH CURAN CULTURAL ATTACHE RICARDO ESCARTIN FERNANDEZ SAME DAY.

LOWPY SAID HE HAD NEWSPAPER AND MAGAZINE EXPERIENCE AND HAD ALSO BEEN RADIO AND TELEVISION ANNOUNCER. ESCARTIN FERNANDEZ HAD AGREED TO SEND HIS LETTER AND WRITING SAMPLES TO CURA IN NEXT POUCH AND TOLD HIM CHANCES WERE MAYBE TWENTY PERCENT, EVIDENTLY OF HIS GETTING A JOB THERE. LOWPY HAS TO WAIT AROUND MEXICO FOR TWO WEEKS, APPARENTLY UNTIL HE HEARS FROM CURA.

2. LEONOV WAS NON-COMMITAL BUT SAID HE WOULD INTRODUCE LOWPY TO SOVIET CONSUL ON 10 JAN. CONVERSATION INDICATES THAT LOWPY HAD FIRST ASKED LEONOV ABOUT A JOB AND LEONOV HAD SUGGESTED HE TRY THE CURAN EMBASSY.

3. REQUEST CABLED HEADQUARTERS TRACES. INFORMATION LOCAL LINFECO.
