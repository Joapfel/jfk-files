RECOMMENDATIONS FOR: Secretary, G.C.S.O.

SUMMARY : Recommendation for Promotion to Grade 60-15 - Jacques G. MARMION

REMARKS : Any recommendation dated 8 October 1973

1. The 8th Division and the Chief of Staff, Tokyo (6/7/73, attached) recommend the promotion of James S. Richardson to 60-15.

2. Dr. Richardson was last promoted 16 January 1973. On 17 February 1973 he arrived in Tokyo to serve as the senior 8th officer in the 78 field area, his previous assignment. In this position he has continued to display excellence of leadership, initiative, imagination and thoroughness which resulted in his rapid rise in the Agency.

3. The very facts of Dr. Richardson's youth in relation to his responsibility and the fact that promotion to his present level has been rapid, second the Division and the Chief of Staff, Tokyo to hold mail this time : recommendation for his promotion which was submitted in June 1973. Best careful consideration has been given so that not only is Dr. Richardson deserving of the recommended promotion, but his elevation to the higher grade will be in the best interest of the force, service and man суави Division requirements for a senior officer at the 60-15 level.
