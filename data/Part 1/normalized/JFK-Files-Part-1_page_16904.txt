October 22, 1969

Contact Report

Subject: Allerande Data/Place of Meet: Oct 22 at the CH at 0945 hrs.

1. Prearranged routine meet and 3 was late. In fact G requested that our meets be scheduled for 0940 hrs, because the traffic conditions in the area he must pass coming to meets are bad and are still getting worse. He has no way of starting the trip to the meet before 0900-0905 hrs because he has an English class from 0800 - 0900 hrs. Next Meet: Oct 24 at the Delicatessen at 0940 hrs.

2. G gave me his LILYRIC and the LICALA contact reports with corresponding attachments. He also brought some prints.

3. G handed in an accounting for his RF.

4. Separately he handed in a contact report on his meet with Jose CONZALEZ. Returned with the report is instruction material which PELLEMENT prepared for G. He discussed briefly the instruction which was self-explanatory.

5. I was obliged to put some pressure on concerning the WUITENAU report which he did not bring as he promised. In fact he has done some work on it, but did not get all the info he believed he could get.

6. I spoke to him again about VARGAS care ( just as I did 2-3 weeks ago) and this time G was rather convinced that he would not be able to work her way to me smoothly and he advocated a direct (cold) approach. I told him to put on paper what he did thus far concerning VARGAS and what were his suggestions. Will the trick work, I do not know.

6. I told him to ascertain with Base Houses whether the personnel had plans for Khan leave including himself and submitted submit it for approval. I requested a list of presents including booze and cost. At the end I gave him the news that there will be no turkeys this year.

BHFernald
