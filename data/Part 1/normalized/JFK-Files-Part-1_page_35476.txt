5. On 3 May (I think) Mr. Jones called to see if we could help him with his taxes. We said yes, and he said how much he owed. He said that he owed $100. We told him that that was too much, and that he should pay us half. He said that he could only pay half. We told him that we could take it all at once, and then he said no. So we told him that if he did not agree to pay us half, that we should have to call a judge. But he said that he would not let us have a judge. He said that he would rather have a Christian judge than a Jewish one. And so we did.

Then finally, after a lot of negotiations, he told them that he would take us to court. (He said so.) And so he did. The判 was very kind to them.
