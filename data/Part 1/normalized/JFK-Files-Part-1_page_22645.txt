I. PERSONAL

NOEMAYR was born on 20 June 1922 in De Kalb, Illinois. He had five years experience in General Air Force intelligence work. He was discharged from the Air Force on 18 February 1949. He attended the University of Costa Rica for three yearsthan transferred to the University of Habana. NOEMAYR speaks fluent Spanish.

He was married to Argentina CORDERO of San Jose, Costa Rica, on 17 December 1945. His wife hopes to obtain her U. S. citizenship when NOEMAYR is transferred PCS to the United States. They have two children.
