Recommendations:

1. CI/OA recommends approval on condition the agent Harold R. HUEMY receives, from the Office of Security, a Cover Security Approval for his new assignment in Mexico City and the results of his LCPLUTTER are satisfactorily resolved. Also, on condition that should the Office of Security request a clearance and LCPLUTTER on this agent's wife, a clearance is issued and LCPLUTTER results are satisfactorily resolved.

2. A new request for CSA for Subject's use in Mexico City was submitted to the Office of Security on 24 April 1958. When the Office of Security issued Subject's last CSA on 3 July 1957, they recommended that Subject be afforded a polygraph as soon as possible. Subject was given an LCPLUTTER a few days ago and although Subject ran clean the polygraph operator began getting some reaction, or hits, regarding the Subject's wife. There is some derogatory information in the Office of Security on what is believed to be the Subject's wife and sister-in-law.

3. The Office of Security has not reached a final decision regarding CSA and LCPLUTTER for Subject's wife. They have stated, however, that unless the wife can be cleared and LCPLUTTER results are clean, it is doubtful whether a new clearance would be issued for the career agent.
