CIVILIAN OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT DEPARTMENT FOR HUMAN CAPITAL DEVELOPMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF MEXICO THE REVOLUTION

Date: 13 November 1968 Title: General Order Issued By: (In words): President of the Republic of Mexico, by the authority of the Executive Branch. To be used as part of an addendum to the Constitution and laws of the country and its states.

Text: May 24, 1968. President of the Republic of Mexico, by the authority of the Executive Branch, issues this order, which may not be photocopied or reproduced in any form without prior written permission from the Executive Branch. Any person whophotocopies or reproduces this document is guilty of a criminal offense.
