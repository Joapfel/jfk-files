SUMMARY OF INFORMATION:

At 1520 hours, Mr. James C. Wray called identifying himself as a Patent Attorney and requested someone contact him concerning Mr. Anthony Cuesta. Mr. Cuesta, according to Mr. Wray, filed a patent application, $472 763, on 19 July 1965, for a high speed boat with a flexible hull. Mr. Wray said that after filing, Mr. Cuesta left the United States and is now in a Cuban prison. Mr. Wray stated the reason he called is that he wants the Agency to either pay $145.00 for the patent issue fee as the deadline is 10 April 1968, or talk to the Patent Office. Advised Mr. Wray that a report would be made of his call.

ACTION TAKEN:

A check in SR & CD was negative re Mr. Wray but two files, 242 095 and 530 946, were found on Mr. Cuesta.
