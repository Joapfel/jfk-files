The American editor is Charles GIBSON, who was born in California in 1931, and was until September 1962, the Executive Secretary of an organization styled "The Volar Play for Cuba Committee in the United States." GIBSON is said to have been previously employed by the CIA now in New York, who dismissed him for his pro-Cuban activities. He is also said to have worked for the F.L.N. in Paris and to have had a Fellowship in African Studies at Columbia University Graduate School at one time.

2. JAGUAR would appreciate any additional information that Headquarters could provide about this man's background and activities.
