3 June 1971

MEMORANDUM FOR : Chief of Station, WH/Miami

FROM : MFO

ATTENTION : Security Officer

SUBJECT : Ralph NOSEDA

1. Reference your memorandum OS 189-71.

2. Pursuant to your request this office contacted a senior official of the Marriott Corporation in Washington, D. C., who approved the travel to Europe of a Miami based Marriott employee who is affiliated with WII.

3. The Marriott official, Mr. James McALLISTER, contacted his Miami representative, Mr. Ralph NOSEDA, and advised NOSEDA that your asset was free to travel to Spain on short notice.

4. NOSEDA was advised by McALLISTER that this was a matter of interest to the Department of Defense. NOSEDA, to the best of our knowledge, has not been approached by anyone else in Miami or Washington on this matter since the initial call from McALLISTER in March.

REUBEN O. HARVELL
