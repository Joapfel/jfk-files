REP: CARLIS 20841 dated 19 May 75

The following information on WKSCARLE-3 was contained in the above referenced telepouch:

CONFRONTED WITH EVIDENCE THAT INFALL PERSONNEL WERE IN ACTIVITY FOLLOWED, WTANGO-1 SAID HE DID NOT KNOW WHO WOULD BE CONDUCTING THE SURVEILLANCE BUT SUSPECTED IT MIGHT BE THE PRIVATE SECURITY SERVICE USING OPERATED BY WKSCARLE-3. HE COULD OTHERWISE NOT IVS UP ALLEGED THAT WKSCARLE-3 WAS RECEIVING HUGE SUMS OF MONEY FROM AN UNKNOWN SOURCE O F FINANCE AN ELECTRONIC SURVEILLANCE OPERATION ON THE HULUMAN EMBASSY, SURVEIL CURAY EMBASSY PERSONNEL AND THAT WKSCARLE-3 WAS ALSO ACQUIRING DATA ON INDIAN EMBASSY PERSONNEL. (IN FEBRUARY 1976 STATION ASKED WKSCARLE-3 FOR ALL WKSCARLE'S MATERIAL ON INDIAN EMBASSY. RESULTING "STUD" PREPARED BY WKSCARLE-3 WAS PASSED UNILALLY TO STATION AND OFFICIALLY TO KNOH, WE SUSPECT THAT EXTENT WKSCARLE-3 ACQUISITION OF DATA ON INDIANS LIMITED TO HIS POSSESSION OF HIS STUD.) WTANGO-1 WAS UNABLE TO GET AN LOGICAL EXPLANATION FOR SUCH A VIDE RANGE OF INTERESTS ON THE PART OF WKSCARLE-3 SPONSOR. HE DID SEEM SINCERELY IN HIS DENIAL THAT WKNOLE-24 WAS RUNNING AN SURVEILLANCE ON INFALL PERSONNEL, AND ON 12 MAY CONTACTED THE STATION TO ADVISE THAT HE HAD REPORTED THE SURVEILLANCE TO HIS PRESIDENT WHO HAD ORDERED AN IMMEDIATE INVESTIGATION. PROOF OF GOV INTERESTS IN WKSCARLE-3 ACTIVITIES WAS OBTAINED BY THE STATION CO-INCIDENTALLY WITH THESE DEVELOPMENTS; AMONG THE WKRREAK CAPES ORIVED FROM KNOH DURING THE LAST WEEK OF APRIL WAS ONE MISLARKED CAPS WHICH THE STATION WAS ABLE TO IDENTIFY AS THE PRODUCT OF A TRAP ON WKSCARLE-3'S INVESTIGATIVE SERVICE.
