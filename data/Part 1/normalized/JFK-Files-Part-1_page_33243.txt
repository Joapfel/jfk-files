9 May 1968

MENORANDUM FOR: Chief, WHB&F SUBJECT : Request for Extension of Due Date for Accounting of Operational Advance for AMSTALK-1

1. It is requested that the due date for accounting for a $200 operational advance made to AMSTALK-1 be extended from 31 May 1968 to 30 September 1968.

2. The above mentioned advance was made to AMSTALK-1 on 19 April 1968 just prior to his departure for TDY travel to Buenos Aires, Argentina. The advance was made to cover AMSTALK-1's expenses for travel and accommodations.

3. AMSTALK-1 is in Buenos Aires for the purpose of performing an operational task for the Station and is expected to remain there until August 1968. Upon the completion of his task in Buenos Aires, AMSTALK-1 will return to the United States where he will make an accounting for Subject funds.

Robert L. Fambrini WII/4/A
