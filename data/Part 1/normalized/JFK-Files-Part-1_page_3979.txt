MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division SUBJECT: Present Activities and Possible Future Plans of Manuel Artime Buena

Present Activities

For the past six weeks Artime has devoted his time to disbanding his paramilitary establishment in Nicaragua and Costa Rica. He has:

returned most of the arms and equipment provided him by us,

returned the motherhip,

returned nearly all the heavy weapons including mortar, machine guns, and artillery pieces.

The small arms not recovered are sufficient to equip an infantry company.

Future Plans

Artime has attempted to peddle a plan to the Venezuelan, Colombian, and Panamanian governments utilizing the needs of his paramilitary force. Essentially this program is the same for all three countries and involves a
