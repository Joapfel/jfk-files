1. Under reference B photographs of hard copies of Soviet traffic for November 1959 were forwarded to Headquarters. (SOURCE: LIEMIT)

2. On 27 November 1959 the following conversation took place: Anatoliy Alekseyevich PAVLENKOV tells Vladimir KOLOKOLNIKOV that he just spoke with New York. PAVLENKOV says that BEGLOV(ph) called. PAVLENKOV says that Moscow wants to know how many luncheons and dinners there were yesterday. PAVLENKOV says he counted 3 luncheons and one dinner. PAVLENKOV says that a cable should be sent saying that on the 26th the bankers gave a luncheon for MIKOYAN and that MIKOYAN gave a dinner for the officials of the Government. Another thing Moscow wants to know what word was left out in KOLOKOLNIKOV's cable which was about KOLOKOLNIKOV'S account at Poza Rica. In BELTRAN's speech there apparently was one word left out by the operators. KOLOKOLNIKOV will look at the text and see what it is. PAVLENKOV will come over and then they will send the cable. KOLOKOLNIKOV then suggests that they call BEGLOV in New York and give him this information. KOLOKOLNIKOV will do that.

KOLOKOLNIKOV tells WO(woman outside) to write down what he tells her. KOLOKOLNIKOV tells her that yesterday the Ministry of Industry and Trade, SALINAS, and the President of the Banco Exterior, ZEVADA, gave a luncheon for MIKOYAN at the Hotel Ambassador. In the evening A.I. MIKOYAN gave a dinner for officials of the Mexican Government. KOLOKOLNIKOV adds that the speech that ROLDAN(should be ROLDAN instead of BELTRAN) made the words "to show" were left out. KOLOKOLNIKOV tells her to send this to Moscow. KOLOKOLNIKOV will call tomorrow and let WO know when he will return. KOLOKOLNIKOV has to get his visa from the American Embassy." Willard C. Curtis

20 January 1960
