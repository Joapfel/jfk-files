CONTACT REPORT

SUBJECT: BESABER

DATE OF MEETING: 7 March 1967

1. Operational: Met BESABER at his hotel room in Del Paso for breakfast. Meet lasted from 0800-0920. Next meet will be after Easter.

Substance

BEWEEKP: BEWEEK phoned BESABER on Friday, 3 March 1967 to invite himself, his wife and the RZADZINSKI couple down for the day on 5 March. They arrived on 5 March at about 1100, stayed until 1700.

BESABER reported that Mrs. RZADZINSKA is three months pregnant (by her admission) and that he, BESABER, thinks that BEWEEKP is the father. He cited the following as evidence :

(1) The RZADZINSKIs admitted they had been married for over five years, yet this is her first pregnancy.

(2) Mr. RZADZINSKI was totally unattentive to Mrs. R. BESABER and his wife assume that after so many years of waiting for a child, Mr. R. should have shown more concern for and interest in his wife.

(3) Likewise, Mr. R. was totally disinterested in the fact that he is about to become a father.

(4) BEWEEKP, on the other hand, showed concern for Mrs. R. when she went to sleep in the sun. Twice he suggested that Mr. R. wake her up so she would not get burned. He didn't.

(5) Mrs. R. appeared interested in BEWEEKP. When he went swimming Mrs. R followed, proceeded to jump on him and frolic in the water. Mrs. R. paid no attention at all.

(6) Mrs. R. sat next to BEWEEKP at lunch (on purpose), kept looking at BEWEEKP lovingly.

(7) BEWEEKP, possibly embarrassed by all the attention Mrs. R. was showing him, made some very complimentary remarks about his wife.
