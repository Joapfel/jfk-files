26 September 1961

MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Director (Plaus)

SUBJECT: Report on Cuban Operations for period 1-15 September 61

This is the second summary report of activities in Cuban operations, and covers the period 1-15 September 1961. This report will consist of a breakdown of such operations into the principal categories of activity.

A. VI-CI Activity

1. Reporting to Headquarters. There was a total of twenty-eight reports received during the period, and they consisted of the following: Nine from El sources, fifteen from descretions conducted in the Miami area, two via the open mail, and two reports via diplomatic courier. Additionally, eleven CI reports were passed to the FBI, and 1,017 CI name checks were processed for Lass.

Agent Activation and Developments

There was a total of eighteen agents within Cuba at the beginning of the reporting period and twenty-four by the period's close.

Comments: Of the seven agents dispatched legally during the period, one agent was a returuse. These agents should provide increased coverage of anti-Castro groups, activities at the airport and new entrées into the diplomatic community.

Four agents were recruited and two agents were terminated during the period.

In cooperation with the German desk, we have initiated an operation to debrief Cuban sailors who pass through
