4. At the present time, fairly reliable reports have been received that certain officials of the Haitian Army are trying to form a junta to take power in the event Duvalier dies. Some members of this junta are reportedly anxious to overthrow Duvalier, should he recover, but so far have not established a timetable or decided how they intend to carry out the coup. Also, the illness of Duvalier has undoubtedly inspired anti-Duvalier plotting by other groups in Haiti but no reliable information is available on their plans. It is interesting to note that Clement Joseph Charles is not identified as a plotter in any of the reports from internal sources.

5. As was mentioned in the VI/7 memorandum dated 16 July 1968, we do not interpose any objection to DCB maintaining contact with Brun for its own purposes. However, we feel that in the interests of security and also in co-operation with U.S. policy, that Brun should clearly understand that we have no interest in establishing contact with any member of the internal group through his channel and further that the U.S. Government has no interest in becoming involved in any anti-Duvalier activities.

Charles B. Dickens AC/VII/7
