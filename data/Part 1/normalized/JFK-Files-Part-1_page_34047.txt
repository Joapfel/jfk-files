UNITED LIBERAL P.arty

Founder & President: REV. HENRY MUIR

746 Spanish Town Road Kingston 13 P.O. Box 9 Jamaica W.I.

15th Jan. 1967

Dear Sirs,

I wish you a prosperous 1967. Give my respect to all your men for me. I have returned to Jamaica on the 16th Dec. 1966. You have promise to send me those leaflet for me to give them out in Jamaica hear that people can read how wicked Communists is and you have not send them Why? Please what ever you are sending for me don't put your address on them only put the United Liberal Party on it. I don't want them to know where they came from. Please let me have even a million or different copies. We are now getting ready for the election soon. I wish you all success in your undertaking

S|OCD IUGRA - 26040

19-126-26
