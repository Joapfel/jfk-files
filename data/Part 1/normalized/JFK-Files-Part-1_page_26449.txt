(HPA)HAARTNIC COMMENT: VICTOR ECO GALAN, MEXICAN HISPANERICAN, ARRIVED IN PANAMA FROM COSTA RICA ON 20 OCTOBER AND LEFT PANAMA FOR CANACAS-22 OCTOBER 1963. ECO GALAN IS A MARXIST, A WRITER FOR SEGRETE AND POLITICS; MEXICAN MAGAZINES, AND IS THE HEAD OF THE PROPAGANDA COMMITTEE FOR THE NATIONAL LIFERATION MOVEMENT (MLN), A COMMunist-FRONT MOVEMENT SPARELY DISTRUGATED BY NUMBERS OF THE MEXICAN COMMUNIST PARTY. IN OCTOBER 1962 ECO GALAN WENT TO CUBA TO PREPARE A POLITICAL BIOGRAPHY OF FIDEL CASTRO. IN MAY 1963 HE ALREADY WENT TO SINA, RETURNING TO MEXICO-22 JUNE 1963. DURING THIS LATER VISIT HE REPORTedly ENTERED CASTRO AFTER CASTRO'S RETURN FROM THE SOVIET UNION AND ALSO SPENT A FIVE-DAY SEA-GOOING VACATION WITH CASTRO, OF WHICH HE IS SAID TO BE A CLOSE FRIEND. ECO GALAN WAS OFFICIALY PUBLISHED INTERIMLY IN JANUARY 1964. COLLECTOR DEPARTS GUATEMALA.
