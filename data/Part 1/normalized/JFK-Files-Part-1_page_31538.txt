17 DEC 1953

MEMORANDUM FOR, CHIEF, INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS DIVISION SUBJECT: AFE Summary Reports REFERENCE: Memorandum from c/10, dated 17 November 1953, Subject as above.

The appropriate AE Branches and Staffs have read the AFE Summary Report for October 1953, which you forwarded to this Division as an attachment to referenced memorandums. We have found the report well prepared and useful, both for AE Headquarters and for our field personnel working on propaganda projects. We would therefore appreciate your sending this Division two copies of subsequent AFE monthly reports, one of which will be retained by AE, while the other will be sent to our Athens Station.

JOHN H. RICHARDSON Chief, AE
