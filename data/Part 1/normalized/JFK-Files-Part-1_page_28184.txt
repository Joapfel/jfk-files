SUBJECT: Meritorious Unit Citation -- DDO/Privacy and Information Coordinator Group

On 12 February 1976 the Acting Director of Central Intelligence approved award of the Meritorious Unit Citation in recognition of the achievements of the DDO/Privacy and Information Coordinator Group since February 1975. The following employees are cited for their contributions to the Group:
