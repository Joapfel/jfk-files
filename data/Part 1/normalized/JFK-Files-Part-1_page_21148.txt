DISPATCH CLASSIFICATION PROCESSING ACTION

TO Chief, European Division

FROM Chief of Station, Paris (JFM)

SUBJECT Admin/Personnel/Medical Departments of Thomas B. CASASIN

ACTION REQUIRED Staff Agent

REFERENCE: OFPS-10932

CASASIN's dependents took physical examinations at the American Hospital in Paris on 20 February. Since, because of his cover there could be no LMIARP connection, CASASIN's dependents made arrangements for their physicals as private citizens, on the pretense that they required statements that they were in good health in order to obtain insurance. Obviously, forms 88 were not prepared; the attached certificates of good health and freedom from contagious disease are all that is normally given to private individuals in France. We hope they suffice.

DENISE H. ZEHNGRAFF

Attachment - U/S/C via TNP As Stated Above
