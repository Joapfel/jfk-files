UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT Memorandum

TO : Chief, Central Division ATTN : Support Branch FROM : Chief, Marine Signal Office SUBJECT: Plan to Unite the Cuban Exiles

DATE: 5-18-1963 ERA: (S) - 1/60

1. At their request, Luis G. Rodriguez met with Dr. Rafael Sardinas, secretary of the Cuban Gene Planters Association in Havana and with Juan Fernandez. Juan said that a Cuban Senator should be elected language teacher in order to get "the periodico" (of Cuba). He advised that they have been meeting with several personalities of the exile colony in an effort to organize all the exiles into a single group which will represent all the exiles regardless of former affiliations.

2. They feel that after the sorry specifically provided by the Cuban Revolutionary Council, they must avoid getting involved in matters which are in conflict with the policies of the US Government. Therefore, they would like to obtain this project with Mr. John Grimes.
