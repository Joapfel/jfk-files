1. Station Mexico City appreciates Headquarters forwarding of the dial recorder pressure tape for project LIFSAT. While trying to expend the regular tape on hand, Station had experimented with the new fiber-tipped pen as a substitute for the standard ball point pen in the dial recorder. However, either the altitude or the dry climate (or a combination of both) dried out the fiber-tipped pen after short periods of use.

2. Station believes that the pressure tapes will provide a satisfactory solution to this problem which has plagued the Project for some time.
