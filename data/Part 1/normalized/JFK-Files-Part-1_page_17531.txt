PROJECT REVIEW

I. Product of the Project

A. Soviet Embassy.

LIFLAT technical surveillance of the Soviet Embassy and Commercial Office telephone lines was discontinued on 22 June 1960 for security reasons. The withdrawal of LIFLAT from coverage of this target hinged on the 20 June 1960 suggestion by LIENISON to Chief of Station, Mexico City, that the liaison tap operation LIENVOY undertake operations against the Soviet/Satellite targets located in Mexico City. In order not to reveal to LIENISON that a unilateral tap operation was already providing coverage of these targets, it was necessary for the Chief of Station to agree to LIENVOY extending its coverage to include Soviet/ Satellite operations. LIFLAT immediately severed its coverage of both Soviet and Polish phones so as not to jeopardize the continuing unilateral LIFLAT operation and the personnel involved therein. No security breach resulted from the switch from LIFLAT to LIENVOY coverage due largely to the thoroughness and speed of the LIFLAT personnel. LIFLAT security and compartmentation was not harmed to any degree what- ever. (Note: LIFLAT again resumed coverage of the Polish Legion-- now Embassy--when it developed that LIENVOY did not have the ready capacity to provide "take" from both the Czechoslovakian and Polish official installations.)

B. Polish Embassy

During the past project year, LIFLAT resulted the primary source of information on this target and the only successful, continuing penetra- tion of the target, technical or otherwise. The interested Case Officer has described the LIFLAT contribution as "unique" and "invaluable". Examples of the positive intelligence product of the LIFLAT/Polish coverage are:

HUMA-15431, 3 August 1960 (Aleksander SFKIER)

HUM-5133, 22 August 1960 (Promotion of Polish Minister in Mexico to Rank of Ambassador)

HUM-5144, 24 August 1960 (Polish Minister of Shipping to Attend Mexican Independence Celebration)

HUM-5126, 22 September 1960 (Projected Arrival in Mexico of Polish Travel Officials)

HUMA-15696, 28 September 1960 (Jan SUSANZ)
