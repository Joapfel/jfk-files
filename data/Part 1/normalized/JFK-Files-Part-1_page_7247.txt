SUBJECT: Carlos BLANCO Solano (P-9328)

SOURCE: LITEMPO-13

1. Following is Source's report on Subject which was given to COS on 13 December 1968 in response to the attached requirement.

2. "Carlos BLANCO works in the judicial police as a confidant of Carlos CASAMADRID (P-10968). Earlier he worked in the DFS and later worked with Carlos MADRAZO (P-5193) in the PRI. When MADRAZO left the PRI BLANCO continued as his aide. Later he went to work with CASAMADRID. In the DFS he never worked on investigations but was assigned to the security of visiting foreigners. I met him last Saturday. (COMMENT: 10 December, evidently in response to our requirement which Source received on 9 December). He said that he is a friend of (Vladimir) LOGINDV of the Soviet Embassy and he had one of LOGINDV's cards. Attached is BLANCO's card. I will submit a photo later. CASAMADRID's office number if 10-33-62. A Sr. SANTOS of the American Embassy."

3. Source submitted BLANCO's car. On the back is written Atenor Sala # 110-7, Col. Narvate (BLANCO's home address) and the telephone numbers 10-33-62 (CASAMADRID's office) and 28-57-40 (to leave messages). Evidently, BLANCO does not have a home phone.

4. The street, Atenor Sala is not in the 1965 plan of the Federal District.

Paul Dillon
