6 Oct. 1930

In re: How many pictures?

"NY Times" book page says "THE PICTURE STORY OF FRANKLIN DELANO ROOSEVELT" by Gene Schoor, which will be issued Nov. 7 by Frederick Pell, will contain

125 illustrations with 20,000 words of text.

This is evidently a second FMR photo-book, and will be competing with the SLEIM & SCHUSTER (Simon Legree & Schuster) one by Stofka Losens, "The Life of FMR in Photographs and Text".
