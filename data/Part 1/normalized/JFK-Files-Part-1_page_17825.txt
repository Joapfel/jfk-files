FROM Chief of Station, Mexico

SUBJECT Projects LIFMAT and LIMIT

ACTION ORDER See para. 5

REFERENCES: HQ-A-13934, 16 Oct. 1959 HQ-A-13828, 2 Oct. 1959

1. During the recent TDY visit to this Station of David M. FREDLUND(p) of KUTUBE D, the interrelationship between the conversations recorded under Project LIFMAT which bear on Soviet communication and the regular provision of the hard copy of Soviet official diplomatic communications obtained by the LIMIT activity were discussed. The Station indicated that frequently LIFMAT obtained conversations which referred directly to specific messages; by content; by seeking clarification of garbled or missing groups; by officials who are to or have signed the message in question; and on one or two occasions the messages (admittedly not sensitive) were dictated to secretaries over the telephone.

2. Examples of the above conversations were forwarded to Headquarters in reference dispatches. The conversations with FREDLUND resulted in the suggestion that KUTUBE D should regularly receive the LIFMAT material when it relates to Soviet messages. Also that KUTUBE D in forwarding this type of LIFMAT material to ODINCH would record that the drop copies of the Soviet messages are normally forwarded to ODINCH (source LIMIT) and that if there is any value to ODINCH in tying the two sources together for further processing by ODINCH, the Station would cross-reference the forwarding dispatch numbers. This Station would appreciate receiving, at an early date, any Headquarters comments.

3. This Station is willing to continue to exploit LIFMAT material for LAURICLE purposes should this be judged of value by Headquarters. At the present time Willard C. CURTIS is personally handling this phase of LIFMAT reporting.

4. KUTUBE D should note that at the present time the LIMIT source is providing Soviet, Japanese, Czech and Polish drop copies to the Station. The traffic is over one-month old upon receipt and is in Station's hands only for a few hours. The source is capable however of obtaining traffic from all sources filing officially in Mexico City and the Station can do this without increasing the operational risk.

5. The Mexico City Station would appreciate any comments or guidance on either this phase of LIFMAT reporting or of LIMIT which KUTUBE D could give.

Willard C. CURTIS

10 Jan. 1960
