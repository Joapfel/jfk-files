3 May 67

LIEBERT'S account of his first two meetings with LOUVROV. It appears to me that at the second meeting, 26 April, he moved in a lot faster than is customary with the Soviets. For this reason I wonder if possibly the Soviets are deliberately trying to get a look at him independent from LEOVON, who may well have been reporting regularly his meetings with him. No understands he is not to be dangled in front of the Soviets, and I told him that after today's tennis match, he is to make himself unavailable for two or three weeks for tennis (or the movies) and see what kind of initiative L is prepared to take.

This would, however, allow us to plant an American girl at the French Club if we wished and play her into L's hands. Will discuss with Paul when he returns. I agree
