SUBJECT : General: AMMUC/1 Debriefing Report #217 Subject: S/W Communications

In s/w communications, the agent is given a name, separate from his own pseudonym, as an indicator. In the Romeo Operation, the use of the same "JULIO" meant that the letter was for "EDUARDO" that is, Rogelio RODRIGUEZ, who had that as one of his pseudonyms and used it in August 1962 when he was in the MA Section at EGI (Cuban Intelligence service) Headquarters. The Valladares Operation similarly used "GENAKO" to indicate that the letter was for "TEODORO," identity unknown, but a case officer in MA Section in August 1962.
