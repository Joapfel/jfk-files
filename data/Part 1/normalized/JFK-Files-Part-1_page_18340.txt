DD/P - 1-1931-b

SEP 16 1954

MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Director (Intelligence)

SUBJECT: Correlation of DD/1 Personnel

1. For the past several months my office has had an urgent and a continuing need for intelligence about Guatemala -- for intelligence in such variety that at one time or another almost every DD/1 component contributed.

2. Each of them -- offices as well as individuals within the DD/I area -- responded with a highly impressive alacrity and completeness. Their contributions were thorough in their coverage and fast in their preparation.

3. It is difficult to cite the work of particular individuals and components of DD/1, because much inconspicuous but essential labor must never have come to my attention. Raising the chance that such work might unfairly be unrecognized, I nevertheless believe that the work of the following individuals and units deserves special recognition:

John V. Armstrong, Milton H. Brown, Archie C. Bush and David L. Camps, ODI,

Miss Dorothy A. Brandt, William P. Burke, Hunter J. Leake and Lloyd A. Ray, New Orleans field office, CO/C.

William Buchanan, Kansas City field office, CO/C.

Wass Betty Didcoct and David F. Haley, Geography Division, ORR.

Norman E. Greenawalt, Charles D. Stockton, Milton S. Venezky and Robert J. Yoskull, Cartography Division, OHR.

Zygmund Lenchert, Richard V. Stamp and John E. Wilson, Photo Intelligence Division, OHR.

Harry A. Mcrea and David R. McLean, CO/C.

Lawrence Snitsler, New York field office, CO/C.
