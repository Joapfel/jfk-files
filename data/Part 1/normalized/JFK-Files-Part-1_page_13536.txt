SOURCE PERSON (B) WITH GOOD CONNECTIONS AMONG GUATEMALAN EXILES IN MEXICO.

1. VICTOR MANUEL GUTIERREZ, HEAD OF THE PARTIDO GUATEMALTECO DEL TRABAJO (PGT-GUATEMALAN COMMUNIST PARTY) IN MEXICO, SAID ON 24 MARCH THAT THE PGT IN GUATEMALA REGRETS THE WAY IN WHICH THE GUERRILLA FIGHT HAS DEVELOPED THERE. ACCORDING TO THE PGT, THE RESULTS OF THE GUERRILLA FIGHTING HAVE NOT BEEN SUCCESSFUL BECAUSE THE-13 NOVEMBER GROUP (MEMBER OF THE-13 NOVEMBER 1960 ATTEMPT TO OVERTHROW THE GUATEMALAN GOVERNMENT) HEADED BY MARCO ANTONIO YON SOSA, IS NOT IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE POLITICAL CONCEPTS OF THE COMMUNIST STRUGGLE.
