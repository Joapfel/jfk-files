(L-9) asked EKATON DUNIN about the "tapado" and DUNIN answered that at this time, in his opinion, not even the President LOPEZ M., knows who is going to be the candidate c. DUNIN stated that a revolution in Mexico similar to that in Cuba would be very difficult. He attributed this to the fact that the masses in Mexico are not used to have "debida preeminencia politics" required for such a rowdy solution. He said that it will take one or two generations before Mexico is ready for such a revolution,
