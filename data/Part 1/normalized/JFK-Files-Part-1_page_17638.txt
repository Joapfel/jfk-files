2 February 1958

MECHANICAL FOR: F1/3P5 - Projects Branch SUBJECT: 3. Amendment to Project LIFET

1. It is requested that Project LIFET be amended to authorize the payment of $1620 compensation to all five. GABRIEL for the period 1 January 1972 through 30 June 1958 (the expiration date of the project). Funds now available in Project LIFET are sufficient to cover payment to CHARLES.

2. CARRIECEK will serve as a processor of LIFET material. She received Provisional Operational Approval (#624) on 12 December 1957. A contract check list to provide a percent of $1.75 an hour compensation has been prepared for Contract Personal Division.

3. CARRIECK has above average motivation to do the work required of her. Motivation is a prime consideration in work of this type. CARRIECK will listen to LIFET tapes (in English until her Spanish language ability permits work on Spanish tapes). She will type summaries of the material on these tapes. The handling of the tapes and summaries will be between CARRIECK and her father-in-law, LINEWATER ('C-75'). Her cover will be that of housewife.

F. S. WHITING, C/M/III
