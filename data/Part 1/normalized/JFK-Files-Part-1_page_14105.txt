15 April 1968

Mrs. Paula R. Rodriguez 9351 S. W. 178th Street Miami, Florida 33157

Dear Mrs. Rodriguez:

I was deeply moved by your recent letter in which you so eloquently expressed appreciation for help the Agency provided you.

I am most grateful to you for sharing with me your thoughts about your husband's life and his dedication to this Agency. It is very comforting for me to know that we have been a source of strength in your time of sorrow.

If we can do of help as time goes on, please to do kind as to let us know.

PERSONAL

RHelms/eed - 15/4/68
