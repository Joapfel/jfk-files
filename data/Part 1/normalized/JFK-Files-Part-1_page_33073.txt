1. ON 26 NOVEMBER NEDECKER AND OTTAWA STATION CABLE OFFICER NOLTHAM MET WITH SMABOVE OFFICERS DON BATSTONE AND NORMAN CYR OF THE MONITREAL COUNTER-SUBVERSIVE SECTION. THE PURPOSE OF THE MEETING ARRANGED BY NOLTHAM WAS TO BRIEF SMABOVE ON THE AMCHALK-5 OPERATION, DISCUSS THE CUBAN PROBLEM AS IT RELATED TO CANADA AND TO GENERAL ACQUAINT SMABOVE WITH WH/MIAMI INTERESTS. PRIOR TO MEETING WITH SMABOVE NEDECKER AND NOLTHAM AGREED THAT FRANKNESS WAS CALLED FOR AND THAT WITHIN SECURITY LIMITATIONS WE WOULD ATTEMPT TO ENGENGER INCREASED INTEREST ON THE PART OF SMABOVE FOR THE CUBAN TARGET.

2. THE OBVIOUS GOOD RAPPORT ESTABLISHED BY THE OTTAWA STATION HELPED IN ESTABLISHING A FREE AND EASY DISCUSSION. NEDECKER FULLY REVIEWED THE AMCHALK-5 CASE AND ALSO THE GENERAL PROBLEM OF WORKING AGAINST THE CUBAN TARGET EMPHASIZING OUR INTEREST IN INCREASE. OPERATIONS AGAINST DGI PERSONNEL.
