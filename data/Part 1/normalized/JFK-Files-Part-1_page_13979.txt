MEMORANDUM

TO : Chief of Base FROM : Howard G. FIGALAN SUBJECT: ISOLOS/Operations Western Hemisphere Targets, Group II: Cuba Monthly Activities Report, July 1969

1. During July 1969 the writer continued in contact with AMBEDEM/3. No Intelligence Information Report disseminations sourced to AMBEDEM/1 were received from Headquarters during July, nor were any operational information squibs received. Results of the photos sent for identification by AMBEDEM/1 via UNDA-4574, 26 June, were not received by the end of July. Although AMBEDEM/1 was "surfaced" on 16 July, the Base was not advised of the vehicle by Headquarters until 23 July, after the Base had queried via DO BASE NEW YORK 6372. Fortunately, AMBEDEM/3 advised the Base of the media of surfacing prior to 23 July. The surfacing had minimal impact locally, as the vehicle chosen for the surfacing does not reach the local observer as would the New York Times, the New York Daily News, or the New York Post. With the turnover of AMBEDEM/1 to WODISH for resettlement, the case, for informational purposes, is closed.

2. DO BASE NEW YORK 6097 concerned the effort to expel Alberto EOZA Midalgo-Gato (201-331638), CITY First Secretary and Deputy Chief of the DGI Contingent; and Leonard ESPINOSA Bonet (201-816438), Third Secretary and DGI officer; and the intent to deny a reentry visa to Jorge REYES Vega (201-816437), Second Secretary and DGI officer, who is currently in Cuba on leave. Subsequently, EOZA left for Cuba on leave on 27 July; his wife already had returned to Cuba in June. Additionally, the following CIN More will be placed on "warning" status and liable to expulsion if any further illicit activities occur: Pedro Luis PINERO Eirin (201-824376), Third Secretary and DGI officer; Miguel Manuel SANTANA Fratz (201-816439), Attache and DGI officer; and Jose Raul VIERA Linarez (201-328953), Counsellor and DGI officer.

3. Shortly before his departure on leave, Jorge REYES Vega introduced on 9 June Base asset WHABIT/1 to REYES' "replacement" for the leave period. This turned out to be Orlando F., PEREIRA Gonzalez (201-848505), newly arrived First Secretary. The three-tonne lunched, and REYES did most of the talking, concentrating on the Cuban election to the Governing Council of the UN Development Programme. WHABIT/1 did ascertain that PEREIRA spoke fair English and good Russian, and PEREIRA volunteered that he had spent two years in the Soviet Union (he was a First Secretary in CS COPY SECRET)。
