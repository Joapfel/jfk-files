Chief, Miami Field Office

MI-004-69

Local OSI Inquiry Regarding Information on Mitchell L. Herboll

1. On 15 May 1969, Special Agent Dave Plouzen, local OSI representative, USAF, visited the Miami Field Office to solicit any information we might have regarding Mitchell L. Herboll who apparently is undergoing a security clearance check as the result of presently being employed by a company with a government contract.

2. Herboll was an unsolicited contact of this office beginning in 1959 and he reached peak notoriety in November 1967 as a result of his involvement in the Roberto Munforrez trial in connection with the abortive National Revolution. John Greaney, COG gas in Miami at the time to oversee Agency interests.

3. Plouzen in contacting other US Government agencies locally including the US Attorney's Office also received an indication that CIA had information on Herboll; thus Plouzen's visit to the Miami Field Office.

4. It was indicated to Plouzen, who is known personally by several members of this staff, that we knew of Herboll and that on occasion he had reported unsolicited information. It was suggested, however, that Plouzen direct his request officially through channels via OSI's agency liaison officer in Washington.

5. This is for background and is being forwarded at the suggestion of local CIA Security.

JAMES P. GLEICHAUF
