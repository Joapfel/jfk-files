SUBJECT: Meeting with AMSTALA-1

1. Background: AMSTALA-1 is a JUPAVE asset who was selected to fill a Station Buenos Aires requirement for a native Spanish speaking agent with a technical background. His task in Buenos Aires will be to work in the repair shop of a firm that has access to all Soviet installations in Buenos Aires. He will accompany a member of the firm when it is called by the Soviets to repair, maintain or install electrical appliances located in one of the installations. AMSTALA-1's initial task will be to case the installation for the purpose of locating a suitable site for an audio installation.

2. The undersigned met AMSTALK-1 in his room at the AIRRAYTE Hotel, National Airport, Washington, D.C. at 1500 hours, 16 April 1968. The meeting followed a telephoned exchange of prearranged recognition signals. The purpose of the meeting was to pass to AMSTALK-1 a Nicaraguan passport in alias and an immunization record in the same alias. No will use these documents for the remaining portion of his travel - Washington/New York/Buenos Aires- and to secure an Argentine tourist visa from the Argentine Consulate General in New York. AMSTALK-1 was briefed on the contents of the passport, and is the manner in which it is to be used, i.e., as a travel document only, and to be turned over to Station Buenos Aires for safekeeping during his TDF in Buenos Aires.

3. AMSTALA-1 was given the undersigned's first name only and some phone number with instructions that he is to call after 1800 hours on the day he secures the visa and purchases his flight tickets. The call will be made at least 24 hours prior to his departure from New York to allow time to inform the station of his travel schedule and to coordinate his departure with the Allies Affairs Staff. AMSTALA-1 had already been briefed by JSFJVK regarding contact in Buenos Aires.

16 APRIL 1968
