Although no evidence has been received from any source which would indicate HUACHID has been compromised, the Chief of Station has been instructed, as a precautionary measure, to place HUACHID on an inactive status for approximately six months. HUACHID's salary will continue during this period, which is designed to permit further study and observation of all radiations of this compromise as well as additional strengthening of HUACHID's local cover. HUACHID's responsibilities with regard to HUGO, HARVEST-2, and HUACHID will be transferred to another American cutout (possibly MARG) or temporarily suspended, at the discretion of the station chief.

7. For discussion with the Chief, SJC and Mr. Johnston, it is recommended that the HUACHID project be approved as attached hereto, with the understanding that its reactivation will be dependent upon favorable results of the six-month period of observation.
