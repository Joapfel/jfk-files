Attachment to UNDA-1136, dated 20 May 1965

"ON FRIDAY, MAY 13, RADIO-HAVANA CITED JUAN B. MEJIA LEADER OF DOMINICAN '14 OF JUNE' PARTY WHO HAD GIVEN AN INTERVIEW TO MEXICAN JOURNALIST VICTOR RICO GALAN IN WHICH HE DECLARED OVER THE TELEPHONE THAT 'MANY OF THE-14 JUNE MOVEMENT HAVE DISAPPEARED BUT AT THE SAME TIME HUGELY CONTINGENTS OF INSURGENTS ARE REPORTED IN THE MOUNTAINS OF THE REGION' AND THAT 'THE-14 OF JUNE MOVEMENT HAS MUCH CONTROL OF THE SITUATION.'"
