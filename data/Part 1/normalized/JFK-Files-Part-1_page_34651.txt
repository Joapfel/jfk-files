1. An APOT subcorevo, Aldardo HUEMÁZ García, reported on 17 December 1961 that he had that day, by chance, met Samuel S. Gildburg, a former delegate of Dr. Arturo Soláez Arango, at the New Orleans Airport on 17-28-61, 5:00 PM.

2. HUEMÁZ stated that GTREGKA said that he was retarding that day from a trip to Pennsylvania and Guatemala. In turn he had talked to President Gildburg and had found out he to be judgmental, but, to the contrary, when he had talked to President GTREGKA in Guatemala no one had found him extremely and/or to have CASTED liquidated. YDREGKA had asked GTREGKA about his visit to GILBERT, and then GTREGKA expressed his opinion, YDREGKA said it was logical because CHIAR was extremely cowardly.

3. According to HUEMÁZ, GTREGKA further said that a short time ago he had been called by Mr. Bob Hurwitz, Chief of the Cuban Desk in the State Department, and that Hurwitz had told him not to fool around with Sánchez or Julio García because the man who had the support of the U.S. was AMB-D-1, and that as long as Hurwitz, had knowledge of the great work done by GTREGKA, he did not want him to continue losing more time.

4. HUEMÁZ added that GTREGKA said that a few days later he received a phone call from AMB-D-1. GTREGKA told HUEMÁZ that he will help AMB-D-1, because it was requested, but he would never have done so by his own wish.

5. HUEMÁZ said that GTREGKA continued his travel on to Washington, where he stayed at 1100 - 1200 Street, N.W., Washington 7.
