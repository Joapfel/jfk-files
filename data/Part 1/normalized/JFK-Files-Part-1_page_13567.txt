VICTOR LABEL/CUTLER, HEAD OF THE PARTIDO GUATEMALANO DEL TRABAJO (ICT - GUATEMALAN COMMITTEE PARTY) IN MEXICO, SAID OF 24 MARCH THAT THE ICT IN GUATEMALA REGRETS THE WAY IN WHICH THE GUERRILLA FIGHT HAS DEVELOPED THREE. ACCORDING TO THE ICT, THE RESULTS OF THE GUERRILLA FIGHTING HAVE NOT BEEN SUCCESSFUL BECAUSE THE-13 NOVEMBER GROUP (MEMBER OF THE-13 NOVEMBER 1960 ATTACH TO OVERNMENT OF THE GUATEMALAN COMMITTEE) LEARED BY MARCO AGONIO /CT/ COSA, IS NOT IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE POLITICAL GOALS OF THE COMMITTEE STRUGGLE. THE ICT ALSO REGRETS THAT THE COMMUNISTS AND MEMBERS OF THE PARTido DE UNIDAD REVOLUCIONARIO (FOR - ENTIRE LEFTIST PARTY) LEARED BY CAMILA ZAPATA/JUANA WERE INTERCEPTED AND FORCED TO FIGHT UNDER CONDITIONS WHERE NOT ADVANTAGEOUS FOR THEM. ACCORDING TO THE ICT THE RESULTS WERE DISASTROUS, WITH THE LOSS OF "MIDDLE RANK COMMITTE PARTY MEMBERS AND NATIONAL LEADERS." THE ICT CONSIDERS IT A "MIRACLE" THAT FAZ TETJADA WAS ABLE TO ESCAPE. HOWEVER, THE ICT STILL BELIEVES THAT THE GOVERNMENT OF NICARAGUA'S PRESIDENTS, PRESIDENT OF GUATEMALA, WILL SOOK FALL.
