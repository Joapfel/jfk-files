ASSOCIATION OF CUBANS IN EXILE "Sevillana" Manor, Los Almendros Avenue, San Rafael de la Florida, Caracas, Venezuela. (Phone:71.33.32)

MICROFILMED

APR. 1 2 1966

DOC. MICRO. SER.

Caracas, March 11, 1966.

Department of State, Washington, D.C.

Gentlemen:

By this means I am pleased to state that I know Mrs. Merle de la Paz Ramón personally, whose son Reinaldo Pico Ramón worked actively for Catholic Labor Youth in Havana, Cuba. I can assure you that Mrs. Ramón Rodríguez was never a Communist and that due to her opposition to the regime she wishes to leave Cuban territory. She has always had democratic ideals.

Hoping that this information may be of use to you, I remain,

(There is a seal of the Mission of Our Lady of Charity, Caracas, Venezuela)
