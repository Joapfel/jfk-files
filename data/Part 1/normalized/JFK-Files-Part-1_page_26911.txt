MIN-056-71

Director, Domestic Contact Service ATTN: John McConnell John Creany (OCC) Chief, Miami Field Office

10 March 1971

Mitchell Livingston Herbell, III

1. On 9 Mar '71 we received a long distance call, presumably from Powder Springs, Georgia, from subject, who is an old and uncavory contact of this office dating from 1959. Herbell has been involved in a variety of scrapes, the latest of which concerned an abortive invasion of Haiti which John Creany was very much concerned about a year or so ago.

2. Herbell stated that the Department of Defense had just revoked his security clearance on grounds of Herbell's "frivolous behavior" in the Haitian caper. Herbell stated that he intended to "fight it to the Supreme Court", as he had always kept us and other elements of the US Government informed concerning developments in the Haitian matter.

3. Herbell asked if there was anything that we could do for him in the way of interceding for him with the DOD, and we informed him bluntly and in no uncertain terms that he was strictly on his own, and we could do nothing for him. He then asked if it would be worthwhile to subpoena Bart Gonzaule. We informed him that it was up to his attorney to make such recommendations.

4. Herbell seemed to get the message, and then offered to keep us advised regarding an alleged forthcoming attempt on Haiti which is being sponsored by Rolando Basferrer, even though Basferrer is currently incarcerated in a federal prison. We told Herbell that if he wished to pass any information along we would accept it but under no circumstances should he attempt to elicit any information on our behalf. He agreed that he would operate on this basis and said he might be in touch with us again.

5. In view of Herbell's record we felt that this latest contact should be a matter of record and that OCC should be informed of developments.

JUSTIN F. GLEICHSUFT
