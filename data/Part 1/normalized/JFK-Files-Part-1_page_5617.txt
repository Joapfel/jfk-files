Headquarters agrees with Station that the continued contact between Subject of 201-357029 and IDEM A, on behalf of WODISH, poses a potential security threat for the LNHARP components in Eaiti. Upon receipt of Ref C, the Desk sent a memorandum to Chief, WODISH, on 19 August 1969, which forwarded a resume of the Ref C information, and requested that the contact between IDEM A and Subject on behalf of WODISH be terminated.

On 29 August 1969, the WODISH officer who is in contact with IDEM, replied to the Desk's memorandum as follows.

"a. We (WODISH) have carefully considered the Desk's comments re IDEM A and his relationship with Subject. We have concluded that in the matter of the nine questions concerning the activity of foreign companies in Eaiti, IDEM A has been guilty at least of sloppy security procedures and (we) have decided to modify our relationship with him.

"b. Subject has never stated that we represented a channel to LNHARP authority which he could use to supplement his contacts with members of the LAPURE staff in Port au Prince. IDEM A
