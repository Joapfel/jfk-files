SUBJECT LIFECAT Coverage: Victor Rico Galan, 20-23 May 1965 MICROFILM

ACTION REQUIRING - REFERENCES

1. 19 May 1965: 0625 - 1745 hours: A. Unidentified caller to Rico Galan: Callor wrote an article on the Dominican situation for SIEMPRE but SIEMPRE director Jose Pages . Llergo rejected it saying the magazine was full of things on the Dominican situation and he wanted the writer to produce something about Colombia. Callor wishes to consult with Rico Galan on his assignment.

2. 20 May 1965: 1745 - 2130 hours: N. NIKOLAY LEONOV, Soviet Press Attache and Identified KGB, to Rico Galan: >Rico tells Leonov that "that man" was supposed to call him at noon today. Where can he find him? Nikolay is sure after 1500 hours. Victor asks him to call again at 1530 or 1600 hours. Nikolay will.

R. Manuel Marcue Pardinas to Rico: Rico tells Marcue that the other person will do the piece on the Dominican situation for Política because he (Rico) is doing the Dominican thing for Siempre .

C. Leonov to Rico: Rico tells Leonov that "that man" hasn't come, what shall they do? He will try to call him later and he promises to call Nikolay afterward. Nikolay says he will be busy between 1630 hours and 1700 hours but that his wife will be in. Victor comments that "that man" was supposed to call him as soon as he arrived.
