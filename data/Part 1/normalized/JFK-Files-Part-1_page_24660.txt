SUBJECT REDTOP - Personality Sketch on 201-132139 MICROFILM

1. Transmitted herewith is a personality sketch prepared by LIFETAT transcriber Carol K. ZINSMEISTER on Nikolay Sergeyevich LEONOV (201-132139), Soviet Second Secretary and Press Attache in Mexico. CAN A MEXICO-2. Of the three persons mentioned in the sketch with whom LEONOV appears to be in clandestine contact, the only one identifiable is Juan REJANO (Parras), 201-109935. He is a Spanish Communist, one of the leaders of the Spanish Communist Party in Mexico, with close ties to other Communist groups, especially Cuban and Czech; and at least once he was seen going into the Cuban Embassy with a leader of the Guatemalan Communist Party in Mexico. REJANO is in frequent contact with the Czech Press attaché. He is editor of the Czech bulletin published in Mexico. He is also frequently in touch with employees of the Soviet Press Office. In December 1967, according to an LNERGO report, REJANO gave NELSON (LNERGO double agent?) material on American bases in Spain when NELSON visited the Spanish Communist Party Headquarters in Mexico. On 28 January 1968, REJANO was among a group of "old friends" attending a dinner in Mexico City for Juan MARINELLO Vidaurreta, President of the Cuban-Mexican Society and Cuban UNESCO Ambassador.

Willaert S. Clininger for/Willard C. CURTIS
