REQ. ST FOR OVERSEAS TRANSPORTA .ON SECRET

SEC. A TO: Assistant Executive for Personnel 8 January 1947

YOUR APPROVAL IS REQUESTED FOR THE OVERSEAS TRANSPORTATION OF ANN JOHNSON GOODPASTURE NAME OF TRAVELER JUSTIFICATION FOR THE TRIP IS:

Miss Goodpasture is being transferred from Washington, D. C. to Bombay, India, where she will be assigned as Administrative Assistant at the American Consulate in Bombay.

Designation has not been requested.

DATE SIGNATURE (INITIATING OFFICER)
