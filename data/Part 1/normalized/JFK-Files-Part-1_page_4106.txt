SUBJECT Operational/TYPIC/AMWORLD/ANOT Possible Arms Purchases from AMWORLD by Bay of Pigs Veterans ACTION REQUIRED - REFERENCE: Association.

Action Required: For Your Information.

References: A. UFGA-21947, 11 June 1965. B. UFGA-22587, 30 July 1965.

1. On 21 July 1965, ANOT-237 furnished the following in ANOT Report ED-1521, which information he had received from ESIRE-1:

Juan Jose PERUYERO Rodriguez (201-294249), President of the Bay of Pigs Veterans Association, Alberto MARTINEZ Echanique (201-290790) and Ruben GONZALEZ de la Heria, are making overtures to buy arms from AMBIDDY-1. The Association's Directorate, and the membership-at-large, have not yet been told officially of this contemplated deal.

ESIRE-1 felt this information was partially confirmed by the following comment made to him by Luis Humberto VIDANA Quash (201-274645) in a conversation: "What do you think that AMBIDDY-1 has been reduced to doing? He now wants to sell the arms he has left over from all that business he was mixed up in ... and to think that a person like him is receiving aid."

ESIRE-1 thinks it possible that the RABON brothers may be the persons financing PERUYERO in the purchase of the AMWORLD arms. ESIRE-1 commented that PERUYERO would not dare tell him about buying any AMWORLD arms as he knows that ESIRE-1 would be opposed to such negotiations on the grounds that this would be immoral.

Previous reporting on AMWORLD/AMBIDDY-1 activities has indicated, as in Reference A, that AMBIDDY-1 was interested in buying arms and ammunition.
