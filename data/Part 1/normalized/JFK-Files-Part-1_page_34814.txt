Case # 144

Andrew VEGA Perez originally designated his wife and child as dependents and named another sister as Trustee. Information obtained from AMTIKI and various records available, indicate that VEGA learned that his wife was a GOC follower and directed that the present Trustee, another sister, assume control of the money. This could account for the discrepancy between the amount of money now held in trust and the total amount of money paid from 1961-1985 since the new trusteeship was not created until much of the money had been spent. In order to protect the interests of the prisoner, some procedure will probably be devised by which a long-range projection can be made for actuary purposes and the trusteeship terminated. Recommendations for handling this case will be submitted to JMAVE in the near future.
