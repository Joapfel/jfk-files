SECURITY INFORMATION

PRESS CONTRIBUTIONS

RFE is a major instrument in total U.S. political and psychological warfare efforts directed at Central and Eastern Europe. It has contributed in the following ways:

RFE made an intensive campaign of Stalin's death and linked this event with the death of Goltwald and the illness of Fleck. During the intensive period of the Stalin story, RFE was on the air 24 hours with all its 91 transmitters on a single frequency, which was switched continuously from country to country. It beat all Western press agencies by 23 hours in announcing Goltwald's illness and is usually about 2 hours ahead of VOX and BBC in announcing events of major importance.

Radio Free Europe was the first to inform the Agency of the recent riots in Poland, the rushville of the hangaroad: Communist Government, etc.

RFE has contributed in the launchin; of two balloon operations: one in the summer o; 1951, when barrages of balloons were bartered at Czechoslovakia and Poland, another on 11 July 1951, when thousands of balloons were launched into Czechoslovakia.

RFE is an effective machinery through which the U.S. Government can work unofficially as cannot be done through VOX or RIAS.

RFE gives hope to those behind the Iron Curtain and its broadcasts have provided incentives to escape: e.g., escape of two Polish pilots, Freedom Train,

interrogation of refugees and escapes from behind the iron curtain. Escapes often prefer to go to RFE reception center rather than more official U.S. outfits. Interrogation reports are of immense value to intelligence as to formulation of U.S. policy.

RFE guidelines: Highly intelligent analyses of current situations and lines to be followed in broadcasts. Of extraneous value to U.S. Government.
