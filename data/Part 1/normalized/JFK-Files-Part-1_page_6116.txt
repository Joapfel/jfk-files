17 November 1976

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chairman, Information Review Committee ATTENTION : Mr. H. G. Bean, Assistant for Information SUBJECT : FOIA Appeal--Elaine Fuller (75-4057) REFERENCES : A. Initial Request dated 22 July 1975. B. IPS Reply dated 19 March 1976. C. Appeal Letter Received 26 April 1976.

Summary of Recommendations:

Sustain initial determination to deny in toto to requester two DDO originated documents under exemptions (b)(1), (b)(3), and (b)(6) of FOIA. Remove (b)(6) from one document; add exemption (b)(2) to both documents. (Tabs B-5 and B-6).

Release additional information in three DDO originated documents previously released in sanitized form--excised portions exempted under (b)(1), (b)(2), (b)(3), and (b)(6) of the FOIA, as appropriate. (Tabs B-1 thru B-3).

Correct the names of the deciding officials in the appeal response to requester.

Advise requester that document B-5 supercedes document B-6 and contains the exact same information.

IPS advise FBI that document B-5 supercedes document B-6 and that the latter should be destroyed to avoid duplicate referrals in the future.

Correct dates on documents B-5 and 6.
