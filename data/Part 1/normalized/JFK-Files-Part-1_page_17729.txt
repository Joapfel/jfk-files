Reel 39-2

2 June 1966

3.2 contd.

WO thinks she won't stay long with Sra. They expect more and more or her. They went to ears today and walked all the way. She (Sra.) won't pay for a cab. This time el Señor took them (there), but after that and other times, never pays for a cab. A lot of walking and WO's feet are swollen from so much walking. Then, WO is not bad off there. She is alone there (doesn't work with another person), not so much work...will see about this.

WO will phone WI on Saturday. WI only goes out in the mornings. And not always...Juanita sometimes goes for chicken and tostillas...

Real off at RT 5.0, 2100, 2 June 1966
