1. YES OR NO: ARE YOU A MARRIED OR SINGLE PERSON? (Check one) ONLY

If you are married, you will receive this type of annuity unless you choose the annuity in 2.

The amount payable to you during your lifetime will be reduced by 25% of any amount up to but not including $3,000 a year used as the base for the survivor benefit, plus 10% of any amount over $3,000 so used.

2. IS IT WITH SURVIVOR BENEFIT TO BE USED AS THE BASE FOR YOUR ANNUITY OR IS IT FOR YOUR WIDOWER'S SURVIVOR ANNUITY? (Mark one)

If you do not use all your annuity used on the basis for the survivor benefit, write the word \"all\" in the box below. If you want only part of your annuity used on the basis for the survivor benefit, write the partly amount of your annuity so wanted.

THE SURVIVOR'S ANNUITY WILL BE 55% OF ALL OR WHAT EVER AMOUNTS OF YOUR ANNUITY YOU SPECIFY AS THE BASE FOR HER (OF HIS) BENEFIT.

3. YES OR NO: ARE YOU A MARRIED OR SINGLE PERSON? (Check one)

If you are married, your wife (or husband) cannot be paid a survivor annuity after your death.

This type provides annuity payments to you only.

G. TYPES OF ANNUITY: UNMARRIED APPLICANTS ONLY (Including Widowed and Divorced)

1. IS IT WITH SURVIVOR BENEFIT? (Check one)

If you are not married, you will receive this type of annuity unless you choose the annuity in G. 2.

This type provides annuity payments to you only.

2. IS IT WITH SURVIVOR BENEFIT TO BE USED AS THE BASE FOR YOUR ANNUITY? (Mark one)

This type is available to all retiring commercial employees who are in good health.

It provides a reduced annuity to you and a survivor annuity to the person named as having an insurable interest.

The survivor's annuity will begin upon your death and end when she (or he) dies.

The survivor's annuity will be 55% of the reduced annuity you receive.

If you choose this type, you will have to undergo a medical examination which will be arranged by the Director of Personnel at no cost to you.

If the person named as having an insurable interest should die before you, no change in type of annuity will be permitted, your annuity will not be increased, nor may you name any other person as survivor.
