J. Various Spot Reports

HU-2081, describing the Soviet embassy reception of 7 November 1956, is cited as an example of numerous spot reports on Soviet embassy activities on which information was obtained mainly or entirely through LI-FAT. These reports are also put out to cover the travel of Soviets in the country of notice(HU-1-907, travel of Pavel IAISKOV, Soviet consul, to nuevo Laredo on 11 March 1957).

J. Czech Legation

LI-FAT has provided considerable information on the Czech Legation during the past year. The information thus obtained is roughly parallel to that obtained on the Soviet Embassy, and periodic general summaries have been forwarded in HU-1-1605, HU-1-1632, HU-1-1693, HU-1-1997, HU-1-1747, HU-2-349 and others.

Personnel data

Personnel data on staff members of the Czech Legation have been forwarded in such reports as HU-1-1896, HU-1-1959, HU-2-330 and others. This data has been of use not only in reflecting personality data, official functions and domestic matters pertaining to Czech staff members but also in planning detection approaches.

Spot Reports

From time to time spot reports on matters of special interest in connection with the Czech Legation have been forwarded, among which reference is made to the following entirely or partially based on LI-FAT:

HU-1-1779, Proposed Barter Transaction; HU-1-1814, Travel of Czech Commercial representatives; HU-1-1935, Vera Marie PIASILLOVA; HU-1-1977, Travel of Enrique CASTRO Garcia; and * HU-1-1937, Czech delegation to Ecological Congress.

LI-FAT Coverage

During most of the year LI-FAT coverage of the Czech Legation was confined to the two phones in the main Legation building but early in 1957 one of the two lines of the Commercial Office was crossed and very high production received. It is projected that this basic coverage will be continued to the extent circumstances permit.
