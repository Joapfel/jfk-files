The plans for the Ilicaragua shipboat fleet this year call for 80 boats. All 40 coastal boats of all types and the total comes to 120 boats which will require repair yard services four times per year (3 beings or normal groundings and an emergency), that is, a total of 480 annualy. The local repair yards are able to service only 100 groundings. This situation has forced many shipboats to seek repairs outside of the country, either at Honduras, Mexico or the United States, with the foreign exchange resultant in loss of fishing time and profits for Nicaragua.

Technical Limitations: The lack of local technical personnel, and time required to import various mechanical parts and materials.

Consideration should be given to future expansion was taken into designing the shipyard and repair yard. Carpentry and mechanic workshops are also planned. There are two factors however which affect the overall operation. The first factor is the lack of technical personnel trained in steel construction of boats, but this will be overcome initially by per- sonnel trained by the Company. The second factor will be resolved only by an efficient coordination between the distribution and supply person- nel of the Company in Miami. In this way it will be possible to have any spare part or material forwarded to Bluff within two weeks after the re- quest is made by the local offices, (via airplane). Furthermore, plans have been prepared to stock the shipyard warehouses with spare parts.
