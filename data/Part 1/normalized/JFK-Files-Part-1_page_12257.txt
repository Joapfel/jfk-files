I. ON 2 OCTOBER 1972 LNAGON/MIAMI REGISTERED AMDESK-1 (A-1) (201-285923) (IDENTITY-A) IN THE NARCOTICS SOURCE REGISTRY AS ITS INFORMANT. SUBSEQUENT INVESTIGATION BY STATION REVEALED THAT A-1 HAS BEEN OF INTEREST TO LNERGO SINCE 1958 WHEN STATION TURNED HIM OVER TO THEM. WHEN THIS FACT CALLED TO LNAGON'S ATTENTION, LATTER COMMENTED THAT NOT ONLY DID THEY REALIZE A-1 WAS LNERGO CONTACT BUT THEY ALSO WERE AWARE THAT A-1 WAS RECENTLY IN TOUCH WITH EXCROWN.

2. LNAGON BASED THIS ASSUMPTION ON INFORMATION WHICH THEY CLAIM A-1 PROVIDED THEM, NAMELY: A-1 ADVISED LNAGON HE WAS INVITED BY EXCROWN AT LATTER'S EXPENSE TO VISIT WASHINGTON ABOUT-10-11 OCTOBER 1972 TO SPEND FEW DAYS THERE. ACCORDING LNAGON, A-1 TOLD THEM HE ACCOMPANIED HIGH RANKING VENEZUELAN GOVERNMENT OFFICIAL (UN-IDENTIFIED) TO WASHINGTON. (COMMENT: IF TRUE, OFFICIAL POSSIBLY COULD HAVE BEEN ORLANDO GARCIA VASQUEZ.) WHILE IN WASHINGTON, A-1 CLAIMS HE SAW THE CHIEF OF THE LATIN AMERICAN DESKTM OF EXCROWN (IDENTITY-F). A-1 IS POSITIVE OF IDENTITY-B'S LAST NAME BECAUSE HE SAID HIS LAST NAME IS THE SAME AS HIS. A-1 MENTIONED HE IS SCHEDULED RETURN TO WASHINGTON TO SEE IDENTITY-E AGAIN IN JANUARY 1973. LNAGON UNDERSTANDS A-1
