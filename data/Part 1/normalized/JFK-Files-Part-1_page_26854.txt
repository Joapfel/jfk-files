Dispatch No. UAPA-0100 Date: 22 JUN 1965

TO : Chief, KUCURL FROM : Chief, IUPAGAN SUBJECT : Helicopter Flight Training for WITHRUSH

Objectives:

The primary purposes of this program was to prepare two WITHRUSH employees (Iden A and Iden B) to operate a Bell Helicopter under simulated tactical situations involving rescue and reconnaissance operations. Secondly, Idens A and B were to be familiarized to the fullest extent with the operational capability of the helicopter and its accessory equipment. Iden A did not prove adaptable to this type of aircraft and failed to complete transition training. Iden B completed transition training. Iden B completed the prescribed training, evidencing consistent performance progress as well as alertness, independence and a cooperative attitude.

Period of Training:

Training commenced for Iden A on 15 March and for Iden B on 27 March. Iden B's training was satisfactorily completed on 15 April. A total of 50 1⁄2 hours of training time was logged during the entire course of instruction.

Instructors:

The training instructors utilized were Alexander V. DECLIPPEL and Iden C. IUPAGAN PDO's provided training as to cargo sling operation, rappelling and operational capabilities of special equipment.

Training Areas Utilized:

Training was conducted entirely within Iden B utilizing suitable terrain in the vicinity of Idens E, F, G, and H. Airports and hotel accommodations in or adjacent to these localities were used.
