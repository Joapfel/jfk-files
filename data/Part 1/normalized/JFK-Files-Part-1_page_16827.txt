SUBJECT Material for Passage to Liaison 101 Questions to a Guerrilla Fighter

1. Attached are two copies of General Alberto Bayo's book (in Spanish) for use in briefing or training agents or members of security forces with which we have liaison. This book is a "do-it-yourself" guide to paramilitary and sabotage techniques. It demonstrates Cuban support for such activities and the need for training in counter-measures by friendly security forces.

2. Headquarters has recommended that all WH Stations have one or zero of these books, especially those where official or unofficial liaison exists with a security force.

3. If Station Mexico is interested in this book, a limited number of copies can be obtained. Prior to releasing any of these copies, Headquarters must know the individual and service receiving the book thereby allowing its clearance release.
