MC

Jan. 14, 1966

1. Mr. Bladerghoen of CGC phoned on 12 January. After checking back with Dept. of Justice he learned that latter is in contact with Army concerning Questa. He suggested I not tell Army this but tell them that Justice has an interest in the man.

2. This dated I called Maykovich who was well aware of the Justice interest. He said that Justice was now waiting for an assessment of the man by Army.

D. Huefner
