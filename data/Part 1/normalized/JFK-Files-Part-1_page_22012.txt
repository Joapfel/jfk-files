*GOLITSYN first met GRYAZNOV as a case officer of the American Department. Second Chief Directorate, on 31 December 1961. He told that time that he had been personally acquainted with GRYAZNOV since 1945 and that, as of the spring of 1960, GRYAZNOV was "working only against code clerks of the American Embassy in Moscow." GRYAZNOV had travelled to the United States at some time in the past, GOLITSYN added.
