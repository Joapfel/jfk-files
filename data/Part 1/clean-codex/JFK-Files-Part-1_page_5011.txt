T 224998 EIA267, page 21, IN C3529. TCR: 1423292 SEP 76, GUAT 1982. CITE GUATEMALA CITY 19622. To Director, INFC, Mexico City, LA/Miami, Santo Domingo. WNINTEL MMSPIKE. Ref 1: Director 912772.

1. Re bombing, which was widely covered in local press. Occurred on 31 August in the Mexican Consulate in downtown Guatemala City. The consulate suffered some damage and a receptionist was slightly injured. Since the bomb exploded within the Mexican Consulate offices, which were located in a large building, it is likely that the consulate was indeed the target. Neither ESMICA-68 nor ESCE--4, who investigated the bombing, had any hard information on the identities or motives of the bombers. Although some suspicion was attached to the guard who was HOMERück on the day of the bombing.
