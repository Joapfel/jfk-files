7 May 1965

5.7 Out to 23-02-91. Busy. Out to 23-48-26. VKG (v) orders a cab.

6.0 In, twice. No answer. Complete. Sra. Kico asks AID, who answers, for VKG. Hangs up, or is cut off.

After 1325, 7 May

6.2 In. VKG (v) asks AID, who answers, for Sra. Inge Kico. IK takes phone. VKG is going to have lunch with Miric. All right. Will he be back early? Yes. Has anyone called? No. Did VKG take “Las 20 líneas”? No, will take them later.

6.5 In. Sra. Kico (v) with Sra. Diener, who answers, for Inge Kico. IK takes phone. VKG is not at home. Sra. Kico called this morning, but hung up on her. Everyone is fine. Invites Sra. K. tomorrow.

6.7 In. NO asks Sra. Diener (v), who answers, for VKG. Not there. Won’t be home for lunch.

After 1515, 7 May

7.0 In. Marianita with Sra. Diener, who answers. Greetings, about children.

Inge Kico takes phone. About children, AID, and agency for maids.

9.0 Out to 20-07-77. AID asks NO, who answers, for Josefina. Josefine (AID) comes to phone. Greetings, how each is. Asks about whether Kona has come to town.

10.7 In. NO with Inge Kico (v), who answers, for Casta (AID). Casta takes the phone. About whether she is angry, seeing one another.

After 1715, 7 May

11.0 In. VKG (v) with Inge Kico (v), who answers. VKG is with Jovan (Miric) at a restaurant. Julian will go by there at 1730. VKG will go there now, but may be late. Keep Julian there; wait for him. Fine.

11.2 In. VKG (v) with Sra. Diener (v), who answers, for Quiche (nickname for Inge Kico). IK takes the phone. VKG is coming home now. This young man, Julian, is now here. Yes, but there are no taxis. Just as soon as he gets a cab, he will be home. IK thinks he is not going to be able to wait. Tell him to wait on VKG. Julian says for him to come right away because Julian has another engagement.

12.2 In. NO asks VKG (v), who answers, for his sister, Casta Hernandez. AID takes phone. NO is Antonio. About visiting her, about family. NO is going home to visit.
