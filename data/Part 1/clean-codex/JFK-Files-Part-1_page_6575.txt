21 June 1957

For: Chief, Contact Division, Alien Branch (Stetson). By: Chief, New York Office, New Haven Resident Agent. Re: Teresa Sulekha (A-1Y-271-65E), Guide 220, Operation Oculash. MH-1119.

1. Our attention was invited by Mrs. Steven Magyar (M.ayar), vice president and program director of the International Rescue Committee (Connecticut), to the fact that the subject individual, noted as secretary (sic) of propaganda at the factory known as Elektromas Vazuletek es aeromamarek Caytaru, is now living somewhere in the Hartford area. I also added that when he first got to Austria he was so frightened lest he be refused entry to the United States that when he was asked whether he had any Communist connections he said he did not.

2. A check with I & IB in Hartford indicated that this man was born 24 March 1923, had been under investigation back in February, that the case was closed, that the file indicated he was again under investigation as of April, and that the case was evidently still open. It was also indicated that he was in the New Haven area, but there was no local address available.

3. FYI, Mrs. Magyar opined that Fellous might be deported, which indicates she knows he is being looked over. This information did not come from us.

Charles A. Lea
