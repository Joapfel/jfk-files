Classified message. Date: 31 May 1962. To: Director. From: Saigon. Action: FE 8. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, CI/OA, FI, WH 7, S/C 2. Date-time: May 31, 1345Z 1962. Routine. IN 43751.

MEXI INFO DIR DJAK CITE SAIG 9766. PEF DJAK 7829 (40807). SAIG 9632 (41003).

1. Guillermo (Guillendo) Contreras Garces, official of Relaciones (Relationes) Exteriores, is in Saigon allegedly on pleasure trip but actually sounding out popularity of Diem government and generally looking around. Arrived 28 May, leaves about 3 June.

2. According to Contreras, Albarran is now in Tokyo being briefed by Ambassador Castrovalle (who until now also accredited to Indonesia), plans arrive in Saigon for brief stopover O/A, 8 June.

3. Did Lieegant ever get to Albarran per para 2 MEXI 05267?

End of message.

CS comment: ODUNIT attache MEXI advised attache here on 21 May that MEXI ambassador-designate to Indonesia Lopez would depart shortly to take up post.
