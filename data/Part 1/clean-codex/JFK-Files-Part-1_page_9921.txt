17 August 1967

Subject: Review of LIRING traffic relating to LIRING/3 and LIRING/4 for the period 2–14 August 1967.

1. A review of the LIRING intercepts for the period 2–14 August 1967 reveals confirmation of the following points previously reported to Newton:

(a) Commercial (cover) activities of LIRING/3. 300-28(0.2) firms that sent César Salvador Vasquez, the friend of Sergio Valdez, was working on the license, as of 2 August, for the importation of plastic flowers from Guatemala for L/3. She states that she is going to court with the petition “right now.” In another intercept on 10 August, Sergio Valdez tells L/3 that he and Alejandra (Urbina Torres) will make up their legal and personal agreements the following week with regard to “Colorlux,” the glass factory located at Plaza Miravalle. They expected to assemble the samples for Venezuela (Venemuels) the following day.

(b) LIRING/3 conversations with his Honduran friend, René Ochoa, in Havana. 501-41 (7.7) reveals that Ochoa is anxious to talk seriously with L/3 and wants to get to Mexico City soonest. He states that the only problem is the visa and promises to send L/3 data so that L/3 can attempt to expedite a Mexican visa.

(c) LIRING/4’s social contacts with the LIRING personnel. With regard to L/4’s general feeling toward the LIRING establishment, L/3 tells Rojas, of the Cultural Office, that L/4 visits LIRING “only occasionally,” and adds that “those people were very hard to get along with and she no longer has anything to do there. Only goes by once in a while to say hello.”

(d) LIRING/3’s contacts with Ernesto Capuano during the week of 7 August, although the substance of the conversations with him is not confirmed. 501-41 (5.0) indicates that Sergio Valdez assisted L/3 in his attempts to locate Capuano and make the first appointment.

(e) LIRING/3’s contact with Césarosal Ponseda and Sra. Herceded Pereira on 10 and 13 August 1967.
