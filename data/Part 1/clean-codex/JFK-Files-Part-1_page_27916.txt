Characteristics of the Course: This course is presented primarily by lecture and reading; it also includes seminars and discussion periods. Each phase of the course is rated by a single multiple-choice test.

Section III — Objectives.

The objectives of the Introduction to Intelligence phase are:

1. To develop understanding of the mission and organization of CIA and its role in the intelligence community and the national security effort.

2. To develop understanding of the functions of CIA components with responsibilities for intelligence, support and other activities.

The objective of the Introduction to Communism phase is to provide a basic understanding of the Communist ideology and of the background, organization, activities and capabilities of the International Communist Movement and the U.S.P.
