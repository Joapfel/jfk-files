Classified message. Date: 310, 20 September 1961. RFL. Routing. To: Director. RE filing correct one: No CS record value. Decline. From: Geneva. All the copy as indicated. Action: AF 7. Sienkiewicz order. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, FI, EE 4, SR 6, S/C.2. None required.

20 September 1961. 25 September 1961. Routine. IN 32112.

To Director, info Bern. Cite GNVA 2486. ZIBET INK.

On returning GNVA from Brus via Fran, Friganza met ex-Redsox candidate AEASPIC at airport. Had very short chat. He said he “dropped in Fran,” does not understand what he did wrong, carrying docs in name Georg Reiner, can be reached through Savoy Hotel, Fran.

Plans depart Fran 21 September for Congo as has nothing else to do.

Was major, will be again. Assured Friganza always willing help KUBARK. Sent best regards “Pat.”

End of message.

David TZITZIVILLI, stateless, date of birth 12 July 1918, place of birth Gori, Georgia, USSR; alias Georg Franz REINER, date of birth 25 July 1921, place of birth Salzburg, Austria.

George Franz REINER, alias of David TZITZIVILLI.

AEASPIC changed to WIROGUE.
