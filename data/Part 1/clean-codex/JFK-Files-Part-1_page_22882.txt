American Student in China

Tucker, Shelby Jr. (James)

File Check Results

CIT-J.S.H. Residence: Pass Christian, Miss. Passport No. 615485, issued 3/29/57. Photo attached.

1. The IO Division's informants who attended the Moscow Festival were of the opinion that the subject was anti-Communist. (IO Div.)

2. Shelby Tucker appeared at the American Embassy in Moscow on 9 September 1957 and was interviewed regarding his trip to China. (Interviewer's name not given.) The interviewer stated that he was convinced from his talk with Tucker, both before the festival and now, that he was a sincere and loyal, if misguided, American and that his refusal to give up his passport to the Chinese was not a trick. (State Department Telegram No. 513, dated 10 September 1957, from Moscow.)

3. The FBI, in answer to a file-check request from the State Department regarding some of the individuals who attended the Moscow Youth Festival, replied that their records failed to disclose any identifiable data of a subversive nature concerning James T. Tucker, Jr. (DFB-88829, 21 August 1957.)
