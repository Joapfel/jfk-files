29 May 1965

Not with a group of PGT labor leaders and members of the FAPL. It was announced that a meeting would definitely be arranged with representatives of the Guatemalan Revolutionary Movement (Guatemalan Revolucionario 13 de Noviembre, SR-13), a guerrilla organization headed by Mario Antonio Velaz Sosa (14).

13–17 July 1966

Was at meetings of the Commission de Asuntos Obreros y Campesinos (CWC; Commission for matters concerning persons and workers), labor commission of the PGT, which were held on 13 and 16 July 1966. He had extensive knowledge about Guatemalans who were already in Cuba and others who were scheduled to go there (14).

Prior to 8 September 1966

Was seen in Zone 6, Guatemala City, with Jose Luis Ramos Martinez, Huberto Alvarado Arellano, and Olegario Reyes. They were riding in one of the “undercover” vehicles used by the PGT and the FAR (1).

22 November 1966

Was in charge of a meeting of the National Labor Commission of the PGT, which met in the home of a Party member in Guatemala City to discuss the present situation in Guatemala insofar as the PGT and its revolutionary fighting arm, the FAR, were concerned (14).

12 January 1967

Gave members of the PGT’s National Labor Commission a situation report on the results of the PGT Central Committee members who had been in the mountains of Guatemala with a leader of the FAR (14).

25 January 1967

Discussed with PGT labor leaders the growing dissension between the PGT and the FAR (14).

24 February 1967

Came to Guatemala City from Escuintla, where he had been in hiding, to meet with PGT labor leaders. He and other Political Commission and Central Committee members of the PGT had been in hiding since 20–22 February as a means to avoid capture by the military and police forces. The Party at that time was underground and almost completely inactive. According to him, for all practical purposes the PGT and the FAR were then two separate organizations (14).

9 March 1967

Called an urgent meeting with Party labor leaders to inform them that during the latter part of March 1967, he would be occupied in organizing and later attending a national plenum of the Central Committee of the PGT (14).

27 March 1967

Gave Party labor leaders a hasty and incomplete version of the so-called plenary session of the PGT Central Committee held at an undisclosed site in the mountains of Guatemala from 20 through 24 March. Among the few agreements reached was that the PGT would form its own guerrilla force and call it “Guerrilla Leonardo Castillo Flores” (14).
