Section IV.

This section is provided as an aid in describing the individual. Your description is not favorable or unfavorable in itself but requires its meaning in relation to a particular job or assignment. The descriptive words should be interpreted literally.

On the left-hand side of the page are statements that apply in some degree to each person. On the right-hand side are categories of descriptions. The scale within each column is divided into three small blocks to allow finer distinctions. Look at the statement on the left, then check the category on the right that best tells how much the statement applies to the person being rated. “Not Observed” means no definite opinion about whether a phrase applies. “Does Not Apply” means a definite opinion that the description is not at all suited to the individual.
