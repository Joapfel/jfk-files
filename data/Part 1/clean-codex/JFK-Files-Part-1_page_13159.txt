References: A. DIRECTOR 01313. B. CARACAS 9646 (IN 13549).

22 May 69 01:15683

A. AMCLEVE-15 (AMGLEVE-15) instructed by JMWAVE to attempt establish correspondence with brothers Raul and Roberto. This done late 1966 after it learned Roberto had travelled England on GOC purchasing mission (JMWAVE 3182, Sept 66). In April 1967, station advised AMCLEVE-15 that Roberto was supervisor telephone company, which information obtained from recently arrived refugee (UFGA-27444, April 67). AMCLEVE-15 told former CO he last saw brother (one or other) at Habana airport prior to his departure for Mexico, Feb 61.

B. AMCLEVE-15 was primarily used by station to report on exile activities in Miami area. Had contacts with many different types of individuals, including ex-station assets, self-styled exile leaders and gangsters. During Rosenthal.
