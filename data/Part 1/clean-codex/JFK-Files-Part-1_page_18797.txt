Job Description

Junior Intelligence Officer

Clerk in State Department establishment abroad — CAP-7.

Performs cryptographic duties for Senior Intelligence Officer; sets up and maintains counter-intelligence card files and such other personality or intelligence files and records as are necessary for the use and information of the principal State Department officer and the Senior CIO representative; assists in preparation of positive and counter-intelligence reports to Washington; collects and reports to the Senior CIO officer such intelligence as she may be requested to obtain; performs secretarial and clerical duties as required by Senior CIO officer.
