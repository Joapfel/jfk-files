Preparatory course. Took course with the Catholic Working Youth. As a delegate to the X Anniversary Congress of the J.O.C., Correia attended some new R.I. conference. Started an underground cell of the 26th of July Movement as Chief of Action and Safe Area in the Havana area. On 3 May 1959 was appointed delegate before the official and employers’ organizations representing the local political union. At the First Congress of Transportation Workers was appointed President of Comments Commission and later Secretary of Foreign Relations of the National Transport Union Federation. Because of the X CTC-8 errors held in November 1959, resigned from both posts.

When he arrived in Miami, joined the TOADC, but left it from April 1961 to March 1964, when he again joined the TOADC. While he was away from the TOADC, he organized the “Coordinación Revolucionaria del Tren Extranjero” (CORT), now with the TOADC, and also the “Alianza de Trabajadores Cubanos,” now also with the TOADC. During 1958 was an activist for the M-26-7, the only organization to which he belonged apart from the JCC. In late 1959 joined the MIUR and was “National Coordinator” until December 1960, when he became an affiliate.
