Although no firm evidence to date, anticipate that MIRR may seek to publicize further “revelations” regarding JMWAVE activity, notably regarding combined operations facilities, activities, and personnel. Information from AMOT and other contacts in the exile community indicates Felipe Rivero and his ANC group are also talking of “exposing” WOFACT activities. The stated aim of both groups is to show the “two-faced” attitude of the U.S. government, which stops anti-Communist exile fighters from using the U.S. as a base for action against Castro Cuba but allows WOFACT to carry out such activity. Further aims are to show the exile community that MIRR and ANC are “activist” groups as compared with other exile organizations and to ridicule and try to intimidate those Cubans who work for JMWAVE.
