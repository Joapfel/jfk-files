Cite Managua 6064, 2117532.

Director info: Panama City, JIMWAVE.

RYBAT TYPIC AMWORLD.

Refs: Managua 6068 (IN 24578).

Desensitized.

21 March 1967, B26753.

1. Barker returned to Managua the night of 28 March.

2. Cache more extensive than anticipated. Time and space available did not permit proper inventory. Condition of arms observed varies from poor to good, with little evidence of waterproofing. Caching obviously done in haste. Some arms exposed, rusty, and moldy. In addition, cache contains canteens, cartridge belts, gas masks, machetes, sighting quadrants, bipods, clips, flame thrower, etc. Examined some ammunition and found it in good order, with occasional slight corrosion. Did not examine grenades, which apparently were near the bottom of the ammunition pile. Cramped quarters hampered movement.

3. Estimated cube: 1,200 feet.

4. About seven or eight man-days’ work needed to securely arrange workspace, repack exposed arms, open all boxes to verify contents, separate incompatible materials, and dispose of any items considered dangerous to ship.
