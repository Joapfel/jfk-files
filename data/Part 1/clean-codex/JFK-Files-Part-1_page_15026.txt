Subject: WIRGUE

Date: 18 April ’63  
Time: 1150  
Place: Ruhwaldst.

Orne K. Daronstad, Case Officer

Comments:

CO took Subject to lunch and found him to be in a reasonably good mood. CO told him that we were making slow but sure progress in solving the problems regarding his immigration. Subject said he would be patient but hoped that something would happen soon.
