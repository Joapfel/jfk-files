Roberts was to receive $100 per week from the firm while on leave of absence. Roberts did receive the following payments:

September 1961: $200; December 1961: $100; 10 January 1962: $100; June 1962: $100; 18 June 1962: $500. Note: Roberts received only $1,000 when he should have received $10,400.

12 July 1961: FBI advised that Roberts was still reporting to them in New York.

25 July 1961: Roberts was given a CIA polygraph interview.

August 1961: Roberts was approved by the Office of Security for CIA employment as a staff agent.

September 1961: FBI advised they had broken off all contact with Roberts.

24 May 1963: Roberts wrote a letter to the firm withdrawing his membership effective 31 May 1963.

May 1965: Roberts received a check from Itkin for $2,500, with the stipulation that Roberts send $1,250 to England in order to get the money out of reach of Itkin's first wife.

26 May 1965: Roberts arrived in Dakar.

16 June 1965: Roberts sent $500 to Itkin in England after asking Itkin about a desk and chair to be sent to Roberts in Dakar.

10 November 1965: Roberts forwarded money to Aida Itkin in England.

January 1966: Itkin advised Roberts about the formation of Conestoga, Ltd.

February 1966: Itkin wrote to Roberts about Senator Lodge.
