For AXP.

Dispatch. Chief, WHO. FTRAT P.N.1-9-495. Date. Coordination. E.C. “L” - 10 December 1961, 8:00. Please place in personnel file.

The name is Joseph F. Kuenebach.

For the information of Headquarters and for the personnel file, WAVE wishes to express commendation of a contribution to the JMAVE project. Beginning with his arrival in the WAVE area in October 1960, [he] initiated an operational program directed to the establishment of a Cuban intelligence service, both for positive collection of intelligence and to establish protective security on JMAVE operations. In a remarkably short time [he] was able to recruit, train, and organize such a service which had effectively responded to intelligence and counterintelligence operational tasks, and has shown willingness and ability to ably perform a number of support functions for other types of substantive operations, principally paratrology. In addition, the Cuban service which [he] organized is likely to be the nucleus of a post-Castro Cuban Government service which will serve JMAVE ends, not only on the basis of liaison, but by means of penetrations of this service already established.

In the swift accomplishment of so many objectives, it has shown executive ability, diplomacy, energy and imagination. Commends with a grade level well in excess of his own.

Robert Reynolds
