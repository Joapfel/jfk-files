HRSA-56

Operational Review

Cryptology: HUMANOID

Country: Costa Rica

Objectives and Tasks:

Period: May 1 to December 31, 1952. No change.

Salary: 71,600 per month effective March 1, 1952, per DIR 1784.

Evaluation of Services: Excellent and of major importance to this station.

Evaluation of Information: Of considerable value with reference to information produced through sub-agents. During the period under review, agent has done independent reporting and has produced several reports of value on political matters and has contributed useful operational background data.

Other Factors: The removal of operational restrictions on agent's activities during this period has permitted fuller use of his capabilities, i.e., increased spotting and recruiting activities.

Comments: Operation of this project was greatly enhanced by the salary raise which was effective in March, 1952. Subject has resigned his position with the School of Agronomy and is now able to devote more time to our interests as a result of the betterment of his financial status. In November Agent travelled to the United States and contacted Elmer C. Mercer, former Chief of Station, San José, at which time the possibilities of reassignment to another Latin American country were discussed. After discussion with WHD officers, Mercer advised HUMANOID a reassignment would be considered. (This matter is being covered in dispatch form).
