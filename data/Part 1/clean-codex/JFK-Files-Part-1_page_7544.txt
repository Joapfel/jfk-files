Analyst: James E. Plaugher. Date processed: 21 Nov. 1963.

Document disposition: Cross referred to this file: KORUKKKR.

Pertinent information.

Para. 17. Harold Robert ("obert") Isaacs was born in New York City on 3 Sept. 1910 and held USA passport 177026. He was the editor and publisher of the China Forum, a Shanghai Communist periodical first published in 1932; the secretary of the Noulens Defense Committee; a member of the Shanghai branch of the Society of Friends of the USSR and of the China League for Civil Rights. He arrived in Shanghai from Manila in Dec. 1930 and worked for the Shanghai Evening Post and Mercury, switching after a few weeks to the China Press, where he was employed until 1931 (May). In June 1931 Isaacs took a trip up the Yangtze with Frank Glass, a British Communist and suspected Third International agent. A handwritten letter written by Isaacs to his fiancée, Viola Robinson, stated that he was turning “Red” as a result of conversations with Glass. Other statements indicated that Miss Robinson, an American then in Germany, was an active member of the Communist Party. Isaacs used the pseudonym of Io Sen and published in the New Militant, organ of the New York District Workers Party, an attack on Stalin entitled “Stalintern Ready to Embrace Chiang Kai-shek.” In 1949 Isaacs was in the employ of the American magazine NEWSPAPER, New York City, as special projects editor.
