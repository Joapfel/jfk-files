RICO, Victor (Galan).

201-336474.

Commie.

Angel Tonaala Buros (OCR: TonaALa Buros), who was exiled to Mexico in January 1965 after he was involved in burning U.S.A. houses in Guatemala.

The last mission of RICO Galan (kICO Galan) was the unification of the Guatemala guerrillas.

Copy of report filed in ARMA file.

HAM-9727, 4 June 1965. LIPPER, CB-311/07174-65, 11 June 1965.

Leaders of the MLN and the FEP met near the end of March or the beginning of April 1965 to draw up a letter of convocation to the National Congress for the Liberty of the Political Prisoners and the Repeal of the Law of Social Dissolution. Subject and members of the group will send out the notice of the Congress. Subject is a member of the FEP and an enemy of the FFS.
