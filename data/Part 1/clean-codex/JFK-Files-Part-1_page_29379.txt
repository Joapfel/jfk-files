A. Non-Nighttime Division — The Technical School.

B. Photographic Documents & Instrument Copy P-161.

C. Training Record.

Grayston Lynch. SOD.

Course No.: 15–23 June 1970.

The student is taught fundamental understanding of the photographic process, manipulation of typical 35mm cameras and film processing.

The student is instructed how to use two different types of 35mm camera copy systems for document photography—the LEXCA X-3 range-finder and the RUMMX single-lens reflex camera. Documents are photographed under available light and artificial light with the camera hard-hard and other unconventional means. Students' results of various assignments demonstrated the following abilities.

A — Outstanding. B — Proficient. C — Below class standard.

1. Manipulate a range-finder camera — A.
2. Manipulate a single-lens reflex camera — A.
3. Manipulate a light meter — A.
4. Manipulate film processing equipment — A.
5. Saturation exposure using a light meter — B.
6. Develop films — B.
7. Develop and evaluate negatives — B.
8. Manipulate 3M Reader/Printer and produce printouts — B.
9. Develop and basic photographic theory — B.
10. Over-all results obtained on assignments — B.
11. Determine camera malfunctions by analyzing negative and documents — B.
12. Develop and print documents using artificial light — B.
13. Develop and print documents using available light — B.
14. Develop and print documents using a hand-held camera — B.
15. Develop and print documents using unconventional light source (tripod, clamps, etc.) — B.
16. Develop or engrave surfaces — B.
17. Repair on the document copy problems — B.
18. Record assignment, direction, instruction — C.
19. Demonstrate ability to photograph most material under any type of lighting condition — C.
20. Cleanliness — C.
