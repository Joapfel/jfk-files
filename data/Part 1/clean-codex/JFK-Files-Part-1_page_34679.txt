20 April 0320Z 1961. OUT 97713. T 7/713.

Topic: ABG stations and bases info. Cite DIE 35831.

1. FYI, senior KUDOVE officials only, a strike force of Cuban revolutionaries, numbering 1,200, invaded Cuba by sea 17 April at Cochinos Bay, Las Villas Province. After three initial beachheads were secured against little opposition, the goal succeeded in establishing air superiority using T-33 jet trainers and MIG-1B. By 18 April, up to 12 Soviet-built tanks were brought into action.

2. The rebels lost one beachhead on 17 April and their remaining foothold on 19 April. This was primarily due to three factors:

(A) Inability to resupply with prop A/C in face of enemy jet air superiority.

(B) Inability to withstand heavy tank assault on a beach combat 20E.

(C) Overheating enemy manpower.

3. This strike force was the main rebel effort during the period 16–20 April.

4. No reliable data this date if rebel wounded, captured, or killed. Beachhead was completely overrun, but local terrain was such that individuals or small groups may have infiltrated inland.

5. Police action, including arrest of thousands, effectively prevented civilian action in support of invasion.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officer. Authenticating officer.
