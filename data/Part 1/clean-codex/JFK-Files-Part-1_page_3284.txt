Central Intelligence Agency

Case: List of Psychiatrists Who Have Attended Medical Congresses and Lectures in the Sino-Soviet Bloc.

April 1965 and earlier. Cuba—April 1965 and earlier.

Report No. 00-K-323/03240-66. Date distributed: 16 May 1966. DCS Case 42491. Responsive to C-S14-50555 (closed).

This is unevaluated information.

Cuban national, A 17 357 520, who arrived in the United States on 14 April 1966. He had been on the staff of the Mazorra Hospital in Havana for more than 25 years. This is one of several reports. Source specializes in psychiatry.

This report was developed by an Army representative assigned to the office of preparation.

The following Cuban psychiatrists have attended medical congresses and lectures on psychiatry in the Sino-Soviet Bloc and/or other countries:

Jose Angel Bustamante—USSR, Communist China, and Italy. He has been a Communist for at least twenty years.

Leopoldo Araujo—USSR, Italy, and Communist China. He is the current president of the Medical College in Havana.

Jose Galigarcia—USSR, Communist China, East Germany, and Czechoslovakia. He took an intensive course in psychiatry in East Germany.

Jorge Abdo Canasi—USSR, Communist China, Czechoslovakia, and East Germany.

Edmundo Gutierrez Agrazmonte—USSR, Spain, United Kingdom, and Czechoslovakia.

Armando de Cordova—USSR, Communist China, and Czechoslovakia.

Galvizu—USSR, Spain, Czechoslovakia, and United Kingdom.

Gerardo Noguerira—USSR, Spain, Czechoslovakia, and United Kingdom.

Margarita Prendes Varela—USSR, Czechoslovakia, East Germany, Communist China, and other Soviet countries.
