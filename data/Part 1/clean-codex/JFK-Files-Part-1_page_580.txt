Deferred telepouch.

Date: 27 April 1978. Washington 213077.

To: Brussels.

From: E/BNL. Information: EUR/A, SA/DO/O, DC/E/NW.

Subject: WINTEL NIFLAIR NIGALE Flemish Order of Militants.

1. The House Select Committee on Assassinations, in connection with its investigation into the death of Martin Luther King, Jr., has asked for information in RTACTION files on a number of individuals and organizations, including Flemish Order of Militants (Vlaamse Militantenorde—VMO). The committee request is not limited to the immediate period surrounding the assassination, but presume its interest focuses on the assassin's escape route and support he obtained while in Europe. We have no information on how the VMO came to the committee's attention.

2. We note that the only information we have so far discovered even mentioning the VMO is that contained in positive intelligence (NIGALE semi-annual reviews of extremist activities). We have not yet dredged these up from the library in view of NIGALE provision of the material.

3. Would like to both honor committee request and preserve relationship with NIGALE, if possible. Therefore, request you discuss committee request with NIGALE management. Does NIGALE have any information which indicates a contact between the VMO and Americans during the period March 1968 to 1970? Would they be willing to provide it? Also, would NIGALE prepare a report on the VMO for passage to the committee? Please also ask NIGALE for permission to pass to the committee VMO excerpts from the semi-annual reviews, either sourced to NIGALE or unsourced if NIGALE would prefer. Please stress that any information provided will be classified and handled as sensitive.

4. File: 8-2-9/3. E2 IMPDET.
