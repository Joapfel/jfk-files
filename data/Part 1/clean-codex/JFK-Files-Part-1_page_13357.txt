JMWAVE 7953 (para 3)

AMCLEVE/15 is currently working with the FBI and following their instructions regarding Rosenthal (30 June 1967).

DIR 16167

Headquarters will propose to the Justice Department that any further questions arising locally be discussed directly between the FBI and the Station representative (1 July 1967).

JMWAVE 8039

At their request, JMWAVE met with local FBI and Justice Department attorney on 6 July to discuss future action in the AMCLEVE/15 case. JMWAVE’s position was to agree to have AMCLEVE/15 aid in the case as much as possible without exposing AMCLEVE/15 or his WOFACT employment.

The following proposal:

The Justice Department attorney requested that AMCLEVE/15 recontact Rosenthal, and the meeting would be covered by the FBI. AMCLEVE/15 would ask Rosenthal for money for a down payment on material (C-4 explosives), but nothing would be furnished at that time. Later, AMCLEVE/15 would furnish Rosenthal a sufficient amount of explosives to constitute a violation. When AMCLEVE/15 and Rosenthal separated, Rosenthal would be arrested.

AMCLEVE/15 was to contact Alberto Larrain-Maestre and exhibit the requested silencer, which would not be furnished. This contact would also be covered by the FBI, but action would be taken by local authorities to avoid possible disclosure of AMCLEVE/15’s involvement in the matter.

JMWAVE was in the process of terminating AMCLEVE/15, but in view of the FBI’s need, he would be retained on the payroll until 30 September.
