26 July 1962

Field Traces

Luis Alberu Souto

Mexico Station traces reveal that AMSTOKE-1, LITAMIL-1, LITAMIL-2, LITAMIL-3, and LITAMIL-7 consider Alberu to be anti-Communist.

An Army report dated 25 August 1959 stated that Colonel Arturo Corona, G-2, Mexican Army, believed Alberu to be a Communist and one of the most influential members of the Cuban Embassy staff. This was reported in MEXI 1506, dated 26 July 1962.
