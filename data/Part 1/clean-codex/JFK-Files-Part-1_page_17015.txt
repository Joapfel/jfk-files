Message Form. AS-OS-Videla:jah. 10 October 1964. File in CS file no. 50-8-74/3. Mexico. Director. WH S. No. SE7, PC 2, C/Z OAB, C/Z/OA, VR, file. CITI SE. MEXI. LIEMPTY. Ref: MEXI 1307 (IN 96996).

1. Headquarters agrees in principle that limited building should be raised rather than repaired. Your paragraph three proposal of commercial income netting venture would immediately make this aspect of LIEMPTY a proprietary, with attendant special administrative handling. Unless there are overriding security and operational considerations, we please not do this. However, if final decision is to build store and two apartments, Headquarters suggests limited be made separate from LIEMPTY, a proprietary support project, in order to reduce confusion of two types of accounting under one project.

2. Headquarters strongly believes the preferable solution would be merely to rebuild the house on the same land for occupancy by basehouse keepers. This will not involve proprietary. Also would be more secure from standpoint of Soviet countermeasures.

3. Alternate solution is to sell limited property to LIMOUINE at fair market value determined by independent appraisal, with understanding he build an apartment on the second floor which he will let us on long-term lease.
