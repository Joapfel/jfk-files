n. Map locations by:

Note: In view of the fact that final maps for operations were not known, very little stress was placed on grid and latitude-longitude systems.

5. Compass subjects:

1. Two compasses were available: U.S. Army Lateral and Swiss-made reflector direct-reading types.
2. Agents selected the Lateral as their choice.

Not too much emphasis was placed on compasses, but enough to enable the agents to use a map.

The following compass subjects were taught:

a. Azimuths
b. Book azimuths
c. Converting from map to compass and compass to map
d. Map orientation by compass
e. Compass, night use

6. Instructors’ observations of students

David is very intelligent and shows an interest in doing a good job on his assignment. He is cautious, outspoken, and desires to talk over any requirements given to him in writing. He diplomatically assumes leadership and discusses aspects of problems with his partner, Al. He also was very interested in the subject of map and compass and personally thanked me for the interest shown in their questions.

Al is also very intelligent and, I think, respects David’s age and knowledge and accords David’s leadership. However, if he does not fully understand David’s comments, he questions David until he is satisfied in his mind that what David is doing is right.
