3. Translation:

Miguel. We have received your letter wherein you express your desire to meet with us in the near future. We accept your proposition to see us in Canada and we propose a meeting on 25 November next in Montreal. Stay in either one of these hotels: Capri Hotel, 6445 Decarie, or Le Decarie Plaza Motor Hotel, 5900 Decarie. There wait for a call from us. Not to tie you down, we will initiate our call at 0700 or 1400 hours. As you know in these cases it is necessary to take certain measures in event that it is impossible.
