2. If you please advise me if I am wrong on any of my points of recollection on an indicated move. If, on the other hand, my recollection is inaccurate, will you please prepare for my signature a brief communication suitable for transmittal to Mr. C. D. Jackson and stating, in effect, that whereas we did in fact go forward with a modest subsidy for a three-month period, we have now had an opportunity to re-examine this entire situation, including the identity and character of Nayorski’s sources and associates, and have reached the conclusion that we have no interest or desire to continue our support and relations with Nayorski (Nayoraki) beyond the conclusion of the trial period.
