To: Caracas. From: LAPNC/V. WINTEL.

1. Following is DCD/Miami report 00A (S) 322-26419-76 on an operational meeting between Sergio Dieguez and Luis Posada Carriles.

A. On 3 September 1976, U.S. citizen Sergio Dieguez called the Miami Field Office and requested to meet with a Spanish-speaking representative. We met with Dieguez on 9 September 1976. He told us that recently, while in Venezuela, he met with Luis Posada Carriles, a Venezuelan citizen of Cuban origin. He told us that Posada, allegedly a former Agency asset, had requested assistance in offering for a price Posada's services to BKHERALD. Dieguez said that Posada has close contact with high-ranking Venezuelan government officials and could provide BKHERALD with valuable information.

B. Dieguez showed us a copy of a lengthy document given to him by Posada specifically for our perusal as evidence of the type.

Releasing officer. Cooperating officer. Authorizing officer.
