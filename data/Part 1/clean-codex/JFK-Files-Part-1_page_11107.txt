Subject: ALBERTO-SOUTO. Alias/various spelling: 501-394/79 after. Date and place of birth: 1915 in Oviedo, Spain. Citizenship: Cuban. Present activity and location: Cuban exile in Mexico City. Occupational specialties and education: professional artist; diplomat.

Subject was previously Cultural Attaché from Mexico City who returned to Cuba in March 1965 and worked for the Cuban National Cultural Council until he left Cuba in October 1966 as a Cuban exile. He will assist his brother in running a plant which extracts oils from coffee beans in Mexico. He is also a stockholder in this venture.

Description: height, 6 feet; weight, 170–175 plus; eyes, black; hair, brown; race, CUBA.

Spouse: Married Karna Verà KACZ-Stros-de ALBERU-SOUTO in December 1949 in Mexico City. They were divorced in 1956. No children. She was born on 17 July 1926 in Vienna, Austria. Father: Maximo ALBERU, born 1889 in France; Cuban citizen, engineer, retiring Cuban. Mother: Josefina SOUTO, born in La Coruña, Spain. They are divorced and she is living in Madrid. Brothers: Leonel, born 1917, and Alfredo. Sisters: Kenia, born circa 1928, and Mariluz, born circa 1929.
