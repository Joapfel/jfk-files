Attachment to AB Memo No. 9562.

Travel Itinerary for Nicaraguan Ordinary Passport No. 86781.

7 March 1968, page 3: Passport issued by Foreign Office, Managua, Nicaragua.

7 March 1968, page 8: Exit permit for departure from Nicaragua, valid until 7 April 1968.

12 March 1968, page 9: U.S. non-immigrant multiple-entry B-2 visa valid until 12 March 1972.

3 April 1968, page 8: Leave Managua at 0940 hours by Pan American Airways Flight 2502 (Boeing 727 Jet Clipper) from Las Mercedes Airport (Ian Mercedes Airport).

3 April 1968, page 9: Arrive Miami International Airport at 1435 hours.

3 April 1968, page 9: U.S. Immigration Form I-94, stapled on U.S. visa, showing permanent Nicaragua residence, anticipated U.S. address, flight information, etc.

3 April 1968: International Smallpox Immunization Certificate issued by U.S. Public Health Service, Miami, Florida, at the time traveler arrived in the U.S. because the previous certificate was lost.

7 March 1969: Passport expiration date.

Note: Biographic information concerning the bearer appears on pages 2 and 4 of the passport.
