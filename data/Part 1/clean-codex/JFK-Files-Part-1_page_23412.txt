Chief of Station, San Jose  
Chief, Foreign Division

Operational Letters  
Financials

1270 Tierra del Fuego  
Tierra del Fuego

1. A review of the financial records on Annals shows that salary and allowance in the amount of $400.00 were charged to this allotment during the first six months of its current fiscal year. This reflects a rate of disbursements slightly in excess of that anticipated in the annual allotment of $500.

2. Regularly 18 would not be necessary to change the allotment at this time, but the pay increase for financial officers 1 April 1950 will definitely increase the total disbursements for the said year to a figure in excess of the $1500.

3. In view of this situation, the allotment for Financials for the 1949–1950 fiscal year is being increased by the sum of $500. The total allotment previously set at $1500 is therefore improved to $1600.

4. Taking this action at this time will eliminate the necessity of making a last-minute adjustment at the end of the fiscal year.

Wm. H. WHEELER, JR.  
By V. J. DEAH
