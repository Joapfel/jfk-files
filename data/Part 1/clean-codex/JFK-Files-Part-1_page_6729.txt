While going through Las Villas Province at the entrance to the city of Santa Clara, he was able to observe a large camp of Russian soldiers in tents. The airport in this city is controlled by Russians.

In Santiago de Cuba and Holguín, many groups of Russians are seen, but in a smaller number than in Havana. He believes that the number of Russians has increased instead of decreased since the October crisis.

Regarding food, the most abundant items are rice and macaroni. Meat, when available, is given every two weeks, one pound per person. When he left Cuba a month and a half ago, there were no eggs or chickens. In short, food is scarce and of poor quality throughout the island.
