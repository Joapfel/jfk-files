1. Attached herewith is a copy of the bulletin, “El Foo de Holguín,” Year 1, No. 1, dated September 1967. This bulletin calls itself the “Official organ of the municipality of Holguín in Exile.”

2. The author of this bulletin is Rolando (Solando) FASPER R Rojas, who uses some of the pseudonyms he formerly used in his articles in the paper, El Tiempo (El Tiemno), of New York, such as “I, motus,” “Miguel Peralta,” and Criao.

3. It is false to say that the bulletin is the official organ of the Municipality of Holguín, as FASPER R and his group do not belong to that organization and, at a meeting held on 9 July 1967, when FASPER R and his group tried to oust the directorate of the organization, they were completely routed.

4. This bulletin follows the same line as the other bulletin put out by FASPER R under the name Ahora (Chora), which was also called the “official organ of the municipality of Holguín.” See E-7314.

5. In this bulletin FASPER R attacks the U.S. government and its departments and agencies. These attacks are underlined in red in the attached copy.
