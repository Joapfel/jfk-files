Date: 10 Sep 62. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 8. Info: CA 2, CI, CA/REG, CI/Ors*. IN 19806. Ref: DIR 34954 (OUT 74407).

On 10 September COS contacted LIELEGANT, who states:

A. President Lopez Mateos should be invited to TAMI.

B. LIELEGANT will push for at least a brief visit to TAMI if invitation received.

C. Schedule is now so far along it may be difficult to add TAMI, particularly in view of President's desire to be out of Mexico as briefly as possible and make trip.

End of message C.

C/S comment: *Re Mateos, Asian tour.
