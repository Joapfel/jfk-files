To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Attention: Mr. S. J. Papich

From: Deputy Director, Plans

Subject: Maurice Hyman Halbig

1. Reference is made to CCI-3/751,165, dated 26 August 1958, and to other correspondence concerning the subject, an American Communist living at Medellín (Medallin) #36, Apartment 9, Mexico, D.F.

2. Enclosed for your information are photocopies of the following correspondence sent to the subject:

a) Letter, postmarked 28 April 1958, from David Halbig, subject's son, a medical student in Chicago, Illinois, concerning David's studies and his health, and mentioning that he was elected to the student government on the Student Representative Party ticket.

b) Note, dated 3 May 1958, from the subject's mother, at 824 Bluehill Avenue, Dorchester, Massachusetts, concerning purely family matters.

c) Letter, dated 9 May 1958, from Sadie F. Bulpstein, 9 Chase Street, Lynn, Massachusetts, giving news of friends and relatives of the Halbigs, including one Elinor Colman.

d) Letter, postmarked 18 May 1958, from David Halbig, concerning his studies and living arrangements.
