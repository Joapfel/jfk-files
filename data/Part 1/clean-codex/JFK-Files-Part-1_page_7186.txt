25 April 1966

Subject: Contact Report/LIDRE-5

25 April 1000; entrance to 200 Lago Arquimedes, in cars.

1. Subject reported that he was at the airport the night of 23 April to receive the INTURIST delegation. AUV representatives Ibarbuen and Roncero were also on hand. Boicenko was much put out because their arrival had been delayed over the matter of obtaining Mexican visas. He told Subject he would be in Mexico only for the first day of the AUV conference in Guadalajara, because he was going on to Montreal on 23 April to inaugurate Soviet ship service direct between Montreal and Leningrad (Leninyrad). The others would remain in Mexico for the conference.

2. The delegates were taken to the Hotel Reforma. Subject, who brought Lev Mikhailov to the Reforma, asked how it was that Mikhailov had left the Foreign Office to be in INTURIST. Mikhailov said he had a 15-year-old daughter in Moscow and wanted to remain there. His card read “Director of International Organizations,” INTURIST.

P 11712. P 8017.

3. Subject had coffee at the airport with Valentin L. Savoy and Vinko. Nothing was said of Subject’s break-off from Lorinov.

4. Subject will await overtures from the Soviets. He said he was not planning to be out of Mexico until 15 May.
