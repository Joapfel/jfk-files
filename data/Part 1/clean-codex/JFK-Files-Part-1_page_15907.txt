Dear Dan,

As you can see, I am back in town and at the same time back to my job in the Air Force. I have crossed the river from Brazzaville (Bruzille) without any trouble, though that way was and still is closed to anyone without a diplomatic passport.

So far, so good, but I have to ask you to send me back, as soon as possible, my two laissez-passer (twoaisser Passer). I showed them to you when I was at Alban Towers Hotel. Those laissez-passer (liisser-passer) are very important for my own security and my traveling, and it will be almost impossible to find two other air identification cards like that.

The situation is a little tense and complicated. The white people are nervous because of the feeling of insecurity between GIGOUGU's growing influence and the Governor's failure to find the right solution for the present complex situation of this country and his man for the cause of the South. When I got back to my office, everybody was happy to see me again, and the secretary at the Defense Ministry told us what happened to you. Mr. Reimer, we are glad to have you here now. We need you very much. I hope your health is good now.

Meanwhile, I have seen Norman and let him know what I was doing. I suppose he will make his report to you.
