The Queen's Jubilee.

Such a chance for us all.

As if we were loud in praise, or singing in a canto,  
The heavens would tell our song, and mistakes bewith.  
If with a feeling of love we sing the glad news,  
The means that were means for our dear husband.  
If with a feeling of guilt we sing the traumatic news,  
The means that were means for our dangerous marriage.  
Together in a canto we will sing the news of God,  
Whose power is overseer of all things.  
The means that were means for the untried.  
Therefore, my little children, we ask your mothers,  
The means that were means for your mothers.  
Such a chance for us all. Such a chance for us all.
