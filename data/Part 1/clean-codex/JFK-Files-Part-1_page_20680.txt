11 December 1957

Memorandum for: Chief, Finance Division

Subject: Equalization Allowance Adjustment for David Philp

1. Effective 15 December 1957, subject individual's equalization allowance is decreased from $980 to $563 per annum due to a decrease in the cost of living at subject's post as compared with Washington, D.C.

2. All other terms and conditions of the original authorization remain in full force and effect.
