Ex-Senator Is Off to Jail

MIAMI, Florida: FBI agents led Rolando Masferrer from the FBI building recently. Masferrer, former Cuban senator and newspaper publisher, was arrested for his part in an abortive attempt to invade Haiti in 1966 and will begin serving a three-year sentence. Masferrer was arrested on the basis of a bench warrant issued in June for failure to surrender to the United States marshal to begin serving his sentence after an appeal was denied.
