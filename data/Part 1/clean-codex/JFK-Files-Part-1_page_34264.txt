29 December 1955. IN 23377. Type ZRMAGON. Reference: DIR 63111.

1. Re paragraph 1 reference: Believe AMSCROLL sponsorship more suitable and logical in view of its daily emphasis upon MVIPER actions, its being known to defecting officers, and its experience with press conferences and public eye. AMSCROLL participation to be limited to organizing conference and presenting officers. It will not take credit for defections. AMWORD-1, -2 and -3 have been briefed regarding AMSCROLL role in surfacing and have arranged for space for press conference at 1130 hours 30 December. Local and international press, radio and TV, including WAVE media assets such as AMHIM, AMICE, AMLYRIC-1, to be alerted evening 29 December that a high-level defector is to be available. Identities will be withheld until conference.

2. Re paragraph 2 reference: Present plan calls for AMSCROLL representatives to meet with QUSAILOR-42 to work out details of statement to be read and given to press, which to some extent will have to follow statement issued in Japan. Will use military cargos rather than arms, which is consistent with local press coverage this far. Assuming QUSAILOR-42 concurrence will.
