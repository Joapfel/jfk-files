From DALHOLO.

1. Obviously, we are ready and willing to assist LIRAMA wherever possible and feasible in efforts to “denationalize” and deport individuals such as RICO GALAN, ARNALDO ORFILA REYNAL, and previously others to come. Thereby, it is our feeling that we could be opening Pandora’s (PARDORAS) box by using our influence in endeavoring to relocate such undesirables in countries such as Peru. It is doubtful that Peru would accede to such a request, and it would not be in our best interests to exile RICO GALAN to an area where he could conscientiously continue to cause trouble. Believe that we should encourage LIRAMA efforts, offering our assistance in compiling material that could be used in denationalization procedures, but pointing out to LIRAMA that deportation destination must be worked out by the Kenyan government and the country of the individual’s birth.
