And soon I was invited to visit the personnel department of MGB (Ministry of State Security). But MGB did not accept me. After that, with the help of my father, I began to deal with the personnel department of the intelligence service of the Ministry of the Military Navy concerning my future work.

I failed Marxism-Leninism in the state examinations and, with a group of those who had failed, took the examinations once more. In October 1950, I finished the institute and received a diploma.

I was accepted into the navy intelligence service on 13 March 1951, and on 17 March went by train to Soviet Harbor (Soviet Harbour), intelligence of the 7th Fleet, as an interpreter in the information department. Before going to the Far East, I began my divorce from my former wife.

At the end of April 1952, I went on leave to Moscow. Immediately after returning to Moscow, I coughed up blood. In the middle of May, I went to a tuberculosis sanatorium not far from Moscow. In July, I finished my treatment and returned to Moscow. Because of my health, I could not return to the Far East, and the personnel department of navy intelligence sent me to the Baltic Sea as a senior interpreter at the navy intelligence point of the intelligence service of the 4th Fleet in Sovietsk, Kaliningrad District.
