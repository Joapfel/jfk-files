Costa Rica (Coqta Rica).

In regard to an earlier report which allegedly originated with Freydue Borges from Luciano Nieves concerning the possibility of Nieves' agitation-propaganda mission to Costa Rica, AMBIDDY-1 said he had no further information on this but had rechecked it again and had been told that Nieves was living very well in Miami, apparently without any obvious employment. He had been unable to confirm the report that Nieves was receiving support from Figueres.
