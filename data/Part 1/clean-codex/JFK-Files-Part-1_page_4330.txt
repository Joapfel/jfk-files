10 May 1966

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: AMBIDDY/1

1. The attached material was furnished by AMBIDDY-1 to the undersigned during a meeting in Miami, Florida on 4 May 1966. As noted, AMBIDDY-1 had only a vague conception of the date when the information originated and was unaware of the identity of the end sources. Obviously, these copies were made from old material in AMBIDDY-1's files on the spur of the moment so that he could bring some material to our meeting.

2. AMBIDDY-1 was told that the attached reports had no significance because of the lack of details as to date and place acquired and source.
