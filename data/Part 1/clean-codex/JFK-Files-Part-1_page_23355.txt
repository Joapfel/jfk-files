Biographical: Consuelo Felicia Cordero Calderon.

From 1 January 1957 to 20 May 1957: San Jose, C.R.; manicurist; employer: Juan Bordallo; address: 62 entre Av. 3–5.

From 20 May 1957 to 1 July 1957: Havana, Cuba; unemployed.

From 2 July 1957 to 13 April 1958: San Jose, C.R.; Salon Bordallo; employer: Juan Bordallo; address: 62 entre Av. 3–5.

From 14 April 1958 to July 1958: 160 Main St., Quincy 69, Mass.; unemployed.

From July 1958 to 27 December 1958: Mexico City; Salon Moderno; employer: Esperanza Herrera; address: Av. Alvaro Oregon No. 174.

From 27 December 1958 to March 1959: 180 Main St., Quincy 69, Mass.; unemployed.

From March 1959 to 17 March 1960: 550 Riverside Dr., New York 27, N.Y.; student at Wilfred Academy, 1657 Broadway, 51st Street; also employed at Glamour Cut, 353 E. 204 St., Bronx, N.Y.; also employed at Ciro's Coiffure (Coffiure), Lexington Ave. between 91st and 92nd; also employed at Jacques Beauty Salon, Inc., 160 West 55th Street, N.Y.

From 21 March 1960 to date: 226 Burgess Ave., Alexandria, Va.; temporarily unemployed.
