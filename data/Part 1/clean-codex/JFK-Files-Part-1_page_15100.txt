Date: 28 June 62

Re: Subject to the File

On 26 June 62, subject called and said that he had come to Brussels on 22 June and was returning there for further discussions on his invention on 27 June. He stated that the people there were interested in his invention and he hoped that something could soon be worked out.

He commented further that the person he brought the letter to (letter of introduction mentioned in previous reports) was with a German firm that was interested in the Congo and in contact with Congolese officials.

Subject would call CO upon his return to Frankfurt on 27 June 62.

David R. Reinbold
