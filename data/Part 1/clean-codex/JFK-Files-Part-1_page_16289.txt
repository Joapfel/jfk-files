2. Hoare further stated that he could not continue to give his men promises and had told them he would have a definitive answer about their pay by 28 August at the latest. Hoare considered that he expected a loss of approximately 150 men if the pay authorizations were not signed, and he could not honestly tell the men that money would soon be deposited to their South African accounts.

3. Field disseminated: State, Army (Attaché Comish), Cinematical; also sent to London, Brussels, Elizabethville, Bukavu.
