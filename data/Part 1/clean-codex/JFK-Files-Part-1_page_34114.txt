Name: Raúl VOLTA Landa

Married, three children

Age 35

Professional Information

1. 1966: Cuban (Cutan) Representative in the International Sugar Council, International Wheat Council, International Coffee Organization; Observer-Delegated to the International Wool Group.

2. 1965–1966: Trade Counselor to the Cuban Embassy in London, August–December 1965; Cuban Chargé d'Affaires in London.

3. 1964: Member of the Trade Delegation to Spain. Tobacco Advisor.

4. 1962: Member of the Trade Delegation to Spain. Tobacco Advisor.

5. 1962–1965: Operative Assistant Manager, “Empresa CUBATABACO.”

6. 1961: Chief of the Leaf Tobacco Section of the “Empresa Cubana de Exportaciones.”

7. 1958–1960: Sales Manager, “Minemar Televisión Co.” and “Teddors.”

8. 1956: Sales Manager, Electronic Department, “Electro Sales Co.” (Sylvania).

U.R. Economic Research Bureau, 10 S.E. 9th Street, Miami, Fla., U.S.A.

Miami, Fla., November 25, 1966.
