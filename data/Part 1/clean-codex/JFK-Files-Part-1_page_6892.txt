Date: 22 Nov 58.

To: Director. From: Vienna. Action: EE 6. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS, WH 4, S/C 2.

Classified message. IN 29931.

To Director, info, cite VIEN 4104.

GROOVY GRENGULF

1. GRENGULF reports that a Maurice (MORICE) Halpin, born 3 March 1906, and his wife Edith, née Frisch, born 6 July 1907, both carrying U.S. passports, entered Austria from Suisse on 10 Nov 1958. Passports had Czech visas. According to GRENGULF, this couple reportedly belonged to the circle of acquaintances of atom spy Klaus Fuchs and during their stay in Suisse were under close surveillance.

2. GRENGULF report, somewhat curiously worded, says Austrian surveillance of the couple was not carried out here since they crossed the border into CSR on 11 Nov with U.S. passport No. 118070, issued in Mexico, in the name of Maurice and Edith Halperin, same birth date as above.

3. VIEN has no traces, no knowledge of this case. Possibly (POSSTJTY), as received. ODENVY requested Austrian surveillance.

End of message.
