CIA Request for Evaluation by Departments of State. Evaluating agency. Date distributed: February 1963. Number: C3-3/538,352. Country: Yugoslavia/Mexico. Field number: Marschall A-H. Subject: Yugoslav Commercial Activities in Mexico. By way of RE/CS—PUPP 6780.

Subject of this report is of major importance in relation to our needs.

Information in this report contributes to our knowledge of the subject moderately.

Accuracy: substantially true.

Evaluator’s comments and follow-up questions: As a status report, document is of moderate value. Generally negative progress of Yugo attempts to establish a commercial foothold in Mexico not in itself of great interest to this office, at this time, but would increase in interest in direct relation to information on decision of Belgrade to grant a loan to Mexican government.

Relation to other information: useful for confirmation.
