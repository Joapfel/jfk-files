E. After World War II (continued)

1950, Paris: Jean Adam was arrested; confessed and committed David. David was arrested on the French-Spanish border.

1950, Paris: David was sentenced to 7 years’ imprisonment. His wife demanded and obtained a divorce from him.

November 1950: She gave birth to their second child.

Paris: Prison de la Santé—David was held there for 2.5 years. Later he was held in Fresnes, Poissy, Riom, and Avignon.

His sentence was reduced by 10% on the occasion of the election of the new President of the Republic, and by three months for good conduct. He was released (he did not mention when). He has not seen his divorced wife since 1950. He did not say anything about his activities after his release from prison.
