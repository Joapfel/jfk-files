27 June ’90

Re: “Stalin’s Photo-Book.”

Target: Stalin.

Basic idea: Stalin means H.I.

For publication: suitable for publication in any country, France to start with.

Item: BS: “It should be so good that Stalin himself couldn’t answer it. Captions to be as far as possible genuine quotes from Marx, Lenin, Stalin, and Molotov, under photos showing undeniable facts such as the assassination of Trotsky (Frosky). Nothing phony. If we have a book that no one in the Kremlin (Arelin) can pick holes in, it will be good for publication anywhere in the world.”

Item: BS: Target is Stalin and his regime. “It should not contain a line that would offend any non-Communist Russian.”

Item: BS: “Don’t use any quotes from avoided anti-Stalinists such as Kravchenko.”

Title: “Joseph Stalin,” “This Man Wants ‘Peace!’,” “This Man Talks Peace!,” “Thirty-three Years of Peace,” “The Trojan Dove.”

BS: Don’t let’s worry about the title now.

Item: BS: It says there is a very striking contrast between photos of the Politburo (Polituro) leaders in the ’20s and untouched photos of them now, e.g. in the official 20-minute newsreel of May Day 1949 in Red Square, bloated potentates (patents). BS: It looks, 28 June, as though this newsreel, and stills from it, may be available. BJ: If it’s technical, so much the better.

Item: BS: “Victorian History of Russia” (“Victorial History of Russia”) by John Stuart Martine.
