Message Form. Total copies: 2. Cable Secretariat Dissemination. By: J.J. Per. COAF: WHB. File: WHB/06-8. Ref: 06-8 c/w A-95. S-P 1 22 20'Z. Cite Director.

To: Caracas, Julave. Ref: A. Julave 8997 (IN 40592); B. Caracas 7694 (IN 41504).

1. Based on info currently available HQ no objection to station contact with AMCLEVE-15 pending confirmation from other station sources he actually working for DIGEPOL and in what capacity. If contact desired AMCLEVE-15 standard clearance procedures must be initiated.

2. Can Caracas discreetly confirm DIGEPOL job?

3. Request WAVE duplicate field 201 soonest and send to Caracas and HQ for review. Re Ref. B. Does WAVE have additional info for Caracas suggesting caution in contact with AMCLEVE-15?

End of message.

WH comment: AMCLEVE-15 reported to WAVE he obtained job with DIGEPOL in Caracas and was involved in DIGEPOL operation culminating in capture Espinoza, a Cuban guerrilla, who landed Falcon Province July 66 with Luben Petkoff. A-15 stated to WAVE he talked with Espinoza day following capture. Betty—file A-15.
