Central Intelligence Agency, Washington, D.C. 20505

16 July 1965

To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Attention: Mr. S. J. Papich

From: Deputy Director for Plans

Subject: Grace Elizabeth Russo

CIA Paris has requested that material on Grace Elizabeth Russo be made available. Subject was born 23 September 1925 in Boston, Massachusetts. She is presently employed by FAO, Rome, and resides at Via Annie Faustina 56, Rome. CIA Washington traces reveal no pertinent information on Miss Russo. It is therefore requested that a check be made of the Bureau's records.

In response to this request, there are also two reports which appear to relate to the subject of your inquiry.

This reply is the result of a check of FBI investigative files. To check arrest record: a name must be submitted.
