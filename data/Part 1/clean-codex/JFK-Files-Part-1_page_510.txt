To: Paris (Eyes Only DEMOLAT), 5 20 G1 Mar 73.

Cite Headquarters 3925.

MHCURE MHCURAOS.

Bern (Eyes Only BACHMAN).

Info: Algiers (Eyes Only BURCHENAL).

From NICERTAIN/5 at 10 Mar 73, Paris meeting CURIEL.

1. Apparatus participating in support of six American Black militants/hijackers who recently lived in Algeria.

B. Curiel said three of them (two men and one woman) are now in France; three (two women and one man) are now in Switzerland. Those in Switzerland are living in “very precarious” clandestine status and should be removed as soon as possible.

C. Curiel said the entire group is determined to return clandestinely to the U.S. and continue revolutionary activity. The group is convinced that before leaving Europe they should accept political and ideological training.
