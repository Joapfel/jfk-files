3. LKERGO source (probably LKYAPA) said several Guatemalan congressmen came to Miami approximately two or three weeks ago and ordered several firearms from Parabellum to be shipped to Guatemala. Among the weapons ordered were those cited in reference C. Masferrer and Alliegro took this order to Colonel Rafael Arriaga, Guatemalan Consul General in Miami, with the explanation that they did not wish to do any business with the congressmen if it was not with the full knowledge and desire of the Guatemalan Government. Arriaga was told by Guatemalan President Carlos Arana that he did not want the arms sold to the congressmen.
