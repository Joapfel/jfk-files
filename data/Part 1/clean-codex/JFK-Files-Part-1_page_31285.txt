Technical Services Staff. Training Division Evaluation. Photo I—Basic Photography.

Name: Wijen, Lee. Office. Staff. Division. Branch. Dates trained: November 1954 to December 1954.

The course is primarily designed to develop skills. Several subjects, however, such as telephoto lenses and stereo photography, are covered only briefly, and the purpose in these subjects is merely to impart an understanding, and not a skill.

This form, therefore, evaluates:

a. The student's level of understanding, signified by the letter “U”; or  
b. The degree of skill attained, signified by the letter “S”;

whichever is applicable.

I. Basic photographic theory and practices. A. Manipulation of camera: very good; excellent. 1. Leica (S): M; X. 2. Signet (S): Pettina Tte; X; X. 3. Camera focusing (S): X. B. Depth of field scales (U): S. C. Relationship of aperture to shutter speeds (U): X.

II. Processing and Printing. A. Developers, hypo, washing, drying (S): X. B. Loading NIKOR film developing tank (S): X. C. Use and function of the enlarger (S): X. D. Contrast papers—selection of proper paper for a particular negative (S): X. E. Print control (S): X.

III. Use of filters (U): X.

IV. Use of exposure meter (U): X.

V. Indoor photography. A. Employing only natural room lighting (S): X. E. Employing accessory room lighting for ID pictures (S): X.
