10 June 1965. DIR CITE NEXI 3762.

1. On 31 May, LIFEAT on Victor Rico Galan (VRG) reported a call from Mireya de Van Raben, member of the Central Committee of Partido Obrero Revolucionario (Trotskyista) (POR/T), Mexican Section, Fourth International.

Wife of Joaquin Van Raben Breton, who was reportedly treasurer of POR/T, Mireya invited VRG to dine with the Van Rabens and some American economists. VRG met these people—the Zinovak (PHOENICI). Another couple was also coming, whose name Mireya forgot; an economist, interesting, well-traveled, whom VRG must also know.

They are Americans and speak Spanish.

2. On A June, LIFEAT on VRG reported VRG's call to the office of Joaquin Van Raben, who was manager of the offset division of Gestetner S.A. Joaquin said VRG was to have phoned him yesterday to confirm whether they were going to meet “there at 1600.” VRG said no, the appointment was confirmed here at 1700. Joaquin insisted it was tentatively at 1600 and added they also had to talk about “that famous trip.”

What about the trip? VRG says, “We are going tomorrow (Saturday) at noon,” or can wait till Sunday. Joaquin will try to arrange things to leave Sunday. VRG said if Joaquin can't, O.K. Will see.
