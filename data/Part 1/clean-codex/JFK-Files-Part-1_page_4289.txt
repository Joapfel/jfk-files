Contact Report

From: AMBIDDY-1, 1 September 1965  
Subject: Costa Rica Communist Activities

1. A-1 told me he had followed up with his delegates in Costa Rica and got some details on his former vague report about a Costa Rican official named Zuñiga, who was anti-American and Communist-influenced. The man's name is Eduardo Zuñiga, a high official of the Instituto Nacional de Vivienda Urbana (INVU), in which there is a Communist cell directed by Architects/Pablo Jenkins and Cano Rojas.

2. Zuñiga has a girl friend named Thelma (lnu), whose family is Communist. Her father is a Communist and a member of the INVU cell mentioned above. He is chauffeur for Macho Carazo, who is running for Deputy under the PLN and is very close to Daniel Oduber.

Carl Jenkins
