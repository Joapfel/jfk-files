KAPOK MHQUITE AMSTALK

1. Marta (Isalque) Rodriguez, who is cousin of AMSTALK-1, arrived Miami via airlift 11 April 67 and advised AMSTALK-1 she had a message for him. Circumstances precluded her delivering the message until 24 April, when she told the following story.

2. Marta applied for airlift some time ago, became discouraged by her chances, and wrote a personal letter to Manuel Pineiro, who was known to her family. Shortly after, she was telephoned by Pineiro, who stated he was taking the case under personal supervision and sent a secretary to obtain the necessary information. After not hearing further news for one month, Marta again wrote Pineiro, who again telephoned and on the following day sent two men who gave her a definite departure date and promised to see that all went smoothly. One, identified as “Menendez,” stated that he was a friend of AMSTALK-1.

3. Two Pineiro aides were helpful and arranged for Marta secret.
