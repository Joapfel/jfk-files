57. Proposed move of secretariat: JNDEVIL/1's desire to move the secretariat from SAGO to CARA is apparently motivated by a desire to rid himself of present secretariat members and strengthen his hand. Such a move would enable him to get rid of Jose Goldsack who, due to the expense involved in moving a large family, would be unable to make the move to CARA. JNDEVEL/1 would like to replace him and other members of the executive committee with Eduardo Garcia, Jose de Jesus Planas, Henri Molina from SDOM, and AMICE/31. (Comment: This probably explains AMICE/31's participation for JNDEVIL/1.) Another JNDEVIL/1 reason for such a move would be to be physically nearer to affiliates who are giving him full support; i.e., Caribbean affiliates. DUBOLD/1 states that this proposed move will be opposed by a solid bloc of campesinos and most delegates from South American affiliates.
