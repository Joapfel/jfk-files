From the outset Mr. Flores has shown many characteristics of an above-average employee. His initial training in the intricacies of his duty assignment was marked by a high degree of application, interest, and achievement. Since that time Mr. Flores has proven himself a conscientious and responsible employee, one whose work record is distinguished by a high calibre of quality and quantity.

Because of his fine record, Mr. Flores was chosen as one of the three specialists for this section. He specializes in the Spanish-Portuguese area and his work is characterized by exceptional proficiency.

At present Mr. Flores is registered at American University as a sophomore. Inasmuch as he has exhibited both potentiality and strong working habits, I recommend that Mr. Flores be given the Professional Battery Test at the earliest opportunity.
