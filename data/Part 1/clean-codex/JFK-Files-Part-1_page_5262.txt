Director.

Colonel Claude Raymond, Dr. Roger Lafontant, Port-au-Prince University militia leader, and Duvalier's private secretary Pierre Biamby applied at the U.S. Consulate on 17 June for transit visas for a special mission to London. The trio requested a two-week stay in the U.K., going, and two weeks on the return trip.

2. Clemard Joseph Charles, traveling as inspector of Haitian embassies and consulates, separately requested a transit visa for a trip to Europe.
