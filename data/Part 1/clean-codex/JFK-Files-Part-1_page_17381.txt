Classified message. Date: 2 October 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 8. Info: ADDP, S/C 2. Routine. IN 31995.

DIR CITE NEXI 2096.

1. Naval attaché advised that Rear Admiral R.C. Benitez, U.S.NR. (ret.), will accompany NEXI presidential party on coming trip.

2. Believe Benitez is executive with Pan Am Latin American Division in Miami.

End of message.
