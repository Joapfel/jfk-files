Dispatch.

To: Chief, SB Division.  
BFO: Chief, WH Division.  
From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Subject: REDUCERICK. Nikolay Sergeyevich LEONOV (201-132139).

1. LEONOV, his wife, Valeriya Ivanovna, and daughter, Irina Nikolayevna, departed Mexico PCS on 22 December 1968. They travelled on Sabena Flight 556 with destination Moscow via Brussels. Upon his PCS departure, LEONOV completed seven and one half consecutive years on his second tour in Mexico.

2. LIFEAT and LIENVOY (LIEUVOY) have reported that Svyatoslav Fedorovich KUZNETSOV (201-087222) is replacing Subject as the Soviet Press Attaché. LEONOV held the title of Second Secretary. KUZNETSOV is a First Secretary.

3. Travelling with the LEONOV family on the same flight was Petr Fedotovich GORELOV (201-265458).

For Willard C. CURTIS.

Distribution: C/SB; C/WH.
