Admission of José A. Ruiz and Annexation of America.

The agreement is as follows between Mr. and Mrs. Sancho de la Torre:

A Cuban citizen, of 20½ years, married, an employee, residing at 500 Park Avenue, New York, New Jersey, of the one part; and Mr. Roberto, a Cuban citizen, of legal age, married, an employee, residing at 41-39 Michigan Boulevard, Flushing, New York, of the other part.

That said Mr. Sancho de la Torre was designated Administrator or Trustee of Mr. Jorge Min Yung's belongings, a Cuban citizen of legal age, single, who died in Cuba at a center of the Cuban Brigade, number 2968. This designation was made by the parents of the deceased, Mrs. Annastasia Yung Cho and Mr. Pedro Min, both Cuban Americans residing in Cardenas, Cuba, and is granted in the special order of attorney signed on January 6, 1964, before the Notary Public, Mr. Miguel Antonio Dolores Santamaría.

Mr. Jorge Min Yun's belongings included a pension granted to him as a member of such a brigade in the amount of $225.00 monthly, during the period March 1961 to December 1968, representing a total amount of $15,750.00.

Of the total amount of $25,750.00, Mr. Sancho de la Torre designated the following payments and expenses: (a) the amount of $215.00 due to Mr. Min's parents; (b) the amount of $150.00 purchased in Ledesma and spent for Mr. Min's parents; (c) the amount of $75.00 paid to the Committee for Liberty of Plaza de Cimon Plaza; (d) the amount of $1,966.00, representing all expenses as Administrator during the entire period.

Consequently, the net amount left by Mr. Sancho de la Torre after deducting the payments and expenses mentioned in Farquharson's number 268 is $115,059.00.

The other 7½%—Mr. Roberto M. Castro—states that he has been designated Administrator of Mr. Jorge Min Yun's belongings by Mr. Pedro Min and Annastasia Yun Cho, father and mother of the decedent. This designation is granted in the written form of attorney signed on June 5, 1967, before the notary public of San Diego County, State of California, by Roberto M. Castro, his substituted son-in-law; and therefore, since today, he is the substitute of Mr. Sancho de la Torre as Administrator.

Today he receives from Mr. Sancho de la Torre a certified check issued by the First National Bank of Jersey City, in the amount of $25,000.00, relieving Mr. Sancho de la Torre of further responsibilities.

In the city of Jersey City, New Jersey, this thirteenth day of September, nineteen hundred and sixty-seven.

Witness: Hilda Peacard.
