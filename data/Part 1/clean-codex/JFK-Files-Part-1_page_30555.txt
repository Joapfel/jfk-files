To: CHANGHAI (DWARF), routine. From: Special Operations, 14 April 1948. Confirmation: CFD (1), OUT 6130. Information: ALSO (2–3), COPS (4), CFD (5), MRS (6–7), CAU (2).

WASH 5386.

To: SHAN. Cite: WASHE.

Re policy on marriages between members of staff. Policy prohibits marriage where either party is subject to the supervision of the other or if both are working in the same office for the same supervisor. Chief of Mission being supervisor over all employees. CA not marry staff employee and have wife continue to work. Staff employees outside this policy may marry upon approval of headquarters, and both continue employment. Example: Staff member of PEPPER desiring to marry employee under MILLEAGH would receive approval of headquarters after request from field.

J. DRY  
Admin.
