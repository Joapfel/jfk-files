Date: 10 July 1961. To: Director. From: Brazzaville. Action: AF 7 (TEMPO copy slotted AF at 1235 10 Jul) (AF div notified 1235 10 Jul). Info: PG 2, CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, OS 2, S/C 2.

Classified message. Cable: BRAZ 135.

WIROGUE.

1. If for any reason WIROGUE/1 not met at Idlewild and he gets through immigration successfully alone, he will go to Taft Hotel. If not able to get room, will leave message at desk for “Mr. Watson.”

2. If this fails, may possibly be traced through New York friend Margaret LaGrutta. Telephone 76-6949.

End of message.
