Termination

1. Project: WIRGUE.

2. Objectives:

A. To provide a long-term, deep-cover asset in the Republic of Congo and adjacent areas.

B. To establish a principal agent in the Republic of Congo, in order to build a covert net in support of operational activities.

C. To provide an asset for utility support for KUBARK personnel under official cover.

3. Reason for termination:

Subject's poor performance and lack of operational security, coupled with his possible arrest by the Congolese authorities, necessitated the removal from the area of WIROGUE/1. Once this was accomplished, there was little or no possibility to continue the project.

4. Effectiveness of project:

WIROGUE/1 did manage to establish himself in a position of potential value and therein implement the objectives of the project. However, the local situation curtailed his effectiveness, and his lack of appreciation of the need for operational security ended his usefulness.

5. Date of termination:

7 September 1961.

6. Status of commitments:

No commitments were made to WIROGUE/1, and quitclaim was procured.

7. Plans for disposition of funds, etc.:

WIROGUE/1 was paid a flat sum of $2,000.00, which constituted his termination bonus; was given his documentation; and was transported to Germany, where contact was broken. He had been given a letter drop with SR/DGR for future contact. It was agreed that he would keep CIA apprised via the drop.

8. Risks:

The only outstanding risk which WIROGUE/1 may present to CIA would be the identification of current members of the Leopoldville Station to a hostile service. The possibility of this occurring is remote, since there is still a hope on W/1's part to work for CIA in the future; however, it does exist.
