10 May 1968, IN 08516. Director.

References: A. Director 71540. B. HVCA-15159.

1. AMCLEVE-15 has proved extremely productive and very valuable source since picked up by Caracas Station in February 1968. We believe he is collaborating fully with WOFIRM and would not expose, inform on, or work against WOFIRM. This does not mean he might not engage in side activities of his own unrelated to WOFIRM activities without informing WOFIRM.

2. Believe good tradecraft requires he undergo SGSWIRL at this time. Also, we would like to regularize his status, reinstate his clearance, and remove him from “hostile” list so we can work with him more closely, levy requirements, and thereby reap full benefit of his potential.

3. We would propose limiting examination to points designed to determine that he was not, is not, and does not plan to engage in activities hostile to WOFIRM. Our only real concern is to be assured he is not working against us. Some much of his value to CIGARS is based on his other activities and his contacts.
