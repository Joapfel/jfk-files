Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

TOE5-12/20/92-07. Distributed 6 February 1997.

Country: Mexico/Cuba/Latin America. Date of information: late January 1967. Subject: Victor Rico Galan's views on the revolutionary movement in Latin America and on Fidel Castro. Acquired 31 January 1967.

Source: Leftist journalist who talked with Victor Rico Galan in the Leonquebri (Lecumberri) Penitentiary in Mexico City. Source is a reliable reporter.

1. Headquarters comment: Victor Rico Galan, a pro-Cuban journalist, was arrested on 12 August 1966, together with some forty other members of the Popular Revolutionary Movement (PRP). Rico Galan was charged with influence rebellion, conspiracy, and accumulation of arms. Under these charges, he is not eligible for bail.

2. Rico Galan believes that violent revolution throughout Latin America is “indispensable,” since the peaceful road to change is blocked everywhere. Revolutionary movements are on the rise everywhere in Latin America, according to Rico Galan. In his opinion, these movements are marked with radical tendencies, and are not as rigid as the International Committee believes. Rico Galan stated that Castro has the dominant influence at work.
