Ref: Santo Domingo 17347 (906509).

1. On 28 April HUGALLY-1 informed COS that Costa Rican Ambassador in Dominican Republic (Callejas, whom HUGALLY-1 labeled as a Figueres man) arranged for entry of Dr. Orlando Bosch into Dominican Republic. No dates mentioned and no other details.

2. FYI, we know from local HUFABRIC liaison that Bosch was escorted/deported to Nicaragua by overland route on 26 March 1976. Local press on 27 March speculated that Bosch was deported to other countries, including Dominican Republic.
