CITE DIR 88863.

1. In addition to reference report, FBIS report of 11 December says Tello, in interview with Novedades, criticized OAS, claiming it is in a state of crisis, and that Cuba's exclusion from OAS is a violation of its charter. These reports suggest Tello deliberately attempting to pre-establish Mexican policy with regard to censure action against Cuba. In view of the importance of the Mexican position in this respect, these reports are a matter of considerable concern here, and it was felt necessary to take appropriate action to counter Tello tactics in a way that will result in Mexican support for stronger OAS action.

2. Headquarters has convincing intelligence from Venezuelan sources as well as our own which shows conclusively that arms discovered in Venezuela are distinctly Cuban. A number of weapons can be identified as especially designed for the Cuban Army. Arms discovered in general were of a type and in sufficient numbers to support a plan for takeover of the capital city of Caracas. Their appearance in Venezuela ties in specifically with known FALN plans with regard to location and timing. The latter is supported by documentary evidence taken from captured FALN members.
