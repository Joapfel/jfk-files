Cite JMAVE 9138. Caracas, Director.

References: A, Director 32719; B, Caracas 7725 (advise 3-32).

1. The following is AMULET pro data:

A. Born 18 February 1928, Cienfuegos. Educated in Cienfuegos; received degree in sugar chemistry in 1957. Employed as a chemical analyst and exterminator in Cienfuegos from 1946 to 1958. Department manager, Firestone Tire Co., Havana, March 1958 to March 1959. Then unemployed and became a political asylee at the Argentine Embassy. Arrived in the United States via Mexico 28 February 1951. Remainder of family—father, mother, brothers, sister—still in Cuba. Trainee for Brigade 2506 but did not participate in invasion. Employed by Firestone Tire Co., Akron, October 1961 to March 1963. Member of the U.S. Army, March 1963 to March 1964; discharged as an enlisted lieutenant. Employed as an exterminator in Miami, September 1964 to March 1965.

B. Recruited by JMAVE in March 1965 and used as a training instructor and subsequently as a reporter on exile activities in the Miami area until termination in July 1967.

C. Has maritime experience and is an expert in weapons.
