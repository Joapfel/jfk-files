3. Sergo KOKREIDZE (GOKH-KHOKH-)

Lives at 5 rue Leconte de Lisle (Métro Église d'Auteuil), c/o Mrs. Koulia. Does not live there but visits often. Is in close contact with Ilse DJABADARI, artist's agent, 16 rue du Cherche-Midi, Paris XVI, phone Little 190. DJABADARI is an agent for ballets and movies. Trying to set up a Georgian ballet together with Sergo KOKREIDZE. They have 12 boys and girls in the troupe, want to expand to 30 members, and come to the U.S. Expect to tour Spain in the near future. Sergo KOKREIDZE is about 5'8", 140–150 pounds. Is a dancer, with a thin body and face. Light hair, partly bald. Narrow mustache. Forty-one years old. Is a World War II (wu II) escapee with friends in Georgia. ALEPADDLE is of the opinion that he is not suitable for our specific purposes, but willing to do something. Can write.

4. Alyosha ABACHIDZE

Expressed a wish to work. Lives at 6 rue Mont-Blanc, Paris XV. Is over 50, 5'10" or 5'11", 200 pounds. Round face, bald. Emotional, flushes easily. Anti-Socialist. Works as a messenger for the Indian Embassy, earns 30,000–35,000 francs per month. Widower, no family. Worked with Germans, apparently as an agent trainer. Strong, fast-thinking, has initiative. Not a talker or writer, but an action man. Had some recent lung trouble. Willing to go to Georgia. Believes the best means of contact with Georgia is through Turks of Georgian descent who live near the Georgian border and who could aid in crossing. Does not believe in frequent crossing.

5. Irakli KALAICIZI

Age about 36–37. French citizen. Keeps in touch with travelers from Georgia. Thinks in terms of a hot-war situation. Fears that action now might jeopardize the existing Georgian underground organization. Feels that even top Georgian Communists have more ability and a larger following than the émigré leaders. 5'8", 160 pounds, bald, married, two daughters, mustache, drives a taxi. Trained as a chemist. Will escape.
