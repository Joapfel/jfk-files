12 206 40Z June 68

Buenos Aires, info Panama City. RYBAT REDTOP AETARGET AERONAUT TECHS.

Reference: Buenos Aires 0772 (IN 28173).

WIL/4/A. Cite: cite Director. CA. FI/CI. Reports: 0.7509.

1. Feasibility study on both local and Westinghouse plugs just completed. Study revealed local Bakelite plugs not suitable since inside plug too easily checked by any FJ stealer with screwdriver.

2. Westinghouse molded plugs adaptable only for non-switched, unmasked transmitter. Since plain-language signal would be on air constantly, we believe such installation would be rapidly discovered by FJ stealers. In view of possible political flap, increased FJ stealer security, and possible jeopardy to other ongoing and potential audio operations in South America which could result from early compromise of Westinghouse plug, regret headquarters cannot approve its use. Other factors such as unknown target areas where machines will be installed and possibility they may be left unplugged for long periods of time also argue against operation.

End of message.
