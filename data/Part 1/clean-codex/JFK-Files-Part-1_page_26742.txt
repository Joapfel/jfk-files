Keel 72-ICO-28

From 0620, 3 May (Monday) to 0620, 4 May 1965.

2.0 Ingeborg Rico (V) answers. VKG comes to the phone. Call goes through with Pundora. VKG still has nothing. Expects he may call him early today and will call F. when he has something.

2.2 Out to 03. Time is 0821 (3 May).

2.5 Out to 25-80-32. VKG asks NO, who answers, for Dr. (Baillo) Corder. BC takes the phone. Asks if EC has any news. No. No one phoned him. A friend of his was in contact with a person in Santo Domingo—that Miraval's (Mirabal's) were killed. It is said that the Commission does not know what to do. They are looking for a Provisional President. But apparently the masses are demanding Bosch. They are still indecisive. There are now 14,000 men (armed), and VKG thinks there are more (14,000 marines?). BC says they have ordered another 4,000, some odd, to enter. Bosch has given the insurrection as finished and said that they should not fire mass against the U.S. and that he (Bosch) did not think that the U.S. had bad intentions. There are about 40 hurt among U.S. and about 4 or 5 dead.

VKG will call EC later from the magazine office. VKG will also leave the office number at home in case Juan B. (Mejia) phones him.

3.5 Out to 23-02-91. VKG (V) orders a cab.

3.7 Out to 34-88-63, three times. No rings, complete. Cuesta (maid) with MO, who answers, for MO's mother. MO takes the phone. Maid says that Sra. does not give enough food. Also Sra. (Dicner) is very demanding. Will leave in 13 days.

4.2 Out to 18-27-27. Maid asks for Antoulo. Not there. Maid is Cuesta.

4.5 Out to 24-92-11. Maid asks for Anita. She is out now.

4.7 In. Wrong number. Maid answers.

5.0 In. Wrong number. Maid answers.

5.2 In. Victor Rico Galan (VKG) asks maid, who answers, for Sra. Out. Has anyone phoned him? No.

After 1100, 3 May 1965.

5.3 Out to 47-55-20. Inge Rico orders gas. Contract 20870.

5.7 In. Sra. Rico with Inge Rico, who answers. Greetings. VKG is not there. Children.
