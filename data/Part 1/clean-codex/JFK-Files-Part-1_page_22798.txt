29 June 1961

Memorandum for: Contract Personnel Division

Subject: Amendment of Contract, case of Harold R. Remayer

It is requested that the Contract for Subject, as amended, be further amended to discontinue that portion of the “Compensation” section which states: “You will receive additional compensation, amounting to $30.00 per month, effective 1 January 1961, in lieu of payment of the regular overtime rates for irregular, unscheduled and frequent overtime, for the duration of this assignment.” It is requested that this discontinuance be effected C.O.B. 10 June 1961.

Herbert V. Juli  
Chief, WH/4/Personnel

Approved:

Fred F. Cyrus  
Chief, WH/4/Support
