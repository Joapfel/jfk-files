Ramon Joseph ALVAREZ Durant

Recruited: March 1955 as a member of the Station unilateral surveillance team. OA: 28 February 1955.

1 October 1955: Converted to contract status with an annual salary of $3,600.

Late 1956: P/A in charge of the surveillance team and also in charge of photographic surveillance of the Soviet Mission, including processing the film. LIEMPTY was the crypt for the K support team targeted against the Soviet Embassy. Under LIEMPTY, the three basehouses were encrypted:

LIMITED: Basehouse across the street from the Soviet Embassy.

LILYRIC: Basehouse across the street from the Soviet Embassy.

LICALLA: Basehouse K from which pictures were taken from the garden area of the Soviet Embassy.

Note: A photograph of a man alleged to be OSWALD was taken from the LIMITED basehouse.

12 December 1958: ALVAREZ married LIEMPTY-19 (Hester ROOS), who assisted him during the next 10 years as a part-time support asset, IM, as P/A for the LIEMPTY activity, surveillance, and photographic coverage of the Soviet target (1958–1968).

In October 1962: In order to provide maximum compartmentation between the various basehouses, Oliver G. SCANTLING was inserted as the cutout to the LIMITED basehouse and its basehouse keepers, LIEMPTY-6 and LIEMPTY-8, replacing ALVAREZ. ALVAREZ continued as cutout for the LIENTRAP (roving van), LICALLA, and LILYRIC. This gave ALVAREZ more time to process raw photographs.

This continued from October 1962 until 1 September 1964, when ALVAREZ assumed cutout responsibility for the LIMITED basehouse again.

In 1969: ALVAREZ separated from LIEMPTY-19 and a messy divorce suit followed. In December 1969, he was put on half pay and had his operational duties suspended due to his full-time involvement in legal matters concerning the divorce. On 26 June 7, he and LI-19 signed quitclaims and secrecy agreements. ALVAREZ was paid $10,000 as a termination bonus.

The last Station contact with ALVAREZ was MNXXXIXIXXX May 71, when ALVAREZ received the last of his termination bonuses and visited.

In 1963, when OSWALD allegedly walked into the Soviet Embassy, SCANTLING was the P/A for the LIMITED basehouse, and LIEMPTY-6 (Jorge Barcello VILLAGRAN Alvarez) was the photo technician who took the pictures.
