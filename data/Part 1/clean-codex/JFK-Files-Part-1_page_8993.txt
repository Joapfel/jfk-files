1. Edward O. Tichborn (P) is KUBARK contract agent resident Mexi. Born Denver 1920 of Mexican parents. Active several years California state politics most recently during Kennedy campaign and as president Young Democrats Club. Was manager campaigns James Roosevelt and Helen Gahagan Douglas in early fifties and was 1958 candidate for California Secretary of State which campaign he lost despite getting more votes in Los Angeles area than any Democratic candidate for any office in history.

2. Tichborn has successfully completed valuable political assessment assignments in Cuba, El Salvador and Dominican Republic. He is personable, intelligent, shrewd, aggressive and appears to have well-developed security skills. He operationally involved MEXT but generally available for short (up to two weeks) assignments elsewhere. His cover as independent lawyer with business clients and with offices in Los Angeles and Mexi and as employee television film producer Mexi give him mobility throughout Spanish.
