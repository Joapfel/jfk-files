Carlos Ariel and Julijana were there talking about the Human Rights Congress at the Del Prado.

Gallo told Subject they were interested in closer relations with Belize (Belice), i.e., mutual collaboration. He talked quite some time about the Cuban Revolution and what it was doing. He told Subject that he was very interested in his union and that it affiliate itself as soon as possible with the XXXXXX Confederación de Trabajadores Cubanos (CTC). In fact, he asked him to write out (dictate) a letter to Rosalío (Rosalíó) Rodríguez León regarding affiliation with the CTC. Subject told Gallo that he would discuss this with Jardón and that the two of them would come up with something regarding the CTC. Gallo told Subject that they would like the CDU (Christian Democratic Union) to be the link between the PGT in Guatemala and the CTC in Cuba. Gallo also asked Subject if he knew anything about a military buildup in Puerto Barrios. Subject believes that Gallo thinks that there has been a military buildup there. Subject told him he knew nothing about this, which is the truth. Gallo also asked about relations between B.H. and British Guiana.

Subject reported that Guillermo Trelor of Costa Rica went to Toluca on an urgent mission on 17 August 1961.

Subject said that Gallo, Rodríguez, and Bordas talked at some length in Spanish in his presence, but he was not able to understand what they were talking about. He cited the following items: something will happen in Mexico which will bring about another situation similar to Guantanamo (Guattanamo). Cárdeas (CARDEAS) was mentioned, and the name Mirandés (MIRANDÉS) of Fernandez (FERNANDEZ) was mentioned on several occasions. Also Oaxaca and Chilcungo.
