18. Cable DIRECTOR-15805, 29 June 1967, requested replies to a series of questions concerning AMCLEVE-15's activity with ROSENTHAL and the FBI. JMWAVE replied in JMWAVE-7953 (IN 02284), 30 June 1967. JMWAVE stated that AMCLEVE-15 had failed to report the October 1966 transactions with Rosenthal until 9 June 1967, and therefore the Miami FBI was not notified until 15 June 1967. JMWAVE stated that AMCLEVE-15's explanation for not reporting the October 1966 transaction was that he “just forgot.”
