4 February 1964

Chief, Security Branch

Greg Maruca

Lt. Van Buskirk, Metro Intelligence

To pass information and receive advice.

0930 hours, 4 February 1964, telephonically.

Lt. Van Buskirk advised that Clifford V. Connell, who was arrested about two months ago for illegal possession of about ½ tons of dynamite, will claim as his defense that he was performing a patriotic service for his country by working with Joseph Raymond Merola, who has on numerous occasions claimed to be working for KUBARK. The trial date has not yet been set. Others involved in the same case: Remer Estuez Odom and Harold Widden. Merola was born 10/9/25, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and possesses FBI #4989299. Van Buskirk wanted to know if any of the above are working for KUBARK. Results of Station traces are attached.

Advise CO. Advise Van Buskirk that the above individuals are not working for KUBARK.

Metro
