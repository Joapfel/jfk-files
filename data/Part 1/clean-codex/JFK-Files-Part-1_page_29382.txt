Technical Division (Technical School)

10-Minute Course (H-101)

Carrying Evaluation

Name: Grayson Lynch  
Office: SOD  
Course Dates: 19–22 May 1970

Course Description

This course touches the student to become more aware of the value in proper initial observation, the method of monthly recording the observations, and finally the mechanical manipulation of the identikit to provide a permanent record.

Evaluation

1. Student understands the principles of identification: Average Low.
2. Student understands the manipulation of the identikit: Average Low.
3. Ability to construct composites from photographs: Average Low.
4. Ability to construct composites from live observation: Not applicable to this meeting of the course.
5. Ability to construct composites by dehydrating: Average Low.
6. Ability to use composites to identify people in: Average Low.
   a. Photographs: Not applicable to this meeting of the course.
   b. Live situations: Not applicable to this meeting of the course.
7. Ability to derive composite code for transmission: Average Low.
8. Ability to reconstruct composite from identification code: Average Low.
9. Student's attitude, cooperation and productivity: Average Low.
