Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Bogota.  
Info: Chief, WH; Chief, FE; COS, Tokyo.  
From: Chief of Station, JMWAVE.  
Subject: TYPIC AMESCHOLL — Letter from Jose R. Mercado.  
Action required: FYI.

On 15 October Jose R. Mercado, President of the Colombian Confederation of Workers (CTC), discussed with AMWORD-3, the Secretary General of AMESCHOLL, the CTC's plans to do what it can to discourage Japan-Cuba trade by bringing influence to bear on the trade seminar in Tokyo that was to have begun on 19 October. Upon his return to Bogota, Sr. Mercado wrote a letter to the Japanese Ambassador to Colombia, a copy of which we forward together with a translation.

Philip G. Elmard.

Attachments: handwritten.

1. Letter (copy) as stated.
2. Translation of letter.
