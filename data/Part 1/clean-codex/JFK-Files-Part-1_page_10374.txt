26255Z November 1952. Cite Mexico City 7945. Director, Guatemala City.

1. LILING-3 received a brief typewritten unsigned letter dated 22 October from “Rafael Arguiza A.” The letter, which contains many typographical errors, expressed happiness that LILING-3 was finally released and hoped he would soon recover from the “gran angustia” caused by “my country.” The writer concluded with apologies for mistakes in orthography and expressed a desire to see LILING-3 again. He asked LILING-3 to reply to him at the following address: 2 Ave 27, 57, Zona 12, Guatemala C.A., rather than to the one appearing as the return address on the envelope.

2. The envelope is airmail stationery: “La Dalia Azul, Enrique Fuente C., Artículos para Caballeros, QU Avenida y 1CA Calle, Tel: 44-72, Guatemala, C.U.” with the return address on the back: Rafael Arguiza A., 11 Av. 25 22, Zona 12, Guatemala C.A. The envelope is postmarked 22 October 1963 and is addressed to LILING-3 at Calle NAVE 7, Col. Juarez, Mexico, D.F. (Salón de la Plástica Mexicana, which LILING-3 was given to Juarez and Miguel Leon Barrios as best secret).
