17 April 1958

Memorandum for: Chief, Office of Security  
Via: Chief, CI/DA  
From: C/WED  
Subject: Harold R. HOBKIN (po)  
C-25507

It is requested that the CSA granted Harold R. HOBKIN (po), formerly called Arthur O. YALVADA (po), on 3 July 1957 for use in Cuba under Project AMPHORARCH, be amended to permit his use in Mexico City. HOBKIN will be transferred PCS to Mexico City in May 1958 where he will be carried under Project LINEAGE which is being prepared for submission to FI/Projects for approval. Attached is a new PRQ Part II which covers our proposed use of HOBKIN in Mexico.

J. C. KING  
Chief, WED

D01-16881
