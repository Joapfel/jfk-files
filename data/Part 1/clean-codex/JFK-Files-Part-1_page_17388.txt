Originator: C. Bustos. Unit: H/3/Mexico. Extension: 6026. Date: 25 September 1962.

Classified message.

To: Mexico City, Rome.

From: Director.

Conf.: C/WH 5.

Info: ADDP, C/CI, C/FI 2, C/WE 2, C/FE 6, S/C 2.

To: Will be info Rome routine. Cite DIR 30128.

For MEXI (NEXI) 1773 (IN 10046).

1. IDEN A, PANAM security officer Rome, has just been assigned to company PANAM plane chartered by Mexican President Lopez Mateos for Far East trip. IDEN A leaves for Hong Kong today, will arrive MEXI approximately 30 September. Here can be contacted at PANAM operations office.

2. IDEN A, KUBARK agent (former staffer), offers to be of assistance during Lopez trip.

3. If Station MEXI has any requirements it wishes to place on IDEN A in connection with Lopez trip, Station can contact him in Mexico City through PANAM operations (officer using name IDEN B, KUBARK staffer, in introduction).

4. Please cable headquarters whether Station plans to make contact so IDEN A can be informed in Hong Kong to expect KUBARK contact MEXI. Please pouch headquarters copy of instructions passed IDEN A.

End of message.

WH Comment: President Lopez Mateos plans departure for Far East trip on 26 September 1962.

C/NE/Liz y-fart

FLCH/Flitcroft (none)

(C/NO)

Releasing officer.

Coordinating officers.

Authenticating officer.
