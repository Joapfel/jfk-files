Dear Mr. Watts:

Re: Congress for Cultural Freedom

In connection with the travel to the Congress for Cultural Freedom which you are sponsoring for Mr. and Mrs. James Burnham, the servicing office has advised us that Mr. Burnham has requested a side trip to Stockholm.

Will you kindly advise us whether this additional transportation is for the account of the Foreign Service Educational Foundation or for the account of the passengers.
