Classified message. Origin: C. Finnley:bc. Unit: SK/0/81. Extension: 6412. Date: 26 June 1964. File in CS file No. 201-286805. Form dated 12-62.

To: Mexico City. From: Director. CONF: SR 7. Info: CI, CI/CPS, CI/OA, Fl. 7, VR.

Rio de Janeiro. Cite DIR 31926. To MEXI. Info RIOD. REDWOOD AECOTTAGE.

References: A. MEXI 0021 (IN 14984). B. RIOD 5073 (IN 12837).

1. OA for LICOOKY/1 amended to authorize one-time use against KOBYSH as proposed in paragraph one reference.

2. KOBYSH born 27 July 1928, Poltava Oblast. Wife Emma. Son Sergey, age about 6. From phone tap information learned subject has occasional disagreements with wife. She accuses him of infidelity. Wife on bad terms with other Sovs in Rio because son suffers undermined amoeba disease, which other Sovs fear communicable. Hence they have their children shun son KOBYSH. Also aggravating situation is wife suffering hepatitis.

3. Career subject: member, radio delegation, Norway 1954. Assigned Moscow 1955. Also in 1955 visited Sweden and Denmark with Soviet pianist. Returned Norway 1956. Assigned New York September to November 1960. Member of Khrushchev party meeting Kennedy, Vienna, June 1961. Assigned Brazil circa August 1962.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer. Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
