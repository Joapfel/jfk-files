After that I ran with a friend (RADCHENKO) home in Moscow (January 1944). In Moscow I studied at the courses (Russian word), finished 9th class, and was accepted again in the military-navy preparatory school, which was located in Leningrad. In August 1944 I went to Leningrad.

All cadets of this school were sent to the forest, about 200 km from Leningrad, to prepare wood for winter, where we stayed for two months. In November I wounded my left hand by chance and was put in the navy hospital. While I was in the hospital, I decided not to return to the school but to finish 10th class in Leningrad, about which I had written a letter to my father, asking his help and agreement with my decision. With the help of my father's friends, I quit the school and entered the shipbuilding college in the second course in January 1945 and studied there until the end of May. World War II finished, and I decided to return to Moscow. The director of the shipbuilding college gave me a document stating that I had studied in this college in the second course and finished this course, though I had not passed the exams. In Leningrad I lived in the hostel of this college (St. Tolmachev).

In May 1945 I arrived in Moscow and lived with my parents (St. Granovski, 3).
