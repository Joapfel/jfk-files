Classified message. From: Mexico City. Action: clWDG. Info: FILE VER, ADDP, C/SRS, C/CI, C/FIZ.

Subject to leave 20 April 80 for Aeroweek trip, New York; Washington. 23 June ’65 M 96432.

SAPOK REDCOAT. IMPROVE LICOZY 5. Ref REXI 31139. IN 6/11/59. Per GSN 43-45.

1. Subject returned next 18 June. Nechiporenko and Kostikov at his office 14 June arranged embassy reservations. Had lunch with N 17 June.

2. Re European trip, W asked if had looked up tour operator associates. Got general affirmative from subject, who explained conducted little business since with family. Out of the blue, W asked if subject knew Ginette Palacci of Sirtam Voyages, 49 Rue St. Roche, Paris. L said superficially acquainted; she was about 27, of Jewish origin, left Cairo after Suez Crisis, went to Paris after staying in Rohe. B said she was of interest since married Brit about a few months ago, and Brit is a NATO employee. Instructed subject to assess and cultivate when next sees her. L said did not catch Brit’s name as given by M.

3. M advised subject of his “promotion” to captain and said would be advised more formally of same by Prikhodko or higher-level person if subject visits Moscow.
