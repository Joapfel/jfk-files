25 September 1958.

Memorandum for: Director of Personnel  
Via: Director of Security  
Attention: Chief, Policy Staff  
Subject: Gifts to Agency Personnel  
Reference: Field Regulation 20-644

The following Agency personnel received gifts as noted from three Cabinet Research Chamber visitors during their recent visit to Washington:

Satoshi Nagano: Box of nori, approximately $2.00; set of chopsticks (lacquered), $0.75.

William V. Broe: One woodblock print, $1.50.

Robert P. Wheeler: Box of nori, $2.00.

William N. Center: One woodblock print, $3.00.

Nageob O. Trabulsi: Woodblock print, $1.50; one jar Japanese sauce, $0.50.

Deshond Fitzgerald  
Chief, Far East Division
