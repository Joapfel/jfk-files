Country: Cuba. Report No. CS CI-316/00696-3. Subject: Shipment of explosives to Mexico. Date distributed: 25 August 1964. Number of pages: 1. Date of information: 1964. Place and date acquired: Washington, D. C., August 1964.

This is unevaluated information. Source gradings are definitive; appraisal of content is tentative.

Source: A former Cuban intelligence service officer who served with the Cuban Intelligence Service until April 1964.

Following is a translation of a report which the source wrote concerning a shipment of explosives to Mexico:

“The Communist Party of El Salvador had asked me to send them twenty-five, one-quarter-pound packages of gelatin explosives. They requested that the explosives be sent to Mexico, where they would recover them and take them to El Salvador.

“In view of the request, I spoke with Manuel Piñeiro (PINEIRO) regarding the shipment itself, as well as the amount. Piñeiro (PINEIRO) answered by telling me to do so.

“During my trip to Mexico in March 1964, I spoke with Rogelio Rodríguez López (RODRIGUEZ Lopez) regarding this shipment, and he told me that there was no need for me to send the explosives to Mexico, as this type of explosive was available in the Embassy. I then told Rodríguez (RODRIGUEZ) that I would answer the request from El Salvador.

“Upon returning to Cuba, I spoke with Piñeiro (PINEIRO), who told me not to send the explosives, as the orders now were to send nothing but money due to the recent exposure of the arms shipment to Venezuela.”

Group I: Transferred from another service and forwarded.
