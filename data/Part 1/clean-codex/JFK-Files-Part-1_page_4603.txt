Dispatch.

All-City and National Media Call.

S-103/CIA Cuban ballot rally held in New York on 19 February 1967.

Action required: Home, FYI.

1. Attached herewith is AMOT report number Dk-2068, dated 18 February 1967, subject: rally held in New York on 18 February 1967 under the sponsorship of the Cuban Nationalist Association (ANC) and the Mirr-Comandoo L Alliance.

2. As noted in the report, Felipe DRIVERO Dias, ANC leader, and Orlando BACH Avila, MIPR leader, violently attacked the United States and CIA during the rally.

Philip G. ELMARD.
