Incoming Classified Message. Central Intelligence Group.

From: Tsingtao. To: Special Operations.

Routine. 4 September 17. IN 22127.

TSIN 009.

To: WASHF. Cite: TSIN.

Re WASH 5513 (OUT 50380): Has received no reply from Tientsin regarding cargoes.

Re WASH 5833 (OUT 50695): He and family presently well.

Reports himself and family ready to proceed Cairen any time passage can be secured. Has made every effort to reach destination. Difficulty caused by Russian refusal to allow entry to other than Russian ships. Present plan is to proceed Vladivostok by Russian ship and transfer there to ship bound for Cairen. Applications for visa to Vladivostok for him and family requested but not yet granted. Does not desire other post if possibility of providing Cairen in near future. Herewith acknowledges receipt.
