UFGA-21693/14.

Subject: LOFORTÉ, f/m, C.I. Cuba (C.I. Cube), a/n Jimmy.

1. Source met Subject in early 1958 while both were working at the Presidential Palace. About 1960, Subject told Source that he, Subject, was working at the Ministerio de Interior, at the department of Foreigners resident in Cuba. Source last saw Subject about February 1964, when Source went to the Ministerio de Interior to secure his exit permits. They spoke for a few minutes, and Source is not able to provide any details on Subject's position there. Source is unable to provide any information concerning Subject's political ideology, if Subject has traveled outside Cuba, if Subject has any relatives outside Cuba, or if Subject has ever received any political, military, or intelligence training.

2. Physical description: DPOB: circa 1940, Guantánamo, Oriente. Race: white. Height: 5'7"; weight: 130 lbs. Hair: black. Eyes: dark. Face: oval. Physique: slim. No visible scars. May be identified as Jimmy LOFORTÉ.

3. JEWAVE traces:

a. One Jimmy LOFORTÉ is either a member of, or the president of, the Comité Para la Defensa de la Revolución, Committee for the Defense of the Revolution, for a sector of Infante Street in Havana (UFGA 3&13, 15 March 1963).

b. Jimmy LOFORTÉ is a member of the Cuban I.B. (UFGA 17532 8-1, 28 August 1964).
