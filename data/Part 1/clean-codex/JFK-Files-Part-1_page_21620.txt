17. Kipalkin—a general; in 1850, he was a deputy chief of the Military Diplomatic Academy. He had two sons: Kapalkin Sergey, who finished this academy in 1950 and works in Yekaterinburg and France; his wife is the daughter of General Timoshenko, Olga, who finished the Institute of Foreign Trade. Kapalkin Victor studied as a student at the UH in 1940 and also works in Yekaterinburg.

18. Kubanov Vladimir—a major and senior officer of the naval intelligence of the 7th Fleet in 1950–52.

19. Khaberov—in 1950–52, deputy chief of the naval judicial detachment of the naval intelligence of the 7th Fleet.

20. Khaytor S.S.—deputy chief for information of the naval intelligence of the 7th Fleet in 1950–52.

21. Kon’kor—deputy chief of the naval intelligence of the 7th Fleet in 1951–52.

22. Kozlovskyi—in 1950, an officer of the Department of Foreign Affairs of the Ministry of Defense.

23. Molchanov—an officer of YHLL (in 1950).

24. Orcher Gennadiy—in 1951–52, an administrative officer of the naval intelligence of the 7th Fleet.

25. Pogedin Boris—chief of the secretariat of the naval intelligence of the 7th Fleet in 1950–1952; knows the Japanese language.

26. Rezantsev—commander of the naval intelligence point of the intelligence of the 4th Fleet in Sovietk (Sovietsk), Kaliningrad District, in 1952.

27. Shalin—a general; in 1940, he was chief of the Military Diplomatic Agency and later chief of YKH. MULIKA.

28. Shaposhnikov Ivolg—a general who works in Yekaterinburg; his father was a marshal.
