Ref: A. Director 896637. B. Director 899232.

1. EMCLIPPER-1 has advised Station that Orlando Bosch called on him a few weeks ago to request that Dominican Government issue visas to his mother and sister to visit him in Santo Domingo. (Visas were subsequently issued by Consulate in Miami (MAIMI).) On this occasion EMCLIPPER-1 asked Bosch if he was entered Dominican Republic with false Dominican passport. Denying this, Bosch brandished Costa Rican passport. When EMCLIPPER-1 asked to see passport, it was in name of Carlos Luis Paniagua Mendez and photo is good likeness of Bosch. Bosch said he was missed operation for cataracts and should be leaving Dominican Republic shortly.

2. Station received report from EMPYREX written by EMPYREX-8 (in Miami (MAIMI)) stating that Frank Castro passed the criminal background check on 20 August 76.
