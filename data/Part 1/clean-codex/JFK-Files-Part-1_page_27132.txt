Synopsis

Subject, a Cuban refugee, entered the U.S. on 8/9/60 from Colombia. Subject defected from the Castro government while employed as Cuban Naval Attaché in Caracas, Venezuela. Subject is a former graduate of the Cuban Naval Academy and in exile has been involved in considerable anti-Castro clandestine activity against Cuba. Subject has affiliated with various anti-Castro organizations in the Miami, Florida, area and in 11/62 was reported to be associated with an individual having contacts with a Guatemala government official.
