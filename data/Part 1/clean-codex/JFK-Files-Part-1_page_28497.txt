6 February 1953

To: Overseas Branch

P.O. FBM

Subject: Cancellation of Transportation — Tenant E. Bagley

It is requested that all travel arrangements for Mr. Bagley be cancelled. The Division is changing his assignment, and he will not be sent to Karlsruhe, Germany.

Elizabeth C. Dunlevy  
For: Chief, PBM

Transportation Chairman. Received 5 February 1953.
