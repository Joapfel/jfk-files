Classified message.

Routing.

To: Director. From: JAWAVE. Action: Dcl/0mfsA 3. Info: DDP, VR.

DESMOITANTE. JUN 64 IN 95616.

01228Z. DIR CIRC WAVE 7218. Remark: TYPIC YOBITE AMWORLD AMRAZZ-1.

1. On 27 May 64 AMRAZZ-1 said AMBIDDY-1 had reportedly bought his mistress, Ofelia Padron, an $85,000 house in North Miami. Padron, according to AMRAZZ-1, has been mistress of various persons during and after the Batista regime.

2. AMRAZZ-1 also said Tony Bustillo, former “third chief of police Habana” early in Castro’s regime, had told him he had heard that AMBIDDY-1’s followers were authorized to attack any of AMBANG-1’s or Gutierrez’s men if encountered on the high seas.

3. On 28 May AMRAZZ-1 advised he had been informed by “Piano” Borrego who, according to the source, is a member of AMBIDDY-1’s group but friendly to SFNE, that AMBIDDY-1 planned to make a hit-and-run raid between 29 or 30 May. Borrego did not reveal sources.
