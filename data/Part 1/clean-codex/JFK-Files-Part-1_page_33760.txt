TO: GEARING

Regarding Sperling. His debriefing for many reasons should not be done here. Would appreciate your doing with the object of getting as complete a story as possible, but with special attention to:

1. Method of programming, control exercised in Munich, and control of Munich by New York in as detailed form as possible.

2. What émigré (amigre) groups are favored, if any.

3. What relations are with our people, British, and Germans.

4. What is the setup of news gathering?

5. What security procedures are followed in Munich?

6. What has been or is being done to determine the effectiveness of programs from all angles: (1) technical, (2) content, (3) audience reaction, etc.
