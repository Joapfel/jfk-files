18 April 1975

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Werbell

1. On 17 April 1975, Annet, DDO/FIO, called DCD on the green line to relay the following OGC priority request.

Please check Agency records for:

a. Mitchell Werbell, IV  
Date and place of birth: 16 November 1949; Atlanta, Georgia

b. Godfrey Werbell  
Date and place of birth: 21 June 1951; Atlanta, Georgia

These two have been charged with crimes and alleged they work for CIA.

2. After checking DCD's records, I found that DCD has had contact with the subject's father in the past. DCD's records reflect no direct contact with the sons. The only mention of the sons appears in an article published in The Washington Post, 27 December 1970, Section D, page 3, and a cable to FR/NY, 17 March 1975 (Director 670514).

3. DDO/FIO was notified of the above by telephone on 17 April 1975.

Distribution: Original—A 127900; 1—DCD/FIO; 1—DC/DCD
