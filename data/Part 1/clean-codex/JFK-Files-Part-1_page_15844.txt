Dispatch. Auto info from: Chief of Base, Sao Paulo; Chief of Station, Rio de Janeiro; Chief of Station, Leopoldville. Subject: Chief, Africa Division; Operational/ZINEV/WIROGUE. Action required: See below. Reference: BDA-4090, dated 12 October 1960; COSM 43-43.

1. This is to advise you that subject of reference has been transferred to the African Division and been assigned the cryptos WIRGOGUE/1 (WIGROGUE/1). The latter is presently in Leopoldville and all correspondence received through your accommodation address is to be passed to COS, Leopoldville.

2. WIRGOGUE/1 was instructed to send only unregistered letters and to instruct his correspondents to do the same.

Charles R. Roustady

Paul E. Leighford

Distribution: Orig. & 1 — COB, Sao Paulo; 1 — COS, Rio de Janeiro; 1 — COS, Leopoldville.
