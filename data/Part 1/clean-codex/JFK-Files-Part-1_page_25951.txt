Outgoing Message

Signal Center and Only.

To release Otero at noon on 20 January. US Embassy reported that as of 22 January it was not clear where Otero was located. One source reportedly told Miami Herald that Otero had left the Dominican Republic, but Miami Herald correspondent who at that time (22 January) was in Santo Domingo told CONGEN he had drinks with Otero the night before. No information since that time on Otero's whereabouts.

2. Because of references B and C, information regarding Otero's activities in Caracas indicates there is a possibility he may be in Venezuela again. In light of reference A, paragraph two, and at request of Secret Service, request addressees attempt to find out if Otero is in either country. HQs will inform Secret Service of results. Otero described as white, five feet eight inches tall, 150 pounds; bushy dark brown hair; brown eyes.
