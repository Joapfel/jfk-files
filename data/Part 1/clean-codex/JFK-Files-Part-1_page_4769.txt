Quigley Message.

To: Santo Domingo. Info: Santiago, San Jose, Caracas, Buenos Aires.

Ref: Santo Domingo 17355 (IN 9083920).

1. State, FBI and Secret Service notified that Bosch now in DOMREP. While they welcome whatever information is obtained, they do not now have priority interest in Bosch and do not expect the Agency to make effort to cover him.

2. FYI. Secret Service has advised us that although Bosch is wanted in the U.S. for parole violation, Department of Justice considers him to be an “unwanted alien” and U.S. Government would probably decline any offers for extradition tendered by another country. Most recent interest grew from reports that he was possibly involved in plot to kill nephew of former Chilean President Salvador Allende while Kissinger was touring Latin America in February 1976. Since this would have embarrassed the U.S., efforts were made to prevent it. He is not considered a threat to U.S. officials, however, and we see no reason to harry him from place to place. Reporting on travel from DOMREP would still be welcome.
