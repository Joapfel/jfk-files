Orig: RIA PERKIL3. Unit: SR/2/CE. Ext: 8757. Date: 15 November 1960.

To: FBI. From: Director. Conf: SR 6. Info: C1, C1/CPS, F1, TSD 4, WE 4, S/C 2.

Classified message.

Cite DIR.

To FBI, info cite DIR: SEMEN POBORILIW.

1. Usually reliable source in Paris, France, reported early November 1960 that DMYTRO GNYP asked source to deliver two letters to ARTIN NYCZYPORUK, was HYKYPRZUK and NYKPORETS, who confirmed metal sanitarium near Paris. GNYP told source he made request because both GNYP and NYCZYPORUK had been in contact with VASILIY MYAKUSEKO, RIS officer with UNESCO, who was case officer GRIGORY DEMYDCZUK, Ukrainian émigré arrested by French in August 1960. See your memo 12 September 1960.

2. Our field office examined letters and found one letter from HOGINSK USER advising NYCZYPORUK his wife and daughter not in HOGINSK, no evidence of secret writing. Other letter from SEMEN POBORILIW of 613 N. 7th St., Philadelphia 25, bore indications of secret writing. If source able to retain letter, field office will reproduce and attempt to develop secret writing.

Continued.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
