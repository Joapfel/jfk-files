137. Electrocardiographical Alterations in the Transventricular Mitral Commissurity

By Drs. A. Hernandez Canero, Dr. M. Joaquín Mon and Dr. J. Castillo Navarro.

Professor of Internal Medicine, Head of the Cardiology Department of the Comandante Manuel Fajardo (Farjardo) Training Hospital.

Assistant Specialists, Department of Cardiology of the Comandante Manuel Fajardo (Farjardo) Training Hospital.

142. Conclusions

1. The electrocardiograms corresponding to 33 cases of mitral stenosis operated transventricularly were studied.

2. The rhythm variations, as well as the favorable changes in the auricular and ventricular complex are those which, according to other authors, are present in patients operated transauricularly.
