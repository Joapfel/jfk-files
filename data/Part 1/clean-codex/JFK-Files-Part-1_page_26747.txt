4 May 1963

6.7 In, from long distance. VAC (v) answers, but operator does not speak. Wai hagga up.

In, from long distance. VAG (v) answers. Habana is calling. Call goes through.

VAD with Fundora. VAS has nothing. Has not been able to communicate with these people. That is why he did not phone Fundora. VAG expects some news today. Will call Fundora (when he dries). Fine. Something about bulletins. VAS says no (did not get the?). Go to sea Jarden (for them). VAS will do so.

7.2 Out to 43-52-81, twice. No rings, incomplete. Ingo nico asks “NO,” who answers, for Sra. (nose—sister-in-law).

Sosa takes the phone. Sosa phoned in the morning at 0900.

7.7 Out to 46-43-24. VAS with NO, who answers at “Siempre,” for Georgina.

NO takes the phone. A Srita. will go by to see VAC at 1200. Ask her to wait for him. VAS will get there a little late, but he will go there.

8.0 Out to 23-02-91. VAS orders a cab.

8.3 Out to 24-92-11, twice. Busy.

After 1240, 4 May 1963

8.5 In. 40 with Ingo nico, who answers, for VAC. Not there. He said he would be home for lunch, but late. Had several things to do. NO will call back later.

After 1430, 4 May

8.7 In. Sra. Torres Alta-Isaacs asks VAC (v), who answers, for Sra. Biener.

Sra. I. takes the phone. NO is mother of Sra. Torres Alta-Isaacs. Won’t take the class.

9.0 In. 40, calling for VAC. Marcia, with Sra. Diener (v), who answers, for VAC. Not there. Tell him that “Politics” called.

9.2 In. Irina asks VAC, who answers, for Sra. Ir.

IR takes the phone. Greetings. About going to see some dresses.

9.5 In. 40 asks IR, who answers, for VAC. Not there. Did she tell VAC about his call this morning? Yes, but some people came by for him and he left in a hurry. She expects him for lunch. NO hopes to see him this afternoon. She will be at home. She will tell him.

9.7 In. Wiric with IR, who answers, for VAC. Not there. Will have Yali phone Wiric.

10.0 Out to 24-74-33. VAS asks MO, who answers, for Capt. Teresona (ph).

MO takes the phone. Greetings. MO says they are preparing to leave tomorrow. Would like to see VAC. VAS has a lot of work. Ask MO to come by. VAS does not have a car either. VAS has some cigarets.
