23 February 1957

Memorandum for: Chief, Finance Division

Subject: Equalisation Allowance Adjustment for Phillips, Howard

1. Effective 13 January 1957, subject individual's equalization allowance is decreased from $3780 to $3145 per annum due to a reduced cost of living index for subject's post.

2. All other terms and conditions of the original authorisation remain in full force and effect.

Signed, Ralph S. Pollock, Chief, Contract Personnel Division.
