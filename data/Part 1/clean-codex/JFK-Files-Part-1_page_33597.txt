American Committee for United Europe, Inc.

April 27, 1951

Finances: A financial audit for the nearly twenty-one-month period from inception, February 16, 1949, to October 31, 1950, shows: cash contribution income, $210,919; disbursements, $173,072. Disbursements include grants to European groups, $95,651; European conferences, $11,963; popular education, $19,674; fund-raising expenses, $26,201; and administrative expenses, $38,583. Cash balance, October 31, 1950, was $17,846.

Contributions to the American Committee are reported to be exempt for income tax purposes.

Budget for the year to October 31, 1951, totals $150,000, of which $100,000 is for European operations.

Comment and conclusion: It is difficult to measure the influence of organizations undertaking to influence the mass public; however, we can report that the Bureau knows of no reason why those interested should not contribute to this Committee.
