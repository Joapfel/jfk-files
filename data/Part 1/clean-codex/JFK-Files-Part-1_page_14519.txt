Ch. Dr. Venage, Cuban, gave R ticket to return to LIS from DR.

He is applied to Committee philosophy. In group, the university students had a fight w/Committees. A/O 12 July 57 wanted to obtain permanent residence visa in U.S. 5 Nov 57 notified he had to leave by 4 Dec 57.

25 Sep 57, living w/son & child at 1440 NW 21st Terrace. Du Nov 57, at 1749 1/2 NW 21st St., Miami. Le M-in-h had formal interview.

Overstayed his time. 11 Feb. 58, ordered deported to Cuba. Represented a/o 14 Feb 58 by firm pastor, Moore & Costanzo (1008 Ansley Bldg.). Notified 4 times of interrogation but had increased addn. mark a/o 25 Jul 58. Order for detention issued 21 Aug 58. Order for deportation issued 6 Sep 58.

Investigation showed he returned & went to Calle 5 #714 on 2 Mar 58 on an expedition by plane. A/c 6 Apr 59 living at Calle 86 #705, Apt. 1, Mirellos.
