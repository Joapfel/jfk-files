Dispatch. Processing action: marred for inking.

To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Info: Chief, European Division; GPI; no determined. From: Chief of Station, WH/Miami; COS, Lisbon. Subject: Chief of Station, Madrid OK.

AIDAS. TYPIC QUTIMID. CORREA. FELIPE DE ECHEVARRIA.

Action required—references. Reference: MADRID 1230.

1. QUTIMID periodically has reported contact between Subject and Guillermo RUIZ Perez, the Cuban Minister in Madrid, mostly over the telephone but also indicating that he has seen her personally. It would appear that he has taken unusually strong interest in her, considering the number of other more demanding tasks he claims to have. Until now, we have not been able to identify either Subject or her relationship with RUIZ.

2. On 28 May QUDOZE (a sensitive joint audio penetration with liaison) reported the following memorandum (presumably to Havana) on Subject as dictated by RUIZ:

“Sr. RUIZ Ojeda. Some months ago we received a ciphered cable dealing with Aida Rosa CORREA Felipe asking that we contact her to determine her situation. At that time we informed you that said Aida Rosa CORREA, married to Pedro Echevarria (EHEVARRIA), Portuguese citizen who was in Cuba during 1959–1968, intended to return to our country, abandoning her husband because of his alcoholism.

“Approximately one week ago the brother of Aida Rosa CORREA—Luis CORREA Felipe—passed through Madrid en route to France on a special mission. He spoke with her at the Airport about definitely returning in order to see that her 12-year-old son might be educated in Cuba.”

Distribution: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division; Chief, European Division; Chief of Station, WH/Miami; Chief of Station, Lisbon.

Cross-reference to: JJ-65-2-10/9. Dispatch symbol and number: OSMA-26265. Date: 3 June 1970. HCS file number: 65-6-44/3.
