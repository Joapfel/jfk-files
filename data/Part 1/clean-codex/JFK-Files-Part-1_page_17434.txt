Classified message.

Date: 30 March 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/TFW 5. Info: DDP, COP, C/C1, C/F1, C/WH 2, S/C 2.

30 March 0522 62. Routine. IN 11142. DIF CITY NEXI OASIS. RYBAT PEPRUKEN. Reference: DIF CC0003 (SJT 65710).

1. COS, in conversation with LIELEGANT on 25 March, brought up the question of asylum in the Mexican Embassy in KABANA. LIELEGANT said included is former “President” Manuel Urrutia Leo.

2. In response to a question, LIELEGANT stated he feels LITENSOR would make a personal request for safe conduct of Urrutia if COS goes to LITENSOR with the suggestion.

3. COS recommends he be authorized to see LITENSOR and make the request.

4. Could LITENSOR be told Urrutia could get a U.S. visa if brought to Mexico and, if wished, go to the U.S.A.?

End of message.

C/S comment: Dissemination applicable to RYBAT GYROSE permanent cables.

Those requested State views are currently feasibility and best channel of approach to LITENSOR for request he attempt to arrange safe conduct out of Cuba for ex-President Manuel Urrutia.
