7. Financially, one possible problem is evident in the new arrangement. Headquarters has limited the costs of the LIEFERACE Project to US$ 50,000 per year and it will not be possible for the Project to remain within this figure with the LIENTRAP vehicle and personnel included therein. The Station does not see this as a great problem because the Operational Program includes the cost of those assets in the LIEMPTY Project and the transfer will merely mean a decrease in LIEMPTY equal to the increase in LIEFERACE.
