26 February 1969

Memorandum for the File

Subject: New Soviet Embassy Car

LILYRIC reported that Valentin Sergeyevich LOGINDV (P-11712) entered the Soviet Embassy on 23 February 1969 in a 1969 Chevrolet Chevelle (Cheyvella). LOGINDV, according to LIENVOY, was scheduled to go to the border the week of 16 February 1969 to pick up his new car.

Files: 50-8-10/7. Copy: P-11712.
