1. Re ref para 7: CHANA desk rep met GREBONY for assessment with view toward his possible later use against Ghana target. Discussion Hodes Edwards was general probing of GREBONY relationship and estimate her personality. While GREBONY may have referred possibility related operation JACOB’S COMMISSION, WOFACT rep avoided suggesting such was planned much less detain. In fact, desk has not developed such detailed HQS plans for GREBONY operation CHANA, given uncertainties in developing situation on ground. When and if clear focus such operation worked out and agreed with Accra station, West EM Africa trip would presumably be first step.

2. While such trip mentioned to GREBONY as probably useful, emphasized to GREBONY that Vietnam is top priority at this time. Strongly feel he should sit tight and play out this string without planning for extensive Africa swings, particularly after having just returned from a VOLADY TDY. With Viet trip and Russell Foundation more dedicated higher priority targets close to home at this time, see little value and unnecessary risks in overextending him in wide Africa swings of LEO3 dedicated interest. This even more true for East Africa given station views.
