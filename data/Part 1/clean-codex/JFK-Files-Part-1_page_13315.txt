19 EST PART OF PETARE. One of them, Carlos José' Gonzalez, knocked on the door of a corner house close to the Nazareno Church. Nobody answered, so he went around to the back of the house, but the Communist shot him down. The other agents surrounded (SURRENDER) the house with submachine gun and a shooting started. Fabricio (El Loco) was in the house; he was firing with a 765 pistol.
