To info, cite DIR XAAZ-13491.

Subject: Maurice and David Halperin.

1. According to a usually reliable source, on 2 October 1962 Maurice Halperin wrote Margaret and Albert Maltz, giving his new address as Apt C-2, Calle 12, No. 103, Miramar, Habana. He was working in a textile factory but planned to teach at the University of Habana beginning 8 October.

2. His son David had a residency in surgery in a Veterans' Hospital in Chicago but was discharged last month for “reasons that had nothing to do with his competence.”

3. This information has been made available to the Bureau representative in Mexico.

End of message.
