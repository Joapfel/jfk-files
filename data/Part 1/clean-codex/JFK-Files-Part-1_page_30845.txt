Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Date.

Subject: Chief, WU Division, ADMIN/CONTRACTS—Jeremy K. SENADAM, retirement annuity.

Action required references:

1. The Retirement Office has advised that Jeremy K. SENADAM's annuity check for January will be an interim payment representing about 75% of basic annuity.

2. The full annuity could not be paid pending receipt of verification of military service and billing for non-deduction civilian service between 1 June 1942 and 1 March 1943. Headquarters has been checking with the Retirement Office on a continuous basis but as yet the information has not been received. Upon receipt of the required info, you will be promptly advised.

3. Please assure SENADAM that he will receive retroactive payment in a subsequent check for full entitlement.

Norman R. PETERSILL.
