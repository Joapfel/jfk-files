Classified message.

Unit: WH/3/Caribbean. Extension: 2056. Date: 1 July 1958. Received.

To: Mexico City. From: Director. Confidential. Info.

OUT 95479. OUT 85479.

To Mexico City.

Info Havana (Hava).

Cite DIR 29005.

Request Harold R. Noemayr report to headquarters immediately for consultation prior to approximately two weeks TDY Cuba to reestablish contact Fidel (Ideal) Castro. Above mission related to kidnapping of U.S. citizens. Call Mr. Robert Reynolds on arrival. Advise ETA.

End of message.

WH/3/MEXI.

Coordinating officers.

J. C. King, C/WH.

Releasing officer.

R. N. Dahlgen, C/WH/3.

Authenticating officer.
