Personnel Evaluation Report.

Items 1 through 10 will be completed by Administrative or Personnel Officer.

Name: Richardson, Jacques. Grade: G. Position title: GS-12 I.O. (Dep. Chief). Office, department, staff or division: SR. Branch: SR-5 Far East. Department: Dept. L. Period covered by report: From 2-18-52 to 2-18-53. Type of report: Annual. Reassignment. Reassignment of Supervisor.

Items 7 through 10 will be completed by the person evaluated.

List your major duties in approximate order of importance, with a brief description of each; omit minor duties.

Assist the Branch Chief in management of the Branch and its overseas stations (3); at present, I act as Branch Chief about 25%-30% of the year. Supervise the planning and preparation for approval of all basic plans, programs, and projects. This involves thorough discussion with each of seven (7) desk chiefs of the purposes and procedures for accomplishing any single mission under any given project, including target analysis (whether FI or PP), preparation of intelligence annexes, logistic and fiscal requirements, specific personnel needs, etc. Am responsible for working directly with the Division's Intelligence Branch for control of requirements, intelligence, and reports. Am responsible for maintaining liaison with appropriate staffs and other Divisions who must coordinate any action or document. Assist or direct, as required, specific mission planning at overseas stations on TDY. Supervise preparation of all periodic reports; interview candidate employee.

8. List courses of instruction completed during report period.

Name of course: International Politics, Graduate School. Location: Georgetown University, Washington. Length of course: 4 months; 3 hours' credit. Date completed: 5 June 1952.

In what type of work are you primarily interested?

I am primarily interested in the conception, planning, and operational management of FI and PP activities, and in policy and control.
