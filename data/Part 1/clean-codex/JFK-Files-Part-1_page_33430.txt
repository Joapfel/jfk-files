Allenco regime. While waiting for instructions from Moscow, Central Committee decided [to] take certain measures, such as sending six organizers into Chile. These organizers were members of Peruvian Communist Youth, who used passports obtained earlier from Chilean Embassy in case of such an emergency. Passports were legitimate, but data in them, including names, were false. The Peruvians were to cross into Chile at the Tacna/Arica border point and were to be accompanied by a number of Bolivians and Brazilians.
