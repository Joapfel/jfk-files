Outgoing Message.

Classification. Date-time group. Staff. Conf. Info. File. Director.

Discontinue any contact with these individuals pending further detailed analysis of each of these cases. Due to the sensitive nature of this matter, this topic will be discussed in greater detail at the RDS conference at HQTRS on 23 February 1976. A brief summary of the five cases follows:

1. On 31 January 1987, the CRO requested checks on SZB-5-0001, Mario Eloy Jimenez-Rojo. Initial results of checks with various federal agencies revealed that Jimenez, a Cuban-born naturalized American residing in Characas (Caracas), was in 1949 known to the Batista government as a member of the Revolutionary Insurrectional Union. Jimenez later told U.S. officials that he was a veteran of the April 1961 Bay of Pigs (Day of Pigs) operation. In 1969, Jimenez was involved with three other Cubans in a plan to assassinate Fidel Castro. When informed that the U.S. government would not sanction the plan and that the four would possibly be subjected to federal prosecution, Jimenez stated that he wanted nothing more to do with the plan. Checks on Jimenez continue.
