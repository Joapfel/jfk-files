Attachment No. 4 to UFGA-21693

Subject: ALEMAN, Carlos

1. Source met Subject about 1953 in the home of Agustine (Augustine) ALDAMA Acosta, calle Tejar between San Lázaro and San Anastasio, Lawton, Havana City. Source became a close friend of the Subject during this period because of their mutual activities against the Batista regime. About 1958 Subject was arrested because of his political activities and was confined at Principe prison until the overthrow of the Batista regime. Source believes Subject was a member of the Policía Nacional Revolucionaria in the early days of January 1959. In February 1959 Subject was appointed to a position in the Presidential Palace, the same as source, where they were under the direct orders of Cussy AULET (mat unk). In December 1959 Subject was a member of the Policía Nacional Revolucionaria (Revolutionaria), stationed in a building located at Ave. Misiones y calle Peña (Pena) Pobre. Subject was transferred to the DTI as chief of the Departamento de Robos about 1961. Source last saw Subject about late 1961 when source went to the DTI to help a friend find his stolen car. Subject was not a communist before 1959, but now he is a staunch supporter of the COC and a communist. When source left Cuba in March 1964 Subject was residing in Reparto Santos Suarez, Havana City. Subject is divorced and has a minor son. Source believes Subject came to the USA about 1952, but Subject has never travelled to any communist countries.

2. Physical Description: DOB (DFOB): circa 1928, Havana City, probably. Race: white. 5'6". 160 lbs. Hair: light and gray. Eyes: light. Shape of face: round. Physique: heavy. No visible scars.

3. JEWAVE Traces: Believed identical with Carlos ALLEMAN Grass, 201-752505. (Believe it or have it)
