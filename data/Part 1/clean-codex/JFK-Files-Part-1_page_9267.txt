6 October 1950

Re photograph of a panel of distinguished literary figures, artists, musicians, etc., who have been banned in the USA, are crystals, and/or because disillusioned with the “Soviet experiment.”

Dateline Berlin, 16 August 1950. The West Berlin “Volkablatt” (Volksblatt) reports that the SED has forbidden the works of the following to appear in official entertainment programs or in public libraries in the East Zone:

Andre Gide: “An enemy of the Soviet Union.”

Ignace Paderewski: “A Polish reactionary chauvinist and enemy of the Soviet Union.”

Igor Stravinsky: “Implacable enemy of the Soviet Union.”

Antwer Poucattini: “Rootless, with Western tendencies” (II).

R. M. Riley, whose work is “sterile and unpopular.”

Stefan George: “A decadent dreamer and champion of the Germanistic infallibility myth.”

Jean-Paul Sartre.

Musicians:
