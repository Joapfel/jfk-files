Dispatch. To: Chief, WII Division. Info. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: (1) Raymond H. GERENDE—Contract Amendment.

Action Required—References. Reference: EMMA-13688, 7 June 1965.

1. Reference notified Station Mexico City that the current contract for Raymond H. GERENDE will expire on 30 September 1965. In recognition of GERENDE's increased operational responsibility and the professional manner in which he carries out his operational assignments, it is requested that his contract be amended to provide for a salary increase from $6,000 U.S. per annum to $6,500 per annum.

2. Project LIEPTY can absorb this additional expense and GERENDE's local cover job can logically cover this additional income.

Cornelius L. Revinon, for Willard C. CURTIS.

Distribution: (5) C/WHD.
