Via: Air (City/Air or Sea Pouch)

Dispatch No. HMY-B-20

Date: 31 December 1959

To: Chief of Station, Monterrey, Mexico

From: Chief, Western Hemisphere

Subject: General—Personnel

Specific—

Ref: HMY-A-7

1. At the present time, it is headquarters' plan to replace RIGGS with a young officer who is expected to arrive in May 1953. Biographic data will be provided in a subsequent pouch.

2. With regard to RIGGS' future assignment, he may be assigned to a headquarters position, which will offer more responsibility than his present assignment, if he so desires.

Oliver G. Galbond

Received: 7 January 1953
