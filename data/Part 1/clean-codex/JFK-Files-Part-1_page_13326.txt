Reference: Caracas 7668 (14 39826).

28 August 1967. IN 40592.

1. No identifiable station traces of Espinoza. AMOT traces pending.

2. AMCLEVE-15, who has just returned to Miami (MAIMI) to obtain a proper visa to enable him to work in Venezuela, reports he obtained a job with DIGEPOL through Rafael Tremol and Gustavo Ortiz Faez and was involved in Espinoza's capture.

3. According to AMCLEVE-15, DIGEPOL received a telephone call about 0600 hours on 25 August from an informant who reported that the black Chevrolet sought in connection with the 24 August bank robbery had been seen on Urdaneta Street near the Perite Building. On arrival, police saw Felix (Feliz) Farias, alias “Comandante Claudio,” who was wanted for the 1965 firing-squad murder of several people. Farias drew a revolver and was shot down. People in Apartment 27 of the Perite Building began firing on police. Police entered the apartment and arrested Espinoza and two others, and found 240,000 bolívares from the bank robbery, arms, ammunition, and grenades.
