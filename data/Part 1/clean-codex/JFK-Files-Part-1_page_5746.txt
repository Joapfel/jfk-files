Subject: COE, Frank

AKA: COE (CCE), Frank Virginius

EX-C1851

Virginius Frank COE, former Secretary of International Monetary Fund, in June 1953, was called before the McCARTHY Senate Investigation subcommittee concerning his alleged involvement in a Communist spy ring.

Prior to this in the 1930's, Subject was implicated in the espionage ring that included Alger HISS, Henry COLLINS (COE's brother-in-law), John ART, and others. He allegedly was a relatively unimportant member of the Nathan Gregory SILVERMASTER espionage network. In 1958 Subject was elected a member of the Awards Committee of the Fund for Social Analysis, formerly Fund for Social Studies, (a Communist propaganda organization). During the year 1958 Subject and his wife, Ruth Coe, were associated with Isidore Gitby NEVILLAN, a suspected Soviet espionage agent. In the fall of 1958 Subject wrote an article for the Chinese Communist publication China Reconstructs (China Fe reconstructs), praising the industrial and agriculture advances of the Chinese Communists.
