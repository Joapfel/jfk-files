5. Evaluation of Trainees:

1. The three DFS trainees, LOZANO, MANCERA, and GUZMAN, because of their previous training in security activities of the DFS, more readily understood the training materials and lectures given, and they were better able to incorporate what they learned into the field exercises conducted by them, because of their previous experience with the training.
