Classified message. IN 63271.

Seems confident they will join him eventually. He feels he has tightened up and inspired his “organization” in Paris. Says he has gained the impression from talks with Cubans here that disillusionment among Cuban embassies and officials inside Cuba has grown considerably. Ray is convinced it is possible to overthrow Castro cleanly and simply. Wants to devote his own apparatus to identifying disaffected elements in Cuba and building up supply apparatus in and outside Cuba to serve them.

8. Ray asked for the following:

A. Up-to-date list of personnel and addresses of all Cuban embassies in Europe to be passed to him in SJAN and UNRUMBLE 6 in Paris (PARI). He wants to begin mailing propaganda and letters to stimulate further disaffection among personnel.

B. Training of himself in SJAN and UNRUMBLE 6 Paris (PARI) in an identical SV system which they can use for correspondence between SJAN and Paris (PARI), and also into Cuba. STA felt both these ideas should be encouraged and Ray was told this could be done. Would appreciate HOS’s provision of item A and designation of SV system.

C. Ray also asked that we line up a job for Cabrera as Europe representative, some paper, as discussed with Mauman per PARI 8947. Would appreciate his follow-up on this.

D. Feel one solid accomplishment of Ray’s trip was re-determination of the working relationship by the RAT. Believe he got it off well with Nauman Grovery and Dervay and gained confidence in our support, which may alleviate something of some past problems in the KUBARK (NUBARK) relationship.

10. Index.

C/8 comment: Ray departing Paris (PARI) AF 015, arriving New York 1300 hours 10 July.
