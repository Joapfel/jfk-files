Villas Province, Cuba. He was a self-employed automobile salesman in Havana until August 64. Arrived U.S. Nov. 64 and was debriefed by station at three meetings for info on personalities of interest. He was active in anti-Castro exile organization, Movement of Revolutionary Recuperation (MRR), headed by Manuel Arturo Ménés. Per refugee source, Torres was living in Miami 1965, but resided Caracas May 70. As of May 72, he worked in massage room of Four Ambassadors Hotel. Info from 1969 indicated he was known narcotics trafficker. He was on IRS list from mid-72 of persons to be indicted for narcotics trafficking.

D. Ronald Condon-Gil, with his brother Francisco, was arrested early March 73 (cocaine trafficking). They received cocaine from Peruvian contact, Fidelidad and E.D.O., who serving of Rodrigo and Francisco Condon Gil 10-year sentence on same charge. Ronald was born 3 April 40, Havana. In 1962, he belonged to Rescate, anti-Castro group operating within Cuba. He was arrested 1961 and sentenced to six years by Cuban Revolutionary Tribunal. Charged with espionage and possession explosives. Entered U.S. at Miami on 24 Dec 62 (although not a member of Bay of Pigs (DAY OF PIGS) invasion force. He was released along with its members).
