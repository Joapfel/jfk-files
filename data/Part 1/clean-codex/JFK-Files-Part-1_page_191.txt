Eleven Unwitting Organizations Which Were Funded by or Collaborated with Project Organizations

32. American Student Aid Fund, Inc. — Manhattan, New York. Received a cover grant from Farfield Foundation (56) (Project QKOPERA) in 1966.

33. Association for International Development (AID) — Paterson, New Jersey. May have received funds from FYSA (SGGUESSER). Thomas P. Melady, Vice Chairman of African Service Institute YQZEUS, was on AID board.

34. Commission for International Development — Manhattan, New York. A proprietary funded and controlled through FYSA (SGGUESSER), 1961–1966.

35. Experiment in International Living, Inc. — Putney, Vermont. Received cover grants from FYSA (SGGUESSER), 1955–1966. Listed as a cooperating group in AAAI (1) (QRFLUENT) brochure.

36. International Center for Social Research, Inc. — Manhattan, New York. Formed by FEC (TPTONIC) and operated by IDF (88) 29 (QRBEND) until 1965.
