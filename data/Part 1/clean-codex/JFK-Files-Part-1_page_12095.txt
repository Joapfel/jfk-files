Memorandum

Subject: Request for lane check

Name: McVICKAP (McVikar), John Augustus, Jr.

Citizenship: U.S.

Relatives:

Father: John Augustus McVikar, U.S. citizen at birth; living at the home of Theodore McVikar on Long Island, New York; former member of U.S. Foreign Service.

Brothers: Theodore McVikar, living in the U.S.; Ralph McVikar, living in the U.S.

Sister: Dorothea Dawson, living in the U.S.

Wife: Ruth Maxwell Warner McVikar, née Ruth Maxwell. Born in St. Louis, Missouri; U.S. citizen from birth.

Daughter: Barbara McVikar, 19 years of age, daughter by previous marriage; living in San Francisco, California, with remarried mother.

Educational background:

St. Bernard’s, New York, U.S.A.

Harvey School, Paris, France.
