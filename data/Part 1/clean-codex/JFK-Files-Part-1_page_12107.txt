1. JAGUAR notes that Ivan Obyedkov (subject reference) is now working as an electrician at the Soviet Embassy, London. They are inquiring why we considered him suspect, probably KGB, and also asking, if possible, for a description of the function he performed as a guard at Soviet embassies in Tehran and Mexico City.
