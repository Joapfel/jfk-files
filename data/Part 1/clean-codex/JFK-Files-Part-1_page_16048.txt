Dispatch No. FTSA-33,059. Date: 30 June 1969. Film: none. Index: none. File: none. To: Chief, Far East Division. Info: none. From: Chief of Station, Bangkok. Subject: Sionics Inc. proposal to RTG for sniper training program. Action: none. Reference: FTBW-16321.

1. Station had seen publication cited paragraph one of reference.

2. Werbell (Webell) first came to attention of Station in late March 1969, when he was in contact with BPP in Bangkok. After receiving DIR 98114 in response to our trace request, we suggested to BPP that they would do well to avoid him. We heard nothing of him for the next few weeks. However, in late April and early May he was back in the spotlight and, we discovered, in touch with a variety of RTG and U.S. military elements. We promptly passed the gist of his reputation to key personalities in the mission, including Ambassador, Defense Attaché, ARPA, SA/CI, U.S. Army CIC, OSI, and J-2 MACTHAI. We particularly stressed that he was not sponsored in any way by the U.S. government. His name has come up consistently for the past several months, and we have pulled very few punches in describing him, either to Americans or Thais. In fact, we wrote a memorandum expressing our reservations, passed it to J-2 MACTHAI, and suggested he brief the J-2 Ministry of Defense accordingly. We also sent a burn notice message, Bangkok 3055, to all FE stations.

3. After dynamic beginning, Webell (Werbell) is now suffering expected.
