6 November 1958

Memorandum for: WH/III/Mexico  
Subject: Raymond H. GERENDE  
Reference: RM-4-11368

1. This dispatch represents the type of operational reporting we need. I think any answer to the Station should lead off with an expression of appreciation for a good job. Maybe it will encourage them to write more of this type.

2. GERENDE is very exposed and should be taken off some of his jobs at once. Specifically, my ideas are as follows:

3. Regarding paragraph 3a, the agent is very capable and should be retained if secure means can be found. Getting rid of him would not solve the problem. As long as he is in Mexico he is vulnerable. Some thought should be given to how he would be supported in the event of arrest. Personally, I believe the Station would do better to sacrifice a case officer than permit things to drag on to the point where GERENDE might tell all to the police.

Recommendations:

a. GERENDE be briefed so as to be ever ready with an innocuous story to tell the police in the event of arrest.

b. GERENDE be told to contact one of the already blown Station officers, like HAZLETT, in the event of arrest and that the Station officer be ready to appear personally at the police station to free GERENDE. If the case officer named for this duty and GERENDE both know the innocuous cover story, there should be little problem. I have done it myself. A good cover story is “looking for draft dodgers” or some such tale which does not have implications of working against Mexican citizens.

c. If not allowed to communicate directly, GERENDE should tell the senior officer present at the police station that he works on a cover story for HAZLETT and insists HAZLETT be sent for.

d. A second blown case officer’s name be given as an alternate for HAZLETT, who is often out of town and unavailable.
