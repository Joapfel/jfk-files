To: 2021328 October 76, CHG 4, FACV 38795.

Cite: Panama City 38795.

To: Director. Info: Miami, OARAS.

Writer: Baltimore Intel.

1. On 26 October HYDROCH-2 passed station copy of report on alleged Cuban exile terrorist activity from an apparently new source of GVSG-2 in Miami. Report is not dated, but GVSG-2 date stamp indicates they received report on 26 October. Even if clipped, writer is not identified in handwritten document; it provides address and phone numbers in Miami area, which should make his identification feasible. Writer appears to be proprietor of “PASCOG’S SEA FILL,” 1250 W. Twenty-Third Street, Hialeah, with phones 669-7344 (office), 388-6207 (home), and additional phone 854-4231 followed by notation “my house.” Have no idea how document reached GVSG-2, but we speculate EILEEN M-1 could have brought it from Miami. The writer is asking $20.00 U.S. for his services.

2. Presumably to establish his son’s files and substantiate his access to information of value to Panamanians, writer submitted lengthy report on alleged recent Cuban exile activity accompanying Sec. 6 T.
