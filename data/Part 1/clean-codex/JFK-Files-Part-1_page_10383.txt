5NOV93 E:27533

1. LIRING-3 received a brief, typewritten, unsigned letter dated 22 October from “Rafael Arqueta A.” The letter, which contains typographical errors, expressed happiness that LIRING-3 was finally released and hoped we would soon recover from the “gran angustia” caused by “my country.” The writer concluded with apologies for mistakes in orthography and expressed a desire to see LIRING-3 again. He asked LIRING-3 to reply to him at the following address: 2 Ave. 27, 57, Zona 12, Guatemala C.A., rather than to the one appearing as the return address on the envelope.

2. The envelope is airmail stationery: “La Dalia Azul, Enrique Puente C., Artículos para Caballeros, [QU Avenida y 1a Calle], Tel.: 44-72, Guatemala, C.U.” It has the following return address on the back: Rafael Arqueta A., 11 Av. 25-22, Zona 12, Guatemala C.A. The envelope is postmarked 22 October 1968 and is addressed to LIRING-3 at Calle Havre 7, Col. Juárez, Mexico, D.F. (Salón de la Plástica Mexicana, which LIRING-3 was given to Juan and Miguel León Barrios as best secret).
