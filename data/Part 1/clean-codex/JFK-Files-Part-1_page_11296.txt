1. According to Mejia Sanchez, Lisandro Otero and Alejo Carpentier arrived in Mexico the morning of 15 March. Otero probably arrived from Europe with Carpentier. Checking airlists.

2. Delegates to Congress left Mexico the night of 15 March and the morning of 16 March for Guanajuato and Guadalajara, where Congress will continue until Sunday, 19 March, at which time delegates will return to Mexico City, where meetings will continue until 25 March.

3. The night of 15 March, LITAMIL-9 reported that in a recent telecom with Concha Fernandez, the latter gave him to understand that Lisandro Otero replaced Carlos Lechuga as head of the Cultural Council, Havana.
