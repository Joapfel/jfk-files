1. 8 February 1965 WAVE received telegram dated 7 February from AMSPICE/I urgently requesting contact with WAVE C/O. WAVE found AMSPICE/I's request rather odd yet intriguing, particularly in view of the fact AMBLINK/I had last contacted AMSPICE/I on 6 February prior to departing Mexico for WAVE area.

2. Not wishing to use phone for reasons of security, WAVE dispatched AMBLINK/I to Mexico City on 8 February in order to determine the nature of AMSPICE/I's problem.

3. 9 February AMBLINK/I personally contacted AMSPICE/I at home. She stated she had received an urgent telecon from IDEN's wife in WAVE area asking her to come to WAVE area to help her carry out a campaign to obtain PBRUMEN government clemency for her husband. AMSPICE/I stated she was fully committed to assisting IDEN and his wife since they were old friends and that, unless WAVE C/O had strong objections, she planned to proceed to WAVE area with her children. She implied she would need WAVE financial assistance to make the trip.

4. AMBLINK/I (AMLINK/I) was instructed by WAVE to inform AMSPICE/I that his superiors were greatly displeased at the frivolous manner in which she beckons assistance and the whimsical nature of her request. AMBLINK/I (AMLINK/I) further.
