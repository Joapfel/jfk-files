3. OJWIN introduced VIROGUE to HOEHUIS. VIROGUE came in with a letter of credit with HOEHUIS to get a good exchange rate. OJWIN reports VIROGUE has purchased three automobiles since arriving in LEOP: a Chrysler for 5,000 francs, a Citroen for 25,000 francs, and a Triumph for 110,000 francs. VIROGUE told OJWIN he sold the Citroen for 15,000 francs, but it was still parked near his hotel.

4. At the 13 December meeting, VIROGUE said he purchased the Chrysler for 75,000 francs. He made no mention of purchasing the other cars. He also said he had rented two stores, one in LEOP European City for 14,000 francs per month and one in Native City for 6,000 francs per month. He also signed a contract for an apartment for 8,000 francs per month. He asked GUTHMAN for three thousand dollars’ rental allowance, which he claimed his HOS case officer told him he would receive in a lump sum. GUTHMAN refused, saying he would have to obtain HOS approval to pay the rental allowance in a lump sum.

5. In discussing local contacts, VIROGUE mentioned OJWIN but did not admit to having tried to recruit him. Then GUTHMAN tried to learn whether VIROGUE had made an approach; the latter claimed he had taken no steps. GUTHMAN was unable to contradict him, as he did not wish to reveal OJWIN’s connection with KUBARK (XUBARK).
