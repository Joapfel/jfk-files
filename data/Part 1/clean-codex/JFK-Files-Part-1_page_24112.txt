18 August 1967.

To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Attention: Mr. S. J. Papic

From: Deputy Director for Plans

Subject: Re FBI's Trip to the United States

1. This is to advise the Bureau that this Agency plans to have SWAP come to the United States during the month of September 1967. In addition to periodic consultation with headquarters elements, the purpose of this trip will be to make possible contacts, particularly among American Negro medical elements, primarily in order to develop information on those groups. It will be under instructions not to contact directly in any civil rights or other protest activity while in the United States.

2. We would appreciate the Bureau's consent in this activity. We would also welcome any Bureau requirements which the Agency would levy on FBI for servicing during his stay in the United States.

MF/C/CA/CF/Duvalier (18 August 1967).
