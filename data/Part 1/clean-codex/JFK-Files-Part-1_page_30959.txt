13 May 69, N56608.

1. At time Project LITEMPO submitted for renewal, it not known that Curtis retiring as of 30 June 69. It station's opinion that if Curtis and Benadum leave simultaneously (1 July 69), Probert will lack time to build up relationship which Benadum has had for some twenty years and Curtis for twelve years with this very important intelligence source (around forty percent of station positive intelligence and half C/CE production) and support project. Also, since Curtis and Benadum are only two well known to LITEMPOs, their joint departure could be misinterpreted and thereby damaging to the station.

2. File Curtis departure changes picture sufficiently to warrant amendment to LITEMPO project to allow for detention Benadum.
