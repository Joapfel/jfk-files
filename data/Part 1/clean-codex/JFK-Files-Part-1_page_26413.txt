Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Mexico / Cuba. Date of information: 21 January 1964.

Subject: Projected travel to Cuba of Victor Rico Galan.

Place and date acquired: Mexico, Mexico City, 22 January 1964.

Source: Person (B) with good contacts in the Cuban Embassy in Mexico City. Appraisal: 2.

1. On 21 January, Victor Rico Galan, Mexican Marxist journalist, asked Jose Miguel Fernandez Roa, third secretary and press attaché of the Cuban Embassy in Mexico, what the first available flight to Cuba would be. Fernandez said 24 January. Rico said he had wanted to go to Cuba on 23 January, but indicated he might go on the flight of 27 January.

2. Field comment. Earlier reports from a number of usually reliable sources have identified Rico Galan as an agent of Fidel Castro Ruz, Prime Minister of Cuba. In late May and early June 1963, Rico Galan spent eight days in Cuba interviewing Castro on the latter's visit to the USSR.
