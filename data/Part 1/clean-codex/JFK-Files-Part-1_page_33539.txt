Began preparing instructions for party members to aid in support of their counterparts in the Communist Party of Chile (PCCH). Initial instructions to PCP/Soviet members were to arrange lodging for members of other Latin American communist parties who were arriving in Lima, to obtain maps of Chile, false documentation, etc.

5. Second stage of support to Chile was to begin during the week of 23 September, when Communists and others were to arrive from country. Venezuela, Panama, Colombia, Cuba, and other parties all were to be lodged in Lima and, later, given false documents and transportation to the Chilean border. Their principal task in Chile was to assist in reorganization of PCCH following the coup d'état, as well as in production of clandestine propaganda. They also were to study the possibility of aiding in mounting concerted armed resistance to the military regime, principally through urban guerrilla warfare. There was no need to send arms, since there were still enough weapons hidden in Chile to make such risk unnecessary.

7. Central and South American Communists were to meet in Lima on 25 September to consider sending persons to Chile to organize.
