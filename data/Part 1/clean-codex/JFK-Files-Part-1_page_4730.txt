C. Frente de Liberacion Nacional de Cuba (Cuba National Liberation Front—FLNC) is an anti-Castro Cuban terrorist organization with headquarters in Miami. Its titular head, Frank Castro, lives in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. Gustavo Castillo is FLNC military chief, and Oscar Angulo its propaganda chief. FLNC reportedly is continuing its naval operations and attacks on Cuban and Russian vessels on the high seas, as well as bombings of Communist-country embassies outside the U.S. At the Santo Domingo meeting, Castro proposed that FLNC carry out harassing tactics against target countries, to include calls to airlines advising that a bomb is aboard a plane in flight so as to force emergency landing. Also proposed was actually planting a bomb every ten or more hoax calls to prevent the target country from distinguishing hoaxes from real threats, placing bombs in unoccupied aircraft so as to prevent injury to innocent persons.
