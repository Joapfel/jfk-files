CSRC: Richard Harrington. JAN: Hughes (HUGES?). Date: 7163/1948. Date: 22 September 1969.

Cite Director: 30886.

To Managua. Info: San Salvador, Tegucigalpa, WH/Miami.

Revision: Managua 7059 (IN 07442).

23 September 1969.

1. Request COS Managua contact AMBIDDY-1 and debrief on reference trip to San Salvador. Of particular interest is any information AMBIDDY-1 has on Honduras/Salvador arms purchases.

2. FYI: On 17 September headquarters learned from IUSTEER-1 that AMBIDDY-1 arranged contact from Miami for a Honduras arms buyer to purchase arms in Spain. For this service AMBIDDY-1 will receive a commission from the Honduras arms buyer based on the total arms purchase price. Do not reveal this information to AMBIDDY-1.

No index. File: 201-267437 and 201-283488.

End of message.

WH/comment: On 22 July COS Managua received an urgent phone call from AMBIDDY-1. AMBIDDY-1 said he had just received a phone call from Salvador and that they (Salvadorans) needed eight pilots and wanted to consult with AMBIDDY. A Salvadoran visa had been authorized. COS Managua instructed AMBIDDY to proceed to San Salvador and obtain information on Salvador war plans. Once he acquired this information, AMBIDDY was to inform the Salvadorans that he could not be involved in any way. AMBIDDY agreed and told COS Managua that he would contact COS on his return from the trip to San Salvador.
