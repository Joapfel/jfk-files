LIREAM DTDORIC

1. Tichborn will attend the International Conference on General Semantics, San Francisco, 9 to 13 August, at the invitation of organizer S. I. Hayakawa. He is scheduled to speak on 9 August and lead a panel discussion on 13 August. At Hayakawa's suggestion, he will mount the LIREAM display and will do whatever else is appropriate to push the product.

2. Plans to be in contact with Edgar Kaiser, through Hayakawa, chairman of the board of Kaiser Industries, and with philanthropist Norman Simon, through June Mayne, head of Tamarind Gallery. Although neither Tichborn nor the Station is particularly sanguine about the possibility of contributions to and from LIREAM from these personalities, they believe such promotional activity on his part helps support his cover.

3. He will also call on Wallace Stegner, head of the Creative Writing Center at Stanford, and on Arthur Coons (Artur Coons), ex-president of Occidental College, through whom he hopes to be introduced to such California cultural foundations, unspecified, as might be interested in LIREAM.
