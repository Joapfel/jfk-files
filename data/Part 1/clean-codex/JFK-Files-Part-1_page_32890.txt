1. Following is text of proposed SCORCH message to AMGHOST-1:

Commandante, we take this opportunity to provide you a private means by which we can communicate with you, and you with us, a means which can eventually be used for the exchange of opinions or, should the need ever arise, for use in negotiations where utmost secrecy and discretion are desired. Along with this letter we are sending cipher materials, directions, and a carbon sheet for secret writing for use in establishing this direct channel. The equipment is being given to your representatives sealed in plastic, with the request that it be delivered to you unopened. We will transmit our radio messages to you each Monday at 0700 Cuban daylight time on frequency 4670. The musical selection Siboney will indicate transmission of a true message.
