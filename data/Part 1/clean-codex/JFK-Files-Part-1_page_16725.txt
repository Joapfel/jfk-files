Ref MEXI 7480 (IN 8/16 5-3).

No night action.

1. PEAK and COS met with LITENSOR alone and without a single interruption for one hour.

2. Meeting at LITENSOR's home.

3. LITENSOR intensely interested; PEAK did an excellent presentation of the entire story.

4. LITENSOR said he had very limited knowledge of the story from Venezuelans prior to this briefing. He was convinced, repeat convinced, that CASTRO was guilty and said at the end, “Cuban Communists will not stop with this defeat.”

4. LITENSOR was given copies of papers and photographs brought by PEAK; he indicated he wished to use these.

6. No mention of any briefing of TELLO by any of the three.

7. Both PEAK and COS believe briefing was a success.
