From: Varela. No. of pages: 2. Report No.: HKI-145A. Local file No. Source report made by: Jose E. Palinger/Co. Approved by: Stanford P.ektson.

Distribution: Washington. By copy to: 1. Santiago de Cuba, orally. Embassy, local attaché; files.

Source cryptonym: See comments.

Subject: Mario Daniel Rivadulla y Carcedo.

Comments: Dr. Jose Antonio Cuenllar Aguilera (CuenILALR Aquilera) arranged an introduction to Rivadulla on 8 July, and he has since been seen by Palinger on several occasions.

The 5 January 1955 report was from AMPLECT (HMG-95L); the 6 November 1952 report was from AMELU (HMG-27); both were appraised as 3. Rivadulla, who was denied a visitor’s visa in November 1955 largely on the strength of those two reports from the Station, is still interested in obtaining the visa and willing to clarify his status vis-à-vis the JS and FP with the local OACID.

It is believed this report will assist in answering that portion of the latest periodic requirements list, forwarded under HMG-W-2379, concerning Rivadulla.
