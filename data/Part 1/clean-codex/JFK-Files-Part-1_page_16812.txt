C/S Comment: For background briefing friendly service only. Requested station cable SITREP info MEXI 10 September.

1. In absence of reply REF B, DJAK appraising situation in wake of West Irian settlement and Asian Games as follows.

2. Indon officialdom indulging in state of euphoria after final realization of two long-sought objectives of West Irian and Asian Games, outcome of which eminently pleasing to them. Sukarno in excellent spirits and obviously riding high in full conviction his personal and authoritarian policies of mobilizing populace to conform to objectives of guided democracy are being justified. Only persistent cloud on his horizon is debilitated economy, which he now marshaling state apparatus to rectify. Actual procedures Sukarno will adopt to attempt resolve severe economic problems still unclear. We well aware, however, that by alleviating economic distress and injecting some vigor into economy he will be able circumscribe whatever latent opposition there may still be to his regime.
