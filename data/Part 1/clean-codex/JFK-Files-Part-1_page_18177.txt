Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Info: Chief, WJ. Subject: Mail Procurement of MV-2320—Project LIEMPTY. Action required: approvals, HRS-1241, dated 5 February 1960.

1. Reference dispatch gives authorization to procure one 2-door, used sedan for Project LIEMPTY, MV-2320. Authorization was valid until 3 May 1960.

End of dispatch.
