Department of the Army  
Office of the Assistant Chief of Staff, G-2, Intelligence  
Washington 25, D. C.

G2-AP

3 December 1954

Subject: Letter of Appreciation

To: Director of Central Intelligence, Washington 25, D. C.  
Attention: Deputy Director of Plans

1. I have received a letter from Major General Boniface Campbell (Doniface Campbell), Commanding General, Army Intelligence Center, expressing appreciation for a presentation by Mr. [signature] at Fort Belair in October 1954. Mr. [signature] also arranged for presentations by two other speakers at the Army Intelligence Center. A copy of General Campbell's letter is attached.

2. The remarks contained in this letter are a source of real satisfaction to me, and I take this opportunity to add my appreciation to that expressed by General Campbell.

Akingus G. T.  
Major General  
A. C. of L.

1 Incl: Copy of letter from General Campbell dated 12 November 1954.
