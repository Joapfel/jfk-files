Dispatch. To: Chief, III Division. Attention: Chief, III Logistics. Info: Chief. Processing action: No indexing required; only qualified desk can judge indexing. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: Transportation Allowance for David H. WILSEND Against the LPRC Project TVA.

Reference: A. HP 45-3F(1), $31.94. B. MR 23-5A(2).

1. Authority is requested to pay David H. WILSEND a transportation allowance of $80.00 per month for the use of a POV for official business. It is planned to dispose of MV-2539, a 1967 Chevrolet Biscayne (Tiscayn) 4-door sedan that has been driven for approximately 50,000 miles in favor of a transportation allowance for WILSEND. The vehicle has been used for a total of 30 months with mileage averaging 1,665 miles per month, 90% of which was official business travel.

2. The recommended transportation allowance is based on costs computed as follows:

a. Gasoline: 1498 miles per month (1665 miles per month less 10% personal release) at 15 miles per gallon; 99.8 gallons at $.32. $31.94.

b. Insurance: Approximately $215.00 per year; less 10% for personal use (193.50 ÷ 12). $16.13.

c. Maintenance: Tire repairs, lubrication, oil change, washing, battery and tire replacement, estimated at $6.00 per month less 10% for personal use. $5.40.

d. Depreciation: Patented cost: $3,200. Value after 30 months: $2,000. Depreciation less 10% (1000 ÷ 30): $1,200. $36.00.

$9.47.

Distribution: 3 - MID. Cross reference to dispatch symbol and number: IPFFT-5P05. Date: 27 September 1965.
