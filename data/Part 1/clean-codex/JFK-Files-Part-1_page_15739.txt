1. Occupational Specialty: Mechanical Electrical Engineering. Cine or portrait photography. Research and innovator of commercial labor- and money-saving devices, electromechanical or photographic in nature. See paragraphs 4, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, and 14 of Employment History for examples.

2. Military Service:

December 1939, Paris: volunteered for French Foreign Legion.

January 1940, Tunisia, North Africa: in 1st Cavalry Regiment, 1st Squadron, 4th Platoon.

February 1940: sent to N.C.O. training course in Legion. Pulled out to be reassigned to an all-Georgian unit.

March–August 1940, Barcares: assigned to Georgian unit.

August 1940, Paris: discharged after the end of Franco-German hostilities as Private First Class.

3. Employment History:

1. 1942, Paris: volunteered for work in Germany.

2. Poelitz, Germany: worked for HIBRYER WERKE on contract for one year as installation electrician.

3. 1942, Berlin: photographer of metals for a factory that produced electrodes for welding instruments.

1942, Paris: self-employed at photo shop. Portrait photography.

4. 1942, Berlin: assembly-line worker in Berlin factory producing steel ammunition belts. Received commendation from factory.
