Classified message. Date: 17 September 1960. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 2 (Mr. Heyn notified 1855, 17 September 1960). Info: DCI, D/DCI, DOP, COP, APOP/A, C/CI, C/CI/RA, C/FI 2, C/FI/D 4, C/FE 4, D/OC 2, S/C 2. 17 September 2214Z 1960. Priority. IN 19365.

Priority DIR CITE MEXI 634B.

RYBAT.

Ref A DIR 00992 (OUT 70506). B DIR 01206 (OUT 70522).

1. Station taking following actions:

A. MKCHARITY—preparing move of sensitive equipment to safe area. Mulford to spend more time in Lifinger cover office. Stehlik instructed to stay away from MKCHARITY base. LIMAT operation indefinitely suspended. Due to Philip L. Alpher's knowledge of MKCHARITY, consider this operation could be most seriously affected.

B. LIMESA/LIMUST—plan to move all technical gear from Unit 11 and suspend Unit 15 LICALLA activities.

C. LIFEAT—revising and reviewing LP cutoff and evacuation.
