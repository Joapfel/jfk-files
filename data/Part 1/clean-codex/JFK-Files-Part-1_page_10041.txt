312335Z. CITE Mexico City 2322. Priority. Director. Information: Priority Guatemala City. KAPOK.

1. LERING-3 reports he unexpectedly contacted, on 23 July, Moises Orozco Leal (201-812145), who said he now has FAR liaison. Orozco claimed not to know Garcia, whom he was in fact replacing, and said he ordered the activation of procurement of arms and supplies via LERING-3 in view of the current desperate FAR situation in Guatemala. Orozco even asked LERING-3 for a doctor. LERING-3 claims that, to expedite matters, Orozco proposed a direct meeting between LERING-3 and Cesar Montes in Guatemala.

2. The station established that holes exist in LERING-3's story, at least regarding the detail of the manner in which the first Orozco meeting came about. There is also the fact that, according to LIREBEL-1 and LIREBEL-2, two separate sources, Orozco, who was in Guatemala in connection with guerrilla activities, apparently got cold feet, recently returned to Mexico, and is currently hiding from FAR.

3. Before going any further, request headquarters provide a Spanish-speaking SGSWRL operator by 4 August. As pointed out in paragraph 3B of HMMA 32635, sent as item 31 of pouch No. 5242 due headquarters 31 July, headquarters.

1 August 1967. M 22584
