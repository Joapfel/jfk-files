Dispatch No. FOB.

To: Senior Representative, Indochina.

From: Chief, FE.

Subject: General administrative—personnel. Specific: Assignment of [REDACTED] and VILLIERS.

Reference: a. SAIO 2922. b. DIR 49205.

1. Headquarters appreciates SLADE's reply to Reference b and will make it a matter for the record. For SLADE's (and VILLIER's) information, [REDACTED] has, in the course of his duties in Europe, come to the attention of the French, probably including the UNION organization. Naturally, his former KUTWIN record is well-known to UNION, and he is personally acquainted, we have since learned, with TUTOR himself.

2. Headquarters appreciates, too, the probable reaction of TUTOR to the advent in the Indochina scene of VILLIERS, [REDACTED], and possibly others with similar histories. This is unavoidable and unfortunate; however, under the circumstances Headquarters considers their assignment a necessary risk.

3. In a letter of no great importance to him, it is stated that, in the case of SLADE [REDACTED], if he was assigned to KUTWIN by ODINEX as a reserve officer on extended active duty, Future actually had been released back to ODINEX and had previously been reassigned by ODINEX. A cable dispatch is being written; those ODINEX orders are in process of being rescinded, and new orders are being initiated assigning [REDACTED] to CUNH.

4. In the case of VILLIERS, whose orders have just been received transferring him to Saigon, if indeed his previous KUBARK (KURARK) connection is known to TUTOR, it can only be stated that he, too, has been released back to ODUNIT, at their or ODFARL's request, in response to their urgent need for an experienced senior officer with his qualifications for a special mission in Indochina.
