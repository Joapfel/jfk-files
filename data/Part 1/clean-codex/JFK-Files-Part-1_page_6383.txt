Central Intelligence Agency. Information Report.

Report No. 00-30984. Country: Latvia/Germany. Subject: Biographical Data—Herrmann Buduls. Number of pages: 1. Place acquired: Germany.

This is unevaluated information.

Source: Foreign representative of a U.S. resettlement agency interested in bringing the subject of this report to the United States. The following information was forwarded to the headquarters of that agency to determine placement possibilities in the United States. To date, no decision has been reached.

Name: Herrmann Buduls.

Date and place of birth: 4 November 1982 at Pebelk, Latvia.

Present address: Germany.

Nationality: Latvian.

Marital status: Married; wife is 65, a biologist and chemist, graduated from Petersburg University in 1917.

Education: Attended Pebelk primary school, Latvia, from 1995 to 1999; Riga Town College, Riga, Latvia, from 1998 to 1905; Dorpat University in Estonia, Section of Nervous Diseases, from 1905 to 1911, received M.D. in Psychiatry; took a three-month course in psychiatry at Berlin Hospital, Berlin, Germany.

Experience: Head physician at Hinsk (Minsk) and Smolensk Army Mental Hospital in Russia from 1914 to 1918; head of the Neurological Society at Riga, Latvia; professor of psychiatry, Department of Medical Faculty of Riga University; private and hospital practice and research work during the period 1918 to 1944; responsible for the care of concentration camp inmates and deportees at the Repatriation Hospital at Wildflecken, Rudolstadt; medical officer of a camp with a capacity of 15,000 at Wurzburg, Germany, for the U.S. Military Government; and medical officer and psychiatrist for IFO mental hospital at Wiesloch, Germany, during the period from July 1943 to the present.

Publications: Has written 26 books on psychiatric subjects; one of the latest is “Healthy Life—Memoirs of a Physician.”

Command of languages: Speaks and writes English fairly well and reads it fluently. Knows German, Russian, Latvian, and French.
