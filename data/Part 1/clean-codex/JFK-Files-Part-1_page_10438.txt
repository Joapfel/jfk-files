Mexico City info Guatemala City.

Refs: A. Mexico City 6223 (IN 47056). B. Guatemala City 9236 (IN 47690).

1. HQS files indicate Arturo Serrano in 1966 was director of PCM publishing house Fondo de Cultura in Mexico City, but there are no details regarding his contacts with third-country exiles or officials. We are therefore unable to comment on the validity of his claim to assist LIRING/3.

2. Concur with Guatemala City comment in reference B. Feel LIRING/4 should continue to press Serrano for more details on his channel to and the identity of Guatemalan officials involved. Believe LIRING/4 and station will then be in a better position to assess the validity of Serrano's claim. Suggest LIRING/4 also check Serrano's story with his leftist circle of friends to attempt to verify this and explore possible sources of funds if feasible. Station.
