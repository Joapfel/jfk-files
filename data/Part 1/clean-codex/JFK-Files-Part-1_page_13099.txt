Deblycer = 19 April 67. Report = 20 April 67.

Attachment #3.

Subject: Jose (pat. and mat. unk.). Alias: “Bepe.” DSE and CP member, installer and repairman for “Area Special” of the Telephone Company.

Source: Lazaro Armandi Mirandetz (MIRANDETZ) Perez. DPOB: 17 December 1937, Plaza Velez, Pinar del Rio Province. Alien registration BA17-405-510.

1. Hernandez met Jose during 1957, when they both started to work for the Cuban Telephone Company in Havana.

2. Up to circa 1962, Jose was employed as installer-repairman for “Area Forte” of the Telephone Company at 19th Street, corner of B Street, Vedado, Havana.

3. In circa 1962, Jose was assigned to work as repairman and installer of telephone equipment for “Area Special” at 19th Street, corner of C Street, Vedado, and since then it has been known that Jose was a DSE agent, as every other “Area Special” employee.

4. Jose, like all other “Area Special” personnel, brags a lot about being a DSE agent. He always wears a military uniform, showing no rank, and is always side-armed.

5. In early 1966, Jose was elected to become a member of the Cuban Communist Party in an assembly to elect new CP members at his place of employment.

6. In the performance of his job, Jose installs and repairs telephone equipment at offices of ministers, DSE offices, military headquarters, COE personnel offices and homes, etc.

7. Hernandez saw Jose for the last time in early 1967 at “Area Forte” of the Telephone Company.

8. Physical description: DPOB: circa 1937, unknown; Caucasian, brunette; wavy black hair; dark eyes, wears sunglasses; round face; good teeth; 5'2" in height and 160 lbs.; strong build. Married (mate unknown). Resides at address unknown, Havana. DSE and CP member; installer-repairman of telephone equipment for “Area Special” of the Telephone Company at 19th Street, corner of C Street, Vedado, Havana.
