Directorio Revolucionario Estudiantil

José Antonio Echeverría: Con tus ideas en marcha.

Funds destined to the Cuban cause, his repeated boycott of everything which holds the name of the cause, and the little labor accomplished throughout the year. In the report which is being made by Mr. Helol González Simón, a plan of labor to be carried through in São Paulo will be presented. The U.S. will engage to accomplish in 6 months a superior labor than that accomplished by the S.E.I. during one whole year on the Cuban problem. Good contacts and a well-traced plan are arranged for such ends. The plan together with the reports will be presented as soon as reach us.
