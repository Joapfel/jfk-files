Dispatch. Chief of Station, Mexico City. Processing action: Mailed for Indexing. Chief of Station, JMWAVE. Processing action: No Involving Required. From: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Processing action: Only Qualified Desk Can Judge Indexing. Subject: LIONION. Processing action: Microfilm.

Action Required—References. Action Required: See Below.

1. Prior to submitting the LIONION project for renewal 1 January 1968, Headquarters requests the Mexico City station comment on the desirability of continuing the photography of persons entering and leaving the PBRUMEN Consulate. As the station is aware, this film is no longer reviewed at JMWAVE and there are no present plans to resume this practice, since in the past it proved unproductive and costly. The film has never been reviewed on a regular basis at Headquarters and has been referred to only once for identification purposes during the past year. At present, the film as received at Headquarters is developed (negatives only) and placed into storage.
