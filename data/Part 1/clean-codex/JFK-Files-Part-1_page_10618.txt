Central Intelligence Agency, Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Haiti/Cuba. Report No. TDCS DB-315/0038-02. Date of information: 15 October 1964. Date distributed: 16 October 1964. Subject: Plans to recruit Cuban exiles to join the Haitian exile group, “Combat.” Place and date acquired: United States, Miami, 15 October 1964. Reference: IN 9A15Z. Source and appraisal: A Cuban exile who has contacts in the exile community in Miami and New York, from David Quintas. Field report No. UPO-6049.

1. On 15 October 1964, David Quintas said that Ovido Miro, a Cuban, and Roman (RIC) Ramirez, a Haitian, recently arrived in Miami for the purpose of recruiting approximately 200 Cubans to augment a force of 150 to 200 trained men of the Haitian exile group, “Combat.” (Field comment: It was reported in TDCSDB-315/00917-64 that Paul Tousliant, a Haitian, and three Cubans were in Miami to recruit Cuban exiles, who would be used to invade Haiti.)

2. Miro and Ramirez asked David Quintas to locate a house in the Miami area in which the trained men could be quartered.
