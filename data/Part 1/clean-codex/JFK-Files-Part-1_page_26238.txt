1. LURING-S informed Wallace B. Riggs at a meeting on 19 January 1967 that Carmen Molina Enriquez y Vazquez Gomez, wife of Juan Carrasco Zamini y Zubaran, had told him privately on the evening of 17 January that the Mexican (MEXI) Communist Party had declared Victor Rico Galan a traitor and had ruled him out of the party. Carmen told L-3 she considered the accusation a malicious lie and hoped L-3 would not believe it. (Comment: Station has no indication Rico was ever a PCM member.)

2. If possible, the accusation may have resulted from a letter signed by Rico and carried out of prison by Elena Garrig de Paz.

3. L-3 told ROTOWN he was very close to Don (DO.) Carrasco and Carmen during their residence in Cuba (CUPA) from 1951 to 1965 and that they frequently visited his home since their return from Havana (HAVA) to Mexico City (MEXI) in 1966. L-3 described Carmen as neurotic and having marital trouble with Carrasco and being about to break up. L-3 told ROTOWN he first met Rico Galan at Carrasco’s home and, through Carrasco and Rico’s close friendship, Carrasco had complained to L-3 in the past that Rico would not take him secret.
