10 March 1978

Memorandum for the Record

From: Robert D. Clark, Office of General Counsel

Subject: Meeting in New York: PDLADLE

On 2 March 1978, the undersigned and Vasia Gmerkin of CI Staff met in New York City with the defectors and his wife at the apartment of Bernard MONCURE. There was a first mortgage on a New York City property of theirs located at 163 East 71st Street, New York, New York, in favor of a devised facility nominee of the Agency (Roush-Walker Company, 48 Alabama Street, S.W.), Atlanta, Georgia, for a recited indebtedness of $167,429.88. They signed a classified document acknowledging that the devised facility is an instrumentality of the United States Government. This document was witnessed in alias by Mr. Gmerkin, and the undersigned registered strenuous objections to the “mortgage.”
