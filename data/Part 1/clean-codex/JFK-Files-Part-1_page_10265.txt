Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City.

From: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division.

Subject: PERMAN/LIRING-3 Operation.

Action required: For Mexico City, please see paragraph 3.

References: A. HBA-35905, 27 November 1968. B. HSA-35904, 27 November 1968.

1. Guatemala City has the following information on Oscar Lopez ROLDAN Espinosa, who is undoubtedly identifiable with the Oscar Abises ROLIAR E. who sent one of the letters to LIRING-3 forwarded with Reference A. ROLIAN was arrested by the Guatemalan National Police in El Salvador (ElSalvad), along with one Oscar Rolando ESTADA Aldana, after robbing a taxi driver of his car. In his interrogation by the Guatemalan authorities, ROLIAN admitted to being a member of the Rebel Armed Forces (FAR). He stated that the car was to have been used for a kidnapping, but he refused to name the target of the planned kidnap attempt. ROLIAN named “CARILLO” (deceased FAR leader Carlos Francisco ORDOZ Montesado), “ARMANDO” (unidentified FAR urban resistance leader), “JANE” (Luis Felipe CARRERA, FAR urban resistance leader), and Percy Adilcar JACQUES de Leon, among others, as FAR members with whom he was in contact. This would seem to indicate that ROLIAN was an important militant in the FAR urban resistance Front. According to information available to the Station, ROLIAN was consigned to the courts. He apparently was later sent to El Favon prison, where he met Likau-3.

2. A check of the files of Miguel and Juan Jose de LEON Barrios shows that the home address given by them to the police was the same as the return address on the letter sent to LIRING-3 by “Tarta,” the mother of the de LEON brothers.

Distribution: 2-COS, Mexico City; 2-C/Mid.
