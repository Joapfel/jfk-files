Acquired copy of Simon report, but it likely was also forwarded by PERS from Costa Rica. Simon possibly identifiable with Luis Angel Antonio Simon Garcia (GOHCAPEPOOULM), per station records. Simon’s Station 201 was forwarded to HOS on 13 December 1968.

Simon report, prepared on “Excelsior de Costa Rica” letterhead paper, states that Merino and Fernando Melo are Cuban exiles and CIA agents. Simon alleges that Merino is Robreno’s friend and the latter under detention in Panama, charged with conspiracy against the life of Torrijos. Per Simon, Robreno’s mother is now in San Jose attempting to contact Jose Figueres and President Oduber (O DUBER). She intends to request their intercession with Torrijos to secure Robreno’s release. Mr. Robreno threatens to create a public scandal for Torrijos if Figueres and Oduber refuse to help her. She threatens public denunciation of the illegal detention of Robreno by Panamanians at the instigation of the Cuban Embassy in Panama.
