When Garceran changed his mind about the proposed meeting with McNamara, who would probably not receive him due to the pressing Vietnam problem, he returned to Kelly’s home on 5 August to request return of the memo. He was received by Blanca Rosa, who told him that Francisco Aguirre had been waiting to see him but had to leave. She then told Garceran her mission for Aguirre, described in paragraph 2, one for which she was especially suited in view of her relationship to Mrs. Tachito Somoza and the fact that her husband was a U.S. colonel.
