Letter from the 6499th Support Group (CR), 11 June 1965, letter of commendation.

1st Indorsement (DI).

Headquarters PACAF, APO San Francisco 96553.

1 July 1965.

To: Headquarters USAF (AFNIN); Central Intelligence Agency (Director of).

The assistance and cooperation given by Mr. William V. Broe have been noteworthy. His professional guidance and constant support have been distinct assets to the 6499th Support Group and its activities. It is a pleasure to add my appreciation for the invaluable contributions made by Mr. Broe.

Donald C. Shultis, Colonel, USAF, Director of Intelligence.
