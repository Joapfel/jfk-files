Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
From: Chief, WH Division.  
Project: OPBURNIN/LERING-3.  
Action required: FYI. Reference: HMA-38250, 28 August 1967.

Headquarters notes that on 9 August 1967 LIRING-3 was in contact via LIFEAT with one Electa LMU. Given LIRING-3's artist background and the subject of conversation—the visit of Rodolfo Heisler, a Swiss artist, to Cuba—it is suggested that Electa LMU might be Electa ARENAL (201-173795), the mother-in-law of David ALFALO Siquieros (SQUERUS), the well-known Mexican Communist artist.

Andrew F. MERTON.
