Classified message.

24 FEB 78 H. 86943. 241905Z.

Priority. DIR INFO GUAT. CITE MEXI 2594. Resident LIRAMA LIENVOY. Ref: A DIR 86998. B MEXI 2587 (not sent GUAT).

Attention DAINOLD.

1. On night 19 Feb, LIELEGANT and CURTIS met with LIRAMA. LIELEGANT raised subject VICTOR RICO GALAN and passed to LIRAMA brief biographical memo and copy of tape (which LIENVOY had transcribed). Tape and memo given as result of DAINOLD and LIELEGANT discussion night 2 Feb at LIELEGANT home. LIRAMA noted tape, transcript and memo. He asked LIELEGANT to make immediate study of MEXI naturalization laws to find out how RICO GALAN could be “denaturalized” and then deported. LIRAMA asked CURTIS if latter could arrange for country such as PERU to accept RICO GALAN at least temporarily. LIRAMA said he was aware that RICO GALAN was a drunken philanderer, and requested CURTIS initiate surveillance in order obtain evidence which would assist “de-naturalization” procedure.
