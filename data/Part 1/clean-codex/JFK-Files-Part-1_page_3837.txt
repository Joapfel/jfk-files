SECRET 1515062. Cite Managua 6055.

Priority. Director. Info: JMWAVE, Panama City. RBY BAT. TYPIC AMWORLD.

15 March 1967.

Re: Managua 6054 (IN 2/59).

COS conferred alone with General Montiel the night of 14 March. Montiel had met earlier with General Somoza and was closeted with Ambassador IDDY-1 when COS arrived. Montiel confirmed Somoza’s willingness to facilitate recovery of arms but had worked out with Somoza his own plan of how best to accomplish this. Montiel is bringing Colonel Brenes, Guardia commander of the Bluefields/El Bluff area, to Managua on 15 March. He will instruct Brenes, who is witting of the AMWORLD operation, to give full cooperation to COS. He proposes that COS and Brenes go to the cache site to inventory the arms. Brenes will then take charge of moving the arms securely and in small amounts to Managua. When all arms are at Managua, a Nicaraguan Air Force plane will fly to the Canal Zone for “routine maintenance,” at which time the arms can be turned over to the designated recipient there.
