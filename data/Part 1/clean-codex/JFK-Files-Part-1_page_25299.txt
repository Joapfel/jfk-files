LEONOV, Nikolai Sersegevich

MEXI-839. 27 June 1956. Personal calling card of subject found in address book of Fidel Alejandro CASTRO Ruz, Cuban revolutionary who was arrested by Mexican police on 21 June 1956. (LIVESTOCK-3; B-Doc)

USArma MexCity, MEX C-20. 28 June 1956. At the time of his arrest, Cuban revolutionary Fidel CASTRO had in his wallet a personal card of Nicolas L. LEONOV, administrative officer in the Soviet Embassy and secretary to the Soviet ambassador.

HMI-1752. 5 September 1956. Translator for Soviet delegates to the Eighth International Radiology Congress at Mexico City, 22–29 July 1956.

Tele 566, AnEmb MexCity. 1 October 1956. The Embassy today issued C-3 visas to Likhail CHERKASOV, born in Moscow on 16 September 1909; Counselor Nicolai Sergevich LEONOV, born in Russia on 22 August 1928; Clerk Valeria Ivanovna LEONOVA, born in Russia on 30 April 1928; Nicolai Dementievich LARIN, born in Riazan on 15 January 1922; Clerk Praskovia Artenovna LARINA, born in Riazan on 30 November 1923; and minor children Alexandre LARIN and Irina LARINA. Departing Mexico 17 October by train via LAREDO; departing New York City 24 October 1956 via QUEEN ELIZABETH.
