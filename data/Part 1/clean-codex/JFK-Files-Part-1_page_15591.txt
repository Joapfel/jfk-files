Classified message.

Date: 07 March 1961.

No.: Director. CI/DA: Leopoldville. AF: AF 7. DPC: DPC 4, CI/OPS, CI/OA, FI, S/C 2.

7 March 1946Z 1961. Routine. IN 15023. Air CITE: LEOP 8957.

WIRGUE.

1. WIRGUE in contact with Paul Arnold Pierrot, who designated head of Congolese Air Force. Pierrot offered WIRGUE job administrator for pilot and mechanic instructors with rank of major in Congolese Army. WIRGUE to see Defense Minister 7 March to sign contract.

2. WIRGUE to provide names and attempt hire three pilot instructors and three mechanics within 30 days with transportation, houses and salary of 33,000 OCF per month paid by Congolese government. More instructors needed later.

3. If WIRGUE accepted, necessary KUBARK (KUGARK) suggest instructor personnel with WIRGUE as channel for hiring. WIRGUE states he needs documentary proof to support story he ex-captain German Army.
