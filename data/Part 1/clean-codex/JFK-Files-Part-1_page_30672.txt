Memorandum in Lieu of Fitness Report

Subject: Newton Scott Miller (MILER)  
(1 April 1968–31 March 1969)

1. Mr. Miller's performance as a counterintelligence officer and supervisor of both officer and clerical personnel has continued to be outstanding in every respect. He has continued to carry a major portion of the workload of the office. Highly favorable comments made in previous fitness reports by me continue applicable in every respect for the current reporting period. He is cost-conscious and economizes in the expenditure of government funds.

2. Mr. Miller has previously been recommended for promotion.

Birch D. O'Neal  
Chief, CI/SIO

Employee signature: 15 May 1969

Reviewing official: James R. Hunt  
Deputy Chief, Counterintelligence Staff
