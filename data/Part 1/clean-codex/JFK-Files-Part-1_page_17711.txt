1 February 1957

Reorder from: F/IPS—Projects Branch

Subject: Amendment to Project LIFETAT

1. It is requested that Project LIFAT be amended to include the purchase of a LIFAT/LIME basehouse in the amount of $20,000. The lease on the present LIFAT/LIME base expires in April.

2. The LIFAT/LIME base is the principal base of operations covering Mexico City's number one priority targets. The location of this base must be moved since the lease on the present base cannot be renewed. Furthermore, a decision has been reached whereby new operational techniques will be initiated which will necessitate round-the-clock operations in the base by station-cleared personnel in order to satisfactorily cover the targets. This will undoubtedly necessitate the acquisition of a more securely controlled base of operations. The station does not believe such a base can be acquired other than by purchasing the property indicated. However, every effort will be made to lease this property, provided leasing arrangements can be made to meet the security requirements of the operation.

3. At present, additional funds are not needed in the LIFAT project to cover the purchase of a new basehouse. Should more funds be needed before the project expires, they will be requested at a later date.

J. C. King  
CWR

Approved: Chief, FI/CPS  
Joseph R. Groves

Approved:  
D/Directors, Logistics
