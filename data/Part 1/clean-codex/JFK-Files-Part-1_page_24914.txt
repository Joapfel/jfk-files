Memo for Tichborn 201 file.

Marginalia from two meetings with Tichborn in Washington in April and May 1966.

Tichborn is planning to write a book tentatively titled “The Humanity Bomb—Latin American Population Crisis.”

As part of his research on the subject, he has prepared two articles and submitted them to Life magazine and The Atlantic Monthly. The Atlantic Monthly has accepted his article “Birth Control Is a Dirty Word—The Semantics of Birth Control,” which will appear in a future issue of The Atlantic.

Tichborn’s article concerning his own ethnic problems, i.e., being a Mexican-American, which appeared in the March–April issue of Dialogos, has been accepted by American Heritage and will appear in the near future. He showed me the check ($450) for this article.

Jore: Any clearance on your Vijak?

Fare.
