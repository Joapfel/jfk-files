Alvy, 30 October 1966, IN 08755.

1. North American Newspaper Alliance (NANA) wishes to distribute a feature article based upon the AMIAFFY-1 story described in reference A. Prior to releasing the article, NANA has asked AMIAFFY-1 to check further for more facts. In attempting this, AMIAFFY-1 was unable to reach Sam Seiner (Beiner), though it has spoken to Mrs. Beiner at the reference B number, who confirms the Beiner home is also the World Seafood Distributors' office. She says Beiner is not available. As a consequence, both NANA and AMIAFFY-1 believe they are on to a big story, and NANA has decided to sit on the material it now has until more background information on Beiner, World Seafood, and their methods of operation can be developed.

2. Request HOS and Ottawa trace Seiner (Beiner) and World Seafood, with an indication that the information can be passed to AMIAFFY-1. From Ottawa also request any additional information on this subject, Sect. I.
