8. After Mr. Oldfield had put a number of questions to Diagrams, the latter asked Oldfield whether Geddes was possibly a British agent, which Oldfield emphatically answered in the negative.

9. Mr. Oldfield said that he would now send off a communication to his service indicating that Geddes might be a phoney—a provocateur. However, he asked that he be provided with the following:

a. CIA appreciation of the United Revolutionaries;

b. Identities of C-1, C-4, and members of the net with whom C-1 was arrested on March 18. (Note: Diagrams acknowledged that besides C-1, conceivably several other avatars of this net might have known of Geddes and his activities.)

c. Identities of the couriers who had contact with Geddes;

d. The identity of the CIA case officer—the name or names used by him in contacts with Geddes;

e. Chronological summary of the visits of Geddes to Miami and returns to Havana; also a list of the nature of his communications to Miami (by telecoa, courier, etc.) and communications sent to him in Havana.
