15 March 1967.

1. On 11 March, Clemard Joseph Charles visited Rachfield's home ostensibly to discuss needed repairs. (Rachfield rents house from him.) After about fifteen minutes of social conversation, Charles asked Rachfield if it was possible to pass a message out of channels directly to Chief Lnpure. Rachfield asked for the message, with the comment that he would make an attempt but could not speak for Chief Lnpure. Charles said the message was not ready. Rachfield said that while Charles prepared the message, he would check with Chief Lnpure. Rachfield then reported immediately to the COS, who stated Chief Lnpure should be notified soonest. Chief Lnpure then gave him permission to accept the message.

2. Same evening, Rachfield went to Charles' home to pick up the message. Charles again stated the message was not ready, but proceeded to tell Rachfield he was very pro-Wolady and well known at high levels of Lnharp. Charles said he would have the message ready by 1000 hours the following morning and would bring it to Rachfield's home.

3. Next morning, Charles arrived on time carrying a record.
