14 February 1964. IN 19935.

PBRUMEN.

1. Following reported by ERYTHROID/1 on 13 and 14 February:

In late January NIC Security sent two agents to Costa Rica to penetrate a group led by Marcial Aguiluz, which was plotting to kill Gen. Anastasio Somoza. Two agents are IDEN A and IDEN B. They were successful, and IDEN A reported on 11 February that Aguiluz had instructed IDEN B to go to Cuba for special training. IDEN B was to go to Mexico City on 12 February, register at Hotel Congreso, Calle Allende, and, on the following day, go to Calle Francisco Marquez Number 160 (por Tacuba), where he was to ask for Señor Vega or Señor Rogelio. When asked by Vega or Rogelio, “De parte de quien,” he was to answer, “De parte de Señor Caneiras,” who had sent him to arrange his papers. Aguiluz told IDEN B that, if he was unable to locate either Vega or Rogelio, he was to call telephone number 14-13-26, repeat 14-13-26. IDEN B was also told that, if he needed something, presumably while in [text unclear] also received 1110 (CBEA), he was to write to Apartado 110, San Jose, Costa Rica. He was marked, however, to write nothing of significance. Aguiluz told IDEN B he would be given a commo system in Mexico at the Cuban Embassy, so he could let IDEN A know when he was planning to return. Aguiluz explained that IDEN B would return from Cuba clandestinely by submarine.

2. This operation is being run by ERYTHROID/1, who is not, repeat not, aware that the station is being informed by ERYTHROID/8. The station is obtaining additional details, which will be reported soonest.

3. For MEN: E/S requests urgent identification registry of Mexico City telephone number 14-13-26. Must have reply by morning 15 February if E/S is to be able to advise his contacts in Mexico City. IDEN B to make contact with Vega or Rogelio—or by phone—by 17 February at 11:00.

14 February 1964.
