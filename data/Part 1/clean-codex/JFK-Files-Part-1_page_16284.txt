Classified message. Total copies: 43.

SECARDB 26A5 H 40041.

1. Colonel Williams informed the station that on the morning of 26 August, Logist and Major Wickes held a meeting with Mobutu regarding the 5th Commando pay problem. After arranging for a loan from Director of the National Bank Noble, Mobutu called in the paymaster and made arrangements to pay some unspecified portion of the outstanding pay immediately. Wickes then went to the bank to ensure that the transfer of funds to Johannesburg was made. Williams felt this was only a stopgap measure and that the problem was likely to come up again in a few weeks.

2. Williams also said that 80 mercenaries had been killed so far in the Congo and that the government obligated itself to pay the families one million Belgian francs for each death. Although the Congolese had budgeted for deaths, they did not believe the death rate would be so high.

3. Williams informed his representative ALEV on 26 August to notify Hoare. Wickes has probably notified Hoare directly.

4. Please pass the above to Guthman.
