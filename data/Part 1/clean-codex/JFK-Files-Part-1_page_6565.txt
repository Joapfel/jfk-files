Chief, Washington Office

14 February 1962

Chief, Contact Division (Support)

New Case 37308

1. EX/IA is interested in (FSU) Nasovic, Yugoslav member of the World Federation of Deaf. They ask you to contact your sources at Gallaudet College in Washington for any information they may have on Nasovic, who, according to a Los Angeles memorandum of 14 November 1961, is Yugoslav and well-known as a charismadan working in the area of the deaf. He was apparently co-chairman on a committee with Dr. Bernard Terwout, a Jesuit priest, who is a PhD and a linguist, and a Dutch national. At one time, Nasovic and Dr. Terwout met together in mid-October 1961 in Italy, at which time Nasovic expressed a desire to defect.

2. To assist you in the assignment, we are attaching a copy of the EX/IA memorandum of request and a copy of the Los Angeles memorandum of 14 November 1961, giving more details about the meeting between Dr. Terwout and Nasovic.

3. Gallaudet College will be host to the International Congress on Education of the Deaf, and sources there may have information on the Nasovic in question.

4. Many thanks in advance for your cooperation in this assignment. Please furnish information responsive by 00-A series reporting.

E. M. AEROMAT

Addendum
