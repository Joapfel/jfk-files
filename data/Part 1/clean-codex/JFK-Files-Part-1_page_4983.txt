Intelligence Information Cable. Priority. IN 064422. Cite: TDFIR DB-315/10100-76 (TDFIDRDB-315/10100-76). Distributed 9 October 1976.

Country: Venezuela / Cuba. Date of information: 30 September–7 October 1976. Subject: Reaction of the Cuban Embassy in Caracas to recent terrorist attacks against Cuban aircraft and installations. ACG Venezuela, Caracas, 8 October 1976. Field No. HVC-7509.

1. Source of paragraphs 2 and 3: A Venezuelan businessman with close contacts in business, government, political and diplomatic circles. His reliability has not been established.

2. On 7 October 1976 Pedro Gomez Fernandez, second secretary at the Cuban Embassy in Caracas, Venezuela, said that the Cuban Embassy was aware that a bomb was responsible for the crash of the Cubana airline flight off the coast of Barbados on 6 October. The Cubans, Gomez said, did not know on which leg of the flight the bomb was placed, however.

(Field comment) The plane was downed with the loss of 78 lives.
