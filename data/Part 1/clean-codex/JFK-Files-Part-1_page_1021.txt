ORTIZ RAMIREZ, Antulio

1. OLC 77-4777, 2 November 1977, regarding visit of Blakey, 2 November 1977.

Blakey provided a handwritten version of an autobiographical work prepared by an inmate at Leavenworth (Levenworth), who claims to be a former Cuban intelligence officer. It included an allegation that the author, while serving with Cuban intelligence in Havana, saw a file on LHO which indicated that Oswald was a Soviet intelligence agent.

2. SOVERN memo to C/LA Division, 3 November 1977: Attached above and forwarded for action. Requests preliminary findings before extensive efforts are undertaken.

3. Memo from C/CI to SOVERN, CI 600-77, 16 November 1977, regarding manuscript. Hopkins of Cuba Desk is reviewing Ortiz-Ramirez (Orgiz-Ramierz)'s file and will forward to the Staff a copy of her memorandum. Six pages written.

4. SOVERN memo to OLC, 25 November 1977: Preliminary response that CI (Holmes) has drawn material together. May be reviewed by Blakey.

5. C/LA Division memo for SOVERN, 25 January 1978: Evaluation of information in manuscript of Mr. Antulio RAMIREZ Ortiz, with four attachments. Copy sent to C/CI.
