Lamberto Besta

April 1955: Resident of Milan at Via Teodosio 23, collaborated for a brief period with Demigod Demigote regarding clandestine traffic in strategic materials. Lives by trafficking in cigarettes and other things—not excluding dope. Also maintains contact with head of French intelligence in Italy in political field. Person of doubtful honesty, braggart, and capable of carrying out any kind of activity for money. (Source: Demigote.)

March 1954: Italian (Ital) service (Ministry of Interior) offered to turn over to Milan station full control of PIC operation in which Besta was involved. Headquarters warned “heads off” because of Besta’s extremely bad reputation—he has been implicated in all sorts of contraband traffic, as well as having a reputation as a completely minimum, unanchored intelligence operator.

November 1953: He was an attempted double-agent operation by Italian (Ital) service into Yugo I.S.
