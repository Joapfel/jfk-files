15 September 1965

Memorandum for: Deputy Chief, WHD for Cuba

Subject: Request for Payment to AMBIDDY-1

1. It is hereby requested that you approve a payment of $532.55 for AMBIDDY-1. This payment is to reimburse him for expenses incurred during a trip taken by him in August 1965 on our behalf.

2. Attached for your information is a translation of AMBIDDY-1's request in which he outlines his expenses.

Approved: DCWHD/C
