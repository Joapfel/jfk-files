Date of info: 27 April 1968.

Subject: Glenn Joseph-Charles has been released from prison and apparently must pay Paidson US$695,000.

Place and date acquired: 30 April 1968.

This is my report No. 00-A(S) 1316368.

Source description: Naturalized U.S. citizen of Haitian birth. Source maintains close contacts with Haitians in the U.S. and with provincial business and political figures in Haiti and travels at regular intervals to the Caribbean countries.

1. Clemard Joseph-Charles was released from prison on the evening of 25 April 1968 and proceeded to his residence. CIA source tells us that Charles is both physically and morally well and is now making preparations to return to work.

2. This source has been requested by Charles, through a party, Edmond Vilaire, Port-au-Prince, Haiti, to travel to anytime as soon as possible, because Charles wants to talk to KIM. Charles has been compelled to agree to take out a letter of credit in favor of the National Bank of the Republic of Haiti in the amount of US$695,000. This letter is to remain valid for 30 days from the time it is taken out. Charles has been given a short time, not specified, to produce the money and the letter of credit.

3. Madame Charles committed her husband to the foregoing course of action. Following her own release from prison, she was visited by an unknown (to our source) party. This individual set forth the conditions under which Charles would be released. The conditions included the US$695,000 mentioned above. Madame Charles accepted the conditions and signed a paper to that effect. Her signature committed her husband.
