Instructions to Appointing Officer.—You must determine whether the following appointment would be in compliance with the Civil Service Act, applicable Civil Service Rules and Regulations, and acts of Congress pertaining to appointment. This form should be checked for holding of office, pension, any record of recent discharge or arrest, age, citizenship, and membership in a family. Also, to establish the identity of the appointee, you should particularly check (1) his signature and handwriting against the application and other pertinent papers, and (2) his physical appearance against the medical certificate.
