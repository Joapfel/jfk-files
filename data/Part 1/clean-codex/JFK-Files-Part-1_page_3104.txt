30 March 1976

Subject: Ivan Gavrilovich ALFERYEV. 201-0236701. (PDBOOR)

RIS: Known KGB.

Suspect KGB Resident, Lima, Peru (DOI: March 1976).

DPOB: 20 January 1928.

Career

September 1958: Granted one-month Argentine visa in Moscow. No indication he ever travelled to Argentina.

13 September 1960 to 24 October 1964: PCS, Mexico City, Mexico, as PRAVDA correspondent.

27 January 1968 to 17 July 1972: PCS, Buenos Aires, Argentina, as Second Secretary, then promoted to First Secretary, Soviet Embassy, Cultural Section.

May 1975: Scheduled TDY Argentina. Was TDY Argentina June–July 1975.

RIS: Known KGB. Per PDSTORAGE, DOI: September 1964. FCD, Fifth Department (Latin America Department).

Replacement Pattern

Mexico: ALFERYEV replaced Vasiliy Mikhaylovich CHICHKOV (201-0207254) and was replaced by Vladimir Agafonovich DOLOEV (201-0762035), both known KGB officers.

Argentina: ALFERYEV replaced Viktor Mikhaylovich FILIPPOV (MD 001708) (201-0243246), carried as Suspect KGB Resident in Buenos Aires. ALFERYEV was replaced by Vasiliy Mikhaylovich MURAVYEV (201-356530), whom headquarters believes was KGB Resident (Suspect) during his tour in Buenos Aires.

Peru: ALFERYEV to replace Arseniy Fedorovich ORLOV (201-0727613) as Counselor of Embassy, Lima, Peru. ORLOV was carried as Suspect KGB Resident in Lima. ALFERYEV will be carried in the same capacity as Suspect KGB Resident.

ALFERYEV arrived in Lima PCS in early March 1976 or late February 1976.
