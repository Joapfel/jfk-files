In discussing financial support for Haitian operations, Dejoie said ex-Dom. Gen. Luis Reiniso Trujillo passed Duvalier $378,000, later gave an additional $148,000 for Duvalier protection of the Trujillo clan. Dejoie did not reveal when the funds were passed but said they were used by Duvalier to purchase weapons in Europe. (Source: UOAMBER-3, 28 June.)

C/S comment: Gave summary Dejoie activities for 24 and 25 June ’64.
