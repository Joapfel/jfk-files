Outgoing Message.

Staff. CONF: CHU/3. INFO: File PDC, “Eyes Only!”

Date-Time Group: 191830Z. Message Reference Number: 411279. Disseminated 28 per 4/18 (FG).

To: Immediate Caracas. RYBAT PLVUCADET. CHOADEN from PARDEE. Ref: Caracas 2L988 [IN 921770].

1. I am pleased to inform you that you have been selected as Chief, WH Division. Your appointment will be effective at such time as you and your family can conveniently return to the Washington area. Department of State orders for your move will be requested on a priority basis on 12 May. Would appreciate your tentative schedule as soon as practicable.

2. Your selection will be announced early this next week. Therefore, you should feel free to indicate to whoever you feel necessary locally the reason for your return and what your next assignment will be.

3. I want to extend my personal congratulations on your selection. I am sure you will handle this very important assignment with the same kind of excellence, dedication and leadership that have characterized your past assignments.

Date: 19 May 1973. Orig: James H. Flannery. Unit: AC/UHD. Ext: 3366. Concur: Director of Personnel. Date: 5/23/73.

Authenticating Officer. Releasing Officer. Coordinating Officer.
