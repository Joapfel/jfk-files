071937Z July 1968. Cite Managua 6617 (Treckler acting).

Director information: JMCOBRA, Panama City; Port-au-Prince.

9 July 1968. AMBIDDY-1 (C-82064) told the ACOS on 7 July that he had received a phone call from his wife in Miami advising that someone had been trying to contact him. AMBIDDY-1 assumes this might be his headquarters case officer and wishes to advise headquarters that he will be returning to Miami on 8 July.

2. Following additional information picked up in a 2 July meeting with AMBIDDY-1 on events that occurred prior to his departure from Miami during the first week of July:

A. (FNU) Blanca Roles, Panama consul in Miami, contacted AMBIDDY-1 and told him the Panamanian Minister of Hacienda, who was visiting in the United States, wanted to meet with AMBIDDY-1 to discuss an unspecified urgent matter.

B. Eugene Maximillian, Haitian consul in Miami, told AMBIDDY-1 that President Duvalier (Duvallier) wanted to see him. The purpose was to carry a private message from Duvalier to President Somoza. Duvalier claimed that if AMBIDDY-1 did this, Duvalier would release the son of Leon Cantave from prison. Somoza has given AMBIDDY-1 permission to make the trip. According to AMBIDDY-1, he and Somoza figure this is a play by Duvalier to try to open more doors for acceptance in Latin America.

7 July 1968. C-82064.
