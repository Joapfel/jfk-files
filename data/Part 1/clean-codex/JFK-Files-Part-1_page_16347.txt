31 July 1964 UMG.

To ADDP, C/OI, G/FI 2, VR.

271118Z. Priority PRET, info priority DIR, cite DURB 0293 3. RYPAT/SECAROS. Reference DURB 0289 N3486C.

31 July 1964, 4:01 OS.

1. SECRET's wife delivered airletter posted Joburg 24 July. States SECARDS actually left Joburg evening 25 July, carrying letter. CHALLEY still in Joburg, talked to her on phone 26 July.

2. Summary letter follows: Leave this evening Rhodesia. Air service charter DC-4, Captain MULLOCK. Whole authorization for movement rests on one telegram from TSCHMEE, which Colonel BUY's (BUYS) security held was ambiguous. Touch and go until 1400 hours when BUYS finally gave go ahead.

3. PUREN-ROT, his soul, had promised contact Security this morning, instead left last night with WICKS, HEDGES, and a few pilots including MACKINTOSH. PUREN very unpopular with Security at moment, ourselves also.

4. About 45 men, all other ranks, fly with me tonight. Expect second flight will be in 7–10 days' time. Meanwhile busy denying whole thing to the press, who have the story, probably from one of the men in pub. Inevitable.
