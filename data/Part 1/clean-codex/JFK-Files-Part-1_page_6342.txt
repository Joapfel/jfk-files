Classified message. Date: 153, 6 October 1960. Cite: MEXI 6502 (WARE acting). To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 9. Info: DCI, D/DCI, DDP, COP, ADDP/A, FRD Mexico. Date distributed: 8 October 1960, 1406Z. IN 24498.

To DIR info MASH. Cite MEXI 6502 (WARE acting).

JANET

1. Problem of Roberto de Varona becoming acute as indicated in following paragraphs.

2. Orrison reported info from LITAIN'T-5, 29 September 60, that Varona rented and furnished independent office on Calle Independencia to handle AMHAWK affairs. L-5 said impossible for anyone to work with Varona, even AMHAWK himself, which is why AMHAWK funds him 2,000 dollars per month. With these independent funds little can be done to restrict his independent representation of AMHAWK, although this plus his refusal to adhere to demarcation wipes work Mexico delegates, makes for continual dissension. Recommend Twicker frank discussion with AMHAWK on this problem to determine if AMHAWK will agree sending Roberto on extended Latin American trip with AMHAWK funding and leaving Rolando Rojas Mexico as his delegate. Strongly feel can weld Mexico delegation into strong vehicle for implementation AMCIGAR objectives if Roberto removed; if he remains here, feel overall efficiency Mexico delegates will be undermined by dissension.

3. LITELL-T has continually pointed out problem of Varona brothers as affects Mexico delegation. Says Roberto continually cuts across lines and runs practically independent operation.

4. On 28 September 60 Roberto Varona told Limask that FRD big plan shaping.
