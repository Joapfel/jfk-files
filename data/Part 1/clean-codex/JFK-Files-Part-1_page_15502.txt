Classified Message. Date: 22 September 1961. To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3 (C/AF TEMPO slotted at 1550, 22 September). Info: COP, C/CI, C/FI 2, C/TSD 2, S/C 2 cc. Routing. 22 September 1914Z 1961. Routine. IN 33643.

Priority, direct cite LEOP 2088.

RYBAT WIROGUE.

Immediate action.

Desensitized per GSH 43-43.

1. ZAPPLEY, when at Beach Hotel, Braz, afternoon 22 September, saw WIROGUE get out of taxi. WIROGUE greeted ZAPPLEY, saying he was “George.” They talked briefly about how to cross the river with ferries closed. WIROGUE asked if it was best to return to Paris or enter the Congo from other countries. WIROGUE said he still had a permit to enter and leave the Congo from the Ministry of Defense.

2. This raises many questions. What was WIROGUE’s attitude on termination? Any information on who is paying his way now? What name and passport is he using? If he plans to use the old permit from the Ministry of Defense, then he is still using the same name as when here before. Does he still have the false passport issued by KUBARK? When did he leave PBPRIME and what was his destination? Is it possible he is on the payroll of another country, or does he possibly want back on the KUBARK payroll? Request MOS views and guidance.

End of message.
