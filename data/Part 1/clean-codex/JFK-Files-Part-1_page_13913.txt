Country: Cuba/Mexico. Report No. CS-3/575,185. Subject: Additional Assignments for Members of the Cuban Embassy Staff in Mexico City. Date distributed: 10 March 1964. No. of pages: 1. Date of information: 18 January 1964. Place and date acquired: Mexico, Mexico City, 30 January 1964. Field report No. HMM-8426.

This is unevaluated information. Source: graduate department. Appearance of content is varied.

Source: Latin American official in Mexico City who has regular contact with Cuban Embassy personnel; he has been a reliable reporter for about a year and a half. LITAMIL-9.

On 18 January 1964 the Cuban Embassy in Mexico City announced a three-month plan, according to which certain staff members will prepare special reports, in addition to their usual duties. Raul Anaricio Nogales, Consul for Cultural Affairs, will prepare a report on the Universidad Nacional Autonoma de Mexico (UNAM—National Autonomous University of Mexico), which will include the physical layout, activities of the student body, and ways in which the Cuban Government can exert influence there. Luis Alberny Souto, Cultural Attache, was instructed to prepare a similar report on the Instituto Nacional de Bellas Artes (INBA—National Institute of Fine Arts). Margot Machado Padron, Consul, will prepare a report on countries which have diplomatic relations with Cuba, emphasizing how contact with their representatives in Mexico will aid the Castro Government. Rogelio Rodriguez Lopes, Second Secretary, was given an assignment as Political Reporting Officer.
