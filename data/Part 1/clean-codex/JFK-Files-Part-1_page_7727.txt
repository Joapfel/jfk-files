4.14 Sleep.

Does he enjoy sleep and fall off easily? Read or walk to prepare himself for sleep? Does he rise easily? Prefer to remain abed until the last moment? Is he an early starter? A late starter?

4.15 Impressions.

Most women would find him sexually attractive. Most men would find him average and sexually attractive. Without prior knowledge, if seen on the street, he would impress as average. On the basis of looks alone, he might be a worker. He is conspicuously athletic. Looks like a boxer.
