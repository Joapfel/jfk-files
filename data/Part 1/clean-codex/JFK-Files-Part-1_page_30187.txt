Since October 1968, as Chief of a division responsible for the Agency's physical security program, he had primary responsibility for the supervision of some 70 professional and clerical personnel, together with the programming, budgeting, and implementing of the total physical security program of the Agency. As a result of his technological experience, he developed and implemented physical security programs which produced for the Agency a physical security posture of pre-eminent quality within the U.S. Government.
