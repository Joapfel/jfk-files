Biographic Data

Subject: Yarly Ivanovich Nosenko (Yarly Ivnaovich NOSZKO)

AKA: Turi Nosenko (Turi NOISSIKO)

Date and place of birth: 30 October 1927, Nikolayev (Nicolayev), Ukraine, U384.

Marital status: 1946 — Married and divorced Flavia Mosetko (MOSETKO), née Shishov (SHISHOV). 1946 — Married Augustine K. Kosenko (KOSENKO), née Telekin (TELEKIN); separated in 1949 and divorced in 1951. 23 June 1933 — Married Ludmila Velinacvna Kosenko (KOSENKO), née Kozhevmkova (KOZHEVMKOVA); separated in January 1964 and divorced in 1969.

Children: Daughters: Czана Kosenko (CZANA KOSENKO), born 21 August 1954, Moscow; Tamara Kosenko (TAMARA KOSENKO), born 13 July 1958, Moscow. Both living in USAR (USSR).

Education: 1942–1943 — Attended various USAR (USSR) Naval Schools. 1948–1952 — Attended Institute of International Relations, Moscow.

Employment: March 1951–March 1953 — Soviet Naval RU (Naval Intelligence). March 1953–February 1954 — Officer in KGB (The Committee for State Security).

Military: Lieutenant in USAR (USSR) Navy while in school and during RU service.

Nationality: Soviet.

Present and past political affiliations: 1943–1954 — Member Komunistol (Komсомol), USA. 1956 — Candidate Communist Party. 1957–1964 — Member Communist Party, USA.
