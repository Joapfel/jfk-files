Dispatch. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. To/CC: REDTOP DIZTAG: Unconfirmed Report of Travel to Mexico of Oldrich Novicky. Advice Required—References.

1. It was first reported by the LITEMPO airport personnel (according to their list of passports photographed by them at the airport) that Oldrich Novicky was among the passengers arriving in Mexico from Havana on 7 June 1968, at 0958 hours. According to the LITEMPO list, he was the only Czech on the CUBANA flight.

2. However, the CUBANA manifest did not list Novicky's name; the only Czech on the manifest was Frantisek Prochazka (E.C.S.R.), OCC Diplomatic Courier, Diplomatic Passport No. 3076.

Therefore, the LITEMPO film accompanying the list was checked carefully, and it was found that the only Czech passport actually photographed by LITEMPO was that of Prochazka (even though his name was not on their list).
