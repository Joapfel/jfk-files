RYBAT TYPIC ZRPERUSAL.

French firm Richard Freres has supplied several hundred tractors to Cuba in little over one year. These included four or five types, of which CDIO probably heaviest. Main unloading port for these tractors appears be Havana. Two shipments involving about 30 tractors of different types were contaminated with abrasives in May and June 1963.
