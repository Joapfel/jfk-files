From: SE (I-27). Agent: I-111. Via: I-1.

DD-2279

22 May 1968

Subject: Letter sent to RECE from Venezuela by Luis Posada Carriles.

Source: Augusto Pineda (mat. unk.).

Sub-source: Aguedo Lugo (mat. unk.).

1. On 22 May 1968, I-111 obtained the following information from Augusto Pineda (mat. unk.).

2. On 19 May 1968, Pineda was visited at home by Aguedo Lugo (mat. unk.), who had returned from a trip to Venezuela.

3. During their conversation, Lugo showed Pineda a photostatic copy of a letter that had been sent from Venezuela by Luis Posada Carriles to Augusto Valdes Miranda, also known as “Polo Miranda,” a RECE leader. Lugo had delivered this letter to Valdes Miranda by hand.

4. At a moment when Lugo was not looking, Pineda took the photostatic copy of this letter, which is attached to this report. In this letter, Posada mentioned the following matters:

a. The aid that had been given to the Association of Bay of Pigs Veterans (Brigade 2506) by the Venezuelan Army and the reasons why this aid was stopped.

b. The possibility of the Ministry of Defense and Ministry of Interior becoming involved in a plan to help Cuban exiles fight against the Castro regime.

c. RECE should do everything possible so that RECE will be the Cuban exile organization to receive this aid, with Posada offering to help RECE in this respect.
