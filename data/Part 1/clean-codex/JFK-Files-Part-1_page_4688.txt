Due 16 March 1978.

Country: Venezuela.

Subject: Status of Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch.

Date: 9 March 1978.

Source: A Venezuelan businessman with good contacts among Venezuelan security officials. His reporting is generally reliable.

1. On 9 March 1978 Francisco Leandro Nóbrega Mora, lawyer for Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch (O S & M), said that a group of Bosch’s Cuban exile followers planned to meet in the Caracas Milton Hotel in Caracas, Venezuela, on the evening of 1 April 1978. To attend this meeting, Leandro said, one individual would be coming from Miami, Florida, and one from Puerto Rico. Representatives from Venezuela would also be attending. (Source comments: The reason for the meeting is not known.)
