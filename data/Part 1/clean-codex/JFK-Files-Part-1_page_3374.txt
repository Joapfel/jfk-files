2. AMBIDDY-1 complained about AMYUN-19's reluctance to exercise the full range of his responsibilities, attributing the reluctance to fear of AMBIDDY-1, excessive preoccupation with anticipated KUBARK views and reactions, and, in this context, concern that by some inadvertent means he might cause a rupture between AMWORLD and KUBARK.

3. AMBIDDY-1 also complained that AMYUN-19 surrounds himself with people whose orientation is strongly, even fanatically, Catholic, and that AMYUM-19 upbraided AMBIDDY-1 upon the latter's return from DOMREP for seeking to cultivate a Protestant minister.

4. Also, AMYUN-19 imparted to AMBIDDY-1 rather sweeping criticism of the PILON raid plan by “Company,” not confirmed, for allegedly overlooking the presence of a machine-gun emplacement in the target area.

5. Underlying all complaints, AMBIDDY-1's feeling that AMYUM-19 may head some kind of cabal masterminded by KUBARK. Believe flat.
