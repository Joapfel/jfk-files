Classified message. Date: 8 Jan 1959. To: Director. From: Paris. Action: SR 6. Info: CI, CI/OPS, FI, FI/OPS, EE 4, WE 4, S/C 2. Date-time: Jan 8, 1959Z. Priority. IN: 45386.

To Director, info FRAN FROB CITY PARIS. Re: REDWOOD REDSOX AEACRE AEASPIC. Re Director 04860 (OUT 54658).

1. IDEN REF, after completing full albeit compressed assessment, believes AEASPIC satisfies all selection criteria.

2. Request authority make definite arrangements transfer subject FRAN about 1 February. Anent handling, and believe it would be wise give subject definite answer before he returns GAP morning 10 January, which date his permit to be away from GAP expires.

3. Please reply priority.

4. Further: consider transfer AEACRE after removal FRAN ought not be protracted. Will follow with VIP pouch from FOB.

End of message.

C/S comment: Requested assessment of AEASPIC, AESATCHEL and Joseph McGourn (IDEN).
