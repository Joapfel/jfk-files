Garcia had connections with Orlando Bosch (BOS CH) and other suspects in bombing. Morales was recruited in Miami in February 64 for use as radio operator and member of paramilitary infiltration team, trained in Miami and at Isometric, but terminated August 64 without being used in any agency-sponsored mission against Cuba. He volunteered, signed up and was trained toward for PM action in Congo September 64, although there is conflicting information in his file as to whether he did in fact serve in the Congo. He was terminated in December 64. For Friends Caracas: Please note this document's statement in Director 93153 that Morales did not serve in Congo. He was arrested in Marianna February 68 for involvement in bombings (BOMBINCS) by Cuban police group. Station passed pertinent information on him to FBI at that time. He has been paid pre information and in October 72 was registered in ISR to ENDD. Was named as CIA agent in Manuel de Armas (MANUEL DE A RMA S)'s “press conference,” April 7, which received considerable publicity in Caracas. DESCRIBET-R, sensitive penetration of liaison, has described Morales as obsessively anti-CIA and disposed to turn any unpleasant situation or event, including Cuban aircraft bombing, into “CIA plot.” Morales has been of considerable date.
