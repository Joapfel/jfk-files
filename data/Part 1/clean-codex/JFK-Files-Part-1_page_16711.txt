Dispatch.

1. Arnold P. ARBART (pe) would like to depart from Mexico on 10 September 1964 for home leave and return about 26 September 1964. He does not wish further leave since it will be necessary to take his younger son out of school. His older son will be entering college in the United States, which is the reason ARBART requested leave at this time.

2. A technician would be preferable to an operations officer as a temporary replacement for ARBART, as the tonnage of the job will be maintenance of equipment and protection of M'SON interests inside the LP. Once ARBART will be away, four weeks, it is requested that Headquarters reconsolidate sending a representative. The representative will be better able to do the job if he has Spanish; this, however, is not essential, as certain LADM's there speak English.

Distribution: 2 - C/WMD. Cross reference to NSA/CHG 5 and number IBM-1741. Date: 23 July 1964. R-50-6-75/2.
