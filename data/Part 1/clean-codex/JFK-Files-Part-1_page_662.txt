Background Information:

Born April 6, 1921, in Amsterdam, Netherlands. Single. American citizen.

Education:

Amsterdam High School, Amsterdam, Netherlands. St. Louis University, St. Louis, Missouri — ASTP, June 1943–February 1944. Georgetown Foreign Service School — two years, June 1947–August 1948. University of Chicago — M.A. in International Economic Relations, 1951.

Languages: German, Dutch, French, and Italian.

M.A. Thesis: “Post-war Economic Recovery in the Netherlands and the Marshall Plan” (received honors).

Employment Record:

June 1950–present: International Economist, GS-7, in the European Program Division of ECA. Mainly concerned with Italian and Trieste economic affairs. Analysed the economic and financial position of Italy in light of the ECA aid program and handled administration of the aid program itself. Also prepared two-, three-, and four-year forecasts designed to show expected economic trends and developments in Italy. These were included in the ECA congressional presentations.

October 1947–June 1948: Research analyst in the Historical Division of the Department of the Army. Engaged in selection of and formulation of reports on German documents that shed light on the West Europe campaign.

October 1946–February 1947: Archivist in the Office of Military Government for Germany, Department of the Army. Served as chief of the Documents Library, collected reports, dispatches, cables, and other materials, and developed an index system.

January–August 1945: Assistant to the Food and Agriculture Officer with the Military Government Section of Headquarters, Twelfth Army Group. Duties consisted mainly of availability and requirements studies connected with food needs of the German population.
