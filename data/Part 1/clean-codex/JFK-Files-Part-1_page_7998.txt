From: Habana. Report No.: HCH-1177. No. of Pages: Two. No. of Enclosures: None. Report Made By: Shelby L. Overstreet. Approved By: Sherwood P. Rozhon.

Distribution by copy: 1 Washington; 1 Embassy; 2 Files. Orally to: None.

References: HCH-1142, 27 March 1957.

Source, Operational Data, and Comments.

Subject: Views of Rafael GARCIA Bárbara on Cuban Political Situation.

Comments: OVERSTREET was interested in meeting Rafael GARCIA García because of the report that Subject was instrumental in influencing the political thinking of Raúl SÁNCHEZ, former Cuban Military Attaché in Washington, now serving a six-year term in prison for conspiracy to overthrow the Cuban Government. The introduction was made by Noé LÉVITS, a law student at the University who had known Subject when he was a professor. OVERSTREET and Subject have become friends because of their mutual friendship for PARQUÍN.
