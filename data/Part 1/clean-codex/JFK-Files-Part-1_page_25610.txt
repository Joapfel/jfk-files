Civilian Office of the President  
Department for Human Capital Development of the Republic of Mexico  
The Revolution

Date: 13 November 1968  
Title: General Order  
Issued by: President of the Republic of Mexico, by the authority of the Executive Branch.  
To be used as part of an addendum to the Constitution and laws of the country and its states.

Text: May 24, 1968. President of the Republic of Mexico, by the authority of the Executive Branch, issues this order, which may not be photocopied or reproduced in any form without prior written permission from the Executive Branch. Any person who photocopies or reproduces this document is guilty of a criminal offense.

The following are exempt from this order:

Governmental employees; students; employees of public schools; soldiers; military personnel; prisoners; foreign visitors; members of the executive branch of government; members of the judicial branch of government; members of the legislative branch of government; members of the military branch of government; members of the paramilitary forces; members of the police force; members of the fire service; members of the national security forces; members of the intelligence services; members of the secret services; members of the penal system; members of the armed forces; members of the national defense forces.
