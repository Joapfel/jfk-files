Staff. To: Guayaquil. Info: LA Miami, Quito.

MINTEL REAM TRACE

Reference: A. Guayaquil 22049. B. Guayaquil 17852 (dated 01 Aug 73).

1. Following are HOS traces on Evelio Duque Miyar (201-0302008):

A. In 1956 Duque started his fight against Batista in the Escambray and obtained the rank of captain. In June 1959 he formed an organization under the name of “Cuban Anti-Communist Youth” in order to combat the Castro regime. In August 1960 he openly raised arms against Castro in the Escambray Mountains.

B. Duque and four others arrived in the Miami area by boat on 5 June 1961. A POA was granted Duque in August 1961 and he was contacted and debriefed. He was put in touch with JMWAVE. Duque asked for our assistance to obtain arms and regroup his guerrilla fighters in Cuba. It was agreed to assist Duque and the group. He received no salary and the only commitment made was to train and infiltrate Duque’s group if they followed our direction and control. In May 1962 his POA was cancelled because Duque refused to permit the team to enter Cuba. Since then we have had no further contact with Duque.

C. In July 1963 Duque, now leader of the Ejercito Cubano Anticommunista, Frente Escambray (ECA), and his ECA group planned to take the keys on the northern coast of Las Villas Province. The true purpose of the mission, however, was to embarrass the USA and force it into direct action against Cuba. Also wanted to seek recognition from OAS and UN with the hope of putting the USA in the middle.

D. In 1966 the FBI reports that Duque and his ECA group have not been active because of a lack of funds.

E. From 1966 until 1973 we have no information regarding his activities.
