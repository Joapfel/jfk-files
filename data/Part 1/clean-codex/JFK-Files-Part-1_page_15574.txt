RYBAT

Documentation for Georg Reiner

Military Certificate

1. Military Certificate issued by the 6th Grenadier Training Brigade (Infantry). The designation O.U. in the upper-right-hand corner means “Ortsunterkunft”; it is the place of issue, which during wartime did not specify a town or area due to security considerations. It merely means the place where the unit was physically stationed at the time the document was issued. The certificate states that Capt. Georg Reiner, born 25 July 1921 in Salzburg, lost his regular military pay book on 2 February 1944 on account of enemy action. A new book has not yet been issued.

Soldiers Ersatz

2. The Soldbuch Ersatz (pay book substitute). (a) The front section (Soldbuch-Ersatz) indicates that the document was issued to Capt. Georg Reiner, born 25 July 1921 in Salzburg. His dog tag number is 214739. His nearest of kin, his father Franz Reiner, lives in Salzburg, Siebenburgerstr. 2. (b) The section Orden und Ehrenzeichen indicates that he received a medal for having been wounded on 9 December 1942. (c) The section Beurlaubungen über fünf Tage shows that he was granted leave for over five days. It was convalescent leave that entitled him to free transportation. The lower portion of the same section indicates that he received shots for dysentery (Dihr) and was vaccinated. The other side of the document shows the following from left to right:

a. Clothing record.

b. Issuance of rationed shaving cream and soap.

c. Name of military unit to which he belongs.

3. It is requested that the subject sign the document in the space provided (Soldbuch Ersatz name over Eigenhändige Unterschrift).

Do not use a ballpoint pen. Have the subject practice his name several times before affixing it to the document.
