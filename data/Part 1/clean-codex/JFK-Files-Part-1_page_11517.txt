19 December 1968

1. LIRING/3 gave Rowton two letters dated 10 December 1968, from Rafael Argueta A. and Rigoberto Argueta A. (Alvarado), in which the former states he is working in Chiquimula and gets home only every fifteen days. Another letter states, among profuse greetings and best wishes, that “Juan José y Miguel hirákxkarríxx tienen un año para hacerle fuerte al destino,” which LIRING/3 interprets to mean that the León Barrios brothers intend to resume activity on behalf of FAR as soon as they are released.

2. Liring told Rowton that he hopes one or both brothers will come to Mexico after release and asked Rowton whether he could determine the probable date of release so that he could be prepared to receive them. Liring will reply to the letters, inviting and hoping that “los leones” will come to visit him when they leave their cage.
