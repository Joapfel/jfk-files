Dispatch. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. From: Chief of Station, JMWAVE FJQ. Subject: Operational/Typic/AMBUD—Transmittal of Biographic Data. References: A. UFGA-25261, 3 June 1966; B. UFGA-26584, 6 December 1966.

1. AMTIKI-1 has obtained biographic data on Case No. 17, which is forwarded under separate cover. The case number is keyed to Reference A, attachment No. 1.

a. Case No. 17: Requirement completed.

2. AMTIKI-1 has received interim replies concerning Case Nos. 75, 135 and 143, which are also forwarded for Headquarters information.

a. Case No. 75: The attached letter did not provide the complete date of birth. However, the provision by the trustee of the address for the mother will enable AMTIKI-1 to obtain the data from her. Further, JMWAVE plans to forward monthly payment directly to the dependent, instead of the trustee, effective with the February 1967 payment.

b. Case No. 135: The attached letter did not provide the date of birth. It is planned to handle this case in the same manner as Case No. 75 by obtaining the data from the mother and subsequently forwarding payment directly to her, instead of the trustee, effective with the February 1967 payment.

c. Case No. 143: The attached letter states that the data requested will be forwarded when received from Cuba.

Attachments: 4 letters.

Distribution: 3-WK/COG w/1 copy (4) attachments under separate cover.

Date typed: 13 Jan 67. Dispatch symbol and number: UFGA-26771. Headquarters file number: 19-124-25126. Cross reference to: CS copy. Date dispatched: Jan 17 1967. Project code: 14-14-2512. Box: Fold: 3.
