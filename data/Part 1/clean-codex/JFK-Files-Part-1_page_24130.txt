Dispatch.

To: C/NOVIEW. Info: COS/London, C/Eur Div, C/MOMUSE. From: Chief, LCPIPIT. Subject: DTCORIC/Operational; OREBONY/British Immigration Status.

1. The USC memoranda sets forth a problem which OREBONY/1 recently encountered at London Airport and how it was solved with the assistance of London Station and JAGUAR.

2. As matters now stand, British Immigration will take no action against OREBONY/1 without checking with JAGUAR.

AARON C. LAGINESTRA.

Attachment: USC. As stated above, paragraph 1 USC.

Distribution: 3 – C/NOVIEW with attachment USC; 2 – C/MOMUSE with attachment USC; 2 – COS/London without attachment; 2 – C/Eur Div without attachment.

S/C attached by RI/AM. Dated 1 May 1967.

Cross-reference to dispatch. Dispatch symbol and number: OEPA-211. Date: 17 April 1967.
