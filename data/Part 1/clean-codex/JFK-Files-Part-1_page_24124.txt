Dispatch. To: C/NOVIEW. Bio: C/MUSE, COS/London, C/Eur Div. From: Chief, ICPIPIT, 2051. Processing action: marked for indexing; no indexing required; only qualified desk can bridge indexing. Subject: LTDOORIC/Operational CREPREY/CREBONY. Microfilm.

Action required—references.

As HQS is aware, GREBONY/1 has been pursuing a legal action against Jacques Verges. He has now received a letter from his lawyer which seems to indicate that Verges is willing to make some sort of settlement—although he can only pay in Algerian dinars. GREBONY/1, of course, is willing to accept a settlement provided it includes some sort of apology from Verges. Armed with this apology GREBONY/1 could then dispose of any lingering doubts regarding his political reliability caused by Verges' accusations and would also have the ego gratification of forcing Verges to eat his own words.

ACH. Aaron C. Laglestra.
