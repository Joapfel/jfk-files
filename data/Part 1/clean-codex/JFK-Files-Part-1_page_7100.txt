Dispatch. To: Chief, MI Division. Info: Chief, SB Division. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: RELAYCOAT/ECI PROVE — Ernesto CIAZARO Ferecerino. Reference: Mexico City 7688.

1. Transmitted herewith is the local OENNY report on Subject which was promised.

2. OENNY still has not been able to contact Subject in order to put him in touch with the Station but ones to do so during the week of 4 July.

For William C. Curtis.

Enclosure: OENNY Report.

Distribution: 2 — CH, with enclosure (2 copies), II/N. L/- CSB, with enclosure (1 copy), II/N.

EC-6/PTT to C/M/I.
