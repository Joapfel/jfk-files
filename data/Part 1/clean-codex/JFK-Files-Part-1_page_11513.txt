d. In May 1959, one Humberto González (GONZALEZ) Vega was reported as being a member of the Batallón (Batallon) Guatemala. This group had been recruited to join the Nicaraguan revolutionary forces in trying to overthrow the Nicaraguan Government of President León Somoza (SOMOZA) Debayle. This same report indicated that González (GONZALEZ) had been involved in the Nicaraguan Revolutionary movement, was a member of the POT, and worked as sales manager for the Amalia de Nicol (NICOL) Company. The address for this company was reported as 6th Avenue and 10th St., Zone 1, Guatemala City. (RSG-1331, 22 May 1959, RSGM/1 from RSGM/15)

e. In late July 1960, one Humberto González (GONZALEZ) was reported as being a leftist who was financing a group which had prepared plans for a coup. (Source: RSG-1766, 2 August 1960, ESTIM/1 from ESRECHSS/3)

2. Headquarters would appreciate any additional information on González (GONZALEZ) that might be developed by addresses.
