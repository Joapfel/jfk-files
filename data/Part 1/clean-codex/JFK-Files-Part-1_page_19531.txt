Section II: Objectives of the Course

The objectives of the course are to retrain changes in organization, policy, and procedures in the Intelligence community with special emphasis on those areas of the intelligence community where the Contact Division serves.

Section III: Additional Requirements of the Course

The course is designed for experienced DCI personnel. The first part of the course reviews the organization for national security, with emphasis on the Intelligence community. The second part of the course includes a survey of the current world situation. Subjects are supplemented by discussion periods and briefs. The students have opportunities to discuss general collection requirements and OCI services with representatives of producing offices.
