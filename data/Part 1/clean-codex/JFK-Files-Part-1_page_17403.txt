Classified message. Date: 16 August 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/W 5. Info: ADDP, C/C1, C/C1/IC 2, C/F1 2, S/C 2.

August 16, 0349Z, 1962. Routine S.

IN 46148.

DIR CITE MEXI 1693.

R/BAT.

Reference MEXI 1691, paragraph 5, C-65535.

1. Station suggests Arehart and LIENVOY-2 TDY HQS week beginning 23 August. Arehart to arrive 23 August followed by L-2 on 22 August. Purpose to study and select smaller recording gear, probably Wollensak (WOLLENSACK), for LIENVOY use. Plan move LIENVOY base into LIELEGANT office where previously located to increase operational security and overall efficiency. This was proposed by LIELEGANT several weeks ago, but station consideration of expense of new gear delayed suggestion until reference proposal arose.

2. Believe two days adequate time L-2 remain HQS. Request HQS approval trip.

End of message.

C/S comment: “Paragraph 5 MEXI suggested possibility present LIENVOY gear could be used by LITEMPO.”
