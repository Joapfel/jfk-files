5. On 29 July the Cuban Congress reserved passages for ten Costa Ricans on Cuban Air's flight on 31 July. Juan Duch Colcel (GUAN DUCH COLCEL), writer and member of the Central Co-Chalet (Central Co-CHALET) of the Partido Comunista Mexicano (PDM—Mexican Communist Party), told Rogelio Roquez Lopez (ROGÉLIO ROQUEZ LOPEZ), second secretary of the Cuban Embassy, when he was to see him on 23 July, that he had been put on the waiting list for 24 July, but it was certain that he could leave on 25 July. Daniel Molina, of Politica, Mexican Marxist Democracy magazine, also had tickets to leave on 25 July. (Feliçon (FELIÇON): It has been previously reported that Mol Más (MOL MÁS) was to attend the celebrations either for Politica or as a delegate of the Movimento de Liberacion Nacional (MLN—National Liberation Movement), pro-Cuba, anti-United States Communist Front.)
