Field comment: Rey, a Cuban émigré who was a cabinet minister under Fulgencio Batista Zaldivar, went to the Dominican Republic in April to act as an adviser to Balaguer during the electoral campaign. In Miami in mid-June, he reportedly planned to return to the Dominican Republic. See also UFG-8378, which reported that, according to Rey, he had been offered the use of a finca in the Dominican Republic for anti-Castro guerrilla training. Another source, who has been reliable in the past, reported that Rey was conferring with Rolando Masferrer and Antonio Rojas on 14 June. Constanzo Palau, Cuban émigré in Miami, has been reported earlier as involved with Rolando Masferrer in plans for anti-Duvalier action.
