Asian-African Journalists' Association (Persatuan Wartawan Asia-Afrika)

Secretariat: Press House, Djakarta  
Tel: 2116

Djokarta, April 3rd 1964

Dear Colleague,

“Arrested greeting!

As you are aware the Secretariat at its Second Meeting held in Cadoek, Bogor (Indonesia), from November 30th to December 1st 1963, decided, inter-alia, to name April 24th, the opening day of the first Asian-African Journalists' Conference, “THE ASIAN-AFRICAN JOURNALISTS' DAY”.

You will readily agree that the naming of April 24th, as Asian-African Journalists' Day, is of immense importance to us Afro-African journalists.

The Secretariat call on all its member organizations and members to observe this Day by celebration and meetings; press statements, articles and write-ups etc. in the various Afro-African newspapers and mass media generally.

This day will also mark the first anniversary of our Association and we earnestly call on you, dear members and colleagues, to mark this day as a historical event in the life of all Afro-African journalists particularly, and the Afro-African peoples generally.

We hope that you have by now received copies of the first issue of “the Afro-African Journalist”. If you have not, please contact us and we will gladly let you have some (state how many).

We further call on you to continue sending us articles on the press-as well as on the struggle either for independence, or for the defence of your independence in your country-for publication in “the Afro-African Journalists”.

We look forward to receiving material, newspapers etc. from you.

So as to inform all our member colleagues of your activities could you write us of or send us clippings of your activities on “the Asian-African Journalists' Day”.

We look forward to hearing from you soon and wish you, in the Bandung spirit, all success and the further solidarity of our association.

Lh/bif.-
