19 Aug. 51

L.G. 201 – AGM

LO 831-7

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individuals concerned

1. Following-named (Fel-named) individuals, M/P on 20 Aug. 51 to Pusan and Seoul, Korea, on TDY for approximately fifteen (15) days in connection with military intelligence activities. Upon completion, will return to proper station.

Capt. John H. Closson, 01647325, STG C  
Mr. Newton S. Miler (Milor), D.C. employee, OS-13 (on detail)

2. Travel by military aircraft (mil aoft), rail, and government motor transportation authorized. In addition, sixty-five (65) pounds of personal baggage authorized while traveling by air. No per diem authorized.

3. Individuals are authorized to perform duties as official couriers during the period of TDY.

4. Mr. Milor (Miler) will wear the Army officers’ uniform, less insignia, in the combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from Cad Sales Store (Cd Sales Stero) upon presentation of these orders.

5. Individuals may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after completion of TDY.

6. Immunizations and physical inspection as prescribed by Cir. 9, FEC, 27 Feb. 50, will be completed by each individual prior to departure.

Distribution:

CFS (2)

2 individuals concerned (15 each) through C2 Soo
