File: “STALINE” photo-book.

SUS new photo-book in his hotel, 19 March 1951.

Lhunson said they are now planning in terms of a book of up to 220 pages, between 1,000 and 1,500 photos, some of which might be two or four photos to a page. “It will cost a lot of money.”

I mentioned again that we have £40,000 appropriated under K.F.U.Y for “similar photo-books at the discretion of C.S.P.,” but Lhunson said there was no need to spend the taxpayer’s money; as long as possible, those books should be made to pay their own way.

To Mr. Bryan.
