Input to US defector machine program. 20 March 1969, ACS. CS copy. 201-746537.

17 March 1969, 151047Z March 1969. Cite Zurich 1629. Director info SERN. Reference: Director 83209.

1. Nagell last appeared at Zurich Consulate the morning of 7 March and stated that if he received no satisfaction by five o'clock that afternoon he would carry out his threats and expose the US Government on radio, television, and in the press.

2. He then disappeared. Nothing further was heard from him, and no publicity was given. On the morning of Monday, 10 March, the Zurich Consulate was advised by telephone from the US Consulate in Barcelona that Nagell had approached them and made similar threats.
