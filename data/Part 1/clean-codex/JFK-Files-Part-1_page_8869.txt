Dispatch. To: Chief, WJ Division. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: Administrative/Finance—Loss of Personal Funds—Edward E. TICHECRHIN.

Reference: HMS-3032, 22 April 1964.

1. The delay in answering reference should not be construed as lack of desire or intent, on the part of TICHECRHIN or the Chief of Station, that the case be submitted to the Board of Review for Shortages and Losses for consideration, but rather as an administrative delay of getting the facts, people, papers, etc., together and in one place for submission.

2. Attached is a statement prepared by TICHECRHIN regarding the loss of funds and containing the pertinent circumstances of the case. The sub-paragraph numberings are in accordance with, and relating to, the requirements of paragraph 3, a of FR 34-250. The information requested in sub-paragraphs numbered 8 through 12 is answered jointly by Stewart R. PATANER and Jerome H. BALSIS who were assigned to investigate the matter by the reporting official.

3. PATANER and BALSIS talked with TICHECRHIN at different times, regarding the shortage of funds and the happenings as related to them; were substantially the same as outlined in TICHECRHIN's statement. Since there is no other way to verify the facts and TICHECRHIN has proven himself to be truthful and forthright in his dealings with the Station his statement regarding the loss of funds is accepted as fact.

4. Since TICHECRHIN is authorized travel expense “on actual, reasonable, and necessary expense basis” it does not seem sensible for him to have reported the loss of funds in other than the way he says actually happened.

Continued.

Distribution: 3 - MD, w/att.
