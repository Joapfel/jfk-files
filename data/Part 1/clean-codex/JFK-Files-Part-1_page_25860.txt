Attachment to Training Memo.

Name: Ramon Joseph ALVAREZ Du Hunt.

Born: 7 December 1933 in New York City.

Citizenship: Mexican.

Father: Fernando ALVAREZ, deceased, Mexican citizen.

Mother: Janio Du Hunt, vdn. de ALVAREZ, U.S. citizen, born 13 December 1906 at Sumter, South Carolina.

Sisters: Maria and Adria.

Official identity papers: Mexican passport No. 41776, issued in Mexico City 19 December 1953; renewed 16 November 1954, No. 7772, good until 16 November 1956; Mexican draft card No. 1963865.

Religion: Protestant, nondenominational.

Education: American High School, Mexico, D.F., diploma July 1952; Mexico City College, B.A. in Latin American studies, June 1955.

Languages: English and Spanish.

Military service: January–December 1953—Mexico City, XVIª Division de Infantaria, 15/o Compania de Transisiones, Corporal.

Physical description: Age 23. Height: 5 feet 9½ inches. Eyes: hazel. Face shape: oval. Complexion: fair. Posture: erect. Weight: 145 pounds. Teeth: normal, straight. Hair: dark brown. Scars: 1-inch horizontal scar on tip of chin. Build: medium.
