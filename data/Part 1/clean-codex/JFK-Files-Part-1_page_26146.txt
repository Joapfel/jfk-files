RICO, Victor (Galan).

201-336474. Card 5. HMM-8262. F XII 7695. IN 9525L. 9 January 1964. TDCS-3/561,563.

Subject, Mexican Marxist journalist, is making preparations to go to the Dominican Republic from Mexico in the very near future, with the considerable purpose of gathering information in preparation for some acts in the Dominican Republic. LIENVOY, B-2.

Field comment: Subject, member of the Propaganda Committee of the MLN, pro-Cuba, anti-U.S. Marxist (Max) Communist front, and writer for Siempre, Mexican (Max) leftist magazine, visited Panama, Guatemala (Quat.), Nicaragua (Nic.), Costa Rica, Venezuela, and Brazil (Brasil) between mid-October and mid-December 1963. According to several earlier reports, subject spent eight days in Cuba during May and June 1963 interviewing Fidel Castro Ruz, Prime Minister of Cuba, during the latter’s stay in the USSR. During this period subject was given an assignment to visit countries in South and Central America, where he was to persuade leftist parties to begin immediate acts of sabotage and violence to take pressure off Venezuela (Venes). He was also instructed to approach competing leftist factions in the countries he visited and unify them so their work would have greater impact. In various countries he visited, subject conferred with leading Communists and leftists.

12 June 1964. H. 9278. 12 June 1964. A. 8. 64. DIR 94930. 16 January 1964. Based on SDOM-2619, IN 99172, 15 January 1964.

Reference is made to TDCA-3/569,940, distributed 11 January 1964, subject: Travel of subject to the Dominican Republic.

Information has been received from the Dominican Security Service that subject left Santo Domingo for Miami, Florida, on 15 January 1964, on Pan American Flight 132. Above to FBX and I&NS.
