Continuation of dispatch. REDA-23487.

5. On 2 March 1968 LIOUNI-1 (LIOUENI-1) reported that she had decided to visit her brother in Paris for two weeks or so, and would take advantage of the trip to renew her tourist documentation. She said that LIOUNI-1 had become extremely difficult to live with since REIBECKER was here, and that she must get away from him for a while. He objected to her leaving, but acquiesced when he became convinced that she intended to return to Mexico. LIOUNI-1 planned to leave on 4 March 1968 and would return on or about 20 March 1968.

William C. CURTIS
