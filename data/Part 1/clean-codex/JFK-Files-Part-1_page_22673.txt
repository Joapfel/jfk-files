Routing. Priority: Deferred. February 25, 19—13:59. Priority: X Routine. Received cable sect. Priority: Operational immediate. Initials.

OUT75968

To Mexi. Info Hava. Cite Dir 14189.

Re Mexi 3392 (IN 22960).

1. On basis HAVA and ODEVY reports on Hogan, has preferred not to improve his chances securing Cuban account. According to ANNAIL, Cuban official in HOS area, 20 February, an account may go to either of two firms in PBPRIME with which KUBARK has contact and either of which would serve ODYOKE interests better than Hogan.

2. However, in event Hogan beats out other contenders, it could become advantageous to have KUBARKER in his group, depending on nature of account. Therefore, request NOEMAYR attempt to keep job offer open without committing himself to Hogan and thereby strengthening latter’s hand with Cubans. Use caution in touching on weak aspects of NOEMAYR cover story, which could be checked on by Hogan.

3. MICKEY is George Michael Kappes, about 24, U.S. citizen who has worked with Hogan since late 1958.

End of message.

WH comment:

*NOEMAYR received letter dated 1 February from “Mickey” sounding out NOEMAYR for job with Don Hogan, who hopes to get major public relations contracts with Cubans.

WH/III/Mexico. Paul Zientz for J.B. R. H. DAllitto.

Coordinating officers.

H. N. DAllitto, C/M/III.

Authenticating officer.
