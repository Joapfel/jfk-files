List of Soviets in Mexico passed out to all Case Officers on 27 August 1969.

LOGINOV, Valentin Sergeyevich

Official function: Third Secretary (Consul). Wife: Aleksandra Dmitriyevna. Residence: Ignacio Esteva 21-2 (Tacubaya). Car: 31 DC (1969 green Chevy Chevelle; “Chevie Cheyelle” in OCR). Intelligence status: Known KGB. Tour in Mexico: August 1965–. Clubs: Consular Association, French Club.

He is primarily targeted against Americans at the U.S. Embassy and specifically RVROCK. He is a ladies’ man who cultivates American women. He is an extremely active and skilled intelligence officer who deliberately needles U.S. citizens whom he believes to be RVROCK. Attempted an open recruitment pitch to LIREALM-1 after so accusing him. Has recently levied requirements for an Embassy phone book and information regarding American female Embassy employees. Additionally, has recently requested an agent to cultivate a Chinese Embassy employee from whom he wants information regarding relations between Soviets and National Chinese against the Chicomos. LOGINOV is scheduled to leave Mexico RCS in mid-October. Avid tennis player; has a wide range of Mexican and third-national contacts. P-11, 7/12.
