25. Military service—dates, country, unit, rank, duty, where services performed, decorations, when discharged, and why. Give details if ever a prisoner of war.

U.S. Army, 1940–1941, Artillery, Private, North Carolina; discharged for entry in Air Forces. U.S. Air Forces, 1941–1945, Intelligence Specialist, Panama, India-Burma Theater; discharged at termination of war. U.S. Air Forces, 1946–1949, Technical Sergeant, Intelligence Specialist, political analysis; discharged at expiration of term of service.

26. Present and past membership in organizations (professional, social, political, etc.)

None.

27. Special skills, abilities, hobbies (radio, photography, etc.)

Photography.

28. Financial status—earnings, bank deposits, securities, property.

Consideration from G.I. Bill of Rights: $120.00. Approximate earnings from photography: $40.00. Owner of house and lot valued at $3,500.00. No current bank deposits.

29. Employment history—type of work, salary, dates, employers, addresses, and reasons for leaving. Include employment by any government.

Summer vacation, 1939–1940: Stapling machine operator, $8.50 per week, California Packing Corporation, DeKalb, Illinois. Left for service with U.S. Army.

August 1940–1945: U.S. Army and Air Force, Operations and Intelligence Specialist, Technical Sergeant, $135 per month; discharged at expiration of term of service.

August 1945–October 1945: Laboratory Technician, Sterling Silica Gel Corporation, $60 weekly; left due to strike complications.

June 1946–April 1946: Stevedore and labor foreman, U.S. Navy, Coco Solo (Coco Sole), approximately $65 weekly; left for entrance into U.S. Air Forces.

April 1946–April 1949: U.S. Air Forces, Albrook Air Base, Technical Sergeant, Intelligence Specialist; discharged due to expiration of term of service.
