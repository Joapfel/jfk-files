Att. to OHL-39,251. 24 September 1964.

A secret source has provided the following information about “Africa, Latin America, Asia Revolution” and its French counterpart “Novelstic,” and about the differences between Jacques Varguet, the publication’s Director, and Richard Ciboch, who was Editor until he was dismissed about two months ago.

2. On 13 August 1964, the Sudan Federal Council approved “Africa, Latin America, Asia Revolution,” which had hitherto been printed in Luxembourg, and the Sudan authorities took possession of previous issues and subscription lists. Ciboch was, however, able to retain possession of one copy of the subscription list, as well as distribution records and most of the unpublished manuscripts.

3. When Ciboch was removed from his position as Editor of the publication, he and his wife were owed a substantial sum in arrears of salary. Jacques Varguet made no attempt to pay these arrears, with the result that the Cibochs took legal action and received judgment in their favour early in September for a sum in excess of £1,500. Ciboch expects that Varguet will be unable to pay this sum or to meet other debts and that he will be declared bankrupt. Ciboch hopes that it will then be possible for File AMICHE, the Swedish printer of the English edition, to take over both the English and French editions.

4. Because of his financial difficulties with AF, Lat. Am., Ana Rev., Jacques Varguet has had to give up the tenancy of the Paris office of the publication at the Francophone lab, and has moved to 78 rue Galante, Paris 5a. Richard Gibson expects he will be declared bankrupt.
