To: Director. Info: Caracas (Camacas).

Reference: San Jose 19265 (N 84/160).

1. Upon receipt of Director [BURBUCATION], contacted HUFABRIC (HUGLIS-8) to advise of a positive threat against the life of Pascal Allende. As instructed by headquarters, HUGLIS-8 said the same information had been previously received by Foreign Minister Gonzalo Facio from the Venezuelan government; Facio had already passed the information to the HUFABRICS. Prior to HUGLIS-8's receipt of the information from the station, he had already personally visited Allende to warn him of the threat.
