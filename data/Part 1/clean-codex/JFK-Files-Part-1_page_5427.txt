27 June 1967, 1859Z. CITE CONTACTS/New York 8312. Contacts/Washington. Operational Support Staff (HUSULIN) from BALOG. Case 4754B.

1. In order to understand this TWX, the following code used by Haitian banker Clemard Joseph Charles and Luis A. Brun is quoted:

A. Bell & Co. — “Major” BALOG and DOD.

B. First National City Bank of NY — U.S. Government.

C. D. K. & Co. — Dorothy Natlack of DOD.

D. The Howard Corporation — Col. Burris, a former military aide to President Johnson.

E. Business transaction — help of U.S. Government to Charles.

F. On 21 June 1967, after, repeat after, his arrest and COMPIMENT to his home in Port-au-Prince, Charles wrote the following French-language letter via air mail to Louis (Luis) Brun.

G. *Etc.* As far as business is concerned, I received at my home a representative of Bell & Co. I am very happy now. I think we have no further worry concerning the business transaction. Me accepted to sponsor the business transaction with D. K. & Co., the.
