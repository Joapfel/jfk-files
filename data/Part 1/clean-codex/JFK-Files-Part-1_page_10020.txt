4 August 07. IN 25136.

COS has put no pressure at LITEMPO/S or LIRAMA level on his case; and since Orozco was found to have immigrant status with all papers in order, and since he claimed he would be killed by either FAR or the government if sent back to Guatemala, no pressure of any kind has been put on LITEMPOS to return him.

2. Will let LITEMPOS and GUATS work out the case, and believe LITEMPOS will release Orozco by noon on 4 August if, repeat if, GUATS have not convinced them to return him prior to that time.
