27. JMWAVE-9080, IN 44174, 1 September 1967, states that AMCLEVE-15 was now uncertain about returning to Caracas. Case Officer Comment: This seems a direct turnabout from paragraph 2 of JMWAVE-8997, sent three days before. AMCLEVE-15 now stated that he would be willing to go back to DIGEPOL for another job in Caracas to work for CIA, but he required a total income of approximately $600 per month to cover the cost of living.
