Alien (Situation) from FERDIN. Case 41,612—Jaroslav Krupník.

1. From chair, statement taken by Jans, using interpreter for German, not Czech; some certificate facts.

2. Dr. Jaroslav Krupník, born 25 April 1926 in Ostrava (CZECHORUVANIA). Wife Zdena (ZEFNA), née Puroverova; married 23 July 1955. One daughter, 2½ years. Father, dentist, Dr. Ludvik Krupník; mother Marie, née Krupek; all residing at Ostrava, Zorovska 22. Sister, Milada (MILITA) Kruplova, same address. No relatives in free world.

3. At Hospital of Steel and Iron Works, Vitrovice, Ostrava, was chief anesthetist and had two staff. Gave instruction wherever anesthesia was used, whether in departments of obstetrics, nose and throat, therapy, or other. Anesthesia was not so well developed in Czechoslovakia (CZECHORUVANIA) as in the U.S., often administered by nurses rather than U.K. TONG. State examination in surgery, June 1956; state examination in anesthesia, February 1958. After completing degree in medicine at Charles University in 1951, was at City Hospital Ostrava No. 1 as an intern, 1951–52; came to plant hospital in 1952, where he was secretary and surgeon of first degree, 1956–59; an assistant at second degree, 1960.
