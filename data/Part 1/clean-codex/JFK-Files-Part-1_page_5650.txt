Country: Haiti. Date of info: 5–19 May 1969. Subject: Request by Haitian opposition group for secret interview with U.S. official.

1. One of the underground opposition groups in Haiti is centered in the following individuals: Fan Laurens DPT Larenes (former deputy, now in semi-viding); Colonel Vestver Blain DPT Blain (head of Army Quartermaster Trademonger); Colonel Cayard DPT Cayard (head of the Coast Guard); Fan Constant DPT Constant (Chief of Staff). They wish to organize a junta, following the death or retirement of Haitian President Duvalier, and exercise power during a transition period. Following the transition period, this group wishes to have free elections and then retire from political activity. Larenes would like to become a minister. Blain would like a diplomatic appointment. Cayard would like to retire to his cattle ranch. The group has no particular candidate for president.

2. This group alleges that it commands wide support among Army personnel in Port-au-Prince and in the Haitian hinterland. The group is reportedly willing to work with any other opposition group, except the Communists, but it does not want to appear publicly beholden to the U.S. or to any other non-Haitian power.

3. Until recently, the plan of this group was to sit back, wait for internal upheavals, and then move in to assume power. They did not want to act on their own, but preferred to take advantage of.

10 May 69
