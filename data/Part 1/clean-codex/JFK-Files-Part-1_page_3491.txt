Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Telephone Conversation between AMEIDY-1 and Martin  
Date: 9 April 1964 at 2000 hours

1. AMEIDY-1 stated he had been unable to obtain the address of the Tampa barge and he was leaving this matter to AMTUN-19.

2. Concerning the DC-3, A-1 stated that Manuel Hernandez had talked to Petrosky. Petrosky did send a letter to Talaria instead of Carrillo as A-1 had informed this office before. A-1 will get a complete story from Hernandez upon arrival in Costa Rica. A-1 was advised to move the DC-3 to Panama under some pretext before Talaria notifies Carrillo of the cancellation of the charter. The logic behind this is should Carrillo decide to cause some trouble it would be difficult for the DC-3 to leave Costa Rica.

3. In conclusion the undersigned conveyed to A-1 our wishes for a successful operation.
