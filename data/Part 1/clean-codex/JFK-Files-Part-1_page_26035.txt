12 May 1969, IN 56236. 13 May 1969. AMCLEVE CIURBAN.

1. Station unilateral liaison asset AMCLEVE-15 reported Manuel Celestino (MARCANO) Cabrasquel (201-797282) and family left Caracas 28 April for Madrid aboard ship “Federico C.” Supposedly, Marcano to reside in Madrid as radio newscaster for “Radio Continente,” but in reality serving as government undercover agent. However, details of specific intelligence objectives unknown to AMCLEVE-15. Marcano under orders not to carry out intelligence operations for first six months.

2. Summary Marcano’s background: born 29 November 1923, Barcelona, Anzoátegui (ANZOCATEGUI) State, Venezuela; Venezuelan citizen, passport 148305, issued 11 January 1965. Married to Leonides Yolanda Montaner Gomez. Profession: accountant, newscaster.

Marcano was formerly intelligence chief, Movement Revolutionary Left (MIR), a pro-Castro-oriented extremist organization.
