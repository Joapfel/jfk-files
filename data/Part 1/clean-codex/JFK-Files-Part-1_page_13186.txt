16205, April 1968. Cite Caracas 9387. Director info JMWAVE.

16 April 1968, IN 92196.

1. AMCLEVE-15 working on CIRASH case involving possible smuggling from Miami to Venezuela. One of those involved in Miami is AGEDO LUGO, alias El Negro Lugo. In memo to Chief CIRASH to give his background knowledge of people involved, AMCLEVE-15 described Lugo as a Negro who infiltrated by LNERGO into Black Power groups in Miami.

2. Headquarters may wish to bring to the attention of LNERGO that possibly one of their agents is known to AMCLEVE-15 and thereby probably other Cubans in Miami.
