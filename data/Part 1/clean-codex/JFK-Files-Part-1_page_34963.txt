Miami, Fla., May 15, 1967

Dear Sir:

Your letter of May 2 was received by me, but as I do not have on hand my son Julio Antonio Yeea Suarez's birth certificate, I had to look for an affidavit.

Enclosed is a photostatic copy of my marriage certificate. This is one of the few documents I could receive from Cuba. I have asked many times for Julio's birth certificate, but up to the present I have been unable to get it. Enclosed is the affidavit signed by my close friend, Dr. Martha A. Garcia Ochoa, attorney at law, member of the Cuban Bar Association, journalist, former congresswoman, and a very honorable and well-known person. I have written again to one friend in Cuba, asking for Julio's birth certificate. If I receive it, I will send it to you immediately.

Thanking you for your interest in my case, I remain,

590 S.W. 10 St.  
Miami, Fla. 33129.
