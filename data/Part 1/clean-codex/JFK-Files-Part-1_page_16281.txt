3. On 27 September, a FATAC C-47 aircraft arrived in Albertville to support the ANC offensive against the Congolese rebels north of Albertville. This aircraft will be used to resupply isolated units and to drop surrender leaflets. Four 7-ton trucks have also been made available to OPS SUD to carry supplies from Elizabethville to Albertville. (Source: Lieutenant Colonel Hardienne, principal advisor to OPS SUD.)

4. On 25 September 1965, the town of Lusangi fell into rebel hands. No definite information was available as to the size of the attacking force or the whereabouts of the defending ANC troops. (Source: Hardienne.)

5. On 27 September 1965, two Somali citizens were arrested at the Hotel Dulac in Albertville. This hotel serves as the principal billet for the European advisors to the ANC in Albertville. The Somalis' papers were found to be in order, and they will probably be released on 28 September.

6. On 27 September 1965, the OPS SUD advanced headquarters plans to move to Bendera. At this same time, the Ninth Commando Battalion is to move from Lulimba to Makungu and Katshoka. Two days later, the Fifth Infantry is to commence its advance from Bendera to Makungu, Katshoka, Yungu, and Kasimba. (Source: Hardienne.)
