BHAY IN07905C.

1. From QUSEQUIN/QUDOZE, 6 May:

A. Carlos Alfaras, Cuban ambassador to Lebanon and Syria, told Antolin (probably Antolin Alfonso Castaneda (CASTENEDA)), Cuban Embassy Rome, that he (Alfaras) and his wife were scheduled to fly Madrid/Rome on 9 May, Iberia Flight 358. Antolin said that Salvador Vilaseca (VILASECA), ambassador to Italy, was in Cuba for a week's vacation; expected to return to Rome 6 May, Alitalia 366.

B. Guillermo Ruiz, chargé in Madrid, said he was traveling to Havana in mid-June for a meeting of ambassadors.
