23 September 1957

Memorandum for: Contract Personnel Division  
Via: FI/018, Projects Branch  
Subject: Raymond H. GERARD (ps)

1. It is requested that the two-year contract of Raymond H. GERARD (LIPSTICK-20), which was executed on 1 October 1955, be extended for a two-year period, effective 1 October 1957.

2. Raymond H. GERARD’s principal duties are administration and operational direction of the LIPSTICK (ILL STICK) team, processing files, contacting agents and acting as cut-out (out-out) for the case officer, conducting individual investigations, and handling cash advances, accountings, and property.

J. C. KING  
Chief, SD
