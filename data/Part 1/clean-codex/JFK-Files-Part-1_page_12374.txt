To Caracas; info WH/Miami.

WKSCARLET

1. On 6 February 1973, LNYUMA KIS advised that LNAGON in Miami attempted to review WKSCARLET-3 (iden for Miami official LNYUMA file) but was told that the file was at CENAL LNYUMA HOS office. LNYUMA HOS has advised its Miami office that LNAGON can review the file in Washington.

2. We do not know why LNAGON wants to review the file nor are we objecting, since we have been assured that any material connecting BKHERALD with WKSCARLET-3 would be removed and LNAGON would see only the normal LNYUMA file. LNYUMA stated LNAGON would not be suspicious because the WKSCARLET-3 LNYUMA file was held in Washington and not Miami, since LNYUMA maintains many files in restricted status in Washington.

3. Will appreciate any information Caracas and WH/Miami may receive which would throw light on LNAGON interest in WKSCARLET-3.

4. Caracas: Please do not convey this information to WKSCARLET-3.
