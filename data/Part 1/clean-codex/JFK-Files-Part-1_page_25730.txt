Classified message.

Date: 17 September 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: EE 6. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, WH 7, S/C 2.

18 September 0435Z 1962.

Routine.

IN 23918.

DER CITE MEXI 1957 (OUSLER acting).

NYXIS BEDOX.

1. Due late word that law school starts 17 September, BETUMBLE departed MEXI 17 September via American Flight 58. Instructed that HQS may contact although finances (including salary through 17 September, ops expenses, travel and 50 dollars living expense authorized oral agreement, but not bonus) cleared MEXI prior his departure.

2. At last meet with POTASZ 14 September, which authorized by CO, POTASZ indicated although not particularly satisfied with life he long ago has chosen Poland. BETUMBLE assessment he would not cooperate with KUBARK. POTASZ does have BETUMBLE address WASH.
