Classified Message 7.

Date: 17 October 1958.

To: Director.

From: Mexico City (Mr. Brady and Mr. Ogden, WH Division).

Action: WH 5 notified 0115, 13 October 1958.

Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS, EE 4, NEA 4, SR 4, WE 4, S/C 2, CI/SP.

Priority. IN 15475.

To: Priority DIR BERN. Info: CITE MEXI 2596.

TELA LISB CARA ZURI.

1. Maurice Halperin and wife, American Communists suspected Soviet agents, left Mexico City KLM Flight 958 for Curacao, Lisbon, KLM Flight 722, on 13 October. (Name of Daniel Pastor.) Then cancelled use of Pastor's packing made for trip Zurich to Tel Aviv.

2. KLM advises reservations made in name Mr. and Mrs. Daniel Pastor, and late on 11 October call from Mexi America Travel Agency advises Pastors had cancelled and requested substitute Mr. and Mrs. Maurice Halperin.

3. Halperins on waiting list Swiss Air from Lisbon to Zurich for 15 October, and KLM advises telephonic request by “Mr. Pastor” asked price round-trip ticket Zurich to Tel Aviv.

4. FC. Probable Halperins plan to join Alfred Kaufman and Martha Dodd Stern in Prague. Reservation technique same as Halperin used when he made plans for Sterns to flee to Soviet Union.

5. Request surveillances where possible, reports.

End of message.

C/6 comment: Relay of this cable to TELA has been stopped by Wash Signal Center. A relay request on Form 12 is required to effect relay.
