ATTACHMENT C

Orlando PIEDRA

Orlando Eleno PIEDRA-NEGUERLA (201-247879)

Also known as Orlando Eleno NEGUERLA-PIEDRA; Orlando PIEDRA; O. ROCA.

Born 13 December 1917, Havana, Cuba.

Believed to be currently a Cuban citizen.

Was Chief of the Cuban Bureau of Investigation in the Batista Government. Told FBI in June 1959 that he was forced to leave Cuba upon the fall of Batista. Reportedly head of an anti-Castro group in Miami in June 1959. In September 1959, reported to be friendly with Rolando MASFERRER Rojas, ex-Cuban senator and publisher. PIEDRA was reportedly furnished with money by Batista.

During the Batista regime, was included with others as a responsible official considered directly involved in the torture and killing of Cuban citizens. Resided in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, in February 1960. During the Batista regime, PIEDRA was in charge of all matters pertaining to gambling, rackets, and contraband. In 1953 in Cuba, PIEDRA was also involved in the business of Chinese contraband.

No indication he was ever used by the Agency.
