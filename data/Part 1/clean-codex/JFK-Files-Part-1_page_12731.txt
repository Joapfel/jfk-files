Mentioned WKSCARLET/3 to COS on one recent occasion, asking what the station's opinion of WKSCARLET/3 was. Committed himself to the job for WKSCARLET/3; the latter believes that since publication of the article it is most unlikely WKNOBLE/S will hire him.

Realize that all the facts are not yet in, but on balance we believe “BASILIO” article of little importance and possibly not related to government discovery of WKNUGGET teletap or nor of discovery of WKSCARLET/2's tap. However, we will lay groundwork so that WKSCARLET/3 can make a hasty departure for a cooling-off period should that become necessary. At the same time, we wish to avoid contact with him as much as possible for several reasons for the immediate future. In fact, station had planned to terminate him effective 32 June, as thus far he has been unable to land a job with access of interest. In view of these developments, we will consider possible termination as of 31 May 74 and pay for another month in view of short notice if necessary.
