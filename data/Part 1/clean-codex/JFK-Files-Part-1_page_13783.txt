Report CC-658, 29 May 64 (continued).

Rodriguez Lopez (Lapez), Rogelio (Rogalio): (DIP PP-D-63/454).

Rodriguez Lopez (Lapez), Rogelio: Left Mexico on 15 May 61, diplomatic visa. (Source: passenger list from “friends.”)

Left Mexico on 7 May 62, diplomatic visa. (Source: passenger list from “friends.”)

In December 62 he appears as Third Secretary in the Cuban Embassy in Mexico City. Promoted to Second Secretary and stayed in the Embassy in Mexico City. Was accredited to Mexico as Third Secretary. Was called to Havana and on 5 July 63 he returned to Mexico as Second Secretary. Dangerous. Reserved and competent young man. Works with interest. Hard core Communist. (Source: 30 July 63.)
