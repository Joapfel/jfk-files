II. Curriculum Vitae.

September–October 1958: Subject was a member of the Soviet Bloc Delegation to the 17th International Railroad Congress Meeting in Madrid, Spain.

June 1959: Subject visited Barcelona after the Congress, listed as an interpreter, and used passport no. S-39990.

June 1959: Subject attended the CEI Congress in Madrid.

September 1959: Subject requested a U.S. visa as a member of KHRUSHCHEV's party. Subject was listed as an employee of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

November 1959: Subject attended the Soviet Industrial Exposition in Mexico City.

6 January 1960: Subject flew from Mexico City to Havana.

February 1960: Subject attended the Soviet Industrial Exposition in Havana, Cuba.

7 March 1960: Subject departed Havana for Europe, still using passport no. S-39990.

19 September 1961: Subject arrived in Mexico City, accompanied by his family, via Sabena no. 555.

8 December 1961: An unidentified male called the Embassy and asked for Aleksandr Mikhaylovich BROVKIN (Third Secretary, suspect KGB officer). The caller was told BROVKIN had left Mexico and Sr. KOSTIKOV had taken his place.

6 January 1963: Subject was in Veracruz (Vera Cruz).

7 April 1963: Subject departed Mexico City, via Sabena no. 556, accompanied by his family for home leave in the USSR.

14 May 1963: Subject returned from home leave, accompanied by his family.

4 December 1963: Subject's address was Progreso 9, Apt. 6. In early 1965, Subject still had the same address.

March 1965: “Diario Oficial” (”Diario Official”) lists Subject at this same address.

7 December 1963: Subject went to Cuernavaca. Comment: Duration of his stay unknown, but believed to have been a pleasure trip.
