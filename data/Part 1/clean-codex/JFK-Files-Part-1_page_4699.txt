Intelligence Information Cable.

Directorate of Operations.

Originated by Responsible Decision Officer, originator from Central Intelligence Branch of CIO 1463, Box 7009, Category III, [Signature] to Director, Bureau of Central Intelligence.

This is an information report. Not finally evaluated intelligence.

Report No. TDFIR OH-515/15285-77. Date distributed: 24 December 1977.

Country: Venezuela/Cuba/Netherlands Antilles. Date of information: Mid-December 1977. Subject: Intercepted message for Cuban exile leader Orlando Rosch (Bosch) on Cuban exile meetings in Curacao. Acquired: Venezuela, Caracas, 23 December 1977. Field No. HVC-7828.

Source: A Venezuelan businessman with a wide range of contacts with Venezuelan security officials. His reliability has not been established. This information should not be discussed with officials of any foreign government, including those of Venezuela.

1. In mid-December 1977 Venezuelan security authorities intercepted a coded message which was being smuggled into the prison cell of Orlando Bosch M., who is currently under detention in Venezuela in connection with the 6 October 1976 bombing (combing) of a Cubana airliner. The following is the text of the note:

“We expect that you will regain your freedom around the first of the year. There will be a meeting of friends on 20 January, at the Hotel Curacao International. We will discuss a very L.”
