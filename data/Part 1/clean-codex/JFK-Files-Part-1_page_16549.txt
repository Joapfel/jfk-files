Dispatch. Processing action: XN. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Info: No indexing required. From: Only qualified desk can judge indexing. Subject: Chief of Station, WU/Miami. Action required—references: Microfilm. Reference: UFAA-1246.

1. AMBIG-1 did not recognize the photograph of Manuel Ignacio Quesada San Martinach (201-327266), which is contained in Volume I of the Cuban logbook.

2. AMBIG-1 recognized two names from the listing of Cuban officials in Spain, which was forwarded by OSHA-27086 of 24 May 1971. They were Federico Crespo Pino Martinez (201-818437) and Federico Crespo Moreira. AMBIG-1 commented that he had known Espino quite well and that he was in periodic contact with him from April 1961 to May 1963. AMBIG-1 added that Espino used to be the director of an import house in Cuba, but was unable to recall the name of this firm. AMBIG-1 stated that he had known a Federico Crespo Moreira from his days in the Cuban police. According to AMBIG-1, Crespo worked underneath him from 1959 to January 1961, when both men were transferred to the Army. AMBIG-1 commented that Crespo was given the rank of sergeant. AMBIG-1 last saw Crespo around early 1962. AMBIG-1 added that Crespo was a Caucasian, would be about 32 years of age, and that the last time he saw him, Crespo had blondish hair, was about 5'9" tall, and weighed about 170 pounds. Station files list Crespo's DOD as being 9 August 1940, which dovetails with AMBIG-1's information.

3. AMCARD-1 information on the subject(s), if any, will be forwarded in the immediate future.

Distribution: Eustace B. Klock. Chief, WAD. Cross-reference: 201-327266, 201-818437. Dispatch symbol and number: UFAA-3472. Typed: 6 October 1971. HQS file number: 201-327266.
