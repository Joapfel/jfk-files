21 September 1949

To Whom It May Concern:

This is to certify that Dr. Kornilov Mykola has been employed with IRO General Hospital Rosenheim as the Chief Doctor of TB Control since 1 October 1948 to date, to our great satisfaction.

Dr. Kornilov Mykola has had long practical experience, is very capable, and precise in his work.

He has a most pleasant manner at all times, willing and well liked by patients and personnel.

Since Dr. Kornilov wants to immigrate shortly, we wish him all the best for his future work and life.

Dr. msc. W. JAVORSKY  
General Hospital Director

Dr., Rosenheim, Rathausstr. 4
