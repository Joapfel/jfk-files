Technical Services Division — Technical School

Short Range Agent Contact Survey (A-105)

Training Report

Name: Phillips, David A.  
Office: WH/COG  
Date: 6 June 1969

I. Objectives:

To provide a general knowledge of:

a. Selected gear used for clandestine short-range agent contacts, including representative samples of one-way RF radio devices, two-way RF radio devices, two-way carrier-current devices, optical communications, and special telephone devices used for establishing agent contact.

b. The philosophy, purpose, considerations, and limitations of short-range agent contact systems, including message security, link security, reliability, and feasibility of agent contact systems.

Instructor: TSD/Technical School
