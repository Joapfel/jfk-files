He says that if there was any idea that he would return, Peters would not be able to control the merchandise. His whole discussion indicated that he envisions that he may return. S advised that he mentioned insured pay for the merchandise through 31 December, but he did not know how things would go after that.

7. S was high in his praise of his case officer, whom he regarded as very helpful, and regretted not being able to bid farewell to COS LEOP.

8. As an aside, S cautioned that what he believed to be Leo's view—that Mobutu (MODUTU) was friendly to KUBARK (KIBARK)—is wrong. He said that he had heard Mobutu (MODUTU) talk often enough for there to be no doubt that he does not like KUBARK (KIBARK). S has no idea what the basis of this dislike is and thinks that it is probably a counter-reaction to the great services that KUBARK (KIBARK) has rendered to the Congo. He said that this was a peculiarity of Mobutu's (MODUTU'S) character.

9. S does not at all exclude the possibility of Modombe becoming one of Mobutu's (MODUTU'S) ministers. He said that Mobutu (MODUTU) has been accepting some advice and guidance from Tshombe (TSHOMBE). As to Kasavubu (KASAVU), S feels that he also might at a later date become a minister under Mobutu (MODUTU), even though he appears to be finished at this time. He believes that a combination of Mobutu (MODUTU), Tshombe (TSHOMBE), and Kasavubu (KASAVU) would be good for the Congo.

10. S's son has completely recovered; however, S had a severe attack of malaria.
