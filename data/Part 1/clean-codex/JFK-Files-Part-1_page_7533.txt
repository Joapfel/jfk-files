From: Hanoi. No. of pages: 1. Report made by Walter J. Ingold. Distribution: None. Source cryptonym: Tunic-2. Report No.: ZFII-89. Enclosures: None. Approved by James M. Lanier. Reference: HANO 2/42 and WASH 01125.

Remarks:

1. In our last message to Tunic-2 (Tunie-2) on 30 July we used the discrepancy concerning Leo Figueroa's visit to NVN as an example in a gentle lecture on the virtues of accurate reporting. By 1 August Tunic-2 answered back. The man obviously takes pride in his work.

2. An open comment relating to this report will be sent in another pouch or via cable.
