David TEIGECKVILI

Brief of Activities

Time/Age, Place, Activities:

A. 1918, Georgia: Born and lived there for 2–3 years.

B. Childhood:

1921, age 3, Paris: The family arrived from Georgia to France.

Age 4–6, Paris: School: Notre Dame. Change of address.

Age 7, Paris: Lycée Montagne. Change of address.

Age 8 or Viroflex: École Communale.

Lycée Hoche. Thrown out.

École Communale. Taken back.

Lycée Hoche. Change of address.

Acquaintance with Jean ADAM and with old Georgian Resistance men.

Paris, age 13, or Vanves: School: Lycée Michelet, 6 months; thrown out.

Age 14: École Communale (the first).

École Communale (the second).

Age 15, Chalon-sur-Marne: Trade school: Arts et Métiers, 1.5 years.

École Breguet, electrotechnics.

1937, Paris: Stole a dictionary from a library.

Three months: Thrown out of home, lived in a hotel, worked in a railroad station at night.

On parole, had to renew documents every 3 months. Failed to do this; detained for 13 days.

March 1938–May 1939, Paris: Back in father's home; sent to School of Photography and Movie. Unfinished.

December 1939, Paris: Volunteered for the French Foreign Legion.
