1. On 12 November 1976, the Washington Post published an article by Joe Crankshaw in Miami which reported that FBI had been told of a DINA plot to use Cuban exiles to kill Luis Andres Pascal Allende and Anne Marie Beausire (Brussier). Following are excerpts from article:

A. Ricardo Morales, Venezuelan intelligence official, reported to FBI that he had been told of a plot to kill Pascal and Beausire by Rolando Otero. According to Otero, he went to Santiago in early February and identified himself to DINA officials as a fugitive from the U.S. At DINA headquarters, Otero said he was told that the secret police support the Cuban exiles' cause and would provide funds and arms for them in return for a “favor.” The favor was to kill Pascal and Beausire in Costa Rica before 22 February 1976.
