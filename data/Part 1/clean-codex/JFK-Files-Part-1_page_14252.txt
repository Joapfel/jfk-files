JMCOBRA, info Director, La Paz. TYPIC. Reference: JMCOBRA 0490 (40 635-07).

1. Station has monitored the activities of Orestes Guillermo Ruiz Perez (OCR: Orestes Guillermo (Ruiz) Perez) and his wife closely through QUDOZE/QUTIMID since the couple's arrival in Madrid last December and is convinced the report by VECIANA is absolutely without foundation. Ruiz, far from being dissatisfied with PEGIME, is a convinced supporter who believes that in serving the Cuban Revolution he is serving the best interests of Cuba. Ruiz is interested in the outside world and in maintaining dialogue with varying types of people, but does this from an intellectual foundation which has accepted Castro's interpretation of communism as applied to Cuba. The only element of dissatisfaction noted is Ruiz's feeling that he is not in a position where he can be most useful. He believes he is wasting his talents in a diplomatic post and would like a position more suited to his interests in either commerce or industry where he can.

9 August 1968. IN67286. 201-735246.
