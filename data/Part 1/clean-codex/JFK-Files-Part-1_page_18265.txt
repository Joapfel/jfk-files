Classified message. Date: 27 May 62. Routing. To: Director. From: JMAWAVE. Action: TFW 10 (Mr. Charles Matt, TFW, notified and copy slotted at 1830 27 May). May 27 2216Z 62. Info: DCI, DDP, ADDP, CA 2, CA/PRG, CA/PROP, S/C 2. Operational immediate. IN 41885.

Copy FIP cite WAVE 4977.

CYEPOSE.

1. Miami Herald 27 May carries front-page item by James Buchanan entitled “Miro Fades as Exile Chief: Three Latin Leaders ‘Behind Ray’.” Article flatly states Miro and council have been replaced by Manuel Ray with provincial MRP head in United States. Further, article states, “From variety sources Herald learned that Miro was notified of change only this week,” and that Ray’s appointment as “chief in exile” came from “under-ground in Cuba,” according to Joseph Godoy, provisional MRP head in Miami after finishing resumed Cuban activities since origin of AMBUD. Article creates Cubans in Miami with saying “new man in field” is Adolf Dahlén, who has been special assistant to State Department and is also member of President Kennedy’s special task force on Latin America.

Issuance being that Berle is running new United States-sponsored Cuban effort.

2. This release causing some concern in local Cuban colony to extent of at least one CPS agent destined for isolation training has agreed to be believed if there any truth this story. Meanwhile WAVE.
