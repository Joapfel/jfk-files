1. Reference is made to secret Caracas 1678 dated 12 February 1976, above file title, which requested biographical information on Ricardo Morales, presently residing in Caracas and working for a sensitive government agency.

2. Morales was an exempt C.I. for Miami R/O, originally established as SG I-1-CGG by S/A Octavio Gonzalez, presently SNAC Bogota (BGOTA) D/O. C.I. was exempted due to the fact he was an active C.I. for Miami FBI working on Cuban terrorists, and was primary witness against Orlando Bosch in famous Cuban Power trial in 1968. C.I.'s number later charged to SG I-2-CG57. C.I. presently inactive at the Miami R/O due to permanent move to Caracas to take up official position within government, working for a sensitive government counterintelligence agency. C.I. presently in charge of the airport in Caracas and plans to acquire Venezuelan citizenship. C.I. primarily working for the government on Cubans residing in Caracas.

3. Morales is a white Cuban male (MILE), born 06/14/39, POS in Cuba, is 5 feet 8 inches tall, 170 pounds, with dark hair. Morales formerly in G-2 in Cuba during the 1950s. C.I. has I and is number 412-377-777. C.I. activated on 02/25/71 at Miami R/O, primarily for intelligence information on ANGOCO2 violators in Miami. C.I. did (DJD) initiate GI-71-0015, Manuel Penabaz et al., which led to purchase/seizure of six kilos cocaine and arrest of seven defendants during 1972. C.I. also participated in GI-72-0166, Orlando Lambrind (LAMBRIND) et al., which resulted in seizure of seven kilos cocaine and arrest of five defendants during 1972. C.I. testified for the government at both trials.

4. Reference paragraph number 3 of Caracas 1973. No photograph of Morales available in Region 5. C.I. has supplied volunteer intelligence reports to Miami R/O on at least 25 different investigations; therefore, copies of R/O's will only be forwarded to Caracas R/O if deemed (DEEDED) necessary by Caracas R/O.

5. For information of Caracas R/O, Morales contacted DEN Miami 12 February 1976 and stated that he had meetings with R.D. Sacramen in Caracas.

6. Morales was an extremely reliable C.I. for Miami R/O, and any utilization by Caracas R/O should be with caution due to C.S.'s present status within government.

John A. Lund, Jr.
