EXH-A-645

Concerning the concentration of arms in the hands of Cuban youth leaders. John F. Kennedy and President Kennedy are well-known leaders of the country. One thing that can be done is to prevent any further exportation of arm equipment against the Batista government, concerning the restoration of the Constitution of 1902 and the holding of general elections. There is no record of Bormarck or ALFARCO Coudin in the election files.

20 January 1978.
