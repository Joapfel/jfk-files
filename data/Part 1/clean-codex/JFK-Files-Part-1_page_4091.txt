220025Z. DIR INFO MANA. CITE WAVE 4157. TYPIC AMWORLD AMOT.

22 June 95: 16327.

1. During the week of 4–18 July 1965, Rogelio Jhones and Lilis H. Vidana (please protect) told AMOT-120 the following:

2. AMBIDDY-1 has transported a steel barge loaded with equipment to Port Everglades, Florida. The cargo belonged to the dismantled base in Nicaragua. AMBIDDY-1, using King (FNU) as mediator, offered Teofilo Babun of Antillean Marine Shipping Corporation the barge for $25,000, which is [illegible] by 27 feet and in very good condition. Babun did not agree on the price for the barge but made an offer to purchase both the barge and other equipment of interest to him.

3. King is offering to sell considerable radio equipment, surgical instruments, rafts and boats, winches, and electric generators, all of which remain in two warehouses in the WAVE area.

4. According to Pedro Acevo, former head of AMYIM’s base in Nicaragua, AMBIDDY-1 is planning to sell all equipment that was used in Nicaragua for his own profit. The wife of AMBIDDY-1, in talking with close friends, said her husband thinks he will be able to sell all the equipment for $200,000, which, added to their savings of $300,000, will make them [illegible].
