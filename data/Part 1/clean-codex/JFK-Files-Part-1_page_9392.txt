On the whole, it can be said of the Congress that all is well that ends well. The criticism made above is not intended to contradict this general favorable impression.

2. I find on reading and rereading this criticism, that it is, in general, sound and sensible. I should like to request that particular attention be paid to the latter part of the memorandum in which five recommendations are made with regard to the future work of the movement initiated by the Berlin Congress. I should also like to draw particular attention to the reaction to the behavior of David Rousset (Roussset) and Ignacio Silone. This is one further indication for justification that Rousset must be constantly and thoroughly watched with a critical eye.
