12 December 1967.

Managua 6377. Director info MVAZE. Type AMBIDGY. Reference: Managua 6328 (MIN 87575).

1. AMBIDDY-I told Barker we tried to contact “Mister Wilson” (GROWERY) several times by phone at the number given to him in early November by Wilson. Persons answering did not know any “Wilson.” As a result, contact was broken. AMBIDDY passed on one bit of intelligence regarding Soviet policy on Cuba to LNERGO Miami.

2. Please cable the correct phone number and notify persons that they are to expect calls for “Wilson.”
