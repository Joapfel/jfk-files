VI

Financial Analysis

Total Estimated Investments for the Project: The estimated total sum of the project is US$ 237,000. The breakdown of this amount is given at the end of this chapter. The construction requested from INFONAC is given below under general headings:

Construction Equipment: Two 2-ton concrete dump trucks, carts and concrete vibrators (we have compressors), crane bucket for concrete, CanyAS for submerged cement work, iron piping, etc. Total value approximately US$ 25,000.

Construction Materials: 20,000 bags of Lone-Star cement; 100 tons of corrugated metal, 1/2 inch and 3/4 inch, with tie wire; 18,000 square feet of zinc sheathing; 5,000 square feet of construction wood; 50 tons of rail sections, 4 inches; 64 sets of hinged vises, 1 1/2 to 2 1/2 feet in diameter; railroad accessories, etc. Total value approximately US$ 40,000.

Carpentry Equipment: Table saw, 2 feet x 10 feet; handsaws; hinged vises; two 2-inch electric drills; two electric sanders; table saws and press; “torsion boxes”; two complete sets of tools and electric portable equipment, etc.

Total value approximately US$ 5,000.
