Refs: A. Director 99095 (sent Lima, Mexico City). B. Lima 4775 (send Santiago). C. Bogota 2114 (send 47944).

1. Station placed articles “La Republica” and “El Siglo” 17 May, which identify Soviet Nikolay Leonov as diplomat who passed one hundred thousand-plus U.S. dollars to Colombian Feliciano Pachon in Mexico City in April 1968.

Pachon arrested entering Colombia with funds. Articles tie 27 June 1968 Leonov to Pachon by revealing that scrap of paper in possession Pachon when captured had written notation of “Avenida Mazatlan.” Leonov office address while press attaché in Mexico revealed as same “Avenida Mazatlan,” No. 206. Fact that Leonov recalled to Moscow immediately after arrest of Pachon also cited as confirming evidence.
