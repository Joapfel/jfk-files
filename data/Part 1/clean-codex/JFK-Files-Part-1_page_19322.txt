Outgoing Classified Message. Central Intelligence Agency.

To: Calcutta. From: Special Operations. Confirmation: TSD (1). Information: ADSO (2-3), COPS (4), GRD (5), FUZ (6-7), CAS (8), SFD (9), CPD (10). Routine. 25 AUG 198. OUT 67539.

Paraphrase not required. Handle as classified correspondence per Para. 51 (1) 60A AR-380-5.

Washington 1350.

To: CALC. CITE: WASHE. Re: ZIZA-03 and ZIZA-04.

1. In connection with reference dispatches, the following is authorized:

A. Rental of suitable quarters at a cost not to exceed $300 per month for occupancy by X and family.

B. Occupancy by X and family of temporary quarters, if necessary in a hotel, for an additional period not to exceed 90 days, with the actual cost thereof being reimbursed by the government.

If either option is exercised, reimburse self from operational funds the difference between the quarters allowance now being paid through State, at the rate of $6.318 per day, and the actual cost of quarters provided in A or B above.

2. If it would be to the advantage of the government, or if necessary for security reasons, we shall request the Department of State to authorize their representative in Calcutta to execute the lease authorized under paragraph 1A above in the name of the Secretary of State. If there is no advantage to the government or if security reasons are nonexistent, authorized to execute lease in own name, in which case disclosure of Headquarters’ interest in matter to any source not authorized. Consider and advise.

Releasing Officer. Originating and Coordinating Officers. Authenticating Officer. TOD.
