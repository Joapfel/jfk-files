16 February 1972

Director, Administration

Reference: MILLS-6919 dated 22 July 1972

1. Jeremy K. Benadum reports receipt of bank deposit notice covering government check in amount $950.00. Only large deposit Benadum anticipates at present time is first retirement annuity check.

2. Reference indicated estimate for Benadum annuity $1,282 per month, which is hard to reconcile with $950 deposit. Fact that deposit for such even amount also indicates it may not be first annuity payment.

3. Benadum requests Headquarters identify deposit if possible, and provide earnings and deductions breakdown if appropriate. Benadum also requests cross-deductions-net breakdown of annuity payments.
