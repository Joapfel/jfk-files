582 HM/DJ/COJB

Classified message.

Origin: L. D. Puckett. Unit: WHD. Extension: 2555. Date: 25 April 1958.

To: Mexico City. From: Director. Confidential: WH 5. Information: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI/CF 2, 3/3 2.

To: MEXI. Information: CITE DIR '19152.

Reference: A. DIR 16715 (OUT 64381). B. MEXI 1446 (IN 20477). C. HMMW-5826.

1. Subject reference C assigned new pseudo Harold R. Noemayr.

2. Appreciate reference B suggestion. However, headquarters arranging Noemayr backstopped cover as representative of a notional public polling services firm in Dallas, which Noemayr used in Cuba, and photographer and collector of travel data for a notional travel service firm in New York which supplies travel information for package tours. Also, he will continue submit articles to Boys Life magazine. Believe above cover will give Noemayr good flexibility and mobility and will eliminate MEXI need to arrange local cover.

3. Noemayr plans ETA MEXI end May as tourist. Will arrange working papers through station-recommended MEXI law firm.

4. Request MEXI concurrence paragraphs 2 and 3 above.

End of message.

CS comment: *Station concurs journalistic cover.

Coordinated in draft with: CI/ICD/WH/MR. Anderson.

Coordinated by phone with: CCB/CNC/MR. Piladeau.

J. C. King, C/WHD, releasing officer.

R. N. Dahlgren, C/WH/III, authenticating officer.
