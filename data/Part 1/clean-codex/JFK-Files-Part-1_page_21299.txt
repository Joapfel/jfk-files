E. J. Sacre

3/4612

F. S. Crichton

From: No date, Havana, Cuba.

To: Dr. Luis Tereschi, Fichycha 959, Buenos Aires, Argentina.

28th July ’0 to 14th August 1942; stated:

The air condition in Spain, as reported by Communist PEO. in vigilants with Paris; period 1942.

This cover contains Reports No. 350-3, 500-3, and 571, compiled from interviews with persons who have recently left Spain, and describes the attitude of the Spanish people to the war and to their government, the extent of German penetration in Spain and of Spanish aid to the Axis, the activities of German political parties or the Left in Spain, and general living conditions in the country. This group reports mainly on living conditions of both Communist and Republican sympathizers.

Present records:

TRI.10.901/42, TRI.11.053/42, TRI.24.07, and TRI.14.201/42

cover groups of reports on the same subject.

S/4655 covers a group of reports on the Spanish political scene, possibly from the same source as the above, which may be Vicente Uribe, the Communist leader, whose headquarters are in Cuba. See also RES.14587/42, SE.14974/42, SER.19252/42.

Addressed to Luis Tereschi, it is shown by TRI.15.26/42 to be a cover address for a U. S. E. (Union Democratica Española) manifesto sent from Cuba. He may also be Tereschi, Estados Unidos 3566, who is given as a cover address in the new message in TRI.14.725/42 (secret).
