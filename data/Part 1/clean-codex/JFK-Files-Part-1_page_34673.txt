G. Support

1. Bases

a. HAVS. Winston staff agents were processed at Headquarters and dispatched PCS to WAVE, bringing the total of PCS personnel to 23 at the close of the period. The renovation of new offices at WAVE is complete.

b. RVMO. Now has responsibility for relaying all traffic to and from WAVE and crypto relay responsibility for all BASS traffic. The Office of Communications has received their approved T/O for RTH and has commenced personnel actions on approximately fifty personnel assigned at this installation.

c. The transfer of JPYG to the Office of Logistics.

1) Mr. Glen Laney, WH/4/Logistics, is now at JPYG for the purpose of adjusting the inventory pending arrangements to be made to transfer all WH material held by JPYG to Supply Division, Office of Logistics.

2) The Planning Staff, Office of Logistics, has acted as coordinator in matters pertaining to personnel, finance, civil and security, the majority of which have been or will be settled shortly.

3) Present plans contemplate the release of JPYG to the Office of Logistics on 1 October 1961.

d. All materiel at JMTIDE has been inventoried and prepared for shipment. Although the intended carrier, the ship JOHAN, arrived at JMTIDE on 13 September, loading was delayed due to the presence of foreign shipping in the area. It is anticipated that loading will commence on or about 18 September and will be completed within four days.
