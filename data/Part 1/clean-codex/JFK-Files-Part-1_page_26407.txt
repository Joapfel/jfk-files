Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Ref. IN 07724.

1. Victor Rico Galan, Mexican Marxist journalist, left Mexico City for Habana, Cuba, by air on 28 January 64. He carried Mexican passport No. 56341 and had a Cuban courtesy visa. Rico's travel documents showed that he was planning to stay at the Hotel Riviera in Habana.

2. Field comment. It is presumed that Rico Galan will interview Fidel Castro Ruz, Prime Minister of Cuba, about his recent visit to the USSR.
