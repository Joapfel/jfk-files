Classified message.

Date: 7 September 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 2 (C/WH TEMPO copy slotted 2315, 7 September). Info: ADDP, C/CI, C/FE 6, S/C 2. Priority. IN 18811.

PRITY DIR CITE MEXI 1888

RYBAT

REF DIR 33992. Out 72932.

1. LITENSOR not available until after 17 September. He travelling outside Mexico City.

2. COS with AMB MANN and materials sent (which now in Spanish) will have meeting with LITENSOR (privately) prior to his departure.

3. Appreciate HOS help.

End of message.

C/S comment: *Mr. Henry Hecksher available during period 10–17 September for briefing of LITENSOR.
