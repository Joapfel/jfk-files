Dispatch. To: Chief, Soviet Air Division. Processing action: Request for information. From: Chief of Station, Russian Airmen. Subject: REDTOP/AKTARGET. Action required: LIHINUS.

1. As part of our continuing effort to effectively “zero in” on selected FISTINAL targets, we requested BIOCENTRIS (BIOCENZIS) to set up telephone tape on phones located in the areas outside of AKRON and 201-025485. BIOCENTRIS complied, and the tap on AIRBORNE’s line became effective on 18 April. Coverage of 201-025485’s line went into effect on 22 April.

2. Although we do not expect this coverage to provide us with dramatic results or some sort of “breakthrough,” we hope that it will contribute significantly to our overall coverage of these two targets.

3. Once these operations have been in existence for a reasonable period of time, we will advise Headquarters of the results.

Distribution: 8 - C/WB; 8 - C/WR. Paul V. LEVESSEL. Dispatch staff and number: PABA-26984. Date: 9 May 1985.
