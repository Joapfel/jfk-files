Classified Message.

Date: 16 December 1958. To: Director. From: Havana. Action: W-5. Info: COP, FI, FI/OPS, FI/RQM 3, PP/PRO, PP/PMD, PD/D, DD/I, AD/C1, S/C 2.

Routing.

Received: 17 January 1958. Routine. IN 38553. To Director, info SACU. Cite: HAVA 6693. Intel. Date: 16 December.

Approved: paragraphs 1, 4 (2); paragraphs 2, 3 (3); paragraphs 5–6.

Source: Paragraph 1, George K. Harper, Las Villas plantation owner (F); paragraphs 2, 3, AMARIA (B); paragraph 4, AMDARK-1 (C); paragraph 5, Harry Graham (F), from DR member (F).

1. Ernesto Guevara has moved westward. His exact location not known but 165 men thought to be under his command are camped near Manacas in west Las Villas.

Guevara's immediate objective believed to be to cut off central highway traffic west of Santa Clara. FC: AMDARK-2 confirms this rebel strategy, says may be planning to try to cut highway at Santo Domingo (Santi Domingo). SC: Heavy trucks cannot travel east of Santa Clara. Backroads can be used by autos and light trucks.

2. 26th of July replacing CTC labor officials with own people on sugar centrales which it captures.

Conrad Dequer Diaz, with HCS in Culina Maestris, in place of the program.
