1328362 JUL 76 STAFF. CITE SAN JOSE 19715. TOI Guatemala City, info Director, Santiago. RVOBAT MUFANG. REF Guatemala 19637 (W 978269).

1. Believe name mentioned para 2 ref is Carole of Victor E. D'Avanço Cintolesi, which is a documented alias of Dr. Orlando Bosch. When Bosch was apprehended in San Jose, Costa Rica, on 10 February 1976, we had in his possession a Chilean passport in that name, No. 26583. It was President Daniel Oduber Quiros who conjured up involvement of Bosch in the arms movement story which was subject of recent traffic. Station has no information to confirm any such involvement by Bosch. Oduber obviously passed his thoughts to Facio re Bosch.

2. When Bosch was apprehended in San Jose, he was found with his daughter, Lourdes Teresita Bosch de Brinder (or Bringer). Born in Cuba on 17 February 1958 and an American citizen, holding U.S. passport No. C-2267692 issued on 6 September 1972. Her husband, Mario Rubin Brinder (Bringer), was born in Buenos Aires on 27.
