Dear Information Review Committee:

Under the provisions of Executive Order 11582, I would like to appeal the CIA's decision to exempt document 3a or any of its portions thereof from being declassified. ML 1-76-311.

As the Agency has done with so many of my declassification requests, should it not be possible to release a sanitised version of the document without jeopardizing national security?

I look forward to receiving your reply and thank you for your consideration of this and previous appeals.
