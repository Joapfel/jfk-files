Activity: U.S. Army Medical Corps, 1942–1943; attorney specializing in trial work; subject active in California state politics and ran unsuccessfully for Secretary of State in 1958. Location: Los Angeles (Lov. A.), Calif. Dates: 1949–57.

Spouse: Mildred McGregor, born in Lexington, Kentucky.

Other members of immediate family and associates: Three minor children, two sons and a daughter. See Green List 077832, April 1959; 116322, July 1961.
