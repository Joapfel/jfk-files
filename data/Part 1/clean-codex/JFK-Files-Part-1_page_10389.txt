Cite Mexico City 7932. Director, Guatemala City. RYBAT PRUMEN LIRING-3. November 69, M 25321.

1. ROWTON reestablished contact with LIRING-3 on 29 October and began detailed debriefing the evening of the same day. ROWTON alleged he arranged release through brother-up business contact who COLONEL GHA. LIRING-3, who accepted story without question, stated he understood ROWTON could not have arranged early release for obvious security reasons and felt almost intolerable suffering worth pain since has come out in best possible position vis-à-vis FAR as well as PERUFEN.

2. LIRING-3 has begun giving complete details of arrest, detention, and contacts established during and after release. Asked ROWTON to keep sensitive points in strictest confidence, particularly from LIRING-4, whom he feels would not approve his continued political activity even on behalf of ROWTON. Highlights of debriefing follow:

A. ROLANDO “CEBOLLITA,” FAR official, who shared cell block 422 with LIRING-3, told the latter that he had sent a report of arrest favorable to LIRING-3 and placing blame on FRANCISCO.
