Outgoing Message.

CIA/DDO. Message reference number: 937064.

To: Federal Bureau of Investigation, Intelligence Division. CIOL-0367-76.

Subject: Report received by Panamanian National Guard on activities of Cuban exiles possibly involved in terrorism.

1. The following information was received from an official of a Panamanian service who is a usually reliable reporter. He provided the information without the knowledge of his service or government.

The information is very sensitive, and no action based upon it should be undertaken without the approval of this Agency.

2. On 22 October 1976, the G-2 Section of the Panamanian National Guard (GN) received a handwritten document which apparently was obtained from a new GN G-2 source in Miami. Although this source is not identified by name in the document, the writer appears to be the proprietor of “Pasco’s Sea Food,” 1030 West Twenty-Third Street, Hialeah, with telephones 885-7344 (office), 888-6267 (home).
