Incoming Classified Message.

Central Intelligence Group.

Information: ARDC (3-4), COPS (5), CRD (6), CAS (7), CFD (8).

TSIN (47.

To: CASHF. Cite: TSIN.

For your information, Principal Officer, this station sending cables to Department and Embassy chiefly describing circumstances under which James Moore and Brian Mosby will be assigned here and requesting early consideration of their transfer, and advising as to whether orders presently being processed. In view of the fact that two new officers are assigned this post and a new Consul General is arriving soon, there has been a lack of office space, and Moore and Brian Mosby have been brought into Principal's small office. Believe that, for reasons of good morale, some definite orders of permanent assignment with positive date of departure be given to the new officers at the soonest.

TOR: 23302 13 Jan. .20.
