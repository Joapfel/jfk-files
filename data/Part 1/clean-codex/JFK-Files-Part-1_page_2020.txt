Hausmann, Cynthia Joy (HAUSMAN, Cynthia), SF#61 423; currently assigned to CI Staff/R&A; assigned to Mexico City, 1965–67. Exposed in CIA Von A–Z, October 1974 Bridgeport, Connecticut, Post, and listed in Agee book.

Steele, Robert Hampton, SF#230 069; former SB case officer who resigned July 1968 to run for U.S. Congress from Connecticut (successful); was assigned to Mexico City, 1966–68. Listed in Agee book and October 1974 Bridgeport, Connecticut, Post; numerous publicity since election to Congress.

Ramirez, Benjamin Adrian (RAMIDES, Dan), SF#188 141; former WH case officer who resigned February 1969; was assigned to Mexico City, 1966–69. Exposed in CIA Von A–Z and listed in Agee book.

Ramirez, Ezekiel Stanley (RAMIDES, Zeek), SF#100 937; former OTR instructor who retired June 1972. Listed numerous times in Agee book.

Hughes, Frank Wallace, SF#57 626; former CI Staff branch chief who retired in June 1973. Exposed in Quicksilver Times, August–September 1969 article.
