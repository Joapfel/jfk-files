TYPIC.

Reference: DIR 58232 (not sent CARA).

1. AMBIDDY-1 confirms ALEGRETTE approach and offer to buy remainder of AWORLD arms. A-1 rejected offer and promised would not sell or give arms to anyone. Weapons, which total 100 pieces, are currently hidden in MANA and Bluefields. A-1 claims only three persons are aware of the location. AMJAVA-4, queried separately, confirms latter point.

2. A-1 leaves for MANA 22 November in order to turn over bases to Anastasio Somoza. In so doing, will turn over a fairly sizeable amount of ammunition consisting of .30- and .50-caliber bullets, grenades, and some mortar shells. Also five carbines. Believes turning over ammunition necessary since cannot use it himself and, mostly unguarded, cases could easily constitute a hazard to local natives near bases.

3. A-1 reiterated that ALEGRETTE’s role in Venezuela, Costa Rica, and Guatemala plots is that of middleman solely interested in gain. As such, in contact with all parties. Further, since ALEGRETTE is aware A-1 is in possession of arms, logically focuses attention on him as possible source for lucrative business deal. A-1 also opines ALEGRETTE being SUQUARED.
