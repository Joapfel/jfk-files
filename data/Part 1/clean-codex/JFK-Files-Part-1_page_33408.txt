Minister of Agriculture: Col Sergio Crespo (ret. Air Force), agricultural and civil engineer.

Minister of Labor: Gen Mario Mackay (Carabineros), anti-U.P. officer named in July as commander in Concepcion.

Minister of Public Health: Col Alberto Spoerer (Air Force), former civilian surgeon described as hard worker.

Minister of Mining: Gen Arturo Yovane (Carabineros), who was scheduled to be retired by Allende in August, close to military.

Minister of Housing: Brig Gen Arturo Vivero. Hard-working staff officer whose performance under pressure somewhat weak.

Secretary General of Government: Col Pedro Eving (Army), described as intelligent, patriotic, conservative.
