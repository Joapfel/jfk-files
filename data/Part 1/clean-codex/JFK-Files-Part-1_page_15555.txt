Date: 7 April 1961. To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3 (tempo copy slotted for AF at 0835, 7 April 1961). Info: CGP, C/CA, C/CI, C/FI 2, S/C-2.

Classified message. Outgoing routing. 7 April 1301Z 61. Priority. IN 32207.

Priority, direct cite LEOP 1141, Bettelheim acting.

RYBAT WIRCGUE.

Reference: LEOP 1140 (IN 31740).

Swanson discussed contract with UNOC acting chief Air Colonel Pierotti. Pierotti stated UNOC policy was to give full support to Pierotti (Pierrot) and CAF, and as long as no Belgians or PBPRIME (PBPRIMER) were involved as advisors, UNOC was in full accord. Swanson was meeting the ambassador to clear the air completely.

End of message.

CS comment: Stated station informed ambassador that Swanson, representative of European subsidiary of PBPRIME parent company, was now in Leopoldville negotiating contract with CAF.
