Mexico City 1654. Director, LIFEAT.

Ref A: HMMA-32038, 19 May 1967.  
Ref B: HMMT-3953, 19 June 1963.

5 June 1967. N 82373.

1. Ref A provides background on decrease in salary or termination proposed to LIFEUD-1.

2. On 1 June 1967, LIFEUD-1 opted for final termination. During discussions of this matter with WILSTED, LIFEUD-1 was friendly and said he realized he was receiving too large a salary for the work he was performing. He said he decided to accept termination because (a) he wanted to immigrate to WOLADY, (b) he did not desire to use his home as a basehouse, and (c) he was afraid other persons involved in LIFEAT would look down on him for accepting a reduction in salary. LIFEUD-1 has not asked for, nor will the station offer, assistance in possible immigration.

3. LIFEUD-1 volunteered that he had kept a complete log of all his activities on behalf of LIFEAT and would turn this log over to WILSTED. He also said that he would be glad to assist in any way in the future if needed, whether on the payroll or not.

By 5 July.

Date: See LIFEUD-1 file. Re: final termination—no problems.
