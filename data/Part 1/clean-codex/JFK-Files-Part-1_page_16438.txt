29 December 1974.

T-291714Z DEC 70, CITE MADRID 2743. London, WH/Miami; info The Hague, Paris, Director. TYPIC QUTIMID.

Ref: WH/Miami 09383 (L-234350).

1. Re paragraph 1 reference, on 21 December Roberto Rodriguez Llompart (LLONPART) called Dionisio Arranz Tremols, Cuban commercial counselor in London, and told him he was leaving that date for London. Llompart, who was travelling with Eugenio Gonzalez Alvarez (ALVEREZ), was to be met by Arranz at the airport.

2. Shortly afterwards, Blanquita (probably Blanca Calvet, administrator, Cuban Commercial Office, London) called Rodriguez Llompart and said she was looking forward to his visit. Rodriguez asked Blanca not to give up the apartment in London, which they were scheduled to give up 1 January, so that he might use it.

3. Both Arranz and Blanquita asked Rodriguez about his brother's eyesight, to which Rodriguez replied he was getting along quite well.
