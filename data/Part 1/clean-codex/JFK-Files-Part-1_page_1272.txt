23 Nov, 1962

Operational Plan—Cuba

I. Situation:

A. The purpose of this plan is to outline the action to be undertaken by the Central Intelligence Agency against the Castro/Communist government of Cuba during TT-63 and for such longer period as may be directed.

B. For the purpose of this plan the following assumptions are made:

1. National objectives with respect to Cuba are essentially as follows:

a. Isolate Castro/Communist from other Western Hemisphere nations.

b. Discredit the Castro/Communist regime in Cuba and in the Hemisphere.

c. Maintain moderate economic and political pressure on the Castro/Communist regime to retard development of the Cuba economy and maintain a drain on Block resources.

d. Maintain maximum coverage of Cuban intelligence requirements.

e. Be prepared to capitalise on any significant uprising, split in Cuba if SWP/CSIP, or split.
