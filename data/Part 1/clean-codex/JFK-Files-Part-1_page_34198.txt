1. On 15 March, WAVE asset AMSCROLL (IDEN) received telephone call from person identifying himself as John Wies, an official of Delhi Export and Service, Ltd., Delhi, Ontario. Said that Delhi had traded for two and one-half years with Cuba until December 1964, when trading was suspended as unable to collect amounts due. Wies said he then went to Cuba in an attempt to collect. Instead, jailed until February 1965. Among charges was that he was a KUBARK agent. Upon return to Canada, he unsuccessfully sought to interest the Canadian government in his case. Thereafter retained a lawyer and is now preparing suit. Said his lawyer also represents other Canadian firms with outstanding claims against Cuba and his suit is being prepared in conjunction with others. Object is to impound Cuban assets to obtain satisfaction of claims.

2. Wies said he had been considering approaching KUBARK or another ODYOKE agency office in Canada to file this case but did not know where to turn. He decided instead to call AMSCROLL, as his firm is a recipient of its economic bulletin.
