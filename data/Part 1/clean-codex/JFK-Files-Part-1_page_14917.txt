# General Debriefing and Assessment of Subject

## 1. Introduction

The Subject was contacted in Cap by Priganza. General familiarisation with him was undertaken en route to Paris by car on the night of 15/16 October 1958. Subsequently he was seen in Paris on 16 October in the afternoon and on 17 October. On the last day the Wechsler Bellsus was given; also, further meeting and communications arrangements were made.

Because of the time limitation—the Subject was unable to stay in Paris and had some business of his own to transact—the debriefing and assessment had necessarily to be held in general terms, the main consideration given to the Subject's overall life-run and the main stress put on the assessment for REDSOX suitability.

There were no personal or security problems. From the beginning the Subject was willing, most eager, and cooperative. He is also willing to cooperate in the future and would not hesitate to take a long trip in order to meet us again.

## 2. General Biographies

### a. Pre-WWII

The Subject is a Georgian national. Both his parents were Georgians. He was brought to France by his parents, who fled from the Soviet Union. Until WWII they lived in Paris or the surrounding areas. For one reason or another they moved frequently. This might have been due to the profession of his father, who was a graduate mining engineer and probably held an administrative or engineering job in France.

Due to the above, the Subject changed schools accordingly. He said that he never had trouble in his studies because of a good memory and fast retention. Consequently, he was usually bored because of frequent repetitions. Owing to boredom and possibly to some sense of opposition, he usually became a nuisance to the teachers. The Subject told us that once he had to be transferred to another school because he got into a violent argument with a teacher. His mother was quite concerned about his behavior and took him to a psychiatrist. From the Subject's narrative, it is not clear what the diagnosis was.

In his free time he liked to perform odd jobs, such as fixing bicycles, helping with electrical repairs, or doing installation work. He also loved to invent gadgets and odd machines.

Undoubtedly the Subject had an inclination toward technical matters. On the other hand, he had a tremendous drive to be financially independent, to make money, and to get ahead in life. It also seems that he did not like to be employed by another person and always strived for self-employment.

After graduating from school he attended several engineering colleges. At Breguet he studied electrical engineering. Subsequently he enrolled in the technicum for cinematography. It is a fact that the Subject had studied a lot; on the other hand, it appears that he did not graduate from any of these technical institutions.
