July 5, 1969

Contact Report

Subject: LEOACOIN-1 (T-4805)

Date of meet: July 2 at Paris-Mexico Restaurant at 1640 hours.

1. Pres arranged meet to which Subject came on time. Seeing me, he drove around several blocks and returned to the prearranged place six minutes later. I paid L-1 $1,000 salary for June 1969. Next meet: July 9, with the pickup at the same place at 1615 hours.

2. L-1 told me about the evening he spent with LIOVAL-1 at the Sov show. He reported the matter exactly as LIOVAL-1 did and added that, in spite of his intention to meet with some members of the Soviet group, he did not find time to do it.

3. With regard to the visit he promised to make to LOGHON, L-1 stated that he was not able to make up his mind about doing it because he feared that LOG might suspect him of showing so much interest in him, when previously he was avoiding him. I repeated my request that he go and see LOGHON before we meet next week. He said he would do it on Friday, July 4, at 1015 hours.

4. I told L-1 that I wanted him to do more writing for us in accordance with our previous agreement. He promised to do it, and I believe that while he is with me he seriously means what he says.

5. As the attachment will show, L-1 spoke about his talk with Karel VENTIL and he was instructed to obtain more information, especially factual information.

6. I gave Subject a copy of The First Circle in Russian, and he was pleased to get the book.

7. L-1 is still living harmoniously with his wife, and L-1 is convincing himself that he wants to live with his wife and does not want to give her reason for quarreling.
