17 May 1957

Memorandum for: Chief/MI/Support

Attention: JH/B & F

Subject: Contract Agents Covert Training

Raymond H. Garthwa (PY)

1. Subject, Project LIPSTICK contract agent, will arrive in Washington on approximately 3–9 June 1957 for five weeks of covert training. Covert training has been arranged and a safe house obtained.

2. The matter of entertainment of Subject was discussed with Mr. Wattles, C/MI/Support, who said that entertainers of contract agents is authorised under certain conditions and that it would be permissible to entertain Subject conservatively while in Washington. His plans to Mr. Price of the General Counsel's Office who supported this view.

3. To favorably impress Subject and to maintain his morale while in Washington, it is contemplated that the desk operations office will entertain him with dinner and drinks approximately twice a week during the first and final weeks and once a week during the intervening weeks.

J. Galbraith  
Chief, #H/III

Distribution: Original and 1 to addressee; 1 to WH/III/Chrono; 1 to Subject—LIPSTICK; WH/III/Mexico/Las Vegas.
