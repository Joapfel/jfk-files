Dispatch. To: Chief, VII Division. Processing action: Mailed for delivery. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Submitter: Victor RICO Galan (201-336474) — Contacts with Communist Officials Stated in Mexico. Processing action: No indexing required; only qualified case can be read England; microfilm.

1. The attached is a listing from Station records of 337 known contacts of Victor RICO Galan with officials of the Cuban (258), Yugoslav (33), Soviet (31), Chinese Communist (7), Polish (6), and Czechoslovakian (4) installations in Mexico City.

2. This list (without sources as listed in the right of each page) was sent to LIKAMA in reply to his personal request. LIENVOY will translate this into Spanish for LIKAMA.

3. A copy of the cover (blind) memorandum which will be put into Spanish (by LIENVOY) is also attached.

Willard C. Curtis.

Attachment: As stated above.

Distribution: Orig & 2 — C/WHD w/atts (1 cy ea.).

Cross-reference to. Despatch symbol and number: HMA-30355. Date: 21 October 1966. MQS file number: P-7354. 201-336474.
