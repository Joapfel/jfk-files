Re: PARI 4214 (IN 58)

1. Altho subject sounded good in principle, we unable approve ref proposal at this time for following reasons:

A. Prior completion prelim four-way check provisional clearance unobtainable.

B. Even if cleared and definite target in mind, $300 considered excessive for untried, untrained (altho well placed) individual compared other starters.

2. As resignation appears fait accompli, we do not consider it advisable attempt persuade subject retain present post, thereby incurring heavy moral obligation support him for indefinite period in future.

3. New cover should still give subject latitude our work if he remains in Europe. Will submit results check and request you formulate new proposal on basis new cover, when we will reexamine and advise.

R. Boulton
