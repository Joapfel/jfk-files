Priority Director

CIFLUTE AMCLEVE

1. AMCLEVE-15 holds a current, important, and sensitive position in the government, of great operational and intelligence value to Caracas Station. Anticipate value of A-15 will increase with arrival of Soviets, as he is in charge of operations against bloc countries’ diplomatic missions. Both A-15 and Station are concerned, however, with the fact that he is not a Venezuelan citizen and the possibility somebody in the government will ask why a foreigner (Cuban) is in such a high and sensitive position within the government. If this happens, there is a good chance A-15 would be removed from his present job. A-15 says CIVISTA-5 periodically asks in a joking manner when he is going to become a citizen of Venezuela. A-15 replies he has all documents required for applying for Venezuelan citizenship ready and will become a citizen in due time. Station feels perhaps the good job A-15 is doing for C-5 is the reason C-5 does not pressure A-15 into becoming a citizen.
