JACETONE-1: David C. HUGBY and Floyd N. ALMONET.

KIJACETONE-1 was used until the conclusion of the meeting to raise his current favorite subject, that of converting the leftist propaganda contained in SIEFEN. Fortunately, HUGBY was forewarned and effectively turned around the thrust of the effort upon KIJACETONE-1's shoulders, without offending him. IDENTITY impressed HUGBY with his interest, intelligence and sincerity. He offered the services of MIAMI to “Mothers” on our behalf but was convinced by HUGBY that the best way to approach the problem was through our organization.
