AKULE TYPIC.

References: A. Director 308883. B. WH/MIAMI 15698 (in 701613). C. Madrid 17199 (in 702392). D. WH/MIAMI 15653 (in 707422).

1. During 12 September contact, AMCHALK-5 mentioned to PACHANKIS a possible target date for the Madrid trip, either late September or early October. AMCHALK-5 replied that either date was agreeable. Even if the target date conflicted with his referenced European vacation, he was willing to delay that in order to assist us. He would appreciate, however, advance information so that he could make appropriate plans.

2. AMCHALK-5 advised PACHANKIS that he had requested and expected to receive Spanish visas for himself and his wife by the end of the week of 15 September. He planned to take her along and would pay all her expenses, as he believed her presence on this ostensible vacation trip would be accepted by AMWOLF-1 as logical.

AMH, 15 September 72. AS. Europe vacation trip.
