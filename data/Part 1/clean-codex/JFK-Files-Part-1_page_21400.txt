Attachment to EAVA-30210.

Subject: PCHORILYI ten Spiridow, LIECHART Vera, Dr. Krijenko (Krivenko) Gregor. Request.

Dr. Gregor Krivenko (Krijenko) (physician, born 3 December 1900 in Kyiv, USSR; Austrian citizen by naturalization since 1955; urologist, Upper Austria, resident) was formerly a physician in Ukraine and came to Austria with his wife, Carla, née Kurotschina, and their two children as a refugee in June 1943. From 1943 to 1945 he worked as a physician at the Bied infirmary, later at the General Hospital in Linz, and then until 1952 as a refugee physician in the refugee camp at Bied. Since 1952 he has had no regular employment. As a welfare recipient, he still practices medicine occasionally, tacitly tolerated. Otherwise, he is active as an organist and choir director in the Greek Orthodox Church in Salzburg. His wife, who was allegedly a singer in Ukraine, is likewise musically active. The son, Dr. Krijenko (Krivenko), is studying languages; the daughter is sickly and has no employment. A brother, Dr. Krijenko (Krivenko), is allegedly still employed as a physician in the USSR.

Dr. Krivenko (Krijenko) lives with his family in very modest circumstances, is deeply religious, and is known as an anticommunist. The entire family enjoys a good reputation.

Veronika (Vera) Burchart, née Chernilewski (born 17 February 1901 in Bar, Ukraine; Austrian citizen by naturalization since 1957; Untereitsing 32, Ried im Innkreis, Upper Austria, resident), came to Austria with her husband, Wilhelm Burchart (born 26 April 1887 in Jedenier, Ukraine).

3 July 1967.
