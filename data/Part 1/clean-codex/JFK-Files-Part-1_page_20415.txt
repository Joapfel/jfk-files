Section 23. General Qualifications.

A. Foreign languages: Spanish. French — slight. German.

B. Sports and hobbies: Swimming — excellent; music (piano) — fair; tennis — fair; trap shooting — good.

C. Qualifications from training or experience: Wartime OSS service as CBI Reports Officer. Graduate Air Combat Intelligence School. Lecturer on Psychological Warfare at Army-Navy (Army-Havy) Staff College.

D. Government departments, agencies, or offices applied to for employment since 1939: Economic Cooperation Administration.

E. Investigations: Office of Strategic Services — December 1944. ECA — June 1943.
