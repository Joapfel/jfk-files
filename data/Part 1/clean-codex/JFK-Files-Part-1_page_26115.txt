152203Z. CITE CARACAS 7805.

Director info JMWAVE.

References: A. JMEAVE (JMWAVE) 9205 (IN 4/6/94). B. Director 34096.

1. CIGIRO I reported evening 12 September that he had nominated Erasto Fernandez Betancourt to be Director, DIGEPOL. CIGIRO I said the President was reluctant to approve the appointment due to Fernandez's bad reputation. CIGIRO I is continuing to press for approval of the appointment on the basis that he will watch Fernandez.

2. Under the circumstances, Station feels (AMCLEVE IS) should phone Fernandez to try to firm up the DIGEPOL job, but delay actual departure till his position is clarified. This is being suggested on the assumption AMCLEVE IS may not immediately locate a job in the U.S. and would be willing to continue trying to keep the Venezuelan option open on the chance it might work out.

3. Request headquarters concurrence.

14 September 1967, 151040.
