Cable. Classified message. Action: WHG. File: VR DOP. ADDL. C/EURS. C/CL. C/FZ. 171407Z October 1972, staff. Cite Madrid 17443. Director, WH/Miami. RYBAT TYPIC. Reference Madrid 17403 (in CHALKTOE).

1. Through QUEVE surveillance it was determined on 16 October that AMWOLF/I left the consulate alone and went to his apartment. On the basis of this alert, positioned AMCHALK/5 near garage where AMWOLF/I parks his car.

2. At 1656 hours 16 October, when AMWOLF/I left the garage, AMCHALK/5 was able to stop him half a block from his apartment and carried on a six-minute conversation. Initially AMWOLF/I was cold, visually upset, but later became jovial. At first insisted that AMCHALK/5 come to the consulate or call him to set up meeting. However, AMCHALK/5 insisted they talk then and AMWOLF/I agreed. After AMCHALK/5 made the pitch, AMWOLF/I reacted by saying, “These ‘JOHNIES’ have everything and now they really stepped on me.” AMWOLF/I ended the conversation by again asking AMCHALK/5 to come to the consulate or call him so they could have another talk. AMWOLF/I returned to the car where his wife was waiting and started the motor and then stopped again and got out. He then had AMCHALK/5 come over and introduced his.
