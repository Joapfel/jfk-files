From: Chief, SR via Chief, NE. Subject: REDWOOD/LCIMPROVE. File title/member: Valeriy Vladimirovich KOSTIKOV (201-305052).

1. We have recently reviewed available information on Valeriy Vladimirovich KOSTIKOV, a consular attaché at the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City. It appears quite possible that KOSTIKOV may be a member of the Thirteenth Department of the KGB's First Chief Directorate, as will be seen below.

2. KOSTIKOV has been positively identified as the Soviet who met AEBURBLE in Mexico City on 9, 11 and 12 February 1963. Mexico City Station identified KOSTIKOV as the only Soviet in Mexico who fit the description, and subsequently AEBURBLE identified him from a photo spread. KOSTIKOV received information from AEBURBLE on industrial and missile sites in PRIME and gave him requirements of the same nature for the future. KOSTIKOV also made arrangements with AEBURBLE for his next meeting, to be held in New York City with a different, unidentified Soviet. On 6 May 1963 AEBURBLE was met in accordance with the above arrangements by Oleg Danilovich BRYKIN (201-292588), a translator assigned to the United Nations Secretariat in New York City. BRYKIN has been identified by a sensitive, reliable source as a member of the KGB's Thirteenth Department. AEBURBLE passed BRYKIN aeronautical charts and was given additional requirements particularly relative to missile bases. Nothing transpired during the latter meeting to suggest that BRYKIN wished to change the nature of the case, and in fact he told AEBURBLE that he was doing a good job. It therefore can reasonably be assumed that BRYKIN and KOSTIKOV both were representing the same KGB department.

3. COS, Mexico City.
