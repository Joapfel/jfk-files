Reference: A. FRAN 1854 (IN 33838). B. DIR 15840 (OUT 91968).

1. Per reference B, KURIOT strongly urges backstopped FREMDENPASS as the best long-range vehicle to resettle and legalize WIROGUE (W). With FREMDENPASS, W could eventually become a citizen of a selected country. Headquarters feels W should obtain a CHWD residence visa using FREMDENPASS or other documentation. Suggest Colombia or Brazil as the best areas, but the question hinges on where W can get himself accepted. KUBARK (KURARK) ability to aid legalization is limited, and it is best to have legalization begin now, backstopped to withstand later checks from the country of choice.

2. Citizenship requirements for the countries mentioned in reference A, plus Brazil, require four to five years of continuous residency. To enter and remain, a residence visa is required, which must be renewed periodically. All countries request that applications be made at the consulate, with an explanation of financial means, the manner intended to make a living, and a policy certificate from the country of origin.

3. Headquarters suggests countries of interest to W might be more receptive to agricultural ambitions rather than business or industrial pursuits. W once mentioned he was interested in opening a chicken farm in the Congo. Since W must show a plan for earning a living, suggest a similar line with an American country approached.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
