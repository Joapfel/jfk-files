A: Mr. Seigle, first, sorry for my bad English.

In the Athenian newspapers I read your letter you sent to the Greek prime minister and Papandreou (Pappandreou). I agree that a new premier must be set out of this com- Greek chargé d'affaires, the Cuban chargé d'affaires in Athens. Many readers of the Athenian newspapers asked to take your address. They want to express their feelings. Many of them wrote that they want to go to Cuba for “to set out in 3(!) days Fidel Castro.”

Anyway, this is the preface of my letter. What I want to tell you is that I am a reporter of a great and old Athenian newspaper. You should like very much to be in such a couple of months to the World's Fair of New York—as I went again last year—and then, it will come. In addition, so, I want very much to meet you and leave an interesting interview with you. And for my newspaper inlishary, I want some.

16 June 1965
