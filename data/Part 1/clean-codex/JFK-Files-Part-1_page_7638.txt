Department of State

Consul, Veracruz (VEMACRUI)

February 28, 1967

Ocean Shipping Co. — communist ships

C-830-50783; Veracruz A-33, 2/13/67, same subject

1. East Germany ships at Veracruz.

The HEINRICH WELTZ (II photos enclosed) arrived as expected on February 19 (see referenced airgram, paragraph 1), direct from Le Havre (La Havre), and left for Jamaica February 21 in ballast. The ship had planned to take a Conviction passenger, Alexander John BLANCHARD (passport and BE-602204, issued Toronto, 12/19/64; age 35, professor of physics, Lecroy, 225 Glen Polar Drive, Toronto 13), but he disappeared at the last minute. Blanchard planned to return to Mexico City from Cuba via the CMI flight, and then come back down to Veracruz to get his wife, Harry Ann (Mary Ann), who was to remain here. The Mexican tourist cards of Mr. and Mrs. Blanchard expired February 12, 1967. The local agent for the East German line stated that recently the honorary Consul of East Germany, a rival shipping agent, had been going aboard each East German ship to offer all possible assistance. On looking into this, the agent said he discovered that West Germany’s representatives throughout the world, wherever East Germany is not represented, had been ordered to act discreetly in representation of East German interests as well as West German interests.
