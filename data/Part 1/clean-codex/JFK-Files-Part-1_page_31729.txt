Security Information.

27 January 1953

Memorandum for: CIO, International Organizations Division

Subject: Exploitation by IFE of Physicians (Plot)

1. Attached herewith the January 17 Washington Post editorial on “Communist Anticentrism,” which you requested.

2. This editorial has not been previously sent to New York. However, I should like to call your attention to the fact that the material covered in this editorial has already been exploited by IFE, as evidenced in their guidance of 13, 14, and 15 January.

Ellen Stern  
IO/TONIC
