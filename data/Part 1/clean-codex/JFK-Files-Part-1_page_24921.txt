170105Z. SDOM MEXI 6568.

ARGOSY LICOLIC.

1. Concur reference; Tichborn enthusiastic about assignment and planning travel SDOM early April, subject headquarters final approval. Suggests he meet with SDOM case officer at San Juan en route DR in order to avoid arriving SDOM cold.

2. Problems will come up in regard on-time publication LIREAM magazine in absence of both Xirau and Tichborn, but believe they manageable. May be necessary for Tichborn to return MEXI for a few days middle of May, but will avoid this if possible.

3. Cover that of writer gathering material for backgrounder. Tichborn writing Harpers to see if they interested commissioning such piece.

CFN 6568 Tichborn headquarters San Juan en route DR Xirau Tichborn May.
