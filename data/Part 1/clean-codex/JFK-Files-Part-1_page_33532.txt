D. The burden on consumers has been heavy. Despite a healthy increase in the minimum wage, working class consumers are finding it increasingly difficult to afford the barest necessities. Austerity has been exacerbated in many cases by dismissals of relatively unproductive and politically suspect employees hired under the Allende regime. An unemployment compensation program has been announced and an extensive public works program is under consideration.

E. The junta is attempting to enlist public support for the austerity program and business is showing renewed confidence. Consumers and labor, however, are taking a wait-and-see attitude. No organized resistance to the junta or its programs has materialized, even though some political factors are grumbling and predicting.
