221517Z April 1969, CITE BOGOTA 2047.

Priority Director, Lima.

REDTOP.

1. GWREN reported Jose Arizala (Arizalan) (P0680M(CRP-85568)), chief of the Novosti office in Bogota and member of the Communist Party of Colombia, requested information from Prospero Morales, CIT Colombia presidential press secretary, concerning the legality of a Soviet citizen, Nikolay Sereyevich Leonov (Nikolay Sergeyevich Leonov), entering Colombia on a tourist card. Arizala possessed the possible arrival information (P0550): Leonov would arrive in Bogota on 22 April on Avianca Flight 71 from Paris and would travel to Lima on 23 April. Arizala added Leonov was an important member of the Soviet Embassy in Mexico but had now given up his diplomatic career and was a member of the APN “team.” Morales later told Arizala that he had learned from DAS that Leonov could enter Colombia on a tourist card.

2. Believe subject is Nikolay Sereyevich Leonov (Nikolay Sergeyevich Leonov) (201-132139), who once stationed in Mexico City as Soviet Embassy press attaché and known RIS. Suggest headquarters traces to Lima soonest.
