20 April 1977

On 20 April 1977, the FBI telephonically granted approvals for Boris Brodetskyy (BRODETSKYY), Boris Palant (PALANT), and Sergey Uzlov (UZLOV).

Regarding Vitaliy Golden (GOLDEN), the FBI has no current address for him. The undersigned advised the FBI on this date that G. was destined for JFS, Worcester, Massachusetts. The FBI stated they would now query their appropriate office and advise us once an address was obtained. Earlier, they had queried another office.

L.G. Karpoff
