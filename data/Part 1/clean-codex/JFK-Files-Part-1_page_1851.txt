Chapter XIII (commencing at page 215. This chapter is apparently misnumbered.)

I. This chapter describes some of the battle problems at the beachhead. Three jets from the Boxer were allowed a reconnaissance run over the beach (p. 227). Finally the President authorized an hour's air cover from the Boxer, but the time was fouled up and the Brigade's B-26's were shot down before the air cover arrived (p. 230).
