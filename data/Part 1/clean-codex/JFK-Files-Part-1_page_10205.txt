Classified message.

Date: 522. From: 3 April 1962. To: Director. April 2, 21512, 1962. Info: Mexico City. RULEN. DDP, COP, CA 2; CA/P&G, CA/PROP, CI, CI/OPS. IN 12825.

DIR INFO WAVE CITE NEXI 0444 MEM-0496. CYROSE IN-12879.

On 29 March 1962, LITAMIL-3 reported that Luis Alberto Souto (Alberu), Cuban cultural attaché, Mexico City, would not return to Cuba, where he had been assigned to the United Nations section, Foreign Office. Souto, who arrived in Mexico City (MEXI) from Havana (HAVA) on 16 March, told LITAMIL-3 (IDEN) that conditions in Cuba were terrible and he could remain in Mexico City. Souto told L-8 to tell L-3 he would visit him in the near future. Station hopes to exploit Souto's defection through L-3.

End of message.
