Director Info Guatemala City. PBRUMEN KAPOK.

Ref A: Mexico City 2358 (IN 24956).  
Ref B: Mexico City 2363 (IN 25077).  
Ref C: Mexico City 2396 (IN 26963).

1. Having heard nothing from Cubans regarding Moises (Orozco) Leal, LIRING-3 obtained the phone number of Ernesto (Capuano) del Vecchio from Cesar (Tejeda) Fonseca, husband of the daughter of Pascual (Ortiz) Rubio, ex-Mexican president. Without identifying himself, LIRING-3 made an appointment to talk to Capuano. This was confirmed by LIFEAT.

2. When Capuano did not show at the meeting, LIRING-3 went to his house on 7 August. During the conversation, Capuano:

A. Admitted he was in contact, delicate and indirect, with Guat guerrillas.

B. Told LIRING-3 that Orozco was arrested and released on 4 August, but doubted he had said anything regarding LIRING-3.

C. Showed great interest in LIRING-3’s story regarding Orozco’s attempt to use LIRING-3 to double-cross FAR and asked for a copy of the letter left by Orozco at LIRING-3’s apartment.

D. Requested that LIRING-3 do nothing in connection with Guat.

10 August 1967.
