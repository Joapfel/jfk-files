Action: FYI.

1. Attached for addressees' information are translations of the Creole language portions of the film tapes forwarded in references. The translations were done by a native Creole. Conversations which have no operational or intelligence value have not been included.

2. In 29 GR 82 J.2 the journal Compatant cannot be identified by the station. Can headquarters identify the journal?

3. The Judy in 29 FR 9d 17.5 is probably Judith Ann, the Costa Rican communist friend of the Planeta-Cartagena.

4. 2y 1a 103 was a very interesting conversation. Evidently Pierre Larouche has just returned from Haiti and San Juan, Puerto Rico, where he went to meet his other. Ron Ton Cliburn is Fritz Clavijo, who is believed to have formed a signature while literally ordering a newsmotion postcard stamp issue. Charles planned to keep the stamps and sell them at a profit to stamp dealers. According to a Paf (Martin CH) clandestine journal, anoadend Hain... was a Haitian delegate to the Pari-continental conference in Havana in January 1960. However, this name was probably a pseudonym. Per consulate files, added Placard, 100, 3 December 1915, Cayey, Haiti, traveled to and from the ... in June 1960. The Cuban approach to landing in San Juan is the first direct confirmation of Cuba's active activity directed at Haiti in Puerto Rico. Gerardo L.
