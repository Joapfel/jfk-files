Date: 12 November 1976. Origin: Mary Ginn. Unit: LA/SB/CH/R. SAT: 1472.

C/LA/MCA.

With Morales. Information in reports alleges that Otero was told that if Venezuelan authorities moved to arrest him, he could receive asylum with the new Chilean ambassador. While in Caracas, Otero allegedly told Morales that he had committed the bombings in Miami, and Morales passed this information to FBI.

2. Much of the information on the plot to kill Pascal and Br ussier apparently came from a 25 February 1976 FBI report, which could have come from Morales. Morales was the source of numerous Secret Service reports during Kissinger’s visit to Caracas in February 1976 concerning a reported attempt by Bosch to kill Kissinger. One Secret Service report indicated that Morales had provided the Caracas legal attaché with reliable information in the past.
