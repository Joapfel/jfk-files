In Panama between 20–22 October, Hugo Garcia contacted Hugo Victor, president of the Partido del Público (PDP—People’s Party—Communist), and David Torrer, a leader of the Vanguard of National Action (VAN—pro-Cuban, radical revolutionary organization). On 24 October a meeting was held to discuss unity of the left, commitment to aggressive action, and a number of leftist units going to the elections as a bloc should efforts to prevent the elections fail. This meeting was attended by Victor, Carlos del Cio, Adalbert Menierez Franco, the MA King, President Pereira, and two unidentified students. (Headquarters comment: An independent, relatively unofficial office, with no contacts in the PDP, reported that the visit to Panama of “a Cuban writer for a New Freeman” was on a mission for the Cuban Communist Party. The visitor, who was supposed to be a source but did not identify himself by name, chose the PDP and the VAN to redeem their differences and unite. His prophecy was violently rejected by the PDP, which intends to protest and propose to the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City.)
