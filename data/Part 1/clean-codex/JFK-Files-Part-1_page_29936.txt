9 May 1969

To: David B. Murphy, Chief of Station, Paris

Subject: Annual Fitness Report

1. Effective with this fitness report cycle, KEYWAY is initiating a procedure by which a copy of the fitness report on a Chief of Station is forwarded to him upon completion by the Rating Officer. Depending upon the grades of the Chiefs of Station concerned, the fitness reports are drafted either by the Chief or Deputy Chief of KEYWAY, and those drafted by Chief, KEYWAY go to Deputy Chief WO for review.

2. Please sign at the bottom of this letter of transmittal on the line indicated, to certify that you have seen the fitness report. The transmittal letter should then be returned to Headquarters for inclusion in your personnel file.

 /s/ John L. Smith

I certify that I have seen my fitness report for the period 10-March 1968 - 31 March 1969.

/s/ David B. Murphy
