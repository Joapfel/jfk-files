DIR INFO ALGI PAPI PALP CITE LOND 7620.

KUDESK STERBENY.

12 May 1965, IN 65581.

JAGUAR have informationally advised us of new report that “rumors have recently revived saying that STERBONY/1 is connected with a foreign intelligence agency.” Station request for elaboration, indication of area in which rumor circulating, etc., met with comment that JAGUAR unable to say any more at present, and cannot “yet” let us have this comment in writing. Will pursue and advise any developments.
