Classified message.

Originator: Birch D. O'Neal. Unit: CI/SI. Extension: 2356. Date: 30 December 1960.

To: Mexico. From: Director. Confidential: CI/SI. Information: CI, CI/OPS, WH 4, S/C 2.

RE Filing: No CS Record Value. Destroy. RI file this copy as indicated. Branch copy filed as indicated.

RE Indexing: Marked. None required.

Cite DR 1899N.

1. ODENVY advises David Halperin, son of Maurice, has scheduled arrive Harry (Dec.-12/29) will surveillance in Mexico City 29 December by car. Is enroute Europe where will meet father. ODENVY endeavoring develop more on itinerary and has not as yet made specific request for coverage.

2. Desire you coordinate with local ODENVY representative and obtain any coverage possible consistent with ODENVY interest.

End of message.

Chief, WH 3/Mex.

Coordinating officers. Acting Chief, CI Staff. Authenticating officer.
