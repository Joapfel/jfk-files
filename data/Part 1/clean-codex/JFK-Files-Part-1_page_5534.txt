1. On 21 April, another WOFACT component passed following information regarding Clemard Joseph Charles, which was obtained from source who was in almost daily contact with Charles. Source claims he spent week latter part February as guest of Charles in Port au Prince.

2. Source reported 20 April he convinced Charles has decided overthrow Duvalier now with aid from unnamed Army friends. Source stated Charles had requested U.S. business partner and another friend to get in touch with State Department “before end of week” to persuade U.S. government that Charles now has the power and determination to get rid of Duvalier. Source also reported that Charles, who fears for his life (WHC YEARS), sent his son to NYC few weeks ago for medical checkup with instructions remain in U.S.

3. Source above information not available for further questioning prior 30 April. Has passed information to Mr. Osborne of State Department. 21 Apr 67-357029 ACOS, please pass to ambassador.

End of message.

Paul V. Harwood. C/WD/C/WH/7. Copy No. William V. Broz. Q/WHD. Disleasing officer.
