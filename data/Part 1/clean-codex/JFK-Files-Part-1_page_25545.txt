Cable: 261809Z November 1969. Cite: Mexico City 2003.

Director. RYBAT LIEMPTY-1 GERENDE.

References: A. Mexico City 1879 (IN82190). B. HMMA 38119.

1. Raymond H. Gerende put on half pay and suspended from operations duty for month of December. Personal problem mentioned in reference A and spelled out in greater detail in reference B is divorce action instituted by wife, who is claiming mental cruelty and physical abuse. Gerende warned by station he is obligated to handle situation in manner best calculated to avoid exposure of his RVROCK connections. Wife promised station her cooperation in this respect, as has Gerende, but situation worsening daily, mainly due battle over eventual custody of children.

2. As Gerende completely preoccupied by this problem, to detriment of his operations activities and without regard for possible security implications, station decided on suspension and 30-day grace period in which he is to pull himself together and settle problem in a way which would not jeopardize security of operations.

3. Station not sanguine Gerende can change attitude sufficiently to salvage situation. Believe termination prior to end of fiscal year 1970 distinct.
