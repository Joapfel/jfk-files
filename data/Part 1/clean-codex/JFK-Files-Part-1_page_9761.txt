20Jun68N3521C. Guatemala City, Director. KAPOK.

1. LIRING-4, who postponed departure to Cuba in hope of helping LIRING-3, has received several distressing notes from the latter, who, contrary to previous information, is still awaiting sentence in jail.

LIRING-3 complains about very meager food, unsanitary quarters, and constant fear of physical violence.

2. In one note LIRING-3 states the Mexican Embassy in Guatemala City is able to do very little for him and hints he may receive a severe sentence unless some effort is made to influence the judge's decision. The judge to be “influenced” was mentioned as Juan Sanchez, Oficial Tercero del Juzgado Cuarto de Primera Instancia. In open code, LIRING-3 requested LIRING-4 appeal to ROWTON (in alias) in connection with the above, as he expects his trial and sentencing in early July.

3. Mexico City Station is definitely not in favor of intervention with Guatemalan authorities, which could finger LIRING-3 as some type WOFIRM asset. Is there some way, however, Guatemala City Station could discreetly ascertain the probable sentence to be given LIRING-3 and advise?
