5. In conclusion, ECONOMIDES said he most sympathetic with aims AMSCROLL and added that previous letters to TSIRIMOKOS on GALBE had been helpful. Urged that AMSCROLL keep him and TSIRIMOKOS informed further developments para 2 subjects. Will pouch AMWORD-3 report this meeting when prepared.

6. For MADR. While in New York, AMSCROLL delegation called upon Spanish Consul-General Manuel Alabart, who related to AMBLEAK-1, seeking entrée to TSIRIMOKOS. Conversation led to ships being built in Spain for Cuba. Point was made to Alabart this activity could backfire on Spain, particularly in view of recent International Longshoremen’s Association (ILA) resolution to boycott Free World ships that trade with Cuba. Alabart responded this subject of great interest to Spanish Minister of Commerce Lopez del Barrio, especially if trade with Cuba would reflect unfavorably upon Spain’s announced economic assistance program for balance of Latin America. Said Del Barrio had left New York for Madrid two days previously and had discussed with Alabart problem of Cuban trade as it relates to plans for rest.
