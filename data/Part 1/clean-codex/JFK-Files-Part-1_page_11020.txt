Central Intelligence Agency.

Country: Mexico/Cuba. Report No. CS-3/539,464. Subject: Mexicans on the Wailing List for the Cuban Embassy Information Bulletin. Date distributed: 7 March 1963. No. pages: 2. References: RD. Date of information: February 1963. Place and date acquired: Mexico, Mexico City (4 February 1963). Field report No. HMM-6991.

Source: Person (B) with good contacts in the Cuban Embassy in Mexico City. Appraisal of content: 2.
