Office Memorandum, United States Government.

To: Record  
From: S. Richelderfer  
Date: 29 Oct 57  
Subject: Piccolo, Joseph Stephan

Subject was advised this date that the Agency would request a directed deferment. He was told the meaning of a “directed deferment” and advised not to contact his local board and they will not contact him. He was sent to see Captain Noble in Reserve Affairs Branch. Captain Noble was not in and subject was interviewed by Sgt. MacKenzie. I talked to MacKenzie and stressed the fact that subject should be advised to be sure to drop out of the National Guard. MacKenzie said that he would have to request to be put in a standby reserve status. This seems to be a satisfactory arrangement, since we are requesting a directed deferment, the L.B. will not contact Piccolo.
