Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City; INRD Chief of Station, Panama City; Chief, WHD; Chief of Station, Dominican Republic. From: Chief of Base, Merida.

Subject: Travel of Victor RICO Galan, Mexican Marxist journalist and agent of Fidel Castro (CASTROruz).

Action required references. RSP: HOMA-22801.

On 15 January 1964, the subject arrived in Merida from Kingston, Jamaica, by Mexicana flight 304 in transit for Mexico City. Source: airlists.

Andrew P. MERTON.

Distribution: 2-Koxi; 1-Facy; 1-XR; GAD; 2-Files.

Cross reference to: Re-October 17th; 25 February 1964; H.A-312; May 18th; Jun 8-9 T; SAND A S P.
