Subject: LICORIL-1 (P-

Date of meeting: 11 June 1969

Designations.

4. Subject had the following comments and observations to make as we went through the cards:

SOLOMATIN (P-7452): Departing POS on 28 June 1969. He is suffering with an ulcer. He takes sleeping pills constantly and has been complaining about the great amount of work he has. Said his mother in the USSR, who neither smokes nor drinks, is very religious. She keeps a picture of Christ hidden at home, and many of her women friends come to pray in front of it. SOLOMATIN did not come to Subject's house for dinner on 1 June as planned because he could not get permission. SOLOMATIN's wife will have three months off when they return to the USSR in order to rest up from her work here, but SOLOMATIN will have only 22 days off. He is going to take charge of some woodworking shops with lathes and other machinery.

PODI SHOY (P-1369): Able to speak very little Spanish.

FUGITIV (F-9027): Speaks very good Spanish. Very aggressive and not very pleasant.

VILLAR (F-820): Strikes Subject as effeminate.

JUAN (F-1657): Scheduled to leave; replacement not known.

ROCHELIX (F-1176): His wife is a doctor.

GONZAY (F-636): Nice guy; speaks good Spanish; warm, diplomatic, and positive type.

HOLLY (F-562): Very smart guy. Makes clock features.

LUCI (C-7141): His wife is a member of the secret police.

For arrivals: On 19 May, two new officials came into the store. One speaking Russian, one speaking English, and one speaking German.
