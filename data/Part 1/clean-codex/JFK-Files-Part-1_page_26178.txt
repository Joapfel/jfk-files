CS 3/525-084, 17 October 1962, B-2: “Plans to write a political biography of Castro.” Left Mexico on 2 October 1962 with Mexican passport 5115 and Cuban courtesy visa. Spent 2 months there.

See p. 6.

A 1291, 9 April 1963: In the 20 March issue of *Siempre* he “advocated the creation of a new political party which would reflect the ideology of Former President Lázaro Cárdenas...” Biography: he is a founding member of the pro-Communist MLN and an ardent supporter of Castro. While in Cuba (October–November 1962), he contributed weekly articles to *Siempre* in which he sustained the thesis that Cuba is now the center of the world's socialist revolutionary movement. The Left in Mexico needs a “party of L. Cárdenas” which would stand for socialism in internal matters and for independence from any bloc in external affairs.

CS 3/552-990, 10 July 1963: To Cuba 25 May–22 June 1963; spent 8 days with Castro.

A-6, Mérida (Verida), 6 August 1963: On 1 August attended the Pro-Castro Mexican-Cuban Institute in Mérida, accompanied by Luis Alhacu, the Counselor for Cultural Affairs at the Cuban Embassy.

CS 3/558-382, B-2, 6 September 1963: Rico, along with Marcué Pardiñas (Marcue Pardinas) and Carrión Villa (Carrion Villa), was one of the principal organizers and participants in a rally in favor of the political prisoners held in Mexico City on 18 August 1963. The rally failed to be held because of police intervention. All were arrested and released the same day. On the evening of 21 August they tried unsuccessfully to see Díaz Ordaz, then Minister of Interior.

CS 3/560-130, 25 September 1963, B-2: “Rico planning trip to Guatemala” to interview Col. Zariaga Peralta Azurdia, Chief of the Guatemalan Government, and also visit Guatemalan guerrillas.

A-451, 24 October 1963: Still on MLN National Committee.

TDCS 3/565-337, 20 November 1963, B-2: Interviewed Yon Sosa in mid-October 1963; listed as head of propaganda committee for MLN. TDCS 3/563-671 reported that Rico had spent 8 days in Cuba with Castro and had been given an assignment to visit countries in Central and South America (Venezuela, Costa Rica, Panama, Guatemala, and Brazil), where he was to persuade Leftist parties to begin immediate acts of sabotage and violence to take the pressure off Venezuela.
