5 August 1967.

1. LIRING/3 reported to Houston at 1800 on 2 August 1967 that he was informed by Sergio Valdez that morning that Alberto Domingo of El Siempre was taking a plane to Havana that afternoon. L/3 stated that he took this opportunity to write a note to Arnold (Arnol) Rodriguez explaining recent developments in the Boasés Crozco legal affair and asking Arnold to expedite new contact for L/3 with FAR, either in Havana or Guatemala City. L/3 explained to Houston that, in order to put heat on Arnold and FAR, he wrote that if he was not invited to Havana within the next two weeks, he would definitely be in Guatemala City, staying at Hotel Florida from 21–24 August, and that the new FAR liaison should identify himself as “Jose Luis.” He added that Arnold (Arpol) should cable him using the code “La medicina esta en el estado” in the event FAR wished to keep him out of the Crozco affair.

2. L/3 stated that Cerillo Valdez personally asked Alberto Domingo to deliver a sealed envelope containing this letter to Arnold Rodriguez. Valdez feels that Domingo will not read the letter, although it mentions him as the person who unwittingly originally referred Crozco to Valdez as LIRING/3.

3. L/3 also reported that he spoke with Ernesto Capoano by phone and has an appointment to meet with him at 1000 on 9 July 1967. Capoano cautioned L/3 that he did not identify himself or give some indication of the nature of the business. L/3 stated it concerned “the medicine,” and it was then that Mr. Capoano gave his appointment.
