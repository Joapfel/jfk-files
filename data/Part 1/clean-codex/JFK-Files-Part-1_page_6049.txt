DKE

Directorio Revolucionario Estudiantil

Jose Antonio Echeverría: con tus ideas en marcha.

The delegation at Fm. performed the following activities:

A. It gave out numerous press communiqués denouncing the presence of Soviet troops in Cuba.

B. Declarations about the attack on Havana’s seacoast by members of the D.F. were made, and great support was obtained.

C. An article that appeared in the “Line” magazine of September 7, 1962, was translated and distributed in great quantity. The article narrated the present and past actions of the Directorio Revolucionario Estudiantil.

Date: 1962-10-19. Handwritten: 19-128-26/3.
