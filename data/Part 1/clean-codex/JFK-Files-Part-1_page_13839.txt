(February 23, 1962)

u) Hilario L.U. looking for Subject urgently. Leaves a message about a “sick” person. (LILIANO, 5 March 1962)

v) One Mr. SILVA wants to speak to Subject. (LILIANO, 6 March 1962)

w) Julio Cárdenas tells the Cuban Ambassador he is leaving for Central America and offers his services. The Ambassador has no need, but says to ask Nogelio. (LILIANO, 7 March 1962)

x) Ricardo LIU wants to come by the Embassy for bulletins. Subject tells him it is better to meet at the same place and time. Ricardo has a Central American accent. (LILIANO, 8 March 1962)

y) Some gentlemen from La Normal are bringing a message from Acque MENDOZA and want to see Subject. (LILIANO, 6 March 1962)

z) Subject tells Yuriy ALISHANSKY of the Soviet Embassy that he could not get to the meeting but will come to see him later. (LILIANO, 7 March 1962)

a) Somebody named Pierre LIU is coming to the Embassy to see Subject. (LILIANO, 8 March 1962)

bb) Some people from “Expression” from Cincoha came to the Embassy to see Subject, but he was not in. (LILIANO, 8 March 1962)

cc) One Lucio Hernandez came to the Embassy to see Subject and was told to come in. (LILIANO, 13 March 1962)

dd) Pedro CONZALEZ Ortega is coming to the Embassy to see Subject. Person of this name was IF Secretary General in Mayarit and leader of small farmers and workers in the coastal area. (LILIANO, 15 March 1962)

ee) One VERONARA, first name unknown, left a message for Subject that it was urgent that he communicate with him. Later contacts concerned the bulletins and family problems. (LILIANO, 19 March 1962)

ff) One T30U2, first name unknown, came to the Embassy to see Subject. (LILIANO, 22 March 1962)

gg) A Venezuelan named MENDOZA, first name unknown, came to the Embassy to see Subject. (LILIANO, 22 March 1962)

hh) Arturo and Luz (Iuz) SERVANO came to the Embassy to see Subject. (LILIANO, 23 March 1962)

ii) One Hilario, a Guatemalan, urgently wants to see Subject. Hilario asks Subject, “Que el recuerda el Jardin o en la Luna.” Subject will look for him at 6:00 p.m. Note in file that Hilario may be identical with Rafael SOLIS Barrios, member of FGT.

jj) Ricardo, with a Cuban accent, tells Subject he waited for him yesterday. Several later contacts with one Ricardo. (LILIANO, 27 March 1962)

kk) Roberto GAYADA and Mr. Garrigica are waiting for Subject at the Embassy. (LILIANO, 28 March 1962)

ll) Victor Manuel GUTIERREZ (GUTIERRAZ), head of FGT in Mexico, met with Subject, who told GUTIERREZ that the person who was going to take over relations with Guatemala in Mexico had still not arrived. Subject planned to see Alfonso Ikosa, a leader of the 13 November group in Mexico. (LILIANO, 28 March 1962)

mm) One MARTEL, first name unknown, visited Subject at the school to deliver something. Later, a señora Martel, Argentine, told Subject she was leaving and asked him to send things she lacked. (LILIANO, 29 March 1962)

nn) One VARGAS, first name unknown, called Subject. (LILIANO, 29 March 1962)
