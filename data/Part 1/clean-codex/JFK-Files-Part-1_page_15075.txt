Report on Contact with Agent or Defector

Subject: AIRGUE

Date: 25 July 1962  
Time: 10:00  
Place: ост.лстр 1,0

Д. В. Малкин  
Case Officer

Comments:

CO visited Subj at his exit and said in E. 100 nonexistence for the period 20 July through 4 August.

Subj was told that a cable was received from Hs indicating that the people are definitely thinking about his resettlement in South America and wanted to make sure whether or not he had a preference. That was why, it was explained to Subj, CO called on 24 July and inquired whether or not he had this preference.

Subj mentioned that he had thought seriously about his name on his Austrian passport and concluded that, if at all possible, he would like to retain the name Kainer. CO mentioned that under no circumstances would it be better and was glad when Subj did not push the point. Subj initially, however, said that when it is certain where he is going, he will bring up the matter again.

To the list of documents he had previously claimed he needed under his new identity, Subj added an International Driver’s License.

Subj was most concerned about his future and asked all kinds of questions to ascertain that everything would be taken care of when he went to South America. To know the matter of the official [illegible] in the U.S., [illegible] inquires only one or neither side with the Austrians. CO replied that if anything ever came up, the officials would not check with the Austrians but with the pertinent South American officials who would have been informed by us to make his entry and life in the new country a possibility. Subj seemed to accept this but expressed the fear that something could be wrong.

Before CO left, Subj informed him that he would go around to the Venezuelan, Chilean, and Colombian Consulates and request tourist information.
