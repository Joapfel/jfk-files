Regarding paragraph 2 of reference A, AMBLEAK-I has developed the following information from Identity A, a radio script writer for AMSCROLL, who formerly worked in Spain on the same paper as GALBE: GALBE was born in Zaragoza, Spain, of prominent and wealthy parents. His mother was insane at the time of her death in 1954 in Cuba. Her brother, who at one time was canon (CANCIGO) of the Cathedral de la Seo de Zaragoza, was also insane at the time of his mother’s death. GALBE is known to have inherited insanity traits from the maternal side and is schizophrenic.

2. GALBE was educated as a lawyer. During the Spanish Civil War, he was active in creating “popular courts” and was prosecutor (Fiscal de los Tribunales Populares; “Trisionales” in OCR) of those opposed to the Spanish Republic, and became famous due to the ferocity of his attacks against defendants. Due to this and his communications at that time, now listed in the Causa General, he is therefore not permitted to return to Spain.
