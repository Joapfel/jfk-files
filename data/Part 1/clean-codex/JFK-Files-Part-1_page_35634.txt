11 October 1965

To: Chief, Cover  
From: Chief, PW  
Subject: Request to Advise Sponsor of Discontinuation of Radio Program

WPU-1018

This is to formalize a verbal request made earlier this month to Chief, Cover Branch. It would be appreciated if the local County Medical Association could be apprised of the fact that the radio program it overtly sponsored entitled “ZI Medico y Unido” has been terminated as of 20 September 1965. The program was carried on by Dr. Antonio MACEO and was heard three times a week over WROL and Radio America. The program was terminated because it was considered of marginal value in the propaganda field.
