10 May 1968, 2305Z. Cite Mexico City 5540.

Guatemala City, info Director.

Perumen.

Ref: Mexico City 5375 (IN 98384).

1. Manuel U. Marcue informed LIRING-4 on 8 May that his sister and daughter-in-law had succeeded in obtaining a Guatemalan visa and traveled to Guatemala City in an effort to see Carlos Minuel (PERSAVAL) Marcue in jail. Marcue said his sister and daughter-in-law, who returned during the week of 12 May, learned that PERSAVAL and LIRING-3 were sentenced to a minimum term of six months.

2. LIRING-4 is currently exploring the possibility of traveling to Guatemala to talk to LIRING-3. The station is in favor of such travel if she can get a visa, as LIRING-3 can be expected to urge LIRING-4 to go to Cuba during his term of imprisonment.

3. Can Guatemala City station confirm LIRING-3's sentence and provide details?
