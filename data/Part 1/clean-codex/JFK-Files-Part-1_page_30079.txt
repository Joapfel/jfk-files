Herman Edward Kimsey.

December 11, 1916.

4. 15 April 1945–1 October 1946. Camp Superintendent. Supervised 100 employees and laborers. Salary: $2694 per annum; $3021 per annum. Grade: SP-7. Yreka, California. U.S. Forest Service. U.S. Forest Service, Yreka, California. Art London, U.S. Forest Service, Yreka, California. Enlisted in the Counter Intelligence Corps, U.S. Army. Supervised a 100-man forest camp for the purpose of controlling timber disease.

5. 5 November 1943–15 April 1945. Laborer. Salary: $0.90 per hour; $1.10 per hour. McCloud, California. Lumber, McCloud River Lumber Company, McCloud, California. James Herbert, McCloud, California. Returned to the United States Forest Service. Operated a lumber carrier and worked as a brakeman on a small diesel locomotive.

6. 19 May 1941–5 November 1943. Sergeant, MOS 631, up to 1st Intelligence Analyst. Intelligence Commanding Officer. Combat Intelligence. Salary: $21.00 per month; $75.00 per month. Sergeant. Continental limits of the United States. U.S. Army. Supervisor: unknown. U.S. Army. Honorable discharge. Supervised the S-2 Section in Brigade Headquarters for the purpose of collecting and evaluating information on enemy military operations.

7. 1 May 1939–10 May 1941. Forest Guard. Salary: $100 per month. McCloud, California. U.S. Forest Service. United States Forest Service. Earl Gullaway, McCloud, California. Enlisted in the U.S. Army under the draft requirement but was not yet called for service. Prevention and suppression of forest fires.

8. January 1935–May 1939. Laborer. Worked as a laborer in the lumber and oil industries.
