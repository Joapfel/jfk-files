1. Following initial items may be of interest:

A. Crimas, chargé Algiers, appointed ambassador Conakry.

B. “Cuco” Calzadilla, chargé Madrid, is security officer according to AMIMP/1.

C. AMZOO/I friendly with Marilu Alberu, who worked at the Madrid Embassy. She has been away from Cuba a long time and is non-commie. Father owned two distilleries, which were taken after the revolution. Before that, mother divorced and took the children to Mexico and later Spain.

Children got $20 a month from father to live on outside Cuba. Somehow all got foreign ministry jobs under Castro. Marilu worked at the Vigo Consulate, then the Madrid Embassy. When AMZOO/I saw her in November 1964, she was talking seriously of marrying a Spaniard and trying to arrange for her sister Xenia to take her Embassy job. Brother Luis Alberu is cultural attaché in Mexico. He had been a painter in Mexico since about 1953. Recently in Cuba for the Tricontinent conference. Like his sisters, he is non-commie, but a hard worker, so keeps his job even though denounced by others.
