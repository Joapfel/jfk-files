3 AUG 67, IN 24956.

031730Z. CITE MEXICO CITY 2358. Immediate Guatemala City; info immediate Director. KAPOK.

Ref: A, Guatemala City 7738 (W24232); B, Director 24844.

1. Station considered there some risk Orozco might be executed if turned over to Guat authorities before taking steps to have him arrested by LITEMPOS. In view of naked FAR terrorist activities in Guat, did not feel, however, such possibility should deter us from acquisition of recent and medium-level information on Guat guerrilla movement, which Orozco no doubt has. Subject information might enable Guats to stop development of new guerrilla area Orozco originally entrusted with and save lives of innocent people.

2. Trust Guat City can make deal with Guat authorities to have Orozco given trial and lengthy jail sentence. Such sentence would probably in the end save Orozco’s life, at least until released, as there is little doubt FAR would kill Orozco—it may even kidnap him in Mexico—at first opportunity. This particularly true since, for operational reasons which Station believes sound, LIRING-3 instructed to denounce Orozco to FAR. LIRING-3 already contacted JACOBO.
