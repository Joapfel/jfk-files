9 March 1966

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Manuel Artime Buea (331-267437)

1. As a result of a conversation with Carl Jenkins, Artime's present C/O, the following was decided:

a) Jenkins will continue to see and handle Artime in Nicaragua. (Jenkins plans to go to Managua PCS within the next two weeks.)

b) Jenkins will set up a Hqs contact with Artime by telephone. Artime is to be given the name “Jim Wilson” and the direct line phone number, located in the 40-section of #1/Cuba, to call when he needs an agency contact and has something important he wants to discuss. The Hqs C/O for Artime will either be Thomas Hazlett or the undersigned.

c) Artime can be reached at his Miami address, 1270 NE 85th St. Phone 751-3648 (Code 305).
