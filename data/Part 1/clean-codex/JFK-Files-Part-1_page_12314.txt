RBYAT PLHABYSS verbally WKSCARLET.

Ref: Caracas 21722 (IN 891871*).

1. HOS TRAIRES DOMINGO OBRERO or “GUAJIRO” TORRES.

2. Ronald Condon (C O N D O N) probably identical to Ronaldo N. Condom Gil, brother of Francisco Condom Gil, also known as Frank Condon. Was a member of Brigade 2506. Arrived in Miami with ransomed Brigade members in December 1962. No current information on file.

3. Syla Cuervo (C U E R V O) possibly identical to Syla Roque Cuervo Castillo, Cuban exile now living in Miami. Subject born 29 October 1920, San Luis, Pinar del Rio, Cuba. Attorney in the Ministry of Labor and Ministry of Transportation, 1945–57. Was an anti-Batista activist, later joined various exile groups to fight Castro. Claims to have been a member of Brigade 2506, although name not on list of those captured at Playa Giron. Cuervo was accused of being leader of group making “anti-U.S., pro-Nazi, pro-socialist, pro-Communist” statements while second lieutenant in U.S. Army.
