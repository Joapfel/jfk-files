Classified message. Date: 17 February 1959. To: Director. From: Frankfurt (FROB). Action: SR 6. Info: FI, FI/OPS, EE 4, WE 4, S/C 2.

To Director, info Paris, Frankfurt. Cite FROB 3477, REDWOOD, REDSOX, AEACRE. References: A. EGFA 24867 (IN). B. FROB 3376 (IN 16580). C. FROB 3425 (IN 19285).

In order to avoid alienating AEASPIC by apparent forgetfulness of assurances given during last meeting, request instructions soonest on what we should write him.

End of message.

C/S comment: Requested HQS advise Paris on what to write AEASPIC, as early letter required.
