DIR INFO LEOPOLDVILLE. CITE PRET 4688 (STRAUB ACTING).

1. Assistant ARMA Leopoldville visited office 23 February pursuant to ACSI request for BI on Michael Hoare and Allister (Allyster) Wicks. Requested reference numbers for any BI we had sent in so he could pass them to ACSI. Told him we had no such BI, but recalled that press had carried considerable information on Hoare's background which might be available at KUSARK (XUSARK) headquarters. As for Wicks, we understood he was Rhodesian, but had no information other than the fact that he was involved in the airlift of mercenaries.

2. Visitor said he was proceeding to Durban (Dursan) and might contact Hoare while there.

CFN 4686. ARMA Leopoldville, 23 February. ACSI. Michael Hoare. Allister (Allyster) Wicks. ACSI. No such BI. Hoare. KUSARK. Rhodesian. No information. Durban.
