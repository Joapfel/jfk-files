Dear Sir,

I wrote a letter on February 15, 1978, to Mr. James Callaghan, Prime Minister. I enclosed registered mail No. 251067.

On July 18, 1963, I was in London, England, at the airport. Immigration Officer No. 175 had no visa; immigration placed me under house arrest. Mr. Smith, in police uniform, Secret Service, brought me a steak dinner, and a police officer interrogated me. He asked me what the Embassy of the U.S.S.R. said, and what did I hear in Vienna, Austria. I said I had heard that one American defector, Mr. Lee Harvey Oswald, to Russia had returned to the United States of America. This man was preparing to kill, to assassinate, President John F. Kennedy. Mr. Smith, the police officer, asked me, “Do you know his name?” I said, “His name starts with ‘O.’ His name was published one time in the newspaper in the Washington Post.” Mr. Smith, the police officer, said, “It’s easy to find.”
