Qualifications & Experience

12 November 1945

Name: Ann Lorene Goodpasture

Personal: Date of birth: 23 November 1918. Place of birth: Colina, Tennessee. Legal residence: Oklahoma.

Parentage: Father: C. H. Goodpasture, born 12 February 1894. Citizenship: U.S. by birth. Mother: Anna W. Goodpasture, born 21 February 1891. Citizenship: U.S. by birth.

Education: 1926–30, Grammar School, Tennessee and Oklahoma. 1930–32, Ward Jr. High School, Tipton, Oklahoma. 1932–36, Tipton Sr. High School, Tipton, Oklahoma. 1937–41, University of Oklahoma, Norman, Oklahoma, BS 1941. September 1941–February 1942, Hill's University, Oklahoma City, special courses; no degree conferred.

Experience: 1937–41, University of Oklahoma, secretarial and clerical duties, part-time while attending school, $20–30 per month.

September 1941–February 1942, Elko Photographic Finishing Co., Oklahoma City. Secretary and photographic technician, part-time while in school, $60–35 per month.

March 1942–January 1944, Office for Emergency Management, Central Personnel and Price Administration. Steno and Jr. Employee Services Officer, $1140–2000 per year.

February 1944–present, Office of Strategic Services, Far East Division, RAA Branch. Secretary to Chief, East Asia Economics Section to November 1944, Washington, DC, $1800 per year.

New Delhi, India; Kandy, Ceylon; Kunming, China. Secretary and Chief Clerk to Chief, OSS/RAA, IB and China Theaters to October 1945, $2000–2300 per year.

Kunming, China; Shanghai, China. Secretary and Chief Clerk to Chief, IRIS/CT, Department of State to December 1945, $2320 per year.

Shanghai, China; Washington, DC. AD, OASW, SSU. Reference Clerk, Order of Battle Section, SI, Shanghai, and Registry, SI, Washington, $2320–2770 per year.

Languages: French—speak, write, read fair.

Travel: November 1944–September 1945, India and Ceylon; two months temporary duty in Ceylon, June and July 1945. September 1945–March 1946, China.
