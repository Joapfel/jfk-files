Subject: V. Lenin Sokolov (SOKOLOV) (P-13,927)

Date: November 4, 1970

From: H. R. P.-1 (P-100b)

1. In accordance with arrangements made in the presence of EIP during the previous meeting with Source, Engineer Manuel MESTORI, on his life anniversary, invited Subject and his wife Lyudmila Sokolov (EMILYLA CONOLOV) for a drink on Sunday, November 1, at 1900 hours. Source and wife were also invited. This get-together lasted 3–3½ hours.

2. The party was pleasant and Subject appeared satisfied to be able to have somebody with whom he could speak his language. He was quite talkative, while his wife was rather reserved. Nevertheless, when Source stated that November 3 would be 13 years since he arrived in Mexico and wanted to celebrate the occasion with a dinner at his place with the same company, Subject and his wife commented that the celebration would have to be postponed because Ann de ROTURI and Emilia Showall-Sokolov would be on a business trip. They both accepted the invitation for the dinner to take place a day or two after Ann RE-TORI and SOKOLOV returned to town.

3. It was interesting to note that Subject and his wife LYUDMILA stated that ABDIKARH lived in Moscow in the same building or the same complex of buildings and that they were very close friends. Lyudmila went on to say that ABDIKARH's daughter was now 18 years of age and that she was quite a beauty. ABDIKARH was proud of the fact that his daughter was admitted to the Institute (Source believes: of Technology) in Moscow. Subject and his wife also stated that ABDIKARH was doing fine back home, but no letters were mentioned as to what was going on.
