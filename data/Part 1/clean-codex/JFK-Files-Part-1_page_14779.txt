Aksenov, D.P., born 8 February 1946 in Minsk. Married to Tanya Kondrakova, French teacher at Minsk Polytechnic Institute. They reside at Apt. 12, Prospekt Lenina (house number not recalled), in the so-called “House of Scientific Workers” near TS.U.M.—Central Department Store. Aksenov’s father is second secretary of the Belorussian CP (formerly secretary of Vitebsk Oblast) and recently elected to the CC of the CPSU. Aksenov graduated from the French Department of the Interpreter Faculty of Minsk Institute, but joined the KGB and is now senior lieutenant and case officer in charge of Hotel “Yubileynaya” in Minsk. He is a very apolitical person and compulsive drinker.
