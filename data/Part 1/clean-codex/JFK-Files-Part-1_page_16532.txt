Classified message.

17 January 1972, 172143Z. Cite WH/MIAMI 13595. Ottawa information Director.

TYPIC SMABOVE.

References: A OCUA 16526; B UFAA 3597.

1. Prior to February conference we intend to review all photographs of Perumens (PBRUMENS) stationed in Canada with those AM staff/AMQUAKE defectors who are responsibility of WH/MIAMI. Please forward any available copies of I.D. photos for individuals listed in reference B.

2. FYI: Value of such a review is underlined by AMMUG-1's recent recognition of Jorge de la Vega Vignon (201-815356) via such a photo. Unfortunately, AMMUG-1 was unable to identify Vega as being INTEL.
