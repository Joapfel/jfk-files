Officer of the 2nd section. This section was newly created for work against tourists. The chief of the 7th department was PERFILIEV; the chief of the 2nd section was GUSKOV.

In 1956 I was accepted as a candidate in the Communist Party, soon received the rank of senior lieutenant, and was promoted to senior case officer.

In 1957 I was accepted into the Party as a member.

In August 1956 my father died.

In 1957 or 1958 I was promoted to deputy chief of the 2nd section. I worked in the 7th department until 1960 and in January 1960 was sent to work as deputy chief of the 1st section in the 1st department of the 2nd Chief Directorate. The chief of the 1st department was KLIPIN, Vlad.; the chief of the 1st section was KOVSHUK.

My family consisted of my wife and two daughters: Oksana, born in 1954, and Tamara, born in 1958. Oksana had bronchial asthma from 1957 and was in hospitals for two or three months almost every year until 1963. In 1960 I was thinking about changing my temporary place of residence, and there was a possibility of going to work in the 2nd departments of the KGB in Lvov and Odessa. But there was another question: if I left Moscow, I would lose the flat in Moscow. At this time the chief of the section of the 2nd department, PIATROVSKI, proposed that I go to work in Ethiopia.
