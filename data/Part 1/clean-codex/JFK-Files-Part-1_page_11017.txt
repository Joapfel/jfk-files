Classified message.

Action: WH-8. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, FI, S/C 2.

DIR CITE MEXI 4258 IN23005. Reference: MEXI 3872 (IN 90763). 22 April 1993.

On 20 April 63, Brother LITAMIL-9 reported that L-9 expected to arrive MEXI 22 or 26 April 63. It was not known whether I-O reactions would be the latest information from Havana (HAVA) to MEXI. L-9's relatives indicated he was being considered for a Paris post. On 21 April, L-9's brother received a letter from his future daughter-in-law in which she stated that she gave L-9 a package and letters to take to MEXI.
