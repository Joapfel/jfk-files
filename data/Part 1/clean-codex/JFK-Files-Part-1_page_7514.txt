FCS 1831; Shanghai Municipal Files, file: 16-4-2-37.

Robson, Viola (VBola). Mrs. M. R. Isaacs.

Born: 10 July 1910, NYC. Nationality: American.

Description: Height 5'9". Build: slim. Complexion: fair.

Documentation: In 1932, holder of U.S. passport No. 382046, issued in NYC on 16 May 1931.

History: In 1931, known to have visited Germany.

Known Communist and follower of Lucy Stone.

Arrived in Shanghai on 23 August 1932.

On 16 September 1932, married Harold Robert Isaacs. She had been engaged to Isaacs for some years before her arrival in China, and it has been stated that the letters between the two indicated that she was a Communist, sympathies on the part of both, and Isaacs' intention of joining the CP.

After her arrival in China, she was employed at the Duh Chung Middle School, Shanghai, as a teacher.

She lived in the Embankment Building, North Soochow Road.

On 7 August 1933, the Isaacs left Shanghai for Japan and on 16 August 1933 proceeded to Dairen, returning to Shanghai by rail on 26 August 1933.

In November 1933, information was received to the effect that Miss R. Robinson was a teacher at the Comparative Law School, Shanghai.
