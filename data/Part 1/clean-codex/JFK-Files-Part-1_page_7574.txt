Confidential information indicated that T. H. B. was in China in the month of May 1954. The case is under the title “The Tragedy of the Chinese Revolution” by APPELWAN.

APPELWAN was examined in the Court of China in the month of July 1954. He was charged with the crime of being a member of the Communist Party of China and was tried by the People’s Court of Shanghai, in the People’s Republic of China, on June 20, 1954. He was convicted of the charge of being a member of the Communist Party of China and was sentenced to five years’ imprisonment by the People’s Court of Shanghai on March 3, 1955.

According to APPERMAN, subject is author of “The Tragedy of the Chinese Revolution,” and APPELWAN met subject through Alexander BICHTAL. Subject was the first editor of the CCP paper, of which APPELWAN later was to become editor. DFB-17477.
