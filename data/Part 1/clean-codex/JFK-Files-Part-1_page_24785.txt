Evaluative Comment:

1. Although Tichborn's services have not been fully utilized, primarily due to the lack of continuing, regular requirements in the area for his special capabilities, there continues to be a need for an agent of his wide experience and talents in the political action field, particularly as an adduce advisor and monitor in priority election operations.

2. The transfer of Tichborn from an exclusive WIND status under UOACE to a wider-ranging assignment under WUDRINY makes him available for DDP-wide use. Because of language and background experience, he will continue to be used primarily in Spanish-language areas, but his ICS/P employment status should also enable him to gradually expand his usefulness to other areas as well.

3. Recommend approval.
