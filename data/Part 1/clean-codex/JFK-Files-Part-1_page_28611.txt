Section V.

A. What are his outstanding strengths? Basic brain power, ambition, determination, organizational ability, education, area knowledge, language ability, social skills, experience, aggressiveness. Bites extra hard, effectively presenting complicated material. Deeply interested in his work, anxious to learn more about it. Has unusually thorough grounding in tradecraft. Understands the “policy” aspect of operations—relationships with other agencies, etc.—to a much greater degree than others of his grade and experience.

B. What are his outstanding weaknesses? Subject accepts responsibility, but sometimes fails to carry on with it; he has great charm when interested in using it, but has had increasing difficulties in relationships with office staff because of the impression he gives of his own importance. Tends to blame circumstances for personal failure to follow through on assignments affected by personal relations. Wants to be in the “inner circle” and tends to sulk when he feels he is not, or when things do not go entirely to his satisfaction.
