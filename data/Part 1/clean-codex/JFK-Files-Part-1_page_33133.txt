Priority Director, Ottawa.

RYBAT AMROSE AMCHALK SMABOVE.

REF WH/MIAMI 5742 C.

1. Meeting held by Nebecker with AMCHALK-5 on 12 November to review and lay plans for operation of reference. AMCHALK-5 eager to undertake effort to either (A) make self attractive to DGI for double-agent purposes or (B) participate in possible operations for recruitment and defection of DGI personnel, dependent upon how meetings go in Montreal.

2. Following is explanation which AMCHALK-5 will provide DGI officer as excuse for reinitiation of contact, i.e., that he is totally disillusioned with actions of U.S. Government vis-à-vis Cuba; that the U.S. Government, as is well known in the Miami area, has terminated all of its militant action capability against Cuba; and that he himself has been definitively terminated. This will be most plausible to DGI since they are undoubtedly aware of talk in exile community of mass terminations. AMCHALK-5 will then explain that since the U.S. Government cannot be counted on for anything, he has taken it upon himself.
