Signatures to be applied by subject:

1. Passport: Page three below picture in the space provided. Use a regular pen and dark ink. No ballpoint pen.

2. Driver's Permit: Between entries 4 and 5 next to the picture, turning the document to write the name from entry 5 to 4.

3. International Certificate of Vaccination: This document should be signed in ink on the inside cover page in the space provided.

No signature required for the other two documents.
