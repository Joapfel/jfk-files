Secret cable, 15 April 1968, 2300Z, Caracas 937 (Lyner acting).

Priority: Mexico City, Director. Info: Guatemala City.

RYBAT.

Refs:
A. Caracas 9356 (IN 88953).
B. Caracas 9364 (IN 89759), 201 AMCLEVE-15.
C. SC Guatemala City 521.

15 April 1968, IN S1426.

In view of reference C, we are anxious to pin down whether Illich [is in] Caracas. Believe possible to go to CIVISTA-5 without blowing AMCLEVE-15 information in reference B. Can we go to CIVISTA-5 with story that Mexico City Station has advised that Illich may be travelling [in] Latin America and they believe he plans [to] stop [in] Venezuela in addition [to] other countries? We would tell CIVISTA that WOFIRM [is] interested [in] being advised should Illich appear on scene. Would also be useful [to] give CIVISTA-5 some background on Illich. If agreeable, would appreciate Illich background from Mexico City by 1500 hours local time 16 April to permit ROGOVER [to] raise [it] at scheduled meeting with CIVISTA-5.
