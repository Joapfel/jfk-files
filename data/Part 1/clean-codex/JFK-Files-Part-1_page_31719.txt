2 February 1953

Memorandum for: TO/TONIC  
Subject: Call from Jim real fast

1. Wants to put Paul Keston (Kesten) on board—has radio experience. Please secure security clearance on him right away.

2. Re Yugoslav publication which I sent. Miller called Bill Durkee about it and has not yet received an answer. What do our friends across the street think of it? Ask Jack Villa if she has any dope on it. They have run 500 contacts off and are just holding them.

TMS:

1. Ellen will start the clearance for Paul Kesten. Cleared back in 1953.

2. Bill Durkee took this up with Stevens at State, who asked that we hold off until he, Illinois, returned from Yugoslavia and they got his views. Illinois talked with Stevens a couple days ago on this and, rather than say to us what to do on the distribution of these publications, he asked if we couldn't hold off a couple more days until Illinois returns. She told Sam Walker this. She has also had a letter from Dollars on it, so everyone is after her on this.
