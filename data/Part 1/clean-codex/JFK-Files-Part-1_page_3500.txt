Memorandum for the Record.

Subject: Telephone Conversation between AMHDDY-1 and Martin Ferrer

Date: 7 April 1964

1. It was brought to the attention of AMHDDY-1 that the MRR is the only Cuban exile organization that has not properly registered with the United States Government. A-1 will attend to the registration.

2. A-1 requested to check his P.O. box.

3. A-1 reported receiving information this morning that ADELAIDA is en route to Miami, N.Y.

4. Concerning his visa, he stated he had received an F-510 form. According to A-1, these forms have to be filled out and submitted to the American Embassy in Managua. A-1 was asked if AMJAVA-4 now has the U.S. re-entry permit. A-4 does presently hold the alien-resident F-4 card, but not the re-entry blue book.

5. A-1 was informed that all village radios will be shipped to Nicaragua and none would be sent to Miami for him to hand-carry. It is hoped that these village radios will arrive in time for the operation. Should this not be the case, they have PRC-10s which could serve the same purpose as the village radios.

6. Mr. Mountain is expected to call Sixto Mesa, advising that the survey has been completed. Mr. Mountain will request that Mesa have his three DM&M engineers return to the States. A-1 was asked to make sure that Mr. Mountain's message reaches the engineers.

7. The soil sample sent from Nicaragua is still in the possession of AMWORLD.

8. A-1 was requested to notify his propaganda personnel not to make any mention of Brazil (Brasil) and their radio broadcasts. A-1 agreed.

The above conversation was conducted in the presence of Mr. H. K. DeSoto.

Raul J. Hernandez  
WH/BA/SO/HH

cc: AMHDDY-1 (201)
