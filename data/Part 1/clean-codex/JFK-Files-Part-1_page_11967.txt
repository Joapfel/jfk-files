1. Joseph R. Merola, DPOB 9 October 1925, Turtle Creek, Pennsylvania. Station cards from the period 1959–1963 indicate he was an ex-convict, notorious smuggler, gunrunner, and general troublemaker. Cards to LNERGO reports indicate he was involved in the Panama invasion plan in 1959, and in Haitian invasion plans in 1958 and 1963. In 1959, he was under indictment in Pennsylvania for interstate transportation of stolen arms. In 1960, he was principal in a proposed Cuban counterfeit PESC deal. LNERGO was contacted in 1961 and indicated that although he had a long record, he undoubtedly could speak authoritatively on international affairs. Also in 1963, he was interested in providing contraband whiskey in Haitian senator, and attempted mule ship restriction which placed on him after convicted for committing sladeestimate bringing in to U.S. the son of a loiterer.
