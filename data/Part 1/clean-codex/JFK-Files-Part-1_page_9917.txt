Dispatch. Secret. Processing action.

Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Chief, CH/COC. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. J.C. MCFOR. Submit: PETERHEN/THIE LIRING-3 Operation. Action request—references: FYI. Reference: HMHA 32778.

1. Forwarded under separate cover are six reports dated 31 July, 5, 11, 14 and 17 (two reports) August 1967 concerning the LIRING-3 Operation prepared by Wallace B. ROLTON.

2. The first three reports, 31 July, 5 and 11 August, deal with the recent noises CROZCO Leal “affair” (see reference) and its aftermath. The 17 August noise, which is one of the latest occurrences on Liring-3, also deals with this subject. LIRING-3’s conversation with Mercedes PEREIRA (report dated 14 August 1967) was reported in MEXICO CITY 2476.

3. ROLTON’s report dated 17 August 1967 is of some interest.

A. The departure of the secretary LIRING-4 previously filled in for reopens the possibility that LIRING-4 may again work at LIRING. LIRING-4, in fact, recently received a necklace from this secretary (a rather unexpected gift at this juncture).

B. The report indicates that LIRING personnel have doubts about some of LIRING-3’s activities (this is not too surprising).

For Willard C. CURTIS.

Attachment: Reports as paragraph 1, under separate cover.

Distribution: C/WHD with attachment, under separate cover. C/WHD/COG without attachment. Files. U.S. copy.

Dispatch symbol and number: HMHA-32850. Date: 28 August 1967. Classification: Secret. Log file number: 201-7983CL.
