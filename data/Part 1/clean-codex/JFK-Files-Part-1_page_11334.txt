Classified message.

3 January 1964.

To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH S. Info: DDP, CI, CI/OPS, CHA, FI, SAS 8, VR.

C-10331. 2 January 64, 91186.

1. Reports that Teresa Proenza was summarily fired from her job as a result of an open cable from Fomin Raul Roa received as of ten or twelve December. Please note attachment to HMMA 22624 giving date 1 December.

2. Says that firing evidently resulted from Proenza's remark to sister-in-law of Roa to the effect that strong Roa note to GOM regarding detention of Silvia Duran was a diplomatic error. Backing this up is remark to Bety (Cety) Solorzano about 8 December to the effect Proenza should watch her step and be more friendly with sister of Roa's wife. Was also witness to Proenza's criticism of Roa note in conversation with pro-Castro official of Fine Arts Institute.

3. Proenza hard hit by firing. Roa probably ordered dismissal because she is Communist and Roa is not. Proenza visited General Heriberto Jara in Veracruz to plead case and reported Jara furious at injustice. On 24 December Proenza showed up at Embassy dinner, appeared slightly drunk, said she had just come from visit with General Lazaro Cardenas. She did not stay at Embassy for dinner.

4. Says her dismissal known by Embassy personnel but not being secret.
