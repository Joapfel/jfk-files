Los Angeles, California. LAS.

Staff President. T 555000. 61587. VCR: 0819938, November 1974. Cite LA/Miami 22011.

To: Immediate Quito. Director.

1. FBI (FUI)/Miami informs us that it was reported that Orlando Bosch (D B S G H), leader of Cuban Action, is in Quito.

2. Bosch has recently served five years in Atlanta Federal Penitentiary for bombing of Polish ship in Miami Harbor. Arrest warrant currently out on him from FBI. FBI has been searching for him for several months.

3. For Director, we understand this information has been reported to FBI headquarters. Suggest you may wish to contact [them] for further details.

4. File: defer, E2. IMPDET.
