Classified message.

Originator: C. Bustos. Unit: WH/3/Mexico. Extension: 5940. Date: 19 March 1963.

To: Bonn, Paris, Hague, Mexico City.

From: Director.

Confidential: C/WH 5.

Info: DCI, D/DCI, DDP, ADDP, C/CA 2, C/CI, C/FI 2, C/EE 2, C/WE 2, D/OCT 2, S/C.

Cite DIR 25991.

RBYAT.

For immediate action.

Reference: MEXI. 3810 (IN 87887).

1. Adolfo Lopez Mateos, Mexican President, making official visit addresses countries March and April. COS MEXI is in clandestine contact with President with knowledge of ODACID. Lopez requested names of friends of COS MEXI in addressee cities who could contact his private secretary Humberto Romero (Perez) at each place during visit.

2. Lopez will want to send and receive messages through KUBARK commo channels to and from high Mexican officials and also KUBARK MEXI. He does not trust his own foreign service commo.

3. HQ authorizes contact between Lopez secretary Romero and addressee stations to pick up and deliver messages. Romero speaks good English and station officer must use alias and give Romero sterile phone number or office phone not readily traceable to KUBARK. Identify self as friend of Senor Blanco, or “friend of Tio,” names which Lopez uses to refer to COS MEXI. Initial contact to Romero can be made by phone or in person after he arrives and subsequent meetings should be held under conditions as secure as circumstances permit, to avoid identifying.

Releasing officer.

Coordinating officers.

Authenticating officer.
