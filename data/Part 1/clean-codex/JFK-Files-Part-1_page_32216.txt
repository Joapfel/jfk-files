Dispatch. Chief, W1 Division. Chief of Staff, Quito. Chief of Base, Guayaquil. Administrative/Personnel — Promotion for C/CS. Action required: see below. Approved by CS Career Service Panel, 17 July 1970.

During the visit of the Chief, W1D, to the Base last November 1969, he mentioned to the COS, Quito, and the COB that the promotion for Flores, from GS-9 to GS-10, should be forthcoming; he also said this to Flores during a private meeting. Thus far, however, there has been no notice of the promotion action. The COB assumes that the delay is related in some way to the dates set for meeting of the promotion panel for Flores' grade. If this assumption is not correct, however, and some further action by the Base is necessary in addition to the recommendation contained in Flores' last Fitness Report, the COB would appreciate being advised.

Distribution: Original and 2 to C/W1D; 2 to COS, Quito.

C/CS Recruit 10. Officer status and degree: HETT-1474, CASCADING, RYAN. Date: 26 May 1970.
