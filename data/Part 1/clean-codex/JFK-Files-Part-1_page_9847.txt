6 April 1962. Chief, Mexico City 5111. Guatemala City info Director, Tegucigalpa.

Ref: Guatemala City-8822 (not sent Tegucigalpa) (IN 80309).

1. Afternoon 3 April 68, LIRING-3 reported he plans depart for Guatemala City via land in company Guatemala citizen Francisco Lara circa 4 April 68. Although LIRING-3 has valid Mexican documentation and Lara has false Mexican Ministry of Education documentation, they will attempt enter Guatemala illegally.

2. In Guatemala City, Lara plans introduce LIRING-3 to woman who logistical officer for FAR in Guatemala City. Trip will them be arranged for LIRING-3 to visit FAR Sierra de las Minas headquarters to discuss future support of FAR from Mexico City.

3. After approximately ten days in Guatemala, Lara and LIRING-3 will travel Tegucigalpa to investigate possibilities of setting up printing press under LIRING-3's true name.

4. Lara's false Mexican documentation is identity card from Secretaria de Educacion Publica under name Jacinto Rangel Cervantes. For identity card photograph, Lara wore glasses, hair dyed grey and BO mustache. Lara told LIRING-3 that for travel to Tegucigalpa from Guatemala City, he will use Guatemalan passport under name
