Identification of Ref A is obviously brother of CIURDAN-1, but according to the press report, and as far as we know, no one was arrested in the raid which netted documents. We do not want to alert liaison to our special interest by querying regarding the identification. We usually are informed by liaison of subversive arrestees, but this is not certain these days. As reported in Ref B, we are now also getting information on the CIURDAN-1 family case from AMULET-15 and therefore hope to be able to monitor any arrests. None of CIURDAN-1's family arrested, according to AMULET-15.
