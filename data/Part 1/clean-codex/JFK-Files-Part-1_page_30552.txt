May 6, 1948

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Newton Scott Milner; Neil C. Stine Milner

The above-entitled subjects are due to return to the States in May and June 1948, respectively.

Harry Litie has advised that neither will return before August and probably later than August. It is probable that Newton Milner will be returned by the Breton (breton), but no action will be taken until final word is received from the Breton (breton).
