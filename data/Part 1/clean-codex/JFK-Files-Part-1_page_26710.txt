Kool 72-WICO-22

29 April 1963

14.2 Out to 12-08-53, three times. No rings, complete. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks MO, who answers, for Mario Gonzalez Marquez. They cannot find him. He may be on the 8th floor. Asks for Editor-in-Chief. Not there now either. MMK says he (Mario Gonzalez Marques) and MMK are friends. Wants to invite him to press conference; leaves details.

15.5 Out to 45-44-40, twice. Busy.

16.0 Out to 35-61-97. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks MO, who answers, “El Die,” for Eduardo Kamirez. Phone 35-61-97.

16.2 Out to 35-61-97. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks MO, who answers, for Leonardo (not Eduardo) Kamirez. Not there. Asks for Muzgut (ph.). Not there. Asks for one of the Directors. Muzgut.

During wait, MMK tells VAG that the first act of Bosch (Boach)’s government, as stated in his recently published book, and what he asked President Kennedy about, was to take away from Standard Oil Company of New Jersey the oil refining company. That was the first obstacle he ran into.

MO returns to phone and asks who he wants: Fuente, Cuenca, Akaxen Arriola, or any of them. None of them is there. Sr. Muzgut will be in around 1300 and Leonardo around 1200. Tell Leonardo that MMK called; leaves details on press conference on Santo Domingo. A press conference of exiled Dominicans. They want to give him some information. If Leonardo does not come in on time, give the information to Muzgut.

18.0 Out to 35-61-70. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks MO, who answers, if Cuenca is there. No.

18.2 Out to 43-04-40. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks MO, who answers, for Santos (?) Arriola. Wrong number.

18.3 Out to 43-04-30, twice. No answer. During outgoing rings, MMK says he has all that was agreed on between the U.S. and Santo Domingo—not here but in Santo Domingo—including further devaluation and permission for intervention in Santo Domingo.

19.2 Out to 23-03-06. Marcio Santos (?) Arriola. Wrong number. 24-03-06.

19.7 Out to 22-03-06. Wrong number.

30.0 Out to 3-12-11. Busy.

30.2 Out to 13-41-23. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks for Head of Information of “Universal.” Call 21-08-78.

30.3 Out to 21-08-73. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks for Head of Information, phone 12-08-73. Asks for his name. Sr. Deacario Holafos.

21.0 Out to 12-09-73, four times. No rings.

22.0 Out to 16-03-01. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks MO, who answers at Kool 72-WICO-22.
