To: WAVE. Info: SJOS.

TYPIC PRUMEN.

1. Headquarters received a phone call from Ambiddy 1 on 18 April during which he said he had received information from FF Syduc Borges that Luciano Nieves was being supported by Jose Figueres in Miami and Figueres had requested Nieves and another Cuban by nickname “Yoyi” to come to Costa Rica on an agitprop mission to create a climate for overthrowing the present regime. Ambiddy 1 claims Nieves is living well in Miami with no visible means of support. Headquarters is under the impression he is impoverished and living hand to mouth.

2. Although we consider Ambiddy 1’s report rather wild rumor, we would appreciate WAVE and SJOS comments and current reading of Nieves’s activities.

End of message.

WE/C/Comment: Ambidny (Ambiddy) reported the above information by telephone.

Coordinating officers.

Releasing officer.
