Office Memorandum, United States Government.

To: Chief, Contact Division  
Through: Acting Chief, Detroit Office  
From: John T. Hazen, Cleveland Office  
Subject: Visit to Mr. Tadeusz Stefan Kucner, 2434 Tremont St., Cleveland, Ohio

Date: 7 January 1954

1. On 6 January 1954, a visit was made to Mr. Tadeusz Stefan Kucner, a Polish displaced person who arrived in the United States on 11 July 1951. His alien registration number is 8 059 181.

2. Mr. Kucner was born in Lvov on 6 February 1905. He attended the Polytechnical Academy of Lvov from 1925 to 1932, when he received his degree in architectural engineering.

3. From 1933 to 1939, Mr. Kucner worked for the city administration of Poznan as a building construction inspector for the Poznan building construction board of control. This was similar to a city planning commission in the United States.

4. In 1940, after the German occupation, the subject was sent by the Germans to Warsaw. He worked in Warsaw as a representative of the inspector of the building construction board of control until 1944.

5. When the Germans evacuated the city, Mr. Kucner was transported by freight car to Leipzig, where he remained until the end of the war.

6. The subject resided in several displaced-person camps in Germany from 1945 until his departure for the United States in 1951. He was employed by IRO as a technical examiner.

7. During his years in both Poznan and Warsaw, Mr. Kucner was concerned only with residential buildings and was unable to supply information on industrial installations in these cities.

8. Some comments made by the subject, which are not worthy of OO-A dissemination, are being forwarded for any interest they may have.

9. Since Mr. Kucner's arrival in the United States, he has been employed as a laborer in a foundry.

10. Mr. Kucner will not be visited again; therefore, no name-check data is being submitted.
