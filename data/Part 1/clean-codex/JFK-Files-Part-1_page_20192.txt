Jacques Gabriel Richardson

17. Military Training (continued)

Rating received at end of this training: Tec 4.

Duty assignment or rating after this training: Assignment to “EISI.”

Dates of duty assignment: January 1946 to August 1946.

Second special service school attended: EISL, Fort Snelling, Minnesota.

Location: Fort Snelling, Minnesota.

Dates attended: June 1946 to August 1946.

Rating received at end of this training: Second Lieutenant.

What were you taught in second special service school? Creation of battle and military terms of the Japanese armed forces; civil affairs, military administration of occupied areas, and propaganda writing.

Duty assignment after this training: Student; translator; instructor.

What did you do during this duty assignment? Translated newspaper articles, ordinances, SCAP directives, etc. Flown from Japan. Made instructor’s guides for civil affairs courses.
