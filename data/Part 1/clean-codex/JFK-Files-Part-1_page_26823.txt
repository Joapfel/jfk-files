Central Intelligence Agency Official Routing Slip.

To: J. Nalkin, II; WH/260AT/KAHN; WH/1/LEHITAN. Initials: E.H.; T.

Action. Direct reply. Prepare reply. Approval. COSPATON. Recommendation. Comment. File. Return. Concurrence. Information. Signature.

Remarks: Re Victor Rice Galan. Punches must be sent to the addressee's station. Did the general want him for it? Runaway? Is he going to be a problem? To be sure? By Konrad A. Patsche for Ricoh minutes as long as I last and I will be back.

Please here to return to sender.

From: Douglas M.D. Date: 11-8-79.
