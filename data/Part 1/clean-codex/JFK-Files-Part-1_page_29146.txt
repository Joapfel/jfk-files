Section XII: Children and Other Dependents.

1. Number of children, including stepchildren and adopted children, who are unmarried, under 21 years of age, and are not self-supporting.

2. Number of other dependents, including stepchildren, stepparents, etc., dependent on you for at least 50% of their support, or children aged 18 who are not self-supporting in total.

3. Provide the following information for all children and dependents: Name; Relationship; Year of birth; Citizenship; Address. Mail room.
