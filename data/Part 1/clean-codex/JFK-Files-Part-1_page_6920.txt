Origin: Jenny G. Cleavin. Unit: W-3/Guatemala. Extension: 2860. Date: 18 October 1958.

Classified message.

To: Federal Bureau of Investigation (Federal Sirisau Cf Investigation).

From: Director, Central Intelligence Agency.

Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS, SR 4, S/C 2.

From: Mexico, Mexico City. Date of info: 13–15 October 1958.

Subject: Travel of Maurice Halperin and wife, Communists.

1. According to a person with access to travel records, who is a usually reliable source, Maurice Halperin and his wife, American Communists who are suspected of being Soviet agents, left Mexico City via KLM Flight 968 for Curacao, and Lisbon via KLM Flight 722 on 13 October.

2. These reservations were made in the name of Mr. and Mrs. Daniel Pastor, on 11 October. A call from the Mexico-America Travel Agency advised KLM that the Pastors had cancelled their trip and requested that KLM substitute Mr. and Mrs. Maurice Halperin.

3. The Halperins are on a waiting list of Swissair from Lisbon to Zurich for 15 October. A telephonic request by “Mr. Pastor” asked the price of a round-trip ticket from Zurich to Tel Aviv.
