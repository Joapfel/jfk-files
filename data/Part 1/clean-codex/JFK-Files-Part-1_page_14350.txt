SECRET 24 June 1970, 1212Z. Cite Madrid 1440.

RYEAT TYPIC GUDOZE

24 June, 110110

1. GUDOZE reports 15 June the following Cuban ambassadorial items:

A. Guillermo Ruiz Perez (261-735295) and wife going to Havana 26 or 27 July for general MINREX meeting of chiefs of mission, latter half July.

B. Person currently chief of secretariat for Carlos Chain Soler going to Warsaw as ambassador.

C. Other ambassadors in Madrid that date awaiting 23 June flight to Havana included Raul Valdes Vivo (261-7), Alipio Zorrilla Mazcra (261-S12115), and Carlos Alfaro Varela (261-S14891).

D. Some discussion held between Ruiz, wife, and above visitors concerning who will be next ambassador to Morocco. In doubt whether will be Chairman Soldier's son-in-law or possibly son-in-law Annartini-1 (261-878443). Cannot clarify since participants equally vague.
