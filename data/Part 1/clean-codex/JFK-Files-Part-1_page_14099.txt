Following statement from Luis Alberto de Jesus Condom Cajikal:

Rejoined anti-communist underground about May 1960. Officers were at Edificio FOCSA, apartment 214, 24 06.

Second floor were located CIA Cubana de Fianza, president Bobby Maduro (BOBY MADURO), in charge of Juan Mendez, now in underground Havana. There asked to join FRD by señores Rivero and El Rubio, who carried FRD credentials. MAR accepted and sent planillas OJAMI of Juan Mendez, war name Jorge Piloto, and of self, war name Jorge Pino. Group had contact with American Embassy Havana named Emilio Fernandez, war name Eugenio. Group asked check reception radio SWAN in provinces Pinar del Rio, Havana, and Matanzas.

Group visited by Pinio Prieto (LATER EXECUTED 12 OCTOBER 60) and delegate Fabio from Miami regarding organization of fifth column utilizing plastic explosive, selecting houses in Havana for arms caches and drop zones. Subject took asylum 2 January 61, NEXI Embassy Havana.

End of message.
