Dispatch.

To: Chief, WH Division.

From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Subject: PBRUMEN/LITAMIL-9/Operational Progress Report.

1. LITAMIL-9 remains in PBRUMEN. On 19 June 1965 the C/O met with L-9's brother who reported he had talked with L-9 by telephone about 1 June. At that time L-9 was in good health and spirits and key words in his conversation indicated that he had not been bothered in any way by the PBRUMEN Security Service since his return. L-9 (I-9) said he was doing well and wanted a new assignment. He did not reveal whether he expected this to be a foreign assignment.

Distribution: 3 - C/WAD; 2 - Files.

Cross reference to dispatch symbol and number: IDOMA 26552. Date: 8 July 1965. File number: 201-329609.

For the enlisted English and Arabic corps of the Tenth Division.
