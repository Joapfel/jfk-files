B. The liaison man between the various Cuban exile groups was, and apparently still is, Juan (or Jose) Antonio PAF (ENFIST-1), X 201-3/73. Got the impression that he lives in Miami.

C. At one meeting during the period Orlando Bosch was residing in Santo Domingo, the participants spoke of placing bombs on Cuban aircraft and in Cuban diplomatic missions, sabotaging Cuban and Soviet ships. They talked of kidnapping the Cuban ambassador to the United Nations in order to effect the release of political prisoners and discussed killing Carlos Rafael Rodríguez (Carlos Rapafel) and Dr. Iguez, “an important Cuban functionary in Lisbon,” because he is contributing to improved relations between the United States and Cuba.
