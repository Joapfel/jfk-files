Reference: MEXICO CITY 0713.

1. FERNALD met subject 23 June. Subject explained he had first met AEDIEHARD in February 1969 at a party at the home of local Communist Dr. Federico (FEDERICAO) Marin. Station traces confirm Marin's left-wing contacts but no previous record of specific FUSTEAL contacts. Subject accepted AEDIEHARD's invitation to dinner, and the relationship flourished on the basis of “friendship and understanding between us and USSR.” In the course of meetings, at least once in subject's apartment, subject dropped names of “influential Mexican friends.” Among those mentioned to FERNALD were industrialist Carlos Trouyet, presidential private secretary Joaquin Cisneros (CISNERSO), journalist Agustin Barrios Gomez, and painter David Alfaro Siqueiros, which apparently led AEDIEHARD to believe subject might be well-informed on the Mexican scene. AEDIEHARD asked a number of questions on political matters, such as: Does Luis Echeverria have a better chance of becoming Mexican president than Antonio Carrillo Flores; and what is the real purpose of the Rockefeller mission?
