Miami, Fla., Nov. 23 (AP)—A Cuban diplomat who defects says Cuba's economy is well on the road to reaching a climax after suffering one setback after another.

Raul Volta, who left his principal office in the Cuban Embassy in London and came (CANT) to the United States for asylum, told us when last night that General Fulgencio Batista (FISCONTEIT) reigned (LEIGNED) in Cuba, because of scarcities and economic mismanagement (WISDOM).

When he last walked in Cuba, in July, Volta said, it hurt him to witness the “economic chaos” there.

Volta, 36, was the fourth person to defect (REFECT) from the Cuban Embassy in London since Fidel Castro's (FILIPPO CAZIER'S) takeover in 1959 (1989).
