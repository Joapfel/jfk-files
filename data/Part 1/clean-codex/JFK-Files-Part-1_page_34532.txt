The Moscow Three Embankments (formerly State Square, Red Square, Novgorod Square), along Gorky Street, Sadovaya, Kolossova, Komsomolsky Prospect, Leningradsky Prospekt, past Moscow University on the Lenin Hills, back to the National.

To see all the Kremlin and Red Square, a visit to the Kremlin is a must for all tourists. The four hours there will give you another day in Moscow. Be sure to see the Exhibition of Economic Development of the USSR (see p. 60), which will give you a good idea of contemporary Soviet life and the cultural and economic achievements of Moscow and the rest of the Soviet Union. The exhibition takes four hours. In the afternoon, pay a visit to the famous Tretyakov Art Gallery (see p. 150); the treasure trove of Russian art is sure to have something to occupy three hours.

Do your best, too, to see Moscow's glorious Bolshoi Theatre.

If you have a few more days in Moscow, pick tours to suit your taste and inclinations.
