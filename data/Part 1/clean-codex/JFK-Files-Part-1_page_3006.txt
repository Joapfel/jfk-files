T 631054. EIA355. IN 805199. TOR: 261252Z January 1976. MMADR 29824. 261330Z January 1976. CITE Madrid 29824. 02 MHSPLASH.

1. Morning 25 January, KALBING received a phone call at home from a young man who said he was Chris Phillips, son of Dave Phillips. He said he was on vacation and requested information on what to see and do in the Madrid area. He also asked if the political situation in Portugal was such that travel there would be no problem. KALBING was on the way to a meeting and asked that he call back later. He did, and said he was traveling to Lisbon that night and would return to Madrid later in the week. He said he did not have an address in Madrid at the moment and would call KALBING again upon his return to Madrid.

2. We are somewhat suspicious of this call since KALBING does not know Chris Phillips, although it is possible his father gave him KALBING's name as someone to help him with tourist questions. We also doubt that Phillips would have called back if he were only probing for the location of CIA personnel, since the first call would have confirmed what he wanted to know. Check with Marine.
