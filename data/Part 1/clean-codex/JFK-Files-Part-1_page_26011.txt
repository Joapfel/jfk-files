121445Z August 1970. Cite Caracas (CANACAS) 5507. CAPALUS acting.

Director, CIGRAPE.

12 August 1970.

1. CIFENCE-4 (CIFERCE-4) informed Station early morning 11 August that a member of the CTFENCE organization responsible for telephone taps informed him that CTFENCE knows that CIGRAPE-2 has telephone tap equipment in his apartment and CTFENCE believes he works for JMLANCE. CIFENCE-4 said that CTFENCE is investigating the matter.

5 October 1970.

2. Station has closed down CIGRAPE operation, taken off all taps at central switchboard, and removed all equipment from CIGRAPE-2 apartment for storage in any place. Any CIFENCE or telephone inspectors entering CIGRAPE-2 apartment will now find only redundant arrangements for effective CIGRAPE-2 telephone answering service.

3. Will keep headquarters informed. GP-1.

8 October 1970.
