Intelligence Information Cable

Country: TOES. Date of info: DSTL. Subject. Place and date acquired: B7, B4. Branch and appraisal. Field report No.

1. On or about 25 October 1965 Antonio Cuesta del Valle, chief of military operations of the Comandos L, a militant Cuban émigré organization, plans to leave Florida for Cuba with a group to conduct an operation against Cuba. The purpose of the action is to disrupt the movement of Cuban refugees from Cuba to the United States, because Cuesta feels that this emigration is an indication that the United States is moving toward a policy of coexistence with Fidel Castro Ruz.
