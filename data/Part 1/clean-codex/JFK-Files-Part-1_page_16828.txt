25 January 1962

Subject: Request for LIEUT. Project Renewal

1. In accordance with the new policy of charging all costs in connection with Staff Agents to Station Support and not to a project, there is an adjustment required in the budget figures quoted in the attached memorandum requesting project renewal.

2. The LIENVOY project should be approved for a total of $43,240; $17,151 for fiscal year 1962 and $84,038 for fiscal year 1963. Funds totaling $8,360 in fiscal year 1962 and $13,670 in fiscal year 1963 will be transferred to Mexico City Support to fund Arnold F. ARBOWT, Staff Agent.

Distribution: LIENVOY/Support; LIENVOY/Dev & Plan.
