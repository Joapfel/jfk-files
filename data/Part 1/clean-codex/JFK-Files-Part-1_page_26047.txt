1. AMCLEVE-15 met with Lugo and Rodriguez at CIRASH-4's direction, and his reports led to their arrest. Unfortunately, CIRASH-4 did not cover AMCLEVE-15, and Rodriguez is convinced he fingered them and has sworn to kill AMCLEVE-15 at the first opportunity.

2. While Caracas understands LNERGO would not have great interest in this case, is it equally true that neither LNERGO nor LNYUMA could arrest Rodriguez for leaving the country illegally and travelling on false documents? We are anxious to protect AMCLEVE-15 and wonder if JMWAVE can prevail on LNERGO or LNYUMA to accept return of Lugo and Rodriguez, followed by immediate arrest and interrogation.
