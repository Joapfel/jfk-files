October 7, 1969

Contact Report

Subject: Jimmy SABORI (P-13,850)

Date/Place of Meet: Mexico (Empix), October 7, at Chez Rene Restaurant at 1414 hours.

1. The meet was arranged by phone and S came about 14 minutes late.

Next meet: October 10 (Friday), Insurgentes Sur and Extremadura, at 1100 hours.

2. According to S, his last meet with AEDIKHARD was on August 25. A had arranged a meet with S for September 10, but A did not come to that meet. Two days later, i.e., after he did not come to the scheduled meet, A phoned S's apartment in the afternoon when he was not home and, after exchanging nice words with Josephine, told her that he was not able to keep his appointment with S two days ago and wanted to apologize for not showing up, and that he would ring up S in a day or two. Because there were no calls from A for a few days (sic), S decided to give him a ring and advise him that he had something for him, namely, S had in his possession a duplicated copy of an “Agreement” concerning the Platinum deal. As is evident from the mentioned copy of the Agreement, the same was signed and the Buyer's Agent's signature was legalized by the U.S. Consul in Mexico City on September 25.

3. Although this story lacks accuracy in terms of dates, it is substantially different from the one S gave to FERNALD on September 12, the date of their last personal meet. Despite the vagueness concerning the dates and absence of details, this story appears to be much closer to the truth than the version of September 12.

4. S gave me for copying a technical copy of the above-mentioned Agreement concerning the Platinum transaction. As is evident from the wording of the Agreement, S would have nothing to do with the Buyer or Buyer's Agent. The Agreement places S on the Seller's side or, at best, makes him middleman between the actual Seller and Buyer's Agent. P did not read the Agreement in the restaurant during the meet and made no comments with regard to the Platinum transaction as such.
