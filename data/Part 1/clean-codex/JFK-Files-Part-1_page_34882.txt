23 February 83

23 1137Z February 03. Cite Madrid 4259. Director info JMWAVE. Type AMBUD.

1. Ramiro Mon Fuertes, mayor of Tineo and provincial deputy of Asturias, was put in contact with the station by LNPURE regarding survivors’ payments for a relative who was killed in the Bay of Pigs invasion. The person killed was Daniel Fernandez Mon, and payments reportedly made to Rafael Sanchez, who was a friend but not a relative. Mon desires payments be transferred to the mother of Fernandez, whose name is Lucia Mon Fuertes and who resides at Marques de Lema 13, 4 F, Madrid 3, Espana.

2. Please advise what reply should be given.

Ramiro Mon.
