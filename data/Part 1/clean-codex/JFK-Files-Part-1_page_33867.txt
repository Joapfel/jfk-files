Mr. Brewer:

George:

FBI asks that you attempt to get from State the information which Tom Braden asks for. He asks that I bring to your attention the following two points:

a. The Director does not feel it would be advisable to launch any balloon operations until after the PA campaign has died down a bit.

b. The Fritts, through Adam Watson, have raised the question of balloon operations with Fid and want to discuss these operations during the forthcoming PU-State meetings on policy.
