5. Plan discussed with General Montiel 6 April. In view of magnitude of cache, may have to fall back on original suggestion: land STOL cargo plane at site for pick-up. Colonel Brenes thinks this could be covered adequately as a flight connected with joint maneuver “Operación Nicaragao,” but Montiel or perhaps Somoza will have to make decision. Brenes feels cache much too bulky and heavy to be transported to Managua by Guardias as Montiel had proposed.

6. Large quantity of web gear, canteens, and holsters put aside for Guardia. Brenes given about 1,000 rounds of .30-caliber machine-gun ammunition and some .30-06 machine-gun ammunition for use by his boats. Three hundred dollars subsistence passed to Ablanedo.
