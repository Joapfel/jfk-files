4. Re personal history, Aemidriff said she was raised by a family of German-speaking Ukrainian Jews. She was a member of Komsomol from ages 14 to 28. She then left Komsomol and claims she did not join the Communist Party because she “married” a nuclear physicist named Mikhail Aleksandrov, who died at age 28 in 1967. Actually, the marriage was not official since Mikhail’s mother was opposed, but everyone knew they lived together. German was Aemidriff’s main language at university. She now can read but not speak it, also reads French, and knows some English words. She got a job with the Ministry of Foreign Trade, and through influential friends made there, got travelling assignments after Mikhail’s death. She went twice to Leipzig, once to East Berlin, and once to Cologne for six days. She says she had wanted to come to Mexico since ten years ago, when she heard Mexican singers who appeared in Moscow.

5. Aemidriff said she went around with Aediehard “all the time” until his recall, but says that recall was not caused by this affair. Aedihard was recalled due to some “fiasco” which occurred about three months prior to recall. Aemidriff claims to know no details. Earlier statement that he had been recalled.
