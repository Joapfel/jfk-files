This is unclassified to the order of disclosure.

Source: News media, A 11 Dec 78, age 80.

Former citizen of a tourist destination at Miami International Airport for three years, became sole prison occupant at Isle of Palm Beach for being anti-Jewish. Retires after 73 months on 29 Dec 79 with the 290th birthday prisoner. Sources stated three years ago at University in Hawaii in again as English fluency, appears to be very pro-US. One of a series of reports from this source.

This report was developed by an Air Force representative assigned to the staff of collection.
