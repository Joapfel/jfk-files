Commissioner of Australia  
Australian Security Intelligence Organisation

May, 1966.

My Liaison Officer in Rome (Home) has told me of the tremendous assistance and co-operation that he and his section received at all times from Mr. W.K. Harvey.

I understand that Mr. Harvey has now returned to Washington and I would like to say how much I appreciate all that he has done to assist the work of my officers in Rome (home).

With kindest regards and best wishes,

Yours sincerely,  
C.E.B. Spry

The Honorable W.F. Raborn (Ruborn),  
Director,  
Central Intelligence Agency,  
Washington.
