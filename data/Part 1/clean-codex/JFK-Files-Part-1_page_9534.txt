Section 9. Mother-in-law.

Full name: first initial, last name. Birth date. Death date or date of decease. Cause. Present or last address: state, country. Date of birth. Place of birth. If born outside the United States, indicate date and place of entry. Citizenship: when acquired? Where? City, state, country. Occupation. Last employer.

Section 10. Relatives by blood, marriage, or adoption who either live abroad or are not citizens of the United States.

1. Name. Relationship. Age. Citizenship. Address: street and number, city, state, country.

2. Name. Relationship. Age. Citizenship. Address: street and number, city, state, country.

3. Name. Relationship. Age. Citizenship. Address: street and number, city, state, country.

1. Name. Relationship. Age. Citizenship. Address: street and number, city, state. Type and location of service, if known.

2. Name. Relationship. Age. Citizenship. Address: street and number, city, state. Type and location of service, if known.
