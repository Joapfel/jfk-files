Classified message.

Central Intelligence Agency.

17 April 93, 10:08.

To: Director, CIA.  
From: Senior Representative, Manila.  
Action: FE 6.  
Information: FI/OPS, FI/ADMIN, DD/P-ADMIN, CFD 3, CPB, TDS 2, PDC 2.  
Routine.  
MAN: 4484.  
Cite: SHANI.

To: Director.  
Admin.  
Re: DIR 42182 (OUT 91080).

1. Broe, William Vincent, submitted resignation, ODACID, 13 April, effective on or about 10 May.

2. Request approval of the following itinerary for self and dependents: Depart Manila for Hungary 14 May, Hungary to Yokohama 19 May. Two weeks TDY Yokohama with Carew, as he plans to depart Yokohama 30 June, prior to return to Yokohama. Depart Yokohama for the States on or about 3 June.

End of message.
