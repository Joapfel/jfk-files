Classified message.

To: Priority Rome. Director info: Madrid, WH/Miami, Buenos Aires, Bogota.

RYBAT PLMHABYSS WKSCARLET

Reference: Caracas 21422 (IN 859671).

Secret 151935Z March 73, Staff.

Cite Caracas 21429.

1. BNDD apparently dropped the ball in surveillance of Luis Clemente Posada Carriles and Camilo Vicente Cusati Cusati in Madrid, as their coverage was based on second-hand information and records. Following summary of BNDD Madrid telegram 1339 of 15 March 73: Subjects arrived in Madrid 11 March via Pan Am 254. Subsequent checks on 14 March revealed that Cusati registered at Hotel Mindano, Madrid. Posada was not registered. BNDD agents then went to the hotel but learned that Cusati and another unidentified male, presumably Posada, had just left the hotel. An airport check revealed that the subjects departed at 1145 hours on 14 March via TWA 900 for Rome. Posada held ticket No. 164401364874 and Cusati ticket No. 164401364875.
