Classified message.

To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH.5. Info: DOP, ADOP, C/CI, C/FI 2, RI/AN DUHN, C/TSD 3, C/SAS 5, VR.

228100Z. DIR CITE NEXI 7006. KAPOK PERUMEN. Reference: A DIR 82999; B MEXI 6967 (in) 64913).

21 March 93.

1. Pro data on LICRAET-1 as furnished by BROTHER 28 not being pouched.

2. BROTHER feels subject willing to remain inside for foreseeable future, is sending subject another letter for definite decision, and will indicate interested party behind AMSEVER-2 willing to establish a two hundred dollar monthly escrow account.

3. Contact will be maintained using present channel through the Embassy diplomatic pouch. Station feels subject should be trained through this channel as soon as possible in SW system to enhance communications security.

4. Subject has access to all significant plans and activities of the Ministry of Industries, is frequently consulted by Ernesto “Che” Guevara, and sits in on all high-level planning sessions at the Ministry. While known as not favoring the regime, subject is considered by Guevara and associates as a highly qualified and capable engineer who will not resist evaluating Soviet machinery as superior to available Western items if this is convinced.
