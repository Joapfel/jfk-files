Report No. TDCS-3/495,601. Date distributed: 9 December 1961. Precedence: routine. References: IN 35694.

Country: Republic of the Congo. Subject: Criticism and dissatisfaction concerning Congolese National Army (CNA) General Joseph Mobutu. Date of info: November–early December 1961. Place and date acquired: Congo, Leopoldville (7 December 1961). Appraisal: 3. Field report No. ACL 623.

This is unevaluated information. Source gradings are definitive. Appraisal of content is tentative.

Source: West European advisor (C) to high-level Congolese military officers.

1. Many officers in CNA/Headquarters are dissatisfied with General Mobutu. They complain that he fails to make decisions or take action and that secrets and plans made by CNA Headquarters seem to be known immediately by others. Source comment: Plans made to launch military operations against Katanga appear to be known by Katanga forces almost as quickly as plans are made.

Field comment: Security is unknown within the CNA. Thus, it is not surprising that “secret” information gets out.
