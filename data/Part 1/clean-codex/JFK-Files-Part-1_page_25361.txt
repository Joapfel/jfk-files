RCME 5842. IF 26718. 22 May 1953.

Nikolai LEBCOV and wife Valeria arrived in Rome on 9 May 1953 from Moscow and took lodgings at Primrose Pensione until 13 May 1953, when they departed for Genoa to proceed by sea to Mexico. LEBCOV (LEOUCY) carries service passport C 10724, issued in Moscow on 29 October 1952. His wife carries service passport G-11507, issued in Moscow 23 February 1953.

Nikolai, born in Biazan (probably Razan), 22 August 1928. Valerii, born in Moscow, 28 April 1928.

80-96339, 22 September 1952: One N. LEBCOV (LECEOV), passport No. 09492, dated 24 April 1952, Visa No. 70, dated 25 April 1952, arrived in Peshawar from Kabul on 2 May 1952. This individual is leader of an Agriculture Delegation from Moscow on the way to Karachi. This information was taken from ROK-176, dated 17 July 1952, and was acquired in Karachi, Pakistan. The purpose of this trip was to attend the Asian Regional Conference on Epidemiology, which was held in Karachi on 3 May 1952.
