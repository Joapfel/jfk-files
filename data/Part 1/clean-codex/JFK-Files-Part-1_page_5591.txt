23:325Z October 1969. Cite Port au Prince 6528.

Director info: DO Base New York. Akule, Domops, Evmend.

Ref: DO Base New York 7875 (Jd 70936).

1. Only possible indication of REF plotting in recent station reporting was Delphic comment by EVLEROM/18 to E-2 on 21 October that while men like Desinor, Rameau, and estimated Frederic Desvapieaux maneuver to replace Duvalier, it is the armed forces which has the last word in such matters. Also that there are times, in politics, when E/18 must be reticent despite his confidence in E/2's intentions; also, when E/2 least expected it, E/18 would tell him everything that was going on. Remarks were made in response to E/2's question about the impact of a recent Nouveau Monde article by Antoine Merard, which was being interpreted by E/18 as a Duvalier plant foreshadowing a purge of more corrupt elements in the government. (Reaction of E-5, 18, and 16 to article being disseminated.) Thus E/18's remarks may simply have referred to this sort of purge instead of the broader plot noted in the reference.

2. Frayne has an appointment with IDEM B, reference 6938, 27 October 1970.
