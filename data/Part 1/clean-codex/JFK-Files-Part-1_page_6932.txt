10. During all the time that the pick-ups were occurring, Carmen Carrasco Molino (GARRASCO Molino), who acted as intermediary for Helen Gobell (GOBELL) with Lic. Luis Sanchez Ponton (GANCHEZ Ponton), was busy at the Maltz residence making attempts to gather funds with which to force the Mexican authorities to rescind the pick-up orders and to help the American Communists as a group in efforts to solidify their status in Mexico.

11. Apparently, the papers of the people who were deported as well as those who were picked up were correct so far as technicalities were concerned.

Willard C. Curtis
