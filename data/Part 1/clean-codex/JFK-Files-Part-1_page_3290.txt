Dispatch. To: Deputy Chief, WH/SA. From: Chief of Station, JMWAVE 12. Subject: Operational/TYPIC/AMOT—Dr. Leopoldo Araujo Bernal (201-).

Action required: See paragraph 5. Reference: UFGA-18378, 4 November.

1. Forwarded herewith are Parts I and II of an AMOT Defection-in-Place Form on Dr. Leopoldo Araujo Bernal, President of the Cuban National Medical Association. Part I contains biographic data on the source, Alberto Antonio del Castillo Guerra (A 12-481-172), a friend of the subject's since 1949; Part II provides background data and an assessment of the subject.

2. Attached to the Defection Form are AMOT traces on the subject covering the period September–December 1964; AMOT traces dating prior to September 1964 were included with the report forwarded in the referenced dispatch.

3. No identifiable traces were found in JMWAVE on the source, Alberto Antonio del Castillo Guerra. There is voluminous information in Station files on the subject, all available to Headquarters.

4. In view of the source's assessment of the subject as a Communist sympathizer and a follower of the regime, which is corroborated by other information available on the subject, we plan to take no further action on this case at the present time and are forwarding the attached form for the record only.

5. It is requested that the subject be assigned a 201 number and that the individuals appearing in the attachment be carded as marked to the subject's 201 file.

Andrew K. Reuteman.

Attachment: Defection Form (Parts I & II), h/w.

Distribution: 3—DC/WH/SA, with 2 copies attached.

Date typed: 10 Mar. 65. Date dispatched: Feb. 12, 1965. Dispatch symbol and number: UFGA-20354. Headquarters file number: 201-.
