4. On 11 Jan 62, Julian Lopez Diaz, Cuban, arrived Mexico (MEXI) from Havana (HAVANG) without Mexico visa en route to Chile. Lopez told L-9 and Benigno Castellar, who met him at the airport, that he was carrying aggregate inland cash. He said he planned to carry propaganda but feared it would be confiscated in Mexico. Lopez scheduled to leave for Santiago (SAGO) via Canadian Pacific the night of 11 Jan 62.
