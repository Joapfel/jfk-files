Dispatch. To: Chief, WH. Info: WH/C. From: Chief of Station, JMWAVE. Subject: DYVOUR/JMDEVIL/AMICE-31 Production.

Attached is AMICE-31's report on the meeting of the Eighth Continental Council of the Confederación (Confederacion) Latinoamericana de Sindicatos Cristianos (CLASC, Latin American Confederation of Christian Trade Unions), held in Medellín (Medellin), Colombia, from 5 to 14 January 1966.

Since the Station has not received copies of any disseminations that may have been made from information cabled during the meeting at Medellín, no attempt has been made to prepare portions of AMICE-31's report for dissemination. If any of the report is disseminated at Headquarters, the Station would appreciate receiving copies, along with copies of any reports that may already have been disseminated on the meeting at Medellín, and any future disseminations on the CLASC.

Also attached, under separate cover, is a copy of a report by Keith G. BONGIRNO which includes a list of AMICE-31's achievements at the meeting and his impressions of and attitudes toward certain delegates and issues.

Philip G. ELMARD.

Distribution: WH w/atts h/w, and u/s/c — ATTO RODIN; WH/C w/atts h/w, and u/s/c.
