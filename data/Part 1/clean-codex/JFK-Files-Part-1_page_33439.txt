4. He said the Cuban government and Cuban Embassy in Santiago were not surprised, however, that an attempt against the Allende government was made because they had long perceived that Allende, while a good Marxist, lacked sufficient aggressiveness toward his enemies.

5. He said this Cuban Embassy in Santiago had plans prepared.
