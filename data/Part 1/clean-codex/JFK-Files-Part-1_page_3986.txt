2 June 1965

Memorandum for: Chief, VI Division

Subject: Interview between Mr. George Cabot Lodge and Manuel Artime Buesa (Buea)

1. Pursuant to your conversation with Manuel Artime on 27 May 1965, the latter has expressed a desire to meet with Mr. George Cabot Lodge in order to further discuss practical means of implementing a civic action program in Nicaragua.

2. Artime's current plans and schedule are as follows: 8–4 June in Miami, Florida; 4–12 June in Nicaragua; 13–14 June in Panama; 14–possible trips to Colombia and Guatemala.

3. Artime emphasized, however, that he could review this schedule if need be in order to meet with Mr. Lodge in Cambridge, Mass., or any other location at Mr. Lodge's convenience.

(Signed) John L. Hart

John L. Hart  
DCWHD/C
