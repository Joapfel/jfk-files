Dispatch.

To: Chief, WI Division (Wl Division).  
From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
Subject: LIFLAT (LIFFAT) Project Renewal Request.  
Action required: As noted.

References:

1. Forwarded herewith and under separate cover for Headquarters processing is the LIFLAT Project Renewal Request covering FY 1962. This consists of a comprehensive review of Project LIFLAT for the period July 1960 through June 1961, and a Project Outline divided into Parts I and II.

2. It is noted that the total of funds requested for the coming project year exceeds that granted for the past year, but it is felt that the additional funds are necessary, and realistically reflect the needs of the operation.

3. It is requested that Project LIFLAT be renewed for the period July 1961 through June 1962 in the amount of $111,673.

William C. Curtis  
9 June 1961

Attachment: Project Renewal h/w and u/s/c.

Distribution: Headquarters w/ cys att h/w and u/s/c; Miles.
