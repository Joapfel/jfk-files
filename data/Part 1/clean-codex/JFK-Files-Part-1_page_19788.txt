3 July 1962

Memorandum for: Chief, Administrative & Training Staff

Subject: O'Connell, James P. — Personal Matter (Last Will and Testament)

On June 28, 1962, Mr. O'Connell communicated with the undersigned and asked that the following information be properly recorded:

He advised that on 28 June 1962, both he and his spouse had executed Last Wills and Testaments. He is maintaining in his possession a copy of the mutual Wills. The originals are in the custody of Joseph Keating, Esq., 150 Little Falls Road, Falls Church, Virginia. Mr. Keating is the attorney who drew up the Testaments.

David E. Hanlon  
Executive Officer
