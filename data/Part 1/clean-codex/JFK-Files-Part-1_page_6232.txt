TYPIC AMSPELL AMBARB.

References: A. HZPA 1482C; B. HZPA 14984; C. HZPA 14737.

31 May 65.

1. From discussions with AMSPELL chief AHINT-2, believe AMSPELL may have encouraged ref A action by AMBARB-43 but not in exaggerated form publicized by AMBARB-43. AHINT-2 disclaimed any knowledge of ref B action, speculated AMBARB-43 trying identify AMSPELL with aims of Panamanian students. WAVE opinion AMBARB-43 ref B action probably completely own initiative as personal pique against KUBARK. AMBARB-43 was possibly awarded deteriorating AMSPELL-WAVE relationship and this could have contributed sour attitude.

2. FYI, for reasons not directly related to AMBARB-43 situation, WAVE made drastic cut in AMSPELL subsidy effective 1 April and told AHINT-2 AMSPELL would have to cease anti-KUBARK activity. After receipt of ref B attachment, AHINT-2 was told AMBARB-43 must be kept under control or removed from Panama.

3. Pending outcome of current discussions with AHINT-2 regarding future.
