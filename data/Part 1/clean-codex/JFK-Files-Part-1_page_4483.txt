292302Z. DIR INFO MANA CITE WAVE 6214. KATOKI/DYVOUR PBRUMEN AMCLATTER/1.

Reference: A, WAVE 5431 (IN 43038); B, DIR 43562; C, WAVE 5849 (IN 54607).

1. AMCLATTER/1 met Dr. Julio (Garceran) del Vall Souza after the latter's return from Washington on 22 September 1965. Garceran reported he invited Blanca Rosa Mayorgas and husband Col. John Kelly to dinner on 20 September 1965. Col. Kelly did not attend dinner, his wife explaining to Garceran that her husband was a heavy drinker and had exceeded his quota that day. From comments made by Mayorgas, Garceran infers Col. Kelly retired but “still works Pentagon in civilian status in allocation of troops in Viet Nam.”

2. Mayorgas told Garceran she had not yet traveled to Nicaragua but still plans doing so “around first part of 66.” Garceran did not further the matter further in order not to arouse undue suspicion concerning his interest in her planned trip.

3. Garceran further learned that Mayorgas was not Cuban but a Nicaraguan citizen. We had known her in Cuba when she was studying.
