4. Source of paragraphs 5 and 6: A foreign diplomat with a wide variety of contacts; his reliability has not been established.

5. During the week of 4 October Luis Martinez Monocal, Cuban first secretary, said that the embassy was aware that Cuban exile extremist Orlando Bosch was in Venezuela. The embassy, he said, was attempting to investigate Bosch's activities in Venezuela and to determine whether he was still in the country. Martinez said that the embassy had received unconfirmed reports that Bosch was moved out of the Caracas area and was then in the Venezuelan interior.

(Field comment for an earlier report on the presence of Bosch in Venezuela, see HVC-7498, TDFIRDB-315/09814-76, of 30 September, from a usually reliable source.)

6. In addition to and in connection with investigations into the possible presence of Bosch in Venezuela, Cuban Embassy officials are concerned about the machine-gunning of the Cuban Embassy in Caracas by unknown assailants on the early morning of 6 October.

7. Field dissemination: Embassy, FBI at Caracas.
