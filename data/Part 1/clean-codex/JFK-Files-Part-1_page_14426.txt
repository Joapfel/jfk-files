TYPIC AMAUTO.

Ref: Director 035471.

1. Roffinoli contacted subject under suitable pretext evening 22 May. Subject living at reference address with one Jorge Borges, a Cuban refugee.

2. Neither subject nor Borges understand English, speaking only a few English phrases. Subject solved the language barrier by asking a Spanish-English-speaking neighbor to interpret. While subject was calling the neighbor, roommate Borges managed to tell Roffinoli that subject was suffering from a severe nervous condition and had undergone electroshock treatment approximately a year ago. Roffinoli noticed some nervousness on the part of subject, but thought it was probably due to initial uncertainty about why Roffinoli was contacting him.

3. Subject is a white Cuban, approximately 5'6" tall, weighs 130–135 pounds. Has a slender build, although a small paunch. Face oval-shaped, complexion slightly blemished. Aquiline nose. Has black hair, cut at average length. No sideburns, etc. Subject has a very boyish appearance and could easily pass as a person 21–22 years old, although he claims to be 29. No overt homosexual tendencies noted.
