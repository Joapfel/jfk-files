2. Robert Leger: Haitian Communist, secretary for Louis Dejoie and associate of Robert Deschamps (WAVE 6104, 16 December 1964; EVLANE/4 contact report, 3 January 1967 (201-727690)). LNERGO traces indicate he was born 16 February 1930, Port-au-Prince, Haiti. In October 1961, he resided at 5840 SW 9th Court, Miami, and was a traveling salesman for Westrade, Inc. In January 1962, he was executive vice president of Westrade, Inc. Interviewed by LNERGO, he stated he had been contacted by Haitian exiles but did not plan to become involved with them. LNERGO closed case 13 July 1962. His sister, Lisa Leger, also worked for Westrade.

3. Louis Dejoie (201-248483): former Haitian senator, born 23 February 1896, and presidential candidate. Since the early 1960s, an exile leader plotting against Duvalier, he traveled extensively. He arrived in Miami in July 1964 and became associated with EVLUCID-1. He was a business associate of Robert Deschamps (WAVE 6541, 5 8792, 8916 (1964), UFGA 17300, 21374, 22527). LNERGO traces indicate he was reputed to be involved in Haitian activities in October 1958 and resided at 16 E. 96th Street, New York City.
