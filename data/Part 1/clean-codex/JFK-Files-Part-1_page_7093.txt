Valentin Sergeyovich Loginov

Date of birth: 23 December 1927  
Position: Third Secretary in the Consular Section of the Soviet Embassy, Mexico D.F.  
Residence: Ignacio Esteva 18-1, Colonia Tacubaya  
Automobile: 1965 Chevrolet Impala, license 19 DA  
Languages: Fluent Spanish, fair English

Career:

1955: Joined the KGB.  
1955–1957: Attended the KGB training school.  
1957–August 1960: Officer in KGB Headquarters, Moscow.  
September 1960–September 1963: Attaché in Havana, Cuba.  
December 1961: Traveled to Panama and Ecuador with a Soviet soccer team. On this trip he carried a large sum of money for the Communist Parties of Panama and Ecuador.  
17 May 1962: Transited Mexico en route to the USSR for leave.  
9 September 1963: Left Cuba to return to the USSR.  
September 1963–July 1955: Officer in KGB Headquarters, Moscow.  
3 August 1955: Arrived in Mexico.

In Mexico, Loginov (LOGNOV) replaced Oleg M. Nechporenko (NECHPORENGKO), another KGB officer, and took over all of Nechporenko’s intelligence agents and duties. In January 1956 he attempted to cultivate and recruit a chauffeur at the American Embassy in Mexico. Loginov (LOGNOV) is an unusually active and capable intelligence officer. Since he has almost no overt duties, he is able to spend about 95% of his time on intelligence activity. We have known that he is a KGB officer since 1953. His immediate KGB chief in Mexico is Rafael A. Yatskov.
