Classified message. Date: 20 Jun 60. Subject: Civilian. Director. 1 Jul 2139Z 60. Mexico City. IN 19430.

DIR CITE MEXI 5813  
RYBAT LIENVOY  
REF DIR 35367 (OUT 89225)

1. Re para 4, LIENVOY will cover five Soviet and two Czech lines with tapes processed by ZINSHEISTER and LIBRIFORM. LIENVOY monitors will extract from Spanish for resume what is considered of interest LITENSOR. LIELEGANT told we will inform him of pertinent info obtained (ostensibly from HOS processing) of Soviet and Czech text for passing LITENSOR. LIFLAT will cover Polish and other current targets. For security reasons Czech lines have not been covered by LIFLAT for past six months.

2. Re para 5: (a) No current problem re exploitation leads since LIELEGANT has evinced no interest this matter. (b) Personnel Unit C terminated and base closed. View LIENVOY lack language and exploitation capability and our ability control what is passed (ostensibly from HQS), station sees no immediate problems. Will pouch details June LIENVOY monthly activities report.

3. This development foreseen by station since LIENVOY began; no intention cut down on LIFLAT (independent) ops and have sufficient work for both.

END OF MESSAGE
