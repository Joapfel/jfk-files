Paris Station personnel known to be in Paris.

Peter J. KIMCHAM — alias not known. Some meetings were held in Kimcham’s home in 1962/63. It is not known if other staff personnel, other than ONKIOH, were met by AMLASH/1.

Jul K. STOCKBLOOD — alias not known for sure, but believed to have used “Roberta.” AMLASH/1 knew STOCKBLOOD as an embassy officer and called him there.

Peter M. CLOADERM — known as “Disk.” True name not used unless told to AMLASH/1 by UNSHAFF/5 or UNSHAFF/7.

Milton P. RODANOR — met AMLASH/1 only once, when transporting UNSHAFF/5 to the hospital. Known to all as “Dr. ......” (cannot remember alias used).

Dr. Lipsich — cleared doctor at the American Hospital, Paris. AMLASH/1 met him briefly when admitting UNSHAFF/5 to the hospital.

Wallace A. CROVETZ — not in Paris in 1962 during explosive training. Details not known.
