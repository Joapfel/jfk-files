4 December 1959

Exhibit for: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation. Attention: L. O. Jones.

Subject: New Rice from Michigan. Subject II. Current file master 6-1030.

1. It has come to our attention through a sensitive source that Benjamin F. Acton, who is residing in Moscow, U.S.S.R., is known to the following individuals: Benjamin F. Acton and wife, “Proklis” Foustov.

2. It is believed that Benjamin Foustov is connected with the Colored Man Manufacturing Company, Ltd., located at 170 Washington Street North, Boston, Massachusetts. NO/CE trace reply dated 17 December 1959.

3. This office has no information identifiable with either Benjamin or “Fredie” Foustov. We would appreciate being furnished any pertinent information on the Foustovs that may be contained in your files.

For the Depot Office (F.O.),

James Abdleton

G-CL-3/757,704
