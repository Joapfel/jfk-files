Cite Caracas 29876. 1316182, February 1976, Staff. To: Immediate Santiago; Managua. Info: Immediate Director; LA/Miami; Buenos Aires. VWLOFTY MHSPIKE.

References: A. Caracas 29063 (L 424723) (L 825993). B. Santiago 33711.

1. USSS headquarters has forwarded to Caracas advance team notice that USSS Miami representative received a report on 12 February 1976 from an “extremely reliable confidential informant” that an attempt was to be made on Dr. Kissinger’s life while he was in Venezuela. The informant was described as “very close” to Orlando Bosch Avila (USSS later identified the source as Ricardo Mcorales Navarrete). Bosch allegedly arrived in Caracas from Managua two days ago.

2. Request action addressees check of travel control for possible confirmation of Bosch’s travel. Consider more likely Bosch would be traveling with a Dominican passport 286768 in the name Pedro Antonio Pena.

3. FYI: WKSCARLET-3, who planned to contact Bosch in Santiago next week, said he sent a telegram to Santiago accommodation.
