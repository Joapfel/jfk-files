Director info WH/Miami.

TYPIC AMAUTO.

Ref: MADRID 1261 C-096012.

8 June 1970.

1. Following is summary of two-and-one-half-hour meeting between SAMKO/AMAUTO-1.

A. SAMKO picked up by AMAUTO-1 in chauffeur-driven Embassy Mercedes.

B. When discussing future plans, AMAUTO-1 said will return Cuba to fight for just cause. That he will probably die fighting for Cuba, which is young, aggressive country, but not press this theme nor speak adversely of United States. SAMKO did not challenge statement, but added that hopes someday AMAUTO-1 assigned to United Nations, thus will have opportunity visit SAMKO in Florida. AMAUTO-1 visibly delighted with possibility.

C. They exchanged addresses and AMAUTO-1 urged SAMKO write. SAMKO had printed out his name, address.

18 June 1970. 9 June 1970.
