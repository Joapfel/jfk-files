DYVOUR ANRGP

1. AMCRIF-1 (IDEN) contacted by Dulce de la Maza Rodriguez, niece of PRSC leader Antonio Rosario, who traveled through JMWAVE area. De la Maza hand-carried letter to AMCRIF-1 dated 4 April from Francisco Cordova, PRSC secretary of international affairs, which requested AMCRIF-1 arrange for purchase and shipment of two or three used cars to be used for PRSC presidential election activities in DOMREP. Letter was accompanied with $1,467 for purchase and shipment costs of cars.

2. AMCRIF-1 telephoned Rosario evening 11 April to confirm letter and agreed to provide aid in procuring cars. Rosario informed AMCRIF-1 that he expected to receive nomination as presidential candidate of PRSC at party assembly on 17 April and stated he would select Cesar Estrella as his running mate. Rosario also indicated he expected some electoral support from an unspecified number of APJ members.
