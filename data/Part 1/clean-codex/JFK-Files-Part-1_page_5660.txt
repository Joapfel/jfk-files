United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, Domestic Contact Service  
Attention: International Division (GAlyea)  
From: Chief, Philadelphia Office

PHI-457-68  
Date: 24 December 1968

Subject: Letters from Cleland Charles, Haiti, to U.S. Officials and Businessmen

Reference: Phil 00-3-321/36501-68, dated 17 December 1968

1. Attached for your information are letters from Cleland Joseph Charles addressed to Vice President Robert H. Humphrey and President-elect Richard Nixon. These letters were received in person from Charles, in Haiti, by Donald Z. Rowe, source of reference, and brought to the U.S. by the latter. The letters have not been delivered as of this date. Rowe plans to ask the New Jersey Third District Member of the U.S. House of Representatives to deliver them.

2. In addition to the correspondence referred to above, Rowe also received letters of introduction from Charles to:
