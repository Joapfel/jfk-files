Munson phoned from N.Y. (see memo). He had a long phone conversation with Shub that morning, and told him in person in a bar on Nov. 21. They both agreed to see the book.

Nov. 20

Sims saw Munson at the hotel. Munson expects to see Shub tomorrow. Sims is going to the book.

Dec. 14

Letter from Shub: “My collaborator is one of the top lay-out men on Life....” Munson will fill in on the details. Phoned Munson: will see him tomorrow morning. He said he was “resumed by developments last week.” And also things are wrong. It seemed he should be moving soon.

Jan. 8, 1951

Munson phoned Sims: “Shub should be moving soon.”

Jan. 15

Memo to LST on status of photo-book, by request. Shub phoned: text of book is completed. See above memo.

Jan. 16

Phoned Munson. He will fly later today to see Bob Winston, who will return to him several papers on behalf of Bob Murkistantam.
