Although Ruiz's health has been described as generally good, he suffers from a back ailment which resulted from a fall in 1955 and which has been diagnosed as incurable. Also he suffers from a liver ailment for which he takes medicine.

Ruiz is scheduled to return to Cuba in mid-1970, where he expects to be with MININT, rather than MINREX or MINCEX.
