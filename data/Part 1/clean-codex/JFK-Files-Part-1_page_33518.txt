Date: 13 April 1974. Time: 12:56:08.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers.

Outgoing message. Departmental information indicator. Staff. CO. WHS. Date-time group: 192044Z. Info: File 19/13/N, EUR 8,555. City: Director. Message reference number: 540262.

To: Hamburg. Info: Santiago, Bonn. Y. FUAMES PSYCH CASPECIAL Y HR.

Ref: RANGELAG 24759 (IN 225031).

1. At this point do not believe OKHERALD attempt improve Chilean image in German media stands much chance of success. Prefer let denigration campaign run its course. In brief we would be willing supply special brief designed to influence carefully selected individuals who are open to persuasion and whose views would carry real weight, but we do not feel OKHERALD should engage in general propaganda support of Chilean regime since latter approach both ineffectual and might well backfire.
