Classified message.

Secret 22 OCT 68. CITE MEXICO CITY 7743.

GUATEMALA CITY INFO DIRECTOR

REDCOAT MHIGLOO

22 OCT 68 IN 18668

1. At home of Jorge Alberto Cardoza Aguilar evening 41 18 OCT 68, LIREBEL-2 received the following information directly from Cardoza.

2. Jose Luis Balcarcel Ordonez and Jorge Alberto Cardoza Aguilar are Guatemalan Communists in Mexico who have direct contact with Soviets in Mexico to handle travel of Guatemalans to the Soviet Union and for receipt of money from Soviets. Their contact is Nikolay Sergeyevich Leonov (Lionov) (201-132139), press attaché who lives at Mazatlan 206 (Press Office).

3. If contact is required for Guatemalan travellers to the Soviet Union, Balcarcel or Cardoza take travellers near the Press Office in a car and then give them an identification card to present to Leonov. They then visit the office alone. If contact is for receipt of money or exchange of documents, Balcarcel or Cardoza go directly to the Press Office. Lately, wife of Balcarcel has had most contact with Leonov as an extra precautionary measure.

4. According to Cardoza, Leonov is to be transferred soon.

Leonov. 201-132139. Lionov (Leonov).
