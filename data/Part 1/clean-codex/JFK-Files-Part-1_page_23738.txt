WAVE not desirous of having AMSPICE/1 contact IDEN's wife at this time, in order to attempt to salvage the AMSPICE operation, if possible, from damages that will in all probability accrue as a result of IDEN's arrest. As stated in paragraph IIW of UFGA 16565, dated 29 June 64, AMSPICE/1, prior to her initial contact with WAVE, confessed her CIS mission to IDEN and her intention to disclose everything to ODYOKE authorities. IDEN allegedly counseled her to do the latter. As will be recalled, part of AMSPICE/1's mission was to penetrate IDEN and his organization.

6. WAVE believes in spite of the foregoing that the AMSPICE operation has a fifty-fifty chance of being salvaged. Since AMSPICE/1 is a CIS agent, WAVE does not feel it likely that CIS will have surfaced her name during IDEN's interrogation, as it is not logical that CIS would discuss their agents with one of their agents' victims. If A/I was not mentioned by CIS, it is possible IDEN may have seen fit not to bring her name up. However, since IDEN's current situation is one of life or death, it is very possible IDEN may have volunteered the fact that A/I betrayed CIS in order to gain one more point in his favor.

7. In order to save the A/I operation, WAVE has instructed A/I as follows:

A. She is to refrain from mixing in the IDEN affair.

B. If confronted by CIS with IDEN's admission, she is to deny she told IDEN her mission and suggest her husband may have been the culprit.

C. She is also to claim she did not inform ODYOKE authorities.
