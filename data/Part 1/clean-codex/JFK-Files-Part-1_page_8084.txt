Ref: Panama City 38665 (L-1).

1. 470th MIGP succeeds in holding ops meeting with Source 301 night of 19 October. In course of that debriefing, 301 reported following:

A. He was sent alone to Miami by GN S-2, Lt. Col. Noriega, on 14 October, returned to Panama night 15 October. His mission was to contact Salvador Aldereguia to obtain information on Cuban exile activity in connection with sabotage of Cubana airliner off Barrados (Barbados).

B. 301 reached Aldereguia through Cuban exile female named Connie Ochga, who functions as mistress to both 301 and Aldereguia. Aldereguia named Cuban exiles Aldo Vera and (ENU) Mayo, a veterinarian, as having had a role in sabotage operation, but said principal figure was Orlando Bosch. Aldereguia said that Bosch had gone to Nicaragua where Anastasio Sanchez gave him unspecified amount of money to mount operation. Bosch proceeds secret.
