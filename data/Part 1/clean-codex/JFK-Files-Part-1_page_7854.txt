Ref: Mexico City 3785 (PH 11829).

2. DEC 67:18308.

1. LIENVOY coverage of the Soviet Embassy produced several items in the last few months showing that an employee of the Mexican Foreign Ministry, later identified as Carlos Sandoval y Nucino, who took care of Mexican visas for communist nationals, was on very friendly personal terms with Soviet diplomats (DIPLOMAIS) Pavel A. Yatskov and Valentin S. Loginov, both KGB. Although Sandoval must have dealt with Cubans, there is no indication he developed friendship with local Cuban officials.

2. The station was becoming interested in Sandoval as a possible asset against local Soviets when Sandoval, per LIENVOY, informed Loginov on 24 November he was being transferred to the Mexican Embassy, Havana. LIEMBRACE surveillance on LIRING-2 spotted the latter at a church ceremony (he stood outside) and reception at Sandoval's wedding to Lucia Blakeley (IS DEC TO LUCIA BLAKELEY). The Soviets, however, did not appear in attendance. Sandoval left for Havana with his wife on 11 December.

3. The station was somewhat puzzled at the sudden appearance of LIRING-2 and absence of Soviets at Sandoval's wedding. LIFEAT coverage.
