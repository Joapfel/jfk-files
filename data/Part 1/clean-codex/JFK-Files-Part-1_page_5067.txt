Memorandum

To: POL/R, Santiago

From: LEGAT Robert W. Scherrer, Buenos Aires

Subject: Orlando Bosch Avila; Letelier Assassination

Date: 5/27/77

The Chilean Department of Investigations advised that the International Police located a record of entry for Pedro Antonio Pena Rodriguez into Chile on 8/30/75 from Colombia as a tourist. Pena Rodriguez was carrying Dominican Passport No. OS6768, which disclosed his date of birth as 1/8/29. Pena Rodriguez was admitted to Chile as a tourist. No record for Pena Rodriguez's departure from Chile could be located.

The Chilean Department of Investigations advised that the Chilean Passport Office located a record for Hector Emilio Ramon Davanzo Cintolesi, who was born on 3/15/27 in Santiago, the son of Hector Davanzo and Amalia Enriqueta Cintolesi. This individual has Chilean Santiago Cedula de Identidad No. 2,357.4 and Chilean Passport No. 27,671, the latter being issued on 10/22/75. Chilean Passport Office records list Davanzo Cintolesi's address in Santiago as Calle Miguel Claro No. 1129.

Attached is a copy of a photograph of Davanzo Cintolesi, which was copied from his Cedula de Identidad.

The Chilean Department of Investigations advised that the International Police reported that Davanzo Cintolesi departed Santiago on 12/28/75 en route to Panama. According to International Police Records, Davanzo Cintolesi was carrying Chilean Passport No. 26,583 when he departed Chile on 4/26/75.
