ER-3-1369

29 July 1952

Memorandum for: Chief, International Organizations  
From: Deputy Director Central Intelligence

Attached is a memorandum which was handed me by Spencer Phönix covering a confidential report made by two NCFE people with regard to NCFE's action in connection with refugees. I have not read this in full and would like your recommendation and analysis so as to spare me this if possible.
