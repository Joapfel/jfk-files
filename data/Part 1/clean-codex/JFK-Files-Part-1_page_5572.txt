231608Z May 79, CITE Port au Prince 6879. Director.

SHAT IM0792S?

1. According to morning press 8 May, Legislative Chamber passed decree 7 May which stripped the following persons of Haitian citizenship and confiscated their goods and chattels: Clemard Joseph Charles, Clemenceau Prosper, Alphone Lakens, and Kešner Blain. The stated reason for the decree was that these persons were accomplices of rebel coastguardsmen. This decree was essentially the same as one passed 27 April according identical treatment to coastguardsmen.

2. Palace also announced that it would not proceed with extradition proceedings against coastguardsmen but would judge them in absentia. This action followed a note received by Embassy on 7 May withdrawing the earlier request for extradition.

3. Net effect of these actions lends credence to the impression that Haitian government is prepared to act in an outwardly legal way in dealing with rebellion. On the one hand, removal of citizenship and confiscation of property leaves families destitute, which probably appears an object lesson to others considering rebellion.
