11. Lubomir BLAZIK (P-1730, 201-206043), former Czech Second Secretary and Consul in Mexico.

On 28 December 1965, P.A. YATSKOV left a message for BLAZIK that Subject’s wife was sick and could not attend the dinner to which the BLAZIKs had invited her.

12. Rosa Martha BRACHO Sanchez (P-12038), left in November 1965 on a scholarship to study ballet in the USSR.

On 21 and 22 September 1966, Rosa’s mother (fnu) was in contact with Subject in order to talk to him about visa problems involving her two daughters, who were both studying dance in the USSR. The name of the other daughter is unknown.

13. EX Erika KAMMERMANN (CAMERMAN) (P-12843), said to be a reporter with The News, but this seems unlikely; no other traces.

On 21 February 1966, KAMMERMANN told Subject that she was going out of town the next day, but when she got back she wanted to invite Subject and his wife to her house. Subject said he had no word on her papers yet. On 28 February 1966, she said she had been in Cozumel. Subject said he still had no word on her papers.
