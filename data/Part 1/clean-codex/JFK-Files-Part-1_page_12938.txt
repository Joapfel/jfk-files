1 September 1971: Subject notified that he was to accompany Venezuelan Minister of Interior Lorenzo (LoreAZo) Fernandez on a 4- to 6-week trip to Europe. Trip speculated to be Fernandez's touching base for political reasons prior to announcing his presidential candidacy upon return.

28 November 1971: OA requested for use as penetration of DISIP.

29 November 1971: Subject polygraphed to determine if DISIP and/or CIFENCE-1 had knowledge of Subject's connections with Station; if he ever requested to work against U.S.; if he had contacted either of his brothers since May 1968; and if he was involved with illegal arms smuggling. Result: answered truthfully, with no indication of deception.

1 December 1971: Traveled to Miami by his employer to purchase 25 nine-millimeter pistols for GOV use. Unable to refuse or evade assignment.

17 July 1972: OA granted.

April 1972: Subject placed in charge of another CIRASH division with more responsibility and importance. Initiated extensive investigation of Prensa Latina and its Caracas Director and the kidnapping of Venezuelan industrialist Carlos BENIM Dominguez.

5 February 1973: To Rome to aid in audio operation.

12 February 1973: Name mentioned in unspecified connection with several members of the UIR who were involved with narcotics.
