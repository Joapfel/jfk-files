31-07-1957

Reorder from: Secretary, (SOS Board)

Subject: Recommendation for Promotion — Newton, Scott Miller

1. This is a recommendation for promotion for Mr. Miller from Grade GS-J5 to GS-Ia.

2. The career of this officer has been notably marked throughout with relatively important responsibilities. His years, intense drive, initiative, self-assurance, capacity for work, natural talent for detail work, recognition of objectives and problems, organization of the work to be done, and ability to interact with and supervise the effort have long been evident to those for and with whom he has worked. Years of service, during which he has steadily matured, have resulted in a recognition by supervisors and subordinates, and fellow workers alike, of his unusually wide experience, proven soundness of judgment, orational, administrative and executive talent, coupled with powers of expression both oral and written and demonstrated capacity for work. He is unquestionably a very extrovertable officer.

3.
