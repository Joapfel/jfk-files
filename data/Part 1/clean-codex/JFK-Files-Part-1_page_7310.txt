10.02 Walking.

10.02.01 Is he the kind of person who does a great deal of walking, walks an average amount, or never walks if he can help it?

10.02.02 When he walks, his pace is fast, average, or slow?

10.02.03 When you walk with him, he most often keeps pace with you or forces you to keep up with him?

10.02.04 When he walks, do his feet swing to the right and left (outwards), maintain a fairly parallel motion, or swing inward?

10.02.05 Does he walk erect with head up, erect with head down, bent forward but eyes up, bent forward with eyes down, or in a very stooped manner?

10.02.06 When he is walking, he looks around a great deal, an average amount, or never?

10.02.07 When he greets someone he knows on the street, his face lights up, shows an average or appropriate expression, or rarely changes expression?
