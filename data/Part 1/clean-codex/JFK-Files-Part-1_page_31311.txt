Section VIII. Agency Employment History.

Inclusive dates: May 1957–May 1965. Location: Headquarters. Directorate office or division, branch: DDP/SR. Title of job: Chief, Research Branch, C.I. Group, SR. Div. Grades held: GS-12, GS-13.

Description of duties:
- Planned and implemented SR Division research program on secret intelligence.
- Supervised work of section (later branch), which at maximum strength included 17 analysts and clerks.
- Edited publications.
- Directed dissemination of publications to Agency and U.S. intelligence community customers.
- Conducted lectures and briefings for U.S. and foreign liaison personnel.

Inclusive dates: September 1957–May 1959. Location: Headquarters. Directorate office or division, branch: DDP/FI/D. Title of job: Biographic Officer. Grades held: GS-11, 12.

Description of duties:
- Conducted research and prepared reports.
- Conducted external liaison.
- Helped develop news programs.
- Supervised personnel.

Inclusive dates: April 1955–September 1957. Location: Headquarters. Directorate office or division, branch: DDP/WE-1. Title of job: Area Operations Officer. Grade held: GS-11.

Description of duties:
- Supported activities of Stockholm Station.
