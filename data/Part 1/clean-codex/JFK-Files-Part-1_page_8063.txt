From: Action:

Priority. Direct cite WAVE 675P. No NIACT. TYPIC.

1. Follow-up initial information regarding Max Lesnik’s role in surfacing the Cuban delegation’s UN press release concerning the text of the 12 October Cuban government note to the U.S. government. Primary source is AMPLINK 1. Some confirmatory data from AMOT.

2. Morning 19 October. Max Lesnik Mendez (MEMENDEZ), 201-209258, was in the office of the Segment Frente Nacional de Escambray (SFNE) when mail was received. Included was a plain Manila (FAHILA) envelope, sent by airmail, with no sender’s address, addressed only to the street address, with the SFNE name not appearing.

3. Lesnik began trying to determine if other exile groups or American press elements had received copies of the release. Checks with USIA and API were negative. Lesnik’s query was passed to Jorge Volsky of USIA, who has now changed his stringer allegiance from the New York Times to the New York Herald.

201-209258. 21 October 1987.
