Dispatch. To: Chief, JMWAVE. Info: Chief, Soviet Eee Division. From: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Subject: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires.

RYBAT REDTOP AERONAUT AETARGEU. Letter for AMSTALK-1's wife.

Action required—references.

1. Forwarded under cover, please find a letter written by AMSTALK-1 for delivery to his wife. AMSTALK-1 tells us (we certainly agree) that he would prefer for JMWAVE to mail the letter rather than trusting the international mail from here.

2. Incidentally, to date we are highly pleased with AMSTALK-1. We greatly appreciate JMWAVE's assistance in the AERONAUT operation.

Eldon W. Sha. Edwin W. SHROYER.

USC attachment. Distribution: 2—C, JMWAVE w/usc att.; 1—C, SBD wo/s; 1—C, WHD wo/s.

Cross reference to. Dispatch symbol and number: HABA-19,386. Date: 26 April 1968. FOIA request: 74-120-221 (8).
