Date: 6 January 1962. From: Mexico City 9790. To: Director.

Received: January 6, 0629Z 1962. IN 47593.

Please deliver the following from Ambassador Mann to Secretary Woodward:

“Recommend I be authorized soonest relay to Tello details on Cuban intervention (portion garbled—being serviced) set forth in my message. (See MEXI. 9759.)

“I believe such documented information will impress Tello.

“Embassy has received various reports from friendly COM officials that high-level consideration is being given to a diplomatic break with Cuba.

“I have solid information that Brazilian ambassador reports to his FON OFF on December 29 that Tello told him SOM would break relations with Cuba in near future. While I cannot estimate chance of break, possibility exists that whatever information I can give Tello may cause him to move either before or after January 22.”

End of message.
