25 March 1987, #39155.

25 1459Z. Cite LCPIPIT 1268. Priority. Director information London. RYBAT DTDORIC MHPROBE OREBONY DIOVERIAX. Reference: Director-29537.

1. Will cable itinerary requested in reference after RODIGAST meeting with GREBONY/I, 25 August.

2. Appreciate that Washington probably most convenient location for HOS meetings with GREBONY/RODIGAST, but because security considerations must be paramount, request that sessions take place elsewhere, preferably New York City. Have so informed RODIGAST.

3. Security risks attendant OREBONY meetings with WOFACT in U.S. are of particular concern. Request that GREBONY contacts with WOFACT officers be held only when absolutely required and limited to essential participants. Also believe it will be difficult and probably insecure to have GREBONY/I set aside block of days for meetings once he arrives in U.S. and that contacts with GREBONY/I will have to be ad hoc instead of ad seriatim. GREBONY/I will do his best to adhere to itinerary.
