DONKEY CHIPMUNK

Ref: Havana 0427, dated 25 October 1977. Subject: New applicant information.

1. Subject: Araujo Bernal, Leopoldo Eduardo (U). DPOR: 9 June 1977, Pinar del Rio, Cuba.

2. Subject had been reported as a militant Communist since 1935, and he had been active in many Communist and leftist revolutionary organizations. In February 1961 he was reported to be an informer of the Cuban G-2 (security police). This information was received from a group of Cuban refugees, trained in intelligence collection, who have provided reliable information since 1961.
