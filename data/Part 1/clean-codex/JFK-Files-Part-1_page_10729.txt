Intelligence Information Cable.

Date: 24 March 1975. IN S31141.

The following report, transmitted from a CIA field station, requires review within the Directorate of Operations before being released outside the Agency. Pending the results of this review, a copy is being provided for your advance information.

This information is not to be included in any other document or publication. Distribution to: SDO.

Country: Panama/Cuba. Date of information: mid-March 1975.

Subject: Joint Cuban-Panama surveillance of Cuban exile visiting Panama City.

Place and date acquired: Panama, Panama City, 22 March 1975. Field No. M&P-13.364.

Source: An employee of the Panamanian government who has contacts in the Panamanian security service. He is believed to be a reliable reporter. He obtained this information from an employee of a security service.

1. In mid-March 1975, Lieutenant Colonel Manuel Noriega (Nomi Ega), the G-2 of the National Guard (GN), assigned Lieutenant Cleto Hernández, a member of the G-2, to work with Manuel Orgallé Zepeda, who is listed as the chauffeur of the Cuban ambassador in Panama, to conduct surveillance of Rolando Masferrer, a Cuban exile.

201-042669. 10 May 1975.
