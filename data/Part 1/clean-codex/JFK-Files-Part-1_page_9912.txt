During visit to Cuban Consulate 11 October, when she found all officers out, LIRING-4 was talking with consulate secretary when a man about 22 came in, said he was Peruvian, had just arrived in Mexico, and urgently wanted to speak with “someone” as he wanted to communicate without delay with compañero in Havana.

When told all officers were out, Peruvian waited for LIRING-4 outside after hearing from secretary that LIRING-4 previously moised in consulate. Peruvian explained to LIRING-4 that compañero in Havana was about to return to Peru and that he would walk into a police trap unless warned, as police were surveilling his house. He said he had been dispatched to Mexico by compañeros in Lima to contact a Cuban whose name, written on paper he showed LIRING-4, was a garbled version of LIRING-9’s name (IDEN for Lima).
