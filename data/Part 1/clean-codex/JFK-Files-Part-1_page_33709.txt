Remembered the effect on NCCT of attacks from certain Catholic circles.

NCFT was at one time in a bad situation vis-à-vis the hierarchy.

Miss Augustine summed up Mr. Dennen's views in his letter of December 28, 1951, as follows:

a) We should aid Polish Christian Trade Unions, but not the Central European Federation of Christian Trade Unions.

1. Another Christian Democratic Exile Trade Union International is already recognized by the International Federation of Christian Refugees and Exiled Workers, not the Confederation of Christian Trade Unions.

2. The question of basic policy contends that there were probably no Christian Trade Unions of any significance, except Polish, in Eastern European countries prior to Soviet occupation. Mr. Dennen raised the question of why help refugees establish organizations in exile which have no counterparts in their own countries.

3. Another Polish Exile Christian Trade Union group, Feliks Nikolajczyk, recognized by CFTC (French approval of JCCTU).
