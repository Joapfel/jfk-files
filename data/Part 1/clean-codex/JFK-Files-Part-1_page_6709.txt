Memorandum

To: Chief, Contact Division  
Attn: Support Branch (MSStunt)  
From: Chief, New Orleans Office

Date: 7 June 1983

Subject: Appointments Made for Carl Teels with Augustine Guitart Camuzano (Ccmuzando), Elio Valdes, and Arturo Finlay

Ref.: Cases 40706, 40331, and 40287; memorandum from MSStunt dated 4 Jun 83

1. In accordance with your request of 4 June 1983, we have arranged for Carl Teels to interview Dr. Augustine Guitart Camuzano (Camuzaa), Elio Valdes, and Arturo Finlay in the New Orleans Field Office on Wednesday, 17 June 1983. The following are the hours set for these interviews:

a. 10 AM, Arturo Finlay.

b. 7 PM, Dr. Augustine Guitart Camuzano (Camuzaado).

c. 5 PM, Elio Valdes.

Valdes and Guitart expressed the hope that the interviewer would speak Spanish. Finlay seems to understand a limited amount of English, but speaks only haltingly.

2. We will arrange for Mr. Teels to call at the local FSI Office during his visit next week.

Lloyd H. Kay
