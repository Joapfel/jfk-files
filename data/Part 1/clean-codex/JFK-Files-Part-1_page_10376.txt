Cite Director: 47408. To: Mexico City. Info: Guatemala City, RYBAT, Perumen, Liring. 01-23 12Z Nov 68. Reference: Mexico City 7900 (IN 25321).

1. Subject to Guatemala Station concurrence, we believe Liring/3 should be candid with Liring/10 and tell him what and as much as he wants to know. We feel if Liring/3 attempted to withhold or distort details of his imprisonment, it could be counterproductive to operation since Liring/10 probably could check other sources for accuracy.

2. There is no urgency for Liring/3 to make a trip to Perumen. We believe it more advantageous for him to remain in Mexico and attempt to become involved in local Perumen/Guatemala exile activities. If, however, Liring/10 suggests Liring/3 go to Perumen, he should not hesitate to do so.

3. Defer to Guatemala City for identification reference war names.
