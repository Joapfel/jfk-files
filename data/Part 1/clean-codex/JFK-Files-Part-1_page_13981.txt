EVI-6507  
8 September 1969

Historical Conference

To: Chief of Base

From: Howard G. Higgins

Subject: ISOLOG/Operational Western Hemisphere Targets, Group II: Cuba Monthly Activity Report, August 1969

Distribution: Unit X (DARKY); Unit B (BROWN); CO/MAR; ECF.

1. AMBERDEW/1 arrived in the area for resettlement on 1 August 1969. Although WODISH is handling the administrative aspects of the resettlement, the writer is staying in periodic contact with AMBERDEW/1 and AMBERDEW/3 for future operational purposes, e.g., debriefing AMBERDEW/1 on new CMUN arrivals. Results of the last batch of photographs forwarded by UNDA-4574, 26 June (Juno), were received via DIRECTOR 25971 in early August.

2. On 20 August, the UN Secretary General was briefed by the U.S. Permanent Representative to the UN on the illicit activities of Lazaro (Lezaro) ESPINOSA Bonet (201-81638), CMUN Third Secretary and DGI officer, and Alberto EOZA Hidalgo-Gato (201-331032), First Secretary and Deputy Chief of the DGI Center. The following morning, 21 August, the CMUN was advised by diplomatic note that ESPINOSA should expediently depart the U.S. and that EOZA, then in Cuba on leave, would not be allowed a reentry visa. Jorge REYES Vega (201-816137), Second Secretary and DGI officer, also was requested to confine his activities to those related directly to his status as an officer of a mission to the UN. See DO BASE NEW YORK 6763 for background. The story surfaced in the press the following day, 22 August.

3. In mid-August ENERGO advised the writer that ANCLOCK/1 would be visiting the U.S. This was the first indication to RVROCK of his possible travel here this year.

4. On 30 August AMBERDEW/1 arrived in the area for resettlement.

5. In late August the Base with local ENERGO monitored, for WE/MIAMI, the ANGAT/1 matter involving Pedro Luis PINERO Eirin (201-824376), CMUN Third Secretary and DGI officer. We were all in accord that PINERO would not show up for the 28 August meeting as it had been laid on prior to the events cited in paragraph 2 above; we were correct, as PINERO did not appear. See DO BASE NEW YORK 6792 for background. It is likely that the local DGI Center will remain more operationally quiet for a time because of the knocks taken by the DGI lately: the expulsion of Jesus SIMENEZ Escobar, the defection of AMBERDEW/1, the expulsion of ES AGUNT AS BAGET.
