HSCA Chronology

17 September 1976: Downing report, Congressional Record, regarding establishment of HSCA.

5 October 1976: Breckinridge memo regarding Downing Committee; procedures for dealing with Congressional investigators.

14 October 1976: Breckinridge plans for working with HSCA.

19 October 1976: Meeting with Richard Sprague.

22 October 1976: FOIA package delivered to Downing on 4 October. Request to assemble photos of unidentified man.

27 October 1976: Relationship with HSCA: plans and proposals.

10 November 1976: Sprague's plans for HSCA research.

24 November 1976: CIA briefing of Sprague.

24 November 1976: Sprague questions and comments regarding transcripts, photos, and travel of Lee Harvey Oswald.

23 November 1976: DCI (Bush) letter to Downing regarding CIA records moratorium.

26 November 1976: Post article, “CIA Withheld Details on Oswald Call.”

1 December 1976: Meeting with Sprague.

8 December 1976: Meeting with Sprague.

31 December 1976: Congressional Record—report, together with additional and supplemental views of the Select Committee on Assassinations.

10 January 1977: CI memo refuting article charging “CIA Withheld Data.”

26 January 1977: IG memo regarding HSCA comments in 31 December 1976 report.

15 February 1977: CRS proposed procedures for relations between HSCA and CIA.

29 March 1977: HSCA report providing for the continuation of HSCA.

29 April 1977: News article, “$2.5 Million Voted for JFK-King Panel.”

5 July 1977: News items charge Warren Commission error; Sturgis interview.

12 August 1977: Memorandum of understanding between DCI and HSCA, with nondisclosure agreement.

6 September 1977: HOLMES memo regarding 1 September 1977 meeting with House Committee. Request, oral, from HSCA for information on:

Loran Hall  
Guy Bannister  
Alexei Davison  
Richard Hathcock  
Gerald Hemming  
Lawrence Howard  
John Martino  
Ruben Miro-Cardona  
Eduardo Pauley  
Eduardo Perez  
William Seymour  
Clay Shaw and Frank Sturgis.
