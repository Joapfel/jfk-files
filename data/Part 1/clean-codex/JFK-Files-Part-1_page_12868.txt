CHOADEN and wanted BRADSPIES help in obtaining business visa from consulate. W-1 prefaced his first request with statement he had been promised by CHOADEN when he last visited the headquarters area that a BKHERALD representative would contact him following his return to Caracas, and that no one had contacted him. (Fact of matter is W-1 had earlier told us that for security reasons he should only be contacted at his office. Immediately following his return from visit, he closed the office and did not respond to attempts to contact him.) W-1 also said he had tried to get help from LEGATT in obtaining visa but had been told “LEGATT not available.”
