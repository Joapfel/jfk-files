Classified message. Date: 5 July 1961. To: Director. From: Leopoldville. 5 July 2200Z 1961. Action: C/AF 3 (tempo copy slotted AF at 1845, 5 July); Mr. Niesciur, AF Division notified at 1830, 5 July. Info: COP, C/C1, S/C 2. Priority: IN 34523.

Priority Director cite LEOP 1599. RYBAT WIROGUE. Immediate action. Reference DIR 17181*. Per CSR 43-43.

1. Station plans exchange Congo francs realized by WIROGUE/I in sale of car at rate 50 Congo francs to dollar. He still has outstanding loan of $4000 made by HOS. Also original HOS travel advance of $1010 which never transferred to Leopoldville.

2. Advise soonest if HOS wishes any WIROGUE/I car money withheld against advances.

3. Station will advance WIROGUE/I cost of tourist ticket plus $100 expenses.

End of message.

C/S comment: *Cable predates CS files. Copy may be seen in Signal Center archives.
