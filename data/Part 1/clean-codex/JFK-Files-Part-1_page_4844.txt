Outgoing message. Staff Director. COM/C/2815. To: file. REF: Caracas 29076. IN 826512.

To: Immediate Managua. Info: Immediate San Jose, Caracas.

RYBAT VULOFTY V.

1. Costa Rican liaison HUFABRIC has reported that Orlando Bosch Avila, terrorist and leader of anti-Cuban government group known as Accion Cubana, was arrested in San Jose at midday on 18 February. He was charged with entering Costa Rica with illegal documents and could be held for minimum 24 hours. At request Secret Service representative Mastrovito, San Jose station arranged for Mastrovito to interview Bosch at HUFABRIC headquarters at 1400 hours local time 19 February. Mastrovito had interviewed Bosch several years ago in U.S.

2. Costa Ricans are discussing how long they can hold Bosch on entry with false documents charge. They are considering contacting ERYTHROID-33 in Managua and deporting him to that country.

3. Various sources reported possible plans of Bosch to kill Dr. Henry Kissinger and/or Cuban ambassador in Caracas or Chilean exile Luis Andres Pascal Allende, nephew of Salvador.
