Dispatch. To: Chief, MID. From: Chief of Station, Mexico. Subject: Maurice H. Halperin. Action required: Information only. Reference: Source: Lieut./L/MUD.

1. Attached for your information and retention are two items of correspondence sent from Moscow by Maurice H. Halperin and/or Edith Halperin to the Albert Maltz (201-5239) family in Mexico.

2. Copies of this correspondence have been given to ODMNY locally for their information.

Willard C. Curtis.

Attachments: 1 - megahertz, 1 hz, 1 post card. 2 - points @ negative.

6 April 1960.

Distribution: 3 - HQS w/atts.
