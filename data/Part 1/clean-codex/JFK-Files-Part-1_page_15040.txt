Translation of German report from the BFV to the American Embassy, OCA, z.H. Mr. C. W. Schmitz in Mehlem.

Concerning Georg Sanders (gebrix86x), born 25 July 1919 in Tiflis, USSR. Present residence in Oberursel/Ts, Hohemarkstr. 145.

Cross-reference: none.

As of 14 July 1962, Sanders was in the custody of a U.S. organization in Oberursel. It was alleged that he had arrived on the same date as a tourist from East to West Berlin and defected there.

On 28 August 1962, he filed an application for recognition as a foreign refugee.

On 29 August 1962, he was interviewed, in the presence of a member of a U.S. organization, by the criminal investigation authorities. During the interview, Sanders made opaque statements concerning his defection.

Among other things, Sanders said that he had been a leading member of a resistance group in the Soviet Union. Moreover, he claimed that he evaded arrest by joining a Soviet tourist group with the assistance of friends.

The tourist group went on a guided tour of the Soviet-occupied East Zone of Germany. The group left Tiflis on 10 July 1962, arriving in East Berlin on 14 July 1962. The subject claimed that upon arrival in East Berlin, he escaped immediately. However, Sanders refused to make more detailed statements about his escape to West Berlin. Allegedly, he did not know how his friends had managed to place him in the tourist group.

We would be grateful if any communications or information concerning Sanders, including a possible interview report, would be forwarded to us.
