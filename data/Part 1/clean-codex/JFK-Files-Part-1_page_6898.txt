Classified message.

Date: 1 November 1958. To: Director. From: Bern. Action: WH 5*. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS, EE 4, SR 4, S/C 2, WE A.

Date/time: November 2, 0114Z 1958. Routine. IN 21415.

To: Mexi. Info: DIR. Cite: BERN 3138. Re: BERN 3096 (IN 17033).

1. Subjects travelled Zurich to Lugano 29 October, made phone call 28 October from Lugano to Prag, where spoke in Spanish with FNU Stein (phonetic) at telephone number Lazaro Pena 67856. Not clear if Pena is telephone exchange or name of subscriber.

2. In phone conversation identified self to Stein as friend of Lombardo, said had met Stein in shop of Dr. Berta Arenares. Said he was waiting in Suisse for CSR visa, wished to see Sterns in Prag. When Stein was vague about knowing Sterns, subject asked if he had not seen them in Intraya (phonetic). Then asked when Lombardo would arrive in Prag, was told he was still in Mexico. Subject promised to contact Stein on arrival in Prag. According to interpreter's note on phone transcript, Stein was very guarded, not enthusiastic about phone call.

3. Subject returned to Zurich 29 October, got passports back from CSR consulate without visa. Now attempting to get tourist visas through travel agency. Moved to cheaper hotel, spends much time in university library. Indications state subjects are running low on funds; many indications they plan to remain in CSR indefinitely. Believe Lombardo mentioned on phone identification with Vicente (Vincente) Toledano Lombardo.

End of message.

4. Will pouch detailed reports.

C/S comment: *Action unit determined as through indicator DYVOUR USEC. **Itinerary Maurice (Marice) Halperin and wife, American Communists.
