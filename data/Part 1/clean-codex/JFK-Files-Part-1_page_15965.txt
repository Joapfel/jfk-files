Classified message.

Date: 5 June 1962. To: Director. From: Frankfurt. Action: C/EE 3. Info: ADDP, C/CI, C/FI 2, CDC, C/TSD 2, C/AF 3, S/C 2. Per CSN 43-43.

June 5, 2046Z, 1962. R. B.

IN 46446. DIR info LEO P. CITE FRAN 0805. RYBAT WIROGUE MARVARD TECHS.

1. Subject not enthusiastic about West German documentation unless political recognition follows. Believe fabrication of life history in order to obtain recognition too risky in this case since slightest flap could place KUBARK in embarrassing position vis-à-vis FEDREP authorities and ODIBEX liaison.

2. Intend return Austrian passport and identity papers as temporary measure, permit subject to carry out assigned tasks for Congolese Defense Minister and return to Congo in order formally abrogate contract with GOC. Will inform subject no KUBARK employment available, but we willing to redocument (REDOCCUMENT) him with Austrian or other passport in name David de Panaskhet, which he considers his true name. He will know new documentation will be held in abeyance pending his return to FRAN.

3. Subject has stated desire resettle in South America. Understand passport would probably be backchecked with country of issue should subject decide to request permanent residency or citizenship in South American country. Request headquarters.
