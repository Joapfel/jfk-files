To: Mexi. LCHARVEST. Ref: MEXI 9468 (IN 83286). Cite DIR. 22 May 1954. 22829.

1. Subject described for years as leftist, but anti-Communist and strongly anti-American. HQS wants developed information on his current political beliefs and why subject wanted to visit China. He obviously considered man of stature by Chicoms, could probably swing new invitation to visit China. We want evaluate answers to paragraphs 2 and 3 questions to determine his ability to provide intelligence, with view toward recruiting him for briefed mission or residence in China. Do LINLUCK-1, LICOOKY-1 feel he is approachable under any guise? Consider if he is assessed unwilling to cooperate with ODYOKE, suggest false-flag recruitment.

2. Chicoms may consider him a commie; if so, he may have been asked to carry back messages, money, or perform other services in Mexi. Suggest LINLUCK-1 or LICOOKY-1 try to query him on these points. Also report dates of trip, itinerary, installations visited, instruction given, if any, and lectures subject gave.

3. As outlined page 15, new Part IV/A China handbook, detune substance of subject's conversations with Chinese leaders, dates.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer. Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
