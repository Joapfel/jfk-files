Guillermo parried this query by asking A-5 why he had not sent the response cable to Havana sooner. A-5 said that he had sent the reply as soon as he knew he could travel to Montreal since this was his only business team. Guillermo never did answer why Guillermo (Guis Ha) had delayed answering A-5's letter. However, in the course of the conversation Guillermo showed A-5 his passport, which showed that he had only received a visa for Canada twenty-four hours prior to his travel to Canada. Passport showed entry date of 14 November.

A-5 asked Guillermo if Anchor-1 had sent him. Guillermo replied affirmatively and said, however, that there was one man whom he did not identify between himself and Anchor-1. A-5 asked whether Guillermo had ever given the package which he had given him in Spain to Anchor-1. Guillermo again replied affirmatively.

5. Since A-5 offered to cooperate regarding the supply of information in Miami without implicating individuals, the conversation gravitated to the Che Guevara adventure in Bolivia. Guillermo questioned A-5 regarding his knowledge of the participation of Anchor-1 (Anchalk-1) in the action against Guevara. A-5 told Guillermo only that he had heard that Anchor-1 (Anchalk-1) had participated in the operation against Guevara, but that this was common knowledge amongst many exiles—which is true. Guillermo confirmed that he knew this to be the case. In order to warm the meeting, A-5 said that he respected Che as a dedicated man despite his disagreement with his philosophy and actions. Guillermo said that he knew that Che had been murdered by Bolivians and that no Americans or Cuban exiles had actually participated in the operation.
