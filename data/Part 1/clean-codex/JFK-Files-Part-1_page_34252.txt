“Habla... Unidad Revolucionaria” (W.A.U.L.)

October 29, 1965: “Russia Rebuffs Castro.” A comment on the news that Cuba and the USSR have signed an agreement to send 7 engineers and 50 technicians to the Caribbean island. It is pointed out that, substantially, this is the Soviet Union’s reply to the request for technical and financial assistance for Cuba’s reconstruction and sugar crop.

“Brazil and the Dominican Republic.” The situation in Brazil and the Dominican Republic shows that it is not enough to face up to Communist aggressions, but that energetic, precautionary measures must also be taken to neutralize the subversive threat and guarantee democratic stability. On the other hand, the Hemisphere’s problems must be focused on in their entirety, and action must be taken against the source of subversion in Communist Cuba.
