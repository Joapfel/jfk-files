19 September 1968. IN 95628.

Cable: Mexico City 7224. Director. REDTOP AEKICK.

Reference: Director 16046, 13 July 1968.

1. LIFIRE reports PCS arrival of Valeriy Vladimirovich Kostikov (201-305052), accompanied by wife, Roza Aleksandrovna, and daughter, Svetlana. They arrived 13 September on KLM Flight 683. Kostikov travelled on diplomatic passport D-022184. Wife and daughter travelled on diplomatic passport D-022185.

2. Per reference/Kostikov, scheduled to replace Aleksandr Mikhaylovich Brovkin (201-185114), third secretary. According to LICOZY-5, Brovkin has a reservation to depart Mexico on 22 September.

3. LIFIRE report indicates Kostikov has the title of second secretary.
