22. Travel (cont.)

1962 – England, France, Morocco, Algeria. Departure from FREC, no fixed objective. Abroad, employed by Jacques Verges and Révolution Africaine in Algiers, November 1962.

1963–64 – Switzerland, France, Belgium, Hollywood, England—residence or trips involved with business of editing Revolution.

1964 – England—stay with wife’s family, seeking employment.

1965 – January and February. Italy, Switzerland, France, Belgium—trip financed by Encounter to research article.

29. Employment (part.)

1961–62 – Acting Executive Secretary, Fair Pay for Cuba Committee—salary ran from A$150 to nothing per week. Left because of unwillingness to continue an unpleasant task.

1962–64 – Editor, Révolution Africaine, and later editor of its English edition—salary 1,000 French francs per month at first and later 1,500 francs per month—rupture based on financial collapse of publication and various disagreements with Verones, its publisher.
