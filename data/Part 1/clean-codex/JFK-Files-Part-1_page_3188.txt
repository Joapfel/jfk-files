Classified message. Cable dated 021845Z October 1969, citing Buenos Aires (BUENOS AIES) 3801. Shroyer acting.

Director.

RYBAT REDTOP AELANYARD.

1. On 28 September AEBOOR and wife vacated AELANYARD apartment after having packed household effects. They moved directly to PSOADAS, where they are presently residing. Contract not due to expire until June 1970.

2. First indication of planned move is from transcript of 28 September, which, due to backlog and the weekend, was not processed until 30 September. In this, AEBOOR and wife argue over the decision to move out. Unfortunately, the landlord is out of town, and we do not yet have the benefit of AEBOOR's future discussion with him regarding the reason for moving.

3. At this moment we are unable to determine the reason for the sudden move, and while it appears compromise of operation a likely cause, we are unable to make a conclusive statement. Audio equipment functioning normally. We are presently inclined to believe AEBOOR may have received instructions to move due to AVDOLMAN flap. Note AEBOOR and 281-925485 only two with.
