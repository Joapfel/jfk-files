Cite BERG 8708, 12 June 70. Director, Bonn, Frankfurt, Berlin; info Zurich.

Ref: Zurich 1629, 17 March 1969, et al. (not needed Bonn, Frankfurt).

1. INDORED has reported that Richard Case Nagell, born 5 August 1930, New York, U.S. citizen, has again come to Swiss attention. In mid-April 1970 he registered at Zurich hotel. On 15 April 1970 reported to police that his raincoat had disappeared. Claimed that an important document intended for the American military authorities in Berlin was in the pocket. Document was entitled “Fiscal Year 70 (USAREUR).”

2. Raincoat was found shortly later in Hotel Sans, allegedly without document. Hotel turned raincoat over to Zurich police, where subject subsequently picked it up.

3. When questioned by police, subject gave as references:

A. Col. Francis H. Dillon, Office of Counterintelligence and Security, DIA, Washington.

B. Francis X. Plant, Special Assistant, Office of Under Secretary of Army.

C. Bruce A. Flatin, Public Safety Section, U.S. Mission.

ANIMAL. AB-X. IN. FC. FN. PG & NPD. FI.
