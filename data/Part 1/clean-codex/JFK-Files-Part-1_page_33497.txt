T 1313352, May 1974, Staff. CITE PARIS 48390. To: Priority Santiago. Info: Director.

TOFUCUS UNSOBER.

Reference: Director 549349.

1. If Socialist presidential candidate François Mitterrand is elected on 19 May, it is most doubtful that UNSOBER/1 could do program on Chile that would be acceptable to BKHERALD (or to Pinochet).

2. Therefore recommend Santiago consider reference within confines of station until results of election known. If majority candidate Giscard d'Estaing wins (he has slight lead in polls), climate will be excellent for favorable treatment of Pinochet. In this case, would be useful to have response to reference by COB 30 May to coincide with TDY to Paris of HOS TOFUCUS officer.

3. File: 201-872997.
