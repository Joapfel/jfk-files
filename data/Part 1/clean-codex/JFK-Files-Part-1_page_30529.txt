Headquarters, Field Research Unit, Far East Command, Yokosuka, Japan.

25 July 1951

AG 201 - PFRS

LO 206-2

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individuals Concerned

1. Following individuals will proceed on/about 27 July 1951 to Pusan and Seoul, Korea, and/or such other places as are necessary for a period of temporary duty of approximately ten (10) days in connection with activities of this headquarters. Upon completion of temporary duty, individuals will return to proper station.

Colonel Benjamin H. Vandoervoort (Vandervoort), 022715 Infantry  
Mr. William E. Duggan (GS-15)  
Mr. Newton S. Miller (Miler) (GS-12)

2. Travel by military aircraft, rail, and government motor authorized. No travel by military vehicle. An allowance of sixty-five (65) pounds personal baggage authorized while traveling by air. No per diem authorized.

3. Individuals are designated official couriers.

4. Officers may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after completion of temporary duty.

5. Mr. Duggan and Mr. Miler (Miller) will wear the Army officer's uniform, less insignia, in the combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from Quartermaster Sales Store (Storo) upon presentation of these orders.

6. Immunizations and physical inspection as prescribed by Circular 9, FEC, 27 February 1950, will be completed prior to departure.

John F. Hook  
Executive Officer
