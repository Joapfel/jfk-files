Refs: A. Panama City 8498, W 85341. B. Mexico City 2744, W 88360. C. Panama City 2510, W 86266.

1. LIRING-3 (identity) reported 27 June that had talked with Lelis Amadeo (Rodriguez) Angulo (281-326789) 25 June. In connection with LIRING-3 effort to get Rodriguez work at CIDOC, LIRING-3 able to question Rodriguez on Panamanian revolutionary situation and surface requirements levied in reference C.

2. Rodriguez told LIRING-3 Vanguardia de Accion Nacional (VAS), an important extremist organization with political line parallel to Panamanian Communist Party. Rodriguez stated VAS (VAN) only Panamanian party “viewed with favor” by Cubans. Rodriguez told LIRING-3 chief of VAS is Jorge Turner (281-48393), who recently arrived Mexico City after release from prison. Rodriguez stated Turner here to conference with Cubans in attempt to get Cuban assistance for VAS.
