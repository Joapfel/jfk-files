ISAACS, HAROLD

FE-1 Amauts.

26 June 1951 — Memo dated 25 June to PT/CS requesting an informational namecheck on subject for the purpose of determining the validity of charges that subject is sympathetic to and/or connected with Communist activities. See file for rest of memo.

According to APPELHAN, subject is author of “The Tragedy of the Chinese Revolution,” and APPELHAN met subject through Alexander BUCYMAN. Subject was the first editor of the CCP paper of which Al L'ANlian was to become editor. DJB-17747.

The book “New Cycle in Asia” by ISAACS, published in 1947 by the Institute of Pacific Relations as a collection of the official texts of various treaties and agreements among South Asian and Western powers during the period of World War II and later. ISAACS supplies a brief editorial introduction to these texts, which is apparently non-political in nature. His article “Korea and American World Policy” appears in the 7 August 1950 issue of the “New Republic.” The article advocates withdrawal of U.S. support from French colonial forces in Indochina and recognition of Red China in the hope of driving a wedge between Russia and Chinese Communists. DIB 25069, 12 March 1953, 201-1846-3.
