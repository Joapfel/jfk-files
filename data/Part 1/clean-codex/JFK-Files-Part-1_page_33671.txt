January 29, 1952

Dear Abbott:

I have just been informed over the telephone that Earle Rogers, of Counsel Services, Inc., has called a meeting of the Crusade for Freedom Committee in this city, and has failed to consult me in any way in this matter.

This has resulted, as usual, in causing utter confusion in the minds of everyone, and bodes no good for the Crusade in this city nor for the sponsoring organisation—the National Council for a Free Europe, for which the Crusade is operating. In your letter of December 7, 1951, to Mr. Herbert T. BiassCHEL, Chairman of the Crusade for Freedom in the District of Columbia, you designated me as Director of the Washington, D.C. office. As I have received no instructions from you to the contrary, I have presumed this situation to be continuing.

The fact that Mr. Rogers would proceed in a matter of this kind without consultation with this office, and without being commissioned by you to undertake such a meeting, is most disturbing. This is the sort of thing that will really kill the Crusade for Freedom in this city.

I shall hope to discuss this matter with you when in New York on Thursday.

Mr. Abbott Washburn  
Executive Vice-Chairman  
Crusade for Freedom  
4 West 57th Street  
New York, N.Y.
