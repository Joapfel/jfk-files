Message handling indicator: Staff. Date: 052139Z. Director. Confidential: CCS2. Info: File WH8.CMG/NOC4.0GCC20. Message reference number: 503060.

To: Caracas (CARAGAS). Reference: Caracas 25040 (IN 309514).

1. If WKSCARLET/3 (W/3) cannot locate copies of his federal tax returns for tax years 1968 through 1973, request he correspond with the Internal Revenue Service Center where he filed original tax returns and obtain necessary copies. Without these tax returns, headquarters cannot accurately compute W/3’s true tax liability for those years.

2. Headquarters would appreciate station maintaining contact with W/3 until tax matters are finalized. Direct correspondence with W/3 regarding this matter would be cumbersome, since he has no knowledge of general covert tax procedure. Should station have no choice but terminate contact, identity address is provided for his use corresponding with headquarters.

3. Copies of Venezuelan tax forms are not required.
