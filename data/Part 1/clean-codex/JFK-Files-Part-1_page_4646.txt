01-8082, November 1974, Staff. Cite Caracas 25943.

To: Immediate Director, Quito. Info: LA/Miami. MIACT Quito.

References: A. Director 619818. B. Quito 21873. C.M. 10089.

1. The following information was passed to the station by Assistant Legat Gordon Ellison. Ellison says the information may be passed by the station to Ecuadoran security forces.

A. Orlando Bosch Avila is currently in fugitive status for having violated his parole. He was convicted and sentenced to 10 years in Miami on charges related to ship bombings, extortion, and conspiracy. He was released on parole December 15, 1972.

B. Bosch allegedly arrived in Quito on November 5 or 6, 1974, in possession of a Colombian passport. No information is available regarding his specific destination in Quito. However, he may contact Roberto Verdaguer, Cuban exile pilot, who now operates a commercial airline transport and fumigation company in Ecuador.

C. The Department of Justice has obtained approval for Copy to Cross Village into 201-0277392.
