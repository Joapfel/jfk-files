7. S stated after some thinking that he had connections in practically every branch of the Mexican government, but definitely had none in the Seguridad de Estado. I commented that this was understandable because the Security keeps itself separated from politicking, which enables this organization to carry out its duties without interference from politicians. I added that the Security people are excellently trained and were amazingly efficient in their work. It was evident that his statements left me worried. I took advantage of the silence and added as an afterthought that, in his opinion, it would be wise if S thinned his meetings with LOG to two, maximum three, per month.

8. I changed his previously prearranged meeting with S from Saturday, July 19, to Monday, July 21, at 2000 hours. S did not have his notebook with him, and he assured me that he would not forget it. This meeting lasted 30 minutes.

P.S. S did not show up for the July 21 meeting.
