Classified message. Cable Secretariat dissemination. Advance copy issued, slotted. Disseminated by D&P. File: VR C1R15D C/D04 DDP ADJE CSL50 CIC CLF12. Action unit: CWL 8. Action No. 245.

26 May 1970, 2038Z. Cite DO Base New York 7800. Director, WH/Miami. RYBAT TYPIC DOMOPS AMROSE.

1. Follow for WOMACE info only.

2. While recognizing that JKLANCE program to uncover relatives and friends of DGI officials assigned abroad has not as yet progressed to examination of DGI officials in New York, local LNERGO informally has asked if survey could be made ASAP of Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez (201-332665), CMUN first secretary and DGI officer. Local LNERGO has received directive from its HDQS to compile detailed report on Rodriguez and his activities and forward “target study” to LNERGO HDQS. Because of previous AMANVIL/I and other reporting on Rodriguez, and because he is not now married, LNERGO HDQS apparently feels he should be prime target for approach. Local LNERGO quite willing informally coordinate activity with Base. Thus, would appreciate whatever information addressees can provide as to any leads to Rodriguez via relatives or friends who may be in U.S. at present.

3. Rodriguez born 3 May 1942, Havana. Formerly married to CS.
