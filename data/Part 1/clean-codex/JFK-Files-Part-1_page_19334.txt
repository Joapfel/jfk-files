Incoming Classified Message.

From: Tsingtao. To: Special Operations. Action: CPD. Information: ADSO, COPS, CRD, FBZ, CAS, SFD. Routine. 5 April 1948. IN 39079. TSIN 74.

To: WASHF. Cite: TSIN.

Re WASH ZCIW 2, paragraph 7, and TSIN ZCJA 8, paragraph 12.

Departure and arrival of new Consul General William Turner and Consul Robert Strong, who are fully cooperative, has clarified situation here with respect to Agency representative.

Visa and immigration and consular duties, particularly of routine nature, continue to take almost all office time. Definitely require clerk so more time may be given Agency matters. Consul General agreeable clerk be expedited so must request Barder if still available; otherwise request Russian linguist who is stenotypist. Clerk must be sent as bona fide State employee for security reasons with (A) FSS classification and pay grade, (B) State travel orders, (C) pay and allowances set up through Consulate so clerk will not appear to be in special category to local alien employees.
