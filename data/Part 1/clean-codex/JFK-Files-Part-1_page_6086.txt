Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Dominican Republic, Cuba.

Date: 2 August 1967.

Subject: Report of the Dominican Air Force and the Dominican air caching operations in the Dominican Republic.

Place and date acquired: Dominican Republic, San's Dominicos (13 August 1967).

PEF IN #35.

Source and appraisal: Dominican government official with excellent contacts in military circles.

Field report No.: 08-068. Appraisal: 3.

1. The Dominican Air Force and the Dominican Navy are allowing craft manned by Cuban enlisted personnel to cache arms and other supplies on the Dominican coast at Las Peral and Punta Luna, south of Monte Friel.

The boats arrive and depart quickly, with delays only from the Dominican Navy. The craft carrying arms and other supplies are not of Dominican or CII origin.

The Cuban enlisted personnel do not remain ashore, but leave in the craft that brought them.

2. Air Force Col. Joan H. Holly Perez, Dominican CH.
