Gomez said that on 30 September the Cuban Embassy had informed the Directorate for the Services of Intelligence and Prevention (DISIP), the Venezuelan civilian security service, that a Cuban exile named Salvador Romani was observing the Cuban fencing team at its Caracas hotel. Romani, Gomez said, is a United States intelligence agent. (Source comment: Gomez implied that the Cuban Embassy is looking into the possibility that Romani's activities are involved with the plane crash.) Furthermore, the Cubans told DISIP that the team should defend itself should Romani cause trouble. Shortly after the Cubans made their feelings known to DISIP, Gomez said that Romani's activities ceased. (Field comment: In HVC-7495, TOFAROS-315/09683-76, of 28 September, the same source reported that the Cuban Embassy had complained to DISIP about anti-Cuban government articles appearing in the local press. In this instance the Cubans indicated their willingness to retaliate against the newsman involved if DISIP did not.
