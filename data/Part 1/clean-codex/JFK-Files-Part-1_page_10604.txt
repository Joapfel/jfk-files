TYPIC YOBITE UOAMBER.

On 28 February 1965, UOAMBER-4 visited the office of dentist Rodolfo Masferrer, who told source he had received a letter from his brother, Rolando, in which Rolando claimed he was visited in his New York office by two ODYOKE officials on an unspecified date. They allegedly asked him if he was ready to participate in armed action against PBRUMEN. He replied he was and had been preparing himself for action from the first days of his arrival in PBPRIME. He indicated in his letter that for the first time he was under consideration by ODYOKE. He estimated he could immediately round up 300 to 400 men.

CFW.

The following action is to be taken: 1. Send a copy of this action, with all details of the encounter. 2. Obtain a better coordinate system for your forces. 3. Send one of our best to track down the forces in question. 4. Report all activity to me by telephone. 5. Return this document by 0800 hours on 27 March 1965.
