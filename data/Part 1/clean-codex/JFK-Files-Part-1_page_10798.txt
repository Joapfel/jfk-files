Via: I-1. From: J/EMI. Trans: I-57. Agent: E-10 (Please protect).

27 October 1969

Subject: Activities of Rolando Hasperrer (HASPERER) Rojas, editor of “Libertad” newspaper.

Source: Rolando Hasperrer (HASPERER) Rojas.

Reference: OTA-1520, 10 September 1969.

1. The following information was obtained by E-10 on 25 October 1969 and reported on 27 October 1969.

2. Rolando Hasperrer (HASPERER) Rojas told E-10 that Engineer Jose Eliseo de la Torriente (TORRIENTE) Ajuria, also known as “Pepe,” is the only person who is involved in a serious activity in favor of the freedom of Cuba. Hasperrer (HASPERER) added that Torriente (TORRIENTE) had not Cuban Communist leaders in Vienna, Austria, and Mexico. Hasperrer (HASPERER) did not mention any names or dates.

3. Hasperrer (HASPERER) also said that the CIA (Central Intelligence Agency) had nothing to do with Cuban affairs and that the latter are in the hands of the Pentagon.

4. Hasperrer (HASPERER) said that he was sorry to say it but that the Cuban Revolutionary Party (Auténtico) is the only group which is effective both in Cuba and in exile.
