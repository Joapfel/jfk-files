351349Z March 1974, Staff.

Cite: DCD/Miami 12494.

DCD/Washington.

Attention: Support Branch/OSS (Grant), A-182014.

Subject: Steven Van Cleave, Intelligence Services, Ltd., Atlanta, Georgia.

Reference: Grant/Hennessey Telecom, as of February, same subject.

I. During the afternoon of 21 January 1974, we received a telephone call from Albell to Allies, SSN 177650, from Florida, a contact of the Miami Field Office, who advised us that an acquaintance of his in Atlanta, Georgia, Steven Van Cleave, wanted to speak with us in connection with intelligence matters concerning Latin America. We told Allied that Van Cleave should contact our office in Atlanta. However, Allies said that Van Cleave did not want to deal with “recruiters”; rather, he wanted to speak with a CIA officer “in the business of intelligence collection.” We told Allied that our Atlanta office has the same status as the Miami Field Office and that we should urge Van Cleave to contact our representative in Atlanta. Allied would not accept this response on our part and insisted that we at least hear what Van Cleave had to say. Finally, we agreed to accept a call from Van Cleave so that we could tell him to contact our Atlanta representative.

11-29-74
