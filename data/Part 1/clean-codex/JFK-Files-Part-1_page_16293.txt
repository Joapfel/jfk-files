Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Congo (Leopoldville)/South Africa.

Date: June 1965. Report No. TOCS-110/0928-65. Distributed: July 1965. Reference: N 03875.

Subject: Developing conflict regarding utilization of South African Fifth Commando.

Place and date acquired: Congo, Albertville (Alfertville), 29 June 1965.

Source and appellant: A competent American observer from Major Alistair (Alistar) Wickes, acting commanding officer of the Fifth Commando.

1. On 29 June 1965 Major Alistair (Alistar) Wickes, temporary coordinator of the South African Fifth Commando (SCOC), cited bitterly the withdrawal of the Sixth Commando personnel from 7 and adjacent areas at the time of the arrival of advance personnel of the SCOC in early June 1965. He stated this development was making it necessary for him to respond to local Congolese anonymous (ANONYMUS) (ANC), Albertville (Alfertville), requests for assistance and support in the 7 area; this was contrary to instructions from Lieutenant Colonel Michael Hoare (SCDE), who was now in South Africa preparing to return to the Congo with approximately 200 new recruits.
