Incoming Telegram

Department of State

Telegraph Branch

Control: 11327  
Received: April 23, 1951, 4:51 p.m.

From: Frankfurt (Frankfort)  
To: Secretary of State  
No. 8583, April 23, 8 p.m.

Recent discussions with Lunt, INP, pamphlet with comic Soviet combative possibilities proposed, with thought giving publication attribution to local group to be determined. Urge INP's publications branch dummy up along following lines for production here: pocket-size format, eight pages. Cover to carry photograph of Stalin in friendly greeting with high-ranking, current party boss in good standing from one satellite country. Title to reflect theme “Stalin's Friends.” Each page of inside book to carry photograph of Stalin in friendly pose with such fallen, one-time heroes or friends of Soviet Union as Trotsky, Hitler, Bukharin (Bucharin), Tito, Clementis, or others dependent on existence of pictures in archives.

Captions for pictures can be taken from one of two sources or both. Either original photo caption or quotation from that era when “friend” was in favor, or a later quote from a denunciatory statement concerning the fallen man. Possibly both quotes could be used, the former above, the latter below the pictures. Suggest most careful research be made for most succinct quote to carry off this idea. Request reaction soonest.

McCloy

PAF:DES
