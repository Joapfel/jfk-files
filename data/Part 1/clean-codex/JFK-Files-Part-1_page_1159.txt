A. MEXI 6620 (1W 45873)

E. DIR 79070

1. Following developments as result of meeting with ANSER-2 30 October and 1 November.

2. Subject in frequent contact with Ernest “Che” Cuevara.

3. Additional motivation behind subject’s plan to leave Cuba in December is to be able to help support children.

4. Pouch communications system now in effect works as follows. Brother gives letters to Miss (FNU) President at Netherlands Embassy, Mexico. Outer envelope marked for Cornelis Djpost at Netherlands Embassy, Havana. Inner envelope marked for subject care of E. Cabanes. Cabanes delivers letters to subject for whom he works. System works both ways.

5. A-2 knows Cabanes, further under Nueva Papelera de Puentes Grandes. He is old, sick, and going blind. Brother of subject sends medicines to Cabanes through pouch. Cabanes arranged communications channel as a result of his former diplomatic connections. A-2 believes Cabanes may have worked for Spanish Foreign Service in Pagi and was naturally a Spanish citizen.
