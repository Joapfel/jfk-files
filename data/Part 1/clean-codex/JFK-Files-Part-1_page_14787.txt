1. Following are headquarters traces for information only on William Gaudet and Antonio Veciana mentioned in the reference.

2. William George Gaudet (G AU DE T).

Date and place of birth: 21 September 1908, St. James, Louisiana, USA.

Citizenship: U.S.

As of circa 1953, Gaudet's address was given as 2176 Esplanade Avenue, New Orleans, Louisiana. He was knowledgeable of Latin America and published in the early 1950s a magazine or bulletin, “Latin American Reports.” He was a contact of DCB from 1948 until November 1961 in the New Orleans area. He was debriefed on his Latin American travels from 1948–1955 and was a casual contact from 1955–1961.

Gaudet was among individuals listed in a letter of 24 January 1978 to CIA from the Senate Select Committee on Assassinations. The committee intended to conduct interviews with Gaudet and inquired if DCB had ever been in contact with him. HOS responded on 27 January 1978, saying we had been in contact with him for a number of years, but had no current contact with or recent interest in him.

3. It should be noted that Gaudet has made this claim before, that of having been an “operative,” and has exaggerated his role with the Agency. As an interesting historical aside, an article in the Washington “Star” on 15 January 1976 reported Gaudet had received a Mexican tourist visa in New Orleans, apparently circa 1963. His visa number immediately preceded that of the visa granted to Lee Harvey Oswald.
