TOR: 3/31/14 42 MAY 74. BANGKOK 59314.

Cite Bangkok 59314. To: Director. Info: Santiago.

Refs: A. Bangkok 59865 (IN 260348). B. Director 554557.

1. Milton C. Repoush and Ralph K. Tolgien met with Emilio Galetezki on 25 May and passed Ref B information. Tolgien had a follow-up meeting with Galetezki on 29 May to discuss his encounter with Arthur Cunning.

2. Galetezki met Cunning at the home of Robert B. Martford, U.S. citizen employed in the Population Division of ECAFE. Martford is pro-Allende and organized a small social gathering of interested persons to meet Cunning and discuss the current situation in Chile. Cunning is described as about forty years old, with black hair, a full beard, and average build. He is a demographer employed by the United Nations at Centro Latino Americano de Desarrollo (CELADE) in Santiago. He arrived in Chile in October 1972 with his wife. Cunning departed Bangkok 27 May for Delhi, Kathmandu, and New York and planned to report Santiago approximately 22 June.

3. Cunning talked very little about the Allende (ALLLENDE) regime.
