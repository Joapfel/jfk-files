APRUPPILZ

PURPLE positively identified a photo of Valeriy Vasilievich Kostikov (“aleriy Vlasili-ovica KOSTIKOV”) as the Soviet who had met him in Mexico City on 9, 11, and 12 February 1963. Kostikov gave PURPLE meeting arrangements for the next meeting or meetings to be in New York City. The Soviet who appeared in New York City was Clag D. Prykin (“Clag D. PRYKIN”). Prykin has been identified as a member of the 13th Department of the KGB’s FCI.

PURPLE is a naturalized German-American, has a crop-dusting business in the Middle East, and furnished information on industrial and missile sites in the area. Prykin was particularly interested in pipelines.

The 13th Department may have exercised its prerogative and taken over the case from the First (American) Department. Former case officer Yuriy V. Kostikov was apparently from the American Department, not the 13th. But it appears that Prykin asked PURPLE for the same type of index information assigned by Kostikov, suggesting that Kostikov was from the same KGB department as Prykin.

This information should not be released without permission of SR/CI.
