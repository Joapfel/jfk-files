TYPIC.

Ref: WH/MIAMI 09883 (J 234350).

1. Depending on the nature and degree of WH/MIAMI interest in the subject referred to, would recommend watchlisting with Dutch liaison, which might produce timely information on the subject’s arrival, residence while in the Netherlands, and onward itinerary. Otherwise, station will at best be able to provide after-the-fact travel data. Please advise whether watchlisting is desired.

2. Would emphasize that watchlisting will signal JKLANCE interest, although we estimate little, if any, liaison concern. Also, that station must, in general, limit overall volume of watchlisting requirements.

3. For Director. Recommend headquarters be alert to traces of the subject referred to in material forwarded under ONHA-37367 and related.
