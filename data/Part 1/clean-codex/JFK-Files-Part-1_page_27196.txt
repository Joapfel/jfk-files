Chief, China Mission

Info: Chief, FE

Administrative/Personnel

William V. Broe — Recommendation for Promotion.

1. Subject is a superior senior CIA official whose maturity and experience are constantly reflected in his work.

2. He has performed his duties as Deputy Chief, FRC, and later as Deputy Chief, China Mission, in an outstanding manner. It is believed that his performance would be equally good in any equivalent position within the OD/P complex.

3. Subject frequently is Acting Chief of Mission in Mr. Horton's absence and on many occasions acts on behalf of him in conferences with NA or elsewhere. His handling at all times is commendatory.

4. Since Mr. Broe is a GS-14 in a GS-16 position and since he has been in grade since 15 October 1951, an early promotion to GS-15 is recommended.

Signed, Chief of Mission
