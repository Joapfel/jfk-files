Report classified secret, now contained control No. 1-603. Guatemala/Mexico. Subject: Views of the ICT on the political situation in Guatemala. 24 March 1962. PADA Mexico, Mexico City, 24 March 1962. Air 2 FEICOM (B), has good connections in Guatemalan exile circles in Mexico.

1. Victor Label/Cutler, head of the Partido Guatemalano del Trabajo (ICT—Guatemalan Committee Party) in Mexico, said on 24 March that the ICT in Guatemala regrets the way in which the guerrilla fight has developed. According to the ICT, the results of the guerrilla fighting have not been successful because the 13 November group (member of the 13 November 1960 attach to government of the Guatemalan Committee), led by Marco Agonio/CT/Cosa, is not in accordance with the political goals of the committee struggle. The ICT also regrets that the Communists and members of the Partido de Unidad Revolucionario (FOR—entire leftist party), led by Camila Zapata/Juana, were intercepted and forced to fight under conditions that were not advantageous for them. According to the ICT, the results were disastrous, with the loss of “middle rank committee party members and national leaders.” The ICT considers it a “miracle” that Faz Tejada was able to escape. However, the ICT still believes that the government of Nicaragua’s presidents, president of Guatemala, will soon fall.
