Dispatch. Info: Chief of Station, Leopoldville. From: Chief, Africa Division. Subject: Operational WIROGUE/1. References: A. DIR 08782 (OUT 86554); B. LEOP 0225 (IN 36750).

1. The plans and development of the WIROCUE (WIROGUE) project have reached a point whereby a summation of the progress made and the plans for the future is in order. At this writing it is anticipated that WIROCUE/1 will be on the Leopoldville scene on the first of December.

2. WIROCUE/1 had originally been recruited in Europe for a REDSOX operation. He was flown to FBERDE “black” and underwent an extensive training program. This included survival, small arms and other similar type FM training. After a year of such training the project failed to materialize; as a result, the subject was preparing for resettlement in South America. The AF Division took interest in WIROCUE/1 and, after an assessment, decided that with his peculiar background, talents and general makeup he could effectively be utilized along the lines cited in reference A. On 21 October 1960 the case was transferred officially to the Africa Division.

3. Since that date to the present, every effort has been made to psychologically orient WIROCUE/1 for this assignment and to prepare him operationally for its fulfillment. To insure the security of the operation, prevent any identification with ODIOTEK and/or KUDARK (KUBARK), and to preclude any unexpected uncovery of WIROCUE/1’s past which would associate him with his pro-KUDARK life, a number of basic steps were taken. These were:

a. WIROCUE/1 underwent plastic surgery, which changed the shape of his nose.

b. A toupee had been made for his constant use. This and the plastic surgery have altered him sufficiently to obviate any recognition.

Distribution: 2—Leopoldville.

Date typed: 14 Nov. 1960. Dispatch symbol and number: ACIW-192. File number: 201-233477.
