Incoming Classified Message. Central Intelligence Group.

Tsingtao Special Operations.

5 December 1947. IF 30065.

TSIN 032

To: WACHT  
Re: TSIN 018 (IN 26037)  
Cite: TSIN (from JRC)

Will be without funds by January. In order to maintain the standard necessary for a consular official while family and self are living on a temporary basis here, I am exceeding salary and per diem by over $100 per month. Transfer to Calcutta with family will be an additional heavy expense, and personal savings are running low. Believe that in order to maintain proper State cover, I should receive the same per diem allowances State gives for family while in transit status. Any assistance greatly appreciated.

TOR: 03492, 7 December 1967.
