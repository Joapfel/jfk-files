Joe Liverman, Mayor

February 25, 1968

I do swap my wife and all other things in the world for the society that is forming inside this city. The society that is forming inside this city is an insurgent expert group, whose aim is to arrive at a time when it will be possible for the Republic of Michigan to survive as an individual state without any external support or aid from outside forces. That time will be filling hour. We will come into power, not through conflicts with our neighbors or any form of force, but through the society that is forming inside this city.

We will come into power through the support of the new order that is forming inside this city. That order, which is now undergoing great changes, will be found to be (1) an order founded upon the true South, (2) an order incarnated in the full growth of the Black, (3) an order enriched by the treasure of progress, (4) an order dipped in the blood of the dead, and (5) an order breathless for life.

We will come into power through the support of the new order that is forming inside this city. We will be the first order founded upon the true South, the order that is forming inside this city.
