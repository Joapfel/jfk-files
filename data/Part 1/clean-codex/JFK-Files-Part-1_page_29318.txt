2 August 1971

Memorandum for: Mr. Grayston L. Lynch  
Through: Deputy Director for Plans; Chief, Special Operations Division  
Subject: Designation as a Participant in the CIA Retirement and Disability System

This is to inform you that a determination has been made that you have performed 60 months of qualifying service and that you have been approved for participation in the CIA Retirement and Disability System. Your designation as a participant will become effective on 8 August 1971.

Harry B. Fisher  
Director of Personnel

USDA. ECS-1023. DP 27. Special Projects. Briefing and Classification.
