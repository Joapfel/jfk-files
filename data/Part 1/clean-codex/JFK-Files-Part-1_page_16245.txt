2. Concerning the OAU meeting in September, SECAROB/1 emphasized that Mobutu told him that it was his intention to get the permanent headquarters of the OAU transferred to the Congo. Further, the GDRC wishes to be a strong military power in Central Africa. With this combination, Mobutu hopes that the GDRC will be the leading power in Central Africa and possibly in all of Africa.

3. While confirming his willingness to disband the S Commando if LNHARP wants him to do so, SECAROB/1 emotionally told HASLANGER that he had great respect for LNHARP and thought he was our man and would be ever loyal to LNHARP, which he believes must exercise more influence in Africa; LNHARP has always been fair to him; and that without VOTACT the S Commando could never have done its job. Prior to leaving HASLANGER's home, SECAROB/1 learned that HASLANGER might be leaving the Congo around August 1967. SECAROB/1 stated that if GUTHMAN and HASLANGER were to leave the Congo, he would be reluctant to take on his new job since personal relationships mean a great deal to him and were important in implementing his new program.
