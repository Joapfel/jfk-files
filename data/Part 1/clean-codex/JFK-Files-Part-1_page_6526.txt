15193CZ, January 1973, Staff.

GITE Contacts/New York, 10535. Contacts/Washington. Support Division/Alien Staff (Section), from Graber.

Subject: Case 59421 — Alexandra Fiz y uk.

1. Subject is named by the International Rescue Committee (IRC) in New York. A-87627.

2. Charles Steinberg, head of IRC in New York, told us that this is a very difficult case and we should wait for at least one month before servicing requirements.

3. To underline the difficult situation, Steinberg told us that he did not want to put up subject in a hotel and placed him with an employee of his organization in New York City.

4. When subject can be contacted, Joe Campoll of the New York office will handle this case.
