Date: 29 Dec 58. Routing. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 6 (Mr. Brady, WH Division, notified at 1800, 29 Dec). Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS, S/C 2. Date received: Dec 29, 1973, 12:58. Received by Cable Section. Priority. IN 42092.

To: PRITY DIR. Info: HAVA. Cite: MEXI 3019. Re: DIR 06062 (OUT 57143).

AMUTTER

1. NOEMAYR suggests following plan:
A. Avoid HAVA due security risk.
B. Arrange U.S. Naval air transportation to Guantanamo.
C. Have required supplies for trip issued from Naval Base and whatever briefing done held on base.

2. NOEMAYR also considering possible need this trip carry sidearm.

3. Alternate plan for supplies could be purchase locally MEXI and carry to jump-off point Naval Air in U.S.

End of message.

CS comment: Headquarters requested HAVA advise MEXI suggested itinerary and contact arrangements.
