Message Form. Total copies: 2. 22 August 1963. Cite Director 29659.

To Mexico City: Rico Galan case appears present excellent opportunity for hemispheric prop play as blatant example continuing effort by Castro to export revolution. Request Mexico City suggestions be best time and place to initiate such campaign as well as material best suited to place case in light which complements goal objectives and contributes to maximum export advantage in hemisphere. Request views soonest.

End of message.

WH/Cuba/CA/Swider.

Jacob D. Esterline, AC/WIL, releasing officer.

Josiah Fisher, AC/WIL/1, authenticating officer.
