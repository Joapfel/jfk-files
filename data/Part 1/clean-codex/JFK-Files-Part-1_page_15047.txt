Report on contact with agent or defector.

Subject: KIROGUE. Date: 31 January 1965. Time: 1100. Place: Kuhwaldstr.

Erne K. Daronnat, case officer.

Comments: CO dropped in to see Subject and found him busy with one of his inventions. He was paid subsistence and given cigarettes and coffee. Subject was told that there was no news regarding his immigration. CO promised to drop by on Thursday the 7th.
