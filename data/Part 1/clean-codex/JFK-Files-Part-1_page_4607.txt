7-542 CITE J'AVE 341S. A/L—Written. Director. TYPIC.

1. Evening 5 October, Orlando Bosch was given a three-year suspended sentence by Naples court when found guilty of transporting explosives without a license in April 1966. Two companions to be tried separately.

2. Evening 5 October, AMOT reported that AMAUP-2 had been telephoned by Jorge Guiterrez (Gutierrez) Ulla, chief of propaganda of MIRR, to attend press conference 6 October. AMAUP-2 also reported that at trial Jorge Vega, Bosch (Sosch)'s defense attorney, had made a statement to court to the effect that Bosch should not be convicted because he was trying to free homeland from Communist domination and in doing so was not acting any more illegally than was WOFACT, which was conducting “same type of activity” from a base located at the “Cayo Gordo” section of Naples.

3. “Cayo Gordo” undoubtedly is garble for Gordon Pass area of Naples at which berthing site for JWAVE Mark I Swift Delta 5 Sep 98.
