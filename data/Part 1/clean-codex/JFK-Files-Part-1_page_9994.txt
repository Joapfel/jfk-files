1582N CSTE Mexico City 2476. Director, Guatemala City. PERUMEM.

1. In initial talks with Liring-3 (Lering-3) in late July, Moises (Moised) Arozco Leal mentioned Mercedes Pereira, whom Liring-3 knew in Havana in 1962–64 as a Spanish revolutionary involved in support of the Guat revolution, now living in Mexico City. Liring-3 obtained her address from Ernesto (Capuano) del Vecchio.

2. Liring-3 spent four hours on 13 August with Pereira and 17-year-old Armando Pereira, who claimed he received guerrilla training in Cuba. Pereira stated her sister, Sofia Pereira, currently working at the Czech Embassy in Havana, expects transfer to the Czech Embassy in Mexico City in the “near future.” Pereira, who is in the process of becoming a Mexican citizen, said she is still in contact with Guat revolutionaries and hopes to continue supporting the Guat revolution from Mexico as a “matter of conscience.” She felt Liring-3 should meet Guat friends whom she planned to introduce in the next few days.

3. Request addressee comments and traces. Station file check produced only 1962 LIFEAT information to the effect Pereira, then living in Mexico City, was in contact with Nicaraguan Communist Edelberto (Torres) Espinoza (201-2528837).

Action: 15 August 1967. N 31815.
