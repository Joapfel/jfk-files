LITANIL-0 saw Antonio Carrillo only once at a Havana reception. L-9's two sisters in Spain lived in Madrid in an apartment above Carrillo's some years ago but told L-9 very little about him other than “nice person.” L-9 knew nothing of Carrillo's recent ouster until told by the station.

L-9 feels some of his comments about Carrillo may have been misinterpreted by FACT. He did not say Carrillo was one of Fidel's most trusted colleagues, but quoted his sister-in-law to say that since he was not a party man, Carrillo must be personally trusted by Fidel to be appointed vice minister of foreign affairs. This, in L-9's opinion, was an opinion shared by a number of people in Cuba. The vice-minister appointment was generally known in Havana functionary circles some three months before it became official. In L-9's opinion, the real power in the Foreign Affairs Ministry is Osmany Cienfuegos (OSMANI CIENFUEGOS), who is a party man.

By REG, info on Carrillo's ouster.

7 December 1966.
