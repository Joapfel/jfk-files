Classified Message. Cable Secretariat Dissemination. Precedence. Advice copy issued 9/17/73. Dissent by 9/15 SEP 107.

Secret, 211615Z February 1973, staff. Cite Caracas (2) 21221 CARA.

To: Priority Director. Info: WH/Miami.

RYBAT PLMHABYSS WKRALLY WKSCARLET-3.

Ref: Director 372698.

1. WKSCARLET-3 contacted the station the evening of 15 February 1973, at which time he first announced he did not depart for Rome as scheduled. At a meeting the following Saturday, which came after two postponements, WKSCARLET-3 stated the trip was delayed due to uncertainty about the location of future Gulf of Maracaibo talks. Tentatively, WKSCARLET-3 and the audio team are scheduled to depart 1 March if the talk city is designated by that time. If Rome, WKSCARLET-3 was instructed not to make contact with the Rome station. He made no mention of MHABYSS matters.

2. Have initiated investigation of WKSCARLET-3 as recommended in the reference. No information that he is presently on a WKSCARLET assignment to investigate MHABYSS matters.

A. Humberto Truev A and Jose Gomez.
