Dispatch.

To: Chief, W Division.

Action required—references. Action: None, for information only.

Attention: Joel N. HUEBCKER.

1. Joel N. HUEBCKER, while in Mexico City during the week of 26–28 February 1964, met with LICOCKY-1, from 1500 to approximately 1600 hours on the afternoon of 26 February 1964, in connection with his review of the LIEBCKER report leaving one meeting site. HUEBCKER noted two unidentified young men who may have been conducting a surveillance of this location. At about 1630 hours, HUEBCKER sent LICOCKY-1 on Avenida Medero, about one mile from the meeting site. The case officer was requested to query LICOCKY-1 on any significant remarks LIELOCK-1, who lives with LICOCKY-1, might have made to LICOCKY-1 before and/or after the meeting with HUEBCKER, and to report same to Headquarters.

From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Subject: LICOCKY-1—Comments on Meeting with Joel N. HUEBCKER on 26 February 1964.

Distribution: 3—C/RED.

Cross reference to dispatch symbol and number: EOMA-23027.

Date: 5 March 1964.
