Official Dispatch.

Via air. Dispatch No. R21-4-5390.

February 1964.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. From: Chief, WMD.

Subject: General—Administrative/LIFEAT. Specific—Procurement of recorders.

Reference: DCS-8143 (KAPOK 1106), 2 October 1957.

Action required: Clarification of request for recorders.

1. Paragraph four, e, of reference contains a request for ten used EXOTAPE recorders. However, it is not clear whether this request is in addition to or in place of the request contained in MEXI 918, dated 9 August 1957. The recorders requested in MEXI 918 were forwarded to the Station under cargo numbers 20369 and 20370 on 16 and 17 September 1957 (see DIR 35369 and DIR 35748), and probably arrived at the Station after the referenced dispatch was written.

2. There are no used EXOTAPE recorders available at Headquarters for shipment to the Station. However, KURIOT has advised the Desk that should any used recorders become available at Headquarters or at other field stations, they will make every effort to send them to Mexico City. As the Station probably knows, EXOTAPE recorders are no longer manufactured.

3. KURIOT requested that the Station be informed that KURIOT appreciates the technical report contained in the reference. They will investigate further the technical problems experienced by the Station; the Station will be advised of any significant developments. KURIOT considers that six months’ use from the reverse heads, under LIFEAT operating conditions, is quite good.

4. In view of the unavailability of used EXOTAPE recorders and the recent shipment of ten new REVERSE recorders, Headquarters will take no further action on the referenced request unless requested to do so by the Station.

Remove from Project KDMANCY.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officer. Authenticating officer.

Record copy.

L. C. KING  
Chief, WMD
