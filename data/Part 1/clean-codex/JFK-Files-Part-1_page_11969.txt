T 832260. IN 134684. SALV 20685.

Cable 1023252, January 1974, Staff. Cite San Salvador 20685. To: Director. Info: WH/Miami.

1. Request traces on Mr. Joseph R. Merola, age 48; born Pennsylvania, residence of Miami Shores, Florida. Merola claims to be an aeronautical consultant with Patterson Aircraft Company of California. We suspect he is trying to negotiate a deal to establish a helicopter plant in El Salvador. During talks at social parties with embassy personnel, he indicated previous work with SKHERALD.

2. File: defer. E2 IMPDET.
