Telephone RYEAT UFAH-671. Dispatch No. LFAX-671. Fill in: 201-307587. Date: 1 December 1969. Index: No. To: Chief of Station, NH/Miami. From: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Subject: MREAT/TYPIC/AMCHALK — AMCHALK-5 meeting with AMGHOST representative. Action: FYI.

1. ANCHALK-5 was met by NEBECKER under secure conditions in New York City from 1600 to 2230 on 25 November following his meeting with an AMGHOST representative in Montreal. The following covers A-5's narrative of events. Previous correspondence covers the preparation for the meeting.

2. A-5 arrived in Montreal as scheduled and proceeded to the Plaza Motor Hotel as instructed by CUIS letter. He confirmed reservations upon arrival at Montreal Airport. The hotel was located in a commercial/residential area about fifteen minutes from the center of the city. (This was confirmed by NEBECKER and NOLTHAM.) According to A-5, no Spanish speakers appeared to be employed by the hotel. French was the dominant language, with little English spoken. A-5 checked into the hotel at 1600 on 24 November. At 0645 on 26 November, he was called by a person identifying himself as a friend of ALBERTO (signer of the letter which made contact arrangements for Montreal). The caller, in Cuban Spanish, said, “Calls are difficult to make. ALBERTO will call you between seven and nine.” A.M. or P.M. was not specified, nor did A-5 have the opportunity to ask. At twenty-five minutes to eight that same evening, 26 November, ALBERTO.
