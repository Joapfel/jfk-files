Extracts from LIREALM-1 contact reports

10 May 1967

LOGINOV played tennis at Mundet with S. S. Abdel Rahman Hassan Abdel-RAHMAN, 1st Secretary and Consul at UAR Embassy. This was the first time they had played together. He played doubles with LIREALM and his wife.

14 May 1967

LIREALM played tennis at Mundet with LOGINOV. LOGINOV's son Dmitriy was there; he speaks absolutely no Spanish or English. Also played with Jacques LEMAIRE. LOGINOV said his wife had had several heart attacks in Mexico and cannot play tennis. LOGINOV said he wanted Dmitriy to learn English, like his sister in Moscow. LOGINOV said he knew Jean BINEAU when asked if he knew any French diplomats. LOGINOV claimed to have been in the Soviet Navy, then immediately entered the Foreign Service Institute and went straight into foreign service after that.

17 May 1967

LOGINOV plays tennis left-handed. An instructor commented that he was convinced LOGINOV (LOG INOV) had played much more than the one year he admits to. The instructor said he had never seen LOGINOV play with an instructor.

20 May 1967

LIREALM saw LOGINOV at Mundet but did not play with him.

25 June 1967

LOGINOVA said she was a chemist, but did not say whether she had ever worked in that profession. The LOGINOVs (LOGNOVs) never eat any food at the club, but LOGINOV drinks beer there. The week before, LOGINOV played in a mixed doubles match with a 16-year-old girl and won a small silver cup. LOGINOV had a Russian movie camera with him and took pictures of people at the club. LOGINOV talked to a Mexican named Jose Manuel LNU, connected with some Mexican ministry that assists SovEmb personnel; LOGINOVA said Jose Manuel was very sympathetic to them; LOGINOV said they had met at the Canadian or French Embassy.

10 June 1967

LOGINOVA seems intelligent, educated, quiet but friendly. Her Spanish is not as bad as LOGINOV had indicated.
