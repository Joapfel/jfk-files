Attention: Miss Jane Hinslow

Chief, Contact Division, CO

Concern about Physical Security of Isola Vassiliow, Inc. Representative in Berlin

1. A source of this division, who has been associated with Isola Vassiliow in the I&O, has transmitted a letter dated 23 February 1953, written by her. She has been Director of the Berlin Office of the I&O for approximately three years. A copy of the letter is attached.

2. The source knows Vassiliow as a calm person not given to hysterics.

3. This information is being transmitted also to TSO.

4. Any inquiry concerning this memorandum should be directed to Mr. A. F. Czajkowski, extension 2775.

G. N. Ashraft
