18 February 1963

Memorandum for: Director of Training

Attendees: Chief, Covert Training/Operations School; Chief, T&D Training

Subject: Request for Covert Training of Luis Alberto Sandoval

1. It is requested that full-time covert training be provided at Mexico City, Mexico, for approximately ten days beginning on or about 19 February 1963 for Luis Alberto Sandoval.

2. The objective of the training is to provide the subject with the basic principles of claustrophobic tradecraft to better prepare him as an FI agent operating inside a denied area.

3. In addition to the basic tradecraft, training should emphasize reporting techniques and include appropriate secret writing instruction, as arranged by the case officer with T&D.

4. Subject is a PARACHUTE cultural attaché in the Ministry of Public Security in Mexico City. A Provisional Operational Approval was requested on 26 July 1962 and its status is being checked. (C-101331; DFOB: 1915, Oviedo, Spain)

5. Responsible Headquarters case officer for the training is N. B. Mullen, extension 6172.

Oscar D. McCollum  
Training Officer/SAS

Approved:

Chief, Special Affairs Staff
