Outgoing message.

Coming to Spain. At first meeting, Macias talked about the possibility of blowing up Cuban aircraft flying from Madrid. This was discarded at the second meeting because of the problem of finding explosives for that mission. Macias instead indicated his organization was interested in killing a senior Cuban visitor to Madrid, a member of the local Cuban Embassy, or, if this was not possible, an important Spanish Communist. The only name mentioned was that of Nicolas Guillen (Nicolás Guillén), well-known Cuban poet and longtime Communist, who is currently in Spain to receive a literary honor. Macias gave “Orlando” the mission of obtaining two or three handguns from Rota military personnel. He commented that “since GIs sell everything,” Orlando should have little problem buying guns for little money. On the same day he left the U.S. (30 January), “Orlando” received a phone call from Armando Lopez Estrada (Armando López Estrada), who confirmed to him that he should purchase “two bedspreads” (two guns). Per Macias’s briefing, “Orlando” is to expect contact at his hotel within the days after his arrival by two men sent by Lopez, who will identify themselves as “Number 6.” They will refer to “Orlando” as “Number 6” and show their knowledge of Brigade 250B history.
