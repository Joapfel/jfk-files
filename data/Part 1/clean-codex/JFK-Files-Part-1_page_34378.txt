Releasing officer. Coordinating officers.

EXFILE in C5, file No. 200-120-38.

To: JIMWAVE. From: Director.

To: WAVE. Info: FID.

RYBAT PERUMEN NHVIPER ZRPERUSAL.

1. Following message relayed: 15 Mar 65, from DOSQUE, MINCON, Havana, to RICHARD CONTINENTAL, France. Send immediately highly qualified personnel for bulldozers C-DIO. Constantly breaking down due to defects in manufacture.

2. WAVE, please exploit. Several acts of sabotage were carried out against RICHARD tractors in 1964 using abrasive in engine blocks. Since abrasive was discovered by Cubans, line should now be, “How do you like our more sophisticated method? Tractors our specialty, the heater is better.”

3. Implication should naturally be that acts were accomplished by anti-Castro group whose strength lies both inside and outside Cuba.

End of message.

Raford W. Herbert, AC/WHD.

C/WHD/C/A.

John L. Hart, DC/WHD/C.

Authenticating officer.
