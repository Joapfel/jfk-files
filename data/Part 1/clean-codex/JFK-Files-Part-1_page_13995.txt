The September 2 issue of the Daily Bulletin of the Government of Mexico states several changes in the personnel of the Cuban Embassy during the month of August. New arrivals were Raúl Arboleyo Rogares, Counselor for Cultural Affairs; Roelio Elidio Lopez, Second Secretary; and Elpidio Furez Cometanos, Attorney. The single departure during the month was Armando Sardina Garcia, Attaché.
