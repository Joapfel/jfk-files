United States Information Agency.

Action: IOS. RH1193.

Message No. 3516. Date: 7 May 1969.

From: American Embassy, London.

To: USIA, Washington, D.C. 802.

For IOS McNichol. For guidance of President of American Correspondents in London, would like to know whether Negro Press International of Chicago is a legitimate clientele.

Also would like to know if U.S. intelligence community has any information of recent activities of Richard Gibson, NPI representative here, which would make his claim to be reputable press representative suspect. FYI Scotland Yard has refused to issue police pass to Gibson and association wishes to support his request unless he has record which clearly inimical to U.S. interests. Would appreciate cabled reply containing estimate which could be passed to association officers purely as guidance. King.

201-300052.

J. B. King, Chairman, House Appropriations Committee; H. M. Johnson, Vice-Chairman; and J. O. Taylor, III, Secretary.
