18. (Embassy comment) The Ambassador comments: “The LEGATT was different information as to whether Bosch was exfiltrated (expiltrated) or is or is not in Venezuela.” Garcia spoke to LEGATT Sunday morning, 18 October, and on the afternoon of 12 October told him “informally” that the President would shortly advise him that Bosch would be turned over to us. This was repeated to LEGATT by DISIP Director Raul Ojimen & Gainza and other DISIP officials (including Garcia) in a meeting the morning of 13 October. Conceivably Garcia can be playing a dark game, but the discrepancies are noteworthy; I have not yet received any official word as to the President's decision.
