283930Z CITE SALISBURY 2573. Director info Brussels, Kinshasa, Pretoria, London.

Ref: Director 23009.

According Brick/1, who obtained info from Capt. O'Donovan, ops manager of Central African Airways, Wicks. Wicks represents Air Trans Africa in London, was there on 12 July, and no indication he subsequently left London. Function in London described as liaison with Autair of London and to arrange mercenary flight to Congo. However, these operations have “virtually ceased” and Air Trans Africa now appears to rely solely on an airlifting petrol from Francistown, Botswana to Livingston, Zambia using company's two Francistown-registered DC-4s.
