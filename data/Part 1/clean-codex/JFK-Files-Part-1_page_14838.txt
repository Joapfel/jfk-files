Classified message.

To: Director. From: Paris. Action: SR 6. Info: CI, CI/OPS, FI, FI/OPS, EE 4, WE 4, OS 2, S/C 2, DPD 4. Date: 485 JM/DLD/26 March 1959. Routine. IN 37247.

To: DIR. FRAN FROB, REDWOOD, REDSOX, AEACRE, AEASPIC, SECUR. Info: CITE PARI 5504.

1. Re subject LCFLUTTER, AEASPIC, OK foreign intelligence, minor exaggerations in life history.

2. Subject instructed to return AMBERT, put affairs in order, and return to Paris (PARI) 6 April when ROSTEDT interview scheduled. He will be prepared to depart, but no report or commitment made. (Repeat)

3. FYI: Subject without any valid document. Ergo, travel somewhat risky and cannot stay in hotels. If disappearance noticed, subject could not safely stay chez ANNICK. Keeping him in a safehouse awkward but possible if not prolonged and also not riskless. You may wish to alert HBELECTOR for trip to France immediately after ROSTEDT interview.

End of message.

27 March 1959.
