1. Amb. BOGO has forwarded recommendation to ODACID through BOGO station channel that prime adviser with experience in elections and public relations techniques be made available to assist Carlos Lleras Restrepo in his campaign for presidency of Colombia. ODACID/KUBARK discussion now underway concerning requirement and desirability of such assistance.

2. In event decision made to carry out this program KUBARK will be action agency. Since Tichborn still under consideration for possible Bolivia assignment, HQS now considers Redlich best qualified individual presently available for this task. Redlich cover would probably be that of private PBPRIME citizen who is specialist in election techniques and has wide experience in this field. HQ and BOGO station would establish nominal cover sufficient to establish bona fides and preclude weakening Redlich security upon return to Mexico.

3. Request MEXI (Mexico City) comments on suitability and availability of Redlich for period three to four weeks sometime prior to Colombian.
