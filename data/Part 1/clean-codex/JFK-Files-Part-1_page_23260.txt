Classified message. Date: 02 January 1958. To: Director. From: Havana. Action: WH 5. Info: COP, CI, CI/OPS, FI, FI/OPS 2, PP 2, PP/OPS, S/C 2. IN 13410.

To Director, info cite HAVA 119. Re: DIR 00452 (OUT 76181).

1. Arthur G. Vaivada believes he can arrange a visit with Castro through casual contact with the provincial 26 July leader he met through AMOURETTE-9. Cover would be as an American representative of Survey Corporation interested in penetrating the censorship barrier to learn the extent of Communist penetration of the movement. The provincial leader is voluntarily arranging Vaivada's interview with Faustino Perez (HKH 1535) in Havana within the next ten days.

2. Vaivada (Viavada) is eager to undertake the mission and well qualified in view of wartime training in jungle techniques, knowledge of Cuba, language capability, and clandestine experience. He would want to send his family to Costa Rica and join them after completion of the task. This would necessitate finding a new AMOURETTE-B cut-out, but the station can find a temporary replacement pending arrival of FREAPANE.

3. Vaivada would need two to three weeks to lay on careful arrangements and probably could leave for the Sierra at the end of January. Request headquarters comments. Released 022105Z.

CS comment: Headquarters attaches sufficient importance to Castro movement to justify (IS-TIFY) coverage from several angles.
