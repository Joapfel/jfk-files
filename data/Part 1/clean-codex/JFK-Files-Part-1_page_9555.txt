Kono: 10 February.

8 February. Discussion with Williams regarding theme of congress and MI possibilities.

Talk with Genby regarding project—he produced outline draft.

9 February. Met Mr. Hilger, preliminary talk regarding personalities involved in Congress and plan for future meetings.

Conference at home of Jim Burnham (Williams, Droller, Smith, Burnham, Glasser).

At this meeting Mr. Burnham, in response to questions, stated his enthusiasm for the idea. He proposed that Sidney Hook be sent over to Germany for a few weeks on an exploratory mission—and to render what assistance he may in connection with planning. He expressed his willingness to go over to Berlin sometime in advance of the congress, under cover of an invitation he has received from the Free University, to actively assist in planning the agenda and other details. He is making up a list of proposed delegates to be invited; thinks it important someone from South America be included. He is particularly opposed to the inclusion of Sartre in the list of delegates; is not sure of Plievier but not opposed. Arrangements made to continue conferring, probably next week.

10 February. First draft of project completed.
