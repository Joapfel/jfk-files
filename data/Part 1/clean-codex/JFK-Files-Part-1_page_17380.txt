Date: 29 September 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5. Info: ADDP, C/C1, C/CCG 2, C/F1 2, C/WE 2, C/FE 6, S/C 2.

Classified message.

IN 31052 A MEXI 2087.

DIR INFO ROME CITE MEXI 2087.

RYBAT.

Ref: A. DIR 38428 (OUT 80619); B. MEXI 2049 (IN 29411).

1. Plan brief Ratigan as follows:

A. Background Ricardo Jose Zevada and Alejandro Carrillo Marcor, and suggest Ratigan observe who their closest contacts are in the presidential party, the degree of their access to the President and other ranking Mexican officials, and identify any obvious foreign contacts in countries visited.

B. Identify individuals in the official party who express anti-U.S. and/or pro-Castro sympathies.

C. Identify individuals who seem to be closest to the President.

D. All information heard about the identity of the next Mexican President.

2. Above requirements are guides only, and station is aware that Ratigan probably will not have sufficient access to report other than fragmentary information. Nevertheless, Mexico will appreciate any information developed by Ratigan.

End of message.
