26 June 1965 (19199)

1. ACC LIENVOY 15 July Mr. Breck (phonetic) called SOVEMB from POPRIME, asked for Soviet consul, who was not in. Called twice again 15 July and was told to contact Kostikov (221-305652), who speaks English.

2. 3952 hours 19 July unknown Mexican called SOVEMB on behalf of an American who had just arrived by bus from POPRIME and who had spoken with Kostikov.

Since K was not in, the man called again at 1124 hours and said he was Mr. Breck. Arranged to see K the same day at 1200 hours at the embassy.

3. LIEMPTY reported a young American-type male entered SOVEMB at 1233 hours 10 July. LIEMBRACE surveillance was mounted when he left the embassy at 1317 hours. He was not dropped until 23 July, when he was identified by LIEMBRACE through the hotel as Eugene Allen Brekke (Eugene Allyn Brekke), “radio announcer,” 1257 Robert Street, Des Moines, Iowa.

4. EMH check showed protection welfare case July 1964: Eugene Allyn Brekke (Eugene Allen Brekke), DPOB 4 May 1937, Britt, Iowa. Residence: 1257 Robert Street, Hillside, New Jersey. Brekke could not pay hotel bill and sought embassy aid.

5. 23 July STA obtained access to large envelope containing letters to Brekke. Envelope delivered from Julia Brekke, 605 Stockdale.
