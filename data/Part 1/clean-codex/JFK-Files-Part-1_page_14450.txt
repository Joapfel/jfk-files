Cite Director. 12 21 05 May 70.

To: WH/Miami. Info: Madrid. TYPIC AMAUTO.

Refs: A. Director 030893. B. UFAA-1978.

1. View ref B, HQs sanguine about possibilities of “chance” approach by SAMKO to AMAUTO-1. As reasonably well-off retired person, SAMKO could naturally go to Madrid as tourist. HQs wonders whether this would hold water unless wife accompanied him. Request Miami investigate this point.

2. Approach by SAMKO would almost certainly not lead to recruitment, but would offer personal reading of AMAUTO-1 based on what appears to be warm entrée provided by fondly remembered past association. If appropriate, SAMKO could provide link to “other world” in case AMAUTO-1 ever needed help. Latter scheduled travel Havana in June 70 and return Madrid prior posting Cuba. Judging from AMBEDEW-1 and AMDELAY-1 cases, brief return to Cuba might prove turning point in any decision to defect. Therefore, any meeting with SAMKO should be prior June departure.

3. In event possible SAMKO travel to Madrid, we would need continued...
