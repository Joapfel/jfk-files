May 17, 1968

A-403. Holman. Case 57,473.

Subject: Barbaro Lisinski, née Larsson-Lagervist (Larsson-Lagerwist), Barberg, Hilma, Maria; International Rescue Committee, Stockholm, Sweden. As for request, the following is biographical data on subject.

Date of birth: 6 August 1918. From: Surahammar, Vastmanlands Lan, Sweden.

1925–29: Elementary school, Surahammar, Sweden.  
1929–32: Private secondary school, Surahammar, Sweden.  
1932–36: High school “Whitlocksa Sanskolan,” Stockholm.  
1936–39: Practice at different hospitals, Stockholm.  
1939–41: Practice as dentist assistant, Stockholm.  
1941–43: “Eastman-Institutet” School for Dentist Assistants, Stockholm; diploma.  
1943–44: Employed as dentist assistant, Costersund.  
1944–46: “Scophianemnet” nursery school, Stockholm.

8 June 1946: Married Dr. Michal (Michaal) Lisinski, journalist, Radio Free Europe, Stockholm.

House life with four children to 1968.

January 1968 to present, with International Rescue Committee, Stockholm. Husband is vice chairman of Polish Refugee Council in Sweden.

Subject speaks Swedish, English, German, and some Polish.
