For RIO: Some members of CHICOM delivery to the Seventh Congress of International Union of Architects (UIA) held Havana 29 Sept–3 Oct afterwards went MEXI to attend UIA General Assembly and International Symposium on Architecture. MEXI group now scheduled leave MEXI 0100 hours 19 Oct for RIO. Following biographical data on three members of group known to be MEXI:

A. Yang Ting-Pao 2799 1694 1405, 201-735346; born 20 Aug 1901, Honan; U.S. educated; member NPC; member Academia Sinica; vice president, UIA.

B. Liang Szu-Cheng 2733 1835 2052, 201-736781; born 1901, Tokyo; U.S. educated; member CCP; deputy NPC and CPPCC; vice chairman China Architectural Society.

C. Wu Ching-Hsiang 0702 2529 4382, 201-736317; born circa 1919, Manchuria; French educated; chairman Architecture Department Tung Chi University, Shanghai.

2. During stay group MEXI unilateral approaches made to Yang Ting-Pao and Liang Szu-Cheng for purposes of assessment and possible demising, defection or recruitment in place. Following contacts with Yang by former partner in architectural firm China and by member U.S. firm Yang previously associated with, Yang accepted interview with KUBARK (RUBARK) staffer who used cover continued.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officer. Authenticating officer. Reproducible by other than this documentation if needed.
