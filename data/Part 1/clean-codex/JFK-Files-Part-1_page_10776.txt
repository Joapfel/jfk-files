4 January 1967.

WAVE. 542117Z. CITE JMWAVE CAES. PRIORITY DIRECTOR. NO NIAAT. FEDERAL PBRUMEN.

Reference: JMWAVE 4587.

1. The following to JMWAVE officer from Key West official on highly confidential basis. Afternoon 3 January, Rolando Masferrer from Key West jail made two telephone calls to New York City, one to a person named “Alex,” who was not there, and a second to a person named “Father Montreal” (possibly Montreal or “don’t real”). From fragments of the overheard Spanish-language conversation, the source, who understands Spanish fairly well, believed that Masferrer told Montreal that the latter should arrange to get Masferrer out of jail, that he should tell him that he had better get him out or he would go to the newspapers. The source stated that Masferrer appeared furious and was talking wildly. Masferrer later made reference to someone getting in touch with “Lang” or “Long,” apparently in connection with arranging Masferrer’s release. The source could not determine the location of Lang or his affiliation or significance in the current affair.

The following officer’s E.S.O. is authorized: M.J. Brinton, C.O.
