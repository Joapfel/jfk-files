FYS: AMLEVE/15 reports telephone call from Gustavo Ortiz informing him that Erasto (Fernandez) Betancourt will arrive in Miami on 27 September 1967. Ortiz stated Fernandez will seek information from Cuban exiles in Miami who may know or have information on Cuban guerrillas in Venezuela. Ortiz asked AMCLEVE/15 to work with Fernandez during his stay in Miami; AMCLEVE/15 agreed.
