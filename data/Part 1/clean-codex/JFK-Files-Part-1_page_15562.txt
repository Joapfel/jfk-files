Classified message.

Origin: Dan Riesciur:nye. Unit: AF/W/2, 1651. Extension: 4618. Date: 3 April 1961. Last.

Outgoing routings.

To: Leopoldville. From: Director. Confidential: [REDACTED] AF 3. Info: COP, C/C4, C/CI, C/F1 2, C/EE 3, S/C 2.

PRIY LEOP. WROGUE RYBAT. CITE DIR 33071.

References: A. LEOP 1112 (IN 29376); B. MUNT 5132 (IN 28066); C. LEOP 1069 (IN 25116), paragraph 3. Par CSN 43-43.

1. Our first interest in A Airforce is to see that it filled by a non-Belgium. All things being possible, we would also like intelligence penetration and still hope for this, but in view priority will not interfere direct Germany contribution.

2. Since Swanson unmitigating (although probably suspects KUBARK interest), you will have to ensure via XI WINGUE/1 that you represent in discussions. Assume role of TPELIDE officer assigned to this type developments. Adequate explanation to Swanson, who reportedly discreet. His company confirmed that a cable in French had been sent W/1 on 28 March and Swanson presently in Leopoldville.

3. W/1 not originally cast for role type he has assumed. We definitely not willing permit him free walk in Europe making contacts and/or contracts at will. Indeed, unless a close and hard hand is kept on him, he may likely end up more of a liability than an agent. We are hard-headed and smart and realize CO will have to be on his toes and equally hardheaded, but he must not be allowed to get out of hand, and our reading is that he will respect this.

Coordinating officers.

Releasing officer. Authenticating officer.

Routing. Lg H. Lg M. C 70 Hw.
