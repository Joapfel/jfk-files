Islamic Publications

Wrote an article entitled “I Break with the Stalinists,” which allegedly stated, among other things, that the expenses of publishing a magazine he was putting out had been borne by the Chinese Communists.

At one time, ISAACS published a monthly English-language magazine, issued first as a commercial job (jay) from the Mercury Press and later printed from a small printing outfit purchased by ISAACS. It has been stated that after publishing the article “I Break with the Stalinists,” ISAACS discontinued the magazine and sold his printing equipment. It has been stated that the small sum realized from the sale was not turned back to the Chinese Communists but was turned over to the Trotsky Cause (Trotaki Cause).
