DIR CITE WAVE 7629. TYPIC AMOT. 1 December 1965. No. 05856.

For your information on basis AMOT report DD 1699 of 30 November 1965.

AMOT-2 obtained the following from Mario Barrera Diaz, who in New York and regular AMOT sub-source. While visiting New York offices “El Tiempo” 30 November, Diaz was informed by Pedro Yanes of MIRR organization that Orlando Bosch Avila would meet Senator Dodd on 3 December, at which time Bosch would complain about “persecution, insults and other atrocities” committed against his person by OBYOKE officials.

CFN 7629, AMOT DD 1699, 30 November 1965, AMOT-2, Mario Barrera Diaz, New York, El Tiempo, 30 November, Pedro Yanes, MIRR, Orlando Bosch Avila, Senator Dodd, 3 December.
