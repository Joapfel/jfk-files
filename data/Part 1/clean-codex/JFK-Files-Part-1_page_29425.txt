Telepouch. Dispatch No. FVSS-11752. Date: 19 March 1968. To: Chief of Station, Vietnam. Info: None. From: Acting Chief, Far East Division. Subject: Nomination of career agent, IDEN. Action: See below. References: None.

HQS pleased to nominate IDEN, a GS-14 career agent, for an assignment to Vietnam. DOB: 14 June 1923. Subject married with two sons, ages 19 and 14, and a daughter, age 22. Slight French. Subject joined WOFRM in February 1961, after completing 21 years of service with the U.S. Army. His last assignment while in the Army was a two-year tour of duty in Laos as a captain in the Special Forces. After a brief training and administrative processing period in HQS, he was assigned PCS to JMWAVE in August 1961 and has been assigned there as a PM special operations officer since that date.

Subject is a proficient and competent operations officer whose performance during his assignment to JMWAVE has continuously shown outstanding proficiency in the supervision and management of indigenous agents. He maintained the morale of his 30 agents and kept them at a high level of proficiency by a strong training schedule and by planning and implementing operations in the fields of reconnaissance, caching, deception, and special operations/intelligence collection operations. Subject is a hard worker, capable administrator, and an able agent handler who gets along well with his contemporaries. He is presently assigned as a section supervisor in the Special Operations Branch and has two officers and one secretary.
