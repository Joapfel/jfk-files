25 March 1985, 301193.

1. On 23 March Mercedes Magalhaes Lirio, personal secretary ALSUPER-2, introduced Mylkes to Annell Matikainen, who was visiting Rio for approximately one month. Matikainen said although her future plans were indefinite, she had no intention of returning to Perumen (PERU), where her parents were assigned. She said she might accept employment in New York City or would return to Europe. However, Lirio is attempting to interest Matikainen in remaining in Rio and asked Mylkes if Matikainen did stay, could he direct her to some American company or U.S. government agency for employment? Mylkes left the matter open, saying he would have to have more information on Matikainen’s qualifications.

2. The only information in Rio station records regarding Matikainen contained in the reference was supplied by Alsurah, who is now on two-year leave from his service and only in Rio on infrequent visits. Will attempt to elicit more details from Alsurah at the first opportunity.

3. Appreciate headquarters forwarding any additional information on Matikainen which would tend to confirm Alsurah’s doubts about.
