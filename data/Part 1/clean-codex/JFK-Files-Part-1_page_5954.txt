Director. M. Havana. C/Bell 13 (HR Croller, WH DIY notified 1000 23 Dec). 23 Dec 22:22, 60 Duque. D. DOP, COP, ADCP/A, WH 3, S/C 2. Priority. IN 21843.

FRITY WAVE INFO PRITY DIR CITE HAVA 7288. JMZIP-AVLT.

1. On 23 Dec, Robert M. Geddes (who works with AMPAN-22) contacted CO after failure to meet Ralph Diaz Hanscom and asked CO to deliver special airdrop map to Miami for “Clarence.”

2. Geddes explained that he had talked with “Clarence” in Miami and was told that earlier drop map brought by Villalon was not suitable. New map is in India ink, prepared by engineer Cresco, who heads the Public Works Department in Cienfuegos. Cresco passed map to Mario Boland, who gave it to Geddes. Geddes said airdrop special for Limas, Ramirez, Montiel, and portion for Duque.

Geddes said preliminary drop signals would consist of one red light, one white light, one rad light about 15 feet distance apart. Clockwise movement of lights indicates prepared to receive. Group requesting arms and ammunition for 400 men plus 100 pistols. Also extra ammunition for those already in place.

3. Map being sent WAVE by courier arriving 24 Dec.

4. Geddes said that he would contact Hector Febles (FR 3-1962) on arrival since Febles had direct contact with “Clarence.”

Filed 23/1750Z.

End of message.
