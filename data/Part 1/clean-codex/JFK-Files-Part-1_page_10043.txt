Director info JEWAVE 985

PBRUMEN LITAMIL-7 LISAMPAN

6 Apr 68 W 86257

1. LITAMIL-7 retired by Embassy effective 31 March 1968.

LIENVOY take 29 March reflecting this development received Station 29 March. LITAMIL-7 last day of work. LITAMIL-7 did not notify C-87966 LITAMIL-3, claiming to be waiting for subsequent meetings with Embassy personnel to determine type and amount of severance pay. Refused to bring out anything from LIRING (address books, keys, etc.) for fear it might affect final settlement. Asked Chief LIRING if she could purchase old typewriter she had been using 22 years, but was refused.

2. LITAMIL-7 was paid 60,000 pesos 2 April. Will get 7,000 on 11 April and final 2,500 end April. Above totals proper compensation for 36 years’ service, based on Mexican law.

3. Although LITAMIL-7 retirement eliminates only Station physical asset at LIRING, development provides opportunity to activate LISAMPAN/CHARLIE. As addressees aware, LITAMIL-7 had been isolated completely from any but most innocuous conversations. Her replacement by wife LIRING staffer may provide interesting information. Station plans activate CHARLIE week 14 April, after week of take-on test.
