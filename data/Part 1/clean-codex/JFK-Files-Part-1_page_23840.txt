Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Paris; Chief, V5. Info: Chief, V5. From: Chief of Station, London. Subject: KUDESK STABCY LCHARVEST QKPREY “Revolution” Address List.

Action required—references. Ref: OPFA 79410, 3 May 1965.

London Station was able to make only a single copy of the list, and, due to its bulk and the length of time required to copy it, we have not retained a copy. We doubt that the entire list, which runs to several thousand names (mostly in the U.S.) would be useful to Paris Station, but Headquarters may be able to supply an extract of names to suit your needs or the whole thing if you wish.

George H. KATZENBACH.

Distribution: Chief of Station, Paris; Chief, V5; Chief, V5.

Copy. CS copy. Date typed: 13 May 1965. Date dispatched: 14 May 1955. Gross reference to. Dispatch symbol and number: OFT-A-43.113. Headquarters file number. SRC PRT.
