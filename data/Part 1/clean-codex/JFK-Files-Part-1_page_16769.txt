SCW/S. C/WN/S.

To: Bonn, Paris, Hague, Mexico City. Info: Lopez, Frankfurt. From: Director. Confidential: D/OC 2. Info: DCP, ADOP, C/E 2, C/W 2, C/WN 2, S/C.

To Bonn, Paris, Hague, Mexico City. Info: London, Frankfurt.

Cite DIR 26254.

RYBAT OOLONG.

Reference DIR 25991 (not sent London, Frankfurt).

1. RYBAT reference alerts addresses to official visits to their countries by Adolfo Lopez Mateos, President of Mexico, in May and April. Also authorizes and sets up arrangements for Lopez SEM/RECETTE messages through KUBARK channels to and from high Mexican officials and KUBARK Mexico City.

2. For clear understanding, the following conference or communication procedures are established:

A. All traffic from the field (Lopez location) will be addressed Mexico City, info Director; CPIM; RYBAT; with pilot “For Lopez.”

B. All traffic to the field (Lopez location) will be addressed to the station, info Director; CPIM; RYBAT; with pilot “For Lopez.”

C. Communicators will ZFF all star transmissions.

If cipher text should be received for transmission, the change still applies.

3. Under no circumstances should anyone use this personnel to pick up or deliver traffic.

DD/P.

Releasing Officer.

Q: AD, OC-S/C, OC-ED, EE, WE, WI.
