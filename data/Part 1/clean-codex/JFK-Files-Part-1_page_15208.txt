Mrs. Elbet's brother's first wife (friends)

Mr. and Mrs. Dr. Louis Elbet  
Tallard (Hautes-Alpes)  
France

26 October 1959

“Tired from a long journey”—signed the Pelican.

And now I am in Cha-cha land. Rio is a magnificent town. The principal industries are pleasure and love. If I were a Brazilian I would say, here we make love. Maybe this name is less poetic than Rio de Janeiro (Rio de Janerio), but it is more poetic in the way it would sense. Louis will make much money if he were to come here in treating venereal disease cases. I think I will be here for several months, and after that I think I will come and see you.

Now I am in São Paulo (San Paolo), which is more magnificent (magnificant) than Rio. In the movies you can see Burt Lancaster (Bart Lancaster) and Brigitte Bardot talking in fluent Portuguese (Portugese).

I am now learning a new language, Portuguese (Portugese), which seems to be very much like Spanish. Exactly! Everything now becomes mixed in my head. And written in English, German, Russian, French, Georgian, Spanish (Spanien). If I go to China I will be lost—if I have to learn that language.

I think often of you. How are you and how are the girls? Blico, Irene, and Cathy, I would like to have a letter from each one. It has been almost a year since I have seen them. At the same time I am writing to my brother.

How is everyone? My best regards to them.
