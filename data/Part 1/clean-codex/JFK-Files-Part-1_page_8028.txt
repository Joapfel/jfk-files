HKH-737. Local File Fol. 10-30. Eman, Ctn Reports, Vol. EN-737. To: Pte.-2. Number of documents: 3. P: First Name, Initials. Alfredo Amoraro, FI. Franklin D. Malloch. Distribution: Paris, via Mass; File. Orally to: Embassy, MA. Reference: HKH-318 (memo to Embassy, 19 May 1954).

Sources, Operational Data, and Comments:

1. AMPUTER advised GOUVERNY on 7 March 1954 that he had been instructed by his chief to prepare a “paquete” (frame-up) of subject, and as directed, he had prepared a false report emphasising subject’s communist connections and activities. He made available the original draft of his memorandum, which is identical to a memorandum received by Ambassador CARDENIER from the head of the Cuban Army on 6 March. Also included with the memorandum to the Ambassador was a copy of a letter allegedly sent from Paris by subject to Fidel CASTRO, leader of the attempted uprising in Santiago de Cuba in July 1953, and presently serving a sentence at the lale (Isle) of Pinochet penitentiary.

2. It is not known at this time whether the letter included with the memorandum sent to Ambassador CARDENIER is a copy of a legitimate piece of correspondence, or whether this also has been fabricated by the SIM to give added strength to their character assassination effort. We will endeavor to clarify this point at a future meeting with AMPUTER. Copies of the letter to CASTRO are forwarded herewith to Headquarters and Paris.
