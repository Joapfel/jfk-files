I. Pinto and other folk sights. It would be necessary for us to invite the Pintos to dinner, to have maintained contact with them through tennis at least twice a week. There have been occasional dinners among our cultural clubs.

We could also invite Valentin with or without the Pintos. Valentin has done us, or at least, one favor which we have not reciprocated: an invitation to the Moscow (Mccow) Circus.

Gloria's feeling about the U.S. I believe that Gloria is less anti-American than the average middle-class Mexican (Texican). She has travelled to the U.S. many times for vacations and shopping. She knows the U.S. rather well, but she does not seem pro-U.S.

Recruitability of Gloria. I am convinced that she would never agree to work for the U.S. She might consider working for a false-flag “American” (Warcrian) if she were told that Val had been observed participating in some heinous espionage act, supplying arms and money to the Communist student agitators, for example. Her ultimate objectives are not clear in my mind. I doubt that she could ever hope to recruit Valentin to defect to Mexico (Nexico). I am sure she could never recruit him as an agent of Mexico (Nexico). It does seem possible that she might be able to get him to reveal his disappointments and grievances (oblivances)—information which might be of value to us.

FBI-I and II. At the moment I have insufficient information about the Pintos to prepare a complete FBI-I or II. If it is deemed of interest, we could exchange invitations during the next few weeks in order to obtain the necessary information.
