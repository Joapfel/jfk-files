Dispatch.

From: Chief, LCPIPIT/London, AP377.  
To: Chief, WOLIME.  
Info: COS, Paris; COS, London; C/EUR.  
Subject: DTDORIC/Operational; QRPHONE/Passport.  
Reference: OEPA-2223.

1. Reference reported QRPHONE/1's having lost his passport and having obtained a replacement at the U.S. Embassy in London.

2. On 17 November Air France returned the lost passport by mail. No explanation was offered regarding the delay in finding and returning the passport. Since QRPHONE/1 had already obtained a new passport, he returned the old one to the U.S. Embassy in London for cancellation.

Aaron C. LAGINESTRA.

Distribution: 3 - C/WOLIME; 1 - COS, Paris; 1 - COS, London; 1 - C/EUR.

Cross reference to dispatch symbol and number: OEPA-2263. Date: 10 December 1969. HOS file number: DOD 85-03. 200-124-15673.
