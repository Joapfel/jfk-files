According to Nosenko, Gribanov emphasized that work against code clerks was to be his primary work in the First Section and that it would take precedence over any other activity. Other than work against code clerks, Nosenko has generally defined his responsibilities as follows:

(a) Responsibility for file of (work against) John Abidian, Security Officer at United States Embassy.
