221715Z, February 70. Cite Mexico City 2539 (Pralletti acting).

Immediate Director.

Celebrated REDTOP AEMIDRIFF (AEMIDRIFP) PUPASTIME.

Reference: Mexico City 2517 (NO2875-4).

22 February. IN 03014E.

1. Debriefer reports slow but steady progress with AEMIDRIFF, who is beginning to accept debriefer as friend. LITEMPO-14 taking lead in debriefing (which AEMIDRIFF seems to prefer at this point), with debriefer chiming in occasionally as “interpreter.” Since Spanish is the common language to all three, most if not all of debriefing will be in Spanish. L-14, who is handling AEMIDRIFF very well and who is very amenable to all suggestions from debriefer, is taking complete notes and is dictating notes onto tape. First tape now being transcribed. Will cable highlights.

2. As of evening 20 February, AEMIDRIFF had started going through photos of Soviet personnel with debriefer and L-14. Early reading is that AEMIDRIFF has no knowledge of intelligence activities or contracts and probably little in the way of operational leads. However, she should be able to contribute significantly to in-depth knowledge of Soviet mission Mexico City.
