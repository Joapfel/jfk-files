Classified message.

Date: 6 January 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5. Info: DCI, D/DCI, DDP, COP, C/CA, S/C 2. Outgoing. IN: 47593.

PRITY DIR CITE MEXI 9792

Please deliver following from Ambassador Mann to Secretary Woodward:

Ambassador Mann suggested that the State Department and CAS give the Government of Mexico (GOM) concrete and irrefutable evidence of Cuban intervention in order to bring about GOM diplomatic break with Cuba.
