28 April 1952

Memorandum for: Chief, RE-2  
Subject: Future Balloon Operation

1. Attached is a copy of a letter from Min Miller in which he suggests the necessity of coming to a definite decision in respect to a future balloon operation.

2. Will you advise me as to (a) whether you wish to have a balloon operation in August or at any subsequent date, and (b) whether you want us to set aside funds and materials now for a future balloon operation at some unspecified time.

Thomas W. Braden  
Chief, International Organizations Division

Attachment: Letter from Min Miller, dated 1 April 1952.
