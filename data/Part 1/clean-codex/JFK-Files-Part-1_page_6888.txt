As a supplement to bits of information hitherto orally transmitted, here is a summarised account of the movements and activities of F. A. L. P. E. R. I. H. Maurice, alias PASTOR Daniel, born 3.3.1906, U.S. citizen, economist, married to Ruth; domiciled Medellin 36-9, Mexico 7, D.F.

During his stay in Switzerland from 15 October to 10 November 1958:

On 15 October, subject and his wife arrived by air from Zurich at the Hotel “Schweizerhof” in Zurich. On 18 October, he and his wife took the 1320 H. train to Geneva, where they stayed at the Hotel “Serenita.” On 19 October, 1730–1930 H., he visited E. P. R. O. F. David, born 1.9.1904, citizen of Argentine, Consul General with the IIº in Geneva, domiciled Avenue Andry 01bis 2, C.S.R.A.-BRONQUIES / Geneva.

On 20 October at Geneva, he inquired at a local travel agency about the possibilities of a flight from Zurich to Prague. On that day the Halperins traveled by train, leaving Geneva at 1245 H., to Zurich, where they occupied a room at the “Schweizerhof” again. At 1830 H., subject visited the information office of the Zurich main station to learn about train connections for Prague and Lugano. On 21 October, he visited the Czech Consulate General in Zurich, where he stayed from 0940–1115 H. At 1630 H., he went to consult the “Swissair” information desk at the main station. He stated that he possessed a flight ticket Mexico–Lisbon–Zurich–Tel Aviv, but had no definite booking so far and wanted his luggage to be stored at Kloten Airport for the time being. He then mailed letters to:

1. Mr. David C. Halperin, 5411 South University Ave., Chicago, Ill.;

2. Mr. Theodore Halperin and family, 22 Kahler Ave., Milton, Mass.;

3. Rabbi and Mrs. Rillol Canorah, 4 De Kalb Place, Horrigashu, New Jersey;

4. Mrs. Annie Frisch, 17 Beach Road, Lynn, Hartford, Connecticut.

Letters 1–3 are of similar contents. Requesting copies of Nos. 1 and 4 are attached.

At 1930 H., he inquired at the “Swissair” office for any communication that may have occurred for him.

On 22 October, subject negotiates with the KLA air travel.
