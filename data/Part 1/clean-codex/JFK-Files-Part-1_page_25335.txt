Director Reception Center.

Country: U.S.A./Austria. Subject: Soviet Personalities in USA. Date of information: Up to December 1954. Place acquired: For many. Date distributed: 23 April 1955. No. of pages: 16. References: RS-2805.

This is unevaluated information.

Source D-623, a Soviet electrical engineer, born in 1914, is a graduate of the Leningrad Polytechnic Institute. From May 1930 to January 1933, he was General Director of the CSIV Siemens-Schubert plant in Budapest. From January 1933 until his defection in January 1935, he was General Director of the BSIA ADC Union plant in Vienna. During the war he became a member of the Communist Party and was always active in Party affairs, primarily because he found such participation expedient in furthering his career. He defected to the West because he feared that lengthy imprisonment would result from a forthcoming investigation of production of faulty transformers at his plant. The source is a well-educated, intelligent, and shrewd individual. The source was relatively reluctant to provide data on personalities known to him personally.
