Chief, Foreign Division B.

1 December 1951.

Chief of Station, Karlsruhe.

Administrative Promotion of Subject.

1. Recommendation is made hereby for the promotion of Subject from grade GS-13 to grade GS-14. Subject was assigned to MSB 2 as Chief of the Soviet Operations Section in February 1951, charged with the overall direction of the 7th & 8th operational effort into the SBBZ. In Bag, Subject took over as Chief of the Combined Soviet Operations Band in 1953, responsible for the joint SACANUS-TAGACTUS effort. Since this overstepping area of operational and executive responsibility, Subject has brought a high order activation, outstanding drive, and use of excellent operational know-how. His devotion to a task, nothing short of commercial and complete with illustrations, is worthy of considerable commendation.

2. As an Officer (O/B), Subject is in direct command over approximately 80 ZACANTS and TAGACTUS personnel, including a very large training installation at KST, 60 miles away from Vat Headquarters. Most of these personnel have resorted to LFB during the past eight months, and most of them have been newly recruited and completely inducted into the intelligence field. Subject has not only the ability of the subordinates thus selected, along with those attendant upon a broad operational effort, with considerable ancesces.

3. Subject confronts the future of 8th Division operations with a clear concept of the operational problem and a definite attitude of our capabilities in coping with it. If anything, the recommendations he will have to shoulder will be greater.

Holtor I. Newhouse
