In the following table is a list of the largest business enterprises in Spain and the names of the persons who own or control them. Whenever possible I have noted the full name of each company, bearing in mind that Spanish businessmen are not always totally candid in their financial statements. But aside from the accuracy or inaccuracy of these reports, one soon realizes that certain individuals and/or families control two or more of these companies and perhaps many others of lesser magnitude. And in my conversations with several Madrileños (Madrilleanos; Echegaray, Uclia, Alonso-Castrillo, Cefdarruti, Carrigues, et al.), some of these same names kept popping up time and again, thus leading me to conclude—at least tentatively—that the following individuals or families are the leading industrialists and financiers of Spain:

1. Juan March Cervera

2. Pedro García Basabe, Conde del Cadaguna

3. Ignacio Villalonra Villalba, Marqués de la Vega Inclán

4. Alfonso Pierre—and brothers Ignacio and Arturo

5. José María Grol, Marqués de Casa Grol

6. Marqués Delclosos (Golán-Hoche family)

7. Luis Ungliloy Llanocóy—and other Urquijos

8. Joaquín Hernández Rodríguez

9. Eduardo Miguel García Pascual
