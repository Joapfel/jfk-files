On 12 December 1968, two team members spotted Valentin S. Loginov (known KGB) walking with an unknown Mexican male. After Loginov departed, the Mexican remained at a restaurant for an hour, appearing to be waiting for someone. The Mexican finally departed after making a phone call and was lost in heavy traffic. The team members obtained the Mexican's license plate number, from which he was identified as Ignacio Vallina Barros.
