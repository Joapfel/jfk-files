4. JMWAVE station traces as follows:

Alberto Caj—probably identical with Alberto (Cao) Hernández (Hernéra), Cuban Negro who, according to AMBLESS debriefing report, was last president of JOC (JOG) in Cuba. Member of Catholic left who selected to go to ICFTU conference in Vienna, July 1963. Called expert in youth problems and “nice person with principles and good faith.”

Nelson Diaz Fernandez—AMOT report of July 1961 lists subject as informer and voluntary collaborator with Castro regime. ARAZZ-I reports of July 1968 list subject.
