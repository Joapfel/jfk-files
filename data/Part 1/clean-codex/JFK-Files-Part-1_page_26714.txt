24-23-15

7.0 Out to 28X18428. Marcio Mejia Kicart asks NO, who answers, for Dr. Nauer (??ph) or where they can locate him. At 20 de Noviembre Hospital. His wife is not there. He will come home around 1400 and then goes to Santaxim Laboratorio Midi (ph).

After 1235, 29 April

8.3 In. MO with VKG, who answers. MO has sent VKG his article on Beato Domingo. Did VKG say anything to Kenato? Kenato did not phone VKG yesterday. They will have their talk until Saturday. They don’t have too much discussion. MO thinks they will all get together to collect money. Can talk tomorrow then.

9.0 In. MO with VKG, who answers. MO tells VKG that he is at Teatro da la Paz arranging a performance of “Historia del Zoologico (?).” Can VKG wait a little more for him to get there. VKG says he is going out now. Can meet tomorrow at 1300 at “Siempre.” All right.

9.2 In. Nojas with Inga (Rico), who answers, for VKG. Call back in 30 minutes. Will he be there a while? She thinks so. MO will come by then.

9.5 In. Miric with Inga (Rico), who answers, for VKG, who called him. He just wants to corner and will be right back. Is Miric at home? Yes. She will have VKG phone him.

10.0 In. Sr. Suarez asks maid, who answers, for VKG. Will be back in 20 minutes.

10.5 In. MO asks VKG, who answers, for Sra. Rico. Sra. Rico takes the phone. Asks to speak with Casta. MO/g her brother...Maid takes the phone...about bringing her clothes...will go for it now...

11.0 Out to 31-06-06, twice. Busy.

11.5 In. Sr. Miric with VKG, who answers. VKG tells him about the press conference of Dominicans (Domín>cans) (exiles) at 1600...Miric knows Marcio Mejia Kicart. Will Make give the press conference alone? No, there are more serious persons there (to talk).
