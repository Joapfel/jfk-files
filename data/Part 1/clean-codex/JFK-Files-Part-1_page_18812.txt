Memorandum in Lieu of Fitness Report

Subject: Ann L. Goodpasture  
18 October 1970–31 October 1971

To the immense regret of this writer, Ann Goodpasture is retiring very soon. It would be silly to rate her competitively against any of her colleagues, male or female, young or merely well-preserved. Concerning her fitness, be it recorded that she is very fit indeed to perform any of the tasks listed as her current ones or almost any others I can think of at almost any grade. I venture to rate her overall performance, thirty years of it, as outstanding. Ann is one of the tough warp-threads on which this organization is woven, and when she retires the fabric of this organization will be weakened.

Employee signature: Ann L. Goodpasture

16 February 1972

Comments of reviewing official:

Miss Goodpasture is indeed a fine officer who consistently turns in a strong performance when she is not doing an outstanding job. She is an excellent office and project manager who is cost conscious and she is a careful, thorough instructor and briefer. Her retirement will leave a great gap in CI/OPS and, as Mr. Johnson has said, in the organization. I hope she gets the official Agency recognition she deserves for the many years she has made substantive contribution to the CS.

N. Scott Miler (Miller)  
Chief, CI Operations

17.2.72.
