Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. From: Chief, WH Division. Subject: LIEMPTY Project Renewal.

Reference: HMA 17999, 31 October 1961.

1. The LIEMPTY Project was renewed on 12 January 1962 for a total of $49,500; $29,500 for FY 1962 and $20,000 for FY 1963. The renewal request submitted in HMA 17999 was approved without change.

2. Attached for the Station's information and records is a copy of the memorandum prepared at Headquarters forwarding the Station's request for renewal. Please note how Headquarters broke down the cost structure into fiscal years. Next time the project is submitted for renewal, it is requested that this breakdown be supplied by the Station.

End of dispatch.
