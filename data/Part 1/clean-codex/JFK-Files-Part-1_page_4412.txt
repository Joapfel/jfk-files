DIR WAVE CITE LIMA 8360. 22 January 1955, 39233.

1. On 21 January, Chief of Embassy Political Section met with an individual identifying himself as Eduardo Garcia Dartayet, Cuban refugee, who until recently resided in Miami. Garcia said he recently sold his business in Miami (business card indicates he was president of Minimax Super Market Food Inc., 1545-47 S.W. (S.V.) 27 Avenue, Miami) to come to Peru to marry the daughter of the ex-Peruvian consul in Miami and seek employment there. He said he was appointed as liaison for ARTIVES' group with Cuban residents in Peru. Also indicated that while in Miami he had collaborated with KUBARK and ODENVY.

2. Request traces on Garcia. If Garcia's bona fides satisfactory, station desires to consider him as a source of information on the local Cuban colony.
