Central Intelligence Agency, Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Republic of the Congo. Date of info: 11 August 1964. Report No. TDCS-314/01482-64. Date distributed: 13 August 1964.

Subject: Instructions of Tshombe and Mobutu for concentration of mercenaries at Kamina; presumed plan of campaign; preparations.

Place and date acquired: Congo, Leopoldville, 12 August 1964.

Source and appraisal: A Western businessman (B) from a former Katanga mercenary (C) from Hoare.

Ref: IN 49806. Field report No.: ACL 2788. Appraisal: 2 (that Hoare made these statements and that they accurately reflect the facts as he knows them).

I. On 11 August 64, European mercenary commander Michael Hoare said privately that at 0915 hours that same morning, Premier Moise Tshombe had sent a car to pick up him and mercenary air commander Jeremiah Puren and wife, for an 0932 appointment. They waited until 1000 hours and then saw Tshombe for “exactly four minutes.” This was Hoare’s first interview with him since arriving in the Congo on 25 July. The gist of what Tshombe said was, “How soon can you bring your men in? This is very urgent, and we want you immediately.” (Field comment: The mention of “your men” is obviously a reference to other European mercenaries standing.)
