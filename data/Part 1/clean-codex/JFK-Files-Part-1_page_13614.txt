Source: London (0-2).

Date: Friday, 20 October 1961.

Subject: Surveillance of Rogelio ORENDAL Jopling.

0900 — On duty at Pachuca 153 and at Cuban Embassy.

0958 — Subject came out of No. 153.

1000 — He went into the store “La Jalisciense,” Zamora and Marquez.

1004 — He came out with another man.

1005 — The other man opened the door to the consulate office, and they both went inside.

1035 — Subject and the man who lives at Tacubaya 74 came out of the Embassy.

1035 — They took a cab at Marquez and Tacubaya and picked up another man at the doorway to the Embassy.

1557 — They arrived at the international section of the airport and went inside, but were lost.

1503 — Subject came out of the airport with two men plus another who apparently arrived on a flight. These three drove off in a cab, and Subject went back inside.

1505 — He met the man from No. 74 and another, and they all went into a restaurant.

1528 — They came out and went to the American Airlines counter, then walked off.

1618 — After just walking around, Subject and the other two came out and drove off in car 385, which was waiting outside.

1640 — They arrived at the Cuban Embassy and went inside.

1749 — Subject came out driving car 446 with another man in the front seat.

1750 — They arrived at a gas station, Marquez and Mazatlan.

1754 — They drove off.

1817 — They arrived at the international section of the airport again and went inside.

1819 — They went into a restaurant.

1842 — They came out.

1843 — They spoke to an employee at the Real Aerovias Brazil counter.

1844 — They went into international platform No. 2.

1850 — They came out accompanied by a man and woman; they went to the Aerovias Brazil counter again, then walked out.

1855 — Subject and the other man drove off in car 446; the couple drove off in a green Volkswagen.
