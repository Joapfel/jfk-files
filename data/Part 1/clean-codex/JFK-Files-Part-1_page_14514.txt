15 November 1976

Subject: Typed Notes from Review of I&NS File of Orestes Guillermo RUIZ Perez.

Subject's file was presumably reviewed in the spring of 1970 at the I&NS office at 119 D St., N.E., Room 547.

Orestes Guillermo RUIZ Perez made application at Miami on 2 April 1957 c/o Osvaldo RUIZ, 826 N.E. 2nd Avenue, Miami, Fla.

Was admitted 5 April 1957 from 3 April primary inspection.

Alias in school: Willie. Address: Quinte 5, #714, Havana. DPOB: Havana, 10 February 1930. Remarks: Cannot go back to Cuba. Havana University student. If he returns, he would be killed by the Batista government.

Left Cuba 8 November 1956 and went to Key West to see his brother at the above address.

Left Miami approximately 17 November 1956 as a tourist for the Dominican Republic by CDA airline on a tourist card. Stayed with Mrs. Rosa HERNANDEZ, Calle Morffa #17, C.T. Family friends of one year from Havana. Stayed two weeks. Went to Feria de la Pax as interpreter and guide, hired by the HERNANDEZ family. Worked for tips. Lived at a boardinghouse at Calle Barahouce #115 for four months.

Wanted to immigrate because it was the only place where he felt free.

Came to the United States on CDA #400 on 2 April 1957. Had previously applied in 1947 for study in the United States from Havana but did not come. Was married at that time.

Had written letters to newspapers in Havana against the Batista government.

Full set of fingerprints available.

Applied to extend time of temporary stay. Statement dated 10 May 1957, with interpreter.

Address given: 121 N.E. 5th St., Apt. 19, Miami.

Passport: 49248, Cuban.
