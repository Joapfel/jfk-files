Polish Legation

As with the Czech Legation, information pertaining to the Polish Legation parallels closely the type of information reported about the Soviet Embassy. By far the largest amount of information obtained on the Polish Legation during the past year has resulted from LIPAT covers. Biographic information on Polish staff members has been forwarded to Headquarters. The following examples of spot reports were forwarded for Headquarters’ dissemination:

May 1974, dated 29 August 1974, “Polish Legation Reception Commemorating 11th Anniversary of May of Liberation.”

May 1978, dated 31 August 1978, “Polish Legation Contacts with Polish Delegates to the Eighth International Radiology Congress.”
