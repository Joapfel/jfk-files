Dispatch.

To: Chief, WH Division.  
Info: Chief of Station, Tegucigalpa.  
From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
Subject: PBRUMEN/Letter received by LIRING-3 for mailing to Rene OCHOA Funez.

Action required—references: Forwarded under separate cover is a letter and photograph received by LIRING-3 on 2 October 1967 from the mother of Rene OCHOA Funez in Honduras for remailing to her son in Havana. OCHOA is a Honduran revolutionary.

Attachment: As stated above, u.s.c. S/C ATT'D BY R.J.H. Dated 10-30-67. Raguer A. Frudy for Willard C. CURTIS.

Distribution: Orig & 1 - C/WHD w/att 1 cy, u.s.c.; 1 - COS/Tegucigalpa w/att 1 cy, u.s.c.
