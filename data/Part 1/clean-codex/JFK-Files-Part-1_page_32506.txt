Report of Honor and Merit Awards Board. Executive Branch. Title: Staff.

The Honor and Merit Awards Board, having considered a recommendation from [Signature] to award CS/WH to [Name], will recommend the following: Williamson, Earl J. (1915–). M Staff.

Status assignment: CS/WH. Signature: D. Submitter: GS. Recommendation: 15.

Be awarded: Certificate of Distinction.

For heroic action on.

For meritorious service or achievement during the period June 1952–June 1972.

Recommends approval. Recommends award of.

Unclassified citation: Mr. Earl J. Williamson is hereby awarded the Certificate of Distinction in recognition of his sustained superior performance throughout his Agency career. Since 1952 he has served in a variety of important positions in Headquarters and overseas in which the superior quality of his performance was sustained by his skillful leadership and dedication. In each assignment he has shown unwavering dedication to duty, good judgment and the ability to respond quickly in demanding situations. Mr. Williamson's overall contributions to the mission of the Agency reflect credit on him and the Federal Service.

Remarks: Recommendation approved by ADD/P on 11 August 1972.
