Casual Leave Request.

Date: 11 October 1949.

Memorandum for the Chief, FIZ.

Subject: Request for Annual Leave.

1. I herewith request 25 days of annual leave, commencing 22 October 1949 and ending 15 November 1949. During leave, I may be reached at 857 Roon Avenue, Abilene, Texas 60634. Telephone number.

2. I certify that my presence is not required in Washington for training or processing in any manner during this period.

3. If my return to Washington becomes necessary during this leave period due to any official reason, I will return promptly at my own expense upon receipt of your instructions.

4. During the present calendar year, I have had 38 non-days of annual leave, and have 73 days of annual leave accumulated as of 11 October 1949.

Concurrences: Chief, Passenger Section, Transportation Division; Chief, Overseas Division, CPB; David D'Arcy Leech.

Approved: J. P. Chief.
