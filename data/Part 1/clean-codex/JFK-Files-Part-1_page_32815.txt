Headquarters concerned regarding possibility AMOTIN 117 true-name travel may draw unwanted attention in view Cubans' knowledge of his identity and past activities, including contacts in JMWAVE area. It is also possible Soviets in Buenos Aires may have access to airline manifests which would reveal AMOTIN 117's entry if he travels in true name. If this has been requested, therefore, station should advise BIOGENESIS capability to provide Argentine passport in alias which could be passed to AMOTIN 117 for his use to travel to Buenos Aires. If this is not feasible, Headquarters can document AMOTIN 117 with Nicaraguan passport. However, this latter will take minimum one week, not counting further delay which will be caused by inability of BIOGENESIS to issue.
