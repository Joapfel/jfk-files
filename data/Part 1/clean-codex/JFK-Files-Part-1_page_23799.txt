STEBONY, KUDESK, KUWOLF, JAGUAR.

1. JAGUAR report states: “After his quarrel with Verges in 1964, STEBONY/1 received 200 pounds as ‘personal financial assistance’ from Jacques Grippa. Nils Andersson also gave him financial help.”

2. JAGUAR does not know when Grippa’s funds were given to S/1, but assumes between July and November 1964.

3. It is possible, of course, that the ultimate source of JAGUAR information is S/1. It might be useful, nonetheless, to get S/1’s comments.
