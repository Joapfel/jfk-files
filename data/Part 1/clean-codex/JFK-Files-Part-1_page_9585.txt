IDEM I

The drug ring that was rolled up was a multi-million dollar operation. The group had a counter-intelligence budget of about US$one million annually, and included (either as active ring members or fitting of its activities) the son of the chief of police in Key West, the state's attorney, the sheriff and chief of police, three members of the city council, the entire police department, over half of the sheriff's department, the fire chief, and various city and county officials. The ring also ran the prostitution, numbers, and theft rings in the area. While some of these persons were arrested (the fire chief, for one), others were not, and all are free on bail at this time.
