The importance of the group of economic advisers headed by Raul Saez, for example, is well known. The Ministry of Health has an advisory board dominated by the Medical Association, while the Ministry of Housing's board is largely controlled by the organization representing private construction firms. Similarly, almost every ministry and many autonomous agencies have permanent civilian advisory boards composed of representatives of groups whose welfare is affected by the agency's activities.
