Virginia Frank Coe

Family: Brother, Robert. Member of the CPI known to N. Stanbrs. Chambers saw Robert in connection with the Underground Cash Group, so that he was a courier in the middle 1930s. Chambers admitted he did not see Robert attend any of the meetings of the Group until Harold Warb.

Passport: In September 1941, Coe returned to the United States from London. He had a diplomatic passport.

Address: 1945, Washington, D.C., N.W. 2700 35th Street. 1948, Washington, D.C., N.W. 1916 M. Roosevelt Street.

History as it relates to the Gregory Case.

1939–1954, Canada. Taught at McGill University.

Prior to 1940, employed by the Treasury Department.

August 1940. Resigned from the Advisory Commission to the Council of National Defense to return to the Treasury Department.

At an unknown date, went to England in connection with U.S. Government work. Returned to the United States in September 1941.

December 1941. Resigned. Listed as the Assistant Director of the Division of Monetary Research in the Treasury Department. This division was headed by Harry Dexter White (Wertz).

December 1942. Attended the Institute of Pacific Relations Conference at Mount Tremblant.

At this time he indicated his position as Assistant to the Executive Director of the Board of Economic Warfare, as well as the position mentioned above.

1945. Still with the Monetary Research Division.

December 1945. Was Assistant Administrator in the Foreign Economic Administration.

1948. Secretary of the International Monetary Fund.

Exploitation Activities in Connection with the Gregory Case:

Member of the Silvermasters Group.

Bentley advised that Coe was considered relatively unimportant to the S Group.

When Bentley first became aware of Coe, he was in South America on a mission for the Treasury Department, but during all but two months while he was away he was in touch with the Silvermasters. He communicated directly with the Silvermasters, sending them reports and other information to which he had access.

Bentley believes that Coe also gave the information to Harry Dexter White (Larry Dexter Wite), and that this information was eventually furnished by White to the Silvermasters.

Bentley in 1945 could not definitely recall having seen any material which could be identified as definitely coordinating with Coe, but she states that the information supplied by him was made available for transmittal to Bentley’s superiors.

It is Bentley’s recollection that he supplied economic information.

Adler, Solomon, also in Gregory Case. It is reported that at one time he directed the work of Adler (Adl’t) in the Treasury Department and in 1941 signed Adler’s (Adl’t’s) efficiency rating. Subject was listed by Adler (Adl’t) as a reference on Adler’s (Alt’s) Form 57. It is also noted that Adler (Adl’t), on his return from China in 1946, was in contact with the subject and visited the Coe household. TTR-21674, 10 October 1952, 100-4-20-4-10.

On 1 December 1952, subject refused to tell a Senate Internal Security Subcommittee in New York if he was ever a Communist or a member of an underground spy ring. He had flatly denied the same charges when they were made by Elizabeth Bentley in 1948. Subject claimed the constitutional right which prohibits a witness from being forced to testify about matters which might incriminate him. Subject was fired as Secretary of the International Monetary Fund (December 1952). Subject lives at 1918 N. Roosevelt Street, Arlington, Virginia.
