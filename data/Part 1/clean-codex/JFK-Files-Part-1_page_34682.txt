Classified message. Date: 16 December 1961. To: Director. From: Panama City. Action: BELL 10. Info: WH 5, S/C 2. Incoming No. 39471.

DIR INFO WAVE CITE PACY 7273

JMZIP/KUCAGE

1. Request POA for Vicente Herrero Diaz, Cuban national born La Habana 22 September 1915. Father Crescencio Herrero Hernandez, Spanish born, now deceased. Mother Eugenia Diaz (Draz) Diaz, Spanish born, now living Cuba. In Cuba worked in radio and television production and publicity research fields. Friend of Dr. Conte Aguero and member of his Frente Anticomunista.

2. In Panama has worked at Market and Auditorium Research, produced now defunct monthly magazine Humanidad. Is one of fifteen members of local Consejo Revolucionario chapter with title Coordinador Auxiliar. Produces and broadcasts local Consejo daily half-hour radio program on Radio Miramar.

3. Local traces in process. WAVE send any derog traces PACY and DIR.

4. Desire use subject for local public opinion research, monitoring and support local JMRAKE activities.

End of message.
