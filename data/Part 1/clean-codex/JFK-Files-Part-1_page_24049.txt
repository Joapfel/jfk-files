A. admitted to the section for infectious diseases.

7 August 1968.

Observation: Malaria.

Medical history: The patient had felt for about a week: definite weakness, lack of appetite, an inclination to erection and distress, a somewhat feverish temperature, and heaviness under the right side of the stomach.

Cervical observation under thin follicles showed: subcutaneous, afebrile, the line plus the epigastrium about 1 P.P., slight feeling accompanied by other regular findings.
