24 January 1957.

AMCLEVE-15 contacted by station officer 22 January. He is still working directly with CIRASH-A and will also work with Uzcátegui, presently involved in attempts to arrest key subversives and handled the case leading to the recent arrest of Lino Martinez.

2. It is evident he is completely willing to work with the station as a penetration of CIRASH. At the meeting, AMCLEVE-15 turned over for station copying several valuable documents from CIRASH providing operational leads on key terrorists.

3. Salary was not discussed, but the station planned to pay AMCLEVE-15 from 1,000 to 1,500 SS. AMCLEVE-15 left for Miami the morning of 25 January. Further discussions will be held when he returns to Caracas the following week.

4. We have reviewed documents forward of reference 3.
