3. The Castro cited in paragraph 2, reference B, is Eulalio Francisco Castro Paz (201-742365), born 4 June 1942 at Marianao, Cuba; also known as Papo, E. Castro, F. Castro (F. Astro), and Frank Castro. Photo available. Reportedly left Cuba for the U.S. clandestinely by boat 11 December 1962 (sic—believed to be 1961). Resided in Philadelphia January to October 1962; New York October to November 1962; served in the U.S. Army from 5 November 1962 through 6 December 1963 as a light weapons infantryman. Reportedly worked for Manuel Artime in April 1965 recruiting people in Miami to fight communism in Central America. As of March 1974, was employed as a salesman with Fabric Bazaar, Inc., Miami, Florida. Moved to the Dominican Republic early 1975. As of March 1975, was establishing interior decorator firm Casteville C. Por A. in Santo Domingo. Reportedly was trying to set up an export-import business in the Dominican Republic in April 1975. During 1972, married Ana Hargarista de Windt D., daughter of retired Rear Admiral Cesar de Windt Lavandier of the Dominican Navy. Castro reportedly told an FBI source in October 1975 that the head of the Santo Domingo police had it in for him and would be replaced by a friend of Castro's father-in-law.
