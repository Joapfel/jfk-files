Subject: Request for information which may have been obtained by the Federal Bureau of Investigation during interrogation of Orlando Otero Hernandez (KOLANDO O T E R O HERNANDEZ) in May 1975.

1. This Agency would appreciate any portion of information regarding connections between Chilean exiles (CHIBA EXILES) and the Chilean Directorate of National Intelligence (DINA) that may have been developed by your office in the interrogation of Orlando Otero Hernandez (KOLANDO O T E R O HERNANDEZ) in May 1975. This information is needed in order to assist the Santiago Field Station in the collection of information possibly related to the September 1975 combined death of former Chilean ambassador to the U.S. Orlando Letelier.
