File No. 783 593. Date: 18 June 1964.

Synopsis

I. The subject site is located in the east wing of the ground floor of an apartment building known as Summerville Hills Apartment Complex and is located a short distance off of (th Street) main on Washington thoroughfare and East-West Highway, Route 24. Transportation and personal service facilities are readily available. Telephone service is on an individual subscriber basis. Fire and police protection are adequate. The only possible security hazards noted for the subject site are the proximity of the subject site's windows to possible penetration from automobiles parked in an adjacent parking lot and people located at ground level outside the subject site. Biological data on the occupants of apartments in proximity to the subject site are contained in the above report.
