Five times I was sent abroad: In 1957 I was in England with a sport delegation; in 1958 was again in England with a sport delegation; in 1960 I was in Cuba with a delegation of specialists of the nickel industry; in 1961 I was sent to Bulgaria with the aim to help 1 Department, 2 Directorate, MVD; in 1962 I was in Switzerland—the conference of disarmament.

Working in MVD-KGB, every year I had leaves for rest. In 1953, with my wife, I was resting in a tuberculosis sanatorium. In 1954 I was with the family at the cottage. In 1955 I was resting at the cottage. In March 1956 I was resting with my wife in Karlovy Vary (Karlovi Vary), Czechoslovakia. In 1957 I was in Leningrad two weeks with my wife and then rested at the cottage. In 1958 I was resting at the cottage. In 1959 I, with my wife, rested in Sochi. In January–February 1960 I rested with my wife in Kislovodsk. In August 1961 I rested with my wife and daughters in Nikolaev (Nicolaev). In October 1962 I rested with my wife in Sochi. In July 1963 I rested with my wife and daughters in Anapa.
