Dispatch.

Action required references: To: Chiefs of Station, Rome, Madrid; CCB, Barcelona. Processing action: No action required. From: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires. Subject: Counter Espionage. Luis TEDESCCHI, possibly Luis TEDECHI. See paragraph 3.

1. A source who has given highly reliable information in the past, and whom we have no reason to question, has stated that during 1943 one Luis TEDESCCHI was either being considered for recruitment by or was actually working for the RIS. According to the source, he was known to have been active as a member of the Spanish Communist Party. The address used by TEDESCCHI during early 1943 was Pichincha 969, Buenos Aires, Argentina.

2. The Buenos Aires Station has determined that a Luis TEDESCCHI, born 2 February 1918, is listed in local police files as living at El DONADOR 3150, telephone 58-3717. Further investigation is being conducted there. Unfortunately, Headquarters does not have the date and place of birth of the subject of paragraph 1. The source has indicated that the subject was probably born in Italy and may have been active in the Spanish Civil War 1936–39. This, of course, could explain his involvement in the Spanish Communist Party.

3. It is requested that Rome, Barcelona, and Madrid check Station and Base files for any traces of the subject. Liaison and police contacts may be approached for any information available in their files.

4. Please address replies in this matter “Attention KUDESK.”

GERALD B. HUTTARD

Distribution: 2 – Rome; 1 – Barcelona; 1 – Madrid; 1 – Buenos Aires.

Date typed: 29 May 1961. Date dispatched: 2 June 1961. RI/Files. Office symbol and number: OTRV-18463.
