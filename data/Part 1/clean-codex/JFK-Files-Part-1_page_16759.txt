Ref A: PARI 7042 (not sent Bonn Hagu). Ref B: MEXI 3947 IN96410.

1. Further repeated efforts to contact Romero have been totally frustrated by aide to Romero, Lt. Rodriguez, who refuses to permit “Wittenberger” to talk to Romero, even when he is available, which is rare because of numerous official ceremonies. When Wetherill called the morning of 28 March, Rodriguez said Romero had just talked to Wittenberger and made an appointment with him for five o’clock that same afternoon. When Wetherill said this was impossible, R insisted he had been present during the conversation.

2. Wetherill called at 18:00 hours, asking if alleged Wittenberger had appeared on schedule. Rodriguez countered by asking the name of Wetherill’s secretary. Wetherill gave him the first name. R said he would call back, and has not done so. Believe this is the end of the line. The only alternative at this point is to abandon completely security which is already badly lacerated.

3. The only explanation we can offer for the story of “other Wittenberger” is that Rodriguez and Romero decided Wetherill/Wittenberger was a crackpot, and thought it appropriate to give him some of their own medicine.
