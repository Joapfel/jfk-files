Editor of La Nueva from 1939 to 1945, when he was expelled from the PCC. At that time, he founded a weekly news magazine called Elconco (Elсоnco) en Cuba (The in Cuba) with a pro-communist, anti-communist, and anti-Castro editorial policy. Later he became a daily newspaperer, was described as the worst newspaperman in Cuba, with a reputation for yellow journalism, complete irresponsibility, and corruption.

After leaving the Communist Party, Hater furthered his own事业 by organizing the MZL, which was active in the suppression (explosivepression) of the Democratic Republic in 1947. Within this organization, he organized a group of personal bodyguards known as the “Flying Five,” which constituted the nucleus of his own private army. Hater was elected to Congress during the presidential campaign of Carlos Prío (Fría) in 1948, but when Prío was ousted (outed), he turned up at Batista’s (theiste’s) side. He was elected to the Senate in 1954 and, in September 1955, formed the “Revolutionary Industrialists” (“Revoltive Industrialists”) (Revilla), a labor movement dedicated to ending his influence over labor unions. Hater remained (realizes) in Cuba until the end of his life, although he allegedly accepted the export (support) principally because he feared the opposition of his friends (友) and his “brothers.”

Hater is married and has a son and a daughter. He speaks English fluently.
