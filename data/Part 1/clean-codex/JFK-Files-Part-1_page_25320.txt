Field Information Report. Country: Mexico. Report No. MM-1464. Subject: Soviet Commercial Activities in Mexico. Date of info: December 1955. Place acquired: Mexico City. Date acquired: same as above. Date of report: 5 March 1958. Source, source evaluation, appraisal of content: LIFEAT/LIMEPICK, B-Docmentary (Documentary).

I. Organization

The commercial office of the USSR is located at Calle General Leon 32, in Mexico City.

The phone number of the commercial office is 15-12-64. The commercial office was formerly located in the Soviet Embassy, main building at Tucubaya (Tacubaya) 204. Due to expansion of activities, additional personnel, and the resultant need for greater operating space, the office was moved in April 1955 to its present address.
