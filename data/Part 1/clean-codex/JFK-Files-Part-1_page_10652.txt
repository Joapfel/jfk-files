This is unevaluated information.

Source: U.S. national, soldier of fortune type with wide experience in Latin America. He is closely associated with Rolando Masferrer (Maesteferrer).

This report was developed by a Navy (aavy) representative assigned to the office of Preparation.

1. During the night of 22 Nov 66 a decision was reached to call off the proposed invasion of Haiti by the group sponsored by Rolando Masferrer (Maesteferrer). The invasion is off indefinitely.

2. Father Jean lePeltiste Georges and Colonelhen Lecu have been arguing with Masferrer (Maesteferrer), which has caused confusion and disorganization in the movement. Many Cubans resigned to participate have pulled out, and military chief Napoleon Villaboije (s.c.), a Cuban national and alleged military expert, has resigned because of dissension in the group.

3. It is believed that U.S. government agencies have facilitated the movement, and the leaders fear that all weapons and equipment would be confiscated if plans for the invasion proceeded.
