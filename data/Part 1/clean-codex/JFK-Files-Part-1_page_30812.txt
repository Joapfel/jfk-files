In the spring of 1949, I was questioned by a Postal Inspector about a letter I had mailed approximately one year previously for a Mr. Halren. He was the display man for Allen and Jefferson (Jeffer son) Hardware Co., in Tupperl Ann, Ala. I had been working with him for some time dressing windows, painting, and decorating.

The letter in question was addressed to Mrs. Halren, who was out of town, and apparently contained some money. I believe it was a dollar. The letter was received, but there was no money in it.

I stated that I had known nothing of any money and that I also had had a fellow (feel ox) with me when the letter was mailed. I wrote to Mr. Halren about the incident but never had a reply (re) from him.

There were apparently no further inquiries, as I have heard nothing since the Postal Inspector questioned me.
