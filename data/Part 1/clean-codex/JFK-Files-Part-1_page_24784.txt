2. Federation of Activity:

UOMAC is a military-family support project which provides funds to cover salary, travel and installation expenses in the city of Maputo, C. Democratic Republic (D.R.), contract agents and political action teams. Tichborn has been used primarily as an advisor in CIAT-connected election operations throughout Latin America. From 1961 to August 1966 he served the Mayor of City station in the development of a cultural magazine Diversa and conducted out spot assignments in other areas. In August 1966, he was assigned PDS to New York City, and (as of October 1966) placed under cover as Latin American reference in the BO proprietary, ICAO Corporation (WUBRINY Project). In the past year, he served briefly as advisor to a Santa Domingo (Santo Domingo) station asset (MANNATHAN-6) and in an unsuccessful attempt to conduct an in-depth study of the FNI Party in Mexico. The present UOMAC extension and termination on request coincides with BO assignment of Tichborn's support costs under Project WUBRINY as of 1 October 1967.

3. Funding:

Since October 1966, UOMAC funds have been passed to Tichborn under cover of the ICAP Corporation (Project WUBRINY).

4. Interagency Coordination and Evaluation:

U.S. Ambassadors in countries where Tichborn's services are utilized in special operations are briefed as appropriate and necessary regarding his assignment. No State coordination or 203 Committee action.
