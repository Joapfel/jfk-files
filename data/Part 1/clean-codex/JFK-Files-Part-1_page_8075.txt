A. Aldo Inocencio Vera Serafin (201-0326634) DP08, 28 July 1928, Caibarien, Las Villas, Cuba. Former underground leader against Batista 1955–59 and prominent member in 26th of July Movement. Held several positions in Castro's early revolutionary government including that of Chief of the Technical Investigations Department (DTI), the main intelligence organ of the Cuban National Police. Arrived in U.S. with wife and baby as a refugee on 13 March 1961. Issued permanent U.S. alien registration No. A-12-486-223 in June 1965. In 1972 moved to Puerto Rico. Vera was very active in anti-Castro Cuban exile groups up until the time he was killed by unknown assailants on 25 October 1976 in San Juan, Puerto Rico.
