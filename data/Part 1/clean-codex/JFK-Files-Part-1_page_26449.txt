Country: Cuba, Mexico, Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Panama, Venezuela, Brazil. Report No. TDCS 3/563,671. Subject: Travel and mission of alleged Cuban agent in Central and South America. Date distributed: 31 October 63. Priority. References: IN 50142; TDCS 3/563,137; TDCS 3/562,790. Date of information: 20–22 October 63. Place: Paraná, Pariana City (24 October 63).

This is unevaluated information. Source codes (coads) are definitive. Appraisal of content is tentative.

Source: Panamanian revolutionary leader (P), with good contacts in Cuba-oriented leftist circles of Panama.

(HPA) Haartnic (HPA) comment: Victor Eco Galan, Mexican Hispano-American (HISPANERICAN), arrived in Panama from Costa Rica on 20 October and left Panama for Caracas (CANACAS) on 22 October 1963. Eco Galan is a Marxist, a writer for Segrete and Politics (SEGRETE AND POLITICS), Mexican magazines, and is the head of the propaganda committee for the National Liberation Movement (MLN), a Communist-front movement sparsely distributed (SPARELY DISTRUGATED) by members (NUMBERS) of the Mexican Communist Party. In October 1962 Eco Galan went to Cuba to prepare a political biography of Fidel Castro. In May 1963 he again went to China (SINA), returning to Mexico on 22 June 1963. During this later visit he reportedly interviewed (ENTERED) Castro after Castro's return from the Soviet Union and also spent a five-day seagoing vacation with Castro, of whom he is said to be a close friend. Eco Galan was officially published (OFFICIALY PUBLISHED) intermittently (INTERIMLY) in January 1964. Collector departs Guatemala.
