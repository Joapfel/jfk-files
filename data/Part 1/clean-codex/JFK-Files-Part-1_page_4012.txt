Panama (PAKAMA), Republic of Panama.

Telephone: 3-1000 (no lines).

Cables, military.

June 15, 1965

Roger Conver

Dear Andy:

I have just come back from a three-day visit to Venezuela where things are going along very well. There are now 9 small cooperatives in the region around San Diego. In the village of San Francisco a large training center is under construction which will house 60 persons for cooperative training courses and for other teaching in rural development. There is territory as well as classroom space. The building was financed as follows: $18,500 from the German-Brazilian, $6,500 from the community of San Francisco itself (population 1,000), a mix which is really quite remarkable and testimony to the truly unique and effective organization which the Bishop and his associates are creating in Venezuela, and $8,000 is being raised in Panama. This Center will be where Father Steele will conduct regular four-month cooperative and rural development training courses for persons from all over Latin America. It will also be used for local training. Father Steele's first course was held in Panama City and was attended by 25 men from Ecuador, Brazil, China, Peru, Bolivia and Panama. This course seems to be the best of its kind in Latin America.

The land has finally been broken free around San Jose. This is the first real land reform in Panama. My two men are hard at work.

I talked to the Bishop about Father and he was most enthusiastic. He wants him to come as soon as possible and work alongside Evaldo Rodrigues (mevaldo Rodrigues), who is the young man in charge of his Development Operations. His organization is called CEPAIS, Center for Studies, Promotion and Social Assistance. He hopes that Father will be able to take up as the executive director and operator of the San Francisco Center once it is under construction. In my view this would be our ideal 20-40. Not yet. Hotel Wilton Alabaster del Mono.
