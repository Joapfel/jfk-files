Official Dispatch

APR-0298

17 March 1949

The following are a number of statements made by the Actionists de la Liberté in Canada. They were delivered in Montreal on October 1st and are here reproduced with the permission of the Actionists:

1. Mr. Benoit was elected mayor of Canada, which also made him a candidate for re-election, but was defeated.

2. On March 5th he was also elected as a Conseiller General in the Council of Canada, but was defeated when running for re-election to this office on March 20th, 1949.

3. On April 1st, he was elected as a Conseiller Municipal in Canada and is currently holding this office.

4. On May 1st, he was elected as a Conseiller Municipal in Canada and is currently holding this office.

5. On May 1st, he was married and has one daughter, aged 20. His address is 63, rue des Anglais, Montreal, on the road to Antibes (Antibea).

B. M. Bishop
