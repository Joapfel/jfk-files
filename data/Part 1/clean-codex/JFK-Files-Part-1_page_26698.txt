Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Washington.

Info: Chief, DC; Chief, WJ (Attn: Wallace R. Parlett); Chief, SR.

From: Chief of Base, New York.

Subject: ISOLOG/Operational — Meeting with MUNDICO/1, 18 May 1965.

Action required: DC — See paragraph 3; WH — See paragraph 4. Reference: NY00-0695.

1. Attached is a Contact Report of a meeting with MUNDICO/1 on 18 May 1965. As indicated, W/L approached Mauro Lucentini and persuaded him to send a modified version of the 2 May Havana broadcast via ANSA channels to Buenos Aires. In an effort to supplement this coverage, Subject also prepared a longer version for the paper Correio do Povo in Porto Alegre, Brazil.

2. As shown in the Contact Report, Lucentini apparently has at least a social relationship with Gregory Kalinen. W/L stated that Lucentini, a friend, is a conservative and, to his knowledge, does not have any leaning toward communism.

3. For DC Base would appreciate traces on Lucentini in order to determine his political leanings and connections.

4. The Base should be advised if either of the two stories mentioned in paragraph 1 is published.

Guy W. Rutherford.

Attachments: 1. Contact Report. 2. Spanish Copy for Porto Alegre Newspaper.

Distribution: Orig. & 1 — COS w/2 atts. in dcp.; 2cc — 8 w/2 atts. (single copy); 2cc — 10 w/2 atts. (single copy); 2cc — SK w/att. 1 only.

Cross reference to: Dispatch symbol and number UNDA-1135. Date: 20 May 1965. NBG file number: 200-6-263/3.
