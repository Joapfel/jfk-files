Message form. Routing and/or initials.

File No. 4167/1973; C/WH/COG 5, C/CI. 1 Nov 68; F12.

To Mexico City KXX. Info: Guatemala City.

RYBAT PBRUMEN LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 7900 (IN 25321).

Cite Director 47408.

1. Subject to Guatemala Station concurrence, we believe LIRING/3 should be candid with LIRING/10 and tell him what and as much as he wants to know. We feel if LIRING/3 attempted to withhold or distort details of his imprisonment, it could be counterproductive to operation since LIRING/10 probably could check other sources for accuracy.

2. There is no urgency for LIRING/3 to make a trip to PBRUMEN. We believe it more advantageous for him to remain in Mexico and attempt to become involved in local PBRUMEN/Guatemala exile activities. If, however, LIRING/10 suggests LIRING/3 go to PBRUMEN, he should not hesitate to do so.

3. Defer to Guatemala City for identification reference war names.

4. File 50-6-141/3, 201-798301 and 201-776733.

End of message.

William V. Hroe for C/WHD.

WH/COG/OPS. WH/2/G.

Coordinating officers.
