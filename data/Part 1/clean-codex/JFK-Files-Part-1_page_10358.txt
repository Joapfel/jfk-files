Dispatch.

To: Chief, WH Division; Chief of Station, Guatemala City; Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Reference: Letter received by LIRING-3 from Guatemala.

Action Required—References. Action required: FYI. Reference: MEXICO CITY-7945, 4 November 1968.

1. Forwarded under separate cover for each addressee is a Xerox copy of the letter recently received by LIRING-3 from Guatemala mentioned in reference.

2. The Station hopes that contact between LIRING-3 and representatives of the Guatemalan rebels will be initiated in the near future. Station plans are to discreetly surveil meetings between LIRING-3 and his Guatemalan contact after the first such meeting.

Approved A. Curtis for Willard C. CURTIS.

Attachment: Letter (1), u/s/c.

Distribution: Orig & 2—C/WHD (w/att—1 copy); 2—COS, Guatemala City (w/att—1 copy).

Dispatch symbol and number: HMA-35822. Date: 18 November 1968. 201-798301.
