Let: Written by A-l's father to A-l. Given to H.S. by A-l at New Orleans on 24 June 64.

Mi grand papa, papá.

How time goes and how long it takes.  
But I know that one day will come  
When I'll be able to see you again,  
And then we'll be together once more.

But for the time being, let us say good-bye.  
Goodnight, my little boy. Goodnight.

Until we meet again, God bless you.

And now, my own little son,  
I'm taking my leave of you.  
Go with God, my child,  
And God bless you, until we meet again.

Adieu, papa. Adieu, mami. Adieu, Papa.

Bye, dad. See you later.

Don't cry for me, son. Don't be sad. Go and play with your friends. We'll see you again, won't we?

Have a good trip, my son.  
Be careful, my boy.  
Take care, my son.  
I'll be back as soon as I can.  
But in the meantime,  
Take care of yourself.  
Love, my son, love.  
Bye, papá. Bye.
