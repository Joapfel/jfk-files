Dispatch. To: Chief, WE. From: Chief of Station, Helsinki, 7 September 1965. Subject: REDCOAT/LCIMPROVE/AELADLE — Tabe Maria Ingeborg SLIOOR (201-768030).

Banned for obituary. Only qualified data can be obtained by microfilm.

Action required references: 19-1 HILL 3059, 2 June 1965. Ref: OPMA-7261, 4 June 1965. Action: FYI.

1. Subject was interviewed for an immigrant visa on 7 July 1965. Unfortunately, Clastonbury could not be present because of other business. The interviewing officer was briefed prior to the meeting and faced the applicant with a series of questions regarding her association with Soviets and any approaches that may have been made to her in light of her unusual life. In response to these questions, we are told that SLIOOR gave thoughtful consideration to each question and probably truthfully replied that although she has met several Soviet diplomats, no approaches for cooperation were made to her. She realized the full implication of the questions and made no attempts to be evasive. Subject was given her visa immediately after the interview.

2. Attachment 1 is a translation of the VALLIX traces on subject. As noted in the traces, SLIOOR was an acquaintance of Georgy STEINING, born 29 June 1913 in Moscow (UKA (201-150156), and Georgiy STEERING, born 29 June 1913 in Moscow (UKA; Georgi STEINING, OPHA-1807, 30 July 1956, and OPHW-1456, 9 October 1956). These names were not used in the interview.

3. Attachment 2 is a thermofax copy of SLIOOR's visa; Attachment 3, birth record; Attachment 4, marriage certificate.

4. From subject's visa application, her address in the U.S. is listed as Fort Hood, Texas.

Walter R. Clastonbury.

Attachments: as stated.

Distribution: 3 — WE with attachments; 2 — SR with attachments; 2 — Hela with attachments.
