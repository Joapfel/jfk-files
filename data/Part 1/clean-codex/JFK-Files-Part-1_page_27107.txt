E. Scott Chas. Burr, of Virginia, born 10-29-1896; died 11-3-1970, at age 64, at her Episcopal minority, residing with husband in Orfeld, N.C.

From the notices in the New York Times.

December 15, 1947:

113-1, Florida 231-57.

Also known as:

E. Scott Chas. Burle, of Norfolk County, Va.; Scott Chas. Burle; E. Scott Chas. Burle; Frank Chas. Burle; Frank Chas. Burleson.

1914, Dec. 6: Born in “Horford,” Va.

1930–1942: Resided at 210 “C” Street, Wilmington, Delaware, Del.

1943–1945: U.S. Marine Corps Reserve (Perla Is., S.C.), Pacific theater.

1945: Honorable discharge—“Valiant,” Tallinn, Oder.

1945: Born in “West, Florida.”

Circa 1945–1949: “Horford,” Fla. Endorsed: Frank St., etc. Joined “Horford” Police Department. Attended William and Mary College. Also attended Virginia Tech Technical Institute.

Circa 1945: Employed as manager of the “Virginia” Tavern on East Main Street.

Circa 1949: Employed as owner and manager of The “Miltons” Tavern on East Main Street.

Circa 1949: Enlisted in the U.S. Naval Reserve at the “Horford” Naval Air Station.

Circa 1950–1952: Honorable discharge.

Circa 1952–1954: “S. Merchant Marine travelling to and from Europe.”

Circa 1950–1952: U.S. Army—stationed in Germany.

Circa 1952: Honorable discharge.

Circa 1954–1956: Officer-manager of Topline, “Virginia” Beach, Va.

Circa 1956–1959: Cuba (Soviet Ukraine), Ireland, Florida, Mexico, Venezuela, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Panama, Holland, Jamaica Is.

1959: Contact with CI at U.S. Consulate—Santiago, Spain.

1959: Contact with CI at US Tobacco—Havana, Spain.

1959: Contact with FIU MI—Washington, D.C.

1960–1970: Day of Rest.

CI control officer: Jim Jozef.
