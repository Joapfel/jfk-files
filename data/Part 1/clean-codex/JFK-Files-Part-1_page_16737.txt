Classified message.

To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 8. Info: OCS 2, OP 2, RF 2. Routing.

221903Z. DIR CITE NEXI 5458. REF DIR 55521.

22 June 1963.

1. ARENART willing remain NEXI as long as need exists. Did not request extension or another tour for two reasons:

A. He disappointed that HOS did not consider him for promotion, which station feels he certainly deserves after three years’ commendable service in very difficult outside sensitive job.

B. He wary his cover status should next presidency not be confidential lieslegant and it necessary disharble LIENVOY.

2. NEXI again requests ARENART be promoted. FLS advise. Will submit new FRG when future LIENVOY known and six months prior ARENART department reassignment.

3. Answering other questions ref in separate cable.

C/S comment: Requested STA discuss possible one-year extension with ARENART.
