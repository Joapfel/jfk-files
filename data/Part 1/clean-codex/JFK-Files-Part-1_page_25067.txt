Valeri V. Kostikov. I have met him, but I do not know his name.

Valentin Longuinov, in charge of consular affairs. His wife Alexandra has been a swimming (skimming) star; both are very nice, speak very good Spanish, and fair English. Their daughter Kirina, sixteen years old, very sophisticated, came here for a holiday. The only child of that age we have ever seen here. They have another child, who is in Moscow. Both have been at our house for a party. In general, Soviet people come alone, leaving their wives behind.

Dmitri Prygov, wife Natalia, have a small child born here. He has been writing a book on the Mexican Revolution. We are friendly, good friends. He was introduced to me by Boris Kozantsev, who invited me for lunch for the occasion. When we go to the Soviet Embassy, both he and Tchaykhov are always very attentive.

I know several other people from the Soviet Embassy, but really I could not place them together with their names.

Extract from HMHA-36436, 12/2/69 (RedTop Ops)
