May 8, 1969.

LIBACON-1

Briefs on meeting held May 7 from 1630 to 2035 hours.

When I came, I asked until what hour he could be with me. He said 1830. At 1815 I paid the bill, but he insisted that we stay a little longer and that he would pay the rest of the bill. He had two additional drinks while I had my third coffee and a drink.

As we got together, he told me that on May 3 he was with his wife at Cuautla, and as he was entering the pool area, LOG was leaving. LOG cornered him for a 15-minute talk, which consisted of pressuring LIBACON into making arrangements that would enable LOG to meet LIOYAL-1. When LIBACON turned him down, LOG insisted that they have lunch on Thursday, May 8, at the restaurant El Ferro Andalusi (or similar) at the Copenhagen, about 1230 hours. LIBACON accepted.

LIBACON brought me a biographical sketch on LOGINT, which was an interesting paper. We discussed the paper, which enabled me to obtain explanations and facts on which LIBACON based his general statements.

LIBACON readily gave me information on Prof. K. LUBAN. There was not much, but sufficient to ascertain that he did not attempt to withhold information from us. This was best; no further details were needed.

He also gave me some information on KASEL WINDEL, who is presently conducting a tour of Mexicans through Europe and was in Prague with the group a day before Dubcek was kicked out.

The teachers of Spanish at the Soviet Embassy other than his mother: LIBACON did not know anything, but promised to ask his mother on Saturday when she would be at his home.

It took me some time to explain to LIBACON the attitude to take toward LOG during the lunch.

A detailed report will be prepared. I am now handing in notes on my instructions to LIBACON concerning how to handle the lunch with LOG. Also handed in is the biographical sketch on LOG.
