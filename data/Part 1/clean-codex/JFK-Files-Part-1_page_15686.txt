IB Memo No. 5238  
29 November 1960

Per CSH 43-43.

Memorandum for: Chief, Africa West/2

Attention: Mr. Daniel Niesciur

Subject: Transmittal of Austrian Documentation for WIROGUE

1. This is to confirm the handcarrying of the following Austrian documentation by Mr. Daniel Niesciur on 18 November 1960:

a. Austrian passport No. 546226.  
b. Austrian driver’s license.  
c. International certificate of vaccination.  
d. Baptism certificate.  
e. Business letter from Arthur Doll and Son, Berlin.  
f. Pocket litter—brochures, Austria, West Berlin.  
g. General information on documentation cover story.  
h. Itinerary as reflected in passport.  
i. Explanation of passport entries.  
j. Instruction for signing documentation.

2. The documentation (above items a, b, and c) is to be aged after WIROGUE has signed them. It is our understanding that WIROGUE will use the subject documentation to travel from West Germany to Leopoldville, Congo, via Switzerland and Italy.

3. It is requested that the subject documentation be returned to TSD/IB when no longer needed for operational purposes.

For Charles Yech, Jr.  
Chief, TSD/IB

JGI-233477
