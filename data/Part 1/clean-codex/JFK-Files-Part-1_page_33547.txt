RYBAT FUOMEN.

1. Following info cleared for oral FUOMEN briefings. Stipulation: no further exploitation. Source: Mexican journalist with good contacts in leftist circles.

2. Subject: Remarks by Cuban intelligence officer in Mexico after the 11 September coup d'état in Chile.

3. Shortly after the coup d'état in Chile, a Cuban Embassy officer who is also a member of the Cuban Directorate of Intelligence (DGI) in Mexico said that the Cuban government was impressed with the good organization of the Chilean military's successful overthrow of the Allende regime on 11 September.

4. He said the Cuban government and Cuban Embassy in Santiago were not surprised, however, that an attempt against the Allende government was made because they had long perceived that Allende, while a good Marxist, lacked sufficient aggressiveness toward his enemies.

5. He said the Cuban Embassy in Santiago had plans prepared.
