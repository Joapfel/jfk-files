Dispatch. To Chief, TS, attention: Chief, SR. Headquarters phone No.: 202-555-3200. From Chief of Station, Paris. Date: 7 December 1960. Received: KONAK (aka Celia) and KOZAK. RE 433. Action required: For information. Reference(s): Indexing can be judged by qualified no desk only.

1. KOZAK, a friend of Austin WATKINSON (see OAK-5552b), 23 November 1960, is a lost Canadian of about 90 years of age. He arrived in Paris just after World War II and was employed as the A GADGET/US operative in France, since he had been involved in this activity in Japan until 1943. As an intelligent man, graduate student of political economy at the University of Nancy, KOZAK was able to learn all about the secrets.

2. KOZAK came into contact with Austin WATKINSON in 1959 and they became fast friends. Ivan WATKINSON is considered that MARY KOZAK was already then a Soviet agent and made contact with KOZAK on his instructions. Then Stalin and others noticed that KOZAK frequently made critical remarks about a GADGETA activity, displayed excessive curiosity about various GALAXIA members and their relations. In the end, they became suspicious of him. He was removed from his job and strictly isolated from his work. His studies were no longer out of date when it was learned that KOZAK had contact with the Soviet industry. He has subsequently maintained his contact with KOZAK.

3. KOZAK is now living at 26 rue Gaston Jaillon, near Châtelet, Loiret, where there is a rat or large mainstay colony.

Wallace N. HAVEDON

6 December 1960

Distribution: SR; E.
