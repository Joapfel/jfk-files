Subject: Evaluation of Contract Management Institute Course

Technical investigation and management of a contract and whenever costs followed a nonlinear curve, most estimates became almost completely unpredictable. NASA and the DoD generally have, therefore, gone from CPIF types to CPAP-type contracts; the CPAP type ends up being simply another CPFF but with the contractor having the advantage in the establishment of an award fee and with the Government having no recourse to redetermination. My reaction was simply that the DoD experience simply reaffirms my own—that for R&D there appears to be no good substitute for a well-managed CPFF-type arrangement.

David L. Christ  
C/AP/CRD/DD/S&T
