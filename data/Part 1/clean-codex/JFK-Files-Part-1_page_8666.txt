LOGINOV, Valentin Sergeyevich

Born: 23 December 1927, Moscow.

Wife: Aleksandra Dmitriyevna, born 20 August 1928, Moscow.

Children: Two.

LOGINOV was an attaché at the Soviet Embassy in Havana from September 1960 to September 1963. He was Third Secretary/Vice Consul at the Embassy in Mexico City from August 1965 to October 1969, replacing NECHIPORENKO. We have no information to indicate he has ever been expelled from a country. He accompanied a Soviet soccer team on a Latin American tour as escort and interpreter in December 1961.

Languages: Fluent Spanish and poor English.
