CIR-316/01680-7B.

Subject: Orlando Bosch Avila.

3. In early April 1976, a source of undetermined reliability reported that Orlando Bosch Avila had arrived in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, using a Costa Rican passport with a false name.

Bosch was reportedly to enter a clinic in Santo Domingo for a cataract operation.

According to an official Chilean service, Bosch arrived in Santiago, Chile, on approximately 5 April 1976. He is believed to be in Chile with a contract to assassinate a high Chilean official. An attempt was made to arrest Bosch on 10 April, but he successfully evaded the Chilean investigators, leaving behind a Chilean and an Ecuadorian passport and his personal clothing.
