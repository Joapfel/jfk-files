29 December 1973. Restricted handling. Cite Leningrad 2651.

Tech C.

To: Headquarters. Info: Moscow (eyes only COSTILLER).

Action: HRGG.

Reference: Leningrad 12545.

1. During Smol'skiy's visit to Tempford's apartment, reported in the reference, he told Mrs. Tempford that an American from a “cultured Boston family,” who defected to the Soviet Union 12 or 13 years ago and is living with a Soviet wife in Leningrad, now desires to leave the USSR and has apparently made feelers about emigrating to Canada. It is not clear whether this would be via official Canadian channels. According to Smol'skiy, before defecting the American worked on “broadcasting codes.” The American is reportedly being discouraged by friends, presumably Soviets, from emigrating to Canada for fear he will be arrested by the “American secret police.”

2. The individual cited above is undoubtedly Vernon Mitchell, a former NSA defector married to Salina Yakovleva, a well-known local musician, pianist or singer. According to carded information based on SX-24455, January 1975, Mitchell has reportedly been a resident of Leningrad since Mrs. Tempford was unfamiliar with Mitchell's P&L dissemination.
