Department of State

Subject: Testifying of Adolph E. Berle, Jr. on behalf of Nicolas Kalava

1. Thank you for your memorandum of 11 March 1952 transmitting to us Mr. Campbell's memorandum of 13 March regarding the testimony of Adolph E. Berle, Jr. (Adolph A. Berle, Jr.) on behalf of Nicolas Kalava. This is to inform you that we are in complete agreement with the views which you and Mr. Campbell have expressed.

2. We feel that it is indeed not regrettable that Mr. Berle is his testifier as certain remarks offensive to the Cretziang-Vislandu group. In March, Mr. Berle received Mr. Cretziang in an attempt to disclose any responsibility on the part of H.T. for Mr. Berle's statements and also to remonstrate Mr. Cretziang that Mr. Berle had spoken in a private capacity and as attorney to Mr. Kalava. This interview was followed by a letter from Mr. Cretziang on 26 March, in which these same views were set forth.

3. We shall make a serious effort to prevent the commencement of such act in the future. We are returning your file as you requested.

Thomas W. Brown
