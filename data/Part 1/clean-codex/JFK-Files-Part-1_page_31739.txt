27 January 1953

(Haller)

Memorandum for: Chief, JO  
From: Chief, FI/Plann/Cover  
Subject: Comments concerning Crusade for Europe

1. In December 1952, Mr. Reichert discussed with your division contact made by C/C representatives with Mr. Buckner of General Motors in Detroit in regard to General Motors' contributions to the Crusade of Freedom. Mr. Buckner recently reported to CC/C the following information.

2. Suzanne LaFollette, Managing Editor of the Freeman Hayes-ino, New York, recently wrote a letter to Mr. Jordan of General Carton Company, Battle Creek, Michigan, in which she criticized the Crusade for Freedom. She stated, among other things, that the promoters of the Crusade are a “bunch of micro liberals and do not know anything about reaching (reacting) iron curtain countries.”

3. The letter is reported to have been turned over to the Battle Creek newspaper, Enquirer, but its contents have not yet been published.

4. According to Mr. Buckner, LaFollette's comments have curtailed efforts to solicit funds for the Crusade and he fears that this type of information might reach other city groups.
