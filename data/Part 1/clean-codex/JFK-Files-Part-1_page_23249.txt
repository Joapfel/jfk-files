Classified message. Origin: H. G. Macauley. Unit: WH/III. Extension: 2056. Date: 10 February 1958. To: Habana. From: Director. CONF: WH 5. Info: COP, CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS 2, PP 2, PP/OPS, S/C 2.

To: HAVA (HABANA). Info: CITE DIR 07387.

Ref: A. DIR 2147 (OUT 80587). B. DIR 4551 (OUT 86187).

1. Project AMPHODARCH amended to include objectives VAIVADA mission:

A. Names, biographical and background data, and political alignment of top officers of Castro staff.

B. Strength, deployment, and equipment of Castro Army.

C. Extent of communication of Castro forces.

D. Extent of coordination and support from other political groups, particularly PSP.

E. Gather operational data for planting future long-term political action agent within Castro movement to detect and counteract communist penetration.

2. Project allotment increased $1,000. Approximate cost of mission estimated as follows: transfer VAIVADA family SANJ, $200; travel VAIVADA, $200; storage HHE and car, $600; miscellaneous emergency operational expense, $1,000. VAIVADA to convert single-status allowance and receive separation allowance while on mission.

Coordinating officers.

Releasing officer.

Authenticating officer.
