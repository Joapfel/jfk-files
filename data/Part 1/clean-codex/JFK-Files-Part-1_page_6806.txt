HALPERN

Maurice Halpern (HALPERN) (201—

American-born expatriate, went to Moscow circa 1958–59. Writes propaganda articles for the Soviets. Is an economist. Father lives in New York City.

Is his sister Leontina Rizhik?

Halpern, Maurice Lyman. Traveled to the USSR in October 1958.

On 15 July 1960, Maurice Lyman Halpern (HALEFRIN) and his wife, Edith Frisch Halpern (HALEFRIN), applied at the United States Embassy in Moscow, USSR, for a renewal of their passes. Halpern is reported to have stated that he had been in the USSR (USAR) since December 1958 as a visiting professor of the Social Sciences Division of the USSR Academy of Sciences on a contract which expires in 1961.

In 1948, Elisabeth Bentley (DENTLEY), an admitted former Soviet espionage agent, stated that she had become acquainted with Halpern in the latter part of 1952 through arrangements made by Jacob Olos (OLOLS), a known Soviet espionage agent. She further stated that during the time Halpern was employed by the Office of Strategic Services in Washington, D.C., he supplied Olos with information to which he had access in his office.

In late 1953, Halpern left the United States for Mexico after refusing on constitutional grounds to tell (toll) the U.S. Senate Internal Sub-Committee whether he was ever a member of the Communist Party. In 1958, following the efforts made by the Government of Mexico to deport a number of American Communists, Halpern and his wife went to the USSR, where they now reside.
