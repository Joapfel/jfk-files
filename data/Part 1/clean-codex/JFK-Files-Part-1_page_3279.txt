Cuban Residents Elect New Officers — Havana, El Mundo, 30 August 1936

The Union of Mexican Residents in Cuba elected new officers. Chosen president was Emilio Espino-Barros Ramírez; other officers are Eduardo Canudas, Carlos Novoya, Miguel Xiriev, and Anira Arbi de Costa.

Curriculum Fertilis in Western Europe — Havana (Havane), El Mundo, 30 August 1938

Leaving for Madrid were Cuban doctors Claudio Palacios, Angel Lopez Valles, Rafael Lamagotiti, Jesus Suarez Rozos, and Rene Yodu, who, along with Cubans Leopoldo Arroyo and Jose Angel Bustamante, will already be in Europe and will participate in the Fourth World Congress of Psychiatry, which will be held from 5 to 11 September.

Doctor Julio Martinez Paez left for France, where he will attend the Tenth World Congress on Orthopedics and Traumatology from 4 to 9 September. He will join Doctor Rodrigo Alvarez, who is already in Paris.

Cuban Doctor Ubaldo Parnot, a specialist in obstetrics and gynecology, will travel to Spain, Great Britain, Sweden, and Norway under a scholarship to widen his knowledge in his fields.
