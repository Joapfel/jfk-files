Travel Order. Number: Amend. p1 to W-237-49. Date: 15 November 1949.

Name: James W. Moore (designer). Title: Chief of Station. Salary: GS-12, $6,000.00. Office or Branch: FDA. Official Station: Calcutta, India.

You are hereby authorized to travel and incur necessary expenses in accordance with Agency Travel Regulations.

Maximum Living and Quarters Allowance authorized in accordance with S. G. C. A. R.

Purpose: Original Order amended to authorize travel by privately-owned automobile at 7¢ per mile, not to exceed cost of common carrier for subject alone and as being more advantageous to the Government, if accompanied by an employee as shown below.

If any itineraries and special provisions: All other terms of the original order to remain in effect.

Date effective. Terminating approximately.

Mode of Travel: Common Carrier; Airplane; Vessel; Privately Owned Automobile; Government Transportation—Airplane, Vessel, Motor Vehicle.

Allowance for privately owned automobile as follows: (a) 87 cents per mile, not to exceed cost by common carrier. If subject alone. (b) 69 cents per mile, as being more advantageous to the Government. If accompanied by dependents.

The change of official station indicated is effected in the interest of the Government and not for your personal convenience.

In connection with change of station, you are authorized to transport your immediate family, your household goods, personal effects and automobile, subject to weight limits, restrictions and provisions as set forth in GAI No. 11.

Travel to first post of duty abroad, including expenses of transportation of immediate family, household goods, personal effects and automobile, is authorized.
