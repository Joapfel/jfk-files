Official Dispatch.

Dispatch No. TDF-4-54.

To: Acting Chief, PT.  
From: Chief of Station, Panama City, Panama.  
Subject: General — Agents and Informants. Specific — HYDROL — Operational Review.  
Date: 12 April 1949.

Operational Review.

Country: YAMAO. Period covered: October 1945–March 1949.

1. Subject is no longer an active operative of this station. He was forced to discontinue his activities on our behalf because of the press of personal business. MELANCHOLY is now in contact with HYDROL and HYDROL I has been taken over by HYDROLCIC.

2. During the period of his connection with this station, HYDROL failed to submit any informational report which in itself merited transmission to the Home Office. None of his operations were clean in the sense that a U.S. Government influence had to be introduced into the actual activation of each of his sources. This, coupled with the fact that he lacked follow-through, has given a slightly sour aspect to all of his operations. He intends to discontinue our connections with Subject.

Grover J. Waring
