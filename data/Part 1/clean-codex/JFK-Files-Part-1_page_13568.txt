2. The main BOS of the PCT notifies on the actions of the Custilla (Castilla) group, organized and arrived in Mexico. Field content: It was reported previously that the 13 November group in Mexico had crossed the Texic T/Cuatlaco at the border successfully and had established a second guerrilla front. The PCT believes that it should come in situ with the survival of this group, “even if the political crisis should evidently end.” It believes that this group should continue to operate in the mountains in order to establish and stay in a democratic regime.
