October 4, 1965.

Contact Report

Subject: LIOVAL-1 (P-8903). Date/place of meeting: October 3 at Restaurant Hoyt at 1330 hours.

1. Prearranged meeting to which L-1 came 10 minutes early. Next meeting: October 16 at the same place at 1430 hours.

2. Since our last meeting on September 19, L-1 had not seen LIBACON-1, who on two occasions apologized two or three days after the class was due for not holding the class because he was not feeling well. In both cases, it was obvious to L-1 that he was dead drunk when he phoned. In view of the fact that LIBACON-1 did not phone L-1 for a whole week, L-1 called him on October 2, and he promised to give L-1 a lesson at 1630 hours on October 3.

3. L-1 explained to me that his working schedule in the new school year keeps him busy in the morning and afternoon, except Friday afternoons, which makes it difficult for him to be asyx tieing himself twice a week for LIBACON-1’s class, which most of the time does not take place. L-1 felt that nothing would be lost if he reduced the Russian-language classes to one per week, which would be more than he ever actually had in the past. I told him to go ahead and suit himself.

4. I told L-1 that AEDIENHARD was scheduled to leave in about a week and that it would be interesting to ascertain whether LIBACON-1 knew it. L-1 will show LIBACON-1 the Excelsior of September 25, in which the Sot End farewell party was announced. L-1 felt bad about the fact that he did not even have a chance to see AEDIENHARD face to face.

5. L-1 asked whether he could remind LIBACON-1 that he had promised to make arrangements that would enable L-1 to meet Alla (GLEVOVA /F-13,482/) and suggest that AEDIENHARD’s departure was opening the possibility of doing something along that line. I told L-1 that there was no reason to expect that LIBACON-1 would accomplish anything, but L-1 should press him on the basis of his former promise.
