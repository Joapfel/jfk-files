Intelligence Information Cable.

CITE TDME DB-315/07585-77.

DST: 28 June 1977.

Country: Venezuela.

DO1: 24 June 1977.

Subject: Interception by Venezuelan security officials of a note being smuggled into the San Carlos prison for Orlando Bosch.

ACO: Venezuela, Caracas, 28 June 1977. Field No. HVC-7690.

Source: A Venezuelan businessman with a wide range of contacts with Venezuelan security officials. His reliability has not been established. He received the information from a Venezuelan government official.

1. On 24 June 1977 Venezuelan security officials intercepted a message which was being smuggled into the San Carlos prison for Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch, who is currently under detention in Venezuela in connection with the 6 October 1976 bombing of a Cubana airliner. Although the message was intercepted, Venezuelan authorities copied it and then allowed it to be delivered.
