Cuban Democratic Revolutionary Front, Finance Department

Other Incomes

“Paula and Sosa”

October 1 to 31, 1960

Annex “I-C”

October 17: Reimbursement — Francisco Carrillo: $300.00. Amount given in excess for the payment of the payroll.

October 19: Reimbursement — Juan Paula: $75.89. Amount not used on check No. 55, account 19314, “Banco del Comercio, S.A.,” Mexico D.F., for his expenses on a trip to Mexico.

October 19: Reimbursement — Ana Tomeu: $250.00. Her salary for September.

October 21: Enrique Llaca: $11.50. Amount not used on check 228, September 8, “Rasco and Paula,” for a trip to Colombia.

October 26: Roberto Fernández: $61.92. Amount in excess on check No. 91, August 8, (“Rasco and Paula”) for the Students Section.

October 27: Paula and Sosa: $50,000.00. A deposit that must have been made in the account “Rasco and Paula,” on check No. 255, and that by mistake was redeposited in this account.
