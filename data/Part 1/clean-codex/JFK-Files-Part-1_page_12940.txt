On 1 August 1966 Luis POSADA began employment with RECE, a Cuban exile organization located in Miami. He is a salaried employee of RECE and is also being paid by RECE to report on the activities of FTEC. Therefore, it is requested that his resignation from State Engineering be recorded and made effective 31 July 1966.
