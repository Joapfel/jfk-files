1. Secret Communications — A Technical School

Introduction to Secret Writing (STW-101)

Record of Intelligence

Name: Grayston Lynch  
Office: GOD  
Date: 2 February 1970

1. Course Content

To present to the officer who uses an overall appreciation of the secret writing process, and who should be aware of its place in the scheme of clandestine communications, but who does not anticipate an immediate need for this technical skill.

2. Content

a. It compares S/W to other forms of clandestine communications and addresses both its advantages and limitations. It discusses the major forms of secret writing—carbon systems, microdots and latent image photography—and provides a demonstration of each.

b. It concludes with an examination of the open tactical factors surrounding the utilization of S/W—paper selection, couriers, accommodation addresses, sponsorship, indicators, cover letters, supplies and portal intelligence.

3. Evaluation

No previous work is included or individual evaluation given.
