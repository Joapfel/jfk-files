26 June 1952

FOIA Form: General Personnel and Technical Intelligence

Transfer: ATR and TRU

Purpose: Robert J. Moore

1. On 2 June 1952, you told me of your interview with Robert J. Moore and of his influences on his decision from NCTC. Mr. Moore was dissatisfied with NCTC on security grounds, and his decision was initiated by this Agency.

2. Subsequently, Moore interviewed Bill Dorkoo in New York for a job in the American Committee on United Europe, but there was none available. During the course of this interview, Moore, though not knowing Dorkoo was an employee of CIC, divulged security information about KCS-CIC relationships, and our security people have been notified of this fact.

Thomas V. Moore  
Chief, International Organizations Division

CIO, TCH/aka
