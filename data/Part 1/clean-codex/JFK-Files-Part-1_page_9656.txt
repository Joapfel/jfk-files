29 March 1967

Miscellaneous FCI: Chief, MI/CA

Response to News on Council for Latin America

The activities of MLI touch upon four organizations listed below. There are no other activities, as they may concern organizations listed on the CA memo attached.

Instituto de Investigaciones Sociales y Sociológicas (Socio政icas) — currently budgeted at $60,000 annually, supports in part this organization, which also receives funds (some $50,000 yearly) from MEXICORP, a German Maehope' fund with headquarters in Mexico, Germany. The objective of this effort is to help establish local or prototypeillian organizations designed to alleviate and hopefully advance the social and economic status of the Mexican populace (popliteus).

Centro Nacional de Estadísticas Sociales — the executive director of this organization was formerly associated with the LIBFI project, but he is no longer an active member of this project, which is funded at the current rate of $4,300 annually and supports LIMET-1, the station's only agent with access to rightist political organizations.

3. Centro Nacional de Estadísticas Sociales — the director of this organization (LIEEEN-3) is no longer an active WOTACT agent. The LIEMAN project was terminated in 1965 and had been funded at an annual rate of some $65,000. The project supported a mass-media propaganda effort directed at the middle and lower classes of Mexico and sponsored by a front group of conservative Mexican businessmen.

American Chamber of Commerce of Mexico — the vice president of this organization is a current Station asset, LIEMT-1. No money is provided from WOTACT sources to this organization, although LIEMT-1 receives $300.00 monthly. The duties of this agent do not directly relate to his.

MI. LIEEEN 1. Mexico.
