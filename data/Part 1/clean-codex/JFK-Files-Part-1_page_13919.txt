Action. Info. Classified message.

Date: 28 February 1964.

Deferred: Routine. Priority: Operational immediate. Initials: MNS. LTC: 292207. Incoming number: 8531.

To DIR MEXI WAVE, info SJOS. Cite MANA 4739. KAPOK PERUMEN. Refs: A. DIR 04121 (IN 8501); B. MANA 4730 (IN 8427).

1. IDEN A returned Managua from Mexico City 27 February and reported IDEN B still Mexico City. IDEN B in daily contact with Cuban Embassy, but has not been told when he will leave. “CHELE” now says possible IDEN B will go directly Cuba instead of making trip Miami first, though this still a possibility. “CHELE” told IDEN B not worry about commo plan (reported MANA commo 4715); that he (“CHELE”) would send info regarding commo system to AGUILUZ.

2. IDEN B said he accidentally learned, when he overheard phone conversation, that “CHELE” is ROGELIO. Probably Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez, mentioned MEXI 8228.

3. IDEN B now calls Embassy each day, usually at 1300 hours, and asks for “CORRIN,” another name used by “CHELE.”

4. IDEN A plans return to Mexico City 3 March and has been instructed by ERYTHROID/3 to remain there until IDEN B leaves.

End of message.

29/0223Z
