TYPIC.

F DIR 2927.

6 April 1964.

C-101331.

1. On 9 March, a Madame Boyer and unidentified man called on Daily Parrot, Curanagar cultural counselor. Boyer told Aparicio she recently received data from Cura, planning a four-month trip through all countries of Latin America to compile information for a book she was planning to publish. Wanted to read books about each country before going and asked Aparicio for help obtaining them. Planned to return to Cura soon with books. Aparicio was not informed that Boyer was covering NEXI but offered assistance and introduced her to LITANIL-3 and the ambassador's wife.

2. Boyer said she was born in the United States, married a Frenchman, now 11, brought up but had French nationality. Maiden name Hungarian “KISAN” (phonetic). Mother Russian, grandparents came to the U.S. before the war (sic). Boyer lived in Paris and seven years in Italy. Speaks Spanish with an accent and some difficulty. (Source: delayed transcript.)

3. Curana manifest shows one Boyer Katzander, French turista with the same passport. Deena Boyer left NEXI for May 15 Mar. Will
