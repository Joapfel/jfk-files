Classified message.

Date: 1 June 1962. To: Director. From: Saigon. Action: FE 8. Info: CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, WHT, S/C 2.

1 June 1955Z 1962.

Routine.

IN 44395.

DJAK INFO DIR CITE SAIG 9792. Ref MEXI 0950 (Original).

On the off chance that in spite of Ref Steadman succeeds in latching on to Albarran (Alsarran) as he passes through Saigon, suggest DJAK provide name of station officer eventually to be given to Albarran (Alsarran) to pass on contact as notional friend of Steadman. This would be done only if deemed desirable after meeting the man.

End of message.

C/S comment: *LIELESCANT UNASLE CONTACT ALBARRAN PRIOR DEPARTURE.
