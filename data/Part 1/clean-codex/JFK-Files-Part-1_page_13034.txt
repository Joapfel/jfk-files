Personal Record Questionnaire, Part I — Biographical Information.

Instructions: 1. Write clearly. 2. Answer all questions. If question is not applicable, write N.A. 3. Attach blank pages if additional space is needed. Despatch No.

1. Name: first, middle, last, net. 2. Name in native language or script. 3. Name at birth, if different than Item 1. 4. Court record: court, date, arrests, charges, punishments, sentences, acquittals. 9. Description: use American standards of measurements, if possible.

Sex: F, M. Age. Apparent age. Weight: F, M. Complexion. Facial shape. Color of hair. Color of eyes. Teeth. Build. Posture.
