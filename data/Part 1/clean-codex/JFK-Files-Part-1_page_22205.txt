3. Report by Psychiatrist

During the year April 1964–April 1965, Nosenko (Nosenko) was under the medical care of a CIA psychiatrist who visited Nosenko at regular intervals, usually weekly, to examine him physically and to listen to any comments Nosenko might have about himself and his situation. The psychiatrist familiarized himself with available materials on Nosenko, particularly with reports of his behavior in the months immediately following the defection.
