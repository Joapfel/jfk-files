SAVITSKI sent me to the Deputy of the Chief of the Second Directory, SHUBNIAKOV, who told me that there was a signed order and I was accepted in the 1st department of the 2nd chief directory as a case officer. SHUBNIAKOV invited the deputy of the chief of the 1st department, GORBATENKO, who was acting as the chief of the 1st Department because the chief of the department, KOSLOV, Anatoli, was appointed to the special department of extraordinary affairs (investigation). SHUBNIAKOV and GORBATENKO said to me that I would be working in the 1st section of the department. Then I went with GORBATENKO to the 1st department, where I was acquainted with the chief of section, KOSLOV, Veniamin. KOSLOV told me that I would be working against the American correspondents, showed me the room and my desk, and acquainted me with the officers who were working in this room: KUTIREV, RACOVSKI, GROMOV, and TORMOSOV. The last officer must give files on the correspondents and agents. I was told to come the next day and begin to work.

When I was resting in the tuberculous sanatorium, I became acquainted with KOJEVNIKOV, Ludmila, a student of the Moscowite University, and in June 1953 we married. Before that I was living with my parents at St. Gorky, 9, but after marriage was living with my wife at.
