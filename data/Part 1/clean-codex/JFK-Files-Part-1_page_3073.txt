Dispatch. To: Chief, Soviet Bloc Division. Info: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. From: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires. Subject: REDTOP/Operational Evening with 201-23671 and wife.

1. After many months of contact and many promises to “get together” some evening, Paul W. LEVERONE received a firm promise from 201-23671 that he and his wife would drop by for a drink and a snack on 9 January 1989. The promise became a reality, and the report of the evening’s activities is forwarded under separate cover.

2. Although no firm date for a future meeting was set, every effort will be made to continue social development of Subject.

Attachment: U/S/C as stated. Attached by: AN, 28 January 1989. Distribution: S—C/SBD, with attachment U/S/C; S—C/WID, with attachment U/S/C.

Cross-reference to dispatch symbol and number: NABA-20210. Date: 16 January 1989. NAS file number: 201-24701.
