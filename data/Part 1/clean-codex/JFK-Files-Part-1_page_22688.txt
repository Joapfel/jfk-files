Classified message.

Originator: R. Reynolds. Unit: WH/III, Carib. Extension: 2056. Date: 20 December 1958.

To: Mexico City. From: Director. CONF: WH 5. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS, S/C 2.

CITE DIR. C5525. AMUTTER.

Consideration being given Noemayr return Fidel Castro area. Duration and nature mission same as Noemayr's trip last March. After consultation Noemayr advise feasibility this op and whether station can release him for approximately six weeks beginning early January.

End of message.

J. C. King, C/MND.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
