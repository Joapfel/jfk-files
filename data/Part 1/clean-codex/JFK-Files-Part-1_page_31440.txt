25 September 1952

Memorandum for: Deputy Chief, Political and Psychological Warfare

Subject: Assignment of Mobile Radio Facilities (MB-50)

1. According to Messrs. Rowland and Kamman Brown, arrangements have been made to utilize the MB-50 in Germany.

2. The completion of the transaction is awaiting a test that will be undertaken by outside engineers through General McClelland (Maclelland). This test, I am told, is necessitated by the fact that a complete specification of the equipment is not available.

3. It is estimated by Mr. Brown that the test will be completed in a few weeks.

J. Lloyd Fonger  
Acting Chief  
Psychological Staff Division

Distribution: Mr. Rowland; Mr. Brown, 10.
