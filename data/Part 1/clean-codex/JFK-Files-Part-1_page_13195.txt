282317Z February 1968, CITE Caracas 9025. Director. RYBAT.

Embassy is number-one target on list of phone taps.

1. Latest info from AMCLEVE-15 on LNPURE phone tap which he following up:

A. Although LNPURE at top of target list, he saw it AMCLEVE-15's impression that coverage is spotty and LNPURE may not at this moment be covered.

B. CIRASH has 36 lines covered.

Man in charge of taps told AMCLEVE-15 that when all lines not being used on specific targets, “as in November and December 1957,” any extra lines put on LNPURE.

C. CIRASH has only three recorders for these 36 lines, each recorder covering 12 lines, even though more than one conversation may therefore be feeding into it at given moment.
