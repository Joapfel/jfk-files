20 July 1964

Memorandum for: CI/OA

Attention: Mr. Lally

Subject: Amendment of the Operational Approval for LICOOXY-1 (201-278841)

1. It is requested that the Operational Approval for LICOOXY-1 be amended to include authority for Mexico City Station to utilize her as an access asset against a Soviet Press Attaché (Attacne) and Soviet Information Bulletin Representative.

2. It should be noted that the 29 June amendment of LICOOXY-1's OA was not implemented since that target's visit to Mexico City never materialized.

W. J. Kaufman  
Chief, WJ/1
