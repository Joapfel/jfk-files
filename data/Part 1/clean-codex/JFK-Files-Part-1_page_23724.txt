Classified message. Cable Secretariat disseminated. Action unit WH8. Info. File. Action No. 192.

191325Z December 1978. Cite WH/MIAMI 9322. 19 December. IN-2299.

Director

TYPIC

1. AMPALM-4 informed station on 11 December that he had been told by Carlos (SARAGAT), the important ALPHA 66 sub-chief, who learned of it from Andres (NAZARIO) SARACENT, leader of ALPHA 66, that ALPHA 66 had recently received a letter from Eloy Gutierrez (GUT IERREZ) MENOYO written from a Cuban jail. SARAGAT told AMPALM-4 that he wanted him to look at the letter and advise SARAGAT on possible courses of action. (Apparently NAZARIO had asked SARAGAT for advice.) AMPALM-4 expected to see the letter during the weekend of 12–14 December. However, SARAGAT informed AMPALM-4 that he could not show him the letter, as ALPHA 66 had decided to limit its readership.

2. According to SARAGAT, the letter appeared to have been written by Gutierrez (GUT IERREZ), although apparently more than one person participated in the editing. Gutierrez (GUT IERREZ) presented three points for ALPHA 66's 19-300-3 file: 201-269349.
