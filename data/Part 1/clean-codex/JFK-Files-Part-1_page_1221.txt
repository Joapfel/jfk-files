To: Mexico-Rome WAVE. From: Director. CONF: C/WE 2. Info: DDP, ADDP, C/C1, C/F1 2, C/WH 5, C/SAC 5, RF, VR.

10 October 1963, 18, 53z. Sitting. Deserved. Routine.

To Mexi, info Rome WAVE, cite DIR.

KAFOX KEYWAY PERUEN.

Ref: Rome C534 (IN 28856) (not sent Mexi).

1. DESIGNET (IDEN A), former long-term resident PERNUNEN, will return PERNUNEN on business trip for IDEN B. Latter is firmly represented along with others. Plans depart Italy 6 October to visit friends Suisse, then proceeding Madrid to take Ireland flight direct PERNUNEN 11 October. Has ITAL service passport. Will attempt exit PERNUNEN approximately two weeks after arrival for brief trip Mexi for debriefing. Briefing by KUBARKER. Hopes return PERNUNEN for two or three months more.

2. DESIGNET called home contact arrangements for passage DESIGNET prior his departure. ESS forwarding Mexi full details this operation.

End of message.

*DESIGNET will travel PERNUNEN; requests contact Mexico City for brief exit.
