4 October 1974

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Former Records of Ray Murphy

1. The attached documents are part of a large collection of records held by Ray Murphy during his tenure as an official of the Department of State. He transferred all of his records to CIA upon his retirement in the mid-1960s. The documents of that collection which did not meet the retention criteria of the DDO or the Office of Security were destroyed, while those which were of utility have been retained and accessioned as appropriate to the DDO records system.

2. The attached records concern Harold ISAACS, who is the subject of 201-0018446 and should be classified to that file.
