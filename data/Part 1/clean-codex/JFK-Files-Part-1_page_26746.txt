From 0630, 4 May (Tuesday) to 0715, 5 May 1965.

2.0 In, from long distance. Habana, Cuba, is calling. Maid answers. Call back in an hour. Holds the line while waiting for a reply. Finally hangs up.

2.5 In, from long distance. Maid answers. Habana is calling. Victor Rico Galan says for them to call back in an hour. After 0835, 4 May.

3.0 In. WO with Inge Kico, who answers. WO asks when they returned from Cuba. In Holy Week. They have some cigars. WO says they are leaving tomorrow. Perhaps they can visit them this afternoon for coffee. WO is not sure he can come by their house. Better they visit WO's house. She will have VKG talk to WO about this. WO will be either at home or at Aspa (phonetic).

Ask if VKG can bring a copy of “Siempre,” the new issue, to take on his trip. This issue is not out yet. It comes out on Wednesday, so this is not possible.

4.2 In, from long distance. Habana is calling VKG. VKG cannot take the call. Call back in an hour. He is asleep.

4.5 In. WO, sister-in-law, with maid, who answers for Inge Kico. Not there. WO is Ross.

4.7 In. WO asks maid, who answers, for Sra. Kico. Not there. And VKG? He is asleep.

5.0 In. WO asks maid, who answers, for VKG. He is still asleep.

5.2 In. Carmen with Inge Kico (In), who answers. Greetings. VG takes the phone. Asks if there is anything. Yes, when will they see one another? VKG has something to say. At 1200 in “Siempre.”

5.3 One extension off.

5.7 In. Sra. Kico with VHO, who answers. Greetings. VKG got home very late yesterday. Anita is ill. Wants to ask VKG for some advice.

6.0 In. MO with VG, who answers. MO to Julian (Cuban). Puts Marie on with VG. Greetings. Marie would like to see VHO. Marie is at Habana; VG will go by there in a little bit. Marie will wait for him. Marie has some books for VG—sent by Sr. Vilaa of Radio Habana, Cuba.

6.3 In. MO, calling for the Association of Students of Law School of the University of Miami, with VG, who answers. They have not set up round-table discussions there to explain the Caribbean situation to students. Would like to know if VG could participate in this on Tuesday or Thursday of next week. All right, Tuesday of next week. MO will call VHO on Saturday to get the exact time. Will she participate in these? They had planned for VG to participate with Alberto Domingo and with Eusi-que also. Is that okay? Please. Do you want me to come?

Reel 72-KICO-29.
