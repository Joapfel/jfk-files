2. FOIL summarizes background and policy regarding Rapacki (PAKACII) plan:

A. The Polish proposal for a Central European nuclear-free zone is introduced at the 2 October 1977 session of the UN General Assembly by Polish Foreign Minister Adam Rapacki (RAPACIK). Acting as sponsor for the Polish government, the formulated proposal stated that should East and West Germany agree to a ban on the production and storage of nuclear weapons on their territories, Poland would unilaterally introduce an identical ban on her territory. Rapacki did not mention Czechoslovakia, but the head of the Czechoslovak UN delegation, speaking after Rapacki, supported the proposal, declaring his country would join the agreement, implying a ban over its territory, too. The East Germans subsequently formally endorsed this proposal.

B. Rapacki’s proposal was accurately cleared and considered with Soviet leaders prior to introduction. Moscow primarily reported this proposal approvingly, and the Soviet press stressed that agreement would be welcomed by peoples.
