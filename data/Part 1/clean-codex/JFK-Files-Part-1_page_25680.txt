1. On 5 September 62, AMLAW-1 called LITAMIL-1 and arranged a meeting at the apartment of Ramon Sinobas at 1800 that evening.

2. AM-1 told L-1 he felt he had an obligation to make contact as promised prior to departure for Havana (HAVA) regarding possible discussions in Mexico. Said upon arrival in Havana he saw Raul Roa and Fidel Castro the first two days, then discussed subject matter of interest to L-1. He did not say with whom he talked regarding the matter. AM-1 said that the Cuban government, in view of threats and aggressive actions including shelling (SKELLING) the Cuban coast, could not consider “talks” in Mexico. Any “talks” would depend on the U.S. clearly demonstrating that it really was interested in “talks”; that is, that the U.S. stop its aggressive (AGRESSIVE) actions and prove interested in a peaceful approach to problems. He added that they in Cuba felt that “talks” in Mexico merely stalled for time.
