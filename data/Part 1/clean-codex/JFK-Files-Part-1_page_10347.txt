Cable Secretariat Dissemination.

Classified message.

Action.

2302322 November 63. Cite Mexico City 8159. Immediate Guatemala City, Director info JMCOBRA.

Ref: Mexico City 7920 (SN 25321).

LIRING-3 22 NewSV #0273.

1. During long discussion LIRING-9 had at LIRING with LIRING-3 on 21 November, LIRING-9 asked LIRING-3 to prepare detailed written report due 1730 hours 25 November on his experiences in Guatemala and his contacts with FAR personalities while incarcerated.

When LIRING-3 mentioned lack of adequate PERUMEN support, LIRING-9 excitedly agreed, stating his capital did not understand FAR and had done many things. He added LIRING-3 should point out on paper how PERUMEN had failed in Guatemala and give his opinion of what was needed. LIRING-9 was also very interested in what LIRING-3 knew of the squabble between Guatemalan leftists. Among many other questions, LIRING-9 wanted to know whether Jeanette FIRN had a FAR mission before her death.

2. LIRING-9 stated Chief LIRING “does not have very much to do with these things” (clandestine activities in Guatemala) and “his opinion does not matter.” Said would arrange for.
