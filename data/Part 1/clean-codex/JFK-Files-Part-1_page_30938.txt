Reference is made to your contract with the United States Government, as represented by the Contracting Officer, effective 30 October 1965.

In addition to this amendment being formally recorded, your official designation as a participant in the “disability system” of this organization is effective 24 August 1965. Your contribution to the Retirement and Disability Fund will be deducted by this organization as of that date. The regulations governing this retirement system give the individual the right to appeal such a determination if he deems the designation adverse to his best interest. In order that this technical requirement may be satisfied, you are hereby notified of your right to appeal. An appeal must, therefore, be received by this organization within thirty (30) days from the date of your acknowledgment of this contract amendment.

Effective close of business 23 August 1965, all contractual references to Civil Service Retirement and your contributions thereto are deleted.

Social Security contributions required by virtue of your cover employment will not be reimbursed to you by this organization.

All other terms and conditions of this contract remain in full force and effect.
