(c) Our CPD memo of 30 June 1966 indicates subject had a social security obligation for the period 1 January 1955 through 31 March 1955. Since this obligation came about retroactively in mid-1955, the possibility exists that neither subject nor the Agency made contributions to the Social Security System. If they were not made, it is possible for such contributions to be made at this late date, and if subject or WfI Division is interested in looking into this, they should contact Rod Brooks of CPD.
