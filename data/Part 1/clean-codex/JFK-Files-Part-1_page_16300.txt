Basingham Intelligence Agency. Central Information Office. 1367-66.

COSTCO COFFEE (LEOPARD'S). Date of order: January 1965.

C1S — 5 February 1965.

Secret Activities and Intents.

Place and date acquired: Beaud, London (1 February 1965).

IN 7395h.

Source and appraisal: An official British service from a source described as “well known, experienced in secret and reliable,” from Alistair Wicks.

1. Major Mike Hoare, commander of 5 Commando, Congolese government mercenary force, together with his second-in-command Alistair Wicks, returned to the Stanleyville area of Congo to resume operations during the third week of January. Their return followed further direct negotiations with President Tshombe during which Tshombe accepted recommendations put to him in strong terms by Hoare and assured Hoare of his confidence in him.

2. The mercenary force leaders were confident they could no longer with the Northern Congo situation provided the 396 additional mercenaries, whose engagement Tshombe (Tshome) has accepted, are brought into the country to come under the protection of the Kingdom of Morocco from the day of arrival in Bissau.

This special operation information being sent to the Red Cross by order of the President of the Republic of the Congo, Tshombe (Tshome), for use only in connection with the conservation and assistance of which it is known to be required, shall not be published by any means whatever.

AD/C1/7 CLA/MCE.

The Red Cross. Advisory Board: Tshombe (Tshome). Directors: AD/C1/7.
