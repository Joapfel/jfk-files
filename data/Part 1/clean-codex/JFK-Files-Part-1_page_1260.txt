December 4. Book Cable (Subsequent). From: Director. One: X. To:

All COS, & COB. REDWOOD L CIMPROVE.

1. 20 December is the forty-sixth anniversary of the founding of the Soviet state security organization. This “Chekist” (“CHERIST”) birthday is traditionally celebrated by KGB residencies around the world by discreet gathering in an embassy or other official installation, or in some of rezident or other rezidentura member.

2. On previous occasions some stations have been able to make new identifications or to confirm previous tentative identifications on the basis of attendance at this party. Suggest addressees mount unilateral surveillance and/or alert liaison services to this date as appropriate.

End of message.
