Office Memorandum, United States Government

To: Mr. Baker

From: JDC

Subject: Clearance status — Melvin J. Leaky

Date: 2 March 1950

1. Regarding provisional clearance — Mr. Garland has informed me that the CIA indices check has revealed derogatory information. In part, the derogatory information concerns the fact that OSS would not utilize the services of subject. Mr. Garland stated provisional clearance would be considered only if there is a written statement concerning status of employment, that is: exactly how subject will be utilized, whether government or CIA interest will be shown, with whom he will be working, and whether that person will be under cover.

2. Regarding operational clearance — Mr. Garland advised that investigation of subject cannot be initiated for at least another month, as subject is currently being investigated by FBI for Voice of America.
