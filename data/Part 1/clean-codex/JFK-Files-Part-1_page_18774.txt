To: George E. Mcll Don

From: Transactions & Records Division

The unvouchered records of the following employees appointed on vouchered funds effective 21 March 1949 have revealed an error in computing periodic pay increases. In order to determine their last salary increase and establish a date they will be eligible for a periodic increase on vouchered funds, the following information is furnished for action you deem necessary.

Goodpasture, Ann L.

Temporary promotion effective 14 December 1947, from CAF-7, $3,397.20 per annum, to CAF-9, $4,149.60 per annum.

Return and change to lower grade effective 7 December 1948, to CAF-7, $3,727.20 per annum.

Pay increase (periodic) effective 8 December 1948, from $3,727.20 to $3,975 per annum. This is a jump of two steps.

The employee was not eligible for the above periodic increase until the first pay period following 14 December 1948, at which time she would have completed the one-year waiting period since her last salary increase. The increase should have been from $3,727.20 to $3,852.60.

Heinemann, Erma R.

Temporary promotion effective 16 November 1947, from CAF-5, $2,770.20 per annum, to CAF-6, $3,146.40 per annum.

Return and change to lower grade effective 28 July 1948, to CAF-5, $3,100.20 per annum.

Pay increase (periodic) effective 29 July 1948, from $3,100.20 to $3,225.20 per annum.

This employee was not eligible for the above periodic increase until the pay period following 16 November 1948.

Hennings, Willie May Connick

Temporary promotion effective 16 November 1947, from CAF-6, $3,146.40 per annum, to CAF-7, $3,522.60.
