While it is possible that Figueres may be involved in contingency planning should Calderón elements assume a predominant position in Trejos government, Station has no indication that Figueres has either funds or inclination to import an agit-prop mission. PLN sources continue to state, however, that they will have to move if Calderonistas return to a position of power, with ensuing police brutalities and other extralegal actions they reportedly used in 1948. Given the present situation, this danger is somewhat far-fetched, although it is possible that Figueres may be thinking of creating incidents, say circa October 1966, when the true complexion of the new government will be more evident.
