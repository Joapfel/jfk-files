Intelligence Information Cable. Date: 12 June 66.

The following intelligence information, transmitted from a CIA Field Station, requires review within the Clandestine Services before being released outside the Agency. Pending results of this review, a copy is being provided for your advance information.

Distribution for background use only to: SDO 2, DCI 20, B/4/1E.

Country: Dominican Republic/Haiti/Cuba.

Date of information: 13–14 June 1966.

Subject: Plans of Rolando Masferrer Rojas, former Cuban senator, for action against Haiti from the Dominican Republic.

Acquired: United States, Miami (16 June 1956), UFG-8382.

Source: Former Latin American diplomat who maintains relations with officials of Latin American countries. He is a trained reporter, and his information has been reliable in the past.

1. On 13 June 1966 in Miami, Florida, Rolando Masferrer Rojas, former Cuban senator, said that he had revised his plans for action against President Francisco deValier of Haiti to include the cooperation of Santiago Rey Perea. Rey was said that Masferrer would be allowed to take a vessel with six or seven men to the Dominican Republic after Joaquin Zalaguer is installed as president.
