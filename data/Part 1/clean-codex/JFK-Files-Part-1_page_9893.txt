14 November 1967

Subject: LINDG-3 (DEN A)

JED (6/87)

The interview was conducted in Spanish by the examiners. Williams A. ROWTON, subject's principal agent, was present during substantially all of the interview except for the hearing period.

Subject is a married male Minnesota citizen, an IDEN B by preposition. Prior to recruitment, his apparent political activities were consistent with the far left, but he was not believed to be significantly involved in radical-left or organized political activities. He was permitted by ROWTON under the cover of an IDEN C. Since his recruitment, subject has had contact with IDEN D elements and with IDEN E elements in his efforts to insert himself and ROWTON into the desired position. The initial goal of this operation was to monitor other trends and pick up whatever additional intelligence information might be incidentally scattered. As subject was and is, in his own idiosyncratic way, a commercial evangelist, ROWTON has not indicated any anti-commercial, anti-IDEN T, or anti-religious aims.

Subject was known to have lived and conversed information on numerous occasions, and it was presumed that considerably more details were not yet known to WOFIRM. On this basis, AGSTEL coverage was to have covered numerous issues concerning other intelligence involvements, including contacts with significant IDEN D and IDEN E figures, and revelation of his association with ROWTON. The issue of revelation of association with ROWTON was rather complex in that ROWTON revealed his time, name, nationality, or interests to subject, and also instructed subject to reveal certain identifiable factors to various specific individuals.

The examiner was presented to subject as an associate of ROWTON of Dallas extraction.
