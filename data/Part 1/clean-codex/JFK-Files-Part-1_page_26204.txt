Gustavo Omar Sanchez Diaz (201-867485) linked to EMPARENT-1 through notation in EMPARENT-1 confiscated papers (May 67): “Mario Molina and Rebeca de Molina, Ave Iquitos 1089, La Victoria, Lima.” (Santo Domingo-3526, 18 May 67).
