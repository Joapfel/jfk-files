Classified message. Date: 17 August 1966. Action: WH 8. Info: FILE, VE, CI, CHOPS, CHICZ, CHOA, FI, FILINTZ, SODA, CELSGO, CAZ.

1722452. Cite Mexico City 2981.

Director.

Reference: A. Mexico City 2533 (IN 82882 FEB 65); B. Mexico City 2594 (IN 86943 FEB 65); C. Director 87826; D. HMMA 25478; E. Mexico City 3262 (IN 77639).

1. From references it will be noted LIRAMA has long been interested in Victor Rico Galan and has considered ways of curtailing his activities, including consideration at one point of trying to denaturalize and deport him. Denaturalization, upon study, appeared too difficult and cumbersome. While Rico Galan and group, as claimed by press and as told to COS by LITEMPO-8 on 16 August 65, were obviously involved in subversive activities, believe Mexico governments' (MEXICO COVTS') knowledge of these activities resulted from long and tedious surveillance and investigation of Rico Galan, the purpose of which was to develop information on which action could be taken.

2. Forwarding copies of two memoranda passed to Library via LIELEGANT in December and April 65 containing information on Rico Galan. This due at record library, who then considering secret.
