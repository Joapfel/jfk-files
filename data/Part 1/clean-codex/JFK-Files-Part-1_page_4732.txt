E. Brigade 250B comprises veterans of the 1961 Bay of Pigs invasion of Cuba. Armando Lopez Estrada (L O P E Z) is military chief, and Pedro “Fico” Rojas Ramirez (R O J A S) is chief of press, propaganda and culture. Brigade 250B previously disclaimed association with militant or terrorist organizations, but will now publicly claim solidarity and a formal pact with the other four organizations, i.e., with CORU. This claim will be made even though M-17-A and PLNC members have never openly declared the existence of these organizations or membership in them. Brigade 250B actions will be carried out within Cuba, as distinct from its radio station on Swan Island beaming anti-Castro propaganda into Cuba. Brigade 250B disclosed an activity in progress in conjunction with the “Bloque Militar Cubano,” which was believed to be a clandestine group operating inside Cuba, reporting to someone in the Dominican Republic.

F. Movimiento 17 de Abril 1964, or April Movement (M-17-A), is a Brigade 250B splinter group formed by Juan Perez Franco of Miami, Florida, after his defeat as Brigade 250B president on 17 April.
