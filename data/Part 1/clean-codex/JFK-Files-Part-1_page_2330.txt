St. Serafimovich, 2 (the flat of her parents). In 1955 I received a flat at St. Narodnya, 13, where was living with my family.

In 1954 I contracted a disease (gonorrhea) and, on the advice of my friend IVANOV, went to the medical point at St. Negliunya. Doctors asked me to show a document. I had with me only an MVD certificate and an operative passport and showed them the passport. Doctors gave me treatment. After that, twice they made tests and asked me to come once more, but I did not come. They wanted to see me once more and sent a letter to my place of work, which was written in the passport. The plant, with the MVD, found out about it. The deputy of the chief, SHUBNIAKOV, was speaking with me. I had written my explanation and was punished by the chief of the 2nd Directorate, FEDOTOV—15 days of arrest. The Komsomol organization also punished me. I received a strict reprimand and was relieved of the head of the Komsomol organization of the 2nd Chief Directorate.

I was a member of the Komsomol organization from October 1943. At the end of 1954, before leaving the Komsomol because of age, the Komsomol organization of the KGB took off this strict reprimand.

In 1955, characterizations were written on all officers of the 2nd Chief Directorate (May–June). In my characterization was written that I was not appropriate for the 1st Department of the 2nd Chief Directorate. In June–July I was appointed to the 7th Department of the 2nd Chief Directorate as a case.
