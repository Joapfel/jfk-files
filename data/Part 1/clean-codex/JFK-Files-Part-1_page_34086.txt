Matanzas Province: Malanga fields were covered with water, which will rot most of the plant roots. Complete loss of corn crops was observed in the Malanga area.

Las Villas Province: “Viands” crops were destroyed.

Camaguey Province: 2% of the plantain, yucca, and sweet potato crops were destroyed.

Oriente Province: In “Mabay,” 1,400 bags of sweet potatoes and 160 quintals of yucca were collected. All vegetable crops in the vicinity of Santiago de Cuba suffered serious damage.

Beans and Rice

Pinar del Río Province: 15 “caballerías” of rice were damaged by the wind and heavy rains at “Patricio Lumumba” farm.

Havana Province: None.

Matanzas Province: None.

Las Villas Province: In the zone from Jatibonico to Sancti-Spíritus, rice plantations suffered serious damage. In El Mégano, 8 “caballerías” of beans were destroyed.

Camaguey Province: 20% of the bean crop was damaged.

Oriente Province: Rice fields near Yara had been leveled to the ground.

Cattle, Poultry and Dairy

Pinar del Río Province: 56 calves dead.

Havana Province: None.

Matanzas Province: None.

Las Villas Province: Numerous animals were drowned.

In El Mégano, 7 dairies were destroyed, 19 suffered minor damage, and one was partially damaged.
