Domestic Collection Division. Foreign Intelligence Information Report. Directorate of Operations.

Country: Zaire.

Subject: Recruitment of Cuban Exile Aircrews.

DCD Report No. OO-E 324/07314-77.

Date distributed: 1 April 1977.

No. pages: 1.

References: DCD Case 64,954.

Date of information: March 1977.

This is unevaluated information.

Source: U.S. citizen, a former professional intelligence officer. He received this information from a longtime acquaintance who was invited to participate in the project discussed in this report.

1. An individual described as chief pilot to Zaire President Sese Seko Mobutu (Mobuto) has been attempting to recruit pilots and ground crews from among Cuban exile groups to counter invading forces. Especially desired are members of the former Cuban Congo brigade.

2. Active in this project is an individual named René García.

3. According to prospective participants, two qualified pilots may have recently left for South Africa, presumably in connection with this project.

4. Zaire is also seeking to purchase Skyraider aircraft.

Initials: J.P. Hm. Date: 4 May 1977. Place: Cubans at Cuban airport. P.G. 322. H. 2007-04-18. Note: No such person or document found.
