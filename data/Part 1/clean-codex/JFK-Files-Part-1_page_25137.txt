DIR CITE MEXI 3151 (EASBY ACTING)  
REDCOAT LCIMPROVE  
Ref A MEXIC 3090 (CN43571).  
Ref B MEXI 3113 (CN45367).

19 April 65. IN 47738.

1. Follow-up interrogation subject, 19 April:

A. Both subjects, seen separately, freely admitted two visits to SovEmb (SOVEMB). They met at first session, 12 April, a Soviet fitting the description of Sergey Nikolayevich Leonov (KGB). The latter acted sympathetically toward their interest in living in the USSR, obtained some basic biographical data, and asked them to return 13 April to see the consul.

B. The Soviet at the second visit fits the description of Valeriy Vladimirovich Kostikov (KGB). They filled out and signed a lengthy Spanish or Russian-language form covering biographical data, and also wrote autobiographies. According to Crayne, Green agreed to join the Communist Party and act as a spy. According to Green, there was no mention whatever of espionage. The Soviet gave them the impression they would get a stuck USSR, said processing would take two to three months, and implied SovEmb (SOVEMB) would give them living expenses in Mexico until then. He told them to return the next day for a possible handout.
