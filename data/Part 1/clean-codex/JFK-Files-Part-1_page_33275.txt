Outgoing message.

Ref: Caracas 25759 (IN 38578A).

1. Recommend you consider enlisting suitable V-number asset to persuade junta to relax its restrictions on free press. Objectives of such move would be these:

A. Demonstrate to world that Pinochet government firmly in power in Chile and that it functions now with at least tacit consent of majority of Chileans.

B. Give substantial demonstration of junta's commitment to democratic process and thereby defuse one of more explosive grievances of world press against Chile regime.

2. To avoid appearing to capitulate to press from Cubans per ref, suggest Chilean move in response to this influence should not follow upon criticism emanating from Cuban-dominated journalists conference in Caracas. Gesture, if addressed to anyone, should be made to IAPA to convey international prestige that obeys or is committed to existing orderly democracy and therefore to reinforce its civilian support.
