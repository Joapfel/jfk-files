1. Attached is a photograph of Soviet Pohoril'v (Pohoril'v), which we would like shown, without name, to an appropriate unilateral source for possible identification.

2. FYI: Pohoril'v's (Pohoril'v's) name appears as the sender of a letter containing secret writing to Austin Biczynskik (Biczynskik) in Paris, France. The letter was sent in care of Dnytro (CYP), another Ukrainian resident of Paris. CYP, in November 1960, asked AROGKA/I to deliver the letter to Biczynskik, who was confined to a mental sanatorium near Paris. CYP told AROGKA/I that he and Biczynskik had been involved with Visually Impaired (WIA) Soviet assigned to UNECO and the case officer of the arrested enigre Grigorii Bemgoecke (Bemgoecke), and therefore CYP feared bringing the attention of the French police by delivering the letters to Biczynskik. MIGRATZ analysis revealed the presence of the secret writing in the letter from Pohoril'v (Pohoril'v), but failed in attempts to develop the secret writing. CODECY and Farid Station are now investigating this case; therefore, in your reply please send an information copy to Paris.

3. The following is biographic information on Soviet Pohoril'v (Pohoril'v).

Date of birth: 2 February 1910.

Place of birth: Crtehovy, Ukraine.

Parents: Spiridon and Alexandra; see INCRUED POHORIL'Y.

Education: Seven years elementary school and one year teacher's school.

Residence: USSR (USNR), 1924 to 1943; Poland, 1943 and 1944; Czechoslovakia, 1944 and 1945; Salzburg (Balzburg), Austria, 1945 to July 1950; United States, July 1950 to present.

Physical description: circa 1951, 5'10", 175 pounds, gray eyes and fair complexion.
