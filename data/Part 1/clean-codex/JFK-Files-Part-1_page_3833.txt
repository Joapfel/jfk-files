Intelligence Information Cable. Date: 18 March 1967. IN 24609.

The following intelligence information, transmitted from a CIA Field Station, requires review within the Confidential Services before being released outside the Agency. Pending results of this review, a copy is being provided for your advance information.

Distribution for background use only to SDQ 2, D/CNE. Distributed from Washington.

Country: Haiti/Cuba. Date of information: early March 1967. Subject: Formation of Cuban-Haitian émigré alliance with the goal of invading Haiti. Acquired: United States, Miami, 16 March 1967, UFG-8833. Source: A Haitian businessman with good contacts among Haitian émigrés. He is an untested source. He obtained this information from a former Latin American diplomat who maintains relations with officials of Latin American countries, and from Eugene Maximilien, Haitian consul general in Miami.
