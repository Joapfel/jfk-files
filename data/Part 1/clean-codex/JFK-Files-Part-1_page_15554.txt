Classified message. Date: 9 April 1961. To: Director L-1077. From: Leopoldville. 9 April 1338Z 1961. Action: C/AF 3 (tempo copy slotted for AF at 1100, 9 April 1961). Info: COP, C/CA, C/C1, C/F1 2, C/EE 3, S/C 2. Priority. IN 33155.

Priority. Directorate cite LEOP 1155.

Per GSH 43-43.

WIROGUE RYBAT.

Reference LEOP 1128 (N3085).

1. KANDOLO minister to sign contract. Details settled but typical Congolese procrastination. Swanson delayed departure twice because contract not yet signed but is setting 11 April as firm departure whether contract signed or not. GCC informed.

2. Have not mentioned suggestion paragraph 3 reference to Swanson. Waiting approval.

3. WIROGUE reported 9 April Stahl expected any day. If possible will arrange they not meet.

End of message.

CS comment: Suggested contact be established for Swanson Wiesbaden.
