Classified message.

Date: 9 August 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5. Info: ADDP, C/CA 2, S/C 2. Routing.

August 9, 2311Z, 1962. Routine. IN 42833. DIR CITE MEXI 1652. References: A, EMBTEL 460, 8 August 1962; B, EMBTEL 418. RYBAT.

1. At AMB Mann's request Curtis gave report regarding Cuauhtemoc Cardenas to LITENSOR on 3 August.

2. On 8 August LITENSOR sent word to Curtis via LIELEGANT that Cuauhtemoc Cardenas would not be a candidate for the position of “water commissioner” in the Mexicali area. Note: See MEXI 1637 (in 4/15), regarding meeting on night of 7 August between Lazaro Cardenas and LITENSOR. Curtis believes the matter of Cuauhtemoc's attempt was discussed at this meeting.

End of message.
