1. For your information: On 6 September 1966, freelance photographer Andrew St. George reported the following to William, Latin American Referent on Postow staff, G. Bowdler, State Department representative at White House:

St. George is on a CBS crew filming a story of gunrunning from the U.S. to the D.R. for use against Haiti. Crew had completed its filming in Miami and soon would travel to Santo Domingo to film operation at that end. Rolando Masferrer and Mitchell Warbel (phonetic) had permitted St. George to film a large arsenal (ARMSAL) in Masferrer's house in Miami. Warbel was described as a soldier of fortune and arms merchant from Atlanta who was assisting Masferrer in acquiring arms. Warbel was supposedly responsible for seizure of Manuel Ray's vessel “Venus,” with arms aboard, in the Dominican Republic. Plotters plan to use Beata Island as base. Arrangements in the Dominican Republic are being handled through Santiago Rey and Martinez Arana.
