3 January 1951

Chief, FDZ

Transfer of Personnel

1. Request is made that Mr. William V. Broe be transferred from Slot #1, Branch IV, FDZ/SEA, to Slot #1, Manila Research Unit. Mr. Broe will replace Mr. Lynn Boyer, who is now in Manila.

2. It is requested that Miss Carterette Choetham, recently transferred to FDZ/SEA from Staff C, be placed in Slot #2, Manila Research Station, to replace Miss Roberta Meyor.

3. Request is made that Mr. John M. Hansell be transferred from Slot #2, Branch IV, FDZ/SEA, to Slot #3, Manila State Station. Mr. Hansell will occupy one of the new slots opened in compliance with FDZ's memorandum of 30 November 1950.

Lloyd George  
Chief, FDZ

Originators: Virginia Pryor; Harry W. Little, Jr.
