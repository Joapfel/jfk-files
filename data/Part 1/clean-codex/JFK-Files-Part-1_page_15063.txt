Report on Contact with Agent or Defector

Subject: WIROGUE

Date: 9 November 1962  
Time: 1100  
Place: Ostendstr. 1, Apt. 9

ORME K. DARONNAT, Case Officer

Comments:

Case Officer dropped by to tell Subject that security advised us to move him before 1700 today. Case Officer told him he would be by after 1350 to pick him up.

Case Officer picked up Subject at 1400 and moved him to 51 Kuhwaldstr., top floor. Subject asked if he could unpack and spread out. Case Officer informed him that this apartment would also be given up, but he did not know when. He advised Subject not to spread out too far.
