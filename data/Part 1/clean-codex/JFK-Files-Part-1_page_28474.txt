Received.

To: Director of Central Intelligence  
TAB: Director of Personnel; Director of Security

Subject: Bagley, Thomas A. (3/15/55), Area Operations Officer, OS-13, SS Division, request for permits to remain in the employ of the Agency after marriage to an Alsatian citizen.

1. It is recommended that Mr. Bagley’s request to remain in the employ of the Agency after his marriage to Maria Lepley, an Alsatian citizen, be approved.

2. This recommendation is based on the following factors, after consideration of this request by the Clandestine Services Career Service panels:

a. Mr. Bagley has served effectively with the Agency since July 1950. He has demonstrated unusual ability in his field assignments and is regarded as an extremely valuable career officer.

b. The situation which this proposed marriage would place on Mr. Bagley’s use in his current assignment should be reviewed, since his normal tour of duty in Austria will expire in September of this year.

3. The security issues of this proposed marriage have been deferred to the Office of Security for its separate recommendation.

Director of Personnel.

11 April 1955
