S. Future Financing of Affiliates:

A. The matter of how funds from CISC, MISSBECK, and ITS will be handled had not been tabled by 9 January, but AMIC/31 stated that seven hundred thousand dollars will be made available over a three-year period for educational activities by the three funds. Methods and means of funding an additional one million five hundred thousand dollars to CLASS affiliates for overhead apparently will be discussed at the Brussels meeting.

B. DUBOLD/1 learned from Andres Mercau that J’DEVIL/1 is trying to obtain funds from Licio Lado, president of ACLI. Mercau alleged that ACLI leaders are also in contact with Africans, specifically Congolese groups, and were putting out feelers to Asian labor groups, and that J’DEVIL/1 is pushing this in hopes of obtaining financial help from ACLI. Comment: CIPU 24700 is of special interest as regards CLASS attitudes seeking financial help from ACLI.
