Dispatch.

Chief, Western Hemisphere Division; Chief, Europe Division; Chief of Station, Madrid.

Subject: TYPIC AMROSE AMBREDEW. Transmittal of photographs for AMBREDEW-1 recognition. Action required—referenced. Reference: DIRECTOR 93245.

1. Attached are 15 photographs of personalities, both Cuban and non-Cuban, who reside in the Madrid area. The majority are Cuban officials. We have added appropriate comments alongside the names of those individuals whom we suspect are Cuban intelligence agents or collaborators. Another list will be forwarded as soon as photographs can be obtained.

Photo 1: Hector GALLO Portieles (known DGI). Name mentioned by AMBREDEW-1.

Photo 2: Ricardo DIEZ Gallart, commonly called Gallart. Code Clerk, Madrid.

Photo 3: Crestes BARREDA Perez, First Secretary formerly in Switzerland; Mercedes ARIAS, wife.

Photo 4 (right to left): Guillermo RUIZ Perez, Encargado de Negocios; Nilda VECIANA de RUIZ, wife of Encargado; Ricardo ESPINO Martinez, Commercial Counselor.

Photo 5: Ricardo ESPINO Martinez, Commercial Counselor.

Photo 6 (arrows only): Guillermo RUIZ Perez; Aristides DIAZ Rovirosa, First Secretary and suspected DGI.

Continued.

Attachment: H/W photographs.

Distribution: 2 to C/WHD with attachment; 2 to C/EUR without attachment; 2 to COS/WH/Miami without attachment.

Distribution reference to: OSHA-35059. Date: 21 April 1969. MOS file number: 65-120-20.
