13 April 1961

An extract from a note sent to Consul General Dearborn from Juan Vicini:

“The three Leica cameras, model M-1 (the three carbines), with their accessories, should be in the proper hands in a day or two. With the ‘small amount of equipment which they are receiving they can only take action at a few specific places and therefore the probabilities are considerably reduced and the time factor considerably extended accordingly. With the rest of the equipment (four Leicas, model M-3) (the submachine guns) the possible places of action are multiplied and the lost time greatly reduced.”
