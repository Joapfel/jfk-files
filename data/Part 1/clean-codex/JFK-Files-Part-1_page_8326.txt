May 8, 1969.

(P-11,712)

Subject: Postponement of Valentin LOGINOV–LIBACON-1 Lunch.

Source: LIBACON-1 (P-4805).

1. In accordance with the prearrangements, I phoned LIBACON-1 at the language institute at 1455 hours to hear how the scheduled lunch went.

2. LIBACON-1 stated that a PM student of his postponed his lesson for half an hour and that he phoned LOGINOV to come to the restaurant where they were to lunch about 45 minutes later than they had agreed to meet. LOGINOV told LIBACON that in that case he preferred to postpone the lunch until the beginning of the next week and that LOGINOV would phone LIBACON-1 to set the date and time.

3. According to LIBACON-1, LOGINOV sounded friendly on the phone.
