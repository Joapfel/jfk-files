Upon further review of file on Bernardo Lemus Mendoza (201-0091575) for clearance purposes, one document which may not be available to station indicates that Lemus was in contact with a KGB officer while in Mexico. According to MGGN-4035 dated 2 Dec 66, information taken from captured PGT documents revealed that Lemus, while in exile in Mexico from 1953–56, was a close personal friend of known KGB officer Nikolay Sergeyevich Leonov (Leo Nov) (201-0132139).

2. Leonov (Leo Nov) was born 22 Aug 28 in USSR. He served in Mexico from 1953–56 as interpreter/secretary to the Soviet ambassador. FYI: Per postdrage from 56–61 he attended KGB school and served at KGB HOS. During 1961–64 Leonov (Leo Nov) was third secretary, assistant cultural officer at Soviet Embassy, and from 64–69 he was second secretary and press attaché. During his tours in Mexico, Leonov (Leo Nov) was reportedly consistently active in the support of Latin American revolutionary and subversive activities, particularly those related to Guatemala. Last known location of Leonov (Leo Nov) was trip to Bogotá from 27 June to 3 July 1977.

3. From above data it would seem that Lemus knew Leonov (Leo Nov) prior to Leonov's KGB training.
