3. Guatemala and Venezuela are the two countries in which the revolutionary movements are the most advanced, in Filo Galan's opinion. He had the following comments to make on the situation in these two countries:

A. Guatemala. In Guatemala, unlike Venezuela, the Communist Party had not initiated the armed struggle: the two groups involved in the armed struggle were the 13 November Group led by Marco Antonio Yon Sosa (Yon Gosa) and the Rebel Armed Force (FAR) led by Cesar Montes, since the death of Luis Augusto Turcios Lima (Turcice). The 13 November Group has stronger influence, while the FAR is “Castroite,” independent with its ties to the Guatemalan Communist Party (PGT) (FOT). The majority of the people are peasants, and the majority of them sympathize with and support the armed struggle.
