Dispatch.

To: Chief, WU Division. From: Chief, SR Division. Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Subject: Operational/Transmittal of LIENVOY Report.

1. Transmitted herewith in attachment is a self-explanatory LIFET report of 28 August. The local speaker is Vladimir Ivanovich SAMOKHIN, Chief of the Soviet Information Bulletin; the Moscow speaker is fmu KALUCIN.

2. The attached report is the first known instance to the Station of Moscow direction of the Bulletin in the informal manner described in the attachment. The Station will forward any future similar reports.

Willard C. Curtis.

Cross reference to.

Date typed: 31 Aug. 1962. Date dispatched: 4 Sept. 1962. Dispatch symbol and number: HNA-19741. Headquarters file number: 201-. Classification: Secret.
