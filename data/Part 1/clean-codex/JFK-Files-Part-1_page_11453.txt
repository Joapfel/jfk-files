Classified message.

5 March 1964.

To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: SAS 8. Info: DOP, CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, CI/OA, FI, FI/INT, WH 7, VR.

331929Z. DIR CITE MEXI 8478. TYPIC AMROD.

1. Following from LITATIL-3, night 1–2 March.

2. Rayar Sinosas, wife, and two children arrived in MEXI 1 March en route to take up a new post at Embassy Santiago, Chile. Wife described as common, “cheap.”

3. Elena Vasquez Gomez, lover of Teresa Proenza, has returned from Cuba, says Teresa will probably work in Cuba but may make a trip to MEXI soon.

4. Enrique (Enricue) Gonzalez Casanova (Casancva) back from Cuba, says he saw Teresa at a party attended by Raul (Rual) Roa and Joaquin Ordoqui (Ordógui), among others.
