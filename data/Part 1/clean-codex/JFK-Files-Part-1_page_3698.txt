I. COS met with AMBIDDY last night, 13 October. He reported on developments of the references as follows: As AMBIDDY was preparing to leave for Salvador in July, he was visited by Salvadoran Humberto Rodezno (Rodez No) (phonetic), who had a long shopping list of arms and ammunition. AMBIDDY explained that he could not get involved in any arms export matters, that he is well known as a Peruvian (Perumen) patriot and does not wish to change his image to that of an arms dealer or soldier of fortune. Rodezno said he was already in touch with an American in Miami who was going to furnish 30-06 ammunition at ten cents a round, shipping it from Inter-Armco in Virginia to Canada for onward shipment to Salvador.
