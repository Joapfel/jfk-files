Classified message. Date: 20 January 1962. Routing. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5. January 20, 0834Z 62. Info: DCI, D/DCI, DDP, S/C 2*. Priority. IN 14775.

Priority DIR CITE MEXI 9914.

Refs: A DIR 36178 OUT 91668. B MEXI 9913 (IN 14761).

C/S Comment: Hqs. asks if fabricated portions contain elements which Amb. Portuondo could deny or prove false.

RYBAT.

1. See Ref B for detail and partial answer to para one of Ref A. Ambassador, in 12 January meeting with LITEMPO-2, did state that COS could provide tapes of transcripts should they be needed. COS later covered, however, by stating to LITEMPO-2 that he thought some tapes had been erased. This stall was strengthened by firm statement that Colombian student tape would be available; it has since been delivered to LITEMPO-2. As indicated in Ref B, Station will find pretext not to deliver tapes other than authentic Colombian.

2. Re para 2 Ref A: Portuondo can certainly deny any or all of materials, authentic or fabricated. Only way he can prove, repeat prove, falsity is by proving his presence at a place other than Embassy at time of conversation. This is impossible since he was there.

End of message.

C/S Comment: Dissemination per originator.

Transcript of Amb. Portuondo's conversation with the Colombians. She recently returned to the Station by Hqs.
