Mother. Genevieve’s (Genevive’s) mother Stella, who was born in Poland and naturalized at Detroit, Michigan, travelled to Poland in 1956 and returned to the United States in that same year. Stella was a member of the International Workers Order, Lodge No. 3503, and was in good standing. Stella was also reported to have attended many receptions with representatives of the Polish Embassy. (DFB-67777, 13 August 1956)

4. Miss Lorraine Nowacki was the campaign manager for Ben Kocel, the candidate for membership in the State Legislative representation of Michigan. Lorraine and Ben obtained over 1,800 signatures within less than 5 hours under the Progressive Party’s petition demanding that the City Council in Detroit extend rent control. (DFB-27456, 15 June 1953)
