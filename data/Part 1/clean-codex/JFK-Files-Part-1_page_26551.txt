A. Rico Galan has been exposed as one of Castro's most important agents and channels for forming revolution against democratic institutions in Latin America. His past activities indicate he regarded Mexico only as a base of operations, a springboard for larger hemisphere operations on behalf of Castro.

B. Although Rico Galan and his group have been described variously as Castroite, Trotskyite, and pro-Chicom, his close and intimate contacts with local Cuban embassy and intelligence officials indicate his continuing loyalty and adherence to Castro. In this connection, a current Mexican newspaper item disclosed that Rico Galan was in close contact with Castro as far back as 1956, when Castro and his followers were hiding out in Mexico and undergoing training in guerrilla tactics at a ranch near Ayotzingo (Ayotzino) in Mexico. This Ayotzingo group later formed the expedition that sailed on the “Granma” from Tampico, Mexico, and ultimately overthrew the Batista government.
