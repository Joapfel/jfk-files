Att. No. 48 to UFGA-21693

Subject: MAZORRA, Rigoberto

1. Source met Subject circa 1957 through Ezequiel GOMEZ, also known as Kelo, at Guanabacoa, where Subject worked in the urban buses (routes 3 and 5). Subject was then engaged in activities against the Batista government, and in 1958 he went with Ezequiel GOMEZ to the Escambray Mountains to fight against Batista, remaining there until January 1959. In 1959 Subject returned to his work in the urban buses (routes 3 and 5), and Subject never held a position in the Castro government. In early 1961 Subject went to the Escambray Mountains to fight against Castro and was arrested about March 1961. When Source left Cuba in March 1964, Subject was at the Isle of Pines prison. Source says he believes Subject has always been anti-communist. In 1959 Subject went to Guatemala with Agapito AMARO, who was trying to start a transport business in that country, but they returned because that business did not work out. Source believes that Subject's wife and children are in the United States but is unable to provide names or addresses. According to Source, Subject has never received any political, military, or intelligence training.

2. Physical description: DPOB: circa 1923, Cojimar, probably. Race: white. Height: 6 feet, weight: 200 pounds. Hair: light brown and gray. Eyes: light. Shape of face: oval. Physique: heavy. No visible scars.

3. JEWAVE traces: None.

Cite: During interview in July 1961.
