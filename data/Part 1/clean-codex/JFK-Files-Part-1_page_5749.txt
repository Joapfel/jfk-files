Subject requested to resign from International (Internet's) Monetary Fund 2 Dec 52, refused to testify before Federal Grand Jury 20 Nov 52, and refused to testify before McCarthy Committee 1 Dec 52. Whittaker CHAMBERS has said that while he was in the Soviet espionage apparatus in the 30s he heard from various sources of plans to bring Subject from Canada so he could be placed in the Treasury Department. In 43, Elizabeth (Eliz) BENTLEY testified, SILVERMASTER told her that Subject had said he met a former friend of his in Canada who was “in same category,” i.e. Communist. BENTLEY doesn't recall the name of Subject's friend but it might possibly be Eric ADALS. It is known that Subject knew ADALS was on the Canadian Foreign Exchange Control Board, but it hasn't been established whether the men were personally acquainted.
