1. LIRING/3 received a letter acknowledging receipt of L/3's letter, postmarked 9 November in Guatemala, C.A., and expressing relief from anxiety. It gives greetings from “los canchitos,” Miguel, Elsa, “La Negra,” “La Chiquitia,” Felisa, Maria Elena, and “La gordita.” The envelope, written by another hand, is addressed to L/3 at his new residence and bears the return address: M. Bol., 28c “B” 8-25 Zona 8. LIRING/3 told Rowton this letter from mother Juan and Miguel León Barrios was apparently sent to test the commo channel. L/3 replied to the letter on 22/11/68, stating he expected soon to make a trip “outside country” and asking Marta to tell “los canchitos” (her sons Miguel and Juan) to send him the assignment (“encargo”) as soon as possible, since he was in a position to help them in some way. L/3 enclosed a clipping from “Siempre” of a letter to the editor describing his experiences in Guatemala.

2. L/3 also turned over to Rowton a letter from “Espinoza,” postmarked 7 November in Guatemala, C.A., sending warmest greetings but containing the phrase, “the León Barrios send you greetings,” which L/3 interprets as a signal from Juan and Miguel that contact with FAR is imminent. The envelope, addressed to L/3 at his new address, bears the return address: Oscar Moises Raldán E., Granja Penal Pavón, Fraijanes (Frajanes), Guatemala.

3. LIRING/3 also reported that he had a private talk with LIRING/9 on the late morning of 27 November, in which L/9 stated he was most interested in any observations L/3 might wish to make concerning the attitude and opinions of his FAR contact with respect to PBRUMEN support. He asked L/3 to put his thoughts in writing and submit them to him at 1730 on 26 November. At L/3's request, he promised to invite LIRING/10 and other interested LIRING officials to this meeting. LIRING/9 expressed the opinion that LIRING/3 should subsequently travel to PBRUMEN for “more detailed” discussions and offered to arrange a cultural-cover invitation. He added that it would not be necessary for L/3 to make a formal application for a visa.

4. LIRING/3 will submit to Rowton a draft of his report on the afternoon of 25/11/68 so that Rowton may amplify it as desired. L/3 told Rowton he planned to take the line that PBRUMENs do not understand local problems and that their aid in the past has been dissipated in factionalism.
