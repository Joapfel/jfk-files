16 Jan. 1951

LUNSON has an appointment to see Shub in NY tomorrow. I told him Shub had phoned yesterday, reporting progress.

LUNSON said he doubted Shub would be fully ready for the printer before LUNSON's (Hunson's) return, and didn't want him to try to handle it with the printer in LUNSON's absence.

LUNSON will try to see Bob Winston later this afternoon.

He will return from NY Friday evening, Jan. 19.

YSS
