12 December 1958

To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Attention: Mr. S. J. Papich

From: Deputy Director, Plans

Subject: Maurice Hyman Halperin

1. Reference is made to our GSCI-3/752,072, dated 30 October 1958, and to other correspondence concerning the subject, an American Communist living at Medellin #36, Apartment 9, Mexico, D.F.

2. Enclosed for your information are photocopies of the following correspondence sent to him:

a. Letter, postmarked 29 September 1958, from Ted Halperin, at 22 Kohler Avenue, Fulton 87, Massachusetts, concerns family matters, including the illness of his father (subject's brother), and mentions anticipation over the visit of the subject's son, David, in December.

b. Letter, postmarked 1 October 1958, from the Maurice H. Saval Insurance Company, at 22 Battarynarch Street, Boston 9, Massachusetts, concerns the renewal of the subject's life insurance policy No. 796864, of the New England Mutual Life Insurance Company.
