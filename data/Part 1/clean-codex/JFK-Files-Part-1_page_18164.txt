6 September 1966

Memorandum for: Chief, CCS  
From: WH Division  
Subject: Project Funding Plan/Project LIEMTY (FI)

A. Identification of Activity

1. Project cryptonym: LIEPTY

2. Approved for 1 December 1965–30 November 1966: $41,500. FY 1966: $25,520. FY 1967: $15,980.

3. Proposed successive fiscal year budget: Same.

4. Brief statement of project aims, character and structure:

The purpose of this project is to provide support for mobile (one truck) and stationary (three base houses) photographic surveillance; one base house is also used as a dispatching base for a physical surveillance team. This project covers one Mexican contract agent who serves as principal agent for the eight Mexican field agents under the project.

B. Funding History

1. The contract agent receives his salary directly from the Mexico City Station case officer ($540/month). He also receives money from the Station, which he uses to pay the field agents their salaries (total of $1,700/month) and operational expenses. Receipts are received.

2. Funds have always been handled in this manner.
