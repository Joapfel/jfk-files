Classified message. File in CS file No. 201-3373. Conf: Anne McDonnell. Unit: WH/3/CAS. Ext: 6312. Date: 29 October 1963. To: see below. From: Director. Conf: WH 8. Info: DOP, ADOP, SOD 6, CI, CI/OFs, CI/IC 2, CI/CA, FI, SAS 8, RF, VR. Cite DIR 79062. To priority MEXI. Info: GUAT, NANA, SJOS, RIOD, RECI, SAOP, PACY.

1. Suspected PBRUMEN agent Victor Rico Galan, Mexican journalist, DPOB 6 February 1920, El Ferbol, MEXI, apparently using matronymic, reportedly travelling Central and South America on mission for AMTHUG-1. Rico Galan left PACY for CARA 22 October.

2. For MEXI: Rico Galan reportedly recently spent eight days PBRUMEN on “vacation” with AMTHUG-1, during which time he received operational assignment for which he is now travelling through Central and South America. Although well-known leftist, he is able to travel because he has some special arrangement with MEXI government which gives him special privileges from MEXI embassies in places he visits. Galan’s mission is to persuade leftist parties in countries he visits to begin immediately acts of sabotage and violence and to try to maintain these in order to take pressure off Venezuela. Galan has also been instructed to bring better unity among competing leftist factors to ensure greater impact of their work. To contact in PACI Galan.
