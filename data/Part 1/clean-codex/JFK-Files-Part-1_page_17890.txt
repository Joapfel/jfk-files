Dispatch. Dispatch station and no. 103-M-7922. To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Headquarters file no. 50-6-32/2. From: Chief, WHD. Subject: LIFEAT/ZONAR. Action required: None. Reference: MTTA-13043, 5 June 1959.

The following system which will give the Station 26 new target symbols for use in LIFEAT/ZONAR has been assigned to Mexico City:

An additional Station symbol “N” has been assigned. Now, repeating the A through Z series in column number 2 will make 26 new combinations. The former targets should be called MA, MB, MC, etc. The new targets will be NA, NB, NC, etc.
