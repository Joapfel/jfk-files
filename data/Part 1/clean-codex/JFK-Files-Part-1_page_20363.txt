Affidavit

Striking Against the Federal Government

Economic Cooperation Administration (Department or Establishment). Bureau or Office. Washington, D.C. (Place of Employment).

I, B. Howard Hunt, Jr., do hereby swear (or affirm) that I am not engaged in any strike against the Government of the United States and that I will not so engage while an employee of the Government of the United States; that I am not a member of any organization of Government employees that asserts the right to strike against the Government of the United States, and that I will not while a Government employee become a member of such an organization.

II. I am loyal to the United States, its Constitution and form of Government, and I am not now a member of any organization advocating, to the best of my belief, contrary views, nor have I ever been a member of any organization advocating, to the best of my belief, contrary views during the period of my membership.

Subscribed and sworn to before me this 17th day of May 1948 at Washington, D.C., State of.

May J. Decker, Appointment Clerk, Economic Cooperation Administration, Washington, D.C.

Act of June 26, 1943, Sec. 206
