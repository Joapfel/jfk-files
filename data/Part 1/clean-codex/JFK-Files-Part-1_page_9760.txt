LNG: WH/1/3E04. Extension: 6924. Date: 11 July 1968. Cable Secretariat dissemination. Reference number prior to crossing: 15576. To: Mexico City.

Should not advance LIRING/4 any funds unless LIRING/4 able securely explain their source.

End of message.

Index: SERRANO.

WH comments: LIRING/4 approached by PCM member Arturo SERRANO, who claimed he has contact with a Guatemalan exile who could arrange a bribe through relatives in Guatemala to assist LIRING/3, who imprisoned that country. Guatemala City requested more information prior to commenting on the feasibility of the bribe offer by SERRANO.
