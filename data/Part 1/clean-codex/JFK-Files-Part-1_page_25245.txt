Subject of document: Cable. Dispatch or source document symbol No.: MEX1-04309. Date: 21 April 63.

Document Disposition. Cross referenced to this file: 201-132139. Transferred to R1 file No.: 201-132139.

Pertinent Information:

14. Leonov, Nikolay Sergeyevich (201-132139).

Nikolay Sergeyevich Leonov (201-132139) stationed mounted LIEMBRACE surveillance for eventuality he might make last-minute clandestine meets. MEXI efforts paid off in that team able spot obvious clandestine half-hour meet evening 19 April. Team tailed Mexican contact whom station has now identified as Lorenzo Cardenas Barajas, CIT Mexico. For info Cardenas, direct headquarters attention Lineuck traffic MEXI 0545 on 11 April 62, MEXI 0563, 0698, 0827, and 0873, and also other MEXI reporting re Cardenas.

LIEMBRACE surveillance Leonov 20 April dropped during late afternoon after Leonov made obvious efforts determine if he under surveillance. Interesting note that Leonov was then driving around same area where had clandestine meet previous evening. Team believes Leonov did not detect their presence.

According latest Leonov (LIEONOV) info, appears likely Leonov returning MEXI after home leave, USSR.

Station files reflect that, basis Lineuck info, MEXI advised LITEMPOS during April 1962 that Cardenas involved smuggling arms Guatemala. April 63 MEXI LITEMPO provided following information: Cardenas enrolled military college.
