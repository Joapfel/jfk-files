26 March 1975

Memorandum for: Director of Central Intelligence  
From: Deputy Director for Operations  
Subject: Consultant Status for James Angleton, Raymond G. Rocca, William J. Hood, Newton S. Miller (Miler)

Reference: HR 20-52 and IIIB 20-8

1. Action Requested:

This memorandum requests the Director to approve the use of Messrs. Angleton, Rocca, Hood, and Miller as Agency Consultants for the period 1 April–30 September 1975.

2. Background:

a. All four officers retired under the CIARDS system 31 December 1974 and are currently contract employees, contracts to expire 30 March 1975.

b. It is proposed that the subjects' services be retained under consultant status on an intermittent, as-needed basis as determined by Acting Chief, CI Operations, Mr. George T. Kalaris. In view of the long and unique experience of these officers in counterintelligence, not duplicated in any other personnel, the new Acting Chief, CI Operations, will want to have available the benefit of their advice and counsel during the next few months.
