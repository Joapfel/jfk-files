Dispatch. To: Chief, WF Division. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: Removal of Oliver G. SCANTLING from Project LIMETY.

Reference: ENA-24650 dated 29 October 1964.

1. As per section VI, subsection E, of the attachment to Reference (the Request for the Renewal of Project LIMETY), Station Mexico City is at present preparing a formal Project Outline to cover KUBARK (KURARK)'s relationship with and the operational activities of Oliver G. SCANTLING. This activity involves the conduct and support of various operations targeted primarily against the local Soviet bloc and PENGUEN installations.

2. It is requested that Headquarters assign a cryptonym and a four-part file number in the 50-6 series for this proposed project.

Distribution: C/WMD. Christina DeMunoz for Willard C. CURTIS. Dispatch symbol and number: HOMA-25254. Date: 27 January 1965. Headquarters file number: 50-6-74/1.
