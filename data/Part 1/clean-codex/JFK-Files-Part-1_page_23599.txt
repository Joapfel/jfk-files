Classified message. Central Intelligence Agency. 28 July 1953.

IN 45639.

To: Director, CIA.  
From: SR representative, Buenos Aires.  
Action: 4.  
Information: COP, FI, FI/OPS, FI/PLANS, FI/RI 2.  
Routine.

00552, 29 July 1953.  
BUEN 734.

To: DIR. Info: SSANJ. Cite: SBUEN.

Re: DIR 11908 (OUT 74476), SANJ 1009 (IN 45027).

Humanoid desired for BUEN use. Objectives: penetration of Argentine Communist Party and performance of cutout duties as assigned. Targets: Argentine Communist Party, Communist student and intellectual circles, Communist labor groups, and PCA penetration of government circles. Tasks: gather pertinent data from all available sources on leading committees (commites) and associates.

End of message.
