Chile

September 14, 1955

The Subsecretary for Diplomatic Affairs of the Colombian Foreign Office, Dr. Ricardo Aram, informed an Embassy officer on September 12 that the La Republica article was accurate only insofar as it reported that Ambassador Vargas had returned to Chile. Dr. Anaya said that, as far as he was aware, Ambassador Vargas had gone to Santiago to make final arrangements for the visit to Chile of the Colombian War College, scheduled for September 14 to 29. The Chilean (Chil-can) Embassy states that the Ambassador returned to Santiago accompanied only by his wife. First Secretary Sr. Manuel Tello Troacoco is Chargé d'Affaires ad interim.

D-203, Bogota, September 14, 1955. OUOnly
