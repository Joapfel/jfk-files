Info: MEXI CARA GUAT

1. HYAB returned PACY from MEXI ref trip 29 March via guest flight 270. Carried $5,000 received from Soviet Embassy MEXI through Martinez Verdugo. These funds to be used by PDP for trips to various congresses, including forthcoming Komsomol Congress, which HYAB believes will be held in Moscow. PDP will send one delegate.

2. On return flight HYAB had long conversation with Deputy Eduardo Machado, leading CARA-CP leader. Machado stated top CARA-CP requirement is to obtain all possible information on U.S. training bases in Canal Zone, particularly any training of Latin Americans in anti-guerrilla warfare, which is in part to be utilized as instructions for Venezuelan Communist guerrillas, as well as to utilize information to counter anti-Communist guerrillas.

3. HYAB further identified “EL ROJO” (reported ref B) as Rodriguez Lopez, as Rogelio LNU, Cuban Embassy functionaries in MEXI, and was shown CP “safehouse” at 175 Calle Montes de Oca, Apt. 1.

4. HYAB also received general briefing on GUAT CP strategy and policy line from Martinez Verdugo, who is following the situation closely. Intelligence cable to follow. Pouching details.
