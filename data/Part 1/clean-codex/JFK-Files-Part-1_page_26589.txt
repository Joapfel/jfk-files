CITE MEXICO CITY 2/23/79

Director

18 August 1966. 78356.

1. To date, press play on arrests of Victor Rico-Galan (Rico Gallen) and fellow conspirators has been confined to routine replay of police handouts. Latest police communiqué issued evening 16 August included “statements of responsibility” (by implication, confessions) made by Rico Gallen (Rico-Galan), Ing. Gumersindo Gomez Cuevas, Doctor Rolf Meiners Huesner, Prof. Rath Sarinana Sarinana, Nina Maria Rico Galan (Rico’s sister), Sergi Lopez, Jose Pumee Rto Juarez Hernandez, Olivier Juan Perez Calicia, and Eduardo Uribe Ayala. No statement by Raul Ugalde published. No editorial commentary noted to date. This appears to be result of tight lid imposed by Governor on press treatment—beyond line and facts contained in police handouts.

2. El Universal Grafico afternoon 17 August noted in front-page article that police investigating possibility of “foreign involvement” in case, given the fact Rico Gallen was ready to board a plane when arrested. Afternoon editions reported that police were about ready to turn over cases of detainees to Attorney General for indictment.

3. Reports of two Molotov cocktails thrown at Cine Encanto by [CHG].
