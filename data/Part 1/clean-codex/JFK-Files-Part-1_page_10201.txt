C/S comment: Requested PRO part 1 info on subject.

PRO 1 punched REF B 31 July 62. No derog traces. CO in direct contact LITAMIL-7, whose assignments include reporting on embassy activities and personnel, as well as procurement exemplars: seals, envelopes (used and those mailed from Cuba). L-7 only station asset inside embassy residence, where Amb. Lechuga, Ana Maria Blanco, Ramon Sinobas Casado, and Radulfo Paez Perez also work.

L-7 not cooperating well but appears to have little access to important information. Her work consists mostly of unclassified protocol. Notes Mexico Government and issuance of courtesy and diplomatic visas, i.e. those not issued by consulate. She also pays clerk local employees. She thus far is not aggressive “Mata Hari” (“NATA HARI”) type and provides only information available through her routine work.

At least meeting 27 July provided first tangible item when she gave CO exemplar courtesy visa given all travelers HAVA who do not wish to have visa passports. L-9 explained in detail manner of filling out courtesy visa, which is issued on sheet of Cuban Embassy paper. This is major step in L-7 development. Next meeting 6 August.

(End of message)
