7 August 1952

Pittsburgh

Index Security

VThorne: Jim

To Kaufmann from Ashitaft by Index (Thorns).

Re NED dated 1 August, IN 52-672, addressed to Mr. Czajkowski, Alien Branch, regarding contact of aliens Kriegsik, Notas, and Loposzno, please forward full names for filing purposes.

It will be appreciated if, in the future, you will forward notification of contact of aliens prior to clearance to Index. Thank you.
