Van Clean told us during one of the aforementioned conversations that he was acquainted with Mitchell WerBell (Webbell), Pender Springs, Georgia, and that he had been “pressurized” into taking WerBell (Webbell) to Argentina with him in late January 1974 by officials in the Coca-Cola Company, Coral Gables, Florida, with which Van Clean had a consulting contract in connection with Coca-Cola’s security system in Argentina. Van Clean advised us that he never again would have anything to do with WerBell (Webbell) because the latter had acted so badly during this trip. He told us that WerBell (Webbell) had commented to Argentine officials that he (WerBell) was somehow connected with the Central Intelligence Agency, and on a first-name basis with “Bill” Colby (Coley), DCI. Van Clean told us that matters had degenerated to such a degree that he returned to the U.S. without WerBell (Webbell). Our memory is a bit fuzzy on the contents of this discussion, but we recall something to the effect that WerBell (Webbell) was involved with wine, women, and song, and Van Clean was afraid that WerBell (Webbell) might be arrested by Argentine police before it was over. Van Clean told us that he personally never has so much as implied a relationship with the Agency to any outsider.
