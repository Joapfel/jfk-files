WNINTEL RYBAT HEAM.

References: A. Director 269199. B. Caracas 33655 (IN 341549).

1. Any additional information we may obtain on the nature of Morales’s contact with SLFAT-1 will be cabled to addressees. At this point all we have is information provided in reference B.

2. While it is possible, we don’t think that Morales’s contact with SLFAT-1 is the result of a WKTANGO-1-directed recruitment operation. We are more inclined to believe that this is some sort of strange liaison or double-agent operation, probably suggested by Morales himself, ostensibly to make himself useful to WASCARLET. It has been mentioned before, and we have long suspected, without any hard evidence, that Morales may be a Castro Cuban agent. It is quite possible that he has conned the Venezuelan government, or WKTANGO-1, into believing that he was some special entrée with SLFAT-1. All in all, based on our present knowledge of the characters.
