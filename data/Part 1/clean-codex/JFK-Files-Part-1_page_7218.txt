30 July 1968

S.B.C.

The Office of the Secretary of State.

1. On 29 July 1968, the C.O. and the following Soviet travel arrangements were confirmed: Serrovy Konstruktivnyy, V. Semy�elev, and child Nikita, on Sabena for 24 August, Leningrad/Moscow, 26 August. Vladimir Gorcharenko, V.V. (wife), and children Horina and Vladimir, on Sabena, 7 August, Brussels/Moscow, 9 August.

Regarding Licenciado Luciano Lara: he dropped into the subject’s office on 28 July (?) and was very officious. When the subject did not have confirmed travel to satellite countries, including Romania and Czechoslovakia, ready, Lara fussed and said he would go to Moscow and make his arrangements from there. He planned to stay in Moscow a month. He had a free ticket on Approved Mexico to Madrid. The subject booked him for travel from Madrid to Moscow, via Amsterdam, and return.

Valentin Logvinov had dropped in to the subject’s office on 27 July, but the subject was too busy to see him. Logvinov called the subject on 28 July and made an appointment to see him at 1200 on 29 July at the Flor de Mexico restaurant near the subject’s office.

The C.O. agreed with the subject that he should continue to play the same tune with Loginov: friends, yes-work, no.

2. On 30 July the C.O. saw the subject briefly in his office. The subject’s wife had accompanied him to the office because she would be remaining there while the subject was away on his European trip starting 30 July. According to the subject, his wife was not seeing the C.O. except as a friend of his. With his wife Esther around, the subject was able to manage only a brief meeting with Loginov on 29 July. The expected power play by Loginov did not take place. He met with the subject just to confirm Pavel Yatskov’s travel arrangements and to ask the subject again if they could bring him anything from Moscow. Tatshev dropped in to the Flor de Mexico too, but contributed nothing to the conversation. The subject said he did not want anything from Moscow; he had given Prilozhsko fifty dollars to bring him back two kilos of caviar.

For the promised additional information on the group who were going to the Microbiological Congress in Moscow (see previous Contact Report for the complete list), the subject turned over to the C.O. the attached list, which gives only telephone numbers of the professionals going to the Congress and, in some cases, their positions.

The C.O. told Esther that he might drop in to the office if he could do anything for her. Note: The subject is not due back until the end of August.
