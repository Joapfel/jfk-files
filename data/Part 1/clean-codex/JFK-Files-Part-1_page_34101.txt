November 19, 1965.

Miami, Fla., Nov. 19 (AP)—A sharp decline in shipping from non-Communist countries to Cuba (CUA) was reported today.

Only seven vessels from these areas touched Cuban ports last month, said Revolutionary Unity (CU), an exile organization which watches such movements. This is the smallest total since it began keeping count four years ago.

Listed as visiting Cuba (CUFA) in October were three ships from Cyprus, two from Great Britain, and two from Greece.

This compares with 17 ships the previous month. Other October totals: 21 in 1965, 23 in 1964, and 26 in 1963.

CU said it did not know what caused the drop.
