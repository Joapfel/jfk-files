21 October 1958

Subject: Victor Rico Galan

Attached is a listing of known contacts of Victor Rico Galan with offices of the Cuban, Yugoslav, Soviet, Polish, and Czech Embassies and with Chinese Communist persons stationed in Mexico.

During the period from 16 January 1957 through 13 August 1968, Victor Rico Galan is known to have had:

a) 250 contacts with Cuban Embassy officials stationed in Mexico;

b) 33 contacts with Yugoslav officials stationed in Mexico;

c) 31 contacts with Soviet officials stationed in Mexico;

d) 7 contacts with Chinese Communists stationed in Mexico;

e) 6 contacts with Polish Embassy officials in Mexico;

f) 4 contacts with Czechoslovakian officials in Mexico.

This represents a total of 337 contacts with officials of these Communist installations by Victor Rico Galan; 327 of those contacts were made in less than a four-year period of time. Most of the persons contacted by Victor Rico Galan as shown on the attached list are known intelligence officers.

Attached to AMMA 30357.
