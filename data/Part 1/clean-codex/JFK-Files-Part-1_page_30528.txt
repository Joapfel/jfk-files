General Headquarters, Far East Command, APO 500.

28 July 1951.

AG 201-100M. AG 209-2.

Subject: Temporary Duty.

To: Individuals concerned.

1. Following individuals will proceed on or about this date to Pusan, Korea, and Headquarters Eighth Army (III-III), APO 301, on temporary duty for a period of approximately five (5) days in connection with military intelligence activities. Upon completion, they will return to proper station.

Colonel Bermantzer H. Vandervoort, 022715 Infantry, GHQ FEC, 8100th AU, APO 500.

Mr. William E. Duggan, DAC employee, GS-15 (on detail).

Mr. Benson S. Milek, DAC employee, GS-12 (on detail).

2. Travel by military aircraft is authorized. Travel will be performed at no expense to the military air transport services. An allowance of sixty-five (65) pounds of personal baggage is authorized for each individual while traveling by air. No per diem is authorized.

3. DAC employees will wear the Army officers' uniform, less insignia, in the combat zone. Uniforms may be purchased from GM Salos Store upon presentation of these orders.

4. Individuals are authorized to draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after return to Tokyo, Japan.

5. Individuals are authorized to perform duties as official couriers during the period of temporary duty.

6. Inspections and physical inspection as prescribed by Circular 9, FEC, 27 February 1950, will be completed by each individual prior to departure.

By order of General Ridgley:

Thomas F. Welly  
Captain, etc.  
Last Adjutant General.

Distribution: Commanding Officer, Eighth Army, APO 301; Commanding General, Seoul Field Command; three individuals concerned through Commanding General, Seoul Field Command; CPD; G1, Seoul Field Command (Civilian Corps).
