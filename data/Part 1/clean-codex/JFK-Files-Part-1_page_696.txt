The following is biographical information on Joseph D. Ardleigh, executive vice president, Research Institute of America, Inc., 539 Fifth Ave., New York, New York.

He was born 24 May 1914 at Scranton, Penn.

His present home address is Alden Road, Greenwich, Conn.

He was previously connected with the law firm of Milbank, Tweed, Hope and Webb of New York, the Hoover Company of North Canton, Ohio, and had his own business, which was known as Sales Methods, Inc., Rockefeller Plaza, New York.

He joined the Research Institute in 1939.
