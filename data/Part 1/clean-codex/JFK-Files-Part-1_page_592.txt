Det. 1, 1045th Operational En[?] Action and Handling (Tendling) Group, Major Billy B. Campbell, Commander.

I. Situation:

Daniel I. Carswell (Miles), Walter H. Purbrick (Allans), and Edward R. Taransky (Alfons) are the only staff employees who were substituted by Cuban authorities in September 1948 and who are now serving ten-year sentences. Efforts are being made to secure their release. Since their release may come without advance notice at a variety of places—in Mexico City, New York, Miami, Mexico City, Switzerland, Hong Kong, etc.—it is imperative that a contingency plan be formulated to secure the safe transit of these employees.

The controlling factor in all phases of the release procedure is that these employees were in case of for alive and lost during the entire period since their capture. Their life is not even assured presently. It is essential that there should be no delays. Therefore it is essential that there should be maintained until such time as the employees have reached Agency control and then have these alive so that they can be immediately as possible.

For planning purposes, the name of person M.L. on Mexico, Mexico City, or “other areas.”
