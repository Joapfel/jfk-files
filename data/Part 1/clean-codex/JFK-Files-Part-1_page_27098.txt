29 September 1977, staff LAfscher — H. Speed handling with AF Division. Cite Geneva 35610.

To: Immediate Director. Info: Immediate London.

1. On 29 September 1977 at 1830 hours local, one Gary Van Dyk, who identified himself as a British subject who was born in Johannesburg, South Africa, on 25 May 1936 and who said he is a literary agent by profession and resides at 62 Redknoll Walk, Chelsea, SW-3, London, walked into U.S. Mission. Subject, who is staying at Hotel Amat in Geneva, originally contacted DOM Sorenson, who asked that COS interview him.

2. Subject gave the following story, which admittedly appears disjointed but may make sense to Africa Division. One reason for incoherency is that subject was concerned about the story leaking to outsiders, i.e. the press, and was very reticent about naming names or dates:
