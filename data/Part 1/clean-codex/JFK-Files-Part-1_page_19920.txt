Memorandum Receipt

7 June 1963

To: W. S. Renehan, WH/Pers  
From: Mrs. Anna Tarasoff  
Subject: Receipt of Advance of Funds for Transportation from Washington, D.C. to Mexico City

I hereby acknowledge receipt of $525.00 in conjunction with the subject. It is understood that this advance is for [REDACTED] lowest first-class rail travel from Washington to Mexico City, Mexico, and related costs, and anticipated per diem for three days for myself and two dependent children. Accounting for the advance will be submitted to the Mexico City Station.

Please return [REDACTED] signed copy (los) of this receipt.

Anna Tarasoff  
Signature of Recipient

Date received: 1963
