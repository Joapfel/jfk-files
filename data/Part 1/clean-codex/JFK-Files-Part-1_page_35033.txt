The Bank of St. Louis Revolving Fund Profit  
Finance and Debt  
General Accounts  
“Full and Ceded”  
December 1 to 31, 1960

In the Statements and Annexes attached, appeared the analysis and sub-analysis of each of the incomes and expenses during the month of December.

The receipts, bills, deposit sheets, checks returned by the bank, and other justificatory certificates of the operations of the month are in our records. In cases where the justificatory certificates were not provided, our records contain the memorandums on account of which the checks were made.

On December 31, 1960, there are still reimbursements to collect in the amount of $350.00, corresponding to check No. 21 of October 4, in the name of Mr. A. Fernandez Lorell, from the Military Section.

The increase of the Revolving Fund of the Military Section was reimbursed to the Comptroller by the Military Coordinator, but the funds were not deposited in the bank, since we have learned in practice the necessity of having cash available for urgent needs on more than one occasion. To solve these cases, we decided to maintain this fund in our hands, at the Military Coordinator’s disposition.

M.L.
