AMTABBY 27 on 17 Jan 66 advised by Jose Alegrette that on 15 Jan Jaime Varela Canosa visited Alegrette, asking Alegrette to contact Somoza to obtain permission for Varela (VERELA) and “his group” to use former AMBIDDY-1 camp Monkey Point. Varela stated “his group” was shortly to receive enough aid to start operations against PERUMEN and would use AMBIDDY-1 Nicaragua camps as bases.

3. WAVE officer on 17 Jan contacted AMLITAINT-1, a longtime friend of Varela, to determine whether there was any basis for Varela’s statements. A-1 stated that he had seen Varela last in Nov 65, at which time Varela stated he was working with ODDEAT along with Peruyero of the 2506 Brigade Vets Association.

4. WAVE comment: Traces indicate Varela, former president of the 2506 Brigade Vets Association, was replaced by Peruyero. Varela also identified as an ACSI source, number 1002, as of 17 Jun 65.
