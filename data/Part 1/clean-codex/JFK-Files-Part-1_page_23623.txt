Classified message. Central Intelligence Agency.

IN 21247.

To: Director, CIA. From: SR Rep San Jose. Action: WH L. Information: COP, FI, FI/OPS, FI/STC, PP 2, PP/PAD, FI/RI 2. Routine.

20542, 3 May 53. SANJ 978. To: DIR. Cite: SSANJ. PSYCH.

1. Early morning 1 May, HUMANOID entered commie headquarters, lifted portion of party files, and made getaway unnoticed. Entry undertaken on his initiative after learning from HUMANOID-3 that commies were thinking of moving files.

2. Take consists of Vanguardia Popular militant visits, membership applications, minutes of Political Commission meetings; Partido Progresista constituent assembly record with lists of all national delegates; miscellaneous party documents. Extent of damage to PPI campaign not known yet. Processing and evaluation by HUMANOID/HUMANOID-3 to be delayed pending party reaction. No mention made in Commission meeting 2400Z on 2 May. At present this is believed to indicate party simply unaware of loss.

End of message.
