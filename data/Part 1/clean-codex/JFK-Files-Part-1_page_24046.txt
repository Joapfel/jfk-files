Dispatch. To: Chief, WOLIME. Info: Chief of Station, London; Chief, EUR Division. From: Acting Chief, LCPIPIT/London (PARFORTH Acting). Subject: DTDORIC/Operational; QRPHONE/Illness.

1. At their meeting on 4 September QRPHONE/1 submitted a report to RODIGAST on his hospitalization for hepatitis. Attached to his report is a copy of this medical record with results of blood and urine analyses written in Serbo-Croatian. We are forwarding a copy of the report under separate cover for evaluation by the Medical Staff. We are interested in having as thorough as possible an evaluation made of QRPHONE/1's illness in order that we may assure ourselves of his condition and the degree to which he ought to be active in the forthcoming months. We will appreciate it if Headquarters will pursue this matter with the Medical Staff and advise us of its findings and recommendations.

2. Also forwarded under separate cover is a memorandum prepared from RODIGAST's notes which elaborates upon QRPHONE/1's symptoms.

Aaron C. LAGINESTRA.

Attachment: u/s/c. QRPHONE/1 Report & Medical Record; Memorandum for the Record.

Distribution: 3 - C/WOLIME w/atts; 1 - COS/London w/o atts; 1 - C/EUR w/o atts.

Cross-reference dispatch symbol and number: OEPA-1312. Date: 10 September 1968.
