Res: Director 14279.

1. Station agrees Charles's life is in imminent danger due to his plotting and ambitions. Believe Charles is placing hopes on American financial associates and U.S. Government to save him in case he is arrested. American flag now flying daily over central office of commercial bank.

2. Unfortunately Rachfield is confined to home for one week due to circulatory disturbance and Chubb has no access. Will arrange EVLEMON-2, who lives fairly close, to make periodic neighborhood checks. Daily drive-by past Charles's house revealed nothing abnormal mornings of 26 and 27 June. Also will ask embassy junior officer tenants of another Charles house to look in on him.
