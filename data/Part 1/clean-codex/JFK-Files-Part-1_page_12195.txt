1. Course of reference A, 10 November 1976 meeting with WKSCARLET-9. Agent revealed that Ricardo Morales Navarette (201-285923) had left Venezuela and was seen off by WKTANGO-1. WKSCARLET-9 did not know Morales’s destination but understood from WKTANGO-1 it was Europe, probably London.

2. WKSCARLET-9 said he had no explanation for Morales’s animosity toward SKHERALD other than he thought Morales believed he had somehow been maligned by SKHERALD, which had ruined his potential for becoming successful and healthy in Venezuela, and that Morales was suffering some kind of emotional crisis. Morales told WKSCARLET-9 and WKTANGO-1 that Manuel de Arvas expose was a SKHERALD operation directed.
