MCC. Comping Chrono. 28 October 1964. P-8349. P-7921. P-7565. Cuban Chrono.

DIR. KANA WAVE. MEXI. XXIV 14:07. Topic: AMBOS. Rev. MEXI 139th 1942.

1. On the morning of 26 October, when it became apparent LITEMPOS was unable to persuade Gobernación to switch Gonzalez’s papers for later arrest, the station placed LIEBRACE surveillance on Gonzalez at Hotel Astoria. (LIEBRACE coverage had been withdrawn when LITEMPOS found Gonzalez, as the station did not want LITEMPOS and LIEBRACES (LIEMBRACES) tripping over each other at Astoria.) LIEBRACES (LIEMBRACES) did not pick up Gonzalez until the morning of 28 October, when he departed for the airport and flew to Cuba.

2. Clandestine photos taken of Gonzalez at the airport by LIEBRACES (LIEMBRACES) clearly show him in the company of Manuel Vega and Rogelio Rodriguez, known DOI officers. Pouching copies.

3. Index Elicio Dardinto Gonzalez Moreno (NORENO).

End of message.

H. C. Choadem.

W. C. Curtis.
