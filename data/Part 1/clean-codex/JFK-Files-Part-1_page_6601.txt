I am an unorganized citizen of the U.S.A., and as neither myself nor my forebears can be claimed to have fled from any kind of aggression, I am clearly not eligible for their legal assistance and must therefore apply to the American authorities myself for remittance. This fine point, delivered with much dignity and profundity, left me more dazed than enlightened when I left their office. Last year, however, by careful observation and quoting precedent, I managed to effect a promise from them to pay for my passage abroad when I receive my visa, but not being B.P. and registered with them, I must arrange to get my own entry or destination visa for resettlement in a new country. I have agreed to all that George will do if I get it, well livened the burden I am already putting on you, but as I have said, this organization will only start functioning when they receive my destination visa for resettlement. If that word and phrase—once they have that in hand—I may stand surety for me and my passage. This has so far been done before and is still being done now.
