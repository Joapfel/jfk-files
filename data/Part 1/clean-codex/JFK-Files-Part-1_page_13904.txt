Classified message.

To: Director. From: Merida. Action: C/W 5. DPO: DOP, ADOP, C/CI, DC/W/SA 5, VR.

MEXI INFO WAVE DIR CITE MRID 9436.

1. Follow-up information dated 5 April 1964 regarding Rogelio Rodriguez, G-2 representative, Cuzemb.

A. Subject identified as Roger Rodriguez, former member of Segundo Frente del Escambray, who reported working in a hospital in Cuba as a doctor, completely against Castro communism.

B. Subject comes from an old Cuban (COME) family in Cuba, but he himself is not Communist (COME), is against Castro communism, and could be persuaded to work in Mexico for the cause of a democratic Cuba.

2. Source of the above information: Max Lesnuck Menendez from Eloy Gutierrez.
