7. At 7 a.m. Monday, 5 June, Brun called Charles in Port-au-Prince. The banker repeated his telephone request made on Monday, 4 June, for Brun’s trip to the Haitian capital on Monday to discuss “urgent business matters.” Charles emphasized that “no politics was involved; the purpose of their get-together is purely business in nature.” After that Brun, overruling his wife’s objections, decided to go. He took off from Kennedy IAP Monday at 11:30 a.m. on Pan Am.

8. As a precautionary measure Brun will register with U.S. diplomatic representation at Port-au-Prince. He has not done that before.

9. Brun hopes to be back Wednesday this week, at which time we’ll call Balog.
