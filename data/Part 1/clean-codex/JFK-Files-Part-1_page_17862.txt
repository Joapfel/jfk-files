Dispatch. Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Aero/Logistics – Transportation Allowance for David MILSTED.

Reference: A. MILSTED-5005, 27 September 1965; B. MILSTED-13913.

1. Headquarters records indicate that Project INDOG was terminated 30 June 1965 as reported by reference B. MILSTED and MV-2539 were both transferred to Project LIFEAT. An amended TVA was forwarded to the station by TH #0561524, dated 9 July 1965.

2. The establishment of a transportation allowance is predicated on a reduction in the number of Organization vehicles and a savings to OBYOXE. Considering vehicle depreciation over a period of several years and charges for gas, oil and maintenance, it is deemed economical for OBYOXE to pay the transportation allowance of $50.00 per month. The availability of a POV, which is intended primarily for personal transportation and used only incidentally for official use, makes it advantageous for the employee to receive a transportation allowance.

3. In the case at hand it is noted that 50% usage of a vehicle by MILSTED, representing 1500 miles per month and a proposed $80.00 per month transportation allowance, is for official business. Since the vehicle is used almost exclusively and very extensively for official use, it seems more appropriate, as well as to the advantage of OBYOXE and MILSTED, that it should be an official vehicle.

4. Headquarters appreciates Station desire to reduce the number of OP vehicles. However, each case must be decided on the basis of the circumstances involved and, particularly, OBYOXE interest. We request that the Station reconsider this case before we submit it to higher authority for approval.

Distribution: 3 – COS, Mexico City.

Department Street Number: HGUS-4372. City of Station: EXETER.
