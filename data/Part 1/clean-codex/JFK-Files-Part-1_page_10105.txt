060230Z April 68. Cite Mexico City 5139. Director info JMWAVE 985.

WHH file.

LITAMIL-7 retired by Embassy effective 31 March 1968.

LIENVOY take 26 March reflecting this development received Station 29 March, LITAMIL-7’s last day of work. LITAMIL-7 did not notify LITAMIL-3, claiming to be waiting for subsequent meetings with Embassy personnel to determine type and amount of severance pay. Refused to bring out anything from LIRING (address books, keys, etc.) for fear it might affect final settlement. Asked Chief LIRING if she could purchase the old typewriter she had been using for 22 years, but was refused.

2. LITAMIL-7 was paid 60,000 pesos 2 April. Will get 7,000 on 11 April and final 2,500 at the end of April. Above totals proper compensation for 38 years’ service, based on Mexican law.

3. Although LITAMIL-7’s retirement eliminates the only Station physical asset at LIRING, the development provides an opportunity to activate LISAMPAN/CHARLIE. As addressees are aware, LITAMIL-7 had been isolated completely from any but the most innocuous conversations. Her replacement by the wife of a LIRING staffer may provide interesting information. Station plans to activate CHARLIE the week of 14 April, after a week of take-on test. Secret. K.
