Personal Record Questionnaire. Part I—Biographical Information.

Instructions: Write clearly. Answer all questions. If a question is not applicable, write “NA.” Attach blank pages if additional space is needed.

Section I—General Personal and Physical Data.

1. Name: First, middle, last, note.

2. Name in native language or script.

3. Name at birth, if different than Item 1.

4. Aliases, nicknames, telecodes, or legal change in name: state line, reason for, and place of use.

5. Date of birth.

6. Place of birth.

7. Personal habits: liquor, drugs, gambling, paranoias. No known standardize habits.

8. Court record: court, date, arrests, charges, punishments, sentences, acquittals. UC known.

9. Description: use American standards of measurements, if possible.

A. Sex. B. Age. C. Apparent age. D. Weight: feet and inches. E. Weight. F. Complexion. G. Face: shape. M, F.

H. Color of hair. I. Color of eyes. J. Teeth. K. Build. L. Posture.

M. Scars and marks or other distinguishing features. Not to be read on page.

N. General appearance.

O. Prominent features.

P. Other identifying features.

Attach samples of signature and handwriting; also dated photograph and fingerprints, if obtainable.
