Classified message. IN 63-1.

5. Ray believes Cabrera will work on Arcos but says the man who could really do the job is Arcos’s uncle, who is in Puerto Rico and known to Ray. He could not remember the name or exact relationship but plans to contact him on return and groom him for the task. Ray asked to be informed when Arcos returns to Brussels.

4. KUBARK staffer (IDEN B) of the Congress of Cultural Freedom met Ray at least twice. Excellent rapport. He arranged Ray’s press interview with Guillon de Moulins (incorrectly reported in paragraph five, PARI 8947) of Le Monde. He also arranged a dinner on 03 June. He was noncommittal but encouraging about the possibility of a job for Franqui should he defect.

5. Ray seems to be confident Franqui will join his camp in the not too distant future and is relying on Cabrera to continue working on him. STA is not so convinced and plans to proceed along the lines of DIR 4815B and PARI 9067 (not sent SJAN BRUS).

6. Possible reference to Gue(de)’s talk with Franqui is in the 8 June UNSOX. Ambassador Gramatges mentions to Sinobas that Mario (presumably Third Secretary Mario Gracia Varela) called from the exhibition, saying that some anti-Castro elements were about and that Franqui asked for some more people from the embassy to be around the exhibition. There is also one UNSIERRA GOVERNAFU 12 comment to UNSNAFU stating that some “GUSANDS” were in the photographic exhibition and that the French police went by Cuban inquiries to ask if they should guard the exhibit. However, this letter may probably not reference Gue(de). Better than this, STA has no indication Franqui has talked about an approach to him.
