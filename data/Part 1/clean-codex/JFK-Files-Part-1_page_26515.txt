One man does most of the talking, about the relative situation of the worker with regard to the UFO (UFCO), the union, and independent farming as to earnings, benefits, etc. They engage in an argumentative comparison of the treatment of the worker by the UFO (UFCO) and the railroad company. Toward the end the discussion leads into the potential for revolution among the former and present workers of the UFO (UFCO).]
