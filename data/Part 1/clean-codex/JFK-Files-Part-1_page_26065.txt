Ref: JMWAVE-1213 (C/N 4/8140).

1. Original source for DIGEPOL information regarding Cubans coming to Venezuela may have been AMGLEVE-15. On 9 February, AMCLEVE-15 informed the station that an unknown number of Cubans may soon be infiltrated into Venezuela, among them Tony Castells and FNU Claderin. AMCLEVE-15 had received this information from Roberto Pardon during AMCLEVE-15's visit to Miami last week, AMGUEV. AMCLEVE-15 described Pardon as an ex-WOFIRM agent who used to exfiltrate people from Cuba and was still engaged in this activity on his own. (Request JMWAVE traces.) AMCLEVE-15 did not mention Susana, but station files suggest she may be Guionar (Yepez) Patino, BRAVO FALN support agent, and Mevaer Yepez Patino family, almost all of whom are active subversives.
