Classified message.

052216Z January 1971. CITE WH/MIAMI 9956. Priority London, The Hague. Info Director, Madrid.

RYBAT TYPIC.

Ref A: London 7124 (not sent The Hague, Madrid) (IN 237738). Ref B: The Hague 8910 (IN 237605).

1. For London: Our interest in Roberto Rodriguez Llompart is basically our assessment that he is recruitable. This assessment [was] provided us by one of Rodriguez's closest friends who is presently residing [in the] United States. Briefly stated, this assessment indicates that Rodriguez has talked against the Cuban Revolution and Communists. According to assessor, Rodriguez had only one principal idea and that was to leave Cuba and go to the States.

2. We do not, therefore, have any derog per se. Here follows a biographic summary on Rodriguez: DPOB: 22 October 1932, Regla, Cuba. In October 1968 Rodriguez appointed director of Mining Industry Supply Unit, under Ministry of Mines, Fuel and Metallurgy. It is believed that subject owes his position to his brother, Hector, who is presently Cuban ambassador to East Germany. Physical description: HT: 64 inches, WT: 110 lbs, hair: straight black.
