3 August 1967. IN 25077.

Mexico City 2363, 832120Z. Immediate Guatemala City, Director.

KAPOK PBRUMEN.

Reference: A. Director 24844. B. Guatemala City 7743 (1-24931). C. Mexico City 2358 (1-24956).

1. Probably because Ernesto (Capuano) Del Vecchio did not help him obtain badly needed documentation when he needed it, and because a relative of Moises (Orzco) Leal apparently worked in Capuano's office, Jacobo Padilla would do no more than set up a Capuano interview for LIRING-3, after which LIRING-3 was on his own. In view of this, LIRING-3 felt he had better not see Capuano about Orozco and pressed Padilla for contact with other FAR representatives. Padilla, who was very nervous about the whole Orozco affair, said he would see them without committing himself.

2. Although they do not know of Orozco's arrest by Mexican police, LERING-3 and Sergio Valdez in fact suspect it after seeing what they thought was a police car in the neighborhood of the address where Orozco was arrested by ITEMPOS. Unable to contact FAR representatives, LIRING-3 felt obliged to protect himself by telling the Cuban Embassy about Orozco. LERING-3 accordingly told Abelardo (Curbelo) Padron, who has acted.
