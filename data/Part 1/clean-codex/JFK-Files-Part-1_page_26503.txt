I think for this reason that you must view your problems not as another group carrying on polemics with other groups but to some extent from the height in which you are placed, even physically. You must realize that the difficulties there have been are due basically to the fact that the comrades in the PGT have long delayed turning into deeds their own ideas on the struggle. They have set forth a line to the effect that aspects may not be discussed but that [phrase unintelligible] correct. The 11-point resolution of December which places the armed struggle and especially the guerrilla phase at the core of the revolutionary struggle seems to me to be correct in general. But they have had many difficulties because there have been debates, obscurities, some of them perhaps did not understand that or were opposed—I do not know, I do not have many details. But of course it has not been possible; they have not found it possible or else have lacked the capability to devote their activity to the armed struggle. Consequently their participation, which there has been—we cannot deny it; I think it would be unjust to deny the participation of the PGT—has been the participation of a rearguard, of support. They have lost cadres here in Ixtabal (?) and elsewhere, suffered the consequences of the struggle; some comrades of the PGT are actively persecuted—but always in connection with support activities.

I believe your role is to drag them into a more active participation in the struggle—not to fight them or fight them only to the extent that such a fight may lead them into the battle and not occasion a split.
