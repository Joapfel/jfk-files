1. During a meeting late 9 January, WKSCARLET-3 said he received a long-distance telephone call from New York from Domingo Abreu. Abreu told WKSCARLET-3 that he could not be reached in New York but provided WKSCARLET-3 with a name and telephone number in Miami through which he could be contacted: Andres Purrino, telephone 261-4581.

A. Abreu, Domingo: AKA Chino; well-documented Cuban violator in the Miami area. (HVCA-18943A0N).

B. Purrino, Andres (PSHRINO, HYNDRESI): documented multi-kilo trafficker (Caracas 21408, 14 March 74).
