Incoming classified message.

Central Intelligence Agency.

From: Paris. To: Special Operations. Action: FDW (1-2). Information: AOSO (3-4), STA (5), STB (6), IID (7).

Routine. 11 December 1943. IN 11158.

PARI 4214. To: WASHF. CITE: PARIF.

1. Unmoved reports subject WFPA 3489 resigned from TWA effective 1 January.

2. Subject appears optimistic of securing work in Europe, HQ Paris, with Harvey Dwight, important airplane spare parts dealer. If Dwight contact materializes, subject would remain Paris six or eight months, then return States to work for Dwight, possibly in WASH.

3. At luncheon with Hare 10 December, subject told Hare all his financial troubles and indicated willingness remain TWA if income could be supplemented. Unmoved concurs with this. Subject stated (RCVD DEHXEB) to Hare he had actually resigned from TEA (TWA), stating he now taking terminal leave prior to resignation.

4. Re WFPM 3032. In view developments above, we feel decision one way or other on our part must be made soonest. Therefore request provisional operational clearance and your authority to approach subject, in principle, offering salary $300 monthly, subject to final security clearance. This in order to persuade subject remain with ideal TWA cover job at least until we make final decision.

5. Time, in this case, is of the essence.

IN 201- 33608. TOR: 1211Z 11 December 1943.
