Action required references. Typic pronunciation.

References: (A) DIR 28665; (B) MEXI 0444; (C) MEXI 0511; (D) MEXI 1467; (E) MEXI 1626; (F) MEXI 2904; (G) MEXI 2967; (H) MEXI 2998; (I) MEXI 3006; (J) MEXI 3012; (K) MEXI 3035; (L) MEXI 3066; (M) MEXI 3078; (N) MEXI 3178; (O) MEXI 3199; (P) MEXI 3217; (Q) MEXI 3245; (R) MEXI 3247.

1. Full name: Luis ALBERU Souto. CIT. Cuba, ecc. Diplomat. Diplomatic or functional title: Cultural Attaché. Place of birth: Oviedo, Spain. Date of birth: circa 1915. Passport number: D/62/80.

Family members: Mother: Josefina SOUTO SELF F. CIT. Cuba. Sisters: Xania ALBERU Souto and of Luis ALBERU Souto SOUTO 201-329609. Brothers: Leonel ALBERU Souto and Alfredo ALBERU Souto, Cuban Cultural Attaché in Mexico City.

Residence: A Micheleit 7–301 (Mexico D.F., Mexico). Photographs: Attached. Date of arrival at post: Date of entry into Mexico unknown, prior to December 1949, however. Has been employed with the Cuban Government since June 1959.

2. See cited references and the LITAMIL-7 and LITAMIL-9 Contact Reports for further information on Subject.

William C. Curtis.
