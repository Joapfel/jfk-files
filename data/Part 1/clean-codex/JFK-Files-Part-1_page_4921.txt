Intelligence Information Cable.

Dated 22 October 1976.

Country: Chile.

Date of information: Mid-April to mid-October 1976.

Subject: Chilean government information on Cuban exile leaders Orlando Bosch and Rolando Otero.

Acquired: Chile, Santiago, 21 October 1976. Field No. HCS-18,772.

Source: See below.

Cite Form D6-315/10576-7b.

1. Source of paragraphs 3-4: A medium-level government official with good access to officials of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. His reporting is fairly reliable.

2. Field comments: Santiago Embassy Telegram 10107 of 19 October reported that the Chilean Ministry of Foreign Affairs was keeping an eye on the activities of Cuban exile leader Orlando S. O. Schm. and was now reviewing all its information, presumably in light of his recent arrest in Venezuela.

3. On 19 October, Foreign Ministry officials assembled.
