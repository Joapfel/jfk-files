Subject: WIROGUE

Date: 16 October 1962  
Time: 1000  
Place: Ostendstr. 1, Apt. 9

Orme K. Daron nat, Case Officer

Comments:

Case Officer dropped in and paid Subject one week's subsistence.

He is still busy with his invention and is firmly convinced that he will make a million dollars. Subject seems perfectly content to stay in our custody and has not mentioned his proposed immigration to South America. It is obvious that he wants to finish his invention and try to sell it. If he is unsuccessful, Case Officer feels that he will probably want to immigrate immediately.
