Aperture Camp Reproductions.

30 April 1973.

1. SSLOGIC-27 (BEN) reports Maria Paula Souza Freitas (Frei Tas), carrying Brazilian diplomatic passport 886358, accompanied Miguel Russo into El Salvador via Valle Nuevo (Valls Nuevo) immigration checkpoint on 29 January 1973, travelling in an automobile with U.S. Florida state plates 10126921, returning to Guatemalan territory via Ciudad (Cuidad) Pedro de Alvarado immigration checkpoint on 30 January 1973.

2. SSLOGIC-27's efforts to obtain Russo's long-distance telephone tolls revealed he often used the telephone of friend Amaury Nogueira de Souza. The latter is an old acquaintance of SSLOGIC-27 who agreed to cooperate in the investigation of Russo's activities.
