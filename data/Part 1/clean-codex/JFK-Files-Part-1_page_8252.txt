*Identifying country. Indicate where service performed, decorations, when and why discharged. Give details if war prisoner.*

I was given medical discharge in 1944 after it was discovered that my eyesight was below minimum requirements. I had memorized the eye chart at induction medical exam in order to pass minimum requirement of 20-200. Actually, I have never had any difficulty since my sight is corrected to 20-20 with glasses.
