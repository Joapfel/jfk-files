Ref: San Jose 19569 (N 928580)

1. Meeting with HUFANG-1 (F-1) on 14 June began with F-1 question to case officer regarding what use would be made of information regarding plan to overthrow Costa Rican government. F-1 was told that USG has no desire to intervene in domestic politics and that sources as well as individuals involved would be protected.

Comment: Individuals involved are close personal and ideological associates of F-1. Without such assurances it is considered that F-1 would not have spoken further of the matter.

2. In early May one Mario Fernandez Piza, ex jefe del estado mayo un, travelled to Chile to attend a military school reunion. Fernandez was a classmate of President Pinochet and they remain good friends. According to F-1, while in Chile Fernandez secured agreement that a quantity of Soviet and Czech weapons captured by the Chilean (CHILIAN) government from leftist forces in Chile after the fall of the Allende (ALLENDO) government would be sent to Costa Rica.
