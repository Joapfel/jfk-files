20 April 1966

Subject: Contact Report/JCOZY-5

20 April 1966, subject’s office.

1. When the C.O. arrived at the subject’s office, one of the male employees of the subject evidently advised him that “Mr. Ferrier” was there. The subject came from his inner office to advise the C.O. that Leonid Andreevich Mikhaforov (Mikhaforov) was in his office making travel arrangements. The C.O. remained in the outer office, but with his back to the passageway and his face buried in a magazine.

Subsequently, the subject told the C.O. that Mikhaforov was leaving for vacation to the Soviet Union on 21 April via Air France. He was due to return to Mexico on 24 May.

The subject also mentioned that the INTERIST delegation coming to the ANZ meeting in Guadalajara, 27–30 April, had not arrived in Mexico as scheduled on 19 April because there had been a delay in their receiving Mexican visas. The delay was occasioned by Mexican demand for minimal biographical information in order to process the visas. The group of four had been rescheduled to arrive in Mexico on 23 April via Sabena.

The C.O. [reviewed] the exchange of telegrams regarding the information required for visa issuance, and noted the following:

Viktor Bolchenko, born 21 July 1925 in Aktubinsk (Aktубинск); president of INTERIST; passport D 01155 B, issued 6 October 1965.

Lev Akhmelov, born 11 May 1924 in Moscow; chief of the International Department; passport C 065257, issued 25 September 1965.

Assan Ahmedov (sic), born 24 June 1924 in Tashkent; chief of the Uzbekistan Tourism Directorate; passport 069757, issued 24 December 1965.

Lyudmila Yuzetova (Iyudnila Yuz etova), born 5 March 1923 in Moscow; Latin American Department; passport C 065784, issued 6 October 1965.

The subject mentioned that at the Copenhagen Congress on Congress Organizations, which he attended from 30 March to 2 April 1966, he met two Soviet “scientists” and the INTERIST representative for Denmark. The “representatives from the Academy of Sciences” were Gennadiy Andreyevich Koshaev and Timur T. Timofeev. Cards attached.

2. The C.O., who had heard from the subject by telephone on 19 April that he had had his confrontation with Valentin Leginov the previous week, requested a full account of that meeting. The subject reported that Leginov had called him on 15 April requesting a meeting somewhere outside; the subject insisted that Leginov come to his office.
