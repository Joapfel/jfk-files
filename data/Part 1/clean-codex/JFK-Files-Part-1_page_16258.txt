I write to you in general terms so as to whether you continue to be interested and whether you approve of the line taken up yourself or not. Of course, possible, but if you may have been contacted, in which case I would be interested to know.

I hope you accept my thanks to you and Pliny for a most enjoyable stay—do please come and really keep in touch. Kindly 50 miles.

Now send her love to you both and hope to see you again one of these days.
