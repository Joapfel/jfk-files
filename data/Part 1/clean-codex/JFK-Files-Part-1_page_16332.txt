On a satisfactory contract. (Field comment: see ACL-2764) From the same source for an earlier reference by Hoare to C'Mall as his “contact man” with the mercenaries on call in Johannesburg. Hoare wants the first planeload of men from South Africa to arrive at Kamina by 17 August and the balance within 10 days. He plans to send Wicks to Salisbury on 16 August to raise 30 men there.

6. Returning to the subject of the contract, Hoare said that the one used in 1961 is not entirely satisfactory as a model for a new one and will have to be amended in “certain respects.” One new clause that Hoare intends to have included is that he must be able to terminate the contract “on his own judgement.” He added that he foresees no difficulty with Mobutu on this score.

7. After the initial half-hour meeting with Mobutu on the morning of 11 August, Hoare and the Purens returned to see him at 1530 hours the same day. Mobutu was just then leaving to see Tshombe, and the group followed. They were kept waiting until 1900 hours while Mobutu and Tshombe conferred in private. When they were finally received, they presented Mobutu with an estimate of expenses to be incurred, which he read over and approved.
