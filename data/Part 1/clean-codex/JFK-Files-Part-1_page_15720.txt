Classified message.

Org: B. HIESCTUR. Unit: AP/W/2. Ext: 3966. Date: 27 October 1960. To: Leopoldville. From: Director J Chroms. Conf.: C/AF 3. Info: COP, C/CA, C/C1, G/EE 3, S/C 2. Routing: 1 A/EW; 3 Chiron. Out 86554. CUT-0554.

To: LEOP. WIROGUE. RYBAT.

Info: per CSH 43-43.

Cite DIR 08782 03782.

1. WIROGUE (IDEN) contract agent being grown for deep cover assignment LEOP in late November. Envision use as utility agent such as (1) organizing and conducting surveillance team; (2) interception of pouches; (3) blowing up bridges and/or (4) executing other assignments requiring positive action. His utilization not to be restricted to LEOP index; may be subject to call by other African stations, although expect him to be resident LEOP.

2. He is inventor, photographer, architect, forger and former bank robber who has served term in prison. Very ingenious, observant, shrewd, glib and relishes intrigue and daring. The latter plays important part in his character. He learns quickly and carries out any assignment without regard for danger. For certain tasks this man unusually qualified and seems to be discreet. Assessment over two years is that he is reliable and “HOMEST.” Delicate to handle for must have trust in handler and his professional competence and will reciprocate in kind. View this and other considerations wish only GUTHMAN handle certainly in initial stages.

3. Being documented as Austrian to depart permanent black for surfacing in.

Coordinating officers.

Releasing officer.

Authenticating officers.

Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
