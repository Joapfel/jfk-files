Staff. Precedence. Re file: V4. Gus C. Civil Engineer. C/C52, CC62, JDA Security. Common GWO 2, DDO SS4.

T-959445 E14769, page 21. IN 897535. CARA 29641.

To: Immediate Director. Info: Immediate Panama City (for largest). State: LA/Miami. F-SPALDING KNOXON SGCHART MRASYSS. Re: 1 Director 614506. Cross-file copy for 201-85923.

1. F.Y.I., Caracas daily newspaper Ultimas Noticias, 22 April, carries a Prensa Latina story dated in Havana 21 April titled “CIA Accused of Murdering Anti-Castro Leaders.” Story centers around alleged press conference in which Manuel de Armas S., claiming to be a former CIA agent, “tells all” about “Operation Abalta.”

De Armas claims that while stationed in New York in 1971 he maintained contact with two CIA officials, FNU Blackenhouse, who recruited him two years previously, and Richard Tomley of Moleman (McLean) Virginia, who had trained him and who evaluated his performance initially in Operation Abalta. De Armas also claims that terrorist attacks against Venezuelan tourist officials in Puerto Rico were coordinated with the CIA and carried out by a person who is now in captain, but is on lapisal for the detention of Orlando. Armas claims to have had an edge.
