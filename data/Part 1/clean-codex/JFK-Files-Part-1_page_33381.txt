1. Agree with Ref B recommendation to await outcome of French election before deciding if and how to assist UNSOBER-1. In making that decision, the following points should be considered:

A. While station was capability via FUTRUNK-1 (IDEN for Paris), recently recruited salaried agent, to assist UNSOBER-1 in obtaining interview with General Pinochet. It should be kept in mind that our intervention, no matter how subtle, would very probably identify UNSOBER-1 to FUTRUNK-1 as agency collaborator.
