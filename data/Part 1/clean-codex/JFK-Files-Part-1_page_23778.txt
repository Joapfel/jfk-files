Dispatch. Dispatch symbol and No.: OELA-43,458. To: Chief, KUDESK. Headquarters file No.: 201-. Info: Chief, LCPIPIT; Chief, WE; Chief, AP. From: Chief of Station, London. Date: 25 June 1965. Subject: KUDESK STEBONY, STEBOFF-related (STEBOFF-MeIated) BAIN items. Re: “43-3.” Action required: Marked for indexing.

References:

1. Attached are selected extracts from BAIN reports. BAIN is a very sensitive SMITH operation, and the items should be treated accordingly.

2. SCHLIEFPER (misspelled in BAIN) is of interest. Pablo is Pable FERNANDEZ, Cultural Attaché at the Cuban Embassy in London. Other items should be self-explanatory. On page 3 of the attachment, “integration,” where underlined, should read “segregation.”

3. As with these items, London will try to bring to Headquarters (and other addressees’) attention STEBONY-related items that we notice. On the other hand, we do not require to be informed on all STEBONY traffic in the future. We would like to see copies of all of his intelligence reporting, whether for passage to the SMOOTHERS or not. On that topic, we would like to recommend that a liberal policy be followed in determining what of his material to pass to liaison here. We will be glad to review his reporting for passage, if there is doubt about whether to pass it. We would also like to see any materials relating to him that may have bearing on London Station operations, relating principally to the U.K., or relating to any of our liaison services here, of course.

George H. KATZENBACH

Attachment: Herewith.

Distribution: 2—Chief, KUDESK with attachment; 2—Chief, LCPIPIT with attachment; 3—Chief, WE with attachment; 2—Chief, AP with attachment.
