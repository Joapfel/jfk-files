Subject: Surveillance of Soviet target at Academy.

Buenos Aires, LOGINOV crossing the bridge on foot accompanied by a Mexican-type man, approximately 55 to 40 years old, 1.75 meters tall, weighing 80 kilograms, with wavy black hair, large receding temples, thick eyebrows, dark complexion, acne-scarred skin, wearing a complete light-brown suit and tie.

1401. They part at Concepción Méndez and Dr. Andrade. Surveillance of Loginov is canceled.

1412. The subject arrives at the Ananece Tapatio restaurant at Hilo Perdido and Obrero Mundial. He walks through the restaurant as if looking for someone.

1415. He leaves and waits at the door.

1500. He reenters the restaurant and talks on the telephone. The number could not be seen and nothing could be heard because there was mariachi music inside the restaurant.

1508. He finishes talking on the telephone, leaves, and walks away.

1511. He gets into a Renault (Renoult) Dauphine, 1961, black, damaged, license plates 380 HS, which was parked at Esparta and Bolívar Streets. He leaves.

1516. He is lost at the entrance to the Viaduct, heading east. He could not be followed because three cars entered slowly behind him and he could no longer be located.
