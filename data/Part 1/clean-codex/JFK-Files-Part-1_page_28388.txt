Origin: C.C. PROMET. Unit: WH/PO/D. Extension: 3503. Date: 22 May 1961. Message time: 1667.

Classified message.

To: Mexico City  
From: Director  
Conf: WH 5  
Info: CA 2, CA/PRG, CI/OA, OP 2, S/C 2

22 May 61, 2304Z.

918 CEN, deferred. Routine.

Cite Dir 41303.

1. Richard G. Tichborn, letter A, departed WASL 20 May for Mexico, where he will be employed as legal counsel to LETN B.

2. Tichborn has been used by KUBARKAS (KUBARK) as political advisor on election CPS. Prior departure has discussed contract possibility with him and agreed to prepare contract, which will be sent to Mexico in the near future for signature.

3. POS anticipates using Tichborn as growing political advisor, providing various field stations with guidance on election CPS. Will also use him to develop enlisted candidates in various countries and may appoint him further for DIFTERHARCO.

4. Tichborn will have no operational responsibilities in Mexico unless station so desires. However, he would require BOMB conferences and administrative support when overt channels are unsuitable.

5. Pouching proposed contract and more installments are possible for use in the near future. Request station officer using NARS “FRANK MILSON” call Tichborn at Hotel ROMELJO, RIFTFORMA, on 29 May to establish initial contact. Attempting to have contract mailed by that time.

End of message.
