8 December 1949

To: Chief, Personnel Division, North Building  
From: Deputy Chief, Employees Division  
Subject: Employment Record of James Walton Moore, Jr.

This is to certify that the above-named individual began his employment with this organization on 29 August 1946 as a CAF-12, Intelligence Officer. On 8 March 1948, he received a periodic pay increase to $6144.60. On 18 September 1949, he received a periodic pay increase to $6714.00. He was converted to GS-12, $6800.00 on 30 October 1949. He resigned COB 5 December 1949 to accept a position on departmental rolls.

Robert S. Wattles  
Employees Division
