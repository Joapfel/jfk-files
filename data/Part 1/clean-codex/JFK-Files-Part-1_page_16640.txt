VIVIAN:

Following situation regarding Laddie:

1. Esants, add balance of his May pay now.

2. John Ryan and Roger Shea are waiting for word from you in order to call the people at Summit Hill Apartments and give them three-day notice before Laddie can move in. Summit Hill Apartments also would like to know, when notice is given, what type of beds to furnish the apartment with (double or single—Laddie does not care which).
