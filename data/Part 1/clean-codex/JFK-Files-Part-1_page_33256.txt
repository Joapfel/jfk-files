30 April 1968, Cite Buenos Aires 0513.

Director info Panama City.

RYBAT REDTOP AERONAUT AETARGET TECHS.

1 May 1969.

Reference: Buenos Aires 0480 (IN 99086).

1. AMSTALK-I introduced to CASTAGNA 29 April and began work at firm. AMSTALK-I's first-hand debriefing of CASTAGNA sheds somewhat different light on CASTAGNA's access, and the apparent BIOGENESIS report (reference) is inaccurate:

A. Only one air conditioner from the Embassy, and no agreement as yet to work on others in the Embassy. The eleven mentioned in the reference are from the Trade Mission.

B. CASTAGNA's access to the Embassy was limited over the past four months to picking up and delivering units to a downstairs room in the Embassy. Withdrawal and installation were done by Soviets. This was not the case with the Trade Mission and POSADAS, where CASTAGNA was allowed to enter, pull, and reinstall units.

2. On 30 April, AMSTALK-I accompanied CASTAGNA to the Embassy in order to deliver a repaired unit. Access was limited to the basement room, where NIKOLAY KUZNETSOV accepted and paid for the repaired unit.
