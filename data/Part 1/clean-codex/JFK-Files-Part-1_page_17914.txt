Official Dispatch.

Dispatch No. 50-6-32/2.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City.

From: Chief, VMD.

Subject: Operational/LINEAT support. Concealment device for use by Daniel K. BIO-GOLD (201-210541).

1. In answer to a special request by Daniel K. BIO-GOLD and approved by the WH Division, KENTOT has constructed a bookcase containing a concealment area for his use in connection with the LINEAT Project. This bookcase is now being packed for shipment. The station will be advised later of the method of shipment and the ETA. Instructions for its use are u/s/c. If the station has no objection to its use by BIO-GOLD, it is requested that it be delivered to him.

2. If the Station believes that additional similar concealment devices would be of use under the LINEAT project, it is requested that you forward to Headquarters your suggestions and comments, furnishing details as to quantity, size, and prospective use. Your request will then be referred to KENTOT.

Attachment: u/s/c. Three copies of memorandum.

10 January 1957. 10 January 1958.
