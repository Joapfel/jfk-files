Re: Cubanos Unidos (United Cubans) internal security—Cuba neutrality matters—Cuba.

Cubanos Unidos (CU) is a Cuban exile anti-Fidel Castro organization in Miami, Florida. Its purpose is to unite all exile groups into a unity organization and begin military operations against the present Government of Cuba.

Wilfredo Navarro Deato is the head of CU, which organization now maintains office space at 1996 S. W. 1st Street, Miami, telephone 642-9741.

On June 9, 1970, HM T-1, a Cuban exile involved in revolutionary matters for the past six years, advised that CU recently decided to start selling bonds issued in the name of the Republic of Cuba. The money collected would be used to purchase arms for proposed military operations against Cuba.

On June 15, 1970, HM T-1 stated that Wilfredo Navarro at that time was claiming that Maurice Ferré, a member of the Miami City Commission, is assisting him in Cuban revolutionary activities. Navarro claimed that about a week previously, a meeting was held in the Ferré Building in Miami, and it was determined that CU had adequate money resources. Navarro said that he instructed Tomas Arencibia, a leading member of CU, to quit his job and Navarro would pay his salary. Navarro explained that he wanted Arencibia to work on a full-time basis for CU. Also present at this meeting were three unidentified Americans, one of whom reportedly has a quantity of arms stored at his house.
