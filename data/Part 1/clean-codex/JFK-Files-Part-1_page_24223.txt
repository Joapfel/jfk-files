Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, London (Attn. KATZER). Info: Chief, KIBOLP. From: Chief of Station, Paris (LCPIMP). Subject: DTORIC/Operational REPRES/REBONY. Ref: OKLA 4525A.

1. At his 6 April meeting with RODIGAST, GREBONY/1 pointed out that his brother-in-law (the accountant) still thinks that GREBONY receives regular income from an American radio or news feature service, paid through Switzerland, and undeclared for tax reasons (GREBONY's and the American company's taxes).

2. RODIGAST thought that if the brother-in-law finds this story hard to believe he may guess that GREBONY is really working for KIBARK and may have passed this information on to the British. Thus he would be the source of the story relayed in reference.

3. At the same meeting, on another subject, RODIGAST asked GREBONY whether he had ever told anyone pointblank that he (GREBONY) is a Chicon agent. He said that he never had. RODIGAST broke in: “or the agent of any other country, including CDYONE?” GREBONY said he had not.

Karen H. ZAPPARESI.

Distribution: COS, London; C/KIBOLP; C/WB. Cross reference to: DEPA-3731. Date: 15 April 1966.

APR 18 1966.
