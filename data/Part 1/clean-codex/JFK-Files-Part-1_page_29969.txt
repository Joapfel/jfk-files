Section IV

The section is provided in two and in describing the individual, four descriptions of character. The following categories are to be used, and not limited thereto.

The section contains a series of statements describing the individual. On the right-hand side of the page are four major categories of descriptions. For each category, fill in the appropriate box under the right column and mark the corresponding statement with an X. Place an “X” in the first category where it applies, and place another “X” in the second category where it applies. Put a line through the category on the right which best fits your idea of this person. Mark the person on the left clearly, and write his or her name on the right where he or she was found. Place an “X” in the “Not Applicable” column when you have no opinion as to whether a phrase applies to him or her, and an “X” in the “Does Not Apply” column when you have the definite intention to fire the person.
