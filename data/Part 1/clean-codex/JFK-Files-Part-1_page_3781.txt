24 Jun 74.

Desensitized.

1. COS and General Montiel discussed REF problem 23 May.

Despite his reassignment as Minister of Treasury, Montiel is still interested in this matter because the President charged him with the CS solution. He explained that the quantity was too great to be brought to Managua and forwarded to Panama by GON as originally envisioned.

This is up to WOFACT to arrange transportation. Suggested that plane or boat be brought to El Bluff. Said President Somoza has made it clear that the material is to be removed from Nicaragua in the manner prescribed by COS.

2. Air lists reveal Primitivo Ablanedo has departed for Miami. Assume Colonel Brenes is guarding the house where the arms are stored. COS and TABOTH feel the best means of removing the arms is to bring a sizable cargo plane into El Bluff airstrip, load at night, and leave the next morning. An alternate means is to bring a boat to the dock in front of the home of Robert Bartlett, manager of Booth Fish Processing Plant. Bartlett lives.
