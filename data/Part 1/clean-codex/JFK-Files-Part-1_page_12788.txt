Also, suggest that W-3's case officer be accompanied to meeting by second officer. Feel that presence of second officer as witness will indicate to UKSCARLET-3 seriousness with which we view allegations against him and also will serve as inhibition should he be tempted to resort to physical violence. Good breakfast candidate for this role would be your forthcoming visitor, Robert F. Stontwist. If for some reason station prefers not use Stontwist, suggest Wilfred O. Clower, who is due to leave Caracas PCS this summer.
