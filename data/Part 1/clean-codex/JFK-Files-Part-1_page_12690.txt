Subject: RLY/INDUSTRY/SOCIAL/LANIER — Status of Station Liaison Relationships.

Reference: CARLIS 20841, dated 19 May 1975.

The following information on WKSCARLE-3 was contained in the above-referenced telepouch:

Confronted with evidence that INFALL personnel were in activity followed, WTANGO-1 said he did not know who would be conducting the surveillance but suspected it might be the private security service operated by WKSCARLE-3. He could otherwise not [illegible] up alleged that WKSCARLE-3 was receiving huge sums of money from an unknown source of finance [for] an electronic surveillance operation on the HULUMAN Embassy, surveil [CURAY] Embassy personnel, and that WKSCARLE-3 was also acquiring data on Indian Embassy personnel. (In February 1976 Station asked WKSCARLE-3 for all WKSCARLE's material on Indian Embassy. Resulting “study” prepared by WKSCARLE-3 was passed unofficially to Station and officially to KNOH. We suspect that the extent of WKSCARLE-3's acquisition of data on Indians [was] limited to his possession of his study.)

WTANGO-1 was unable to get a logical explanation for such a wide range of interests on the part of WKSCARLE-3's sponsor. He did seem sincere in his denial that WKNOLE-24 was running surveillance on INFALL personnel, and on 12 May contacted the Station to advise that he had reported the surveillance to his president, who had ordered an immediate investigation. Proof of government interest in WKSCARLE-3 activities was obtained by the Station coincidentally with these developments; among the WKRREAK cables derived from KNOH during the last week of April was one mislabeled cable which the Station was able to identify as the product of a trap on WKSCARLE-3's investigative service.
