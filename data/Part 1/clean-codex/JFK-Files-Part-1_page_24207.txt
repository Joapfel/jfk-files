Classified message.

25 Apr 86

SECRET 251622Z CITE LCPIPIT 7388 (Gilberti (Cilberti) acting)

Director info Brussels, The Hague.

DIDORIC OREBONY

1. RODIGAST (R) QREBONY/1 (Q) as scheduled 23 August. Balance VOLADY reporting being pouched as well as new reports. Q planning travel Paris, Brussels-Rotterdam, possibly Frankfurt, Vienna, 2–12 September. ROD will meet Q Antwerp 8 September.

2. Robert Williams, in letter to Q dated 25 July from Peking, said he hopes to be in Europe later in the year, probably going to Sweden to make a speech. Also advised he may try to publish Crusade from Peking and asks Q’s help with distribution. Attacked hypocritical racist Cubans. (This information being disseminated.)
