Memorandum for: Deputy Director for Plans

Subject: Transmission of booklet written by Manuel Artine Bessa

1. Attached is a booklet entitled Points for Meditation written by Manuel Artine. It is essentially written for the younger elements of his Revolutionary Recovery Movement and in effect assimilates a list of quotes and observations of an essentially political or philosophic nature.

2. The inscription on the inside cover is dedicated to you and translated reads as follows:

“To Mr. Arthur—the generous lawyer of difficult causes—with affection in Cuba or Nothing.”

(Signed)

Manuel Artine Bessa

John L. Hart  
Deputy Chief, MD for Cuba
