I ask if you do not want recapitulation of all references on this subject. Generally Fullen and mercenaries who were recruiting here prior to the return to Congo was no money but were always expecting big payment from Tungsue. Evidence this in separate diary referred to first date is statement regarding Tshome (Tschomee)'s 18 July cable to Full— — Tshome (Tschome) Beleshim about six thousand pounds. At another point in diary after Tshome (Tschome) had told Secaros proceed with building up mercenaries, Secaros noted, “Told him we must have the contract by midnight so that my men could return to Salisbury and combine to bring up the small army. Rates of pay must be finalised; no ticket, no laundry.” At another point, “If any of them had fifteen thousand pounds transferred to Joannee Belag & Co., I’d asking to pay on aircraft repairs, travel tickets, may be advanced.” At another point he noted that he was leading Stallion to Johannesburg and Kicks to Salissory but “if it will have to fit for my skay,” i.e., signing of contract.

See Taft 3476 be sending Jolly (Purple) Johannesburg with the money. In view above plus previous dissections would clearly indicate that Tshome (Tschome) is paying mercenaries.
