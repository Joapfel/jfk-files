To: Williamson  
Subject: Comments on Trident  
From: Chaconas  
Date: 16 May 1966

1. I do not regard Trident as a particularly useful vehicle for propaganda activity in Central America. The Trident is closely identified with Artime and as such is less flexible a convincing propaganda outlet than would be the case otherwise. I would not therefore favor financial support (KUBARK) of the Trident, especially since KUBARK is not in position closely to monitor the contents prior to publication. Also, the circulation is extremely limited to warrant placing a case officer in charge for this activity, i.e. guiding the propaganda line of Trident.

2. We have not used Trident in the past (as far as I know) for planting material and I don't for the moment visualize using Trident for outlet in the near future. However, the “MIG-21 Project,” conceivably might find Trident useful—that is, if it is desirable to add a quasi-official aura to the proposal.

Chaconas
