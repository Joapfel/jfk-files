Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Panama / Cuba / UCR / Mexico. TDCS DB-3/659,422. Date of information: 28 January–5 February 1954. Distributed: 7 February 1954. Place and date acquired: Panama, Panama City (6 February 1954). Reference: TD SDB-3/659,136.

Source and appraisal: Latin American politician (F) with good contacts in Central American revolutionary circles. The primary source has probably reported accurately, but the reliability of the subsources cannot be determined.

1. Thela Kino, pro-Cuban deputy to the Panama National Assembly, was scheduled to leave Panama on 5 February for Curaçao, from where she was to go to the Soviet Union via Cuba. (Headquarters comment: TD SDB-3/659,136, of 21 January, reported that Mrs. Kino would leave for the Soviet Union and Cuba on 26 January. She was making the trip to seek financial assistance for Panama from Moscow (MOSكو).)

2. On 28 January, Mrs. King met “Pranko” and told her that Victor Rico Galan had asked her to go inside to Cuba and then to the Soviet Union and to drop her plans to go to Cuba. Pranko gave Mrs. King $1,000 for his trip, and this is expected to meet him in Curaçao. If field office: Dranko.
