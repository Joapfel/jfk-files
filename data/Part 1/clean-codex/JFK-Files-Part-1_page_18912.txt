Training Evaluation: Counterespionage Operations Course I, 1.

Name: Goodpasture, Ann L. Sex: Female. Dates of course: 19 March–6 April 1958. Number of students: 15. Date of birth: 23 November 1918. EOP date: February 1944. Grade or rank: GS-11. Office: WE/71. Proposed assignment on present position: FI Operations Officer.

I. Course Objectives: To familiarize the students with the various types of CS operations and with methods and procedures similar to CE in the field and at headquarters, to outline CE objectives, and to increase proficiency in the recognition and exploitation of items.

II. Course Characteristics: The Counterespionage Operations Course is of three weeks' duration. The curriculum material is presented by means of lectures, seminars, and directed reading. It is divided into sections as follows:

A. Introduction (definition of terms, the CI Staff, operational security and risk assessment, sources of CE leads, CR reporting and records)

B. Interrogation

C. Foreign Intelligence Services (with emphasis on currently hostile services including a lecture on CE in Liaison)

D. Penetration and Defection

E. Double Agent Operations

During the course each student is required to present orally a CE case and to submit a written version of the case. Enrollments are based on problems, the case analysis presented by the student, and objective tests.
