Att. No. 5 to UFGA-21693

Subject: VALEJO Iniguez, Delfín

1. Source met Subject about 1940 when they were neighbors at Calle San Lazaro between Concepcion and Dolores Streets, Lawton, Havana City. Subject, as a young man, worked as a piano repairman. Source believes that Subject worked in the Ministerio de Obras Públicas during the Batista or Prio regimes. In 1959, Subject was working at the Bolsa de Confecciones (Control Office for textile factories), which was located in the Retiro Odontologico building at 23rd and L Streets, but Source does not know exactly what type of work Subject did. Source believes that Subject worked in that position until he left Cuba for the U.S. in late 1960 or early 1961. Subject now resides at 1529 SW 7th St., Apt. 6, Miami. Source believes that Subject has never been a communist and that he was offered the mentioned position at the Bolsa de Confecciones because of his activities against the Batista regime. Subject has never traveled to any of the communist countries, and Source believes Subject never received any political, military, or intelligence training.

2. Physical Description: DPOB: circa 1926; Source does not know the place of birth. Race: white. Height: 5'10". Weight: 165 lbs. Hair: light brown. Eyes: light brown. Shape of face: round. Build: medium. No visible scars.

3. JEWAVE traces: None.
