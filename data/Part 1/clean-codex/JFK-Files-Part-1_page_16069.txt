A. Objective

The arrest or neutralization of Communist terrorists in the border areas of Thailand.

B. Program Title

Neutralization of Communist Terrorist Threat in Thailand (CNTT).

C. Scope

The scope of this program encompasses a twelve-month, five-phase operation as follows:

Phase I—Planning, preparation, and installation of training center.

Phase II—Selection of personnel, organization of first two Provisional Special Operations Hunter/Killer Platoons (H&K Pltn.), and training program.

Phase III—Insertion of platoon into Tactical Area of Operational Responsibility; initiation of hunter/killer operations; organization, training, and insertion of two additional 50%/1% platoons.

Phase IV—Evaluation of operations to include amending operational concepts, organization, planning, logistics, and administration.

Phase V—Expansion of operations with three 50%/1% platoons. Continuance of operations to include deep penetration missions. Preparation for further expansion of CNTT as required.
