Director

QUIHELP

Ref: Director 09712

1. Fazzare had originally expected to bring family to Madrid, with tentative idea of renewing contract at end of year. Due to family complications (business affairs of his aged father-in-law, health of his aged mother and aunt), however, his wife found it impossible to leave home city. He has therefore not seen wife for a year, for reasons of WOFIRM convenience and not his own. He has promised wife they will have an unbroken two weeks’ vacation in New York from the moment his plane lands. She has therefore made engagements for both with family friends in New York and Connecticut and expects Fazzare to return home with her thereafter.

2. More important, his cover offers no plausible reason he could give the friends with whom his wife has made engagements, if he should suddenly break firm plans and go to Washington for three days.

3. Fazzare can make no solid plans with WOFIRM until he has talked seriously with wife and two sons and consulted his secret.
