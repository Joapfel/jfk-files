8 November 1968, IN 30215.

Refs: A. Mexico City 7965 (IN 2845.17). B. Director 48227.

1. Review of LIENVOY tapes reveals name of subject calling Provorov (Provov) is Douglas (Dougals) Patillo. Patillo speaks good Spanish with definite American accent. Conversations are in Spanish. On 5 November, Patillo called Provorov at Soviet Embassy to ask why Provorov had not gone to see him the evening of 4 November. Provorov stated he would go the evening of 5 November 1968.

2. LIEMBRACE surveillance of Douglas (Dougals) Patillo initiated morning 6 November. He identified himself as Patillo to LIEMBRACE team member who knocked on his door looking for someone else. On the afternoon of 6 November, LIEMBRACE followed Patillo to Soviet Embassy. Patillo walked up to Soviet Embassy and spoke to three Soviets at the gate, one of whom was Valentin Sergeyevich Loginov (Loganov), known KGB. Patillo showed Loginov some papers he had in his suit jacket, then entered Soviet Embassy grounds at 1311 hours. He exited the Soviet Embassy at 1351.
