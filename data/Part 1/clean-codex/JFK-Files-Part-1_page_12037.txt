Outgoing Classified Message.

Central Intelligence Agency.

To: Karlsruhe. From: Special Operations.

17 August 1951. OUT 71605.

WASH 04608.

To: KARLF. Cite: WASHF. Re: KARL 2945 (IN 18811).

1. Santiago advises Chilean FONOF received dispatch dated 31 July from its FRAN (French) consul, Riccioli, requesting authority to issue “ordinary visa” to subject. Stated American intelligence requested visa on same basis as previous defectors.

2. In view of complications in the Chilean defector situation, no disposal to Central or South America is to be initiated without prior Headquarters approval. P.L.S. advise who initiated request in paragraph 1 and hold up action in this case pending instructions from Headquarters.

3. Advise: (A) Why subject was not turned over to DRC for debriefing per current operating procedures? (B) Subject’s present status and location. (C) Clarification of B03 interest in subject and subsequent MOB action.

4. Re MGSA 4038. No identifiable traces of Rotnicki or Teplitz-Semblitsky. Assume CIC following up CE aspects and activities in West Germany.

Released office: H. Cunningham, VLH WT; W. Janey, TUD.

01.02Z, 18 August 1951.

Originating and coordinating offices.
