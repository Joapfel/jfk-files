Originator: AL WEDEMEER. Classified message. Unit: WH/3/MEXICO. EN: 6026. Date: 12 July 1962. To: Mexico City. From: Director. CONF: C/WH 5. Info: DCI, DDP, ADDP, C/CA 2, C/C1, C/C1/IC, C/FE 6, S/C 2. OUT: 5/31/62. CITE DIR: 2262, 22662.

To Mexi (Priority). Info. CITE DIR.

RYRAT.

Ref MEXI 1359 (IN 26600).

1. HOS concurs with proposal in para 3 ref.

2. In discussions with LITENSOR, COS may at his discretion indicate following reasons why visit to SUKARNO a bad idea:

A. SUKARNO is currently engaged in military offensive against Dutch in New Guinea and has thus far frustrated peaceful settlement.

B. Dutch are members of fast-growing and economically powerful European Common Market. Mexico is seeking to increase trade with Common Market countries and to obtain investment capital from them. Believe Mexico's interests would not be served by trip likely to offend Common Market country.

C. Consider this poor time for LITENSOR, through his presence, to give SUKARNO benefit of his and his country's prestige.

End of message.

WH Comment: LITENSOR planning visit SUKARNO in late October or early November. Ambassador MANN mentioned; to COS he personally believes this visit not good. COS suggests he can tell LITENSOR that “my chiefs think visit to SUKARNO this year a bad idea.” Request his comments.

Coordinating officers: Richard Helms, DDP.

Releasing officer: Vern-T. Gresfam, AC/WHD.
