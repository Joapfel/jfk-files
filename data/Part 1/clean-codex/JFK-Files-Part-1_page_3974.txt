Distribution: CARA, BOGO, SJOS, MANA, GUAT. Cite PACY 6583. RNGT: DFTYPIC, AMWORLD.

1. AMBIDDY told ZABOTH we had meeting with Pres. LECNI in March, Pres. VALENCIA 7 April, and plans to see Pres. ROELES 22 or 23 April. Plans return via Costa Rica for talks with Pres. ORLICH and FonMin ODUBER, and Nicaragua to see Pres. SCHICK and Gen. SOMOZA. Then to Guatemala for meeting with Pres. PERALTA and give him complete briefing on ROBERTO ALEJOS coup plotting, which includes some former FRR people for whom A-1 wishes to disclaim any responsibility. During week 3 May he hopes to meet DAINOLD to report on termination of AMWORLD and discuss any other secret.

22 June 65, W 50512.
