CITE MANGIA SORBA

Guard so can furnish coat for an unduty required. Would appreciate authorization pass small quantity 52 caliber ammo to BEMES (BREMES) for practice use by his boats. This will help cement already good relations with BREMES, who [is] worthy [of] independent development by station.

S. Agree desirability upon O.E.R. canteens, web goods and similar material to Guardia. Will help alleviate inevitable drooling over arms and ammo.
