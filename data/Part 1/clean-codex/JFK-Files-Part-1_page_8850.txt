29 June 1965

Memorandum for: Chief, WH Division

Subject: Self-Employment Credit for Social Security Coverage

Edward G. Tichborne

1. The responsibility for filling SEDS (Form 1040), “U.S. Report of Self-Employment Income,” with the Social Security Administration for credit to the taxpayer’s Social Security account has been delegated to this office for those individuals who have filed cover returns.

2. In order to discharge this responsibility and to ensure that each contract agent receives full Social Security credit for any self-employment tax paid through the Covert Unit, it is necessary for this office to prepare Schedule SF (Form 1040) for each taxpayer for secure transmission to the Social Security Administration. In order to complete Form SF for the subject contract agent, it is necessary that the following information, designated by an “X,” be furnished:

True name, as it appears on the subject’s Social Security card.

Social Security number.

Stateside (State/side) address currently applicable, or the address used when the taxpayer last resided in the United States.

Type of business activity to be reflected in reporting Social Security credits from self-employment, such as management consultant, translator, writer, etc.

3. Since a three-year statute of limitations applies to the filing of self-employment schedules for Social Security coverage, it is requested that the above information be furnished as soon as possible. Any questions regarding this subject should be directed to CO/MD/Covert Tax Branch.

S. M. C’Thary  
Chief, Covert Tax Branch
