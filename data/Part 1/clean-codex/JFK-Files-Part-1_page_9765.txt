Dispatch.

To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division.  
Submitted by: Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
Action required: PBRUMEN/Activities of LIRING-3 and LIRING-4, 1 April to 10 May 1968.  
Action required: FYI.  
Reference: HMMA-34484, 16 April 1968.

1. Forwarded under separate cover are eleven contact reports on the activities of LIRING-3 and LIRING-4 for the period 1 April to 10 May 1968, prepared by Wallace B. Rowton.

2. The main points covered in Rowton's reports are:

a. LIRING-3's association with Guatemalan rebels in Mexico City, his departure for Guatemala, and his arrest and sentencing there;

b. The reaction of friends of LIRING-3 and LIRING contacts to the news of his arrest;

c. LIRING-4's efforts to “case” the offices of the LIRING Consulate, particularly the “safe room”; and

d. The obvious provocations of LIRING-4 by Sergio Valdez.

Continued.

Attachments: Reports (under separate cover).

Distribution: S/C STJ 3 — C/WHD w/atts (under separate cover). CS copy.

Gross reference to dispatch symbol and number: HMMA-34682. Date: 17 May 1968. NOS file number: 2ul-.
