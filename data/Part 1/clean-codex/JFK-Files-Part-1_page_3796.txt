Central Intelligence Agency (Central Inteluigence Agency) Official Routing Slip.

From: WH/COG/RR, Lt. Harding. Date: 10 May 87.

Remarks: Attached for your review for information of possible dissemination interest are copies of letters (with translations) from Cuban exile leader Manuel Artime following his return from Nicaragua, where he attended the funeral of Luis Somoza (Somora). The letters were written to a CIA officer. Please return to WH/COG/EXO.
