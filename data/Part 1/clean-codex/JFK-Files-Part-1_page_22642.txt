10 February 1960

Note for the record:

NOEMAYR is a Career Agent in Mexico City on Project LIEDE. According to Charlotte Zehrung, he is to depart Mexico City for headquarters upon the arrival of HEGARTY. HEGARTY is scheduled for a medical release about 23 February 1960 and should arrive in Mexico about 20 March. After a few weeks’ overlap, NOEMAYR should depart for headquarters about 1 April 1960, ready to go to work.

According to Charlotte, he prefers to take no home leave.

Apparently, Mr. Gomez has been working with the Cover Training people in an attempt to place him in that type of activity in a cover status. Per Colonel King on 10 February, Cover Training indicated that they had no interest in the subject, and Colonel King requested the above information for use in his contact with Colonel Baird in a further attempt to place NOEMAYR with OTR.

CB Bowers
