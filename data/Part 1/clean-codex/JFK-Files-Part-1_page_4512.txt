WAVE DIR CITE/LEOP 3961 (LAMPRIGHT ACTING). CHAPPIS, WITHRUSH, PBRUMEN. 10 February 1966. 52472.

1. Following itinerary for BARTES: Depart LEOP 9 February via SABENA to Madrid. Arrive New York via TWA 901, 11 February.

2. For WAVE: BARTES very unhappy over termination, claiming he was not treated fairly by AO's or TRAVIESO. Fact is he should have been sent home a month ago. Request LORENTZ may want to use TRAVIESO if BARTES proves to be difficult.

3. BARTES paid USD 200 and signed receipt stating he has no further money due him.
