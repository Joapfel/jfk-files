Classified message.

IN 24498 MEXI 6502.

To: Info. Cite principally in Miami involving 1,500–2,000 men, including artillery, planes, and landing craft, to take place in November or December. Said AMHAWK to take part in invasion. Said in MEXI that FRD recruiting and screening Cubans for training in camps MEXI. Using Maria Alfaro Alvarado, Nicaraguan (NICARAGJAN), and LIMASK, set up and began military training of small group of Cubans MEXI. Camp at Ninca El Penon of Toluca Road, property owned by Jose Puebla. ARL.

5. On 5 October, SHYROCK and GESTETNER debriefed Mr. William Felder, American businessman MEXI, regarding contacts with Roberto de Vargas and AMHAWK. Roberto told Felder and Hugh Montague, apparently a British friend of Felder, he wanted a training base MEXI for Cuban exiles.

6. On 6 October, Colonel Jose Maria Rego Rubido told station case officer AMHAWK had sent him word via AMHAWK's brothers to assemble a group of 75 or more recruits for training and to prepare a landing plan for Victory Santa Cruz del Sur, including the type and number of arms necessary.

7. Station could have a Gobernación agent advise Roberto to leave the country or he would be subject to arrest and deportation.

8. Request headquarters' MASH views.

End of message.
