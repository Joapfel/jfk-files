8300092, January 1969. Cite Mexico City 2201. Director. 3 January, No. 00731.

1. LITEMPO's (LITEMPOS) report: attempted jailbreak staged by political prisoners at Lecumberri Penitentiary, Mexico City, on 1 January 1979, taking advantage of the holiday visit of families and friends.

2. Entire affair reportedly pre-arranged, with political prisoners believing they could enlist the support of other prisoners. Riot erupted, with other prisoners joining authorities in putting down the disturbance. Undetermined number of political prisoners wounded; no deaths reported.

3. Complaints being drawn up against the following political prisoners who were ringleaders of abortive breaks:

Rafael Danzos Palomino (RAFAR DANZOS PALOMINO), 291-46288.

Victor Rico Galán, wounded (BOUNDED), 291-336474.

Manuel Marcué Pardiñas (MANUEL MARCUE PARDINES), 291-299511.

Rodolfo Echeverría Martínez (RODOLFO SCHEVERNIA MARTINEZ).

Raúl Álvarez Cabrín (RAUL ALVAREZ CABRIN).

Federico Emery Ulloa, 291-822998.

Prof. Ernesto Olvera Sotres (ERNST OLVERA SOTRES).

Rafael Jacobo García, badly wounded (BADLY MOWED).

4. Will advise further developments.
