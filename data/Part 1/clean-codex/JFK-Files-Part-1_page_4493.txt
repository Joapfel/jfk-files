10 September 1965

To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Attention: Mr. J. J. Papich

From: Deputy Director for Plans

Subject: Involvement of Blanca Rose Mathurgas, wife of U.S. Army Colonel John H. Kelly, in plans to overthrow the Government of Nicaragua

1. Reference is made to paragraph 4 of TUCoD-313/68-64 (c), dated 1 September 1978, subject: Remarks dated by General Anastasio Tomaza concerning Daniel Ojoeba of Costa Rica and Communist plans for revolutionary action against Costa Rica, Nicaragua, and Panama.

2. The source of the following information, who was also the source of the above reference, is a Miami businessman who was a resident of Havana for several years and who has contacts who recognize him as a channel to U.S. intelligence. The information was received on 1 August 1978.

A. Dr. Julio Garceran del Vali Sanchez, former Cuban Supreme Court magistrate, said there is a plot of action to overthrow the Government of Nicaragua.

B. On 3 August 1963, Garceran visited Blanca Rose Mathurgas, whom he knew in Cuba, at her home, 1310 Longfellow Street, N.Y., Village Ave., N.Y., where she met with her husband, U.S. Army Colonel John H. Kelly, who is employed in the Pentagon. Blanca Rose told Garceran that she would soon be leaving for Venezuela, where her husband, the married Technician “Venezuela,” lives. At the request of “Tranglino” Fernandez, sister of President Lee America and brother of President Adolpho, mapping officer, Blanca Rose has agreed to act in cooperation with Fernandez and report to a number of Nicaraguans who, with Transalpine Agudad, are linked in plans to overthrow the Monika government.
