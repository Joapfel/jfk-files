Classified message. Date: 24 April 1957. Routing.

To: DIR

Re: DIR 13777 (OUT 98246)

Cite: HAVA 891.

1. Station considers Vaivada’s continued assignment HAVA very desirable but dependent on secure cover arrangement. Suggest HQs set up adequate cover, then have Vaivada come HAVA for a trial period to determine advisability of continued assignment. Recommend independent poll-type cover, already discussed.

2. Student activities now minimal owing to university closure, with no probable opening prior to next school year. However, feel Vaivada, with firm cover, could perform valuable service for station in subversive and Commie penetrations.
