7. Morning 15 July, Hoare phoned Migell to advise he talked to Wicks in Salisbury the previous night. Turned down Wicks’ proposition but did not elaborate. Wicks said Peters has pulled out and is in London. Planning for coup continues to proceed Salisbury. Target date now 21 July. No indication of any plans for movement of recruits from Johannesburg as yet.

8. No further information on role Malumba, Idens, other major Congolese figures involved, or degree of involvement of sovereign governments in plot. Participation of South Africa and Rhodesia governments being handled by security services as indicated. Proof of official participation lacking. Urge utmost protection Hoare as source since his position as U.K. citizen in South Africa most tenuous.
