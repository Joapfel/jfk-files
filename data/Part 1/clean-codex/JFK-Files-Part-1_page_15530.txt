Date: 31 May 67. To: Director. RYBAT WIROGUE. Action: C/AF 3. Routine. Info: COP, C/C1, C/F1 2, S/C 2. IN 18147.

On 28 May WILDCAT/S requested a name check for WIROGUE on behalf of VIROOT. Apparent cause was application by VIROGUE (WIROGUE) for permanent residence card, though Station not aware he was applying until name check requested. VIROOT/2 suspicious [of] Major Pierrot, who has been in opposition [to] WIFLAT/3. This VIROOT suspicious [of] WIROGUE in view of his contacts with Pierrot. Station replying no traces. Depending on depth, check could cause difficulty for WIROGUE.

End of message.
