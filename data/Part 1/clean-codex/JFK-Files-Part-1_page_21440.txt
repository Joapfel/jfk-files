Date: 255, 11 April 1961.

To: Director. From: Paris. Action: SR 6. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, FI, WE 4, S/C 2, CI/OA.

Classified message.

DIR CITE PARI 4355

REDWOOD AEDOGMA UNBOUND

Ref: DIR 33187 (out 9/903).

1. STA wished explore telling UNBOUND that GNYP and NYCZYPORUK had received S/W mail from unnamed source in PBPRIME. Since UNBOUND still interested in MYAKUSHKO contacts, might profitably put mail cover on G and N.

2. STA therefore asked ODENVY representative opinion whether ODENVY HOS investigation of POHORILIW would be adversely affected if above information regarding G and N given to UNBOUND.

3. ODENVY representative states he cabled query to his HOS regarding above.

Re ref:

A. “PBPRIME angle” refers to investigation of POHORILIW, which is aspect of case most directly touching ODYOKE interest.
