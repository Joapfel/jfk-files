Dispatch. Chief of Station, Panama City: Panama City. Chief of Camp, D.C.: D.C. Camp. Gulch, Western District Division. Grid of a Telegraph concerning the Divisions Intransmissible de Recovery. Received: Recoveries (All) are forwarded directly by Grids to H.M.S. Avila (801-300-50). Action: Panama City—see paragraph 3; Jackson—see paragraph 4. References: A. DEC-2326, dated 15 July 1968 (not sent Panama City or JC); B. DEC-2043, dated 27 July 1968 (not sent Panama City or JC).

1. The Divisions Intransmissible de Recovery Receivers (All) is an anti-Fidel Castro organization of Cuban cells headquartered in Miami, Florida, under the leadership of Orlando Busch Avila, who has been involved in numerous revolutionary acts, some of which have involved violence. He allegedly issues press releases and other communications which are signed “Ernesto, General Delegate, Cuban Power.”
