(v) Light Hunter Operations

Light Hunter Operations will be conducted to mark targets. A sniper team of two men placed in a lead helicopter observes the ground with starlight scopes mounted on their rifles. When the enemy is sighted, the snipers engage with tracers. Helicopter gunships, following at a higher altitude, engage the targets. Artillery and illumination are adjusted as necessary.

(vi) Special Operations

The Special Operation Team, sniper-trained, will be employed under Platoon Commander control for a couple of days. They are directed mainly toward sabotage/abolition and sniper activities against the enemy, as dictated by the situation and requirements of the CO/SP/unit counter-terrorist plan.

Subsequent Operations

As experience is gained with combined sniper teams androst unit TSS or DPF operations, the Special Operations and Hunter/Filter Teams will gradually expand operations to fulfill their basic roles—that of independently operating teams moving silently to interdict, amass, and destroy the enemy.
