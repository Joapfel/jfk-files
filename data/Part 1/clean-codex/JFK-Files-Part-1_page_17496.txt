9 July 1962

Project LIFEAT, WH/Mexico (Audio Operation).

1. FI/INT files contain 37 positive information disseminations attributed wholly or in part to Project LIFEAT during the year 1 July 1961 through June 1962. They were concerned principally with contacts between Mexican Communists or leftists and Cubans or individuals of the Soviet bloc, and with the travel of Latin Americans to the Soviet bloc and Cuba. Six reports of particular interest consisted of information on Transcontinental S.A., a company allegedly engaged in the illegal transshipment of U.S. goods to Cuba. Other reports of significance dealt with the contacts and activities of former Guatemalan President Juan José Arévalo Bermejo (AREVALO Barmoja), now living in Mexico. There was reporting on Nicaraguan and Spanish exile activity in Mexico and on the revolutionary sentiment and expectations of other Latin American nationals.

2. LIFEAT has provided positive intelligence of value and of relevance to customer requirements and to objectives of the PNIO. It has been pointed out in the renewal request that the production of positive information is incidental to the project's primary function as a support and CI/CE operation.

3. Renewal is recommended.

HAYDEN ESTEY  
Chief, FI/INT/RE
