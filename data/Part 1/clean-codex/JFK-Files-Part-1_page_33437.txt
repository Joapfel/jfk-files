RYBAT FUOMEN CAGER.

References: A. BONUM 26432, IN 035549. B. Director 456615.

1. “Social area” is term for state-controlled sector of economy. State control over a wide variety of economic activities was significantly expanded during Allende regime. Through purchases, expropriations, requisitions, and interventions of private-sector enterprises, the GOC by mid-1973 had control over the production of goods and services accounting for almost half of Chile’s GDP, compared to an estimated 20% at the end of the Frei administration in 1970.

2. Since taking office, Allende government had taken over at least 400 firms. The executive had submitted to Congress on various occasions bills that would authorize increases in scope and range of government-controlled sector, but there had been no agreement on what sector’s limits should be. Allende government, therefore, had continued.
