6 March 1957

Memorandum for: Chief, Benefits and Services Division, Office of Personnel

Subject: Samples of 2506 Brigade Working Files

1. Following the 10 February meeting in your office with the Director of BEC, Station Miami was requested to send a representative group of files concerning deceased, missing and imprisoned Cuban Brigade members and their dependents who are presently receiving monthly support payments.

2. Attached is one of the eight files received from Miami for your review. Since all the documents are in Spanish, a resume of those documents considered to be most important for establishing the legal relationship between the deceased and his dependents has been attached for your convenience.

3. An identical resume will be made of the documents in the remaining seven files in order that they may also be reviewed whenever it is convenient for you to schedule a meeting. We may logically assume that a certain number of the remaining files will be lacking some of the vital documents included in the attached file. If these documents are required to meet BEC criteria on an individual basis, they could be obtained in practically all cases from those dependents who reside in the United States.

4. The attached file is a rather impressive example of the type of documented proof we can submit for BEC scrutiny; perhaps we can avoid having to run the gauntlet on an individual basis.

Nancy Gratz  
WIL/COG
