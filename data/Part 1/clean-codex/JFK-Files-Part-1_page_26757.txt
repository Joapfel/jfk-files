6 May 1965

17.2 continued.

They are going to talk to Césario today. Apparently they have named Myjia Kicart as A-b in the United Nations and another one of the boys (exiles) as A-b here. Will talk at 1700. It would be good for Deschamps to go there. VRC cannot go there. They are at Heriberto Prias I421-10. They took VRC's recorder. Deschamps should go there. If he misses the call, they should have it recorded anyway. They just picked up VRC's recorder.

After 1710, 6 May.

18.0 In. Elio Cordero with Inge Rico, who answers for VRC. Not there. Asks Elio to leave his phone. He is at 24-07-15. Cordero is there with Marcio (Myjia Kicart).

18.5 In. -O with Inge Rico, who answers for VRC. Not there. -O wants an editorial from VRC about the President's speech before the CEPAL. VRC will be home around 2200. He is out now. He said he was going out to dinner.

After 1910, 6 May.

19.0 In. Pablo with Inge Rico, who answers. Greetings. Pablo has rheumatic fever. Isn't Pablo in bed? No. Pablo can't move his right arm. VRC is not there. VRC is very busy with Dominican affairs. I invited them to fix my car over on Friday. MO had planned to take out his wife, Lupe, since it was their anniversary but also has to go to the dentist. Best some other day. If they are at home, and there is no dental problem, they can get together for dinner.

20.0 In. -O asks Inge Rico, who answers for VRC. Not in. Should be home around 2200.

20.2 In. VRC with Inge Rico, who answers. They (exiles) still have not gotten the long distance. VRC is there waiting on this man and will return “there” in a little bit. Does he know where? Yes, VRC was there. It is the house of Jose Luis. VRC had the address but he did not have the phone. Didn't Jose Luis phone him? No. I think it was Jose Luis who just phoned. Did VRC just get “there”? Yes. Ask them there if Jose Luis called. No. Besides, VRC has an appointment now. VRC left there (exiles) and will return there. Will he be home late? Not sure—when they get the call. He will come home right away to call Habana.

20.7 In. Sr. Leonov with Inge Rico, who answers for VRC. Not there. Leonov wanted to postpone an appointment with VRC. He will be home late today. Call him tomorrow between 0900 and 1000. VRC may be at home all morning since he has to write. Will go to “Sic-pre” around noontime. Leonov will phone back tomorrow morning.

21.0 In. VRC with I, who answers. “This stupid man has not come. Hasn't he come by there?” No. It is now 2020. “I did” has not phoned either. Leonov phoned VRC. Leonov said something about having an appointment with VRC. VRC does not know about it. I will phone back tomorrow. Marcio called saying he wanted an editorial on the President's speech this morning. VRC will wait there another 10 minutes for me to appear at the meeting.
