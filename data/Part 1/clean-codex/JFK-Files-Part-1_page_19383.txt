Request for Overseas Transportation. Sec. to: Director, CIG. 5 March 1947.

COAP approval is requested for the overseas transportation of Mr. James Walton Moore.

Justification for the trip:

1. Subject will be Chief CIG station in the American Consulate in Dairon, China.
2. Subject's designation as Vice Consul, American Consulato (Consulate), Dairon, China.

Dates: 2-9-47; 9-7-47; January 1947.

The following actions concerning the above traveler were completed on the dates indicated below:

Date assessed and approved: February 1945. Date security approved: 8 December 1944. Date entered on duty: 29 August 1946. Date of overseas physical examination: 21 January 1947. Date immunization initiated: 21 January 1947. 6 March 1947.

Signature (initiating officer). Date. Signature (transportation clearance officer).
