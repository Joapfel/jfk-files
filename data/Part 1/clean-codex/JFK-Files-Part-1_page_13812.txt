TEGU-INFO DIR CITE HEXI 1416. PERUEN.

FYI PER LIENVOY: Honduran named Raul Eraun called Cuban EMS 22 October. Wanted to see about going to Cuba; was referred to consul.

On 25, 26, and 27 October, asked for Roselio (Roselio Rodriguez Lopez, second secretary, known). Was told to keep calling since nothing had arrived.

30 October 1964.
