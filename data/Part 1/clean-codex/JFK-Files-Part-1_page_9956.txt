16 August 1957, IN 33264. Guatemala City information director. KAPOK.

Reference: Mexico City 2455 (IN 30815).

1. Mother of Moises (Orozco) Leal phoned LIFING-4 on 14 August and asked to meet her. At the ensuing meeting, Orozco’s mother stated that Alma Marina Arrecla (ALRA MARINA ARRECLA), her husband, and son were recently deported to Guatemala by Mexican police, where they were executed on arrival.

In view of the manner in which the reference information was obtained, do not plan to check the above with LITEMPOS, but try to obtain details of their interrogation of Orozco, conducted on the basis of station requirements and Orozco background information.
