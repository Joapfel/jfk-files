10. Mr. Heegard stressed that each applicant is processed thoroughly to avoid sending an unqualified person to South America.

11. Basic Spanish, according to Mr. Heegard, can be learned by an applicant over a period of three months—at least three hours of instruction per week. This should be done prior to departure from Germany.

12. During the processing, the applicant receives a pamphlet explaining the economic, social, political, and other conditions of the country of his choice.

13. Mr. Heegard said that, in general, promotional opportunities are excellent in certain fields due to the lack of qualified personnel.

14. The Special Program is the only one under which ICEM operates for foreign refugees desiring to emigrate from Europe to South America.

15. Professions and skills required in South America are: Colombia: electrical machine construction (Elektromaschinenbau): engineer, technician (drafting and operating), draftsman, master, cutting-tool maker (Schneiwerkzeugmacher), matrix maker for plastic material. Metallurgy: skilled foundry worker; moulder (metal foundry); rolling-mill worker and technician; casting-mould matrix maker (Metallguss-form-Schabionenmacher). Miscellaneous professions: repair mechanic, photo-engraver, offset-retoucher, technician for plastic material, papermaker.

16. Professions and skills not required in South America: lawyers, office workers, specialists for commerce and sales, construction engineers, construction technicians and construction workers, carpenters, leather manufacturers, waiters, servants, unskilled laborers.

17. Housing: ICEM does not find housing for this migrant but may give advice if needed.

18. ICEM needs Göresberg (GÖRESBERG), coordinator with ICEM Genève on the processing of an applicant.

19. A job opportunity may be found at the Zentralstelle (ZENTRALSTÄTLE) for abrushing, Escherheimerstr. 1–7. The ICEM Genève office performs the job late as the above organization.
