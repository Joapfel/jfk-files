19 November 1957

REMC:JUNO for: WH/Logistics

Subject: Purchase of Metal Storage Cans and Deluxe Carrying Cases

1. The Mexican desk has received an cels request from the field for metal cans to replace the cardboard boxes which are now used for audio tapes in the LIFET project. The cardboard boxes used to protect the tapes wear out in a very short time because of the frequent handling which they receive at the Station.

2. It is requested that you purchase and ship to the Mexican Station the following items, which can be purchased from LAPAFETTE MAGIC (LAFAYETTE MAGIC), 165-08 Liberty Avenue, Jamaica 33, New York (seen in catalog 305):

100 metal storage cans for 7-inch, 1,200-foot reels, $0.39 each (in lots of 6).

3 deluxe carrying cases, holding 35 reels per case, $5.40 each.

3. The costs of the above items should be charged to Project LIFET.

Robert J. Dahlén  
C/M/III
