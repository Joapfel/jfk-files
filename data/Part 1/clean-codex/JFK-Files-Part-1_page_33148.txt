Dispatch. Info. From. Subject. Action Requiring Reference.

Chief, Western Hemisphere Division; Chief of Station, Madrid; Chief of Station, WJ/Miami. Subject: Berafin Pineiro Losada, aka “Tifo.” Transmittal of correspondence.

Attached for the information of addressee are copies of a letter purportedly written by Berafin Pineiro Losada to Argelio Martinez del Toro, former mayor of Matanzas now resident in Miami. The original letter has been returned to AMCHALE-5. Most of the letter seems devoted to complaint that Berafin has been unjustly accused of being a member of the C-3. Of interest is the writer's reference to the death of “Papito” (probably Jose Pineiro Losada, 201-735948) some three years ago.

Frederick C. Bradspies.

Attachment: Letter.

Distribution: 3 – Chief, VND, with attachment; 3 – CCS, Madrid, with attachment.

Cross-reference to dispatch symbol and number: UFM-1339. Date: 30 September 1969. HCS file number: 201-973664.
