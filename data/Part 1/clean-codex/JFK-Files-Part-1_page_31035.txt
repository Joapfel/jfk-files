H. F. Parnell

Although writer has bank, mortgage, and accounts in the U.S., he feels ties for security reasons to old-time friends in his native city of Chicago. He feels loyalty to the state of Illinois. He also intends to remain a member of Congress until his term expires. Mr. and Mrs. Walter Parnell have three children, all under age, and Mr. Parnell intends to remain on active duties in the state.

Marino, D.F.

19 March 1961

7 June 1961
