Memorandum for: Chief, Audit Staff

Subject: Jeremy K. HENADUE, Cover Income

Reference: Audit Staff Memo, dated 17 March 1969, subject: Clarification of Cover Income Status of Career Agent, Jeremy K. BERNADUH.

1. Reference indicated Jeremy K. HEMADUE may be receiving cover income, and requested a review be made to determine whether he should be refunding such income and whether his contract should be amended in accordance with FN 20-32.

2. The Mexico City Station has been requested to:

a. Forward Cover Earnings Reports for 1968 without delay.

b. If reports for previous years are not readily available, BERNADUE is to prepare and provide them to the Station for transmittal within a reasonable period.

c. If HENADUE has received cover earnings which have not been offset against his contract compensation, forward recommendations on amending his contract to provide retention of cover earnings in accordance with FN 20-32.

3. Upon receipt from Mexico of the requested information, the Division will take appropriate action.

William V. Broo  
Chief  
Western Hemisphere Division
