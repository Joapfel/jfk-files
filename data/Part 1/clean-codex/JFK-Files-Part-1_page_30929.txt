Dispatch. To: Chief, W1 Division. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: ADMIN/FINANCE — Jeremy K. BENJAMIN, living quarters allowance.

Action Required — References. Action required: Paragraph 3.

1. Subject purchased his local residence in Mexico City on 1 December 1964 at a cost of $5,270.00 and has been residing in it since that date. Station has paid BENJAMIN a quarters allowance as follows: 1 December 1964 to 31 March 1965 — $4,024.92 per annum; 1 April 1965 to present — $4,128.96 per annum.

A review of subject's quarters accounting for the period 1 April 1965 to 31 October 1969 reflects his average monthly utilities costs at $75.00. (See Attachment A.)

2. Subject's base quarters allowance has been as follows: 1 December 1964 — 83,776.00 per annum; 25 April 1965 — $4,350.00 per annum (*); 16 July 1967 — $4,756.00 per annum; 18 July 1968 — $4,775.00 per annum; 5 October 1969 — $4,706.00 per annum. (*) Effective date of family plan.

3. In view of separate cover attachment B received from the embassy, Headquarters' guidance is requested in determining whether any adjustments should be made in BENJAMIN'S quarters allowance.

Attachments: A — W1 draft — 8/8. B — Embassy News — 1/8/65. Distribution: (G) W1 copy as above.
