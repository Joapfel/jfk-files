Coded message.

DIR WAVE CITE MEXI 3531. 7 December 1965. 09866.

1. LIENVOY of 3 December reported request by Cuban Alfredo Vinas (Viñas) in Havana (HAVA) to Victor Rico Galan for a series of interviews with prominent personalities regarding the Tri-Continental Congress. Rico Galan to send tape of interviews to Havana.

2. Names suggested to Rico included Vicente Lombardo Toledano, PPS secretary general; former President Lazaro Cardenas del Rio; Deputy Enrique Ramirez y Ramirez (Ramírez y Ramírez), also publisher of El Dia; Luis Cardoza y Aragon, Guatemalan Communist in Mexico; Guillermo Carneiro Hoke; Jose Pages Llergo (Lleroo), publisher of Siempre; and others Rico thinks suitable. Rico said Pages impossible but will contact others at once.

CFN 5531, 3 December: Cuban Alfredo Vinas (Viñas), Havana, Victor Rico Galan, Tri-Continental, Rico Galan, Havana, Vicente Lombardo Toledano, PPS secretary general, former President Lazaro Cardenas del Rio, Enrique Ramirez y Ramirez (Ramírez y Ramírez), El Dia, Luis Cardoza y Aragon, Guillermo Carneiro Hoke, Jose Pages Llergo (Lleroo), Rico.
