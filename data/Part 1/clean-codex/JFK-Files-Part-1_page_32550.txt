Dispatch. 1st: Clerk, F, 27-263. No.: Chief of Staff, World, 19 January 1962. Ave.: Administrative/Finance, FLS—Chief S O/S. Reconnaissance for Promotion—REDACTED. Wars for Indoing; No Piling Upon. Sig.: Rec. Belge. Officer Can Be Judged by Qualified H.O. Sex Only.

Proprietors.

1. Subject is a mature, experienced, well-rated operations officer and administrator; for the past fifteen months he held the position of Deputy Chief of the staff. In an extended period of time during which he had serving Chief of Staff, it has been observed that he has at all times demonstrated his qualifications in an outstanding manner. In addition to the above already four months that I have been able to observe subject's performance at this post, I also have the privilege of serving with him at Belgrade and later on, as a non-commissioned officer. This past year, subject is a dedicated officer who works “hard” the clock in his efforts to expand and improve covers in this area.

2. Considerable credit is due to subject for the improvement of our relations with the local security services. During the past three months we have started a considerable amount of work and effort in bringing about needed reports are essential in the success of our “outside activity,” cutting as many double agents as possible. In addition, subject has been particularly effective in developing and recruiting in-depth Secret Service men (Liegende Vertreters) for one such operation.

3. The attached letter shows for itself. Subject has been in his current position since January ’58. Given his age, his experience and maturity, and his consistently high performance in his current position, I STRONGLY urge that he be activated; considered for promotion to NCO-1 at the next appropriate grade.

Jenner R. 1958. Signature.
