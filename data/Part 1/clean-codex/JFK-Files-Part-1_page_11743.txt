Mrs. Arthur Trevena, Sr., of Vila d'l Mar, Pit Santiago Monday for Buenos Aires where she will stay until her boat leaves for England about December 3rd. Mrs. Trevena plans to spend a year in England, visiting her children, relatives and friends.

Mr. and Mrs. Albert Haney were guests of honor at a dinner given last Thursday morning by home. Sr. Jean Lu's de Berthet and Sra. Sonja Edwards de Berthet returned to Santiago last Friday after an extensive honeymoon in Europe.

The North American Institute celebrated Thanksgiving with a turkey dinner last Thursday night. The staff, members of the board of directors and members of the Institute attended. Impromptu entertainment was enjoyed by all.

Sr. Javier Valdez will return to Santiago Sunday after a business trip to New York and Rio de Janeiro.

Mr. and Mrs. Thomas Lewis and son, Donald Roy, left for their home in Chuquiramata after a month's visit with her mother and father-in-law, Mr. and Mrs. I. Berkwood Hobsbawn, of Santiago.

Mrs. Felix Caffarena was hostess at a bridge-canasta tea for Mrs. Albert Haney, who, with Mr. Haney, is leaving Chile on December 12th.
