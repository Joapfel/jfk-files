22 September 1959

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Conversations with Reichardt (Reichhardt), 8, 9, 16, 17 September 1959

1. On 8 September Reichardt (Reichhardt) phoned to report the following:

a. He took Marcos Diaz Lanz to the office of INS on Monday, 7 September, to take care of his immigration status. (Comment: I was later called by Bill Cotter of the Alien Branch, GS, concerning this. Cotter said that when they reported this to INS, that service said that Aegens, the INS man in Miami, had told them that Diaz's hearing had already been held shortly after his arrival. Cotter implied that Bernie was not reporting accurately on the situation in Miami. I have not repeated this to Bernie, because Alien Branch seems to be in something of a tizzy in regard to the new people in INS with whom they are dealing as a result of recent transfers of personnel.)

b. Bernie asked me to clarify what I wanted in the way of an affidavit of support for the wife of Marcos Diaz. I dictated a simple statement and told Bernie to have it notarized and accompanied by a bank statement or similar document indicating Williams's ability to support Mrs. B should it become necessary.

c. Bernie reported that Mrs. Catherine Takes, the international busybody, claims that she is going to Cuba on our instigation. She is accompanied by her entire family. This information came from the Miami Chief of Detectives to the CO Representative.

d. Bernie asked how he could identify himself to the three evacuees in Miami whom we have instructed him to contact regarding going to the Dominican Republic on our behalf. I told him to use Dave Morales's name.

e. He asked me for information on our dispatch to Cuba in connection with the PRCs for AMCO and the gift. I told him I would check and let him know. (Comment: The same day I saw a dispatch from Havana answering the questions, an information copy of which went to Bernie.)
