SAGO. REDWOOD LCIMPROVE.

Reference: MEXI 6866 (IN 95002).

The following is for information only:

1. Nechiporenko, born 4 July 1932, wife Lidiya N. Attended conference in Caracas, also BOSB, possibly BUEN, December 1957. Vice consul in Mexico from August 1961 to August 1965. Speaks fluent English and Spanish. Able to pass as Mexican. Clever, intelligent, reserved. Thorough background in Mexican culture.

2. Kostikov, born 17 March 1933, wife Roza Aleksandrovna, born 2 March 1932. In 1958 and 1959 attended conferences in Madrid; in 1959 and 1960 attended industrial fairs in Mexico and Havana. Employee, then vice consul in Mexico from September 1961 to August 1965. Speaks good English and Spanish.

3. Both subjects known KGB per extensive activities in Mexico. Kostikov believed in Thirteenth Department, First Chief Directorate.

4. Extensive traces on subjects available at headquarters. Please advise soonest if subjects arrive in Santiago. Will then pouch SPRS with portion for liaison.

End of message.
