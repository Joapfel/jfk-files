Classified message. Date: 25 January 1962. To: Director. January 25, 0429Z 1962. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5 (WH tempo copy slotted 0030, 25 January). Info: DCI, D/DCI, DD/P, S/C 2*. Priority. IN 17139. Routing.

Priority DIR CITE MEXI 9960.

RYBAT

For GALBOND and SAYRE from Ambassador Mann:

1. As you know, some weeks ago we became convinced that the only chance of getting Mexico to break relations with Cuba would be on the basis of Cuban intervention in Mexican internal affairs. This, of course, was on the assumption that a two-thirds Rio Treaty vote could not be obtained at Punta del Este.

2. With this objective in mind, the Department and CAS authorized me to turn over the materials described in MEXI 9855 to an appropriate Mexican official. I chose Gustavo Diaz Ordaz.

3. Diaz Ordaz has informed CAS that he discussed this evidence with Lopez Mateos, who expressed surprise and interest. We discount Lopez Mateos' statements because, as you know, he has much better evidence of Cuban Embassy intervention, which he keeps strictly to himself and has never told the Cabinet about. The evidence we turned over to Diaz Ordaz has psychological importance because the President is now aware that Diaz Ordaz also knows part of the story. He can never be sure that Diaz Ordaz will not use.
