1. Request headquarters consider not cancelling Project CIELBOW. Reference B, pouched 14 November, contains cover recommendations and requests that headquarters seek a replacement for DIGERVENO, whose death was reported by BANASIAK on return from JMWAVE.

2. HUNKELER and COS met the night of 14 November to discuss business developments subsequent to drafting reference B. HUNKELER believes that if a Spanish-speaking officer with business experience can be obtained, there would be no problem in replacing DIGERVENO in the proposed cover mechanism. HUNKELER reports good progress made in obtaining outlets for his lines of merchandise. At COS’s request, he will prepare a report for headquarters and the station on this progress upon his return to JMWAVE.

3. JMWAVE: Request station express COS’s sympathy to the wife of DIGERVENO, whose death was both a personal and official loss.
