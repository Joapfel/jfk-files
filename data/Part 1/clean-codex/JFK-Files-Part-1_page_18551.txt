27 August 1947

Personal History Statement on William King (KNO) Harvey.

Personal: Birth: September 13, 1915. Legal residence: Mayville, Kentucky. Place: Danville, Indiana.

Parentage: Father: Drenan R. Harvey, born 1888. Mother: Sara Jewel King Harvey, born 1890. Place: Danville, Indiana.

Relatives abroad: None.

Education: Wiley High School, Terre Haute, Indiana, 1928 to 1931. Indiana University, Bloomington, Indiana, 1933 to 1937. LLB degree.

Experience: Danville Gazette, newspaper, Danville, Indiana, 1931 to 1933. Indiana University, Bloomington, Indiana, 1933 to 1937. Practice of law, Mayville, Kentucky, 1937 to 1940. FBI, special agent, 1940 to August 22, 1947.

Military: None.

Travel: Visited Canada for one month in September 1940 on vacation.

Marital status: Married to Elizabeth How McIntire Harvey, born February 3, 1916. Dependents: None besides wife. Father-in-law: James Marvin McIntire, Sr., born 1880. Mother-in-law: Mennie Rose McIntire, now deceased, born 1886. Place: Fleming County, Kentucky.

Washington address and telephone number: 2627 39th St., N.W., Washington, D.C. Tel: Ordway 2914.

Emergency addressee: Mrs. Elisabeth M. Harvey. 2627 39th St., N.W., Washington, D.C. Tel: Ordway 2914.
