EAVA-1939. ADM/2519.

To: Chief, EE

From: Chief of Mission, Austria

Subject: General—Administrative. Specific—Promotion for.

Date: 27 February 1953

GROOVY/GRBOUNCE: Tennent H. Bagley.

1. Joined KUBARK in July 1950 and arrived at this Mission on 15 June 1951 as a CB-9.

2. Since his arrival has continually shown himself as a great asset to the Mission. After a period of technical work and agent handling, he was made chief of the Counter Espionage Section for the Mission in November 1952. He has done an excellent job in organizing this section and in handling and training the four persons whom he supervises.

3. Upon his arrival in Vienna, he knew little German. He has now mastered the language to such a degree that he can handle German-speaking agents. His handling of Grabysm 1 and Leland D. Slaughter has been outstanding in that, by carefully training these agents, he increased considerably their value to this Mission. In addition, he has an excellent grasp of basic tradecraft, which enables him to perform his functions with great efficiency and to teach this tradecraft to the junior members of his section. He is one of the regular instructors in the Vienna Operations Base tradecraft seminars. He has shown an unusual social ability in Vienna, a quality which has done much to help him move freely in the local scene. His duties frequently call for large quantities of written work, and he has proven himself able to handle this speedily and with clarity of expression.

4. He is a very promising case officer, and at a meeting of the Mission Promotion Board was unanimously recommended for promotion to CB-11. It is with great confidence that I submit his name to Headquarters for this action.

Morton A. Woonley

Recommended and Approved

Henry P. Dagenham

Attachment: Job Description.
