Subject: Requiring Information — Gloria Pinto. Date of information: December 21, 1968, 1100 hours. Place acquired: French Club.

I saw Gloria talking to Iouri Chernyeh. I joined them. Gloria was turning on all of her not inconsiderable charm. She invited Iouri to her posada. Then she asked whom she should see at the Soviet embassy about a business deal he had. She was told this would be Ivanov. Gloria said she knew him and described him as big and corpulent. Apparently this was correct, according to Iouri's reaction.

Gloria asked Iouri where he worked and was told with Valentin. He added that this is the department you must come to if you want to visit the Soviet Union.

Later I saw Iouri with his wife and son. She is rather attractive and tall. Pavel is about three years old and a very handsome little boy.

Date of information: December 23, 1968, 2000–0200 hours. Place acquired: Posada at Gloria's.

Val and his wife, Iouri and his wife, and about 40 to 50 other people were present. Val's wife brought petrojki—delicious.

Gloria invited us to spend next weekend with them and 3 or 4 other couples at Valle de Bravo. Valentin and his wife will be there. We accepted.
