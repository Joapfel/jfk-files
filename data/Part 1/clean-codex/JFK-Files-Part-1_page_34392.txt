1. Assumption paragraph 1 reference A correct. Summary release follows:

UNIDAD REVOLUCIONARIA (UR) in receipt of considerable evidence from numerous sources that SOVS may announce withdrawal of economic aid to Cuba after party meeting 1 March in Moscow and that SOVBLOC officials studying ways to disengage as soon as possible since Cuba now costs the bloc 12 billion a year, which is more than it is worth. Russian leaders have to consider (1) Soviet Union internal problems, which are negative to Castro since, according to statements by Kosygin, Mikoyan and Brezhnev in December 1964, the USSR is far from reaching farm, cattle and industrial goals; (2) attitude of East European satellites, which is also unfavorable to Castro, judging from articles in Czech “Literarni Noviny” and “Kulturni Zivot” and the British “Financial Times,” which indicate COMECON considers further aid to Cuba beyond bloc capabilities; (3) Castro’s own problems, e.g., lack of dollar credits and depression in the world sugar market, with the price dropping from the December 1963 peak of 12.6 to 2.35 cents per pound.
