Cable Secretariat Dissemination. Classified message.

Secret Group I. Routing. Action unit. File. Action No.

Secret 1823CSZ May 70. Cite Managua 7336 (finish) 085604. Lima info Director, WH/Miami.

Ref: Managua 7332 (IN081565).

1. Following additional from Ambiddy to COS Managua 14–13 May. Leigh stayed with Ambiddy in Miami 10–13 May. Looking for stateside sales agent for Peruvian fishmeal, offered job to Ambiddy. Explained General Artola, Minister of Interior, put another general (name unknown) in charge of government consortium fishmeal industry. Peru now trying to sell on world market. Unidentified general went to Europe trying to peddle fishmeal while Leigh in States on same mission. If stateside deal can be arranged, Ambiddy will have exclusive sales rights in return for two percent commission. Ambiddy phoned Minnesota and Florida chicken feed manufacturers and was told they would buy all fishmeal he could furnish. He sensed something “fishy” in being offered job as middleman for product so readily marketable and soon learned that he would be expected to kick back half his commission to generals. This might amount to over one hundred thousand dollars. Leigh has invited Ambiddy to visit Peru end of May to discuss details. He plans go.
