Agency Message.

TOR: 0612482 July 1973. Paris 3566.

Cite (Paris) 3566.

To: Priority Headquarters.

RYBAT MHOURS MHCHAOS.

References: A. (Paris) 3451, 7 May 1973. B. Headquarters 4525, 2 May 1973.

1. (UNBOUND) passed the following report 3 July from source (“BABOUCHE”). Black Panther members George Edward Wright, George Brown, Melvin McNail, his wife Jean Carol McNall, and Joyce Rillerson (wife of George Wright) arrived in Paris from Algiers in late March. Henri Curie Lis’s (Henri Curiel’s) clandestine organization has provided them lodging and training during their stay in France. The group is now trying to arrange plastic surgery and false documentation to permit their clandestine re-entry into the United States.

2. (UNBOUND) requests on an urgent basis any available photos of the above individuals to aid in identifying them before surgery takes place. Please advise. (UNBOUND) has not passed this information to the legal attaché.

3. E-2 IMPDET.
