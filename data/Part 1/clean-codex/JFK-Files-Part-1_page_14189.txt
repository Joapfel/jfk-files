Message from: Spain. Unit: WH/COG-OPS. RT: 7144 R. 1819. Date: 10 July 1969. File: VD. 202, ADD, C/EUES, /S/. FLA. Payer: K. Cite Director 1979A.

To Madrid. Info: WH/MIAMI RYBAT TYPIC AMROSE AMBEDEW. References: A. OSMA 25059, 21 April 1969. B. DIRECTOR 16159. C. MADRID 8882 (IN 92987).

1. Photos forwarded with Ref A shown to AMBEDEW-1, who identified the following, numbered per reference: No. 1, recognized as “GALLO,” former chief of center, Spain. No. 4, identified as “ENCARGADO DE NEGOCIOS” in Spain, Guillermo (Ruiz). A-1 added that Ruiz was very “dictatorial” in his dealings with DGI officers, and that he had serious personality conflict with Gallo. No. 6, recognized as the man A-1 thinks replaced Gallo as COC in Spain, whose pseudo is “DOMINGO.”

2. Although A-1 thought photo No. 2 looked very familiar, he was unable to identify it. He did not recognize any of the remainder of the photos.
