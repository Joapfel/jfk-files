1 August 1967. M 23361.

012041Z. Cite Mexico City 2331. Immediate Guatemala City. Info immediate Director. KAPOK.

Ref: Mexico City 2322 (IN22584).

1. With ROWTON info that Moises Orozco may not have been telling the truth about Orozco's alleged contacts with FAR guerrillas (info based on LIREBEL-1 and LIREBEL-2's statements per reference), LIRING-3 successfully confronted and “broke” Orozco at his apartment the night of 31 July in the presence of Sergio Valdez and Raul Moreno. Orozco admitted he recently went to Guatemala to organize a guerrilla area but said he was unable to accomplish his task due to the stupidity of FAR representatives. Orozco demanded to know the source of LIRING-3's information. When LIRING-3 refused, saying only that he was in contact with FAR, Orozco said, “It must have been Roberto.” He went on to throw insults at Cesar Montes and other FAR representatives, mentioning war names, saying that David Cassasola, who was with him in Guatemala, was undisciplined and responsible for the capture of many of these men and probably his own. Orozco claimed he was the only true Guatemalan revolutionary and admitted he had hoped to enlist LIRING-3's help in obtaining arms with which he hoped to rearm FAR followers loyal to him and form his own revolutionary group. Orozco was given LIRING-3's phone number.
