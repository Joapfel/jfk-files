Date: 10 March 1969. IN. From: 201-845437; 201-798301.

Dispatch. Chief of Station, Guatemala City. Chief, W. Division. Chief of Station, London City. H.M.K./L.M.I.K./Humberto (Jumberto) GONZALEZ Vega. Received: JAN-36603, 27 February 1969.

1. Attached for Addressee is a translation of a report received from LINDUCK on 23 February 1969 from Humberto (Nezahurto) GONZALEZ Vega (201-845437). Guatemalan Communist cells in Mexico who have been in contact with LINDUCK are (201-798301). The report was submitted by LINDUCK in response to the query for additional information on GONZALEZ mentioned in paragraph 9 of reference.

2. It will be noted that LINDUCK's information tends to confirm earlier reporting that GONZALEZ is connected with the PGT rather than the FNC, though he would appear to have some contacts with the latter organization. LINDUCK's report is based in part on details picked up from the past; it is believed that his comments may be of interest to Addressees.

3. LINDUCK has promised to provide any additional information on GONZALEZ which he may be able to develop.

Willard C. CURTIS.
