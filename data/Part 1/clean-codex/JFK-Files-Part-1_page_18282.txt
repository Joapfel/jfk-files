23 October 1962

Mr. William P. Barke  
6300 Saint Charles Avenue  
New Orleans 13, Louisiana

Dear Mr. Burke:

As you bring to a close eighteen years of service to your country, I want to join your friends and co-workers in wishing you well and hoping that you find the years ahead filled with enjoyment and satisfaction.

It takes the conscious efforts of many people to do the important work of this Agency. You leave with the knowledge that you have personally contributed much to our success in carrying out our missions. Your faithful and loyal support has measured up to the high standards and traditions of the Federal Service.

May I express to you my appreciation and sincere best wishes in the years ahead.

Harry W. Little, Jr.
