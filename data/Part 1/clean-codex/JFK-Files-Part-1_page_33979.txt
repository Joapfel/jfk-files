CITE SANTIAGO 4743. Director info: Mexico City, Caracas, Montevideo, Bogota, Buenos Aires, Rio de Janeiro, JMWAVE.

TYPIC REDCOAT AMSCROLL. ANL. 3F. AES TR 07 X. CW/C/CA.

1. PEC 28 July center spread carries JMSCROLL study Cuban total dependence on Soviet Union. Subtitles: “Fidel Castro has made the country of Marti a colonial nation subjected to Soviet imperialism”; “Foreign trade monopolized by nations in the Soviet orbit.” Clipping to JMWAVE. Henceforth pouching PEC to JMWAVE regularly.

2. Same issue has good items on Olas, Nasser and poison gas, anti-Semitism in the Soviet Union, battle for power in Communist China, and Harrison Salisbury article on Soviet censorship.

26 July 1967.
