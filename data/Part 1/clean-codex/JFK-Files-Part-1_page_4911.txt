Outgoing Message.

Federal Bureau of Investigation, Investigative Division. FIRCI-318/03816-7B.

Subject: Contact between Orlando Bosch (BO SCH) and the Novo Brothers in Miami shortly before the assassination of Orlando Letelier.

3. Source of paragraph 2: A Venezuelan government employee whose position gives him access to official reports on various subjects. His reliability has not been established. This information should not be discussed with foreign officials, including those of the Venezuelan government.

2. Since his arrest on 25 October 1976, Orlando Bosch Avila, Cuban exile leader, has admitted to an official of the Venezuelan Directorate for the Services of Intelligence and Prevention (DISIP) that he had contact with the Novo (SBNOVO) brothers in Miami shortly before the assassination of Orlando Letelier.
