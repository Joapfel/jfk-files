g. We think that it need not cost more than $25,000 for the next year to maintain the Congress organization, pay for administration, traveling expenses, including the salaries of personnel, which would assume a General Secretary who would be an O.C. agent. (This latter concept may not be absolutely necessary; but if not, then provision should be made for OPC contact some other way.) In addition to the $25,000 specified above, there should be an allotment of $80,000 for the holding of the next Congress. Another $26,000 should be added for special purposes largely connected with publicity. The major part of the expenditures for the next year for collection, printing, and dissemination of Congress material and which could conceivably amount to as much as $250,000 as a top figure, would be provided, it is hoped, by PCA funds.

h. The activities mentioned in Part II of subject report could very well be handled under the setup outlined above with certain exceptions which seem to be too broad in scope to be justified at all.
