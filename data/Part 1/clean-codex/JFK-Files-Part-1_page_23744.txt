3. Canvassing of exile reactions shows the impact of Menoyo's capture and subsequent statements to be heavier and more demoralizing than Manolo Fariasco's. Particularly upsetting was Menoyo's advice to exiles that their efforts were causing unnecessary bloodletting without a chance of success because people inside support Castro. Menoyo's unemotional delivery and warning to exile adventurers that their fate would be worse than his was interpreted as evidence that he was either a coward or a traitor who knew he would not be shot. AMCORE-2 reports that exiles are unable to reconcile Menoyo's alleged background as a brave guerrilla fighter with last night's television performance and feel the only explanation is that Menoyo was a Castro agent all along.

4. Most exile leaders consider the worst damage from the Menoyo episode to be the underlying promotion of the Castro government as a powerful regime against which exiles or inside resistance elements can do little. AMCORE-2 reports that SFNE people are wishing out loud for a raid by Artime or Orlando Bosch to counter the effect Menoyo must have had among anti-Castroists.
