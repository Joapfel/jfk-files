182122Z. CITE JMWAVE 9802. Caracas INFO Director.

AKULE PBRUMEN.

REF CARACAS 7805 (1-5/6.49).

1. AMCLEVE/15 reports telephone call from Gustavo (Gistavo) Ortiz Faez on 13 Oct 67. Ortiz informed AMCLEVE/15 that Erasto Fernandez Betancourt (Betabcourt) will be named Director of DIGEPOL late Oct 67. Ortiz, on behalf of Fernandez, assured AMCLEVE/15 DIGEPOL employment at proper livable salary.

2. AMCLEVE/15 plans to fly to Caracas 23 Oct 67. Will stay temporarily at home of Ortiz. Station has asked AMCLEVE/15 to notify JMWAVE when he has a more permanent address, and we will advise Caracas. If station desires contact, person should identify self to AMCLEVE/15 as friend of Lawrence R. Derome.
