Summary of current events.

1. Following is for your background and for passing local authorities as you see fit:

2. Four-man military junta that revolted against Marxist regime of Salvador Allende on 11 September has gained full control of the country, with exception of pockets of resistance in capital. On 12 September there was fighting in downtown Santiago between troops and snipers holed up in government buildings and in industrial areas where Allende supporters were barricaded in factories. Sporadic firing and explosions were heard throughout day but strict curfew kept observers from witnessing action. Curfew extended to noon 13 September.

3. Authorities estimated death toll to be over 250 military.
