One Roman Fedorovich Dedkov, born 28 March 1927 in Ariansk, USSR, and his wife Vilasa Nikolayevna Dedkov, born 31 December 1930 in Minsk, USSR, applied-for were listed as applicants for entry visas to India sometime prior to April 1962. Dedkov, who was issued Soviet passport number 207451 on 17 September 1960, was scheduled to arrive in New Delhi for two years as an English language teacher at the Soviet Embassy. It is not known whether or not Dedkov actually arrived in New Delhi.
