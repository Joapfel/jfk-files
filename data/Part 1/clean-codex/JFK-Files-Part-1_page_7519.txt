Eugard ISMCS, Viola R. LEACS.

Born: 10 July 1910, NYC. American.

Height: 5'9". Build: medium. Hair: black, wavy, brushed back. Eyes: brown. Complexion: fair.

Passports in early 1930s: U.S. passport No. 177026. U.S. passport No. 382046, issued in NYC 14 May 1931.

In addition to Agnes SMEDLEY and Frank CLAUS, ISMACS is known to have been in contact with the following while in China:

Williams (FILLIAMS) DURDIN. For a time during his stay in China, ISMACS is reported to have lived with DURDIN, then correspondent of the New York Times and at one time managing editor of the China Press.

JOHN M. MURRAY, Shanghai representative of the Pacific News Agency in Vancouver (Vam-conver). MURRAY is also known to have been in contact with E.E. KLECH, Agnes SMEDLEY, and Irene UNTERREITER or UNTERREITER and one F.I. ROVER during his stay in China.
