References: A. MEXI 2528 (IN 82883). B. MEXI 2393 (IN 73062). C. GUAT 4537 (IN 70216). D. GUAT 4499 (IN 61938).

From DAINOLD.

1. We are still most desirous of exploiting RICO GALAN tape. Despite MEXI PESWING objections to surfacing in Mexico, we believe that this is golden opportunity to start burning RICO and therefore urge GUAT station to make strong approach to GUAT government to surface locally. Perhaps ESPILL ONE or even PERALTA if necessary would be better channel for this than MINCOV SERRANO. Since GUATs raised subject of tape during recent ministers meetings, they presumably would be amenable now to local surfacing.

2. Once surfacing accomplished GUAT, we will be in position to effectively exploit not only tape itself but also other known info on RICO GALAX activities throughout hemisphere on behalf CASTRO regime.

End of message.

Coordinating officer. Assisting officer. Authorizing officer.
