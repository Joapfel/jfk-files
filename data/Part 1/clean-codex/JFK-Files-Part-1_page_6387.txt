Central Intelligence Agency. Information Report.

Report No. 00-B-30680. Country: USSR/Germany. Subject: Biographical Data—Eugen Diskowski. Date distributed: 5 April 1951. No. of pages: 2. Place acquired: Germany. Date acquired by source: up to February 1951. Date of information: up to February 1951.

This is unevaluated information.

Source: Foreign representative of a U.S. resettlement agency interested in bringing the subject of this report to the U.S. The following information was forwarded to the headquarters of that agency to determine placement possibilities in the U.S. To date, no decision has been reached.

Name: Eugen Diskowski.

Date and place of birth: 23 July 1891 at Kharkow (Kharkov), Ukraine.

Present address: Munich (Nuenchen) 8, Kirchenstr. 13/0, U.S. Zone of Germany.

Nationality: Ukrainian.

Religion: Orthodox.

Marital status: Single.

Education: Graduated in 1909 from secondary school at Kharkov (Ukraine), Russia. Attended the Imperial University at Odessa, Russia, from 1909 to 1913; received law diploma. Attended Military Law Academy at Petersburg, Russia, until 1917; received diploma in military law. Attended college at Kiev from 1925 to 1929; received diploma in biology.

Experience: Was lawyer on legal board in Odessa, Russia, from 1913 to 1914. Served as officer in army in Russia from 1915 to 1918. Taught public school from 1924 until 1929 in Kiev; taught biology at college at Mirotsk, USSR, from 1930 to 1934; Professor of Biology (Biochemistry) at Pedagogical College at Gluchow, USSR, from 1935 to 1936; Biochemist at TBC Institute at Kiev from 1936 to 1938; Biochemist at Veterinary Institute, Bacteriological Laboratory at Kiev from 1938 to 1943; Chemist (forced labor) at German sugar plant in Regensburg, Germany, from 1944 to 1945; DP in Germany from 1945 to 1948; self-employed in Munich, Germany, from 1948 to 1950.
