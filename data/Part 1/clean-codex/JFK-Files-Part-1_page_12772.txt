Reentry permits were issued for subjects on 21 April 1972, and will have been extended valid to 20 April 1974. The permit may be used for unlimited entries into the United States during validity.

Validity of permit may be extended for a maximum period of twelve (12) months only. Extension of validity of permit must be made on the attached “Application for Extension of Permit to Enter the United States” (I&NS Form I-145). In certain foreign areas, U.S. consuls and U.S. immigration officers are authorized to extend the validity of a permit. Where such delay or cover considerations warrant, the permit will be forwarded to Alien Affairs Staff in order to have the validity date extended through cleared liaison with Immigration and Naturalization.
