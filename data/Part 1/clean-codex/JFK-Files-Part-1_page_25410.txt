28 April — Tony Lohman says that McDonald will come to D.C. if the Agency has some real and honest problems to solve. He does not have the time to come to Washington for a general discussion on the subject.

Col. West has convinced one part of TSD, and several people are interested in sitting in, but the interest is not sufficient to justify bringing McDonald (Vice Donald) to Washington. Col. West approached his D&E people and was told that Joe Tercek had asked them some time ago and had already arranged a meeting.

4/29/69. Area 213-393-3564. Talked to Tony Arnold (Abnald): “We need this one out.” Tercek will make his own arrangement to bring or not to bring McDonald to D.C.; too many indications after the passage.
