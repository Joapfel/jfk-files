1 May 1963

To: Coordination Outline — Donald E. Thomas, Bell Labs

From: New York Field Office

Following is the coordination outline for D. E. Thomas. Dr. W. O. Baker requested that Thomas be given a defensive briefing by us before going behind the Iron Curtain. As you are already aware, Baker is Vice President at Bell Labs and a member of the President's Scientific Advisory Council and President's Intelligence Board.

1. Name: Donald Edward Thomas.
2. Date and place of birth: 18 May 1907, Denver (Touchup), Pella.
3. Address: 48 Fairview Avenue, Madison, New Jersey.
4. Place of employment and position: Bell Labs, Murray Hill, New Jersey; electronics engineer.
5. Previous: Subject leaves the United States 3 May for Moscow to attend the Popov Society meeting, 5–14 May. Following the Moscow meeting, he will visit laboratories in Western Europe for approximately one week.
6. Mission and reason for trip: Subject was invited to give a paper at the Popov meeting by the IBM Academy of Sciences via X.E.S.E. (International Electronics and Electrical Engineers).
7. Security status: We hold favorable HAG completion on subject dated 1 January '99.
8. Previous intelligence activities: Subject was contacted by GCS in 1958–59 concerning information on Soviet work in his field, which he acquired from contacts with Soviet scientific counterparts attending an international meeting in the United States.
9. Associates or close friends in denied areas? Yes.
10. Contact on previous trips to denied areas? No.
11. Analysis of subject's competence: Pella State College, B.A., B.S., 1949; Columbia University, M.A., 1952; U.S. Army Signal Corps, 1949–54; USEW, 1944–45, on radio countermeasures, 1944–45; retired lieutenant colonel, USEW, U.S. Airborne civilian personnel on Electronic Countermeasures MAD Board, Department of Defense, 1948–51.
12. Status of personal assessment: During our limited relationship with subject, he has given every indication of being disorient, securityensems, cooperative, will activated, etc. He is well regarded in his particular field of competence.
13. Future of subject briefing proposed and requirements to be met? No. Subject will not be briefed on operations of any kind. No photography or insurmountable note-taking will be requested of him.
14. Objective and realities briefings: Subject will receive routine defensive and fraternal realities briefings.
15. Paying for trip? Subject's trip will be funded by his agency.
16. Briefing office and participants: Briefing will be conducted by 8/19 New York Office personnel.
17. Coordination outline: 8 May 1963.
