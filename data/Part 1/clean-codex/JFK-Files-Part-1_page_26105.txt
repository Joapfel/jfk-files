111509Z April 1968, CITE Caracas 9364 (Lyner acting).

Director information: Guatemala City, Mexico City.

Reference: Caracas 9356.

1. AMC LEVE-15 reports that “Monsignor from Mexico” arrived in Caracas about two weeks ago to do a study on extremists in Venezuela. He called at the Ministry of Interior to tell of his plans and was directed to NR CIVISTA-5. Monsignor told CIVISTA-5 he had been in Guatemala recently to conduct a similar study and had spoken to a number of guerrilla leaders there. Monsignor’s plans call for interviewing subversive leaders in Venezuela. He told CIVISTA-5 that his objectives were strictly scholarly, hence hoped CIVISTA would abandon any thoughts of tailing or arresting him.

2. Information addressees: Please advise whether Monsignor Illich has been in Guatemala in recent months.

11 April 1968, IN 89759.

12 April 1968.
