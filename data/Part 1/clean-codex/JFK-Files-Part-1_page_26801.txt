1. Following are additional topics taken up with LIRAMA by LIELEGANT and CURTIS on the night of 19 February.

LIRAMA volunteered that he had cancelled the PENEX contract which Pascual Gutierrez Roldan (former director of PEMEX) had signed with the Soviets. This contract was for equipment and technical assistance. LIRAMA said the turbo-drills which had already arrived—he thought three in number—would be the only part of the contract allowed.

3. LIELEGANT (LILLEGALANT) gave LIRAMA a brief memo on the Fondo de Cultura Economica and its director, Abelardo Orfila Reynal (ABALCO ORFILA REYNAL). This was discussed briefly by DAINULO and LIELEGANT on 2 February. The memo was prepared by Station and translated by LIENVOYS. LIRAMA said this was a “real coincidence,” since some six weeks ago he had asked Antonio Ortiz Mena (ANTONIO ORTIZ NENA), Ministry of Treasury, to get rid of Orfila Reynal (ORFILA RETNA) as director of FCE, “since Orfila is a Communist.”
