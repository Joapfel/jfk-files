References: A. UFGA-5641, 7 September 1962. B. WAVE-8248, 6 September 1962.

1. Request priority POA for DUNEY /PEREZ/ ALAMO, born 11 July 1937, Baire, Oriente, Cuba, son of EMELIO /PEREZ/ SUAREZ, born circa 1903 in Spain, and FELIBERTA /ALAMO/ FERNANDEZ, born circa 1911, Holguin, Oriente. Subject is married to CELESTE /MARTINEZ/ DE LA VEGA, born 1937, Santa Cruz del Sur, Camaguey. From 1956 to 1959, subject was second in command of 9th Column of Rebel Army in Camaguey Province, which was commanded by HUBER /MATOS/. In 1959 subject was Chief DIER, Camaguey Province, and in 1960–61, Chief of Inspectors, People’s Stores, Camaguey.
