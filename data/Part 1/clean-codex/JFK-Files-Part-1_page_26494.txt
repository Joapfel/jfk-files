Victor Rico Galán (RICO Galan)

Mexico

President Adolfo López Mateos (LOPEZ Matos) of Mexico has promised to grant a group of prominent Mexican leftists, the majority of them journalists, an interview relative to the case of Cuba on 20 April 1961. The group of leftists involved includes Alonso Aguilar Monteverde (ACUILAR Montoverde), Dr. Gonzalo Guzmán (GUZMAN), Jesús Silva Herzog (SILVA Hernandez), E. Guillermo Montaño Islas (MONTANO Islas), Janitzio Miguel (MIGUA), Elvira Vargas, Rodolfo Lantes (RodolFOLOANTES), Renato Leduc (RETATO Lado), Victor Rico Galán (NICO Calan), Carlos Fuentes (FUEZES), José Alvarado (JoS ALVARO), Fausto Castillo, Ricardo Zermeño (ZERAMA), and others.

TK-3/472/253, 21 April 1961, Mexico City
