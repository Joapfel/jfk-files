Text of January 9, 1955, cable.

Local units are advised that effective immediately Soviet defectors who escaped since January 1, 1945, may be considered eligible for complete program assistance. Units should complete registration of this group in each area and advise OFC of number registered. Every effort should be made to create maximum program impact on Soybeans in each area. It is realized, however, that extension of eligibility to this group may cause some retribution among other nationality groups and the agencies supporting them. It is pointed out, however, that the great majority of Soy defectors are eligible under provisions, paragraphs 6 and 7, of Provisional Guide §1. Moreover, units should take all feasible measures to minimize retributions by exercising utmost discretion in implementation of this instruction, e.g., by discussing it primarily only with agencies interested in this group.
