In 1964–65, had a series of exclusive interviews with Fidel Castro in summer 1964, commented upon in VALE 8640.

17 August 1964. Worked for New York Herald Tribune at that time. Denied Cuban visa in fall 1965. Allegedly wrote report on foreign procurement in U.S. of T-28 aircraft critical of WOFIRM and LHERGO in 1966. This document was offered for sale to a station contact by local hotel operator Bill Thompson in October 1966; no offer was made and matter dropped.
