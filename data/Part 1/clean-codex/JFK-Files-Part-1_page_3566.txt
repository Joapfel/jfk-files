15 October 1969.

I. COS met with AMBIDDY-1, night 13 October. He reported on developments of refs as follows: As AMBIDDY was preparing to leave for Salvador in July, he was visited by Salvador Humberto Rodezno (phonetic), who had a long shopping list of arms and ammunition. AMBIDDY explained that he could not get involved in any arms export matters, that he is well known as a PBRUMEN patriot and does not wish to change his image to that of an arms dealer or soldier of fortune. Rodezno said he was already in touch with an American in Miami who was going to furnish .30-06 ammunition at ten cents a round, ship it from Interarmco in Virginia to Canada for onward shipment to Salvador.
