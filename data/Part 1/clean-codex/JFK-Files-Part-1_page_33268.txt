Reuters story datelined Santiago in 17 October “Times” is headlined “Russia Responding to Chile Call to Free Prisoners.” Highlights as follows:

“The Soviet Union is preparing to release a limited number of top political prisoners in response to a challenge by the Chilean military junta,” informed sources said today.

“A Red Cross emissary was due to arrive here tonight from Geneva with a list of 100 Chilean prisoners the Russians want released in exchange for the freedom given to its own detainees, the sources said. There was no official confirmation or denial of the report from either government or Red Cross officials.”
