12 March 1968

1. Rodolfo Reyes Cártes informed Rowton on 8 March 1968 that, while in Havana, he discussed with his friend Sukendah Hanafi, former Indonesian Ambassador to Cuba, the possibility of collaboration with his Greek leftist businessman friend, both in international commercial deals and politically. According to Reyes, Hanafi expressed immediate interest, accepting Reyes’s word that he could place full trust in Rowton. Hanafi stated that he felt something could be worked out to their mutual advantage and that of both the Greek and Indonesian revolutions, since he himself had contact with the Indonesian liberation army.

2. Hanafi asked Reyes not to tell anyone about his meeting with Rowton upon his return to Mexico City, stating that he would never refer to Mexico City, where Reyes could make the introduction personally, but indicating that he might run into difficulty obtaining a visa. He added that, if Mexico City was not possible, he would travel to Paris, where he had never had visa trouble.

3. Reyes reported to Rowton on 11 March 1968 that he had talked to Hanafi by telephone and asked him to arrange a trip to Mexico City as soon as possible. Hanafi replied that his initial check had revealed that the Mexicans would insist on clearance with the Indonesian Embassy in Mexico City before issuing a visa. Hanafi added, however, that he was taking the matter up with Raúl Roa and might be able to arrange some sort of official travel to facilitate issuance of the visa. He promised to advise Reyes as soon as possible, since he was aware that Reyes had to leave for Chile by the end of March, as soon as his wife, Tatiana, obtained a Chilean visa.
