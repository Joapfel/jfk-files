1. References b., c. and d. contain Vienna's instructions to Salzburg regarding identifying Subject; our initial reply stating there was no information in VCOF files; and Vienna's permission to run unilateral police traces through GREYHOG-1, respectively.

2. GREYHOG-1's initial report, received on 31 October 1961, states that Seventeen PORILLIJI was born 2 February 1910 in Usterkassy/Riv, married, a teacher, and a Ukrainian citizen. He resided at the refugee camp in Siegenstein, Salzburg, from 19 July 1956 to 23 August 1956. Prior to 15 July 1950 he lived at the Helbrunn refugee camp in Salzburg. According to police records, Subject left Salzburg for the United States on 23 August 1950.

3. Additional information in the form of a photographic copy of the official police registration card and a negative of an identification photograph of Subject, both dated 7 November 1947, reveals that Seventeen PORILLIJI was born 2 February 1910 in Przegal, is a Polish citizen, married, a teacher, 176 cm tall, with gray eyes, blond hair, an oval face, and no prominent or identifying features.

The date on the registration card presumably indicates the date on which Subject was first officially registered by the Salzburg police.

4. We are returning to Vienna the photograph sent us under reference b. for their retention. We have also attached the identification negative noted in the preceding paragraph. Would Vienna please make prints for SR and Paris and attach them to their copies of this dispatch.

MAXWELL E. UTTERIDGE

Attachments: H/W.

Distribution: Vienna with photograph and negative; ES without attachment; SR with photograph; Paris with photograph; Germany without attachment; OOS.
