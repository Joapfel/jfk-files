Wicks, Alastair.

PRET 2818 (IN 31010). 30 September 1963.

Early August. Subject came from Salisbury with msfs for PUREN from Pierre Laureys and Jack Malloch (of Rhodesian Air Services). Laureys was arranging for Malloch to fly out arms and ammunition to Royalists in Yemen. Malloch was to get a contract to fly arms from Europe to Yemen also. PUREN was to proceed by plane via Salisbury to Yemen as official observer for South African Defense HQS.

AHS 1016. CS DB 312/641-64. 18 September 1964.

Alastair Wicks, former RAS official, returned to Salisbury to begin the recruitment of Southern Rhodesians for service as mercenaries with Tshombe's forces in the Congo. In a newspaper ad calling for recruits, interested parties were requested to call Salisbury 55715, which is the number listed for Wicks in the Southern Rhodesian telephone directory.

KINSHASA 0864 (IN 17389). 9 March 1967.

Hoare stated he was going to London about 12 March to check on the Yemen deal. Hoare had previously received a letter from Alastair Wicks in London offering him a job with good pay and conditions. He believes this job concerns Yemen.

AZPA 2201. 16 March 1967.

Attached a letter from Wicks to Hoare, indicating Wicks was unable to develop the proposition further. Hoare estimates that the Wicks proposition relates to recruitment of mercenaries for service in Yemen. His only basis for this is development reported in AZPA 2200.
