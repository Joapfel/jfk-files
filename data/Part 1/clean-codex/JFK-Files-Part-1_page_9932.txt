260100Z CITE MEXICO CITY 2605. Director info Guatemala City.

KADOV

Refs: A. Director 30629. B. Mexico City 2564 (1-399175).

1. Afternoon 25 August 67, LIREBEL-2 reported that meeting held on 23 August to discuss Carlos Jurado case and other items proved to be of little importance. Ernesto Capuano said an investigation had to be made, but he has not been able to locate Moises or Orozco Leal (Ozco Leal), the prime witness. In the meantime, Capuano will contact Carlos Navarrete to review how Jurado originally made contact.

2. Per suggestion Ref A that station should not feed info to LIREBEL-2 in order to protect Jurado, LIREBEL-2 was told only that case sounds interesting and station would like to be kept advised of developments. Station especially interested in Guatemalan Communist exile investigative techniques and capabilities.
