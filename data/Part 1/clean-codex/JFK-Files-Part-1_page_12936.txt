6 July 1967: The Justice Department requested that the Subject recontact Rosenthal for meetings for the purpose of arresting Rosenthal for possession of goods passed to him by the Subject.

The matter was handled amicably.

11 July 1967: The Subject was terminated by JMWAVE at headquarters instructions for loss of access (he resigned from his position as a military coordinator/member of RECE) and for security reasons (he was involved with Miami gambler Frank Rosenthal).

1 August 1967: The Subject informed KICOBALT-1 that he had offered his services to the Bolivian Consul in Miami as an interrogator/guerrilla fighter in Bolivia. He offered $800; headquarters was in no way involved. He proposed to organize a group of Cuban guerrilla training officers. He left the impression that he was departing soon.

10 August 1967: The Subject traveled to Caracas to seek employment through an old friend who resided there.

17 August 1967: JMWAVE advised that no contact be made with the Subject because he was mentioned as a CIA agent on radio and television at the LASSO conference and was also involved in shabby deals in Miami.

28 August 1967: The Subject returned to Miami to obtain a proper visa enabling him to work in Venezuela. He said he had obtained a job with DIGEPOL through Rafael Tremolst and Gustavo Ortiz Faez.

11 September 1967: The Subject’s contract was held in abeyance until he was in place. The Subject was to be recontacted by the Station when he returned to Caracas and started his new job.

28 October 1967: The Subject was picked up as a unilateral penetration of CIRASH and CIRASH-4.

1 November 1967: The Subject began working with DIGEPOL (DIEGPOL).
