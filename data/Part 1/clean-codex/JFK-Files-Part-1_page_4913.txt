Outgoing Message.

CIA. 230003Z.

To: Immediate Federal Bureau of Investigation, Intelligence Division (INTD/3).

Subject: Arrival in Puerto Rico of Cuban exile Gustavo Castillo. CIR-316/03983-76.

3. The following information acquired abroad is for your information and whatever action you deem appropriate. It is felt that the subject of this report may be involved in international terrorism. On 15 October 1976 Gustavo Castillo, DPDB 28, info sent to FBI, arrived Santo Domingo from Caracas on VIASA 970. As Castillo listed by local authorities as belonging to terrorist group of Orlando Bosch. He was not allowed enter Dominican Republic. Authorities planned to put Castillo on return flight to Caracas. But they acceded to his request to be put on 15 October Dominican Airlines flight LO3 to Puerto Rico because he is an American.
