Memorandum for: CI/0G/SOV/SAT

From: SR/2/CE/Rita L. Perkins

Subject: Outline Communication re PONORILIW Case

1. OFPA 55220, 4 November 1960. Request to MKTOPAZ to analyze letters.

2. EGTA 15911, 8 November 1960. MKTOPAZ reply that PONORILIW letter bore definite traces of S/W.

3. PARI 2339, 10 November 1960. PARI STA informed HQS of AEDOG/A/1 receipt of letters, relay to MKTOPAZ, latter's reply, and asked for guidance.

4. OUT 92770, 16 November 1960. INFO PARI 2339 relayed to FBI; long delay in informing FBI because cable came in on Thursday evening and was not given to c/o until 14 November. Dr. REED consulted 14 November regarding the wording “definite traces” and advised he could not elaborate until MKTOPAZ report came in.

5. OUT 92118, 14 November 1960, to PARI. Stated HQS opinion that prime concern was to determine if P'ONORILIW (PONORILIW) involved RIS and directed PARI to return letters to MKTOPAZ and that the latter attempt to develop the S/W.

6. OFPA 55369, 16 November 1960. PARI returned letters to MKTOPAZ per paragraph 5 above.

7. PARI 2426, 18 November 1960. Referred to item 6 above; directed MKTOPAZ to make forged copy of PONORILIW letter and then attempt to develop S/W.

8. FRAN 7364, 18 November 1960. MKTOPAZ reminded PARI that letters would be destroyed in development attempts. Holding action until PARI advises.

9. FRAN 7388, 19 November 1960. MKTOPAZ reports efforts to develop S/W failed.

10. EOTA 15915, 20 November 1960. MKTOPAZ returned letters to PARI; full report on examination will follow.

11. EOTA 15927, 22 November 1960. MKTOPAZ report in full. My copy does not indicate date arrived in HQS, but I believe approximately 1 December; at this time in telephone communication with Dr. REED, who advised S/W detection was made by a new sensitive method, that FBI laboratory was aware of this method, and he would be responsible for relaying this information to FBI.
