19 June 1968.

Contact Report.

1. Agent: LURING/4.

2. Date and site of meeting: 1300, 18/6/68; Diana Restaurant.

3. Date and site of next meeting: 1030, 20/6/68; Diana Restaurant.

4. Information/reports passed by agent: LURING/4 (LERING/4) reported to Rowton that she had received two distressing letters from LURING/3 complaining about the miserable conditions under which he is living—very meager food, unsanitary quarters, and the constant fear of physical violence. LURING/4 showed Rowton a paragraph of one of the letters in which LURING/3 asks her to appeal to the “société” to influence the judge by a gratificación of 100 quetzals so that he would dismiss the case when it finally came to trial early the following month. LURING/3 stated that the judge to be influenced was a certain Juan Sanchez, Official Tercero del Juzgado Cuarto de Primera Instancia. LURING/3 added that he felt there was no other solution, since it had become evident that the Mexican Embassy could do nothing to speed up the final release and that if Rowton could not intervene, anything could happen.

LURING/4 also reported that she had been asked by LURING/9 to see him the next day (19/6/68) at his office. She did not know the purpose of the invitation, but assumed LURING/9 wanted to be briefed on the latest developments in the LURING/3 case.

5. Information/guidance given to agent: Rowton sympathized with LURING/4 in her distress over the desperate situation being faced by LURING/3 but stressed that intervention on his part was not as easy as LURING/3 might think. He pointed out that since LURING/4 to date had not been able to make the BATES trip he could not ask his own colleagues in Europe to intervene through their Guatemalan government contacts and that therefore anything that he could do himself would not be effective. LURING/4 did not volunteer any indication of her intention regarding the trip, and Rowton felt he could not press her in view of her frame of mind at the time. He suggested that they meet again after LURING/4 talked with LURING/9 in the hope that the latter might make some important comments on the case.

W. Rowton
