28 Aug. 1950

BORIS

Mrs. Poland is going to get the 62 duplicates made just as fast as she can. She couldn't give a time limit on it this a.m.

Incidentally, regarding the 68 photos for which you got the agencies' bill of about $150 this morning, she says agencies like those often sell you photos for a dollar or two for your own private use, but that the usual fee for use for publication is 66¢ or more. So you'd better make sure, when on the point of publishing, whether you've paid for the publishing right.

The photos were in doubt about one of CIA's.

VSS

Paul Breitweiser (Breitsweiser) is charging the $150-odd temporarily against SCHOUSS.
