Country: Peru/USSR

Date of information: 12 May 1969.

Subject: Comments by Violeta (Vigleta) Carrero Hoie de Valcarcel, Novosti (Kovosti) representative, regarding the visit to Peru of Alexei Bogdanov, Soviet geologist.

Acquired: Peru, Lima (13 May 1969). Field No. KFL-4959.

Source: A member of the pro-Soviet Communist Party of Peru.

Source has been reporting for more than one year and his information at times has been confirmed by other sources.

Violeta (Vigleta) Carrero Hoie de Valcarcel, Novosti (Kovosti) representative in Peru and long-time member of the pro-Soviet Communist Party of Peru (FCP/Soviet), said that Alexei Bogdanov, Soviet geologist who arrived in Lima (JLYA) on 7 May, is maintaining close contacts with the Ministry of Mines and Energy.
