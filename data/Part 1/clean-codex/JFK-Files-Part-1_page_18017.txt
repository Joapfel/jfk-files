A. Processing of Information

No changes have been made in the manner of processing LIFAT material.

B. Exploitation of Leads

LIFAT continues to be utilized as a protective security measure in connection with the dismissal of LIESSTOCK-2.

LIVERET provided several useful leads for the Case Officer.

LITVUS provided information enabling the Station to identify a safehouse presently used by the Polish IS.

LITERODE continues to be a major source of information regarding exile activities.

LEADERICK material provided information on Soviet communications which was forwarded to HQS for KUTUBU D processing.
