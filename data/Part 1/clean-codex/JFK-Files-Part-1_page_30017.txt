Affidavit Striking Against the Federal Government.

Dept. or State: G.J. Bureau or Office: 505. Place of employment: Washington, D.C.

I, David Edward Murphy, do hereby swear (or affirm) that I am not engaged in any strike against the Government of the United States and that I will not so engage while an employee of the Government of the United States; that I am not a member of an organization of Government employees that makes the right to strike against the Government of the United States, and that I will not while a Government employee become a member of such an organization.

Daniel E. Murphy, signature of employee or assistant.

Subscribed and sworn to before me this seventh day of April, 1963, at Washington, State of Washington, by this signatory.

J. C. Credaich
