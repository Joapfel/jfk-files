Cable Secretariat Dissemination. Classified message.

052039Z September 1969. Cite: WH/Miami 5099.

Caracas, information Director. AKULE, CIGOOBER, AMCHALK.

Reference: HVCA-16655.

1. FYI, CIGOOBER-1 told AMCHALK-5 that he planned travel to Venezuela 8 September in connection with lawsuit filed against him by former partner in automobile business. CIGOOBER-1 said he was seeking a $25,000 judgment and his lawyers had requested his presence in Caracas. Indicated would stay in Caracas in excess of 30 days. AMCHALK-5 believes CIGOOBER-1 is in financial straits and, although he can offer nothing specific, he feels CIGOOBER-1 is not being entirely truthful about the purpose of his trip. AMCHALK-5 plans to see CIGOOBER-1 again prior to departure and will advise if anything of interest is reported.
