231432Z GITECONTACTS/MIAMI 1714

Contracts/Washington

Attention: John McConnell

Subject: Alleged assassination attempt

Re: A. Miami 1710

A. McConnell/Gleichhauf telcons of 21 February and 23 February 1967

Mitchell, per Bell, called Gonzales (Gontzales) yesterday to advise that he had received two phone calls from Gray expressing concern over the fact that no one had contacted him for details concerning the alleged assassination attempt referred to in P-47-54, Miami 1710. Gray stated that unless we heard from somebody immediately, “I cannot take his information to the Governor of California on the morning of 23 February 1967.” We currently have an address for Gray: 128 Northern Oakhurst (Oakhubst) Drive, Beverly Hills, telephone 374-4922.
