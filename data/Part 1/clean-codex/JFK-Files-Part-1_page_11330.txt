19 June 1964.

LICOZE case reviewed thoroughly by SHRYOCK, who handled in Mexi. SHRYOCK comments follow: LICOZE was difficult to handle because while truthful in answer to any specific question he seldom elaborated, seldom volunteered information, and generally was reluctant to furnish information which did not concern people whom he considered compatriots.

2. Review established that LICOZE, at the time when he saw FERNANDO AVILA at Habana airport, sent message through LITAMIL-9 to Ambassador ANTONIO PORTUONDO that AVILA was an American agent. This information came from LITAMIL-9, who is considered completely reliable. Granting memory lapse on part of LICOZE as a result of passage of time and psychiatric difficulties, it still is difficult to believe that he did not recall above when discussing AVILA case. Do not repeat, not repeat, recommend STOCKWOOD mentioning above. Continued.
