Classified message. Date: 5/21 May 65. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: FE 80. Info: C1/OPS, WH 7, S/C 2, CI/IC 2. Routing. June 1, 0022 62. Routine. IN 44048.

SAIG INFO DIR DJAX CITE NEXI TRO

Refs: A SAIG 9766 (“4375-1”); B MEXI 0526 (“425499”).

1. LIELEGANT unable to contact ALBARRAN prior to departure. ALBARRAN said to have left Mexico about 20 minutes ago.

2. LIELEGANT does not know MEXICAN personally; station has nothing to add to Ref B.

3. EXCELSIOR of 24 May contained announcement that ALBARRAN was being sent to Indonesia as ambassador.

End of message.

C/S comment: *Action unit determined as through indicator KOBIRD used. **Queried if LIELEGANT ever got to ALBARRAN.
