10 July 1975

Memorandum for: Director of Personnel

Subject: Request for Voluntary Retirement—Mr. David E. Murphy

1. This memorandum submits a recommendation for your approval in paragraph 3.

2. The employee named above has applied for voluntary retirement under the provisions of Headquarters Regulation 20-50H.

Grade: GS-18. Career Service: Operations. Office/Division: Office of the DCI. Date requested for retirement: 18 July 1975. Years of creditable service: 32. Years of Agency service: 27. Years of qualifying service: 16. Position: National Intelligence Officer.

3. The applicant's Career Service recommended that the request be approved.

Donald Cansu  
Chief, Retirement Affairs Division

4. The recommendation contained in paragraph 3 is approved.

S. Burrows
