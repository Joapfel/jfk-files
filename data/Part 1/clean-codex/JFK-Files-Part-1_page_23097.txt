Dispatch.

Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Chief, WH/COG and Chief of Station, JM'AVE U.F. Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Project. Referencier. See paragraph two.

Reference: HMMA-31953.

1. Forwarded under separate cover attachment are eleven letters sent or received by Dr. Jose Angel Bustamante, Director of the Institute of Neuropsychology and Psychology in Havana, via his office, which acts as a postman receiving and mailing these letters in Mexico City. We would like to make the following observations in connection with these letters:

A. The Cubans are planning to hold a symposium in psychology in Havana from 24 to 30 November 1967. Two of Bustamante's letters include invitations to American psychiatrists to attend the symposium.

B. Dr. Bustamante is planning to attend the XI Interamerican Congress of Psychology to be held in Mexico City from 17 to 22 December 1967.

C. Dr. E. Roy John from the New York Medical College spent a month at the Institute directed by Dr. Bustamante in Havana.

Continued.
