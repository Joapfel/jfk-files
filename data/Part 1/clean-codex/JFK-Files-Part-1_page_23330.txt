Classified message.

RLG/BP/J 481. Date: 14 March 57. To: Director. From: San Jose. Action: WH.5. Info: CI, CI/OPS, FI, FI/OPS, FI/RI 2, S/C 2.

Routing. From. Action. Info.

Received Cable Section. IN 18323.

To: HAVA. Reference: HAVA 833 (IN 17296); SANJ 2474 (IN 11647). Info: DIR.

Routine processing. Cite: SANJ 2495.

1. Visa file on VAIVADA's wife not yet received here although local consul has made two official requests of Consulate HAVA. VAIVADA does not want leave without her and wants her to acquire citizenship for own protection in future. Station unable to pressure local consulate until file received here.

2. View above not feasible for VAIVADA leave on 18 March. Station hopes wife's visa case will be settled by 24 March.

End of message.

It is forbidden to make a copy of this message.
