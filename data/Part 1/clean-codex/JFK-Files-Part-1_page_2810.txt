Nosenko could not initially recall how long he supervised service attaché operations; he later said “several months” and finally “five or six months.” Neither could Nosenko recall what other pressing duty or duties caused him to be relieved of this function. He said this resulted from Kovshuk’s proposal that supervision of the service attachés be assumed by Aleshin, who was Deputy Chief of the Second Directorate’s American Department and coordinator for Second Directorate operations against military intelligence officers of all nationalities.

Supervisor of Service Attaché Operations: Summary and Conclusion

Because of the tardiness of Nosenko’s claim to have been responsible for operations against service attachés and because he was not able, when challenged, to substantiate his claims by providing any details of KGB activities against these targets, it is concluded that this aspect of his claimed KGB service is a fiction.
