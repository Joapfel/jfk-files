Following are station traces: Jesus (Jimenez) Escobar (201-749244), Navy captain of “Sierra Maestra”; studied at maritime school near Havana; from communist family (Revolution newspaper, 4 January 62). Graduated Mariel Naval Academy as navigator for Cuban Merchant Marine; circa 1960 deck officer for Nambisas; 1961 sent to Soviet bloc to assume captaincy of ship “Sierra Maestra”; incompetent and thus under orders of German officers on first trips of “Sierra Maestra”; married German girl, Monika, during trip to Germany; mid-62 had accident with ship and lost job of captain; completely integrated with regime (AMOT-54, 13 October 63). Travelled Mexico-Havana 12 July 65 (Cubana Airlines manifest). Commercial policy specialist, MINCEX, delegate GATI meeting Uruguay January 67 (Sensitive Report, January 67 and MONTHVIDEO-2778, 16 January 67). Born 16 September 65, Nambisas R.E.P. stationed Havana as of early 67; formerly Nanbisas rep Matterbal; X.O.s around shipping lines as communist secret.
