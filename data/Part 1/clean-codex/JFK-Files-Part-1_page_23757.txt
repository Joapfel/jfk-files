FURNACE  
RIDING 70281

All communist prisoners held liable if Gutierrez gets death sentence.

17 February, 8:39 a.m., 1963.

3. Amateur analysts among local exiles knowingly point out expensive late-model arms carried by Gutierrez and apparent “green light” from U.S. and Dominican Republic authorities as evidence he was sponsored by KUBARK, but leftist broadcaster Max Lesnick refuted this possibility on Radio Replica (VMIE) 28 January.

4. Most talk of Gutierrez now limited to groups and individuals having direct interest or involvement. Some disinterested parties opine he may be dead already. Others losing interest because recent reports lack detail. Three captains of vessels expressed sorrow for Gutierrez’s plight but relief that he is no longer in a position to endanger KUBARK maritime operations.

5. Gutierrez issue diminishing in importance on local scene. There are presently no reported reactions on arrest from Cuba via telephone calls to exiles in this area; thus it is difficult to judge what impact this arrest has had on operations climate in Cuba.

6. PLA restricts press coverage on Gutierrez arrest to straight news reporting for next few days, i.e., will publicize Dominican Republic denial of their involvement in this operation, etc.

28 January confusion still requires views on nearly all aspects of Gutierrez Menoyo case.
