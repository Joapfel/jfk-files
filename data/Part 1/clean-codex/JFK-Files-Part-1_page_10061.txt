Classified message.

28 March 1954.

SECRET 250132. Air Cite VEX: 5382 (Easily Acting). G-101663.

Following from LIT-TIL-7, 25 March: Teresa Proenza visited the Peruvian (PERUEN) Embassy 24 March with sister Cachita. Said she was returning to Habana soon on an unspecified date. Her passport marked “PARA DETENTRERSE” by su cargo, but L-7 tells of cable preceding Proenza’s arrival: “TERESAR A LIQUIDAR SUS ASINTOS. TENIAMOS INFORMADO.” Embassy staff warned not to tell Proenza of the mention of the cable, which indicates something done behind her back despite what she may have been told. P said she now prefers work inside Peru rather than in an EMS since she can do more good there. L-7 believes that even Habana is not sure what to do with Proenza.
