Second course in 1947. In 1951 the Ministry of Navy had given me also the rank of junior lieutenant when I was accepted in the Navy intelligence. In September-October 1952 I received a rank of lieutenant.

In Sovietsk the work was not interesting and for me there was nothing to do. Besides this, the climate was not good for my health and I decided to change the job. With this purpose, before New Year at the end of 1952, I took a leave and went to Moscow. January 1 I was with my parents at the evening party at the cottage of General MGB Kobulov, whom I did not know before, but I knew his son-in-law, Vahrushev Vasili—a former student and my friend. I told him about my job and that now I was thinking about changing the job. Kobulov was speaking with me on this theme and proposed that I work with his help in MGB, but nothing more definite was said about my work. This month I reported to the head of the personnel department of the Navy intelligence, Kaloshin, about my decision and that I would be working in MGB.

In the end of January I went again to the tuberculosis sanatorium, where I was in 1952. In the days of Stalin’s funeral I came to Moscow and visited the ministry where my father was working. There I saw General Kobulov, who had come to my father, and he said that he would settle my question concerning my job. After several days, in the middle of March, I received a telephone call from MVD.
