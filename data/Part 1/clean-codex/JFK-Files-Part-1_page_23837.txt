Classified message. Total copies: 23. Person/unit notified. From London. Action: OS 2. Info: VR. FILE EE Y WEB, CHA, CL, AF G, S/OPS.

271700Z. DIR INFO FRAN GMNY. CITE LOND 7909. FJDUST LCFLUTTER KUDESK STEBONY. Ref: DIR 13891.

27 May 65. IN 77375.

Due his nationality, we do not need SMOOTHERS okay to FLUTTER STEBONY/1. However, our general policy is not to FLUTTER here anyone who likely react and raise fuss causing serious political embarrassment for us or HMG.

Because of points in his history and personality, we have reason to doubt STEBONY/1 will cotton to FLUTTER, and prefer IFDEFER decision until TIFFANY briefed and has talked to STEBONY/1. May then decide, if FLUTTER in order, conduct in Europe while he en route Algiers.
