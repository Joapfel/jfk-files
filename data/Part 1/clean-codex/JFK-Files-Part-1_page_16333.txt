Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable. Priority.

Country: Republic of the Congo. Date of information: 17–20 August 1968. Report No. TDCS 00-315/00489-6A. Date distributed: 21 August 1968.

Select:
1. Expected arrival of 40–60 mercenaries and the projected organization of the mercenary striking force.
2. Plan for attack on Albertville and subsequent objectives.
3. Cooperation from Belgian military advisors to ARC.

Place and date acquired: Republic of the Congo, Leopoldville (20 August 1968). Source and appraisal: Western businessman (a); from Gratanova mercenary (c), from Hoare. Appraisal: 2 (that Hoare's statements accurately reflect his views and the facts as he sees them).

1. On 20 August '64, mercenary commander Michael Hoare said privately that Mrs. Jeremiah Puren, wife of the mercenary air force commander, had arrived in Johannesburg from Leopoldville on 17 August with the Congolese government funds needed to proceed with mercenary recruiting. Although Hoare has had no news of her movements since then, he confidently expects the first draft of mercenaries, between 40 and 60 of them, accompanied by his second-in-command Alastair Wiggs, to arrive at Kamina Base from Johannesburg aboard a Rhodesian Air Services flight on 21 August.
