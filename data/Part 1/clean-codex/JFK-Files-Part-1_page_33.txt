Miscellaneous notes on Jews married to foreigners.

Adolf Zaslavsky (ZASLAVSKY) has a husband; has hired Harold Berman (BERMAN) to plead the case and get him out of the USSR. (2 October 1962)

Yelena Knyazkina (KNYAZKINA), Leonard Kirsch's (KIRSCH'S) wife, has arrived in the USA. Joseph of FOND EM, resident of former British Cameroons (BritCamers).

Joseph of FOND EM, resident of former British Cameroons (Brit Camersons), came to the USSR on 24 December 1960. He has been visiting the American Embassy and is continuing his studies in the U.S. Married to a [unclear] girl. Claims she is ostracized by family and friends. She, he, and their baby son have to scrounge housing in Moscow by staying in people's extra rooms at 30–40 rubles per month, which is illegal. Claims his plight is shared by a number of other African students married to [unclear] girls. Authorization is a compound problem because wives are not allowed to leave the country. Embassy notes FOND EM as questionable and opportunistic. American Embassy Moscow Despatch A-308, 31 August 1962.
