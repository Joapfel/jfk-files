6. Kind and number of additional measures which we would seek in OAS would vary with choice between alternatives. For example, our tentative thinking is that the minimum OAS package acceptable to the US under Alternative A would be: authorization to use force; condemnation of the Castro regime; and reaffirmation of invigorated anti-subversive measures. Under Alternative B, the minimum package now estimated to be: defensive system with search and seizure within national territory; condemnation; reaffirmation of anti-subversive measures; and obligatory break in air and surface communications between OAS countries and Cuba.

7. We consider the attitude of Mexico and Brazil critical. An OAS vote authorizing use of force in which one or the other did not join would be politically undesirable. END FYI.
