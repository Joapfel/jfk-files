9. L-9 said that he had security duty all day Saturday, 31 May 63. L-9 said that Jose LLAGOSTERA is taking the day off and will not return until about 28:00 this evening. He told L-9 to report any similar information, particularly regarding Armando SARDINAS and others who live at the Embassy.

10a. L-9 finally identified Jose FERNANDEZ ROA as the person who, working with Buenaventura REYES Acosta and Ramon S.H.ORES, reported this previously. L-9 said he asked FERNANDEZ if he was related to the Foreign Minister, and FERNANDEZ did not say yes or no. L-9 believes that he might be because he does not seem very sharp and yet is associating with BIRNEMANS and BUENAVENTURA. FERNANDEZ told L-7, who asked him about his relationship with ROA, that if he was related to ROA, it was a distant relationship.

11. Coty BOLORZANO, sister-in-law of General Lázaro (Lagaro) CARDENAS, is scheduled to leave for Habana on 13 May 63.

12. L-9 said he attended a meeting at the Academia Mexicana de Derecho (Eresocho) Internacional on 8 May 63, and the Mexican Ambassador to Israel spoke regarding non-intervention and autodeterminación. L-9 said the speech was typical of Mexican government policy regarding Cuba.

13. L-9 said that he saw Rafael PEDROSA Perez and Antonio ARRUZA Mon at the Embassy on 9 May 63. They were in session with Ramon SINORAS. PEDROSA, the mulatto type, told L-9 at the inaugural session of the Microondas Seminar that “it was necessary for him to make a lot of contacts in Mexico.” He also told L-9 that he was in contact with Raul ROA, saying, “Estoy en contact con ROA.” L-9 said that on 9 May at the Embassy, PEDROSA was sick—pale green, bad breath, etc. L told L-9 to make contact with PEDROSA and ARRUZA if possible and size them up. He said it would be difficult because he had no reason to see them. He merely attended the inaugural session of the Seminar. However, L-9 did identify both men from LIERODE’s take, which I showed him on this date.
