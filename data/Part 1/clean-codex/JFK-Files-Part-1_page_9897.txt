Dispatch. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. From: Chief of Station, JNWAVE. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. V. 24-10-85 1:50 AM. WETON 3/20/67 = 193911. Subject: PBRUMEN/LIRING-3/Rodolfo REYES-Cortes, Carmen MOLINA de CARRASCO, and Carmen LOZADA de Ramirez. Action required—references: Addressees' comments (see paragraph 5). Mexico City 3351.

1. Forwarded attached are three reports dated 23, 28, and 30 October written by Wallace B. ROWTON on his contacts with Rodolfo REYES-Cortes, Carmen MOLINA de CARRASCO, and Carmen LOZADA de Ramirez. ROWTON first met these three persons when he accompanied LIRING-4 to a party given by Carmen MOLINA on 27 October, passing himself as a wealthy Greek client of LIRING-3 who had called at the latter's house to pay for a painting he had ordered but had been invited by LIRING-4 to come to the party. As previously agreed with the Station, ROWTON directed his main attention to REYES. The latter has resided in Havana since 1960 when he founded the Cuban Ballet Folklorico. Until recently REYES has received the monthly salary of one thousand pesos from the Cuban government, and, according to LIRING-3, is well acquainted with political and government personalities in Cuba. As pointed out in Reference, ROWTON found REYES to be apolitical, a superficial sympathizer of the Cuban revolution, and a great admirer of American choreography (he told ROWTON he would very much like to go to New York to see some of the shows there.) REYES complained about the relatively low salary he is currently receiving from the Mexico City Belles Artes for organizing a Cuban Ballet Folklorico show to be held in Mexico City next December. He readily agreed to ROWTON's offer.

Attachments: 1 photo; 3 contact reports.

Distribution: O&I—CWHD, with one copy of each attachment; JNWAVE, with one copy of each attachment.
