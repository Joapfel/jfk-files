Affidavit Striking Against the Federal Government.

Central Intelligence Group (Dept. or Estab.): Washington, D.C. (Place of Employment).

I, William P. Durke, Jr., do hereby swear (or affirm) that I am not engaged in any strike against the Government of the United States and that I will not so engage while an employee of the Government of the United States; that I am not a member of an organization of government employees that asserts the right to strike against the Government of the United States, and that I will not while a government employee become a member of such an organization.

(Signature of employee or appointee)

Subscribed and sworn to before me this 11th day of September 19, 1967, at Washington, State of D.C.

Chapter 145, Title II, Sec. 206. Act of June 26, 1963.
