RYPAT. Disp. No. UFAA-4533. File No. 200-11-11/7, 201-285923. Date: 8 November 1972. Indexing: No. Microfilm: No. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. From: Chief of Station, WH/Miami. Subject: CRYBAT TYPIC PLMHABYSS/AMDESK-1.

I. On 2 October 1972, LNAGON/Miami registered AMDESK-1 (A-1) (201-285923) (Identity-A) in the narcotics source registry as its informant. Subsequent investigation by Station revealed that A-1 has been of interest to LNERGO since 1958, when Station turned him over to them. When this fact was called to LNAGON's attention, the latter commented that not only did they realize A-1 was an LNERGO contact, but they also were aware that A-1 was recently in touch with EXCROWN.

2. LNAGON based this assumption on information which they claim A-1 provided them, namely: A-1 advised LNAGON he was invited by EXCROWN, at the latter's expense, to visit Washington about 10–11 October 1972 to spend a few days there. According to LNAGON, A-1 told them he accompanied a high-ranking Venezuelan government official (unidentified) to Washington. (Comment: If true, the official possibly could have been Orlando Garcia Vasquez.) While in Washington, A-1 claims he saw the chief of the Latin American desk of EXCROWN (Identity-F). A-1 is positive of Identity-B's last name because he said his last name is the same as his. A-1 mentioned he is scheduled to return to Washington to see Identity-E again in January 1973.
