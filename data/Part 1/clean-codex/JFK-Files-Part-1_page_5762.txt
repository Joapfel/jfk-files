Also present were deputies to the National People's Congress, members of the Kiangsi provincial and Xanking city committees of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, and leading members of the provincial and municipal committees of the Revolutionary Committee of the Kuomintang: Wu I-fang, Liu Kun-chun, Chi Yu-ting, Liu Shu-hsueh, Chang Ching-lin, Chang Kuang-chung, Jen Chung-kao, Chen Ho-chin, Pan Tse-chung, Ting Kuang-hsueh, Chan Chung-fan, Liao Yun-tse, Liao Yun-sheng, and Lai Ti-an, and 300 cadres and representatives of local people.

During Ho Hsiang-ning's illness, Vice-Chairman Soong Ching-lin, Premier Chou En-lai, and other leaders went to the hospital to see her.

Ho Hsiang-ning's remains were encoffined on September 3. The coffin was placed in Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall in Peking's Chunghwa Park. Party and state leaders paid last respects to her at the hospital. Also paying last respects to the deceased were members of the Central Committee of the Revolutionary Committee of the Kuomintang, leading members of departments concerned, and Ho Hsiang-ning's friends. Among them were Li Chen, Wang Shu-sheng, Su Yu, Fang I, Shen Kuang, Tseng Tso, Liu Hsin-chuan, Chang Hsiang-shueh, Chang Wen-chin, Yang Chi, Ting Esueh-sung, Lin Yun-chin, Wang Ting-kao, Liu Chung-jen, Tung Hsiao-feng, Li Chih-chung, Chou Jung-hsueh, Shen Chien, Pu Chieh and his wife, and Paul T.K.
