Subject: Delegates to Youth Festival in Berlin. Place acquired: Colombia, Bogotá. Evaluation: C-2. Source: local detective. Report No. TLS-1634. Date of information: August–September 1951. Date acquired: January 1952. Date of report: 29 January 1952.

1. Source reports that an informant in Paris has submitted information to the local police concerning delegates of certain other countries to the III Festival Mundial de la Juventud held in Berlin in August and September 1951. These names, together with the comments of the informant, are included in the following paragraphs.

2. The delegation of Guatemala consisted of the following:

Edelberto TORRES Rivas, Novena Ave. & No. 110.  
Octavio REYES Ortiz, 17 C. O. No. 78.  
Hugo FARRIOS Klee, 12 Calle Oriente No. 58.  
José PONZALEZ, 2 Ave. Sur No. 11.

3. The following is a list of the Venezuelan delegates:

Juvemal HERRERA, estudiante de derecho.  
Francisco MIERES, peligroso.  
Cordova ARMANDO, estudiante de derecho en Cuamand.  
Nicolas CURIEL Agosta, asesor de teatro.  
Juan Pedro ROJAS, pintor.  
Yolanda Steffens de ROJAS, casada con el anterior.

4. The Ecuadorian delegates are as follows:

Enrique GIL Gilbert, asesor.  
Enrique QUINTESBOA.  
Patricio CUEVAS, estudiante de medicina en Praga.

5. The delegation of Cuba consisted of the following:

Nicolas GUILLEN, poeta y asesor.  
Santiago ORAJALES, obrero.  
Antonio NUMEZ Jimenez, profesor de Pedagogía and “responsible for the delegation of Cuba.” He is peligroso, and when it was necessary to photograph him in Paris, he refused.

Francisco GARCIA Bonítez, asesor de Holguín.  
José CANETTI, asesor de Santiago de Cuba.

EPA # 51-58.

In addition to these four names were Henrty Pats ALVAREZ and ELISA CHAVEZ (Tob. 1351).
