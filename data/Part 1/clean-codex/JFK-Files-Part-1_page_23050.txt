Central Intelligence Agency. Information Report.

Report No. CS-311/07089-68. Date distributed: 8 August 1968. Country: Cuba. Date of information: 1967, except as indicated. Subject: (a) National Center for Scientific Research (CNIC); (b) National Research Institute. Place and date acquired: Spain, Madrid, 19 February 1968. Field report No. OSM-9009.

Source: A member of a group of Cuban émigrés who have been trained in the techniques of information collection and who have provided useful reports since early 1962, from CC-3524, Ramon Alberto Mendoza Gandarilla, a Cuban refugee and former resident of Havana City who left Cuba on 22 January 1968. Source studied medicine at Havana University until September 1966, when he completed his fifth year before abandoning his studies; from September 1967 to January 1968, he worked as a translator of medical texts at the National Information Center for Medical Licenses, 23 and N Streets, Vedado, Havana City. He visited twice the main building of the National Center for Scientific Research (CNIC), the second time in mid-July 1966. Paragraphs 8, 11 and 14 from Elizalde Maso Marcoleta, who had worked with Dr. Mora and was familiar with “all of the research projects of the CNIC.” Paragraph 9 from Carlos J. Finlay’s brother, Miguel Finlay, with whom source spoke in mid-January 1968. Paragraph 36 from Dr. Jesus Perea, professor of endocrinology, from whom source acquired the information in July 1966, and from Elizalde Maso Marcoleta. Source visited the Gastroenterology Institute in mid-August 1966 and Dr. Llanio accompanied him on a tour of the installations (Paragraph 40). Source visited the Psychology and Neurophysiology Institute in mid-May 1968 and Dr. Leonel Valles, who was employed at this institute, accompanied him on a tour of the installations. Paragraph 45, and most of paragraph 44, from Dr. Valles. Paragraph 47 from Dr. Cosme Ordonez, a former professor of source’s and Director of the Hygiene and Epidemiology Institute.
