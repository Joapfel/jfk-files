FUWomen PSYCH CASPECIAL

1. Chile's (CHIL'S) image as reflected in West German media comments has reached an all-time low, and even conservatively inclined outlets, by and large, no longer bother (DOTHER) to tell “the other side.” Eastern radio stations maintain relentless drumfire of anti-Chilean propaganda, and there is no apparent dearth of information to sustain a rather effective denigration campaign.

2. In spite of dismal auspices to turn the tide, unless Junta decides to show some decent regard for opinions of mankind, we can still promote fairer understanding of imperatives of Chilean situation to which Junta apparently feels impelled to respond. Toward that end, require some type of objective appraisal of recent developments and of predictable trends.
