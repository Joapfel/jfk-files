Classified message. Action: C/WH/C-G-5, red copy. Info: V.R. C/WH Ta, C/C2, C/FI. 2, C/SOD 3, DOL. MDF. File: MANA. Cite Managua 6344. Director, Panama City. Staff assist. Type A World.

8H48:317221

1. As addressees are aware, when AMWORLD was closed out two years ago, ANDIDY-I was authorized to hold a rough equivalent of weapons to arm one rifle company. Weapons stored at the Monkey Point CPS base, at El Bluff support base, and in Managua. About one year ago, Monkey Point base was closed and weapons there moved to El Bluff. Storage in sealed concrete vault under the floor of safehouse which is on uninhabited side of El Bluff next to airstrip built by AMWORLD. Strip is 1,800 by 39 feet with clay base mixed with natural rock.

2. Arms in Managua have been stored in a safehouse outside of city and consist of two wooden crates, each about 150 pounds and 37 by 25 by 14 inches. Approximate contents are:

25 pistols, .45 caliber.
5 rifles, AR-15.
3 SMG, M3.
