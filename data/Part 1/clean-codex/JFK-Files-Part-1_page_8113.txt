May also be identical with subject of same name. Patronymic unknown, who reported as member of Panamanian consular corps stationed Panama as of October 1973. FBI gives their subject's job at Panama City, 10 May 1973, and residence as Villa Espada 3565, Panama City. Masconana Correa's P.O. is Panama City.

1. April 1942; last known address changed to 8th and Avenida B.
