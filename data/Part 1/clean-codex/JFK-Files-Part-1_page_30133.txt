Section IV.

This section is intended as an aid in describing the individual. Your description is not favorable or unfavorable in itself but serves to show its bearing in relation to a particular job or assignment. The descriptive words are to be interpreted literally.

On the left-hand side of this page below are a series of statements that apply in some degree to each person. On the right-hand side of this page are four other categories of descriptions. The scale given here is divided into three special sections to allow you to make further distinctions if you so desire. Each of the statements on the left will check the category on the right which best tells how much the statement applies to the person you are rating. Placing an “X” in the “Not Observed” column means you have no opinion on whether a phrase applies to an individual. Placing an “I” in the “Does Not Apply” column means that you have the definite opinion that the description is not at all suited to the individual.

A. Able to set another’s point of view — Does Not Apply.  
B. Practical — Applies to a reasonable degree.  
C. A good reporter of events.  
D. Can make decisions on his own when need arises.  
E. Cautious in action.  
F. Has initiative.  
G. Unemotional.
