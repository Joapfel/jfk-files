18 September 1976

DO Base New York, info Director. Typic Amrose Domops.

Reference: DO Base New York 815S.

1. Station went through files of AMMUN-1 and AMDAUB-1 and had outside officers contact them to determine feasibility of ref proposal.

2. AMMUN-1 and Station feel that any publicity given him at this time would only be detrimental. He is happily settled and does not want his past brought to the attention of his neighbors or persons he works with.

3. AMDAUB-1, on the other hand, is settled but currently has financial problems due to inactivity construction business, family problems, etc., all of which are not conducive for any interview.

4. In light of the foregoing, we feel that the time is not propitious for any articles.

19 September 1976
