Dispatch. Dispatch symbol and No.: HIMMA-11731. To: Chief, WH Division. Marques P. No.: 201-128561. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Date: 10 December 1958. Subject: American Communists in Mexico, Maurice HALPERIN. Reference: “43-1.”

Action required: Document microfilm service, marked for indexing. No indexing required. Indexing can be judged by qualified H.O. desk only.

References.

1. Forwarded herewith to complete Headquarters and ODENVY records on subject are one photocopy each and film of the following letters addressed to him in Mexico City.

A. Letter of 25 August 1958 from Leonard B. BOUDIN of RABINOWITZ & BOUDIN, New York attorneys, cutting HALPERIN’s fee due for professional services to $300.00. (Comment: Professional services probably dealing with HALPERIN’s U.S. passport.)

B. Letter of 27 August 1958 from Paul BARAN, Stanford University, California, commenting on an economic study of Latin America which HALPERIN is preparing.

C. Letter of 5 August 1958 from Maurice H. SAVAL, insurance man in Boston, Massachusetts, who is apparently a long-lost friend of the subject’s. (No film.)

D. Letter of 23 August 1958 from Ted HALPERIN in Milton, Massachusetts.

E. Letter of 18 August 1958 from HALPERIN’s mother and father in Massachusetts.

2. Source is LIBIGHT/LIMUD. No local dissemination.

Willard C. CURTIS
