9 October 1959

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Alien Registration of AEASPIC

1. SR/DOE Case Officer Fred Kirkpatrick drove AEASPIC to 119 D Street N.E. for a 1000 appointment with Miss Byard in room 57 of Immigration and Naturalization for the purpose of alien registry. Full fingerprints and true biographical information were taken at this time. Certificate of Registry will be forwarded to agent by Immigration people in the name David Durvard.
