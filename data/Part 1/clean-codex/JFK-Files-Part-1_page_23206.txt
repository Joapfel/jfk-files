Classified message. 482/F/M/CG/. Date: 16 April 1958. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 5. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS 2, S/C 2. IN 20477.

To DIR INFO CITE MEXI 1446. Re: DIR 16715 (OUT 64381 9).

1. Mr. Joseph Ravotto, Deputy PAO, who knows Benjamin as well as anyone in the Embassy, states he is a sound, respected newspaper man with good Mexico City social and official contacts. Our files, ODENVY, and BIOG Section Embassy traces negative.

2. Station concurs journalistic cover.

3. If headquarters approves, Station will talk to Benjamin in order to furnish details regarding reimbursement, flexibility, durability, and other aspects of good cover. Would not mention any name or target.

4. If Benjamin under consideration, urge Vaivada be permitted to meet, evaluate, and agree over acceptable. Advise.

End of message.

USGS B&W C/S comment: Concerned Vaivada cover.
