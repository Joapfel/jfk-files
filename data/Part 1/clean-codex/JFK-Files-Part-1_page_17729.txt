2 June 1966

3.2 continued.

WO thinks she won't stay long with Sra. They expect more and more of her. They went to errands today and walked all the way. She (Sra.) won't pay for a cab. This time el Señor took them there, but after that and other times, never pays for a cab. A lot of walking, and WO's feet are swollen from so much walking. Then, WO is not badly off there. She is alone there (doesn't work with another person), not so much work...will see about this.

WO will phone WI on Saturday. WI only goes out in the mornings. And not always...Juanita sometimes goes for chicken and tortillas.

Read off at RT 5.0, 2100, 2 June 1966
