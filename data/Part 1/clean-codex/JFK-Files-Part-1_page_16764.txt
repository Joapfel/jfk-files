OPIM DIR INFO OPIM MEXI ROUTINE FRAN CITE PARI 7042

RYBAT

Ref DIR 25991

27 March 1963

IN95070

1. Having determined that Romero was staying with Lopez Mateos at the official guest residence, Palais d'Orsay, Wetherill tried to contact him by phone on the evening of 26 March. He talked to an unidentified member of the entourage who said Romero was not there but was returning shortly. Wetherill gave the name Wittenberger, friend of Señor Blanco, Tio, etc. He called back later and got the same man, who said he had given the Wittenberger name to Romero, who did not recognize it or know who Señor Blanco was. He would not put Romero on the phone without further explanation of what business Wittenberger had with Romero. In view of the increasingly awkward security situation, Wetherill broke off the conversation.

2. Subsequently delivered a personal note to Palais d'Orsay for Romero using the same recognition names and giving two phone numbers for contact, one embassy and one home. Have heard nothing.

3. MEXI: Please give us some further identification material we can use on the phone with anyone who answers the phone in the presidential suite. Was Romero actually given the name Wittenberger?

C/S comment: Lopez requested names of friends of COS MEXI in addressee cities who could contact his private secretary, Humberto Romero, at each place during the visit. Will use KUBARK commo channels.
