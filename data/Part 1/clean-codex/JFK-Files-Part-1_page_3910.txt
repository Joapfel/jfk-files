UFGA-21693/14. Att. No. 33 to UFGA-21693.

Subject: GOMEZ, Ezequiel, aka Kelo.

1. Source met subject about 1955 at the home of Agustin Aldama, located in Calle Tejar between San Lazaro and San Anastasio, in Lawton, Havana City. After they met they became close friends and were engaged together in revolutionary activities against the Batista regime. Subject fought in the Escambray Sierra and was there when Batista was overthrown in January 1959. At the time source met subject he was a labor leader of the Urban Buses (Routes 3 and 5). Source believes in early 1959 subject went back to his work at this bus line. In early 1960 subject was appointed to a position at Customs at the Jose Marti Airport, Havana, but source is not able to provide any information on the tasks the subject performed there. Source last saw subject in late 1960 or early 1961 in the Ministerio de Obras Publicas. Some days after this visit subject went as an “alzado” again to the Escambray Mountains where he remained until 17 March 1961, at which time he was arrested. When source left Cuba in March 1964 he knew from newspapers that subject was at the Isle of Pines prison. Source is of the opinion that subject has always been against the communists and that he had been honestly engaged in activities against the Castro regime in Cuba. Source believes that subject has a 16-year-old son who is residing with his mother in New York, address unknown. Subject made a trip to Mexico about 1958, and also a trip to Miami about 1958.

2. IDOS: circa 1925, Guanabacoa, Havana Province. Race: white. 5'8", 135 lbs. Hair: black. Eyes: dark. Shape of face: round. Physique: medium. No visible scars.

Training for the interview #201-754938.

3. JEWAVE traces: One Ezequiel GOMEZ was identified as a pro-Castro infiltree at the Isle of Pines Prison. GOMEZ is a former member of the MEP (DOI: September 1962) (UFGA 6761, 2-2, 4 December 1962).
