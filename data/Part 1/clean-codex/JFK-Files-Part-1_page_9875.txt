1. On 5 March 68, LIRING-3 reported he would meet with Carlos Persaval Marco, members of Manuel Marce Pardoés, director of the now-defunct magazine “Politica,” communist bimonthly publication. Persaval asked LIRING-3 if he could obtain a false passport for the wife of an important Guatemalan guerrilla leader in Mexico who must travel to Guatemala. LIRING-3 said he would do what he could to obtain documents.

2. On 8 March, Persaval said two important Guatemalan revolutionaries had to travel to Guatemala from Mexico by mid-March and asked if LIRING-3 was still willing to help. LIRING-3 answered in the affirmative but said there was limited chance of obtaining false documentation. He could, however, brief these revolutionaries on the Guatemala-Mexico border in order to make the illegal crossing easier. If they desired, he could personally guide them across the border.
