February 20, 23:17 Z.

CITD Director.

JMWAVE, Santo Domingo.

Info: Caracas.

PBRUMEN, JMDEVIL, AMICE.

References: A. JMWAVE 1346 (IN 55924). B. Caracas 8993 (IN 54407). C. Santo Domingo 5082 (IN 56151) (not sent JMWAVE or Caracas).

1. Headquarters has active interest in CLASC meetings and believes it would be worthwhile to have AMICE-31 attend the CLASC council meeting of references. (Separate cable follows regarding meeting and background in compliance with reference C.) As of two years ago, AMICE-31 was quite close to Maspero and may be able to give us good access to the inner Maspero clique for intelligence purposes. Naturally, we would want him to take an active part in support of Maspero's position at Congress. JBDELAPSE funds are available for AMICE-31's expenses up to 500 dollars.

2. For JMWAVE: Could AMICE-31 plausibly explain the source of funds for his travel?

3. For Santo Domingo: Although we are in favor of AMICE-31's attendance, leave the final decision to you. His handling in Santo Domingo would not have to be close; his briefing and debriefing in the JMWAVE area before and after Congress would be of greater importance.

4. For your information, all addressees: AMICE-31 was one of two WOFIRM reporting sources attending the Fifth CLASC Congress held in Panama in October 1966. Station Panama City then found the agent cooperative and a good reporter.

End of message.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officers.
