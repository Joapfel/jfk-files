Laidi, Ahmed

Algeria

Biographical: Oujda (Oudja), Morocco. Date of birth: 20 April 1934.

Named in August 1963 as advisor on African Affairs in the presidency and now listed as Ben Bella’s chief of cabinet. While he is a relative newcomer to the scene here and not known personally to the Embassy, it appears likely that he is close to Ben Bella and therefore a useful contact.

Department: 1, #658. 4 October 1963.

In accordance with a decree published on 12 November 1963, Ahmed Laidi was appointed chef du cabinet of President Ben Bella.

Journal Officiel, 15 November 1963, Algiers.

Subject is new Director General of Political Affairs, IFA. Algiers EBTEL 964, 21 October 1964.

Filed: Alg/Cuba.

Laidi, Director General, Foreign Ministry in charge of Political, Economic and Cultural Affairs, member of September 1965 delegation to UN General Assembly. Described as reserved, intelligent (ingent), and probably something of an operator; he was Ben Bella’s chief of cabinet before joining the foreign office in 1964 and, although technically still number three man in foreign office, has come to function more on a special-assignment basis than as part of the chain of command. His most recent existing project has been Bandung 2 (B ndung 2). Algiers EBTEL 660, 28 September 1965, Alg/UN.

Appointed Algerian Ambassador to Spain, October 1966.

A-168, 25 October 1966.

Filed Algeria/Spain.
