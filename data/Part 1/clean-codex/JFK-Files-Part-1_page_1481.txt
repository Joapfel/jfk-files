3 March 1976

Memorandum for: Associate General Counsel, OGC

Via: Deputy Director for Operations

Subject: Mitchell Livingston WerBell (WER BELL) III

Reference: Your memorandum of 24 February 1976, subject as above

1. Per reference request, we are forwarding information in our files concerning contacts that have taken place between this Agency and WerBell (WER BELL).

2. The following is a summary of all DDO contact directly with WerBell (WER BELL) which took place in 1959:

a. A 6 July 1959 memorandum to C/WH Division signed by Thomas J. Flores stated that General Cabell called Flores to report that WerBell (WER BELL) had called him. WerBell (WER BELL) discussed with General Cabell a trip WerBell (WER BELL) had taken to the Dominican Republic and offered to come to Washington at his own expense to discuss the trip. No commitments were made to WerBell (WER BELL) by General Cabell.

b. A 7 July 1959 memorandum of conversation signed by T. J. Flores stated that WerBell (WER BELL) was met in Washington, D.C., by Flores, who discussed with WerBell (WER BELL) information concerning the Dominican Republic, Fidel Castro, Fulgencio Batista, and General Pedraza.

c. An 8 July 1959 memorandum of conversation signed by T. J. Flores stated that on 8 July 1959 Flores called WerBell (WER BELL) to set up an appointment. During the meeting, WerBell (WER BELL) was told that the United States would not support any revolutionary activities by General Pedraza.
