0121322 NOV 6. CITE MEXICO CITY 7928.

Director, Guatemala City.

RYBAT PRUMEN LIRING-3.

November 1969.

1. ROWTON reestablished contact with LIRING-3 on 29 October and began detailed debriefing that evening. ROWTON admitted he arranged release through the brother of a business contact who was a colonel in the GNA. LIRING-3, who accepted the story without question, stated he understood ROWTON could not have arranged early release for obvious security reasons and felt almost intolerable suffering was worth the pain, since he has come out in the best possible position vis-à-vis FAR as well as PRUMEN.

2. LIRING-3 has begun giving complete details of arrest, detention, and contacts established during and after release. He asked ROWTON to keep sensitive points in the strictest confidence, particularly from LIRING-4, whom he feels would not approve his continued political activity even on behalf of ROWTON. Highlights of debriefing follow:

A. ROLANDO “CEBOLLITA.” FAR official, who shared cell block 400 with LIRING-3, told the latter that he had sent a report of arrest favorable to LIRING-3 and placing blame on FRANCISCO.
