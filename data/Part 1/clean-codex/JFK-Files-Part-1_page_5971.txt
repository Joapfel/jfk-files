2 February 1960

To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation. Attention: Mr. S. J. Papich.

From: Deputy Director (Plans).

Street: Susan and Richard Frank.

1. In response to your 22 December communication (File No. 100-339235, 100-401762), we wish to inform you that we have received the following from an official source:

“Richard Frank (born 1922), U.S. citizen, film technician, resident of New York, arrived at the consulate in Berne (Bera) with his wife Susan on 10 December 1959; they stayed there until 12 December, leaving for Zurich. Frank inquired for a flight ticket to Prague from Zurich. While in Berne, Frank had contact with the Chinese Communist Embassy on two occasions; he or she. Frank having called at the Embassy. No information is available on the nature of these contacts, nor do we have more detailed personalia.”

2. We have observed that persons desiring to travel to Communist China from Switzerland frequently fly via Prague (Pragga).

3. The following summary has been drawn from material which you have provided us on subjects (see M 100-43879, M 100-107383 and Bureau 100-339235). Please advise if we may pass this summary to our Swiss source, but without revealing that your organization supplied the information contained in it!
