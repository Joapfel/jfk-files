Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Date: 27 September 1965. Sitrep as of 0600 hours local time. Place and date and area code: Albertville, 27 September 1965. Source: report from two Soonlis in Albertville.

This is an Albertville situation report prepared at 0600 hours local time on 28 September 1965 by CAS.

1. On 27 September 1965, a column of the Fifth Commandos (5,000) reached Lulimba and was preparing to move toward Lufonoja on 26 September. His column, led by Major Alistair Wickes, had 3 of its men wounded during the movement from Bendera to Lulimba. Source: pilot descending. Congolese (Consolese) aircraft have had the only contact with the Fifth COE since 26 September.

2. On 27 September, Congolese (Consolese) aircraft pilots observed a 5,000. Instruction, which lens: 10-deg. entering Baraka. This same.
