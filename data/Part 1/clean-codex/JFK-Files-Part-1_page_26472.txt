E. In his discussion with HYSAGE, GALAN gave his itinerary as follows; however, HYSAGE does not know dates:

From Mexico he traveled to Guatemala, where he met with guerrilla leader GOMEZ AYAU (FNU). He gave him the pitch for action.

He had a similar meeting with a Nicaraguan guerrilla leader whose name HYSAGE tried for ten minutes to recall without success.

GALAN then traveled to Costa Rica and met with one of the MORA brothers. From Panama he intended to travel to Venezuela, where his contact was to be ARGELIA LAYA. HYSAGE did not believe he intended to go to Colombia, but said he would proceed to Brazil, where he planned to meet with PRESTES and JULIAO. HYSAGE commented that GALAN pointed out to her JULIAO’s prime advantage in the revolution, i.e., the fact that JULIAO’s wife was famous in CUADR, where they save. GALAN indicated that HYSAGE would be wise about the same thing. (Comment: HYKIRK, SO DANK YOU, and my JADE-1 have both reported on a recent visit of an unidentified foreigner.)
