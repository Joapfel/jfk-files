Report on Contact with Agent or Defector

Subject: WIROGUE  
Date: 13 December 1963  
Time: 1030 hours  
Place: Kuhwaldstr.

ORNE K. DARONNAT, Case Officer

Comments:

C. O. dropped in and paid Subject two weeks’ subsistence and after a short chat took him to lunch. C. O. and Subject spent several hours window shopping and just generally bumming around. Subject’s morale was greatly improved as a result and C. O. feels the time was well spent.

Subject said that he has been worried; that he knows he will be turned loose on his own soon and asked C. O., if possible, to let him have sufficient notice so he can make plans. C. O. told him that he would try to give him at least one month’s notice.

E.O.P. 1. Enclosed from receipt. Attention of the Case Officer’s.
