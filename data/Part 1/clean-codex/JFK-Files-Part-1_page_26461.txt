1. Victor Rico Galan arrived in Managua on 13 October via Pan Am flight 501 from Mexico. He left Managua for San Jose on 17 October via TACA flight 101. According to immigration records, Rico was born 6 February 1928 at El Ferrol, Spain. His address in Mexico City is Providencia 939. Immigration records show he entered Nicaragua with Mexican passport number 16897, but left using Mexican passport number 53541, issued 9 September 1963. (Probably one of the numbers is a passport number and the other is a registration.)

2. Rico stayed at the Gran Hotel, room number 407, while in Managua. His visit was widely publicized, and he gave interviews to the local press. He met with various Nicaraguan officials from the Ministry of Press and was received by the Mexican ambassador. He sought an interview with President Schick, but the latter did not grant an interview.

3. Unable to develop information regarding the visit with the unidentified guerrilla leader mentioned in the reference, but Rico did meet one or more times with Alvaro Ramirez Gonzalez, secretary-general of Movilizacion Republicana (MR—Communist political front), who is also a member of the Central Committee of the Nicaraguan Communist Party (PSN). His visit with Ramirez was publicized in the press and on radio as a secret meeting in Rico's hotel room. Ramirez answered by writing.
