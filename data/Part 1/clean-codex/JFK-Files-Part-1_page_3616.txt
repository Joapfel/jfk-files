Cite Panama City 38861. 30 September 1974. Staff.

To: Director. Info: San Jose, Managua.

WYEPOCH.

1. HYGRUFF-1 (liaison) requests any information indicating that Manuel Artíme Buesa is connected with movements plotting against the Government of Panama. HYGRUFF-1 “knows” Artíme is closely connected with the Government of Nicaragua and travels to Costa Rica, where he connected with “movimientos subversivos en contra de Panama” (sic).
