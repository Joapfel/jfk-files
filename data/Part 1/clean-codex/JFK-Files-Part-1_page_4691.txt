Date: 10/2/78. Dated: 10-2-78.

This is an unclassified report, not finally evaluated intelligence.

Cite TOFIR 08-315/01669-78. Distributed 06 February 1978.

Country: Venezuela/Cuba.

Subject: Possible release of Cuban exile leader Orlando Rosch (Bosch) (0011 late January 1978).

Source: A Venezuelan businessman whose activities bring him into close contact with various government officials. He is a fairly reliable source.

1. In late January 1978 an adviser to Venezuelan President Carlos Andres Perez said that Perez is currently considering giving financial aid to Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch in return for Bosch's agreement not to molest Venezuelan personnel or facilities. Bosch, who is currently under detention in Venezuela in connection with the October 1976 bombing of a Cubana airliner, will be brought to trial soon. The adviser said. He admitted, however, that there is no chance of getting a legal conviction against Bosch.
