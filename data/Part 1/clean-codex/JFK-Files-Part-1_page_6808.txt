New Aggression from the Cubans—The Cubans consider as a non-aggression against Cuba, by the United States, the measure taken by the Inter-American Defense Force preventing the Cuban delegate on the board from visiting U.S. bases. (Mudar Trujillo, Radio Caribe, October 13, 1960, 10:00 GMT—E)

Cuban-Soviet Committee—In Moscow the Cuban-Soviet Committee was formed, sponsored by the Soviet Association for Friendship and Cooperation with the Latin American countries. Cuban Ambassador Faure Chomón (Chauman) and several other Cubans attended the installation ceremony. (Claud Trujillo, Radio Caribe, October 13, 1960, 10:00 GMT—P)

Bogota Political Conference—Captain Antonio Núñez Jiménez, director of the Cuban National Institute for Agrarian Reform, has been invited to attend the meeting of Latin American political leaders, which will begin on October 22 in Bogota, Colombia. So far, 23 persons from nine Latin American countries have said they will attend the meeting. (Havana, Prensa Latina (FRENSA LATINA), October 7, 1960, 17:05 GMT—E)

Students to GDR—Three Cuban students on scholarships will study economic sciences and medicine at Leipzig and Karl Marx (Khrii Herx) University in the GDR. The students, Angel M. Alfonso, Consuelo Mesa, and Juan P. Oliva, will leave for the GDR on October 14 to begin the university course, which will last a year. (Havana, Prensa Latina (FRENSA LATINA), October 13, 1960, 15:00 GMT—E)
