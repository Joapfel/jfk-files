See 201-234750 on Tamara S. Kungirova.

On his second trip to the USSR in January 1959, Philip Robert Nielson, REDSKIN agent, was given an INTURIST interpreter/guide named Tamara Stepanovna Kungirova. A romance developed between them, and Nielson became determined to marry her, which he finally did in the spring of 1962. His family did everything in their power to discourage this romance, and John Haines of the State Department received a letter from Nielson’s father regarding the affair—not seeking help but advice on what to do.

Nielson later received a letter from his father in fall 1960 giving details of the contents of a conversation his father had had with John Hanes (Haines) of State.

Hanes stated that:

a. All American-Soviet marriages had turned out to be unsuccessful.

b. The Soviets never let one of their nationals out of the country under such circumstances unless the person had been recruited as a spy.

c. The Soviets would probably not give Nielson a visa. He tried unsuccessfully all summer and fall of 1960 and in 1961 to get a Scy visa.
