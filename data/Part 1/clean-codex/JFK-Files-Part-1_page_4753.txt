To: Director. Minister.

Refs: A. Director 862914. B. Santo Domingo 17449 (IN 935896). C. Director 861876.

1. Lincoln Rondon, well-known Cuban residing in Miami, visited Santo Domingo a few days ago. Asked EMGARNET-1 if we knew anything about recent visits of rightest Cubans to contact Orlando Bosch. EMGARNET-1 expressed serious doubts that such meeting took place, pointing out that airport controls of entering Cubans are very tight. Rondon, who is an old Bosch watcher, said he was certain the meeting took place and claimed Cubans entered the country with false Puerto Rican documents, which are being used increasingly by extreme rightest Cubans because they are so easy to obtain.
