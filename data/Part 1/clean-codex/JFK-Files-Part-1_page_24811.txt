WH/6-509

15 February 1967

Memorandum for: Chief, CI/OA

Subject: Travel of Edward G. Tichborn (201-253203)

1. In accordance with your request for information on Tichborn's foreign travel (CI/OA memorandum dated 22 November 1966), the following is forwarded.

2. Edward G. Tichborn left New York City for Mexico City on 9 January 1967, returning on 21 January. He traveled primarily on cover company business but extended his stay to consult with Station Mexico City on a possible research project of interest to the Station and Headquarters.

Paul E. Oberst  
Chief, WH/6
