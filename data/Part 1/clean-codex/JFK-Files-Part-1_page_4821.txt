To: San Jose. Info: Managua (MANAGUA.Y).

Refs: A. Managua 12202 (IN 848642), being relayed to San Jose. B. Managua 12179 (IN 835555). C. Secret Service advance, San Jose 105, 21 February 1976.

3. Please furnish station traces on Ignazio Palomera Perez, DPOB 16 March 1940, Havana, Cuba. References indicate he resides in Managua, Nicaragua, and holds Costa Rican passport number 3208-68 or 3208-69. He may be a contact of Orlando Bosch Avila (201-299292). He has plans to notify Secret Service and FBI of Palomera's intended travel to the U.S.

2. Please advise whether Bosch is still being held by Costa Rican authorities.

3. File: 201-299292.

LA/Comment: Palomera obtained Nicaraguan exit permit on 5 March for travel to the U.S. He may be a contact of Orlando Bosch Avila, Cuban exile activist arrested in San Jose on 18 February on charges of false documentation. Bosch reportedly involved in plot to assassinate Chilean Andres Pascal Allende in San Jose.
