13 February 1965. IN 80247. 132358Z. Priority. DIR CITE WAVE 8261. RISKY TYPIC YOBITE AMWORLD.

1. AMCLATTER/1 told Frank Fiorini on 13 February 1965 that Manuel Artime’s house at 1270 NE 85th Street, Miami, was ransacked during the evening of 12 February. Artime told Fiorini that intruders had stolen jewelry, a number of documents, and a telescopic lens rifle that Fiorini had given Artime as a gift.

2. Artime asked Fiorini to help him by getting in touch with local authorities, unspecified, with a request that they keep the matter quiet. Fiorini said Artime does not want the incident publicized.

3. For security reasons, WAVE has made no effort to obtain confirmation from local authorities. However, WAVE has received no other report on this matter as of preparation of this report.
