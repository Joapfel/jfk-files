Cable Secretariat Dissemination. Classified message.

Secret 282234Z August 72, Staff SDON, cite Santo Domingo 13299, Director. WOMUSE EMPYREX.

WOMUSE EMPYREX.

1. EMPYREX liaison advises Herbert Itkin arrived Santo Domingo 22 August 72 from New York PAA 235, prohibited entry, and returned to New York same day PAA 236. Itkin was born 19 November 1926, New York, U.S., PP B 1220178.

2. No station traces. EMPYREX records indicate subject blacklisted because he engaged in espionage activities and has Mafia connections. According to the same file, the New York Times reported subject provided information to BKCROWN on persons in the Dominican Republic.

3. Traces requested.

4. Index. File 201-83680, 22-2-20/3.
