Dispatch symbol and No.: E7A-20253. To: Chief of Base, Salzburg; Chief, SR; Chief, ME. Headquarters file No.: 201-289249; 29-5-106/3. From: Chief of Station, Vienna. Date: 19 July 1961. Subject: REDWOOD/LCDPRUY/A3DOG4L/1. No indexing required.

Action required: See below.

References: LGM-10664, dated 23 May 1961, 201-289249.

Chief of Base, Salzburg is requested to check USRP and any other appropriate files on subject of reference (a copy of which is herewith attached for Salzburg). The principal object of this exercise is to determine whether the person shown in the attached photograph is identical with subject. As a minimum please ascertain the addresses at which subject lived in Salzburg from 1945 to 1950. Depending on what you find, we can then carry out some unilateral method of confirming the identity of the attached photo.

Lee J. Parry, 13 July 1961.

Attachments: A) Copy LGM-10664, h/w; B) Photo VSC.
