2 October 75

Memorandum for: Chief, Information and Privacy Staff  
Subject: James G. Bruce  
Reference: Request No. 75-5742

This Directorate has no file on James G. Bruce.

Two Agency documents, however, were surfaced. These documents are denied under (b)(1), as being properly classified under F.O. 11652 and (b)(3) (sources and methods) FOIA. Release of the portion concerning the subject would probably identify the source to him. Thus, these documents must be denied in full.

Charles A. Briggs  
Chief, Services Staff
