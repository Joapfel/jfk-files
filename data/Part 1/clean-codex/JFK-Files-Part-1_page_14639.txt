Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Santiago.

Copy to: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division; Chief, Europe Division; COS, VII/Miami.

From: Chief of Station, Madrid.

Subject: AMULET/OUTRIDA/PUREMEN/Manuel Tello Troncoso.

On 29 October QUTIID reported a conversation between Guillermo Ruiz Perez, the Cuban Minister to Spain, and Manuel Tello Troncoso, identified in the Diplomatic List as Counsellor of Embassy, regarding the postponement until 31 October of a meeting they were to have on 29 October. We have no information on the purpose of the meeting. We note that Tello arrived in Spain in January 1969.

Howard R. Sacoder.

Distribution: COS, Santiago—to out pouch; C/WMD; C/EUR; COS/VII, Miami—to out pouch.

Cross reference to: OSHA 25624. Classification: Secret. PES file number: 201-735 296.
