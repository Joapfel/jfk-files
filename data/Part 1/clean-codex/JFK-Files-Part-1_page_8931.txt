DIR CITE MEXI 5813. REF DIR 61786 KUWOLF.

C/S comment: Requested project renewal requests for LICHEW, LICOAX, LILISP C, LILISP X, LINOODLE, LITAIN'T. Also requested STA forward new project cutlines to cover TICHBORN and RIKON.

1. HMMA-21914, punched 9 Aug, is General Station comment on KUWOLF renewals. As reference indicates, dispatches punched 9 August were not received at the MEXI KUWOLF desk as of 16 Aug. Suggest value obtaining HMMA-21914 ASAP.

2. Station unaware apparently significant lag between arrival of dispatches at headquarters and delivery to desk. HMMA-21864, according to reference, had not hit desk as of 16 August, yet should have been received at headquarters 8 Aug. Please advise if this is normal delay. If so, certain matters now treated in dispatches will of necessity be subject to cable traffic.

3. Reference paragraph two. TICHBORN recruited and being paid from headquarters. Station has avoided utilization of TICHBORN in projects so time-consuming as to prohibit travel, as it was understood he was to be kept in ready status for international assignment. Should this understanding be retained for a new project for TICHBORN, or can MEXI now consider him as primarily a MEXI agent?

Routing. Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
