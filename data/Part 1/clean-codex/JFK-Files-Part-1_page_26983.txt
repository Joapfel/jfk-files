2. Van Cleave called us at his expense from Atlanta about five minutes later. We advised him at the outset that he should speak with Bill Pangburn in the Atlanta President Office at his earliest convenience. We explained that our work force is divided geographically rather than functionally, and that since he lives in Atlanta, he must deal with the Atlanta No. 19. We provided him with the telephone number for the Atlanta No., and he indicated that he would contact Pangburn the same day. We called Pangburn to advise him of Van Cleave’s intention to contact him. We later learned from both Pangburn and Van Cleave that the contact was made.

3. We presumed that we would have no further contact with Van Cleave. However, we were erroneous. Van Cleave placed at least two more telephone calls to us from Atlanta, at his expense, and one from Dade County (Cade County), Florida. Each time we advised him to call Pangburn rather than us. His response on these occasions was that he already had or intended to make Pangburn advised of that we wanted to keep us informed also. The local Dade County call was made to advise us that Van Cleave was in Miami in the company of two Argentine (Arcenting) police officials, whom we could confirm if we so desired. Again told him that we could report to Atlanta.
