10. Kalistrat SALIA (SSALIA)

Born 26 August 1980. Wanted to see AEPADDLE, but there was no opportunity. Address: 8 r. Berlins. Not working, except on Georgian publication. Travels to Germany and Belgium. No visible means of support. Publishes journal in French. AEPADDLE says he has best brains and is most influential individual in Georgian emigration. From 1945–1948 he was closely involved with the Soviet Embassy in Paris. He helped distribute Soviet films and was associated with the repatriation movement. In 1953 or 1954, Levan ZURABICHVILI was Michael KEDIA in Geneva, shortly before KEDIA died. KEDIA worked for Germans during World War II and warned ZURABICHVILI that SALIA was a Soviet agent. SALIA has a good literary journal, and AEPADDLE said that he tries to nullify émigré political activities by tying up some of the best minds in purely cultural activities. GABACHVILI and ZURABICHVILI say SALIA is a Soviet agent, but that they do not know what to do about it. AEPADDLE says SALIA is in close contact by mail with Civi KOBAKHIDZE, who works for VOA in Washington.

11. Jora ASSATIANI

35 years old. Married to a French girl, has one child. Lives in Louville, apparently in a communal house with BERESHVILI et al. Wife is a schoolteacher. Subject arrived in Paris in 1947, after escaping forced repatriation. Applied for a U.S. visa in 1949, but did not come because of lack of friends there. Works at the Indian Embassy. Wants to fight communism. Claims his job gives him contact with legal travelers to the Soviet Union, who could be used for taking in letters, newspapers, etc. Subject is well educated and is close to some of the higher officials in the Indian Embassy, whom he considers Communists. His French is good, and his English fairly good. Subject said he could not go back to the USSR on a mission himself. He is 5'6", 170 lbs., bald, round face, light complexion, quick thinker, well dressed. Well regarded by all who know him. Feels that the older generation of émigrés is too rigid. Considers BERESHVILI a good asset, also Sergio KOKHREDZE.
