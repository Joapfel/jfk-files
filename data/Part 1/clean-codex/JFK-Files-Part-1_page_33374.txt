Staff, 301122 May 74. Cite Bangkok 59314. To: Director. Info: Santiago. REDLEG AEFIS-. References: A. Bangkok 59065 (to 260348). B. Director 554557.

1. Milton C. Repoush and Ralph K. Tolgien met with Emilio Galetezki on 25 May and passed reference and information. Tolgien had a follow-up meeting with Galetezki on 29 May to discuss his encounter with Arthur Cunning.

2. Galetezki met Cunning at the home of Robert B. Hartford, U.S. citizen employed in the Population Division of ECAFE. Hartford is pro-Allentice (PRO-ALLENTICE) and organized a small social gathering of interested persons to meet Cunning and discuss the current situation in Chile. Cunning is described as about forty years old, with black hair, a full beard, and average build. He is a demographer employed by the United Nations at Centro Latino Americano de Desarrollo (CELADE) in Santiago. He arrived in Chile in October 1972 with his wife. Cunning departed Bangkok 27 May for Delhi, Kathmandu, and New York and planned to return to Santiago approximately 22 June.
