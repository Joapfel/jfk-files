Ref: Mexico City 2289 (UN 78356) 76.

22 August 1966. IN 81196.

1. Except for straight news accounts of “declaraciones” and legal indictments of Rico Galvan (Rico Galavan) and fellow conspirators, very little editorial commentary has been noted on the Rico Galvan (Rico Galavan) case since reference, indicating the tight lid imposed by the government is still in effect. What little editorial commentary has appeared has expressed either mild concern over the constitutional rights of arrestees, e.g., a 24 August Siempre (Simepre) editorial article, or has related the alleged conspiracy to larger speculation about Mexico as a target for stepped-up Castro and/or ChiCom subversion.

2. Defensive-type commentary has been noted from leftist quarters but has been minimal. At the end of the 19 August account of legal developments in the Rico Galvan (Rico Galavan) case, El Día coupled a brief announcement of PARM President Juan Barragán’s condemnation of the Rico Galvan (Rico Galavan) verdict with a “Nuevo Comunicado” from the MLN, reiterating that constitutional guarantees of arrestees have been violated. Same theme elaborated upon in a paid advertisement placed by MLN in 23 August Excelsior.
