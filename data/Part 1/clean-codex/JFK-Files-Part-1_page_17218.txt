DIR CITE MEXI 1762. LIEMPTY.

REF DIR 65696. MEXI, 1307, 20 October 1964.

C.P. 28 November 1964. IN 31737.

1. Station has two bank evaluations of property, one by Banco Nacional de Mexico obtained by SCANTLING in PSEUDO and the other by Banco Azteca at LIMOUSINE's behest. Respective evaluations are: lot, 185,266 and 180,600 pesos; total tax value, 255,575 and 249,242 pesos, which of course includes present house. Discrepancies between two appraisals are not sufficient to worry about since, for building purposes, only land value is pertinent, and difference here is less than 5,000 pesos, so believe can disregard other figures.

2. LIMOUSINE has also had plans made for a four-story building on property, ground floor commercial and three floors of apartments, one of which would guarantee reserve for LIMITED and alter as station wishes. Smaller building not deemed worthwhile investment. Cost study shows building could probably be put up for about 535,900 pesos, of which LIMOUSINE could borrow about 433,000 from bank.
