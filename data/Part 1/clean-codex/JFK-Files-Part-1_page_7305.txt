9.04 Drinking.

9.04.01 Does he drink? Average.

9.04.02 Does he drink alone (salicylary drinker), with others mainly for sociableness, on formal occasions only, or after work at home?

9.04.03 Does he appear to drink primarily for the taste, primarily for the effect, or for both?

9.04.04 His favorite drink is beer, wine, whiskey, vodka, gin, or no preference.

9.04.05 He prefers cocktails, highballs, short drinks, or straight shots.

9.04.06 He drinks average.

9.04.07 Does he gulp his drink or sip his drink? Sip his drink.

9.04.08 Does he go out of his way to get a refill, usually wait until asked, or always wait until asked? Always wait until asked.

9.04.09 Attitude toward wines: very knowledgeable; enjoys but not particularly knowledgeable; indifferent about wines; or knows nothing, could not care less. Knows nothing, could not care less.

9.04.10 Does he talk at length, and obviously get pleasure from talking, about his liquor preferences; talk about his liquor preferences no more than anyone else; or never talk about his liquor preferences? Never talks about his liquor preferences.

9.04.11 Is he openly proud of his native drinks and does he urge others to try them? No.

Is he conventionally aware of his native drinks and offer them when appropriate? Yes or no.

Does he rarely mention his native drinks and not “push” them? Yes or no.
