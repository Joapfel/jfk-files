1. On 21 August, IDEN, WODISH New York representative who handles Louis A. Brun, subject of reference, phoned WODISH Washington office to inquire if EVMEND-1 or the EVMEND organization was of interest to RVROCK. Basis for inquiry is that Brun has repeatedly mentioned to IDEN that EVMEND-1 and his organization are deeply involved in exile work in New York and that Brun suspects they have some connection with RVROCK. WODISH representatives speculate that Brun is seeking confirmation of the RVROCK connection and that Brun may try to penetrate the EVMEND organization.

2. WODISH Washington representative was informed that EVMEND-1 and his organization are of interest to WOMACE and that IDEN should refrain from making any contact whatsoever with them. WODISH agreed to pass the above to IDEN and suggested WODISH relations with Brun be discontinued.
