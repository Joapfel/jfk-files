Dispatch. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. From: Chief of Station, Miami. Subject: TYPIC/DGI-DSB Officials Known to AMMUG-1.

Forwarded herewith is a contact report prepared by Eustace D. KLOOCK on his most recent meeting with AMMUG-1. During this meeting, Volume I of the Cuban Hugbook was reviewed with AMMUG-1. The individuals AMMUG-1 was able to identify are listed in paragraph 2 of the attached contact report. It is requested that all the names be indexed.

Eustace D. KLOOCK.
