Classified message.

Cite Mexico City 6885. Priority: Rio de Janeiro. Info: Routine, Director. REDCOAT.

24 August 1968. IN 77213 U.

1. According to LIENVOY, 22 August 1968, Francisco Juliao de Paula contacted Nicolay Leonov, Soviet press attaché in Mexico, and declared his solidarity with the Soviet Embassy in Mexico and with the Soviet Government, apparently referring to the Czech situation.

2. Station plans include this in roundup of local reactions to invasion of Czechoslovakia by the Soviet Union.
