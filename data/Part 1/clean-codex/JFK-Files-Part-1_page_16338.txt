Channel will be from Wicks to Latz to Belgian Major Coomhaux (advisor to ANC C-4) to Belgian concern to S in Kamina. Obviously such channel will keep lots of other people in picture as well. FYI Belgian concern has direct radio communication with Kamina base. S said he picked Latz because his old friend of Wicks.

2. Latz is long-time resident Associated Press correspondent and has business investments Eville. British subject. Have no derogatory information.

3. Request addresses Latz, specially indications any intelligence connection notable by Smith. Same for Wicks.
