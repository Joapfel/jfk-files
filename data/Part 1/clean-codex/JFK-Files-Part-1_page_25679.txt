Classified message.

Date: 17 October 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/TFW 5. Info: DDP, ADDP, C/SOD 3, C/C1, C/F1. 2, C/WH 5, S/C 2*. IN 40768.

DIR GUAT INFO WAVE CITE MEXI 2221  
GYROSE/LITAMIL  
Reference: MEXI 2222, Ca 50773J.

C/S comment: *Dissemination applicable to RYEAT CAELES.

1. On 16 October 1962, LITAMIL-2 reported a meeting of PRO-UNIDAD “clandestine unit” on the night of 15 October, composed of the following: LITAMIL-1, LITAMIL-2, LITAMIL-3, LITAMIL-5, Horacio Wieszesuk Guerrero, Jose Manuel Lopez Alfonso, Tulio Prieto Ruiz, Fernando Crozco, and Dr. Alfredo Ferrer.

2. At the meeting, Lopez told the group that Col. Enrique Peralta Azurdia, Guatemala Ministry of Defense, who came to Mexico City on an official visit in September 1962, made contact with Lopez and offered arms, ammunition, and a (slow) vessel. Peralta is a longtime friend of Lopez. Lopez said that Peralta’s courier arrived in Mexico City a few days ago and said Peralta’s offer still stands, but that direct contact must be made with Peralta in Guatemala. The group chose LITAMIL-2 or LITAMIL-3 as the most likely PUC couriers, in view of the former’s business interests in Guatemala and the latter’s diplomatic contacts. According to Lopez, contact with Peralta should be made before the end of October. PUC plans to collect funds and send a courier to Guatemala soonest. Will advise. Lopez did not identify Peralta’s courier.

3. L-2 reported on 16 October that he was gaining more confidence in Wieszesuk. Does Guatemala have any information regarding paragraph 2?

4. Above not being disseminated in view of operational interest. (End of message.)
