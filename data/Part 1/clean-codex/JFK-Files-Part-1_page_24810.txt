United States Government Memorandum

To: C/WH/6  
Attention: Mr. Patton  
From: WH/Pers/Contracts  
Subject: Edward G. TICHBORN  
Date: 27 February 1967

1. Edward G. TICHBORN has sent three checks to Wm. Rodgers, P.O. Box 10146, Woodridge Station, Washington, D.C., without any accompanying explanation.

$125.00 dated 15 February 1967  
$125.00 dated 15 January 1967  
$25.89 dated 15 February 1967 (for “medical insurance”)

The above checks were received 27 February in an envelope postmarked 21 February 1967.

2. Please advise if you have any idea as to what the two $125 checks are for.

3. It would facilitate processing of checks (payments) if TICHBORN were asked to forward a brief explanation with the check as to what individual checks are for when such cannot be indicated on the check.

Wm. Renehan
