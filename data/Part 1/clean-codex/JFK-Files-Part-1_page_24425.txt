DTDORIC/Operational/MARINE/JAGUAR Reporting.

Through London Station's liaison with JAGUAR we are sometimes able to monitor the reactions and attitudes of ORPHONE/1's enemies within radical circles in the U.K. We are forwarding, u/s/c, copies of two such recent JAGUAR reports. In this case they were particularly helpful in underlining the continuing need for attention to the cover backstopping of ORPHONE/1's sources of income.

Aaron C. LAGINESTRA.
