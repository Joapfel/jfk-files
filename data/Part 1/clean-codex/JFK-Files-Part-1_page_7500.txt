Document Transfer and Cross Reference.

Subject of document: Agnes Smiley, was. RI file No.: 201-414. Dispatch or source document symbol No.: DFB-16151. Date: 5 Mar. 52. JEP.

Document disposition: XIX — cross referred to this file.

Pertinent information: According to confidential informant T-17, of unknown reliability, Agnes Smiley attempted to obtain from the American Express Co. in Shanghai certain mail addressed to Harold R. Isaacs while Isaacs was absent from the city. One of the return addresses on this mail was Smith and Isaacs, 344 East 149th Street, Bronx, New York. Harold R. Isaacs, mentioned above, was identified by T-17 as an American journalist of Communist sympathies who resided in Shanghai in the early thirties.
