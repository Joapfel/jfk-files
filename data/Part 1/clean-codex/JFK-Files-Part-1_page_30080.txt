IV. Empirical Background

1953 to 1962: Intelligence with U. S. Government, Central Intelligence Agency, OS-13, $11,415. As chief of a small branch of highly specialized analysts, I exercised a wide variety of talents and was responsible for the following: the direction and administration of the branch operations; determined the course of action for further research effort within the branch; monitored and supervised a program of special personnel assessment; evaluated technical analyses and their relationship to specific cases; advised and consulted with members of senior staff components of CIA; delivered numerous technical briefings to both U. S. and foreign police and intelligence services; initiated and maintained projects carried out by private organizations such as universities, private consultants, and private research corporations. During the above period, I trained and briefed numerous others engaged in police and intelligence work. I also initiated and developed four technical systems currently in operation on a world-wide basis within CIA and wrote numerous articles for an intelligence periodical. In January 1962, CIA presented me with one of the largest individual Monetary Suggestion Awards ever given by that Agency.
