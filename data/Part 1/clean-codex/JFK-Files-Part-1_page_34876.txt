012217Z MAR 68, CITE CARACAS 9043. Director info: JMWAVE, LFG. CIMOTIF TYPIC.

1. In recent 15 to 20 February personal trip to JMWAVE area, CIMOTIF-1 met Teresa Suarez, who [was an] old friend and widow of IDEN A. According to Suarez, IDEN A executed in PBRUMEN around August 1961 while on op for WOFIRM. As a result, she and seven-year-old daughter now receiving 100-dollar-monthly pension from IDEN B, which she states not according to terms of agreement worked out by IDEN A and WOFIRM representative in early 1960. Suarez states amount should be 300 dollars monthly.

2. Although CIMOTIF-1 does not know whether IDEN A ever worked for WOFIRM or what terms of agreement were if IDEN A did, he still genuinely concerned about subsistence of Suarez and daughter on 100-dollar-monthly pension. CIMOTIF-1 feels responsibility to them because he states he recommended IDEN A to WOFIRM officer in early 1960.
