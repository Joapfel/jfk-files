Directorio Revolucionario Estudiantil

José Antonio Echeverría, con tus ideas en marcha.

Report

During the recent trial I made three in the State selections in Latin America, the results of the sending of projects made by the will to Cuba were reported to me. For the better understanding of the same reports reached, we will now explain them within each selection visited.

Introduction

The selection at Venezuela sent some months ago the projects, and we sent them to be mailed to Cuba. They used the lists of addresses given by Roemer.

Venezuela has continued this system since the properties are edited by the State and the projects measured in Venezuela. In several of 1,700 letters sent during May, June, and July, mailings were made from Caracas one month, from Maracaibo another month, and from Paramaribo another. All security means were studied: that they be sent by different persons with different handwriting, that no administrative stamps be used, ordinary envelopes, and mailed in different post offices, etc.

March

The selection at Brazil also sent the projects given by Roemer, but no reply was obtained from any person. The security means and the conditions for the sending of the projects were studied, and in doing so, the selection remarked on the risk run with the mail and the violations to which it is subject in Brazil. They explained that the salaries of post office employees are so low that they take off the stamps from the letters when they have not been marked, to sell them, and then they destroy the letter.

They sent 1,500 letters during May, June, and July.

June–July

We did not send the projects to Brazil (巴西) since then we had no selection there.

Cities

The selection at Manila sent the third project and sent it by Roemer, which was the project made on the members of the Left of July who are imprisoned or have been executed. They sent 1,000 letters.
