File: STALEN PHOTO-BOOK

From CRH's LOG, 2 May 1951

Bill Kintner has suggested that Mr. Burris-Heyer consult with Boris Shub in New York tomorrow on themes for black PW against the USSR.

Finky and I see no objection to this meeting, and I have notified Shub to expect a call from Burris-Heyer.

Shub told me that his Stalin picture-book now promises to run to 172 pages, or about 80 double-spreads, comprising some 500 photographs. He expects the layouts to be completed by 20 May, and the text by mid-June. Its price will be $2 or thereabouts.
