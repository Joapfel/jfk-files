Guillermo scoffed at this and again said the Canadians were friends. A-5 returned to his hotel after further contact and departed for New York City at 1320, 27 November. In preparation for meeting with Neebecker, he was told that no further contact would be made with him until CULIB had heard from him using the channel established through the letters from Brown.

A-5 was instructed to write to the Havana address to which he sent a cable acknowledging the meeting in Montreal. A-5 requested that they might hold the next meeting in Mexico, but this was immediately ruled out by Guillermo, citing the difficulties caused by the LIEACK case. He also ruled out Spain because he said he was known there. Kingston was chosen as the next meeting place. A-5 told Guillermo that, due to pressure of business, he could not meet in Kingston before Christmas, but depending on the release of Rojas, he would write when he could make the meeting and then await instructions.
