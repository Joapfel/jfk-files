Priority WH/Miami, info Director.

30 November, IN 481013.

1. CIFENCE-4 will travel to Miami 1 December via VIASA Flight 740, arriving 1200 local. Traveling in true name with Venezuelan official passport, which shows his DPOB as Caracas, 17 February 1928. CIFENCE-4 was sent by his employer to purchase twenty-five nine-millimeter pistols for use of the government. He was unable to refuse or evade the assignment.

2. CIFENCE-4 is a registered resident alien of WOCORK. Station does not anticipate any difficulty except in the remote possibility Immigration learns of his resident alien status. He has been advised by Station to say nothing of this and stick to the story that he is a government official, as indicated in the passport.

3. If Miami has liaison capability, would appreciate monitoring the arrival of CIFENCE-4, with capability to intervene if necessary. Sending identity cable to Miami.
