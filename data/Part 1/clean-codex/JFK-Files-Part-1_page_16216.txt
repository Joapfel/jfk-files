History

Incorporated in Nevada November 1, 1968; authorized capital, $500,000. Michael Oliver, born 1920, married. Prior to starting this concern, engaged in land development as an officer in Oliver-Boza Corporation. Prior to that, was associated with the Philco Corp. in Palo Alto, Calif. Prior to that, employed by Volt Tech Corp. at San Diego. The directors are residents of the State of California; antecedent information is not currently available.
