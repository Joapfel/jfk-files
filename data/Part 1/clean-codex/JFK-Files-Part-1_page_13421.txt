Ref: SAGO 8707 (IN 7/7/87).

1. FUBIAS-7 reports as of 11 February: Maspero and all executive secretaries presented their speeches, and discussions of proposed resolutions have now begun one by one. No dissension is yet apparent, and according to Eduardo Garcia, Maspero appears to be trying to appease everyone and is willing to accept all recommendations. Due to the slow start, the meeting will extend through 17 February. The general atmosphere appears closed, but most proposals are open and yet to be discussed.

2. F-7 said Vanistendael's conversations with Maspero have helped. Van told M all his bad points and said to correct them or aid would cease. M accepted the criticism and promised reform.

3. For WAVE: AMICE-31 is not sitting in on the closed executive committee meeting, only allowed to speak to the executive committee on the resolution establishing fraternal relations between the A-31 union and CLASC. F-7 said Maspero has given consent to the resolution and thinks highly of A-31. A-31 and F-7 are traveling to Valparaiso with CLASC leaders to organize a maritime boycott; CLASC (CLASS) appears willing to help. A-31's estimated time of departure is now 19 February.
