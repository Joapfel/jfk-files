To: Director. Immediate Madrid. Info: JMWAVE, Frankfurt, Germany. Crypto-received targets.

Typic: Ref Madrid, 9088 /N 92840.

1. Typic desk traces subjects referred:

A. Dr. Jose Angel Bustamante (Bustamente) O'Leary, hard-core Communist for at least twenty years, and do not believe he is vulnerable to any pitch.

B. Dr. Leopoldo Araujo was one of the directors of the Medical College in Havana and one of the most influential medical men in Cuba. He rose to this position out of comparative obscurity. He is a Communist of long standing and considered to be one of the top Communist Party members in Latin America.

C. Dr. Luis Yodu Prevez. The only traces show that he traveled to Czechoslovakia in 1961 and 1962.

D. Dr. Rafael J. Larragoiti, born 9/8/12, Matanzas, Cuba. Graduate of University of Havana, 1940. From 1940 to 1943, interned at Calixto Garcia Hospital. From 1943 to 1946, resident in the Department of Nervous and Mental Diseases, University Hospital. Took postgraduate training in Illinois at 201-022-396.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
