ATTACHMENT

July 9, 1969

Subject: Valentin LOGINOV (P-11,712)

Source: Jimmy SABORI

1. According to Source, LOG phoned him on Friday, July 4, at 1100 hours and told him that he would be out of town on July 5 and 6 and, consequently, would want to reschedule their meeting. LOG proposed Monday or Tuesday, and Source told him that he would be busy next week with some American businessmen. LOG concluded that he wanted to visit Josephine ALCAYAGA Seguez this week and would do it probably on Thursday (July 10), but would phone earlier.

2. According to Source, LOG does not miss a week to give Josephine ALCAYAGA a ring just to say hello.

3. During the phone conversation mentioned in paragraph 2 above, LOG reminded Source that he was interested in having, next week, in writing what Mexican officials thought about the American promises made at the Inter-American Economic Conference in Port of Spain.

4. The following are LOG's statements made to Source during some of their previous meetings:

a. LOG has life insurance for 1 million Pecos with Monterrey Compañía (Comania) de Seguros, S.A., in Mexico City.

b. LOG told Source that it was good to have a friend “on the other side,” meaning the American side, because one can never tell what might happen.

c. LOG brought out that having money of his own.
