371628Z October 1970. Cite Tegucigalpa. 9118 AIGU Director, DO Base, New York; info Managua (MANGUA), WH/Miami. Ref: Book Dispatch 7552. Meeting with AMBIDDY-1.

1. Lawrence F. Barker (COS Tegucigalpa) met AMBIDDY-1 (AMBIDCY-;) by chance while in Managua on 4 October. AMBIDDY said he had been asked by Nicaraguan President Anastasio Somoza to join the Nicaraguan delegation to the General Assembly as a special advisor on Cuba. AMBIDDY believes Somoza may make a strong anti-Castro speech when he addresses the Assembly later this month. AMBIDDY will go to New York with Ambassador Guillermo Lang in advance of Somoza.

2. Defer to HQS on contact with AMBIDDY. Feel free to use Barker's name in making contact.

3. No index. File: AMBIDDY-1 201. GP-1.
