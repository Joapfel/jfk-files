June 22, 1969.

Contact Report

Subject: LIBACON-1 (P-4305).

Date of meeting: June 21 at the Cafeteria JET at 1100 hours.

1. This meeting was held in lieu of the prearranged meeting for June 18, which L-1 cancelled by phone. Due to the peculiarities of L-1's working habits, I was not able to reach him by phone until June 20 at 1600 hours, when this meeting was arranged.

Next meeting: Wednesday, July 2, at 1615 hours at the previous pickup place.

2. On June 18, L-1 was without a car and felt obliged to cancel the meeting. He stated that he was happy to have made up with his wife and to be living at home again. He told me about his arrangement to go to the Russian show with LIOVAL-1 and his wife. He described the developments as LIOVAL-1 reported them to me.

3. With regard to his contact with LOGINOV, which he hinted at by phone, there was nothing to it. L-1 was at a party given by Andrea CATANO (P-13,545) at her house on June 14, and YURIY CHIRNISH (P-13,042) was there. They exchanged a few words about LOGINOV (see the attachment). L-1 stated that he had not been with LOGINOV since the encounter in Cuautitla and had not spoken to him since the attempt to reschedule the lunch that was missed on May 9. Therefore, the story L-1 gave to LIOVAL-1 on June 15 about LOGINOV being disappointed with the fact that he had to return home, etc., quite likely has no connection with any recent developments. Further, it is quite obvious that L-1 did not know then that LOGINOV's return was postponed three to four months.

4. L-1 stated that A. CATANO and her mother told him that they had expected EOGATYREV (P-11,351) to attend their party, but he neither came nor excused himself by phone. L-1 did not know how it came about that EOGATYREV was invited to the party. L-1 considered it a significant fact that CERMINISH came to the party with his wife. L-1 interpreted this to mean that CERMINISH gave up the idea of having a romance with ANDREA CATANO, but wanted to preserve and maintain his friendship with her and her family. According to L-1, it is quite certain.
