Ref: A. The Hague 8217, CIN 137093, divorce. B. HMMA 39384.

10 November 11 204131.

1. 5 November Raymond H. Gerende (P), husband of LIEMPTY-19, told us that the 13 October court decision found no grounds to grant divorce. This shocked both Gerende and attorney, in view of the fact LIEMPTY-19 failed to appear for the 5 and 8 August hearings (which normally should forfeit her case by default) and the fact she left Mexico illegally with children. Gerende is appealing the decision, which may take a year to resolve.

2. On recent trip to Arizona to the brother of LIEMPTY-19, Gerende confirmed his suspicion that his wife was in the Netherlands (which we had avoided telling him), and presumably learned her address. From the Dutch Embassy in Mexico, he also learned that the Dutch MFA had been in almost weekly touch with the Embassy on LIEMPTY-19's behalf, attempting to have the Embassy procure an attorney for her.
