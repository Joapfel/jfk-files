4 December 1953

Memorandum for: Chief, IO Division

Subject: “News from Behind the Iron Curtain” — Mail Subscription for Mr. Arvo Tuominen

Reference:

a. OSSA-1638, 20 November 1953

b. Memorandum to WE/PP-1 from IO/Branch I, 8 October 1953

c. Memorandum to IOD/TOMIC from WE/PP-1, 28 September 1953

d. Memorandum to IOD/TOMIC from WE/PP-1, 6 June 1953

1. A recent dispatch from the Stockholm Station (reference a) reports that Mr. Tuominen is not receiving the subject publication. In light of reference memorandum b, which states that he has been on the mailing list since June 1952, it is apparent that there has been some slip-up in the distribution channels of this publication. A check of address indicates that Mr. Tuominen’s address is the same as given in reference d.

2. It is requested that appropriate measures be taken to insure that Mr. Tuominen will receive this publication in the future. Please inform WE/PP-1 of the action taken regarding this matter.

Paul Birdsall  
C/WE
