Dear friend,

I am sending you mine with certificate and at my own marriage with field picture, and I am sending you my daughter with certificate.

I want you to come to me, for I am not going to find the office, but in the letter you sent, say the address is only the P.O. box.

If I have to go to your office, send me the address, and I'll go, please.

I want to thank you for your kindness.

(DS) If you need or want the certificate, you can keep it, because I have another. Thanks.
