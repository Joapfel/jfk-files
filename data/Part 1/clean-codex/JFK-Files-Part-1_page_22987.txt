18. Relatives in service of any government, names and positions held.

James Earl Crozier, Corporal, U.S. Army in Korea.

U.B. Passport, issued San Jose, Costa Rica, August 14, 1950, No. 180 (FS) 119.

20. Religion—degree practiced, membership in religious orders.

Roman Catholic, practiced occasionally.

21. Present and past political affiliations.

Not applicable.

22. Travel outside country of present residence—country, dates, purpose, people and firms visited.

With U.S. Army, departed U.S. in November 1941 and covered Panama, Puerto Rico, Ecuador, Costa Rica; then departed U.S. in November 1944 and covered Australia, India, Burma, China, Iraq, Iran, Egypt, Morocco, Bermuda. Departed U.S. in November 1945 for Panama as a civilian to work for the U.S. Navy at Coco Solo, then departed C.Z. in April 1949 for enrollment at the University of Costa Rica, San Jose, Costa Rica.

23. Education—school, location, course, degree, dates.

Graduated grammar school at Five Points District School, Sycamore, Illinois, 1937.

Graduated high school in DeKalb, Illinois, by virtue of recognition of credits attained through Armed Forces educational work; actually graduated in 1949.

Currently attending University of Costa Rica, third year, Economic Sciences.

24. Languages and dialects.

English: speak—good; read—good; understand—good.

Spanish: speak—good; read—good; understand—good.
