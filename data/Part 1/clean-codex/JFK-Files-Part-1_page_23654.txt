Dispatch. To: Chief, Special Affairs Staff. From: Chief of Station, JEWAVE. Subject: TYPIC/Operational Transmittal of Operational Plan for ANTRONIX VII. Proposed action: Marked for review. No action required. Only qualified Headquarters (ECS) can amend review.

Action required: Request approval. References: A. UFGA-9312, ANTRONIX Op Plan, 1 June 1963; B. UFGA-10436, Amendment of ANTRONIX Op Plan, 21 July 1963; C. UFGA-10523, Op Plan ANTRONIX V, 31 August 1963; D. UFGA-12725, Op Plan ANTRONIX VI, 27 November 1963; E. UFGA-12118, Annexant to ANTRONIX VI Op Plan, 19 December 1963.

Transmitted for approval herewith is the operational plan for ANTRONIX VII. The ANTRONIX team is scheduled for infiltration on 7 February and for exfiltration on 17 February. A five-man team with Intermediate Craft will be infiltrated into northern Batemanas Province, from which point the team leader, AMICE-14, and ANTRONIX-13 will proceed into Lavena to accomplish the MEAPRAN objectives of this operation. Approval to be requested at the earliest possible time.

End of dispatch.

Attachment: Op Plan (3 cys) - H/V. Distribution: S - C/DAS w/attn.
