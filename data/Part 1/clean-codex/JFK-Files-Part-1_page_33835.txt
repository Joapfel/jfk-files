The Dalle told me this am.

The Categrama sent a copy of this letter to the Breuer, together with a letter inquiring, “How come?”

Breuer and Alder hanged yesterday and agreed that Biele was way off base, and Breuer is preparing a letter to Categrama telling him that Biele had my right to attend C., as he did. A copy of this letter will go to Biele for mailing, for his comments.

As Alder put it to me, “It’s in the name of—there’s nothing more for us to do about it at this time.”
