Classified message.

271 LB/WCJ. 14 April 1959.

To Director. From Paris.

Action: SR 6 (TEMPO to SR at 1150, 14 April). Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, FI, FI/OPS, FI/D 3, EE 4, WE 4, S/C 2.

14 April 1959, 1636Z. Operational immediate.

IN 44422.

To DIR. Info: FRAN, FROB, CTE PARI 5705. Re FROB 3977 (IN 42861).

1. FRIGANZA PARI awaiting HQS decision AEASPIC.

2. Requests concurrence paragraph 3. Reference. Regrets note urgency but in circumstances somewhat difficult to manage without early decision.

3. We need safehouse subject using and concur there are definite security and arrest risks if he is quartered other than in safehouse.

End of message.

CS comment: *Action unit determined as through indicator REDWOOD used. **If HQS concurred, FRAN would move subject to PBPRIME via FRAN. Gave details of crossing Franco-German border.

15 April 1959.

SR/DUB. 10 April 1959.
