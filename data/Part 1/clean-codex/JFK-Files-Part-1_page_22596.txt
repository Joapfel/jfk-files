Reference is made to your contract with the United States Government, effective 1 June 1957.

Effective 26 November 1960, the contract is amended by adding thereto the following extract:

“Health Insurance: You are herein authorized to apply for enrollment in a health insurance program for certain selected (SELECTED SELECTOR) of this organization, subject to all the terms and conditions of that program if accepted. This organization is presently authorized to bear a portion of the premium cost; you will bear the remainder. Your financial contribution will be affected either by payroll deduction or by direct remittance at periodic intervals to be established by this organization.”

All other terms and conditions of the contract remain in full force and effect.

United States Government
