Dispatch. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division; Chief, European Division. Info: Chief of Station, WH/Miami; Chief of Station, Madrid. From: CUTINID QUDOZE. Subject: Sonja Gomez Aragon, Cuban MINREX official.

Action required—references. AR: None, FYI. Please assign 201 number. References: A. MADRID 0473; B. WH/MIAMI 6576; C. DIRECTOR 011307.

1. The following information about Subject’s trip to Spain and her activities while here was obtained from CUTINID and QUDOZE.

2. Subject arrived in Madrid on 10 February 1970. She traveled to Barcelona during the week of 23 February and returned to Madrid 6 March, whereupon she then left for Portugal. She returned to Madrid on 17 March and waited for Guillermo Ruiz (R U I Z) Perez’ return from the Spanish/Cuban commercial negotiations in Cuba. Sonia (Sonja) departed Madrid for Cuba on 27 March 1970. It appears that she intended to travel to Vienna, Austria next, since several cables were sent to Vienna from Madrid advising Sonia’s travel status. During her stay in Spain, Sonia was in frequent contact with one Jose Agustin Goytisolo (GOYT ISOLO), Spanish writer in Barcelona, telephone 228-4063. Station traces are OSBA 02385, 17 February 1965; OSBA 02718, 11 August 1967; and OSBA 02795, 1 March 1968. She was not known to have been in contact with any other locals, and what little time she had for diversion she spent with the mission officers and their wives.

Distribution: C/WHD; C/EUR; COS, WH/Miami.

Cross-reference to: 65-2-10/9; 65-6-44/4; 201-735296. Dispatch symbol and number: OSHA-26218. Date: 13 May 1970. HOS file number: 201-.
