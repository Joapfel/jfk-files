L. Expansion of Influence in Iberian Peninsula.

Subject said he knew of no DGI plans in this regard. He said that he knows Guillermo Ruiz Perez well and considers him DGI. (Will debrief subject on Ruiz in New York.) Subject does not know Rafael Fernandez Sardinas. Subject expects Cuban relations with Spain to deteriorate because Cuba voted against an important Spanish resolution at the recent ILO conference in Geneva.
