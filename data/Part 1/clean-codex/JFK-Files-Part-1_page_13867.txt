Memorandum for: Chief, WH/3 Mexico

Via: Chief, WH/PO/A (Mr. Horton)

Subject: General: Debriefing Report No. 59 of AMNUG-1. Specific: Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez (201-332668)

Reference: OTTA 1611 dated 23 April 1964

1. Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez is the Cuban intelligence officer (DCI) stationed in Mexico City. He uses the pseudonyms “Eduardo,” “Casimiro,” and “Jose Antonio.”

2. RODRIGUEZ has the following physical description: age approximately 24 years old, 5'8" tall, husky build, blond hair, and a reddish complexion.
