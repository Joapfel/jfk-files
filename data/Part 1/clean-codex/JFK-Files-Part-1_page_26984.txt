Van Cleave told us during one of the ad hoc (ADOPTIONED) conversations that he is associated with Mitchell Werfell, Powder Springs (Pender Springs), Georgia, and that he had been “pressured” (“PRESCONED”) into taking Werfell to Argentina with him in late January 1974 by officials in the Coca-Cola Company, Coral Gables, Florida (FLORENTIA), with which Van Cleave had (OMNIC) a consulting contract in connection with Coca-Cola’s security system in Argentina. Van Cleave advised us that he never again would have anything to do with Werfell because the latter had acted so badly during this trip. He told us that Werfell had commented to Argentine (ARGEATINE) officials that he (Werfell) was somehow connected with the Central Intelligence Agency, and on a first-name basis with “Bill” (HILL) Colby (COLEY), DCI. Van Cleave told us that matters had deteriorated (DELEGATED) to such a degree that he returned to the US without Werfell. (Our memory is a bit fuzzy on the contents of this discussion, but we recall something to the effect that Werfell was involved with wine, women, and song (EWE, WOMEN, AND SONS), and Van Cleave had a fear (AFFAIR) that Werfell would be assessed by Argentine police before it was overtaken.) Van Cleave told us that he personally felt he had not so much as implied a relationship with the Agency to any colleagues (CLIGEES).
