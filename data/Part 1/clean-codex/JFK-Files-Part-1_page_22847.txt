Joanne Grant has written for “Rights,” a pamphlet sponsored by the Emergency Civil Liberties Committee (ECLC). The subject was among those invited to the ECLC “Bill of Rights” dinner given on 15 December 1954. In October 1953, she contacted the American Committee for Protection of Foreign Born, and in October 1956, the subject’s name and address were in the possession of the Socialist Workers Party. (DFB-92684, 23 October 1957)
