The dispute was settled in favor of M-37-A, which will handle all naval operations against Cuba.

Ramon Orosco and Raul Cabrera (C A B R E-R A) will be chief of naval operations and chief of maintenance and supply, respectively, and Perez the political leader and representative within CORUA.Y.
