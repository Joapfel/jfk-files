Release Contingency OPLAN 52-2

Chart or Map Reference: Sectional aeronautical charts—as required.

Task Organization: Det. 1, 1045th Operational Evaluation and Training Group. Major Billy B. Campbell, Commander.

1. Situation:

Daniel L. Carswell (Alfus), Roderic H. Durbrunt (Alfus), and Edward R. Taransky (Alfus) are TID staff employees who were apprehended by Cuban authorities in September 1960 and who are now serving ten-year sentences. Efforts are being made to secure their release. Since their release may occur without advance notice at a variety of places, i.e., Havana, Miami, Mexico City, Switzerland, Israel, etc., it is imperative that a contingency plan be formulated to secure the receipt of these employees.

The controlling factor in all phases of the release procedure is that these employees were in Cuba under aliases and last during the entire period since their capture. Their missions and other activities apparently have been maintained. Therefore, it is essential that there shall be maintained until such time as the employees have returned to Agency control and then have these aliases about them, lose disorderly as quickly as possible.

For planning purposes, the release schedule will be as follows: City or “Other areas.”

2. Mission:

Det. 1, 1045th Operational Evaluation and Training Group provide black air transportation on part of which COUS to the designated safe area.

3. Execution:

A. Black air transport will be provided under this plan at the request of JIN, CCS Mexico, or Operational Support Districts/US/DOE, depending on the release point and port of entry.
