A CITE NYOR 35U.

Support (Connell) from Major.

Case 19830 X 19830. Subject: Peter Whit.

Subject advises that Robert J. McAllister is leaving the International Refugee Committee and has been given a Ford Foundation grant. I.R.C. is looking for a replacement. If refugee have any suggestion.

Subject was asking that Dave Gestler return the letter he loaned to him as soon as possible since he meant to deliver it. He could also like Gestler to return the ticket status to him as soon as convenient, as we have to have it in order to make claim for his transportation expense from the Vietnam government.

Subject reports that the National Catholic Welfare Organization is stepping up their relief activities in Vietnam beginning next quarter. They plan to do a lot in one quarter as they had done in one year previously. Subject stated that Monsignor Harnett told him that rapport between the U.S. Operations Minister and the Minister for Refugees in the Vietnam government is very poor. Indeed, Harnett is returning to Vietnam either next week or the second after.

6 June 1956

Kc
