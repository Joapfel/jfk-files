252203Z. CITE JMWAVE 9448. Caracas. Info: Director.

PBRUMEN.

FYI: AMCLEVE/15 reports telephone call from Gustavo Ortiz informing him that Erasto (Fernandez) Betancourt will arrive in Miami on TDY 27 September 67. Ortiz stated Fernandez will seek information from Cuban exiles in Miami who may know or have information on Cuban guerrillas in Venezuela. Ortiz asked AMCLEVE/15 to work with Fernandez during his TDY in Miami; AMCLEVE/15 agreed.

25 September 67. 55289.
