Classified message. Date: 8 December 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5. Info: ADDP, C/CA 2, C/C1, C/F1 2, C/MS 2, S/C 2. Routine. IN 32097.

DIR CITE MEXI 2787 IN 32097.  
REF DIR 03898*.  
RYBAT LIENVOY.

1. COS had two hours alone with LITENSOR and LITEMPO/2 on 5 November, at which time LITENSOR was as vigorous as ever. He was thin; but he said this was on doctor's orders to lose weight. COS has noticed LITENSOR much thinner and has aged rapidly in four years in present post; but no evidence of his slowing down.

2. On 8 December COS talked with LIELEGANT, who saw and talked with LITENSOR at a wedding on the night of 6 December. LIELEGANT says LITENSOR is in excellent health.

3. Station will report any indications of ill health.

End of message.

C/S comment: *Desired COS comment on report that LITENSOR in ill health.
