Date: June 1962.

1964. 70.

April 1965. Began work in the Information Department of the Intelligence Unit of the Seventh Fleet.

October 1966.

End of 1951. NOSENKO involved in apprentice work in the Agent-Intelligence Section at Bukhta Postovaya. This involved going out to sea on subchasers to pick up and drop off, and conducting meetings with agents from Hokkaido and Sakhalin. NOSENKO did not actually meet agents, but was merely along to learn how they were picked up at sea.

January–February 1952. Filed action by mail to divorce TELE-GINA in Moscow court and published intention notice in a local Sovetskaya Gavan newspaper in January or February.

NOSENKO was planning to divorce his wife before he left Moscow but did not start proceedings because he was afraid she would bring charges against him in the KOMSOMOL. Sometime in late 1951 or early 1952 he published his notice.
