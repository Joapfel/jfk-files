January 25th, 1968

U.S. Senator Robert F. Kennedy  
110 E. 45th Street  
New York, N.Y.

Senator:

As President of the Mothers, Wives and Sons of the Prisoners Bay of Pigs (Pig's Bay) Association, I beg you and pray to God that it could be possible for you to do something in this particular case. I don't want to make a long letter, because I know how busy you are, but this is a very good opportunity to bring home my husband RICARDO MONTERO DUQUE and the other prisoners left in Cuba after the 1962 trading. At that time, when all the other prisoners belonging to the Bay of Pigs (Pig's Bay) invasion came in the trading, Castro did not want to include them in the list.

The last time I tried to be in touch with you was in 1964 at your office. On that occasion you were out of the city, but your Secretary, Mr. Nolan, was very nice with us, and he told us he was sure you were going to do everything possible to bring them home. It was impossible, and they stayed in Cuba.

Now, you know there is a possible trading of prisoners between President Barrientos (Presidente Barrientos) of Bolivia and Fidel Castro of Cuba. In it are involved prisoners belonging to the CIA Department and others that the Pentagon can include in the trading list. We are sure you will not hesitate to do anything to bring them home, but it is our obligation and duty to try for their freedom.

The possible trading between President Barrientos (Presidente Barrientos) and Castro is because Castro wants all Communist prisoners and the body of Ernesto (Che) Guevara now in Bolivia; that's why he agreed to accept all names that the CIA Department, Pentagon, and President Barrientos (Presidente Barrientos) put in the trading list.

At the same time, I'm writing a letter to the CIA Department, from which you will find an attached copy. Please let me know anything about this and, of course, if this letter got into your hands.

Thank you very much in advance for your attention in this matter. I remain,
