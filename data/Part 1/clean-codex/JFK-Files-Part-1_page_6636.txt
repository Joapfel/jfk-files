26 February 1953

Allen Brench (AFCzajkowski/Acr)

Iron Curtain Refugee Committee

1. On 24 February 1953, Mr. Hulick in the IEP/II's office requested information concerning the Iron Curtain Refugee Committee. Specifically, he wished to learn:

1. What is the relation between this and IFC?

2. Identity of the new man who runs ICRC.

3. Do we have close relations?

2. Czajkowski telephoned the New York Field Office and transmitted the request to Walter Freund.

3. This has been set up as Case 12,667.

Original: Case 12,667.
