1. LITENSOR has asked via LIELEGANT for any briefing papers KUBARK can give on France, Yugoslavia, Poland, West Germany, Holland and England.

2. CURTIS would appreciate anything Headquarters can make available and believes HQS may wish to attempt to influence LITENSOR on certain points in some of these countries.

3. Will need reply soon and, if HQS wishes to prepare papers, appreciates receipt by 20 March.

JEC [Signature]
