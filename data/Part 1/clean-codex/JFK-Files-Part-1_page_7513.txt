GLASS.

1. Shanghai Municipal Police files, filed 16-4-2-37, p. 2.

In Shanghai, GLASS made the acquaintance of one Damaree C.B.II., an American from Kansas City, born in 1933. This was some time after April 1932.

Name: O.P. GLASS, Cecil Frank GLASS (OLASS). Born 25 March 1901, Birmingham, England.

Passport: British passport No. 94153, issued 5 January 1931 in Pretoria, South Africa. Political affiliation: member of the CP. Overt profession: journalist.

Believed to have left England in the early 1920s for South Africa, where he resided in Johannesburg for many years.

Arrived in Shanghai from Dartmoor in May 1931.

On 12 June 1931, made a trip through the Yangtze to Shanghai with Harold ISMUS, an American Communist and writer for the editor of the China Forum. A letter written by ISAACS to his fiancée, Miss Viola ROBINSON, during this trip contained information that ISAACS had decided to turn “Red” as a result of his conversation with GLASS.

In September 1931, GLASS became a member of the “CENTRAL DEFENSE COMMITTEE,” and two months later was appointed Shanghai Representative of the Anglo-Asiatic Telegraph Agency (Angasta), a news agency with headquarters in Hainan, which position he held until 1933.

In August 1933, he was living in Room 510, No. 1276 North Suenchem Road, Shanghai.
