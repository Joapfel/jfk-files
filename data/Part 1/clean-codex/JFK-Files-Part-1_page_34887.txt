January 25th, 1968

To whom it may concern,

In the name of the Mothers, Wives and Sons, Bay of Pigs (Pig's Bay) Prisoners' Convention, I beg you to make your biggest effort to get in touch with the Commission or Department in care, to confectionate the list of names in the possible trading between President Barrientos (Presidento Castro) of Bolivia and Fidel Castro (idel Castro) of Cuba. In order they include the names of the nine prisoners of the Bay of Pigs Convention left in Cuba in the 1962 trading because at that opportunity Castro did not want to trade them. As you know, involved in this trading are prisoners belonging to the CO Department and others that the Pentagon can include in the trading list.

I know always the CIA Department, the Pentagon and the Government of the United States of America have their names in mind. Of course, they want to bring these nine prisoners to freedom. On this opportunity there is a very good chance, because Castro agreed with President Barrientos (B. Trientos) to change all comandante prisoners and the body of Ernesto (Che) Guevara for the prisoners he has in Cuba that will be stated on the trading list.

We are sure you will do your best in order to include the nine Bay of Pigs (Pig's Bay) prisoners left in Cuba in 1962 in the trading list. Please let me know something about it. Also, we are enclosing a copy of the letter we are sending to Senator Robert F. Kennedy asking the same thing.

Thank you very much in advance for your attention in this matter.

I remain,

cc: Senator Robert F. Kennedy
