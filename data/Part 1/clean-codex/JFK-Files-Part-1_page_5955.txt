Ref DIR 13981 (out 9/10/75).

1. AMYUMS reported COC picked up the following: 23 pistols, 6 submachine guns, and one bazooka. Surmise above may have been contents of one bundle which fell on Trinidad Highway. 04T 9437J-D12 12516. IN 42921.

2. Remainder cargo believed in hands of anti-Castro forces. MSALD (FNU) Pacheco, leader of unidentified group, recovered sizable quantity, which he divided with (FNU) Nesa, who also has small independent group. Latter not to be confused with Nying[o] Mesa (NYINGO MESA), whose brother was guide used in AMPANIC-5 operation.

3. Despite abortive drop, AMYUMS feels it had good morale effect on Escambray fighters, who see genuine effort being made to support them. Estimate present anti-Castro element in Escambray numbers 2,000 men. AMYUMS also reported two successful drops to Evelio Duque forces, 3 and 11 November, by AMHAWK using Cessna aircraft. Each drop consisted of 63 submachine guns. Can HOS confirm for INFO HAVA only if such drops actually made.

Filed 15/1410Z.

End of message.

C/S comment: Headquarters has reliable information that at least half of bundles dropped on 6 November were picked up by COC.
