16. Sanchez Gonzalez, Hector (201-2337), borderline Cuban diplomat and suspected DSE officer.

Between 11 and 18 October 1968, Cardoza met Hector Sanchez Gonzalez, who informed Cardoza that he had authority from the Cuban government to do whatever was necessary to reorganize FAR.

17. Solis Barrios (Barrjos), Rafael (201-225483), PGT member.

In 1957, Solis, Cardoza, and their families were photographed together. In the transmittal dispatch, it is not clear whether the Solis and Cardoza families are related.

Curriculum Vitae:

1922: Born in Guatemala.

29 August 1952: Issued an official visa for China.

30 August 1952: Issued an official visa for Mexico.

Issued an official visa for Cuba.

1 September 1952: Traveled via Mexico en route to China by TACA, according to Nuestro Diario (Euestro Diario), 31 July 1952.

5 September 1952: Arrived in Mexico City via TACA Flight 100.

6 September 1952: Departed Mexico City for Miami via Cuest Flight 100, with reservations for connections with EAL Flight 602, from Idlewild to travel on to Zurich.

7 September 1952: Was to depart Idlewild for Zurich via TWA Flight 902. He was traveling without a visa for the United States but with an official Guatemalan passport.

8 September 1952: Arrived in Switzerland.

14 September 1952: Departed Switzerland.

14 November 1952: Arrived in Switzerland.

17 November 1952: Issued an official visa for Mexico.

18 November 1952: Departed Switzerland.

18 November 1952: Arrived in France.

23 November 1952: Departed France.

Arrived in Switzerland.

25 November 1952: Departed Switzerland.

February 1954: Traveled from Guatemala to Mexico City.

29 June 1954: Requested political asylum in the Mexican Embassy in Guatemala.

8 September 1954: Arrived in Mexico at Tapachula, Chiapas, from Guatemala with his family.

14 December 1956: Departed Mexico City for Tapachula, Mexico.

23 December 1956: Arrived in Mexico City from Tapachula.
