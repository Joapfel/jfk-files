Dir info: MEXI, BONN. CITE MAGU 3620. Ref: DIR 25991. 6 April 7858. IN 12890.

1. Station attempts offer services to president frustrated by:

A. Language difficulties with Romero. Spanish speaker called hotel twice 3 April with complete lack of response on part of person who said he was Romero.

B. Attempted contact with Col. Gramos later 3 April and was told by another English-speaking colonel, Cardunas, that Gramos was in Mexico City.

C. Upon calling Romero again 4 April was told he was not registered there.

2. Regret failure to make contact but presume that if president had needed station services he would have had aide call number already provided in MAGU 3494. CIN 90740.
