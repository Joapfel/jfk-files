From his written notes.

On June 28, Subject saw the short American woman, previously known as Rhonin, leaving the Soviet Embassy at about 1130 hours. He did not see her again. She came into the shop to use Subject’s phone, but the phone was out of order. About the same time, he saw two men who appeared to be Americans enter the Embassy. One was white and the other was a Negro. Subject went to pick up some bottles and brandy and then spoke to each other in English. According to Subject, SILNIKOV told him that two Americans had visited the Embassy to see him about visas because they did not want to fight in Vietnam. We have not been able to identify the woman, but the Americans are SILNIAKOV (SILNIKOV). Subject said they left the Embassy at 1157 hours.

They subsequently made two visits to the Embassy. Once in the morning, Subject was seen in the early afternoon.

Subject said that SILNIKOV told him that they wanted visas. But after an hour or two, SILNIKOV knew nothing about these visitors and SILNIKOV was no longer on the main gate of the Embassy.

The next day, SILNIKOV dropped by and made the comments above. He also told Subject that Viktor KALYGIN had been placed on the main gate once more, confirmed by the basehouses, and that he, SIL, was “officially” the administrative officer. He said he could now find time to eat Mexican food with Subject and said he would be by the following day about 0000 hours.

Saturday, 25 June. SILNIKOV came in about 0000 hours and ate lunch at his house. CARMINITA fixed him some special Mexican food for an hour or so before Silva’s Day. He was there for about half an hour. Alexander LOGINOV came in and ordered 75 persons’ worth of Border Artisan’s food delivered that same day by 1100 hours. Subject was then delivered at 1000 hours. Alexander arrived in his car, and at 1000 hours LOGINOV called Subject and said, “Three more cases of code.” The call was received by Subject’s secretary. “It is already there,” and asked Subject to send some personnel (人员) to the Embassy.
