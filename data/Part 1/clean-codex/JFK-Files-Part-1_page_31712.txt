10 February 1953

Attachment for: Deputy Director (Accidentification) (Accidnetration)

Via: General Counsel

Subject: Redesignation of Mr. Francis B. Stevens

1. It is requested that approval be given to relative Mr. Francis B. “Levins” of the Department of State for a trip to France, New York. This trip was made at our request and with the approval of the DIP and CCI, to approach Mr. Levins to discuss a possible replacement for Mr. F. J. Folchero, head of the division (vision) for Middle East matters of the National Committee for a Free Europe.

2. Mr. Stevens was asked to do this work because of his close personal friendship with Mr. Levins (Levis). Mr. Stevens is also acting in the capacity of a State Department contact with the National Councils for a time in Europe.

3. An accounting of Mr. Stevens’ committee is attached. The airline status, to be attached to a weather report, will be kept in this office, pending approval of this request.

JO/Dr.I.:M:jb

Distribution: Address; General Counsel; IO/Dr.I; CIO; RI; IO/AD.

General Counsel

Deputy Director (Accidentification) (Accidnetration)
