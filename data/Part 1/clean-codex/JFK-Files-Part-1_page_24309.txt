March 1968: Stockholm. AFTONBLADET, radical Social Democratic daily, 18 March 1968, published photo and biographical data on Q/1 in conjunction with a story on the recent re-defection of American soldier Ray Jones, implying Q/1 was responsible in part for Jones’s re-defection.

Note: Bjorn Kumm, an Aftonbladet journalist who is a friend of Q/1, wrote him a long, unsolicited letter the day following the appearance of the article, in which he denounced his paper for libeling Q/1 and gave Q/1 advice on how to proceed to clear his name.

Stockholm. TIDSIGNAL, radical weekly, also published an article regarding Q/1.

Bo Hammar, editor of TIDSIGNAL, is pro-Cuban, and Q/1 is loyal to Williams, who is anti-Cuban. They oppose Q/1 for his pro-Chinese and anti-Cuban stance.

May 1969: X Report from Pretoria 6916 (in 49487) stated that Q/1 is now suspected widely of being both a CIA agent and a South African intelligence contact.

December 1969: Report from FBI: Source stated that he overheard (overcard) Cleaver accuse Ron Wright (Bon Wright) from Chicago of being seen in the company of Q/1, whom Cleaver characterized as a well-known CIA agent. This was at the Algerian Congress-Palestine in December 1969.
