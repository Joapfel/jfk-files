Classified message. From: Mexico City. Action: W/18. Unit: NH. Time: 7:30. By: BZ. Info.

12 May 65. IN 64702.

Priority. DIR CITE MEXI 3370.

No night action.

1. LIFEAT coverage: Rico Galan from 0625 6 May to 0620 7 May.

A. Jovan Miric and Rico: Miric says he had an appointment at 7:00 with his source in the Office of the Presidency. It was rumored that Mexico was going to break with OAS, but this did not happen.

Rico expresses hope there will be a declaration along these lines today.

Miric says this is no longer possible. He has a copy of the text of the President's speech, which was written 48 hours ago. He quotes: “Mexican government is united in the conviction that Dominicans and only Dominicans are the ones to decide their form of government and their future.” Rico says good, but why are Carrillo Flores (CARPILLO FLORES) and De la Colina acting like S.O.B.'s? Rico thinks the only thing to do is break with OAS. Miric advises he will be busy with CEPAL the next few days.

B. Rico and Rodrigo Asturias (presumably the Guatemalan student leader): The latter expressed secretly that he did not see Rico yesterday.
