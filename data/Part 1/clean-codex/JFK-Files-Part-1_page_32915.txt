19 May 1967. M71143.

192024Z. Cite JMWAVE 7202. Director, Madrid.

KAPOK TYPIC MRGUILE AMSTALK.

Ref: JMWAVE 7188 (ON 67460).

1. Per ref, on 16 May at station direction AMSTALK-1 sent telegram to LUIS-supplied address stating, “Have vacation mid-June. Need assurance meeting possible before planning costly trip. Confirm. Guillermo.” (Guillermo signature indicated meet to be in Spain.)

2. On 18 May AMSTALK-1 received telegram from Havana, addressed to his home, reading, “All well. Guillermo.”

3. Station interprets prompt answer as requested confirmation. Thus station plans send AMSTALK-1 and JERSAWI Madrid mid-June and, per contact arrangements outlined in LUIS letter LIED JMWAVE 6942, another telegram will be sent giving precise location.

4. EGSWIRL administered AMSTALK-1 on 17 May. No deception indicated.
