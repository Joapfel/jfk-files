1 June, continued.

17.10 hours: Mark Schleifer calls from the Embassy to Yepe's flat, speaks to Yepe's wife, Galina, and says he, “Maria and the children” will spend the night with the Yepes. His wife and children are at present in Pablo's flat. Both flats are in Park West.

17.30 hours: Mark Schleifer was called at the Embassy by Richard Gibson, who suggests their sharing a flat in Algiers. Both need to brush up their French before “the others pour in” to Algiers. Mark agrees to go to Richard's flat, 50 Stockleigh Hall, Prince Albert Road, to discuss various points. Mark mentions that his wife is an “Afro-American.” Richard says that integration is much worse here than in the U.S.A.

2 June.

11.00 hours: Yepe calls the C.T.E., speaks to the telex room, and asks whether they can send a telex to Yepe's brother in Rumania. He explains that his brother is Cuban Ambassador to Rumania and is due to go to Cuba today or tomorrow. He wants first to establish whether his brother is still in Rumania. If he is, Roberto wants the following message passed: “If you see Leo Hubbevan (HUBBEVAN) in Cuba, please pass this message to me—‘If you come to London, Dennis Phombeah is anxious to see you.’ (Signed) Roberto Yepe.”

3 June.

12.30 hours: Pablo calls the Cuban Embassy, believed to be in Paris, obtained by direct calling. He speaks to Esther, then asks for Paco, who mentions that Guillermo has to return to Cuba because his daughter has a cerebral haemorrhage. Pablo says he and Kamija are also “going to return.” Paco asks, “But they haven't called you?” Pablo replies, “No.” He gives no further explanation but will write to Paco. He mentions that Mark Schleifer is coming to Paris and will telephone Paco. Guillermo, Paco remarks, has already caught his plane to Prague. Miriam is alone. Guillermo had been in touch with Franqui, and Roa had agreed to his going. This leaves the Commercial Attaché.
