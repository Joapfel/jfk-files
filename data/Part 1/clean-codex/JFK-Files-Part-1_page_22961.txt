Fishing, fresh water and sea; sailboating; hunting; archery; football in US and USAP; track, FS and USAP; high jump; broad jump; discus. National record in Guatemala, 1954, in discus. Pistol (expert). Chess. Jungle lore. Photography—freelance and commercial.
