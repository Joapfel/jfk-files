Security, Information

30 January 1953

Memorandum for: Chief, International Organizations Division

Subject: Telephone Conversation with Jean McKnight, Department of State

1. Mr. McKnight called to report that Mr. Herbert Pierce from the UN Public Affairs Office is very disturbed about the conduct of “some RFE man” in New York, who is approaching members of the US Mission to the United Nations and asking them all sorts of questions, for example, their views on the recent Doctors’ Trial.

2. Mr. McKnight feels that this practice should be discontinued, especially in view of the fact that the new members of the US Mission are not yet fully informed about, or sympathetic to, RFE (RFU). He feels that any questions concerning US policy on particular issues should be cleared with Mr. Kretzman, the VOA Policy Chief in New York.

3. I think this matter ought to be discussed with Bob Lange. Would you let me know whether you will take it up with him, or whether you wish me to do so.

ELLEN STERN  
IO/TROPIC
