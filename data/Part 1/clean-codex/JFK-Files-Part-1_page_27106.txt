Re: Freedom of Information Request—Notice of Charges

Dear Mr. Wilson:

Before exercising my right to a Freedom of Information request for copies of any and all available, in accordance with the declassification processes initiated in Executive Order 11959 and its amendment of January 1974, files of records and documents, both computerized and manual, except as to my activation under my present and former names and reissues, I hereby request the complete and final estimate of charges for search and duplication services.

In regard to the charges for these services, two matters may be worthy of consideration. The first of these is this: given the proper access codes, the execution of a computer search is neither difficult nor costly. The second is that, in light of the controversial nature of my past, a waiver or reduction of charges may contribute to the furtherance of the public interest. I therefore respectfully request that those factors be considered in the making of a decision as to the cost of services.

In compliance with your request for identity verification, pursuant to provisions of the 1974 Privacy Act, section 532, subsections b and d, I hereby state before a notary public my name and former names, my recent address, my date and place of birth, and my citizenship.
