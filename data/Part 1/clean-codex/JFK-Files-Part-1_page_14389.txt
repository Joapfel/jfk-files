1. On 12 June Guillermo Ruiz dictated a report for the disappearance of AMMARTINI-1. Among other things, the report said the following: Subject demonstrated he was incapable of handling the job; had no knowledge of foreign trade. CESETA officials so informed, and Crestes Ruiz of CESETA had admitted these facts. Francisco Leon had been chosen to replace subject. No evidence of political deviation, however, except on one occasion he had spent too much on clothes for celebration of Fiesta Nacional. His wife did not attend Women's Federatic (Women's Federation) meetings and was not normal. Subject was superficial with his suppliers. What is more, he drank in excess. After learning subject's “traitorial” analysis showed he had planned the act in advance: items were that one of his children left the country; his wife had remarked that she had done much for the revolution, now the revolution owed her something; and subject's distance (or strangeness) with respect to his nephew, Orlando de la Muez.
