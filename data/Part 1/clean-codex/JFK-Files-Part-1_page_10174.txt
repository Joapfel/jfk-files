Date: 28 Dec 62. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: TFW 10. Info: DDP, CA 2, CA/PEG, CA/O 4, CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, FI, FI/INT 2, WH 7, S/C 2.

Classified message.

Dec 28, 2104Z 62. Routine.

IN4J878

DIR CITE MEXI 2948

GYROSE LITAMIL-7

Ref MEXI 2904 (N 39430).

On 21 Dec 62, CO not able make contact LITAMIL-7, who unexpectedly visited by Gladys Lechuga, wife of Cuban ambassador, at scheduled time meeting CO and L-7. Wife Lechuga, who accompanied by parents, stayed two hours at home of L-7.

2. On 27 Dec 62, CO and LITAMIL-3 went to home of L-7, where she reported that on that afternoon she and Ramon Sinobas Casado made out Cuban diplomatic list for 1963, which was forwarded to Foreign Office. L-7 showed CO copy of list. Cuban diplomats listed in the following precedence: Carlos Lechuga Hevia (absent), Ramon Sinobas Casado, Andres Armona Ramos, Teresa Proenza Proenza, Luis Alberu Souto, Manuel Vega Perez, Pedro Lara Muñoz, Jose Antonio Garcia Lara, Benigno Castellar Ramirez. L-7 queried Sinobas regarding Garcia’s status, and Sinobas told her to put him on list as he is expected to return.

3. Although wife Lechuga stayed two hours at home of L-7 per paragraph 1, L-7 said she did not talk politics nor discuss husband entire evening.
