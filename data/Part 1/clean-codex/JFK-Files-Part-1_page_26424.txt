TDCS-3/570,355. IN GCF755.

Jamaica via Pan American. Tavarez was killed by the Dominican Armed Forces on 21 December 63. No one has yet been named to replace him. Emma Tavarez, however, was unable to get them for him. Rico also requested the photographs of Rafael Herrera, director of Listin Diario, but Herrera refused to release them.

Source comment: The photographs had been taken as the bodies were removed from a common grave, where they had been stacked crosswise. All the victims had been shot through the head. The back of Tavarez's head had been torn away by gunfire. He had also suffered a knife or bayonet wound in the stomach. The pictures, which have never appeared in the press, are being held in the archives of Listin Diario.
