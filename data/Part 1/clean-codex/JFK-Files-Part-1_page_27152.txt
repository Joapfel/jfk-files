PERS 73-2171. DD/M&S 73-2347.

14 June 1973.

Memorandum for: Director of Personnel. Subject: Consultant Appointments.

William Broc and Kenneth Greer have been closely involved with preparation and review of documents and other material concerning the Agency's alleged involvement in the Watergate affair. In order that their personal knowledge and assistance may continue to be available, it is requested that they be employed as Consultants to the Director, effective upon their retirement.

Harold L. Brownman, Deputy Director for Management and Services.
