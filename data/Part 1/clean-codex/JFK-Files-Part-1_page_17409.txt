Classified message. Date: 022, 20 July 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5. Info: DCI, DDP, ADDP, C/CA 2, C/CI, C/CI/IC, C/FE 6, S/C 2. 21 July 0405Z 1962. Routine. IN 32427.

DIR CITE MEXI 1469

RYBAT

REF DIR 22662 (orig 53750)*

1. Points in reference taken up night 18 July with LITENSOR in meeting attended by LIELEGANT.

2. LITENSOR obviously not repeat not happy about having got himself into position of possible visits. LIELEGANT, after meeting, expressed belief LITENSOR will not make visits, will find reasons postpone.

3. LITENSOR asked COS for:

A. Briefing papers on communism and important personalities who are Communist or pro-Communists in Indonesia, Japan and India. He referred to papers given him prior to his visit to South America in January 1968.
