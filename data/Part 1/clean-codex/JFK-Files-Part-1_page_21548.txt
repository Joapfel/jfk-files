Operational Memo # N-4

Subject: NOSENKO, Yuri Ivanovich

The following is a typed copy of a handwritten memorandum furnished by the subject on 1 November 1967, following a request on 31 October 1967:

What were the motive and the reasons which have led me to the decision to break with Soviet Russia? The only definite is an understanding of the situation in Soviet Russia, knowledge of the methods of the communist regime, knowledge of the real foreign and interior policies of the Soviet government, and faith in the rightness of the free world.

It was not a decision which was accepted or could be accepted in a month or a year. This decision was slowly growing in me. I think that the beginning was in my studentship.

Living with my parents and being in the circles of my parents' and my acquaintances, I knew more than there was written in newspapers and periodicals and that was propagandized by radio and TV. Working in the Far East and later taking trips to different regions and cities of Russia, I found out much better the life and conditions of the people of Soviet Russia.
