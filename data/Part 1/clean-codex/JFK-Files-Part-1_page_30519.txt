Headquarters, Field Research Unit, Far East Command, Yokohama (Yokohima), Japan

22 October 1951

AC 201 - PERS

LO 295-1

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individuals Concerned

1. The following named individuals of this organization are authorized to proceed from this station on or about 25 October 1951 to Sapporo (Supporo), Japan, and/or such other places as are necessary, for a period of temporary duty of approximately five days in connection with the activities of this headquarters. Upon completion of temporary duty, individuals are to return to their proper station.

Mr. William E. Duggan (GS-15)  
Mr. Grant A. Fielden (GS-13)  
Mr. Newton S. Miller (GS-12)  
Mr. Arseny G. Yamkovsky (GS-12)

2. Travel by military and/or commercial air, rail, and/or motor is authorized. Civilian travel is directed as necessary in the government’s interest and will be performed in accordance with standard and government travel regulations and/or applicable statutes. Direct costs will be paid by and/or reimbursement secured from the finance officer of this organization only. No other disbursing agent is authorized to effect payment.

John F. Hook  
Executive Officer
