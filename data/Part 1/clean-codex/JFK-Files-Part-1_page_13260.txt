021356Z CITE CARACAS 8227. 2 Nov 67. N 85222.

Classified message. Director. Ref: Director 48508.

1. Appreciate prompt HQS action.

2. Rumor that Leandro Mora will shift from Ministry of Interior to Foreign Affairs and be replaced by Manuel Mantilla is about a month old. While not impossible, believe such a change probably not in the wind.

3. Orlando Garcia mentioned in para 3 ref is Orlando Garcia Vasquez (201-67560), who formerly worked very closely with Erasto Fernandez in DIGEPOL. Since Erasto is reportedly Carlos Andres Perez's creature, Garcia Vasquez could have been sent for by either Perez or Fernandez. Most probable that Garcia Vasquez is being recalled to form part of Fernandez's team, as has AMCLEVE 15. It is not beyond the realm of possibility, however, that Carlos Andres Perez may have recalled him to handle politically oriented work requiring Garcia Vasquez's particular talents.

Conclaves.
