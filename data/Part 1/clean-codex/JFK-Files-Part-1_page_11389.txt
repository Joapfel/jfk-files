To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: TOW TO. Info: CUP, CI/GPS, CI/IC 2, CI/CA, WH 7, S/C 2.

Air info have site HALI 1956. CIRCUS. Report: 1955 (out 9/8/61). NEXI 20-6 (in 4/3/54). Other notes: (1) (W. H.); (14) (W. H.).

1. Luis Alberu Souto, Cuban cultural attaché, NEXI. Born circa 1915, Oviedo, Spain. Father: Manilino Alberu, born Cosmo Finance. Mother: Josefina Souto, born La Coruña, Spain. Father living Nava. No further data mother. In 1940 married Xclara Vera Racz Stross. Born Vienna, Austria, British citizen. Divorced 1956. No children. Occupation: artist. Has been employed by Cuban Foreign Service since June 1959. Salary December 40, including representation allowances: $202.00 “take none” pay. Present address: Nichelet 7 301, Mexico, D.F. Brothers: Alberto, married Concepcion “Conchita” Fernandez, Castro secretary, legal, now living Mexico, formerly employed by Cuban government. Has two sisters, one living Vigal, where one Cuban consul [is in] that city. Speaks Spanish frequent. (Survey: LIKAYAN-2, LINCOLN-1, LITAMIL-10, 3/2).

2. Station traces several years back. LITAMIL-1, LITAMIL-2, LITAMIL-3, LITAMIL-7 all consider Alberto anti-Communist. Order (LINOLEE).
