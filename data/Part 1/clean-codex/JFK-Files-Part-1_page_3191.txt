Reference: MONTEVIDEO 8705

Transmitted herewith for SS Division are copies of the passports of Soviet couriers Viktor Sergeyevich MIRIN and Petr Leontyevich SAMOYLOV (Diplomatic 12303 and 12133 respectively). These documents were obtained by BIOGENESIS Travel Controls. MIRIN and SAMOYLOV first transited Buenos Aires on 8 September on reference Alitalia flight from Montevideo to Santiago. They did not deplane but delivered four packages to local Soviet Embassy officials who boarded. (BIOGENESIS did not identify the officials.) They retransited Argentina on 15 September via KLM 792 from Santiago to Montevideo and again remained on the plane. Purdy Furyevich MOSKOV (201-824296) and Ivan Gavrillovich ALFARAYEV (201-236701) boarded to deliver four packages. (This is perhaps the first time that ALFARAYEV, known KGB, has not a courier flight. The explanation for his having done so may rest in the fact that Consul Antanas Igoovich YERASHEMAS (201-779979), the suspect KGB officer who usually has this job, suffered a serious heart attack on 19 July and has been confined to his apartment since.)

Him T. MARGANIS
