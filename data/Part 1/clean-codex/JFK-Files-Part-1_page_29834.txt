15 March 1950

Finance Officer

CO3 – Karlsruhe (Karlruhe)

Administrative

Unpaid debt to AMEXCO

1. Request that the undersigned be given the sum of $4.85 in Military Payment Certificates for the purpose of paying AMEXCO, Karlsruhe, Germany.

2. This amount represents an unpaidimmer reserve debt (Paris to Karlsruhe) of 75,000,000, dating back to 26 December 1949.

3. Amount to be T/A'd to Washington.

Norah T. Craveen

Distribution: Finance; Registry; A & S Unit.
