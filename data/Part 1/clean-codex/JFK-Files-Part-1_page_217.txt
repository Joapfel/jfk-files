27 January 1978

Memorandum for: SA/DO/O

From: H. Clark Schor  
Chief, Plans and Review Group  
Central Cover Staff

Subject: House Select Committee on Assassinations (HSCA) Request

References: A. Memo for SA/DO/O from C/CCS/PRG, dated 25 January 1978, same subject.

B. Memo for SA/DO/O from OLC (OLC 78-0070/1), dated 12 January 1978, same subject.

C. Letter for OLC from Chief Counsel/Director of HSCA, dated 6 January 1978.

1. This memorandum supplements Reference A and provides the additional information per paragraph 6 therein, in response to the request in Reference B.

2. CCS files contain information on individuals and entities cited in certain numbered items of Reference C as follows:

a. Item 5 — George DeMohrenschildt — five documents comprising four contact reports with a commercial asset who was in touch with Subject regarding a business activity in Haiti, and one memorandum detailing the results of a trace request on Subject. The dates of the documents range from 25 April to 21 March 1963.

b. Item 9 — Leo Cherne — 21 documents (six from 1954; one from 1955; two from 1957; two from 1958; one from 1960; four from 1962; and one each from 1965, 1967, 1968, 1976, and 1977), all of which concern the establishment, maintenance, and use of cover provided by Subject from 1954 to 1965 for a NOC officer assigned to Paris. Additionally, CCS maintains a file on the Research Institute of America, of which Leo Cherne was Executive Director, comprising approximately 100 documents covering the period 1949 to 1964; Mr. Cherne's name appears throughout this file.
