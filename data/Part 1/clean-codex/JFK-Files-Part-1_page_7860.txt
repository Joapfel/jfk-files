1. Per LIENVOY on 17 October, Foreign Office official told LOGINOV he received a cable from MEXEMP (MEXEMB) in Moscow asking for a visa for Oleg M. Nechiporenko (201-335370), who is replacing YATSKOV. LOGINOV asked him to expedite the visa, since “we have been waiting for it for several days.” Official asks when YATSKOV is leaving. LOGINOV answers he cannot leave until after Nechiporenko arrives.

2. Both LOGINOV and PRIKHODKO, on return from respective home leaves, told LICOZY-5 that Nechiporenko would return to Mexico in October. Both said that he would not return to consular work but would be given other work in the Embassy. Note that YATSKOV, on return from home leave in September 1966, quit his consular job and “was given other work in the Embassy,” per LOGINOV to LICOZY-5.

3. In view of the above and absence of other candidates, it appears Nechiporenko will take over YATSKOV’s KGR job.
