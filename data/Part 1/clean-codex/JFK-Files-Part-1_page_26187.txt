Victor RICO Galán (Galan). This tape consisted mainly of an exhortation for unity between the PG&T and the 13 November Group, and counseled against letting doctrine replace action. RICO's talk to the guerrillas under YON Sosa (Bosa) reveals him as an intellectual radical with a pragmatic approach toward current situations. The text of the tapes indicates that they were made between February and mid-October 1964. On 13 January 1966, El Heraldo, a Mexico City newspaper, published an article quoting RICO Galán's taped speech as evidence of his efforts to undermine the January 1968 visit of Díaz Ordaz (DIAZ Ordaz) to Guatemala. The text of the tape was possibly passed to El Heraldo by the Mexican authorities in line with the government's campaign to neutralize all potential and existing opposition to the impending visit.
