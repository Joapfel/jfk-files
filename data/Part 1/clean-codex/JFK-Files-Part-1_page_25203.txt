United Press International

Mr. Nicholas Daniloff

Position (January 1965): Member, United Press International bureau in Moscow, USSR.

Very nice, intelligent man. Not Jewish. Overworked by Shapiro, who tends not to like him. Very sick when he left Moscow (circa March/April 1965). Saw a good many Russians and tended to be well informed. Has good promise as a journalist.

15 September 1966
