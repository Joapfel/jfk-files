Mexico

September 27, 1969

1. Talking to various people, such as Guillermo Tardiff, no information has been circulated regarding the fact that Carrillo Flores did not attend the interview of Raul Roa, foreign minister of Cuba, and President Díaz Ordaz (Diaz Urdaz).

2. By going to the General Assembly of the United Nations, Carrillo Flores, secretary of Foreign Relations, is out of the presidential race for constitutional reasons.

According to people who may be considered knowledgeable about politics, Carrillo Flores never had a chance to be nominated because of his close associations with United States bankers.

2. On September 18, I went to the national day celebration at the Chilean Embassy, where I met Kazantsev, who invited me to go to their embassy on October 1 for a farewell party for Valentin Longuinov, who is leaving soon. The new Czechoslovakian (Chilecoslovenian) ambassador was with Kazantsev. I arrived late, and many people had already left. Wallace Stuart was there, the new Rau ambassador, the Ghanaian ambassador, the Paraguayan chargé d'affaires, and several other diplomats of minor importance. The Chilean colony seems to be quite large.
