Outgoing Classified Message. Central Intelligence Agency. Santiago. To: Special Operations. From: WHD. Date: 29 August 1951. Out 71942. WASH 04857. To: SAGOF. Re: SAGO 395 (IN 21787). Cite: W-SHF.

This Agency had no knowledge of plans for additional defector prior receipt SAGO 395. Matter being checked and you will be informed of circumstances, but Headquarters will oppose sending more disposal cases to Chile at this time.

V.L. Gresham. HJU. MJC. H.J. McKellops.
