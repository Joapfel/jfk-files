REF: Memo of 4 August 77 from LEGATT Buenos Aires forwarded by TM.

1. Please pass the following comments to LEGATT Scherrer keyed to his reference memo on debriefing Rolando Otero Hernandez:

1) No station, DAQ traces on Major (FNU) Torres.

2) No station traces Marcelo (Marcell[o]) Estobel or Estrobel (PH). The only name close is one Mario Estibil (SIC) Mahuida, who was described in February 72 as a first-class detective who entered the service in 1954.

3) No station traces Alfredo Villavaca.

4) There are several parachute clubs in Santiago, but we have not yet identified one called “Los Scorpiones.”

5) This is probably a reference to Tobalaba air strip, which is located on the southern outskirts of Santiago.

6) No traces on Captains Juan Williams or Alejandro Royal, in addition to those passed to you during your recent visit here.
