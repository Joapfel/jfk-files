130237Z JUL 69 CITE MANAGUA, 7841 (MILA) MAR 13 JUL 69 NO 01386.

Immediate: Director, San Salvador, Tegucigalpa.

1. Following from AMBIDDY-1 to COS 12 July.

AMBIDDY received phone call 18 July from (CU) Alvarez, Cuban businessman, San Salvador. Alvarez doubletalked that he was calling on behalf of Salvadoran MINDEF, which urgently needs six P-51 pilots (“Mustang drivers”) and will probably also need some “heavy truck drivers,” which AMBIDDY says may mean pilots for larger planes or tank drivers.

Alvarez said that MINDEF would like to have AMBIDDY serve as technical advisor in upcoming operations.

2. AMBIDDY wisely deferred, said needed time to think matter over, explained that he needs get along with both countries, Salvador and Honduras, for business reasons. Is expecting another call from Alvarez momentarily.
