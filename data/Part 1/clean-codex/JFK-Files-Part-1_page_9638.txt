Official Dispatch

Dispatch No. T2P-9-102

Via: Air

Classification Date: 20 October 1947

To: FBT  
From: T2P new  
Subject: General—Operations

Specific—Alfonso Rudolph WICHTRICH, alias Al WICHTRICH

1. Subject is a United States citizen born 30 December 1915 in Mexico. He is a Catholic and the owner of a house located near Radio Station HOX in Golf Heights, R. de P., which he purchased from Jack Fidencue. His business partner in the Pan American Industries Co. Inc. is George M. AKLAS, who is Secretary and Manager of the company. Other key men are Enrique Itefavre, Vice-President; and Amadeo Lupi, Treasurer. He speaks fluent Spanish and appears to be getting along very well with the more influential Panamanians. The telephone number of the Pan American Industries Co. Inc., chartered for bottling Royal Crown Cola, where WICHTRICH has his office is Panama 3490-A, located at Calle 30 Justo Ariasemena. His office hours are 0900 to 1200 and 1430 to 1730. His post office address is Box 1121 Acon, Canal Zone. It is believed he was decorated by the Mountain government for his service in China.

2. His wife is Rachel E. WICHTRICH, also a U.S. citizen. She was born 13 February 1917 in Avoca, Texas. She is the holder of U.S. passport No. 146432. Her brother is believed to be employed by the Federal Bureau of Investigation.

3. WICHTRICH is the father of two children, a girl approximately age 7 and a boy of about 4. He is planning a business trip to Mexico City, Mexico, on 2 November 1947. He will be accompanied by his wife and remain in Mexico until 15 November, at which time he will return to Panama.

4. Subject states there is a complete file concerning him in the hands of the Military Intelligence Division of the Department of the Army. It would be appropriate if you could obtain their file and forward all pertinent information to this station.
