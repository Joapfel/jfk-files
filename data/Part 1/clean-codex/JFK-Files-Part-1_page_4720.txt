01 August 78.

Country: Venezuela/Cuba/Netherlands Antilles.

Date: Late August 1977.

Subject: Intercepted message for Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch (B O S C H), possible exile activities in Curacao.

ACG: Venezuela, Caracas (30 August 1977). Field No. MVC-773B.

Source: A Venezuelan businessman with a wide range of contacts with Venezuelan security officials. His reliability was not been established. This information should not be discussed with officials of any foreign government, including those of Venezuela.

1. In late August 1977 Venezuelan security authorities intercepted a coded message which was being smuggled into the prison cell of Orlando Bosch (B O S C H), who is currently under detention in Venezuela in connection with the 6 October 1976 bombing of a Cubana airliner. The following is the text of the note:

“Activities suspended, we await instructions.”
