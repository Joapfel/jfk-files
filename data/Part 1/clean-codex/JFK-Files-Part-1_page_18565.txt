13 April 1972

Memorandum for: Calvin W. Hicks  
Subject: Quality Step Increase

1. I was pleased to learn that you have been granted a Quality Step Increase. Each recognition is proof of the high esteem in which you are held by your supervisors in the Clandestine Service.

2. Please accept my personal best wishes. I am confident that your future performance will be of a continuing high quality.

 /S/ Cord Moyer, Jr.  
For Thomas H. Karamessines  
Deputy Director for Plans
