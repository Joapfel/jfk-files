Official Dispatch via Air.

Dispatch No. 8-34-630. Class: SA/PT. Date: 08 April 1958.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
From: Chief, WSD.  
Subject: Special—Operational; Special—LIFETAT Special.
