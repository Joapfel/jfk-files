1. Following are station and Sligo traces subjects ref:

A. Domingo Obrer (OBRERQI) has no station traces.

B. Syla Cuervo (C U E R V OI), probably identical with Syla Roque Quervo Castillo, DPOB 29 October 1928, San Luis, Pinar del Rio, Cuba. He is a lawyer and worked for Cuban Ministry of Labor 1945–57; at National Transportation Corps 1959–68, he was a member of the 26 July Movement in Cuba and in November 1959 was described by the Cuban press as interventor of Havana metropolitan (METROPOLITIAN) buses. Subject arrived in the U.S. in April 1961. In Miami he became a member of the Bay of Pigs force and has belonged to other anti-Castro exile organizations. However, Laergo (LAERGO) reported in October 1964 that he was possibly a leader of a group accused of anti-U.S., pro-Nazi, pro-socialist, and pro-Communist statements.
