Proposed Biography

Broe, William V.—born in Amesbury, Massachusetts, August 21, 1913; Amesbury High School, graduate; Governor Dummer Academy; Bowdoin College, A.B. 1939; rubber company, 1939–41; U.S. Government, 1942–51, appointed Foreign Service. Staff officer of class three and assigned as attaché at Manila February 21, 1941; married
