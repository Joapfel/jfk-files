RICO Galan, Victor. Spanish refugee writer, nationalized in Mexico. In Mexico defends Fidel (Videl) Castro and his revolution. Source: newspaper “Hoy,” 26 January 1960, page 7.

Correspondent in Cuba (Cuta) of the Mexican magazine “Siempre” who interviewed the President of the Republic of Cuba. Source: newspaper “Evolución,” 6 December 1962, page 1.

Mexican PP-57115. Trip Mexico–Havana, 25 May 1963. Source: passenger list “Amigos.”

Trip Mexico–Havana on 29 January 1964, PP-Mexico 53641-Courteay.

Trip Mexico–Havana on 29 March 1965. No. 1: HT 465—PP-20062 Mexico. Tourist.

Source on these two trips: CCA passenger list.
