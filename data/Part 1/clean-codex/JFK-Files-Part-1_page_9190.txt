Publication of a book of photos with text exposing the evils of Stalin and his regime throughout his career.

a. Great progress has been made in collecting the books, by E. B. H. Headley and a collaborator. They are planning a book of 175–195 pages, with some 600 photos.

b. The publisher is proceeding for a September 10 press date and has lined up a Chicago press.

U.S. Slim
