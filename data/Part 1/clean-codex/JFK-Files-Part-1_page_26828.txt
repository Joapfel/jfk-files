Origin: Alvin H. Hall. Unit: VP/2 Guat. Extension: 5801. Date: 19 January 1965.

Message Form. Index. No Index. File (if C3 file no.). Routing.

To: Guatemala. From: Director. Conn.: CH/8. Info: CI, CI/OPS, FI, Filipina, VR, File.

19 January 1965, 15Z. Routine.

To: Guat. Ref: GUAT 2499 (IN 61,38). Cite DIR.

Concur para two ref. No need to use unilateral assets to surface Rico Galan (Onlan) tapes in Guat.

End of message.

Comment: Since the Rico Galan (Onlan) tapes were acquired from the Guatemalan Army and permission to release them to the Mexican Government was requested unduly and by the Station and granted by the Guatemalan Government, the Station points out that it might be insecure to surface them in Guatemala using unilateral Station assets.

Desmond Fitzgerald, Chief, WHD.

WH/POA D-. WH/1, W. E. Anderson.
