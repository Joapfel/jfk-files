RYOT met REDTOP after all the facts (OS 2.44), 7 March 1968.

Ref: BULBOS AIRS 13-1 (10/1/94).

1. On 6 May, AMERLAK I accompanied CASTAGNA to the Soviet trade delegation in order to deliver a refrigerator and three air-conditioner units. They were received by Viktor Nikolay Shpilev (201-8251267), who asked them to first install the refrigerator. CASTAGNA presented a bill for 35,000 pesos, but SHPILEV said this was too cheap and raised the cost to 55,000 pesos, giving CASTAGNA 40,000 and evidently pocketing 15,000. SHPILEV then said the three air conditioners were to be delivered to the embassy, although they were pulled by CASTAGNA from trade delivery.

2. CASTAGNA and A-I transported the three units to the Soviet embassy, where Mikola I-Milyevich Kuznetsov (let us know) asked them to take them to the basement office. After a short discussion, A-I believes an argument occurred between KUZNETSOV and SHPILEV, who had come to the Soviet embassy.
