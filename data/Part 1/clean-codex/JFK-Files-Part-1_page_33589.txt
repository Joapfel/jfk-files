20 March 1952

Material for: Chief, Southeastern Europe Division; Chief, Eastern Europe Division

Subject: Future of Strasbourg Free Europe University Students

1. Attached hereto is a copy of a letter I am forwarding to Dr. Tyson on this subject.

2. Chief RF is on record as having advised the former case officer of TTP-NIC that he should be offered an opportunity for evaluation and convert screening of students of Ei origin for future potential operational use, on the understanding that no student would be approached for this program during his scheduled term at the University. It probably has a similar interest in this matter.

3. This memorandum is to ask that you keep me informed on a continuing basis of your views on this subject, and also to let you know if there can be expected from the information which shall come to us in reply to the attachment.

4. Any comments which you may have on this subject and would like to pass to me either indirectly or by e-mail address, by 15 April, would be appreciated.

Thank you, Frazer

Chief, International Organizations Division
