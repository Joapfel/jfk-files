GENERAL HEADQUARTERS  
FAR EAST COMMAND  
APO 500

12 December 1951

AG 201-AGM  
LO 347-1

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individuals Concerned

1. The following named individuals will proceed on or about 13 December 1951 to Pusan and Seoul, Korea, on detail for a period of 10 days TDY in connection with military intelligence activities. Upon completion, they will return to their proper station.

Mr. Grant A. Fielden (G3-15)  
Mr. Earl M. Lockard (G3-13)  
Mr. N. Scott Miller (Miler) (G3-13)

2. Travel by military aircraft, rail, and government motor transportation authorized. An allowance of sixty-five (65) pounds of personal baggage authorized while traveling by air. No per diem authorized.

3. Individuals are authorized to perform duties as official couriers during the period of detail.

4. Individuals will wear the Army officers’ uniform without insignia in the combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from the CM Sales Store upon presentation of these orders.

5. Individuals may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after completion of detail.

6. Inspections and physical inspection as prescribed by Circular 9, FEC, 27 February 1950, will be completed prior to departure.

By command of General Ridgway:

Thomas F. Whaley  
Captain, ACC  
Assistant Adjutant General
