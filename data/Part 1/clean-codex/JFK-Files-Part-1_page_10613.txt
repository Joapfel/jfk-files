Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable. Reference: IN 22194.

1. On 10 November 1964 Eladio del Valle, aka “Yito,” a representative of Rolando Masferrer Rojas, asked Eliseo Gomez Fernandez, a partner in the Antillean Marine Shipping Corporation of Miami, if he could use Gomez’s boat to transport “invaders” out of the United States to an unidentified location at sea.

2. According to Gomez, Teofilo Babun, Gomez’s partner, learned from his cousin, Rudolph Baboun, Haitian consul in Miami, that Rudolph had informed American authorities that the Masferrer people in Miami were preparing an invasion of Haiti. Rudolph also said that Masferrer was very upset because his plans leaked out.
