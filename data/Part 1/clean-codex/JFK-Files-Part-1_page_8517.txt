January 24, 1968.

Valentin Sergeyevich Loginov, P-11712.

Source: BHPernald.

1. A check at the building confirms that the subject lives at Ignacio Esteva 21, apartment 2. Near the doorbell at the main entrance, in the slot corresponding to apartment 2, the subject's name appears: Y.S. Loginov.

2. The building is quite new and has three floors. The first (ground) floor houses the Instituto de Cronometrologia, and on the second and third floors there are two apartments per floor. The building has no garage.

3. Apparently, there is no portero in the building because there is no bell for the portero. The interphone enables tenants to identify visitors and open the door electrically.
