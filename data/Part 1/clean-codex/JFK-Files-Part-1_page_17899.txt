The World's Only Correspondence Course in Wire Tapping.

A complete course in Electronic and Photographic Surveillance Equipment (illustrated with photographs, drawings, and schemes of special equipment). This course, written for professional detectives, presents many new techniques in Wire Tapping and Bugging. A catalog of Electronic and Photographic Surveillance Equipment is included. Each lesson is concluded with a short story example showing the practical use of the material of that lesson.

Quizzes are given, and questions answered; a certificate of seminary is awarded.

The following is an outline of the course:

Lesson I, “Wire Tapping”: The Wired Tap; Wireless Wire Tapping; Fiction (Faction), “Tom Makes a Tap.”

Lesson II, “Micro & Info Photography”: Microphotography; Cam Processing; Nonscene Photography; Domiine Photography; Fiction (Faction), “Tom and the Red Spy.”

Lesson III, “Bugs”: Bug Bags; Bugnet Bags; Directional Microphones; Recording Equipment and Techniques (Tech-ques); Fiction (Faction), “All Wet Brakes Lever.”

Lesson IV, “Telescopic Photography” (Telescopie Photography): Aerial Photography; Teloscopic Photography; Gonstrative Remotives of Aerial Photography; Fiction (Faction), “Tom Spots An Outlaw.”

Lesson V, “Telling”: The Wireless Cell; Radio Direction Finders; Fiction (Faction), “The Loaned Can-Artist.”
