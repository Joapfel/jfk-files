Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Telephone Conversation between AMBIDDY-1 and Martin Ferrer

Date: 9 April 1964

1. A-1 will obtain the address of the Tampa barge today and call our office. A-1 is departing for Nicaragua on 10 April via Lamica at 0730.

2. Manuel Hernandez has gone to Panama and should have returned to Costa Rica by now. A-1 will report on Hernandez's findings upon his arrival in Costa Rica.

3. A-1 was told not to forget the budget before his departure. The quarterly charter payment to Talaria for the C-47 is due this month.

4. Petrosky will send a letter to Carrillo stating he would like to charter a C-47. Carrillo will then write back saying that he has a C-47 to sub-let. A-1 hopes that this exchange between Carrillo and Petrosky will culminate in the C-47 being based in Panama.

5. The undersigned asked A-1 if he had his Q signals to be used for the operation. A-1 stated he does have them.

6. A-1 was requested to send the list of those expelled.

7. A-1 should make certain that the Nicaraguan Consulate in New York is alerted to the heavy equipment shipment and the consular-stamped invoice.

8. After signing the Libermeer contract, General Somoza (Somosa) should mail the contract to the Libermeer people.

9. A-1 was advised that someone should now be working on the registration of the DOLORES.

10. We would appreciate A-1 following up on his visa while in Nicaragua.

11. The following was passed to A-1 concerning the village radio: Will arrive in Managua at 14:25 hours on 10 April, Pan Am flight no. 2501. Air Bill number is Pan Am 026-4-674334. Consists of 27 packages, weighing 2,014. Has been prepaid $603.00. Consignee: Consolidated Electrical Equipment Co. It is identified as transceivers and batteries. No. 20763 appears on each case along with the address.

Above conversation conducted in prearranged frequently changed privacy ends.
