Dispatch. To: Chief, Africa Division. From: Chief of Station, Pretoria. Operational/SECAROB—letter regarding mercenary affairs. May 1970.

Reference: AZPT-2258, 4 March 1970.

1. Forwarded herewith is the substance of the latest letter received by SECAROB/I from subject of 201-801702 regarding mercenary affairs and Zanzibar.

2. During reference trip, PHILBROOK will discuss this letter with SECAROB/I for further clarification and forward the results to Headquarters.

Kenneth H. PHILBROOK.

Attachment: handwritten letter.

Distribution: Original and 2 copies to C/AF with attachment.

Cross-reference: 201-801702. Dispatch symbol and number: AZPA-3050. Date: 5 March 1970. File number: 201-801702.
