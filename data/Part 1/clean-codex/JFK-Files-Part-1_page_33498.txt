CATHATCH CAQUARRY TOFOCUS FUOYNX.

Ref: Bonn Base 31234 (IN 230053) WH/6/Soc'y.

1. On 1 May, ZDF Magazin, with CAQUARRY as moderator, carried a 15-minute feature on Chile, a theme long discussed with CAQUARRY. The main part of the feature was an interview with SPD parliamentarian Friedrich Beermann (BEERMA NN), subject of reference, who recently returned from a visit to Chile and spoke with General Pinochet. Beermann stressed that the Allende government had ruled illegally, conditions were not as bad as they seemed, and that overall the Chilean population favored Allende's overthrow. The significance is that an interview with an SPD politician, and the SPD, as influenced by its left wing, has been strongly anti-junta.

2. Hans Matthöfer (MATT HOEFER), left-wing SPD parliamentarian and State Secretary for Economic Cooperation, has been leading Germany's opposition to the junta and also trying to block economic aid. Drawing on FUJAWL's background information (see EGN-5363, November 73), we have discussed Matthöfer in detail with CAQUARRY.
