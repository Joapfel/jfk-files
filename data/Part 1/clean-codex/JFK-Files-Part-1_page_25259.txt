LEOSON. MILOLAY SERGEYEVICH. 22 August 1928. USSR. Ryazan, USSR. Probably an interpreter.

SR/6/Bio 706.

Administrative clerk at the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City between March 1953 and October 1956. In February 1960, accompanied the Mikoyan (Mikotian) entourage to Cuba for the Soviet Cultural and Technical Exposition.
