Reference: Chief, FE.

Subject: Receipt of Gifts from Foreign Official.

Reference: Memo for D/Pers from C/FE, 28 October 1959 (DO/P 4-90JB), same subject.

1. Reference reports the receipt of one silk necktie and one lady's seat by each of the following officials: Mr. Robert A. Amory; Mr. William V. Broe; Mr. Herman Horton; Mr. Lothar Metal; Mr. Edward H. Korn; and Mr. Kinloch Bull.

2. Considering the circumstances under which these gifts were tendered and their insignificant intrinsic value, we assure you in our recommendation that they be retained by the recipients for whatever use they may foresee.

Gordon M. Stewart  
Director of Personnel

OD/Pers/EIBond:sjc, 3 November 1959.
