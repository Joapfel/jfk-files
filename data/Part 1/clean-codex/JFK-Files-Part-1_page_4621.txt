DIR CITE WAVE 7633.

TYPIC AMOT.

1. FYI ANCT report DD-1698, dated 30 November. Report is gist of 27 November telephone conversation between AMTAUP-2 and Marcelino Garcia Jimenez of MIRR. Garcia stated he had talked on the telephone on 27 November to Orlando Bosch, who was in Hartford (Hariford), Connecticut. Bosch indicated students at Hartford University had made a $5,000 bond for Bosch, permitting his release; bond originally set at $15,000, subsequently reduced to $5,000. According to Bosch, university students have asked that Bosch be tried in “public trial,” but according to Bosch, this information is confidential and not for public consumption. Same caveat passed to A-2 by Garcia. Bosch also indicated he would spend three or four days at Hartford, have a meeting at the university, and attempt to collect funds “for an operation we (Bosch) had planned.”

2. Garcia indicated to A-2 that previously planned protest demonstration was cancelled when Bosch was released, and added that henceforth all efforts of MIRR would be directed towards effecting planned air raid over Pinar del Rio Province.
