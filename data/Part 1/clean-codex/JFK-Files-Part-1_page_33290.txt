Pinochet also said Foreign Ministry and Chilean embassies are receiving hundreds of letters daily from relatives of Soviet prisoners, and that these letters were being forwarded to International Red Cross. Commenting on Peruvian Foreign Minister’s statement against CIA activities in Latin America, editorialist wrote that the KGB should get out of Latin America. “...that sinister red political organization which has committed so many dastardly crimes in our country such as the assassination of Trotsky. KGB has established book publishers used by the fascist leftists to infiltrate parties, meetings and literary gatherings. KGB used the press and groups of artists, intellectuals, writers and journalists to sing the praises of a red dictatorship. ...the KGB should get out with its thousands.”
