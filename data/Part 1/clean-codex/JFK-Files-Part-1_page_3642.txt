The thoroughly review of the evidence attached to each of the testimony's cases, by two experts in law, making a new explanation of the evidence. For the first time in history, the results of science are made available on a scientific and comparative scale.

From June 20th to August 1st, 1873, at two central stations throughout the country, there resulted from the combined action of some 15,000 persons, of which number 10,000 were women, and 10,000 children under twelve years of age.

These results, with an analysis of them, have never before been published.

The following table gives the results.
