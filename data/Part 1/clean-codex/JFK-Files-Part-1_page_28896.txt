20 May 1968

Memorandum for: Chief, Compensation and Tax Division

Via: Contract Personnel Division

Subject: Contract Termination—Arthur R. SCHWICKRATH

1. Arthur R. SCHWICKRATH has been notified by the Civil Service Commission that his disability retirement has been approved.

2. In view of the above information, it is requested that Subject’s contract be terminated effective 1500 hours on 15 July 1968. Termination of Subject’s contract on this date is requested to allow him to use all of his accrued sick leave as well as his excess annual leave.

William R. Nelson  
Chief, Far East Division
