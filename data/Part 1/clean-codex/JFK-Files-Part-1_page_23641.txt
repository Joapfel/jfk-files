Staff. Date/time: 042052Z March 1980. Director 627924. To: Priority Madrid.

MINTEL RYNAKULE AMLASH

Reference: Madrid 48679 3918232.

1. Department of State has sent a message to the consulate authorizing the granting of visas to the AMLASH family, but has apparently attached a proviso—so long as the consular officer is satisfied that they are bona fide visitors and not immigrants.

2. The officer who debriefed AMLASH-1 in Madrid on 11 January is an experienced and savvy professional. He has been interviewing refugees for the past several months and has consistently provided accurate assessments of refugee intentions and motivations. AMLASH-1 told the debriefer that he only wanted to visit the United States and that he wants and intends to settle in Spain, where he can more readily set up a medical practice and be less news policy than would be the case in the United States. The debriefer believes AMLASH-1 means what he said.
