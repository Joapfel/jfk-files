He said he was at the Hotel Capri, four blocks from the hotel at which A-5 was staying and, as is known, the alternate location for A-5's residence in Montreal, as received in the letter of instruction. Alberto enlisted that A-5 meet him at the bar of the Capri after A-5 asked him to come to his hotel. This was in keeping with the instructions given A-5 prior to his departure from Miami to accommodate the initiative. A-5, after appropriate delays, acquired [contact]. He said that he recognized the voice of Alberto as being that of Guillermo, whom AMCHOST-I has sent to Spain for a meeting with A-5 several years ago. Twenty minutes later A-5 entered the Capri bar and met Guillermo (Eduardo Aracz). Upon entering the bar, which was empty except for Guillermo, he noted one other person in a corner booth watching him.

This was through a device he later discovered to be another CUIS officer. This officer had dark, thinning hair combed to the side and appeared more English- or American-looking than Cuban, according to A-5. He was of medium height and build and clean-shaven. The following four hours were spent with Guillermo alone, alternating between the bar, the restaurant, and a small nightclub in the Capri Hotel. Entire tab picked up by A-5. Guillermo made no offer to pick up the tab and jokingly, on one occasion, alluded to the prosperity of A-5's business in Miami. Suspecting that the man in the booth of the bar was CUIS, when passing back through the bar from the restaurant on the way to the nightclub, A-5 said to the man in Spanish, “You can go to dinner now; you must be hungry after your long vigilance.” The man, apparently caught off guard, answered something rapidly in Spanish, at which time Guillermo became furious at the man for falling.
