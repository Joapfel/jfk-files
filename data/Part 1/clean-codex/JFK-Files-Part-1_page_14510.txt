Dispatch.

Processing action: Marred for ordering; no dated requiring; only qualified desk can adole scaring.

To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division.  
Info: Chief, Europe Division; COS, WH/Miami; COB/DO, New York.  
From: Chief of Station, Madrid.  
Subject: TYPIC/QUTIMID/U.S. Company in Touch with Cuban Embassy.  
Action Required—References: A. OSMA 25995; B. DIRECTOR 014234; C. MADRID 0620.

1. According to QUTIMID on 26 February, Miss SUAREZ de Cepeda of the Grolier Company called AMAUTO-1 and spoke with his secretary to explain that her company is giving their book to a number of persons in order to obtain their opinions about the product and AMAUTO-1 was one of the persons on Grolier's list. AMAUTO-1's secretary said that he would call when he returned. Later, Miss SUAREZ was told that an appointment was made for 10 March.

2. This would appear to be a routine item which probably would not merit further investigation, unless we can discover someone of higher caliber in the Grolier Company who could approach AMAUTO-1 as a means of cultivating a meaningful friendship with AMAUTO-1.

Philip C. KENTLAND

ACCT E 1B/5.

Distribution: C/WTHD; C/EUR; COS, WH/Miami; COB/DO, New York.

Cross-reference to dispatch symbol and number: OSMA 26025. Date: 13 March 1970. MOS file number: 86-120-80.
