182122Z CITE JMWAVE 9802

Caracas info Director

AKULE PBRUMEN

Ref: Caracas 7805 (1-51649)

1. AMCLEVE/15 reports telephone call from Gustavo (Gistavo) Ortiz Faez on 18 Oct 67. Ortiz informed AMCLEVE/15 that Erasto (Erasto) Fernandez Betancourt (Betabcourt) will be named Director of DIGEPOL in late October 1967. Ortiz, on behalf of Fernandez, assured AMCLEVE/15 of DIGEPOL employment at a proper livable salary.

2. AMCLEVE/15 plans to fly to Caracas on 23 Oct 67. He will stay temporarily at the home of Ortiz. The station has asked AMCLEVE/15 to notify JMWAVE when he has a more permanent address, and we will advise Caracas. If the station desires contact, the person should identify himself to AMCLEVE/15 as a friend of Lawrence R. Derome.
