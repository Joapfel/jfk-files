Conference has broken up into four commissions, which are drafting or discussing various programs to be submitted to forthcoming Congress: Political Commission is headed by Emilio (Emilo) Maspero; Commission on Doctrine by Jose Golsack; Commission on Statutes by Eduardo Garcia; and Declaration of Principles by Fulgencio Barreiro, executive secretary for Atlantic States.

3. Post-conference plans:

A. According to DUBOLD/1, CISC has sent seven tickets with names left open for council delegates to go to Brussels to discuss matters to be resolved. DUBOLD/1 states that delegates not present on Executive Committee will be included on trip to Brussels. AMICE/31 stated that entire Executive Committee will go to Brussels.

B. After delegates return from Brussels, a conference of Caribbean affiliates is slated to take place in Guatemala under direction of Augusto Bonilla.

C. Question of where Congress will take place not yet resolved. Both agents had heard that Santo Domingo (Santi Domingo) had been picked as site.
