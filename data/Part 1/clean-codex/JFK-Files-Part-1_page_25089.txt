SAGO INFO DIR CITE MEXI 6866. REDWOOD. 12 April 1965.

LICOW-1 (LICOWL-1) reports that on 2 April, Soviet Embassy guard Stanislav Sergeyevich Silnikov (Silnxev), 201-271103, told him that Vitaliy Borisovich Shubin, 201-266547, was now consul in Chile, and added that probably Oleg Maksimovich Nechiporenko, 201-305870, and Valeriy Vladimirovich Kostikov, 201-305852, would also be going to Chile.

Kostikov.
