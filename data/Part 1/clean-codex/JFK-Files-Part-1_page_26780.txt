E. Rico and Deschamps (presumably Eduardo Deschamps, writer for Excelsior, on whom Station has some derogatory information, including LIHUFF report that he is a Communist): Rico advises Deschamps they are going to talk to Caamaño (CAAMANO) later in the day, and suggests Deschamps be present when the call is made.

Leonov (presumably Nikolay) calls for Rico and leaves a message that he wants to postpone the appointment. Will call again tomorrow morning.

G. Miric to Rico: Is informed that Rico is at the house of Jose Luis Gonzalez (GUSTALEZ), that they are waiting on a call from D.R. Miric says he has had official communication “on this matter” and would like to tell Rico.

H. Rico and Miric: Rico says they are waiting on a call from Caamaño (CAAMANO), but it did not go through; it will be tomorrow. Miric says apparently Caamaño (CAAMANO) will name Mejia as ambassador to the UN. Rico does not think this good.

Miric says it is all right for the state they are in. Miric has the communiqué. Deschamps gave it to Rico. The most important part is to point out that Harriman is lying. Miric says what this man is doing follows a cause and he is doing some foolish things. Secrecy.
