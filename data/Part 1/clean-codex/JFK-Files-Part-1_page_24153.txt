Classified message. From: London. Action. Info.

221745Z.

PARI DIR INFO PALP CITE LOND 5337 CAWOOD ACTING ZIBET LCHARVEST. Reference: PARI 8052.

1. UNBOUND has made available to ODENVY the same information as contained in the reference. ODEVNY/LOND insist they have no operational interest in GIBSON. Seriously doubt either SMOOTH or JAGUAR have any relationship with GIBSON, since this would be a gross (and sooner or later apparent) contravention of our agreements regarding use of one another's nationals.

2. LOND phone directory lists Jeffreys Alfred Henry and Marks (chartered accountants), 9 Basinghall St., E.C.2. No STA traces.

3. ODENVY files here reflect further information on charges that GIBSON is a KUBARKer, although nothing to indicate the basis for such charges. (See ODENVY/WASH letter to KUBARK information, ODENVY/LOND and PARI, 1 Dec 64, captioned Richard Thomas Gibson.) Can HOS shed any light?

4. Since arrival in LOND, GIBSON has been in touch with Cuban EMB.
