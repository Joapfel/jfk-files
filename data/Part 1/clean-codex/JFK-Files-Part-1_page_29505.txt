Dispatch. To: Chief, Special Affairs Staff. From: Chief of Station, JMWAVE A.R. Subject: TYPIC/Personnel—Award of Intelligence Star—Irving C. DEVUONO.

Action required: References. Reference: UFGS 3125 dated 17 May 1963.

Irving C. DEVUONO will be available for the presentation ceremony at Headquarters at any time during the next 30 days. Subject desires that any group present be small and he, himself, will be accompanied by his wife.

End of dispatch.

Distribution: Original and 2 copies to addressee.

Date typed: 18 June 63. Date dispatched: 25 June 1963. Dispatch symbol and number: UFGT-5536. RYBAT.
