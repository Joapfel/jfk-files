Fund for International Social and Economic Education (FISEE), Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

Funding conduit for international labor operations under Project QRSENSE/KMOCHROID.

Ramparts April 1968 listing incorrectly states FISEE was formerly Center for Rural Development (CRD), Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. CRD actually replaced FISEE.

Peace with Freedom, Inc., Manhattan, New York.

Action organization under Project QRBIIB. Related to Projects FOMAIDEN, QRSHIEER, VPDAMSEL, QRPLUTO and certain European socialist activities. These are known to be of current interest to Ramparts.

F. Eight Area Division Project Organizations

African-American Institute, Washington, D.C.

Action organization under AF Project YQFLUENT. Transferred to CA Staff, at that time IO Division, in 1961 as Project QRFLUENT and terminated shortly thereafter.

Friends of India Committee, Washington, D.C.

Organization under NE Project PAFANFARE.

Synod of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church Outside of Russia, Manhattan, New York.

An organization subsidized under SB Project AESILVER.

International Development Foundation, Inc., Manhattan, New York.

Developed by CA Staff under Project TPTONIC, later redocumented as separate CA Staff Project QRBEND and then transferred to WH as Project UOALIBI.

Pan American Foundation, Washington, D.C.

WH funding mechanism under Project JMPOPLAR/10.
