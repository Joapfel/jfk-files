Contact Report

Subject: LIEbeck-1 (=4805). Date of meeting: July 30, at Restaurant Firense, at 1630.

August 1, 1969.

1. Prearranged meet to which L-1 came 10 minutes early. The meet lasted 2 hours and 30 minutes.

Next meet: Wednesday, August 6, at the same place, at 1630 hours.

2. L-1 came to this meet in much better shape than he was last time, in spite of the fact that he complained of being tired. He wanted to eat something, but he did not feel as having a full meal. He had 4 drinks (rum and ced).

3. L-1 handed me a personality sketch which he prepared on DROVIN, which is not much by itself, but it could be useful for personality assessment of B.

4. I questioned L-1 as much as I was able, without tipping my hand, about his visit to the Soviet Embassy on July 9. I detected no indication of deception or withholding of information on his side.

5. In the course of debriefing L-1 on his recent trip with LOG, I made my comments and gave him necessary instruction, which are covered in the following areas.

6. As L-1 mentioned LOG's questioning about LIUVIL-1, he immediately pointed out that the whole thing made no sense to him after LOG's repeated statements at the last meet that he was not interested in LIUVIL-1. I told L-1 that it made little sense to me, except if LOG wanted to ascertain whether L-1 was telling him the truth. Then I added that I wished LIUVIL-1 had made some statement which L-1 could relay to LOG to see what would be his reaction. L-1 commented that once in the past he told LOG that LIUVIL-1 told him that he used to know a Soviet diplomat here whom he considered to be his friend and was disappointed when the Soviet diplomat tried to recruit him, because it was obvious that the friendship was a pretense.

Then LOG heard this, he laughed and said that this was one of the old clumsy methods which are no longer used. (NOTE: I did not know about the P-117/2)...
