SECRET 142020Z MAY 62 CITE BUENOS AIRES 0605  
DIRECTOR INFO PANAMA CITY  
RYBAT REDTOP AERONAUT AETARGET TECHS

Ref: BUENOS AIRES 0546 (1N056).

1. On 13 May, AMSTALK-1 accompanied CASTAGNAS on a visit to BIBANDIT-6. Under pretext that business was slow due to cool weather, CASTAGNA attempted to drum up business from B-6. The latter, cooperative and pleasant, said the following:

A. Within 10 days, three air-conditioner units from Posadas will be turned over to CASTAGNA for overhaul.

B. The trade delegation plans to buy around 20 refrigerators from CASTAGNA in the near future.

C. The three units delivered 6 May (see ref) actually belonged to the Embassy but had been loaned to the trade delegation.

D. The Soviet Embassy presently has some new units, presumably never used, which belonged to an Embassy officer who committed suicide two years ago, BORIS SUKHOMLINOV. These may be used by the trade delegation. If not, new units may be purchased from CASTAGNA.
