File No. 242 369.

Date: 26 May 1955.

Synopsis: File at Immigration and Naturalization Service, Miami, Florida shows that one Antonio SOTO (Vazquez), who was born 20 August 1934 in Cuba, arrived in the United States at Daytona Beach, Florida, on 1 January 1959, and departed from the United States at Miami, Florida, for the Dominican Republic on 20 June 1959. He again arrived in the United States at San Juan, P. R., on 18 August 1959, using the name and passport of one Orlando Pedro RODRIGUEZ (Alvarez), which he explained by saying he could not get a visa in his own name due to service in the Cuban Air Force during the BATISTA regime. His foreign departure from Miami, Florida, to Guatemala was verified 28 September 1959. The father of the subject was said to be in jail in Cuba in 1959 for participating in an attempted invasion of Cuba. The subject arrived in the United States at Miami, Florida, on 11 January 1961; his foreign departure from Miami, Florida, was verified 25 February 1961, and he again arrived in the United States at Miami, Florida, on 24 May 1961. He was granted a re-entry permit in October 1963 for travel to the Republic of Congo. Background data set forth.
