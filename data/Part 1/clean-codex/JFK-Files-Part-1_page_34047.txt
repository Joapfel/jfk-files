United Liberal Party

Founder & President: Rev. Henry Muir

746 Spanish Town Road  
Kingston 13, P.O. Box 9  
Jamaica W.I.

15th Jan. 1967

Dear Sirs,

I wish you a prosperous 1967.  
Give my respect to all your men for me.  
I have returned to Jamaica on the 16th Dec. 1966.  
You have promised to send me those leaflets for me to give them out in Jamaica. I hear that people can read how wicked Communists are and you have not sent them. Why?

Please, whatever you are sending for me, don't put your address on them, only put the United Liberal Party on it. I don't want them to know where they came from. Please let me have even a million or different copies. We are now getting ready for the election soon.

I wish you all success in your undertaking.
