12 January 1953

M. Kelley

Re: Agreement (16 September 1950) between CIA and Headley (Healey) for production of photo-book.

You will recall that in the agreement, which you drew up and which was signed 16 September 1950 by Mr. Benson and Robert E. Healey (Headley), it is provided that Headley (Healey) “will be required to transfer the gross royalties to CIA.”

Soon Publishing Co. is now to be the publisher of the book. I am Benson Dec. 19, who told so he has already agreed to pay Headley (Healey) for expenses, and has authorized me to pay his collaborator (a life lay-out man) on a fixed fee basis, i.e. not more than £35 per diem.

Soon plans to pay all the expenses of production. It is his understanding that:

(a) Soon will, on paper, pay Healey (Headley) standard royalties, which Headley (Healey) will return to Soon, and thus

(b) Soon will own all rights in the books (which will facilitate Soon’s putting out foreign editions).

In other words, it is Benson’s hope, and mine, too, as he put it, Soon, to bring a wholly CIA-controlled corporation, “will simply step into the shoes of ‘the Government’ and ‘CIA’ in the agreement.”

Benson said you had done to the papers for Soon. He both hoped this way of handling the matter, whereby two financial details would all be handled in Soon’s books and not occupy the time of OPC personnel, will be legally acceptable?

When I mentioned the matter to John Bischoff, 5 January, he said he thought we should check with you whether:

(a) we can simply let the 16 September 1950 agreement stand as is, or

(b) whether the agreement should be amended to read “to Soon” instead of “the Government” and “CIA.”

SP/M. S. Stine

To Kelley
