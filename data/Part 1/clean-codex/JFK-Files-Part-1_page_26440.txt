Classified message. Routing. To: Director. From: Rio de Janeiro. Action: WH 8. Info: DOP, ADDP, CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, FI, FI/INT B, RI/AN, SAS 8, VR.

121859Z. DIR POJCH LØRE INFO MEXI CITE RIOD 2998. Ref: DIR-70262.

1. About 3 Dec ALGLAB-1 met Mexican Victor Rico Galan, editor of Siempre, who said came directly from Cuba without stopping Mexi and intends return Cuba same route very soon (sic). Rico was considering traveling to Porto Alegre on 5 or 6 Dec.

2. LØRE, Rico suspected Perumen agent on special mission for AMTHUSIA. In Brazil was to meet with Prestes and Juliao. DIR requests any info his travels and on his contacts. Cable any info PACY. Rico DPOB 6 Feb 1933, El Ferrol, Mexi.

3. Index.

C/S comment: *RE reports that Victor Rico Galan is traveling through WH area.
