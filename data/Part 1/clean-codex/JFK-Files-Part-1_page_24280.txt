Instructions to Case Officer

1. This form must be filled in by the case officer or appropriate authority on the basis of the best available information. It is not to be shown to the subject.

2. Normally, headquarters will be unable to issue an approval unless the requestor furnishes answers to all questions in PRQ Parts I and II. However, in an emergency provisional approval may be obtained by complying with the applicable provisions of FR 10-215, paragraphs 6a, g, 12b, and 13d. Thereafter the usual method to document or complete the case must be followed as set forth in FR 10-215.

3. Three completed copies of PRQ Part I, including a copy in the subject's handwriting, if possible, and two completed copies of this form must be sent to headquarters as soon as possible.

4. Data acquired later must be sent to headquarters in PRQ format in two copies.

5. Fill in all items if information is obtainable. If an item is not applicable, write “NA.” If additional space is required for an item, use the reverse side of the page keyed to the appropriate item number.
