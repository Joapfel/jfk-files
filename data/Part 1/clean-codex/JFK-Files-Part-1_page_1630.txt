Subject: Support to Perfect Seal—Linderman Key

(UTAR'S LEFT) #219 600 F CO/N

Attention: Mr. Ene

1. About 0630 hours on 22 April 1971, Mr. William Losner, senior vice president, Bank of Perpetual, and a cleared and witting contact of this agency for many years, reported a long-distance phone call from Les Whitaker, associate of Jack Anderson. Whitaker inquired as to Losner's association with this agency and received alleged activities of the agency at Liar's Lair of Linderman Key, located about 80 miles southeast of Homestead Air Force Base (HOMEOSTAT AF) across Biscayne Bay (DISCAYNE DAY).

2. Losner denied knowledge of and association with this agency. He reported he is officer in corporation which leased Liar's Lair from University of Miami (UL IV OF MISHI) several years ago. This is true, and neither site for corporation was present according with this agency. He also reported that the only activity there was corporation workforce rebuilding at old firm during last weekend.

3. Losner speculates that Whitaker may have obtained his information from state corporation agents. Also, someone with past knowledge of agency.
