From: Psych.

1. If eventually already known to addressees, call attention to newly published book on Chile by Swedish journalist/author Andresén. Enjoy “Couture,” entitled “To Som Socialism and War Fascism,” and which seems unusually balanced, coming from young Swed. According to 1 May 74 review in Finnish Swedish-Langlade Calympus Adsbladet, Hung-Who visited Chile before, during and after Allende’s (Allente’s) time—labels the Swedish debate on Chile as “one-sided” and analyses in detail Allende’s (Allente’s) economic policies and the increasingly critical economic problems under Allende’s (Allente’s) three years, and the concomitant political problems that led Allende (Allente) to measures which were “incompatible with democratic principles and existing laws.” Muno notes that “agressive class politics, whether reactionary or revolutionary, can bring about a move against a socialist or fascist dictature,” but, he adds, “no condition or mistake during Allende’s (Allente’s) time can be used as excuse for the coup of Cunfidential.”
