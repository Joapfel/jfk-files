MEDINA — Bernard Franciacc (Bernard Francisco) BONEMA.

1. Headquarters traces are requested on:

Name: Bernardo Fraccacc (Bernardo Francisco) NURMEA.  
Date of birth: 16 June 1944, Argentina.  
Occupation: Director of Diputacion Argentina de Espectáculos y Telefónicas (DANFA).  
Names: Bernardo and Artisticana cba DECADI.  
Marital status: Married; wife is a nonsmoker.

2. Station traces show that MOKMIA, apparently a member by association, has a long record of association with Communist groups. He was one of the founding members of the Allmentz, as United & Central-itary (SCC), and reportedly a member of the Communist Party of Argentina. He was also formerly the Secretary General of the Argentine Union of Musicians. Specific references include:

a. RAP-OCTOBER, 16 October 1944, which describes him as the leader of the striking in which the Buenos Aires Central Line Committee had its headquarters. This document also identifies KORDEA as leader of Executive.

b. MARCH 17-18 1944, 27 October 1944, which reports OKIMEA’s travel to Galle at the invitation of the Confederation of Trabajadores de Galle.

c. BUENOS AIRES-9-10-53, March 1963, which states BONEMA attended the All Conferences for Solidarity for Galle.
