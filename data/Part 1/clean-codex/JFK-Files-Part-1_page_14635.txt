CIA TDCSDB-315/01333-69. Distribution: 2 April 1969. Country: Cuba/Chile/France/Spain. Date of report: 25 March 1969. Subject: Proposed travel of Chilean Senator Salvador Allende (ALLLENDE) to Cuba. Acquisition: Spain, Madrid, 27 March 1969. Source: An official Spanish service from a source described as “sensitive and usually reliable.” Field No. CSM-9523.

1. As of 25 March 1969, Chilean Senator Salvador Allende (ALLLENDE) intended to visit Cuba about 5 April for high-level talks. Field comment: His Cuban interlocutors and the subject of the talks were not known, but the latter apparently concerned in part Allende’s political future.

2. Preparations for Allende’s trip were made by intermediaries in Madrid, including Counselor Manuel Tello Troncoso of the Chilean Embassy and Cuban Chargé Guillermo Ruiz Perez. Cuban commercial counselor.
