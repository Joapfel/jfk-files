Date of employment: October 1947 to December 1951. Position: Assistant Attaché. Classification grade: F3-9. Final salary: $290 per year. Places of employment: Washington, D.C., and Caracas, Venezuela. Title: Ambassador.

Department of State. Employees supervised: 3 clerks. Immediate supervisor: Carl Frantz, Chief. Organization: Procter and Gamble, soap and chemical manufacture. Location: Cincinnati, Ohio. Employees supervised: 2 clerks. Description of work: Desire to enter Foreign Service.

General duties of Foreign Service Staff Officer, Embassy political section: preparation of reports for the Department and memoranda for the Ambassador; consultation with officials of the local government and American companies, especially petroleum industry, operating in Venezuela; assistance in visa affairs, consular section.
