To: Director, REAN.

1. According to local DEA representative Manuel Artime Buesa (ARTIME BUESA), he visited Costa Rica during the last week of October. He stayed at Hotel Costa Rica, where we registered in an alias. Artime reportedly met with high GOGR officials, including HURILL-1, MUSTRAIGHT-1, and MUGALLY-11, and allegedly told the latter that we were sent by “high officials” of NVIDIA to assure MUGALLY-1 that USG policy toward Cuba was not changed. The charge is unaware of any current contact between ARTIMER (ARTIME) and NVIDIA officials. MUFABRIC-2 verified Artime’s presence in Costa Rica.
