Name: Laszlo Beke. Date and place of birth: 1 November 1904 at Felsodobsza, Hungary. Present address: IRO Camp Asten-Linz, Post Heinrichsdorf, Austria. Nationality: Hungarian. Religion: Calvinist. Marital status: Married, four dependents: wife, Maria, age 27, occupation hand-weaving; daughter, Maria, age 11; son, Attila, age 10; son, Andreas, age 7.

Education: Graduated with a degree in Electrical Engineering from Technological School, Budapest, Hungary, in 1923. Taking refresher courses in Electrical Engineering at present at Technical School in Linz, Austria.

Experience: Was supervisor for Hungarian Railroads in Hungary from 1938 to 1940. Was supervisor for Institute for Industrial Planning at Budapest, Hungary, from 1940 to 1941. Was chief engineer at hydraulic power plant at Eridunanas, Hungary, from 1941 to 1944. From 1945 to 1950, has been employed as draftsman, mechanic, and engineer with the U.S. Army, the British Army, and firms including Brown-Boveri Company.

Knowledge of languages: Speaks, reads, and writes some English. Knows Hungarian and German.

D.P.S. 65-46.2 (cavalry), 65-46.3 (cavalry). HUNTERLY, HARSH.
