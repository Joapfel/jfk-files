Official Dispatch

WPMA-0186

6 October 1948

To: Chief, FBW

Through: Chief of Station, Paris

From: Chief of Station, Marseille

Subject: Organisation of the French Communist Party in the Marseille Area

DUREL - BOOK 7-3

Intel:

The following is a survey of the organization and membership of the French Communist Party in the Marseille Area:

Departmental Federation: Headquarters, 16 rue Leon Gambetta.

Federal Secretary: Denis BIZOT.

Federal Secretariat: Pierre DOIZE; Joseph LEVAUT; Marie COLOMBANI.

Federal Bureau: Denis BIZOT, in charge of political section; Pierre DOIZE; Joëtte REIBAUT; Murius COLOMBANI; Lucien MOLLENO, 19 Blvd. Bordillat; Jean CRISTOPOL; Pierre ERIANURLLI, in charge of Press-Propaganda; Ludovic TROUIN, in charge of organisation; Pasal POSADO; Henri BENTINI; Rene ZALLERAND; Yvonne FERRAGHY; Paul COURTIJU; Jean CLAVELLE, alias POLUK, in charge of recruiting and interior police. A particularly dangerous person.

Federal Committee: COLLINET; BATCHER.
