Memorandum for the Record.

Subject: Contact with BESABER. Dates of meetings: 23 and 28 February 1967.

A. Operational

1. As arranged by phone, CO met BESABER in the latter's room at the Hotel Del Paseo on 23 and 28 February. Mrs. BESABER was present at the second meeting. BESABER plans to be in Mexico City on 9 March and will call to arrange a meeting.

B. Finances

1. CO picked up BESABER's accounting for the revolving fund. I will reimburse him at the next meeting.

C. Substance

1. Soviets—At about 1000 on 18 February, Vladimir Ivanovich SHPAKOVICH (P-8167) phoned BESABER from Cuernavaca. BESABER had never met him before. S explained that he was a friend of Valentin Sergeyevich LOGINDV (P-11712), and that L was unable to make it because some people had arrived in town. S showed interest in buying some of BESABER's products; therefore, BESABER asked him to come to the factory. S arrived with his wife and a young girl who works in the Embassy, 18–19 years old, tall, good figure, dark brown hair, spoke Spanish, and wore a kerchief. This was probably Tatyana MARKINA (P-12945).

2. S brought almost 2,000 pesos' worth of items, including two large screens. BESABER opines that this was not a personal purchase, but rather either for the Embassy or an operational expense. S also insisted that the purchases be delivered to the Embassy, not his house.

3. S invited BESABER and his wife to an Army Day reception at the Soviet Embassy on 28 February 1967, saying that he had forgotten to bring a written invitation but that it would be at the gate of the Embassy. BESABER was reluctant to go, and I thought it better for him not to appear overly eager, so he did not go.

4. Before leaving, after they had stayed about 45 minutes, S told BESABER that he would probably come to Cuernavaca the following day, Sunday, about noon. He did not.
