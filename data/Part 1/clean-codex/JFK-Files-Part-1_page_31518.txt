Memorandum.

I would certainly be inclined to agree with Fow on the importance of a dignified “worthy cause” sort of campaign. Moreover I would scrupulously avoid any activity which might cast doubt on the good faith of the Crusade (e.g., “We gave our money for balloons at Christmas—where are they?”)
