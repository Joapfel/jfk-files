Memorandum

20 May 1964

To: The Files  
From: Floyd H. Aimonet  
Subject: Debriefing of LITANIL-3 on his meeting with LITATL-7  
Next scheduled meeting: 27 May, car pickup on Lerma, Guadalquivir, 0930

1. Once again, our bid to establish a small monthly deposit in the bank for LITANIL-7 has failed. L-7 appreciates the thoughtfulness but won't accept a cent. No hard feelings, and L-7 will continue to help in any way possible. Aimonet (Aironet) has decided against seeing L-7 personally because of the fact that a PBPRIKER's presence in the home of L-7 represents a disturbing factor, and we do not want to rattle L-7 now that L-7 is calmed down and producing.

2. Following tidbits from this week's meeting:

a) A long locked-door session between Echuga and H. Armas on 18 May. p-8412.

b) Ron officially placed in charge of all statements released to the Mexican press.

c) The full name of the new diplomat coming is Enrique Villavicencio Argudin. Will send cable requesting traces.

d) José Lain, consul Florida, PCS to Habana. p-7365.

e) Rogelio Rodriguez returned with wife but left child behind in Cuba. p-7921.

f) Vega Perez placed in charge of all fares and flights for those invited to Cuba from or through Mexico. p-8515, p-8591.

g) Mirabal and Dra. Machado have had long daily sessions (meetings, that is). p-7669.

h) Ruiz and Garcia Lara have had long closed-door sessions recently with Hernandez Armas.

i) The Cuban Foreign Ministry cabled all personnel abroad to be “on alert, view current rumblings” (w.t.m.).

j) On 16 May, 16 Mexican chess players left for Cuba, guests of IDER. p-7921.

k) On 16 and 18 May, a total of 13 Colombians left for Cuba with fares paid and separate pieces-of-paper visas.

l) Dr. Augusto Barber Uroco (suplente for Lechuga) arrived with Lech.

m) LITANIL-7 finally told off Perez. He stopped bothering L-7. p-8591.

n) Instructed LITANIL-3 to tell Hernandez to visit AMFALL-1 as initial step in showing good faith and establishing home sides.

W.t.m. equals “what we mean.” p-7365, p-8515, p-8591, p-7889.
