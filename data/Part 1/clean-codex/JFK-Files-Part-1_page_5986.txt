Dispatch. To: Chief, F**. Info: Chief, WOMUSE/80; Chief, EUR. From: Chief of Station, Ottawa. Subject: KOBIRD/CO SMABOVE — Susan Warren. Reference: OC0A-16222, 10 August 1971.

1. SMABOVE advises that on 12 July 1971 Lo Yi-ching (New China News Agency) mentioned to a reliable source that Yao, Jen-liu (Y a o, Jen-liu) (People’s Republic of China Embassy, Ottawa), a suspected intelligence officer, requested the address of Susan Warren.

2. The foregoing information was also passed LIMEROO.

Theodore D. HOLTHAM.

Distribution: Orig & 1 — C/FB; 2 — C/WOMUSE/80; 2 — C/EUR.
