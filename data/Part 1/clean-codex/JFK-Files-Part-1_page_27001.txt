CITECONTACTS/MIAMI 1714. Contacts/Washington.

Attention: John McConnell.

Subject: Alleged assassination attempt.

Reference: A. Miami 1714. B. McConnell/Oleichauf telecons of 21 February and 23 February 1967.

Mitchell was called Dan Gonzales yesterday to advise that he had received two phone calls from Chet Gray expressing concern over the fact that no one had contacted him for details concerning the alleged assassination attempt referred to in P-12-54, Miami 1714. Gray stated that unless he heard from somebody immediately, he would take his information to the Governor of California on the morning of 23 February 1967. We currently have an address for Gray: 324 North Oakhurst Drive, Beverly Hills (Severly Hills), telephone 274-4922.
