6-23-67.

To the President. To Secretary of State. To Director, CIA. To Director, Defense Intelligence Agency. To Department of the Army. To Department of the Air Force. From Director, FBI.

On June 23, 1967, a confidential source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that he had learned the following concerning current developments within Haiti:

Joseph Clemard Charles, president of the Commercial Bank of Haiti and financial advisor to President François Duvalier, upon leaving a hospital in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, after having had a tonsillectomy performed, was arrested by Duvalier henchmen on June 21 and immediately incarcerated in an unknown location.
