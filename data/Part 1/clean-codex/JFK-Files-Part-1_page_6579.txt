Other may be of interest:

A short time ago SOCONY pledged $1,000 × $5,000 to Angier B. Duke's International Rescue Committee for assistance of Hungarian refugees. As a follow-up, they advised their Austrian affiliate of the action. The reaction from Austria was that the pledge should be withdrawn immediately or, if that was not possible, the fact should not be publicized. The reason for this was that an ad had appeared in the Paris Tribune soliciting funds for the IRC and stating that the money was to be used for the support of Hungarian partisans fighting against the Communist government (no mention made that it was for assistance to Hungarian refugees in Austria). The Austrian reaction to this ad was that while such a contribution might be a good idea, they did not want to be accused by the Soviets of actively supporting the fighting. SOCONY withdrew the pledge and gave the money to the Austrian government fund to assist the refugees.

SOCONY's reaction was that IRC should be a little more accurate in their soliciting campaigns.
