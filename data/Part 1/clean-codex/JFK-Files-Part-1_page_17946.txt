Dispatch.

To: Chief, Task Force W, attention Willard NAUMAN.  
Info: Chief, WH; Chief, KUDESK.  
From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
Subject: GYROSE/ Jose Ordoqui.

Action Required: References.

- Salient reports from other agent sources.
- References to close contacts of Subject.
- Exemplars of various types of Mexican police agent reporting.

Willard C. Curtis.
