Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. From: Chief, WH Division. Subject: LIFCAT Project Renewal. Action required: For your information only. Reference: H01-A-17199, 12 June 1961.

1. The LIFCAT Project was renewed on 8 September 1961.

2. LIFCAT Project was approved for $98,973. Please note that this means a cut of $12,700 from the station's original request for $111,673. The cut at Headquarters was made for several reasons: 1) to fall within the approved programmed figure of $99,000; 2) during FY 1961 only approximately $81,000 was obligated and since no expansion was planned, an increase of almost $30,000 could not be justified; 3) to keep the approving authority within KUDOVR.

The cuts were made in the following places:

Equipment: Local purchase—station request $2,500, approved $1,800. New equipment—station request $4,500, approved $2,500. Other Op's expenses—station request $15,000, approved $5,000.

3. KUTOBE and KUDESK Staffs concurred in recommending renewal. KUTOBE/D remarked that the project included coverage of highly selective KUTOBE/D interests through technical means and stated that the project provided several items of operational interest not otherwise obtainable, as well as operational loads and background data useful in target assessment. KUDESK Staff reiterated that coverage should continue to be given to carefully selected known or suspect Soviet/Satellite agents in support of the Station's intensified KUDESK type program.

End of dispatch.

Distribution: 2—Mexico City. Date typed: 14 Sept. 1961. Date dispatched: 15 Oct. 1961. Dispatch symbol and number: H01-H-10085. Headquarters file number: 50-6-32/1.
