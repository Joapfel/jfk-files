23 March 1962.

IN 47511.

DIR CITE LEOP 3462. RYBAT WIROGUE.

1. VIROGUE contacts station at periodic intervals. On 19 March, W appeared discouraged and a little desperate. He still works for Congolese Air Force, making 15,000 BCF per month. Says he needs more just to live. Feels KUBARK used him unfairly. W wants to travel to Germany and Italy to try to make business arrangements concerning his inventions. Also would search for another job. Said he still owns a car for which he thinks he can get about 260,000 BCF to cover the cost of the trip.

2. Station concerned by W's presence in Leopoldville and the possibility he could, if his financial situation becomes sufficiently desperate, work for another service. Doubt he would cooperate with bloc services but cannot be certain. Also there is possibility he might work for SMOTH, UNION, WIROOT, etc. LEOP does not believe he has yet turned to other services. Appears to hope KUBARK will again take him on. In discussing his desire to go to Germany, said he would like to meet with a KUBARKER to determine if any job is available for him. SALVACK finessed direct reply on this point but said LEOP was not able to do any more for him than advise headquarters of his desire.
