Office of Logistics

Training Report

Program: OBL 70-104.18

Lee H. Wigren  
OS-12  
SR/DTF

Lee H. Wigren participated in the Office of Logistics-conducted training program. The training program was designed on a needs basis during the period beginning 30 October 1960 and consisted of 30 hours of classroom instruction, including an orientation tour of the Agency printing plant.

The program was primarily designed to provide general background and knowledge of Agency policies, responsibilities, and related matters. Agency personnel with positions involving the functions and organization of the CIA relating to printing and reproduction services were instructed in printing, duplicating costs and cost estimating; various administrative and bibliographic processes were explained.

Certificate of Course Completion

The above-named individual has successfully attended the program.
