Origin: Ben Evans. Unit: WH/3/H&E. Extension: 3054. Date: 9 June 1961.

Classified message. Secret. Routing.

To: Ciudad Trujillo (operational, indicates). Info: Mexico City (routine). From: Director. CONF: WH 5. Info: CI, CI/OPS, FI, S/C 2.

9/25/61 15:48Z. Office CEN. Routine.

OUT64603. 6-06-61.

To: Truj (OPIN). Info: Mexi (routine). Cite DIR 44408. Ref: MEXI 8186 (IN 21801).

Lopez is due to arrive in Trujillo at 4:05 PM on Pan Am flight 431. He will probably stay at the El Embajador (REBAJADOR) Hotel and has been told to contact Raynock upon arrival. Lopez can only remain in Trujillo for three days, but will probably be able to (APLE) return after 18 June.

End of message.

WH/Comment: Discussed efforts Lopez made to obtain a visa and observed that the time was inadequate for a full assessment of the situation. Lopez and Raynock were well acquainted from previous contact in Havana.

S. C. King, C/MID. Releasing officer.

Coordinating officers: H.D.P. Holly, C/W/3.
