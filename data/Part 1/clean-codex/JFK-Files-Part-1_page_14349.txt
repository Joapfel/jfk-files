16 July 1975. Director info WH/Miami. TYPIC.

Reference: Madrid 1639 (M124057).

1. Reference reported arrival 15 July of Narciso Martin Mora Diaz (210-301123), replacing Guillermo (Huillermo) Ruiz Perez (231-735296), and Roberto Mulet del Valle (201-821693), possibly replacing Crestes Barreda Perez (201-821007).

2. In view of Barreda’s close association with Ruiz, must consider possibility that he was DGI station chief Madrid instead of Aristides Diaz Rovirosa (201-791154). Mulet’s assignment, therefore, merits particular study as possible new DGI chief.

3. Request full traces on Mora and Mulet, including AMOT.

4. File 201-301123, 231-821693. CP-1.
