Ref: Rio de Janeiro 2153.

8 October 1966, 12580.

I. AMICE-31 reported CLASC congress voted 6 October to affiliate CSB. At same time voted decidedly to disaffiliate “Circulo Obrero” (A-31 could not identify this organization as CSIC but described it as “organization which supported military coup of Castelo Branco in Brazil”). Said they were disaffiliated for taking this position. Circulo Obrero representative only had opportunity to propose creation of a new organization which CLASC could support. When asked whether fight ensued on this issue, A-31 said no. Congress was in agreement.

2. No further details at present. Will advise when more information available.
