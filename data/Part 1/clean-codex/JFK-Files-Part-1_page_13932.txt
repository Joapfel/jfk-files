Cable Secretariat Dissemination. Classified message.

251614Z August 70. CITE DO Base Washington 27909698.

DO Base, New York. Info: Director.

AKULE/DOMOPS/AMROSE/WUSLAB.

Ref: DO Base Washington 2781 (C-146109).

1. (WUSLAB) Lunched with Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez (201-332665) at Beaux Arts Hotel, New York City, 21 August 1970.

2. Rodriguez stated he interested opinions, not documents, and suggested WUSLAB/I talk to prominent U.S. and Latin American officials in government, international organizations, and foundations to determine what is in the wind regarding U.S. policy toward Cuba. Rodriguez contended that there are many factors indicating increased U.S. pressure on Cuba. This situation forces Cuba to accommodate its own revolution within the rigid system of international communism and away from the independent principles of the 26 July Movement. Thus Cuban society is not the joyful one Fidel promised.

3. Rodriguez gave WUSLAB/I a large HTBASTE interoffice envelope containing a book by Raul Roa, a broadside by Victor Vaccaro, and copies of Granma and Verde Olivo.

4. Rodriguez exerted no pressure on WUSLAB/I and seemed satisfied when WUSLAB/I remarked that he would probably not go to New York City again for some.
