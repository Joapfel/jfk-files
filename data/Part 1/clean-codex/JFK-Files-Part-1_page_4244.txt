DIR info WAVE CITE CAPA 3117. Reference: DIR 6C849.

1. Maj. (FNU) Gutierrez refers to identical retired Major Jose Isabel Gutierrez Rodriguez, Venezuelan Military Academy class 44. Historically a rightist (rightest) plotter, in later years influenced by Marxist ideas of Lt. Col. Juan de Dios Moncada Vidal, who was in jail. Gutierrez declared himself an enemy of the Accion Democratica Party and Leoni (Leomi) government. He was involved in several conspiracies, the last being that of General Jesus Maria Castro Leon. According to CIWHIM-5, the Venezuelan Army G-2 and SIFA were aware but not concerned about his activities. A military arrest order was pending; therefore, it was doubtful Gutierrez openly traveled to Caracas.

2. According to CIWHIM-9, Gutierrez was involved in contraband between Miami and Venezuela in conjunction with several Venezuelan pilots, such as (FNU) Parodi, probably identical with Gonzalo Parodi Granes; see WAVE 6881 SJAN 65. Gutierrez represents Anselmo Alvarado, who was married to the sister of Gutierrez’s wife. Alvarado is a Union Republicana Democratica (URD) politician and owner of racehorses.
