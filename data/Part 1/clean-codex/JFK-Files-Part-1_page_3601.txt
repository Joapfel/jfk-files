1. HQS requested AMBIDDY-1 (A-1) not involve himself in Alejos-Ponciano-Somoza meeting, which seemingly is to take place in Managua during week 22–28 November. He was asked to postpone trip in order to avoid grist for rumor mills. A-1 understood but explained he had a long-standing appointment with Somoza relating to other matters, such as turnover of bases, etc. As a compromise, he offered to depart Managua 23 November for San Jose, thus avoiding meeting with Roberto Alejos, who was due in from WAVE the same day.

2. In San Jose, A-1 is hoping to receive an invite from IDEN-1 via the Venezuelan Embassy, San Jose. If this falls through, he will return to Managua in order to ascertain what transpired at the above-mentioned meeting. However, if he receives an invite, he plans to travel to Caracas on or about 29 November. He has been instructed to stay at the Tamanaco Hotel and register under the name IDEN-2. He was told if KUBARK wished him continued.
