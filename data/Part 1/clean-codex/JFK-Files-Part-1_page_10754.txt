Dispatch. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Info: Chief of Station, Tegucigalpa. From. Subject: Political/General—Rolando Masferrer reportedly to arrive in Honduras.

Action Required—References: Enclosed is an unsourced newspaper article from “El Cronista” announcing the expected arrival in Honduras of Rolando Masferrer. It is said that he’s coming to stay indefinitely. Further, the article asks mysteriously what powerful forces have brought about this resettlement. Any information Headquarters can offer on this matter would be appreciated since the Station no doubt will be asked about him.

Joseph E. Ragalie.

Attachment: Newspaper article H/W. Distribution: 3—DC, WHD.

Correspondence to: 1st Lt. Dett. Dispatch symbol and number: HHTA-6426. Date: 30 August 1972. AQIS file number: 201-042669.
