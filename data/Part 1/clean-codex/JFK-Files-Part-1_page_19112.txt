Official Dispatch

Dispatch No. WPA-19526

To: Chief, FIS  
From: Chief of Station, Paris  
Date: 26 March 1951  
Subject: General transmittal. Specific: List of Allied Communist-dominated organizations.

1. Attached is a copy of a list given to Leon H. Chesman recently by Jack West, Legal Attaché. The attachment was originally given to West by Keith Angell, Security Office, TCA-OSS, Paris. Mr. Angell advised that he had received this list from a source in the Prefecture of Police, Paris, and that it is based upon information in Prefecture files and investigation by that agency.

2. It will be noted that this list is apparently quite recent date in that these international organizations recently expelled from France (IFDU, WFDY, FTU) are noted as “dissolved.”

3. Mr. West also transmitted a copy of this list to Mr. William Crawford in the Embassy here.

Gustav Jøterson

Index: 29.4.13.727
