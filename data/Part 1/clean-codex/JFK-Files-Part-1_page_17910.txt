Official Dispatch via Air (Special Air Mail pouch).

Dispatch No. H-19-5982.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Date: 14 February 1958.

From: Chief, WMD.

Subject: General—Operational/LIFZAT Support. Specific—The Daniel E. BROWN Bookcase.

References: A. EER 5865, 21 January 1958; B. EEU 1020, 6 February 1958; C. EER 06768, 5 February 1958; D. EEA 1032, 7 February 1958.

Action requested: Destroy or return all copies of the attachment to reference A to Headquarters.

In view of references B and D, it is requested that the copy, or copies, of the attachment to reference A be destroyed by the Station or returned to Headquarters. If destroyed by the Station, please advise Headquarters by dispatch so the date of destruction. This procedure is necessary to insure the security of this service should EIEROT later use the bookcase in another area.

10 February 1958.

Distribution: Mexico City; W/II/III/Mexico/Jack; W/II/III/Chron; RI; Subject: W/Logistics E.R. Heilerman.

Coordinating officer.
