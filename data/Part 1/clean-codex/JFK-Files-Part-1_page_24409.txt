1. SELEVER/17 provided the following memo on 2 May:

“Subject: Richard Gibson 201-306052.

Subject is believed to be an Afro-American journalist residing in London. He takes a keen interest in African political affairs.

It has come to our knowledge that subject, who has often been in touch with South African political expatriates, is now suspected widely of being both a COFIRM agent and a South African intelligence contact. The Tanzanian Assistant High Commissioner in London has apparently also been informed of the suspicions against subject and has promised to alert his government with a view to having subject declared persona non grata in Africa, above merely FYI.”
