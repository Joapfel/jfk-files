Via: Air. Dispatch No. IIRSA-198.

To: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires. From: Chief of Station, San José.

Subject: General—Operational. Specific—HUMANOID.

Reference: DIR 90960, 23 June 1953. Date: 7 July 1953. Information: Wash.

1. Transmitted herewith is the HUMANOID file, which, through a regrettable inadvertence, was not forwarded to your station sooner. After perusal by your station, it is requested that the file be returned to San José for use in briefing the incoming Chief of Station on the overall scope and ramifications of these activities formerly handled by subject, which will be reassigned upon HUMANOID's departure.

2. In view of the fact that subject has sold his house and furniture and must effect delivery on 31 July 1953, and because a tentative departure date of 1 August 1953 has been set, your early attention to reference would be greatly appreciated.

3. The HUMANOID file will be returned to Buenos Aires for permanent retention after it has served the purpose set forth in Paragraph 1.

James K. Russo.

Enclosure: 1. File.

Distribution: 1—Buenos (Buen) w/enclosure; 1—Wash w/o enclosure.

6 July 1953. SPYKET.
