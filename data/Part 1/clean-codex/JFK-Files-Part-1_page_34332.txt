Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Athens. Bio: Chief, WHE; Chief, EE. From: Chief of Station, JHWAVEZ. Subject: ANSCROLL/Press Release and Jose Luis Galbe. Action required: For Athens to determine. Reference: WAVE-5789 (15 September 1965).

Will be identified as United States Marine Corps, Camp Eugene in Houston, TX. No insisting proposal. Abstract.

1. We attach under separate cover a copy of the press release on Jose Luis Galbe that the reference indicated would be forthcoming. Also attached is a list of the newspapers and other organizations and individuals that were mailed a copy of the release. The so-called “Black List Firms” are those Greek firms that according to ANSCROLL have had or do have commercial contacts with Cuba. The letters that were mailed to the Minister of Foreign Affairs and to the Home Minister were, of course, originals and these two gentlemen were not provided with a copy of the press release itself.

2. WAVZ is anxious to keep the Galbe pot boiling and we welcome any further suggestions to this end.

Philip G. Elmard.

Attachments: 1. Press Release; 2. List.

Distribution: 3 - COS/Athens, with attachments, USC; 2 - C/EE, with attachments, USC; 2 - C/WH, with attachments, USC; 2 - WH/C, with attachments, USC.

Date typed: 24 Sep 65. Date distributed: 30 September 1965. Dispatch symbol and number: UFGA 23284. Headquarters file number: 19-132-49/3.
