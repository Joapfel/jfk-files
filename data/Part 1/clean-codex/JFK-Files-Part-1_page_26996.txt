CONTACTS/MIAMI  
From McDonnell.

John Dreaney, OEG, will be coming to Miami weekly in December in connection with the trial of the first individual alleged involved in the HORSINS of Port-au-Prince.

New subject: Mitchell Livingston Kempell of Atlanta is mentioned in a Washington Post article today in connection with alleged receipt of guns shipped from Vietnam through OILIN Air Force bags. Mr. Dreaney suggests that you adopt, if possible, any contact with Kempell.
