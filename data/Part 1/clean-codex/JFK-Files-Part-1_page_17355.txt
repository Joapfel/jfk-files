19 April 1963. IN21426.

1. On 17 April 1963, at 1035, Scantling called Ramon Sinobas and told him that he went embassy on 19 April but saw many strange people in area and was afraid to enter. Sinobas then asked Scantling get together with him some place outside embassy on 19 April, suggesting a cape. Scantling then told Sinobas that the important thing was for Sinobas to follow the instructions in his letter that he sent on 7 April 1963. Sinobas tells Scantling that this is what he wants to talk about. Scantling guarantees Sinobas that if he follows instructions it will benefit both Sinobas and him. Sinobas then asked Scantling call him 20 April and they can agree on time and place to talk. Scantling then told Sinobas that if Sinobas is not in agreement with the instructions he would be obliged to get in touch with one of his superiors. In reply Sinobas said that was Scantling's problem, not his. Scantling closed the conversation by saying he would call Sinobas on 20 April. (LIENVOY B/2).

2. Scantling's written report re contact with Sinobas 17 April substantially same.

3. Station comment: Fact Sinobas requested Scantling call him 20 April possibly due expected arrival Manuel Vega or AMSTET-1 19 April.
