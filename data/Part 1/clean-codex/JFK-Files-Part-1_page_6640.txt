Classified message. Date: 302, 9 July 1960. Routing.

To: Director. From: Mexico City, July 10, 0029Z 60. Action: WH 5 (MR Reynolds, WH Division notified 2050, 9 July 60). Operational immediate. Info: DCI, D/DCI, DDP, COP, ADDP/A, CA 2, CA/PRG, CA/PROP CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, 10D 4, S/C 2. IN 2264.

To: DIR. Info. Cite next 5853. Ref: A. DIR 38758 (OUT 94420); B. MEXI 5852 (IN 22332).

1. Late night 8 July COS had conference para 7 ref 6.

2. LIFENSOR states did not approve either in general or specific text speech Emilio Sanchez Piedras nor items to be treated speech in advance. Sanchez spoke as individual Congressman, did not speak for government, nor did he speak for whole party (PRI). He said senators and congressmen “as in U.S.A.” speak freely and on own.

3. LIFENSOR says portion speech regarding Cuba was “widely misquoted.” He said in no place did Sanchez mention Castro or Cuban government. He said Sanchez expressed friendship and sympathy for “Cuban people” and he pointed out this had been U.S. government policy also. Reporters, he said, have interpreted this speech to mean Cuban government and Castro regime, which LIFENSOR states is not Mexican policy. He said, “The sooner Castro and Trujillo are gone from the Caribbean, the sooner this portion of the world can return to peace and normalcy.”
