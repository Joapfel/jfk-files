From: Mexico City. Number of pages: 8. Report made by: Arthur S. Stough. Distribution: 5 copies to Headquarters; 2 copies to Files. Report No.: HU-1464. Number of enclosures: none. Approved by: Peter R. Aumont. Source cryptonym: LIFAT/LIPRICK. References: none.

Subject: Soviet Commercial Activities in Mexico.

1. Forwarded herewith is the first in a proposed series of monthly reports on Soviet commercial activities in Mexico City. Source LIFAT has since December 1955 developed so much more information on this subject than ever before that this Station feels a separate monthly summary should be put out.

2. This initial report contains some general background and personnel information on the Soviet commercial office in Mexico for the guidance of Headquarters.
