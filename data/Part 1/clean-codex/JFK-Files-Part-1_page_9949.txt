Dispatch. Proceedings.

To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Marked for indexing.

Ref: Chief of Station, Tegucigalpa.

From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Project: PBRUMER. Letter received by LIRIG-3 from Rene OCHOA Funesz.

Action required—references: FYI.

Forwarded as Attachment One, herewith, is a report written by Wallace B. ROWTON dated 5 August 1967 about a letter recently received by LIRING-3 from Rene OCHOA Funes, the Honduran revolutionary in Tijuana. Forwarded as Attachment Two, under separate cover, are photocopies of this letter. It does not look, from the sound of the letter, that OCHOA's morale is too high.

Raymond A. Freuden for Willard C. CURTIS.

Attachment: I—Report. II—Copy of letter under separate cover.

Distribution: 1—COS/Tegucigalpa with Attachment II only; 2—C/WHD with Attachments I and II; 2—Films.

Cross reference to: DEGA-32815. Date: 17 August 1967.

Classification: Secret. QCS file number: 201-798301.
