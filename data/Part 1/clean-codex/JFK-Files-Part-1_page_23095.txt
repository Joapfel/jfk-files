Bustamante appeared grateful for this offer and gave four letters when he had lunch with him on 30 April. Photocopies of these letters are forwarded under separate cover. We leave any exploitation of the information contained in these letters to Headquarters. Of some interest is the fact that Bustamante hopes to attend a medical congress in Mexico in December 1967. Also interesting are the details which the doctor gives on his work in Cuba. Bustamante mentions, for example, in his letter to Dr. Alfonso Millan in Mexico, that he is currently making two separate investigations, “Material and moral inducements for productivity” and “Motivation of a religious group in Cuba.” A reply to one of Bustamante’s letters received by on 26 April is also attached under separate cover. Information provided by Bustamante on the rise and fall of Drs. Machado Ventura and Rene Vallejo as Fidel Castro’s private physicians and confidants was disseminated in a separate intelligence report.
