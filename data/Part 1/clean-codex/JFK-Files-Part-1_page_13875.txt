29 April 64. IN 71516. Priority OTTA DER CITE MEXI 9137.

Reserved for APOK DYVOIR ZRIKOTICK.

References: A. OTTA 1609 (IN 69508); B. OTTA 1611 (IN 69843); C. DIR 17103.

1. Per reference S: Alfredo Mirabal Diaz, AGS 40, Cuban consul, arrived in MEXI 2 September 63. Reportedly does not know his job as consul and is held in contempt by his staff for incompetence. Knows Juan Rejano (RESJANO) and had one known social contact in October 63 with Pavel Yatskov, Soviet (SCVLET) consul, MEXI.

2. Manuel (MANTEL) Vega Perez, third secretary, known Cuban, returned to MEXI 16 April 64 to replace Rogelio Rodriguez for unspecified time.

3. No traces of Noel Guerrero (GUERRERC) since November 62, when he reported in Cuba.

4. Rogelio Rodriguez, known Cuban, left for Cuba 25 April, apparently PCS. Had numerous contacts in MEXI, which were reflected in target study now (HOW) in process. Will forward.

5. Roque Dalton Garcia, contact of LICOCRY-1 August 62, went to Cuba for 26 July celebration in 62. In January 64 entered MEXI illegally from GUAT with no documentation.

6. No information on Victor or Laura. All significant information on Thelma (FELMA) King, Jorge Turner Morales, and his brother David Turner Morales previously forwarded HOS.

7. No traces subject reference A.

8. For your information: Check flight lists for 24 and 25 April; they fail to show Cuba travel by any subjects of the references.
