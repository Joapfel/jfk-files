Section 22. Clubs, Societies and Other Organizations.

List names and addresses of all clubs, societies, professional societies, employee groups, and organizations of any kind, including membership in or support of any organization having headquarters or a branch in a foreign country, to which you belong or have belonged.

1. Central Methodist Church, Brockton, Mass., U.S.A. Dates of membership: attended since birth; member since circa 1935.

2. History Club, Brockton High School, Brockton, Mass., U.S.A. Dates of membership: 1937–1941.

3. Dramatic Club, Brockton High School, Brockton, Mass., U.S.A. Dates of membership: 1939–1941. OSAMEQ JIN Chapter.

4. Order of DeMolay, Main St., Brockton, Mass., U.S.A. Dates of membership: circa 1940–46; no exact records.

5. Boston University Wesley Club, Boston, Mass., U.S.A. Dates of membership: 1942–43. Methodist Student Club.

6. AXA Fraternity, Boston University, Boston, Mass., U.S.A. Dates of membership: 1942–43; pledged, but did not complete membership.

7. Yale Lucky Foundation, First Methodist Church, New Haven, Cov., U.S.A. Dates of membership: 1943–44; 1946–47.
