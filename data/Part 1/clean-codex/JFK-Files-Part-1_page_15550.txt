Classified Message. Date: 12 April 1961. To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3. Info: COP, C/CA, C/CI, C/EE 3, S/C 2.

April 12, 1519Z 1961. Routine. IN 34729.

DIR INFO FRAN FROB CITE LEOP 1169.

RYBAT WIROGUE. Reference: DIR 34136. Per CSN 43-43.

1. CAJEEP/2 departed Leopoldville 12 April. Will remain Wiesbaden until 17 April. He then has business Middle East. Informed would be contacted if contract signed.

2. XALONJI recalled Acting Minister of Defense KAZADI, who left for Sud Kasai 11 April. ADOULA, who already [sic] Minister of Interior, now also Defense. Said discussed contract with ILEO, which they favor but have not signed. At least part of reason is fear any Congolese minister commit himself to positive action. If contract signed by 16 April, cable notification to CAJEEP/2 will set in motion implementation of contract.

3. For headquarters: dispatch follows.

End of message. C/S comment: Apparently wrong reference. Possibly DIR 34146 (OUT 94186) intended.
