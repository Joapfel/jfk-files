7 March 1953

Memorandum for: Chief, IO/PP

Attention: Mr. Thomas Braden

Subject: Mr. Ramsey

1. Reference our conversation sometime ago, I would like to give you the results of our Registry checks on Ramsey, who may or may not be identical with the present British citizen employed by EFE in Hamburg.

PTT-289. 19 August 1948.

Subject arrived in Trieste under sponsorship of Charles K. Moffly, director of AIS. He speaks fluent Sorbian and during the war was commander of a Yugoslav camp in Egypt. Has been assigned to planning section of AMG. AIS is cover for SIS.

FTTA-291. 3 June 1949.

Individual believed identical with subject signed interrogation report either as or for the Trieste Security Officer.

IN-HO33. 24 September 1949.

Antomije, suspected EA for British, has for three weeks been under constant AIS interrogation by an Italian-speaking British sergeant in the apartment of Maj. A. I. E. Ramsey. Surveillance reveals apartments of several FEE officers in building. Gold, SIS chief, seen entering and leaving building with subject and several unidentified British women.

PTT-371. 5 October 1948.

Subject is the head of five British officers who are in Trieste making a survey of the entire area prior to conducting special mission in Yugoslavia.
