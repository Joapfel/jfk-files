Grids: C. CON. JMAT: WH/3/N. Extension: 4521. Date: 31 August 1967.

Message form.

Cite Director.

1 September 1967, 2207Z.

To Caracas, JNWAVE.

Reference: A. JIMWAVE 8997 (IN 40592). B. Caracas 7894 (IN 41504).

1. Based on information currently available, HQ has no objection to station contact with AMCLEVE-15 pending confirmation from other station sources he actually works for DIGEPOL and in what capacity. If contact desired, AMCLEVE-15 standard clearance procedures must be initiated.

2. Can Caracas discreetly confirm DIGEPOL job?

3. Request WAVE duplicate Field 201 soonest and send to Caracas and HQ for review. Re reference B. Does WAVE have additional information for Caracas suggesting caution in contact with AMCLEVE-15?

End of message.

WH comment:

*AMCLEVE-15 reported to WAVE he obtained job with DIGEPOL in Caracas and was involved in DIGEPOL operation culminating in capture of Espinoza, a Cuban.
