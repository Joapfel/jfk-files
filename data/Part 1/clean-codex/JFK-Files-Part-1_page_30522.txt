General Headquarters, Far East Command, APO 500

7 September 1951

AG 201 - AGCM

LO 250-3

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individual concerned

1. Following named individual will proceed on or about 8 September 1951 to Pusan and Seoul, Korea, for a period of indefinite temporary duty in connection with military intelligence activities. Upon completion, will return to proper station.

Mr. Newton S. Miller (Miler), DAC employee, GS-13.

2. Travel by military aircraft, rail, and government motor transportation authorized. An allowance of sixty-five (65) pounds personal baggage authorized while traveling by air. No per diem authorized.

3. Subject is authorized to perform duties as official courier during period of temporary duty.

4. Mr. Miller will wear the Army officers' uniform, less insignia, in combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from QM Sales Store upon presentation of these orders.

5. Individual may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after completion of temporary duty.

6. Munitions and physical inspection as prescribed by Circular 9, FEC, 27 February 1950, will be completed by each individual prior to departure.

By command of General Ridgway:

Thomas F. Whaley  
Captain, AOC  
Assistant Adjutant and Commandant
