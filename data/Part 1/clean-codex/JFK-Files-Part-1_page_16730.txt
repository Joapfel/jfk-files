C/S comment: Requested information regarding Cuban arms discovered in Venezuela be brought to the attention of LITENSOR.

1. COS can of course deliver reference information directly to LITENSOR. Suggest, however, that such clandestine passage does not place GOM in position where positive cooperation of other OAS nations is stimulated.

2. Certainly government realizes Mexican posture on this matter is extremely important. Would not government then be willing to send a member of Betancourt’s staff to Mexico (MEXI) for a personal meeting with LITENSOR to present new documentary evidence, perhaps immediately before international surfacing? Same Venezuelan representative could hold press conference after LITENSOR meeting. This would insure full press coverage, with editorial follow-up by station assets. Perhaps GOM would feel forced to take a more positive stand because of fear of local public opinion reaction.

3. Should government representative have difficulty obtaining interview with LITENSOR, COS might be able to expedite.

4. In any event, COS can do should paragraph two action be unfeasible.
