7 February 1950

Memorandum to: GOP

Via: DS III, CPT

Subject: Berlin Intellectual Congress

Reference: GOP's memo to FB II, 23 January 1950

1. FB II agrees that the proposed congress deserves thorough support.

2. We would like to send Gerald Stroller to Berlin, to arrive a few days before the congress opens and to remain a few days after it closes. Mr. Stroller is a native of the city and is specially qualified to represent FB II's interests.

3. Our substantive recommendations have already been expressed to Mr. Baker.

J. Reynan, III

FB II/JEITI/cma
