Dispatch. Processing.

To: Chief, Special Affairs Staff.  
From: Chief of Station, JMWAVE.  
Subject: Typic/Administrative Award of Intelligence Star – Irving C. DEVUONO.

Action required: See para 2.  
Reference: UFGS 1382 dated 18 May 62.

A. JMWAVE would appreciate being advised as to a date which would be convenient for the director of KUBARK to make the presentation of the Intelligence Star to Irving C. DEVUONO.

B. In order that DEVUONO can make arrangements to be in Headquarters to receive the award on the date selected, a cable reply would be appreciated.

End of dispatch.

Distribution: 3 – Addressee.  
Date typed: 11 Mar 63. Date dispatched: MAR 13 1963.
