DIR @INFO MANA CITE WAVE 5431  
DYVOUR PERUMEN AMCLATTER-1

1. According to AMCLATTER-1, Dr. Julio Garceran del Vall Soñza, former Cuban Supreme Court magistrate, said there was a plot afoot to overthrow the Schick government.

2. According to Garceran, on 5 Aug 65 he visited Blanca Rosa Mayorgas, whom he knew in Cuba, at her home, 1319 Longfellow St., NW, Washington, D.C., where she resides with her husband, U.S. Army Colonel John H. Kelly, who was employed by the Pentagon. Blanca Rosa told Garceran that she would soon be leaving for Nicaragua, where her cousin lives, who is married to Tachito Somoza. At the request of Francisco Aguirre, owner of Diario Las Americas and brother of Horacio Aguirre, managing editor, she had agreed to deliver messages and reports to a number of Nicaraguans who, with Francisco Aguirre, were involved in plans to overthrow the Schick government.

3. Garceran explained that on 4 Aug he had accompanied “his Washington delegate,” Gerardo Mora, to the home of Col. Kelly, who, according to Mora, had wide military contacts in Washington by virtue of his position.
