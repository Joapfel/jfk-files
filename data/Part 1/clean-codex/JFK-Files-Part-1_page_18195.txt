20 October 1958

Memorandum for: WH/Personnel

Subject: Request for Staff Agent to fill BAP-8100 slot in Mexico City

1. It is requested that Mr./Personnel locate a Staff Agent to fill the BAP-8100, ID (FI), OS-12 slot in Mexico City, which was recently vacated by Wilfred O. CLUER.

2. This Agent must be bilingual in Spanish and should have operational experience, especially in physical surveillance techniques. It would be helpful if the candidate also has area knowledge. He will be expected to spot, develop, recruit, and direct surveillance assets.

3. It is requested that a suitable candidate be located as soon as possible so that the Contract Agent now performing those functions can be relieved of this responsibility and can devote his time to other tasks.
