20 May 68. IN 14784.

Director info: JMWAVE, Madrid, Paris. PBRUMEN LISASSY.

References: A. Mexico City 5535 (IN 08508). B. Madrid 4911 (IN 09784).

1. LISASSY-1 returned to Mexico City 15 May. After the Madrid visit, Ref. B, LISASSY-1 visited Frankfurt, East and West Berlin, and Paris. He did not call on the Cuban Ambassador in Paris because he decided it would be counterproductive to his own relationship (NR LIRING-9) with MEXI. He also felt that although they had met and he knew the Ambassador slightly, there was a strong possibility the Ambassador would not remember him and would refuse to see him. The purpose of the contact LISASSY-1 planned was to inform the Ambassador that Dr. Enrique Huertas, who considers himself the most representative of Cuban exiles, is willing to meet Fidel Castro to discuss the possibility of rapprochement with the Huertas group. During a 17–18 April meeting in Miami, Huertas claimed he was in contact with high-level persons in the Castro government who may be receptive to a proposal for rapprochement without Castro.

2. LISASSY-1 told the Station originally that his trip to Europe was to visit pharmaceutical firms on behalf of his company regarding a new contraceptive.

Classified message. C-82064.
