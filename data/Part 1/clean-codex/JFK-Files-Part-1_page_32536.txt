DIP CITE MADR 6406. Administrative personnel.

Embassy has received WIR 624 regarding the resignation of Mr. Earl Williamson and shipment of HHE to Jacksonville, Florida. Williamson and wife sailed 1 December and arrive New York 9 December. Presume he will report headquarters 13 December. Embassy may not ship HHE until full shipping address supplied. For your information, employee did not submit resignation to State Department prior to departure from Madrid. Embassy is requesting shipping address information from State by telegram.

CFN: 5406. WIR 624. Mr. Earl Williamson. HHE. Jacksonville, Florida. New York 9. Headquarters 13. Not ship HHE. FYI, not submit. Madrid.

3 December 1985. IN 07186.
