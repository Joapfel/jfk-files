Attachment to CO-01-3/753, 048.

As a supplement to bits of information which only transmitted here is a summarized account on the movements and activities of Halford Maurice, alias Pastor Partial, born 3.3.1926, U.S. citizen, economist, married to Edith; domiciled at Bellin 36-9, Mexico 7, D.F., during his sojourn in Switzerland from 9 October to 10 November 1958.

On 15 October, the subject and his wife arrived by air from Lisbon at the Hotel “Schweizerhof” in Zurich. On 18 October, he and his wife took the 1320-hour train to Geneva, where they stayed at the Hotel “Bernina.” On 19 October, from 1700 to 1900 hours, he visited Eregn David, born 1.9.1904, citizen of Argentine, counselor with the ILO in Geneva, domiciled at Avenue André Gide 2, Chile-Southernes/Geneva.

On 20 October in Geneva, he inquired at a local travel agency about the possibilities of a flight from Zurich to Prague. On that day, the Halfords traveled by train, leaving Geneva at 1245 hours, to Zurich, where they again occupied a room at the “Schweizerhof.” At 1830 hours, the subject visited the information office of the Zurich train station to learn about train connections for Prague and for Lubano. On 21 October, in answer to a telephone call, he visited the Czech Consulate General in Zurich, where he stayed from 0940 to 1115 hours. At 1630 hours, he went to consult the Swissair information desk at the train station. He stated that he possessed a flight ticket for Mexico–Lisbon–Zurich–Tel Aviv, but had no definite booking so far and wanted his luggage to be stored at Milotan Airport for the time being. He then mailed letters to:

1. Mr. David C. Halford, 5411 South University Ave., Chicago, Illinois.
2. Mr. Theodore Halford and family, 22 Sahler Ave., Milton, Massachusetts.
3. Rabbi and Mrs. Hiller Cameran, 4 de Kald Place, Morristown, New Jersey.
4. Mrs. Annie Frisch, 17 Beach Road, Lynn, Massachusetts.

Letters 1–3 are of similar contents. Photostat copies of Nos. 1 and 2 are attached.

At 1930 hours, he inquired at the Swissair office whether any communication had come in for him.

On 22 October, the subject negotiated with the KLM air travel office to cancel his two flight tickets to Tel Aviv and obtain a refund.

On 23 October at 0925 hours, he inquired at the information office of the Federal Railways about train connections to Paris and fares. At 1025 hours, he entered the Czech Consulate General but reappeared in the street three minutes later.
