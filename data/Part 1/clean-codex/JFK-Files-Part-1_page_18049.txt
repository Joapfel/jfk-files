Classified message.

7 August 1969. M 16170.

Director: LIONION.

1. In an attempt to eliminate marginal operations, station intends to terminate LIONION-I pulse camera coverage effective 1 September unless headquarters indicates it considers product of sufficient value to warrant retention. Station presently obtains many pictures of LIRING consulate visitors since most first-time LIRING visitors try to enter through main gate, which is primary target of LIONION photo coverage. Also many consulate visitors later photographed at airport. It not possible have LIONION-I cover both entrances simultaneously, but he able move equipment to cover consulate if need arises.

2. File 50-6-122/3.
