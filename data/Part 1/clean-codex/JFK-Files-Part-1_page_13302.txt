When they saw Felix Faria coming, he was a wanted man. Felix Faria's alias was “Comandante Claudio.” The police had been looking for him since 1965—the guerrilla hero.

Carlos Navarro Infante accused him of killing by firing squad in the mountains of Falcon State the following people: Conchita Jimenez, 19 years old; her brother, Cristobal Campero; and Ramon Jimenez.
