27 November 1968. IN 43465.

282132Z November 1968. CITE Mexico City 8223. Director, Guatemala City; info JCOBRA.

RYBAT PERUMEN LIRING-3.

References: A. Mexico City 8159 (K), 201 WH, July. B. Guatemala City 9739 (F), 10612.

1. LIRING-3's written report, per LIRING-9's request, was reviewed by the Station with assistance from Leonard S. Tormet. The report was limited to actual experiences while incarcerated in Guatemala City and information actually received from FAR members in the same jail. LIRING-3 was briefed to declare himself ready to help Guatemalan rebels for ideological reasons and at the same time imply that he was in need of material help after long imprisonment.

2. LIRING-9 was waiting at the LIRING gate at 1730 hours when LIRING-3 arrived with the report. The meeting was held in the back room of the consulate and was limited to LIRING-3 and LIRING-9. Following are the high points of this meeting:

A. LIRING-9 did not read the report but had LIRING-3 give him the main points orally. He appeared most interested in LIRING-3's statements on how Guatemalan rebels feel toward PERUMEN (PBRUMEN). He nodded when LIRING-3 stated that what the rebels need is adequate funds, not additional funds.
