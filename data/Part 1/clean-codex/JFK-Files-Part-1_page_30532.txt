Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individual conscripted, D.C. Engineer, US-12

1. A nedition is left at O/A 17 April 1951 to Jusan, Troga and Soul, Koron, on detail for a period of approximately thirty days as official courier in connection with intelligence activities. Upon completion, will return to proper station.

2. Travel by mail, soft with TD. An allowance of sixty-five pounds personal baggage with while traveling by air. No per diem authorized.

3. Individual will wear the Army officer’s uniform, less insignia, in combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from A & A Sales Store upon presentation of these orders.

4. Individual may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Koror. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two hours after completion of detail.

5. Lamination and physical inspection is prescribed by Cir. 9, 27 February 1950, and will be completed prior to departure.
