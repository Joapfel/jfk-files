Classified Message. Date: 20 September 1961. To: Director. From: Geneva. Action: AF 7. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, FI, EE 4, SR 6, S/C 2.

20 September 1961.

IN 32112.

To DIR, info BERN CTE GNVA 2486.

ZIBET INK.

On returning to GNVA from BRUS via FRAN, FRIGANZA met ex-REDSOX candidate AЕASPIC at airport. Had very short chat. He said he “dropped in FRAN,” does not understand what he did wrong, carrying documents in the name Georg Reiner, can be reached through Savoy Hotel, FRAN. Plans to depart FRAN 21 September for Congo as has nothing else to do. Was major, will be again. Assured FRIGANZA always willing to help KUBARK. Sent best regards, “PAT.”

End of message.
