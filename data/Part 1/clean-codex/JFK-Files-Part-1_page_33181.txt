RYBAT-TYPIC-AIRCE AMBEDEW ANGHOST

26 March 1969. IN2231J.

Reference: PARIS 7500 (N 20502).

1. Subject of investigation mentioned paragraph I.E. reference is AMCHALK-5, in which station has current operational interest. Appreciate fact that in-depth demands affecting AMBEDEW-1 must necessarily be delayed, but in interest of operational security would appreciate HGS inclusion [and] further exploration [of the] topic of investigation. Next requirements sent Paris. Specifically, we are most interested in learning whether investigation involved use of contacts or agents in Miami area and, if so, their identification.

2. I.F., would be interesting to determine whether AMBEDEW-1 [was] within any aspects of approach to AMCHALK-5 which followed QUIN, estimated by two or three months, and the eventual AMSTALK/ANGHOST approach to ANGHOST.
