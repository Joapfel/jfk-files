Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
Into: Chief of Station, JMWAVE.  
From: Acting Chief, WH Division.  
Subject: Identification of Florida license plate 1-105538.  
Action required: For your information.  
References: A. MEXI-8361, 24 August 1966; B. DIR-31174, 27 August 1966; C. MEXI 8394, 26 August 1966.

1. Contained herein is supplemental information to Reference B.

2. Florida license (lineweaver) plates 1-105538 were registered to Lewis WOLSHIN for use on a 1964 Ford.

3. JMWAVE investigation revealed that WOLSHIN was born in 1908 and is presently residing at 684 9th Avenue, Hialeah, Florida, with his wife Olga A. WOLSHIN. Their telephone number is 887-7907. He has been employed as a pool manager or assistant park manager by Dade County since 1953. He had a satisfactory credit rating. Police checks in Miami indicated the following arrest record:

a. In 1934 he was arrested in New York City for felonious assault. The charge was later reduced to simple assault.  
b. In 1941 he was arrested in Savannah, Georgia, for investigation of auto larceny. He was released to New York.  
c. In 1941 he was arrested in New York City for violation of probation.  
d. In 1947 he was arrested in Las Vegas, Nevada, for peddling without a license.  
e. In 1951 he was arrested in New York City for petty larceny.

4. Neither RI nor WOBONE indices contained information on WOLSHIN or his wife.

5. Permission is granted to pass the above information to LNERGO. We are also making it available to their Headquarters.

Laura J. UTSTINE. LIEN/PAY.

Distribution: 2 - COS, Mexico City; 1 - COS, JMWAVE.
