Dispatch.

Subject: Edward C. Ticherman (p).

Closure Reference.

1. In February or March 1961 Subject, a contract agent (identity under separate cover) presently based in Mexico, was sent on TEI to El Salvador to make a private evaluation of general social, economic and political conditions in that country. Upon his return, Subject wrote a lengthy report which has been useful as background information concerning El Salvador as of early 1961.

2. On an official visit to El Salvador in early February 1962, the Chief, NTEU Support, visited Ambassador Williams. The Ambassador said that he was impressed with Subject's accomplishments during the visit described in paragraph 1, above, and requested that Subject again be sent to El Salvador on TEI. This is the first word to reach Headquarters that the Ambassador had expressed such a desire.

3. The COS, San Salvador is requested to ascertain what specific tasks the Ambassador has in mind for Subject at this time.

End of dispatch.

Attachment: Identity, u/s/c.

Distribution: 2 - San Salvador, with attachment as noted; 2 - Mexico City, with attachment as noted.
