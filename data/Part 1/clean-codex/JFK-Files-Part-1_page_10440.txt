25 May 1968, 0105Z. Cite Mexico City 5683.

Guatemala City. Info: Director.

Refs: A. Mexico City 5590 (IN 12717). B. Guatemala City 9002 (IN 13509).

1. Hector Rodolfo (Sandoval) Alegria is a Guat rebel who, together with Francisco Clara, engineered LIRING-3 and Francisco Lara’s trip to Guatemala, where [he is] now incarcerated. Sandoval saw LERING-4 several times after LIRING-3’s arrest, at one point saying he might have to hold up a bank to come to LIRING-4’s assistance. According to Manuel (MARCUE) Pardinas, Sandoval was arrested 5 May shortly after his wife returned from a trip to Guatemala. Sandoval’s wife is now staying at a house owned by MARCUE together with Francisco Lara’s family.

2. In an attempt to counter the eventuality that Mexican police, on the basis of information provided by Sandoval, might be looking for LIRING-4 for interrogation, the Station has asked LIRING-4 to give up both apartments she and LIRING-3 have been renting. LIRING-4 is now living with her girlfriend. Before moving, she received a visit from a man who described himself as a lawyer of the Mexican Embassy, Guatemala City.
