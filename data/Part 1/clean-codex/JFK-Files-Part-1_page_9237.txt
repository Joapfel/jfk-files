Nov. 17, 1950

LUNSON FIRED FROM NY., 10 a.m.

He will phone Shub now. Perhaps they can lunch together.

Lunson “definitely wants” the photo-book. Is agreeable to whatever size, etc., we think it should be. Told him I had suggested to Shub that he proceed on an estimate of about 150 pages.

I forgot to mention Joe’s suggestion that Simon & Schuster (which Joe says would be better than Doubleday, probably) might do the book in the US, with Swen to have the foreign rights. Will tell Lyle about that this weekend.

Lunson comes to Washington this afternoon or evening. I will give him the KIFUNNY pictures this weekend.

WSS
