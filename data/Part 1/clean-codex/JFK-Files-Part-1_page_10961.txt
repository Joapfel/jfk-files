JFK Microfilm File Summary.

Summary: Reel 12, folder(s) 4A and 5, created by Bryant Rogers.

Subject of folder: LITAMIL-9.

201 number: 329609.

Inclusive dates of file material: July 1962 through August 1972.

Relevance or non-relevance to Oswald and JFK assassination: None.

Reason or assumed reason for inclusion in this collection:

In the HSCA testimony of Silvia Duran, she indicated that she knew him and that he was the Cultural Attaché at the Cuban Consulate when she was there. This information was not in the 201 file.

Summary of information within file:

LITAMIL-9 was recruited by the Mexico City Station in late 1962 while he was assigned to the Cuban Embassy as Cultural Attaché. He provided the Station intelligence while he was in this assignment. He was deactivated in March 1965 when he returned to Cuba and was seen only occasionally after that. His contract was terminated in 1970 because of his lack of access. Only one relevant document was found, which indicated that Proenza, who worked at the Cuban Embassy, believed that the strong objection by the Cuban Embassy to the Mexican Government over Duran's detention was a diplomatic error. This document was processed for release.

CIA/U.S. Government relationship: Agent.

Pages of non-JFK-related material: 491.
