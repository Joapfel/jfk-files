Rm-A-17G4.

Shouldering up to the present time, we are thoroughly thankful to all the personnel assisted by us on this service for the care and effort displayed in helping us realize our goals in this joint venture. We ask with hope that the future may be brighter than the past, and that all the work of the new century may be fulfilling the promises of the old.
