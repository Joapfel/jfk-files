On 31 October the Salpeters moved from Hotel “Schweizerhof” to the smaller hotel “Linnæus.” On this day they finally resolved to be requested referred by the FAA airline’s office.

Sunday, 19 November, the requested tourist visas were issued to the Salpeters, valid for 6 days and including hotel reservation for 5 days at St. Petersburg. They immediately departed by train and left Switzerland via Buchs/rail at 10:24 E. for Austria, travelling on paid-class tickets. PRICE-FNAGEL returns.

In the period from 31 October to 18 November, the couple did not make or receive any telephone calls and had no visitors, nor did they call on anybody.

19 November 1956.
