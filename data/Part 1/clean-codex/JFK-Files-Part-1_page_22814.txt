3 July 1963

Memorandum for: Chief, Contract Personnel Division

Subject: Termination of Contract – Harold R. NORMAYR

It is requested that the contract for Career Agent Harold R. NORMAYR, as amended, effective 1 June 1957, be terminated upon close of business 30 September 1963. Subject was advised of this termination on 1 July 1963, and the 90 days actual notice required by para. 15(a) of the contract expires on 30 September 1963.

Desmond FitzGerald  
Chief, Special Affairs Staff
