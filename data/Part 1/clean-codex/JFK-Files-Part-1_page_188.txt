C. Six Project Organizations

23. American Friends of Refugees, Inc. (AFR) — Manhattan, New York.

Instrumentality of Project AEMANTILLA/CAMANTILLA, which provided resettlement support for the Defector Reception Center in Germany.

The P/A, Augustus G. Elmendorf (T), was also an officer of the Tolstoy Foundation and International Research on Communist Techniques (AESTRIKER). AFR was funded by the exposed San Jacinto Fund.

23A. American Friends of Russian Freedom — Manhattan, New York.

Former name of American Friends of Refugees, Inc. (23). See above.

24. International Research on Communist Technique (IRCT) — Manhattan, New York.

Successor organization to Committee to Combat Soviet Kidnapping (CCSK) (AEPROCESS), which was established in 1954 at the initiative of Vladimir Rudin, head of the American branch of the Russian émigré organization NTS, following the Khokhlov affair. NTS was and is subsidized under SB/CA Project AEGIDEON. CCSK changed its name to IRCT. The two organizations successively received sterile grants under Project AESTRIKER, fiscal years 1954–1956, at $14,885 a year. IRCT published a magazine, Behind the Soviet Lines, which was distributed in the U.S. and the U.K. to generate opposition to Soviet acts of violence.

In addition to Rudin, IRCT officers included Augustus G. Elmendorf (T), who was also associated with AFR, Project CAMANTILLA, and the Tolstoy Foundation, and Eugene Lyons (T), former president of the American Committee for Liberation of the Peoples of the USSR (Project QACTIVE). Its attorney was John F. B. Mitchell, Jr. (T), former OGC staffer and currently the backstop for Victoria Projects, an entity under DO Project WUPSYCHE.
