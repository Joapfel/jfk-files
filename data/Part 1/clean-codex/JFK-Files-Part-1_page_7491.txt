Subject of document: LIU TSUN-CH'I.

RI File No.: 201-43745. Dispatch or source document symbol No.: DFB--30147. Date: 24 Dept. 53. Analyst: JP. Date processed: 3 Dec. 63.

Document disposition. Cross referred to this file: XXXX. Transferred to: RI File No.

Pertinent information: Comments made by Francis McCracken Fisher.

Fisher also recalled that sometime after the subject had gone over to the Communists after 1950, he, Fisher, met Harold Isaacs, a writer who formerly was a Trotskyite in his views, at which time Isaacs advised Fisher that he had known that the subject was a Communist as early as the 1930s in Peking, China. Isaacs stated that the subject had worked for the Tass News Agency in Peking after having graduated from college and had ever been sent to jail due to his Communist activities.
