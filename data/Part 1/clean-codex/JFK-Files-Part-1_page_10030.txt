J22195Z, Guatemala City 7738. Immediate Mexico City; info immediate Director. KAXOK.

Refs: A. Guatemala City (G-23416). B. Mexico City S-2339 (G-23436).

1. COS discussed Orozco case with ESLYRIC-1 on afternoon 2 August. ESLYRIC-1, who indicated lack of confidence in ability of Guatemalan ambassador to take effective action, said he will phone Guatemalan military attaché at 1600 hours 2 August and request that he initiate action through Mexican military to have Orozco held pending arrival of officers described below. Although channels which Guatemalan military will use are not known, they will probably go through high-ranking Defense Ministry officials who are close contacts of ESLYRIC-1.

2. ESLYRIC-1 said that Lt. Col. Jorge Manuel (Esteada) Estevez, official of Guatemalan Ministry of Defense, and Israel (Chinchilla) Morales, deputy chief of Guatemalan Regional Telecommunications Unit (special investigative unit under Office of President), will travel to Mexico City on 4 August to make arrangements for turnover of Orozco. Contacts of these two officers will probably be with Mexican Defense Ministry, and arrangements will probably be made to make Orozco.

April 29, 1972, Mexico City
