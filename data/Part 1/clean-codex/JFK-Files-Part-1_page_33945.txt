25 March 1952

Memorandum for: Chief, Finance Division

Subject: TPTONIC and TPREDIC

It is requested that the $4,000,000 revolving fund for TPTONIC be replenished by a total of $1,302,000 to cover disbursements from TPREDIC to TPTONIC under date of 18 March 1952 in the amounts of $924,500 and $377,500. Receipts for these disbursements from TPTONIC are being forwarded to the Finance Division, attention Mr. Edward Downey, this date, under separate cover.

George E. Duder  
Chief, Psychological Staff Division

Approved:

Thomas W. Bredon  
Chief, International Organizations Division
