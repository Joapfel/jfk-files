Suarez presented a plan for the formation of a Military Coordination (MC) group consisting of military chief of each group. It would jointly plan general actions and issue guidance relative to immediate objectives and existing need of Cuban exile community. This proposal approved; MC's first meeting was set for 29 June 1976 at a location in Florida.
