Director, Domestic Contact Service

24 March 1967

Attention: John McConnell, Chief, Miami Field Office

MN-086-67

Mitchell L. Harrell / 127900

Reference: MND-060-67

1. Harrell called the Miami office on 23 March 1967 and was told that Bart Grumley was not in, and advised the secretary that the Federal charges against him had been dropped. We have not confirmed this. We do not intend to renew our contacts with him for the time being at least.

2. Please advise John Grumley of the Office of the General Counsel of this development and give us whatever recommendations he or you may have as to our future relations with Harrell.

Juan F. Garcia Hay
