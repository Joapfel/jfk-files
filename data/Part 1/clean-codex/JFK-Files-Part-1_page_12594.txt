17 January 1969, 171925Z. CITE CARACAS (CAPACAS) 1618.

Director, WH/Miami.

RYBAT.

Reference: CARACAS (CAPACAS) 1552.

NH/316.

1. AMCLEVE-15 returned to CARACAS (CAPACAS) on 15 January. He reported that in Miami he had a telephone conversation with Edgar Puttari Pepozo, a rich Cuban who was formerly a minister in the Prio Socarras government. Buttari told him that he, Puttari, is a close friend of GPVALIANT and that GPVALIANT told him that although he could not say how or when, he was going to find a way to get rid of Castro. Puttari Pepozo estimated that within eight months after GPVALIANT takes office there would be action against Castro.

2. AMCLEVE-15 also reported that there are now three anti-Castro groups in Miami seeking U.S. assistance: the Nunez Portuondo group, the Penapaz group, and the Puttari group. AMCLEVE-15 added that Manuel Artime (Artine) was associated with Puttari in his movement and that this group has the inside track due to Puttari’s close personal relationship with GPVALIANT.

3. Index Buttari, Nunez, Penapaz, and Artime (Artine).
