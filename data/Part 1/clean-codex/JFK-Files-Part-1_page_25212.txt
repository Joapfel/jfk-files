United States

Mr. Roger A. Provencher

Position (January 1965): Second Secretary, U.S. Embassy in Moscow, USSR.

They are the most socially adept members of the U.S. Embassy.

They entertain well and their parties are fun. They both dance well. She serves wonderful buffets. She is French. Has visited SOURCE's father in Paris on one occasion. SOURCE has had the Provenchers to her apartment about 15 times since October 1962. SOURCE speculates Provencher may be an intelligence officer based on the fact that he has a “too good” apartment for his rank and more care is taken of him than of Mr. Logan in the political section.

Date acquired: September 1966.

Source says her husband has changed his mind about Provencher's being an intelligence officer. Possibly he is one, but not in Moscow.

15 September 1966
