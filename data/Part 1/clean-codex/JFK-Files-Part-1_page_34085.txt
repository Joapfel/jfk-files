In the Yara region, the coffee crop was 50% destroyed; the coffee beans had been ready for harvest and therefore were easily shaken off by wind. In Buey Arriba (Buoy Arriba), Regional Cauto-Bayamo-Jiguani, 500 women picked up about 10,000 full cans of coffee.

Fruit

Pinar del Río Province: In Guane-Mantua, 1,000 banana plants were damaged. In Regional Pinar del Río, 5,700 banana stubs were destroyed. In Regional PR-2, the guava plantations were damaged by the winds.

Havana Province: None.

Matanzas Province: None.

Las Villas Province: In Valle de Trinidad, the banana plantations and the fruit crop were completely destroyed. In El Mégano, 120,000 banana stubs were destroyed.

Camagüey Province: None.

Oriente Province: Between 80 and 90% of the fruit crop was destroyed. In Regional Baracoa, 40,000 banana stubs were destroyed. In Regional Palma-San Luis, 14 caballerías of banana were damaged. In Josué País farm, 30% of the 80 caballerías of banana were destroyed. In Buey Arriba (Buoy Arriba), Regional Cauto-Bayamo-Jiguani, 300 caballerías of bananas were damaged; only 70 were destroyed. All fruit in the vicinity of Santiago de Cuba suffered serious damage. Near Holguín, banana plants were leveled by wind. Near Yara, banana plants had been broken. In Regional Guantánamo, 35 caballerías of coconuts were destroyed.

Vegetables and “Viands”

Pinar del Río Province: None.

Havana Province: In Regional Ariguanabo, there were losses in agriculture, especially vegetable seedbeds.
