6. 1943, Berlin: Employed with Schutzpolizei (SCHUPOHIZEI) as a French-German translator.

7. 1946–47, Paris: Employed by Architects Carré Associated as an electrical installation planner and constructor of prototype models. Earned roughly 4 dollars an hour on the American scale, plus extra commission work.

8. 1951–52, Paris: In prison. Devised and sold on the French market a pocket automatic transit indicator. Received a cash settlement for this while in prison.

9. 1953, Lyon (Elyon): In prison. Devised and gave the prison administrator an invention for making cardboard boxes. The device was built and used by the prison administrator.

10. December 1955–April 1956, Gap: Employed by M.D. as an X-ray machine maintenance technician and operator.

11. 1956–57, Gap: Invented, patented, and sold a calendar device to the French commercial market.

12. 1958, Ambert: Invented and patented a commercial advertising device, photo-mechanical in nature. Models were built and sold to French advertising concerns.

13. 1959–60, U.S., September–May: Devised and passed drawings for two pieces of equipment to be used for Project ARSENTINEL.

14. July 1960, U.S.: At present, Subject is devising a low-cost telephone answering device.

Experience in Forged Documents:

May 1942, Aachen–Berlin: Falsified his own travel documents by using a pen to change effective dates.

1942, Berlin–Paris: Filled in German travel blank forms. Altered an old German authorization stamp for travel forms. Then made other falsified stamps for people interested in traveling across German-held territory.

1943–44, Berlin: Constructed and used various falsified travel document stamps copied from old travel documents for various people to travel throughout Germany. Used certain photographic techniques for changing and/or aging pictures in documents. At the same time, Subject also reassembled falsified old passports to form new ones, as well as counterfeited German financial vouchers.
