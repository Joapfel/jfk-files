Classify to file no.: TET-20747. File ID: return to branch. Action: Caracas; with #8 WHISA; VR. file DDP, WHISA, C/10A. Date: 26 January 1965, IN 67522. Time: 1802. By DD: 37.95.

T 262010Z. DIR INFO WAVE CITE CARA-9835. Immediate action.

On 26 January, Dr. Armando Fleites, of Segunda Frente de Escambray, urgently called on Pol. Counsellor Edward Long. Two weeks prior, via mutual friends, Fleites had requested a one-week visitor visa “on compassionate grounds” to visit family in WAVE area; Bowdler, in a personal letter to Long, advised that Fleites had forfeited the right to special treatment and would have to apply for an immigration visa. Fleites, who accepts as probably accurate press stories regarding capture of Gutierrez Menoyo, now claims that, in his capacity as number two leader of Frente, it is urgently necessary that he travel to WAVE area to spend one week with his group.

He states he would not even want to see his family, adding that now was the time for PEPRIME to prove whether “friend or enemy” of Cuban freedom. On the offhand chance KUBARK may have operational equities in Frente, Counsellor Long asks STA to check with headquarters. Tentatively advised by him KUBARK is not involved, but would inquire in any case. Suggest headquarters advise Bowdler and ascertain whether, in light of the above new circumstances, ODACID’s position regarding Fleites’ entry is as stated in his letter to Long.
