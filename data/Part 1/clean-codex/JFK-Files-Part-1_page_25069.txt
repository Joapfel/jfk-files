Dispatch.

To: Chief, BB Division. From: Chief, WH Division. Subject: REDTOP—Alexandr Mikbaylovich BROVAKH (201-185114). Action required—references. Reference: HSS-6229, 19 December 1968.

1. LIFERE reported the PCS departure of the BROVAKH family on 22 September 1968 aboard Sabena Flight 556. Subject travelled on Soviet Diplomatic Passport D-008133.

2. BROVAKH held the position of Third Secretary at the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City. He was replaced by Valeriy Vladimirovich KOSTIKOV (201-305052), who assumed the title of Second Secretary.

3. Forwarded herewith are recent LICALLA photographs of Subject.

For Willard C. CURTIS.

Attachment: Photo h/w.

Distribution: 2—C/SB with attachment h/w; 2—C/WH without attachment.

Cross-reference to: Dispatch symbol and number HOMA-36121, dated 27 December 1968; HOS file number 201-185114. Cross-reference: 201-305052.
