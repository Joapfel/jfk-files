CITE JAWAVE SSUS, 29 September 1962.

Director info Ottawa, Paris. Typic MRVIPER ANSCROLL.

1. Using disguised sensitive source info and overt data, station prepared study of Cuban misadventures in selling tinned and frozen lobsters to world seafood distributors of Montreal and Jacques Fourchtein & Cie of France.

2. Study released by ANSCROLL 26 Sept. AFP filed story 26 Sept and AMTAFFY-1 provided excellent coverage 29 Sept. Believe additional publicity of these facts, especially abroad, will further exacerbate at least Cuba/Canada trade. Release and clip forwarded HGS. Also airmailing Paris, pouching Ottawa.

3. Follow-up story in preparation.
