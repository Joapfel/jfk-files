LOGO is a logo design by a foreign artist. It represents the official logo of the state of Mexico. It was designed in 1949 by Mexican designer Ignacio Aponte Salinas. It has been used as the national logo of the country since its foundation. The logo consists of a square frame with a horizontal line through it, and above this a vertical line. In the center of the frame there is a figure which represents an ear of corn. Below this figure there are two rays or vertical lines that meet at the center of the frame. The base of the frame is where there is text written on it which reads “STATE OF MEXICO.” On the side of the frame there are two small circles or marks that are white in color on a black background.

The state of Mexico has always had its own flag. Its colors were first mentioned in 1510 when King Philip II of Spain, who was ruling over the area, ordered his son to make a flag for the state. This is how the current state of Mexico got its name, because it is said that Philip II wanted to honor his son, so he chose the name Philip II. The state’s current flag was created in 1893 by an engineer named Carlos Manuel Garcia. He used many different designs before he settled on what we see today.
