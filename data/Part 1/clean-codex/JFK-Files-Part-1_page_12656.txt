2121232 February 1976. Staff.

Cite San Jose 19250.

To: Immediate Buenos Aires. Info: Immediate Director, Caracas.

RYBAT VWLOFTY.

Ref: Buenos Aires 35654 C 834057.

1. Tell WKSCARLET-3 that, based on information passed to liaison by FBI, Bosch has been a target of interest to local liaison for about one year. Liaison is aware of Bosch’s bombing background, and their interest in him increased as a result of a series of local bombings and the imminent visit of Secretary Kissinger. Through their informants they learned that Bosch was getting support locally (housing, etc.); that he was using the name Conrado Cortez or Cortes. In early February, they learned that he was known to be using a Chilean passport in another name. In late January they pinpointed the neighborhood in which he was living and began an intensive search (they also had reports of his presence in the downtown area). Through postal authorities and other local sources they pinpointed the exact location of his house in Jardines de Cascajal and arrested him (this was done before San Jose Station was advised by W-3 via Buenos Aires as to his exact location). All above can be passed to W-3 and he should secret.

WKSCARLET-3 201.
