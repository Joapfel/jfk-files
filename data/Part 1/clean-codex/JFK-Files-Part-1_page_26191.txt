Contacts:

Victor RICO Galan is known to have been in contact with the Soviets as early as November 1956, when he was invited to attend a reception at the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City.

Umanislaw RACZKA, an employee of the Press and Propaganda Section of the Polish Legation in Mexico City, was in contact with RICO in July 1959.

Aleksandr BEKIER, counselor of the Polish Embassy in Mexico City and a known intelligence officer, was introduced by RICO to Carlo COCCIOLI, a homosexual Italian pro-Communist writer for Siempre magazine.

Mariano GARCIA Perez, Cuban consul in Merida, was in contact with Victor RICO Galan while in Mexico City in July 1966.
