Re: Whitehouse, Eduardo J.

Dear Mr. Medell,

Mr. Whitehouse was examined by me today for routine Federal Aviation Agency Class II physical, which he successfully passed, and was issued a medical certificate.

At your request, a chest X-ray and hemoglobin test were done. The chest X-ray was normal, and his hemoglobin was 16.2 gms. or 104.8%.

Mr. Whitehouse was given the following injections: typhoid-paratyphoid, typhus, smallpox, cholera, and diphtheria-tetanus.

Thank you for referring this airman.
