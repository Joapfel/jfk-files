Message Form. C.R. Hallwell, WII/1. 21 June 1965. File in C8, file no. 201-336474.

Routing.

To: Mexico City. From: Director. Confidential. Info: VR. File copy: CA2, CA/PROP, CA/PEG, SIG CEN, WH/CB, MILAPS, CULIC2.

To: Mexico City, MIISPAWN.

Cite DIR: 21420.

Ref: MEXI 3822 (IN 92168).

1. Concur with Station's approach. Under first phase of denigration (para 1A), ref could Tichborn make use of Emilio Pacheco, who [is] on both payrolls, to encourage Paces Llergo [to] oust Rico Galan from Siempre?

2. Hope that in second phase, you can expose subject directly. If papers refuse print original, maybe chance they reprint from LILISP-E bulletin. Major security caution is not [to] play denigration [to] all assets simultaneously.

End of message.

*Ref article provides long-awaited peg launch press campaign aimed at exposing Victor Rico Galan as Castro-Communist agent.

C/CA/PROP/Fryerson (in draft).

Desmond Fitzgerald, Chief, WHD.

R. J. Kaufman, C/Mid/1.
