9.04 Drinking

9.04.01 Does he drink? Not at all; average ✓; a little; heavily.

9.04.02 Does he drink alone (solitary drinker), with others mainly for sociableness, on formal occasions only, after work, or at home?

9.04.03 Does he appear to drink primarily for the taste, primarily for the effect, or for both?

9.04.04 His favorite drink is beer, vodka, wine, gin, whiskey, or no preference.

9.04.05 He prefers cocktails, straight shots, highballs, or short drinks.

9.04.06 He drinks slowly, average ✓, or quickly.

9.04.07 Does he gulp his drink or sip his drink? Sip his drink ✓.

9.04.08 Does he go out of his way to get a refill, usually wait until asked, or always wait until asked? Always wait until asked ✓.

9.04.09 Attitude toward wines? Very knowledgeable; enjoys but not particularly knowledgeable; indifferent about wines; knows nothing, couldn't care less ✓.

9.04.10 Does he talk at length, and obviously get pleasure from talking, about his liquor preferences; talk about his liquor preferences no more than anyone else; or never talk about his liquor preferences? Never talk about his liquor preferences ✓.

9.04.11 Is he openly proud of his native drinks and does he urge others to try them? Yes; no ✓.

Is he conventionally aware of his native drinks and offer them when appropriate? Yes; no.

Does he rarely mention his native drinks and not “push” them? Yes; no.
