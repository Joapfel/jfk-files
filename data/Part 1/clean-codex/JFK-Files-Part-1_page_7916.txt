Continuation of dispatch. Distribution statement and number: MMA-31099.

9. The Station sees no reason why BESABER cannot handle the development of BEWKEP and LOGINOV at the same time. Where possible, we will try to have BESABER arrange to cultivate them separately, but since the two targets are personal friends and see each other quite frequently, we plan to have BESABER be quite candid in admitting his relationship with one to the other.

10. Finally, we are well aware that since BESABER may well be compromised to the Poles, his relationship with LOGINOV will have to be considered in this light. On the other hand, there seems little to be lost, and possibly a great deal to be gained by having BESABER continue to try to develop a close relationship with this Soviet target.

For Willard C. CURTIS
