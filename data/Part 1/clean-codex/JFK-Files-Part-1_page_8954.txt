John H. Whitten:Jz. Unit: CHU/3/Mexico. Extension: 3940. Date: 16 November 1962.

Classified message.

To: Mexico City, Guatemala City.

From: Director.

Conf: WH 8.

Info: CA 2, CA/PEG, CA/PROP, CA/D 4, S/C 2.

Cite DIR. 49455.

1. Request Tichborn be made available soon to go to Guat and assess public opinion on main political parties, presidential candidates, and trends. In recent Guat City mayoralty election, Arevalo supporter, Villagron Kramer, ran surprisingly strong second, even without Arevalo's express endorsement. Desire check particularly on sentiment outside Guat City; events show that leftist trend in that city does not prove similar trend in other areas. PL's advise.

2. For Guat: Tichborn is U.S. citizen lawyer in Mexico City who also writer. Has done valuable survey of opinion in San Salvador. Recommend Tichborn consult station on arrival. PL's comment.

End of message.

WH/3/GUAT. WH/COPS. J.C. King, C/WHD.

C/WH/3. Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer. NED P. HOLMAN.
