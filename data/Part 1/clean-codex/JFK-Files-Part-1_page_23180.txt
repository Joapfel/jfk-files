Report No. CO-A-357/0.555-5. Date distributed: 17 August 1964. No. pages: 1.

The report of Sodium Pentathol to John 800 Confessions/Dr Jose Bustamante.

Date of information: 30 April 1964. Place and date acquired: La Vibora, Havana, April 1964 and earlier.

This is unevaluated information.

Source: Cuban national, A 14 013 601, age 52.

Source was a pediatrician who obtained his doctor's degree from the University of Havana. He worked for Clinica Santa Isabel, La Vibora, Havana from 1954 to 30 April 1964 and maintained a private office at his home. He departed Cuba via Spain 6 May 1964 with his wife and three children, arriving in the US 9 July 1964. One of several reports from this source.

CAC. This report was developed by a representative assigned to the office of preparation.

During many doctors' gatherings I heard various doctors comment that the GOC obtained confessions from people under interrogation by injecting them with pentothal (sodium pentathol). This practice was carried out by Dr Jose Bustamante, a well-known Cuban psychiatrist. He maintained an office in a large house (exact location and description of house unknown) near the new university annex in Reparto Country Club, Marianao (Marianoo), Havana. It was there that interrogations were conducted under Dr Bustamante's direction and supervision. I did not know Dr Bustamante personally.
