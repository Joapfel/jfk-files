WCRALLY WKSCARLET.

Ref: Director 552369.

1. Station carefully considered the risk in meeting WKSCARLET-3 today, but in view of the government’s discovery of WKNUGGET teletap operation felt that, if necessary precautions were taken—and they were—in arranging the meeting, WKSCARLET-3 might possibly be able to shed some light on whether or not the story on him related in any way to the government’s “spy search effort.” WKSCARLET-3 is not concerned about the story and on 15 May discussed it with friends in WKSCARLET-3. According to WKSCARLET-3, WKSCARLET is not concerned, and he notes no surveillance and no apparent interest in his activities and adventures. He claims that the story was planted by Erasto Fernandez.

2. Fernandez (W Fernande) originally hired him but since 1969 has been unfriendly to WKSCARLET-3. This unfriendliness appears to have been caused by WKSCARLET-3’s defection to COPEYARDS, working for WKSCARLET for eight years, and in 1973 failing to leave COPEYARDS and work for ACECO VICTORY.
