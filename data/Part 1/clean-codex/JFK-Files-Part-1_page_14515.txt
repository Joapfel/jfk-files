RUIZ, Orestes

Was a prisoner in Dom Rep, not in a prison, but in a concentration camp with 11 others for 15 days because he tried to escape, because he was taking military training. Had been asked there by one Jose Antonio ECHEVERRIA, who was killed in Cuba in an attack on the Presidential Palace on 13 57. ECHEVERRIA approached RUIZ at Havana University. When he went to Dom Rep, he found a bunch of Cuban gangsters instead of decent people. POLICARPO Soler, also known as Joaquin RODRIGUEZ (RODRICUEZ), was in charge of the group and was the one who arrested him. Had actually had 5 months of military training from November 56 to April 57.

Was in student demonstrations and passed propaganda against the Batista regime.

One Dr. VENAGE, Cuban, gave RUIZ a ticket to return to the U.S. from Dom Rep.

He is opposed to Communist philosophy. His group, the ____ university students, had a fight with Communists. Committee comments.

On or about 12 July 57 RUIZ wanted to obtain a permanent residence visa in the U.S.

On 5 November 57, he was notified that he had to leave by 4 December 57.

On 25 September 57, living with wife and child at 1440 N.W. 21st Terrace, Miami.

In November 57, at 1749 N.W. 21st St., Miami, and had joined there.

Overstayed his time. On 11 February 58, ordered deported to Cuba. Represented on or about 14 February 58 by the firm Walters, Moore, and Costanzo, 1008 Ainsley Bldg.

Notified 4 times of interrogation but had moved address the week of 25 July 58.

Order for detention issued 21 August 58.

Order for deportation issued 19 March 59.

Investigation showed he returned to Havana to Calle 5 #714 on 2 March 58 on an “expedition by plane.” As of 6 April 59, living at Calle 86 #705, Apt. 1, Miramar.

Chris Hopkins
