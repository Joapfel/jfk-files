11 December 1965. 12989. Priority. DIR INFO NEXI. CITE WAVE 78510. NO PIACN.

Type ARKNOB MNGUILE AMNIP.

E. Propose have AMKNOD-1 pass following to CUIS via [SY] letters.

A. From Alfredo Borges: Manuel Artime arrived Miami 6 December from Nicaragua, where he inspected base which he still has there. Artime continues to be very friendly with President Luis Somoza (SOHOZA).

B. From Osiel Gonzalez: “El Cojo” Roche was formerly C-2 chief in Camagüey. Later transferred to Havana. Took exile in embassy of Ecuador and left Cuba via Brazil (BRASIL). According to Osiel, Roche has made visits to Puerto Rico and Miami, but is now in New York.

C. From Osiel Gonzalez and Hector Ponce: Both II Frentes and Ejército Libertador have developed new momentum and are currently making plans for action. Neither source gave any details.

D. From Father Inurrieta: Domínguez Loyo, subdirector, Oficina de Gobernación in Mexico, is completely anti-Communist and is opposed to the revolution.

E. From Father Inurrieta: Name of priest who is confessor of President Díaz Ordaz is Father Mendoza, who is from Puebla. Meets.
