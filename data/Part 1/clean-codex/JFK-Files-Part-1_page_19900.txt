SIGNATURE

Miss Anna A. Taraseoff

Dear Miss Taraseoff:

Reference is made to your current contract with the United States Government, as represented by the Contracting Officer.

Effective 22 August 1966, said contract is amended by deleting all reference to income taxes therefrom, and by adding thereto the following paragraph:

“8. Taxes. As an employee of the Government you must satisfy your Federal tax liability on taxable income paid to you under para. (a)(one) (1) above. Such income may be paid you by or through a cover facility because of cover, operational and security considerations. The Tax Committee of this organization is authorized to make certain determinations and establish procedures which will result in the full satisfaction of your Federal income tax liability on said income. It is specifically understood and agreed that such determinations and procedures, whether oral or written, constitute an integral part of this contract and are legally incorporated herein by reference.”

All other terms and conditions of said contract remain in full force and effect.

UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

Contractor, Dancer

MAY 20, 1966
