Office Memorandum

To: Chief, IRS Staff.

Date: 19 December 1950

From: Employee Division

Subject: William V. Broe #35706

1. This is to advise your office that the subject has been granted permission to file application for employment with the Department of State.

2. In the event an offer of employment is made to the subject, this office will interpose no objection to the release.

3. For the convenience of the Department of State, it is requested that the appropriate security certification be prepared and forwarded to the Department of State as soon as possible.

H. C. Clinkscalp
