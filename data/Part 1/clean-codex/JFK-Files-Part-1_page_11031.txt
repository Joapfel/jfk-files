1. In September 62, briefed Leon Antich, Cuban diplomat, while Mexico City en route post Chile (7), told Carlos Lechuga Hevia, Cuban and Mexican, in presence of LITAMIL-9, that Cuban Foreign Office planned recall all ambassadors for consultation regarding innovations in Foreign Office based on suggestions to Cuban diplomats Havana. Since that time, however, no information available from LITAMIL-9 indicating diplomats being recalled.

2. Transfer of Cuban diplomats from Mexico City and believed reasons for same as follows. Carlos Lechuga Hevia left Mexico City 26 October 62 for Havana to take post, U.N. delegation, New York. Lechuga expected return Mexico City 22 December, where will again take up ambassadorial duties, probably after going Havana to report U.N. developments. Jose Antonio Garcia Lara left Mexico City 23 November 62 for Havana. Return to Havana to straighten out commercial problems, take exam and possibly report in person on contact KUBARK in Mexico City. Return uncertain. Ramon Cuenca Montoto left Mexico City 22 September 62 for Havana. Detection of Pedro Roig Ortega and poor administration of commercial activities probably account for transfer. Ana Maria Zlanco Tabrake left 12 December for Ottawa. Lechuga “love” affair believed reason.
