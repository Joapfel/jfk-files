Secret, 28 August 1968, cited Mexico City 6949. Rio de Janeiro and Recife for information, Director. REDCOAT.

Reference: Recife 467D (W 79641). 29 August 1968, IN 80609.

1. According to LIENVOY, on 24 August 1968 Francisco Juliao de Paula contacted Nicolay Lecnov and reaffirmed his solidarity with the Soviets in reference to the Czech situation. He then mentioned a book he had just finished, which should be published at the end of September. He has a copy in French, which he wants to give to Lecnov. The book is to be published in Mexico, France, Italy, Germany, and Sweden. Lecnov might be able to have it published in the Soviet Union.

2. Per paragraph three of the reference, the station is attempting to obtain additional information on the book. Will advise.

3. Above may be passed to CONGEN.
