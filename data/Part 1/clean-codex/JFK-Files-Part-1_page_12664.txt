RYBAT VWLOFETY.

Reference: Caracas 29135 (830540).

1. Orlando Bosch Avila was subject of interest to the FBI in early 1975. An itinerant FBI representative from Mexico City, Camarota, mentioned the name to the station, and it is believed he did the same to the local service, HUFABRIC, and may have left something in writing with them. Bosch’s name was also mentioned to the station by the local DEA representative, but it is now determined this was basically on behalf of the FBI.

2. On the occasion of Costa Rican President Daniel Oduber’s (Oduger’s) meeting at his ranch with Central American presidents on 10 July 1975, the itinerant FBI representative from Mexico City called the station to warn the Costa Ricans of an alleged clandestine infiltration by Bosch on the Pacific Coast to disrupt that meeting. HUGLIB-3, who had security for that meeting, checked out the information to no avail. This information was subsequently passed to the station by HOS and the Embassy, which received it via the Costa Rican Embassy, Washington.
