26 January 1971. Type: AMBROSE.

1. During his recent TDY (IDY) to Mexico City on the KOITUCK-16 operation, Woodrow G. Hoeberlin discussed with Terence D. Sistone our proposal to use AMMUG-1 to approach KDACOUSTIC-1. AMMUG-1, whose relationship with KDACOUSTIC-1 dates back to 1954, assesses KDACOUSTIC-1 as only feigning loyalty to the Preumen regime, as he is basically an opportunist.

2. When provided with the gist of information contained in the reference and asked to categorize KDACOUSTIC-1, AMMUG-1 commented that KDACOUSTIC-1 would have to be grouped with those who had come under fire for being something less than RASID revolutionaries, while AMMUG-1's approach to KDACOUSTIC-1 would probably have to assume the form of an “accidental encounter,” if [they] are willing to entertain any ideas addressee may have to devise a less contrived approach to KDACOUSTIC-1.

5. Request addressee comments on the above proposal.
