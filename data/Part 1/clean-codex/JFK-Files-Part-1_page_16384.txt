To: Director

042225Z. DIR CITE TEGU 5653. DYBOUR PERUMEN AMBIG. Ref: TEGU 5625 (M.C. 5/6). Following from Langosch.

5 Sep 91, 16:37:38

1. POSVING CHIEF and Langosch had private meetings. A sept with Chief of State Col. Oswaldo Lopez. Langosch briefed on DGI operations and organization, including information on Mario Sosa N. 'Arro', Maglio Moreno Espital, Luis Banegas (BANECAS), and Antonio Fernández Iza 'Cirle', who uses Honduran documents in the name Miguel Mancia Madrid. Also furnished recent travel lists and copy of DGI requirements on INDJ 06.

2. Lopez examined keen interest, reviewed all notes, said if knew some personally including Sosa, promised provide all fusible information on all notocrans through Posving Chief and expressed willingness to end full cooperation. Said government has about 25 Communists under arrest and has had to release others for lack of evidence but.

Per production by other than the issuing office is required.
