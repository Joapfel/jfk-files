LIEMPTY-6 was recruited in June 1954 as a member of the physical surveillance team. In December 1955, he and his wife occupied the LIMITED basehouse. He took pictures for 13 years and was terminated 31 May 1968, signed a quitclaim and secrecy agreement, which was witnessed by EER ALVAREZ.

ALVAREZ was presumably the individual who developed the photos which LIEMPTY-6 took, as well as the photo technical coverage of the other two basehouses.

SCANTLING's true name is Juan N. FRAIS-RAMIRREZ. (Contract agent.)

Soviet Embassy Coverage.

Project LIEMPTY concerned primarily with photo coverage of the Soviet installation. Active from mid-1955 through 30 June 1972. It supported two of the original three basehouses, which were:

LIMITED had been terminated.

LICALLA, activated in 1957 to provide photo coverage of the rear (garden, parking lot area) of the Soviet Embassy.

LILYRIC, activated in 1956 to provide photo coverage of the front entrance of the Soviet Embassy. Terminated 30 June 1972.

LIMITED, activated in 1955 to provide photo coverage of the front entrance of the Soviet Embassy. Terminated 31 May 1968.
