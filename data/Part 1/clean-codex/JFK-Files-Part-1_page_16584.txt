RY3AT AMMUG AMETROPIA.

1. AMMUG-1's wife said 31 January that AMMUG-1's employers called her on 27 January to inquire when AMMUG-1 would return to work. As AMMUG-1 had told his employers that he had to visit a sick relative in New York, she replied that the relative's condition was still grave and that she did not know just when AMMUG-1 would return to work.

2. For Madrid: We are reluctant to withdraw AMMUG-1 at this point. However, we do not want to jeopardize AMMUG-1's job security, which could in turn adversely affect his role future ops. We believe, therefore, we should set a cutoff date on AMMUG-1's present IDY if AMETROPIA-1 remains unlocated. Appreciate your thoughts this matter.
