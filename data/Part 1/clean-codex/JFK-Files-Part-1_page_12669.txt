18 2002 February 1976, Staff.

Cite San Jose 19235.

To: Immediate Director. Info: Caracas, Santiago, Buenos Aires.

References: A. Director 808822. B. San Jose 19195 (not needed Caracas or Buenos Aires) (812972). C. San Jose 19215 (not needed Caracas or Buenos Aires) (818963). D. San Jose 19227, paragraph 2 (not needed Caracas or Buenos Aires) (826806). E. Director 808856.

1. Re paragraph 3, references A and E: References B, C, and D reported the arrival on 2 February and presence of Andres Pascal Allende (Andrés Pascal Allende), repeat Andres Pascal Allende (Andrés Pascal Allende), nephew of Salvador Allende, in Costa Rica. After spending the day in a local hotel, he moved to the home of Marxist Daniel Camacho Monge, political science professor in the Political Science Faculty of the University of Costa Rica. Camacho’s home is located in the suburban town of Curridabat, 25 meters north of the home of former President Pepe Figueres. Pascal was known to be at the above location as of 18 February.

2. Pascal and his girlfriend must remain in Costa Rica for sixty days after arrival to give Costa Rican courts an opportunity to determine the validity of the Chilean government’s request to extradite them.
