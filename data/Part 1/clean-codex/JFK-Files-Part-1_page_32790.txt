Memorandum for: Office of Security/Alien Affairs (Alion Affairs) Staff  
Attention: Mr. Louis Weinchowski

Subject: AMSTALK-1 Travel

Reference: 8 April 1968 memorandum requesting documentation for AMSTALK-1

1. It is requested that a Form I-512 be issued in the name of Juan Hidalgo ELIZONDO Romano (travel alias to be used by AMSTALK-1) to allow his re-entry into the United States from Buenos Aires, Argentina. Upon receipt, the Form I-512 will be pouched to Station Buenos Aires. It will not be turned over to AMSTALK-1 until just prior to his return to the United States.

2. AMSTALK-1 has been used operationally since 1961.

William V. Broe  
Chief  
Western Hemisphere Division

15 April 1968
