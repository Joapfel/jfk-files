12 August 1958

Memorandum for: Deputy Director of Central Intelligence  
Via: Deputy Director (Plans)  
Subject: Commendation for Mr. Ross L. Crozier

The signature of the Deputy Director on the enclosed commendation is requested. It will be recalled that Mr. Crozier made an outstanding contribution to the intelligence mission of the Western Hemisphere Division in March 1958.

J. C. King  
Chief, Western Hemisphere Division

1 Attachment

DD/P/WH:RReynolds:fem, 12 August 1958
