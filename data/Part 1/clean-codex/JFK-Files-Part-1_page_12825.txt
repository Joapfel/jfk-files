1. Lead from Bogota BNDD suggests possible identification of another member of Humberto Trueva, Jose Gomez, Luis Posada group, according to BNDD Bogota telegram 2397 of 26 March.

U.S. fugitive Ralph Serrano (Serrand) telephoned Hotel Club Americana (71-44-11) in Caracas from Barranquilla, Colombia, during the approximate period 15 February–27 February 1973. Serrano (Serrand) was in Barranquilla during the aforementioned period at a residence whose phone number is 44910.

Serrano (Serrand) also called 32-24-50 in Caracas; 634-9532 in Miami; and 42-20-22 and 77-48-72 in Medellin.

2. Comment: Interesting aspect of Hotel Club Americana call is that Trueva and Gomez were staying at hotel at time call made. CAN SECRET CS COPY.
