Official Dispatch.

Air. Via: specify air or sea pouch.

Dispatch No. ZJL7-46.

John C. Fitzroy, Tokyo, Japan.

Date: 16 August 1960.

Attention: Honor D. Colwell; Patrick J. Lodge.

From:

Subject: General. Specific: Correspondence.

2. I request to command Major A. Farrar (KRS), Major D. Colwell and Mr. Control (Murgatroyd in Saldanha) as a last. I particularly wish to comment on the organizational and analytical aspects of this study.

3. This work is the best and most complete of its kind that we have, and will contribute materially to the advancement of United States intelligence interests throughout the world. It will give each of you satisfaction to know that the study will also be used in our European offices for its pertinent operational content and as a guide to the method of compilation and analysis of this type of operational information.

Robert A. Schon.

Releasing officer.

Coordinating officer.

Authenticating officer.
