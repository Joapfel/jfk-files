Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Reference: IN 63139.

1. Field comment: Information dated 20–23 February 1966 from three sources whose reporting in the past has been reliable indicates that Rolando (Rrolando) Masferrer Rojas, former Cuban senator who has been reported planning an invasion of Haiti in February using Haitian and Cuban émigrés, is having difficulty recruiting men to participate. According to earlier information, Masferrer is disliked and distrusted by many Cuban émigrés in Miami.
