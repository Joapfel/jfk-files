Classified message. Date: 6 March 1963. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/SAS 5 (SAS tempo copy slotted 2000, 5 March) EP. Info: DDP, ADCP, C/CA 2, C/CI, C/FI 2, C/SR 4, C/LH 5, S/C 2.

6 March 0055Z 63. Priority. IN 79811.

Priority DIR INFO WAVE cite MEXI 3653. RYBAT TYPIC LITAMIL-9. Ref A. MEXI 3633 (N-K8U&J). B. DIR 18853.

1. LITAMIL-9 left Mexico on 4 March 63 for Havana via Cubana Airlines. (LIFIRE/2)

2. CO held last meeting with L-9 early morning 4 March. L-9 refused to take single-wire equipment of any kind or physical bona fides. Willing to accept contact after three months in Havana. Meeting should take place at bookstore cited in Ref A after 1600. Fumasoli or a person known to Fumasoli, not Cuban, should approach, saying “De parte del Sr. Fumasoli.” L-9 will accept the person’s bona fides, viewing the fact that the meeting is at the bookstore and the use of the Fumasoli name. Sees no possibility of provocation.

3. L-9 will send letter to the Mexico City “drop” after approximately one month in Havana. Will write brother about every two weeks. Open code to brother will provide answers to general questions in Ref A, as well as the existence of Soviet rockets and the political position of Raul RCA. L-9 briefed again regarding requirements in Ref B.

End of message.

C/S comment: Stated possibility remains that LITAMIL-9 will take single-wire pencil or green lead only for emergency use.
