Dear Mr. Durvard:

In order to provide adequate telephone service in your vicinity, it has become necessary for us to make certain rearrangements of telephone equipment. These rearrangements will result in some changes of subscribers' telephone numbers.

We sincerely regret that your telephone is among those affected.

The change will take place during April 1960, and your new telephone number will be Federal 8-1263.

Before the change takes place, one of our representatives will call on you and leave her card on your telephone.

At your request, of course, your telephone number does appear in the Directory. However, to cause you minimum inconvenience, we will refer all of your calls from the old number to the new number for a reasonable period of time. If you should have any questions, please call our Business Office.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
