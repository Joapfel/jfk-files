There were numerous telephone numbers with no indication to whom they belonged. AMRADOR traced the numbers, but only the following were of persons with Communist connections. (Telephone numbers are not conclusive in view of the custom in Cuba to sell the telephone and number to anyone willing to pay the price. The buyer may have no other relationship to the previous owner of the phone.)

A-5-8741 Rosario Jimenez de la Osa  
Calle Águilas 100, bajo, esquina San Martín y San Rafael, Havana.

She is the mother of Antonio Fuelas Jimenez, who was a friend of Soto’s when they were both students at the University of Havana. He is now in Santa Clara, Cuba.

A-5-6752 Herminio Maria Futuño Basa and Herminio Maria Futuño Rojo  
Calle Sitios 229, entre Locaito y Companario.

Rojo is the father of Soto’s former wife, Gloria Maria Fueltes Dasa. Herminio Maria Fulites Dasa is her brother.

B-3667 Dr. José A. Solis Linares, M.D.  
Avenida 17 No. 310, entre 34 y 36, Marianao (Marianeo), Havana.

No previous record.

P-3136 Salomon L. Putuma  
Calle 3 No. 302, esquina a 15, Vedado (Yedado), Havana.

Possibly identical with a former consul of the Dutch Consulate in Havana. He is travelling outside of Cuba at present but will return in a month or two.

I-6857 Javier Gutiérrez Plasencia  
Calle 0, Sta. 126, entre 2 y 3, Víbora (Vibora), Havana.

He is the father of Amelia Jacinta Pérez Carmona (HI-95) and the stepmother of María Ofelia Maria Yánez Pérez Perelló. Under investigation by SIM (1957), she admitted knowing Licoral Soto.

X-1763 Sherwin-Miller Company of Cuba  
Calle P Fc. 212, entre Línea Habana Central y 12, Reparto Patista.

It is not known when Soto might be calling there; however, Sherwin-Miller has had labor trouble with the Communists in the past.
