Cuban Democratic Revolutionary Front, Finance Department.

Checks of October Cancelled in November. November 1 to 30, 1960. Annex “I-T.”

October 4 — Manuel Comellas, payroll — check No. 82 — $125.00.  
October 6 — Luis Farajón, payroll — check No. 92 — $350.00.  
November 18 — Antonio Locada, to be justified — check No. 205 — $200.00.  
November 28 — Tacuba Printing, publicity — check No. 276 — $300.00.  
November 29 — Tacuba Printing, publicity — check No. 278 — $200.00.  
November 29 — Vicente A. Romeo, publicity — check No. 281 — $105.00.  
Total: $11,280.00.

Note: The amounts of these checks was considered in October, as part of the total amount of the accounts to which each one of them corresponds. They were no cashed in the bank, because they were cancelled on November, reason for what the total amount of them is considered in this month as an increase in the balance in bank shown in our books.
