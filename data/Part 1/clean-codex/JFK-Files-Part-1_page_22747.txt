429 SE/DJB/. Classified message. The secret signature is: SHERLOCK HISTORIAN. Date: 8 April 1958. To: Director. From: Mexico. Action: WH 5. Info: FI, FI/OPS 2, S/C 2. 18 April 1968, 22:58. Received, Cable Section. Routine.

Re: Indicating, if necessary, that the indicated information has been obtained by other means. Marked: L.H.истич. Signature: L.H.istich.

To Director. Info Secc. Cite MEXI 1364.

1. Re Vaivada cover, Robert S. Benjamin will in principle assist in procuring work papers and covering any friend Nauman. As headquarters informed, expect Benjamin will be satisfied with nominal sum to compensate additional expenses.

2. Benjamin told nothing except would appreciate his making a place for a friend. No commitment or obligation of any kind.

3. Leave decision to headquarters. In event of any interest, it would perhaps be wise to have Vaivada come meet and talk with Benjamin.

End of message.
