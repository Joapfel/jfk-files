Caracas, Panama City

(Immediate) CARA

PACT

Previously Premum.

Re: PACT 1059 (IN 47783), not sent CARA.

S. PACT 1054 (IN 47786), not sent CARA.

1. February agent Victor Rico Galan, Mexican Office, Hispanic American born 6 Feb 1939 at 31 Febril, Mexican permanent address Provincial 958, Mexico 8, P. Neuarte. Panama 13 Oct via FAA flight WSC 819 for Canead. Makefast for flight WSC 819 left him Custco Patricies Galan.

2. ANF N. stated in part Galan travelling the New Central and South America with Mexican passengers last year, parts in continent as Venus, begin dates Matey and Auto Larry, and will be back in Aug to maintain them in condition. Take premium Cup Verde Volla. Promised to have arrangement with Mexican government which gives him special privileges as my Mexican Bagabasted in place as Venus. Galan contact in Verde Volla may be on Archellia Lata.

3. Request CARA develop all possible interaction Galan activities.

Fark Cole by Michael at WU. M. Room O/ON/GFF.
