Classified message.

Routing. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: SAS 8. Info: DOP, CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, CI/CA, FI, FI/INT, WH 7, VR.

5 March 1964.

331939Z.

DIR CITE MEXI 8478.

TYPIC AMROD.

1. Following from LITATIL-9 night 1–2 March.

2. Rangs Sinusas, wife, two children arrived Mexi 1 March en route to take up new post Embassy Santiago, Chile. Wife described as common, “cheap.”

3. Elena Vasquez Gomez, “lover” of Teresa Proenza, has returned from Cuba, says Teresa will probably work in Cuba but may make trip to Mexi soon.

4. Enrique Gonzalez Casancva (Casanova) back from Cuba, says he saw Teresa at party attended by Raul (Rual) Roa and Joaquin Ordoqui (Ordógui), among others.
