Domestic Contact Service – Organization Information Sheet.

1. Name and address of organization: International Rescue Committee Inc., 386 Park Avenue, New York, New York. 55564. OK.

2. Principal contacts, positions, degree of cooperation, security status, etc.: Charles Sternberg, Chief of Office in New York. Very cooperative. Approved SECRET in May 1971. Walter Freund of the New York Office has been a long-time contact of Charles Sternberg.

3. Estimate of the value of the organization as a source of FPI, type of business, subjects and countries or areas involved, and restrictions regarding access to sources: The organization has no value as a source of FPI. It helps new immigrants to settle in the U.S.

It is of value to us by helping us to locate East European immigrants wanted for debriefing.

4. Receptivity to operational requirements and influencing factors: Sternberg is likely to be receptive to operational requirements, but may not have the authority to commit the organization.

Field office: New York. C.S. Norman N. Graber/ewk. Date: 6 March 1972.
