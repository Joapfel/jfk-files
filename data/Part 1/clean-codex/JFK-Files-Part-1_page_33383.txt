TOFOCUS UNSOBER.

Ref: OFAA-2356, 9 April 74 (not needed Santiago).

1. TOFOCUS activity seeks to develop assets to influence news and other programming with political content in European TV services. In France this has been a particularly tough nut to crack, but Paris Station has made recent progress with UNSOBER/1. This former staff journalist for prestigious French news weekly L'Express recently completed a 30-minute reportage on current events in West Germany. It was well received and resulted in his being tapped to do a second 30-minute program. As UNSOBER/1 appears to be on the way to a position of substance within French television, we are anxious to assist his rise and insure that Paris Station's most promising TOFOCUS asset achieves the most influential status possible. To this end it would be of value if UNSOBER/2 could do a program on the current situation in Chile.
