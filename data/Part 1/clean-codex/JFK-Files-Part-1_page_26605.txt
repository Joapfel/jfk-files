Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Cite: TDCS-314/10419-66. Distributed 18 August 1966.

Country: Mexico.

Date of information: 16 August 1966.

Subject: Comment in the Popular Socialist Party on the arrest of Victor Rico Galan and others on charges of subversion.

Place and date acquired: Mexico, Mexico City, 16 August 1966. Field No. 4411-13,614.

Source: Veteran and trusted member of the Popular Socialist Party (PPS) who has close personal connections among ranking members of the party. He has been a prolific reporter of reliable information. The reported information was obtained from Captain Sanchez Cardenas of the National Executive Directorate of the PPS.

1. The Popular Socialist Party (PPS) has not formulated an opinion on the 12 and 13 August 1966 arrests of Victor Rico Galan and some forty other persons on charge of subversion. However, certain members of the National Executive Directorate of the PPS have said that the PPS will do nothing to help them.
