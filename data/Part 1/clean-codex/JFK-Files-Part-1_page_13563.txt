Information Report

Central Intelligence Agency

Country: Guatemala/Mexico (see above). Report No. TDCS-3500,346.

Subject: PGT views on the political situation in Guatemala. Date distributed: 19 March 1962. Date of information: 24 March 1962. Place acquired: Mexico, Mexico City (24 March 1962). Appraisal: 2. Field report No. HM 8-72.

This is unevaluated information. Source gradings are definitive. Approval of content is tentative.

Source: Person (H) with good connections among Guatemalan experts in Mexico.

1. According to the PGT, the results of the guerrilla fighting have not been established until after the 13 November Group (number of 1 November 1960 attempt to overcome the Guatemalan government in the area by Marco Antonio Yon Sosa) is not in accordance with the political concepts of the Cortierist strategy.
