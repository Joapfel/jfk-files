17 March 1969

Memorandum to: DC/WH/1

From: C. Schaefer

Subject: Conversation with Mr. Scott

1. The concept and feasibility of the False Flag Approach targeted against both the PCM and the Cubans.

2. The possibility of using either AMCHALK/3 or AMCHALK/2 to inspect the Guatemalan-Mexican border for indications of arms smuggling.

3. The possibility of using AMCHALK/5 as a replacement for ROWTON.
