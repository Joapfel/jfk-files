26232 APR 68. CITE MEXICO CITY 5141.

Guatemala City. Info: Director, Tegucigalpa.

Ref: Mexico City 5111 (GJ85161).

1. On 5 April 68 station received photographic copies of Guatemalan passport which Lering-3 (LIRING-3) said Francisco Lara would use to enter Honduras from Guatemala. Copies indicate there was a discrepancy in name originally reported in reference and name on passport. Correct name is Jose Maria Oliva Carranza, ordinary Guatemalan passport number 134825, Serie ‘D’, registry number 02743, issued 27 May 1963 in Guatemala City. Oliva born San Felipe, Retalhuleu, on 9 February 1935.

2. Other documents photographed include military inscription in Guatemalan Army, card number 49259, birth certificate number 2825671, cedula de vecindad 287165, and electron card 107817.

3. These documents were photographed by Lering-3 (LIRING-3) when they were passed to him by Francisco Lara to use in making false Mexican documentation. Pouching copies of documents to addressees.
