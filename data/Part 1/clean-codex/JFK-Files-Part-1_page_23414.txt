Via air. Dispatch No. T3A-564.

To: Chief, Foreign Division T

From: Chief of Station, San José

Subject: General—Operations; New Operations Staffing Plan. Specific—Project Humanoid.

Reference: TRSW-447

Date: 31 March 1950

1. In accordance with verbal agreement made by the Chief of Station in Washington and formalized in the referenced memorandum, the monthly salary paid to Humanoid will be increased from the present $500 to $750.00 effective 1 April 1950.

2. A revised “Field Project Outline” applicable to Project Humanoid is submitted herewith, in accordance with GOI No. 2.

3. Possible existing confusion with regard to our charging of expenses of Project Humanoid and allied projects for which Humanoid supplies cutout services will be clarified in the near future as revised “Field Project Outlines” applicable to these latter projects are prepared and forwarded.

4. The attached “Field Project Outline” supercedes TRSA-347.
