002618 APR 76 STAFF. CITE SANTO DOMINGO (SANTO DOMINGOS), 17290.

To: Priority Director, San Jose. Info: Priority LA/Miami.

1. ENGARNET-1 reported 5 April that Balaguer (Balaquer) Bosch is in the Dominican Republic despite recent public statement made by Police Chief General Nivar that Bosch is in Spain. ENGARNET-1 learned from local Cuban contacts that Bosch arrived in a false name on a Costa Rican passport issued by GOCR, that travel was cleared by GOCR with President Balaguer, who acceded to the request of Admiral de Windt (father-in-law of Umberto Lopez, who was deported to the U.S. a few months ago).

2. ENGARNET-1 confronted (CONFORTED) EMPFETCH-9 with the above information. EMPFETCH-9 denied any knowledge and subsequently raised the matter with President Balaguer, who finally admitted that Bosch was in the Dominican Republic because Admiral de Windt had appealed to him on humanitarian grounds. It appears that Bosch needs a cataract operation and is scheduled to enter Perez Patino Clinic.

3. No file. E2 IMPDET.
