Classified message.

Originating office: Charles W. Anderson. Unit: WH/FI. Extension: 3995 648. Date: 3 November 1960.

To: Mexico City. From: Director. Confirmation: AM 5. Information: CIVIL E, FI, FI/INT B, S.

Outgoing cable 88628.

To Mexico (MEXI), priority information. Cite DIR 09020, 09820.
