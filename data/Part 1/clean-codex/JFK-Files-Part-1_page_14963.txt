AEPADDLE Debriefing

3 and 4 April 1956

I. Previous Operations of British and Georgian Émigrés

A. Georgians involved

1. Noe JORDANIA, late President of Georgian Republic in the 1920s.

2. ROVES CABACHVILI, approximately 75 years old. Lives with wife at 133 r. l'Abbé Groult, Paris XV, no phone. Gets Social Security and aid from Georgians.

3. ELISE PATARIDZE, approximately 55–60 years old. President of Georgian Association in Paris and of Georgian National Democrats. Lives with wife and children at 35 r. du Cea. Brlanger, Auteuil 6589. Has grocery store at 84 r. Michelange. Three children: one 20 years old and twins, a boy and girl, 14.

4. LEVAN ZURABICHVILI was possibly involved. AEPADDLE surmised this, although L.Z. did not so state. Approximately 51 years old. Married, boy 7, girl 5. Budget director for SIMCA automobile plant. Lives at 2 r. Jacques Mawas, phone Lk Courbe 0212. Graduate of École des Mines. Father of L.Z. was friend of PATARIDZE and CABACHVILI (National Democrats). L.Z. considers himself a National Democrat, but felt the need for a broader organization to embrace all non-Socialist Georgians. He has established such an organization, possibly known as the Comité des Georgiens Libres.

All the above four have lived in Paris since the 1920s.

5. Fnd SOUMEATACHVILI (SOUMBATOFF) was the Georgian contact man with the British.
