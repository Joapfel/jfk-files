8. LIDUZE seems to have had honest difficulty recalling details of past intelligence activities but has responded to patient questioning, providing more data every meeting. As reported previously, he told of mental and physical exhaustion on departure from Cuba. Now has explained he was under psychiatric treatment and, as a security measure, had tried to forget intelligence matters and associations. Has given the following information of priority interest:

12. LIDUZE got Eusebio Azcue's job in the Ministry of Foreign Relations, an architect who fought in the Spanish Civil War. Drinker and talker. Not suitable for or connected with intelligence. After serving as consul in Mexico, left PORNOF and now employed by Junta Central de Planificaciones (JUCEPLAN).

13. Roberto Romero Adams, half-blind musician, acted as DOZE informant on anti-Castro activities.

14. Roselio Rodriguez, also known as El Rojo, son of CP member, previously worked in PORNOF exclusively on Mexico matters in the Latin American Politics Department. Probably working for Piniero. Now in Mexico as first secretary of Cuban Embassy.

15. Elpidio Interian is a CP member, probably working for Cuban intelligence. His brother is a CIP courier.
