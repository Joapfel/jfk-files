23. Frank Cabral is probably identical with Dr. Frank Cabral Calcagno, who was born in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, on 16 February 1942. Cabral served as civilian advisor to President Joaquin Balaguer from 1970 until his arrest in late June 1974 as a result of the discovery of numerous weapons and wiretap equipment in his home. Cabral had used his residence as a center of operations while running his own intelligence network. Cabral was sent into exile and returned to the Dominican Republic from Spain in July 1975. He has served as Secretary General of the dissident faction of the Democratic Quisgueyan Party (PQD).

This Agency has no additional biographic data on Juan Pou. However, his brother Joaquin, who was born in Santo Domingo on 1 March 1935, gained notoriety by “neutralizing” terrorists when he was associated with the Special Operations Group, Military Intelligence Section (J-2) of the Dominican Armed Forces.
