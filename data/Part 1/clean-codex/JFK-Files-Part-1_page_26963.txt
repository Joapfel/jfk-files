Dear Sir:

Reference is made to your letter of December 18th, 1975, concerning our client, Mitchell Livingston WerBell, III, and his request for information under the Freedom of Information Act.

I am enclosing a notarized statement providing you with the address and place of birth of Mr. WerBell.

With regard to your last paragraph asking for clarification as to which records he desires to have searched, we, of course, are not familiar with your files or filing system. Mr. WerBell spent some time in the United States Army during World War II and has traveled extensively since that time. He was in Cuba at the time of the Castro military operation, in the Dominican Republic during that military operation, and in Thailand and Viet Nam during the Viet Nam War.
