Subject stated that the leitmotif of LOG during those meetings was “help me and I will be able to help you,” and “get me (or provide me with) the information I need and I will provide you with information you might need.” Among the information most sought by LOG was personality information on Mexican politicians and, especially, indications as to who would be the next President of Mexico. LOG was very much interested in whether Echevarría (ECHÉVARRIA) stood a better chance of becoming the next President than Carrillo Flores (CARILLO FLORES). Later, it became an obsession to find out what was the real reason for Rockefeller’s trip to Latin American countries. LOG discounted fact-finding as any reason because, according to him, the U.S. embassies and the CIA in those countries knew everything that was to be known. Consequently, LOG was convinced that there must be an important reason for ROCKY’s trip. Subject added that there were many other minor questions concerning the Mexican scene that interested LOG, and to a fair extent he was able to get the answers. Nevertheless, LOG started asking Subject whether he had friends in the U.S. Embassy there. Subject answered casually, “of course.” On the next meeting Subject had with LOG on June 9 at La Cava, LOG told him that he needed an embassy phone directory and asked Subject if he could get him one. Subject promised to try. LOG brought to Subject’s attention that he needed the new directory because the Embassy changed the directory every six months.
