Operational Termination of Projects

1. In order to formalize headquarters records, the appropriate staffs have been notified of the termination of the FULMINATE Project, and have been informed that in the future no charges will be made against the FULMINATE Project, or against FULMINATE. If this agent is used temporarily in the future and not in connection with an approved project, please charge any expenses incurred to Other Operational Activity.

2. Notice of the termination of FULMINATE has also been furnished to the headquarters Staff, and any charges for future information from him should also be charged to Other Operational Activity.
