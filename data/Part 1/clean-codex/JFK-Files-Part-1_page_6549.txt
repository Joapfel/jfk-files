031450Z September 1969. CITE CONTACTS/WESTERN EUROPE.

Contacts/Washington.

Soviet FLOC Division (Welch) from Freund.

Subject: Case 51.638 — Czech origin — NY ASYNT.

1. International Rescue Committee (IRC), assistant director in New York, was first and main volunteer agency to move specifically on behalf of Hungarian refugees in 1956 (1996) and again in September 1957 (1997). In late August, regarding Czechoslovaks, top man, Lt. Chaney (LTO CHANEY), had just returned from Vienna, where he studied dimensions of hoard at present. Charles Steinberg, resettlement supervisor (RESIDENTMENT SUPERVISOR; not exact title), is himself of Czech Jewish origin and speaks Czech.

2. As of Labor Day, several thousand Czechoslovaks in Austria (OOSTRIH) had indicated desire to seek permanent residence in Ireland (IRLAND). As reported in press, departure from GSR if still legally possible, and UK Embassy has instructions (INSTILUTIONS) to admit applicants. Switzerland issues visas to Czechoslovaks in Austria (and Switzerland in gross). Canada is expected to admit refugees (REFUGES).

3. Up to one-third of the total of 88 Thule enlisted expected in pipeline within schedule two weeks after arrival to air for emigration to the U.S. INS will handle many cases, “may be it!” (“AY BE IT!”) Upon to cooperate in secret lists for specific intents WTS.
