DIR CITE MEXI 5412. REF DIR 52792. INS1732.

1. Much impressed with Horsfall. Transcription tests very satisfactory. Believe linguistic capability and experience in Buen and Bang qualify him exceptionally well for LICAPER supervisor or LIENVOY, if it continues. Appears capable of adapting to Mexican peculiarities and not averse to being cut off from official colony.

2. Main question is one of timing. Know he favors returning here and getting settled. However, feel it undesirable to expose him to LIENVOY personnel if he is to work ultimately in LICAPER. Do not wish to lose him, however, and can guarantee keeping him busy doing PBBAND translations for LITENSOR and LIENVOY PBRUMEN tapes, which would remove him from LIENVOY for this purpose. We are told SAS wants these verbatim, which LIENVOY present staff cannot handle.

3. Station requests therefore return Horsfall here as soon as feasible in tourist status. Would live in furnished quarters until new presidential candidate named, early October at latest, and fate of LIENVOY and LICAPER known. At that time, candidate conferenced, would arrange cover and Horsfall status would change from tourist, and household effects could be brought in. Please . . .
