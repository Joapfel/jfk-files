Herman Edward Kinsey

December 11, 1915

4. 15 April 1945–1 October 1946. Camp Superintendent; 100 employees, laborer. Salary: $2,694 per annum; $3,021 per annum. Grade: SP-7. Location: Yreka, California. Organization: U.S. Forest Service. U.S. Forest Service, Yreka, California. References: Art London, U.S. Forest Service, Yreka, California. Returned to the U.S. Forest Service. Supervised a 100-man forest camp for the purpose of controlling timber disease.

5. 5 November 1943–15 April 1945. Laborer. Salary: $0.90 per hour; $1.10 per hour. Location: McCloud, California. Organization: Lumber. McCloud River Lumber Company, McCloud, California. Reference: James Herbert, McCloud, California. Returned to the United States Forest Service. Operated a lumber carrier and served as brakeman on a small diesel locomotive.

6. 19 May 1941–5 November 1943. Sergeant, MOS 631; Intelligence NCO; Combat Intelligence. Supervised up to 14 intelligence analysts. Salary: $21.00 per month; $75.00 per month. Grade: Sergeant. Location: Continental limits of the United States. Organization: U.S. Army. Supervisor: Unknown. U.S. Army. Honorable discharge. Supervised the S-2 Section in Brigade Headquarters for the purpose of collecting and evaluating information on enemy military operations.

7. 1 May 1939–10 May 1941. Forest Guard. Salary: $100 per month. Location: McCloud, California. Organization: U.S. Forest Service. United States Forest Service, McCloud, California. Reference: Earl Sullaway, McCloud, California. Enlisted in the U.S. Army under draft requirements but was not yet called for service. Prevention and suppression of forest fires.

8. January 1935–May 1939. Laborer. Laborer in the lumber oil industry.
