Att. No. 70 to UFGA-21693

UFGA-21693/14

Subject: WALDES Meneses, Pedro

1. Source met the subject when source began to work at the Ministerio de Obras Públicas in 1960, where the subject also worked, from which time they became very close friends. Source last saw the subject on 31 July 1962, on which date the subject left Cuba and came to the United States. The subject used to work in private construction companies before the Castro regime. Source says the subject has never been a Communist and that, although he was working at the Ministerio de Obras Públicas, he was against the Cuban Communist regime. Source believes the subject has never traveled outside Cuba before. Source says the subject has some relatives outside Cuba but is not able to provide their addresses. Source says the subject has never received any political, military, or intelligence training.

2. Physical description: Date and place of birth: circa 1938, Marianao, Havana. Race: white. Height: 6'0". Weight: 150 lbs. Hair: light brown. Eyes: brown. Shape of face: long. Physique: slim. No visible scars.

3. JEWAVE traces: none.
