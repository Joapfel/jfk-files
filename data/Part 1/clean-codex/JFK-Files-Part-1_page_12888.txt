Cite Georgetown 21369 to Director, info London. WHINTEL RYBAT.

Ref: A. Georgetown 21356 (to 071334). B. Georgetown EMSTEL 2276 (wc 30242).

1. For the past ten days, the atmosphere surrounding our embassy has been one of apprehension, reviewing events:

A. On 11 October, FORMIN Fred Wills issued a statement which referred to destabilization in the context of the Cubana crash. The embassy was peacefully picketed on 13 October by two groups, one (YSM) with government approval. There followed two telephone threats to the embassy on 14 October.

B. On 15 October, the State Department made a démarche to Guyanese Chargé Halder, of which the key phrase focused upon by GOG was that GOG was “straining US patience.” The embassy did not receive the actual text of the démarche, but Secretary Leurs, in a telephone conversation with Chargé Blacken, denied that phrase was used. Nevertheless, this heightened Burnham’s emotional brooding over the crash, and there followed Burnham’s speech (see text ref 6).
