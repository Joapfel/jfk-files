In this field, we shall concentrate our efforts on the following:

1. Project for the processing of coconut and vegetable oils.

2. Factory for desiccating coconut for export.

3. Vegetable oil extracting plant for local consumption.

4. Toilet soap factory.

5. Extension of palm oil plants and production of oleaginous.

6. Cocoa production along with a processing plant.

7. Extension of cotton production for a modern spinning farm.

8. Spinning farm.

9. Plant for the preparation of hydrophil and absorbent cotton (100 cons).

10. Creation of a mechanical industry with branches over the whole territory for the manufacture of agricultural tools and spare parts for all kinds of machines.

11. Plant for the manufacture of low-cost electric ranges and all electric accessories.

12. Plant for the production of organic fertilizer.

13. Assembling industries to work for export.

14. Oil, fruits, sugar cane straw processing plants.

15. Bottle manufacture.

16. Safety matches factory.

17. Paper mill.

18. Cattle-raising industry to intensify the development of our livestock.

19. Fishing industry.

20. Tourism.

In preparing our plan, we will not abandon the fact that transportation and communication, determining at all their status the rhythm and the trend of economic development, will have to be given due consideration in the improvement and extension of our road and rail network. In order to enlarge the rural market, only through good roads and sufficient, low-cost means of transportation shall the agricultural products reach the non-rural at moderate prices.
