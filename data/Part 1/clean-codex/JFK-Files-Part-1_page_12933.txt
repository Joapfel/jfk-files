Luis Clemente POMPA Carías (201-300/89)

MOSCUEY-3 (formerly ANGELINE-15, CIPFENUS-4)

EPCOS, 15 February 1980, Cienfuegos, Cuba.

U.S. Permanent Resident Allen (PRA) at least until 30 May 1975. Possibly lost status as a result of allowing his reentry permit to expire, but this is not clear. We protected his PNA status while he worked for us in Caracas until termination in June 1974.

Also holds Venezuelan passport.

Entered the U.S. as an exile on 20 February 1961. Served in the U.S. Army as a 2nd Lieutenant at Fort Benning from March 1963 to March 1964. Member of JCSA until early 1965.

April 1965: Granted POA and recruited by JMMAVE as an instructor working for the Training Branch and as a source of information on exile activities in the Miami area. OA October 1965.

August 1966: Used by JMMAVE CI Branch to report on activities of the “Central Junta” being organized to govern activities of RECE, Comandos L, MRN, and AVBC. Subject associated with RECE.

June 1967: Subject known to the FBI and Department of Justice as a CIA employee. Cooperating with FBI/Miami in investigation of Frank “Lefty” Rosenthal.

July 1967: Annually terminated by JMMAVE. Continuing to cooperate with the FBI.

August 1967: Mentioned as a CIA agent in broadcasts from the LASO conference.

October 1967: Having obtained a job in DIGEPOL, Caracas, subject was picked up by Caracas Station as a unilateral penetration of DIGEPOL. Held several responsible positions: Chief of 4th Division, Explosives and Armaments, and Chief of the CI section. Continued working for DISIP, successor to DIGEPOL, during the COPEI administration and was used by DISIP Chief, Dr. Renéerto UZCATEGUI, on various sensitive operations.

March 1973: Suspected by DEA of involvement in narcotics trafficking. He was in Europe on DISIP business at the time. In May 1973, “passed” a polygraph on this subject.

March 1974: Dismissed from DISIP, along with many others, when the AD administration was inaugurated. Terminated 30 June 1974 due to loss of access. Salary at time of termination was $400 per month.

Contact with subject continued because of an outstanding problem regarding U.S. taxes, which was finally settled in February 1976. At that time, subject was informing the Station regarding the movements and activities of Orlando BOSCH. Occasional contact continued at least through June 1976.

The Agency protected subject’s PNA status from March 1970 until his termination in June 1971. In January 1975, a memo was sent to the Alien Affairs Officer telling him that the I&AI file could be “decentralized.”
