Diary, 23 February 1967.

Subject: Mitchell (Hitchell) Vertrell; Clet Gray.

Schulich called to say that Vertrell had heard from Gray both yesterday and the day before, expressing concern over the fact that no one had contacted him concerning the alleged accommodation attempt at a foreign diplomat.

Vertrell stated that unless he heard from someone immediately, he would go to the Governor of California, 23 February. This information was passed to San Francisco by phone.

cc: Chief, Mind Office. SchulicConversting.
