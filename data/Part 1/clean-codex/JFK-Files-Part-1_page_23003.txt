Office Memorandum, United States Government

To: FI/STC/SPB  
Attention: Mr. Burton  
From: Chief, WHD  
Date: 12 April 1954  
Subject: Use of Contract Agent Anthony G. VAIVADA as Cut-Out in ESCROW Project

1. The Guatemala Station has advised Headquarters that subject contract agent, who holds Provisional Operational Clearance for PESUCCESS, is currently also being used as cut-out in the ESCROW Project.

2. Pursuant to telephone conversation this date between Mr. Burton and the III desk officer concerned, it is requested that the above-referenced Provisional Operational Clearance be amended to authorize the use of VAIVADA in the ESCROW Project as indicated in paragraph 1 above.

(C) This document was brought to the attention of Dr. Burton and Dr. Brady on 26 April 1954. CMH
