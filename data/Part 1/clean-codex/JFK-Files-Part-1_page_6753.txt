Name: Dr. Agustin GUITART Campuzano. DPCB: 25 April 1910, Cardenas, Cuba. Residence: 4009 Elba St., New Orleans 25, La. Occupation: Doctor of Science (Mathematics & Physics). Presently teaching at Xavier University in New Orleans.

Employment history: Formerly a professor at the University of Havana. Served as Minister of Education during the early part of Carlos PRIO's tenure as head of the Cuban Government.

Court record: Was imprisoned on the Isle of Pines during MACHADO's term (date unknown).

Spouse: Helena TORO.

Children: Agustin, Jorge, Vilma Helena, William.

Cuban address: Havana, Calle 64 No. 1502.

Note: Subject arrived in the U.S.A. in February 1962 through Opa-Locka (Opa_Locka), Fla., where he was debriefed by CO/C. CO/C has no current interest in subject.
