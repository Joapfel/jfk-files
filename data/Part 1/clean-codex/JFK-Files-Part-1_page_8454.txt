Memorandum for the File

Source: LILYRIC

Subject: Visit to the Soviet Embassy, Mexico City, of unidentified American-looking couple.

3 May 1968

3 de Mayo de 1968. Friday.

16:07: Mr. and Mrs. enter south (North Americans).
16:37: The same leave north.

They were with Log.

The man was approximately 25 years old, fair-haired, wearing sports clothes.

The woman was approximately 23 years old.

File: I 50-8-10/11.
