Contact Report.

Agent: AMMUG-1. Case officer: Eustace D. KLOOCK. Funds passed: None. Place: AMMUG-1's home. Date: 1630–1700, 19 May 1971.

1. At the time and place indicated above, I reviewed with AMMUG-1 Volumes II and III of the Cuban Mugbook, with the following results:

Volume II.

Armando U. Estrada Fernandez (201-809440), Photo No. 39. AMMUG-1 correctly identified the pseudonym of Estrada as “ULISES.” AMMUG-1 added that he had last spoken with Estrada in February 1964.

German Miguel Ramos (201-739084), Photo No. 42. AMMUG-1's only comment was that he was not sure if Ramos was his correct name.

Roque Dalton Garcia (201-215097), Photo No. 118. AMMUG-1 commented that he had spoken with Dalton in September 1964.

Manuel Carbonell Dique (201-739084), Photo No. 159. AMMUG-1 commented that he knew Carbonell from circa 1960, when Carbonell was a captain in the police.

Volume III.

AMMUG-1 did not recognize any of the photographs contained in Volume III of the Cuban Mugbook.

2. AMMUG-1 was also shown the photograph of Luis Martínez Menocal (201-769095), which was forwarded in UFAW-1118 of 7 May 1971. While AMMUG-1 thought the face familiar, he was unable to identify the photograph.

3. In addition to the above, AMMUG-1 was also shown the photographs of the following individuals with negative results: Jorge López García (201-838320) and Sergio López Otoña Valdes (201-754447).

Eustace D. KLOOCK
