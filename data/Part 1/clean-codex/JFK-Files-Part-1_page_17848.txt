Dispatch. Chief of Station, Mexico City. Processing action: marked for indexing.

Subject: CLIFEUD Salaries.

The salaries listed for LIFEUD in the project renewal request do not jibe in most cases with the memoranda of oral commitment which we have on file, and in some instances (LIFEUDs 7, 8 and 9) the salaries are almost doubled. Would you please verify for us that the salaries as submitted in the renewal are those being paid, and if this is the case, would you also prepare revised MOAs and send us copies.
