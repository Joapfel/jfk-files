RYDAT TYPIC AMROSE AMBEDEW AMCHALK

Reference: WH/MIAMI-3326 (IN 22810)

1. AMBEDEW-1 debriefed on ref matter 2 April. Said became aware of penetration in AMCHALK-5 group when working in counter revolutionary section. DGI collaborator who acted as principal agent this op was Marta Gonzalez, authoress of “Bajo Parole.” Gonzalez came U.S. as exile circa 1963 and lived Miami, was handled by “ROLÓ” Martinez, DGI case officer in United Nations who met Gonzalez New York and Miami. AMBEDEW-1 said Gonzalez “bi-sexual.”

2. Gonzalez recruited at least two agents Miami, at least one of which was female. She had “infinity” of contacts Miami and sent detailed reports to Havana on exile activities. Her mission was to penetrate Alpha 66.

3. AMBEDEW-1 recalls Gonzalez lived in house of woman she recruited. Gonzalez (CONZALEZ) returned Havana sometimes 1964–65 via Mexico.
