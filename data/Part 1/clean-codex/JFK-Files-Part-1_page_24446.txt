Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Paris. BNO: Chief, NE. RCO: Chief, WL. Name: Chief of Station, New Delhi. MSA: KOBIRD/LCHARVEST/PADRAYON. Status: PABRAYON/1 relationship with Jacques M. Verges.

Action: FYI. RI: Index this dispatch to the 7-15 file of Jacques M. Verges, French citizen, director of the pro-Chinese Communist magazine “Revolution.” Also index to the 201 file of Richard Obeea, U.S. citizen, editor-in-chief of the English-language edition of “Revolution.”

Reference: 1. Forwarded herewith as Attachment 1 to this dispatch is a copy of the letter dated in the reference from PADRAYON to Jacques M. Verges. Attachment 2 is a copy of the reply written by Richard Obeea on behalf of Verges. Attachment 3 is a personality sketch of Verges prepared by PABRAYON/1.

2. Regarding Attachment 1, Tilak is a mutual friend who currently is studying in Canada. “Algerian Sidor” refers to Jaffa Benarhead. The remainder of the attachments are believed to be self-explanatory.

3. Headquarters asked (DIR 05476) if PADRAYON could furnish any information on Hameau Alavi of Pakistan, who is on the editorial board of “Revolution.” PABRAYON/1 had not previously heard of this individual.

Attachments 1, 2, and 3 as stated.

Edmund F. LAHSENE.

Cross reference: Dispatch staff and source, NPDA-10,089. Date: 12 March 1964. Refile here: 20-6-138/3; 201-. (Verges, Obeea).
