Subject: MINTEL RYBAT PLAERONAUT POSEEK.

1. Subject is Sergey Uzlov (Uslov). Born 19 November 1949, Minsk, of Russian father and Jewish mother. INS No. A-21-723-173. Departed USSR 26 November 1976, departing Rome sometime in March 1977. Subject assessed by SESDINS 4 March 77 for information on Soviet interpreters travelling abroad and KGB officers in Minsk.

2. After graduating from high school in 1966, subject worked at several manual labor jobs and in 1969 enrolled in Minsk Pedagogical Institute of Foreign Languages, where he studied at the German teacher training faculty. He dropped out after the third year since he felt that there was no future for him as a high school teacher of German and that, as a Jew, he would not be given foreign assignments. From 1972 to 1974, subject was employed as a radio lab worker with Belorussian State University, simultaneously studying at the history faculty.
