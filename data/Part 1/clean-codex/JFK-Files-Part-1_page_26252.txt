132323 Z. CITE MEXICO CITY 9925. Priority. Director. Info Rome.

RYBAT.

Ref: Mexico City 9800 (G).

14 Jan. 77S85.

1. LEZHOLD had a successful one-hour meeting with RICO GALAN at Lecumberri Penitentiary on the morning of 13 January. The meeting ended abruptly by a call from the director of the prison that a lawyer was there to see RICO.

2. LEZHOLD had a letter introducing himself as an Italian writer for “Corrispondenza Socialista” and as an acquaintance of CARLO COCCIOLI, an Italian pro-Commie journalist who was a friend of RICO in Mexico and who was currently in Europe. As a result, RICO assumed and accepted LEZHOLD as a fellow Marxist, and a friendly rapport was established immediately. It is most probable that RICO expressed his real thoughts in discussion and in answer to questions.

3.
