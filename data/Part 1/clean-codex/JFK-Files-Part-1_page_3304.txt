File tracks:

Arquito, Bernal, Leopoldo.

President of the Comité Pro-Liberación de Nicaragua (Committee for the Liberation of the Republic of Nicaragua). Reference: Newspaper “NY,” July 22, 1959.

Doctor. General Secretary of the Pan-American Medical Congress. He requests that the subsidiary associations send a cablegram to the President of the United States expressing their protest over the air raid on the city of Havana. Reference: Newspaper “NY,” October 27, 1959.

In connection with Justo Cazilló and in the company of José Angel Estavinas and Jorge Quintán, he suggests statements supporting the Covenant. Reference: “Bohemia” magazine, section titled “Occidente en Cuba” (Cuban Section), July 10, 1958, page 61.

Doctor. Office Manager of Administración de los Departamentos de Orientación y Rehabilitación de Menores (Management of Departments for the Orientation and Adjustment of Children). In an article appearing in “Bohemia” magazine, he explains the operation of the Adjustment Program for Minor Boys at Torrons. Reference: “Bohemia” magazine, July 31, 1950.

Vice-President of the Medical College. Member of the Deliberation of Cuban Physicians arriving at Santiago de Chile. Reference: Newspaper “NY,” December 10, 1960.

Doctor. Director of Prevención de Bienestar Social (Prevención de Bidénstar Social, Prevention of Social Welfare). Member of the PSP (Popular Socialist Party). Reference: José Paz Novas, January 17, 1961.

Physician. Communist. Informer of the G-2 (Secret Police). Reference: February 25, 1961.

AMG-77.

Arrived in Mexico on December 19, 1961. Reference: 001143, sheet no. C0102. Reference: “Azigos” passenger list.

Doctor. Professor of Medical Psychology at the University of Havana. Salary: $409.66. Reference: CC-32.
