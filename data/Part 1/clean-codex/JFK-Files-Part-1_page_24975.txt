5. Victor Aybar—no station traces. LNERGO traces indicate full name Victor Leon (Leo N) (Aybar) de Sosa; DPOB 15 Mar 28, Santo Domingo, D.R.; married to Mary Boswell, American citizen; former D.R. consul in Chicago. LNERGO had an allegation that he was an agent of SIM. Interviewed in July 1961, he denied any SIM connections.
