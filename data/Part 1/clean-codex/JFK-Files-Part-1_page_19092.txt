The president and the secretary are authorized by the order of the board to select the candidates for election, with the exception of the president, who is elected by a majority of two. The number to be elected shall be at least one third, and in case of tie, it shall be elected by a majority of two.

All other officers, 理事, directors, and trustees of the bank shall be elected under the act of incorporation of the bank and its predecessor, and shall hold office until their successors are appointed. However, people are not entitled to more emoluments and greater benefits in their office than they have received under the preceding officers.

The following persons are exempt from payment of taxes: children under ten years of age, the aged and infirm, the blind, the deaf, the maimed, the insane, the deceased, and the widowed. They are also exempt from taxation on the value of their property, whether real or personal.
