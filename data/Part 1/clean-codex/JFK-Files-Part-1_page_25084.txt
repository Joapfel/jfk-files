On 22 Sep 1965, LIENVOY (LIEUVOY) reported that “Modesto BERMUDEZ, panamáno” called from inside the Soviet Embassy at 1128 hours to a local travel agency (T.( 1./Greyhound) and spoke with Luis LAESTIDA, asking her about the price of the ticket to Prague under the same system they had used to send the other people. LAESTIDA asked for one of the names in order to pull the card to see how it had been done, and “BERMUDEZ” gave her the name Guillermo AGUIRRE. LAESTIDA gave him various possible flights and asked that he call back in the afternoon for more definite information. Station traces show that Guillermo AGUIRRE Rodriguez, 201-245711, is a Nicaraguan who was at the CPBU school in Moscow in 1965. LILYMIC reported that the man in the attached photograph, who is undoubtedly identical with Alejandro BERMUDEZ Alegria, visited the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City from 1111 to 1157 hours. There therefore seems no question that he is the “Modesto BERMUDEZ, panamáno” who has been reported by LIENVOY in all the above conversations.

2. On 29 Sep 1965, LIENVOY reported that “Modesto BERMUDEZ, panamáno” called KORUSKENIN and said he had some things (“encartos”) for him. KORUSKENIN said he would see Modesto at seven o’clock in the usual place.
