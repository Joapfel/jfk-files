From June 1949 to February 1951, for March 20. Grade 9+II, salary 4500–6000. Office: FE. Position title: I.O. Duty title: Station Chief.

Description of duties: Establish and maintain station under Army C.I. cover in liaison with all civil agencies of Army (C-2, CIC, CID, etc.) in Northern Japan. Plan and implement agent operations into USSR territory. Establish repatriate interrogation program. Compile operational research studies. Support communications and logistics. Operations and other CIA activities tied into station. Supervise employees.

Duty station: Sapporo, Japan.

From April 1949 to May 1949. HOD HOS. Grade 9, salary 4500. Office: FE (OSO). Position title: I.O. Duty title: Case Officer.

Description of duties: Temporary period waiting for Sapporo assignment. Agent and operations report writing, debriefing defector, general support work to field case officers.

Duty station: Takao, Formosa. Taipei (TAIPAI) farewell.
