1. During meeting 24 October, HYSAGE-1 provided the following amplification and clarification of the role of Mexican journalist Rico Galan:

A. Galan, contrary to HYSAGE's earlier statements, recently spent eight days in Perumen (PERUMEN) on “vacation” with AMTHUR-1, during which time he received an operational assignment on which he is now traveling through Central and South America. Although a well-known leftist, he is able to travel because he has some special arrangement with the Mexican government, details of which are not clear to HYSAGE. The arrangement gives Galan special privileges from Mexican embassies in places he visits. HYSAGE commented that many Mexican ambassadors will be angry when they learn how they have used Galan.

B. Galan's mission is to persuade leftist parties in countries he visits to begin immediately acts of sabotage and violence and to try to maintain these in order to take pressure off Venezuela. Galan has also been instructed to bring better unity among competing leftist factions to ensure greater impact of their work.
