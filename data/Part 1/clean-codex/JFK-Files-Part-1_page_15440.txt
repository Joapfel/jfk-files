Classified message. Unit: AF/5. Extension: 5-57. Date: 21 May 1962.

Routing. To: Fran Lepouvillis. From: Director. Info: ALDP, C/C1, C/F1 2, C/TSD 2, C/EE 3, S/C 2.

Deferred. Routine.

To: Fran. Info: LEOP per CSH 43-43. Cite DIR 11783.

RUBAT WIROGUE QUATIC.

Ref: FRAN 04-04 (IN 37347).

1. Background information. Subject: BORI, 20 December 1919, Tiflis, USSR. Immigrated with parents to France in 1921. Married in Paris in 1948, two children, divorced in 1950.

Committed numerous crimes, convicted last of bank robbery in France in January 1953.

Released from prison in December 1955 on parole to reside only in Havre, Alpes Department, France. Contacted by AMENDABLE on spotting mission in early 1958, interviewed in October 1958, and as a result slated for REDSON project. Given AEPASCIC crypt, POA in September 1955, brought back to PERPETRATOR in April 1959. After extensive PM TIG, REDSON mission postponed, then cancelled in June 1960. Given new TIG for transfer to another component. In August 1960 arranged to resettle in Mexico. Plans cancelled and transferred to AF DIY in October 1960. Crypt changed to Wirogue. Flew back to FRAN in November 1960, purchased Australian passport, driver's license, and birth certificate.

Sent in July from FRAN to LEOP in December 1960 for new operations assignment. Became advisor to COMING AS, but as a result of security and financial trouble was ordered returned to PERPETRATOR. Returned to Paris in July 1961 and services terminated on 8 September 1961 at FRAN. Performed COCO, via STRAZENVILLE, in violation of termination agreement.

Already took up with CAN, presented LEOP station in an effort to revive KUBARK (KURARK) association.

COMPANY LIMP gave attention: various authorities and station feels agree security.
