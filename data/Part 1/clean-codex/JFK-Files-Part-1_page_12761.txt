Extract and Cross Reference. Document identification: Doc. symbol and no. HUCA-19475. Date of document: 17 July 73. Source cryptonym: WKSCARLET-3.

Text of extract: 3. From a negative point of view, WKNUGGET coverage of the home phone of WKSCARLET-3 was useful in that it did not reveal any indications that he was involved in narcotics trafficking. Since he also successfully passed his SGSWIRL, and satisfied the Station in a debriefing that he was not trafficking in narcotics, the WKNUGGET coverage of WKSCARLET-3 was discontinued.
