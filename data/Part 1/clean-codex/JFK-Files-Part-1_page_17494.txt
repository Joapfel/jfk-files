For the future, the Station has hopes of employing LIFETAT assets in “hot mike” operations, which will require access to telephone centrals and calls. It is the opinion of the Station that the support provided by Station operations, the CI/GE information, and the ability to cover sensitive targets without revealing our interest to Liaison services justifies the expenditure of funds, time, and personnel under this project. The overall performance of all agents in the complex continued to be good; no security problems were encountered which were not solved by the personnel involved.

4. Appropriate Special Staff components recommend renewal. The FI Staff notes that the project related to a most useful operational asset of the Station and, in addition, has produced thirty-seven positive intelligence determinations, six of which were of particular interest concerning the illegal transshipment (“fileal transpianent”) of goods to Cuba. FID comments that one third of direct interest to FI/D was received from Project LIFE, and that the technical coverage of the diplomatic installations would afford valuable assistance to the plans and conduct of any FI/D endeavor to mount operations. The CI Staff ratifies that the facilities of this project are and should be free for the coverage of known or highly suspected agents of the CIA/CI/MI/II satellite Cuban Intelligence-services in Mexico. CI notes that some twenty of these agents have been documented in detailed target studies which were forwarded to the Station.
