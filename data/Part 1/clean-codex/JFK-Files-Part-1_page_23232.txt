Classified message. Routing: VANADA; COVER.

Origin: L. D. Puckett. Unit: WHD 656. Extension: 2555. Date: 9 April 1958.

To: Mexico City. From: Director. CONF: WH 5. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI/OPS 2, S/C 2. Out 6438!

To: Mexi. Mexi 1017 (IN 26340).

Info.

1. Discussions with Arthur G. Vaivada indicate journalistic cover suitable for Mexi assignment. This based on his published fictional and news stories during past few years and published news photos taken during recent visit to Fidel Castro camp.

2. Headquarters currently investigating U.S. cover arrangements. However, to insure consideration of all possibilities, request station opinion on use of Robert Spiers Benjamin (request current traces), who is known to Nauman.

3. Request station concurrence for Vaivada to use journalistic cover.

End of message.

C/S comment: Vaivarda acceptable, suggested arrive Mexi on 1 May for orientation.

Coordination with: WH/III/Caribbean R.R.; DC/CI/TCD Paul J. Benz; CCB/NC/Mr. Lukoskie by phone L.L.D.

J. C. King, C/MHD, coordinating officer. R. H. DarlGreI, C/M/III. Releasing officer. Authenticating officer.
