Antecedents:

Alberto DO EYO does not have any police or ideological antecedents. Until the year 1961, he worked at Editorial “ESTAMPA,” then at “PETERSEN THIBLE CRUZ y Cia.”

It should be pointed out that in order to establish his activities and connections with ALFERIEV, starting 22-10-69, surveillance and shadowing were carried out on the subject.

On the other hand, as a result of the use of clandestine microphones, it was learned that Ivan ALFERIEV maintained conversations with a person by the name of MOQUILEVSKY, who was a member of the directive council of the Metropolitan Federation of Chess and the Argentine Federation.
