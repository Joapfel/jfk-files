Incoming Classified Message. Central Intelligence Group.

From: TS INSTAO. To: Special Operations. Action: CPD. Information: ADSO, COPS, CRD, FBZ, SFD, CSS, CAS. Routine. 5 December 1947. IN 30058.

TSIN 031.

To: WASHF. Cite: TSIN (from RAFORD). Re WASH 9560 (OUT 55224).

1. Happy accept assignment Calcutta. Does this also apply to security reasons? Deliver better assignment Harry Stinlake to Darryl Stinlake different post. Having waited together this long for assignment, Dairen would look suspicious if proceed Calcutta together. If incur, arrange clerk-stenographer, preferably male, to arrive Calcutta shortly after our arrival.

2. Security reasons necessitate following prior departure for Calcutta:

A. Classification as foreign staff officer.

B. Bona fide state travel orders.

C. Pay card with account number same as state official.

D. Authority to turn in per diem account from time of departure TSINSTAO to arrival Calcutta to finance clerk there.

TOR03492. 7 December 1947.
