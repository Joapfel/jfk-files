29 September 1966. IN:06358.

1. ANSCROLL radio on 21 September reported Cubaexport was negotiating to sell Cuban beef to Comisaria General in two units of one thousand tons each. Broadcast recalled December sale of beef to Spain which, upon arrival, was found to be short by 135 pieces. Broadcast for 29 September will update this report using information contained in reference.

2. JMHOPE and other radio assets will hit theme of GOC exports of meat while Cuban people suffer severe shortage. Will point out that hard currency obtained by GOC in trade with Spain and other free world nations is used by GOC to export subversion and violence abroad and to strengthen internal police-state mechanisms. This subans lose beef and gain more controls.
