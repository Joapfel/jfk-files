Dispatch. To: Chief, W5. CWO: Chief, KUBOLF; Chief of Station, Paris. Chief of Station, Paris (LCPIPIT): Chief, KUDLIK. CWO: Chief of Station, London. Subject: JAQUAR Paper on Revolution. Ref: OELA-40,542 (15 April 1984).

1. Reference forwarded a JAGUAR paper dealing with the well-known Communist magazine Revolution. Attached are (1) another JAGUAR note from SWOCK's office, and (2) the paper received with it. Please note JAGUAR's caveats on the use of the material.

2. SWIMPISH also remarked to me, on one recent occasion, that he had learned that Richard GIBSON had been expelled from the staff of Revolution (as is indicated here), but the reason that SWIMPISH gave was that GIBSON had been accused of working on behalf of “subversive” elements, which SWIMPISH implied to mean KUBARK. Maybe SWIMPISH was speculating on or misquoting this JAGUAR report. I haven't been able to go back to SWIMPISH on this, because he has subsequently gone on leave.

3. He (and JAGUAR) would appreciate any comments on this report.

George R. WATKINBACH

Attachment: herewith. 1—Letter. 2—Report.

Distribution: Chief, W5 with attachment; C/KLWOLF with attachment; C/LCPIPIT with attachment; C/KUDLIK with attachment; COS/Paris with attachment. CS Carl.
