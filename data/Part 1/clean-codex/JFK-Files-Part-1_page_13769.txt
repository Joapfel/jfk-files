Dispatch.

To: Chief, W.D.  
Info: Chief of Station, Mexico.  
From: Chief of Station, Panama.

Subject: L.M.E./Operations—Contacts in Mexico with Soviets and Cubans.

The Panama Station has completed the preparation of Field Information Reports based on information provided by EYAB from his contacts in Mexico with IDENTITY 1 and IDENTITY 2. Such reports did not, however, include operational details of such contacts, summary of which follows:

1. EYAB checked into his hotel in Mexico City on a Friday morning and immediately telephoned IDENTITY 3, making an ostensibly innocuous inquiry using specific terminology in line with procedures arranged by IDENTITY 1 during previous meetings with EYAB in Mexico City. Although this inquiry was intended to alert EYAB's contact for a meeting the following day (alternate meeting twenty-four hours later), the system failed or IDENTITY 1 did not follow through for unknown reasons. Five days later (Tuesday), EYAB requested the assistance of IDENTITY 4 in contacting IDENTITY 1. IDENTITY 4 instructed EYAB to meet him the following night (Wednesday) at 7 p.m. on the corner of San Luis and Constitution Avenue. An alternate meeting was fixed for twenty-four hours later.

2. IDENTITY 4 did not appear for the first meeting, but did appear for the alternate meeting on Thursday. He and EYAB entered a taxi and drove to the corner of Xola Boulevard and Bolivar Street in the Colonia Alanos, where EYAB walked one block while IDENTITY 4 went to determine.
