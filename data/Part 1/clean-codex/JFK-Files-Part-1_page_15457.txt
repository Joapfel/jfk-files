Classified message. Date: 19 December 1961. To: Director. Routing. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3. Info: COP, C/CI, C/FI 2, S/C 2. IN 40416.

DIR CITE LEOP 2898

RYBAT WIROGUE

1. WIROGUE (W) reported 16 December BUNHULL said DE GROEVE now in Tehran (TEHERAN) because planes there, not Europe. BUNHULU afraid purchase planes because they not thoroughly checked, sent DEGROEVE cable ordering him return Congo or be fired.

2. W says his entire position with GOC shot. He does not have enough money on which to live is looking for job. Asked if anything anywhere for him with KUBARK. W said he now feels his return to Congo mistake.

(STATION COULD NOT AGREE MORE). HE ONLY WANTED TO DO SOMETHING.
