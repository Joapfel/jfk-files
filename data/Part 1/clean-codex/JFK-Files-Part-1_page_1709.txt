5 April 1968

Request for Alien Documentation for ALCUTIN-117

To: Chief, TRO/AB  
Via: Office of Security/Alien Affairs Staff

1. In accordance with preliminary conversations between Mr. Robert Fambini, VII/4/A, and Miss Tina Japira and Mr. Patrick Phelan, TUD/AD, it is requested that an ordinary Nicaraguan passport be issued in the name of Juan Eidalgo ELIEONDO Romano for ALCUTIN-117's use to travel from the United States to Argentina and return. In addition, it is requested that an Arrival-Departure Record (ILMR Form I-94) be prepared for the subject.

2. The requested passport should contain a forged United States multiple-entry tourist visa and a cachet showing entry into the United States via Miami, Florida, in early April 1968. Inclusion of the forged United States visa in the subject's passport will not afford him any privileges he does not already legally hold in his true identity. The passport should be valid through the end of 1968.

3. The following information should be included on the Form I-94 to be prepared for the subject:

a. Permanent address in Nicaragua, Nicaragua: Calle 15 de Septiembre No. 403.
