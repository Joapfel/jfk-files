Document Transfer and Cross Reference. Subject of document: Victor Mussik’s visit to Amoy. Document identification: RI file No.: China CIA OP-1; D 1129. Analyst: JEP. Date processed: £3 Dec. 63.

Pertinent Information: The Japanese Consular Authorities at Amoy are of the belief that H. R. Isaacs went to Amoy between Dec. 24, 1933 and Jan. 3, 1934, under the name of Victor Mussik. The local Japanese authorities therefore request information regarding the movements of Victor Mussik during the aforementioned period to ascertain if he actually visited Amoy during this period.

Document disposition. Cross referred to this file. Transferred to RI file No. File this form in file No.
