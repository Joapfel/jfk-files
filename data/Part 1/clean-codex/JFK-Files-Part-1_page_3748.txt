10719372 JUL 68 CITE MANAGUA 6617 (TRECKLER ACTING)

Director info: JMOGORA, Panama City, Port au Prince.

1. AMBIDDY-1 told ACOS 7 July that he had received a phone call from his wife in Miami advising that someone had been trying to contact him. AMBIDDY-1 assumes this might be his headquarters case officer and wishes to advise headquarters that he will be returning to Miami on 8 July.

2. Following additional information picked up in the 2 July meeting with AMBIDDY-1 on events that occurred prior to his departure from Miami the first week of July:

A. (FNU) Blanca Roles, Panama consul in Miami, contacted AMBIDDY-1 and told him the Panamanian minister of Hacienda, who was visiting in the United States, wanted to meet with AMBIDDY-1 to discuss an unspecified urgent matter.

B. Eugene Maximillian, Haitian consul in Miami, told AMBIDDY-1 that President Duvalier (Duvallier) wanted to see him. The purpose was to carry a private message from Duvalier (Duvallier) to President Somoza. He claimed that if AMBIDDY-1 did this, Duvalier (Duvallier) would release the son of Leon Cantave from prison. Somoza has given AMBIDDY-1 permission to make the trip. According to AMBIDDY-1, he and Somoza figure this is a play by Duvalier (Duvallier) to try to open more doors for acceptance in Latin America.
