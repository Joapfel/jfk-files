d. Lt. Colonel Solís Antonio MARTINEZ

Date of birth: 3 August 1926, San Luis, State of Falcon, Venezuela.

Marital Status (Martial Status): Married, three children.

Formal Education: High school, Caracas, 1940–1944; Venezuelan Military Academy, 1944–1947 (graduate). Also completed specialized courses in infantry tactics and tank warfare, as well as the course of study given by the Superior War College of the Venezuelan Ministry of War. He has served in a variety of capacities within the Venezuelan Army since 1947, commanding everything from platoons through battalions, as well as serving as an instructor of artillery. He has seen action against the guerrillas in the State of Falcon. He served in Colombia, dates unknown, as a member of the Venezuelan mission to “Operation America.”

Language Ability: Native Spanish; reads but does not speak or write English. His competence in understanding English is given as “fair.”
