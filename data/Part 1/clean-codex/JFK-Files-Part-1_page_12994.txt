2. Re references B and C: We have serious reservations regarding proposed attendance as observer of TUMIX/S. At best, he would be an extra-continental factor without vote and little voice on central issue of the MASPERO line. The latter advocates would undoubtedly exploit TUMIX/5's presence to inject into discussions the U.S. position on Viet Nam, which is not germane to CLASC problems and not an issue in the context of Dominican politics, despite efforts by Dominican Communist parties through propaganda events like “Viet Nam Week” at the university about a year ago, which backfired because the Communists raised the Communist flag higher.
