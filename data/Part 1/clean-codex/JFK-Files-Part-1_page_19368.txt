Outgoing classified message. Central Intelligence Group.

To: TSHEGTAD. Priority. From: Special Operations. 22 October 1947. Confirmation: FBZ (1-2). OUT 53490. Information: A660 (3-4), COPS (5), CPD (6), CPD (7), GAS (8), SFC (9).

Paraphrase not required. Handle as secret correspondence per Purs. 51 (1) 60.1 .IR-380-5.

WASH 8147

To: 1SIN (attention). Re TSIN 019 (IN 26097).

Cite: WASHF

Inability to reach DAFEN disappointing. Change of assignment advisable. Need experienced man for chief Korea under State cover. Also have opening as chief of Calcutta station. Indicate your preference. If you accept Korea position, desire personally accompany you to Seoul. Request his reply on Korea assignment. Will take month to arrange new State designations. In view of incomplete change of assignment do not uncrate furniture.

Re paragraph 5, answer will follow.

D. De Caar de Lebsen

Releasing officers: RSM PE

Harry W. Little, Jr.

Originating and coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.

T0B97, 21 October 1947.

It is forbidden to make a copy of this message.
