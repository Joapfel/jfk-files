Classified message.

7 August 1964. MIN 44977.

DIR'CITE MEXI 0492.

REDCOAT.

Immediate action.

Ref.: MEXI 0444 (in 4/17/79).

1. According to 1 August LIENVOY, Sergey Semyonovich Kukharenko arranged packing of certain personal effects for shipment to the USSR. Per other LIENVOY reporting and information from subject, paragraph 2 reference, departure is for home leave. Same day Mrs. K. told suspected KGB guard Ivan Ivanovich Obyedkov that when K. took her home that afternoon, he pulled a fast one on her, “stepped on the gas and disappeared in cloud of smoke and dust. I don't know the place where he goes, but I know (KCOW) the (unintelligible) perfectly (PERFECTGY).” At 11 a.m. hours same evening Mrs. K. again called (CALLZD) Obyedkov, told (TOLDR) him that K. returned completely drunk and had wrecked his car.

Not late yet, Mrs. K. pleaded with O. on 12 July to prevent K. from driving the car that date since he was drunk and Mrs. K. feared he would wreck the car.

2. 5 August LIENVOY reporting connected above unclear, but content of short Mrs. X-O conversation indicates possibility X, M.D., to appear at an MOM office, and that unnamed higher-level secret officer's patience is exhausted with K. Would also appear.

Group I. Includes the following units: Group B and Group C.
