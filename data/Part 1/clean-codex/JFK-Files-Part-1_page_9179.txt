21 May 1951

File: “STALIN – PHOTO-REPORT”

Re: Reproductions of CIA graphics photos, given to MSS by DCS on 17 May 1951. DCS had requested them from Graphics several weeks before. They were sent to him with a covering note from Dick Cornish, saying that Graphics forgot to omit the CIA captions. Cornish cut out the CIA numerals but left the captions.

I have filed most of these in my folder PHOTOS.

The following I am sending to Shub, having cut out the CIA-typed captions and written some, in sterilized form, with the XYZ numbering, on the back.

KABATKA

I am reminding Shub that they cannot be used without clearance by Graphics.

XYZ-1: USSR, Asiatic RSFSR, Primorsk Region, Vladivostok (Pladenostok), J 43 08 N, 131 54 E. Second River concentration camp. 1946.

XYZ-2: Germany, Brandenburg, near Oranienburg, 52 45 N, 13 15 E. Sachsenhausen, former Nazi concentration camp, now used by Soviets. It is reported that there are 14,500 men and 1,500 women, mostly political prisoners, confined in this camp. June 1949.

XYZ-3: Latvian SSR, Riga, 56 56 N, 24 41 E. Soviet deportation car. 1941.

XYZ-4: Hungary, Pest-Pilis-Solt-Kiskun, Budapest, 47 30 N, 19 02 E. Hungarian loops strangling mice about the neck of an enemy of Communism in the courtyard of old Marko Jail. 1948.

XYZ-5: Hungary, Pest-Pilis-Solt-Kiskun, Budapest, 47 30 N, 19 02 E. Escapees (Escapies) of Communism are strangled in the courtyard of old Marko Jail. 1948.

XYZ-6: USSR, Lithuanian SSR. Corpse of the rector of Lankeliskalai tortured to death by the NKVD. 1941.

XYZ-7: USSR, Lithuanian SSR. Row of corpses of Lithuanian workers in a sugar refinery, murdered by the NKVD. 1941.
