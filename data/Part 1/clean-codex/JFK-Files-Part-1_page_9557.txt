Am. Express.

Interview under name of Martin E. Williams, of Kingston, Pa., with Mr. Gilmore, Assistant Passenger Traffic Manager of the Travel Department.

American Express will accept money (check, draft, or cash) in one place—the New York office—or in several places (Paris, for instance), provided one central office knows the details and can coordinate.

They will then write to all offices involved, authorizing transportation, etc., for a list of persons, which may be added to at the last moment. We specify what credentials persons must present. We also specify whether transportation is to be by air, steamship, rail, etc.; first class or otherwise; and whether or not “expenses” are to be allowed. In this connection, they suggest that traveling expenses be allowed (for meals, etc.) not to exceed 10% of transportation. They suggest that we act as soon as practicable because of the rush for travel to Europe, but Mr. Gilmore says they can be reasonably certain of providing air transportation.

They will render a complete accounting of transportation and expense money provided and remit unexpended balances.
