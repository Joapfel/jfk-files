Orig: N. Judkins. Unit: A WH/3/Mexico. Classified message. Ext: 5940. Date: 22 April 1963.

To: Mexico City

From: Director

Conf: VH 8

Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/RA, FI, TSD 5, S/C

Cite DIR 35066.

To: Mexi

Ref: MEXI 4201 (IN 19807).

1. HQS approves loan of one sterile spare B-10 dial pulse recorder to Durkin.

2. HQS urges close support Treasury/Narcotics investigations. Mexi may provide Durkin with occasional LIPEAT coverage provided no LIPEAT take given to Mexicans.

End of message.

WH comment: William J. Durkin, Treasury representative assigned to Mexico investigating narcotics traffic, approached Station regarding dial recorder for phone tap on Mexican target.

TSD Bruce La Rose (by phone).

CI/OPS Frank Hughes. F.L. Hugle.

WH/CORP.
