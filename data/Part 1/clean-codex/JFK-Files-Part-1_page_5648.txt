Clemand Joseph Charles, who has stated on numerous occasions that he will be the next president of Haiti and who has gathered about himself a personal following of individuals covertly opposed to the government of President Duvalier (Collector's note: see 90-2-321/76913-69), has now declared that action against Haiti's president is imperative and that Duvalier's overthrow must and can be accomplished during the month of June 1969. He has set a 1 July 1969 deadline for the accomplishment of his goal. Charles has also stated that time is of the essence because other opposition groups (Collector's note: previously, he refers to one or more of those groups listed in OCR-321/769.3-69) may try to act before he can. Charles lists no reason for the specific time limit of 1 July 1969.

2. Charles has requested that “the Pentagon” (specifically, his former intermediary, Colonel Howard Burr) dispatch a secret envoy to talk to him. He lays down no modus operandi for the envoy and presumably will talk to him in Port-au-Prince. This request has been labeled “Urgent.”
