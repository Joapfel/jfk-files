Outgoing message.

Date-time group: 030001Z. Message reference number: 403810. To: WH/Miami.

VUVAPOR

1. Following summary State 80538 dated 27 April 1973 (pouching copy):

A. British emboff Washington gave note to Department on behalf of Bahamas Government (GOBI) on 27 April 1973 re new reports in United Kingdom concerning two people involved in anti-Bahamas independence activities. Reports identify two people as U.S. citizens. They are Edwin Marger, forty-four-year-old attorney, who claims to represent “Council for a Free Abaco,” and Mitchel Beli (Mitchell Bell), fifty-five-year-old arms dealer. GOBI has unconfirmed information that Marger is a Florida attorney and Bell is president of Defense Systems International, Inc., Powder Spring, Georgia. “Council for a Free Abaco” unknown to GOBI.

B. News reports say both men arrived in the UK claiming to represent Abaco citizens who wanted to remain British, and interested in hiring British mercenaries, though neither man said mercenaries were intended for Abaco.

Releasing office. Coordinating office. Authenticating office.
