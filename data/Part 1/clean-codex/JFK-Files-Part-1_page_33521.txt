To: Director, Paris, Helsinki, Bonn, Tokyo, London, RYBAT FUUMEN.

Ref: Director 499469, 15-124-52.

1. Wish to offer following suggested changes in ref, which are based on latest information available locally. Para 31: “...Central Bank devalued the escudo for imports/exports by slightly over 120 percent in an effort to curb import demand, encourage exports and ease pressures...” “...Other price rises brought inflation for the year to 508 percent...” (For Director: While previous estimates by both Embassy and IMF in November placed inflation for the year at probably between 750 and 800 percent, the end-of-year figure of the National Statistical Institute was 500.1. See Santiago Embassy telegram 0173 of 11 January.)
