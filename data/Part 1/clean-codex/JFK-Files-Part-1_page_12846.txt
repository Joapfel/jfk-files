13 March 1973, 131350Z. Staff. CITE Caracas 21396. To: Director, Madrid, Rome, WH/Miami. Info: Buenos Aires, Bogota, Paris.

RYBAT PLMHABYSS: Posada travel plans.

Refs: A. Caracas 21349 (not sent Bogota, Madrid, Paris) (W854093). B. Caracas 21369 (not sent Rome, Paris, Madrid) (J855389).

1. According to BNDD headquarters telegram 1554, which originated Miami BNDD, Luis Posada (IDENT) scheduled to arrive in Miami 9 March via Pan Am 552 from San Juan, Puerto Rico. From Miami he ticketed to New York, then Madrid. No mention made of Rome. Posada carrying Venezuelan diplomatic passport No. 65 and traveling with Camilo Polali or Pusali. BNDD Miami planning surveillance of Posada while in Miami.
