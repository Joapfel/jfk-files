Re: Bogotá 18318 (IN 519768).

1. Precise whereabouts of Captain Jose Abranantes are not known to us, but we have no reason to believe that he is outside of COBA. Given his rank (Vice Minister of Interior and head of the DSE), it seems highly unlikely that he would be engaged in a mission with such patently high flap potential. Our file on JOYE does not indicate that he has ever used war name “FIDEL.” Conjugation of our most recent (circa 1965) photograph of Jose with Photo 189 (of Juan) in mug book leads us to conclude that Jose and Juan are not identical. Physical description of Jose as follows:

Thirty-nine-year-old Caucasian with black hair and eyes. Dark-complected, medium build, round face and generally regarded as “good some.” Different sources estimate his height and weight as ranging from five foot seven to five foot nine and from 110 lbs to 175 lbs.

2. We have no physical description of Juan Abrahamdes and can provide no telegram on his current location.
