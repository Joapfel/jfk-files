Classified message. Date: 19 Mar 59.

To: Director. Mar 19 2329Z 59. From: Mexico City. Routine. Action: TSS 4. Info: CI, CI/OPS, FI, FI/OPS, WH 4, S/C 2. IN 34110.

To: DIR. Info: GUAL SANJ. Cite: MEXI 3522. AQUATIC. Re: DIR 16813 (OUT 82047).

1. Raymond H. Gerende advised by Cuban Embassy 17 March that does not need visa enter Cuba as tourist on Mexican passport since MEXI-PP good for 29 day stay and can easily be reviewed once in Cuba.

2. Cuban Embassy advised ODACID by phone 13 March that tourist visa not required for any native-born Latin American, U.S., British, French or Swiss citizen. Tourist visa is required for any above naturalized citizen. Transient visa required on any nationality who travels via Cuba to country other than his own.

3. Does HOS desire naturalized Mexican citizen obtain tourist visa?

End of message.

CS comment: Requested immediately on loan Cuban tourist visas in original passports other than U.S.
