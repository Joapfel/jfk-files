Date: 9 October 1961. Classified message. To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3 (AF copy slotted at 1545, 9 October). Info: CO, C/CA, C/CI, C/FI 2, C/IW 2, C/EE 3, S/C 2 (suspense DD/1). 9 October 1928Z 61. Priority. IN 42712.

PRITY DIR INFO FRAN MUNI CITE LEGF 8218-43

RYBAT WIROGUE UPHILL

1. WIROGUE contacted SALVACK 7 October. Said Congolese Air Force (CAF) Chief PIERROT now permanently ousted. Also said MALIERE told him CNA plans put European in charge CAF and considering three persons for position: Col. STAHL, WIROGUE and Captain Card PETER DE GROEVE, who also CAF adviser.

2. WIROGUE asked PBPRIME reaction and interest in STAHL. SALVACK said STAHL was West German and PBPRIME willing see STAHL in position. SALVACK denied any ODYOKE contact with STAHL other than through AIRA. WIROGUE said he willing support STAHL as long as STAHL did not try undermine his position. Added he thought he could get STAHL thrown out of Congo if ODYOKE so wished.

SALVACK told WIROGUE ODYOKE does not desire this and said such.
