Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Paris. From: Chief, SR via Chief, WE. Subject: REDWOOD/LCIMPROVE/AEDOGMA.

Action required: Show attached photo to AEDOGMA/1. Reference: OFFW-30103, 17 March 1961.

1. In answer to our request of ODEWY to compare the handwriting of Semen PORORILIW with that attributed to him in the letter to Antin MYCZYPOEK, ODEWY has informed us that at this time they do not have sufficient specimens of PORORILIW's handwriting to reach any conclusion.

2. ODEWY has furnished us with the attached photograph of Semen PORORILIW. We suggest that this photograph, unidentified, be shown to AKDOGMA/1 for possible recognition. We are sending this photograph to MJB, also, for showing to appropriate sources.

SHULBY K. LIPHOTT  
MORTIMER F. BURCHAM
