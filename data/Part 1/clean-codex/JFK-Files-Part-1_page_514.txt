1. (UNBOUND) passed the following report 5 July from source (“BABOUCHE”): “Black Panther” members George Edward Wright, George Brown, Melvin McNair, his wife Jean Carol McNair, and Joyce Rillerson (wife of George Wright) arrived in Paris from Algiers in late March. Henri Curie’s clandestine organization has provided them lodging and training during their stay in France. The group is now trying to arrange plastic surgery and false documentation to permit their clandestine re-entry into the U.S.

2. (UNBOUND) requests on an urgent basis any available photos of the above individuals to aid them in identifying them before surgery takes place. Please advise. (UNBOUND) has not passed this information to the legal attaché.
