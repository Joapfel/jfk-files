Subject about 45 years, former vice president of Cuban railroads, allegedly well connected with Cuban exiles in Europe. Family fortune seized by Castro government; subject’s father died of heart attack shortly after. Married, one child; family in Rome with subject.

3. OIRA-39287 describes 16 January 1965 Rome meeting with subject, Bunce, and Growery, during which subject provided ops leads and information. Left KUBARKers with excellent first impression.
