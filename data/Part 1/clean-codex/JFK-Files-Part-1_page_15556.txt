Classified message.

Date: 6 April 1961.

To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3 (TEMPO copy slotted C/AF 1430, 6 April). Info: COP, C/CA, C/CI, C/FI 2, G/EE 3, S/C 2.

For Lieutenant, per CH 43-48.

PRITY DIR CITE LEOP 114D (BETTELHEIM acting).

RYBAT WIROGUE.

Ref: LEOP 1128 (IN 30853).

1. Station informed Ambassador that Swanson, representative of European subsidiary of PBPRIME parent company, now in Leopoldville negotiating contract with CAF. This presented as deal strictly between GOC and company with no mention KUBARK interest. Ambassador concerned because may be against 21 February resolution UN. Ambassador states cannot approve or back Swanson if he does not obtain UNOC approval of contract.

2. Ambassador told our interest only aroused last couple days when Swanson appeared. We interested in who instructors are and kind of deal made with CAF.

Per CH 43-43.
