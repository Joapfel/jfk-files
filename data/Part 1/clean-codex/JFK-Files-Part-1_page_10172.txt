DIR INFO WAVE CITE MEXI 2994 (DUSLER ACTING)  
GYROSE LITAMIL-9

1. On 3 Jan 63 Ramon Sinobas Casado, Cuban chargé, told L-9 that Manuel Vega Perez, Cuban G-2, was going to Havana (HAVA) 6 Jan via Cubana. Vega, according to Sinobas, will remain in Havana (HAVA) from 15 to 20 days. LITAMIL-7 gave Vega a farewell luncheon (LUNCHECK) afternoon 3 Jan 63.

2. On 3 Jan Sinobas told L-9 and Andres Ariona Ramos, commercial counselor, that in future plans Cuban government L-9 and Ariona will no longer be considered diplomats except when assigned overseas embassy posts. When in Havana (HAVA), L-9 and Ariona will not be considered diplomats. L-9, according to Sinobas, will fall under jurisdiction of Cultural Department, Ministry of Education, and when necessary Foreign Office will ask Ministry to provide personnel for overseas assignments. Sinobas told Ariona his situation similar, with Ministry of Foreign Commerce responsible for his assignment.
