U.S.A. Embassy (UZAN EMEAASSY). 15-61-55. 3 May 1969. L35-M22-T1151. C854-1406.

27h.

Out at 1350. LOGINOV talks to YAKOVETS and tells him that LOG would like to go to Cuernavaca tomorrow to see SYRETSKIY. So can YAK give him the phone number of SYR, because several comrades are going on home leave and would like to buy a few things. OK, minute. LOG will have to call early in the morning to the factory and go there after 2 PM. His phone number is 2-09-15, his name is NISHEL ZARETSKIY, and the factory is on Calle 7/00 Taxco. OK.

Thanks.
