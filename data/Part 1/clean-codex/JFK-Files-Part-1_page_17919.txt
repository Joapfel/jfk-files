Classified message.

Date: 9 December 1957.

To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 5. Info: FI/OPS, SR 4, OF 2, S/C 2. Cite: MEXI 729.

Departure Stough leaves station without translator Limerick. Station requires one translator with official cover besides Bronund. Recommend Miss Ann Stefen, who performed excellently TDY here.

End of message.
