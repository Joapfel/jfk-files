17. Military service

18. History of Employment

a) Subject arrived in Mexico City on 10 May 1961 (LIFIRE, 15 May 61), along with eight other young diplomats between 19 and 25, accompanied by a member of the Cuban Foreign Office. The purpose was to make known the Castro Revolution in Mexico, Chile, Colombia, Argentina, Venezuela, and Brazil. Subject was Third Secretary. (Excelsior, 13 May 61)

b) Assistant Press and Cultural Attaché, Cuban Embassy, Mexico (LITALUS, 11 Oct 61).

c) Returned to Cuba on 4 May 1962. Replaced by Manuel VEGA Perez (LITEMPO-1, May 62).

d) Arrived in Mexico on 3 September 1962. Orders from Cuba that he not be placed on the Diplomatic List. Leaving for Cuba on 28 September 1962 (LIFIRE, 4 Sept 62; LITANIL-7, 7 Sept 62; LIENVOY, 27 Sept 62).

e) Unfortunately received a scholarship—not known what kind or where (EMLA-21403, 21 May 63; LITANIL-9).

f) Arrived in Mexico on 6 July 1963 as Second Secretary (LIFIRE, 9 July 63).
