To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Attention: No. D. J. Jacobson

From: Deputy Director (Plans)

Subject: Security and Privacy Matters, also known as Security Interest; Internal Security—Russia

1. References are made to your letter dated 12 July 1968, entitled as above, to our U5C(3)/773,735, dated 25 September 1968, sent to your file number 125-9/295.

2. Information from our representative in Paris indicates that one Joseph K. Bel, born 10 May 1941, living in Paris, possibly a Soviet citizen, living at La Chapelle, France, and a Russian refugee, was listed by the French Ministry of Interior as potentially dangerous during his visit to Moscow. Arrangements were made for his safe departure from France from 15–20 March 1969. Approximately two thousand other individuals were also listed.

3. An official French liaison service report dated 9 November 1968 stated that Josef K. Bel (sic) had not attracted their attention. Our representative advises that this is a translation of a “no trans” reply.
