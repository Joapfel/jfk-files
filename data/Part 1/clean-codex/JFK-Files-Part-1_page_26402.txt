Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Info: Chief of Base, Merida. Chief of Station, Penang City. From: Chief of Station, Santo Domingo. Subject: 10. Travel of Victor Rico Galan. Processing action: Approved for indexing; no indexing required; only qualified DEK can be added indexing; microfilm; action required—references. Action required: For your information. Reference: UNMA-22S01, 24 January 1964.

Subject said at the airport in Santo Domingo on departure that he would travel to Miami. However, he was manifested to Kingston, and per KNGS 1097, traveled there and departed on Mexican flight 304 to Mexico. PAVE did not have any information on his arrival there, per PAVE 0874. (Note: Neither KNGS 1097 dated 23 January nor PAVE 0874 dated 22 January were sent to Panama or Mexico.)

End of dispatch.

Distribution: Mexico City; Headquarters; Mexico; Panama City.

Panama reference to UNCA-22S01. Submission date: 4 February 1964. Apr 5 February 1964.
