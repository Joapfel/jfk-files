Classified message. Date: 8 November 1962. To: Director. From: San Juan. Action: TFW 10. Info: DDP, DOP/SIT 3, CI, CI/OPS FL ISD 4, WH 7, S/C 2. Routing. Routine. IN 15095.

WAVE INFO DIR HAGU CITE SJAN 0835. GYROSE. Reference HPSW 1362 (not sent wave).

1. In initial discussion with IDEN A, he informed KARNLEY that he is in contact locally with DRE (for DIR: see para 1 ref). IDEN A said his contact has been with JOSE ARTECONA, brother of CARLOS “TRIPA” ARTECONA.

2. ARTECONA had sought IDEN A’s assistance obtaining miniature cameras which said he wished to transmit to PBRUMEN through channel which his organization has now established with IDEN B. According to ARTECONA, IDEN B cooperating as communications channel using “diplomatic pouch” (POUCHT) and has not only transmitted messages but on one occasion transmitted one unit small arms.

3. When queried further, IDEN A said ARTECONA has never identified individual who actually in contact with IDEN B nor indicated extent IDEN B’s government involved. Whole story fairly loose.

4. KARNLEY declined IDEN A’s suggestion that he talk directly with ARTECONA. Suggested instead that IDEN A advise ARTECONA to present his request for assistance to his KUBARK contact.
