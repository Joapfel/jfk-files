War Department Notification of Personnel Action (Field). Date: 29 August 1946. To: J. Walton Moore. Through: 880.

Office in which Employed to be Empowered. This is to notify you of the following action concerning your employment, which is subject to the provisions on the reverse hereof. This form is an official record of your service history in the War Department and should be retained for future reference.

Nature of action: Boosted Appointment. Effective date: 29 August 1946. Position title: Intelligence Officer. Service grade and salary: CAP-12, $3505.20 per annum. Force and service or command: 880. Installation and location: Shanghai, China.

Remarks: Plus quarters and cost of living allowance at the rate authorized in Budget Circular A-8.

For the Commanding Officer. Signature. Mr. O. Tharp, Chief of Personnel.
