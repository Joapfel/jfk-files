Bogotá, D. E., October 17, 1966

Exmo. Sr.  
Embajador (Subajador) del Japón  
en Colombia  
E. S. D.

Innovation:

On account of the forthcoming Trade Seminar that will take place from the 12th to the 15th of this month in the city of Tokyo, with the participation of some Latin American countries, the Confederation of Workers of Colombia, CTC, wishes to reiterate through you, to the Democratic Government of Japan, the request we have formulated on other occasions, referring (reforent) to the continuation of the permanent trade that Japan has established with the Cuban Commonwealth, considering that the currency obtained from the sale of products is used by the Government of Cuba to finance subversion and terrorism in Latin America.

We formulate our request in the hope of saving the interests of the French democratic workers and the institutional integrity of our country, since Colombia, the same as other American countries, forms part of the infiltration (in - filmation) program that has been planned from Cuba by the International Communist Party, as was confirmed in the newspaper “El Tiempo” in one of its editions on October 12.

We insist on this petition because we consider that if Japan continues to trade with Communist Cuba, it would be the same as contributing to the annihilation of a regime in America which has put an end to democratic freedom, has promoted the mass murder and imprisonment of thousands of democrats, leaders, and workers. If this situation persists, we would be forced to terminate, together with other countries of America, the boycott of all Japanese products, as we have done with the ships of those countries that are holding trade with Cuba.

Looking to hear from you soon, we remain,
