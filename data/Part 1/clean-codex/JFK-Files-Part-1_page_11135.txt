1 December 1965. 50769. Cite Mexico City 9456. Director (DIRECror) info JMWAVE (JTWAVE), Oslo. RYBAT TYPIC AMADAGE.

Refs: Director (DIRECTR) 54581; B. JMWAVE (JHWAVE) 4321.

1. With his usual intuitiveness, LITAMIL-9 convinced AMADAGE-1 was not pro-Castro but could only provide the following to back up his conviction:

A. A-1 is a well-educated intellectual (he attended the Sorbonne (SORROW)) of bourgeois origin who lost property after Castro took over and is definitely not the revolutionary type.

B. A-1 and wife appeared very anxious to leave Cuba and go to Norway.

2. L-9 states A-1 is far too intelligent and discreet to make anti-regime statements even to close friends. L-9 heard from his sister-in-law, who is usually well-informed, that A-1 is going back to Cuba in December 1966 to stay. L-9 does not know whether A-1 asked to return or is being recalled. Has no idea whether A-1, if recalled, would defect rather than go back.
