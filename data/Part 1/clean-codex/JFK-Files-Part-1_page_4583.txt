Sunny South Airport Service, Inc.

Cessna and No. 138

To all of whom it may concern:

It is our special pleasure and privilege to extend to you, Mr. Cessna, a most sincere and grateful wish that we were one of the many friends you have made during your lifetime. It is with deep personal thanks and admiration that we would like to express our sincere thanks for your kindness and consideration in making possible our present happy life. We will always remember your kindness, and shall ever be glad to see you again.

Mr. Cessna,

Sincerely yours,

John R. Cessna  
President

Jesse Miller

Dr. John Cessna

I am very happy today and grateful to God for having me here today with him. He has made me so much happy. I know he would be very pleased with what his friends are saying about him. They say that he was a kind-hearted man, who always wanted to help people, and if there had been any problem that he could have helped with, he would have done so. He was also a very good speaker. He was a man of great faith, and I know that he believed in God more than in any other person on earth. He was a man of great knowledge and skill, but above all, he was a man of great integrity. He was a true Christian and a true American, and as such, he would be very glad to see you again.

Sincerely yours,

William H. Seward
