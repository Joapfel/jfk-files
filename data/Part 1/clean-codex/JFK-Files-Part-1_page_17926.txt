Classified message.

Date: 076 SE/LB/J. Subject: 25 October 1957. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 5. SPO: FI/OPS 2, FI/RI, S/C 2.

26 October 1957. Received, Cable Section.

Cite: NEXI 457. Director information: CATE NEXI 457.

1. Daniel K. Bremund departed NEXI 25 October. Station requests his return with RENTISTA cover. He has full details, including return on a six-month tourist card and arrangement of RENTISTA status with local law firm, which he made without Station intervention.

2. Request increase LIFEAT funds to cover $600 U.S. duty on personal car, $350 immigration and visa fees, and $250 lawyer fee. Will also be necessary to establish $20,000 deposit in order to back cover as RENTISTA. Similar CLACMORE cover.

3. Recommend liberal travel advance in view of possible border difficulties.

4. Request cable ETA.

End of message.
