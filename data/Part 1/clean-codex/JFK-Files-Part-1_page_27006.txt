Cite: Contacts/Miami 1658.

Priority: Contacts/Washington. Attention: INT-DIV (Mellen). Country: Haiti. Subject: MIMI 19706 — false reports of invasion/reported change of plans. Date of information: 22 November 1966. Date acquired: 22 November 1966. Contact: Mitchell L. Werbell, Powder Springs, Georgia, U.S. national, soldier-of-fortune type with wide experience in Latin America. He is closely associated with Rolando Masperner.

This report was developed by a Navy representative assigned to the Office of Preparation.

1. As of 22 November 1966, in spite of widespread press reports, no invasion of Haiti has taken place.

2. Rolando Masperner (Masperner) Rojas SOC is sponsoring the alleged invasion and has been telling Cuban exiles that his group has secured the airport at Port-de-Paix and that they should be prepared to leave at any time.

3. Masperner has given up the idea of moving his group, which consists of approximately 500 Cubans and 100 Haitians, by SKAT and is trying to make arrangements for chartering eleven aircraft.
