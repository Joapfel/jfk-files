4. Also, that day, Alberto Velamute, pro-Communist director of the University of Michael Konn, was planning, along with another unidentified professor, to leave for Habana on 22 July to attend the celebrations.

5. On 26 July the Cuban counselor reserved passages for ten Costa Ricans on Cubana's flight of 27 July. Joan Duch Collell, writer and member of the Central Committee of the Partido Comunista Mexicano (PCH — Mexican Communist Party), told Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez, second secretary of the Cuban Embassy, when he was to see him on 23 July, that he had been put on the waiting list for 24 July, but it was certain that he could leave on 25 July. Daniel Molina, of Politica, Mexican Marxist semi-monthly magazine, also had tickets to leave on 25 July. (Field comment: It has been previously reported that Molina was to attend the celebrations either for Politica or as a delegate of the Movimiento de Liberación Nacional (MLN — National Liberation Movement), pro-Cuba, anti-United States Communist front.)

Fernandez asked Jardon if Carlos Catalan Fuentes, reporter for the agency, had his visa ready.
