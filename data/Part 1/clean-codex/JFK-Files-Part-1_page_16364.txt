OSIG: Stephen I. Munger. Unit: WR/4/X. ET: 5559. Date: 13 June 1963. No index. Classified message. To: Rome. From: Director. CONF: WE 6. INFO: EOP, CI/OPS, CCS 2, SAS 8, S/C.

To: Rome. Keyway Perumen AMWHIP. Reference: Rome 9337 (IN 55801).

Info.

1. No tracks Karin Espinosa 06.

2. Many tracks her husband Miguel Angel Espinosa Bravo 06. Born 29 September 1962, Havana; former Cuban (Curan) ambassador to Norway. Diplomatic career began in 1931 when assigned Rome (Bome) as third secretary. Later served Madrid, ERUS, Berlin, HEXI. Told ODACID officer in January 1960 could no longer support Castro government but did not intend to resign for economic reasons. Arrived Buenos Aires in May 1961 as Cuban exile and became member of anti-Castro group there. Other information available if desired by Rome station.

End of message.

AMWHIP/1 Rome. Latter offered use of new business cover and said had hired Karin Espinosa 06.

SAS/DOD — Mr. Armstrong, coordinating officer in draft. WR/8 — Mr. Sherman C/W/E/S. C/ME/INT. Releasing office.
