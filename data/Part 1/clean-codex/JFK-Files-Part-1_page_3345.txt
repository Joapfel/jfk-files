Classified message. Routing. To: Director. From: JMWAVE. Action: DC/WH/SA 3. Info: DDP, VR.

241548Z. DIR CITE WAVE 8396. RANMED. YPIC YOBITE AMWORLD.

24 June 64. IN 78641.

1. 22 June AMYUM/19 had meeting with AMJAVA/4 and AMBIDDY/1 at latter's request to resolve leadership crisis within AMYUM which had potentially serious implications for AMWORLD. A/I admitted having control problems in camps and said he finally understood what A/19 had been complaining about. As result discussion A/19 believes A/I convinced A/19 not attempting usurp his position in AMYUM and will permit A/19 assume real control over activities in P8PRIME (including authority over international and propaganda activities), in return A/19 to use his good offices resolve personnel problems in camps.

2. According A/19 recent analysis, which being forward HGS, basic problem is growing belief among AMYUM activists and intelligence that A/I using them for personal ambitions and that A/I exhibiting totalitarian leanings which unacceptable AMYUM.

3. While accepting necessity military discipline ops matters, AMYUM leadership, which more concerned furthering ideals than serving as machine—to substitute one periodic leader for other.

Deemed A/machinely substituting one periodic leader for other, please A/I, in effort capture control of organization for self.
