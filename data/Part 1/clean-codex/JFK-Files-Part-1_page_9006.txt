Classified message.

Date: 17 July 1961. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: BELL-10 (TEMPO copy slotted for BELL at 0545, 18 July). Info: COP, ADDP/A, CCG 2, CA 2, CA/PRG, CA/PROP WH 5.

18 July 0553Z 1961. Priority: Fairly high. IN 40077.

FRITY TRUJ DIR CITE MEXI 8435

1. Due previous business/travel commitments, TICHBORN unable make ref trip until last week July. Willing to go DOMREP that time but feels tourist cover too light for extended TRUJ visit to obtain sitrep in depth as requested, particularly in view smallness and tight security of target city.

2. FYI per MEXI-8235, LIBELER still attempting make cover arrangements for DOMREP trip. MEXI newspapers refused grant him cover documentation as special reporter, but it now possible he able arrange cover backstopping and interviews/entreés with top DOMREP officials using his past background as Mexican independent writer and book author of LA POLITICAL ACTIVITIES AND PERSONALITIES. L expects final cover and travel arrangements within next seven days.

3. STA attempting finalize LITEA-1 DOMREP assignment per MEXI-8416.

4. In view T's cover limitations per paragraph 1 above, advise soonest HQS views re desirability T make trip.

Requests: HQS requirements indicated reference for possible use TICHBORN, LIBELER, and LITEA-1 travel to DOMREP.

End of message.

C/S comment: Dissemination applicable to DYVOUR 4 cables.
