Classified message. Message No. 532. Date: 15 February 1961. To: BELL. From: WAVE. Action: BELL 15. Info: COP, ADDN/A, WH 3, BELL S/C. CI info. 15 February 2347Z, 1961. Priority. IN 0044.

Priority. Cite WAVE 3691. JNZIP/ANDIP-3/ANHAZE.

1. ANDIP-3 courier arrived WAVE area from HAVA 14 February.

Informed A-3 he on wanted list and due to security incident his radio link temporarily cut off.

2. Several days ago A-3 S/H raided by G2. House located near 15th Ave and 198 St, BILMORE, HABANA, and occupied by Andrea (ADREA) “Louisa” GIQUEL. Picked up and detained 48 hours. House contained one Viking II radio, two hand generators-GN58, A-3 codes and message files; none found during search, which apparently accounts for GIQUEL release.

3. Agent 2515 dispatched to HABANA legally on 12 February with orders to contact GIQUEL about 14 or 15 February and join A-3 movement line to ESCAMBRAY as W/T operator for Osvaldo Ramirez group.

4. Due to GIQUEL incident, ZABOTH instructed Agent 2517, who departed WAVE legally for HABANA on 14 February, to contact 2515 on arrival and warn him to avoid contact with A-3 group and lay low until he is given further instructions by S/W.

5. WAVE and A-3 consider it essential to establish secure communications between WAVE and A-3 groups. Propose dispatch AMJAVA-4 by Black Sea infiltration to Cuba for this purpose. Infiltration planned.
