Henry P. Lopez

From Druon de C] /OA, from Deputy, Leciton g Security (J-15), dated 10 Sept. 59.

1. Three developed informants commented favorably regarding Suley's character, reputation, and loyalty. One informant stated Suley had been married three times and that his present marriage had almost ended in divorce recently.

2. Suley was fined $15 for a 1954 traffic violation and his subsequent failure to appear at the hearing. One informant advised Suley had recently been the subject of investigations by local police officers, believed to have resulted from an improper parking ticket.

3. Subject's name appears numerous times in subversive files on the West Coast.

These items contain Civil Air information regarding his activities in his Democratic minority conference and list his professional associates. In 1949 he was listed as a member of the Bar Committee against loyalty orders for lawyers. He was also an associate.
