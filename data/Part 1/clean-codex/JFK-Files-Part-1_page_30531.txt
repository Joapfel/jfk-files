Dispatch No. JCA-183.

To: Chief of Mission, FRU/FEC; Chief of Administration. From: CO, Sapporo, Japan.

Subject: General—Administrative. Specific—Indigenous Personnel. Date: 4 May 1951.

1. On 7 April 1951, KOMATA, Kazuhiko terminated his employment as houseboy for Fnu Sapporo and proceeded to Yokosuka to work for Magnin.

2. On 8 April 1951, SATO, Hiroshi (前田浩二), recommended by KOMATA, was employed by writer. SATO has been employed by Occupation Forces since May 1949 and was working at Northern Command Motor Pool prior to his employment by writer. He was given a very good verbal recommendation by Lt. Col. Reinking, 7th Division Chaplain, for whom he worked as houseboy from November 1949 to March 1950.

3. At the request of Writer, SATO was given a check by 6th CIC, Sapporo Area, for possible CP affiliation, which was found to be negative.

4. His salary will be 12,000 yen per month.
