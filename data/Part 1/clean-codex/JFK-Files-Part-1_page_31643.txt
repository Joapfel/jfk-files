Executive Summary. 4-3-67.

7 July 1953.

Noted by: DJ. 7/13/67.

Memorandum for: Director, Central Intelligence Agency (Central Intelligentive Agency).

Subject: Proposed Balloon Operation.

1. The following message was received from Whitney Shepardson on the proposed balloon operation.

Shepardson talked at length with Conant yesterday, Monday. Conant did not object to the proposed balloon operation, but on the contrary “endorses” the project, but said he could not “request” acceptance. Thus Shepardson says we have “improved upon our minimum expectations.”

Shepardson says further that Adenauer has been away since last Saturday but that Lentz and Hallstein were to have returned last night and MCCS is trying to arrange an appointment with either or both for today, Tuesday.

Shepardson will send further word after these talks.

Thomas W. Heldermann  
Chief  
International Organizations Division
