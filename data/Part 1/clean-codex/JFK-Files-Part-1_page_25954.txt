Outgoing Message.

Staff. Conf: CA 10. Info: File L, Alysa L. Date: 7/2/85. Cname: Director. Message reference number: 705055.

To: Caracas, info Santo Domingo.

Ref: Director 704047.

1. Source of ref furnished following additional info 3 June 75: According to Armando Lopez Estrada, Rolando Otero is in Caracas as a “pathfinder” or advance man for group of Cuban exiles who are to arrive in Caracas by July 1975 to explore possibility of an assassination attempt against Fidel Castro Ruz, who is expected to be in Caracas for a conference of Latin American heads of state in July 1975. This plan may be a joint effort by members of FLNC, Accion Cubana (anti-Castro organization of Cuban exiles led by Orlando Bosch (Bo S Coh), Cuban exile terrorist, who is parole violator believed to be in hiding in South America), and Brigade 2506 (organization of Bay of Pigs veterans) with whom Lopez Estrada is friendly. Otero is trying to obtain info regarding Fidel Castro’s visit and security measures planned to protect him. Otero recently spent several days in Colombia before returning to Venezuela where he is being closely watched by Venezuelan authorities under direction of Orlando.
