RASK, Walter. 06.

NOSENKO'S INFORMATION

RASK, the president of World Wide Tours, was recruited in the USSR in 1960. He has returned to the USSR several times since. He is used for spotting tourists suspected of having intelligence affiliations. The KGB has received only one open code message from RASK since recruitment. RASK was recruited by Seventh Department case officer Ye. N. NOSKOV while NOSENKO was assigned to the American Department. On subsequent visits to the USSR he was met by Seventh Department case officers of the American-British Commonwealth Section.
