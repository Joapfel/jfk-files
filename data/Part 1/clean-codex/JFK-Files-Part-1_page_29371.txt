Note for File

Subject: Irving K. Devuono (P)

Paul Seidel (ROB) called Mr. Brooks on 7 September 1971 to advise him that the DGI had approved the recommendation for Mr. Devuono's involuntary retirement and that it was all right to release the separation amendment providing for a $10,000 terminal payment that we had been holding.

Amendment released to DIV for subject's sign on 7 September 1971.

OP/CPD Rita
