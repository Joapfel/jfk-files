Return of Documents and List of Birthdates for ARTIKI (AMTIKI) Files

Paragraph 3.

References: A. UFGA-26504, dated 8 December 1968. B. UFGA-26626, dated 15 December 1968.

1. Forwarded under separate cover as attachment A are the replies received in references from AMTIKI. It is requested that these letters be returned to AMTIKI for retention in individual subject files.

2. It is recommended that AMTIKI assume responsibility for reviewing the status of minor dependents in order to automatically terminate payments to those individuals who become 21 years of age. This should be done on a regular 30-day basis since many of these dependents will become ineligible for payment during 1967. A list of birthdates compiled by Ellen P. Margany is forwarded under separate cover as attachment B to assist AMTIKI in monitoring this operation. Some procedure should also be established by AMTIKI for periodically attempting to establish proof of marriages contracted by dependents.

3. A quarterly station report is requested on all future payment terminations in order that Headquarters' records may be maintained on a current basis.

Ellen P. Margany

Distribution: COS/JENNAVE; WH/COG/ESEC; WH/COG/MO; RID.
