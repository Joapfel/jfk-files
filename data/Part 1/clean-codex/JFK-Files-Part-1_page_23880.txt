Dispatch. Processing. Proposed. Action. Allocation pushed.

To: Marked for indexing. Info.: Chief, WE; no processing required. From: Daily qualified headquarters desk; CAR judge indexing. Subject: KEDESK/LOCHARVEST—Debriefing of Richard T. GIBSON. Abstract.

1. Transmitted herewith are the tapes of the 17, 18, 19 and 23 February sessions with GIBSON. Also transmitted is a copy of the Station-prepared transcript of the tapes. The quality of the transcription varies: the first session is mostly summarized, the subsequent ones are almost verbatim. Roberta L. HORRELL did the transcripts, and I subsequently reviewed them, filling in comments, names, etc., that HORRELL had missed.

2. As rough as these transcripts are, a careful review of them is fairly revealing about GIBSON, and about his knowledge of the topics discussed. Further comment from this end will be contained in an operational dispatch. Right now we are anxious to get the present material into Headquarters' hands for further processing. This is about as far as we can take the material here, due to extreme time limitations. We are retaining a copy of the transcript, however, for further use and future reference.

Attachments: Herewith.

Distributions: L-J-Chief, WE w/att h/w; 1-CC; Attachment X filed c/c 20 marks; restacked; 5/9/67 attached; Latti.
