On 3 January 1936, ISAACS visited Mr. and Mrs. Nolls (NOLLS) while they were on a hunger strike in prison.

On 14 January 1936, the parents of ISAACS, Mr. and Mrs. I. H. ISAACS, arrived in Shanghai from Los Angeles. During their stay in Shanghai, they resided at the Metropolitan Hotel Annex. On 20 February, they left Shanghai for Port Said (Fort Said), en route to Marseilles on the S.S. Rawalpindi (33 Rawalpindi).

On 4 April 1936, ISAACS, under the pen name of Lo Sen, had an article published in the New Militant, the organ of the BY District Workers’ Party. The article was an attack on Stalin and was entitled “A Stalintern Ready to Embrace Chiang Kai-shek’s (Chian Kai Shek’s) Officer (O’icer) Chinese Red Army in Return for Resignation of Disastrous (disasterous) 1927 Bloodiest.” At this same time, ISAACS was also lecturing on Japan, China, and the USSR (USCR) and the War Crisis in the Far East.
