2204352, October 1969. Cite Caracas 3864. Reference: Caracas 3859 (IN 70765).

1. CIWOOD intelligence section chief F. has asked Nikolako P. Hart to be assigned if there is an arrest order by James Hart in Miami and, if so, for what reason? On questioning, he advised Hart that Haycock may have left for Miami from Naturin night of 21 October. We advised CIWOOD that we have no information about an arrest order since Hart apparently has broken no U.S. law but, due to his known involvement in January Kupunuri incident, it is possible FBI or Customs may wish to question him in Miami. Request WH/Miami agency ask if an arrest order does exist or if any government agency plans to interview.

2. CIWOOD’s query and knowledge of Hart’s activities strongly indicate he is cooperating with CIWOOD. Reference information indicates he is being investigated by CIVISTA-5 group, which has sent AGENCY-15 to Miami to investigate activities of Richard Hart. Coordination between Venezuelan services being what it is, a situation like this is entirely possible.

23 October 1969
