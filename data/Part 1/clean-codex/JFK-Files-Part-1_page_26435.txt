TDCS. JAN 24, 10 24 PM ’64. IN 07185.

Have identified Rico Galan as an agent of Fidel Castro, who in May 1963 was given an assignment by Castro to visit Central and South American countries to confer with leading Communists in order to initiate programs of anti-US violence and sabotage. Since October 1963 Rico Galan has visited Venezuela, Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Guatemala, Brazil and Panama.
