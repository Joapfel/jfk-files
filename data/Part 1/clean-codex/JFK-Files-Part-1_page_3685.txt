Source: V. LUCI. March 1963. Manuel Artime Buesa, Cuban exile leader, flew from Miami, Florida, to Managua, Nicaragua, on invitation of Artime and S. Rene Schick. Artime's express purpose for making the visit was to determine if Schick would support his (Artime's) army, Castro paramilitary campaign by making military training stages and operational bases available to Artime.

Statement: 2. “Artime also said he hoped to determine whether Schick was an extension of the Somoza dynasty or whether Schick was a true step toward democratic government in Nicaragua.”
