3 August ’67

Three-page typewritten letter and 27/10/67.

1. LIAING/3 received a letter from René Cohna Firmers (Rivier) and 8/27x, but it reached Mexico City on 2/2/67 by “N.C., Carrera P. Janeiro 100, Mexico 7, LF” after having been hand-carried from Havana by the wife of a “compañero de trabajo” of Cohna. Copy being punched.

2. In the letter, Cohna states he understands L/3’s situation “since it is necessary to move ahead with one’s own resources and to prove with deeds.” He adds that he hoped to send “concrete and complete news,” but he is still laboring under difficulties known to L/3. He assures L/3 that he delivered L/3’s last letter to Arnulfo, the “compañero” (Arnol Rodriguez), but could not talk with him privately because of the many visitors who had arrived that day. Arnol’s wife, Ruby, told him that they had received L/3’s letters and had answered them. L/3 told Rowton that he has never received these replies from Arnol. Ruby Rodriguez also informed Cohna that the “organizaciones correspondientes” were going to invite L/3 to Havana. Cohna promised L/3 that he would do whatever he could to expedite this trip (“hacer nuevas gestiones”).

3. Cohna complains of the “realidades que se han tocado vivir acorde,” but indicates that he has no intention of resigning himself to bureaucracy, commenting that it is necessary to “producir la ruptura, ayudando a rascarse con las propias uñas,” taking the initiative and not expecting anything from anyone. He adds that he has finally cut the ties which bound him to Cuba and, although he feels that he can do a great deal there, it is “infinitely small in comparison with what he must do outside.” He assures L/3 that he is serious and that it concerns a “planteamiento serio.”
