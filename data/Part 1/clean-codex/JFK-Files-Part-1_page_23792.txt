To: Chief, KODESK  
From: Chief of Station, London  
Attachment to CELL-43,353

16 June 1965

26 May, continued.

16.40 hours: Mrs. Sarah Gibson (ph), of the Press Department, Tunisian Embassy, calls Cuban Embassy and explains to Margo that she wants Pablo to send, as previously discussed, some copies of “Revolucion Pueblo y Cultura” and “Lunes de Revolucion,” either on temporary loan or, if some copies can be spared, for retention by the Tunisian Embassy. The Press Secretary in the Tunisian Embassy is on the point of leaving to return to Tunis on 26 May and, before doing so, very much wants to look at those papers. Margo promises to arrange this matter with Pablo, for delivery of copies to the Press Department, Tunisian Embassy, 29 Prince’s Gate. Sarah Gibson asks for delivery today if possible, or otherwise the following morning.

16.50 hours: Pablo calls Mrs. Gibson at III 4133 on Christian-name terms, asks how her husband, Dick, and the children are, and confirms that he will let her have some copies of “Ruega” (ph), given to him by a Cuban in London, and also “Pueblo y Cultura,” but has no copies of “Revolucion.” He confirms that he will come to Sarah’s house on Saturday afternoon, 29 May, with Karuja and the children if the weather is fine, but will come anyhow, as he wants to have a talk with Sarah. The Gibsons’ home number is PRI 8778. Sarah remarks that the Tunisian Press Secretary is being sent back to Tunisia to take charge of a newspaper, and he has been most impressed by the Cuban layout in their publications. He needs the copies to give him ideas for his own work, and Sarah wants the influence to be Cuban.
