Source: Pan-American revolutionary leader (F), with good contacts in Cuban-oriented leftist circles of Panama.

Victor de Coco Alonso (Deco Alonso), national minister for foreign affairs, arrived in Panama from Costa Rica on 20 October and left Punta Norte for Caracas (Carrickers) on 26 October 1963.

Deco Alonso is a writer on cigarettes and politics for Mexican magazines and is head of the propaganda committee for the National Liberation Movement (MLN), a committee more notable if widely supported by those of the Mexican Communist Party. In October 1963, Deco Alonso left for Cuba to write a political biography of Fidel Castro (Fide Castro). In May 1963, we agreed to leave, entering Mexico 22 June 1963. During this latter visit he reportedly interviewed Castro after Castro's return from the Soviet Union and also spent a five-day sea-going vacation with Castro, on which he is said to be a close friend. “Rico de Leon.”
