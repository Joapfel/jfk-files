Dispatch. To: Chief, JM/AVE. Info: Chief, Soviet Bloc Division. From: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Subject: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires; RYBAT RESTOP AERONAUT AETARGET; Letter for AMSTALK-1's Wife. Action required: RETERMINES.

1. Forwarded under cover, please find a letter written by AMSTALK-1 for delivery to his wife. AMSTALK-1 tells us (we certainly agree) that he would prefer for JM/AVE to mail the letter rather than trusting the international mail from here.

2. Incidentally, to date we are highly pleased with AMSTALK-1. We greatly appreciate JM/AVE's assistance in the AERONAUT operation.

Edwin W. SHROYER.

USC attachment.

Distribution: 2 — C, JM/AVE with secret attachment; 1 — C, CBD without attachment; 1 — C, WND without attachment.

Date: 26 April 1968. Attention: SMWH. 74 (c) 70-221. HABA-19, 5/66.
