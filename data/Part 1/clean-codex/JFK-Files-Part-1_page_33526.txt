9. Internally, the elimination of price controls and adjustment of exchange rate has practically eliminated the once flourishing black markets in commodities and foreign exchange. However, the burden of austerity on Chilean consumers has been heavy. Despite healthy increases in wages and renewed confidence by businessmen, working class consumers find it difficult to afford the barest necessities, and working class labor is taking a wait-and-see attitude. There is, however, no organized resistance to the junta or its programs. Some political factions, particularly the Christian Democratic Party (PDC), are grumbling, but this dissatisfaction is largely due to government efforts to set up new non-political-party mechanisms for reaching the public.
