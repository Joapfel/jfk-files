28 January 1968: P.O.A. requested on subject for use as DIEGPOL penetration but cancelled because of his involvement in clandestine sabotage (sabatage) activities and failure to report complete stories of his activities. Subject handled as hostile until his motives completely ascertained.

22 May 1968: Polygraphed. Purpose: to determine conclusively that he broke off completely with Lefty Rosenthal group and that he was not currently involved in any illegal activity which could cause embarrassment to the U.S. Government; 1) status of his relationship with his brothers; 2) degree of his involvement with the theft and disposal of demolition and other sabotage (sabatog) devices; 3) past illegal activities or association with gangster elements that made him vulnerable to blackmail; 4) that he was not engaged in activities hostile to the U.S. Government. Subject was willing and eager to take SWIRL. Passed SWIRL exam 23 May with satisfactory clarification on all points of concern.

7–12 August 1968: Attended Latin American student conference at Mérida.

17 January 1969: Subject returned to Caracas from Miami—unclear in 201.

29 August 1969: Subject ordered by CIVISTA to make surreptitious (surrepitious) entry of Guayana Embassy in Caracas to get “any info of interest.”

19 October 1969: Left Caracas for Miami to investigate purchase of $300,000 worth of weapons by Richard Hart.

17 November 1969: Subject making efforts to preserve rights to become a U.S. citizen while becoming a citizen of Venezuela, which might be necessary for operational reasons, i.e., to maintain his high position in the government. Sought Station’s help, which, through Headquarters, was able to solve the problem.

18 March 1970: Subject and family in Miami to process application for U.S. re-entry permits. Returned to Caracas 20 March.

14 April 1971: Subject sent by CIFENCE service to Lima to assemble a bomb to be sent to former Venezuelan dictator Pérez Jiménez (Perez Jimenez). Subject requested Lima Station’s help in the mission. Station recommended against it.
