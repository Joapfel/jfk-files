L-9 said he was into LILAC-3 on the street and L-3 has been trying to talk with him. L-9 said he is embarrassed and asked about the propriety of getting in touch with him. He was told to go ahead but to discuss only the amenities of life and not get into detailed discussion, to which he agreed heartily.

L-9 explained how he requested leave without pay, etc., through Manuel VELA, but this is not allowed out in documents provided to us on 9 and 10 November. Copies of all these documents regarding L-9's departure, photos, etc., will be copied.

12. Carlos FRANQUEO — L-9 knows nothing about FRANQUEO but did recall that when Wilfredo LUM, colored painter, arrived in Habana for the TRIEN, FRANQUEO was at the airport and in an aside told LUM that he should avoid getting involved with Marta FRAILE because she was anti-regime and anti-Castro.

13. Marta FRAILE — FRAILE, according to L-9, is known to be anti-CASTRO and makes no bones about it.

14. Fidel CASTRO — Castro, according to L-9, is in trouble but his security forces keep things under control, imposing a curfew after dark. Castro says, according toovichita FRIARTEZ, that he has to depend on his young scholarship students. He can't trust anyone else. He is obviously turning toward sugar after having turned toward industry, etc. Graciela BISEE demonstrated a bit of anti-CASTRO feeling when, at the 24 July fiesta, the crowds applauded CASTRO. BISEE commented in a sarcastic tone: “Aplandiran,” “splandiran.” L-9 recalls that CASTRO was operated on for rectum trouble but does not recall when. He believes Dr. RODRIGUEZ Diaz was the surgeon.
