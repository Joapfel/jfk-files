Dispatch. Processing action: marked for indexing. To: Chief, VI Division. No inquiring required. Only qualified desk can adobe indexing. From: Acting Chief of Station, Mexico City. Microfilm. Subject: Raymond H. GERENDE'S (GENEDE'S) contract. Action required—references. Reference: HINS-6090, 19 August 1968.

Please extend GENEDE's contract, without amendments, for one year. We had hoped to have answers to HINS-5971 prepared in time to have amendments included in the annual extension, but GENEDE'S “cover company” has recently undergone a change of management, and we have been waiting for GENEDE to provide us with a written statement of his current cover status, as well as a statement of the retirement rights and benefits provided legitimate employees of the cover company. Our intention is to have GENEDE's contract provide, in so far as possible, termination benefits equivalent to those granted by his cover company to a normal employee. As soon as this information is available, a comprehensive reply to HINS-5971 will be moved.

For/Bernard A. EGGERBOOK.
