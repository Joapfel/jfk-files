Classified message. Routing. March 20, 1959. Priority. IN 34410. Cite: Paris 5409. To DIR. Info: FRAN FROB. REDWOOD REDSOX AEACRE. RE DIR 17554 (OUT 83806).

1. Friganza contacted AEASPIC in Ambert. Persuaded him to come to Paris without police permission. He arriving evening 21 March. Can remain two or three days.

2. Have secure site house and test agent.

3. Send operator.

End of message.
