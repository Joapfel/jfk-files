H. Plans

1. The Station plans to continue to exploit LIONICN-1's experience, training and location to provide the photographic and technical coverage cited above. In addition, the Station will continue to make use of LIONICN-1 as a dispatcher in surveillances of LIRING personnel.

2. In order to check the LISAMPAN/DOG take for items of possible value to other Station operations, LISAMPAN/1 will be instructed to record the DOG traffic for the three week period while LIONION-1 is on vacation. These tapes will be given to Richard C. HABUSH for an analysis of their operational worth and technical quality. HADQUISH will transcribe them if the contents warrant.
