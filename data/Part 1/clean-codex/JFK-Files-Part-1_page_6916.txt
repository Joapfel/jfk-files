Classified message.

Origin: Stephen Millett. Unit: CI/SPD. Extension: 2831. Date: 20 October 1958.

To: Tel Aviv. From: Director. Confidential: C/CI/SP 3. Info: COP, S/C 2.

Routing. Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.

OUT 85727

To: Tela

RYBAT RHYTON

KKCOOT RJ-1340

Cite DIR 6465.

1. Maurice Halperin and wife, American Communists suspected Soviet agents, left Mexico City KLM flight 988 for Curacao, Lisbon KLM flight 722 on 13 October.

2. KLM advises reservations made in name Mr. and Mrs. Daniel Pastor and late on 11 October call from MEXI American Travel Agency advises Pastors had cancelled, requested substitute Mr. and Mrs. Maurice Halperin.

3. Halperins on waiting list Swiss Air from Lisbon to Zurich for 15 October and KLM advises telephonic request by “Mr. Pastor” asked price round-trip ticket Zurich to Tela.

4. Probably Halperins plan join Alfred Farran Kaufman and Martha Dodd Stern in Prague. Reservation technique same as Halperin used when he made plans for Sterns flee to Soviet Union.
