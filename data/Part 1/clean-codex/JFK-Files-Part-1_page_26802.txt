He asked Lielegant to include Orfila Reynal in his study of “denaturalization possibilities” and said if denaturalized, Orfila Reynal would be “returned to his native Argentina.” He had asked that Salvador Azuela (Abzuela), who was favorably known by Station, be put in as director of FCE. He added that now, however, people would think Orfila Reynal was fired because FCE published “The Children of Sanchez” by Oscar (Oscarr) Lewis, which has created a great deal of adverse Mexican comment.

C. Concerning the newspaper Excelsior (which Lielegant raised), LIRAMA said he did not believe the fight was between pro- and anti-Communists. He said there were some “too far right,” including one or two members of “Los Tecos,” a right-wing Catholic group. He believed the fight for control of Excelsior would come out all right.

D. LIRAMA then mentioned that Manuel Espinosa Iglesias and Gabriel Alarcon, who founded “El Heraldo,” a new Mexican daily of the “right,” have claimed they have LIRAMA’s backing. He said he had been advised but was not involved. He described Alarcon as a “contrabandista” and gangster.

E. Security procedures in LIRAMA’s office were discussed briefly.
