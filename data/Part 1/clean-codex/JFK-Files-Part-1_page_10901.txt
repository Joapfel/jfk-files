Project: MP. Date of information: late March 64. Field No.: Report RDM-3706. Source: LITANIL9. Date acquired: 1 Apr 64. Cable in. Subject: Plans of the Algerian Government to Establish Diplomatic Relations with Latin American Countries. Field distribution date: 14 Apr 64. Grade: 8a. Reports officer: F.S./jet. Eval requested: C S M.D. area Mexico/Cuba/Algeria.

Comments: This document is inaccurate. Possibly “open embassies” was meant, rather than “establish diplomatic relations.” Both Spain and Brazil have ambassadors in Algiers, and have had for some time. Algeria has announced that it planned to open embassies in several countries, including Brazil and Argentina.
