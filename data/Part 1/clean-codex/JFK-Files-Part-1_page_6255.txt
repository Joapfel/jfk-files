"LIBERATED BY THE COMMUNISTS"  
Graphic Evidence of Marxist Lies About the Actual Events in the Congo.

The missionary Paul Carlson lies dead with a card tied to his throat, assassinated by Congolese communists on a street in Stanleyville. Dr. Carlson, in long and difficult years full of sacrifice, brought a message of hope and encouragement to innumerable Congolese as well as help of all kinds and his personal collaboration. He was eliminated tragically, as shown in the photograph, because of the affection that the masses held for him, and because of his fame and prestige and the adverse influence that he signified for the Communists. He had committed the sin of doing whatever was necessary to help the sick and console the afflicted, and the Reds do not pardon such things. In their final orgy of blood the Communists respected neither women nor children. These were very dangerous "military spies" according to the Communists. These photographs are the facts that the propaganda of the Castro Government has suppressed in an attempt to present the assassins of missionaries, women and children as "heroes" and "champions of liberty".
