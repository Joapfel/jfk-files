Subject: Contact Report on SLANK-1 (S-1).

1. S-1 was contacted on the evening of 27 February at his Miami home, 2761 SW 24th Terrace, telephone 43-0155, regarding his availability to approach his old friend, Roberto RODRIGUEZ Llompart.

3. S-1 is presently employed as a bus driver for the Metro Transit Authority, 3300 NW 32nd Avenue, Miami, telephone 633-9735.

The persons who must grant permission are, in this order of rank: H. I. CROSS; B. L. WALLACE; K. C. BUTLER.

5. I have again thanked him for his sincere and loyal cooperation. I have promised to keep him abreast of developments.

Henry S. PUGH
