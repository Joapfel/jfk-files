26 June 1964

Memorandum for: Chief, CI/OA

Subject: Amendment of LICOCKY-1 Operational Approval

It is requested that the Operational Approval for LICOCKY-1 (LI-COOKY-1) be amended to include authority for Mexico Station to use her on a one-time basis as an access asset against a Soviet newspaperman, who will be visiting Mexico and who has been a target for defection of Die de Janelle Staffon. (See MEXI 0021 (IN 14984) and preceding traffic.)

W. J. Kaufman  
C/WH/1
