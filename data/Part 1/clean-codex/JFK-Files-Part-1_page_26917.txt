Date: 31 July 1967

Instruction to: Chief, Miami Field Office  
Municipal: Director, Domestic Contact Service (Services Division)  
Subject: Security Check Completion — U.S. Citizen  
Resume: (a) DCS R 10-30, dated 1 July 1965; (b) Operations Memo 88-64

Appended is a notice of security check completion on the individual named below. The OS approved Subject's exploitation as a source of intelligence information, but the level of classified information which may be disclosed is left to your discretion.

2. As indicated in the attachment, the OS provided a summary of derogatory information for you to consider in determining the level of classified disclosure. It will be forwarded by Services Division by an “eyes only” memorandum which will bear a number identical to the one marked on the lower left corner of the completion. Please use this number to identify a summary with an individual.

3. The OS directs that the field chief, after reading the information, shall use it to establish the level of classified disclosure, if any, and to provide guidance to the Contact Specialist in his relations with the source. It is noted that, at his discretion, he may disclose the nature of the derogatory information to the Contact Specialist. The field chief, after making an appropriate decision in the above files of the office with respect to the security status of the individual, will then either decrease the information or place it in a special envelope which will carry the exemption: “To be opened by the Chief or Acting Chief — Field Office.”

JOHNSON, MITCHELL, LAMBERT, III

Classification: “Restricted” — No classified information to be revealed. Check with Mr. Gleichauf before contacting.
