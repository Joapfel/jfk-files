Chief of Station, Paris

Chief, SR through Chief, WE

Information: COB, Germany; COB, Frankfurt.

RED-OAK REDBACK AEGRE AEPADDLE debriefing.

Action required:

Attached for the information of Paris and Frankfurt is a copy of the debriefing of AEPADDLE. The Paris station has already received some of this information directly from AEPADDLE, but there was probably not enough time for him to pass all of it to Platipodis during their meetings. There are a number of items that the field stations may find of use, but only one person named appears to have any genuine REDS-X potential. This is David BATESON/WILI (cf. IV of Attachment No. 2).

Pros: No wife or children; not living in large Georgian colony; no occupation; during, initiative.

Cons: On parole; relatives in Georgia; possible émigré surveillance; difficult to approach without attracting attention.

Request Paris comments on feasibility of further efforts—possibility of finding out more about crimes for which Subject and AIM were sentenced; also about nature of parole and possibility of having it lifted.

Michael RAE  
John O. PHILLIPSBURG

28 April 1958. SA/DOB/McNulty.

Attachments: 1. Bona Fides (1 copy). 2. Debriefing (2 copies), under separate cover.
