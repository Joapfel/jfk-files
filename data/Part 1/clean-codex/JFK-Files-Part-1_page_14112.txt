24 October 1967. N78360.

J/CCS-3, C/CSPC, C/DA-1, C/DPSC.

Secret 241842Z. CITE CARACAS 8124.

Director. Info: JMWAVE, DO, Base New York.

RYBAT AKULE PERS.

Ref: JMWAVE 9868 (JN77993).

1. Caracas hoped to have DIGERVENO on duty by about 1 January 1968, since this would have provided sufficient time for cover training and the timing would have been good for school transfers of his children.

2. While would still like to adhere to timetable originally envisaged, believed it important that as much time as necessary be spent on cover training to insure that this phase of the operation is successful.

3. Suggest headquarters and JMWAVE set a date for release of DIGERVENO from PBRUMEN duties so that he can thereafter spend full time preparing for Caracas assignment. Realize importance AM CLOCK I operation, and if DIGERVENO's presence continues to be essential to that operation, suggest preparations be made to release DIGERVENO from PBRUMEN duties about 1 January; that training be conducted with due deliberation to insure thoroughness; and that consideration be secret.
