Fisk, Norman

Nosenko's Information

Fisk visited the USSR as a tourist in 1959, when he was assessed to be a homosexual. The KGB planned to attempt to recruit him if he returned to the Soviet Union. Nosenko noted Fisk's name while reviewing files of Yefremov and Volkov (see p. 145) in 1963.

Comment: Fisk visited the USSR with his wife and parents in 1959. In an interview with the FBI in December 1964, he claimed that his only Soviet contacts in the USSR were with Inturist personnel and a spinster niece. Nosenko has never explained why he learned of the case only from the files of Volkov and Yefremov in 1963, since he claims he was their case officer at the time Fisk visited the Soviet Union, in 1959.

Ginsberg, Michael

Nosenko's Information

A professor, Ginsberg (Ginsderg), visited the USSR in 1957 or 1958, when operational contact was established between him and the KGB.
