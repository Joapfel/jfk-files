Office Memorandum, United States Government

To: Mr. Byron C. Sarnis  
Chief, Procurement Section

From: Personnel Officer, CIG

Subject: Memorandum re: Lt. Col. Al R. Wichtrich (Inactive)

Date: 6 September 1946

The attached memorandum of 30 August 1945 from Kingman Douglass, with regard to Colonel Wichtrich, is referred to you for consideration.

Since it would appear that Colonel Wichtrich would be of more value to S.O., it is forwarded for consideration by officials in that Branch.

This correspondence has been recorded in the Central Records of CIG, and I should be advised if you desire to retain it in your files.

Judson H. Lightbay  
Personnel Officer, CIG

Attachment

cc: Central Records, CIG (2)
