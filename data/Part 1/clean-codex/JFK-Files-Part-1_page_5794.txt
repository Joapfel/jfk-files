9–10 Jan. 1962

I have been informed by a source that may be correlated with references that an American by the name of Fau, COE, is arriving in Prague as Angels and will arrive until September (July, October) and as a tourist, and his hotel room booked beforehand by the Party and quarters to be lived in is the new hotel “International.”

During their stay there have been several conferences with the Communists. American resident in Prague, George J. WHEELER and his CIGS. COE is apparently a man in the fifties with dark hair and rather small hands, about 45 cm.

Regarding the recent U.S. defector to Cuba, Prof. Charles JURICE, who came to Prague with wife and children, everything was properly prepared before his arrival. His son Larry had a Czech letter after arrival and the hotel room had been booked.
