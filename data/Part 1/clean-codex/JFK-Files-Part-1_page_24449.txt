Dispatch. To: Chief, VE. From: Chief of Station, London. SGACD: ORLEANS JETZT CHARLES RIGGS, COSTA MESA, U.S.A. Coded: Provide traces. CHG/SGS: Louisville Special.

1. JAGUAR have reported that a secret and delicate source has provided information about a pro-Cuban revolution in Mexico is editing the English edition of a French publication entitled “African Revolution.” French editions of this paper are being published in Algiers under the title of “Revolution Africares” and the English edition is being published in Lausanne where the journal has established an office. The American editor is Charles Gibson (GIBSON), who was born in California in 1931, and was until September 1962, the Executive Secretary of an organization styled “The Volar Play for Cuba Committee in the United States.” Gibson (GIBSON) is said to have been previously employed by the CIA now in New York, who dismissed him for his pro-Cuban activities. He is also said to have worked for the F.L.N. in Paris and to have had a Fellowship in African Studies at Columbia University Graduate School at one time.

2. JAGUAR would appreciate any additional information that Headquarters could provide about this man’s background and activities.

Lawrence L. Holdy.

Distributions: Chief, VE.
