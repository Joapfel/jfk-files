1. On 19 May, Mr. Honorio I. Viray called upon KUBARK-controlled ANSCROLL (IDEN A), seeking information on Cuba refugee problems and economic problems facing Cuba. Said he was employed by Manila Times and currently director of National Press Club of Philippines, in States with a group of 15 other newsmen studying the War on Poverty, and that he became interested in refugees while passing through Miami. Said he would use information on refugees in articles he would prepare for Times after return. NAILA in July.

2. ANSCROLL briefed Viray on 20 and 21 May on Castro’s sugar difficulties, including problems Japan was having in dealing with Cuba. AMWASP-1 (IDEN S) was made available for interview on 20 May and made the point that Japan is a natural market for Philippine (PHILLIPINE) sugar. Viray took extensive notes and accepted documentation of statements. He was also briefed on a rash of Cuban merchant marine defections and the meaning of this to Castro, and ANSCROLL arranged for him to get a former ship captain who defected (CUBAILOR 13).
