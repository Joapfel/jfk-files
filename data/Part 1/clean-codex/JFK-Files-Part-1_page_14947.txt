Dispatch. Dispatch symbol and No.: OFFA-39907. To: Chief, SR. From: Chief, M; Chief of Base, Frankfurt (ATTN: Anthony H. Friganza); Chief of Station, Paris, EUTUEE. Received: Operational/FREDWOOD/FREDSON/AEACRE; FREDSON Candidate in France. Action required (references): Headquarters' comments on Paragraph 2. References: A. EGFA-22011. B. PARI-2156. C. DIR-30031.

1. Pursuant to Reference C, the Station requested UNEAVE traces on the subject of Reference A. The resulting report has been translated and is attached herewith.

2. The Station concurs with the suggestion that the REDSOX section of FOB undertake the recruitment and processing of the subject. For reasons that need not be discussed in detail in this dispatch, the undersigned has become extensively known in the Georgian colony in true name, and plans to continue spotting and assessing Georgian candidates for other SR activities in France. The Georgian colony in France is small and the Station already has ample evidence that news of intelligence activity within the colony often becomes common knowledge very quickly. Therefore, it is best to risk an unknown outside asset for something so unequivocally compromising as a REDSOX recruitment.

3. Attached also is one of two copies of subject's signature which was obtained by AEPADDIE as a means of establishing bona fides (see OFFI-20982).

4. The Station will be glad to discuss the various problems of this case with Friganza or anyone else designated by FOB. It is requested that Headquarters advise what further action will be required of the Paris Station.

Wallace N. Havedon

7 August 1958

Attachments: A. UNEAVE report. B. Bona fides.

Distribution: SR, with attachments A and B (2 copies A, 1 copy B); WE, with attachment A (1 copy); Frankfurt, with attachment A (2 copies).
