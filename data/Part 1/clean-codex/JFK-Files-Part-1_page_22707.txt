3 May 69, M 03837.

Mexico City, info Director, Madrid. TYPIC AMICARUS.

Ref: Book dispatch 6572 (not sent Mexico).

1. Ref requests certain stations locate contacts and possible access agents to obtain assessment AMICARUS/1 (iden to Mexico only).

2. While visiting Mexico City March 68, ANCONCERT/1 met Orlando de Jesus (Ventura) Reyes, Cuban exile doctor and former member 13 Marzo Directorio Revolucionario. Ventura, DPOB 15 July 31, Santa Cruz del Sur, Camaguey, arrived Mexico City 17 Nov 67, resides Apt 1, Calle Zamora 112, Colonia Condesa. Ventura was close friend Jose Antonio (Echeverria) Bianchi, considered martyr by Directorio, and was close friend AMICARUS/1 and other Directorio leaders. Ventura arrested during AMLASH/1 trial and sentenced two years for antiregime activities.

3. ANCONCERT/1 believes Ventura willing cooperate with WCFIRM but not with Cuban exile groups. In order

C-101080.
