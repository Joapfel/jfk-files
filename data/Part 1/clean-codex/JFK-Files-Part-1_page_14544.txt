Because of confusion and contradictions in conversations reported in 2288, it appears possible woman may have been seeking amorous adventure, which may also have been based on chance social encounter or on reputation of AMAUTO-1 as “Don Juan.” Problem naturally complicated by fact that when she was talking to AMAUTO-1’s secretary, she was in fact talking to his wife. At no time in the conversations in question did she become aware of this fact.
