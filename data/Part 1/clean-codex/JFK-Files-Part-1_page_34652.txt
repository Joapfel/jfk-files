Classified message. Typed O/DDP. URIO: L.B. Kirkpatrick. Unit: I.G. Routing. EAT: 8656. Date: 2 May 1961. To: see attached list. From: Director. COMP: IG. Info: DCI, D/DCI, DOP, COP, ADD/A, EE 4, FE 5, M 2, AF 4, WE 4, WH 4, S/C 2. May 3, 01:72. OUT 52128; OUT 52125.

Cite DIR.

To see attached list info. Book message. From ASCHAM.

1. As result of defeat of the Cuban operation, considerable comment concerning KUBARK now being made in press and in Congress. Many proposals are being and will be raised concerning KUBARK and other activities in this area of ODYOKE. Actually much misinformation generally reported, and while some proposals are from responsible individuals, there are many distortions of actual situation. Details of Cuban operation are sufficiently covered in DEPCIRTEL 1637 and suggest you obtain this for your info. It is my view that KUBARK acquitted itself well in most respects, although I favor full review of operation after reasonable period has elapsed. In the meantime President has directed General Maxwell Taylor to take close look at all practices and programs in area of military and paramilitary, guerrilla and anti-guerrilla activities which fall short of outright war. He will give special attention to lessons.

Coordinating officers. Releasing officer. Authenticating officer. Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
