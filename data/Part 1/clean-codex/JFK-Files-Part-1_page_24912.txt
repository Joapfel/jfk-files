132330Z. MEXI information report to DIR, cited SDOM 0326. EMARGOSY.

1. Edward O. Tichborn advanced S375 (U.S.) for travel and other expenses in DR on EMARGOSY assignment. Since an airline ticket was involved as well, accounting could not be submitted prior to departure. Would appreciate if MEXI could obtain accounting and pouch to SDOM.

2. Tichborn departed SDOM 13 April for New York and expects to be back in MEXI 17 April.

3. For DIR: In New York Tichborn will attempt to firm up cover for next visit to SDOM. He will telephone HOS, hoping (HOPIG) to meet with HOS officers for discussion of several subjects, including income tax, prior to return to MEXI.

CFN 0326. Edward O. Tichborn, S375 U.S., DR, EMARGOSY. 13 April, New York, 17 April.
