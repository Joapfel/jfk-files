MC

January 14, 1966

1. Mr. Bladerghoen of CGC phoned on 12 January. After checking back with the Department of Justice, he learned that the latter is in contact with the Army concerning Questa. He suggested I not tell the Army this but tell them that Justice has an interest in the man.

2. This date I called Maykovich, who was well aware of the Justice interest. He said that Justice was now waiting for an assessment of the man by the Army.

D. Huefner
