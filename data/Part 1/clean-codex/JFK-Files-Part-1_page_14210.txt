20 MAR 69. IN 24937.

1. Request traces on Maria Jesusa (Martin) Villar and Jose Antonio (Fernandez) Grego, both Spanish citizens who are applying for immigrant visas for the United States. Martin is currently employed as a domestic in the home of AMAUTO-1. Regret having no further biographical data on the couple, but both are of advanced age. Should have some biography on 31 March. Information below gleaned from QUTIMID.

2. Fernandez returned to Galicia because of difficulty finding employment in Madrid. Martin is anxious to join him soonest, but is trying to save money for travel. QUTIMID on 20 March revealed that Martin is processing for a visa to the States but is keeping this news from AMAUTOS. She receives mail from the States at the home of a female friend, and her phone conversations with friends, as well as letters, are hush-hush. She appears purposely trying to withhold information from AMAUTOS.

3. QUTIMID reports that Martin will be leaving.
