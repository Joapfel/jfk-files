Country: Mexico. Report No. CS-311/14014-66. Subject: Eighth Regional Congress of the Union of Local Societies of Collective Farm Credit 40-66 in Torreon, Coahuila. Date distributed: 18 October 1966. No. pages: 2. Date of info: 19–21 August 1966. Place and date acquired: Mexico, Nuevo Leon, Monterrey (7 September 1966). Field report No. IMX-1267.

This is unevaluated information. Source ground truth appraisal of content is tentative. Source: Medium-level member of the Mexican Communist Party in the Laguna area whose Party position requires attendance at policy-making meetings. His reporting on Communist activities has been reliable.

1. On 19–21 August 1966 the Union of Local Societies of Collective Farm Credit 40-66 (USCE 40-66—Union de Sociedades de Credito Ejidal 40-66), a Mexican Communist Party (FCH (PCM)—Partido Comunista Mexicano) peasant front, held its 8th Regional Congress at the USCE 40-66 headquarters in Torreon, Coahuila, with an attendance of 800 to 1,000 people. Every state in Mexico was represented. The meeting was presided over by Arturo Orona Gomez, member of the PCM Central Committee and Secretary for Peasant Affairs of the PCM Coahuila State Committee, and Ramon Danzón Palomino, also a member of the PCM Central Committee, and head of the Independent Peasants’ Central (CCI—Central Campesinos Independientes), another PCM-controlled group in the Laguna area. In addition to these two who spoke, the speakers were Arnoldo Martínez Verdugo, First Secretary of the FCH (PCM) Central Committee, and Agustín Borrgel Gomez, Secretary of Organization of the PCM Coahuila State Committee. Conspicuous by their absence were Antonio Morín Rodríguez, member of the PCM Presidium with special interest in PCM political affairs in the Laguna area, and Juan de los Reyes, member of the PCM Central Committee who lives in the Torreon area. The main theme of the Congress was the aim of the USCE 40-66 to promote the nationalization of all privately held agricultural land in the Laguna area. It wants to divide all land in the Laguna area into communal farms and completely do away with small private land holding. Martínez said that the Mexican Government was trying to plot against the FCH (PCM) and gave as an example the 12–13 August 1966 arrest of Victor Rico Galan and some 40 others of the Popular Revolutionary Movement (MOP—Movimiento Revolucionario del Pueblo). He said that the FCH (PCM) did not oppose the armed revolutionary policies of Galan, and that the Party was pacifist.
