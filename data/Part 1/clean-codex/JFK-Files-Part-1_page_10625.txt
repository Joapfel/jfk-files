Central Intelligence Agency. Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Haiti. Date of information: 20 September 1964. TDCS DB-315/00917-64. Date distributed: 2 October 1964.

Subject: Recruitment of Cuban exiles for proposed invasion of Haiti.

Place and date acquired: United States, Miami, 22 September 1964. Reference: IN 85442. Source and appraisal: Field report No. UFG-5966. Cuban exile (C) who has contacts in the Cuban exile community in Miami and New York. Appraisal: 2 (that these recruiting operations are going on and the statements were made as reported).

1. On 26 September 1964, Paul Toussaint, accompanied by Ricardo Zaragoza, Jose A. Martinez, and another unidentified Cuban, arrived in Miami from New York. Martinez, Zaragoza, and the unidentified Cuban came to Miami to contact Cuban friends of theirs for a proposed force of 1,000 Cuban exiles who would be used to invade Haiti “within a ten-day period.” Allegedly, the invasion would be supported by the United States Government. On the evening of 26 September, Toussaint returned to New York by air in answer to what he said was “an urgent request from United States Government representatives.” Toussaint had placed several calls to New York after he arrived.
