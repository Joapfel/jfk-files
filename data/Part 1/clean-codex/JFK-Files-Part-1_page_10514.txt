1954:

Subject reportedly terminated his employment with Sandwich Brothers between June and July 1954, exact date not known, and established his own firm under the name of Paris Associated Enterprises.

The 1954 Japan Directory of Foreign Firms lists Sandwich Brothers (SUNDERVICH BROTHARDS) as a United States firm, with offices at 31 Tamao-cho, 4-chome, Shiba, Minato-ku, Tokyo, Japan. Telephone: 63-1809, 63-4369, 63-4287, and listed E. Ostrovsky as the Tokyo representative.

E. Ostrovsky, of course, is Emmanuel Abraham Ostrovsky, who is known to have been the Tokyo branch manager of the above firm, who resided in 1954 at 19 Kouncho-Mita, Minato-ku, Tokyo. Home telephone: 63-6773.

1955:

Subject continued in his position as president of Paris Associated Enterprises.

During 1955, exact dates not specified. Subject was named to the position of honorary deputy Greek consul in Yokohama, in which position he became the deputy to Antonio Pappadopoulos (PAPPADOP-OULCS), the honorary Royal Greek consul in Yokohama.
