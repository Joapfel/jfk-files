19 FEB 68 IN 77077

WAVE INFO DIR PALP CITE SAGO 8783

REF: WAVE 8029 (MS#5365)

TYPIC (ORSHIP)

According to FUBIAS-7, AMICE-31 arrived SAGO 7 February and is staying at Hotel Ritz. F-7 said MASPERO apparently sent a cable and invited A-31 to attend the CLASC executive committee meeting. A-31 told F-7 he is asking for fraternal (FRAN-termAL) affiliation with CLASC and plans to travel LA, visiting various maritime unions and trying to engage them with CLASC. A-31 asked F-7 for contacts in Valpo to start a strike in favor of PBRUMEN exiles and ban PBRUMEN shipping. He said he plans to work the strike through CRIT. A-31 is leaving SAGO 16–17 February for Antofagasta, Lima, and Bogota.
