Topic: AMSCROLL.

1. On 24 October, Rev. Henry Muir, who said he was president of the United Liberal Party, called upon IDEN A, who was secretary general of AMSCROLL (IDEN B). Muir asked for assistance for his party's fight against Communist forces in Jamaica. Muir said he was friendly with Chief Ingold (LNGOLD) in Jamaica and explained he had insufficient “means” for his party. IDEN A responded that AMSCROLL (SMCROLL) hardly had means for its own efforts and that it was unable to render support to other groups. IDEN A showed Muir around AMSCROLL premises and gave him samples of AMSCROLL publications. Muir expressed interest in at least one publication and said in future he would like additional copies for distribution in Jamaica.
