Classified message. Date: 19 April 1961. To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: IC/AF 3. Info: COP, C/C1, C/EE 3, S/C 2.

19 April 1838Z 1961. Routine. IN 38501. Per CSH 43-43.

DIR INFO FRAN-FROB CITE LEOP 1200.

RYBAT WIROGUE.

Ref A: DIR 34146 (last 94186). B: FROB 9126 (N 37367).

1. CAJEEP/2 highly cooperative when LEOP. Contract drafted but never signed due fluid political situation.

2. KAZADI, GOC Minister of Defense, recalled by KALONJI to Sud Kasai just when contract negotiations began. ACCULA, in addition to Minister of Interior, took over post of Defense. Air Force (CAF) directly under Defense, but 11 April PIERROT, Chief CAF, left LEOP for week. Returned 17 April with status of CAF in GOC structure very uncertain. Strong possibility it may be put under command MOBUTU and Army.

AIR C/AY 43-43.
