By long list of Cuban exile activists. Document contains familiar allegations of CIA involvement in this activity. Document states that Orlando Bosch is under arrest in Venezuela together with Luis (Louis) Corona Carriles Altas, “El Sánchez.” Bosch was traveling on a Costa Rican passport and also had Nicaraguan, Dominican, and Chilean passports.

3. Report lists Bosch’s contacts as Manuel Forne in Nicaragua, described as close to General Anastasio Somoza (Somocay); Orlando Junea (Junea) in Costa Rica; and Mario Rivadilla in Santo Domingo. Posada is described as a former U.S. Army ranger and worked for Venezuela’s DIGEPO for eight years, then organized a private detective agency that employs over 1,200 men. Posada, according to the writer, directed all violent actions in Venezuela against the Communists. His alias was “Inspector Basilici” and also “El Calighe.” The recent operation against the Curana Plan was mounted by Posada, assisted by Aldo Vera. The writer continues by stating that Bosch’s recent trip to Venezuela was an attempt to settle the differences between “Aldo’s group” and CORU. Bosch’s mission was successful, as it appears to be, and Aldo’s group will enter. Also, Vera is reportedly in Público Hicu. The writer adds that Adrenal Up will enter.
