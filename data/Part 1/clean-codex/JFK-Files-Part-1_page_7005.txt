Classified message. Date: 11 Oct 62. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 8. Info: CI, CI/OPS, S/C 2.

RE filing: RI this copy as indicated. RE indicating: Surface Weathered. Signature: Mathilda Litten.

12 October 1962, 9247Z. Routine. IN 38214.

MIR CITE MEXI 2188.

1. Maurice Halperin wrote letter 2 Oct Margaret and Albert Maltz giving new address Apt C-2, Calle 12, No. 103, Miramar, Habana. Working in textile factory but plans teach at University Habana begin 8 Oct. Son David had residency surgery in Veterans Hospital Chicago but fired last month for “reasons that had nothing to do with his competence.”

2. Source: LIMUD. Passed to ODENVY Mexico.

End of message.

Routing: RID/AN, initial L. RID/MIS, initial (L).
