Cite Contacts/New York 19085. Contacts/Washington. Support Division/Alien Staff (Schweich), from Graber.

Subject: Case 6913E — Nikolay Rudakov A-176258.

Reference: ALY-219-73.

1. C/S Graber met Rudakov at the TRC (International Rescue Committee) office on 19 May 1973. Rudakov is about to get a job and is looking for a place to live. He was not anxious to be interviewed by us, but wanted to get settled first. We promised to call him Wednesday, 16 May, to let us know if he found a place to live and where.

2. Rudakov told us his father was a worker in the same institute where he was employed and not a famous person at all. He died about five years ago. We could not get any more details at the time. Who is the famous Rudakov he could have been related to?

3. We will keep you informed of any further progress. To IMPDET CL by 7/4476.
