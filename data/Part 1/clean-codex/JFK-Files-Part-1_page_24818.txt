9 December 1966

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Contact Report, 8 December, 1205–1600 hours

Participants: Edward G. Tichborn and Lee E. Patton

Place: Hotel Roosevelt and Charley Brown's Pub

1. Tichborn arrived at the meeting site in the lobby five minutes late. We went up to the hotel room where the following business items were discussed.

a. He was told of his overpayment of $872.37, which he said he would return at once by check to Manuel P. Goodtwo, mailed to Mr. William S. Rodgers, P.O. Box 101.46, Woodridge Station.

b. Tichborn was reminded of the requirement that he pay his premiums of $8.63 for his health insurance. He was told he owed for the period of October through January, and it was suggested that he pay by check directly to the above box. He was told that there was no expiration notice sent out on the premiums being due; hence, it was up to him to keep the matter current.

c. He was told that Finance was interested in obtaining his monthly payments of $125 in repayment of his loan of $3,000. Apparently, he had last paid in July. He requested an extension or moratorium through January 1967 due to his heavy expenses involved in moving.

d. Tichborn was shown copies of the “White” FBIS publication. After he has looked at these, he will notify LEP as to whether he wants copies in the future mailed to the post office box which he has now obtained.

e. Tichborn was told that the $575 which he had lost on a TDY trip about a year and a half ago has now been officially written off.
