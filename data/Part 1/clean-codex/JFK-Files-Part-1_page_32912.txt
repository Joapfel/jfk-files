19 May 1967

A'STALK/1 has probably already sent a cable to the CITS agreeing to meet them in Madrid since we should have fallen all over backwards not to hurt JENAVE's delicate sensibilities. However, the attached is a pretty good try and we should at least make it a matter of record that we don't think it is a well-thought-out operation when JAVE has to send a CO when there is a three-man Peruvian section in the Madrid Station.
