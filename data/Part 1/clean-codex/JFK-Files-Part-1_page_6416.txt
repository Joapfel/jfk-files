Official Dispatch.

Via: Air.

Dispatch No.: CFFL-02 (101-1975).

To: Chief of Staff, Paris (A).

From: Cide, VE.

Subject: Frederic SECO.

Service: Bury SEDG.

Re: GPA-09; 2 February 1975.

A. 1. The following information on Frederic SECO, believed to be identical with your subject, has been translated into a French report in German resolved from a “well-placed source in the East German Government,” and transmitted to headquarters in Hqquar, St. Petersburg, 1 March 1975.

Name: SESCH, Frederic (frd).  
Born: 31 December 1915 in Berlin.  
Address: Montreux c/o Solka, 31 All. Avonnoire Prescriptive.  
Profession: Journalist, currently correspondent of the Swiss newspaper “Beruf und Zeit,” the German daily “Die Welt,” and of “Radio Stuttgart.”

A politically doubtful person who received an entrance visa for West Germany valid for six months on 24 January 1975. According to information from the French Ministry of Interior (the Se. II.), SESCH was working for the Communist paper “Die Zunft” (Huntenhager) before the war and during the war for the Swiss Communist newspapers “Die Zeit,” “Volkswohnung,” and “Arbiterzeitung.” Allegedly not a Communist! At any rate so stated the present Press Attitude of the Consul General (the German Consulate General, Paris), December 1975.

Theative: Paul Ehrich.

Birth date: 6 March 1924 to 30 June 1948, where he acquired French citizenship. According to his own statement, he was admitted by the East German Press Office in Bonn to apply again for German citizenship as he,

Releasing Officer: Paul Ehrich, Chief Whitehouse.

Authenticating Officer: Prakt. G. Coletan.

Date: 13.02.1975.
