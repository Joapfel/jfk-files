VII. Medical

16. State of Health:

16.01 To your knowledge, does he have any sort of health problem?

If yes, please explain.

16.02 Does his wife or children have any sort of health problem?

If yes, please explain. Wife?

16.03 Has he ever indicated that he had health problems in the past?

If yes, please explain.

16.04 To your knowledge, does he regularly take pills or medication of any kind?

If yes, please explain.

16.05 Is he the kind of person who never talks about his health or how he feels; talks about his health and feelings to an average degree; or frequently talks about his health and feelings?

16.06 When he feels bad, is it obvious to others; is it obvious to an average degree; would most people never know it; or have you never known him to feel bad?
