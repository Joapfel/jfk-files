Topic/AMBIDOT/AMBUD: Support for Dependents of Operational Personnel

For your information.

Reference: BMA 3798, dated 23 March 1967.

1. When Wallace A. GROWNEY met with AMBIDOT on 13 January 1967 to formally terminate the AMWORLD operation, he discussed in detail reference dependents who were to be provided financial assistance from AMWORLD funds and what social support would be available following termination of their AMWORLD monthly financial assistance. An investigation was conducted by both JENAVE and AMBIDOT and Headquarters to determine if the agents named by AMBIDOT had received any financial support payments. If so, an investigation would be begun to determine what, if any, additional financial support payments had been received by the dependents, and what, if any, obligations might remain to provide financial assistance to the dependents. The results of the combined Headquarters investigation will be summarized below for Station Banga.

Subject was a VOFACT agent under MC and provided payment of his $100 monthly salary to his dependent parents for two years following his death while engaged in a VOFACT mission. After Subject's execution in VOFACT, his regular monthly salary continued until 25 March 1963. The parents received that amount until Headquarters decided to liquidate the remaining balance of $8,400 and to pay them this amount in satisfaction of all legal and moral obligations. Headquarters considers that the parents have received approximately $10,000 from VOFACT and is not inclined to assume any further financial obligations to them.
