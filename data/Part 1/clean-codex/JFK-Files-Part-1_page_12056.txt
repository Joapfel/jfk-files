Memorandum to: OD/OP/Sec

11 February 1952

From: FE/III/PC

Subject: Gordon Barton McLendon

1. Operational clearance, for use as outlined below, is requested for subject, on whom available information is submitted separately.

Operational Clearance. Security Clearance. Operational Support. Operational Inquiry.

2. Use of Subject: State in detail the scope and nature of activities to be performed, their duration, the area involved, project involved, etc.

Subject is the president of a company which will provide accreditation in Japan to a contract agent of this organization. It is not expected that subject will learn of the agent's connection with this organization; however, clearance is requested so as to inform subject of the connection if the need may arise at a later date. Subject will know nothing of operations conducted by this agent, only that he is connected with CIA.

3. “Cover” story suggested for investigation: Commercial.

Details: General Credit Other.

4. Limits, if any, to investigation: None.

5. Is subject aware that an investigation is in process? No.

6. Is subject aware of CIA interest? No.

7. Has subject been briefed on “cover” story? No.

8. Has subject established “cover” for investigation with probable contacts? No.

9. Is provisional operational clearance necessary? No.

10. Investigation to be routine. Must be completed by: 1 June 1952.

11. Remarks:

Security Officer

Case Officer: Owain J. Christy

Principal Chief: James G.I. Kol’1o

GODEN
