7 August 1977. Washington. R. S. Travis 2268. New York.

Commit from Support (Travis) WA 21187. Be closed DASE 9145. Millard of cover has requested that we try to determine when Leo Cherne will return to NYC. It appears that our boy, IDID, for whom CHERE is providing cover, is back in the States on leave, and when he tries to contact Cherne, found that he was out of town. Will you please see if you can find out desired information. Thanks.

8 August 1977.
