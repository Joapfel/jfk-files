Dispatch No. UFAW-731

File No. 201-507337, 14-2-12. Date: 2 January 1970. Index: No. Film: No. To: Chief of Station, WH/Miami. Info: Chief of Station, Ottawa. From: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Subject: TYPIC/OPERATIONAL/AMROSE review mug book photos with AMCHALK-5. Action: See below. Refs: None.

In an effort to identify the DGI types who participated in the AMCHALK-5 meeting in Montreal, request WH/Miami show AMCHALK-5 photos of the following Cuban diplomats who are currently stationed in Canada:

A. Ricardo Escartin Fernandez. Mug book Vol. I No. 895.

B. Walfredo Garciga Oramas. Mug book Vol. I No. 83; Vol. II No. 62.

C. Ricardo Cabrisas Ruiz. Mug book Vol. I Nos. 391 and 909.

D. Angel Hernandez Gonzalez. Mug book Vol. II No. 61.

E. Orlando Rodriguez Ramos. Mug book Vol. I No. 908.

F. Humberto Castaneda Barban. Mug book Vol. II Nos. 63 and 35.

Edgar N. Vassalotti.

Distribution by tape: Chief of Station, WH/Miami; Chief of Station, Ottawa.

Originated by: Michael Olejarz, WH/COG/OPS. Coordinated by: L. Sulc, DC/WH/COG/OPS. Released by: D. C. WH/COG, W. Loyd.
