Central Intelligence Agency. Routine Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Nicaragua/Costa Rica/Panama. Report No. TOCS DB-315/02854-65. Date of information: 27–28 July and 4 August 1965. Date distributed: 1 September 1965. Subject: Remarks made by General Anastasio Somoza concerning Daniel Oduber of Costa Rica and communist plans for revolutionary action against Costa Rica, Nicaragua, and Panama. Place and date acquired: 27 August 1965.

Source and appraisal: A prominent Cuban exile, who is a fairly reliable reporter. He has a large number of direct and indirect contacts with influential Latin American nationals. The following information is from discussions held with General Somoza, who was aware that his remarks would probably reach officials in the U.S. government.

1. During conversations held on 27 and 28 July 1965 General Anastasio Somoza, Chief of the National Guard in Nicaragua, said that he would soon meet Daniel Oduber, presidential candidate in Costa Rica, at the border and that they would make a “non-aggression pact,” agreeing that neither would make trouble for the other during their campaigns for president of their respective countries. Oduber had asked for the interview. Somoza said that he thought Oduber had made a “live and let live” agreement with the Communists and that at their meeting he would demand from Oduber a public denunciation of the Communists to be followed by effective control measures against them.

This is a false statement affecting the National Defense of the United States within the meaning of the Espionage Law, title 12, U.S.C., Secs. 71 and 72. The transmission or revelation of which in any manner to an unauthorized person is prohibited by law.

Distribution: State/Int’l, DIA, Army/ACSI, Navy, Air, JCS, SECDEF, NSA, MIC, AID, USAO, CI, ONE, OCR, CIA/NMCC, INS, FBI, DCS, D/OCI, Secret Service (PRS), EXO.
