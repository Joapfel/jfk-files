Office Memorandum — United States Government

By hand.

To: Control  
From: Acting Chief, EDC  
Date: 25 February 1947  
Subject: Issuance of Passport for James M. Moore, Semi-Covert Representative for Dairon, China.

1. The assignment of the representative is Dairon, China.

2. Estimated date of departure: 17 March 1947.

3. Estimated date of arrival at field station: 5 April 1947.

4. Countries through which the representative will travel: China, Japan, and Korea.

Reported to Division of Foreign Activity Correlation, State, on 8 February 1949. See file No. C.013.
