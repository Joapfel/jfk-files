111555Z CITE LONDON 5123.

Priority: Director, Kinshasa, Precipia. Information: Bukavu, Brussels.

ZIBET WIQUIT SECAROS SMOOTH.

Ref: Director 26813. LOND 5/08.

1. At 11 August meeting, SMOOTH confirmed they had had indirect contact with WICKS in the past, but that he was an unwitting source. Contact man presently unavailable, but they plan to put him back in touch with WICKS about 17 August, in hope he can elicit information as to WICKS' future plans, etc.

2. Meanwhile SMOOTH have asked Special Branch (directly, not through JAGUAR) to cover WICKS, but to date they have not located him. We urged them to follow up return address on letter, offering to make available flat next door occupied by female station personnel as OP if desired. One problem is presumption WICKS travelled on U.K. passport, in which case Immigration will have no record of his entry or intended address. Have addressees any clue as to documentation on which WICKS travelling?

3. Without indicating any knowledge of future planning reflected in ref, we speculated along at possibility SECAR03/1.
