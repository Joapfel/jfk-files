Outgoing Classified Message. Central Intelligence Group.

To: TSINGTAO. From: Special Operations. Confirmation: FBZ (1-2). Information: ADSO (3-4), COPS (5), CRD (6), CPD (7), CAS (8), SFD (9). Routine. 22 October 1947. OUT 53566. WASH 8212.

To: TSIN. Cite: WASHIF.

Re TSIN 018 (IN 20097), re paragraph 3 for [Signature].

You are on per diem basis, not L and Q, until you arrive at final station. In view of the fact that the trip to Chefoo was necessary to preserve cover and was on competent orders, actual, reasonable expenses of the trip, not to exceed the per diem which would normally be paid for a State person making the trip, are authorized to be paid from station funds. An itemized account of expenses should be submitted with the regular accounting.

T. F. Bland. Admin. GMD. RK/DDEB EDE/VRT JSW.

Lloyd George.

Releasing officer. Coordinating and coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
