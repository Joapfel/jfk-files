Dear,

I thank you for your letter of 23rd September, replying here.

I am sorry I cannot come and see you any way here since passing through Melrose a week ago.

I think I have an idea of the subject which will be discussed with me. I met a friend in Perth en route to Perth and he wanted me to join his team on the eve of taking up any job for reentry five months from now. As a result I can be terminated by one month's notice on my side although it is on a permanent basis.

I am an ardent anti-communist and can dedicate my whole life to combating this enemy which is aiming to destroy our freedom. However, one must make plans for one's future, I am reluctant to take up anything on a short-term basis. I would not like to be in an awkward position like that of the old contractor in Vicinity when revenue was not considered when it expired after two years, despite an assurance when the offer was made, that there would be no difficulty at the extension after the initial two years.
