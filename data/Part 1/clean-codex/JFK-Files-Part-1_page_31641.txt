The following received from Condon, Munich.

Following is the full story as of 1700 local time on shooting. At 1400, two, possibly three, jet aircraft, presumably MIGs, which kept well within Czech territory, managed after considerable maneuvering to shoot down two, repeat two, balloons. Aircraft departed the area and have not returned since. Small-arms fire during most of the afternoon accounted for a total of three, repeat three, balloons. Small-arms fire was effective only during periods when weather conditions did not permit rapid rise of balloons crossing the border at a height within range of submarine guns and carbines.

Walker's report: Temper of residence in launching area calm and unworried.

End of message.
