Extract and Cross Reference. Document Identification. Doc. symbol and no.: DIRECTOR 932728. Date of document: 22 Oct 76. File classification of record copy: 73, 6, 46/3. Document subject: PLMHBLANKET.

Text of extract: Per 20 Oct 76 issue of Washington Post, Orlando Bosch is described as a right-wing Cuban exile leader who is alleged to have been involved in the bombing of the Cuban airplane on 6 Oct and the murder of Orlando Letelier on 21 Sept. Bosch is apparently now under arrest in Venezuela as a result of investigations into these activities. The Post article says that Bosch is the leader of an anti-Castro umbrella group known as the United Revolutionary Organization. Bosch is wanted in Miami for a parole violation stemming from the 1968 conviction for terrorist attacks.

Subject of 201: Bosch, Orlando Avila. 201 file no.: 201-299202.
