Priority, 23 1912Z, Mexico City, 2564. Priority director info, priority Guatemala City.

Afternoon, 22 August 67. LIREPEL-2 reported the following storm, received from Ernesto Capuano Delvecchio (DELvecHio) on 19 August:

For the past several months, Mexican painter Carlos (Jurado) Delmar has attempted to make contact in Mexico with top leaders of Fuerzas Armadas Rebeldes (FAR) to offer the sale of medicine and arms. He first made contact with Carlos Navarrete, known to addressees, and explained he was a Greek friend who sells arms all over the world. Greek became interested in selling arms to FAR and asked Jurado to make the necessary contacts. Navarrete found Jurado too eager and too insistent on making contact with FAR and immediately began to suspect him as CIA or Mexican Security Service provocation. Navarrete did not help him, and Jurado somehow made contact with Moises Orozco Leal. Capuano does not know what relationship Orozco had with Jurado except that the latter offered him arms, medicine, and money, 300 dollars, the latter of which Orozco did not accept. Recently, Jurado called Capuano and asked to meet with him. He showed Capuano a letter written by Orozco which clearly indicated Orozco was a traitor.

23 August 67, #37717. FAR. LIREPEL. Abstract: #37717.
