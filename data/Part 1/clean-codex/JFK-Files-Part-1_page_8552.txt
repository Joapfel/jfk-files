Attachment

August 6, 1969

Subject: Jimmy SABORI (P-15830), report on his meeting with LOGNOV (P-11,712) on August 4 at Eamborne-San Angel at 2000 hours.

1. The meeting was prearranged and lasted 2 hours and 15 minutes. As in all previous cases, & and LOG met in front of the restaurant. LOG came 3 minutes earlier.

Their next meeting: On August 16, & and Josephine are to come to Quautla at the “Banos” about 1000 hours to be guests of LOG and his family, to celebrate &’s birthday, which is on August 15. On August 25 at Eamborne-San Angel at 2000 hours, a business meeting will be held.

2. LOG and & had 2 vodka cocktails, a club sandwich, and a beer. LOG claimed that he had already eaten something and & felt stomach discomfort.

3. LOG asked & if he had finished reading the book in English about Rasputin, which LOG had given him about a month ago. & answered affirmatively, and they spoke about the book. LOG promised to give & another book, written by a British author, titled “USSR 1941–45” or similar. LOG wants & to be familiar with the suffering of Soviet (Scv) people during World War II.

4. Then LOG asked & what the situation was with the U.S. Embassy phone directory. & stated that the fictitious girl Patricia showed up for a few days and returned to the States for family reasons. He brought up the directory in a conversation with her, and she told him that it was tightly controlled. & left it at that point, and he told LOG that he would have to start working on the matter from scratch if LOG wanted the directory, but & would appreciate LOG’s suggestions as to how to proceed. LOG told & to forget it because he knew that the directory was well controlled.
