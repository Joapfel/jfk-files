Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Venezuela/Cuba.

Date of information: Mid-September 1976.

Subject: Arrival of Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch in Venezuela; and his comments on the activities of his group.

Acquired: Venezuela, Caracas (18 September 1976). Field No. HVC-7483.

Source: A Cuban exile who maintains close contacts with other exiles. His reliability has not been established.

1. In mid-September 1976 Orlando Bosch, a Cuban exile leader, said that he had united all of the various Cuban exile groups into one closely-knit organization which follows his lead. Bosch gave the group no title, but said that it was well-organized and planned to continue fighting the regime of Cuban Prime Minister Fidel Castro.
