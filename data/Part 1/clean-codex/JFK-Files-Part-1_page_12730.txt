WKSCARLET-3 claimed that a friend and reporter on the staff of “El Mundo” was invited to Miraflores Palace (13 May), along with reporters from other newspapers, to “be given the story.” “Ultimas Noticias” was the only newspaper which carried the story.

2. GRADSPIES had a regularly scheduled meeting at noon today with Deputy Chief WKSCARLET, and the subject of the “Basilio” article came up. Deputy said WKSCARLET attaches no importance to the article, considers it a personal attack on WKSCARLET-3 or just press sensationalism, and that he personally does not believe that WKSCARLET-3 is working for either EMERALD or LEADERGO. Deputy said he believes it unlikely that the government will pursue the “Basilio” case, for the government intends to use TELLAPS extensively and is leery of any publicity which might result in embarrassment to it.

3. Apart from the above, WKSCARLET-3 was reported to have had several recent meetings with ANKORNE-6 in an effort to arrange a job on one or more projects ANKORNE-6 is trying to get for LEADERAY. ANKORNE-6 is also secret.
