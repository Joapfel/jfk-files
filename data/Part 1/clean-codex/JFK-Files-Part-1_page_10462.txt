United States Government

Memorandum

To: Chief, Central Division  
Attn: Support Branch  
From: Chief, Marine Signal Office  
Subject: Plan to Unite the Cuban Exiles

Date: 5-18-1963  
ERA: (S) - 1/60

1. At their request, Luis G. Rodriguez met with Dr. Rafael Sardinas, secretary of the Cuban Cane Planters Association in Havana, and with Juan Fernandez. Juan said that a Cuban Senator should be elected language teacher in order to get “the periodico” (of Cuba). He advised that they have been meeting with several personalities of the exile colony in an effort to organize all the exiles into a single group which will represent all the exiles regardless of former affiliations.

2. They feel that after the support specifically provided by the Cuban Revolutionary Council, they must avoid getting involved in matters which are in conflict with the policies of the U.S. Government. Therefore, they would like to obtain this project with Mr. John Grimes.

3. The following personalities have been approached and have indicated their desire to cooperate to the fullest extent in an effort to unite all the Cubans.
