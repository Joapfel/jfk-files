20 March 1973. Staff.

Cite Caracas 21468.

To: Director, Rome, Paris. Info: WH/Miami, Madrid, Buenos Aires.

RYBAT PLMHABYSS.

References: A. Caracas 21429 (IN 860750). B. Director 383866. C. RCME 28609 (IN 863762). D. Paris 37356 (IN 863806).

1. Assume all addressees except WH/Miami have seen BNDD Rome 2088 of 16 March 73, which reports arrival of Luis Posada and Camilo Cusati in Rome on 14 March. Following summary this telegram:

Pair stated they would be staying at Claridge Hotel, Via Liegi 62, Rome, but did not do so. Pair residing at Hotel King, Via Sistina, Rome, in company of Venezuelan diplomat Adolfo Emilio Reyes-Mejias, DPOB 6 October 43, Caracas. Pair have stayed at King Hotel on previous occasion. Cusati made phone call to 53-52-20 listed to Monsignore Camilo Forte.

20 March 1973.
