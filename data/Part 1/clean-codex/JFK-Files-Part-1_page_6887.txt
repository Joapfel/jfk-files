Dispatch.

To: Chief, BS through Chief, SS; Chief of Station, Mexico City.  
From: Chief of Station, Bern (Born).  
Date: Invoice/Operations, Maurice HALPERIN.  
Issued: 10 November 1958. Order can be judged by qualified headquarters department only.

Attached is the final summary report of the INAMITONs on the stay in Switzerland of Maurice HALPERIN and wife, who departed for Prague (Prag) on 10 November 1958. Copies of letters written by HALPERIN while there are forwarded to headquarters only...

GEORGE J. POMFRET

Recipients: A — INAMITON Report; B — Letters written by HALPERIN.

27 November 1958.

Distribution: 2 — SS; 2 — SS with enclosures A and B; 2 — Mexico City with enclosure A; 2 — Bern with enclosure A.
