Subject: LEONOV, Nikolay Sergeyevich (U/AIUO)

PTR: X. Date: July 1977. Coded Copy No.: 05 (S). Subject: Nikolay LEONOV. From: Sergeyevich.

1. Reference is made to your telephonic request dated 19 July 1977 for information concerning Subject.

2. Our files show no indication of any connection between Subject and prominent Soviet officials.

3. According to a reliable defector source, Nikolay LEONOV was identified as a KGB officer.

4. Subject was graduated from the Institute of Foreign Relations in Moscow, at an unknown date. From July 1953 until October 1956 he served as an interpreter and Secretary to the Ambassador at the Soviet Embassy in Mexico City, Mexico. He traveled to Mexico in November 1959 and to Cuba in February 1960 as an interpreter. In July 1961 he returned to Mexico City as Third Secretary and Assistant Cultural Attaché at the Embassy and served in this capacity until June 1964. He was assigned there again in October 1964 as Second Secretary and Press Attaché. He remained in this position until December 1968. From April 1969 until July 1969 he served temporarily as representative of the Soviet News Agency Novosti in Lima, Peru.
