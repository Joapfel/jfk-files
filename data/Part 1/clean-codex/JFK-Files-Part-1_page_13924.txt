Classified message.

Date: 14 February 1964. File: P-7763, P-7865, P-8480.

To: PRITY, PACY, DIR, RYBAT, ZRZNICK, HYSAGE-1.

Ref: MEXI 8199.

1. Per LIEMBRACES, 13 February, HYSAGE-1 made reservation PAA for PACY, leading MEXI 0930 Sunday, 16 February.

2. Per LIENVOY, 11 February, HYSAGE-1 called Rogelio Rodriguez, second secretary, Cuban Embassy, and asked if everything left yesterday. Rodriguez said yes. HYSAGE-1 said she would like to go Saturday (sic). Also, she is bringing package to embassy 12 February for next CUBANA flight. (FYI, CUBANA had a flight 10 February, another coming 14 February.)

3. Night 12 February, LIEMBRACES again surveilled Bernal and “David.” They drove to vicinity Plaza Miravalle. Bernal left the car, walked to the plaza, took a seat, then walked to a street corner where he was approached by an unidentified man. They both lit cigarettes, then the man followed Bernal to the car, not speaking until they entered the car and drove off. Subjects were checking so closely for surveillance that the team had to drop off. Found them again in a restaurant on Avenida Insurgentes Sur, but subjects were checking all cars driving past, so team dropped surveillance. Unidentified man described as “foreign looking, about 33 years, 5'6", 160 pounds, short reddish straight hair, round face with protruding cheeks, slit eyes, reddish complexion.” LIEMBRACES, 13 February, saw this man entering Cuban Embassy. Station trying to identify and will advise.

End of message.

Coordinating officers: JLN (in draft); M. C. Chaaden.

Authenticating officer: Secretariat officer.

Outgoing. Courier officer.
