The reason given within the Foreign Ministry for Cruz's recall is that a niece of President Augusto Pinochet (PINOchet) had occasion to request consular assistance from him during a private visit to Costa Rica, and found him absent from his office during normal working hours. Under current conditions in the Chilean Foreign Ministry, this is a plausible explanation, and there is no contradictory information available.)
