ITKIN, South

Eric Eisenstadt of Funding Branch denied on 1 April 1968 that he found a piece of paper saying that the subject was once registered as a Cavalier agent: III/7/III. He checked with State, which in turn checked with Mr. Lanvin of the Registration Section of Justice, who said there is no record of Itkin as ever having been a Cavalier registered agent. In June he registered as a representative of the Deboe-Hignole provisional government in exile and was to have been paid $50,000 when this government took power. No further information.
