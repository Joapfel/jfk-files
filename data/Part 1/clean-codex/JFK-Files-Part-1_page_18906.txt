Counterespionage Operations Course No. 1. Name: Goodpasture, Ann L. Sex: Female. Dates of course: 19 March–6 April 1956. Number of students: 15. Date of birth: 28 November 1918. LOC date: February 1944. Grade or rank: GS-11. Office: WE/FI. Proposed assignment or present position: FI Operations Officer.

I. Course Objectives: To familiarize the students with the various types of CE operations and with methods and procedures peculiar to CE in the field and at Headquarters, to outline CE objectives, and to increase proficiency in the recognition and exploitation of leads.

II. Course Characteristics: The Counterespionage Operations Course is of three-week duration. The curriculum material is presented by means of lectures, seminars, and directed reading. It is divided into sections as follows:

A. Introduction (definition of terms, the CI Staff, operational security and risk assessment, sources of CI leads, CI reporting and records)

B. Interrogation

C. Foreign Intelligence Services (which emphasizes currently hostile services including a lecture on CI in Madison)

D. Penetration and Deception

E. Double Agent Operations

During the course each student is required to present orally a CE case and to submit a written version of the case. Evaluations are based on problems, the case analysis presented by the student, and objective tests.
