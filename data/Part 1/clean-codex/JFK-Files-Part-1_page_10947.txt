Section XIV. General.

2. Subject is a European type, but because of his Cuban accent would probably be recognized as such.

3. Information in Pro Parts 1 and 2 was obtained through research, files, agents, and direct questioning.

4. No problems are envisaged in terminating agent.

Section IV. Emergency Conditions Recontact Arrangements.

1. Subject is in possession of CO's telephone number (home) and CO's office phone. CO can make contact with Subject at his home or at the Prime Minister's Embassy if necessary. Subject and CO use the same agent on the telephone.

Section XVI. Miscellaneous Operational Data and Remarks.

Subject, who is well disposed toward PERIDE, is anxious to remain a long-term agent for ODYOKETSU. His intelligence, coupled with his contacts in leftist and Communist circles, makes him an excellent prospect.
