Request for Overseas Transportation.

Section A. To: Assistant Executive for Personnel. 8 January 1947.

Your approval is requested for the overseas transportation of Ann Johnson Goodpasture.

Name of traveler: Ann Johnson Goodpasture.

Justification for the trip: Miss Goodpasture is being transferred from Washington, D.C., to Bombay, India, where she will be assigned as Administrative Assistant at the American Consulate in Bombay. Designation has not been requested.

Date. Signature (Initiating Officer).

The following actions concerning the above traveler were completed on the dates indicated below:

Date assessed and approved: 25 August 1944. Date security approved: 19 February 1944. Date entered on duty: 1 December 1946. Date of overseas physical examination: 9 December 1946. Date immunization initiated: 6 December 1946.

10 January 19. Signature (Transportation Clearance Officer). Date.
