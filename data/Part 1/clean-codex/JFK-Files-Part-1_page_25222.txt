Mr. Foy D. Kohler.

Position (January 1965): U.S. Ambassador in Moscow, USSR.

Source wonders why, in view of his position, Mr. Kohler has a Chinese cook—she thinks this is a poor security practice.

Source knows of no specific things about the ambassador’s cook which would make him a security risk other than his nationality. The fact that the American ambassador has a Chinese cook is often the subject of conversation on the Moscow lip circuit. Wife has no taste in clothes.

15 September 1966.
