Classified message. 102135Z, Mexico City 9195 (Easby acting), to Director. 14 October 1975. 10H:37303. C-TG:31. Type: Perpetual Litigation. Reference: Mexico City 9168.

1. LILATIL HUNGRILY fed five months in November and six months in November, which resulted in a considerable amount of intelligence, partially in the fields of cultural activities, relations and organization. POMS cultural field and others partially fulfilled with extensive documentation in case form. L-9 provided with his nine folders containing memoranda and correspondence dealing with CNC activities. L-9 not away from QUIS activities within CNC, but assignment of Manuel Vega Perez as Director of International Relations within CNC certainly significant.

2. L-9 again faced with dilemma: return to Cuba or remain in Mexico to work as administrator of Mexican company where brother presently employed. Considerable pressure being put on L-9 by brother and healthy Mexican benefactor F-0, who owns company. L-9 getting along in years (52), health beginning to fail, suffering mental strain, now anxious to settle down in Mexico and take job above, which amounts to security. This will allow him to paint and do things he was always wanted to in twilight career. L-9 pathetic figure taken between.
