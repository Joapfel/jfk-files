References: A, DIR 17103 (IN 9616); B, OTTA 1611 (IN 9609); C, OTTA 1609 (IN 9590).

1. Station files contain references to the following persons, references D and C:

A. “ROGELIO” (Ref C, paragraphs 1–3): probably Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez, second secretary, Cuban Embassy in Mexico City. Iden B of MANA 4717, agent of NIC Service who sent CUBA via MEXI, was instructed to contact Rogelio by Marcial Acuiluz (see MANA 4716). Iden P was in contact with Rodriguez, who used alias “JCHELE” with Iden B, from 14 February 1964 to 10 March, when Iden B left for Cuba. From other traffic in this case, including HNMA-3083, HNMA-3022, and MEXI 8228, it appears COVIOUS was involved in intelligence activities in MEXI.

B. Manuel Vega (Ref C, paragraph 1-E): Iden P, above, was also given the name “VEGA” as a contact at the Cuban Embassy in MEXI (MANA 4716). Iden P may be in contact with “VEGA,” and MEXI 8228 reported that he was probably Manuel Vega Perez, who was no longer in MEXI.

C. Noe Guerrero (Noel Guerrero) Santiago (Ref B, paragraph 6-D): undoubtedly Noel Guerrero Santiago (201-301510), born 12 April 1914 at Leon, Nicaragua.

Guerrero was a longtime Nicaraguan communist who had also been a member of the Mexican Communist Party and had been involved in communist activities in several Latin American countries. He was active in founding Castro/communist Nicaraguan exile organizations in Cuba. In late 1962 and early 1963 he was leader of the Frente de Liberacion Nacional (FLN) (Frente de Liberacion Nacional), communist-dominated and Cuban-directed exile group, which was in training in Honduras; in late July 1963 he led a group from Honduras into Nicaragua. MN-267, which reported activities of this group in Honduras and Nicaragua, reported that Guerrero and four other FLN members left Nicaragua, leaving the main group behind, in early August and returned to Honduras, reaching Tegucigalpa sometime in late August. In the FLN, Guerrero used the pseudonym “PEDROMYN”—“El Patriarca.” The most recent information available to the station was SJOS 630, 12 November 1963, which reported that he was in Cuba.

2. The station has no information on Thelma King or Ramiro Valdez, which is not available from other stations.
