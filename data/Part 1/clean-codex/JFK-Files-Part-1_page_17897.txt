Dispatch. To, order and no.: HMMA-11537. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Date: 12 November 1958. Subject: Project LIFEAT—request for spare parts. Action required: Station Mexico, that the items below be procured and forwarded.

1. It is requested that the following items be procured and forwarded to Station Mexico for use in Project LIFEAT:

A. Vacuum tubes: 6V6, 100 each; 6J5, 200 each; 6X5, 200 each; 6K6, 50 each; 6SN7, 25 each; 6SL7, 50 each; 6AU6, 50 each; 12AX7, 50 each.

B. Grommets for EKO recorder: These grommets are listed in the EKO parts catalog as symbol E40, Motor Mounting Grommets (3), part number P9477. It is requested that the soft live rubber (flat) grommets be sent since the rounded ones are hard and transmit a lot of noise and rumble. If these grommets are available, Station Mexico could use approximately 120 of them.

C. Fast Forward Cable Assembly: number 242-12297, 25 each.

D. Take-up reels: empty take-up reels, 30 each.

2. If there is a kit with assorted condensers for the Pulse Recorders (DR-2) and for the Reverse Recorders, it is requested that such a kit be forwarded.

Willard C. CURTIS

EPS/vps. 10 November 1958. Distribution: 3 Hqs.
