20 April 1966

Memorandum for: Thomas B. CASASU (SA)

Through: Chief of Station, Paris

Subject: Notification of Designation as a Participant in the Organization Retirement and Disability System

Reference: Book Dispatch 5098, dated 12 August 1965

Recent correspondence on the above subject informed you that I had determined that you met the criteria specified in the applicable regulation for designation as a participant in the Organization Retirement and Disability System. On the basis of this determination and your recently executed election to remain in the System, your designation as a participant was made effective 10 April 1966.

Richard B. Egan
