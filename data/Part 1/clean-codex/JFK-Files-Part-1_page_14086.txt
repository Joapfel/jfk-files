Classified message.

Date: 16 December 1958. To: Director. From: Havana Document Various Service. Action: WH 5; Havana FED/10; LITVILUCO, FEDE. Info: COP, FI, FI/OPS, FI/RON 3, PP'2, PP/PRO, 'PP/PYO' 2, PP/PMD, 100 4, DD/I, AD/CI, S/C 2. Routing. In 38553.

To: DIR. Info: SACU. Cite: HAVA #693.

Intel. Date: 16 Dec.

Approved: paragraphs 1 and 4 (2); paragraphs 2 and 3 (3); paragraphs 5–6.

Source: paragraph 1, George K. Harper, Las Villas plantation owner (F); paragraphs 2 and 3, Amaria (B); paragraph 4, AMBARK-1 (C); paragraph 5, Harry Graham (F), from DR member (F).

1. Ernesto Guevara has moved westward. His exact location is not known, but 165 men thought to be under his command are camped near Manacas in west Las Villas. Guevara's immediate objective is believed to be to cut off central highway traffic west of Santa Clara. FC: AMBARK-2 confirms this rebel strategy, says may be planning to try to cut the highway at Santo Domingo. SC: Heavy trucks cannot travel east of Santa Clara. Backroads can be used by autos and light trucks.

2. 26th of July replacing CTC labor officials with its own people on sugar centrales which it captures. Conrad Bequer Diaz, with headquarters in Sierra Maestrí, is in charge of the program.

Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
