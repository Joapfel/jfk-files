I

High (Digit Span), Low E.

Internalizer: self-contained; inner-directed; physically inactive, contemplative; values ideas and symbols.

Externalizer: responsive, reactive; outer-directed; physically active, relating; practical, concrete, realistic.

R

High (Block Design), Low F.

Regulated: organized; focused; persistent; rate learning; emotionally isolated; memory good for procedures and routines.

Flexible: loose; diffuse attention; high imagination; trial and error; spontaneous, expressive, sensitive; memory good for feelings and impressions.

A

High (Picture Arrangement), Low U.

Role: Adaptive. Versatile role player; socially responsive and suggestible; social initiative; reflects group behavior; overestimated.

Role: Uniform. Limited role player; socially guarded and negativistic; often ignored; socially specialized or stereotyped; often in conflict with group behavior; underestimated.
