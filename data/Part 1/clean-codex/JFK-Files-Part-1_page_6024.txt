A.M.T-2, S.A. BEATLES

27 September 1963

Tip-Top Restaurant, Coral Gables.

1600–1850 p.m.

Harold R. Norman

1. The meeting with AMT-2 was to clarify the continuing misunderstanding (disunderstanding) and disagreement over AMBARB funding. The original AMBARB policy meeting with AMT-2 was reviewed, and AMT-2 pointed out that it was not agreed that AMT should pay all AMBARB costs, but only the covers. Without going over the entire evolution of the AMBARB project funding, it was pointed out to AMT-2 that the project was a two-way arrangement, of great mutual convenience, and we must find a functional “middle ground” upon which to operate, although it may not be entirely satisfactory to either party. AMT-2 agreed, and the following ground rules were arrived at as a workable funding plan:

a. AMBARB’s assigned overt AMSPELL representatives will be funded by AMSPELL from Miami on all future assignments. Those now being funded by the station will remain in that status quo.

b. Cover AMBARBs for station contact will be assigned by initial processing, both parties agreed, and the station will fund and control.

c. Of the four pending cases where stations have requested funding, a meeting is to be held as soon as possible where each case will be considered individually and decided upon.

2. While this arrangement is not entirely acceptable, it is believed that upon the return of AMT-2 to operational control, a better arrangement can be made.
