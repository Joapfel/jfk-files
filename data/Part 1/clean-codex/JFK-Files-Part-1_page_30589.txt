From: Shanghai.

To: Special Operations.

Action: EXEC (1).

Information: ADG (2–3), COGS (4), REG (5), TEC (6–7), SP FD (8), TRAN (9).

To: BACHS. City: Shanghai.

The following personnel arrives on board the 3 January, 1947: Captain Heathcott, John Clodson, George A. Smith, Nelson Miexer, and Warren Hartz.

Each employee paid U.S. dollar, $27.50 for dent part which includes $37.50 transportation tax. We will redeem the payment for later arrival only until December 25. This advance to each in service. Will you make individual amounts to obtain taxes hereon?
