Case. A CITE NYOR 4652. Support (Connell) from Major. Case 28972, 28973.

Subject: Marek Hlasko’s trip sponsored by International Rescue Committee.

Through Mr. Charles Sternberg (Stepnbers) of IRC, we have learned that the man who will have most to do with the subject while he is in this country is the secretary, a Mr. McAllister. The subject will not be arriving in this country as planned since he has been ill in Berlin. He will not attend the dinner on 25 November and, if he does come—and this has not been firmed up—he will be coming sometime in January 1959 for the opening of the film based on one of his books. Directly, we have learned that there may be some question as to whether the film will actually be distributed in this country on account of censorship. Evidently, it is loaded with eroticism to the point where a DD film (BB film) would look like a Sunday school picnic.

File: International Rescue Committee.
