30 June 1953

Memorandum for: Chief, International Organizations Division

Subject: Funds for Balloon Operation

This will authorise you to inform officials of TPTONIC that the restrictions imposed by the Project Review Committee, at its meeting on 29 May 1953, of $3,000,000 for operating expenses of TPTONIC for the first quarter of fiscal year 1954, may be exceeded by the funds necessary to prepare and/or execute a balloon operation.

Allen W. Dulles  
Director
