April 1969. CITE CONTACTS/LTC. ANGELES 877. CONTACTS/WASHINGTON. To OSS, MUSCULIN.

Subject: Case 52372.

Hugh C. McDonald, World Associates Inc., indicated that someone provided ISO/ISO with incorrect information. He did not offer to demonstrate his voice identification system, which he regards as a highly sensitive system from a security standpoint. He also did not plan to be in Washington within the next three weeks. His so-called world tour will begin sometime this fall with a visit first to Latin America, a return to the U.S., a trip to Europe, a return to the U.S., and a final trip to the Far East.

The Chicago representative of the firm plans on representing the firm at an ISAF meeting with Financial General Group Co. in Washington.

McDonald agreeable to going himself and to meeting with Agency people if you will provide us with names, telephone numbers, etc. Advise.
