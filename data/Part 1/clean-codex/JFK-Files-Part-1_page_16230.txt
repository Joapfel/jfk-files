James Wicks (WICKS), Alastair, Major.

Date of birth: 17 November 1917. Place of birth: England. Citizenship: Rhodesian.

July 1966 address: 38 Montague Square, London W 1.

March 1967 address: 199 Bickenhall Mansions, London W 1, listed under Janis Morris.

Mercenary in Katanga gendarme, 1961.

Former RAS (Rhodesian Air Services) official.

Recruiter of South Rhodesian mercenaries for Tshombe forces, 1964.

Second in command to Mike Hoare in 5 CDO.

Left Leopoldville in December 1965 at termination of mercenary contract for Johannesburg.

One of the mercenaries involved in the July 1966 plot to overthrow Mobutu (MOBUTU), although still in the pay of Mobutu.

Also tried to involve Michael Hoare (HOARE) in the plot. He offered Hoare £20,000 for a six-month contract and the rank of full colonel.

Recruiting officer in South Africa for Tshombe's forces, 1966.

Unwitting SMOTH contact, 1966.

Went to London in October 1966.

Unscrupulous and undependable.
