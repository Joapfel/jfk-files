Office Memorandum, United States Government

To: CPD  
From: CSB  
Subject: William Vincent Bros  
Date: June 16, 1943

Reference your memorandum of June 8, subject as above, requesting security approval on the above-named subject, pending full clearance.

Robert M. Curry
