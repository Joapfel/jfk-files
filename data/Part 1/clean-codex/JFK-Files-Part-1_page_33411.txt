Chile's image as reflected in West German media comments was reached all-time low and even conservatively inclined outlets, by and large, no longer bother to tell “the other side.” Eastern radio stations maintain relentless drumfire of anti-Chilean propaganda and there is no apparent dearth of info to sustain rather effective denigration campaign.

2. Toward that end, require some type of objective appraisal of recent developments and of predictable trends.
