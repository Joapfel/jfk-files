Precedence.

From: CO and Chief, MI.  
To: CO, MOVED.  
Destination: Outgoing.

S/C NR: 317.  
Date: 29 April 1947.

Miller (Miler) departing Shanghai (SHAROHAI) approximately five hours for your station. Miller (Miler) is to work as investigator. Subject to your decision, we believe Miller (Miler) would be useful advice. He has been thoroughly briefed here on Korean (Borean) operation.

He has had some experience with twenty-four groups in Seoul. We believe he will be a valuable addition to your staff.
