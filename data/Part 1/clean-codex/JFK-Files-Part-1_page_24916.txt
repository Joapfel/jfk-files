Dispatch. To: Chief, UNITSKY, attention Victor D. UNITSKY. By: Chief, WJ Division, Office of Finance. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Subject: Admin/Income Tax—Request for Direct Assessment of Cover Tax Liability for A.T.N. P.O. Box: References Edward G. TICHEBORN.

Action required: Per part 3.

References: A. DIR 93148, dated 26 February 1966. B. MEXT-6266, dated 14 February 1966. C. MEXT-6062, dated 27 January 1965. D. HATT-6033, dated 14 December 1965. E. IMAS-1128, dated 10 May 1965. F. HATT-5226, dated 24 February 1965. G. HAMS-3952, dated 12 January 1965. H. HATT-5111, dated 17 December 1964.

1. Furnished herewith is a request for direct assessment of Cover Tax Liability form, dated 30 March 1966, for Edward G. TICHEBORN. The forms were left by Victor D. UNITSKY for completion by TICHEBORN, since TICHEBORN was not in Mexico City during UNITSKY's visit.

2. The TICHEBORN tax case was discussed with UNITSKY, who was made aware of the multitude of problems. The series of cable correspondence in regard to the location of TICHEBORN's 1964 tax returns and check, the final cable of which, Reference A, stated that they could be located in a folder titled “Cover Taxes,” was of no avail in locating the missing documents. A thorough search of Station files failed to uncover them.

3. It is hoped that UNITSKY can effect an assessment for 1964 as well as 1965 taxes as a solution to the matter. Also, it would be appreciated if UNITSKY could review the reference concerning the 1963 taxes to ascertain if there is any further action required.

WILLARD C. CURTIS.

Attachment: As stated above, hand carried.

Distribution: Chief, KUVST, with attachment; Chief, VMD, without attachment; Office of Finance, without attachment.

Cross-reference to dispatch symbol and number: HATT-6453. Date: 31 March 1966.
