Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, Paris; COS, Vienna. From: Chief SA, via Chief, WE and Chief, EE.

Subject: REDWOOD/LE GROND Semen Spiridon PCHORILYJ (POBORILYJ), also Bezen PCHORILIW, 201-289249, and Jozef KOZAK. Aka Csyp KOZAK.

Action required: See below.

1. Attached for your information is an FBI report on an interview with Andrij DIDKOWSKYJ, contact of Semen POBORILYJ and correspondent of Antin NYKYPORUK.

2. The FBI has requested any information available in our files or through LIBRARY sources concerning KOZAK's current activities and contacts with the Soviets. The information on KOZAK in the last paragraph on page 3 and the top of page 4 of the attachment is the latest information we have on KOZAK. This same information was received from Paris in CP8-55817 of 7 December 1960 and gave KOZAK's address as 26 rue Gaston Jaillon, Vesines Chaleste, Loiret.

3. To Paris: We would appreciate any information available on KOZAK's current activities and contacts. You may query liaison.

4. To Vienna: The interview with DIDKOWSKYJ contains a statement that KOZAK left the camp in Salzburg for France with NYKYPORUK in 1948 or 1949. Other information indicates that KOZAK came to France in 1946 from Munich. We would appreciate your checking Station and liaison files for any information on KOZAK, including biographic data and the date of his departure for France (or Munich). Please send a copy of your reply to Paris.

Doris M. Killgo

Paul K. Stockwood

Distribution: Original and 2 copies to Paris with attachments; 2 copies to Vienna with attachments.

Date typed: 10 September 1962. Date dispatched: 23 September 1962. Dispatch symbol and number: OPPM-38223. Headquarters file number: CS COPY.
