(Counter-intelligence work among Soviet specialists in Ethiopia.) The chief of the 2nd Chief Directorate agreed and the question was almost decided, but at the last moment the personnel department of KGB did not agree. The reasons were the case of 1954 (illness and use of the passport for “cover”) and a check at my place of residence (some agents reported that I drank and, on this basis, had quarrels with my wife).

I was working in the 1st Department until 1962. In January 1962 I was appointed again to the 7th Department as chief of the 1st Section (work against tourists from the USA and Canada).

In December 1959 I received the rank of captain.

When I began to work in the 7th Department, I knew that soon I must be promoted to deputy chief of the department, when a place became free—the deputy chief of the department, BALDIN, was preparing to go to work in East Germany.

In July 1962 I was appointed deputy chief of the 7th Department (the chief of the department was CHELNOKOV), and I worked there until January 18, 1964.

During my work in MVD-KGB I did not study at any school; only in 1953–1954 did I attend foreign-language courses of MVD-KGB at St. Kiselni.
