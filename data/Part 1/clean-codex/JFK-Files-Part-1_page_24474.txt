LORRIS Draft of Round-up on Settlement.

Attention: BOSCH.

Report: OELA-47,233. 5 January 1965.

1. It is noted in reference, a question was raised during one of LORRIS's bi-weekly meetings that I did not attend, of a “round-up in preparation.” The apparent absence was Richard Thomas DIAECK, and I undertook to find out from LORRIS just what was meant.

2. As it turns out, LORRIS was preparing a round-up of information in preparation, rather than on DIAECK. In response to my request what agitated query (due to recent traffic relating to JAPAN, in particular), LORRIS pushed forth with the rough draft of their round-up, which is attached. They point out, however, that this draft is completely unrelated; it has not made the rounds of the responsible political departments and should not be regarded as a final product. In addition, it should be noted that it is intended, when completed, only for circulation within the BOOKEER government—principally in overseas elements—and not for general publication.

3. LORRIS, after expressing the foregoing qualifications, invite any contributions that KURK would like to offer. The substance of the round-up seems to offer little room for controversy, although KURK/ICU may be able to contribute additional information. We also render whether the English-language edition has made any impact in the Japanese Reservoirs.

4. It is assumed that the opposition of personality data will be forwarded to us along with the final draft, or the final edition.

George H. CUMMINGS.

Attachments herewith.

Distribution: Chief, VE; Chief, EUWCL; Chief, LOFETT; Chief, AF; Chief, KURK; COS, Paris.
