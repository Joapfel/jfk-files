Dispatch.

To: Chief, SB Division; Chief, WH Division.

From: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires.

Subject: RYBAT/REDTOP/AERONAUT/AETARGET/TECHS air-conditioner plugs.

References: A. Buenos Aires 0624, 15 May 1968. B. Director 01179, 19 May 1968.

1. Attached please find the following (we have marked each in accordance with our lettering):

A. Two plugs which were actually cut off of the REDTOP air-conditioners by José Castagna. You will note that one of these plugs is fused together to a local adaptor. This adaptor is the type utilized by the local FJSTEALERS on those air-conditioners which still have original plugs.

B. A locally produced plug which is utilized by the FJSTEALERS to replace the original plugs (A above). This locally produced plug (B) was actually taken by AMSTALK-1 from José Castagna's workshop.

C. A locally produced plug which, though slightly larger than (B) above, can be placed on the target air-conditioners.

2. In accordance with Reference B, we hope Headquarters can install an audio device in either of the above three plugs or plug/adaptor units.

Attachment: As stated, h/w.

Distribution: 2 - C/SB w/att h/w; 2 - C/WH w/o att.

Cross reference to dispatch symbol and number: HABA-19,520. Date: 28 May 1968. NOS file number: 74-120-221.
