TYPIC QUAXE QUTIMID

1. On arrival in Madrid 28 October from Havana, the Spanish consul general, Joaquin (Thomas) Garcia, told QUAXE-1 that an unidentified INRA official was assassinated around 20 October. During the same week, Thomas' chauffeur was in the vicinity of Hotel Capri, which is near the MININT building, and heard shooting. One or more shots struck the consul's car; the consul was not with the chauffeur. The chauffeur was not injured. One of the “revolutionaries” approached the consul's car, and the chauffeur was threatened by a revolutionary who was carrying a submachine gun. The revolutionary said he had just killed one person and demanded to be driven out of the area. The chauffeur drove the individual to the outskirts of the city and returned to the Spanish Embassy. Thomas said that in recent weeks such events had been happening with increasing frequency.

2. Above is an interim report. Thomas promised to give details to QUAXE-1 before returning to Havana.
