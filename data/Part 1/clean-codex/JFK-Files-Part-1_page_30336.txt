DD/S 65-3075

27 September 1985

Memorandum for: Director of Security

Subject: Commendation

1. The Director was pleased with the attached letter from Mr. Rowley in which he expressed deep appreciation for the excellent and dedicated assistance rendered to the U.S. Secret Service during the past year. The Director wishes to continue this cooperation and assistance.

2. I appreciate very much this kind of performance which reflects credit to the U.S. Air Agency and I compliment you and your staff for another job well done.

Ad: Letter did 13 September 60 to DCI: Mr. Rowley, re: Appreciation

Deputy Director for Support
