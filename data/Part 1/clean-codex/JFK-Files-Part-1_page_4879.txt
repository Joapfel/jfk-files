1. WKSCARLET-3 (former unilateral asset) contacted Station 11 February 1976 to advise he and WSKCARLET-1 plan travel to Santiago, Buenos Aires, and possibly Miami during the week of 15 February. The purpose is investigation of the attempted letter bombing of the Cuban ambassador in Caracas.

2. The bombing incident surfaced when Cuban Ambassador Norberto Hernandez Curbelo reported to WKSCARLET about two months ago the alleged receipt of a bomb. WKSCARLET-10 deactivated the device, which was contained in a large Manila envelope bearing the return address Dr. Gustavo A. Vollmer, WKSCARLET-10. The former WKSCARLET chief is a security consultant to Vollmer, and he has retained WKSCARLET-63. A former WKSCARLET officer now operating a commercial investigations service is determining who used Vollmer's name. WKSCARLET-3, a Cuban exile, retains significant contacts with extremist exiles and has regular correspondence with Orlando.
