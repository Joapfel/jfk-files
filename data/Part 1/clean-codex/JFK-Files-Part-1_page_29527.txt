12 May 1961

Reorder from: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division

Subject: Recommendation for Awards, Cakes of Mr. William Robertson and Mr. Gruyere Lynch

1. The purpose of this recommendation is to recommend each candidate for subject employees.

2. In recent parliamentary operations against Cuba, Mr. Lynch and Mr. Robertson served as officers of the Central Intelligence Agency vessels FLINTIL and BARBARA &c. respectively. Both of these employees, in the course of extremely hazardous operations, repeatedly exposed themselves to fire by opposing land, sea and air forces. Their fearlessness and skilled leadership enabled indigenous forces to conduct an exalted landing under the most difficult conditions, and their courageous determination to keep their vessels in position to support the operations, although under heavy air attack, was in keeping with the best traditions of the American people at war. Furthermore, their repeated landings, in person, on a hostile shore for the purpose of rescuing Cuban survivors of the invasion force, was a demonstration of extraordinary valour. Their exemplary conduct throughout the combat action was above and beyond the call of duty.

3. Military personnel performing in combat in such a manner would be eligible for the highest decorations for heroism.

4. In view of the extraordinary heroism displayed by Mr. Robertson and Mr. Lynch, I strongly recommend that they be awarded an appropriate commutation, and, in addition, a cash bonus award of five thousand dollars ($5,000.00) each.

J. Inkling  
Colonel, U. S. Marine Corps  
Date of: 11/1/74

Approved: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division

Concern: Acting (OSSCOM)
