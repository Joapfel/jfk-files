Classified message. Date: 22 May 1962. WLB. To: Director. From: Frankfurt. Action: C/AF 3 (AF Cable Desk notified and copy slotted 1450, 22 May). Info: ADDP, C/C1, CDC, C/EE 3, S/C 2. Routing.

L-DEC-OP-THA. Det L. Department of Opinion. 22 May 1962. May 22, 1851Z 1962. Operational immediate. IN 39135.

OPIM DIR LEOP INFO FROB CITY FRAN 0495

RYSAT WIROGUE CABEZONE

Ref A FRAN 0404 (37347). B DIR 11029 (OUT 83947). C LEOP 3939 (35915).

1. WIROGUE arrived FRAN 1400 22 January. Met by Stephan R. Lacklen who posed as administrative officer interested in determining reason for travel Germany. W said we here on official business Congolese government and had documentation to prove it. V non in blown CABEZONE safe house.

2. Lacklen able acquire passport and all other documentation, except birth certificate and Austrian driver's license, under pretext need photograph. Rapport excellent but anticipate serious problem when V told we will not return documents.

3. W interested remain in clandestine work but stated unwillingness remain Germany. Absolutely no commitments made to V. Air ticket (in our possession) is round-trip LEOP-FRAN-LEOP. Intends return LEOP after completion of mission.

4. Details re above information follows.
