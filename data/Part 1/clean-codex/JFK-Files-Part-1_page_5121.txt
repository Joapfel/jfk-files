1. Believe subject para 3(A) ref identical to Ernestino (Ernesto) Cecilio Ambreu Horta (201-327664), born 11 November 1924 in Amarillas, Matanzas, Cuba. Cuban chemist with long association with Movimiento de Recuperacion Revolucionaria (MRR—Cuban counter-revolutionary group) during early 1960s. Was MRR coordinator in Matanzas Province, where he organized guerrilla movement prior to Bay of Pigs invasion. Managed to escape arrest/capture by Castro forces through asylum in Venezuelan Embassy, Havana. First arrived in U.S. at Miami on 10 August 1961 after two-month stay in Venezuela. POA granted 8 August 1962 for intended use as member MRR infiltration team into Oriente, Cuba, but Miami Station decided subject more familiar with Matanzas Province than target area. POA reinstated 27 June 1963 to allow basic tradecraft and paramilitary training with view towards making subject training instructor for MRR cadre. In October 1963 subject traveled to Nicaragua, where he established two MRR camps and trained.
