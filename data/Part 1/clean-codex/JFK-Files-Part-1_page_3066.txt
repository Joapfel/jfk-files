Dispatch.

To: Chief, Soviet Bloc Division.  
Info: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division.  
From: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires.  
Subject: REPTOP Arikice—ALFARAY contact with Lev YABANIN.

Action required—references:

1. Forwarded under separate cover for your information is a copy of a BIO/CULAR report of 8 January describing a conversation between Ivan Gavrilovich ALFARAY and soccer player Lev YABANIN (DPOB: 22 October 1969, Moscow; passport 331432). YABANIN and the rest of the Moscow Iynama team had just arrived at Excela Airport on Varig Flight 845 from Rio and were departing for Santiago on BAD Flight 955 after a layover in Argentina of three and one-half hours. The team was on a Latin American tour covering Chile, Peru, Colombia, and Ecuador.

2. During the conversation, EOB officer ALFARAY and YABANIN, whom we believe is EOB, showed that they are fairly close friends. They also mentioned mutual friends: Ivan Gavrilovich ALFARAY (201-731434), a suspected EOB officer formerly stationed in Argentina; and Vitaliy Borisovich BEBANIN, the former Consul/EOS officer in Chile.

Tim T. MARGANINS.

Attachments: U/A/C as stated.

Distribution: 3 - C/WEB, with attachment; 3 - C/WEB, without attachment.

Cross reference to dispatch: SABA-20280. Date: 8 February 1969. EGB file number: 201-336971.
