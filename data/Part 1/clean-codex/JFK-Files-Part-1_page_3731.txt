231327Z APR 69. CITE WH MIAMI 5427 (NEBECKER ACTING). Director, Managua. 3 Apr 69. IN 28439.

1. During recent informal session with LNYAPA Miami representative, he asked if WOFIRM still had operational interest in Manuel Artime. On being told no local interest, he said Artime is now in the export-import business, has contacts with the Nicaraguan military, and is using NICA Airlines to ship export-import merchandise. Said Artime had shipped medical supplies among other items to Nicaragua.

2. LNYUMA representative obviously suspects Artime is involved in anti-Castro activities in Nicaragua which may be backed by WOFIRM. At present he undecided whether to open an active file on Artime or to drop the matter. Seeking station advice.

3. Request comments.
