2 June 1948

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Newton S. Miller (Miler) and Nell C. S. Miller (Miler)

1. Newton Miller (Miler) arrived in Shanghai on 3 January 1947 under an 18-month agreement.

2. Nell C. Stine Miller (Miler) arrived in Shanghai on 7 June 1946, also under an 18-month agreement. The subjects were married 8 March 1948.

3. The subjects will be returned on TDY for 90 days, upon completion of which they will be expected to execute new 24-month agreements, and they will be returned to their permanent stations.

4. No per diem is to be paid to the subjects while on leave.
