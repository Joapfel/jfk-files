Conein, Lucien Emile.

Born in Paris in 1943. Prints found. Divorced wife, Rose Marion — September 21, 1941; divorce 3/31/42. Brought to the U.S. as a child; naturalized in 1947. Date of birth: November 29, 1919, Paris. ASN 01322768. Living in France, 1919–1925. Entered the U.S. on 9/19/25; naturalized 8/1/42. Divorced.

American studies, Kansas City, 1935–36. Type seller, 1936–39. First marriage, 1940–41. Two patents, U.S. applications, 1941.

Overseas. Enlisted as a soldier in the French Army; attended USA, September 1941. Applied for forfeiture, OSS, June 1943. U.S. records in Army and U.S. states: not applicable.

July 1948. Monique Veber on April 23, 1946. Richard Helms required, by order of 10/2, information on relatives by U.S. with permit. Divorced February 1948. Two, December 23, 1947.

Entered on duty with CIA in May 1951.
