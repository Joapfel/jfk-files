2. ESMOOSE-1 reported that on early morning 28 March ESMARVEL-1 alerted special commando unit of Guatemalan Army (SCUGA) to be prepared for important operation. He said information had been received that a group of thirty Castro Cubans planned to enter Guatemala from Mexico and their arrival tied in with a wave of terrorism and sabotage which reportedly was to take place 31 March. ESMARVEL-1 did not indicate source of above information. He said that reinforced SCUGA teams would be sent to the departments of San Marcos and Huehuetenango (KUEHUETENANGO) on the Mexican border, with a backup team in the department of Quezaltenango.
