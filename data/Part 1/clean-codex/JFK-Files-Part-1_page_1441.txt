CCS recommends deletion of that part of the last sentence, following the semicolon of the first paragraph on page 10 of Chapter XI, which identifies Camp Perry as the site of Agency training courses. Camp Perry is an Agency training installation (under DOD cover), and while its CIA affiliation has been alleged numerous times in the press, no confirmation of this affiliation has been made by the Agency. Mr. Phillips' comments as a former senior Agency officer can be interpreted as constituting official acknowledgement of the CIA/Camp Perry relationship.
