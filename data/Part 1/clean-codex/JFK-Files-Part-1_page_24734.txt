SB/X/IWH.

1. In rechecking Ref A story with IDEN A, DUBRIDE 20 learned Soviet involved was not Nikolay Romanov but KG 8 officer Nikolay Sergeyevich Leonov, who was here on TDY from 23 April to 17 July 1969. However, IDEN A clarified Ref A statement on “quiet” PNG action by stating “it is presumed” that diplomatic note requesting Leonov’s departure was passed to Soviet Embassy. IDEN A also stated that reason for PNG action was based on report which revealed that on 8 July 1969 Leonov delivered speech at luncheon meeting of FLN committee.

IDEN A added that this report, which went to Army Intelligence, provided confirmation of his political activity in Peru.

2. Station attempting confirm quiet PNG action. We know DUBRIDE kept close watch on Leonov, particularly after receiving station traces on him. SIE interest probably aroused as result of meeting between Nadelhoffer and DULAMA/2 on 4 July 1969, when latter’s CS copy of PNG was given Leonov paper. Quiet PNG discussed as possible course of secret.
