Classified message.

Date: William Johnson. SUN: CI/OPS/FB. SK: 5918. Date: 26 November. 3-22-67 to 3-29-67.

To: By telephone camera. From: Director. CONF: C/C1. Info: DCI, D/DCI, DOP, ADDP, C/FE 6, VR. RIBAT KOREK UJIM.

To: Stanley. Cite DIR: 85059(N). Info: Cambrera (Cabrera).

1. Reference ALUSNA Cabrera cable CHG-015 of 26 November, which became AD to Secret.

2. Cable details telephone conversation, including verbatim text remarks, possible connection of Soviet government with assassination.

3. Cable details telephone call of 15 October 1962.

4. Cable plans any contact or liaison contact with Polish driver and results of such contact. Use SEC ID-5 assigns responsibility for this activity to Kurakin.

5. All aspects of assassination have highest investigation and operational priority.

C/S comment: Assist. ALUSNA received telephone call on 23 November from person claiming to be Polish driver of Russian vehicle. Information touched on several areas, including possible Soviet connection supplying money to an individual in the U.S. to assassinate the President, possible Soviet implication in the U.S.S. Thresher incident, etc.

ADDP, assisting officer.

Chief FS, cooperating officer.
