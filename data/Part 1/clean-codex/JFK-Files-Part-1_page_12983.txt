5 JUN 68. IN 25149.

STATION CARACAS ESTIMATES AMICE-31 WOULD REQUIRE $500 PER MONTH PLUS HOUSING AND EDUCATION ALLOWANCES OVER AND ABOVE JMDEVIL OFFER IN ORDER TO ENABLE HIM TO LIVE EVEN MODESTLY HERE. ATTEMPTING TO COVER MONETARY SUPPLEMENT OF THIS MAGNITUDE COULD BE DIFFICULT. WHILE WE DO HAVE INTEREST IN MONITORING JMDEVIL, IT IS RELATIVELY LOW PRIORITY IN TERMS OF LOCAL REQUIREMENTS AND STATION WILL TAKE IT ON ONLY IF HQS BELIEVES IT CONSTITUTES OVERRIDING NATIONAL PRIORITY. PERHAPS EVEN MORE IMPORTANT THAN COSTS AND COVER CONSIDERATIONS, HOWEVER, IS BALPA.
