(2) In a document from a usually reliable source dated 27 September 1951, Louis Fischall, an American citizen born in Philadelphia on 2 February 1933, was reported as staying at the Hotel Indochina in Borneo from 3 April to 23 April 1951. He was accompanied by his alleged wife, Dora Fischer, born in Latvia on 2 January 1930. He was in possession of passenger No. 35133, dated 1 November 1950, and his wife had No. 15733, dated 23 February 1948, both born in Washington. Dora was reported as departing for Florence on 7 April 1951, at which time he was joined by her. According to hotel personnel, Fischer was a commercial [agent]. He claimed to be a journalist, but there were some doubts as to this claim. When leaving Borneo, he left his trunk with hotel personnel, and it was later picked up by an unidentified friend. In his hotel, he had contacts with fellow Americans. The purpose of his trip to Italy was unclear.

(3) A report dated 19 September 1951 stated that one GRABHICK and wife, and CIVARI and wife, spent the Easter holiday of 1951 at Verona, near Milano, together with the “Wall-inwan” anti-Stalinist European, Louis Fischer. This report also stated that GRABHICK had Communist affiliation but that CIVARI gave no indication of being aware of this.

(4) In a report dated 22 December 1951, Louis Fischer was included among delegates to a conference of the International Labor Organization to be held in December 1952 in Vienna, India. This report also stated that it was presumed that Fischer was from France, but that this was not definitely known.

(5) In a document dated 20 January 1951, Louis Fischer was reported to have made contributions to the SOE, a political organization composed of the [believed] segments of the resistance movement. SOE carries on both court and parliamentary activities directed at eventual overtures of the Soviet Politburo. His activities in this organization have been firmly denied by Fischer, and indirectly by Harvard University through the Institute for the Study of the Soviet Union, which is staffed wholly by SOE personnel.
