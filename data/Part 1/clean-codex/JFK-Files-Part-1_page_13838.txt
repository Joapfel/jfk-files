90. Telephone Contacts

a) Lorenzo DELAY, number — Mexican delegate in Mexico — sent Gustavo Carrasco to see Subject. Carrasco was a Venezuelan instructor in the use of farm machinery and the son of a revolutionary group in Venezuela who could not return to his country and who was practically discharged from his teaching job at Charingo because of his ideology. (LIEVOC, 6 August 1961)

b) Guillermo TRUJO (Cviedo) of Prensa Latina (Irensa Latina). (LIEVOC, 14 September 1961)

c) Humberto H. TRUJO (Cviedo) of Prensa Latina (Irensa Latina). (LIEVOC, 22 September 1961)

d) Lupe LNU — Subject arranged to meet her at her home. (LIEVOC, 29–30 September 1961)

e) Roberto LNU — Esther, Subject’s girlfriend and later wife, informed Subject that she did not know Roberto’s address. (LIENOL, 2 October 1961)

f) Some “compañeros ferrocarrileros” asked for Subject, who was not in. (LIEVOC, 7 October 1961)

g) Seen with Ramiro DELPIDO Perez coming out of the Cuban Embassy. (LIENAC, 19 October 1961)

h) One VILLAR, first name unknown, called the Cuban Embassy for Roberto FRINAZ, who was unknown to Teresa FROLINA, who answered the phone. Villar-Ariza said Subject told him to call. (LIEVOC, 17 October 1961)

i) Subject was driven home from an unspecified function in a car with license 86. In 1960–61, license D-86 was registered to Vladimir S. MAROVIN, a Soviet military attaché.

j) Subject told SARAHINE (Vladilen) of the Soviet Embassy that he had good news for him and wanted to come over to tell him. SARAHINE preferred to go to see Subject and said he would come in a half hour. (LIEVOC, 9 November 1961)

k) Subject is a contact of Norman Lalonde. (W. C. CURTIS memo, 29 November 1961)

l) Edmundo JARACIO of Prensa Latina (Irensa Latina) told Subject that a Guatemalan named Farrelilla was waiting for him. (LIEVOC, 14 December 1961)

m) Enrique BORRES of Prensa Latina (Irensa Latina) came to see Subject at the Embassy. (LIEVOC, 11 January 1962)

n) ESREJSL, first name unknown, of the Sindicato Mexicano de Electricistas, told Subject he had a letter from Federico LND, and inside the letter was one for Subject. (FREQUENT CONTACT) (LIEVOC, 17 January 1962)

o) One Jose Arturo de la CANA insisted on getting in touch with Subject about the information bulletin. (LIEVOC, 20 January 1962)

p) One JUNTE arrived from Tamaulipas and Tampico and told Subject he had told LOBARDU he would bring money for two round-trip passages. He also wanted photos, bulletins, and other things for Tampico. Subject told him to call at the Embassy to pick them up. (LIEVOC, 20 January 1962)

q) Teodoro ALTAIRANCRODES, president of the new generation of ISTC, who were loyal to the Cuban revolution, wanted materials from the Embassy. Subject asked for his address. (LIEVOC, 29 January 1962)

Costa Rica #57, Miraflores, La Paz, Bolivia. Letter addressed to Rogelio ROQUE F. (LIREC, 12 February 1962)
