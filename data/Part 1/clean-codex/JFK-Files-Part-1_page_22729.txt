29 September 1965, N 62264.

1. Night 28 September, LIFTER-3 said VLT called a meeting of the National Directorate on 21 September, at which LIMITED-1 was present, and told the group it planned to call a press conference on 23 September to expose ODYOKE intelligence activities in Mexico.

2. On 25 September, VLT spoke briefly to a number of officials at JOM about the press release and said that, to gain maximum impact, he added the charge that L-1 and RACIEL HERNANDEZ were threatened. On questioning by L-8, VLT said no new attempts had been made against positive persons but believed some ODYOKE agency possibly had one member of the National Directorate, or probably one Central Committee member, working for it. He added that this type of thing was difficult to uncover and prove.
