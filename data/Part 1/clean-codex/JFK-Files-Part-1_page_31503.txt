24 July 1952

Memorandum for: Chief, IO Division

Subject: G. Popesou-Botosani; J. Radovan; J. Aron

1. This office has no objection, with respect to security, to the employment of the three subject Romanians, who have requested State Department assistance in getting on the ECFT payroll.

2. However, it is recommended that no action be taken on their behalf until the present attempt to settle the refugee situation is completed, in order to avoid any appearance of ECFE's favoring one faction.

Gratian M. Yatsevitch  
Deputy for Policy Coordination, SE
