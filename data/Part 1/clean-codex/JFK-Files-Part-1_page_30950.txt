13 May 1969, IN 56608.

Director. RYBAT ADMIN LITEMPO.

References: A. Director 99663. B. Mexico City 0023 (19 3312).

1. At the time Project LITEMPO was submitted for renewal, it was not known that Curtis was retiring as of 30 June 1969. It is the station’s opinion that if Curtis and Benadum leave simultaneously (1 July 1969), Probert will lack time to build up the relationship which Benadum has had for some twenty years and Curtis for twelve years with this very important intelligence source (around forty percent of station positive intelligence and half CI/CE production) and support the project. Also, since Curtis and Benadum are the only two well known to LITEMPO, their joint departure could be misinterpreted and thereby damaging to the station.

2. Feel Curtis’ departure changes the picture sufficiently to warrant amendment to the LITEMPO project to allow for retention of Benadum.
