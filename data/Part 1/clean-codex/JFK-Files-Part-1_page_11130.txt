340402 Z, Mexico City 9483. Director info: JMWAVE. KAPOK, TYPIC, LITAMIL-9. 4 December 1965.

1. After much soul-searching and lengthy talks with his brother and friends, LITAMIL-9 has decided not to return to Cuba.

A. L-9 feels he has very little time left to provide for old age. He is most interested in actively participating in the growing edible-oil-from-coffee-grounds business established in Mexico by his brother and friends, in which he owns 8 percent of the stock. He has a good chance to become manager of the oil firm if he remains in Mexico.

B. L-9 believes he has no guarantee he could return to Mexico if he went to Cuba. A job in Cuba does not provide the possibility of travel outside the country.

C. Life in Cuba and the resulting mental strain have not allowed L-9, a professional artist, to develop his talent.

D. L-9 is very much aware he is going nowhere career-wise in the Cuban Cultural Ministry. He also realizes he has provided no factual information of the type—military, political, or economic—in which he is really interested.

7 December 1965, 52268.
