Date: 612, 8 January 1961. Classified message.

To. From: Mexico City (NEXICO CITY). Action: MH 5. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/SI, S/C 2.

January 7, 0220Z 1961. IN 27546.

DIR CITE NEXI 7055.  
REF DIR 18890 (OUT 55500).

1. DAVID HALPERIN IN LIFEAT SEBORER TRAFFIC 22 DEC 63.

On 23 December LIFEAT informed he at Nuevo Hotel Waldorf with two unnamed classmates and wife of one classmate. Planned see Anna Seborer 23 December at latter's home. Re: surveillance subject.

2. LIFEAT take above dates given local ODENVY 23 and 27 December 1963.

3. Per ref station asked local ODENVY if physical surveillance or other station support desired. No. Station opinion ODENVY has surveillance subject.

End of message.

C/S comment: Stated ODENVY endeavoring develop more information on itinerary to travels of David Halperin.
