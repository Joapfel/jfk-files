11 April 1950

Leader: Mr. Daker  
Subject: PDQUICK and TFPIKER

1. I refer to the attached memorandum of 7 April 1950 addressed to you by Mr. Glaser.

2. I refer particularly to paragraph 4, sentence 2, of the above memorandum and to FRAN 117, paragraph 1, B.

3. My suggestion in this regard is as follows:

I suggest that we do not desire for PDQUICK a Rhino circular to embassies similar to the circular supporting QIVORY, because a circular of that sort is not essential and would unnecessarily invite attention to the U.S. interest in PDQUICK.

4. A copy of the QIVORY circular is attached for your information.

J. C. Debry
