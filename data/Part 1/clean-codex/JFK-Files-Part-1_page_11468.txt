To: Director, Guatemala City.

References: A. Director 36584. B. Guatemala City 9517 (GNO2560). C. HMMA 35522, paragraph 5.

Section No. 06501.

WHT, FEB: LIRING-3 w/c!

1. Station wishes to reiterate once more its reluctance to somehow try to free LIRING-3 from jail in Guatemala City by going to liaison and exposing his WOFIRM connection. Mexico City Station is in agreement with reference B’s statement that more time should be allowed to see if LIRING-3 will not be released in the not-too-distant future without indicating WOFIRM interest. As stated in reference C, such indication, if it leaked (and Station assumes this possible in Guatemala) or if it attracted suspicion of Guatemalan rebels, Mexican leftists, or perpetrators, would render further operational exploitation of LIRING-3 and LIRING-4 problematic. It might even prompt Guatemalan rebels to take reprisals against LIRING-3 and LIRING-4. Furthermore, Station is not at all sure that freeing LIRING-3 through intervention by liaison might not jeopardize Station security.
