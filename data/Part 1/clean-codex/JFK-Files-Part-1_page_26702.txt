19 May 1965. IN 71360. 192138Z.

Priority. DIR INFO: Rome, Mila, Carla. CITE MEXI 3071.

15 May IFFAT coverage: Carlo Coccioli reported going to New York 28 May via Eastern flight 500, arriving New York 05 hours. Coccioli said he was advising certain friends in New York of his arrival. Later told Victor Rico Bahan he was returning to Mexico 31 May.

2. Same day, LIFE reported Coccioli reserved a room at an unidentified hotel for 6 June in the name Mr. Sarler Orengo (phone: 4, Wenchman). Orengo arriving in Mexico by air from New York at 11:51 on 6 June. Coccioli told the hotel Orengo would definitely arrive, that Orengo was on an official mission, and that his program, including travel, had been planned for some time.
