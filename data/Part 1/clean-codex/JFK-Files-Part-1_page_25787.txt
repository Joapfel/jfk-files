17 November 1955

Memorandum for: M/S—Projects Branch

Subject: Wedding Gift for Raymond R. Jones (ps), Contract Agent

1. It is requested that the Mexico City Station be authorized to give Raymond R. Jones (ps), as a wedding gift, two airplane tourist-class tickets from Mexico City to San Francisco and Washington, D.C., and return to Mexico City. Although this trip may appear to be a generous and unusual wedding gift, the station considers it more than justified in view of Jones’s outstanding performance of his duties for the station and his future value as a principal agent. Jones has been working for the station since February 1955.

2. The station estimates that the cost of the plane tickets will be $600.00. The LRTICK Project, which covers Jones, can absorb this added expense.

R. S. Daughren  
Chief, LTR/III
