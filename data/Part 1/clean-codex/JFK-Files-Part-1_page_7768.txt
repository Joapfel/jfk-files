Valeri V. Kostikov, I have not met him, but I do not know him much.

Valentin Longinov, in charge of consular affairs; his wife Alexandra has been a skating star. Both are very nice, speak very good Spanish, and fair English. Their daughter Ekaterina Kirina, sixteen years old, very sophisticated, came here for a holiday. The only child of that age we have ever seen here. They have another child, who is in Moscow. Both have been at our house for a party. In general, Soviet people come alone, leaving their wives behind.

Dmitri Polygov, wife Natalia, have a small child born here. He has been writing a book on the Mexican revolution. He is a friendly, good friend. He was introduced to me by Boris Kazantsev, who invited me for lunch for the occasion. When we go to the Soviet embassy, both he and Tchernykhov are always very attentive.

I know several other people from the Soviet embassy, but really I could place them together with their names.
