Contribution by DC-COM. Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Congo (Leopoldville). Date of information: 21 August 1965. Report No. TDCS-314/11599-85. Date distributed: 23 August 1965.

Subject: Fifth Commando pay problems.

Place and date acquired: Congo, Albertville (21 August 1965). Field report No. ACA 29. Source and appraisal: a competent American observer from Lt. Colonel Michael Hoare.

1. On 21 August 1965, Lt. Col. Michael Hoare, commanding officer of the Fifth Commando, stated he had just received a message from Major General Mobutu (MCButu) to the effect that Mobutu (MCButu) planned to come to Albertville 24 August to discuss Fifth Commando pay problems. Hoare noted, however, that Mobutu (MCButu) frequently did not keep his promises and therefore Hoare was sending his deputy, Major Alistair Wicks (ALLSTAR WICKS), to Leopoldville (LEOPOLDOVILLE). Wicks on 23 August will present Mobutu (MCButu) with pay authorization forms for Mobutu's (MCButu's) signature. Hoare said if Mobutu (MCButu) or other appropriate officials had not signed the pay authorizations.
