This mortgage, made the 2nd day of March, nineteen hundred and seventy-eight, between and husband and wife, both residents of the State of New York. Roush-Walker Company, 48 Alabama Street, S.W., Atlanta, Georgia, is the mortgagee. The indebtedness is $167,429.88. The mortgaged premises are at 163 East 71st Street, New York, New York.
