Subject: Meetings with ARBITRATOR

1. Subject was met in Room 603, Power Smith Hotel, Washington, at 10:00 hours, 18 March 1968 [19 April 1968]. The purpose of the meeting was to discuss the problems Subject ran into at the Argentine Consulate in New York. He said that one of the problems of the New York office was that he presented a letter of introduction from the Argentinean Consulate in New York. This letter was relayed by phone on 17 April, and resulted in Subject's return to Washington. During the interim between the 17 April phone call and the 18th [19 April] meeting, it was decided to have Subject make an effort to secure the visa from the Argentine Embassy in Washington. Accordingly, Subject called the Embassy on 19 April and was informed that no visas were granted by the Embassy, but that he could get one from the Consulate in Baltimore. Subject called the Baltimore office and was informed that if he showed up prior to 1500 hours, a visa could be issued without waiting. Subject asked about requirements such as a letter of introduction from the Argentinean diplomatic installation, and was told that there was no such requirement.

Following the call, Subject was told to take a bus to Baltimore and make application for the visa. Upon receipt of the visa, he will return to Washington, and will be contacted at his hotel room between 1500 and 1600 hours.

While in New York, Subject purchased his flight tickets to Buenos Aires as follows:

Pan Am (Panam) Flight 203, departing from Kennedy Airport at 2100 hours, 21 April 1968. This information has already been passed to Mr. Louis Winchowski, Alien Affairs Staff, for coordination with INS.

Subject reported his expenses to date and noted that he only had $150.20 left of the $1,000.00 advance made by INS. He pointed out that at the present rate he would arrive in U.S.A. with little cash, and might draw the attention of customs because of this. Accordingly, a $200.00 advance will be drawn for Subject and turned over to him on the morning of 20 April 1968.
