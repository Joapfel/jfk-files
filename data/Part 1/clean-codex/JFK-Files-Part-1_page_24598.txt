Issued message. Secretariat dissemination. Person/unit notified.

Secret 25204Z April 69, CITE LIMA 4575. Director info Bogota, Redtop, Aekick. Reference: A. Bogota 2047 (CN 4/5/92); B. Director 91987, Nikolay Sergeyevich.

1. Leonov arrived Lima from Bogota via APSA Flight 61 on 23 April. According to DUHIKE surveillance, Leonov was met by DULAUREL-1 and family, Ricardo Tello Neyra and wife Beatrice, and one unidentified subject. Leonov was taken to Hotel Savoy, where he checked into room 525. Early afternoon of the 23rd, Leonov with the above persons went to DULAUREL-1's home, where they remained until early evening. Leonov left DULAUREL-1's home in company of all the above persons, took a drive along the ocean, and had dinner before being dropped off at Savoy with the unidentified subject.

2. On the morning of 24 April, Leonov visited the Foreign Ministry with DULAUREL-1 and husband and met the above unidentified subject at the Foreign Ministry. During the afternoon of the 24th and the morning of the 25th, Leonov was observed with the above unidentified subject and, on separate occasions, with Gustavo Valcarcel and Raul Acosta Salas.

3. According to DULINDEN-8, DULAUREL-1 instructed him not to go to the airport to meet the new visitor, as no photos were to be taken of.
