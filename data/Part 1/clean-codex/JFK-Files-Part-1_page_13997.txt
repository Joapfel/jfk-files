Dispatch.

Office symbol: C/DO. Date: Gordon Jorgensen. Releasing officer's signature: F. M. Brown.

To: Chief of Station, WH/Miami. Info: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Submit: Typic/DOMOPS/AMBEDEW. Action requested: Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez.

1. Reference your telephone request of 24 June 1969 requesting a photograph of Rogelio RODRIGUEZ (Eugelio RODRIGUEZ) which is better than the one appearing in the Cuban Mugbook, it is believed that subject of your request is Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez, whose photograph is included in the Mugbook. There is no photograph of Rogelio RODRIGUEZ.

3. AMBEDEW-1 has been shown and has positively identified the attached photo as Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez, a DGI officer in Section III who was known to AMBEDEW-1 as “Rogelio.” A-1 last saw Rogelio in Paris on 21 November 1968, at which time Rogelio was en route to Conakry on what A-1 thought to be an important mission. It appeared that someone in Conakry could not leave until Rogelio arrived. A-1 understood that Rogelio had been in the UN Mission in New York, or in Canada, in 1964–65.

4. The above information may be passed to LNERGO.

Olive S. ECKHOLM.

Distribution: 2 – COS, New York, att. h/w; 1 – COS, WH/Miami, w/o att.

Cross reference to: 19-6-139/4. Dispatch symbol and No.: UNDW-2947. Date: Sep 30 1968. Originating: S JUL 1968. HCS FDP number: 201-332665.

Distribution: AN X – DO/I/B2; WH/COG/OPS (Betty); Originator: David A. Phillips; Gordon Jorgensen; AB; IN – RID.
