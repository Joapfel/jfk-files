United States Marine Corps

Certificate of Honorable and Satisfactory Service in World War II

This is to certify that Tenent, Harrison, Pa., has satisfactorily completed active service and is this date discharged.

Entered the United States Marine Corps: 11 March 43.

Began active service: 11 March 43.

Upon relief from active duty held rank of: Second Lieutenant.

Died June 26, 1946.

Robert R. Sedgwick, Capt., USNCR.
