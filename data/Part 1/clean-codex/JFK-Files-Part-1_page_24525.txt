For Yu. Kuznetsov

International Information

Lima (Lina), 3 May 1969

Peruvians Are Becoming More Determined to Defend Their Independence.

From ancient days, there has existed a custom in Latin America to convene in the time of great danger open meetings by representation of all the estates to adopt special measures. So in times of attacks by pirates, of large-scale domestic disturbances, unusual natural calamities, and at present in connection with the threat of economic intervention in Peru as a revenge by the US for the nationalization of the property of the American oil firm International Petroleum Company, this ancient custom has been resurrected.

Not very long ago such a meeting took place in the largest Peruvian port, Callao, where the participants adopted a “Declaration To Proclaim The Economic Independence of Peru.” It was pointed out there that the people of Peru will not abandon their just demands and that they will achieve their economic independence in the same manner as they won their political freedom.

Yesterday an all-people’s gathering took place in Arequipa, the second largest city in the country. The public court dealing with the imperialist policies of the U.S.A. lasted approximately 4 hours. It has been a long time since Arequipa heard such passionate and patriotic speeches. The speakers petitioned the government to organize a general campaign of protest against USA policies to get the people out on the squares and streets, to arm them if possible to repel any attempts by the USA to carry out military or economic attacks.
