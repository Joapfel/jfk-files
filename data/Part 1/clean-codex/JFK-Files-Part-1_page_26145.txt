EPA 7451, H.D. Report on Victor Rico Galan's statements from the meeting of the Chamber of Commerce, July 1963.

June (3).

A-198, 19 August 1963. Victor Rico Galan, in the 21 August 1963 issue of Siempre, answered the charges of Romulo Sanchez Mireles, presiding officer of the Chamber of Commerce, which is Mexico's House of Representatives. Sanchez, before a meeting of the PRI, had attacked those who undermine Mexico's institutions, customs, government, and political leaders. Rico answered that he and thousands of other foreign-born Mexicans are discriminated against.

HONI-SAY.

27 August 1963, evening; 21 August 1963: 15 known Marxist, Communist, or leftist ideology [individuals] appeared at the offices of Gustavo Diaz Ordaz, Minister of Interior, including subject, CS-3/558,382, prominent leftist journalists. The group had made no attempt to make previous contact with Diaz. Later informed his aides to inform visitors that requests for an appointment with him must be in writing and explain the applicant's business, and visitors were to do it immediately. They left consequently, saying they would send Diaz a telegram demanding an audience. Subject, one of the principal organizers and participants in a rally in favor of political prisoners held on 18 August 1963. Rally failed to be held as a result of police intervention. Subject and other leading participants were arrested and released the same day. HONI-SAY, TDCS-3/557,000.

LITEMPO-E.

A-1291, 20 March 1963, of leftist weekly Siempre published an article by subject, regular contributor to the American Embassy, Mexico City, magazine, advocating creation of a new political party to reflect the ideology of former President Lazaro Cardenas, 9 April 1963.
