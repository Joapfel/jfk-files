Subject: CUIS recruitment attempts against EULEX in the U.S.

2. AMSTALK-1 (Identity B) received a recruitment letter in a very sophisticated operation involving a frame of 35 mm film rolled up and concealed in a hollowed-out crucifix on rosary beads sent via his cousin, who came out on the airlift on 11 April 1967. The letter, believed to be from AMGHOST-1, was mildly threatening to AMSTALK-1's family and friends in Cuba and suggested a meeting with the CUIS in Spain or Canada. Local LNERGO was not advised. AMSTALK-1 and the JMWAVE (JMWave) case officer are currently in Madrid on this operation. It is believed that this operation was initiated by AMGHOST-1 for unknown reasons, but the possibility exists that it is to establish a communications channel to WOFACT.

Extracted from report prepared by CI Section, JMWAVE (JMWave) Station, June 1967.
