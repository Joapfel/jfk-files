1. Subject: Diaz-Isalgue, Miguel Angel  
DOB: 14 February 1937  
POD: Matanzas, Cuba (CUBBA)

2. Reason for request: Investigations.

3. The files of this office contain the following information on subject from reports dating from April 1967 to September 1972 from sources of varying reliability.

4. In 1956 subject helped found the Directorio Revolucionario Estudiantil, which helped Castro come to power in Cuba. Subject held numerous executive positions in the Cuban (COBAN) Ministry of Interior in communist Cuba (CUBRA). He was the Interior Minister's personal delegate in Pinar del Rio Province and later in Havana Province. At one time he was head of the Cuban driver's license bureau (BURBEAU).
