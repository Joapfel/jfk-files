February 1970: SIMIC, a London publication of leftist orientation, carried an article mentioning Q/1 by name and stating that he is widely believed to be a CIA agent. Two of the four persons listed as contributors to SIMIC were at the Algiers Palestine Solidarity Meeting, which Q/1 attended in December 1969. They were members of the British delegation representing the Palestine XAN Solidarity Committee (PSC), which was a rival delegation to that led by A. Manchanda, in which Q/1 sat. This M/I, Q/1 believes, was printed in order to discredit the Manchanda-led Palestinian group, which is in competition with the PSC.

8 February 1970: Beirut 6963 (IN 262712). A reliable source reported that during his conversation with Ghassan Kanafani (KANAFANI), the latter mentioned Q/1 and accused him of being a CIA agent. Q/1 believes Kanafani picked up suspicion of Q/1 due to accusations against Q/1 spread in Beirut by Palestinian personalities who were formerly in London and who picked up the story as a result of an accusation leveled against Q/1 in SIMIC (SYNIC).
