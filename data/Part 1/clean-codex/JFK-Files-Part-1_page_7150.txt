United States Government Memorandum

To: Regional Security Officer

From: Francisco Díaz Gómez, Sr., Security Assistant

Subject: Cristobal TORRES Ponce — Embassy Chauffeur

Date: January 21, 1966

Yesterday noon, Mr. TORRES came up to my office and reported that he was sent to the Russian Embassy in order to pick up Higs. MUELLER’s passport. He was approached by Valentin S. LOGUINOV, 3rd Secretary, who asked him if he would like to go to Russia and if he would like to receive Russian magazines. He also asked if the U.S. Embassy uses to send somebody to follow its employees when they go someplace in town. This individual finally invited Mr. TORRES to have supper with him next Monday, January 24, 1966. They will meet each other at the corner of Insurgentes Sur and Baja California, under the big advertisement “CANADA,” at 1830 hours.

Mr. TORRES also commented that this individual told him that in order to get a visa for Russia, it takes time because they have to call Russia in order to get authorization—the same process the U.S. Embassy follows when the Russian Embassy requests a visa for one of the members of its staff.
