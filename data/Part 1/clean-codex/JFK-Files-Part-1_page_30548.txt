Official Dispatch

Via: Air Pouch  
Dispatch No. 15-05.1

To: Chief of Station  
Date: 13 May 1948  
A Hosti, Chief, Admin Section  
From: Commanding Officer  
Mukden, Manchuria  
Subject: General Personnel  
Specific: Reserve Status

1. In answer to your memorandum dated 29 April, subject as above, the following information is transmitted on two employees at this station:

a. Newton Scott Kiler — Reserve status undetermined due to lack of records of physical examinations. Mr. Kiler states his reserve status will have to remain pending until he can return to the zone of the interior for additional examinations and collection of records.

b. Malcolm Misida — Reserve status undetermined due to lack of records at this station. Mr. Misida states that he is not certain whether he is a member of the enlisted reserve or not. All records pertaining to subject should be on file at the Washington Headquarters.

John R. Lloyd  
Major, Infantry
