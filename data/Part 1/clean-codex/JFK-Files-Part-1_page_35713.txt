Dispatch. Subject: DYVOUR/ADMIN/PERSONNEL—James B. Wilcott Jr. Debriefing—Resignation in field.

Action required: FYI. Wilcott.

1. Arrived PCS at JMWAVE on 26 April 1965 and submitted his resignation at JMWAVE from KUBARK to be effective COB 15 April 1966. While at JMWAVE, subject was under the cover of JMOCEAN.

2. Resigned in order to seek employment in private industry. A résumé of his KUBARK employment is as follows:

a. March 1957 (EOD) to May 1960—in headquarters—overt employee.

b. May 1960 to July 1964—Tokyo, Japan, under ODIBEX cover.

c. August 1964—leave (13-3).

d. September 1964 to April 1965—in headquarters—ODIBEX cover.

e. 26 April 1965 to 15 April 1966—JMOCEAN.

3. Subject was instructed that, in the event he ever seeks employment with ODYOKE, he should contact the Station for guidance as to what he will show as his former ODYOKE employment.

4. Under separate cover, there is being forwarded to Headquarters a copy of the résumé that he is submitting in seeking employment with private industry. This résumé was written by him and was concurred in by Chief/Finance and Chief/Cover of JMWAVE.

Attachment: 1 (résumé), under separate cover.

Philip G. ELMARD.

Distribution: 3—WH with attachment under separate cover; 2—KUVEST with attachment under separate cover.
