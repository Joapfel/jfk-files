Classified message. Action. Information.

032223E April 1962. CITE JMWAVE 1911. Priority Director, information priority Buenos Aires. No night action.

RYBAT REDTOP AERONAUT AGTARGET TECHS.

Reference: Buenos Aires 2016 (IN83405).

1. Because of AMSTALK-1's extensive WO/FIRM operational history, it is essential that we be given Puerto Rican documentation in alias for travel to Buenos Aires and that his true identity be withheld even from BIOGENESIS.

2. Recommend consideration be given to documenting AMSTALK-1 in alias as Puerto Rican tourist for travel to Buenos Aires. After arrival in B.A., suitable alias documentation devised by Station and BIOGENESIS, in line with cover requirements and possible as citizen of Argentina, should be provided. Obviously, his documentation while in B.A. must be able to withstand whatever check the Soviet Embassy is likely to make on people servicing its premises.
