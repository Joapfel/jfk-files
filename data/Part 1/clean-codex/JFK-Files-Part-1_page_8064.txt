Responded: Miami coordinator, Cuban affairs, who replied he had no knowledge of the matter. Volsky (VOLOSK) then phoned Barnard Collier, New York Herald Tribune Latin American correspondent, who checked by phone with the Cuban UN delegation. The person who answered the phone, apparently a secretary, confirmed the existence of the press release but was vague regarding possible distribution. Lesnik read from the release and commented on its contents during his radio program the night of 18 October. Collier broke the story in the New York Herald Tribune morning edition of 20 October.

Cuban exile circles speculate that a person within or having contact with the Cuban UN mission stole a copy of the release and sent it anonymously to SFNE. There is no evidence to support this or any other theory.
