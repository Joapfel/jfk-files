On 14 May 63, I met LITANIL-9 at a visual suspicious point, and we drove to an outlying area to hold a meeting. No security problems noted. Time: 2200–2300.

LITANIL-9 provided the following information:

I. L-9 had dinner at the Embassy on 14 May and identified VEGA Galero as a TDYer.

L-9 said that Emmanuel CARBALLO told him that “Chiqui” SARSARELLI (sic), employed by the Casa de las Americanas, is coming to Mexico soon for a couple of weeks. SARSALENDE, FM, subject’s father, lives in Mexico and, according to CARBALLO, was disappointed that his daughter was not going to stay in Mexico. Subject’s mother is living in Miami.

To Cuba.

L-9 reported that Seydon DIALLO, Guinean Ambassador, arrived in Cuba on 14 May 63, and he saw to it that DIALLO had no problems at the airport. DIALLO is the Guinean Ambassador to the U.N. also.

DIALLO told L-9 that Carlos LECHUGA�roña, Cuban FM to the U.S., was highly respected by all members of the U.N., including the U.B. He said that LECHUGA knew how to handle himself in the very complicated U.K. and Caribbean. DIALLO said that Raul FRIEDRICH and LECHUGA make a good team. L-9 said that DIALLO is very much pro-CASTRO and undoubtedly a member of the Castro U.N. clique.

DIALLO told L-9 that he has known Vicente VONCARDO Talavera for many years.

4. L-9 identified the following LIBRODE photos:

Marcelo MÁNTINEZ—L-9 said he saw WHITLER in Havana recently. (Colored)

Rafael PEDROSA Arandia—TDYer for conference on microwaves.

L-9 also identified Jore Verduin DEEZ Ron in LIFE photo at 5:00 p.m. in the afternoon of this week.

L-9 said that Manuel VEGA Perez told Rafael PETROSA Arendia and Antonio ARRUZA Mon. that she should not leave any documents or papers in their hotel room. In fact, both men left immediately to get papers from their KAXX room. L-9 believes they are staying at the Hotel Agnes.
