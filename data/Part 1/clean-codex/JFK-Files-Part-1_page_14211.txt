4. Operational break occurred morning 28 Mar when name of female friend uncovered. Request traces and priority clearance on Tomas (Hidalgo) Gato (commonly use Gato as last name), born Havana, 22 Aug 1911, and on wife, Alina (Freyre), born Havana, 4 Sept 1915. Both U.S. citizens. Father of Tomas is Tomas Gato, born Key West, Florida, 26 Dec 1885. Mother Celia Gonas, born Key West, 1893. Alina naturalized 12 June 1968. Hidalgo is manager Woolworths Madrid and good friend of American consul. It was from consul that we obtained information on Martin and husband. Alina came to embassy 24 Mar to inquire into procedure for obtaining visa for Martin and husband. QUTIMID led us to consul.
