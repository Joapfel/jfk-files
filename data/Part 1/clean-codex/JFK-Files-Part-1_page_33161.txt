RYMAT telephone. Dispatch No. 781. File No. 001-327337. Date: 13 April 1959. Indexing: none. Microfilm: no. To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. From: Chief of Station, WH/Miami. Subject: TYPIC/AMROSE/ANCHALK/ANDREDEW/Jose Massip, Antonio Massip and Daisy Miguel. Reference: WH/Miami 3556.

1. Supplementary to reference, ANCHALK-5 said in meeting 13 April that his cousins Jose and Antonio Massip had both received their elementary and secondary schooling in the United States. In the 1950s Sara and Salvador Massip, their parents, were employed in teaching positions at Harvard University. (Note coincidence that Marta Alicia Fiedad Conzalez Lopez was reported employed [at] Harvard University library early 1950s.) Jose and Antonio are bilingual without trade Spanish accept their English; Antonio also speaks German and Russian. ANCHALK-5 understands that Antonio during his absence from Cuba has lived about two years [in] East Germany and five years [in] Poland.

2. (FM) Miguel mentioned paragraph two reference is married [to] Daisy Miguel. Daisy is divorced, sister of wife [of] Gustavo Mart and currently employed as clerk [at an] air cargo company [in] Miami (not Eastern Air Lines as reported [in] reference). Her family is from Cienfuegos and father, who.
