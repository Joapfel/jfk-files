15 September 1961

Memorandum for: Secretary, GS/CS Panel (Section B)

Subject: Recommendation for Promotion to Grade GS-13 — Miss Ann L. Goodpasture

1. Miss Ann L. Goodpasture has been assigned to the Mexico City Station since early 1957 as a Case Officer in support of CI/CE operations directed against Soviet targets in Mexico. The Chief of Station recommended Miss Goodpasture for promotion in February of 1961.

2. Miss Goodpasture is a highly competent employee who has shown great strength in performing operational tasks which require a high degree of analytical ability, patience, and initiative. She is particularly strong in the CI/CS field and has made an invaluable contribution to the Mexico City station's program directed against personnel of the Soviet Embassy and other individuals believed to be engaged in Soviet spy activities. Miss Goodpasture is highly motivated, performs all her assignments with great dispatch in a most professional manner, and does have the qualifications found in more senior Case Officers.

3. It is recommended that Miss Goodpasture be promoted to grade GS-13.

J. C. King  
Chief,  
Western Hemisphere Division

Approved November 1962
