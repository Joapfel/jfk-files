To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation. Attention: Mr. S. J. Papich.

From: Deputy Director, Plans.

Subject: Maurice Hyman Halperin.

1. Reference is made to our CSCI-3/751,349, dated 4 September 1958, and to other correspondence concerning the subject, an American Communist in Mexico.

2. On 19 August 1958 an appointment Halperin had with Narciso Bassols, known Soviet agent, was changed from 21 to 22 August, since Bassols would not be in Mexico City on 21 August. On 22 August Halperin was informed that Bassols could not see him until 25 August.

3. The above information was passed to your representative in Mexico City. It was obtained from a usually reliable and highly sensitive source and should not be disseminated further.

CSCI-3/751,633.

22 September 1958.

Based on: RAM-3680. Project cryptonym: LIFERAT.
