092000Z September 76 Staff. Cite DCD/Miami 1350S.

To: Priority DCD/Headquarters. To: Office of the Chief (Spilman) from Hennessey. WNINTEL.

Subject: Field office assistance to OGC.

Reference: Spilman/Mainwaring telecon, 9 September 1975, same subject.

1. In response to a telephone call from Deputy Chief, DCD, on 23 August 1975, we made hotel reservations in Coral Gables, Florida, for Walter Lloyd, OGC, for the night of 23 August. The Miami Field Office was held open that evening for a meeting attended by Mr. Lloyd, Samuel Sheners, U.S. Department of Justice, and William Warner, U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration. Jack Hennessey, DCD/Miami, also attended and provided a short general briefing on DCD operations. For the most part, the meeting consisted of a discussion of the past relationship of Mitchell L. Wersell III with BRHERALD, especially with DCD as revealed in the DCD memorandum to OGC dated 8 March 1975.

2. We were advised by telephone during the evening of 27 August by A. Thomann, DCD duty officer, and subsequently by Walter Lloyd, that Lloyd would visit Miami again on 30 August in connection with the Wersell case. Lloyd did not task DCD/Miami in any way during this visit. However, he did check in with us locally by telephone upon arrival in the Miami area. We called him again during the evening of 31 August after his return flight to Washington, D.C., as a courtesy since he did not have the time to contact us prior to his departure from Miami.

3. Mr. Lloyd again returned to Miami, this time onboard a USAF Jet Star aircraft, early on the morning of 2 September. He contacted us that afternoon by telephone to request that we keep open the field office so that he could deposit classified files in our vault overnight.
