IMPORTANT.—The filing of this form will completely cancel any Designation of Beneficiary under the Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance Act you may have previously filed. Be sure to name in this form all persons you wish to designate as beneficiaries of any group life and accidental death insurance payable under that act at your death.

Examples of Designations

How to Designate More Than One Beneficiary. Alice M. Long; address: 509 Canal Street, Red Bank (Rod Bank), N. J.; relationship: aunt; share: one-fourth. Joseph P. Brady; address: 360 Williams Street, Red Bank (Rod Bank), N. J.; relationship: nephew; share: one-fourth. Catherine L. Rowe; address: 792 Broadway, Whiting, Ind.; relationship: mother; share: one-half.

How to Designate a Contingent Beneficiary. John M. Parrish, if living; address: 810 West 180th Street, New York, N. Y.; relationship: father; share: all. Otherwise to: Susan A. Parrish; address: 810 West 180th Street, New York, N. Y.; relationship: sister; share: all.

How to Cancel a Designation of Beneficiary So That Amount Due Will Be Payable As Provided in the Law. Cancel prior designations.
