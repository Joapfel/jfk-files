Incoming classified message. Central Intelligence Group.

From: Tsingtao. Priority. To: Special Operations. 2 January 1948. Action: SFD (1). IN 31971. Information: ADSC (2-3), COPS (4), GRD (5), FEZ (6-7), CAS (8), GPD (9).

TSIN (42). To: WASHF. Cite: TSIN (From:). Re: WASH 6169 (OUT 55651).

Resecret: Have not kept receipts or accurate record of ordinarily non-reimbursable living expenses. Cover demands certain standards and while presumably drawing state allowances on temporary duty, expected to live up to such. Realize difficulty, however, and will turn in regular accounting upon arrival at permanent station. Believe you have accounting up to 9 June. Please advise amount allowed, not including pay, as have no duplicate receipts.
