TRSA-586

Operational Review

Cryptology: Humanoid. Country: Costa Rica. Period: 1 January to 31 March 1950.

Objectives and tasks are set forth in detail in TRSA-564.

Salary: $500 per month during the quarter under consideration. $750 per month effective 1 April 1950.

Evaluation of services: Services have been of considerable value to operations. The agents which he handles are the most productive of those now being used by this station, and his management of these informants has been excellent, from both an information and a security standpoint.

Evaluation of information: The information provided by the agents whom subject handles, a great part of which is owing to his own industry and devotion in their development, is easily the most valuable produced by this station.

Other factors: No comment.

Comments: No changes are contemplated in subject's status.
