CITE MEXICO CITY 8467

Director info: JMWAVE, Madrid, Managua.

KAPOKYPIC LITAMIL-9

LITAMTI-17 reported 2 September that LITAMIL's will come to Mexico in mid-September for approximately a three-month stay to complete certain projects he was involved in when here PCS. No precise date given. L-17 received the news in letters from L-9 and from their father.

2. L-17 told Barker he hopes to be able to convince L-9 to remain in Mexico, where L-17 can set him up in a job. In the interest of harmony with L-17, C/O did not comment on this desire, although hopefully L-9 can be induced to return PBRUMEN to cooperate in PLANCE.

3. L-17 has been traveling almost constantly during the past year setting up industrial plants; presently in San Pedro Sula, Honduras. He may not be in Mexico when L-9 arrives. Barker has a contact plan with L-9, and the station believes he should remain in Mexico until contact is effected, although he has tentative plans to depart for Managua on 17 September.

4. Request Shryock stand by to take over the case once contact is effected. Since L-9 is averse to meeting any new WOFAC personnel (complained to C/O that he was exposed to too many people before and after previous PBRUMEN trip), believe best if contact can be confined to SECREI.

6 September 1965. IN 90306

Jane for L-9
