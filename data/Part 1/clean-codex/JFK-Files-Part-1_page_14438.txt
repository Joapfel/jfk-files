191224Z May 78, CITE MADRID 1131. Director, WH/Miami.

TYPIC AMAUTO.

Ref: Director 033355.

I. We believe that a chance encounter with AMAUTO-1 alone would be too difficult to arrange since AMAUTO-1 is frequently in company with someone from his staff or having lunch with Spanish officials or businessmen. Moreover, an encounter under this situation, we feel, would not permit relaxed exchange between SAMKO and AMAUTO-1. We would like to suggest the following alternative:

A. On arrival in Spain via Aeronaves de Mexico Flight 450, leaving Miami Friday, SAMKO would go directly to the hotel of Station selection. Station officer will also occupy the hotel under an alias, and initial contact with SAMKO will be made in the hotel. Incidentally, believe no need for PACHANKIS to accompany SAMKO to Madrid.
