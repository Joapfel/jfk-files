UFGA-21693/14.

Subject: Carrasco, Iran.

1. Source met Subject in 1962 when Subject was residing in an apartment building located in Marianao, behind the Colegio de Belen, where a friend of Source, Dr. Emilio Fors, used to live. Source maintained the social relationship with Subject until March 1964. Afterward, they maintained correspondence from Spain, where Subject has resided since September 1964. According to Source, Subject is not a communist and is against the Castro regime. Source believes that Subject has never previously traveled outside of Cuba. Subject informed Source that she has a brother, name unknown to Source, who has been in the United States since about 1961. Source believes Subject has never received any political, military, or intelligence training. Subject resides at Calle Martin de los Heros 74-1, Madrid, Spain. In Cuba, Subject used to live in Reparto La Ceiba, Marianao, Havana Province; complete address unknown. According to Source, Subject intends to emigrate to the United States from Spain.

2. Physical description: DPOB circa 1940; place of birth unknown to Source. Race: white. Height: 5 feet 5 inches. Weight: 130 pounds. Hair: dark. Eyes: dark. Shape of face: oval. Physique: medium. No visible scars.

3. JEWAVE traces: none.
