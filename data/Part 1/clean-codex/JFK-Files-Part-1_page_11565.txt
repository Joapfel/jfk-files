Of Station, Guatemala City. In CIRING-3 operation.

Action recipient: NUNEZ. Note: HMA-36114, 27 December 1968.

The “Humberto GONZALEZ Vega” mentioned in reference is probably identical with Angel Humberto GONZALEZ Vega. The only Station trace on that individual was forwarded with HGG-3098, 14 June 1963, from a source of undetermined reliability. In this report GONZALEZ was listed as active in the peace movement of the Guatemalan Communist Party. GONZALEZ’s date of birth was given as 2 October 1927. Biographic information and a photo of GONZALEZ have been requested from liaison and will be forwarded to addressees when received.

According to Station information, Gustavo COLINDRES Barrios was detained on 3 July 1967 by the Guatemalan authorities. He was described at that time as a member of “the supply network of the Rebel Armed Forces of Guatemala.” Although no further information is available in Station files, Colindres was apparently sentenced and sent to El Pavon.

In reference to the names mentioned in LERING-3 of GONZALEZ (above), Alegria, Jorge Torron and Avecillas, Guatemala City feels these properly refer to Hector SANDOVAL Alegria, Jose Samuel TORONTO, Ricardo Israel VALLECILLOS Perez. All of these individuals were recently arrested by the Mexican authorities in May 1968 for anstry. (MEXICO CITY 5590, 17 May 1968). In addition, it is likely that the “Nunez” mentioned later by GONZALEZ is identical with Roberto NUNEZ Sanchez, a Guatemalan Rightist who was also reportedly involved with SANDOVAL, TORON, and VALLECILLOS.

I am sorry to say I cannot help but feel that I must continue to do so. I did not realize that you were referring to Sandoval, TORON, and Valllecillos. It would have been more convenient if you had said that. I am sorry.

Mexico City Chief, WHD Files.

Cross reference: Dispatch HGGA-9721, 14 January 1969. File number: 201-56301.
