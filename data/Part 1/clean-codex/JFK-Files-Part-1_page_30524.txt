Field Research Unit, Far East Command  
Yokoguja, Japan

22 August 1951

AG 201 - PERS

LO 234-3

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individuals Concerned

1. Following named individuals will proceed on or about 25 August 1951 to Pusan and Seoul, Korea, and/or such other places as are necessary, for a period of TDY of approximately twenty (20) days in connection with activity of this headquarters. Upon completion of TDY, individuals are to return to proper status.

Mr. George E. Autell (63-25)  
Mr. William E. Woodson (CC-25)  
Mr. Chatham C. Clark (68-15)  
Mr. Newton S. Mille (72-13)

2. Travel by military aircraft, rail, and/or government transportation authorized. An excess baggage allowance of one hundred (100) pounds (official baggage) is authorized for each individual in addition to sixty-five (65) pounds personal baggage authorized for each individual while traveling by air. No per diem authorized.

3. Individuals are designated as official couriers for the period of TDY.

4. Individuals will wear the Army officers' uniform, less insignia, in the combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from QM Sales Store upon presentation of these orders.

5. Individuals may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after return to Tokyo, Japan.

6. Immunizations and physical inspection as prescribed by Circular 9, FEC, 27 February 1950, will be completed by each individual prior to departure.

John F. Hook  
Executive Officer
