Date: 1 December 1962. Classified message.

To Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 8. Info: CCS 2, OP 2, S/C 9. Routing: Bite; Sados. DEC 1 0330Z 62. Routine. IN 27561.

DIR CITE MEXI 2722.

1. Request no TVL orders for home leave TVL of Jeremy K. Benadun, wife, 2 children full fare, 4 children half fare, and 1 baby under 2 yrs.

2. To depart Mexico City about 6 Dec for San Francisco (home leave point) via air, return to Mexico City after home leave.

End of message.
