Classified message.

LET: 2356. Date: 19 July 1955. To: Mexico City. From: Director. COM: CI. Info: COPI, C1/CPS, C1/IC, FI, FI/CPS, FI/RI 2, WH 4, S/C 2.

DIR 14470 (OUT 57188).

19 MAR 55.

Routine transmission.

To: Mexi.

Cite: DIR.

(Held for identification within knowledge of the President, Governor and other experts.)

Ames Leigh White, born 1904, Vermont, newspaper correspondent and author since 1933. In Mexico City June–August 1941. Wife Yne Maria Cruz Carbogira White, born 1911, Spain; Sylvia Agellott, mistress of Jacques Hormard (Acques Hormard) and reportedly in Mexico City at time of Trotsky murder. Sisters Hilda and Ruby: Constanza de la Nora allegedly killed in Guatemala; Fernando de los Rios, born Spain in 1997. Spanish Republican Ambassador to U.S. 1937–38. Reported recent years representative Spanish CNTA of Liberation. Mexico City: J. Alvarez del Vayo, born Spain 1912, was active Spanish resuggest activities in New York; son of London World War II Julian Cornin, prominent Trotskyite in U.S. and Spain in 1940s. Writer close to Trotsky prior to murder; Nancy Johnston reported key CP official in Mexico.
