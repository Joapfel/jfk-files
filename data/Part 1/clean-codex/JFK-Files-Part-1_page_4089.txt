PRIORITY DIR INFO HANA. CITE WAVE 4156.

KAPOMAWORLD AMKNOB.

LIFNIGHT action.

Reference DIR 28960.

22 July 1965, 16311.

1. AMKNOB-1 learned from Alfredo Borges Caignet of ANYUM Group that AMBIDDY-1 and wife visited Nicaragua the week of 11 July and might return to Miami soon. AMBIDDY-1 plans to travel through Central America in order to maintain contact with those governments. The only ANYUM base left is in Nicaragua, where about 50 trusted men remain. The group has arms and equipment cached throughout Central America.

2. According to Borges, ODYOKE assistance to ANYUM was reduced “several thousand dollars monthly from hundreds thousands monthly.” Current subsidy is for psychological operations, maintenance of two PT boats, and travel expenses.

3. Borges said that although ODYOKE has forbidden further belligerent (ELLIGERENT) operations, AMBIDDY-1 is preparing aggressive operations against PERUHEN, especially blowing up Spanish, British, or Soviet ships involved in PBRUMEN trade. This is being done without ODYOKE’s knowledge. At the same time, an air attack is being planned against Cuba for 25 or 26 July.
