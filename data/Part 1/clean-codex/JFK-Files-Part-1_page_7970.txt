HIS-1-072

27 November 1957

Otto WILCHER, Calle Estrella 209, Habana.

Files reflect that he was a Communist sympathizer at the University of Habana during 1955. He was a member of the Committee for the Defense of Guatemala in 1954.

María LÍNCH, Calle E No. 205 y Líneas, Habana.

He is a leader of the youth section of the Ortodox Party. An unevaluated report dated 16 July 1951 from a fairly reliable source stated that LESNICK is a Communist sympathizer and a very good friend of Lionel (Lional) SOTO. In a recent interview in connection with his application for U.S. visa, LESNICK admitted knowing SOTO but said he did not share SOTO's views.

Baldomaró A. RIOS, Calle Industria 512, Tel. W-2665 (Havana).

SOTO's notes contained the name Baldomaró A. RIOS, and directly beneath it in parentheses the name “Susana.” RIOS was a Puerto Rican student at the University of Habana. He was identified as being a member of the Student Union but claimed to be anti-Communist. “Susana” is unidentified.

Marqués de TIERIA, Tel. F-647b, Calle F No. 130, ext. 17 y 19, Vedado, Habana.

The Marqués de TIERIA is not identifiable from Station files. Her husband, the Marqués de TIERIA, was a Spanish monarchist who is believed to have died in 1953.

Dr. León PAVILIO, Catedro Teuskyo, Calle Jardín López (Hardín Lopes) 70.

No pertinent information in files.

Galaheu SORiano, Silega, Tels. U-3109 and W-8291.

Wife of Lionel (Lional) Soto. In 1955 she was vice-treasurer of Inglaterra-Tiupo, a Communist-front cultural organization.

Carmel FRANCIS, Calle Valle 102, 2nd floor; his girlfriend, now unknown, is at Calle S. Francisco 102, 2nd floor.

In 1956 FRANCIS was a law student at the University of Habana and attended the National Law School in Havana in May and June 1956. He returned to Cuba and became active in Juvenile Socialism.

Dr. Alberto ERITZA, Calle Campanario 306, Tel. A-265a.

No previous record.

Luis MACHU, Tel. V-3896.

He is a printer and was active in 1969 in the Pro-Peace Movement in Cuba.
