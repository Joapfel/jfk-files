Classified message.

22 May 1965 (N) 73624. 212358Z. Dir info SEDOM; cite MEXI 3520.

1. Lifetime coverage Victor Rico Galar 3733, 15 May to 0840 15 May.

A. Emilio Cordero to Rico: Cordero wanted to see Rico to tell him some things. Cordero talked to Luis with Rodrigo yesterday. He will come by Rico's house in about 15 or 30 minutes. (Station comment: Luis may be Luis Suarez, Siempre correspondent.)

B. Cordero to Rico: Cordero asks if Rico has something urgent to discuss with him. Rico says no; he thought Cordero wanted urgently to talk to him. No, he only wanted to talk to him about some things Luis told him. Rico had only talked to Luis by phone. Cordero says that Rico's article in Siempre is very good. The thesis of the piece is that a new regulation has been created in the OAS admitting that intervention is very good. Rico comments that the idea of leaving the OAS is popular. Alberto presented it at the round table at the university as a possibility. There was a lot of applause when it was mentioned. Also a lot of good commentary.
