July 16, 1969

Contact Report

Subject: LIOAI-1 (P-8903)  
Date of meet: July 17 at Hoyo 19 at 1430 hours

1. Prearranged meet, which lasted 90 minutes.  
Next meet: August 17 at the same place.

2. On Friday, July 11, LIBACON-1 gave a regular language class to L-1, and L-1 stated his interest in meeting the Soviet girl at LIBACON-1’s house. In the course of conversation, L-1 explained to LIBACON-1 why he believed this was important to him; in other words, he gave him the cover story about which we spoke at our previous meet. LIBACON-1 assured L-1 that he would bring the Soviet girl, Alla KIEFOVA, to his house.

3. On July 16 at 1000 hours, at their next class, LIBACON-1 told L-1 that he had background on the Soviet Embassy and that he had found him this time. In the course of conversation with LOG, he, LIBACON-1, told him about meeting Alla and his plans to invite her to his home and show her around. LOG told him that Alla was his secretary and that he should not dare get close to her.

4. L-1 felt dejected with this development because he believed that he was developing something for us. I told L-1 that there was no reason to feel bad about this fact, especially because LOG was leaving relatively soon and the girl would remain here.

5. L-1 told me that on July 11 LIBACON-1 had taken an old friend from his office to see a movie, and he asked L-1 how long he would be there if he wanted it. Apparently, LIBACON-1 promised the magazine to L-1 and wanted to joke with the picture, but LOG became very angry about the fact that “international figures” are used for cheap pornography. L-1 suspected that LOG might have asked from there if he saw it. LIBACON-1 got his name.

6. L-1 planned to talk to LOG on July 20. The meeting took place as scheduled.
