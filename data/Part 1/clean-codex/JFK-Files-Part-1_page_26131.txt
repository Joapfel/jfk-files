292210Z CITE CARACAS 7694. JMWAVE information to Director.

References: A. JMWAVE 8865 (IN 3-1026); B. CARACAS 7589 (IN 3-1169); C. JMWAVE 8997 (IN 4-0522).

1. It is clear from reference C that it is to the Station’s advantage to have contact with AMCLEVE-15 in Caracas, as he could have informed us of the capture of Cuban considerably before we heard of it from the government.

2. Station does not feel reference A objections to contact are valid, especially with AMCLEVE-15 working for DIGEPOL. Unless JMWAVE has further objection not presented in reference A, request AMCLEVE-15 be told Station Caracas desires contact with him. Please advise when AMCLEVE-15 will return to Caracas and how we can be contacted.

BT

29 August 1967. M 41504
