KNO PN: The File

Subject: Michael N. CHOCADEN (P), Verification of Contract Service

19 October 1970

Subject, now a staff employee, asked Don DeFelice to see if any of his old contract time is creditable toward CTALES retirement. Currently, subject is assigned PCS in WJ field, but is currently in limits on TIF.

21 October 1970:

Met with subject and discussed all aspects of his questions and his prior contractual service. Provided his social security information concerning his service, as well as the criteria used in CPD in determining independent contractor service versus contract employee service, applicability of the “social Security rule” preceding the use of this contract or employee time under the social security plan, creditable retirement time, etc. Also provided subject with a sample of a memo which could be written to CPD requesting old independent contractor time be reconsolidated as contract employee time. Also indicated to subject we would help him draft an appropriate memo if he felt he had a good case. In general, from information provided by subject, it seemed that subject’s old independent contractor time was not of a type that might lend itself to conversion to contract employee time.

3 December 1970: As of this date, JPD had heard nothing more from subject and it was therefore assumed that subject had, at least for the immediate moment, decided not to pursue the matter further.

Paul Wilson

3 December 1970
