On 23 January 1968 Sergio Valdez told LIRING-3 that a FAR rep had recently arrived in Mexico from Havana with a message for Alberto Domingo that three unidentified FAR reps in Havana wanted to express their thanks for the letter concerning traitor Orozco. FAR reps also wanted “persons concerned” to be warned that there was another FAR traitor, a Guatemalan working for WOFIRM. Domingo, who was not aware of the contents of LIRING-3’s letter to FAR, passed the message on to Valdez. At a subsequent meeting with Valdez, the FAR visitor identified the traitor as one Carlos Salazar. Valdez expressed appreciation but asked for additional information on Salazar as well as proof that he was a FAR traitor, saying he did not personally know a Guat by that name. The visitor promised to check and pass on further information.
