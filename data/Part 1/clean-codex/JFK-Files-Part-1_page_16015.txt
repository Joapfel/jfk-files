12 November 1971, 121148Z. Cite Bangkok 24725 (Segerquist acting). Director: Phnom Penh, Vientiane, Saigon.

Ref: Director 88114 (notal).

1. Mr. Mitchell A. Werbell, Military Armament Corporation (formerly an element of Werbell's Georgia-based firm of Sionics, which reportedly went bankrupt), is back in Bangkok attempting to sell his wares (Chiming pistols and sniper rifles). The reference message cites the fact that HOS has a lengthy 201 on the subject (201-259910) and that he is characterized as unscrupulous, a wheeler-dealer, and a promoter of grandiose schemes. He has on occasion allowed either to being an LNLUTE rep or having LNLUTE backing.

2. Subject met with several LNCUFF officers on 5 November 1971 and related that he was attempting again to interest the Thai government in purchasing his products. Subject claims to be a personal friend of Air Chief Marshal Dawee and that his sample weapons were consigned to Dawee in order to bring them into Thailand. TO ORDERED.
