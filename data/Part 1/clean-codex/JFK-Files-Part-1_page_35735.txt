Memorandum for: Chief, Building Security Branch  
From: Joseph K. Moore and Joe C. Liggett  
Subject: Tour of Duty Report, 0900–1700 Hours

1. 0900: Entered on duty. Reviewed the current OSDO instructions and the previous Tour of Duty Reports.

Efforts to contact Mr. Harrington, Building Manager, and/or an elevator mechanic met with negative results. OSDO directed Mr. Carter to close the door by any means and to secure it. JKMKL

7. 1010: Reference item 6 above. Mr. Carter advised that the bay door had been closed manually and secured. Request Monday day shift advise GSA Building Manager. JKMKM

8. 1038: Mike Lander, Safety/DU, advised he may be reached via Unit No. 68 or his home phone the remainder of the weekend. JCL
