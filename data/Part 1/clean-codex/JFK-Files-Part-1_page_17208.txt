8. Emergency Conditions

A) In the event of civil disorders or the evolution of complete or enforced neutrality on the part of the host government, it is now believed that regular operational contact could be maintained with the active LIENTLY agents via Raymond H. GERENDE or, in his absence, Oliver J. SCOUTLING.

B) In the most unlikely event of the establishment of a regime hostile to ODINCS, limited contact could probably be maintained with the active project agents through GLENDES or SCOUTLING. However, under the conditions described above, it is doubtful whether this project would be able to operate against more than one or two selected targets.

C) Emergency communications plans for Oliver J. SCOUTLING and Raymond H. GERENDE are on file in the Station and at Headquarters (see RAM-15982 and DMM-15981, of 23 November 1960). In addition, in late 1960, a review was held with all active agents of their recontact plans and verbal recognition signals.
