War Department Report of Personnel Action (Field).

Date: 17 October 1946. To: J. Walton Moore. 4 through: SSU.

This is to notify you of the following action concerning your employment, which is subject to the provisions on the reverse hereof. This form is an official record of your service history in the War Department and should be retained for future reference.

Nature of action: Involuntary Separation. Effective date: 19 October 1946.

Position title: Intelligence Officer. Grade: CAF-12.

Service grade and salary: CAF-12, $5905.20 per annum.

Force and service or command: SSU.

Duty station and location: Shanghai, China.

Remarks: *Liquidation.

Last working day: 18 October 1946. Terminal leave: 8:30 21 October through COB 23 October 1946.

For the Commanding Officer, Wm. G. Tharp. Signature: WM. G.
