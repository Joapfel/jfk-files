Section VIII. Agency Employment History.

Description of duties: Supervision of the Office of Security audio countermeasures program domestically and overseas.

Inclusive dates: Aug 64–Aug 65. Location: Montgomery, Alabama. Directorate, office or division, branch: OS. Title of job: Student—Air War College. Grade held: GS-15. Description of duties: Agency-sponsored student at Air War College, study of national strategy and international affairs.

Inclusive dates: June 62–Aug 64. Location: Germany, Frankfurt. Directorate, office or division, branch: EE/Security Branch. Title of job: Chief, Security Branch, German Station. Grade held: GS-15. Description of duties: Chief of Security Branch activities in behalf of the German Station and supervision of certain security functions, such as polygraph and audio countermeasures, performed elsewhere in Western European area for the Agency.
