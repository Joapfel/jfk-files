Message form.

Originator: J. Linton, BD. Unit: WH/DR/CA. Extension: 4221. Date: 11 March 1966.

To: Mexico. From: Director. Confidential: clwolh. Info: Santo Domingo.

To Mexico. Info SDOM. Cite DIR 87630. EMARGOSY.

1. SDOM has inquired whether Edward O. Tichborn could be made available for use in DOMREP during three weeks preceding the 1 June election. HQS agrees Tichborn's qualifications and experience in the election field would be valuable for last-minute checking of trouble areas. Although recognised problem due XIRGAL is not now in force, and issues in DOMREP may, would appreciate MEXI comments on Tichborn availability and on any cover or other problems such assignment might involve.

2. If MEXI has no objection, SDOM would like to have Tichborn come for a two-week TDY early in April to read in on the situation, then return during the first week of May for a concentrated election effort.

3. Please advise.

End of message.

Coordinating, WH/DR: B.B. Tichborn 201.
