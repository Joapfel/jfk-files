Classified message.

Date: 13 September 1962. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Section: WH B. To: Add. CA 2, CA/PEG, CA/PROP, CA/O 4, CCG 2, S/C 2.

Table of Contents.

13 September 1962, 09:02.

Routine. IN 21383.

DIR CITE MEXI-1927.

1. Edward G. Tichborn has received invitation to be guest speaker, session Harvard Center Latin American Studies seminar 17–22 September. Understand this will be kick-off meeting young Latin political leaders prior two-month observation of American elections.

2. Station feels Tichborn attendance valuable opportunity cultivate Mexican delegates. Also would bolster his cover, and provide excellent contacts for future requirements other countries.

3. Tichborn must respond to invitation immediately. He will accept unless Headquarters advises otherwise prior COB 14 September.

4. Will advise Tichborn travel plans when firm should HOS wish to contact.

End of message.
