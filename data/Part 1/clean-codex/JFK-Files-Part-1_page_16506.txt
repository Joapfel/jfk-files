1. Mr. James Tillman of local INS contacted station and informed us that AMMUG-1 had contacted him for assistance in initiating naturalization proceedings. We had previously alerted Tillman of possible AMMUG-1 contact for such assistance.

2. Tillman stated he was not authorized to assist, as the private citizenship bill was handled by his central office, which must issue instructions to the local office for action. Tillman informed us that he gave AMMUG-1 various forms to complete and return to station. Completed forms should be forwarded to BKTRUST headquarters, attention Alien Affairs Staff, which should revise and then forward them to INS Central Headquarters. INS Headquarters will then advise the local office what action to take, after which AMMUG-1 will be granted citizenship.

3. Please advise soonest whether above procedure is correct.
