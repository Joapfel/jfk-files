Strengthened considerably if he becomes citizen of Venezuela. A-15 perfectly willing to become Venezuelan citizen if receives assurance doing so will not annul his and wife's status as residents of United States (both he and wife have fulfilled requirement of five years continuous residence in United States) and destroy their chances of becoming United States citizens. He says first love Cuba and second love United States. Since he cannot return to Cuba, prefers to become citizen of United States. Wife feels same way.

3. Request HQS advise priority if arrangement can be made whereby A-15 and wife becoming citizens of Venezuela will not annul United States residency status and wreck chances of becoming citizens. During recent trip to Miami they applied for but were refused new permits to reenter United States because (according to A-15) A-15 is residing and working outside United States and has no need for such permit. Permit for A-15 expired 25 July 1969 and permit for wife expired 13 November 1969.

4. Station strongly urges HQS do everything possible.
