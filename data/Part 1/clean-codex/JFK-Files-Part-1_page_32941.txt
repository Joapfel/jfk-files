1. AMSTALK/1 (formerly AMICE/14) received note from Cuban Intelligence Service, which was delivered by his cousin who arrived in Miami aboard a Freedom Flight. Note hidden inside hollowed-out crucifix and addressed to AMSTALK/1. Contents of note indicated that CUIS is aware of subject's anti-regime activities and is also aware of anti-regime activities of his close friends. They also suggested AMSTALK/1 meet with them in either Spain or Canada. AMSTALK is to advise CUIS of his decision by cabling address given in note, which is in Cuba.

2. As station aware AMSTALK/1 is cousin of AMTRUNK/19. Suggest station bear paragraph 1 background in mind future meetings with AMTRUNK/19.

End of message.
