Classified message. Madrid information to Director.

TYPIC: AMAUTO.

References: A. WH/MIAMI 7628 (IN087031); B. WH/MIAMI 7633 (IN087093); C. MADRID 1151 (IN087675).

1. SAMKO has been instructed to make reservations on Aeronaves de Mexico flight to Madrid on 29 May.

2. SAMKO thoroughly understands the following cover for trip:

A. His wife passed away, was broken up and still not completely recovered. Decided trip to Europe would help forget.

B. Saw “JULIUS” last year at SAMKO’s Hollywood delicatessen. SAMKO, who heard AMAUTO-1 had been killed, was told by “JULIUS” of AMAUTO-1’s current position in Madrid. SAMKO, therefore, looking up AMAUTO-1 for old times’ sake.

3. If contact established, SAMKO instructed to follow these lines with AMAUTO-1.

21 May 70. 201-735296.
