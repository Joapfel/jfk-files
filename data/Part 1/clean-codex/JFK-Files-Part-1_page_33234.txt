Director; info Panama City. RYBAT REDTOP AERONAUT AETARGET TECHS.

Refs: A. Buenos Aires 0546 (05-644). B. HABT-4889. C. Buenos Aires 0606 (IN 1958).

1. Thorough review AMSTALK-1/CASTAGNA activities over past 4 weeks has pulled together the following pertinent information:

A. There are approximately 20 air conditioner units used by FJSTEALERS. All are U.S. manufacture and were obtained in the U.S. All units are 5 years old or older. All are either Frigidaire, Westinghouse, or Philco and are for cooling only (no reverse cycle). Most of them have regular U.S.-type plugs. The remainder have had U.S.-type plugs replaced with local type. Capacity 14,000 BTU, working voltage 230 or 208. Of these units, 6 are utilized by the Embassy; others are sprinkled throughout trade and residence sites.
