Classified message. WH/MIAMI 5878.

Repeat not to make contact with NEBECKER unless most unusual or emergency situation arises. Contact plan given AMCHALK-5. He will not make contact with NEBECKER in case of no show by CUIS or if only normal meetings held with CUIS.

(3) Following Montreal trip, meetings arranged with NEBECKER on 29 and possibly 30 November in New York City.

(4) AMCHALK-5 instructed to reprimand CUIS for delay in contact and indicate his frustration and extreme time lapse between his letter to Havana in July and reply in late October. He will tell them that when he was in Mexico in October on business, he was sorely tempted to approach Czechs or Soviets with his proposition, i.e., the release of his friends in return for cooperation. In particular, he will cite the following individuals he wants released, all are old friends: RAMON GUIN, ANDRES (UNDRES) VARGAS GOMEZ, ROLANDO CUBELAS, FERNANDO ROJAS. All of the above have been sentenced to 30 years for anti-Castro activities. GUIN and CUBELAS were Army officers accused of assassination attempts against FIDEL. VARGAS was picked up during infiltration.
