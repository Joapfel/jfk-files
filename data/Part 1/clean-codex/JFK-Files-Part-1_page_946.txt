Secret Station and Bases.

22 November 1953.

Signature. Deferred. Routine.

Stations cable information. Bureau Zapria. Telegram: DIR 22218.

1. Recently concluded ECS review of Zaprima program shows that KUBARK (HUBARK) efforts have not achieved any significant results, and continuation of program as such is no longer warranted. Main reason for KUBARK (HUBARK) failure is that clean technician recruiting process, Fidel's, has been a miserable failure when measured against original objective of 2,000 technicians from Western Europe alone. Although our figures are not complete, we estimate probably less than fifty candidates have received contract approval, and we are able to confirm about ten who have actually made it to the island. KUBARK (HUBARK) succeeded in inserting about thirty recruited technicians into the various hiring channels, but so far only two have received contract approval from HAVA.

2. Clean failure to recruit in significant numbers appears due largely to reluctance of Foreign Ministry and Foreign Service officers to pursue technician recruiting mission aggressively, bureaucratic incompetence in Havana, and fear of infiltration by hostile agents.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
