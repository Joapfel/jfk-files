Subject: Lee Figueroe.

Place acquired: Viet Nam, HanBI (Hanoi).

Evaluation: B-3.

Report No.: LFM-89.

Date of info: as indicated.

Date acquired: 1 August 51.

Date of reports: 2 August 51.

1. According to Hong Hui, who works in the Viet Ninh (Viet Minh) Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Lee Figueroe was in Saigon in early 1940. The French did not allow him to remain in Saigon and he was forced to return to France. Figueroe later went to Hong Kong in late August 1940, and then proceeded to the Viet Bac via the Hoa Nam (F.C.S. South China) road to attend the Third Congress of the PGI held in early October 1940.

2. There had been several previous cases of individuals who were unable to reach Viet Ninh (Viet Minh) territory from Saigon because of French interference. In July 1949, Mr. A. Roth, editor of the Sunday Tribune, arrived in Saigon and obtained all necessary papers; the French did not allow him to proceed because of his pro-communist articles. Others were Walter Brisco of the Christian Science Monitor, Harold Teague from Neweywood, and S. J. Perelman, because he criticized Bao Dai (Mao Dai) in Westward, Help (He'p).
