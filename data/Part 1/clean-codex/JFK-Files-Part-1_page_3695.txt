Action Unit WHP 8. Secret 121859Z May 70, CITE Managua 7332 (CONFIL). Lima info Director, WH/Miami. Reference: Managua 7157, 14 October 1969 (IN 65339).

1. AMBIDDY-1 visited COS Managua night 7 May. In course of long conversation mentioned; MAD had been phoned; night before in Miami by General Guillermo Leigh of reference. Leigh passing through Miami en route New York, said would contact AMBIDDY on return 10 May. Apparently interested in placing some money in U.S. banks. Remarked that Velasco essentially weak, being run by civilian advisers among whom some Communists. AMBIDDY will report on results and station will relay details Lima.

2. AMBIDDY clarified that subject is not legitimate general. Was military classmate present with officers of that rank, left Army owing to seriously impaired eyesight, is called “General” as courtesy by Army friends with whom he still keeps close relationships.

3. File: 201-860098, 201-267437. GP-1.
