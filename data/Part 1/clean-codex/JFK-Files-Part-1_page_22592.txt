Classified message.

Date: 407 DMCL/WST/S AGL.

Routing. Received Cable Section. January 1, 03, 11:59.

To: 31 December 58, Director. From: Havana. Action: WH 6. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FI/OPS, S/C 2/l.

To PRITY SACU. Info: DIR. Cite: HAVA 0766. Re: DIR 06245 (OUT 57838).

1. Subject reference arrived HAVA evening 30 December.

2. For SACU: Subject is VAIVADA. Headquarters wishes send him to Fidel Castro territory on mission similar to that undertaken last March. Is SACU in position arrange his entry into rebel territory as in previous cases? If not, HAVA probably can arrange through 26 July contacts here but prefers it be done through established channels SACU if at all possible.

3. Cable reply priority.

End of message.

C/S comment: Advised NOEMAYR enroute HAVA, CMA flight 565, ETA 30 December.
