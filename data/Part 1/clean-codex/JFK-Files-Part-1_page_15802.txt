30 rue Cabanis  
Paris 14

Dear Dat (Eduardo),

I am asking myself if Brazil (Brasil) is really existing, if the planes have found him... If the mail is invented there... are they in the moon? We have sent 7 letters. No answer. You may cry that they are not writing to you... It is the last one. Colette had written twice, Annick twice, I have four times, Nelly twice, Nina once, with different names. That is extraordinary... because I hope you are still alive. It is not necessary to talk about our life... That is all. I open again my letter to tell you your uncle (oncle) Keresselidz is dead. Atchiko was here and said he had written also and had never received an answer.

Mine Kwinitadze is dead and also Mr. Galoblichvili, one of these days it will be my turn... Will I see you again before?

If that letter didn't arrive, I give up.

We are kissing you, all of us.

Grandma.
