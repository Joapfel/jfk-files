MCC. Commo Chrono. 28 October 1964. P-8849; P-7921; P-7365. Cuban Chrono.

DIR. KANA WAVE. TYPIC AM000.

Ref: MCCI 2338A 1342. MEXII 1407. XXIV.

1. On morning 26 October, when it became apparent LITEMPOS was unable to persuade Gobernacion to switch papers of Gonzalez for later arrest, station placed LIEEMRACE (LIEMBRACES) surveillance on Gonzalez at Hotel Astoria. (LIEEMRACE coverage had been withdrawn when LITEMPOS found Gonzalez, as station did not want LITEMPOS and LIEMBRACES tripping over each other at Astoria.) LIEEMRACES did not pick up Gonzalez until morning 28 October, when he departed for airport and flew to Cuba.

2. Clandestine photos taken of Gonzalez at airport by LIEMBRACES clearly show him in company of Manuel Vega and Rogelio Rodriguez, known DOI officers. Pouching copies.

3. Index Elicio Darento Gonzalez Moreno.

End of message.

H. C. CHOADEM

W. C. CURTIS
