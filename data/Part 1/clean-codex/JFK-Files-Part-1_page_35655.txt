Lago Pereda, Vicente (Dr.) (Lago Pereca, Vicente /Dr/; Lago-Pareda, Dr. Vicente). Sex: M. DOB: circa 08. Country: Cuba. Citizenship: Cuba. Occupation: medical.

Cuba, Havana, Vibora, Estrada Palmanro 57. General practitioner. Ex-congressman. Ex-local chief of health, City of Havana, 1944–50. Member of Partido Revolucionario Cubano Autentico and currently out of politics, with no connection with Cuban Health Ministry. Anti-Castro and pro-US. Telephone No. 4-1042. OSM.

In House of Representatives, 1950–54; health chief for City of Habana, 1944–50; leading AUTENTICO member. 19-5-3/4-2, Volume VII, UFG-976, 9 May 1962.

19-6-63/3-2, AMCOG-3 OPS Volume II, August 1963 Report. Source of information of AMCOG-3.
