Captain Vicente Fernandez

Fernandez has publicly committed himself and given his word that he will be fighting in Cuba in the next month to come. In a veryative (veryative) letter addressed to the National Council of Cuban Independence Veterans (in exile), he formally pledges himself to the nation and the people in exile, by his honor, to face all the responsibility of the war in Indian Sail. He goes on telling them in his letter, full of courage, that he will face all the action in the just and reasonable anti-electra war in St. Petersburg, by consulting the majority of the communists.

A New Hope

Una Nueva Esperanza

From Exile to Freedom

Vicente Fernandez is only known in his native land as being against Castro, but he has never shown it—stated in seeing his name in the paper or assuming the leadership of the revolution.

He has always chosen to be unknown, and believing that soon he will go back to Cuba, it is necessary that all Cubans who want to help for the freedom of their kindred also lie down in it, and his merits cannot stand in the way of his life.

The actual headquarters of the revolutionary army in Cuba was taken by Fernandez when his followers, taking all the assets left to him by the revolution, met at the last conference to discuss the future of the revolution.
