Dispatch. To: Chief, WOACRE. Info: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division; Chief, MKSPICE. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: AQUATIC/KDAFGHAN — Renewal of Passports for KDAFGHAN-1 and KDAFGHAN-2.

Action Required — References.

Reference: HMMW-17955, 11 June 1970.

The WOACRE-issued Cuban passports for Subjects have expired. It is requested that new passports be prepared for them. According to KDAFGHAN-1, the new passports should carry the same numbers as the old passports. The passports, and current photographs of Subjects, are being forwarded under separate cover. It is requested that the old passports be returned.

Elsie I. SCALETTI.

Attachment: As stated u/s/c.

Distribution: Chief, WOACRE (with attachment u/s/c); Chief, WHD (without attachment); Chief, MKSPICE (without attachment).

Gross Reference To. Dispatch Symbol and Number: HMMA-41480. Date: 7 January 1972. MOS File Number: 301-305604. FFN=P-7,097.
