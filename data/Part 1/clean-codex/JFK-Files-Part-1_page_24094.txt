CITE DIRECTOR 44750, 19 October 1967, 1111Z. LCPIPIT, info London. RYBAT DTDORIC QREBONY.

1. For security reasons relating to its actual descriptiveness, it has been decided to change the QREBONY crypt to IDEN A. This new crypt will be used henceforth.

2. To assist in easier communication in an area of high sensitivity, the following additional crypts have been assigned: QRPEONY (IDEN B); and QRPETTY (IDEN C).

3. In future, please separate all QREBONY/1 personal correspondence, excise QREBONY/1 name, and forward by RYBAT channel.

End of message.
