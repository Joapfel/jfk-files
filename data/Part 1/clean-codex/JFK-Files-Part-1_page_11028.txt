Classified message.

Date: 12 January 1963.

To: Director.

From: Mexico City.

Action: WH 8+ (WH copy slotted 1550, 12 January 1963).

Info: DDP, ADFP, CA 2, CA/PEG, CA/PROP, F-14E RFPS, F-16, FI/INT 2, TFW 5, S/C 2.

Priority. DIR info WAVE SAGO. Cite MEXI 3878 (Ousler acting). Reference MEXI 3052 (IN 47807).

GYRO SE LITAMIL-9.

IN 49031.

1. On 11 January 1963, Coty Solorzano Vda. de Ramirez, sister-in-law of General Lazaro Cardenas, told LITAMIL-9 that the Cuban Embassy should have a car without diplomatic plates on occasions like the afternoon of 10 January, when Carlos Lechuga Hevia went to visit General Lazaro Cardenas. Coty Solorzano said that the Cardenas house was under surveillance and that use of the car Ramon Sinobas had was not wise. L-9 reported that Lechuga and Sinobas left the wreath-laying ceremony for Jose Artcho Mella about 1145 on 10 January in Sinobas’ car. Likely they were on their way to visit Cardenas at that time.

2. Coty Solorzano told L-9 that the assignment of Sinobas as chief of the mission in Mexico in Lechuga’s absence was poor judgment on the part of the Cuban government. He was not adequately prepared to do the job, etc. Solorzano is godmother to Sinobas’ child.

Solorzano said that Cuauhtémoc Cardenas (CUANTIDAD CARDENAS) agreed with her on this point and had asked her what L-9 and Eusebio Azcue were “doing,” implying that, in his opinion, either one of the two should have been given the post.

3. On the morning of 11 January, Lechuga took L-9’s gift for his sister from L-9 but told him he was not leaving for Havana (HAVA) until 14 January. Lechuga and Sinobas arrived at the Embassy about 2003 on 11 January, probably returning.

LITAMIL-9.
