REF #: MORTON/STARLING/HENNESSEY TELECON, 7 MAY 1974, SAME SUBJECT

1. At 1055 on 7 May 1974 we received a telephone call on the listed line from Frank Sturgis, who indicated that he wanted to meet with us as soon as possible in order that he might convey information of extreme importance to the Agency. We mentioned at the outset that if he had arranged to meet with Matt Macvane of the Miami Field Office at an earlier date, but Macvane had failed to appear at the assigned time and place, we did not comment on the agentive Macvane mentioning. However, we immediately advised Sturgis that we are not in a position to meet with him. We suggested that if he reclaimed Agency contact he should do it at the Washington level. We asked who in Washington we might call. We indicated that we were challenged to suggest a specific name.

2. Sturgis then said that we did not want to play games and that he was prepared to give us his information over the telephone.
