Office Memorandum — United States Government

To: Files  
From: Coleman  
Subject: Case 9145  
Date: 4 March 1954

On 3 March, Ormond requested that a meeting be set up for him with Leo Cherne, President of the Research Institute of America. Ormond said that he would not need more than half an hour. Called (Galled) Connor, who had Bob McCann set up a meeting with Cherne for Monday, 8 March, at 3:00 p.m. Ormond will go to the New York Office at 2:15 on Monday to meet McCann and take off for the meeting. This meeting was requested as a CAT III, and McCann will have to bow out after the introduction. Ormond is happy, New York is happy.
