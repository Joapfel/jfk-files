B. Bosch was arrested on 19 February entering Costa Rica with a false passport and charged with plotting to kill Secretary of State Henry Kissinger, Pascal and Brussier. No trial was held and Bosch was later deported. He returned to Santiago and apparently remained there until going to the Caribbean to organize CORU.

C. Info on this alleged DINA-Cuban exile conspiracy was suppressed during the September ’76 federal bombing trial of Otero in Jacksonville. When Otero started to testify about his activities in Chile, government prosecutors objected that the information was immaterial to bombing charges. Otero was found innocent; his trial on related state charges was to begin in Miami on Monday (8 November) but was ordered postponed until early 1977.

D. Otero had lived in Florida until late December 1975 when he was told by an FBI informant that the FBI was preparing to have him indicted on bombing charges. Otero then went to the Dominican Republic where he was arrested and paroled. When the Miami federal grand jury returned indictments against Otero, he flew to Caracas where he stayed.
