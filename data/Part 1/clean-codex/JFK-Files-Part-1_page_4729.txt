B. Accion Cubana (Cuban Action—AC) is a small terrorist group in Miami headed by Orlando Bosch Avila. AC is to concentrate its efforts against Fidel Castro and his allies, with Colombia, Mexico, and Panama specifically mentioned. Gaspar Jiminez (Jimenez) (aka Gasparito) is AC military chief. The military unit twice has attempted to blow up a Cubana Airlines plane since the Santo Domingo meeting: a bomb placed aboard a Cubana plane in Panama, circa mid-June 376, which did not explode, and a bomb in a suitcase to be loaded into a Cubana plane in Kingston, Jamaica, on 19 July 376, which exploded near.
