Dispatch. To: Chief, VR Division. Info: Chief, CO Division. From: Acting Chief of Base, San Francisco.

Subject: ISOLOG TFAST; Dr. Robert S. Coe. No indexing required: 201-196934. Action required—references. AR: FYI. Ref: USPA-6217 dated 14 December 1971—201-196934.

Attached herewith is a copy of DCB Memorandum SP 68-72 reporting the results of contact with Professor Aaron C. Waters of the University of California at Santa Cruz concerning the individual and organization which contacted Santa Cruz in early summer 1971 with reference to the travel to China of Dr. Robert Coe. As will be seen, this contact did not resolve the issue; however, from the details given, it appears possible that the contacting organization was LIBERGO.

Oliver C. WHITHEART.

Attachment: Memorandum, h/v.

Distribution: 2—C/FR w/att; 2—C/CO w/att.
