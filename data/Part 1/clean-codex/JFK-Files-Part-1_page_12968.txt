24 September 1969

Memo to the effect (1978).

Talked to Tom Dollar, 12/4, and John Sears, WH/Calg, in his case 25 and 26 September 1969. 12/4 had prepared a dispatch to Caracas which appeared by stating 0A value for travel and of use planned. This op use detained in the 15 September ’69 dispatch to Caracas in file.

The 12/05 file on subject secured because WH/COG had 0A for seeing via labor activities in Miami. Per the telephone call to John Sears above, it developed that WH/COG was stronger than any operational interest in subject. The telephone conversation with Tom Dollar, the last talked with the same carrier, was had asked if the equivalent of whether a new 0A/84 could make piggyback: a WH/COG clearance and their requirements through WH/COG. Any answer had been yes, if WH/COG agreed. This was the basis for the statement in the proposed dispatch to Caracas that subject 0A was valid.

Per a teleconference call with Barbara Bewley, 12/84, this date, I asked her to prepare a memo to CA/OA requesting that responsibility for the case be transferred to 12A/84 and that it be amended to add the document-use issue. In agreement to the text, routing the message through WH/COG.
