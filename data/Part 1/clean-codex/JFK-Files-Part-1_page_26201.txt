From: Acting Chief of Station, Mexico City.

Subject: JMROD/Victor Rico Galán (RICO Galan).

Action recorded—encloses.

Reference: HMMA 31108, 17 January 1967.

1. Referenced dispatch forwarded a memorandum written by Zeno B. LEEHOLD regarding his meeting with the subject on 13 January 1967. LEEHOLD used journalist cover in his meeting with RICO Galan.

2. RICO Galan was released from prison on 2 April 1972. HMM-13,013 reported that on his release he and two other prisoners released with him held a conference for students, during which the subject indicated that conditions for a violent struggle in Mexico do not exist. The subject indicated he was going to establish a new political group known as the Socialist Front. On 9 March 1972, a newspaper article reported that the head of the Popular Socialist Party of Mexico confirmed that RICO Galan had renounced Trotskyism (trotskism). Since his release from prison, he has been very quiet, and the Station has received no reporting on his whereabouts or activity other than a series of leftist articles he is writing for “Siempre” magazine.

Elsie I. SCALETTI.

Distribution: C/WMD.

Cross-checked: HMM-12155, 17 July 1972. V-201-336474.
