Name: Burke, DDI/00, William P., Jr. Grade: GS-15. Position title: Intelligence Officer (Chief). Department/field: Special.

Office staff or division: Contact. Branch: New Orleans.

Period covered by report: From 9/9/53 to 9/8/54.

Major duties: General and detailed supervision, direction and support of the activities of personnel attached to the New Orleans Office, with a view to insuring the highest standard of professional and clerical performance and the most complete coverage of the area within this office's jurisdiction. Interpretation and implementation of policies originated by higher echelons. Maintenance of liaison with field installations of other IAC agencies for the purpose of effecting coordination of domestic intelligence collection. Collection and reporting of positive foreign intelligence information from domestic nongovernmental sources. Provision of support to other components of CIA.

Courses of instruction completed during report period: None.

Primary work interest: In the type of work in which I am presently engaged.

Performance on major duties: He performs these duties conscientiously and efficiently.
