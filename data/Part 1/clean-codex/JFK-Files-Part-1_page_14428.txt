21 May 1975, 087675.

Ref: A. Director 035742. B. WH/Miami 7628 (in 087021).

1. Pleased with appearance of “JULIAS” into ops picture. We are now prepared to implement approach to AMAUTO-1 using SAMKO; however, Ref B highlights an important aspect of SAMKO’s character which could have an adverse effect on the success of the operation. Before providing contact instructions, need clear indication that SAMKO, although doing a favor for his government, is willing and able to follow instructions. He must understand that his mission to Madrid is to obtain an assessment of AMAUTO-1 and, through renewal of old friendship, to open a channel to AMAUTO-1 should the latter ever need a friend in the U.S. Also, that he cannot—repeat, cannot—express or imply to AMAUTO-1 that the visit to Madrid was at the behest of the U.S. government or JNKLANCE. With this assurance, see no reason not to proceed with operation. On the other hand.
