032343Z April 69. Cite Mexico City 9722. MENT Director.

4 April 1969. IN 28943.

Descriptors: TOP LIREALM AEDIEHARD AEKICK.

Ref. Mexico City 9456 (IN 12827).

1. On 22 March, LIREALM-1 saw AEDIEHARD at a club. There was no opportunity for an extended private conversation. They discussed a movie camera which AEDIEHARD wants to sell, and LIREALM said he might find a buyer. LIREALM then told AEDIEHARD that he had something he wanted to talk about and hoped to get together soon to do so. AEDIEHARD said he would call LIREALM during the week and take him to lunch.

2. On 24 March, AEDIEHARD called LIREALM at the latter's office from a phone booth, with street noise in the background, to make a luncheon date. LIREALM-1 suggested 26 March and invited AEDIEHARD to a key club which LIREALM uses for business lunches. AEDIEHARD did not recognize the club but agreed.

3. On the morning of 26 March, AEDIEHARD called LIREALM at the office to cancel lunch, giving his son's dog bite as an excuse—a thin one since it happened a week previously. Recomment: Station suspects AEDIEHARD cased the key club and found it not to his liking since it is private.
