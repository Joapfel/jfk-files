21 July 1967, 14:05.

Director of Information Management. Permittee.

Reference: Mexico City 2853 (W. 93509). Director of Secretariat.

1. Check all Cuban manifests from 12 June to 17 July. Only two Cubans remained in Mexico any length of time during this period. One was historian Epasmo Compieppе Afenas, who was in Mexico from 24 June to 19 July for historical research. LIENVOY confirmed he contacted people in his field to work with them. The other was Jesus Cruz Gonzalez, who was in Mexico from 12 June to 15 July in connection with Prensa Latina matters, together with Oplando Fundira Lopez and two Luscon Battle who spent shorter time in Mexico. LIENVOY showed Cruz in working contact with LIBRINS-9 and 281-775733.

2. LITAIN-NEW: Raphel Wipabal Fernandez arrived 3 July in company of the chief of Cuban vice. Wipabal was treated as a visiting official worthy of consideration. Wipabal surprised LITAIN-NEW Liz Ziening by turning down a lunch invitation to stay with the Cuban visitor. LITAIN-7 stated 19 July that three Cubans unknown to her “had been running around the embassy,” not detected during the previous week, with portfolios and files.
