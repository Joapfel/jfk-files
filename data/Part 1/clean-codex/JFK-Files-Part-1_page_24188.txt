Dispatch. Dispatch symbol and No.: OELA-43,589. To: Chief, KUDESK; Chief, WE; Chief, AF; Chief, KUWOLF; Chief, LCPIPIT; Chief of Station, Algiers. Headquarters file No.: 201-306052, June 1965. From: Chief of Station, London. Subject: KUDESK QPREY STEBONY; Old Letter from STEBONY/1. Date: 13 July 1965.

Reference(s).

1. Cleaning out some old papers, I ran across a letter from STEBONY/1 (copies attached). The letter was sent by TIFFANY, RODIGAST, et al., during the busy week they all spent in London, and is sent along now to bring the record more or less up to date.

2. A batch of other materials, which arrived through our postal arrangement, were turned over to TIFFANY, and they are presumably now a part of Headquarters' records.

George H. KATZENBACH.

Attachments: Herewith.

Distributions: Chief, KUDESK with attachment; Chief, WE with attachment; Chief, AF with attachment; Chief, KUWOLF with attachment; Chief, LCPIPIT with attachment; Chief of Station, Algiers with attachment.
