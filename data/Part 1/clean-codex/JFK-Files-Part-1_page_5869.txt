Country: Cuba/Dominican Republic. Report No. UFG-6676. Date of report: 10 February 1965. Subject: Attempts of Antonio Cuesta del Valle of Commandos L to establish a training camp in Puerto Rico or the Dominican Republic. No. of pages: 1. Date of information: 3 February 1965. Place: United States, Miami. Date acquired: 8 February 1965.

This is unevaluated information. Source grading is DEF-MY-OL. Approval of content is tentative.

Source: A Cuban exile with good access to activities of exile activist groups. His reporting on raider activities has been generally reliable since 1962. This information was obtained from Luis Diaz.

1. According to Luis Diaz, a member of Commandos L, a militant Cuban exile organization, Antonio Cuesta del Valle has been in Puerto Rico for several months attempting to set up a camp there for training Cuban exiles. He has also been attempting to obtain permission from the Dominican Republic to use the Dominican camp which was used by Eloy Gutierrez Menoyo, a military leader of the Segundo Frente Nacional del Escambray (SFNE, Second National Front of the Escambray), and has been making regular trips to the Dominican Republic from Puerto Rico for that purpose.

2. On 3 February 1965, Rason Font Saumell, second in command of Commandos L, told Diaz that the recent accusations made against the top Dominican military personnel by Gutierrez after his capture in Cuba in January had jeopardized his organization's good relations with the Dominican Government.
