b. Antin HYCZYTOPORUK (HYCZYPORUK; according to CHYP, his true name is HYCZPORYTZ) was born about 1910. He is married and had a wife and two children in Hoginsak. Prior to 1944, he was a professor of Ukrainian and West European literature at a pedagogical institute in Kiev. HYCZYTOPORUK left the USSR in 1944; from 1945 to 1948, he was in an International Refugee Organization camp in Salzburg, Austria; he has resided in France since 1946.

4. The following details on CHYP's and HYCZYTOPORUK's (HYCZYPOHUK's) involvement with Soviet intelligence were obtained by our source from CHYP and other Ukrainian émigrés in France. During the period 1958 to 1960, CHYP and Vasiliy NYAKUSHKO (Vasiliiy NYAKUSHKO), Third Secretary of the Soviet Delegation to UNESCO (UNEECO), frequently visited with each other at the Soviet Embassy and at CHYP's apartment at 211 Rue de Bercy. At the end of 1959, CHYP introduced HYCZYTOPORUK to NYAKUSHKO. NYAKUSHKO asked HYCZYTOPORUK to contribute literary articles to Soviet publications; it is not known whether HYCZYTOPORUK wrote any such articles. Early in 1960, HYCZYTOPORUK developed a persecution complex and was confined to a sanitarium for a short period. CHYP, fearing that HYCZYTOPORUK might expose the CHYP-NYAKUSHKO relationship, provided HYCZYTOPORUK with free board and lodging in his apartment and with spending money. NYAKUSHKO dropped HYCZYTOPORUK as soon as the latter's mental condition became obvious. In May 1960, the Soviet Embassy advised HYCZYTOPORUK to repatriate to the USSR as soon as possible and offered to help locate his family in the USSR. As you already know, in October 1960 the Soviets sent HYCZYTOPORUK a letter reporting his family was not in Hoginsak. See attachment.

5. There is a rumor in the Ukrainian émigré colony in Paris that HYCZYTOPORUK has been a Soviet agent since about 1949. This is based on his close relationship with Josef (also known as Osyp) KOZAK since 1949. KOZAK moved to France from Munich in 1946. Until his expulsion in 1950, he was quite active in the Organization of Ukrainian Nationalists/Bandera (OUN/B) in France. He was ousted from the OUN/B because of his excessive curiosity about OUN/B members and their relatives in the USSR (USAR). Subsequently, it was learned that KOZAK was in contact with the Soviet Embassy in France. KOZAK is presently residing at 26 rue Gaston Jaillon, Vesine Chalette, Loiret.
