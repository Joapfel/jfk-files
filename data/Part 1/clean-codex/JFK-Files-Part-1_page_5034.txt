3012452 AUG 76 STAFF

CITE SANTO DOMINGO 17646

To: Immediate San Jose, Managua. Director info: LA/Miami, Mexico City, Caracas, Bogota, Buenos Aires, Panama City.

WNINTEL REAM MHSPIKE

Refs: A. Santo Domingo 17643, not sent Managua, San Jose. B. Santo Domingo 17629, not sent Managua, San Jose.

EMI X: Transport order. FLY: Not flying in drops. BIO: No bio in drops.

1. EMWISE-15 advised station at 0945 hours on 29 August that Orlando Bosch had been deported on Iberia Flight 97 at 0600 hours, with destination San Jose via Managua. Decision to put Bosch on this flight was made on very short notice. Bosch travelled on the same Costa Rican passport with which he entered the Dominican Republic in the name of Carlos Luis Paniagua Mendez. EMWISE-15 had previously reported that Bosch had entered with a Costa Rican passport in the name of Pedro Antonio Pena (this information was in error).

2. On 27 August station obtained a copy of the Paniagua passport from ENCLIPPER-1, which was being processed to action address.

3. No objection. Action advises stations, indicating to liaison that they are aware of Bosch’s travel with Costa Rican passport in name.

30 Aug 76
