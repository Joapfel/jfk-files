To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation

From: Deputy Director for Plans

Subject: SAUREL, Jean K.  
Date of birth: 17 December 1929  
Place of birth: Port-au-Prince, Haiti  
VMA: Haiti

1. Reference is made to the I-5 form regarding the subject, who entered the United States on 2 October 1964. The title of this office contains the following pertinent information.

2. According to another office of this Agency, a Pan American Airways employee who spends considerable time in Haiti stated that during her many trips there she learned the subject had demanded and received things from members of the Tonton Macoutes (Ten Ten Macosteas; TTM, President DUVALIER's internal security force). One incident occurred at a DUVALIER rally held at the Presidential Palace on 1 May 1964. A palace guard refused to allow the subject to leave the grounds while someone was speaking. SAUREL called a member of the TTM, demanded to be released, and was allowed to leave. On 20 September 1964, a subordinate (suborder) of unknown reliability, whose family was well acquainted with the subject, commented that the subject was a member of the TTM.

3. According to a report received in September 1964 from an untested source, the subject, a former employee of Pan American Airways in Fort-de-Pruess, who was recently named Director of the Haitian Tourist Directorate in New York, is an agent of President DUVALIER. DUVALIER is putting heavy pressure on SAUREL because of the latter's experience in dealing with American personalities and situations.
