5 April 1957

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Arthur G. VAIVADA  
Naturalization of subject's wife

1. Mr. Jerry MAHSETT, Alien Affairs Officer, Office of Security, Room 2503, I Building, X-2141, stated that the following requirements and procedures are necessary to submit the subject's wife's application for naturalization:

a. Memorandum from C/WHD to the Director of Security, Attention: Alien Affairs Officer, requesting that the application for naturalization be expedited so that the subject's wife may accompany him overseas on or about ________________.

b. Application for Naturalization forms to be filled out in duplicate. They are not to be signed by the applicant.

c. Three passport-size photographs.

d. One fingerprint card, obtained at the Immigration Office in Room 313, Old Post Office Building, 12th and Penn. N.W.

2. The above memorandum and documents are to be submitted to the Office of Security (OS). After a security check, an appointment will be made for the subject's wife to appear for questioning and signing of the application. Once this formality is completed, the application is placed in the District Court for the final swearing-in by the judge. The swearings take place each second Tuesday of the month.

3. There will be a fee of $10.00.

4. VAIVADA can be one of the two witnesses required.
