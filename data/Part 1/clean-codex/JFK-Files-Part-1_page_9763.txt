1. Rector Rodolfo (Sandoval) Alegria is GLAT rebel who, together with Francisco Clara (Clara), engineered LIRING-5 and Francisco Larm (Larma)’s trip to Guatemala, where he is now incarcerated. Sandoval saw LIRING-4 several times after LIRING-5’s arrest, at one point saying he might have to hold up a bank to come to LIRING-4’s assistance.

According to Manuel Marcue (Marcue) Pardiñas, Sandoval was arrested 5 May shortly after his wife returned from a trip to Guatemala. Sandoval’s wife is now staying at a house owned by Marcue, together with the family of Francisco Larma.
