20 March 1964

Memorandum for: DDP/OP

TECH: Chief, Clandestine Services Personnel Office

USS.1: Charlotte Z. Bustos-Videla — Request for Quality Step Increase

1. It is recommended that a Quality Step Increase for Mrs. Charlotte Z. Bustos-Videla be endorsed by you for the reasons presented in the attached memorandum prepared by the Chief, Western Hemisphere Division.

2. A review of Mrs. Bustos-Videla's Official Personnel File will be made according to the standards of this office or agency. Without exception, the performance of this officer will be identified by various and all supervisors as “Superior” and “Outstanding.” Likewise, nothing in other records maintained by the Office of Personnel contracts or modifies the impressive record made by Mrs. Bustos-Videla.

3. Testimony to the high regard which officials of MI Division have for this officer's work is furnished by noting that Mrs. Bustos-Videla is the first female officer to be proposed for a QOI by MI and one of a total of but three officers nominated by that Division for the award since the QCI provision of the Federal Salary Reform Act of 1962 became effective in CIA approximately fifteen months ago.

SANDIAR

MI Division

James E. Johnson, Q.O.B.

May 11, 1975
