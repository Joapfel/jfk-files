Agency Employment History (Cont'd).

Section VIII. Inclusive dates: November 1955–June 1959. Location, country, city: Headquarters. Directorate office or division or branch: Security Supplemental Division, OS. Title of job: Chief Support Branch. Grades held in job: GS 1–4. Description of duties: This was basically the same as previously mentioned assignment. The Support Branch, because of its expansion of assignments, was elevated to Division status.

Inclusive dates: December 1954–November 1955. Location, country, city: Washington, D.C. Directorate office or division or branch: District Field Office Special Security Division, OS. Title of job: Assistant Special Agent in Charge, G-13–GS 14. Grades held in job: GS 1–4. Description of duties: Assisted the Special Agent in Charge in administering the District Field Office. This entailed the supervision of fifty investigative agents who conducted investigations on agency applicants, individuals who may be of interest to the Agency in either an overt or covert manner. Was responsible for the national agency checks conducted by DFO in the various government agencies. Also had supervisory cognizance over the operational support assignments given the DFO.

Inclusive dates: July 1952–December 1954. Location, country, city: Headquarters. Title of job: Chief Project Desk. Description of duties: Responsible for seven supervisors who processed covert clearance actions on individuals who were to be employed with or associated with the Agency. Represented OS at interagency committee monitoring proprietary projects. Gave notice and guidance on security problems involving operational projects. Delivered lectures on security matters for OTH.
