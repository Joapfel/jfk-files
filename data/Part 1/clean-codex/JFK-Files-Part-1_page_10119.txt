Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Rome. DEF: Chief, WE Division. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Approved for distribution.

Subject: O PBRUMER/LIRAVINE/Procurement of Certificate of Papal Blessing for LITAMIL-7.

1. LITAMIL-7, an aging spinster, is a penetration agent of the Mexico Station inside the BRISTOL Embassy. She has recently been seriously ill on two occasions and has been shabbily treated by her employers while the Station has lavished attention on her. As a result of this contract in demonstrated concern for her wellbeing, LITAMIL-7 has shown a determination to be of much more active service to the Station than in the past.

2. LITAMIL-7 is a devout and practical Roman Catholic. To do the recent understanding that certificates of Papal blessing can be obtained without too much difficulty in Rome. If this is the case, and the Rome Station can securely obtain such a certificate, it is believed that its presentation to her would cinch her already demonstrated determination to help the Station with its anti-AMTUG work.

3. A blessing of LITAMIL-7's home or prayer for her continued health or any other appropriate form of certificate would be much appreciated. Please T/A any costs to the Mexico Station. LITAMIL-7's identity follows by separate cover attachment.

Willard C. CURTIS

Attachment: Identity u/s/c.

Distribution: Orig & 1 - COS/Rome w/att u/s/e cent/judge; 1 - C/WED v/o att.
