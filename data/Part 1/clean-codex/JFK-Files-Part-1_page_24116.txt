Dispatch. ABDONY JAGUAR LEADER. ABDONY's group. Action required—references.

Refs: OEPA 158, 5 May 1967; OELA 4737, 10 May 1967.

1. We now have JAGUAR's comments on the proposed “Group” designed to allow ABDONY to expand his access by “representing” something—even if only a purely notional group. The letter from JAGUAR is attached.

2. We would observe that JAGUAR's comments are obviously made in a constructive vein. Similarly, JAGUAR seems to take a protective, almost proprietary interest in considerations affecting the ABDONY project. We naturally welcome this feeling on their part, since ABDONY continues to exist as a U.K.-based operation at their pleasure. And we think that JAGUAR's comments are sound, in substance. Their final comment also veils, to say the least, the understandable SEPARATE reluctance to see yet another splinter group sprawling, with the rightful political implications if a LCD BY spy were exposed while running a phoney “pro-Chinese” group in the U.K.! (It would probably be not too good in LCD BY, for that matter, but a government could fall here as a result.)

3. We have now had an opportunity to talk this over with CILIBERTI, LAGINESTRA and RODIGAST, and all are agreed that ABDONY's trial balloon will not be re-floated. We think no harm is done. If JAGUAR should come into possession of a copy of ABDONY's letter, and ask us about it, we will acknowledge that he wrote it to try out on a limited number of friends and aimed at his Swedish audience, preliminary to (he hopes) LOU WILLIAMS' arrival in Sweden. We are now merely replying to JAGUAR that we are grateful for their cogent comments, and that “the group” will not be activated unless there is a significant change in the ABDONY operational situation such as to require “the group” to be created, in which case we will again seek their concurrence.

Arthur T. JOELVICK
