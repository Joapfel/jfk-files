4. The most recent informers concerning President Duvalier's (Dixalier's) health have, however, caused the group to change its plan. The members now declare that time is of the essence and that as soon as President Duvalier (Dixalier) “goes on the operating table” they will go.

5. The group recognizes, however, that consistent difficulties lie in its path and is anxious to disclose the entire matter in secret with a U.S. envoy. What they have to say to this envoy is not known.

6. The group has organized the following procedure for the envoy to follow. First, we must send a cable to the following cable address: ESTSLAVAS, Port-au-Prince, Haiti. The cable must read as follows: “Arriving on (list date) to discover the signal DEAC. Please reserve a room for me at the Sans Souci (San's Souoci) Hotel.” The name signed to the cable does not matter. The cable address is that of Establishers Slavers-Plain & Co., Port-au-Prince, Haiti. Second, we must register at the Sans Souci (San's Souoci) and await a call of visit from a man named Edgard Boni. Boni is Kestner Blain's brother. The original family name is “Boni.” Blain changed his name. Boni will either take the envoy to talk to Constant or will inform him of arrangements made for the interview. The only persons present at the interview will be Constant and the envoy. Third, when the envoy meets Constant he must establish his bona fides by saying: “I have been sent by Mr. Eisenhower (Eisenhomer) to discuss SIGAL.” The key word is “Eisenhower” (Eisenhomer).
