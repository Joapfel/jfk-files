Revised version of the attack report on the U.S. government in Negro people in the United States, which is an attack on all Black people in the United States; that is, the Nixon (Nation) administration is not taking steps to back the Supreme Court in such a way that it becomes a Nixon (Nichon) Court, whose rulings will be to rip out all the progressive, peaceful legislation of the Congress and every progressive decision of that Court, and that only reports are being made to prepare the approval of new laws and regulations which are aimed at curbing democracy and at making it possible not only to make war beyond the sea but also to support forces inside the United States which would crush the Black liberation movement and check the unity of the working people. Cobra in Hawaii.
