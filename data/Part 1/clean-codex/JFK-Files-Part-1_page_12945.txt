Classified Message.

Date: 4 May 1959. To: Director. From: LUXEMBOURG 15-A. Action: WE 6. Info: COP, CI, CI/OPS, CI/IC, FI, FE 4, S/C 2. Routine. IN 12839.

To Director. Info. Cite.

Ref: (LUXE) 00094 (IN 10377), DIR 24181 (OUT 98923).

1. Had already asked QJBANNER 1 to get description, details, etc., but since he was unable to do so with clarity, RABNEY under HBFINCH cover questioned subject 4 May. Subject states following is all he knows but considers probable he knows more than he is telling or was involved in some deal for which he hopes to protect himself against legal action by stating he was working for Americans.

2. Armand Delacour, resident at 28 Rue Erard (also received as Emard), Paris, and three-time convict, approached subject in mid-April with offer for first reference. Stated two Sicilians living in Paris were already successfully engaged in this traffic, one of whom received 50 kilos of opium from Hong Kong and delivered it personally in the States, entering via Canada. Delacour gave no details other than that Chicoms provide of US gratis on condition of delivery to the States. When subject asked regarding entry into the States, Delacour replied that for U.S. $200 he could get full French documentation needed for an American visa.

3. Delacour also uses the name Delamare, age 47, height 175 cm, weight 70 to 72 kilos, brown hair, balding, oval face, brown eyes, crooked nose, thick lips, heavy cigarette smoker. Born Basque.
