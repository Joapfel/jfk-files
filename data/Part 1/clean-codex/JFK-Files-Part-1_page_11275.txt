Memorandum for: CI/OA

Attention:

Subject: Traces on Luis ALERU-Souto  
201-329509

Reference: CI/OA memorandum dated 15 June 1967

1. Per your referenced request, the FBI documents were located and reviewed with the following results:

a. The first document dealt with a Luis SOTO who was appointed Commercial Attaché of the Dominican Embassy, Madrid. This is not our subject.

b. All documents referenced Luis ALERU-Souto's assignment as Cuban Cultural Attaché in Mexico City, his residences, telephone numbers, and his relationships with the Spanish Communist group in Mexico.

c. The next three items deal with a Luis SOTO, a member of the PPS of Mexico. This is not our subject.

2. Subject was and his activities per paragraph 1.b are well known to this Agency. These activities were considered to be in line with his assignment and are not presently considered to offer a threat to operational security.

3. PRG Parts I and II will be forwarded to your Office upon receipt from the Station.
