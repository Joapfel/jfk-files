Director. RYBAT TYPIC.

1. Following is update significant developments within militant exile community.

2. Jose Torriente has told AMCHALK-I that negotiations are well along for obtaining two islands as training and operational bases for exiles. One is the Isla Beata off the south coast of the Dominican Republic. Negotiations with unidentified Dominicans for use of this island are taking place through the Argentine consul general in Puerto Rico. Torriente was in Puerto Rico last week. The other island, which is being negotiated for by ex-President Carlos Prio Socarras, is Ile a Vache, which is located off the southern Haitian peninsula. Training activities and recruiting for a maritime and infiltration course are currently taking place in the Miami area; however, thus far all training has been conducted under classroom conditions in private homes throughout the Miami area. Station is in possession of many of the gates of individuals.
