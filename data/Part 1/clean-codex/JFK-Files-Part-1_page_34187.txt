Continuation of Dispatch. Distribution symbol and number: UFGT-16628.

22. AMRUBY. No substantial change. See reference B. Project continues to produce an average of one PONY edition (30,000 copies) every three months for mailing into Cuba. As of 1 June, two of four AMRUBY-sponsored satire programs on AMTHIGH have been dropped.

23. AMICE. No substantial changes. AMICE continued effective boycott propaganda operations and served as a vehicle for launching the QUMALT-1 “DOLCE VITA” series. Review of project activities resulted in economies achieved by modest personnel cuts, use of a more selective mailing list, and elimination of two foreign-language editions of the monthly AMICE bulletin.

24. AMSPELL. As reported in reference B, financial support to AMSPELL has been cut sharply and is now limited to $1,000 monthly. This provides funds for a bimonthly bulletin, press releases, and special printed propaganda. AMSPELL has been active in station campaigns regarding political prisoners and the Latin American student movement (CLAE), and the principal agent has continued to be responsive to station guidance.

25. AMNON. No major changes in project activity. AMNON carried out a special radio and press campaign in connection with the forthcoming CLAE meeting in Havana. A special pamphlet on this subject is being prepared by AMNON for publication by AMHIM. AMNON is also the station’s main asset for the AMBASE program and has taken over AMBASE personnel from the terminated AMRUSTY project.

Philip G. Elmard.
