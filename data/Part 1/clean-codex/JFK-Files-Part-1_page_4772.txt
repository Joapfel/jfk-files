1. Over the past few weeks, EMPYREX and EMWISE have claimed to have no evidence that Orlando Bosch is in the Dominican Republic. However, EMPREDICT coverage of Admiral de Windt on 27 April indicates that Bosch is in Santo Domingo. Was not entered [sic] local hospital for cataract operation (per para two Ref C). Was picked up by one of the Dominican security services afternoon 27 April. Present whereabouts not known.

2. EMPYREX-2B says he has no recent knowledge where Bosch is located or who obtained him on 27 April. EMPYREX-2B advised Station that he would take no further investigative action since he convinced Bosch matter being handled out of palace by President Balaguer (BALAQUER).
