Classified message.

Date: 15 March 1965.

To: DIR. Info: MEXI.

Cite: WAVE 9539. Number.

Typic.

References: A. WAVE 9233 (5502). B. DIR 91534 (not received). C. MEXI 2752.

1. AMCOG-1, who accompanied PRIO to MEXI, reported the following information possibly related to the references. In a hotel room on 11 February, PRIO told A-1 that on the previous afternoon, while walking down a Mexico City street, an unidentified man (PRIO gave no description) approached him, shook hands, and identified himself as a G-2 official. PRIO said they then held a short talk on “general topics.”

2. AMCOG-1 unable to amplify story. According to A-1, PRIO did take meanwhile talks while MEXI City, but usually accompanied by group Cuban enemies. A-1’s view would be unusual for members of the Embassy to approach PRIO in the street.

3. Attempting to elicit further details from PRIO through AMPALM-4.

4. MEXI: Please forward photos mentioned in reference C. Will attempt to have PRIO review them for possible identification.

End of message.

16/01372.

Time. P — Carlos Prio Scully.
