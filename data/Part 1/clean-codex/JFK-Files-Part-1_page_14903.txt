Classified message. Originator: George A. Fill. Unit: SR/DOB. Extension: 8331. Date: 10 December 1958. Routing: 703 HM/WS/V.

To: Frankfurt. From: Director. Confidential: SR 6. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, FI, FI/OPS, EE 4, S/C 2. Received by Cable Section: 10 December, 14:58. Out: 52886.

To: Probable information, Frankfurt. Cite DIR 04034.

REDWOOD REDSOX AEACRE. Reference FROB 2891 (EN35499) ohogh.

1. Initial verbal results AEASPIC WB tests generally favorable. Prefer conduct further psychological interview in place prior to any movement.

2. HOS now attempting arrange for psychiatrist to meet with subject and FRIGANZA in Paris soonest.

3. Suggest push work complete assessment all available candidates. But movement to AEACRE to be compartmented and on highly selective base. (End of message)

C/S comment: Stated that AEASPIC suitable for black mission but not sure that he would be fit partner for AESATCHEL due to language problem and that temperaments markedly different.

SR/3. SR/DOP/FI. 11 December 1959. AC/SR/DOP.

Coordinating officers. George A. Fill.
