Classified message.

To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 8. Info: QOP, CI, CI/OPS, FI, RI/AN, SAS 8, VR.

341914Z. PACY info DIR cite MEXI 8494.

1. On 2 March, Panamanian Jaime de Leon left a message at Cuban EMS for Humberto Harry of Panama to call Leon at the latter’s hotel upon arriving in Mexico City. On 29 February and 1 March, one Harry had called Cuban EMS and asked if there was any message. He was told to call back later since “he” had not arrived. Also on 1 March, Herberto Jorrin of Cuban EMS called an unidentified man and asked if there was any message for Harry or Morales. The answer was no.

2. Both Leon and Harry asked for Rogelio (Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez, second secretary), but he was not in. (Source: LIENVOY.) Index.
