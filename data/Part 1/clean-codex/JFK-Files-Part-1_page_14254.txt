3 July 1968. IN 43255. Director, info JACOBEA.

On SOUTIMID of 15 June ’68, LIFET of ANAUTO-1 said ANAUTO-1 had made reservations for their departure for Peru (PERUEN) on 6 August to go on vacation. She commented that possibly (POSSISELY) ANAUTO-1 might not want to return to Madrid.
