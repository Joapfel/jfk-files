041527Z AUG 68 CITE MANAGUA 6689. Director, Mexico City; info JCOBRA.

1. Manuel Artime Buesa visited COS Managua night 3 Sep. Deeply distressed, reported his mother, Dra. Otilia Buesa Beaujardin, over 80, gravely ill in Havana, not expected to live much longer. Artime spoke with President Somoza 3 Sep asking help in obtaining permission for mother to leave Cuba. Somoza promised to call Mexican President Gustavo Diaz Ordaz to intercede with Fidel Castro.

2. Artime asked COS if WOFIRM could help in any way, perhaps through Diaz Ordaz, whom he considers best channel to Cuban government. Feels Castro might be willing to let his mother leave to prove he is “humanitarian” or to keep on good side of Mexican government.

3. COS expressed sympathy, said did not know what, if anything, could be done, but would relay request to headquarters.
