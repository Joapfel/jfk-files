Dispatch. RYBAT. Processing action.

To: Chief, WOLIME. Marked for BEROERING.

Info: COS, London; C/EUR. No BEROERING required.

From: Chief, LCPIPIT London. Only qualified desk can aid BEROERING.

Subject: DTDORIC/Operational ORPHONE/Verges.

Action required—reference: For the record we are forwarding, u/s/c, a copy of the letter to QRPHONE/1 from his Paris lawyer which outlines the status of QRPHONE/1's action against Jacques Verges. As Headquarters will recall this letter was used by QRPHONE/1 to good advantage in connection with his recent trip to Algiers. The letter says, in effect, that Verges has proposed a settlement of the matter and has agreed to pay this settlement in Algerian dinars. Needless to say, QRPHONE/1 has yet to receive payment. Nevertheless QRPHONE/1's purposes are well served by this attestation of Verges' expressed willingness to pay, with its implied admission of guilt!

Aaron C. LAGINESTRA.

Attachment: u/s/c. QRPHONE/1—Verges Letter.

Distribution: C/WOLIME with attachment; COS, London without attachment; C/EUR without attachment.

JATT. CS copy.
