Classified Message.

Secret 191640Z July 68. Cite Port au Prince 3289.

Director info Santo Domingo, JMCOBRA.

Henri Siclait, director of tobacco monopoly and intriguer against Clemard Joseph Charles, and who is now rumored in difficulties since Charles’s release, left Port au Prince 17 July on Pan Am 234, press-stated purpose to visit wife’s sick mother.

BT

19 July 68.
