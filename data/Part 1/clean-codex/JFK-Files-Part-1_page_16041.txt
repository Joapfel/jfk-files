20 January 1970

Memorandum for the File.

Subject: Mitchell Livingston Werbell III.

Reference: Memo from C/SOD/GB to C/WH/7 dated 15 January 1970. Subject: Possible Clandestine Planning Involving Haiti.

1. Andrew Bacuzzi, SOD/GB, advised 19 January 1969, after my phone call to Mr. Barquin pointing out the third-agency aspect of the Werbell report, that Col. Williamson had checked with his friend Col. Cochrane, and that the latter stated he had taken no other action with this information and intended to take none. Col. Cochrane apparently regards the information as being none of his own official business and was telling his friend, Williamson, about it as a private citizen.

2. Suggest we just use Williamson as the source.

James P. Mullen  
C/WH/7/HT
