10 August 1947

Dear Bob,

Thank you for initiating action on my promotion and for allowing me to remain on a transfer. I understand the situation about the transfer and realize that nothing more can be done in the matter, as settled in advance; however, since no complications will arise from my being carried—Corporate Head Office.

Have signed the personnel (personal) action papers, and this is, I hope, a rough draft of a job description.

Collection and dissemination of SI information; establishment and direction of intelligence nets; interrogation of POWs and refugees from Communist areas and North Korea; coordination of intelligence nets being run into North Korea, Communist Manchuria (atanchuria), Lairon, and Siteria; at present, I am directing a CL net in Mukden, which will eventually turn into an SI net.

Thanks again, Bob

Scott Miller
