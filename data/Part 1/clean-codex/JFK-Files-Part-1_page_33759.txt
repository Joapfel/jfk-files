23 June 1952

Memorandum for: Chief, WE

Subject: Debriefing of Harry Sperling by L. de Neuville

1. L. de Neuville recently cabled that H. Sperling, who is to be relieved as Chief of RFE information gathering in Munich, was talking freely of his removal and making comments about his replacement.

2. De Neuville suggested that Sperling be sent back to Washington for a debriefing. For many reasons, including our relations with NCFE, we would prefer it to be done in the field.

3. I therefore suggest that de Neuville be allowed to do this for us and would appreciate your approval of my sending him the attached message.
