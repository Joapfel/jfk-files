26 October 1964. P-8436, P-8544, P-7365, P-8789. Cuban Chronology.

Marcelino Miksal left for Havana on 22 October. Oscar Concepcion and Esther Gavilan, wife of Rogelio Rodriguez, left for Havana on 24 October. Joaquin Hernandez Armas and family left for Havana on 25 October. Per LIENVOY, 25 October, Armas said he would be gone only a week.

End of message.

MC C[h]oaden. FC Curtis. G. R. Pelleb.
