GHP. Commo circ. no. 26 October 1964. P-8436; P-5644 8544; P-7569; P-8789. Cuban Chrono.

May 1, 1964.

Typic JOGIN LIPIRE.

Marcelino Misal left for Havana (HAVA) 22 October. Oscar Concepcion and Esther Gavilan, wife of Rogelio Rodriguez, left for Havana (HAVA) 24 October. Joaquin Hernandez Armas and family left for Havana (HAVA) 25 October. Per LIENVOY 25 October, Armas said he would be gone only a week.

End of message.

MC Choaden. G. R. Pellen. W. C. Curtis.
