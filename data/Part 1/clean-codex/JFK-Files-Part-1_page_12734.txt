Cite Caracas 24453. To: Director. WKRALLY/WKSCARLET.

1. Ultimas Noticias published a story regarding “Watergate Criollo” (page 44) on 14 May. Both in the headline and in the story, WKSCARLET-3 was mentioned using only his WKSCARLET alias.

2. According to the story, WKSCARLET-3, a Cuban who worked for SKHESALD and LNARGO in the past and has a vast intelligence background, brought various spying devices into Venezuela: binoculars with which one can read license plates at 1,028 meters, a mechanism to locate routes of cars, and other objects to tap telephones.

3. The supposed purpose of the equipment was to obtain pre-election information from Torre de las Delicias, where the offices of Carlos Andres Perez were located while he was campaigning for president.

4. The story attributed the information to “a source,” and according to “the authorities,” the case would be taken to court with a record copy.
