Via: Air or sea pouch. Dispatch No. Date: 7 September 1951.

To: Chief Sub-station, Monterrey. From: Acting Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Subject: General—Specific—Administrative.

1. You are hereby notified that approval has been granted for subject to take Spanish lessons at Government expense abroad.

2. The following conditions govern this authorization:

a. The employee should receive this training under secure circumstances for reasons of immediate operational necessity;

b. The cost to the Government will not exceed $250.00 during any fiscal year for subject; and

c. Such language lessons will directly benefit the Government.

3. You may reference this dispatch as authority for payment when recording the disbursements in your monthly accounting reports.

Dennis T. Copeland  
Dean K. Copland
