Madrid 4825, 02 November 1968. Director, info JMWAVE. Topic: Debriefing Center. Reference: JMWAVE 3925, 3 November 1966; IN 3-4-94.

1. Dr. Salomon (Mitrani), Ruso, PP 267543, born Havana 23 January 1921, arrived Madrid 28 April. Wife and son in U.S. Reputed to have had one of finest clinics in Cuba and was at one time Doctor Fidel Castro. Though has not treated Castro since about 1961, will be interviewed by QUWARDS on this and other related subjects. If HOS or JMWAVE have special requirements, please forward soonest.

2. Mitrani unable to exit via Varadero airlift and he very nervous about possible refusal U.S. visa. He will submit to any interviews and cooperate to maximum to prove his current and fast pro-U.S. position.

3. Only info station files is that in reference.
