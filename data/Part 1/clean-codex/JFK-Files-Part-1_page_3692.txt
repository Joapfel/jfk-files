REF: MANAGUA 7336 (IN085604).

I. Following comments re ref may be useful COS Managua:

A. Peru making real effort become world's largest producer and exporter of fishmeal. Expected kickback on commission to generals not surprising. Generals probably include Artola.

B. Re para two ref, station has no knowledge of ousting leftist civilian adviser to President Velasco Alvarado, with possible exception of Ruiz Eldridge, who had been named Peruvian ambassador to Brazil. Not clear whether Ruiz being eased out because of leftist/pro-Communist leanings as a result of pressure from Army or whether appointment represents reward to Ruiz—possibly a combination of both.

C. Government spokespersons, particularly Velasco and Artola, continue to deny Communist influence in government, despite this being common knowledge. Government officials privately.
