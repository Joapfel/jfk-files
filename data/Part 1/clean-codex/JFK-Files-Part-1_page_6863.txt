On 29 October the Halperins left Legue at 0400 hours by train for Zurich. During their sojourn at Leger, no contacts of the couple with strangers were made; they went for walks at random in town or in the vicinity, bought papers and periodicals by the door, but did not receive any mail or telephone calls.

At 1253 hours on 29 October they arrived in Zurich, went to the Hotel “Schweizerhof” and later to the Czech Consulate General. At 1550 hours the subject inquired at the ALM airline agency about refunding the tickets to Tel-Aviv, but the agency had not received directions from Amsterdam yet.

On 30 October the couple again went to the Czech Consulate General at 1035 hours and remained there for 75 minutes. At 1215 hours Halperin inquired at the Muoni Travel Agency about tourist visas to Prague and was given the necessary forms to fill in. He returned these to the agency at 1410 hours together with the passports. At 1715 hours he mailed two letters to:

Mr. David C. Halperin, 5411 South University Ave., Chicago, Ill.

Rabbi and Mrs. Hillel Cameran, 4 de Fald Place, New York, N.J.

Photostat (numbered 5 and 6) of the contents are attached.

On 31 October the Halperins (Halferins) moved from Hotel “Schweizerhof” to the cheaper hotel “Limathof.” On this day they finally received the requested refund from the ALM airline’s office.

Monday, 10 November, the requested tourist visas were issued to the Halperins, valid for 6 days and including hotel reservations for 5 days at Sw. Fre. 23 per person. They immediately departed by train and left Switzerland via Bucha (Buchs) by rail at 1824 hours for Austria, traveling on second-class tickets Zurich-Prague return.

In the period from 31 October to 10 November the couple did not make or receive any telephone calls and had no visitors, nor did they call on anybody.
