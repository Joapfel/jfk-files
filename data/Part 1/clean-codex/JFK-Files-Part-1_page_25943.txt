Intelligence Information Cable.

IN 826592.

Information report. Cite TDFIR DB-315/0169A-7b. Distributed 17 February 1976.

Country: Venezuela/Chile.

Date of information: 16 February 1976.

Subject: Possible attempt on the life of a relative of former President Salvador Allende.

Acquired: Venezuela, Caracas (17 February 1976). Field No. HVC-7328.

Source: A businessman with close ties to the Cuban exile community. He is a usually reliable source.

1. Anti-Cuban government exiles are currently in Caracas, Venezuela, with the intention of killing Luis Andres Pascal Allende, a relative of former Chilean President Salvador Allende. Source comment: Pascal's exact connection with Salvador Allende or his current activities in Caracas are unknown.

2. The attempt against Pascal is to be carried out by Rolando Otero Hernandez and other members of Cuban exile groups. Although it is unclear as to whether Otero had actually arrived in Caracas, he is expected to be in Caracas by the evening of 17 February 1976.
