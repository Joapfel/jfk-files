Report on Contact with Agent or Defector

Subject: WIROGUE  
Date: 16 January 1963  
Time: 1100  
Place:  
Comments:

ORNE K. DARONNAT, Case Officer

Case Officer dropped by to see Subject and paid him subsistence.

Subject's morale is high but he would like to get some word soon on what is to be done with him. He was told that we had queried headquarters and asked them to advise as soon as possible and that there was nothing more we could do for the present.
