28 October 1970

Memorandum for: CI/OA

Attention: Mr. Lally

Subject: GICITRON-4 (201-267298)

1. We have received your memorandum of 22 October 1970 informing us that GICITRON-4's file has been closed. However, from Bogota Station correspondence we know that GICITRON-4 is still of operational interest and there is a strong probability that the missing PRO Part II has been delayed by the recent restriction on pouching material to and from the field.

2. It is requested that the file on GICITRON-4 be reopened and his POA be extended for an additional 30 days to allow time for the PRO Part II to arrive or to be prepared if need be.

Burdell Merrell  
WH/3/Colombia
