Classified message.

Origin: A.D. Wedmeyer:jp. Unit: WH/3/Mexico. Extension: 5940. Date: 7 September 1962 1865.

To: TAIPAI, MEXICO CITY. From: Director. Confidential: WH 8. Information: CI, CI/OPS, FI, NE 4, FE 6, S/C 2.

8 September 65, 0044Z.

SIG CEN. Deferred. Routine.

OUT 74407.

To TAMI, MEXI. Information. Cite DIR 34954.

1. Mexican President Lopez Mateos planning 15–20 day Asian tour starting 10 October in New Delhi, followed by visits to Tokyo, DJAK, and possibly MANI.

2. Request TAMI and MEXI views by 12 September regarding advisability of TAMI station prompting GRC to invite Lopez Mateos to include TAMI on tour.

End of message.
