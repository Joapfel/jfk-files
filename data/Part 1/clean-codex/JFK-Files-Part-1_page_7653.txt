28 February 1967

Memorandum for: Mr. Nathan L. Ferris, Legal Attaché

From: Winston M. Scott

Subject: Travel of Soviets

1. A sensitive, reliable source has reported that Soviet Minister Boris Aleksandrovich Kazantsev went to Veracruz on 25 February 1967, and that Soviet Consul Valentin Sergeyevich Logindov (Logindv) and Soviet Commercial Counselor Viktor Mikhaylovich Ivanov planned to go on 20 February 1967 in order to attend to three Soviet ships there in port. It has also been reported that Soviet Cultural Attaché Svyatoslav Fedorovich (Fodorovich) Kuznetsov planned to be in Querétaro on 27 February 1967 to attend a ceremony at the university there.

2. Any information available to your office on the activities of these Soviets in Veracruz and Querétaro would be appreciated.

This information is from a confidential source. It must not be disseminated in whole or in part outside your office without prior approval of the originating office in Mexico City.

Prepared by State Department.

Jonathan L. Weening/fkm

28 February 1967
