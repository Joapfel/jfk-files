NYOR CITE WA 40143

From EE/USSR (REAMS).

Following is for your information.

Elliott Beach MacRae, president of E. P. Dutton, was briefed on 28 and 29 February in the DD/I's and DC/I's offices by Walter Pforzheimer, Messrs. Lowery and Koehne of OCI, Shryoch of ONE, and Reams for CO/C. DD/I was not present. The briefing was the result of a letter from MacRae to DCI in which he noted his plan to leave 13 March for a book-marketing trip to London and Paris. Following this business trip, he will spend three or four days each in Belgrade, Sofia, Budapest, and Bucharest, “just for fun.” In a letter to OCI, MacRae apparently solicited guidance, so the briefing was not really an intelligence one but a matter of providing answers to such questions as:

(A) Can I take a transistor radio and a camera into Bulgaria without having them appropriated?

(B) Should I have a typhus shot?

(C) Is water in Bulgaria and Rumania potable?

(D) Who is the “Shakespeare” of Bulgaria (a tough one, that)?

(F) What is the usual attitude of Bulgarians and Rumanians?
