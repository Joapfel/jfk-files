23 October 1967

1. LIRING/3 reported to Rowton on 23 October 1967 that Carmen Lozada, a neurologist, and Canudas (FZU), a chemical engineer, visited his home on the evening of 21 October in the company of Carmen Carrasco (Carasco). LIRING/3 and LIRING/4 state that they knew both Carmen Lozada and Canudas in Havana since 1961; both are Mexicans and were under contact with the GOC. They were surprised to learn from them that they would not return to Cuba because of disillusionment.

2. Carmen Lozada stated that she would divorce her husband. Enrique Barírez, a civil engineer, was still in Nueva and would resume work at the Polytechnic in Mexico City.

3. Canudas informed LIRING/3 that their mutual friend Rodolfo Reyes (Rolfo Reyes), of the ballet folklórico, much to everyone’s surprise, would return to Cuba next month. LIRING/3 promised Rowton to get in touch with Reyes to determine his motivation for returning. LIRING/3 surmises that the real reason may be Reyes’s disgust with his family in Mexico and desire to be reunited with his Cuban wife, who, according to LIRING/3, is talented and very attractive.

4. Carmen Lozada informed LIRING/3 that her family, who are very close to Ambassador Armas Hernández Armas (Ármex Hernandez Armas), a frequent guest at their home, would be disappointed to learn of her decision to divorce her husband and remain in Mexico.
