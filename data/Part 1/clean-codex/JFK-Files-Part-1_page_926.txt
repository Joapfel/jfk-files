Trucks hauling boxes arrived at the port of Batabano from Isla de Pinos.

3. On 29 October at about 3:00 p.m., two olive green ten-wheeled trucks driven by militiamen arrived in Batabano and were shipped aboard the Isla de Pinos ferry, which left the slip at 4:00 p.m. The trucks had an enclosed metal body with small side windows, and each truck pulled a four-wheeled trailer. (Field comment: A usually reliable source reported that on 28 October ZIL trucks were on board the Isla de Tesoro ferry, which sailed for Gerona.)

4. Field dissem: CINCSO, CINCLANT, COMNAVKEYWEST.

End of message.
