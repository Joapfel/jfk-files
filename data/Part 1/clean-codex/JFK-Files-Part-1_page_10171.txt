Date: 12 Jan 63. Classified message.

To: Director. From: Mexico City. Routing. Action: TFW 10 (TFW copy slotted at 2200 14 Jan 63 DM). Jan 15 0300Z 63. Priority. Info: DDP, ADDP, DODS, DODS/M 4, CA 2, SROG, CA/PEG, CA/PROP, CA/O 4, CI/OPS, CI/IC 2, FI, FI/D, FI/INT 2, IW 2, WH 7, DD/I, AD/CI 2, AD/NE, S/C 2. IN 4:0880.

Priority DIR info WAVE cite MEXI 391.

GYROSE LITAMIL-7

1. On 14 Jan 63 LITAMIL-7 reported that Ambassador Carlos Lechuga, his wife and child left Mexico for Havana via Cubana on that date. When queried whether they were returning to Mexico, Lechuga’s wife told L-7, “We do not know,” and Ambassador Lechuga told her he was returning to Mexico as he was still Cuban delegate to the United Nations. L-7 has a feeling Lechuga is not returning as Ambassador to Mexico.

2. L-7 was completely surprised at the departure of Lechuga’s wife, whom she saw 12 Jan and who made no comment about the impending departure for Cuba. Lechuga did not make an appointment with Manuel Tello scheduled for 1960 on 11 Jan, and on 12 Jan Ramon Sinobas asked L-7 to call the Foreign Office to ascertain whether the appointment scheduled for 14 Jan was confirmed. L-7 called but received no answer from the Foreign Office. L-7 believes the Lechuga-Tello meeting was held the morning of 14 Jan, as Lechuga left the Embassy early.

End of message.
