To instruct HYSAGE that she is to withdraw from the National Coalition Party and overtly join one of the leftist parties or try to form a new one. The instruction is clear that the left is to get together. HYSAGE was also again enjoined to get her son out of PBPRI ME. After hearing the instructions that GALAN had re unity, HYSAGE told GALAN that she, as a woman, could not accomplish all this, and she did in fact decide to bring together HUGO ALEJANDRO VICTOR, her closest friend in Partido del Pueblo (PDP, People's Party Communist), and DAVID TURNER, leader of Vanguard of National Action (VAN). (Comment: Her remarks indicated that she did not include JORGE TURNER because he is apparently seriously ill.) GALAN urged VICTOR and TURNER, as representatives of their respective organizations, to make a new effort to settle differences and bring about a more coordinated effort.

As a result of GALAN's visit, a meeting took place at noon on 24 October for the purpose of discussing unity, aggressive action, and merger of all leftist units to go to elections as a bloc if attempts to stop elections should fail. This meeting was attended by VICTOR, CARLOS DEL CID, RENATO PEREIRA, THELMA KING, ALVARO MENENDEZ, and two unidentified students. Also during the meeting, “NEGRO” MARTINEZ, a professor of Spanish at the university, whom HYSAGE identified as a member of the Communist Party secret apparatus, called VICTOR aside for a short conversation. (Comment: Believe this may be JOSE DE JESUS MARTINEZ, philosophy professor at the university.)
