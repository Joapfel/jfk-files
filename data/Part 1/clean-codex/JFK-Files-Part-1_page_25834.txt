Name: Raymond H. Gerende

Division: WH

Subject: Photography briefing

Dates submitted: 3 July, 0930–1230

On 3 July 1957, a photography briefing was given to Raymond H. Gerende. Mr. Gerende was briefed on film processing, printing, telephoto lenses, and fixed and mobile surveillance. He was shown the following equipment: Leica, Exakta (Exacta), Nikon (Nikkons), Minox, and Robot cameras; the hidden device for the Robot camera; Horse 100 ft. tank; and Nikor multiple tank. The briefing lasted three hours.
