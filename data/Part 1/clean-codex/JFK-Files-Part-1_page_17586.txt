Reference: HDM-W-6820, 24 Sept. 1959.

1. There is attached a Review of Project LIFEAR for the period 1 July 1958 to 30 June 1959.

2. Part I of the Outline has been revised this year. Station Mexico felt that with the number of revisions which have been made since a complete project renewal request was submitted for FY 1958, this was warranted. It should be noted that for purposes of uniformity all indigenous personnel in the LIFEAR complex have now been given a cryptonym, and at present this breakdown goes from /1 to /16. Any future personnel changes will be added to this numbering series.

3. Part II is submitted in its entirety.

4. It is requested that Project LIFEAR be renewed for FY 1960 for the amount indicated herein.

Willard C. Curtis

Attachments:

1. Odd pages (5 sets).

2. Even pages (5 sets).

Distribution: 3 copies to Headquarters with 5 sets of attachments.

21 May 1959.
