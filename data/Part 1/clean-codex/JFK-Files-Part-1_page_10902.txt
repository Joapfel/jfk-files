Central Intelligence Agency. Official Routing Slip. To: Lah - Algeria 6.35; date: C.S.I.; initials: KAB. To: Attn: A. J. Bobb. To: Bob. Action. Direct reply. Prepare reply. Recommendation. File. Return. Information. Signature. Approval. Dispatch. Comment. Concurrence.

Remarks:

'6-6: Recommend that no dissem be made of this. Zirout probably mean that Algeria would soon open up embassies in these countries, rather than "establish diplomatic relations". Both Spain and Brazil have ambassadors in Algiers, and here had for some time. A Cairo MDA item 6 April 64 announced that Algeria planned to open embassies shortly in several countries, including Brazil and Argentina. Our Embassy in Algiers confirmed this on 21 April - unknown and the Brazilian Arb in Algiers said that as far as he knew no changes had been made in these plans in spite of the fall of the Goulart over.
