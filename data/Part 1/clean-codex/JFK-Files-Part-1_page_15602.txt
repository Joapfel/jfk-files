Classified message.

To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: AF 7. No.: DFD 4, CI/OPS, CI/OA, FI, S/C 2. Routine. IN 15023.

Air Cite LECUP 1957

WIRGUE

1. WIRGUE in contact with Paul Arnold Pierrot, who designated head of Congolese (CONSOLESE) Air Force. Pierrot offered WIRGUE a job as administrator for pilot and mechanic instructors with rank of major in Congolese Army.

WIRGUE to see Defense Minister 7 March to sign contract.

2. WIRGUE to provide names and attempt to hire three pilot instructors and three mechanics within 30 days, with transportation, houses, and salary of 33,000 ECF per month paid by Congolese (CONGOBLESE) Government.

More instructors needed later.

3. If WIRGUE accepted, necessary KUBARK (KUEARK) suggest instructor personnel with WIRGUE as channel for hiring. WIRGUE states he needs documentary record of hiring. WIRGUE can support story he is ex-captain German Army.

Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
