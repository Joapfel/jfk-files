5 October 1950.

“STALENT” Photo-Book Project (to be named KILLNOUT)

This is a project for preparation of a book of photographs plus text on the career of Stalin.

Then formally written up, it will be titled KILLNOUT, and will be a sub-project of SUTRI, requiring only the approval of IST.

Some photographs have been assembled from CIA Graphics and from agencies in New York, and a legal agreement has been drafted and signed between CIA and Robert H. Headley, who will prepare the book for publication in the United States under his own name, retaining the copyright, which, together with royalties, he will assign to CIA.

Mr. Headley is now working on the book. It is planned that, after publication in the United States, there will be editions, some perhaps subsidized, in many foreign areas.

I assume you have the details on this.

I am listed as responsible for this, but know nothing about it. I am going to look at the file today. Ray Lillard and IST know about it.

“Stalin’s Biography”

This, I believe, is not technically a project. But I understand that Bob H. Headley (Feadley), a contract agent, has instructions from IST, after finishing the “Stalin” photo-book, to complete a biography of Stalin for publication in the United States under his own name, with the expectation of foreign editions to follow.

FSS
