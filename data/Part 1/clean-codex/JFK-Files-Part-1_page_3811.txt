Dear Jin:

Just a few lines to report the following:

1. I believe that Communism will concentrate on Nicaragua as a point of attack.

2. The men who have returned to Nicaragua after receiving training in Cuba only acted indirectly in the bloody events of the 22nd because, according to information gathered in Nicaragua through “Pepe Fernandez,” the “Sandinistas,” communists who follow the Castro line, were awaiting the assassination of Somoza in Leon, and this was never realized. These men were expecting an outbreak before Tacho Somoza took over the Presidency of the Republic.

3. I am structuring an anticommunist intelligence net in Nicaragua, which I have begun to instruct. This net is composed of:

Jose Fernández (“Pepe” Fernández), manager of Casa Hook.

Rodolfo Jerez, owner of Casa “Venu,” capitalist of various enterprises.

Orlando Castrillo—studies law, fifth year; he has a grocery business.

Duilio Cabrera—studies law, fourth year. Works in the Ministry of Agriculture, in the Department of Livestock. He is 25 years old.
