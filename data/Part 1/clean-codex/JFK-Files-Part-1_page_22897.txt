EC533. 15 August 1968. Contacts New York. From OSS (Musulin).

New case 51,605—request to identify vehicle consigned to Soviet embassy, Buenos Aires, Argentina.

1. This is a new case opened to your office.

2. GOD/Maritime requests information on the make and model of the car consigned to Ivan Gavrilovich Alferyanz and reportedly destined for the Soviet embassy, Buenos Aires, Argentina. The car was placed aboard SS Mormacpride, Moore McCormack Lines, merchantman (MERCHANDMAN), 4 August 1968, due to arrive Buenos Aires 18 August 1968. Shipping records should provide the desired identification and other pertinent information. Please expedite reply ASAP.
