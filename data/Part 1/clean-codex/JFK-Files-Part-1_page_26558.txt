Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Santo Domingo; Chief of Station, Mexico City. Department: Chief of Station, WII Division. Reference: KMOFOR-1/Victor RICO Galan. Signature: KMOFOR-1/Victor RICO Galan. Reference: See below; HDC-3048 (not sent Mexico).

1. Attached are two pictures of Victor RICO Galan. Headquarters requests that the Station ask EMFOR-1 if this is the same individual as the subject of Paragraph 6 of the Reference.

2. If the pictures are identified as the subject of Paragraph 6 of the Reference, Headquarters requests that EMFOR-1 and other Station sources be queried for all available information concerning the Subject's activities and contacts while in the Dominican Republic as well as all possible travel information.

Eugene P. TANGUIST.

Attachment: 2 pictures, Pages 1 and 2 HDC-3048.

Distribution: 2 copies to COS, Santo Domingo, with pictures; 1 copy to COS, Mexico City, with HDC-3048. Date distributed: 5 September 1966.
