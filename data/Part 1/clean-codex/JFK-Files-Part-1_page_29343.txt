Dear Mr. Pearson:

Reference is made to your inquiry dated 13 February 1976 concerning Mr. Grayston L. Lynch.

Mr. Lynch was employed by the Central Intelligence Agency from February 1961 until his retirement in September 1971. He was a loyal and dedicated officer whose performance was considered exceptional. His character and general reputation while with CIA were above reproach.
