Telephone dispatch to USN AAR 2. File No. 821-57357. Date: 15 September 1972.

To: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. From: Chief of Station, WH/Miami. Subject: Typicakile—Archalkys. Reference: WH/Miami 15782.

1. During the 12 September 1972 meeting mentioned in reference, Archalkys commented on AMBREW/1's latest dock and the fact that he was mentioned in three or four pages. Sensing Archalkys discomposed, Pachankis inquired as to the reason. Archalkys replied, “It's not that I'm upset or not. I just see no reason to bring me out in this rock.”

2. The above is forwarded to headquarters for whatever interest it may have, especially in light of the proposed use of Archalkys in the AMWLF/1 operation.

Stanley F. Speckler.

Distribution: C/WHD. E2 IMPDET.
