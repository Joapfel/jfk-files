Classified Message. Date: 4 August 1962. To: Director. Date/time: 4 August 2025Z 1962. Priority. IN 40150. From: Mexico City. Action: C/TFW 5 (Mr. Mulloy TFW notified and copy slotted at 1745, 4 August). Info: DDP, ADDP, C/C1, C/WH 5, s/c 2.

Priority, director information. WAVE cite MEXI 1614.

Ref: MEXI 1475, IN 32730. GYROSE/LITAMIL-1/RYBAT.

Per CSN 43-43.

1. Night 3 August, Carlos Lechuga Hevia, Cuban ambassador Mexico, called LITAMIL-1 and asked him what had developed regarding contact PARA and reference. L-1 said he had not heard but supposed case still under study.

2. Lechuga moving highly interesting, as L-1 was to have made contact with Lechuga when news regarding contact available.

3. Mexico City opinion L-1 should contact Lechuga soonest in order to expand discussion regarding reference and seek common ground regarding Lechuga’s personal position. In view of Lechuga’s scheduled trip to Havana 10 August, request headquarters comments soonest as to what L-1 might say. Station opinion Lechuga interested in U.S. contact in order to discuss Cuban-U.S. relations or personal situation.

End of message.

C/S comment: LITAMIL-7 told Cardos (Carlos) Lechuga that LITAMIL-1 interested in talking to him regarding personal matter.
