Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Venezuela/Cuba.

Date: Late September 1977.

Subject: Efforts by prominent Venezuelans to ensure impartial consideration of the case against Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch.

ACD Venezuela, Caracas, 30 September 1977. Field No. HVC-7761.

Source: A Venezuelan businessman with a wide range of contacts with Venezuelan security officials. His reliability has not been established. This information should not be discussed with officials of any foreign government, including those of Venezuela.

1. In late September 1977, a group of prominent Venezuelans met with President Carlos Andres Pérez (PerEZ) to discuss the case against Cuban exile leader Orlando Bosch (Dosc) Chávez, who is currently imprisoned in Venezuela in connection with the 6 October 1976 bombing of a Cubana airliner, and to speak in Bosch's behalf, while not soliciting preferential treatment.
