In view of Ref B, we note with alarm AMGLEVE-15's continual tendency to become involved in clandestine sabotage activities, and his failure to report the complete story to WOFIRM or CIRASH of his activities which he feels he wants to withhold for whatsoever reason. Therefore, request for POA for AMCLEVE-15 has been cancelled and he is to be handled as a hostile per CSI-F 10-5 until his motives are completely ascertained. We are doubly concerned over the same report, which lists demolition items smuggled into Caracas from JMNAVE area by AMGLEVE-15, including devices peculiar to WOFIRM and whose use could tie WOFIRM to this type of activity and become a source of political embarrassment to the government during election year.

2. We have no objection to continued contact as a source of information on activities of CIRASH and UZCATEGUI organizations available to him through the normal course of his official duties, and no objection to payments for information received. However, there to be.
