1. Based on conversations with HUFABRIC-14 evening 18 February and morning 19 February, what follows are details of events leading to arrest of Orlando Bosch Avilas.

A. Based on GARLIGER background to this case (paras 1 and 2 ref), specifically FBI involvement, Bosch was been of interest to HUFABRIC liaison since about February 1975. Their interest was intensified by their knowledge of Bosch's involvement with bombings, not only in connection with recent bombings in Costa Rica but also in connection with Kissinger's visit.

B. As indicated para S ref, liaison had pinpointed neighborhood which Bosch was believed to reside. Recently liaison learned that Bosch was interested in talking to the chief of operations of the liaison service (HUQLIB-8, a station penetration of that service) but was advised by other.
