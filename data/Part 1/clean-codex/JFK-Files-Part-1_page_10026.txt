In discussion of this case with ESLYRIC-1, COS stressed the desirability of attaching Detective Orozco, as has been the case with “Gallo Girl.” Public denunciations of FAR/LEADER/K CURANS by Orozco would undoubtedly have great impact, particularly at this time when internal dissension is rampant in the Guatemalan Communist movement. ESLYRIC-1 agreed and stated that if Orozco were turned over to Guatemala, then attempting to secure his destruction would be the primary effort. Much would depend on Orozco’s willingness to cooperate, and the station, of course, cannot guarantee that he might not be executed.
