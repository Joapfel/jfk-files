2 November 1970

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Retirement Annuity  
Mr. Grayston Lynch

1. Retirement Operations Division of the Office of Personnel (Linda Birch) provided the following computations for a proposed annuity for Mr. Grayston Lynch if he retired on the dates indicated. These figures were computed on 30 October 1970 and were based on the following:

As of 31 December 1970: age 47 years, 6 months (DOB: 14 June 1923); creditable service, including S/L, 31 years, 8 months; high average (3 years), $19,655; basic annuity per annum, $9,979; basic annuity per month, $832; basic annuity reduced for survivor per annum, $9,251; basic annuity reduced for survivor per month, $771; survivor annuity per annum, $5,488; survivor annuity per month, $457.

As of 30 April 1974: age 47 years, 10 months; creditable service, including S/L, 32 years; high average (3 years), $20,261; basic annuity per annum, $10,478; basic annuity per month, $873; basic annuity reduced for survivor per annum, $9,700; basic annuity reduced for survivor per month, $808; survivor annuity per annum, $5,763; survivor annuity per month, $480.

2. The above annuity would be recomputed when Mr. Lynch becomes 62 years old. This recomputation is required to drop off post-1958 military service, which applies to Social Security eligibility. Subject has no choice in this matter if he is eligible for Social Security benefits. If he is not eligible for Social Security benefits, then the military service will remain creditable toward his Civil Service retirement annuity. The recomputed annuity would be as follows:

Basic annuity per annum: $8,695; $9,145.  
Basic annuity per month: $725; $762.  
Basic annuity reduced for survivor per annum: $8,096; $8,500.  
Basic annuity reduced for survivor per month: $675; $708.
