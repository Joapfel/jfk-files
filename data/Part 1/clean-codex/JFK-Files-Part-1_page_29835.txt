Official Dispatch.

To: Chief of Station, METS.  
From: Chief, PEC.  
Subject: General—Promotion.  
Date: 13 August 1969.  
Specific—Hence: David E. Marpley.

Dispatch orders to FZ., Personnel; promotion of Roland L. Jenkins, 28(1)-46, dated 21 April 1949.

To fully endorse the estimate of Roland L. Jenkins’s qualifications and accomplishments set forth in the referred dispatch, however, the promotion board is at present unable to consider his promotion because of the following:

a. He has been in the grade of G-12 since 5 April 1948. This is several years after the time in grade necessary to be considered for promotion.

b. The grade of G-13 generally requires more than one tour of duty with the existing rank.

C. B. Swift, Jr.

Prepared by: C. Stoltz Y.D.

Releasing Officer. Coordinating Officer. Administrative Officer.
