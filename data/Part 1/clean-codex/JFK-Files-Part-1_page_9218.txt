File “STALIN” Photo-Book

20 February 1951

Re: SHUB and BRAINWAVE

At the request of Mr. Winston, I phoned Shub (AC-2-0164) to ask about progress on the photo-book and the earliest date at which he might be through with it. No answer.

RAW says Kintner is asking for him for BRAINWAVE. I suggested not interrupting him until he had finished the photo-book. RAW consulted LST, who said to say Shub might be made available on a part-time basis.

RAW understands he would do his work for BRAINWAVE in New York. I said this seemed okay, if it were to be only part-time.

21 February 1951

I phoned Kintner regarding Shub. He had recently seen Shub in New York.

Shub said he had reached the stage in his work on the photo-book for us where his collaborator would have to do most of the work for about a month.

Kintner said the work Shub would do for him would be in New York and only part-time. I said this all sounded okay. Shub was not referred to by name over the phone.

WCS
