TSS/PB/Training Division Evaluation. Basic Photography No. 1. Darkroom.

Jame Spetzler, Timothy Jones. Division JTP, Branch. 1963. Dates trained: from 9/24/57 to 9/28/57.

This course is primarily designed to develop the following skills: use of 35 mm cameras with accessories, document copy, processing and printing.

I. Manipulation of camera: Leica — None and Fair; Retina II C — None and Fair; Recordak — None and Fair.

II. Processing and printing: Film loading — Good; Film processing — Good; Enlarging — Good; Reflex and contact printing — Good.

III. Use of accessory equipment: Exposure meter — None and Excellent; Filters — None and Excellent; Telephoto and wide angle lenses — None and Excellent.

IV. Document copy and small objects: Available light — Excellent; Accessory illumination — Excellent; BOOWU, portra lens, focus slide — Excellent.

V. Ground photography: Coverage — Excellent; Report — Excellent.

VI. Casing: Coverage — Excellent.
