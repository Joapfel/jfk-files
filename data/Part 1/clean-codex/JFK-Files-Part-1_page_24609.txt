Dispatch. Processing action: DQ. To: Chief, WH Division. Info: Chief, SB Division. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Subject: POA and Trace Request.

Action required—references. Reference: (A) HUHU-15480, 4 May 1967; (B) MEXICO CITY 7743, 22 October 1968.

1. We would appreciate receiving Headquarters traces on Identity, subsequent to the negative results per ref. (A), and a POA for contact and assessment. We are considering Identity as a possible access agent in the REDTOP field, specifically against Nikolay Sergeyevich LEONOV (201-132139), known KGB, and his eventual replacement.

2. As Headquarters is aware, the Station maintains an active interest in LEONOV (LEOBOV). Per ref. (B), he has been a principal contact and source of both funds and documents for Guatemalans travelling to the USSR and for Central American exiles, and he has also been linked repeatedly and prominently with the support activity provided to Latin American Communist parties.

3. Although LEONOV may depart before Identity can be used effectively against him, we feel certain that his replacement will be engaged in similar extra-Press Office activities of prime interest to us.

For Willard C. Curtis (CUETIS).

Attachment: Identity Sheet, under separate cover.

Distribution: 2—C/WH, with attachment. CS copy in 201-542977. 12—C/SB, with attachment.
