102-1809. 26 February 1980.

Victor RICO Galan, writer for Siempre, was moderator at the first meeting of the Political Study Circle, which had been formed in UNY and through which Communist sympathizers hoped to take part in student council elections. (C-3).

CS.

Victor Rico Galan was among those journalists who, on 11 July, were elected to the Mexican preparatory committee for the Second International Congress of Journalists and had to be held incommunicado in October 1960. (LIFT R B-2)

Current: He is a leftist journalist working for an organ of his party associated with Politics, not a leftist semimonthly.

HAI-5258. 26 October 1978.

Manuel MARQUE Fardinas, director general of Politica, a leftist semimonthly magazine, planned to send the subject, a member of his reporting staff, to see Allílio SA Chiliz Fiedrues, an anti-US deputy in Maxcals, with predelex.
