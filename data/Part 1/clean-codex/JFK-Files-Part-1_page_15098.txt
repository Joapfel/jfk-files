Report on Contact with Agent or Defector

Subject: HIRORSUE  
Date: 5 July 1962  
TDE: 1100  
Place: Osterdstr. 1, Apt. 9

Orrie K.uiror, Case Officer

Comments: CO and Mr. Minor went to see Subject in response to a telephone call from him. His major problem was that he was broke and needed money. CO gave him Dl 100.00 to eat on for a week, but Subject says that it is impossible to live on that amount. It is obvious that he is getting edgy and wants some action from Headquarters. He was told that if anything is to be done for him in S.A., it will require correspondence between our people in S.A. and Headquarters, that this will take time, and there is nothing he can do but wait. Subject thanked us for our help and said he is sorry to cause us so much trouble.

12, 19, 26 July — substance paid.
