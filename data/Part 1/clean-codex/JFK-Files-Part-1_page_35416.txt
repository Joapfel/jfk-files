1. For WAVE: Please see paragraph 15 of reference A. Mrs. A-9 fears that AMTRUNK-9 was caught in the Cárdenas-Matanzas area 7 January may be allayed if she is shown an article in the Miami Herald, 9 January, which stated four persons were caught while attempting to land 7 January and seven others were being sought due to an attempted landing. As A-9 departed on 21 December with only two persons, it seems unlikely he did not arrive before 7 January or that he is in the group referred to.

2. Also, Castro, cited in the Christian Science Monitor, 29 January, said Eloy Gutiérrez Menoyo (MENYOLO) landed the first week of January and was almost immediately spotted and reported to authorities by Cuban farmers. This seems likely to be the group of four caught 7 January, and the seven others sought could be part of what Castro called the 20- to 30-man group infiltrating on Gutiérrez's orders.

For WAVE.

WISH: If Mrs. A-9 knows the names of A-9's two crewmen, advise search of various SI traffic, since names of persons caught during the landing period and shortly after are cited therein.
