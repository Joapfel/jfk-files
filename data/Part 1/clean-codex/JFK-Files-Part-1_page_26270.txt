132323Z. CITE MEXICO CITY 9925. Priority. RYBAT. Reference: Mexico City 9925.

14 January 1961. 77985.

1. LEENOLD had a successful one-hour meeting with RICO GALAN at Lecumberri Penitentiary on the morning of 13 January. The meeting ended abruptly by a call from the director of the prison that a lawyer was there to see RICO.

2. LEENOLD had a letter introducing himself as an Italian writer for “Correspondenza Socialista” and as an acquaintance of CARLO COCCIOLI, Italian pro-Sommie journalist who was a friend of RICO in Mexico and who was currently in Europe. As a result, RICO assumed and accepted LEENOLD as a fellow Marxist, and a friendly rapport was established immediately. Most probable that RICO expressed his real thoughts in discussion and answered questions.

3.
