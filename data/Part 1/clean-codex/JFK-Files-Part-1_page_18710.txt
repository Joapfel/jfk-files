Report of Honor and Merit Awards Board.

The Honor and Merit Awards Board having considered a recommendation that:

Serial or ID No.: 057303. Name: GOODPASTURE, Ann L. Birth year: 1918. Sex: F. Type employee: Staff. Office of assignment: DO/CI. D: GS. Schedule: 13.

Be awarded: Career Intelligence Medal.

For meritorious service or achievement during the period September 1947–June 1973. Recommends approval.

Unclassified citation: Miss Ann L. Goodpasture is hereby awarded the Career Intelligence Medal in recognition of her exceptional achievement for more than 25 years. A charter member of the Central Intelligence Agency, Miss Goodpasture has excelled as an operations officer both overseas and in headquarters. Her performance throughout her career has been outstanding and marked by a high degree of competence and unusual dedication to duty. Miss Goodpasture has contributed substantially to the mission of the Agency, reflecting credit on her and the Federal service.

Remarks: Recommendation approved by ADD/O on 2 August 1973.

Approved: /s/ W. E. Colby, Director of Central Intelligence, 4 September 1973.

Signature: /s/ John F. Blake, Typed name of chairman, Honor and Merit Awards Board: John F. Blake.
