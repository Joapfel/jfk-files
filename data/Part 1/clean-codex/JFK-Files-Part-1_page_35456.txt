Classified message.

Date: 11 OCT 62. DTG: 101008Z. Incoming number: 1155S.

To DIR.

GYROSE AMBUD.

Ref A. DIR 40582.  
B. KNGS 0202.

Info WAVE.

Cite KNGS 0208.

1. COS believes his Ref B appraisal Jamaican political climate re establishment AMBUD delegation here correct. Jamaican govt wants not be overtly involved in Cold War and out of concern for safety large Jamaican community Cuba will make strenuous effort to stay out. However they will help covertly wherever possible. Any attempt involve Jamaican govt against its will at this time would result in net loss of cooperation now and in future, Station regards increasing cooperation of RUABBE SERCIVE in Cuban objectives of considerable value.

2. If HQS believes AMBUD rep should visit Jamaica to test political climate in govt, Station would concur most reluctantly in (PTN GELD will fwd) with Frank (DIAZ) Silveira during Caribbean Games when govt wanted to throw out for his statements to press about proposed establishment AMBUD representation here. In any event Station would insist no public announcements precede Jamaican govt ruling. Should govt reject AMBUD following advance publicity this could be major victory for Cuban Consulate, shot in arm to facing “Friends of Cuba.”
