6 October 1930

In re: How many pictures?

The New York Times book page says “The Picture Story of Franklin Delano Roosevelt” by Gene Schoor, which will be issued November 7 by Frederick Pell, will contain 125 illustrations with 20,000 words of text.

This is evidently a second FMR photo-book, and will be competing with the Simon & Schuster (Sleim & Schuster) one by Stofka Losens, “The Life of FMR in Photographs and Text.”
