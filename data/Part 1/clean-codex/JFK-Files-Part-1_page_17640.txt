Official Dispatch. Per GSH 43-41.

Air. Via: Direct Air or SeaPOW.

Dispatch No. HU-5708.

To: Chief, Station, Mexico City. Date. From: Chief, MID – Operational/LIFEAT. Subject: General; Specific; Project General and Amendment.

Reference: THA-7063.

1. Project LIFEAT was renamed on 30 October 1957 for the period 1 July 1957 through 30 June 1958 for a total of $130,154 for FY 1958. The Station will note that Headquarters remote the Project Outline and Project Review which were submitted in BTA 7063. This was done to facilitate subsequent renames of the complex LIFEAT Project. As regards the Project Outline, if no major changes occur during the next project year, the Station will only need to submit a Part II to the Project Outline, and any changes which may have affected Part I, principally those concerning personnel. The rewritten Project Review should be used by the Station as a format for next year's Review. By following these procedures, it is believed that we can avoid the rewriting and retyping of the entire project and make it simpler for the Field Case Officer to prepare the renewal request.

2. Project LIFEAT Amendment was approved on 5 December 1957 for a total of $26,493 for fiscal year 1958, thus raising the total amount authorized for the present project year to $156,647. This Amendment authorized the establishing of a bank account of $20,000 for Daniel K. EBMUND for cover purposes, against which a Letter of Credit will be obtained so that he may obtain Rentista status in Mexico. See HU-5691 for details on this Letter of Credit.

3. The Amendment also requested authority to incorporate Roland E. WINGMEE'S as an additional Staff Agent in the project. A total of $6,493 for the remainder of the project year was requested to cover WINGMEES' expenses and this amount was broken down as follows:

Salary ($7,785 per year): $3,893. Alliances (quarters up to 9,000): $1,000. Other (Educational—in accordance with R-20-610 dated 9 February 1956, estimated at). Releasing officer. Coordinating officer: $300. Authenticating officer.
