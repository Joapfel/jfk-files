Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Buenos Aires; Chief, WE; Chief, E-H. Headquarters File No. 201-6145. From: Flying Chief of Station, Madrid. Date: 25 August 1961. Subject: Counter Espionage—Luis Tedeschi, aka Luis Tdeeschi. Action required: FYI. Reference: OEN-18463, 29 May 1961.

On receipt of reference, Madrid Station traces were run on the subject with negative results. Traces were also requested of the QUANTUM service and on 21 August 1961 they informed us that they had no record of the subject in their files. In this case the QUANTUM service also carried out traces in the QUOTA files for KUBARK (KUHARK).

Anthony B. Feldrake. 26 August 1961.

Distribution: Buenos Aires; C/HE; C/AH.
