U.S. Army

Special Orders  
Number 26

1. The following named American civilian employees are directed and invited to proceed from this station on 23 May 1949 to Tokyo, Japan, and/or such other places as may be necessary, for a period of TDY of approximately thirty (30) days in connection with ESD activities. Upon completion of TDY, each individual will return to this station.

Amos O. Moscrip, Jr. (G.F-15)  
Harrison S. Miller (Miler) (G.F-12)  
John W. Sturmill (G.F-12)  
Sigfried Offenheim (G.F-11)

Authority to enter Japan granted by G-1, GHQ, CINCFS, Tokyo, Japan.

TDY by sea, commercial air, rail, water, and/or motor is directed as stated. TDY. Civilian TDY is directed as noted in the Government Service and will be performed in accordance with Standard Government TDY Books and/or applicable statutes. Air costs will be paid by and/or reimbursement secured for the Finance Officer of this order only. No other disbursing agent is directed to effect payment.

Appropriation chargeable is 2195900.001, Working Fund, Army, General 1948/9.

Authority: Radio 1548, COMNAV&SPAC, 2 January 1947.
