Info: DD/P, ADDP, DOP/SIT 3, C/SOD 3, C/CA 2, C/CA/O 2, C/CI, C/FI 2, C/FI/D, C/FI/INT, C/IW 2, C/SR 4, C/TFW 5, DD/I, S/C 2. Priority. IN 45628. OCT 25 0432Z 62.

Priority DIR cite MEXI 2292.

LIEMPTY-3.

Ref A DIR 43984, OUT 89913. B DIR 43877, OUT 89977.

23 October: Met Soviet commercial office employee Valeriy Aleksandrovich Andrianov and elicited full report regarding current Cuba situation. Andrianov said he had read President Kennedy's declaration but did not think there would be war. Elaborately denied that Cuba has Soviet missiles, said photos of same were fabricated and for provocative purposes. Said Khrushchev (Iranuschchev) would answer provocation, however not with missiles but with words, just words; nobody wants war. Andrianov concluded with Marxist line that Soviets did not need war, that the course of history has seen the downfall of feudalism, that the downfall of capitalism is inevitable, and socialism will succeed.

Records officer stamp.

End of message.
