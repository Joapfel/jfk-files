Classified message.

Date: 9 July 1959. To: Director. From: San Jose. Action: WH S. Info: COP, CCD 2, DD/S, SSA, C/MS 2, MS, OP 2, S/C 2.

Routine. IN 38892. To MEXI. Info DIR. CITE SANJ 3427. Ref: MEXI 4103 (IN 37896); SANJ 3425 (IN 38658).

Noemayr returned Sanj 8 July. Entire family confined to bed, seriously ill with flu. Epidemic spreading through CR. Will advise when subject plans return to Mexi.

End of message.

C/S comment: Requested Noemayr's ETA.

Noemayr and family were in PACY, but expected to arrive Sanj very soon.
