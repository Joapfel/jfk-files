IN, FRAN, BRIN, COPENJO GMNY; FR, PARI, STOC, MNJY, HAGO, BEIJ. AT/KUDESK/TPMURILLO/CAT 19a.

Notes: Traces Joachim Joesten, DPOB 29 Jul 07, Cologne. Entire adult career and author of approximately 30 books and numerous newspaper articles. Post-WWII works very anti-KUBARK. Last book title—Oswald: Assassin or Fall Guy? Has written under noms and pseudonyms: Franz von Nesselrode; H.F. Likin; Walter Kell; Paul Delathuis.

2. According to captured Gestapo documents, he joined the German Communist Party in May 1932 and had membership no. 532315. Owned lending library in Berlin (Brin) and sometime after May 1932 went to the USSR, where he remained until 1933. During his absence his library was managed by fiancée Anna Schinsky. Last residence in Germany: Berlin (Brin), Luetzowstrasse 40, bei Hess.
