Outgoing message.

Date: 092322. Director.

To: Priority Caracas, Santo Domingo.

References: A. Director 803889. B. Director 705055. C. Director 704047.

1. Following is per Santo Domingo EMBTEL 325, 22 Jan 76:

Warrant for arrest of Rolando Otero Hernandez (201-284811) issued mid-Jan 76 for five pipebomb explosions which took place in Miami 4 Dec 75. Miami FBI sources claimed subject flew to Dominican Republic 4 Jan 76, and on 22 Jan 76 prominent Dominican newspaper stated Otero had been arrested 17 Jan on charge of violating prohibition against his entry into Dominican Republic. In conversations with FBI and U.S. Embassy consular officer, Dominican police officials indicated disposition to expel him from country and to detain him until this was arranged. U.S. Embassy chargé informed appropriate Dominican officials for passage of information to President Balaguer of U.S. interest in Otero's return to U.S. to face charges against him. However, police chief informed consul general that he had orders.
