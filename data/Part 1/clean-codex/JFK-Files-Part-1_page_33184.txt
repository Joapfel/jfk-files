Classified message.

19 February 1969, 121924Z. CITE WH/MIAMI OSII.

To: Director, Tokyo; Hong Kong.

RYBAT AMERCAU AMCHALK.

1. Headquarters: Please advise if there is any information subsequent to July 1968 on the current whereabouts and activities of Basilio Rodriguez Rodriguez (201-766532), who was shown in the latest list of Cuban diplomatic personnel abroad as posted ambassador to Pyongyang. Station is evaluating the possibility of exploiting the old AMCHALK-5 relationship for current access.

2. Tokyo and Hong Kong: Would appreciate comments stations may have on any observable pattern in the rest and recuperation program for Cuban diplomatic personnel in Korea and other denied-area posts; i.e., do they periodically visit Japan, Hong Kong, or other free-world countries, and if so, is travel closely monitored by stations?

3. File: 201-766522. Reference 19-136-1/3.
