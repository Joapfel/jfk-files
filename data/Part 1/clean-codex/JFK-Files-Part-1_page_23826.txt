25. Military service—dates, country, unit, rank, duty, where services performed, decorations, when discharged, and why. Give details if ever prisoner of war.

Entertained U.S. Army in Trieste, Italy, December 1952. Served at Fort Dix, N.D., for basic training, later transferred to Fort Benning, Ga., then to 3rd Engineer Corps (Const), Gettysburg, Germany, as Public Information NCO. Discharged in Germany, December 1954.

26. Present and past membership in organizations (professional, social, political, etc.): FPCC—see No. 21; member of NAAGD, Urban League, and American Society of African Culture.

27. Special skills, abilities, hobbies (radio, photography, etc.): Can operate camera and tape recorder.

28. Financial status—earnings, bank deposits, securities, property.

Owe £1500 to my brother-in-law (Gordon Kaye). Various lesser debts in Paris resulting from Revolution Fiasco.

29. Employment history—type of work, salary, dates, employers, addresses, reasons for leaving. Include employment by any government.

Cub reporter—Philadelphia Afro-American (newspaper), August 1950–spring 1951; salary approximately $50 per week. Left for literary fellowship at “Yaddo,” Saratoga Springs, N.Y.

1951–1952—Rome; worked freelance for Italian Films Export, film publicity outfit, and Edmund Stevens, then Rome correspondent of Christian Science Monitor.
