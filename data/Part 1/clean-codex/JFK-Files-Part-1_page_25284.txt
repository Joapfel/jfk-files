5. On 11 September ROSCHIN attempted to make arrangements for Admiral KARLO FODRIJZ Malicsa to visit Grigori Kornyevich NIKOROVS, Naval Attaché, at the latter's home. However, FODRIJZ was leaving for Guaymas (Quaynas) early the next morning and would not be back until January. ROSCHIN expressed the hope that NIKOROVS would still be in Mexico when he returned so they could renew their “friendship.”

6. On 12 September Major Isver (KANUKURI of previous record) left word with TROFIMOV for NIKOROVS and the Military Attaché that he would be pleased to have them attend a luncheon at “Jardines del Pedregal” on Friday, 14 September, to celebrate the inauguration of his equestrian school. He also invited TROFIMOV and told him that the school is on Boulevard de la Luz at its furthest point.

7. On 13 September RENZOW informed Barrière BALTARAZ Holt, acting as C-in-C of the Navy under COECCO, that ROSCHIN had not received an invitation to the Independence Day parade. BALTARAZ later contacted ROSCHIN to say that the Defense Department had sent out invitations to four officers in the Naval Attaché's office, but they had obviously been mislaid. A duplicate would be sent to ROSCHIN, but in case it was not received in time ROSCHIN would have no problem getting into the stand, as he was expected. ROSCHIN was business-like in the conversation to the point of being curt. Later KALEDI called Major Alfred MURRAY Arguello, Army Second Section, concerning the same matter of the invitations.

8. Comandante SANDOVAL of the Naval Staff contacted ROSCHIN on 28 September and said he had just received a letter from Luis MURRAY Corina (ph), whom he described as a very small man. ROSCHIN knew to whom he was referring. MURRAY had asked SANDOVAL to convey his thanks to ROSCHIN, stating that the injections had a marvelous effect, and that his wife would require further injections in four or five months. He said he would make another request for serum at that time, and ROSCHIN promised to order five more ampoules for December. ROSCHIN also expressed his pleasure at the recovery of Comandante ZETENA and sent greetings to ZETENA and SANDOVAL. Field comments: The Soviets appear extremely polite with all members of the Naval Staff, surpassing all efforts at courtesy with other officials in the Mexican government. This may possibly be attributed to better indoctrination of the Naval Attaché's staff.

After checking, REMIZON advised that the following would attend: NIKOROVS.
