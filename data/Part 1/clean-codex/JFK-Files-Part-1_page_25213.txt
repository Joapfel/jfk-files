Washington Post

Mr. Stephen Rosenfeld (ROSENfeld)

Position (January 1965): Correspondent, Washington Post (USA) in Moscow, USSR.

Noted for the fact that he has a very large apartment by Moscow standards. Wife knows what she wants and gets it from the UPDK by constant badgering of this office. Lives in same block with source but entryway three. Wife was in Moscow before as a single girl correspondent. They both speak very good Russian. Both are Jewish.

Date acquired: September 1966.

Source heard no comments from the Soviets about Rosenfeld's (ROSENFELD's) PNG for the Washington Post publication of the Penkovskiy Papers. Third National Colonies also had no comments but were very sorry for Rosenfeld.

15 September 1966
