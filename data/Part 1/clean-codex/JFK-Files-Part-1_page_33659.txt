Actuarial. Control: 28C5.

From: Ambrose. To: Secretary of State. No. 717, February 5, 6 p.m.

In view of well-known Turkish sensitivity (DEPT'L 649, Jan. 31), E.B. doubts that N.E. proposal aid will be favorably acted upon by Turk Government.

We also believe any such approach as contemplated by N.E. might seriously jeopardize possibility of Turk acceptance of VCA relay base proposal.

Assure delay in reply caused by matter in DEPT'L requiring service.
