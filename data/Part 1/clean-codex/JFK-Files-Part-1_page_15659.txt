1. WIROGUE EOD 2 December. HESTON made initial contact and GUTHMAN now handling. WIROGUE given following instructions:

A. Build cover during initial period. In this respect, WIROGUE said planned set up shop with automatic camera to take identification pictures. Said would install Polaroid camera so client could push button and take own picture. GUTHMAN agreed this worth exploring, as would appear provide inexpensive cover.

B. WIROGUE told try spot persons for surveillance team, intel assets (with emphasis on Orientale Province) and PM assets. He told submit names of any likely candidates to station for traces prior to making approach.

2. QJWIN, who resides in same hotel as WIROGUE, reported latter told him he had lived in Alaska, Japan, South America, Germany and other parts of Europe. QJWIN said WIROGUE smelled as though he was in the intel business. Station denied any information on WIROGUE. 14 December, QJWIN.
