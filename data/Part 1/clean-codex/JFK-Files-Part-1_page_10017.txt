CITE MEXICO CITY 2428.

Director. KAPOK PERUMEN JMROD. Reference: Director 24435.

9 August 1967, 7:288E2.

1. In reply to ROWTON queries, LIRING-3 said he first heard of the arrest of Teresa Procenza sometime in 1964 from Hermes Caballero, Cuban high functionary in the Ministry of Education, during the latter’s visit to Mexico. Caballero warned LIRING-3 not to reveal he had known her or had anything to do with her since she was imprisoned as a WOFACT agent and LIRING-3 might unwittingly fall under suspicion in the investigation then going on. LIRING-3 recalled he subsequently discussed the Procenza case in the course of a conversation in Cuba with Arnol (Rodriguez) Camps, with the Cuban Sugar Ministries, Rene Ochoa Funez (Honduran revolutionary), and Max Figueroa Araújo (Cuban Vice Minister of Education), but could not remember exactly what was said.

2. LIRING-3 commented that the family of Teresa Procenza was most probably under suspicion but noted that her sister, Caridad, wife of Jorge (Rojas) Mendoza of Cubana de Aviacion, Mexico City, seemed to be circulating freely. He added that Procenza was secretary to Diego Rivera and was involved in the Marquita Island case.
