NY-12587. 12 May 1967. Once 47,500 (Beopanel).

Subject: Additional Information Concerning Plans of Haitian Bureaucrat, Charles Joseph Charles, to Overthrow the Duvalier Government

1. Louis A. Ebrin, B and B International Corporation, 1110 Broadway, New York, NY, purchased the following additional information concerning new developments in Haiti and plans of Haitian bureaucrat, Charles Joseph Charles, and his military and civilian associates to overthrow the Duvalier government.

2. Colonel Guillard, Chief of the Haitian Coast Guard, is definitely one of the key military figures on whom Charles plans his hope. Guillard is thoroughly disinterested with Duvalier and is willing to cooperate with Charles. Guillard feels that if Duvalier remains in power, the economy of the country will be completely ruined and that Haitian soldiers now being trained in guerrilla warfare in some way attempt to rise to power. As a preparation for later, whose wealth is estimated to be approximately $300 million, Guillard has a large stake in the future of Haiti. He feels that “patriots such as Charles and himself” must eliminate Duvalier and his henchmen.

3. Although Charles did not name Colonel Pierre Louis, officer in charge of the Guerilla Desalination, Port-au-Prince, as a fellow-conspirator, there is reason to believe that Charles knows and approves the plans to overthrow Duvalier. Charles and Louis are good friends; the leader was best man at the wedding of the Colonel’s daughter. When the government was able to pay Louis and his soldiers on time, on several occasions, Charles advanced money so that Louis and some of his trusted associates “outlived.”

4. Engineer Louis Lépine, a member of the Democratic Planning Board of Haiti, is also aware of Charles’ plans. Lépine is a respected public figure who has made several trips to the US to an effort to obtain capital for the Haitian economy. He has given up all hope and is convinced that the Duvalier government must be eliminated.
