Chief of Mission’s Evaluation of Performance.

Officer being rated: Chief of Station. Post: Brussels.

Rating period: May 13, 1969–December 29, 1969. Date of report: December 20, 1969.

Signature of reporting officer: John S.D. EISENHOMER. Title: Ambassador.

Evaluation of performance: Outstanding.

Does this officer properly understand and perform his role and his functions as a member of your staff under existing Presidential directives? Yes.

Has he seen this report?

Narrative comments: This officer is undoubtedly one of extremely high professional competence. In addition I have complete confidence in his discretion.

This officer is presentable, tactful, and enthusiastic. He constitutes an outstanding asset to this mission.
