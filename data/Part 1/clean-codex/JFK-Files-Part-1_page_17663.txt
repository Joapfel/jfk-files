The station continues to face recruitment problems, however, since the LIFAT development qualifications are quite rigid. The basic qualifications are:

a. Absolute discretion.

b. Willingness to cover a base 24 hours daily for long periods of time.

c. Willingness to obey complex orders for long periods of time.

d. Willingness to live in a situation of constant danger.

Additional comments concerning the production, security and cover of individual agents will be found in the attached field project outline.
