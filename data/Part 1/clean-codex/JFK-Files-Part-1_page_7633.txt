Ramon Patuel.

Born 28 July 1926, Madrid, Spain.

Date of naturalization: 11 April 1956, Supreme Court of N.Y. at Rochester, N.Y.

Naturalization Certificate No. 7457155.

Emergency addressee and address: Mrs. Frances L. Patuel, wife, same address in Mex. Local address: Sierra Grande 907, Lomas, Mexico 10, D.F. Address in U.S.: 23 River Meadow Drive, Rochester 23, N.Y. Occupation: Manager.

Father: Enrique Patuel, born in Valencia, Spain; nationality: Spanish.

Mother: Eulalia Sanchez de Molina Patuel, Madrid, Spain; nationality: Spanish.

Spouse: Frances L. Patuel, Madrid, Spain, 4 December 1922 (U.S. father), U.S. citizen.

Date and place of marriage: 18 March 1950.

Wife native born.

Date of last arrival in country: 20 February 1961.

Passport No. 524029, issued 15 May 1957, Washington.

Child: Raymond J., born 13 February 1951, Rochester, N.Y.

U.S. residence since naturalization. Residence outside U.S. since naturalization.

Residence history: 1956–1957, Spain; 1957–1959, Spain; 1959–1961, Mexico; 20 February 1961–present, Mexico.

Date card initially typed: 1 March 1962.
