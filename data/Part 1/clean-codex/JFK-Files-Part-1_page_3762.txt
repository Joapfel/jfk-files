TYPIC YOBITE

SITREP 18–12 June 1967

I. Operational Developments.

A. PV.

JMWAVE 7674 reports station plans to exploit Venezuelan boycott of Spanish ship on 12 June.

B. Maritime.

PETREL departed El Bluff, Nicaragua, 121045Z, after loading 18 tons cargo and proceeding on schedule, returning to base.

C. SP.

1. IANS processed 175 airlifted refugees plus third-country arrivals from Mexico on 9 June. CAC screened 36 males. Of JMWAVE interest: employee, Planning Department, Electric Enterprise, Camagüey City, with general information on enterprise and on union activities. Group included 69 females, 53 children, and 15 males over 60 years.

2. IANS and CAC also processed and screened group.
