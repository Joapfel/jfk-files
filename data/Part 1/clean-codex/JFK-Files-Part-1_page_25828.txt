Official Dispatch.

Dispatch No. F. No. 4930, 201-119523.

To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. From: Chief, HD. Subject: General; Operational/LIP TICK; Raymond N. CERARDEK.

Reference: No. 14-4554, 21 May 1957.

1. Subject departed from Washington, D.C. on 13 July 1957 to visit his relatives in South Carolina prior to returning to Mexico. He completed five weeks training on 12 July—an evaluation of the subject by his instructors will be forwarded in the near future.

2. Subject was advanced two weeks per day at the rate of +12 by Mexico Station. Since his lodgings were provided by Handbookers it was decided to pay him per day at the rate of $1. This station's payment was extended to cover four weeks and subject was advanced +2 on 3 July to cover the fifth week, 7 July through 13 July.

3. The net amount of Subject's telephone bill was not known at time of Subject's departure. One month will probably be returned to Subject whereupon he should be reimbursed for the net amount of the telephone bill.

25 July 1957.

Distribution: 3—Mexico City; 1—WH/Mailroom; 1—WH/III/Chrono; Subject; 1—RI.

Releasing officer: Charles E. Apple. J. C. KING, Chief, HD. WH/B&F.

Coordinating officer: WH/III/Mexico.

Automatic booking officer: R. DAHLBERG, C/WH/III.
