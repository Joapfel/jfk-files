29 May 1958

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: AMPHODARCE/2  
C-25507

1. On 23 April 1958, Mr. Lou Hamby of the Office of Security called in connection with the above-designated case, specifically regarding the contingencies upon which the CSA dated 3 July 1957 was granted.

2. Mr. Hamby advised that the investigation of the subject has been completed and he has been given an LCFLUTTER, both with favorable results.

3. In view of the foregoing, restrictions (contingencies) in the 3 July 1957 approval are now removed. WH Division has been notified.

4. Jim Burton, Project Branch, CI/OA, was given the above information also. He advised FI/Projects in order that further action could be taken in connection with the project renewal.

John B. Brewer  
CI/OA
