5. 1942, Berlin. Drivers’ mechanics course for the NSKK (Nazi Party Motorized Corps). Drove trucks during construction of the Atlantic Wall by Germans.

6. 1943, Berlin. Employed with Schutzpolizei (SCHUPOŁIZEI) as a French-German language translator.

7. 1946–47, Paris. Employed by Architects Carre Associated as electrical installation planner and constructor of prototype model builder. Earned roughly $4 an hour on the American scale, plus commission work extra.

8. 1951–52, Paris. In prison; marketed a pocket automatic transit indicator. Received cash settlement for this while in prison.

9. 1953, Rion. In prison. Devised and gave to the prison administrator an invention for making cardboard boxes. Device was built and used by the prison administrator.

10. December 1955–April 1956, Gap. Employed by M.D. as an X-ray machine maintenance technician and operator.

11. 1956–57, Gap. Invented, patented, and sold a calendar device to the French commercial market.

12. 1958, Ambert. Invented and patented a commercial advertising device, photo-mechanical in nature. Models built and sold to French advertising concerns.

13. 1959–60, U.S. Devised and passed drawings for two pieces of equipment to be used for Project ASENTINEL.

14. July 1960, U.S. At present, subject is devising a low-cost telephone answering device.
