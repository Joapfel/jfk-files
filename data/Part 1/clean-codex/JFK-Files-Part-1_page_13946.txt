10 April

1. Jose Paul Viera Linares proved WUSLAS/1 morning 8 April. Said most likely would not be returning to New York. Understood nature WUSLAS/1's health would prevent his travelling. His friend “Rogelio” would contact WUSLAS/1. Gave no details future contact.

2. Viera did not answer WUSLAS/1's question as to whether we would be assigned another post outside Cuba.

3. Local LIMERGO believes “Rogelio” may be Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez, First Secretary, Cuban Permanent Mission.

4. File: A 201-328053 and 200-6-337/3. SP-1.
