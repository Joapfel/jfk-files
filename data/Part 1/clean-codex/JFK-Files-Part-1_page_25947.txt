Outgoing Message.

Message date: 12/3/94. Message reference number: 605724.

To: Priority Secret Service//Intelligence Division.

Subject: Rolando Otero Hernandez.

CIR-3126/20.024-7b.

1. This agency queried the appropriate representatives in Caracas and Santo Domingo regarding the whereabouts of Rolando Otero Hernandez, as requested by your service in a telephone conversation on 6 February 1976. Our representative in Caracas responded that according to a usually reliable source Otero was required expelled from Venezuela to Santiago, Chile, on 3 February 1976. It is possible that the Federal Bureau of Investigation has additional information on subject.
