3. Folgar left Cuba for Miami 21 September 63 but was denied entry to the U.S. and, after a week’s detention by INS, proceeded to Venezuela. During Folgar’s subsequent efforts to gain admission to the U.S., President Betancourt personally vouched for him, stating he was a longtime personal friend and physician who had once saved Betancourt’s life. Among those who wrote letters in support of Folgar was Dr. Manuel Antonio de Varona of the Cuban Revolutionary Council in Miami. V HAC-3037, 15 October 64, reported that Folgar had handled most of the appointments for delegates of Cuban Representation in Exile (RECE) who visited Venezuela during a tour of Latin America. Officials whom RECE delegates met included Foreign Minister Ignacio Iribarren Borges (I R R I B AR N BORGES), Interior Minister Gonzalo Barrios (B AR N I O S), Presidential Secretary Manuel Mantilla (M A N T I L L A), and congressional leader Carlos.
