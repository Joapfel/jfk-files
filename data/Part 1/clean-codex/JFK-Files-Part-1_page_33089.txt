Orig: John Mertz:ak. Unit: WH/COG/OPS. Ext: 5061 R. 1255. Date: 24 November 1969.

Message Form. Cable Secretariat Dissemination. File in CB file no. Conf. File: VR.

To: Ottawa. Info: WH/Miami.

RYBAT TYPIC SMABOVE AMCHALK.

Refs: A. Ottawa 6713 (IN 86341). B. Ottawa 6727 (IN 90746).

1. Ordinarily Ref A type of proposal would be appealing, however, for following reasons believe AMCHALK-5 operation not the proper vehicle.

A. Possibility of DGI revenge against AMCHALK-5, his family, and friends both in Cuba and U.S.

B. Possibility that operation would jeopardize any future contact between RVROCK and official Cubans abroad, as Cubans naturally would regard all future contact as possible provocations.

C. Cubans would probably make public protest to Canadian government saying all was RVROCK plot to damage Cuban-Canadian relations. Could further mention this second case, first being LISAGA case in which Cubans may say we also tried to damage Cuban-Mexican relations. Cuba might reinforce its case by mentioning AMCHALK-5's true name and what Cuban government knows of his activities on behalf of RVROCK.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer. Copy no.
