Cable. Draft No. 2. 1 May 1969. Priority: Mexico City.

RYBAT ADMIN CONTRACTS LITEMPO

Refs: A. Mexico City 0023 (IN 47116). B. HMMA-36678.

1. Unless Station memo to Jeremy K. Benadum concerning retirement was in context that, due to reduction of LITEMPO project and thus abolishment of his position, and as no other Station requirement for his services, he might prefer “discontinued service retirement” rather than transfer, Station had not been authorized to inform Benadum he would be retired effective 30 June. Benadum does not qualify for retirement under WOFIRM Retirement and Disability System, as he does not have the required ten years’ service with the organization, and retirement would be under Civil Service. Benadum, as cited paragraph 2, reference A, is correct in that retirement would be involuntary. Request forward copy of Station memo of 7 April to Benadum with Benadum’s memo of 24 April.

2. In view of references, unable to understand why or where Benadum could be used as Station suggests in paragraph 5, reference A, as contract agent, which appears contradictory to no requirement for services as career agent.

3. As Station has no apparent job commensurate with Benadum’s grade level and experience, and in view of his long stay in Mexico, therefore considering Benadum for reassignment.

A. Request Station contact Benadum and have him complete FRQ soonest for transmittal Headquarters. Reassignment preferences to be cabled.
