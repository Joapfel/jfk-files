16 June 1969

Memorandum for: Chief, Audit Staff

Subject: Jeremy K. BENADUM, Cover Income

References: A. Audit Staff Memo, 17 March 1969, subject: Clarification of cover income. B. WHD Memo, 12 May 1969, same subject.

1. Reference A indicated that since October 1961, Career Agent Jeremy K. BENADUM had not submitted cover income reports.

Reference B indicated that the Mexico Station had been requested to forward information in relation to Reference A.

2. Attached is a copy of the certification by Jeremy K. BENADUM in which he states he has received no compensation, allowances, or benefits in kind from cover activities for the period 1961–1969.

3. In view of the above certification, it is not necessary to amend BENADUM's contract to provide for retention of earned cover income in accordance with IN 20-32.

William V. Broe  
Chief  
Western Hemisphere Division

DDP/WH/Contracts: WASHenan:ad (9 June 1969)
