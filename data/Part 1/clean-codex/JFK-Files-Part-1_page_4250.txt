Message Form.

Original: R. Hernandez. Unit: NH/3/Venezuela. Date: 22 November 65. No index.

To: Caracas. Info: JMWAVE. From: Director. CONF: 1048. Info: VR. File. RID copy SIG-EN.

To Cara. References: A. DIR-59581. B. H'CW-6746.

Info Wave. Cite DIR. 60049.

1. AMBIDDY-1 contacted in Miami by Venezuelan Major (FNU) Gutierrez, claiming he is local representative for a group of plotters currently training for a coup against Leoni regime. A-1 was unable to obtain names other than Col. Martin Parada. According to A-1, the leader of the plot is located in Caracas convent Nuestra Senora de Fatima or Nuestra Senora del Rosario.

2. A-1 said Gutierrez commutes between Caracas and Miami. Telephone in Miami: 887-0795. No Gutierrez is listed in the Miami directory with this number.

3. Gutierrez gave A-1 the following instructions for contacting the leader: A-1 should proceed to Caracas and call Dr. (FNU) Morelos, whom A-1 suspects may be a priest. Morelos will then take A-1 to the leader.

4. A-1 reported conspirators have enlisted support of a prominent COPEI leader; A-1 was unable to supply the name. Purportedly, garrisons in Maracay, Oriente, and Bolivar have already given tacit consent to join.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer. Copy No. Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
