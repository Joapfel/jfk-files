To the director of the Jewish Legion, of 21 Hyde Park Street, London, W. 2. The Jewish Legion was reported to be a subversive organization. (WEL-3908, 26 Feb 48.)

4. S. Hochman departed Paris (Pari) via Air France at midnight 1 Dec. for Idlewild, arriving the morning of 2 Dec. Concerned about procedures at Idlewild regarding passports. Attempting arrangements to be met by sufficient people to forestall a passport incident. Fears contempt of Congress charge, says he will not plead the Fifth Amendment but will refuse to give information on other China travelers.

(Paris 0169, IN 40350, 28 Nov. ’57)

Samuel Edward Hockman (Hochman)
