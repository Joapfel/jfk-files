DIR CITE MEXI 2126

TYPIC LICRAFT

REF DIR 75551

1. Following from LICRAFT-1: Joaquin (Joacuin) Mirabal is not brother of Alfredo (Alfado) Mirabal Diaz (Daz). Former consul MEXI, has only one sister. Sister’s official title is Delegado del Ministro del Interior en la Provincia de La Habana (La Higana).

Has authority to represent Ramiro Valdes, enter any security office in Habana Province, and issue orders under Valdes’s authority. Wears army captain’s uniform and insignia. In 1951 was second in command of G-2. Has [residence] at Quinta Avenida and Calle 14. The man in command then is now police chief, Habana.

2. Mirabal [is a] close friend [of] Valdes since long before Castro takeover. Is from Habana. Mother reported to be old-time Communist. Family home was on Calle Goss, Vibora, Habana. Is about 35 years old. LICRAFT first met [him] in 1957, at which time Mirabal was customs agent. In Habana working for UNGLE, also named Mirabal. First had Sears Roebuck account, may have had Woolworth account. Mirabal admitted to LICRAFT he has been making lots of money smuggling liquor. Prior to 1957 had had a fling as popular singer; had a very good voice. Was very friendly with Gustavo.

8 June 1965, IN 565-16
