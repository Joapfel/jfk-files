Classified message.

Date: 27 February 1963. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: C/WH 5. Info: ADDP, C/CI, C/CI/IC 2, C/FI 2, S/C 2.

27 February 1963, 0411Z. Routine.

DIR CITE MEXI 3575  
RUBAT  
Ref A: DIR 35657  
B: MEXI 1919 Co 20593  
IN75331

1. LITENSOR has asked via LIELEGANT for any briefing papers KUBARK can give on France, Yugoslavia, Poland, West Germany, Holland, and England.

2. CURTIS would appreciate anything Headquarters can make available and believes HQS may wish to attempt to influence LITENSOR on certain points in some of these countries.

3. Will need reply soon and, if HQS wishes to prepare papers, would appreciate receipt by 20 March.

J.C.K.
