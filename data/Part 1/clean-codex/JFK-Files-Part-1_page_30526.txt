Headquarters  
Field Research Unit, Far East Command  
Yokohama, Japan

16 August 1951

AG 201-PERS  
LO 228-2

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Individuals Concerned

1. Following named individuals will proceed on/about 18 August 1951 to Pusan and Seoul, Korea, and/or such other places as are necessary, for a portion of temporary duty of approximately twenty (20) days in connection with activities of this headquarters. Upon completion of temporary duty, return to proper station.

Captain John M. Cloeson, 01617205, Signal Corps  
Mr. Donald S. Miller (GS-13)

2. Travel by military aircraft, rail, and government motor transportation authorized. TDN. An allowance of sixty-five (65) pounds personal baggage is authorized while traveling by air. No per diem authorized.

3. Individuals are designated official couriers.

4. Officers may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after completion of temporary duty.

5. Civilian will wear the Army officer's uniform less insignia in the combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from QM Sales Store upon presentation of these orders.

6. Immunizations and physical inspection as prescribed by Circular 9, 27 February 1950, will be completed prior to departure.

Jim F. Hook,  
Executive Officer

Distribution: C
