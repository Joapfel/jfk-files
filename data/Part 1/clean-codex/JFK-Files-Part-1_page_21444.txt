24 April 1961

To: Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Attention: Mr. S. J. Papich

From: Deputy Director (Plans)

Subject: Semen POHORILIW, 201-289249  
Internal Security - R

1. Reference is made to the following correspondence:

a. Bureau report of 14 February 1961, made at Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and subject as above;

b. Memorandum from Deputy Director (Plans), subject as above, dated 9 January 1961, and numbered CSCI-3/763,211;

c. CIA teletype, subject as above, dated 16 November 1960;

d. CIA teletype, subject as above, dated 17 February 1961.

2. He would appreciate being informed whether or not you have had an opportunity to compare the handwriting of Semen POHORILIW and that of the letter bearing his name and address, which we sent to you with reference 1.b.

3. In a review of our files, we note that the lack of elaboration in our earlier correspondence might cause your Bureau some difficulty. Our statement in reference 1.c. that the POHORILIW letter bore indications of secret writing was based on the findings of our technical staff. One of our technicians applied a test that permitted him to see writing which, although it was not legible, could be seen to be other than that of the overt text. This writing remained visible long enough for another technician.

201-289249
