1. Consulate will be reporting via State channels on the visit to the consulate on 27 February of Richard Case Nagell, also known as Joe Cramer, Joe Crane, and Robert C. Nolan, 201-746537. He was not interviewing the consul unless promises made to him by U.S. officials were honored. He would reveal to the press the entire story of his alleged contacts with WOFIRM and would expose individuals with whom he had dealt in the organization.

2. Unless advised to the contrary, Base will, as it has in the past, leave this matter strictly to the consulate. Bern, please advise LNER GO.

3. The most recent State message was U.S. Mission Berlin 2567 of 26 October 1968.
