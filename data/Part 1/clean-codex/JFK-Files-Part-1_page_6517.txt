OSA/Alien Branch (MIEVIER), from Burdick Gentel.

Subject: CAS: SS372—Carlos Manuel Molina, Alvaral.

Reference: ALI-284-76, 22 September 1976.

1. A cooperative source at the International Refugee Committee informs us that Carlos Manuel Molina, Alvaral chose not to return in New York and travel to Miami after arriving in the United States. Our source is not sure of his address, but believes Molina is probably living in the Cabana Hotel, Ponce de Leon (Porce de Leon) Street, Coral Gables, which is next to the Cuban Refugee Center. If he is not at this address, his location should be available from refugee assistance agencies in Miami.

2. Attempts to obtain Molina's location through Roberto Alvarez were negative since there is no city called Clifford Park, New Jersey. Information has no listing for a Roberto Alvarez in either Cliffside Park or Clifford Park.

3. Sorry Molina got away so quickly. We were hoping to give Pearl Austin a chance to use her Spanish. Should we close this case and transfer the correspondence to the Miami FO (F0)?

File: Case SS372.
