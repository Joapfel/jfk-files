Cable Secretary’s Denomination: No more than 3 cables, if possible. By Lt. Z, Nr. 3. Full Film File No. Corr.: Client 6. File V.S. Client Logins 5, clear. Client S. Raised. BD copy (pico).

Cite Director 36584.

To: Mexico City, Guatemala City.

By BAT TIPIC liaison.

Ref: Mexico City 7335 (IN 99476).

1. ROWTON return still uncertain pending resolution development access to AMCAGE/1.

2. Re LIRING/4, ROWTON could send money ostensibly from L/4’s father in New York to bail her out of present desperate situation, but this would be only temporary solution in addition to inherent security risks (father is reportedly hospitalized and not in position to provide financial help at this time). L/4’s refuge in LIRING establishment, on the other hand, which ROWTON could not convince her to take as long as he was giving her money, will serve to improve her access and may lead to repatriation. ROWTON feels latter eventuality likely only in event of his continued failure to recontact her since L/4 would possibly have no other alternative. In that case, rebriefing and resupply S/W precluded prior departure. ROWTON could debrief L/4 upon her return to Mexico if and when LIRING/3 is released from prison and contact reestablished.
