Disp. No. UFGT-22352. Date: 29 July 1968. To: Chief, WDE Division. From: Acting Chief of Station, JMWAVE. Subject: Arrival in U.S. of brigade dependent from Cuba and settlement of trustee account. Action required: Request cable concurrence of JMWAVE's plan in paragraph 3.

I. Mrs. Clara Molina Cabrera arrived in the United States from Cuba (Cuban) and contacted AMTIKI-1 by letter dated 2 June 1968. Mrs. Molina is the mother of Mariaho Santos Molina, killed during the Bay of Pigs invasion. The history of this case is as follows.

A. Mariaho designated his mother and father, both in Cuba, as his dependents. Mariaho also designated Carlos Fernandez Campos as his trustee to handle all monies for his dependents.

B. AMTIKI-1's records show that Carlos Fernandez Campos was paid at the rate of $225.00 per month from April 1961 to April 1962. According to the record, all payments to Carlos Fernandez Campos ceased at that time because “Carlos Fernandez did not account in a satisfactory manner for the money received.” AMTIKI-1 opened a savings account in the Coconut Grove Bank in May 1962 in the name of Mariaho Santos and/or Juan Santos (the father), with himself as trustee. Deposits of $225.00 per month were made to this account from May 1962 until September 1966, when all deposits to trustee accounts were ordered stopped by JMWAVE. Total payment to this account was $11,925.00, and with the earned interest of $1,920.57, the account balance as of 23 July 1968 was $13,905.57.
