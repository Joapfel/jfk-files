LIRING-3, afraid Orozco might commit suicide in his apartment, tried to enter his place a little later but found Orozco unwilling to open up. LIRING-3 and Valdez then spent the rest of the night in front of LIRING-3’s apartment to make sure Orozco would not escape. At Rowton’s suggestion, LIRING-3 hired a taxi, which waited nearby. 3. After reviewing the above case, the station decided to call ITEMPO’s and have them place Orozco under arrest. The station felt ITEMPO’s could have recognized Orozco when staking out his mother’s place. Orozco has kept away from his mother’s address in hiding for security.
