Guatemala, 28 April 1969

Dear LIRING-1:

I am very happy to be able to write to you. Excuse me for not having been able to do so until now, but it was only last week that I was able to leave the sanatorium. My departure from such a place was quite a relief. My health was declining and I almost had a relapse. Everyone at home celebrated. My brother sends you his best.

My letter has two purposes: first, to excuse myself for not being able to get to your home. It is because, since I am still not recovered, the doctor has told me to recuperate in the country, and, to avoid problems these days, I am planning to go to Uncle Tio's farm. In any case, they'll be coming to see you on behalf of Walenta, since those are still talking about the business here and are interested. That should take place any day now.

My other purpose in writing to you is to bother you for a favor. I should tell you that I have married Liz and she is now expecting a baby. Finally, I was able to follow your advice. Now that I am leaving for the farm, she will be left alone, with some financial difficulties. Since you had invited both of us there, and this is not possible, I hope you will be able to send her something. I think she could make about Q. 100 very good use of. If you could send it to Doña Luisa, she and I would be very much obliged.

Everything else is going very well. I am

/s/ Julio Antonio
