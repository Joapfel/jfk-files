DIR CITE WAVE 6148  
RYBAT TYPE YOSITE AMTRUNK

RE: A. WAVE 6313 (IN 4/6/61)  
B. WAVE 6314 (IN 4/6/57)

1. OP AMTRUNK X postponed to late January since AMTRUNK/9 had not returned from reference trip. AMTRUNK/9, along with AMTRUNK/5 (former guide who terminated mid-63) and a third person, left WAVE area 21 December 64 for AMTRUNK infil area, thence Havana, to rescue JOSTE (VASQUEZ) HEREY and three others who were in hiding at home identified as A. ANICE/14 received information from both wife A/9 and IDEN B that funds for operation were provided by AMFAUNA/3, ostensibly from KUBARK. AMFAUNA/3 is ACSI source No. 180, under whose net VASQUEZ worked until A/3 exfiltrated by AMTRUNK's September 63.

2. According to wife A/9, the trip was planned for one week’s duration, but no word has yet been received from AXO to explain the delay. A/9’s wife states A/9 planned to contact AMTRUNK/15 and 18 to arrange transportation to Havana. A/9 intended to notify his wife by S/M code commercial calendar upon safe arrival in Havana and also prior to exfiltration. On 30 December, wife A/9 stated AMFAUNA/3 telephoned IDEN A in Havana. Reportedly, VASQUEZ himself answered the phone and stated A/9 had not yet arrived, as of 8 January.
