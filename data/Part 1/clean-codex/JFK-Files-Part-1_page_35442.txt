TYPIC MHSPAWN MEAPRON.

1. Reuters dispatch datelined Havana 16 March reported widespread purge underway of military officers, high officials of Cuban government, and leaders of the Communist Party. Fidel Castro also revealed that several Cuban labor union leaders are under arrest on embezzlement charges. In addition, Reuters sources stated that several highly placed officials would soon fall in the cleanup and that “full investigation of officials’ moral and political practices already underway in government ministries.” The Ministries of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Trade will be most affected by the purge. On 15 March, the Foreign Trade Ministry in a special meeting “approved the purge” and adopted a complete check on members working abroad. New restrictions already imposed forbid the bringing into Cuba rationed or hard-to-get durable goods.
