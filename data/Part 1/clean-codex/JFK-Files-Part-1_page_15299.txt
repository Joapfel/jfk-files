I. Debriefing of TZITZICHVILI, David.

Time of debriefing: 28 and 29 April 1959.  
Place: Safehouse.  
Language used: German, partly French.

A. General Data:

1. The subject himself: David TZITZICHVILI, nickname: Dathouna, shortened: Dat.  
Born: 12 July 1918, in GORI, Georgia.  
Nationality: Stateless.  
Family status: Divorced.  
Languages spoken, in sequence of fluency: French, German, Georgian, and some Russian.  
Profession: ?  
Visible defects: The tips of the thumb and forefinger of the left hand are missing, less than one joint.

2. Divorced wife: Renée, née PARROD, born on 30 August 1917.  
Married in Paris, after World War II.  
Children: daughter Elisabeth, born in March 1949; son Gabriel, born in November 1950, after the divorce.  
Divorced in 1950, after David was jailed, on his wife’s request.

3. Father: Simon TZITZICHVILI, Georgian.  
Date of birth: possibly 2 February, year unknown.  
Profession: mining engineer, special petrol refinery.  
Studied in Russia, and one year in Freiburg, Germany.

Historical background: Tzitzichvili was the second-largest family in Georgia. One area in Georgia is called after this family. Simon has the title of “Count.” He is one of the two pretenders to the throne of Georgia, the name of the second pretender being BAGRACION.

In 1921 Simon, together with his wife and three children, fled from Georgia via Turkey to France. The family remained for two weeks in Marseilles, and then moved to Paris. They were wealthy then. Simon took a hosiery shop. Later things went worse, and Simon was for a while a taxi driver. Still later he tried with some inventions, but had no material success.
