Dispatch. To: Chief, WH Division. Info. HMMA-15252. Date: 50-6-32/1. From: Chief of Station, Mexico City. 29 June 1960. Subject: LIFEAT (LIFETAT) Project Renewal Request. Action required.

References: HMMW-7955, 14 August 1959.

1. There is attached a comprehensive review of Project LIFEAT for the period July 1959 through June 1960 and a project outline divided into Part I and Part II for easier processing.

2. It is to be noted that the amount requested in the attached renewal request, $114,411, is $19,419 under that amount approved for Project LIFEAT for the past project year. Station Mexico feels this reduction in cost will not adversely affect the operational impact of the project.

3. It is requested that Project LIFEAT be renewed for FY 1961 in the amount of $114,411.

Willard C. Curtis

23 June 1960

Attachments:
1. Odd pages (five copies)—attached herewith.
2. Even pages (five copies)—under separate cover.

Distribution:
WHD (with five copies of odd and even pages).
3. Files (with complete project renewal request).
