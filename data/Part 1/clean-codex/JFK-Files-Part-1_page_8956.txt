C/S comment: *IDEN (unidentified) coming U.S. on unofficial visit beginning 10 October. Headquarters considering Tichborn as escort officer 16–28 October. Queried any reason he not available or unable undertake job.

Ref DIR 41118 (OUT 85000)*

1. Headquarters hopefully awaiting favorable word Tichborn able take reference assignment as it consensus here he man for job, on assumption all favorable please instruct Tichborn:

A. Arrive New York City earliest date, if possible by 12 October.

B. Check into hotel of choice.

C. Phone Metropolitan 8-0469, Washington, D.C., identifying self true name, reporting name of hotel and room he staying and ask that this information be relayed to Mr. Nelson. (This is 24-hour answering service and upon receipt of information headquarters case officer will phone Tichborn to arrange earliest meeting in New York for cover and assignment Lieutling.)

2. Advise.

End of message.
