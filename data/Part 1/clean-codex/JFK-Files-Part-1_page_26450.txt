Report No. TDCS-3/563,672 (TOCS3/563,672).

Chief of Government, and with Marco Antonio DN 30SA, Guatemalan guerrilla leader.

1. Victor Rico Galan, Mexican newspaperman who “recently” spent eight days in Cuba on “vacation” with Fidel Castro, was given an assignment during this period to visit countries in Central and South America, where he was to persuade leftist parties to begin immediately acts of savage and violence to take the pressure off Venezuela. Rico Galan was also instructed to try to persuade competing leftist factions to shift so that their mark will have greater impact. (Headquarters comment: Rico Galan clearly implied to the source of the information that the assignment he is now fulfilling was dated May 19 in June 63.)

2. Rico Galan said that he had come from Mexico to Guatemala, where he put with Gomez Atay (PHU), a guerrilla leader whom he encouraged to commit savage and violent action. He also made a similar approach to a higher than normal guerrilla leader. (Headquarters comment: It is unclear whether Rico Galan actually visited Nicaragua or whether he saw this plan in other situations.) In Costa Rica he talked with one of the Mora brothers. (Headquarters comment: Eduardo Mora is presently in Costa Rica. His brother, Manuel Mora, left Costa Rica for the USSR, via Mexico, 29 September 1963. Both are leaders of the Costa Rican Communist Party.) Rico Galan arrived in Panama 22 October from Costa Rica and left Panama for Caracas 22 October 1963. In Caracas he intended to contact Artuella Laya. (Headquarters comment: Probably Artuella Laya as director, 2 October.)
