18 May 1965. Cite London 1613 (Lauterbach acting). Director: RUBAT KEYWAY. Authorization: JKLANCE.

1. SMOOTH (SMUPROOT) expects to be asked by HMG to comment on accuracy of London Telegraph article 16 May to the effect that Mr. Herbert Itkin, American labor lawyer, was on JKLANCE secret mission in Britain in 1965. Article says JKLANCE has formally acknowledged in paper submitted to New York Supreme Court that Itkin worked as an agent in Britain and that this is only second time JKLANCE has publicly acknowledged the assignment of an undercover agent not officially on JKLANCE payroll (POWERS was first).

2. According to local LNERGO, it actually handled Itkin in London as informant on criminal matters in 1965. JAGUAR aware of this. However, local LNERGO as confused as we are as to Itkin's JKLANCE connection at that time.

3. Request HQS advise what we may tell SMOOTH.

4. No station record Itkin.

5. File defer.
