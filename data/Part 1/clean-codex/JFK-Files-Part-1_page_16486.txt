FILS CABLES HRS; INFO J. GARS.

27 March 1976

1. At the 27 March meeting, PACHANKIS discussed latest CARACAS Station requirements with SARK-1 (S-1).

2. S-1 has not seen SARK-1 (P-1) since circa 1964, thus is unable to provide an updated assessment. S-1 does not know F-1’s wife.

3. S-1 considers it highly improbable that F-1 would accept a private meeting via phone. F-1 could be suspicious of S-1’s motives and might round up his own welcoming committee for confrontation.

4. S-1 is confident a direct street meeting—one on one—would offer the best opportunity to communicate the Agency offer to F-1.

5. S-1’s second choice would be a home approach, were it known that F-1 was alone or only with his wife.

6. S-1 is disciplined, discreet, devoted, and very professional. He can adapt to any Station need and will go anywhere, anytime to cooperate. He is prepared to take up to five days’ sick leave without pay to participate in the approach to F-1. He could leave Miami on Saturday, returning no later than the Sunday of the following week. S-1 is an American citizen and has a U.S. passport. S-1 needs circa one week’s prior notice to prepare for the CARACAS operation.

7. S-1 reviewed the names of Cubans on the latest Diplomatic List. The only positive identification was Norberto HERNANDEZ Curbelo, the Ambassador, who was a former OGI colleague of S-1.

Henry S. Pachankis.
