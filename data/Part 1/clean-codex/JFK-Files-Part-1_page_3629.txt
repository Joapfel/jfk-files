20 November 1973

Memorandum

Subject: Eduardo SUAREZ-Rivas y Morales

Eduardo SUAREZ-Rivas Morales, born 8 January 1935, is a Cuban national and a son of former Cuban ex-Senator Eduardo SUAREZ Rivas. SUAREZ was arrested for counterrevolutionary activities in Cuba in the early 1960s and was released from prison and allowed to come to exile in the U.S. in 1962 with return of the ransomed 2506 Brigade members. He was involved in public relations for Manuel Artime's exile group, the Revolutionary Recovery Movement, in the mid-sixties. He did not work for CIA, and there is no record of any attempt by CIA to recruit him.
