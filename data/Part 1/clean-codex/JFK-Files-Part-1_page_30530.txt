General Headquarters, Far East Combined, APO 500

5 June 1951

AG 201-AGO!  
LQ 156-2

Subject: Temporary Duty

To: Officers concerned, GHQ FEC 8100th AU, APO 500

1. Following officers will proceed this date to Pusan, Taegu, and Seoul, Korea (Koron), on TDY for the appropriate number of days indicated in connection with military activities. Upon completion, they will return to their proper station.

Lt. Col. Richard T. Greenwood, 0889874 INF (indefinite period)  
Lt. Col. Francis J. Fenton, A0835906 USE (10 days)  
MSN Antonio E. De Silasi, 01568927 INF (30 days)  
Capt. J.C.K.L. Palmer, 01330956 INF (30 days)  
MSN Fenton S. Miler (Miller), D.C. employee, GS-12 (10 days) (on detail)

2. Travel by military aircraft, rail, and government motor transportation authorized. A total of sixty-five (65) pounds of personal baggage is authorized while traveling by air. No per diem authorized. Officers are designated official couriers.

3. Mr. Miller will wear the Army officers' uniform, less insignia, in the combat zone. Uniform may be purchased from the GM Sales Store upon presentation of these orders.

4. Individuals may draw currently authorized clothing and equipment for travel to Korea. All clothing and equipment issued will be returned within seventy-two (72) hours after return to Tokyo, Japan.

5. Inspections and physical inspection as prescribed by Circular 9, FEC 27 Det 50, will be completed prior to departure.

By order of General Ridge:

Thomas F. Nalley  
Captain, ASC  
Adjutant General

Distribution: G2 Section; five individuals concerned; CPS; G1 Section (Civilian Force).
