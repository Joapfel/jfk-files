Report—Same Situation

In all my time in Habana and until 6 March 1962, the date of my departure, I never saw more than three Cuban Air Force jet planes in the air at one time. I have talked to my friends, particularly one who lives near the airstrip at Campo Libertad, and no one has seen more than this number in the air at one time. This leads us to believe that the Air Force does not have as many jets as they say they have.
