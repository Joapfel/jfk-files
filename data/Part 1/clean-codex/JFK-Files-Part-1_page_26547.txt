30 August 1966.

Secret 3C0103Z, Cite Mexico City 8419.

To: Caracas, Bogotá, Guatemala City, São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, La Paz, Panama City, Santiago, Montevideo, Buenos Aires.

Info: Director.

Subject: Rico Galan conspiracy.

Reference: Director 30551.

1. Pursuant reference, following is synopsis of developments in Rico Galan case, following up basic information relayed per reference paragraph 2.

2. In 18 August press release Mexico City police stated that 6 of the 19 persons arrested for subversion had admitted conducting a school of guerrilla warfare aimed at overthrowing the Mexican government. In separate release police issued extracts of statements quoted the six as saying their school held classes in terrorism, economics and politics. Raul Ugalde Alvarez (Rail Ugalde Alvarez), who, together with Victor Rico Galan, was one of two leaders of the conspiratorial group, admitted guerrilla school was organized and run by clandestine organization called “Movimiento Revolucionario del Pueblo,” whose tactics included use of violent means (vigilent means) to produce a “popular and democratic revolution” in Mexico.
