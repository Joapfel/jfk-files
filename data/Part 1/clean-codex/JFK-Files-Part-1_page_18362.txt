12 September 1947

Memorandum to: Chief, Personnel Branch; Executive for A & M

Subject: Detail of William P. Burke, Jr.

Reference: 37-3, “Personnel Action Request,” dated 10 July 1947.

It is hereby requested that your records be marked to show that Mr. Burke has been designated Acting Manager of the New Orleans Interagency Office. It is not intended to recruit anyone against the P-8 Manager position.

Hayden Channing  
Executive Officer
