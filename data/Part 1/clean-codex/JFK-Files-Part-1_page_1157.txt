Director. Communications: Mexico City. Action: VN 8. To: DOP, CI/CA, SAS 8, RF, NR.

Secret 222330Z.

DIR INFO WAVE MNT Y. CITE MEXI 6620.

PER UMEN.

1. Request traces and POA Jorge Antonio Abasolo Garcia, Cuban, DOB 1927, industrial engineer, graduate Georgia Tech. Address: Calle 96 Oxaber 308, telephone 2-6301, Miramar, Havana. Subject divorced from Marie Paz Someillan, who now reportedly lives with two children, ages 5 and 7, in MNT Y. Subject is chief of division in Ministry Industries and works closely with Ernesto “Che” Guevara. Controls the wood furniture industry, a portion of the paper industry including paper sacks, the toy industry, and one other unspecified industry. Has had some contact with Soviets in connection with cement procurement; recently reported belief Soviets constructing submarine base in Cuba. Came to Mexico on special passport 2 August 1963 to see children, but former wife managed to prevent contact. Returned to Cuba 21 August.

2. Brother of subject is Gustavo Abasolo, Cuban, about 42, left Cuba about 1959 or 1960, going first to Bermuda and later to Mexico. Employed continuously 22 years by Compania Interamericana de Seguros. Now assistant manager of sales for Mexico. Strongly anti-Castro, friend of AMSEVER-2. Corresponds with subject through Netherlands Embassy diplomatic pouch. At station request through A-2 Gustavo.
