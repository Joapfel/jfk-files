16 June 1985, IN 92094.

DIR CITE WAVE 3048 (ING HURST ACTING). TYPIC MHVIPER.

1. AMSCROLL SECGEN reports final session of Chamber of Commerce of the Americas unanimous passage of resolution drafted by AMSCROLL denouncing Castro regime and recommending member nation firms desist trade with Cuba, direct or indirect, and use influence [on] other free world firms to do same. Curacao, Surinam, and Aruba abstained, lacking authority from Dutch VOGT, but expressed sympathy.

2. Motion introduced by Manuel Castillo, former Panamanian consul [in] Miami, who also [was] taping message from President [of] Panama to Cuban people for AMSCROLL radio program. AMSCROLL issuing press release, carrying resolution AMITHIGH 17 June. Pouching details.
