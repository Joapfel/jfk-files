Central Intelligence Agency Official Routing Slip.

To: Izzy Di Stefano (30-96); Don Rolffing (66-19).

Action. Direct Reply. Prepare Reply. Approval. Dispatch. Recommendation. Comment. File. Return. Concurrence. Information. Signature.

Remarks:

1. Detailed are copies of Geo. Lodge's report of the day with Action—which you should find useful.
2. Underigned passed Lodge on 1/9 and to all him that we encourage Action & his case action training plane and regularly, Th. P. Newton meets with an approval.
3. Any trouble in "chicken running" in Central Area.

Please return to sender.

From: Andy Cragoe. Date: 1/9.
