OFFICE OF STRATEGIC SERVICES  
630 FIFTH AVENUE  
NEW YORK 20, N.Y.

March 9, 1945

Memorandum

To: Mr. Spencer Phenix  
From: Robert Devecchi

Alan Grover, Vice-President of TIME, LIFE, at my request checked with one of the men in his organization who knows Harold R. Isaacs personally.

He told Mr. Grover that Mr. Isaacs, in his opinion, is a very solid and substantial citizen, knowledgeable, hard working, good character, and an able correspondent.

Mr. Isaacs can be reached at the following address:

HAROLD R. ISAACS  
War Correspondent  
U.S.F. CT  
APO 627  
c/o Postmaster New York, N.Y.
