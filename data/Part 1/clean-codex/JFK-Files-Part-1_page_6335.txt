CTA-170

26 April 1968

Chief, Task Force/W  
Chief of Station, JMAVE

Operational/CYROSE  
DRE/FOAC-Habana

Attention requested: KUCAGE

1. On 24 April 1968, Ignacio Pla Martinez, A-12003-19, born 10 December 1943, Habana; Miami address: 2315 SW 25th Street, was interviewed.

2. Source said that he was a member of the DRE and the FOAC in Habana. When asked what the FOAC is, he said that it is the Federation y Organizacion Anticomunista Cubana. He explained that the DRE is trying to reorganize and that the FOAC, a large united organization, is trying to help the DRE. The FOAC, he said, is instructing the DRE in its reorganization.

3. Source said that his DRE contact is Manuel Sonora, Jr., living on Calle Milen number 330 entre Lourdes y Villollio, Arroyo Apolo, Habana. He said that Sonora is in direct touch with a FOAC coordinator and that his job is to organize student groups for passive resistance activities. This includes, he said, propaganda work, distributing pamphlets and starting rumors.

4. Source said that the identity of FOAC members is kept quite secret, but he was aware that one, Lazaro Hernandez, is a FOAC leader in Habana. He knew no other details.

Nelson L. Raymeh, Jr.
