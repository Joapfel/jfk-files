March 20, 1967

Subject: Review of Certain Hero and Martyr Case Files

The “Heroes and Martyrs” cases were not considered under the same regulations as the dependents of the invaders of the Bay of Pigs. The persons supposed to receive payment were designated, in some cases by “the Department” and in other cases by the C.R.C. through its executives. There are cases in which a written memorandum was given to us, but there are others in which no memorandum was given. We were instructed to pay no more than $175.00 monthly to each, and as can be seen, many cases are receiving lower amounts; the number of dependents was never taken into consideration. There is only one case receiving a higher amount, Mrs. Hilda R. Carballo (widow of Jesus Delgado), who receives $250.00 because we were specially instructed to pay this amount.

Regarding documents, the files are very poor, and they mostly have only letters informing changes of address.

1. Modesta Acosta (Case No. 95), widow of Nemesio Rodriguez. No documents—only letters. Payment: $25.00.

2. Rosa M. Cabrera (Case No. 96), widow of Sori Marin. Memorandum from Dr. Miro instructing to pay her $175.00. No other document but letters. Payment: $175.00.

3. Hilda R. Carballo (Case No. 97), widow of Jesus Delgado. No documents—only letters. Payment: $250.00.

4. Clara Delgado (Case No. 98), widow of Porfirio Ramirez. No documents—only letters. Payment: $100.00.

5. Rene L. Diaz (Case No. 99). Only case of trustee. Receiving payment for Luis O. Mendez and Julio Ci sielle’s relatives. There is a letter to Dr. Miro from “Comando Electrico de Liberacion” asking for assistance to the widows and a letter designating Rene L. Diaz as trustee. No other document but letters. Payment: $66.00.

6. Maria de las Mercedes Eirea (Case Nos. 100/101), mother of Anastasio Rojas. Memorandum from Dr. Miro instructing to pay her $50.00 monthly. No other document but letters. Payment: $50.00.
