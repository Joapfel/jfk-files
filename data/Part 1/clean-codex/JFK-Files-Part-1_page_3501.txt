7 April 1964

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Telephone Conversation Between AMBIDDY-1 and Martin Ferrer

Date: 7 April, 1010 hours

1. AMBIDDY-1 confirmed what AMJAVA-4 had related to this office earlier, that is, he wanted one large, two medium and two small village radios. This would suffice for one mothership, two Swifts and two rubber boats to rehearse.

2. A-1 stated the Libernic representative would not be needed in Nicaragua to confer with General Somoza since all the arrangements have been made. Gen. Somoza has found the charter very acceptable.

3. A-1 was asked flatly if the Adelaida is ready to begin operations. He replied that it is ready.

4. A-1 was advised that we would like to schedule the dry-run for 10 April, if this would be acceptable to him. A-1 agreed with this date.

Above conversation conducted to prearranged frequently changed privacy code.

File: AMBIDDY-1 201. Conversations. Dolores.

Group I: Included his wife, son, and brother-in-law.

LJ
