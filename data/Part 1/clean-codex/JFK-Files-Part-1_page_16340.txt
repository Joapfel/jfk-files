Central Intelligence Agency, Intelligence Information Cable. Country: Congo-Republic. Date of information: 15 August 1964. Report No. TDCS-314/01684-64 (TOCS-314/01684-64). Date distributed: 16 August 1964.

Subject: Reconnaissance mission for mercenary pilots.

Place and date acquired: Congo, Leopoldville, 15 August 1964. Reference: EN 52651. Source and appraisal: A Western businessman (B) from an ex-Katanga mercenary (C). Field report No. ACL-2805. Appraisal: 2.

1. At 0500 hours on 14 August 64, mercenary Air Force Commander Jeremiah Puren, Mrs. Puren and the mercenary pilots, James Hedges, Errol Mingman and Peter Mackintosh, attended a briefing on the military situation given at Congolese National Army (ANC) headquarters by Belgian Colonel (FNU) Bouzin. From what the pilots said afterwards, the purpose of the briefing was to prepare them for a mission to which Congo Premier Moise Tshombe attached great importance. Their remarks indicated that it was a reconnaissance mission in the vicinity of Lulubourg, where there is an ANC training school that must be held against the Demesus.
