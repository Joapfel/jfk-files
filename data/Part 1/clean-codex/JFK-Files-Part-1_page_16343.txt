4. Mobutu offered Hoare the command of major, which he accepted with the proviso that there should be no Belgian of superior rank in the chain of command. As agreed with Mobutu, the command line will run from Tsangobe to Mobutu to Major-General Louis Bobozo to Hoare; and the whole force will be integrated into the ANC. Hoare said, “He has christened the operational unit ‘Five Commando-ANC.’” Bobozo, he added, is now waiting-forward at Kavlaara; and there is a possibility that he and Alastair Wicks, his second-in-command, will be flown there on the right of Lacoust. Hoare said he is preparing a list of the supplies and equipment he will need and claimed that Mobutu agreed, “without actually saying so,” that the list could be amended after Hoare had personally checked out the situation.

5. Hoare said that the military objectives as he sees them are to be Manong, Kongol's and Albertville. He plans to send Patrick O'Malley, who arrived in Leopoldville on 5 August ostensibly (COSTENSIELY) as journalist but actually on Hoare's instructions, back to Johannesburg on 19 July.
