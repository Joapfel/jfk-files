SECABOB.

1. On 3 March 1967 SECAROB/I had a meeting with Haslanger and reported the following about his meeting with VISTATIC/1.

A. SECAROB/I tried unsuccessfully to get VISTATIC/I to agree on what GDRC was going to give him for phasing out the S Commando and for recruiting new men in England. Based on VISTATIC/I's attitude that SECAROB/I was something of a cad for bringing up money matters, SECAROB/I told VISTATIC/I he had no intention of phasing out the S Commando unless this was followed up with recruitment of 400 men from England, with eventual expansion of this force to 800 men, most of whom would be artisans and professional men; and finally SECAROB/I was to be allowed to develop tourism into the eastern Congo. As a result of this conversation SECAROB/I stated that he now has reservations about being a key man for the S Commando because he has no assurance that the GDRC will permit him to carry out the other aspects of this programme.
