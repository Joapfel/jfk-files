Visiting Panama City. Masferrer is a resident of the United States. In Panama City he stayed in Room 405 of the Continental Hotel. Hernandez (Mernandez) and Orgallez, working as a team, located Masferrer at his hotel shortly after his arrival and kept him under constant surveillance until he departed Panama on 21 March. According to Orgallez, Masferrer was a well-known Cuban suitan and an exile troublemaker. Source comment: Orgallez's description of Masferrer is not true. He is the son of a prominent member of the Cuban exile community in Miami. Field comment: WEP-13,367, TDFI RDB-315/81842-75, from the same source, reported that in January 1975 the GN G-2 detained a Cuban exile in cooperation with the Cuban Embassy.
