CITE SANTO DOMINGO 1023. 30 May 1966, 1:26936.

Director info Mexico City, Santiago de los Caballeros. EMARGOSY.

1. Tichborn departed Santo Domingo for Washington on the late 29 May flight. Station gave him the sterile telephone number of Ponchay, the only one it had, instructed Tichborn to call the morning of 31 May, ask for Ponchay by his true first name, and introduce himself as “Hank,” with regards from “Dave.”

2. Station would like to express appreciation for Headquarters and Mexico City support in making Tichborn available for EMARGOSY activities. For the record, Tichborn has done a brilliant job with EMANTHEM-1 in establishing quick rapport, credibility, and handling difficult assignments with difficult personalities and situations. He had to do a good deal of quick thinking on his feet in addition and, not once, did he get crossed up in the very complex and fluid situation into which he came cold not many weeks ago. Station knows of few, if any, people who could have handled this assignment the way Tichborn did. In addition, he assisted Station a great deal with suggestions on political moves, getting up organizers.
