Interview of Visit to: Julio Publico Marín, Jr., Negera  
3300 N.W. 75th Avenue  
Miami, Florida

1. Mr. Frank Waterman, representative of the U.S. State Department in Miami, advised this office that Subject arrived in the U.S. on 31 May 61 as an embassy and that he might have information of interest to this office.

2. On 4 Apr 61, Lino J.C. Rodriguez interviewed Subject in Washington's office. An examination of Subject's passport disclosed that he was born on 23 June 1943 in Miami, Ohio.

3. Subject stated that he is a member of a clandestine organization named UnitedREVILUESO (UR), which is composed of 26 underground groups. The Coordinator for military operations of UR was Major Huberto SUEL Marín, who was arrested recently. Among the leaders of UR are Reggio CUNELAZ, better known by his nom de guerre of “Travellers,” and Rafael DIAZ, also “Ralph.” All those individuals were arrested recently and UR is completely disengaged as a result.

4. Subject also stated that his mission with UR was to carry a courier between the organization and the Catholic Chaplain at the Cabana Fortress. The liaison between the Chaplain and Subject was Proy SALDAN, who is stationed at the home of Miramar Church, in the center of Miramar, near رضا.

5. Subject also made a trip to Sanofi Spirits in the Province of Las Villas, bringing a radio transmitter for the anti-Guava guerrillas operating in the area. Through the above-mentioned prior contact, Subject obtained some information furnished by prisoners held in the Cabana Fortress, which is being distributed in secret.

6. Subject intends to remain in the U.S. initially and has been interested in meeting a Cuban national named “Marie,” who resides at 1800 E. 20th St., Miami. Marie is the U.S. representative in Miami.

7. No further contacts with Subject are anticipated.

JOSE F. GUTIÉRREZ

Luis Rodriguez/cia
