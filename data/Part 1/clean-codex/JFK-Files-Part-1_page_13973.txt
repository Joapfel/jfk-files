Dispatch.

To: Chief, WH/COG. From: COS, WH/Miami; Chief, DO. CPO: Acting COS, New York HGD.

Subject: Type: DONOPS AMROSE—debriefing of AMCLOCK/1 on Cuban UN mission and delegation personalities.

Action required: Extract for appropriate 201 files. References: a. UNDA-1675, 14 October 1969. b. UNDA-1676, 14 October 1969.

1. Attached herewith for C/WH/COG and COS/WH/Miami are two copies each of three memoranda concerning results of an 11 October 1969 debriefing of AMCLOCK/1 by Howard G. FIGALAN on CMUN and delegation personalities. The meeting with AMCLOCK/1 was necessarily brief, and thus the results are not as extensive as those forwarded by the two referenced dispatches, from a 6 October meeting with AMCLOCK/1.

2. A copy of each of the memoranda has been passed by FIGALAN to the local LNERGO Cuban Squad, per our existing informal arrangement. “ELGIN” is the code name for AMCLOCK/1 as used between the Base and LNERGO here.

3. Items should be extracted for the appropriate 201 files.

Howard G. FIGALAN

Attachments: As stated above.

Distribution: Original and 1 to Chief, WH/COG, with attachments; 2 to COS, WH/Miami, with attachments; 2 to Chief, DO, without attachments.

Gross reference to dispatch symbol and number: UNDA-4684. Date: 23 October 1969. Headquarters file number: 19-6-133/4.
