26 June 1952

Memorandum for: Chief, IO/TOMC

Subject: Stanislav Oskierka

1. Walter Pforzheimer called me to say that a Congressman with whom he is in touch had asked whether we were taking on a Mr. Stanislav Oskierka. He was informed by the Congressman that Tom Myers told him that there was no job for him in HOPE but that he might be able to get a job in CIA in Washington.

2. This is the kind of thing Myers shouldn't do, and I think he should be called on it.

3. I find, incidentally, that Oskierka has not requested employment through Myers to John Sudlows and I will tell Mr. Pforzheimer that he should check with CIA Personnel.

Thomas W. Braden  
Chief  
International Organizations Division
