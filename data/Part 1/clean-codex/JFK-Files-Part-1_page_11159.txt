7. Manuel VEGA Perez — VEA was named to his present post in the Consejo by Carlos LEONINA. VEA, although not a cultured man, is a “big fan” about him that gets him through. I mentioned to L-9 that VEA was formerly G-2 in Mexico, and he agreed but gave little attention to this in terms of his new role.

8. Eduardo CORONA — L-9 said that the removal of CORONA from his post was looked upon very critically by members of the top corps. CORONA, an extremely well-educated and knowledgeable type, was replaced by Manuel VEA Perez. CORONA, according to L-9, was removed from the post in order to give him more time to work on the new constitution. UMA is also a pro at the U. O. ofNaana. I changed CORONA’s despedida address because, according to L-9, the way CORONA had written it, it looked as though CORONA had been fired. L-9 commented thusly to CORONA.

9. Carlos LECHEGA — L-9 said LECHEGA is very happy in his job and MÁXIMILIAN, his wife, is a different woman. She is very much at ease; apparently the various problems have been solved, etc. She is writing articles for “El Fondo.” LECHEGA, according to L-9, was apparently scheduled to go to Paris, but CASTELLANI went in his stead. LECHEGA was actually studying French for the job. LECHEGA comments often on the personnel who were in Mexico and are now in the CSEU: ELIZABETH L-9, Jose Luis POSADA, Manuel VEGA, LECHEGA, etc., among others.

10. Jose Luis POSADA — POSADA is in the Consejo de Cultura and will fill in for L-9 while he is away.

11. Paul FA Garcia — FA is way down on the totem pole of power and has enemies throughout the port. FA was operated on by Dr. SAINTNARIA for prostate trouble.
