Security Approval

From: Chief of Inspection and Security  
Subject: BAGLEY, Tennent Harrington

1. Security approval is granted subject for access to classified information contingent upon the receipt of derogatory information at some future date.

Unless the applicant enters upon duty within 60 days from above date, this approval becomes invalid.
