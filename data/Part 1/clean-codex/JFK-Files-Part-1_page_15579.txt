Classified message.

Date: 28 March 1961. To: Director L-10. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3. Info: COP, C/CA, C/C1, C/F1 2, S/C 2. Priority. IN 26480.

Priority, director cite LEOP 1086.

RYBAT.

WIROGUE.

Per CSN 43-43.

Reference: LEOP 1069 (25118).

1. WIROGUE was informed by Pierrot (Peirrot) on 27 March that the CAF school would definitely begin 3 April. At the same time, he asked if he could leave immediately for Europe to get instructors and again queried for names of people to whom he had written. He has still not obtained the name of the German colonel who is a Congolese contact, but CAF is willing to bypass.

2. WIROGUE is worried because thus far he has been all talk and no results. Pressures on WIROGUE from WIFLAT/3, KAZADI, and PIERROT are greater all the time. GOC claimed it can get instructors here without passports. If some names are supplied, it can stall, but without any names the situation for WIROGUE is becoming very difficult. WIROGUE believes.
