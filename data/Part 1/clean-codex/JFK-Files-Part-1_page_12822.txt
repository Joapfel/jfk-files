Agency Message.

T 124748, page 01. IN 872323. TORI2721422, March 73, Caracas 21536. CITE CARACAS 21536.

To: Director, Rome. Info: Paris.

RYBAT PLMHABYSS WKRALLY WKSCARLET CUSATI.

Reference: Caracas 21468 (85173).

1. Following summary BNDD Rome telegram 2364 of 27 March 73.

A. Salerno (Slaerno) number called by CUSATI listed to Alfonso Greico, born Salerno, 23 October 1932, residing Via Lungomare (Lungnare) Colombo 39, Salerno. INP reports Greico coffee importer with no criminal record; no traces Rome BNDD.

B. On 26 March 73 CUSATI called Caracas number 45-24-78. On 27 March he departed Rome for Naples with proposed stay of three days.

2. Caracas number listed to Genaro Cataldo Saturno, Puerto (Puento) Hierro, Avenida 3, Las Flores. Checking to determine present user of this number and will advise.
