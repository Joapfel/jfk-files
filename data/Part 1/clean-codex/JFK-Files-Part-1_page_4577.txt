Section XVII. Miscellaneous Operational Data and Remarks.

From 1947–1960, subject acted as vice president and later president of family railroad and agriculture enterprise. He made a commercial success of these operations, which were later confluenced by the Castro regime. He lost a large personal fortune, as did other close members of his family. He was an ardent anti-Castro, anti-communist leader of resistance groups both in Cuba and in New Orleans.

Subject previously used his own private aircraft in managing his commercial interests. It is hard to judge from the subject file whether he has applied for a flying position in the past for reasons other than obvious financial amounts.
