Memorandum for the File

Subject: General: AMMUG/1 Debriefing Report #271. Specific: Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez—photographic surveillance of Cuban Consulate, Mexico City.

1. On 9 February 1965, AMMUG/1 identified a photograph of Rogelio RODRIGUEZ Lopez, Second Secretary at the Cuban Embassy in Mexico City and a member of the DGI (Directorio General de Inteligencia). He also identified a photograph of RODRIGUEZ's wife, whose name he could not recall: Esther Gavilán Rosales de Rodriguez.

2. These photographs are part of the surveillance on the Cuban Consulate in Mexico City and were taken from the following:

Film No. 373, photo 10, date 22 February 1964 (Rogelio RODRIGUEZ).
Film No. 279, photo 15, date 26 November 1963 (RODRIGUEZ's wife).

3. Additional information on RODRIGUEZ is contained in the following AMMUG/1 Debriefing Reports:

Report #153, dated 24 August 1964.
Report #181, dated 9 September 1964.
Report #217, dated 2 March 1965.
