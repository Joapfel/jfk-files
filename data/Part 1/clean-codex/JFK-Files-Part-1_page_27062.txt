File No. 212 369.

A-11 881 502 pertaining to one Julia Rosa Vazquez (Acosta), also known as Edelma Marina Sanchez (Izquierdo), who may be identical with the mother of the subject. This person was said to have been born on 16 April 1909 in Matanzas Province, Cuba; to have arrived in the United States at San Juan, Puerto Rico, on 18 August 1959 with a Cuban passport issued to one Edelma Marina Sanchez (Izquierdo); and to have admitted this passport to be false insofar as she was concerned. In September 1959, her address was 65 N.W. 60th Street, Miami, Florida, and her foreign departure from Miami, Florida, for Cuba was verified on 2 October 1959. She again arrived in the United States at Miami, Florida, on 5 October 1960, and gave her address as c/o her daughter, Cecina Soto, 4413 N.W. 9th Street, Miami, Florida.
