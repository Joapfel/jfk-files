CITE MEXICO CITY 2121.

Distributor information: Ottawa.

RED COAT.

Reference: Mexico City 9919 (please relay to Ottawa).

1. Per LIFE, Don Lowry again called LEONOV 19 January, identifying himself as from Canada. Lowry had been unable to contact Escartin (Escartín) at Cuban Embassy, but Leonov said he could not help him and that he should keep trying.

2. Local LNERGO has record of one Donald Robert Lowry, born 25 November 1914 or 1915 in Walkerville (Walkeeville) or Windsor, Ontario. Local LNERGO is notifying their representatives in contact with Washington and Ottawa to see if Lowry is still on wanted list. Suggest Embassy Mexico City.

3. Station has LION KING photo of man who visited Cuban Embassy on 9 January and shows some resemblance to very old LNERGO photo of Donald Robert Lowry. Can pouch prints if Ottawa station wishes.

4. Substance of the above and information in PEF may be passed to SMAROVES, disguising source. Request Ottawa station coordinate any action with LNERGO.
