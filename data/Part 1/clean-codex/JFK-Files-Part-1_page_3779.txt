012147Z. CITE MANAGUA 6151.

Priority JMVAVE. Info: Director, Panama City. Ref: JMVAVE 7476 9/94.

1. Managua unable to comment on JMVAVE 7380 as have not yet received it.

2. Believe recovery by PETREL can be made with no difficulty. Colonel Brenes can arrange port clearance.

3. Ref assumption correct that “DIABLO” is Robert Bartlett.

4. Primitivo Ablanedo back in Nicaragua. Met with COS 1 June, returning El Bluff 2 June, will inform Brenes of plan to remove arms by ship. COS informed Colonel Genie, Chief, Office of National Security, of plan to remove cache by sea. Genie will inform President Somoza.

5. See no objection to having PETREL captain seen by Bartlett, Brenes, and others at El Bluff.

6. Welcome visit Walter J. Dahleiden. Cable firm ETA.

1 June 1967.
