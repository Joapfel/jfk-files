On Bostroen from Governacion files:

A. Nicolas Juan Bostroen, born in 1915 in St. Petersburg, Russia; Venezuelan citizen. Employed as “Director of Projects” by Stephens-Adamson de Mexico. Formerly served in the U.S. Navy.

B. Valentina Grusdow de Bostroen, born in Breslau, Germany. Formerly was a translator or teacher of Russian at the U.N.; now teacher of Russian at Ibero-Americano University, Mexico City. Venezuelan citizen.

5. Station traces:

On 18 February 1965, Dr. Richard L. Poits, American general practitioner in Mexico City and station contact who recently returned to the U.S., reported the following on Mrs. Bostroen, who was his patient: Mrs. Valentina Boestroem (Bostroen), 41 years old, resides at Calle Christian Anderson 419, Mexico City. Was born in Venezuela, went to the U.S.S.R. when a small child, and was in Stalingrad in 1942.
