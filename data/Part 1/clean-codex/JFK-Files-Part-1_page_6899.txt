1. Reference is made to our GCI-3/751,683, dated 2 October 1958, and to other correspondence concerning Subject, an American Communist living at Medellin #36, Apartment 9, Mexico, D. F.

2. Enclosed for your information are photocopies of the following correspondence sent to Subject:

a. Letter, dated 10 August 1958, from Jane Constance MORGAN, at Hill Valley, California, mentions that she and Abraham Aaron NELSON have been at Hill Valley since 1 August. She also mentions that Albert MELTZ is in Los Angeles and that she and NELSON planned to travel through there in late August. She refers to two “youngish” lawyers from Los Angeles who came to San Francisco for a breath of “fresh air.” She mentions Tim KATLY’s kindness to her and NELSON at Hill Valley and that MELTZ sends his love to Pablo OHARO. She also encloses six photographs of herself and NELSON. (GCI-3/751,016, dated 12 August 1958, reported that MORGAN had asked HALLEN to look for an apartment in Mexico City for her for the winter of 1958–59.)
