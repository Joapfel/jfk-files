RYBAT REBECE LITENSOR.

Refs: A. EMDH-11371. B. MEXI 3789 (IN 87153).

1. Ref A pouched by error 11 Mar. Summary as follows:

A. Materials prepared by KUMONK and KUWOLF. To expedite pouching, HQS defers further editing to MEXI. MEXI may select, edit and translate material it considers most appropriate.

B. Attachment A is a list of references to pertinent articles in specific issues of current intelligence weekly summaries.

C. Attachment B contains KUWOLF-prepared materials not sent MEXI except Cuba item. These may be used to promote CAZYONE position on Common Market, Berlin problem, Cuba, etc.

D. Attachment C compiled by KUMONK. Includes biographical sketches on officials of each country within which LITENSOR will probably meet during trip. MEXI may not pass biographical information through which line is drawn.

End of message.
