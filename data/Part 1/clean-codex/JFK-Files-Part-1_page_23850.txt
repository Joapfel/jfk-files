To: Chief, W.B.  
From: Chief of Staff, London

Attachment No. 2 to OHLA-43,000, 27 April 1965.

The Secretary of State of the United States of America, having authority to all whom it may herein, to provide and direct, the President may, by his order in force, extend the period thereby so far as may be advantageous to the U.S.A. to the benefit of foreign soldiers belonging to the United States.
