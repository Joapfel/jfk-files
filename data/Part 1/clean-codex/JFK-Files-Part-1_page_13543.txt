7 September 1962

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez

On 6 September 1962, LITAMIL-7 told LITAMIL-3 that Subject had arrived in Mexico. She added that she was told not to put Subject on the Diplomatic List, orders from Cuba.

Subject arrived in Mexico on 3 September 1962 via Cubana Airlines (LIFIRE B/2).

File: Rogelio Rodriguez Lopez. P-7365.
