Central Intelligence Agency. Priority. Intelligence Information Cable.

Country: Republic of the Congo, Leopoldville, Arliswater. TDCS-319/01366-6.  
Subject: Doutheu Aridordels I. Date: 6 August 1968.  
Date of information: 3–5 August 1968.  
Place and date acquired: Leopoldville (6 August 1968). RF: 44755.  
Source and approval: Western Sector (S) from El-Metasoah Merch-Tart (C), from Purch and Market, as indicated—approved 3. Field report No. ACL 2755.

1. On 5 August 69, Jeremy Puren, one of the white mercenaries now in Leopoldville, said privately that Marco Kalibiona, chef de cabinet to Principal Moise Tshombe and his liaison officer with the mercenaries, had seen him and James Hedges at 1955 hours. Kalobiona (Kalibiona) told them that Tshombe, Interior Minister Godefroid Munongo, and Congo-Les-Français National Army (ANC) Commander in Chief Major-General Joseph Mobutu (Modutu) had met that same day and decided that the European mercenaries would be used; and that Puren would be in charge of the air force.

Information comment: Kalibiona is very close to Tshombe and has access to him at all times.
