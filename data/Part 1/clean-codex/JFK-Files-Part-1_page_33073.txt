CRAT AT. AT Telephone. CIP No. UFAW-675. Film No. 201-307537. Date: 2 December 1969. Index: No. To: Chief of Station, WH/Miami. From: Chief, Western Hemisphere Division. Subject: WH/Miami representative meeting with SMADOVE (SMABOVE) in Montreal; discuss Canadian items—RYAT/TYPIC/SMADOVE. Action: FYI. Reference: UFAW-671, 1 December 1969.

1. On 26 November, NEDECKER and Ottawa Station cable officer NOLTHAM met with SMADOVE (SMABOVE) officers Don Batstone and Norman Cyr of the Montreal counter-subversive section. The purpose of the meeting arranged by NOLTHAM was to brief SMADOVE (SMABOVE) on the AMCHALK-5 operation, discuss the Cuban problem as it related to Canada, and to generally acquaint SMADOVE (SMABOVE) with WH/Miami interests. Prior to meeting with SMADOVE (SMABOVE), NEDECKER and NOLTHAM agreed that frankness was called for and that within security limitations we would attempt to engender increased interest on the part of SMADOVE (SMABOVE) for the Cuban target.

2. The obvious good rapport established by the Ottawa Station helped in establishing a free and easy discussion. NEDECKER fully reviewed the AMCHALK-5 case and also the general problem of working against the Cuban target, emphasizing our interest in increased operations against DGI personnel.
