Affidavit: Striking Against the Federal Government.

Central Intelligence Agency, Washington, D.C.

I, William Vincent Broe, do hereby swear (or affirm) that I am not engaged in any strike against the Government of the United States and that I will not so engage while an employee of the Government of the United States; that I am not a member of an organization of government employees that makes the right to strike against the Government of the United States; and that I will not, while a government employee, become a member of such an organization.

Subscribed and sworn to before me this 21st day of June 19th at Washington, D.C.
