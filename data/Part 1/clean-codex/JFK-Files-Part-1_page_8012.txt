Subject: Political Opposition to Batista Corporation.

Report No. HGI-1133. Date of information: Oktogon. Place acquired: Sabana. Date acquired: 5 October 1958. Date of report: 21 October 1958.

Source: Cuban active in political opposition (BJ).

Appraisal of contents: J.

The following report was received from an active member of the political opposition:

As a result of differences which arose between Carlos Prío Socarrás (Sosarres) and Dr. Ramón Grau San Martín (CRAU San Martín) after Prío succeeded Grau (ORAU) as President in 1948, the PRIO(a) is now split into two groups, one headed by Prío known as “Intelectuales” and the other headed by Grau known as “Auténticos”. “Electoralists”. After the break between Prío and Grau, Grau endeavored to form the “Partido de la Cubanidad” to enter the 1952 elections. However, with Batista’s coup d’état on 10 March 1952 and Prío’s exit to the U.S., Grau remained in Cuba speaking in the name of the Auténticos. When Batista announced elections for November 1954, Grau reorganized and registered the Partido Auténtico, of which he was actually no longer a member, and declared that he would oppose Batista as presidential candidate in the national election. Grau’s more left [than] Prío’s group, represented in Cuba by the official partyholders under the direction of Intelectuales, without legality.

Prío, Varona and other Prío leaders therefore declared themselves against Grau and his supporters and refused to support them during the elections. Prío has thus since refused to recognize any of the Auténticos elected to public office in the November 1954 elections.
