RYBAT ANGLEVE-15.

2. Rosenthal allegedly approached U.S. citizen Norman Rothman in DOMREP who put him in touch with ANGLEVE-15 (AMCLEVE-15), whom Rosenthal knows to be affiliated WOFACT.
