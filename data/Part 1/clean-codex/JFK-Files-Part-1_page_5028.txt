Outgoing Message.

Date-time group: 312043Z. CIA. Message reference number: 906703.

To: Federal Bureau of Investigation, Intelligence Division; Department of State, SY/I/PIB; Secret Service, Intelligence Division.

Subject: Dominican Republic–Costa Rica. Orlando Bosch Avila (Orlando B O S C H Avila), Cuban exile terrorist.

FIRCI-312/02647-7b.

1. In reference to your message dated 16 July 1976, entitled “National Liberation Front of Cuba,” copy sent addressees. This Agency has the following current information on Orlando Bosch Avila:

On 29 August 1976 an official Dominican service reported that Orlando Bosch Avila had been deported to Costa Rica using his Costa Rican passport in the name of Carlos Luis Panagua Mendez. Bosch departed Santo Domingo 29 August 1976 on Iberia Flight No. 979 for San Jose, Costa Rica, via Managua, Nicaragua.

Relaxing office. Classification. Recording officer. Authorizing officer. Record copy.
