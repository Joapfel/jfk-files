CAIS PREMAR 71. CITE CONTACTS/NEW YORK 16133.

Contacts/Washington. Alien Staff (Kohicki) from Graber.

Subject: Case 56074.

Reference: Your WA F8/39 and DIP NY 16133 B5-5564 OK.

1. We have repeatedly checked and are continuing to check with the International Rescue Committee on the whereabouts of Marinin. Charles Stepman, head of IRC, is making effort to locate Marinin. We are also checking with INS to see whether the file was returned from POE, Italy.

2. According to Stepman, Sergin, on which we have received PID data and forwarded a name check request to headquarters, does not know Marinin's whereabouts.

3. You have a matter. Submit central files of INS Washington be checked.

4. You may consider everything done station concerning Marinin.

File: IPC and Case 56074 SP-1.

May 4, 1972, No. 11.
