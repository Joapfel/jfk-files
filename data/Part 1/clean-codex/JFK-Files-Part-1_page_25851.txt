15 May 1957

Memorandum for: Chief, Safehouse Branch, Office of Logistics. Through (TIRACOUGH): Chief, Central Cover Branch, BO/P. Requesting office: WH/TIII/Mexico. Case officer: Laurence B. Sule, extension 2555. Project: LIPSTICK allotments: 7-3570-83-130.

Reference: R 45-1025, 25 June 1956.

Type of safehouse requested: Furnished apartment (efficiency apartment or larger). The apartment should be large enough to show motion pictures to trainees.

Location and environment: Washington, D.C., middle-class or above type of environment.

Maximum price range: $10 per day.

Occupancy from: 8 June 1957. To: 14 July 1957.

Intended use: Safehouse will be occupied by one agent for residence and training. The agent is a Mexican (completely bilingual in Spanish and English, was born in the U.S.), aged 23, a college graduate and purchasing agent for a Mexico City firm. He will occupy the safehouse most of the time, both day and night, for five weeks. Three or four other persons (from OTR and possibly the case officer) will be on site at the same time in the safehouse during the day. Sensitive material will be in the house during the day.

Cover arrangements: Subject will be in the U.S. as a tourist from Mexico. He has American relatives in North Carolina. WH Division's memorandum requesting training for subject, and which included this cover plan, was approved by the FI and CI Staffs on 14 May 1957.

Funds are available.

Budget officer.
