31 March 1965, IN 35201.

1. From discussions with AMSPELL Chief AMHINT-2, believe AMSPELL may have encouraged Ref A action by AMBARB-43, but not in exaggerated form publicized by AMBARB-43. AMHINT-2 disclaimed any knowledge of Ref B action, speculated AMBARB-43 trying to identify AMSPELL with AMBARB-43's aims among Panamanian students. WAVE opinion AMBARB-43's Ref B action probably completely own initiative as personal pique against KUBARK. AMBARB-43 was possibly aware of deteriorating AMSPELL-WAVE relationship, and this could have contributed to sour attitude.

2. FYI, for reasons not directly related to AMBARB-43 situation, WAVE made drastic cut in AMSPELL subsidy effective 1 April and told AMHINT-2 AMSPELL would have to cease anti-KUBARK activity. After receipt of Ref B attachment, AMHINT-2 was told AMBARB-43 must be kept under control or removed from Panama.

3. Pending outcome current discussions with AMHINT-2 be future.
