11 June 1957

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Raymond H. GERENZ (ps) Training

1. At 1000–1030 hours, 7 June 1957, I met with Mr. Robert Helberg, Chief Instructor, OEP/Covert Training, and Mr. George Orton in the office of Mr. Oscar Sells, C/Covert Training (x2778, x8175).

2. Subject's training was discussed. Mr. Sells will take me to Subject's apartment (safehouse) at 0930 on 10 June to be introduced to Subject. He will be known as Mr. Stoker. At 1000, Mr. Helberg (to be known as Bob Brioce) and Mr. Orton (George) will arrive to take over training.

3. At 1100 hours, 7 June 1957, I talked by telephone to Mr. Mexell (safehouse procurement officer). He informed me that a check for four weeks' rent had been sent to the apartment firm by Mr. Rufus King's office to cover the cost of the apartment (safehouse).

L. SULC  
W3/III/Mexico/I
