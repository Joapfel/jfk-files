Classified message. Date: 23 May 62. To: Director. Routing. From: Frankfurt. Action: C/AF 3 (copy issued to AF cable desk 0900, 23 May). Info: ADDP, C/CI, CDC, C/TSD 2, C/EE 3, S/C 2. Priority. IN 39623.

Priority. DIR LEOP. Info FROB. Cite FRAN 8582. RYBAT VIROGUE TECHS CABEZONE.

Ref: FRAN 8495/N. 39(35).

1. Virogue (Wirogue) stated he arranged with Congolese Defmin to come to Europe to attempt to trace 30 million francs which had been given officials to purchase airplanes. Money allegedly deposited in Swiss banks and then disappeared. He showed an order de mission, now in our possession, dated 17 May and signed by Segen, Congolese Formin. W claimed he intends to complete the above mission, but the real reason he is in Frankfurt is to reestablish contact with “Service” (obviously KUBARK was meant).

2. The following documents are now in our possession, but because of circumstances of the approach, we were unable to obtain the Austrian birth certificate and driver’s license which were given to W by MKTOPAZ at the time his passport was received:

A. Austrian passport 090058.

B. Return SABENA air ticket 8096473 to Leopoldville.

C. Congo driver’s permit.

D. Carte d’identité d’étranger.

E. Pass from Defmin/Congo concerning technician status (civilian).

Group 1. Excluded from automatic downgrading and declassification.

Reproduction by other than the issuing office is prohibited.
