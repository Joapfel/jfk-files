1. Second LACKLEN/WIROGUE meeting 23 May AMIARLE. V stated his greatest desire is to legalize himself. Willing to accept German documents if backstopped. Claims he has a moral obligation to carry out “Congolese Army assignment in Europe” and must come up with answers regarding 30 million francs within 3 weeks and mail results to Congo. Desires to return to Congo thereafter for 2–3 days to conclude contract with GOC.

2. Our intentions are to handle V as a Harvard resettlement case and issue backstopped German documents. As first ploy, will tell him we have word from Vienna that an official query from Congo has resulted in a determination that his documents are phony and that he would be arrested immediately upon return to Congo. Second ploy: that even ODYOKE assistance could not forestall arrest since the period of his work for us ended September 1961. We have no choice, for his own protection, but to keep his Austrian-and-Congolese-dogs secret.
