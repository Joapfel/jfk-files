Classified message. 3 August 1964.

To: Director. From: Paris. Action: WE 8. Re: FES, CA?, CA/PED, CA/PRI?, FI, VR. Added: CI/IC 2, VR.

OJ. #2135Z. DIR CITE PARI 5567. Bingham acting. LCHARVEST.

1. VEROES magazine Revolution combined issues Nos. 18–11 appeared on Paris newsstands 30 July. STA buying up copies for stuffer psych op. Will advise.

2. FYI bulletin inside front cover states:

Following grave accusations conveyed by comrades in New York, London and Accra and confirmed on inquiry, Revolution informs its readers that Richard Gibson, who misused his office as former secretary of “Fair Play for Cuba Committee” in order to penetrate ranks of international revolutionary movement, must no longer be considered collaborator of this review. No edition of review may be held responsible for his doings.

3. During our own previous consideration of approaching Gibson (ASOKIDJEVAY) for VTRK, JERVAY asked local ODENVY if he was their agent and was told no.

Security.
