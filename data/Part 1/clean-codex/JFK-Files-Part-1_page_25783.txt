Classified message.

Date: 8 December 1958. To: Director. From: Mexico City. Action: WH 5. Info: CI, CI/OPS, CI/OA, S/C 2.

Received Chile Section. Routine. IN 35553.

To Director. Info re: A. MEXI 2781 (IN 26585). Cite MEXI 2915. B. MEXI 2836 (IN 30929).

Since Gerende's wedding is set for 12 December, request answer references by 10 December.

End of message.

CS comment: Requested authority to purchase plane tickets to the U.S. for Gerende and bride as a wedding gift in recognition of the good work he has done.
