19. OUT 74849, 17 February 1961. Relayed paragraph 18 to FBI and asked if any action PARI would abet their investigation of POHORILIW.

20. Mrs. ROMAN telephoned interim reply to Perkins on 20 or 21 February that FBI investigation was delayed by incorrect address; unfortunately paragraph 3 cable gave address as 613 N 7th, whereas letter stated 631 N 7th, cf. paragraphs 16 and 17.

21. Mrs. ROMAN telephoned Perkins on 27 February 1961 and reported FBI did not have a report on S/W aspects. Perkins called Dr. REED and was told that this information had been passed to William Magee, Chief of FBI Laboratory, about 1 December 1960, and that Dr. REED, in speaking to Mr. Magee, had cited our teletype OUT 92770 and the name POHORILIW; further, he would call Mr. Magee and remind him of the same.

22. I believe Mr. Magee and Dr. REED spoke on the matter on 28 February. I was absent from the office on 28 February and 1 March, but I spoke to Dr. REED on 2 March 1961, and he assured me he now remembered the matter.
