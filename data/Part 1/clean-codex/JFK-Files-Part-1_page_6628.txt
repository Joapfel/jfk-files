Case 12667.

Further to NY 5811 and NY 5824, presumably not of immediate interest to requestor, we have been told by Dicker that Dr. Reinhold Niebuhr was chairman of the Resettlement Campaign for Exiled Professionals, but that this post has been “dormant” since Niebuhr’s withdrawal from IRC for health reasons. Because of the smaller influence of DP’s (and perhaps also because of shortage of funds), there has been a drastic drop in the resettlement caseload. A third department of IRC, in addition to ICRC and Resettlement Campaign, used to be what is now American Committee for Emigre Scholars, Writers, and Artists, Inc. This committee, which was historically separate from IRC, was merged with IRC because the combined operation was more likely to receive Ford Foundation grants than the two committees separately. A five hundred thousand dollar grant by the Ford Foundation was not followed up by additional funds, as had been expected, and the American Committee for Emigre Scholars, etc. was subsequently re-established as a separate organization. With Else Staudinger as executive director and Nelson P. Mead as chairman of the board, the last-named outfit continues to occupy facilities adjoining IRC’s and to maintain close and friendly relations with IRC. If we have a contact at the Scholars Committee separate from IRC (see also Case 12832).
