3. Egypt cancels order for Soviet planes. Cairo, 29 September. Weekly newspaper “Akhbar Al-Yom” (“AKHEAR AL-YOM”) reports that Egypt has decided to cancel its order for eight Russian-built TU-154 jet airliners, citing “numerous and major defects” in aircraft’s performance that jeopardize safety of passengers. One of planes crashed in flames during training flight 10 July, killing two Egyptians and four Soviet crewmen. Exact cause of accident has not been established and other planes of this type have been grounded. Newspaper said Egyptian Aviation Organization would file claims with the Soviet export firm, “Favia,” for reimbursement of $7.5 million already paid on the $47.5 million deal.
