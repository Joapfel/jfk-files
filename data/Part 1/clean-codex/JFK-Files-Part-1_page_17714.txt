50-6-32/4. 13 January 1974.

Mexico City 99. To Director. FEDCOAT.

1. LIFFAT reports that a man giving his name as Don Lowpy (redacted), citing American, was in contact with Soviet Press Attaché Leonov, 221-132139, on 9 January and also with Curan (Cuban) Cultural Attaché Ricardo Escartin Fernandez the same day.

Lowpy said he had newspaper and magazine experience and had also been a radio and television announcer. Escartin Fernandez had agreed to send his letter and writing samples to Cura (Cuba) in the next pouch and told him chances were maybe twenty percent, evidently of his getting a job there. Lowpy has to wait around Mexico for two weeks, apparently until he hears from Cura (Cuba).

2. Leonov was noncommittal but said he would introduce Lowpy to the Soviet consul on 10 January. Conversation indicates that Lowpy had first asked Leonov about a job, and Leonov had suggested he try the Curan (Cuban) Embassy.

3. Request cabled Headquarters traces. Information local Linfeco.
