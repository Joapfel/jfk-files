4. AMCLEVE-15 talked with Espinoza 26 August. Espinoza stated he was 33 years old, born in Palma Soriano, Oriente. Came to Venezuela from Cuba in July 1966 with 13 other Cubans under the command of Luben Petkoff and landed at Chichiriviche Beach in Falcon Province. Had approximately two months’ training in guerrilla warfare in Cuba. AMCLEVE-15 said Espinoza was very cooperative. Had a not too serious wound in the back of the head, “probably on account of the interrogation.”
