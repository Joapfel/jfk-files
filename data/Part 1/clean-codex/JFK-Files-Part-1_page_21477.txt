Dispatch. Project number and No.: CPPA-55220. To: Chief MTOPAZ Germany. From: Chief of Station, Paris. Info: Chief, HZ and Chief, SR. Date: LACQUER AFTER HE TO. Subject: General; AQUATIC/RED-GOLD/Operational Specific: Testing letters for possible secret writing. Action required: See below.

1. YURIOT/Paris requests that the attached letters be examined for secret writing. These letters have been opened by a REDCC agent and photographed by the Paris Station. It is possible that these letters contain SW and Sh traps.

2. MTOPAZ technicians should keep in mind that these letters have to be firmly returned in the 9 February November pouch, a copy letter has to be integrated in the mail system and sent to the address without his knowledge that these letters have been tampered with on the eleventh or twelfth November. If the work cannot possibly be done, the letters should be sealed and returned untested.

James B. Winterhalter

4 November 1960

Attached herewith: 2 letters for Frankfurt only.

Distributions: 3—Germany; 2—SW.
