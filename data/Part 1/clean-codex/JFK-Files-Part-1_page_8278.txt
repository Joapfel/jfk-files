July 9, 1969

Contact Report

Subject: Jimmy SABORE

Date of meet: July 7 in subject's car and Insurgentes Sabornos Far.

1. A meet was prearranged with Subject for July 7 at Del Paseo at 1400 hours. Subject phoned our office in the course of the morning to ask to confirm the arrangement. FERNALD was trying to reach Subject to change the place of the meet, but he was not able to reach him. Subject did not show up at Del Paseo, and after waiting 40 minutes I went home to his office. At 1535 hours I was informed by the office that Subject had called several times and his latest statement was that he would be in front of the Cine Latino, across the Reforma from our Embassy. I came there on time, but Subject was not around. Due to heavy rain I was bound to stay there, and at 1630 hours Subject showed up in an impressive Pontiac Bonneville (Boneville), blue with light top, plates 1968. HBD 755, Texas. Hemisfair (black). Subject waved to me to get in the car.

In the car Subject stated to me that he had goofed because he went to the restaurant where we had met twice previously and, after some waiting and phoning, he looked at his notebook and realized that he was at the wrong place. He apologized. Then Subject stated that he had to return the Bonneville and take another car, and he asked me to have a ride with him. Subject took the car to Leibnitz and parked it in front of the apartment building at 297. He excused himself for a few minutes, took newspapers, a small bundle, and two miniature bottles of some liquor, and entered the apartment. About five minutes later he returned, and we entered an old, heated-up Valiant (Valient) without license plates, and the case is hard to tell.

During the first ride Subject told me that LOG phoned him on Friday, July 4, and told him that they would have to postpone their next scheduled meeting for the next day because he, LOG, would be out of town on Saturday and Sunday. I called to meet with Subject on Monday or Tuesday.
