Subject became a member of the Cuban Liberation Army (Ejército Libertador de Cuba) and had the responsibility of fundraising among the exiled Cuban population. Subject also was believed to be a member of the group Cuban Power, but he was only related to the leader of that group, whose surname is also MORALES Navarette. Subject was a confidant of Manuel ARTINE and may still be one. In March 1968, Subject was arrested and charged with involvement in a terrorist attempt in Miami.
