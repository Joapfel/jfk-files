Intelligence Information Service.

GTE TCFRDD-315/10454-7b. Distributed 39 October 1976.

Country: Venezuela.

Date of information: mid-October 1976.

Subject: Venezuelan government attempts to tie the Social Christian Party to individuals linked to the bombing of a Cubana airliner.

Acquired: Venezuela, Caracas, 16 October 1976. Field No. HVC-7521.

Source: See below.

Summary: Officials of the Directorate for the Services of Intelligence and Prevention (DISIP) have been ordered to fully investigate the Venezuelan connections to the 6 October 1976 bombing of a Cubana airliner. Unofficially, however, DISIP officers have been instructed to build a case linking copies to those individuals implicated in the incident. On the other hand, DISIP officers fear that information linking some DISIP officials to Cuban exile Orlando Bosch might become public knowledge. Encouraging the government and further tainting DISIP, end of summary.
