Classified message.

Date: 14 May 62. To: Director. From: Leopoldville. Action: C/AF 3 (AF tempo copy slotted at 0805 14 May). Info: ADDP, C/CI, C/EE 3, S/C. Desecretized per DEN 43-43. Priority direct info FRAN, cite LEOP 3885. RYBAT WIROGUE. Reference: LEOP 3462 (not sent FRAN), IN 47511.

1. CNA adviser Louis Maliere informed Guthman he [was] checking out WIROGUE (W). Does not trust him. Said V [is] suspect as [he] drives expensive car and lives in expensive house. Maliere said this far UNION has reported nothing on W. Maliere also [is] querying Stahl for V traces.

2. GOC has asked V for police clearance from country of origin. V told them [it] would take time and spoke of forging owner. V has told GOC Ministry [of] Defense he needs to go [to] Europe for medical purposes and GOC is paying V round trip. VIROOT is asking V to check bank accounts in Belgium and Switzerland for GOC officials Bohulu and Bhunde, who [are] suspected of graft in recent GOC aircraft purchases. VIROOT is giving V mission order and V states he intends [to] contact police in both countries for this purpose.

3. V asked if any word for him and requested a contact in FRAN. Salvack told V contact would be requested but could [not] promise. Nothing, contact might be refused. V stated if nothing for him in Europe [he] would return [to] Congo. Said he [is] ready to leave week [of] 14 May.
