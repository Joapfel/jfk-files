Source: Foreign intelligence.

Guy Banister - 428810

Banister approved on 10 Nov 1960 for contact use as a routine Investigative Report - dtd 13 Sept 1960 conducted in the event Guy Banister & Associates. Due might be used as a cover mechanism.

Synopsis:

General business reputation is good, but his business is not very sound. He was incorporated on 21 Jan 1958 & is President, his Wife Vice-Treas. & See-Treas & Joseph A. Oster Leen has been placed on Company an NSF Checks have been returned. Your informants interviewed: They advised Banister suspended as Audit Sept. of NOPD because he was involved in Doron. Town bar incident during Mardi Gras in March 1957. Suspension from NOPD lifted & Banister re-assigned to position with no authority - he resigned 2 days later. Purchased a newspaper in Pretna, LA. which he had for 11⁄2 years, during which time he was charged with defamation of character.

Began private detective business & became associated with Kent Courtney, publisher of right- wing publication, Independent American. Checks done on Banister, Oster & Ferdees in course of investigation (Credit & police).
