U. S. SENATE COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES

RE: INTERVIEW OF FORMER ASSISTANT TO THE DIRECTOR JOHN F. KOHR BY DOC STAFF MEMBERS

Mr. Kohr also recalled that during his interview of May 9, 1975, he had advised Miss Genty that the official and confidential files of Mr. Mark Pelt of the FBI had been turned over to Mr. Gitenstein which amounted to one-half a file drawer. Subsequently, Mr. Kohr learned from Miss Genty that the official and confidential files turned over to Mr. Pelt amounted to one and one-half file drawers. This information was subsequently furnished to Mr. Gitenstein by Mr. Kohr. On that occasion, Mr. Gitenstein asked Mr. Kohr whether an itemized list of the official and confidential files was made and if so where the list was. Kohr told Gitenstein that he was sure a list was made and that in all probability the list accompanied the official and confidential files turned over to Mr. Pelt by Miss Genty.

In addition to the above, Mr. Kohr on May 12, 1975, recalled that Gitenstein asked about a requirement of all Bureau employees to turn over any confidential files in their possession ordered by Mr. Hoover following the revelation that former Assistant to the Director William C. Sullivan had turned over certain confidential files to former Assistant Attorney General Hardin, Mr. Kohr advised Gitenstein that Mr. Hoover had in fact levied such a requirement on Bureau officials and that all confidential files were to be turned over to Mr. Pelt along with a memorandum explaining the nature of these files. Gitenstein asked Kohr where those memoranda would be filed. Kohr responded by saying he presumed they were confidential memoranda and would have gone with the confidential files turned over to Mr. Pelt by Bureau officials maintaining such files.
