Ministry of Information

On February 23, 1969; the Deputy Minister of Information, Marcus "Chaka" Walls, prepared handbills informing the people of the great multitude of racist police action in the black community, pointing out the industrial equipment those "pigs" are using to suppress black people. This handbill also noted the extreme exorcitation of the black community and gave details and facts of the black people being victims of overt capitalism and racism.
