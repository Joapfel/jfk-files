Testimony of Gilpatric

Gilpatric served as Deputy Secretary of Defense throughout the Kennedy Administra- tion. (Gilpatric, p. 5; Gilpatric Ex. 1, p. 5.) In that capacity, Gilpatric represented the Department of Defense as a member of the Special Group and the Special Group Augmented during the Mongoose operation. (Gilpatric, p. 5.)

Gilpatric testified That he understood the mandate of the Special Group during Mongoose was not to kill Castro, but to "so under- mine, so disrupt the Cuban system under Castro that it could not be effective." (Gilpatric, p. 28). Gilpatric emphasized that "it was the system we had to deal with" and words such as "get rid of Castro" were said "in the context of the system, of the .... government he had installed and was presiding over, but of which /Castro/ was only one part." (Gil- patric, p. 29).
