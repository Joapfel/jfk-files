Czechoslovakia- USSR

Czech President Novotny returned from Moscow on Thursday after signing a renewal (for another 20 years) of the treaty of friend- ship and mutual assistance.

He apparently got little else out of Khrushchev. The final communique was conspicuously cool.

There were no Soviet con- cessions, nor were there the usual references to Czech "progress" or to public support for Novotny's leadership.
