4 18 Oct. 1960 Conversation with Major Vector M. Panegue (Commandante Diego, 6 October at 900).

When Panegue arrived in Miami, he got together with Alianza de Liberación through Dr. Luis Loute Agüero. He could not get immediate help from this group, so resigned. Also said he had tried to get help from U.S. Govt. but unsuccessful.

Panegue is convinced that the only thing needed to overthrow Castro is arms and supplies. Has a plane which he intends to fly into Cuba with cargo of arms.

Author of memo recommends that CIA offer assistance to Panegue independent if all other organizations as he seems to be capable of becoming an important factor in the future.

5 Nov. 2, 1960 Cable to Director from Maale Panegue & 8 men leaving for Cuba overnight on 40-foot yacht, Trudy Lou. Craft purchased by Orlando Beach.
