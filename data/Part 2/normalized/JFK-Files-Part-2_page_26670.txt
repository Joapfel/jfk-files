Q -- that would be considered covert action?

A Well, as I say, a lot of propaganda, press placement, media placement. It has been so long ago I really don't remember. I know we had a number of things going against him. We used suasion, moral suasion, otherwise. We did some extra-legal things. Q Let's talk about each of those in turn. You said you used moral suasion. What kind of activities are you referring to?

I guess as you are aware, Cuban industry was totally U.S.-oriented.
