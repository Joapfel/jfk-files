Title: BLACK PANTHER PARTY (BPP) NATIONAL COMMITTEE TO COMBAT FASCISM (NCCF) DETROIT DIVISION

Character: RACIAL MATTERS - SMITH ACT OF 1940; SEDITIOUS CONSPIRACY, REBELLION AND INSURRECTION

Synopsis: The Detroit NCCF continues to operate under the direction of BPP National Headquarters, Oakland, Calif. Detroit continues to sell "The Black Panther" newspaper and operate a Free Breakfast for School Children Program. EDGAR ROBINSON and KENNETH HORSTON, both Detroit NCCF members visited BPP National Headquarters in 4/71. Four NCCF members were arrested by the Detroit PD for Murder I as a result of a holdup in which a man was killed. A purged Detroit NCCF member has been contacting the Cleaver Faction of the BPP in New York, however, no attempts have been made to start a Cleaver group in Detroit.
