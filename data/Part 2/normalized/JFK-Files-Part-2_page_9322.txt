Open Letter To The Community

THE NATIONAL COMMITTEE TO COMBAT FASCISM IS AN ORGANIZING BUREAU OF BLACK PANTHER PARTY. THIS MEANS THAT WE WILL BE IMPLEMENTING AND ANIZING THE VARIOUS SOCIALISTIC PROGRAMS FOR THE PEOPLE IN THE COMMU- AS ESTABLISHED BY THE BLACK PANTHER PARTY.

THE FREE BREAKFAST PROGRAM WHICH IS SOON TO BE STARTED, WILL FEED THE YOUTHS IN THE BLACK COMMUNITY A GOOD WHOLESOME MEAL. WE RECOGNIZE A CHILD CANNOT STUDY, LEARN OR CONCENTRATE AS WELL ON AN EMPTY stomach AS HE CAN AFTER HAVING EATEN A HEALTHY MEAL. CAPITALISM, THE WAY OF ALL PEOPLE, HAS CAUSED MANY BLACK FAMILIES TO HAVE TO SEND THEIR KIDREN TO SCHOOL IN THE MORNING WITHOUT FOOD. BUT THROUGH OUR PROGRAM, PROBLEM WILL BE ELIMINATED.

THE FREE MEDICAL HEALTH CARE CLINIC WILL SUPPLY THE PEOPLE WITH MUCH NEEDED FREE HEALTH CARE. TIME AND AGAIN, BLACK PEOPLE HAVE DIED FROM LACK OF MEDICAL TREATMENT BECAUSE THEY DIDN'T HAVE THE MONEY TO PAY THE MONEY DOCTORS. WE MUST SUFFER FOR HOURS IN CROWDED DOCTORS OFFICE'S TO MEDICAL TREATMENT WHICH IS SIMILAR TO BEING TREATED LIKE A PIECE OF WINERY ON A FACTORY ASSEMBLY LINE. OUR FREE MEDICAL CLINIC WILL GIVE TREATMENT AND MEDICINE TO THE PEOPLE THROUGH THE SERVICES OF QUALI- DO LICENSED DOCTORS AND NURSES.

WE TOO, REALIZE THAT DOPE ADDICTION IS A VERY PRESSING PROBLEM IN THE BLACK COMMUNITY. IT IS LITERALLY ASSISTING THE ENEMIES OF BLACK PEOPLE THEIR PLANS. OF EXTERMINATION OF OUR RACE. OUR YOUNG BROTHERS AND SISTERS AND SONS AND DAUGHTERS ARE LOSING THEIR LIVES CONSTANTLY BECAUSE OF WERDOSE OF SOME POISON DRUG. THE BLACK PANTHER PARTY AND ITS ORGANIZATION, THE N.C.C.F., HAS STOOD FORTH BOLDLY AND CONDEMED THOSE WHOM BLAME SHOULD REALLY REST ON. IT IS THE GREEDY AVARIOUS BUSINESSMEN, THE CROOKED BRIEF TAKING PIG POLICEMEN, WHO ARE LETTING AND PROMOTING ENTRANCE OF HERION AND COCAINE INTO THE BLACK COMMUNITY. AND NOW THEY TRYING TO COVER UP FOR IT WITH A PROGRAM THAT GETS THE ADDICTS STRUNG ON METHODOINE, A DRUG SIMILAR TO HERION. WE NOW HAVE A PROGRAM WHICH WILL END THIS PROBLEM ONCE AND FOR ALL.

WE ALSO HAVE A FREE CLOTHING PROGRAM WHICH WILL MEET THE CLOTHING S OF THE PEOPLE THROUGH THE DONATIONS WE RECEIVE, AND ARE WORKING ENGENTLY TOWARD OPENING A FREE SHOE FACTORY, A FREE PEOPLE'S LIBRARY A NO-INTEREST LOAN PROGRAM.
