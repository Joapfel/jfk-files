9. GEORGE VAN LAETHEM (s) [REDACTED]

Pages 97 and 98 relate to GEORGE VAN LAETHEM, Assistant Army Attache, at the American Embassy from March, 1951, to March, 1953. NOSENKO had reported that ALLA LARIONOVA was placed on a train going from Berlin to Moscow with an assignment to strike up an acquaintance with VAN LAETHEM.
