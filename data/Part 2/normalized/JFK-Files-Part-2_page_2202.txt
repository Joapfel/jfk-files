1 October 1968

Mr. C F. Loche Director National Conference of Bar Examiners 135 East 60th Street Chicago, Illinois 60617

Dear Mr. Loche:

With reference to your letter of 26 September 1968 concerning Mr. Birch Dimeart O'Neal, I have learned Mr. O'Neal since the late 1940s. In 1947 we were establishing formal puppets with the Bureau of Immigration and Naturalization and, because a thorough knowledge of the complex immigration laws was essential, I proposed that this position must go to a lawyer. Mr. O'Neal was selected and performed very well in that job, which required constant supervision. And this office on the legal aspects.

In Mr. O'Neal's assignments since that time, I have had occasion to work with him from time to time on a variety of problems. I have found he has had a good grasp of the legal aspects of those matters. From this long association my conclusion is that Mr. O'Neal's moral character is good, he has sound background in the field of law, and is generally fit for the practice of law.
