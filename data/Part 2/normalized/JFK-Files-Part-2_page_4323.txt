5 December 1963

MEMORANDUM FOR THE SPECIAL GROUP

SUBJECT: Cuban Operations

Special Group members approved by telephone on 5 December the following:

Operation No. 3711, the exfiltration of three members of an existing resistance net.

Operation No. 3118, the infiltration of a five-man team for the purpose of sabotaging a railway bridge.

Operation No. 31110, the establishment of a ratline into Cuban ports.

(Signed)

Paul Eckel

Distribution

Mr. Johnson Mr. Gilpatrick Mr. McCone
