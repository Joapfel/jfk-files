FREE THE DETROIT "16"

THE DETROIT "16" IS "16" BROTHERS AND SISTERS WHO ARE BETTER HELD IN THE MEDICAL DUNGEONS BETTER KNOWN AS WAYNE COUNTY JAIL, ON CHARGES OF MUDER AND CONSPIRACY TO COMMIT MURDER.

ON OCTOBER-24, THESE BROTHERS AND SISTERS WERE TAKEN OUT OF THE BLACK (16th & MYRTLE) COMMUNITY FOR FEEDING HUNGRY KIDS IN THE MORNING, FOR TRYING TO ESTABLISH A FREE MEDICAL CLINIC WHICH WOULD SERVE ALL THE PEOPLE, AND OTHER SURVIVAL PROGRAMS. BUT THE PIGS JUSTIFICATION FOR-TAKING THE PEOPLE'S WARRIORS OFF THE STREET, WAS FOR THE MURDER OF PIG GLENN SMITH AND COMPILING TO MURDER PIG GLENN SMITH.

5 OF THE-8 SISTERS ARE OUT ON $5,000 BONDS EACH. ONLY ONE OF THE EIGHT BROTHERS, LORRIE DARDEN, HAVE BOND WHICH IS $20,000 AND TWO SUSTITUTES. THE REMAINING 7 ARE WITHOUT BONDS.
