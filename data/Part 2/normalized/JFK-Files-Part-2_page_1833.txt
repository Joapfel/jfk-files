SECTION II: OBJECTIVES

The objectives of this course are to provide the student with (1) an understanding of the inter-relationships between operating functions and administrative support functions; (2)-an understanding of some of the basic principles and techniques of Agency operations and the organization and functions of the various administrative components; (3) a knowledge of procedures, regulations, and problem-solving skills to address problems in Finance, Administration, and other areas of administrative support.

SECTION III: SPECIFIC CHARACTERISTICS OF THE COURSE

One week of the course is devoted to learning in the area described in category 1 on review of this page.. The material in categories 2 and 3 is presented both by lecture and by practical exercises during the running two weeks of the course. Categories 4 through 9 are concerned with the application of knowledge of Agency organization, structure, procedures and regulations in practical situations.

SECTION IV: STUDENT CONDUCT

All materials and equipment in the course shall be used in accordance with the policies and regulations for the education, experience, etc. These policies are defined as follows:

EXCELLENT: The student indicated exceptional ability or proficiency in meeting this goal or objective, no demonstrated an unmitigated through absence or the material presented.

SATISFACTORY: The student met this objective in a competent and effective manner; his demonstrated a good group and understanding of the material presented.

INSATISFACTORY: The student did not achieve this goal or objective; the student's performance indicated a serious lack of knowledge concerning material presented.
