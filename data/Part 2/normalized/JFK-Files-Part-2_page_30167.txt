USE BLACK PENNIT'S INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE AS EASILY ON INSTRUMENT.

3. WASH AND DRY HANDS THOROUGHLY.

4. PULL FINICHT FOR A PAR TO ALL AND AVOID ALL HANDS TO SLIP.

5. PRESS IMPRESSIONS, IF NEEDED, IN CORRESPONDENCE ORDER.

6. IF AN AMOUNT IS DUE THAT MUST BE PRINTED A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME CERTIFIED CONDITION TAKEN IT MIGHT BE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE CATALOGED WITH A HUNG STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CAUSE.

8. EXAMINE THE CONDUCTED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERS MUST FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INFERREDLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
