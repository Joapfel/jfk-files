SECTION-6. PUBLICATIONS, PRESS, AND PUBLIC CONTACTS

I. VISITS TO FIELD OFFICES

Non-Bureau governmental officials

Do not discuss Bureau policies, plans, or relationships. Courteously advise the visitor that inquiries concerning such matters are to be made at the Bureau at Washington.

Advise the Bureau of the visit, identity of the visitor, purpose, matters discussed, courtesies extended, and observations of interest to the Bureau.

Departmental officials

Be courteous.

Do not offer office space, clerical and stenographic assistance, or chauffeuring in Bureau automobiles.

Do not furnish information unless prior notification has been received that the official would contact the field office for information. If no notification received, before furnishing information clearance must be obtained from the Bureau.

Advise the Bureau in case of doubt.
