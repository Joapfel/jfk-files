Zogbey, Harvey Halpern Helms McCONE Dec 1961 Castro

OCCURRENCE:

"Zogbey," sometime in Dec of 1961, described to Halpern a meeting at the director's office in which McGone had stated that Helms would henceforth "be his man on Cuba." (13) Zogbey told Halpern that Helms had no inkling that he would be appointed head of the Cuban operations before this meeting (13). Sometime after the meeting Helms came to Zogbey's office, checked into the organization of Zogbey's operations and directed that Cuba would be a separate activity and no longer part of the MI Division (14).

SOURCE: Halpern 6/18/75
