OCCURRENCE: Lansdale asked Cheever about covert operational assets both inside Cuba and outside, if they had stop orders on action. Cheever said that they all had such orders, "but pointed out that some of the Cubans were quite emotional about stopping actions against Castro and he wasn't certain that everyone would hold to the orders"
