SUBJECT: WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WOMAAC) DEMONSTRATION, NOVEMBER TWENTY INSTANT, SAN FRANCISCO, IS - NEW LEFT.

APPROXIMATELY FIVE HUNDRED INDIVIDUALS, EIGHTY PER CENT OF WHOM WERE YOUNG WOMEN IN THEIR TEENS AND EARLY TWENTIES, GATHERED AT THE EMBARCADERO, SAN FRANCISCO, THIS DATE AND BEGAN MARCHING AT ELEVEN TEN AM FROM EMBARCADERO TO CIVIC CENTER, SAN FRANCISCO, CARRYING SIGNS SUCH AS "FREE OUR WOMEN" AND "SEPARATE CHURCH AND STATE". THE MARCHERS HAVE ARRIVED AT CIVIC CENTER AND A FEMALE ROCK BAND IS PLAYING. CROWD HAS REACHED APPROXIMATELY FIFTEEN HUNDRED INDIVIDUALS AT THIS TIME, RALLY IS SCHEDULED FROM TWO TO FOUR PM. NO INCIDENTS OR ARRESTS.
