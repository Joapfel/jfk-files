For the information of Philadelphia, as San Francisco LIEI related to a scheduled demonstration of captioned group 11/20/71 in the San Francisco area and noted that a JUDY KLEINBERG who has been in the San Francisco area since 10/30/71 and is formerly from Massachusetts was publicity coordinator for captioned group in San Francisco.

Philadelphia is requested to forward to San Francisco a copy of photograph enclosed to Philadelphia in re letter to enable San Francisco to establish whether the JUDY KLEINBERG mentioned in re San Francisco communication is identical with Boston subject JUDITH KLEINBERG.

Philadelphia should also establish through postal or other sources whether JUDITH KLEINBERG has furnished any indication of a change of address from 6606 North 11th St., Philadelphia, which address she had listed as a change effective 9/23/71 when she moved from the Boston area.

If the above identification is established by San Francisco, that office is requested to determine KLEINBERG's residence and employment status and advise Boston and Philadelphia in order that Boston may submit FD-128.
