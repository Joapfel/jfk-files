Berlin

We have had an indication that Soviet harassment of Allied access routes to West Berlin will shift this weekend to the air corridors.

The Soviet man at the Ber- lin air traffic control center told the Allied representatives yesterday that Soviet transports would be using the air corridors for brief flights during the next five weekends.

Moscow has tried on a num- ber of past occasions to assert its right to restrict Allied use of the air corridors. In early 1962, the Soviets attempted to reserve "blocks" of air space for their own use. They have since demanded that Western flights stay between stipulated al- titudes, that we file flight plans in advance and provide prior notice of border-crossing times.
