Czechoslovakia (USSR)

Czech President Novotny returned from Moscow on Thursday after signing a renewal (for another 20 years) of the treaty of friendship and mutual assistance.

He apparently got little else out of Khrushchev. The final communique was conspicuously cool.

There were no Soviet concessions, nor were there the usual references to Czech "progress" or to public support for Novotny's leadership.
