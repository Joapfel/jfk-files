Assistant Attorney General Criminal Division

Re: Mail Intercept Programs

NOTE:

The incoming letter delivered on 8/1/75 is undated and it requested that the FBI make available for review by the Criminal Division attorneys, all memoranda, records, letters or other correspondence from 1951 to date which the FBI had received or generated with respect to its contacts or involvement with the White House, the Attorney General, Post Office Department or Postal Service and CIA in connection with the inception, implementation or declination of participation by the FBI in mail interception programs. This relates solely to the information in Bureau files concerning Bureau Informant 200 also known as Project Hunter which was the CIA code name for its interception program. In accordance with discussions held with members of the Staff of the Senate Special Committee on 8/15/last, it was requested that this program be cleared with the Criminal Division first. The Hunter policy file has been reviewed and documents considered to be pertinent have been Xeroxing and will be held for review by Departmental attorneys.
