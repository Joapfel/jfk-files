INTERNATIONAL SECURITY - CHILE

A recent State Department report assessing the Communists' efforts to weaken Chile's ties with the U. S. and joining it toward the Soviet Union and other block countries. The same report notes that trade with Chile is being used as a cover for political and intelligence operations and that it is primarily a cover for political and intelligence operations and a means for carrying out functions of a diplomatic mission. The possibility exists that Chile could take a favorable position with regard to the Soviet Union and block countries.

The Bureau in order to be in a position to obtain current intelligence data concerning Chile on July 4, 1962, obtained state Department clearance to immediately indicate investigation designed to develop sources at the Chilean embassies.

Accordingly, the recipients should institute a survey for the purpose of identifying persons employed at the AmericanConsul general of Chile, Washington, D. C., the Chilean Mission to the United Nations and the Consulate of Chile, both in New York City, particularly United States citizens, who might possess potential as sources or informants. Indicate your request for investigation in this regard in accordance with a request for bureau clearance to institute investigation in each individual case.

Submit the results of your initial survey under instant caption to reach the Bureau by May 10, 1962, and thereafter on a bi-weekly basis setting forth progress being made to develop confidential sources and informants until such time as adequate coverage is assured.
