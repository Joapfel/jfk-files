FEDERAL DUREAU OF INVESTIGATION

Date June 6, 1965

On June 4, 1969, SAs and BLBSS, arrested LOCKETT F. BIBBS on the sidewalk in front of 2350 West Madison. This address houses the offices of the Chicago Chapter of the Black Panther Party (BPP), and BIBBS was about to enter same at the time he was arrested.

BIBBS was subsequently transported by Bureau automobile to the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) Office at 219 South Dearborn, where he was photographed, fingerprinted, and provided with a printed warning and waiver form which he read but declined to sign.

BIBBS was interviewed by SAs regarding his association with the BPP and he advised as follows:

BIBBS stated he has been a member of the BPI for about four or five months. He then amended his statement regarding his membership with the BPI and advised he is merely a friend of the party and works for them. lie stated, however, he has never formally joined the BPI. BIBBS stated that at the time of his arrest he was about to enter the offices of the BPI to help supervise their "breakfast for Children Program." lie stated that his duties are to set tables and help serve the children as they are being fed. lie stated that the BPI tries to help the people in the neighborhood and he feels it is a very good and worthwhile organization. lie advised that he has never seen any guns or other weapons in the BPI headquarters and that he has never seen a member of the BPI carrying a gun on his person.

BIBBS was shown a photograph of GEORGE SAWS, Jr., and stated he did not know Saws by name or photograph. BIBBS advised he had been in the BPI headquarters several times during the previous evening, but again denied having seen Saws on the premises.

On 6/4/69 at Chicago, Illinois
