Zogbev Halpern Helms Dec 1961 Castro

OCCURRENCE:

Zogbev sometime in Dec of 1961, described to Halpern a meeting at the director's office in which McConne had stated that Helms would henceforth "be his man on Cuba." (13) Zogbev told Halpern that Helms had no inkling that he would be appointed head of the Cuban operations before this meeting (13). Sonetime after the meeting Helms came to Zogbev's office, checked into the organization of Zogbev's operations and directed that Cuba would be a separate activity and no longer part of the FI Division (14).
