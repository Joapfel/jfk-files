Adams before the Church Committee. You indicated that this was authorized for the exclusive viewing by FBI personnel.

This is to advise you that the videotape of Mr. Adams' testimony is being restricted solely to FBI personnel. We certainly appreciate your consideration in this regard. Thank you for this authority.
