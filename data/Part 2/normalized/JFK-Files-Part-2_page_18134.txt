UNITED STATES SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE TO STUDY GOVERNMENTAL OPERATIONS WITH RESPECT TO INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES (SSC)

RE: REQUEST FOR IDENTITIES AND CURRENT ADDRESSES OF FORMER AND PRESENT SPECIAL AGENTS (SA) OF THE FBI TO BE INTERVIEWED REGARDING FBI'S RELATIONSHIP TO MAIL OPENING ACTIVITIES

Reference is made to letter dated August 19, 1975, to Special Counsel for Intelligence Coordination, Office of the Deputy Attorney General, from John T. Elliff, Director, Domestic Intelligence Task Force of captioned Committee.

The SSC made the following requests:

1. The SSC desired to interview all present and former Special Agents of the FBI responsible for supervising the communications between the FBI and CIA regarding the FBI's relationship to mail opening activities. The names, present assignment, and/or whereabouts were requested.

The following listed Special Agents (SA) were responsible for supervision of communications between FBI and CIA regarding CIA Project Hunter:
