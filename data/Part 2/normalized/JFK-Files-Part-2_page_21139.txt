COMMENT:

On December 6, 1967, (N-57), the name VIKTOR KOPEYKIN was displayed to NOSENKO who identified him as a Lieutenant Colonel working as a Senior Case Officer in the 2nd Section of the 1st Department in 1960-62. He stated that KOPEYKIN "was keeping my former agent VERA" who [married PHILIP NIELSEN]. He stated that she had left Russia with [NIELSEN] but was coming back to Russia and KGB was also studying the question of whether [NIELSEN] was connected with American Intelligence.

On December 11, 1967, (N-64), NOSENKO listed TAMARA KUNGAROVA among his agents for the 1955-59 period. On January 11, 1968, (N-93), NOSENKO stated that she was "in close relations".
