TO GET A CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINT:

USE THE BLACK INK.

2. DILUTE THE INK SOLY ON THE SLAB.

3. DO NOT USE FOR IDENTIFICATION.

4. PULL THE LINES FORTUNE TO THE BAL, AND ALLOW ALLOWS FOR FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. IF POSSIBLE, RECORD ALL INDIVIDUALS IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF ONE IMPRESSION IS NOT ENOUGH, MAKE IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT END OF THE LINES, AND MAKE AN INSCRIPTION TO THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF TWO OR MORE IMPRESSIONS ARE NOT ENOUGH TO DETERMINE THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER, USE THE BEST IMPRESSION, SUBMIT THE STARS THAT ARE, BE CERTIFIED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CRITERIA.

8. DRAW IN THE CORRESPONDING POINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT ONLY THOSE IMPRESSIONS THAT FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INCORRECTLY) ARE NOT SHOWN HERE.
