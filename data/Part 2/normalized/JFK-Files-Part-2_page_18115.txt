Reference is made to memorandum from Mr. Michael E. Shaheen of the Department of Justice to Mr. John A. Mintz of this Bureau, captioned "Senate Select Committee Request," dated September 9, 1975. Reference is also made to letter dated September 5, 1975, to Mr. Shaheen from Mr. Mark Gitonstein of the SSC staff.

The issue raised in referenced communications is whether employees of the Internal Revenue Service who supplied tax returns or tax return information to Special Agents of the FBI in response to formal or informal requests are to be characterized as "confidential sources" and, as such, to be spared being interviewed by the SSC.

It is the position of this Bureau that regardless of the formality or informality of requests made in the above context they cannot be characterized as "confidential" since they represent official responses to official requests. Therefore, we cannot consider them in the same light as the "confidential sources" issue raised earlier in Cointelpro matters.
