20 April 1961

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, U.S.

SUBJECT : CI Program for JMAT Project

I. Objectives:

To add to our knowledge of objectives, operations and assets of the Castro services,

To bring about the defection of selected key personalities in the Castro Government and military services.

To plan and conduct deception operations involving agency-controlled double agents and W/T play backs. To date, four W/T operators in Cuba are deemed appropriate. The fate of our two double agents, who may have accompanied the MIAMI in its assault on Cuba, will be determined during the course of the next few days. It is anticipated that other candidates for double agent operations will be identified in the States; their exploitation will be coordinated with the Federal Bureau of Investigation.

To support and direct the AMOT Intelligence and Security Service in Miami in the fulfillment of agreed upon objectives.

To support and direct 48 AMOT officers and agents now in Cuba, in the collection of intelligence pertinent to Project needs, and in the fulfillment of such other covert action assignments which may be deemed within their competence.

To introduce into Cuba unilaterally-controlled counter-intelligence agents.

To intensify the Project's Communist Party penetration program.

To increase counterintelligence operations run into Cuba in conjunction with liaison services of friendly countries.

To counter theespionage, subversive and sabetage operations directed by the Castro services against other States within the Western Hemisphere. This to be done in conjunction with the services of those countries with which we maintain liaison.
