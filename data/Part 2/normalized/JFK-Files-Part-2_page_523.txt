REF: MEXICO CITY 8692 (N~ 07422)*

LIRING/3 HAS RECEIVED REQUEST FROM JOAQUIN HERNANDEZ ARMAS FOR MEETING AND MEETING SCHEDULED NIGHT OF 2 OCTOBER 1966 IN CUBAN EMBASSY. NO DOUST BUT THAT HERMANDEZ REQUEST FOR MEETING RESULT OF LIRING/3 CONFRONTATION OF JULIAN LOPEZ MENTIONED REF.

2. LIRING/3 PREPARED GIVE HERMANDEZ SAME STORY HE GAVE LOPEZ BUT WITH TWO ADDITIONAL POINTS WHICH WILL BE EMPHASIZED:

A. ACCORDING TO LIRING/3 FRIENDS, WHICH HE INSTRUCTED NOT TO IDENTIFY, ARRESTS CANE AS RESULT LOPEZ BABSLINGS AT DRINKING PARTY TO EFFECT CUSANS SUPPORTING REVOLUTION IN GUATEMALA AND HE AT HEAD OF EFFORT IN MEXICO. AS RESULT THIS DISCLOSURE MEXICAN POLICE HEARD OF BRAG AND BEGAN FOLLOWING LOPEZ AT LEAST TWO WEEKS BEFORE ARRESTS MADE.

B. LOPEZ FOOL FOR TAKING ACTIVE PART IN REVOLUTIONARY SUPPORT AND SHOULD HAVE PROTECTED HIMSELF AND CUSANS BY USING SUCH PEOPLE AS LIRING/3 AND OTHER MEXICANS OR CENTRAL AMERICANS. HOPE BY THIS MEANS ELEVATE VALUE OF LIRING/3 TO CUSAN EMBASSY AND ITS ILLEGAL/CLANDESTILE ACTIVITIES.
