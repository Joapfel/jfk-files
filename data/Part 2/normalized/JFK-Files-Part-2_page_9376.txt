DE T-3 advised on October 18, 1971, that no complaint has been made to the Detroit Police Department regarding alleged shooting into 2591 Pennsylvania, Detroit, and consequently no investigation will be conducted by the Detroit Police Department.

DE T-3 advised that an informal inquiry by the Detroit Police Department failed to substantiate the charges made by KENNETH HORSTON.

DE T-12 and DE T-4 are not aware of any shooting at the BPP office, 2591 Pennsylvania, Detroit, and believe that HORSTON was attempting to obtain publicity by making the above charge.
