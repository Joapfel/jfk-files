MATERIAL FOR JOINT COMMITTEE ON INTERNAL REVENUE TAXATION

1. TITLE/SUBJECT: FBI's Investigation of Extremist Organizations and Individuals

ORIGINATING ORGANIZATION: FBI

3. NATURE OF MATERIAL: Letter from FBI to Mr. Laurence N. Woodworth, Chief of Staff, Joint Committee on Internal Revenue Taxation, dated 4/10/75.

DOCUMENT CLASSIFICATION: Unclassified

5. NATIONAL SECURITY INFORMATION STAMP: NA

6. SUMMARY OF CONTENTS: The Joint Committee requested the names of FBI employees who made the decision to provide the Special Service Staff (Activist Organization Committee) of the Internal Revenue Service with FBI reports relating to the FBI's investigation of extremist organizations and individuals. The FBI response was that the matter had been thoroughly researched and it was established that the decision in the above matter was personally made by the late J. Edgar Hoover, then Director of the FBI.

7. REQUESTER/DATE OF REQUEST: Joint Committee on Internal Revenue Taxation. 3/28/75 REC-100 6-116325-121

RELEASING AUTHORITY: FBI

DATE OF SUBMISSION: 4/10/75

10. LOCATION OF FILE COPY: 62-17909-1104 MAY 13 1975

11. RELATION TO INTELLIGENCE COMMUNITY PROBLEMS: This matter is of joint interest to the FBI and the Internal Revenue Service.
