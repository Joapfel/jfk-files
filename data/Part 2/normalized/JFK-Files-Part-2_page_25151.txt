7/63 DIEM

OCCURRENCE:

Undertakenly, the officer, a Major (and) Lieutenant (gents), who had the mission to escort Captain No. Two Goyen, Commanding Officer of the Naval Forces, killed the Captain prior to 1200 hours. This nearly upset the timing of the plan. Fortunately, he was driven directly to the Military headquarters and no one knew of his death except the copy principals and the sources loyal to them.

SOURCE: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit one, Page 11. STAFF: Dawson
