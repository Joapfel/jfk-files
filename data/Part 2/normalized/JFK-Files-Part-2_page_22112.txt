HARVEY, William O'DONNELL, Justin BISSELL, Richard LUMUMBA TOP SECRET

OCCURRENCE:

HARVEY testified that he worked with a man called [JUSTIN O'DONNELL] in Staff. He stated that [O'DONNELL] came to him and said that he had been approached by RICHARD BISSELL, who was then the Deputy Director of Plans (DD/P) to undertake an operation in the Congo, one of the objectives of which was the elimination of PATRICE LUMUMBA. HARVEY testified that [O'DONNELL] told him that he had declined to undertake this assignment.

HARVEY testified that he subsequently had a conversation with BISSELL, in which BISSELL stated that he had requested [O'DONNELL] to undertake such an operation. (9-10)

HARVEY testified that he did not know about any steps undertaken on behalf of the Agency in connection with any proposals to eliminate LUMUMBA. (10)

SOURCE: HARVEY TESTIMONY, June 25, 1975 (pp. 9-10)
