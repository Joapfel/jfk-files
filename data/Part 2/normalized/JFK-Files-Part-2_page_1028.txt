RECOMMENDS AWARD OF

UNCLASSIFIED CITATION

Mr. Joseph B. Smith is hereby awarded the Career Intelligence Medal in recognition of exceptional achievement throughout his Agency career. During more than 21 years of service, Mr. Smith, a dedicated intelligence officer, served in a variety of important positions at Headquarters and abroad--consistently demonstrating skill, imagination and enthusiasm. Mr. Smith is a recognized authority in his field. His overall performance and the high quality of his work have contributed significantly to the mission of the Central Intelligence Agency, reflecting credit on him and the Federal service.
