THIS IS UNEVALUATED INFORMATION. SOURCE GRADINGS ARE DEFINITIVE. APPRAISAL OF CONTENT IS TENTATIVE.

1. FIDEL CASTRO CALLED ON A GROUP OF ARMY COMMANDOS ABOUT TWO WEEKS BEFORE THE OUSTER OF ALLIANCE ESCALANTE ON 26 MARCH 1962 AND TOLD THEM THAT THE COMMANDOS WERE TRONG TO TAKE OVER THE GOVERNMENT IN CUBA. THE ARMY OFFICERS CONTACTED BY CASTRO HERE POLANDO CUERIA, JOSE MARIA DE LA ACQUILINA, FELIX GUERRA MATOS, JUAN GIPL, JORGE CORRALA RIVERA, FIDEL CASTRO himself, and FIDEL MATRIM FIESTO. CASTRO ASKED THEM TO BACK HIM UP, AS HE INTENDED TO REGAIN FULL CONTROL OF THE SOCIALIST REVOLUTION, AND THEY AGREED.

2. AT THE TIME OF ESCALANTE'S OUSTER CASTRO ALSO ORDERED THE TRANSFER OF A SOCIALIST LEADER IN MATANZAS PROVINCE TO AN AGRICULTURAL CO-OPERATIVE IN ORIENTE PROVINCE. (FIELD COMMENT: SOURCE COULD NOT PERMISSIVELY THE NAME OF THE LEADER TRANSFERRED).
