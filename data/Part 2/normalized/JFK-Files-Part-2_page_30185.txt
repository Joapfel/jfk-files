APPLICANT

TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

USE BLACK PRINTERS' INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE IN EYEWILL ON TIMING SLAB.

WASH AND乾 FINANCIALS THOROUGHLY.

4. DROP FINGER FROM MAIL TO MAIL, AND A VOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. BE SURE IMPRESSION IS CLEAR AND CORRECT IN ORDER.

6. IF AN AMOUNT OR DEFORMITY BAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO POINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL DIFFERENCE WAS MADE IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A DEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE GROUNDS.

8. EXAMINE THE COMBINED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERPRINTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INFREQUENTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
