It is the Committee's intention to insert in some of the blanks in the documents an identifying description of IRS personnel who assisted the FBI in certain instances, such as "an IRS Special Agent" or "an IRS Inspector". We will make these identifications based upon our notes from the documents, and will supply you with copies of the documents in which such descriptions have been typed in order that you may check the accuracy of the descriptions. In this way, we will avoid public identification of the IRS personnel even though Bureau agreement has previously been obtained that these individuals are not "sources".
