L. LER OSFALD LEE HENRY OSFALD

1 OCT. PROBABLY IDENTICAL BORN-18 OCT 1939, NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA, FORMER RADAR OPERATOR IN UNITED STATES MARINES WHO DEFECTED TO US SS IN OCT 1959. OSFALD IS FIVE FEET TEN INCHES, ONE HUNDRED SIXTY FIVE POUNDS, LIGHT BROWN Wavy HAIR, BLUE EYES.

2. ON 31 OCT 1959 HE ATTEMPTED TO RENOUNCE HIS UNITED STATES CITIZENSHIP TO THE UNITED STATES FIB IN MOSCOR, INDICATING HE HAD APPLIED FOR SOVIET CITIZENSHIP. ON 13 FEB THE US FIB MOSCOR RECEIVED AN UNDATED LETTER FROM OSFALD POSTMARKED Hinsk ON FIVE FEB 1961 IN WHICH SUBJ INDICATED HE DESIRED RETURN OF HIS US PT AS WISHED TO RETURN TO USA IF "THE COULD COME TO SONS AGREEMENT CONCERNING THE DROPPING OF ANY LEGAL PROCEEDINGS AGAINST ME." ON 8 JULY ON HIS OWN INITIATIVE HE APPEARED AT THE FIB WITH HIS WIFE TO SEE ABOUT HIS RETURN TO STATES.
