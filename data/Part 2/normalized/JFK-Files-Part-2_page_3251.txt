In addition to planning for sabotage, WH Divi- sion's anti-Castro activity also included extensive plans for the formation of goon squads throughout much of Latin America in an attempt to counter the pro-Castro elements of the various nations. This goon squad program also was of considerable interest to the Deputy Director for Plans, Mr. Bissell. Be- cause of its areawide nature, the activities of the goon squads were run out of WH/D rather than out of WH/4.
