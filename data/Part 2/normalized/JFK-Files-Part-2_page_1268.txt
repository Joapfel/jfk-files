DECLARATION OF APPOINTEE

This form is to be completed before entrance on duty. Answer all questions. Any false statement in this declaration will be grounds for cancellation of application or dismissal after appointment. Impersonation is a criminal offense and will be prosecuted accordingly.

INSTRUCTIONS TO APPOINTING OFFICER

The appointing officer before whom the following declaration is made is bound to take such action as he deems proper, in his own discretion, under the authority of law, by the Civil Service Act, applicable to all persons employed in the service of the State Government, and is further bound to comply with the requirements of that act.
