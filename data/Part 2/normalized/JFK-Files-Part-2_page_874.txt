Mr. Fox is a thoroughly competent case officer who has been a major asset to the Philippine Desk. He has worked extremely hard with a minimum of supervision. He has not limited himself to his assigned duties, but has interested himself in other aspects of the Desk. In this regard he contributed considerably to the complete reorganization of the desk files.

In carrying out his duties he has been extremely thorough. His analysis of his cases has been excellent. While he writes clearly and meaningfully he still tends to be slow in handling correspondence. He will have to pay extra attention to this in his coming assignment as a Chief of a one-man base.

Mr. Fox is personable intelligent, and mature officer. He is a self starter who should do extremely well in his upcoming assignment.

Mr. Fox has had no supervisory responsibilities during the period under review. He is commendably cost-conscious.
