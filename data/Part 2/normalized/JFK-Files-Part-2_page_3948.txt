Our records show that Richard G. Fecteau was aboard an American plane shot down over Manchuria in November 1952 and that he is serving in Peiping a twenty year sentence on spy charges. Fecteau, an OPC employee, was quoted in the press as saying that he worked for CIA.

F. Parker was not identified. He may possibly be John Floyd Parker, an Agency employee in 1964 who was in Japan in 1952.

There is no pertinent identifiable information about Mrs. fnu Guthrie's. She may be Frances Agnes Guthrie, an Agency employee in 1964.

C. Churchill was not identified. He may be Creighton Churchill, who was in Japan in 1952.

J. Sloss may be John Richard Sloss, an Agency employee in 1964.

E. Leibacher could be identical with Ernst Leibacher who in 1964 was in the Los Angeles field office of the DCS.

J. Davanon may refer to Joseph Frances Davanon, also in the Los Angeles field office at the time.

John Floyd Parker, Frances Guthrie, Ernst Leibacher, Joseph Frances Davanon, and John Richard Sloss all stated to the best of their knowledge they had never met Nagell. It is possible, however, that Nagell knew or knew of Parker, Churchill, and Guthrie through their work in the Far East. As for Leibacher and Davanon, Nagell lived in Los Angeles in 1962.

On 16 April 1964 Nagell sent to the FBI a letter claiming that in September 1963 he had sent to the Bureau a registered letter signed "Jack Kramer". The 1964 letter implied that the 1963 letter had warned the FBI about the impending assassination. The FBI, of course, did not receive such a letter.
