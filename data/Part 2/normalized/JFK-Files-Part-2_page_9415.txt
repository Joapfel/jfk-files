DE 157-3075

C. Funds

Gestetner (phonetic) Corporation, from whom the NCCF purchased printing equipment, have threatened to obtain a court order to confiscate the equipment because it has not been paid for.

DE T-l July 17, 1970

OLIVER MC CLURE, leader of the Detroit NCCF, who is currently in San Francisco, contacted Detroit on August 5, 1970, requesting that he be sent $100,000 plane fare in order for him to return to Detroit as the NCCF is broke after putting up the money for HUEY P. NEWTON's bond.

DE T-l August: 5, 1970

As of August 7, 1970, the Detroit NCCF owed National Headquarters about $3,000.00 for "The Black Panther" newspaper.

DE T-l August 7, 1970

The Detroit NCCF held a picnic at Belle Isle Park, August 30, 1970, but the turnout was poor and the NCCF lost about $200.00.

DE T-l August 31, 1970

The Detroit NCCF receives most of its funds through sale of "The Black Panther" newspaper. They also acquire some money through donations and raids on dope houses. Most of the NCCF money is spent for payment of the newspapers, for food, and housing for the NCCF members. The telephone, when they can afford to pay for it, is also a heavy drain on NCCF finances.
