1. USE BACK OF FORM 6491.

2. DISTRIBUTE THE FORM 6491 ON TRADING SLAB.

4. DOLL FINGERS, FROM MAIL TO MAIL, AND AVOID WELDING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. BE SURE THE DOCUMENTS ARE RECORDED IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN AMOUNT IS NOT PERMANENTLY MADE IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL CONDITION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A WIRE STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CONSTITUENTS.

8. EXAMINE THE COMPLETED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERSPOTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INFREQUENTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
