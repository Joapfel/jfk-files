Re: SGT. DANIEL R. GROTH, ET AL CHICAGO POLICE OFFICERS; FRED A. HAMPTON (DECEASED) ET AL VICTIMS - SUMMARY PUNISHMENT - CR

Specimens received on 2/17/73 and were personally delivered by Special Agent

Q281 Left hand cast of Hampton Q282 Right hand cast of Hampton

Also Submitted: Control sample of paraffin taken from Hampton

Results of examination:

A microscopic examination of Q281 and Q282 revealed no unburned gunpowder fragments.

Specimens Q281 and Q282 were examined by neutron activation analysis for the elements antimony and barium. Antimony and barium are components of most primer mixtures and can be deposited on the hand when a firearm is discharged. From the results of the examination, it could not be determined whether Hampton had discharged a firearm.
