3/20/69

TELETYPE

TO : DIRECTOR, FBI FROM: SAC, CHICAGO AND SAC, DETROIT

BLACK PANTHER PARTY (BPP), RI

CHAIRMAN, CLEVELAND COOK, FIELD SECRETARY (MARSHALL) AND ROBERT SATCHEL, MINISTER OF HEALTH, ALL CHICAGO BPP MEMBERS, DEPARTED CHICAGO, INSTANT, EN ROUTE DETROIT, MICHIGAN. MAY ATTEMPT CONTACT WITH ROBERT WHITE, FIELD SECRETARY (MARSHALL) AND DETROIT CHAPTER, BPP WHO IS IN PRISON. GROUP TRAVELLING IN COOK'S ONK NINE SIX FOUR MARCON PONTIAC, BEARING UNKNOWN SIXTY-NINE ILL. PLATES. ALLEGEDLY HAMPTON AND SATCHEL ARE ARMED.

YVONNE KING, ACTING MINISTER OF LABOR, CHICAGO BPP, AND SATCHEL ALLEGEDLY WERE IN DETROIT OVER WEEKEND THREE FIFTEEN TO SEVENTEEN LAST, STAYED WITH WHITS'S PARENTS. WHITE'S UNCLE IS ALLEGEDLY TED WILLIAMS, HEALTHY EX-HUSBAND OF ARETHA FRANKLIN-NATIONALLY KNOWN NEGRO VOCALIST. HE ALLEGEDLY PROVIDED MONEY FOR KING AND SATCHEL'S FLIGHT BACK TO CHICAGO.
