19. NOSENKO'S Knowledge of [MORELL] and [GARLAND] On page 26 it is stated that NOSENKO's apparent ignorance of KOVSHUK's relationship with [MORELL] and KOSOLAPOV's connection with [GARLAND] refute his contention that he would have known of all KGB recruitment attempts.

COMMENT: During the April 10, 1968, (N-178), interview, NOSENKO stated that KUSKOV had the file on [MORELL] who was the [Economic Advisor] at the Embassy. He advised he never heard anything particular about [MORELL]. With regard to [GARLAND], NOSENKO on April 6, 1968, (N-177), stated KOSOLAPOV had his file; however, NOSENKO insisted that he had no knowledge of KOSOLAPOV's trip to Helsinki in connection with [GARLAND] and he would have known about it if anything important developed. It is noted that [GARLAND] denies any recruitment approach.
