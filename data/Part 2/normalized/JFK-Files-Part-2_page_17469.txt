UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT Memorandum

TO: SAC, CHICAGO

FROM: SA JAMES W. GERBLICK

SUBJECT: RACIAL TENSIONS MAYWOOD, ILLINOIS, 1969 RM

DATE: 6/17/69

Where appropriate, the information has been disseminated to local authorities.

Informant advised on 6/9/69:

The equipment and records from Black Panther Party (BPP) Headquarters, 2350 West Madison, Chicago, were transferred to Chuck's Record Shop on 17th Avenue, south of Madison Street in Maywood for storage. This record shop is run by PETER HAMMACH. These records are what was left after the FBI raid on BPP Headquarters.

Informant made available a flyer captioned "Deputy Chairman FRED taken political prisoner," and a copy of 'The Black Panther" newspaper 6/7/69.

Informant advised on 6/12/69:

JWG
