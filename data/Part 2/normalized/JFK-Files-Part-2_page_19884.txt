FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION COMMUNICATIONS SECTION

MAY 6, 1972

TELETYPE

FROM: WASHINGTON FIELD (100-55966) (C) 3P

CABLELIGHT MARCH TO UNITED STATES CAPITOL, WASHINGTON, D.C., SPONSORED BY DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA CHAPTER OF WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (D.O. NOMAAC), N.Y. SIX, SEVENTY TWO. INTERNAL SECURITY DASH COMMUNIST (TROTSKYIST).

RE: INFO TELETYPE DATED: APRIL TWENTYEIGHT, LAST.

ON MAY SIXTH, INSTANT, A CONFIDENTIAL SOURCE WHO HAS FURNISHED RELIABLE INFORMATION IN THE PAST, ADVISED THAT THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (C.O. NOMACO) HELD A FORUM MEETING AT ST. MARK'S EPISCOPAL CHURCH, THREE AND A STREET, SOUTHEAST, WASHINGTON, D.C. THIS MEETING WAS ATTENDED BY APPROXIMATELY ONE THOUSAND INTERVIEWED AND LAST REC-110 SEVEN WHIREY FROM THE THIRD PM ON MAY SIXTH INSTANT. THE SOURCE STATED THAT THIS FOLLOWING THE MEETING, THE GROUP LEFT THE CHURCH AND PROCEEDED IN A CABLE- LIGHT PROCESSION TO THE EAST STEPS OF THE CAPITOL BUILDING.

6 MAY 12 1972

51 MAY 19 1972

Included in summary to White House and Attorney General. Date 5/8/72 am
