2 May 1961

MEMORANDUM FOR: Mr. Richard N. Goodwin Assistant Special Counsel to the President

SUBJECT: Possible Covert Activities against Cuba

Attached is a brief statement regarding covert operations against Cuba. In view of your familiarity with this subject and our conversations over the past few days, I have not attempted to provide you with much detail.

Richard M. Bissell, Jr. Deputy Director (Plans)
