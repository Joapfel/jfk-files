ON JANUARY FOURTH INSTANT, FIVE SEPARATE AMBIGUOUS ADSPPEARED IN "THE VILLAGE VOICE", A WEEKLY NEWSPAPER IN NYC. RUBY DEE, LINDA MORSE, MRS. BENJAMIN SPOCK AND SUSAN SONTAG APPEARED BY NAME AND PHOTO SEPARATELY IN FOUR OF THE ADS REQUESTING "JOIN ME IN WASHINGTON JANUARY FIFTEENTH," AND INCLUDED IDENTICAL COMMENTS REFERRED TO IN "NEW YORK TIMES" AD OF JANUARY THIRD LAST.

THE FIFTH AD STATED "WE WANT TEN THOUSAND WOMEN IN WASHINGTON JANUARY FIFTEENTH" AND INCLUDED THE FOLLOWING COMMENTS:

"THE BROADEST COALITION OF AMERICAN WOMEN IN HISTORY-WOMEN YOUNG AND OLD, FROM CHURCH, POLITICAL, PEACE AND MINORITY GROUPS, FROM THE ARTS, PROFESSIONS AND PUBLIC AFFAIRS-WILL SPEAK WITH A SINGLE VOICE THE DAY CONGRESS OPENS. WE WOMEN, REPRESENTING FIFTY ONE PERCENT OF THE VOTING POPULATION, WILL DEMAND THAT AS ITS FIRST ORDER OF BUSINESS, CONGRESS WITHDRAW US TROOPS AND ACT TO MAKE A DECENT LIFE FOR OUR TWENTY THREE MILLION BLACK PEOPLE AND ALL CITIZENS.
