JAFFE, born in Russia about 1893, having received derivative citizenship through the naturalization of his father who entered the United States about 1894 and was naturalized at Philadelphia.

There is no information in the files of the Philadelphi a Office which is identifiable with SAMUEL ADAS ON J AFFE or ABRAM S. JAFFE.
