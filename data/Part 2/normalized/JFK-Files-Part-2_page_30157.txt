APPLICANT

TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE PHOTOGRAPHS:

USE BLACK TRANSPARES.

2. DISTRIBUTE THE PICTURES ON LUNG SLAB.

3. BACK AND HOLD PAPER THOROUGHLY.

4. ROLL PHOTOS: FIRST RUN TO AIR, AND SUBSEQUENT RUNS TO SLIP.

5. IF DUE INSURANCE IS NEEDED, MAKE IT IMPOSSIBLE TO DETECT A PAPER THOROUGHLY.

6. IF AS ANNOTATION: ON CERTAIN THOUGHTS, IF NECESSARY, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL PAPER BLOCK.

7. IF SOON MAY BE OF HELM CONSTRUCTION, MAKE IT IMPOSSIBLE TO DETECT PATTERN IN PAPER BLOCKS; SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE ESTIMATED WITH A LINO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CORRESPONDENCE.

8. ENSURE THE CLASSIFIED PRINTS TO SEEM IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED; BEARING IN LIND THAT BEST PAGES ARE PUFF INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS COOK INTERLEavedly and are not shown here).

9. WHO'S CARD FOR USE BY:

1. LAS ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES IN PHOTOGRANTEE APPLICATIONS FOR UNAUTHORIZED USE OF STATE AND LOCAL GOVERNMENTS FOR PURCHASE OF TINT CARBON, LICENSING, AND PERMITS, AS AUTHORIZED BY STATE STATUTE, AND ATTACHMENT BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE VILLAGE OF THE PERMITS. DO NOT USE AS A PUBLIC SERVICE STATE PUBLISHED AD IN CLEAR AND ANDS.

2. USE ONLY TO CHECK IF A PAPER OR LINE IS CLEAR.
