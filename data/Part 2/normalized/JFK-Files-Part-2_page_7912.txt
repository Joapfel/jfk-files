COJGUT: Extracts from Monthly Counterintelligence Reports, OBI, AAC

COMB: DIR OBI, AAC

DATE OF INFORMATION: 1 - 30 April 1956 DATE OF REPORT: 14 May 1956

Extracts of interest taken from the OBI Monthly Counterintelligence Report are as follows:

SECTION B

Further CP member arrives in Alaska. CP member confined in Federal Jail at Anchorage for non-compliance. Activities of possible subversives in fourth. Alaska Labor Defender starts publication again.

1. An aircraft passenger met from the June Daily News revealed APLNW arrived in that city from Anchorage, Washington on 3 March 1955 and departed for Anchorage on 5 March 1955. A later meeting in the same newspaper revealed OBI again flew to Anchorage on 29 March 1955. Was interviewed by the FBI in 1951, OBI confessed being a member of the Kotaisin branch of the Communist Party from 1953 to 1955.

2. Another passenger met in the June News revealed a new AMNWNW departed June for Anchorage on 8 July 1955. Information as this office indicated Mr. D. E. Stock, June News, was to return to Alaska from the 21 coming the last week of March 1955. Further inquiry by this office has disclosed Mr. D. E. Stock is confined in the Federal jail in Anchorage and charged with non-compliance of his former wife. MNDW was released on 15 April 1956 when he was cured to pay the completed costs and to reportedly planning to work in a self-employed contractor in the Anchorage area.

3. The first MNDW was recently reported in newspaper for Anchorage, N.Y., and in the letter's copy of the city Council over a pending contract. As an indication of MNDW's commitment, he has recently represented the following companies and/or individuals in highly controversial cases: Cugon Electric Association, Minnesota Gas Co., Eau Claire Electric Association, Fiski Line, Inc., Cooper Valley Electric Association, and to date none of these cases. In connection with the MNDW case, a local company reported that a Cugon worker started a fund raising activity which was turned to "moral defense fund."
