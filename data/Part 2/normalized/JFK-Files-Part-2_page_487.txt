1. WALLACE B. RAYTON IN MEETING-19 JULY WITH CARLOS JURADO OBTAINED FOLLOWING INFORMATION WHICH UNDOUSTEDLY CAME FROM LILING-1 WHO RETURNED TO MEXICO CITY FROM HAVANA-15 JULY.

2. SITUATION IN VIETNAM GRAVE BECAUSE NORTH VIETNAMESE GOING TO EXECUTE U.S. AIRMAN RECENTLY CAPTURED.

3. ROWTON ELICITED THIS STATEMENT FROM JURADO AFTER JURADO TOLD HIM LILING-1 WAD BROUGHT JURADO MESSAGE FROM ARNOL RODRIGUEZ CAMPS WHO RECENTLY WAS REPLACED AS VICE MINISTER FOREIGN AFFAIRS. MESSAGE TO JURADO FROM RODRIGUEZ VIA LILING-1 WAS TO EFFECT HE REPLACED AND AWAITING REASSIGNMENT BUT EFFECT OF REPLACEMENT NOT SERIOUS. JURADO TOLD ROWTON LILING-1 WAD SAID THERE MUCH ORGANIZATION GOING ON IN CUBA NOW BUT IT NOT SERIOUS; HOWEVER, LILING-1 SAID SITUATION IN VIETNAM WAS SERIOUS BECAUSE NORTH VIETNAMESE WERE GOING SHOOT AMERICAN AIRCRAFT AND AS A CONSEQUENCE THIS WOULD ESCALATE WAR THERE.

4. ROWTON HAS SCHEDULED MEET WITH JURADO EVENING-20 JULY. WILL ATTEND TO LEARN MORE DETAILS AND ESTABLISH CAS FOR SWAP SECRET
