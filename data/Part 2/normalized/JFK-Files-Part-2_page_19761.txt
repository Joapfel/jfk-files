SUBJECT: WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION COALITION WASHINGTON, D.C. EM CO: Washington Field Office

SF 3215-E, a highly confidential source, furnished the following information 11/15/71.

A female individual by the name of BILLY NOBLE (phonetic) of the Women's National Abortion Coalition in Washington, D.C. was in contact with BPP National Headquarters Oakland, California speaking with ERICKA HUGGINS.

NOBLE wanted to know whether or not HUGGINS would be able to come to Washington on 11/20/71, to participate in a demonstration by the captioned group. HUGGINS informed NOBLE that she had not heard as yet from the people she must contact in order to get permission to make such a trip, but she promised to notify NOBLE as soon as she receives some word.
