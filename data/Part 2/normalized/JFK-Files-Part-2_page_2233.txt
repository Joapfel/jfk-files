10 September 1951.

SUBJECT: Request for Appointment, Birch D. O'NEAL

It is requested that the Emergency Section, Personnel Division, be given leave of absence so that he can obtain a foreign service appointment for the following employee:

Name: Birch D. O'NEAL

Station of Assignment: Guatemala City, Guatemala

Foreign Service title requested: Most Secretly

Designee as a replacement for Collin D. Urry, who held the title of Attache at Guatemala City, Guatemala.

Validity date for departure re: 15 October 1951

The Guatemala City station has been without a Chief for six months. The appointment being requested is now well suited to strengthen the staff of our representative.

Attachment(s): BOPs (c)

SECRECY
