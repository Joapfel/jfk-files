Investigation of persons mentioned in this report as being staff members of the "Furies" Newspaper is being conducted and reported through individual cases.

Extensive physical surveillance of the "Furies" location at 219 11th Street, S.E., Washington, D.C., as well as individuals who frequented this address, has been conducted by WFO, particularly during period August-November, 1972.

This surveillance, as well as resulting follow-up investigation, did not reflect any activities of a revolutionary or subversive nature by any member of the "Furies."

As set out in details of this report, a thorough review of all issues of the "Furies" Newspaper did not reflect a radical revolutionary or anti-government theme.

It was noted that the August, 1972 issue of the "Furies" was the last issue published.
