Title: BLACK PANTHER PARTY

Character: EXTREMIST MATTER

Synopsis:

BPP headquarters is located at 2228 Bewick, and a BPP "pad" is located at 4200 Buena Vista, Detroit, Michigan. Detroit BPP Chapter leader is PHILANDERS LONNIE DARDEN, also known as Lonnie Darden, the Minister of Information The BPP operates a Free Breakfast Program and a Free Busing to Prison Program and continues to sell "The Black Panther" newspaper. Identities of leaders and members as well as BPP activities and financial summary set out.
