SUMMARY OF EMPLOYMENT

James A. Noel

From 1931 to 1944 I served as a Foreign Service Officer in various posts in Mexico. In 1944 I joined OSS/SSU/CIG and from that date until 1947, I worked in Europe and Latin America. In 1947 I again joined the Department of State, and served in many posts in Latin America until 1966 when I returned to the United States for a two (2) year detail with the Department of Army. In 1968 I retired from the Department of State and since then I have been living in the San Francisco area doing occasional work on a consultant basis for the Department of Defense.
