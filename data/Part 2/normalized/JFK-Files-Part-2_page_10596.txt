SENSTUDY, 1975.

ON JULY-24, 1975, JOHN F. KEHOE, JR., (FORMER SA), COMMISSIONER OF PUBLIC SAFETY, COMMONWEALTH OF MASS., ADVISED THAT ON JULY-23, 1975, HE HAD RECEIVED A TELEPHONE CALL FROM PAUL WALLACH, WHO STATED HE WAS A STAFF MEMBER OF THE UNITED STATES SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES. WALLACH GAVE HIS TELEPHONE NUMBER AS 202-224-1700. WALLACH ASKED KEHOE IF HE WAS A FORMER SPECIAL AGENT OF FBI AND KEHOE REPLIED IN THE AFFIRMATIVE. WALLACH THEN INQUIRED WHETHER KEHOE HAD BEEN INVOLVED IN ELECTRONIC SURVEILLANCE OR OTHER COVERT INTELLIGENCE GATHERING ACTIVITIES IN NEW YORK CITY IN THE 1960'S.

KEHOE TOLD WALLACH THAT HE HAD BEEN AN SA ASSIGNED TO THE NEW YORK OFFICE IN 1946 AND 1947. WALLACH STATED YOU DIDN'T GO BACK THERE TO DO SOME JOB DURING THE-60'S DID YOU?" KEHOE REPLIED IN THE NEGATIVE. WALLACH THEN
