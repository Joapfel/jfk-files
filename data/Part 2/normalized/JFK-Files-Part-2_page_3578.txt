Egypt

Pamphlets and posters bitterly lampooning Nasir, especially for his involvement in Yemen and Algeria, have appeared on the streets of Cairo and Suez.

Such public expression of anti-Nasir sentiment is highly unusual.

It is still in a very low key, however, and there is no sign of organized opposition.

We do not consider Nasir to be in any immediate danger of overthrow, though he could be forced to modify unpopular policies if the discontent spreads.
