Intensify Psychological Actions.

(1) Create problems for the Castro regime and the subversive programs by an intensified psychological effort emphasizing the hardships and sufferings being endured by the Companions while subversive trainees are given favored treatment.

(2) Publicize the contrast between living conditions of USIS instigators and those of the Cuban scientists.

(3) Increase greatly the amount of VOA broadcast time tailored to Cuba, and intensify emphasis on the degradation and suffering brought on the Cuban people by the Castro subversive effort.

(4) Conduct airborne television broadcasts aimed at the Cuban people and Latin American students in Cuba, dwelling on these themes that Castro's subversive efforts are designed to destroy the tranquility of the Hemisphere.

Intensify Physical Intelligence Gathering Efforts.

(1) By non-attributable means - Cubans and third country nationals + require such more hard intelligence on the nature and scope of subversive training conducted in Cuba and methods of exportation.

Movement of Personnel.

Through multilateral and bilateral political arrangements with Latin American states, seek to expedite the movement of persons into and out of Cuba. (This to be expanded by State.)
