San Francisco, California

January 14, 1968

I, GARY THOMAS RONE, on this date, January 14, 1965, have accepted ten thousand dollars cash ($10,000.00) from the undersigned, representatives of the FBI.

This amount has been accepted by me in full and complete satisfaction of services rendered voluntarily to representatives of the FBI up to and including January 14, 1965.

My acceptance of this amount in fulfillment of the above brings to a close a very pleasant relationship which I have enjoyed with representatives of the FBI, who have on all occasions treated me fairly and with the utmost consideration and I wish to take this opportunity to express my appreciation.

/s/ GARY THOMAS ROME

Witnessed by: JAMES S. CARLILE Special Agent, FBI EDWARD C. LANE Special Agent in Charge, FBI

At San Francisco, California January 14, 1965
