SECURITY OFFICE Investigation Report

Date: 6 January 1945 Number: 12951

Subject: NOEL, James A.

To: Mr. E. M. Terrell.

Investigation directed by: ACT.

Sources of information: S.

Summary of information:

An American born citizen, 32 years old, son of American born parents.

Subject is a high school graduate and has had twelve years experience in the Foreign Service of the United States Government.

Confidential informants recommend the subject as to loyalty, ability and personal character.
