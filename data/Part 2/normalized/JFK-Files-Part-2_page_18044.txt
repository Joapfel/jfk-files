BY: B.P.Melnyk DATE: 10/18/75

On 9/16/75, Paul Wallach, Senate Select Committee Staff Member, advised that he desired to interview SA Homer W. Schweppe of the Washington Field Office concerning his knowledge on mail openings. Wallach requested that SA Schweppe be made available Tuesday, 9/23/75, at 10 a.m.

RECOMMENDATIONS:

(1) That any existing employment agreement be waived for purposes of an interview with SA Schweppe by the Senate Select Committee.

(2) That the Legal Counsel Division advise SA Schweppe of the request for interview.

(3) That the Legal Counsel Division will orally advise the Committee of the whereabouts of SA Schweppe and arrange for the interview.
