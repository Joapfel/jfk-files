Dear Sir:

The Free Breakfast for School Children has covered the country and has been initiated in every city where there is a Black Panther Party and National Committee To Combat Fascism. This program was created because we as black people and human beings understand that our children need a nourishing breakfast every morning so that they can learn.

These breakfasts include every nutrient that they need for the day. For too long our people have gone hungry and without the proper health aids they need. It is beautiful to see our children eat in the morning for it is a fact this enables them to think.

To begin and continue this program it takes donations from concerned people, businessmen, merchants and grocers who draw their profits from the black and oppressed people in Detroit. We need donations of foods which can be stored, such as cereal, grits, sugar, cocoa, salt and pepper, jelly and cooking oil, also syrup.

We need daily and weekly donations of perishable foods such as eggs, milk, oranges, bacon, sausage, ham, butter, bread and cheese, etc..

We need donations of money to buy food and utensils with which to prepare the breakfast.

If you want to help make the Free Breakfast for Children a program that truly satisfies the pressing needs of Black People, donate or offer your services.

POWER TO THE YOUTH

Chuck Holt, Breakfast Co-ordinator Detroit Chapter National Committee to Combat Fascism
