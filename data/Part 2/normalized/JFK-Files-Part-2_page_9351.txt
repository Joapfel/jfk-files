FREE LUNCH PROGRAM and LIBERATION SCHOOL

THE PEOPLE OF DETROIT:

THE DETROIT BRANCH OF THE BLACK PANTHER PARTY IS HAVING A LIBERATION SCHOOL AND CHEON PROGRAM TO BETTER SERVE THE NEEDS OF OUR YOUTH HERE IN THE COMMUNITY OF DETROIT.

LIBERATION SCHOOL PROVIDES FOR OUR YOUTH A FURTHER EDUCATION WHERE AS HELPING

TO IMPROVE THEIR LEARNING ABILITY, CLASSES TAUGHT AT LIBERATION SCHOOL WILL BE: MATH, PROJECTS, HISTORY, IDEOLOGY OF THE BLACK PANTHER PARTY, AND PRIMARY SKILLS, FOR THOSE WHO ARE BETWEEN THE AGES OF 3 TO 11⁄2 YRS. OLD.

IF YOU WOULD LIKE FOR YOUR CHILDREN TO ATTEND LIBERATION SCHOOL BRING OR SEND THEM TO 2435 PENNSYLVANIA.

IONS OF FOOD, SUPPLIES, AND MONEY WILL BE APPRECIATED.
