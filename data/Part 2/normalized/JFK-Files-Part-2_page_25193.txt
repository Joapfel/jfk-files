PERSONS/ENTITIES: DATES: CATEGORIES:

McNamarra Lodge McCone Richardson Smith Oct 3 - 4,1963 DIEM

OCCURRENCE:

On October 3, Lodge wrote a personal letter to McCone stating that "I... agree with what I understand McNamarra said to you. The time has come for a new face, but I also think of Richardson as a devoted and a patriotic American..." On October 4, 1963 in a CIA cable from Saigon, (SAIG1397) the following report appeared:"....it is clear that Lodge wants a change in chief of station....departing Saigon 18 - 20 hours...". In a comment in the IG report it is noted that Richardson's departure was based on a headquarters request that he return to headquarters on TDY as soon as possible. Upon Richardson's departure his deputy David R. Smith, became acting chief of station."

SOURCE: IG Report 1967 (CIA) p. 26
