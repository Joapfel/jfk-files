Q In what manner did you use U.S. companies and businessmen?

A Well, we simply used them as the experts. Sugar is the lifeline of Cuba as far as any exchange, foreign exchange, this type of thing, and it was naturally our Number 1 target. So it was only natural that we would go to the American businessmen.
