CONEIN COLBY, WILLIAM October, 1963 DIEM

OCCURRENCE: COLBY stated that "The CIA officer (CONEIN) received a call on October 30 (1963) to come over and join the general staff and come to the meeting and essentially sat with them as they continued the operation from thereon. They didn't move to the decision to run the coup and he sat in the headquarters during most of the time. He went home at one point, etc."

SOURCE:

Hearings, SSCI, June 20, 1975, Colby, pp. 11-12
