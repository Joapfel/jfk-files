Gen. Lansdale 30 Oct 1962 Operation Mongoose

John McCone

Bundy Bruce Creer (CIA)

OCCURRENCE:

Lansdale said that he had been told by Bundy on Sunday, Oct 28, to hold up action on the twenty teams by submarine for 24 hrs. and Lansdale had promptly notified CIA. Lansdale added that yesterday (Oct 30) upon receipt from Bruce Creer on the subject Lansdale had again taken up w. Bundy, and Bundy had told Lansdale that he would tell McCone to have CIA hold everything. Lansdale opines that he presumes Bundy had so informed McCone.

SOURCE: Memo for the record by Gen. Lansdale dated 30 Oct 1962. (00739)
