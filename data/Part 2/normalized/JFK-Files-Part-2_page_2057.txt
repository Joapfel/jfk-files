FITNESS REPORT

The Fitness Report is an important factor in agency personnel management. It seeks to provide: 1. The agency selection board with information of value when considering the application of an individual for membership in the career service; and 2. A periodic record of job performance as an aid to the effective utilization of personnel.

INSTRUCTIONS
