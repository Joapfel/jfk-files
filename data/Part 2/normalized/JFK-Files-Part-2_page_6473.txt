SUBJECT: Meeting with President on Cuba, 1100 hrs, 19 Dec 1963

1. The following persons were present at the meeting:

White House: The President Mr. McGeorge Bundy Mr. Smith

State: Mr. Thompson Mr. Ball Mr. Johnson Mr. Martin Mr. Crimmins

Defense: Mr. Gilpatric Mr. Vance General Wheeler

CIA: General Carter Mr. FitzGerald Mr. Helms

Treasury: Mr. Dillon

USIA: Mr. Wilson

2. Mr. FitzGerald briefed the President on Agency operations covering the following areas:

Collection of intelligence.

Propaganda activities.

Economic denial actions.

Exploitation of Cuban military disaffection.

Sabotage and harrassment activities.

Support of autonomous anti-Castro groups.
