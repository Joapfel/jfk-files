MEMORANDUM IN LIEU OF FITNESS REPORT

SUBJECT: Birch D. O'NEAL (1 April 1966 - 31 March 1967)

Mr. O'Neal has been Chief of the Special Investigations Group since its inception. As a former FBI employee he brought a wealth of investigative knowledge to the Staff. He continues to handle detailed research and analysis of special complex and sensitive counterintelligence cases in a highly effective manner. He is a competent supervisor and realizes the value of economy.
