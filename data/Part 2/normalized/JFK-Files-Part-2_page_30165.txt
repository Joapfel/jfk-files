3. BACK AND FORWARD COORDINATES PROPERLY.

4. DO NOT USE LESS THAN TWO LINES TO INDICATE AN ANGLE/ALGONIC FIXER TO SUPPLY.

5. IF SUBSEQUENT FIXERS ARE REQUIRED AS IN CORRECT ORDER,

6. IF AN IMPAIRMENT ON GENERAL LEAF IS NOT POSSIBLE TO PRINT A DUNGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGERS BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL CONDITION MAY BE IT IS NECESSARY TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A NEW STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CRITERIA.

8. EXAMINE THE COMPLETED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERPRINTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INFREQUENTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
