The source advised that the SWP-YSA members of WONAAC felt that the conference was a success and that the influence of SWP-YSA members continues to dominate. The source advised that the SWP-YSW women felt that they overcame opposition put forth by PLP and a group calling itself Revolutionary Women, which attached WONAAC as a group led by SWP-YSW who are nothing more than bourgeois rebellers.

Revolutionary Women is self-described as an organization that is addressed to the special oppression of women and has a program that ranks this struggle to the class struggle.

The source stated that opposition proposals submitted in workshops were turned down in favor of those proposals backed by the SWP-YSA women.
