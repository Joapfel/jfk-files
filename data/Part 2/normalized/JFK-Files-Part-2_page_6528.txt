II. Measures to Impede the Spread of Communist Subversion by and Through Cuba

Programs Now in Motion.

The following existing programs are all having some adverse effect on the basic capabilities of the Communists to organize, train, teach, equip, underwrite and export subversive strength into Latin America from Cuba:

Reconnaissance and Surveillance activities, which unite to provide information and to represent a continuing deterrent to subversive movement;

(1) Picket ship patrol of the Windward Passage.

This action contributes to the restriction of Alliegt Cuban sea traffic to Haiti and Dominican Republic area.

(2) Aircraft patrol flights out of Guantánamo.

These flights, augmenting surface surveillance,

keep watch for small craft, fishing and merchant shipping operating in the Caribbean south of Cuba, and waters adjacent to and north of Jamaica.

(3) U.S. Military execution of specific counter-subversive tasks.
