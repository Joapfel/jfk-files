EXPERIENCE

BOE., James Arthur.--b. New York, N. Y., Mar. 19 1911; San Diego (Calif.) High School grad.; bookkeeper, paints and varnishes firms, 1929-30; asst. to traffic agr., chamber of com., 1930; app. clerk, in consulate at Mexico City, Jan. 26, 1931; v.c. at Mexico City Sept. 25, 1935; at Manhattan Oct. 6, 1938; temp. at Houston, Tex., from Oct. 1939 to May 1940; at Guadalajara July 17, 1941; at Bilbao Aug. 15, 1941; at Madrid Dec. 23, 1945; at Barcelona May 1 1946; app. wr. ser. Staff officer of class six and assigned to the Sept. Oct. 27, 1947; asst. at Státscitán Mur. 20, 1927; class four May 2, 1949; asst. at Houston Airs June 17, 1949; married.
