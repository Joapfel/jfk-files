20 February 1947

TO: Control, Special Operations

FROM: R. C. Lddy Acting Chief, CIJ

VIA: Chief, Special Operations

SUBJECT: Request for Designation of Birch D. O'Connell.

1. Will you kindly request the State Department to designate Birch D. O'Connell as an Attache, American Embassy, Caracas, Venezuela.

2. The Caracas office has not previously been in operation under CIG and will be opened upon the arrival of Mr. O'Connell. Intelligence work is, at the present time, being carried on in the Caracas embassy by the Federal Bureau of Investigation, whose representative in that city carries the title of Legal Attaché.

3. The FBI has no approximates to its personnel stationed at this post.

4. R. O'Connell served from January 1948 to May 1949 as a Social Agent of the Federal Bureau of Investigation. In 1942 he spent six months in Ecuador, 1943-1945 was with the FBI in nine months in Colombia, and in 1948-1949 he spent sixteen months in Mexico. He was Civil Attaché in the American embassy, Mexico City.

5. R. O'Connell now located in Washington where he is completing arrangements for the opening of the Caracas station.

6. To designation has been removed him. Subject will perform the duties previous; carried out by the Legal Attaché and will be the running representative of CIO, reporting directly to Washington.

7. Subject's action will be comparable to the present FBI representative, who is assigned an official attaché to the embassy.

8. This designation does not involve a replacement or an increase in personnel but concerns the opening of a new CIO office.

9. In order to enable subject to operate securely, it is believed essential that he be given dibs. His status must be issued a diplomatic passport in the capacity of Attaché.

Sade, J. E. J. 1911
