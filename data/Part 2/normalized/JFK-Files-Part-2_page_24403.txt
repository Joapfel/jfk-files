"it was certainly evident to me that he was able to accomplish things in Las Vegas when nobody else seemed to get the same kind of attention" (Maheu, 7/29, p. 60).

O'Connell had previously met Roselli at Maheu's home (Maheu, 7/29, p. 8). O'Connell and Maheu each claimed that the other had raised the idea of using Roselli, and Maheu said O'Connell was aware that Roselli had contacts with the gambling syndicate (Maheu, 7/29, p. 8; O'Connell, pp. 15-16).

Maheu was at first reluctant to become involved in the operation because it might interfere with his relationship with his new client, Howard Hughes. He finally agreed because he felt that he owed the Agency a commitment (O'Connell, pp. 12-13, 103). O'Connell recalled that Maheu was to approach Roselli using a cover story that he represented business firms suffering heavy financial losses caused by Castro's actions (O'Connell, p. 16).
