MEETING-1 AUG WITH LUIS FERRE PRODUCED FOLL:

A. FERRE HAD CONVERSATION 25 JULY WITH FRANK CHAVEZ AFL-CIO LEADER IN PUERTO RICO. CHAVEZ PROPOSED FERRE GO DOM REP ACT AS CATALYST BETWEEN CONFLICTING FORCES AND USHER IN DEMOCRATIC GOVERNMENT ARGUED THAT MAN FERRE'S STATURE COULD SERVE IN PLACE OAS COMMISSION. FERRE FAVORABLY INCLINED IN PRINCIPLE. FERRE IN CONTACT CARLOS CHARDON, WELL KNOWN PUERTO RICAN AGRONOMIST WHO NOW IN DOMREP AT INVITATION BALAGUER CONNECTION AGRARIAN REFORMS. CHARDON FAVORS IDEA FERRE MEDIATION. (COMMENT: FERRE REQUESTED NOTHING; YAUGER VOLUNTERRED NOTHING. FOR HAS INFO YAUGER EXPECTS TALK NEXT WITH FERRE & AUG).

B. CHAVEZ HAD JUST RETURNED FROM SEEING ARTURO ESPIALLAT IN CANADA. AS LONG TIME FRIEND ESPIALLAT, CHAVEZ CLAIMS CREDIT FOR GETTING RAMFIS TRUJILLO RELEASE ESPALLAT. CHAVEZ ALSO FRIENDLY WITH RAMFIS WHO, ACCORDING CHAVEZ FAVORS DEMOCRATIZATION DOMREP BUT WANTS LEAVE COUNTRY, LIQUIDATING FAMILY ASSETS AT TEN CENTS ON THE DOLLAR. ASSETS IN DOMREP ESTIMATED AT 800 MILLION DOLLARS. FERRE COMMENTED THAT IN LONG RUN IT WOULD BE CHEAPER FOR DOMINICANS
