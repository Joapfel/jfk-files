ON AUG. TWENTYFIVE, INSTANT, SAS OF THE FBI OBSERVED APPROXIMATELY FIFTY INDIVIDUALS ASSEMBLE AT NOON IN PUBLIC SQUARE, CLEVELAND, OHIO. OF THIS GROUP, APPROXIMATELY SEVENTYFIVE PERCENT WERE FEMALES AND THE REMAINING TWENTYFIVE PERCENT WERE MALES. SEVERAL INDIVIDUALS CARRIED PLACARDS WITH SLOGANS, "RATIFY THE EQUAL RIGHTS AMENDMENT," ABOLISH LAWS THAT MURDER WOMEN - REPEAL ALL ANTI-ABORTION LAWS." THE ASSEMBLED GROUP, SOME OF WHOM WERE CURIOSITY SEEKERS AND UNAFFILIATED PEOPLE ON THEIR LUNCH HOUR, HEARD SPEAKERS DELIVER SPEECHES, PRINCIPALLY ON REPEALING ALL EXISTING ANTI-ABORTION LAWS AND WOMEN'S END PAGE ONE
