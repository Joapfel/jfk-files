8 Total—Add lines 7(a), (b), and (c)

9 Line 6 less line 8

11 Total—Add lines 10(a) and (b).

12 Investment credit—Amount from line 5 or line 11, whichever is lesser (enter here and on line 50, Form 1040; line 6(b), Schedule J, Form 1120; or the appropriate line on other returns).
