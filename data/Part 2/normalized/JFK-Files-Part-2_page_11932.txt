Above information should be submitted separate and apart from correspondence setting forth derogatory information, and should be addressed to attention of Administrative Division, Voucher Unit. 1. Submit annual letter to Bureau including following:

Symbol numbers of all informants and confidential sources being paid under Bureau authority (not SAC authority) for services or expenses. If confidential source has no symbol number, list name.

Symbol numbers of all informants not paid under Bureau authority

Comment concerning present informant coverage of office, together with information concerning actions being taken to improve coverage

List under "Headquarters City" and each resident agency all subversive organizations broken down into each division, such as clubs, committees, and chapters.
