In respect to Item G (c), there are enclosed, communications (see Exhibits A through X) disclosing the authorization of the above-described misur.

As previously set forth, a misur was installed on at numerous FBI files in an effort to locate an FBI Headquarters (FBIHQ) communication disclosing an authorization to place this misur. However, the search was unsuccessful. There was located, however, a Radiogram dated from the (see Exhibit F) revealing that a misur was installed which in effect acknowledged that Bureau instructions were carried out regarding this misur coverage and indicates that Bureau authority was obtained.

In regard to the matter involving the authorization of misur, the procedures in effect at the time most of these misur were in operation did not require specific written authority from the Attorney General. At that time, authority for FBI misur was maintained at FBIHQ and authorization for utilizing a misur was made by the FBI Director or his designees based on a memorandum from Attorney General Herbert Brownell dated May 20, 1954, which recognized FBI authority to institute such surveillances. (see Exhibit V). Subsequent to March 30, 1965, the aforementioned procedure was changed wherein the FBI, in requesting authority for the installation of a misur, in each instance forwarded such request to the Attorney General for his approval or disapproval. (see Exhibit X). Other exhibits enclosed with this memorandum and pertaining to the approval of misur by FBI officials contain the initials of the late Director J. Edgar Hoover or his designees to make such approvals.
