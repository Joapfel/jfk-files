Project ZRRIFLE

1. Identification: The purpose of Project ZRRIFLE is to spot, develop, and use foreign agent assets for Division D special operations. Agents will be spotted in several areas, including the United States, but will be posted for operational security reasons will probably not be used in their countries of residence. Present developmental activity is being conducted in the WE and EE areas, but it is anticipated that this will be extended to other divisional areas. The project will be operated against third-country installations and personnel.

2. Objective: The objective of this project is the procurement of a code which can be used in accordance with requirements levied on the Intelligence Services, primarily by the National Security Agency. Since these requirements are subject to frequent revision, no listing of targets would be valid for the duration of the project. Specific operations will be mounted on the basis of need and opportunity. The project will be conducted by Division D with assistance from area divisions and stations as needed.
