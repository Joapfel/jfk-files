APPLICANT

TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PAPER OR INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE ONLY ON INMOBILE SLAB.

3. WASH AND DRY HANDLY THROUGHLY.

4. ROLL FINGER THOROUGHLY OVER ALL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. BE SURE IMPRESSION IS MADE CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN AMOUNT IS LACKING, OR QUALITY MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT END. IF THE MEMORIAL PINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL IMPRESSION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE GETTEN WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CIRCUMSTANCES.

8. EXAMPLE THE CORRECTED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERPRINTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR FREQUENTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
