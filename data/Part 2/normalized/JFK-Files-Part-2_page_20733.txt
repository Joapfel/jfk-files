A CONFIDENTIAL SOURCE ADVISED INSTANT LATE SECOND SESSION OF THIRD WNAAC CONVINED NINE AM AT HUNTER COLLEGE NYC. SOURCE STATED MORNING SESSION NINE ELEVEN AM INCLUDED ORGANIZATIONAL WORKSHOPS NOT COVERED AT SEVEN FIFTEEN SESSION SUCH AS I "PRESS WORK", "FUND RAISING", AND "STRINGTHINING OF LOCAL COALITIONS".
