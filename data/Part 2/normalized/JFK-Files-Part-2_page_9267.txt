David Millard A BLACK PANTHER FREE BUSING PROGRAM TO PRISONERS

WITH THE MAJORITY OF THIS COUNTRY'S CARS AND OTHER MOTOR VEHICLES PEAKING MANUFACTURED IN DETROIT, MANY PEOPLE IN THIS STATE OF MICHIGAN STILL CAN'T GET TO THE PRISONS TO VISIT THEIR LOVED ONES.

REALIZING END-LESS FOR FELONY FORMATION, THE BLACK PANTHER PARTY ENTERED A FREE BUSING PROGRAM FOR FAMILIES AND FRIENDS OF PRISONERS. IN MANY OF OUR CHAPTERS AND BRANCHES, SUCH PROGRAMS ARE UNDERWAY, SERVING THE COMMUNITY.

WE (THE BLACK PANTHER PARTY) WOULD LIKE TO CONTINUE TO SERVE YOU ON A CONSECUTIVE BASIS TO THE PRISON. ON SUNDAY JUNE-4, 1972 THE BUS WILL BE LEAVING FOR THOSE WHO ARE VISITING REGULAR INMATES, AND ON SUNDAY JUNE-18, 1972 THE NEXT TRIP ARE FOR THOSE WHO ARE VISITING TRUSTEE (ONLY).

THE BUS WILL BE LEAVING AT 9:00 A.M. AND WILL RETURN BY 6:00 P.M. BT IS IMPORTANT THAT YOU ARRIVE AT 157 COLLINGWOOD AT 8:00 A.M. FOR EARLY SANDING ARRANGEMENTS.

NECESSARY ITEMS OR INFORMATION NEEDED TO VISIT INMATES IN JACKSON 8113 PENITENTIARY ARE:

(1) INMATES NAME AND I.D. NUMBER.

(2) WHETHER H2 IS AN TRUSTEE OR NOT.

(3) YOUR NAME, ADDRESS, ZIPCODE AND PHONE NUMBER.

(4) YOU MUST BE ON INNATE'S VISITING LIST. IF NOT, WRITE INNATE ON CALL-867-7742 OR 43 TO OBTAIN METHODS OF GETTING ON INNATE'S VISITING LIST.

(5) YOUR RELATIONSHIP TO THE INMATE.

MAKE YOUR RESERVATION TODAY, AS SEATS ARE LIMITED, FOR FURTHER INFORMATION.

CONTACT:

BLACK PANTHER & A.J. MINISTRY OF INFORMATION 157 COLLINGWOOD
