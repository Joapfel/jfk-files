NY 65-15026

In addition to information set forth in enclosed letterhead memorandum, "at least advised that CBS is making a motion picture film of MIKOVAN'S visit to the US which will be given to MIKOVAN. This is an effort on the part of CBS to restore itself to the good graces of the Soviets." It is noted that CBS no longer has any representation in Morocco since their correspondent was forced to leave Morocco at the request of the Soviets.

JAFFE told this to BARKOVSKY and asked BARKOVSKY if he could arrange an interview for him with MIKOVAN. BARKOVSKY told JAFFE that it would be impossible for him to arrange an interview for JAFFE with MIKOVAN, as much as he represented CBS.

BARKOVSKY said that he would, however, endeavor to obtain an invitation for JAFFE to a reception which may be given for MIKOVAN. BARKOVSKY also asked JAFFE when CBS was going to do something to restore themselves in the eyes of the Soviets. JAFFE, other than telling BARKOVSKY about the film being made of MIKOVAN's visits to the US, knew of no other effort being made by CBS along these lines.

Concerning JAFFE's desire to interview MIKOVAN, and the invitation which he would try to get for JAFFE to a reception which might be given for MIKOVAN, BARKOVSKY said that the decision would be made by a press conference.
