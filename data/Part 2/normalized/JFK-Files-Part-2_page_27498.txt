EDUCATION

August 1972 to May 1975 GEORGETOWN UNIVERSITY LAW CENTER, WASHINGTON, D.C. J.D. received in May 1975. Graduated in top third of class. General courses of study pursued, with emphasis on litigation, constitutional law and antitrust. Appellate litigation clinical seminar: Only student to argue three cases in D.C. Circuit, including United States v. Agurs, 510 F.2d 1249, reh. en banc denied, 520 F.2d 82 (1975), rev'd., 96 S. Ct. 2392 (1976). Also participated in writing United States v.
