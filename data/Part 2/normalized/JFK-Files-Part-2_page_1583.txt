Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO : Chief, Cover Personnel Division FROM : Chief, Personnel Security Branch SUBJECT: WHEELER, Robert Prentiss - 6/16/52

DATE: 8 May 1952

Reference is made to a memorandum dated 1 May 1952 from Lloyd George, Chief, FE, which is as follows.

*1. Full security clearance was issued 11 March 1952 for Subject's employment in the field as an I.O. (C) assigned to the Japan Composite Unit.

2. Our field representative advises that Mr. Wheeler is still on duty with the Security Division of O-2, FEC; however, he will be available for assignment to the Japan Composite Unit on or about 15 June 1952.

3. It is therefore requested that Subject's security clearance be extended for a period of sixty days."

This is to advise that the security clearance granted 11 March 1952 is extended to 30 June 1952.
