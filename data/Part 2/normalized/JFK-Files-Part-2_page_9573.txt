V. EVIDENCE OF NATIONAL UNITY

New York received 51,199 copies of "The Black Panther" newspaper from National Distribution, San Francisco, California, and in turn forwarded 1,700 copies to Agusta, Georgia, and 4,800 copies to Detroit.
