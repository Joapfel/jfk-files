(Priority) WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WONAAC) SPONSORED DEMONSTRATION NOVEMBER 20, 1971, WASHINGTON, D.C. IS - NEW LEFT aor3 near 2 S/H/WS

RE WFO airtels 11-2-71 and 11-5-71.

On 11-9-71, HO 1349-S and HO 1342-S advised that at a regular weekly meeting of the Houston Socialist Workers Party (SWP) Chapter, discussion was had regarding the forthcoming Women's Abortion demonstration to be held in Washington, D.C. November 20, 1971.

A show of hands was requested as to who was planning to attend this demonstration activity and approximately ten persons raised their hands, indicating they planned to attend. Of the individuals who indicated that they planned to attend, none could be considered well-known personalities and they are merely regular SWP members and their boyfriends.

No informants of the Houston Division plan to attend the demonstration activity and the method of travel of the Houston contingent at the present time is unknown.
