FREE BUSING TO PRISON PROGRAM

ONE OF THE PURPOSES OF THE NATIONAL COMMITTEE'S TO COMBAT FASCISM TO IMPLEMENT PROGRAMS AND SERVICES THAT ARE FREE AND BENEFICIAL THE BLACK COMMUNITY. THE INMATES THERE ARE JUST ALLOWED THREE VISITS A MONTH AND ARE SUBJECTED TO DEHUMAN- ING LIVING CONDITIONS. BLACK PEOPLE ARE BEING JUDGED BY A JURY OF RACIST WHITES AND BLACKS OF A DIFFERENT PEER GROUP THAT HAS NO UNDERSTANDING OF THE AVERAGE MAN FROM THE BLACK COMMUNITY. WE'RE ASKING ALL CONCERNED ORGANIZATIONS, GROUPS, SOCIAL CLUBS, CHAIRCHES AND INDIVIDUALS TO CONTRIBUTE TO THIS PROGRAM.
