The Attorney General

Director, FBI

UNITED STATES SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES

Reference is made to the request of the Senate Select Committee (SSC) dated August 26, 1975, for FBI materials. Included in this request, Part I, Number 6a through r, was a request for extensive material relating to the discontinued Counterintelligence Programs (Cointelpros) of this Bureau.

Attached for your approval and forwarding to the SSC is an original of a memorandum which responds to the request of the SSC.

A copy of this memorandum is being furnished for your records.
