**PARKING INSTRUCTIONS**

**20 am:** Train departs from Pennsylvania Station, New York. Coffee and snacks will be available on the train; please eat your lunches *before* the train arrives in Washington. Sandwiches available for purchase on return train from Washington.

There will be a Marshall, identifiable by her blue arm-band, on each car of the special train, to answer questions, etc. She will collect $1.00 from each woman for bus fare in Washington, which includes the ride from the Capitol to the Shoreham Hotel, and from the hotel to Union Station in time to catch the train back to Newark and New York.

**11:30 am:** Assemble at Union Station Depot and proceed **IN SILENCE** to Capitol behind state-name signs which will be available at the station in Washington.

AT THE CAPITOL, we have requested that a delegation of 50 present our petition to House Speaker McCormack and Senate Majority Leader Mansfield. We are still negotiating for that plan. We will be orderly, peaceable, law-abiding in our demonstration.

AFTER THIS DEMONSTRATION, proceed to chartered buses at Supreme Court Parking Area. We will take us to Hotel Shoreham, 2500 Calvert Street, N.W. (about a half-mile ride).

**3:00 - 6:00 pm:** WOMEN'S CONGRESS at the Hotel Shoreham. The program includes speaking from all parts of the Coalition of Individuals, who will analyze women's role in ending the war and ending our domestic neglect. The Congress will be launching ground for community action proposals.

**5:15 pm:** Philadelphia woman only leave the Shoreham by bus for their train, which leaves from Union Station at 6:00 pm.

**6:00 pm:** For people returning to Union Station only, buses leave Hotel Shoreham for New York and Newark leaves Union Station at 7:10 pm.

**8:30 pm:** Workshops at Hotel Shoreham for those staying over.

**Tuesday, January 16:** Plans will be detailed Monday night for Tuesday's activities around lobbying Congress.

The Jeannette Rankin Brigade

January 15, 1968
