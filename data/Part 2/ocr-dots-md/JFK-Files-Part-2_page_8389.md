UNITED STATES INFORMATION AGENCY
WASHINGTON

July 20, 1957

Dear Mr. Dimitroff:

I very greatly appreciated your thoughtful letter of July 17. I shall certainly take occasion to watch the television program to which you refer at my next opportunity.

Please accept my best wishes.

Mr. Dim. Ad. Dimitroff
1236 S. Menlo Avenue
Los Angeles 6, California
