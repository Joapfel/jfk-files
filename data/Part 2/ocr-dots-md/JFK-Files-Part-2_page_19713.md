SOURCE - CID, Hartford, Conn., PD, advised on 10/24/72, that there is no record for BERSON and that the name of HYMAN BERSON and his wife, SYLVIA, are unknown to the Intelligence Division.

On 11/20/72, INFORMANT advised that HYMAN and SYLVIA BERSON are unknown to source. INFORMANT was contacted on 12/13/72, also with negative results.
