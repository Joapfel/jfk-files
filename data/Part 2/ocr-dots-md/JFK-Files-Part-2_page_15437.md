The Attorney General

In view of the decision of the Department of State we are unable to implement this program further with regard to the countries of Central and South America and in view of your interest in developments in that area it was felt you would like to know about the situation.

1 - Mr. Byron R. White
Deputy Attorney General

Note:

Classified "Secret" since unauthorized disclosure of our objectives in this program could result in severe damage to our foreign relations.

This letter has been written pursuant to memo, 10-6-61, Donahoe to Sullivan re Intelligence Coverage of Friendly Foreign Countries. The word "Friendly" has been omitted from the caption of this letter since it does not accurately apply to all of the countries involved in this program.
