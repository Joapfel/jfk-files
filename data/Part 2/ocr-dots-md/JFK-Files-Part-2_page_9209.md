Title: BLACK PANTHER PARTY

Character: EXTREMIST MATTER

Synopsis: BPP headquarters formerly located at 2228 Bewick and 10438 McNichols, Detroit. The office on Bewick closed during month of November, 1973, and office at 10438 McNichols closed February, 1974, both facilities vacated. BPP leader PHILANDER LONNIE DARDEN and majority of active members relocated to Oakland California to assist the National Headquarters in that city. All community programs and related activities terminated. Identities of leaders, active members, relocating members set out. Activities conducted and financial status set :out.
