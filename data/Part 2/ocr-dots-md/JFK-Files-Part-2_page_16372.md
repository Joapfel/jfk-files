On Monday May 12 the National Office of SDS was raided by Chicago pigs and fireman who broke through the front door and attacked staff people and SDS members. Five were arrested: Mike Klonsky - national Secretary, Tim McCarthy, Ed Jennings, Les Coleman, Dave Slavin. They were charged with absurd charges, ranging from "battery on an officer" to "interfering with a fireman" to "inciting mob action." Bail was set at $12,000. In addition the five were restricted from leaving the state of Illinois.
