INFORMATION CONCERNING
(INTERNAL SECURITY)

The January 16, 1968, edition of the "Courier-Journal", a Louisville, Kentucky, newspaper of general circulation, reflected an article that reported that Jeannette Rankin and some 5,000 other women marched near the United States Capitol in Washington, D.C. on January 15, 1968. The article further reflected the group was known as the Jeannette Rankin Brigade and that the purpose of the march was to protest the Vietnam war.

It was indicated therein that the marchers were "met by a seemingly disinterested Washington and a reconvening 90th Congress that, not unexpectedly, paid them little heed."

Miss Jeannette Rankin, described in the article as the march leader and first woman member of congress, and thirteen others presented a petition to two congressional leaders. One Sarah Burnside, Richmond, Kentucky, is depicted as a member of this group and a delegate from Appalachia.
