Though it is still in the developing stages, it undoubtedly will reach the required level of action through continuous struggle. The remaining road is still a long one, but it befits us to pause and analyze the future prospects of the Revolution and to shed some light upon its state since the June War.

It is evident that the Revolution today is passing through a new phase in which two opposite forces are active:

First: Escalating of commando activity which has qualitatively changed the nature of the confrontation and created a new spirit of commitment and unity among the Palestinian people.

Second: The counter-revolutionary forces attempting to destroy our movement and abort our Revolution by limiting our actions and forbidding us from acting in two critical fields: confronting the enemy, which when blocked prevents our commando activity from evolving into a full-scale war of national liberation; and, interacting with the Arab masses in a revolutionary manner whose inevitable effects, the counter-revolutionary forces fear.

These two opposite orientations point directly to the responsibility of the Palestinians and the Arab citizens. They are to oppose all attempts to impede the Revolution by following clear systematic and organized action aiming at the escalation of the armed struggle to the point where all aborting attempts will be fruitless.

1. It is noticeable that after each catastrophe we have suffered starting with 1948 and ending with 1967, the Arab nation found itself subjected to tremendous political pressures in which the international aspect completely overshadowed the Arab aspect, in general, and the Palestinian aspect, in particular. These pressures inevitably assert the new "fait accompli" for Israel and further legitimize Israeli military gains by giving them international sanctions and protection. The pressures following the June War, however, are of a special and more dangerous nature, since they aim at completely liquidating the Palestinian issue under the guise of "political settlement" or so-called "peaceful settlement." Those efforts revolve around three main points: returning to the pre-June 5th situation in exchange for ending the state of belligerency, ensuring safe frontiers for Israel along with other international guarantees, and destroying the identity of the Palestinian struggle by creating what they call a "frail political society," or the "dummy Palestinian identity."

2. If we can say that the partition resolution of 1947 opened the dossier of Zionist usurpation of Palestine, then it is equally correct to infer that the projects for "political settlement" aim at closing that same dossier permanently. Likewise, as the 1947 partition resolution gave the Zionists a political entity upon our land, the present efforts aim at completing the past efforts by legitimizing all the demands of that entity on one hand, and putting, on the other hand, Palestinians and the Arab people in the impotent position of being unable to undertake or to justify any future action for liberation.
