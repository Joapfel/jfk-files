# Julie Andrews Signed for Hitchcock Film

NEW YORK - Alfred Hitchcock signed Julie Andrews to star opposite Paul Newman in his forthcoming spy tale, "Torn Curtain," to be released by Universal Pictures. Mimi Matthews will report to the director on completion of her role in Hawaii."

Torn Curtain" will be Hitchcock's sixth picture and his first under his recently signed three-picture deal with Universal. He plans to begin production Oct. 1, with exterior sequences to be filmed on foreign locations and the interior shot at Universal City Studios.

**AG Pact**

(Continued from page 1)

The fee formula sets seven base categories against which payments are computed. For an actor who acted one day in a picture, the base $100; two days, $150; three days, $200; four days, $250; five days or week, $300; more than one week not more than two weeks, $400; more than two weeks, $500.

For the first TV release of a film, actors will receive 30 per cent of base. When the producer's grosses $125,000, players will receive additional 12½ per cent; at $200,- an additional 25 per cent; at $200, an additional 22½ per cent; $400,000, an additional 22½ per cent; $500,000, an additional 20 per cent; for each $100,000 above $100, an additional 10 per cent.

When a film is released on a major work in prime time, players are offered $70 per cent of the base.
