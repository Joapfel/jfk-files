SUBJECT: Meeting with DCS Headquarters' Officers

Shirley STETSON, for at least ten years the backbone of DCS/OSS and recently reassigned to DCS/Washington, and John WILSON, recently designated as Jim DURBIN's replacement in Raleigh-Durham-Chapel Hill Office which covers North and South Carolina, visited CI/SO on 9 July 1970 and were briefed by CIA officer and the writer from CIA officer 1100 to 1200 hours. This was followed by lunch (could not accompany) and discussions until 1315 hours.
