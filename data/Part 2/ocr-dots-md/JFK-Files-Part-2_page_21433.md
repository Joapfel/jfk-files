Letter to Director, FBI

RE: INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE TO SUPPORT PALESTINIAN PEOPLE'S ARMED STRUGGLE (ICSP)

02/26-28/69

Our French source stated further that these individuals reportedly were about to depart for Communist China. We have alerted the RGPN to our interest in any additional information concerning such travel and they have agreed to advise us expeditiously. In this connection it should be noted that we have received similar comments from the Direction de la Surveillance du Territoire (DST), French domestic counterespionage service, but during a recent discussion with a representative of the DST, he advised that the statements relative to possible travel to Communist China were nothing more than speculation on the part of a DST employee. We will continue to keep the Bureau promptly advised of further developments.
