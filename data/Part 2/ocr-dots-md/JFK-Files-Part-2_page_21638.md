Mr. Schwarz. Now, that purports to be from LTG Bennett
to Colonel Wimert, and has the text in it which you sent on
to Colonel Wimert.

Mr. Phillips. Yes.

Mr. Schwarz. And what is your best recollection as to
who handed you that document?

Mr. Phillips. I cannot say definitely who did that.
There were many occasions when Bill Broe would walk into the
office and say, here is something to send. There were others
which would come in a manila envelope with a yellow
forwarding slip on it. There are times, I am sure, when I
would go into Bill Broe's office, and he would say, by the
way, send this.

I simply cannot recall the circumstances in which some-
one handed me that and said, send it to Chile. And I was paying
less attention to those things, because I was in effect the
postman for people doing other things.

Mr. Schwarz. Do you have any recollection at all
about this document?

Mr. Phillips. No, I am afraid I don't.

Mr. Schwarz. What is your reading of the signature on
that document?

Mr. PHillips. Well, it certainly looks like the sig-
nature which was on the other document which was said to be
Philpott's. But I am not an authority on these things.
