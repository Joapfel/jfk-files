11:26 PM URGENT 1-14-68 DHV
TO DIRECTOR AND WFO
FROM CINCINNATI (100-17137)

DEMONSTRATION PROTESTING UNITED STATES POLICY IN VIETNAM, WASHINGTON,
D. C., JANUARY EIGHT, NINETEEN SIXTYEIGHT, INFORMATION CONCERNING. (IS).

INSTANT DATE A CONFIDENTIAL SOURCE WHO HAS FURNISHED RELIABLE
INFORMATION IN THE PAST ADVISED THAT APPROXIMATELY FORTY WOMEN DEPARTED
YELLOWSPRINGS, OHIO BY BUS, NINE PM THIS DATE FOR CAPTIONED DEMONSTRAT-
ION, WASHINGTON, D. C., JANUARY FIFTEEN NEXT. THE BUS WHICH RE-
SEMBLES A GRAYHOUND TYPE BUS WAS CHARTERED FROM THE SOUTHEASTERN
BUS LINES, INC., JAMESTOWN, OHIO.

SOURCE ADVISED THAT ALTHOUGH IT WAS SNOWING AND ROAD CONDITIONS
HAZZARDOUS THE WOMEN WERE DETERMINED TO REACH WASHINGTON, D. C., FOR
CAPTIONED DEMONSTRATION.

ADMINISTRATIVE:

RECITEL JANUARY THIRTEEN, LAST.

SOURCE IS CHIEF JAMES A MCKEE, POLICE DEPARTMENT
YELLOWSPRINGS, OHIO.

END
