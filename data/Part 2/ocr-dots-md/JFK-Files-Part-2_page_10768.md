PH 52-7165 Sub S

BARRY JAMES MUSI

As previously noted, NUSI was apprehended by Special Agents of the FBI on August 22, 1971, in the courtyard of St. Paul's Church, 422 Market Street, Camden, N. J. On the same date he was afforded a hearing before U. S. Magistrate CHARLES L. RUDD, Camden, N. J., and remanded to the custody of the U. S. Marshal in lieu of $5,000 good bail. He was released on September 7, 1971, after posting ten per cent cash or security on a $5,000 bond which had been set by the Third Circuit Court of Appeals, Philadelphia, Pa., on September 3, 1971.
