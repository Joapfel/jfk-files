To: Interview of Retired Special Agent (SA)
by Senator Walter F. Nondale and
Staff Members of the Senate Select
Committee (SSC)

upon arrival in Washington, D. C., he reported to Inspector
John Dotis of the Legal Counsel Division and Paul V. Daly of the
FBI at which time he was advised of what obligations he had to
the Attorney General as a former employee of the FBI to protect
information obtained by virtue of his former employment in the
FBI. Subsequent to Devlin's meeting with Mr. Dotis and Mr. Daly,
he spent the remainder of the morning with SA William O. Cregar.
Nondale desired to know specifically what Devlin and Cregar dis-
cussed. Devlin advised that other than discussing mutual
friendships in the FBI, Cregar advised of what Cregar's role
would be in accompanying him to the interview and specifically
that Devlin could feel free to ask for the opportunity to con-
duct with Cregar during the interview if the line of inquiry
could possibly reveal the identity of sources, sensitive methods
or investigative techniques, information derived from third
agencies, or information which could adversely affect sensitive
ongoing investigations.

NOTE:

Original and one copy to the Attorney General (AG) for forwarding the copy of the memorandum to James A. Wilderotter, Associate Counsel to the President. The information contained herein was orally furnished to SA W. O. Cregar by former SA Devlin following his interview of May 19, 1975.
