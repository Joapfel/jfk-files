Initial Contact with: Mr. John S. Annarell, Jr., Vice President
Wackenbut Services Inc.
3200 Ponce de Leon Blvd.
Coral Gables, Florida
[Phone: 445-1481]

1. On 27 Nov 67 Gus C. Guerrero met with Mr. Annarell, Vice President for Wackenbut Services Inc., with offices at 3290 Ponce de Leon Blvd., Coral Gables, Florida.

2. Mr. Annarell, by his position as Vice President, is cognizant of all contractual services in being with the company in foreign countries. Even though he does very limited traveling, members of his staff and Mr. George Wackenbut, President, do a considerable amount of traveling.

3. Mr. Annarell will make arrangements for Guerrero to meet with Mr. Wackenbut and other members of the corporation when he feels will be able to assist us.

4. The Wackenbut corporation provides various types of security services to private industry and government agencies. They are attempting to establish investigative and guard service agencies in several Latin American countries and are well established in Colombia at the present time.

5. Mr. Annarell is most cooperative and even though he feels that some of the corporation security projects are privileged information he also feels that members of the corporation will be able, at times, to provide useful information.

JULIEN F. GLEICHAUP
