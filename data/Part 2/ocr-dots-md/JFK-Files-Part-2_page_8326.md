TO: WASH G INFO PARI G CITE ATHE G

QKSTAIR

1. DO YOU KNOW OF DREAMER POLITICAL COMMITTEE CALLED (1) PURPOFTED TO BE FORMED IN ZRMETAL? HAVE NO DETAILS. POSSIBLY GLANTZ SPLINTER?

2. NEW SUBJECT: POSSIBILITY WE WILL SOON PUT LYLE O. KELLY ON ICE. REASON: SMALL SUCCESS HAS GOED TO HEAD AND IS OUT-GLANTZING GLANTZ ON FUNDS AND DICTATORIAL APPROACH. THEREBY BEGINNING TO ALIENATE FOLLOWERS. ALSO POSSIBLE INVOLVEMENT IN SUBJECT OF WASH 33606 (OUT 9/2/64). OKINSANE WOULD CONTINUE UNDER MORE MATURITY AND ABLE LEADERSHIP OF (2) WHO IS PRESENTLY COMMITTEE MEMBER. SHOWDOWN NOT YET OCCURVED THEFEFORE THIS-1S FYI ONLY UNTIL FURTHER INFO FROM US. IF WE DO REORGANIZE KELLY, APE CONSIDERING POSSIBILITY BRINING (3) FROM HBFAIRY TO ADD HIS TALENTS TO COMMITTEE. ANY COMMENT FROM HBJOINT ON THIS?

3. NEW SUBJECT: FYI (4) OF 19 MARCH PRINTS LONG ARTICLE ON KMSCHEME. QUOTES SOME TEXTS. WE HAD BELIEVED JAMMED-OUT.
