Convert to MarkdownThe following was the Agenda for the conference as originally prepared by the June 27 Working Committee:

Saturday, July 15

9:00-10:00 Registration
10:00-11:30 Plenary Session: Greetings and organization of conference
11:30-1:30 Workshops I: General discussion of major action proposals
1:30-2:30 Lunch
2:30-4:00 Workshops II (Project Workshops): anti-abortion attacks and the Catholic Church, petition campaigns and the Abortion Rights Act, court actions, tribunal, referenda, defense cases, legislative action, forced sterilization, research and education, elections, referral and clinics, August 26, workshops on states facing similar abortion situations
4:00-5:30 Workshops III (Constituency Workshops): campus, Black, Raza, high school, gay, Catholic women
5:30-7:30 Dinner, with provision for statewide or regional meetings over dinner
7:30-10:00 Plenary Session

Sunday, July 16

9:00-11:00 Workshops IV (Organizational Workshops): press, fundraising, building and strengthening local coalitions
11:00-3:00 Plenary Session: further discussion and vote on major action proposals

During the Saturday session a proposal was made that WONAC organize a "National Tribunal" on abortion, contraception and forced sterilization to be held in NYC this Fall.
