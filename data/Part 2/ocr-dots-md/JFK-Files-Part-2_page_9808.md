Reference is made to SSC letter dated January 7, 1976, requesting certain materials from the FBI.

Enclosed for your approval and forwarding to the SSC is the original of a memorandum which constitutes this Bureau's response to the request for material set forth in paragraph two of referenced letter.

A copy of this memorandum is being furnished for your records, along with a set of the materials which is to be delivered to the SSC with the memorandum.

Attention is invited to a number of references in the materials to Mr. John T. Elliff, Director, Domestic Intelligence Task Force, SSC. Because of these references, it is our intention that when the materials are to be delivered to the SSC, we will invite Mr. Elliff to review the materials at FBI Headquarters and respect his desires as to whether or not any of the references should be excised prior to actual delivery of the materials. This procedure will thus insure the privacy considerations relative to Mr. Elliff.
