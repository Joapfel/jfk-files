PAUL BERWARD COUMING

As previously noted, COUMING was apprehended by
Special Agents of the FBI on August 22, 1971, inside the
Post Office Building, 401 Market Street, Camden, N. J. On
the same date he was afforded a hearing before U. S. Magistrate
CHARLES L. RUDD, Camden, N. J., and was remanded to the custody
of the U. S. Marshal in lieu of $25,000 good bail. He was
released on September 7, 1971, after posting ten per cent
cash or security of a $10,000 bond which had been set by the
Third Circuit Court of Appeals, Philadelphia, Pa., on Septem-
ber 3, 1971.
