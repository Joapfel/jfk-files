DATE: JUN 1 1954

On 4/9/54 advised that SAM JAFFE, Judson 6-1212, Ext. 120 spoke to TARASOV and called him by his first name, IVAN. JAFFE stated it has been a long time since he spoke to TARASOV. According to informant, JAFFE told TARASOV, "We are still working on that China thing and we are dealing with Konstantin Fedoseev in Washington". JAFFE mentioned to TARASOV he wanted to get hold of "Red Star" magazine, March issue, which contains a story JAFFE wanted to see. TARASOV said he would look it up and call JAFFE. (S)

It is noted that IVAN V. TARASOV is the Second Secretary of the Soviet Mission to the UN. (U)

It is to be noted that Judson 6-1212 is listed to Time, Incorporated, 9 Rockefeller Plaza, NYC. (U)

On 5/14/54 Miss (identity to be protected), at Time, Incorporated, 9 Rockefeller Plaza, NYC furnished the following information regarding SAM A. JAFFE, the only individual with that name presently employed. JAFFE is presently employed as a Culture Researcher for "Life" magazine and has previously been in most sections of research for that magazine.
