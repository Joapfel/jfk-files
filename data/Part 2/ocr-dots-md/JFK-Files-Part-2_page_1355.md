17 AUG 1959

**MEMORANDUM FOR:** Deputy Director (Plans)

**SUBJECT:** Appointment of Mr. Robert Prentiss Wheeler as Deputy Chief of Station, Tokyo, Japan

1. The appointment of Mr. Robert Prentiss Wheeler as Deputy Chief of Station, Tokyo, Japan, effective on or about 15 August 1959 is recommended. Mr. Wheeler will replace Mr. William E. Nelson who is scheduled to return to Headquarters approximately 2 September 1959 for reassignment.

2. Mr. Wheeler has been an employee of the Agency since 28 September 1952 and is currently assigned as an Intelligence Officer, GS-15. A biographic data sheet, including information regarding his Agency experience and training, is attached.

The recommendation in paragraph 1 is approved:

25 AUG 1959
