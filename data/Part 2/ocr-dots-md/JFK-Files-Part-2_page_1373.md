# Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO : DI Career Service Board

FROM : Chief, FE

SUBJECT: Mr. Robert P. WHEELER

ATTACHMENT; FJBA-7751

DATE: 19 December 1956

1. Mr. Robert P. WHEELER, I.O., FI, OS-14, will complete his overseas tour (as extended) with the Japan Base, Tokyo, in June 1956. Subject will return to the U. S. for Home Leave and requests that he be permitted to return to the Japan Base for a new two year tour of duty upon completion of TDY at Headquarters for orientation and training.

2. Mr. WHEELER is Chief of Liaison for the Japan Base, Tokyo. He will resume this position upon his return to his official station (refer to Form 202 dated 9 November 1955 for subject).

3. Subject's request has the concurrence of the Chief, Japan Base, and this Division.

APPROVED:

DI Career Board
