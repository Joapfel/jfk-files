10 DEC 81

MEMORANDUM FOR: Mr. William Kent
VIA : Chief, Western Hemisphere Division
SUBJECT : Personnel Evaluation Panel Service

1. The Chairman of the Nonofficial Cover Board has reported to me on your recent service on the NOC Board Sub-Panel for the FY 1974 Personnel Evaluation of officers at the GS-12 level.

2. I take this means to thank you for this important service and to express my hope that it was as valuable an experience for you as it has been for many of your colleagues in the past. The continuing and unstinting contributions being made by persons such as you are helping the Directorate and the Agency not just in improved personnel evaluation but also in markedly enhancing our use of our most valuable asset.

William E. Nelson
Deputy Director for Operations
