Those who accused us before, now view us as heroes who have given back to this Arab nation its confidence and its hope for victory at a time when it was sunk in the shame of defeat."

I said: "Some explain the accusations against you as a result of not clarifying your ideological identity... " Abu Amar moved and said: "This is the root of the matter. Let us first define our terms. What is meant by ideological identity? Is it to declare to people my belief in Marxism? Is this what I have to do at this stage? Everybody inquires about the social content of our movement and our revolution. Is this the time for defining the social content? Are we not, still, at a stage of national liberation? How do you expect me, then, to deprive many classes of the Palestinian people of their right to participate in the struggle for liberation?"

I said, arguing: "Let us define, at the same time, the forces we are fighting. The enemy is Israel, Zionism, and the imperialist powers that support it..."

Abu Amar interrupted me: "Usually people describe Israel as a fort guarding imperialist interests in the Arab World ... I prefer to compare Israel to the East India Company."

I then continued: "The battle then is not only against the state of Israel, but against all imperialist presence on Arab soil. Thus it is not a battle for Palestinians alone, but for all Arabs. Israel now occupies, in addition to Palestine, Egyptian and Syrian territories, and its expansionist designs include Lebanon, East Jordan, and parts of Iraq."

Abu Amar said: "That is true. But I am a Palestinian first, and as a Palestinian I am a refugee, homeless, without a country or a land. Is it expected of me to wait and slumber until the Arab revolution explodes? I have to struggle to regain my country. Others, too, need to struggle. The Syrian people must struggle to liberate their occupied territories, the Egyptian people must fight to regain Sinai. As for me, my battle is here, on Palestine's land and for it." I said: "But you do not exist in isolation. You move within specific conditions imposed upon you by the imperialist presence."

Abu Amar continued, as he pointed to his Soviet machine gun: "For me, as a Palestinian, I made the choice and chose to face the Imperialist presence...with the gun alone I confront it." --"You are free in your choice, but you will certainly clash with the present Arab 'establishments'."

Abu Amar said seriously: "Yes, the clash did, in fact, occur. Before June 5th, we were accused of aiming at involving Arab governments in a war for which they were not prepared. It was said that, as agents of CENTO, we were conspiring to embarrass the progressive governments and preparing for their overthrow. After June 5th, we were told, 'If you fire one bullet we will consider you criminals and traitors... your action now would be irresponsible, and will lead to the destruction of the Arab nation, so calm down and stop your play... or else.' " Abu Amar smiled and went on: "If our movement was not genuine and was not expressing the consciousness and will of our people, we would be hanged in a number of Arab states for high treason." He added: "When they (the governments) saw the Israeli alarm about our Movement and the Arab popular support we received because we refused to accept defeat and surrender, they rallied to us."
