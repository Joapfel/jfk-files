On 7 April 1967, Cecil Tighe, SRS, wrote Wiley a letter which confirmed Paul Gaynor's oral instructions given Wiley in Washington on 29 March 1967. Wiley's instructions were as follows:

Limit your activities to:

1. Spotting contacts
