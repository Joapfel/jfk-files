Reference is made to the letter from the Chairman of the SSC to the Attorney General dated August 28, 1975, requesting delivery of previously-requested FBI materials pertaining to Martin Luther King, Jr. Reference is also made to prior FBI communications which have, on a continuing basis, effected delivery to the SSC of materials concerning King and responsive to SSC requests dated July 8 and 14, 1975.

The purpose of this memorandum is to effect
delivery to the SSC of information and materials relating
to the July 8 and 14, 1975, SSC requests, and specifically
to the following items in those requests:

July 8, 1975: Items 2, 6, 8, 15, and 20

July 14, 1975: Items 19 and 20

In respect to Item 2, a through f, of the July 8,
1975, request, effort has been made to divide the material
being delivered into specific categories a through f. However,
in some instances material overlaps and information in one
communication may actually apply to more than one category.
