The information contained in this document is confidential and may not be used without the written permission of the sender. This matter has been disclosed to me by a relative of the recipient. This letter is not intended for distribution outside the United States.

I did not receive this document until about 7:00 AM on Sunday, October 28, 1975. It was sent to me by mail delivery personnel at my home address. The postmark indicated that it had been delivered to me on Saturday, October 27, 1975. Therefore, I must have been somewhere else when the post office arrived with the envelope.

The only way I could contact you would be through the U.S. Post Office system, which would mean that your letter was delivered to me on Saturday or Sunday. However, if it had been delivered earlier, I should have received it by now. If it had been delivered later, I should have received it on Sunday or Monday. In either case, I should have received it on Friday. This means that the only way I could have received this letter would be from the post office, which is impossible. Therefore, I cannot accept any responsibility for its contents. My letter has been opened and read by someone other than the addressee.
