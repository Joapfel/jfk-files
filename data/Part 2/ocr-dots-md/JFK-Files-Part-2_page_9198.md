A check of the records of the Honolulu Police Department under File Numbers G83259, G82369 and G82301, reflected that Michael Halsey Brown, born September 11, 1942, Montgomery, Alabama, was arrested on April 25, 1957, for burglary and released that date to Juvenile Crime Prevention. Those files reflect Brown, then a resident of 2648B Rock Road, was a 14-year-old juvenile attending St. Louis High School. He had been arrested along with Robert Vincent Fletcher of 1839 Keller Road who was 13 years old and attending the University High School. Police files reflect that on about April 13 or 14, 1957, forced entry had been made into the university snack bar. Cones, icecream, coin wrappers, and possibly coins were taken. On the same date the RCTC Armory and Supply Room were entered through a forced entry. Apparently nothing was taken from the Supply Room but two .22 rifles valued at $60 were taken from the Armory. The rifles were found before police were notified - one on the rifle range and one in the RCTC garage. On April 20 - 22, 1957, the RCTC Supply Room was again entered. Fletcher and Brown, when questioned by the police, admitted the above burglaries and the fact they had taken some $6.95 in cash, $5 in school supplies and $10 worth of RCTC equipment. They also admitted entering a campus service station by taking screws off door hinges on April 14, 1957, and taking some $5 in tools.

Police File under Number K40001 notes a notice of AWOL of Michael Halsey Brown, Army Serial Number RA25434648, Social Security Number 576-40-3284. The notice reflects that Brown was AWOL on August 6, 1962, from Company E, First ABG, 501st Infantry, Fort Campbell, Kentucky. The AWOL notice listed Brown's father as Robert Arthur Sylvester Brown, whereabouts unknown; mother as Dorothy Snodgrass Brown, 2865 Oahu Avenue, Honolulu.
