MAIL INTERCEPT PROGRAMS

Reference is made to your letter dated July 22, 1975, captioned "Criminal Division Investigation of CIA Mail Intercept Programs," and to your letter received August 1, 1975, captioned as above.

A compilation of documents in the possession of Federal Bureau of Investigation Headquarters pertaining to the mail interception program operated by the Central Intelligence Agency known by that Agency as Project Hunter has been made. In accordance with your request these documents are available for your review. It is requested that you contact Special Agent James P. Lee, on extension 4562, at FBI Headquarters to arrange for this review.
