TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PRINTER'S INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE IN ENVELOPE OR INKING SLAB.

3. MARK AND DENT FINES THOROUGHLY.

4. ROLL FINGERS AS FURTHER TO HAIL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. BE SURE INK IS APPLIED AND INCREASED IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN AUTHENTICATION (AUTHORIT) MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION OF THAT RESULT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER CLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL CONDITION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT ONE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CIRCUMSTANCES.

8. ENRAIN THE CAREFUL PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERPRINTS GO INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INCORPORALLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
