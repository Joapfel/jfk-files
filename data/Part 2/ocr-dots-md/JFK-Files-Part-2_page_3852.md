15 January 1963

**SUBJECT:** BRADLEY, Edgar Eugene
(see also BRADLEY, Leslie Norman)

**ALLEGATION:**

Edgar Eugene Bradley, field representative for an Eastern radio evangelist, Dr. Carl McINTIRE, was arrested on 29 Dec 1967 on a charge by GARRISON of being a conspirator in a plot to assassinate President KENNEDY.

**SOURCE:**

News media articles for
20, 24, 26, 28 Dec 1967
and 3 Jun 68.

**OS INDICATIONS:**

**GIA CONNECTION:**

None. Personnel records reveal there was an applicant by the same name as Subject but the file was destroyed in Dec 1966 as it was 2 yrs old. Subject's employer, Carl McINTIRE was approved for contact use in May 1954 but according to Contacts Office, he was never used by them as he was reported to be unreliable.

**CS INDICATORS:**

Negative for Subject.

His employer, Carl McINTIRE, was approved (caution) for contact use on 22 November 1954. However, according to Domestic Contacts Office, he was not used by them as he was reported to be unreliable. His file reflects that he is an "anti-American Rightist." He spoke on "How Communism is Using the Churches" in Hollywood, California on 14 December 1954. On 30 April 1954 an article in the New York Herald Tribune quoted him as saying "the seriousness of Communism in the Churchess is far greater than we realize". TIME magazine for 16 May 1954 identified him as the founder and head of the small American Council of Christian Churches which it described as "the farthest tip" of the right, while one of American Protestantism. The LUTHERAN magazine for 30 December 1954 stated that the revised Standard Version of the Bible has been adopted as part of incessant attack by Carl McINTIRE and his "Twentieth Century Reformation Hour" broadcasts.

**DETAILS:**

Subject is alleged that his arrest by GARRISON is a case of mistaken identity. A New YORK magazine for 8 January 1963 reported that it seemed possible indeed that GARRISON had him mixed up with two other men. "(One is a sensitive pilot named BRADLEY who was once jelled in Cuba for anti-Castro activities.) The other, an ex-cmn who used the alias BRADLEY and who changed his name and address several times before being connected on a bad-check charge in Dallas on 22 Dec. 1967.)"
