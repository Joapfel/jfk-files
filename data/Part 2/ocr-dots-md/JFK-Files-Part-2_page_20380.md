January 16, 1968:

RE: DEMONSTRATIONS PROTESTING UNITED STATES POLICY IN VIETNAM, WASHINGTON, D. C., JANUARY 15, 1968

Reference is made to St. Louis memorandum of January 11, 1968.

Source Two advised on January 13, 1968, that BERNICE WERMMEYER of the St. Louis Peace Information Center, 6217 Delmar Boulevard, said that the St. Louis Delegation going to Washington, D. C. to take part in the Jeannette Rankin demonstration would number about twelve women. They would travel by Trans-World Airline. They had reservations on the 6:25 P. M. flight departing St. Louis on January 13, 1968. They planned to demonstrate and try to see members of Congress on January 15, 1968. On January 16, 1968, they would attend a Women's Congress and return to St. Louis, Tuesday evening, January 16, 1968. They had reserved three hotel rooms in Washington, D. C., near the White House.

Source One advised January 15, 1968, that there had been eleven reservations made by women leaving St. Louis on the 6:25 P. M. TWA flight for Washington, D. C., and returning on January 15, 1968. Only nine reservations, however, were used. It was apparent that all of the reservations were related to each other.
