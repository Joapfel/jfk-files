It was decided that a national march on Washington, D. C. (WDC), with a simultaneous march on San Francisco, California, on November 20, 1971, in a public show of support for abortion law repeal, would be the first step in launching their campaign on a national scale.

(WF T-1, 7/21/71)

## C. Headquarters

WONAAC opened a National Office at 917 15th Street, N.W., WDC, during September, 1971, which was transferred to 150 5th Avenue, New York City, New York, on December 20, 1971.

(WF T-1, 2/14/72)

JOANNA MISNIK, Treasurer, WONAAC, leased Room 843, 150 5th Avenue, New York, New York, on December 14, 1971, at an annual rental of $5,700.

(WF T-2, 2/18/72)
