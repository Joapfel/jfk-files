21 April 1966

**MEMORANDUM FOR:** Director of Finance

**VIA:** Chief, Contract Personnel Division

**SUBJECT:** Joyce H. FINCH (p) - Tax Assessment

1. A covert Federal income tax percentage of two and eight-tenths (02.8) per cent of FINCH's gross, taxable Agency entitlements has been approved by the Cover Tax Committee. This percentage assessment is effective 1 December 1965, the beginning date of tax year 1965.

2. The retroactive application of this assessment to tax year 1965 will result in a dollar assessment of $84.00 (02.8% of $3,000.00). After applying the 1965 covert tax withholding of $540.00, a refund of $456.00 will be due FINCH for 1965. This eliminates the requirement for a covert tax return for 1965.

3. An acknowledged letter of instruction will be forwarded at a later date.

JOHN F. PATION
Secretary
Cover Tax Committee
