DEMONSTRATION PROTESTING UNITED STATES
POLICY IN VIETNAM, WASHINGTON, D. C.

JANUARY 8, 1968

Detective C. F. SMITH, Intelligence Squad, Richmond,
Virginia, Police Department, advised that he had received
information that ICHIRO HASEGAWA, who is known to be active
in peace movement groups in the Richmond, Virginia, area was
planning to attend the demonstration sponsored by the Jeannette
Rankin Brigade originally scheduled for January 8th but now
planned for January 15, 1968.

SMITH stated that his information indicated that HASEGAWA planned to travel to Washington, D. C., to participate in that demonstration with other unknown individuals. He stated that he has no information concerning HASEGAWA's mode of transportation to Washington nor her participation in the demonstration. SMITH also has no information concerning the number of individuals planning to attend the demonstration with her.

A confidential source advised on September 8, 1965, that the publication "Four Lights," described as the official publication of the Woman's International League for Peace and Freedom (WILPF) in a supplement to its July 1965 issue, listed the nominees for the National Board of the WILPF. Included in this listing was the following name - MARII HASEGAWA, also known as ICHIRO HASEGAWA. Within the framework of the WILPF, her activities were listed as follows:

* President - New Jersey State Branch - 1956 - 1960
* National Membership Chairman - 1960 - 1965
* Member of the National Board and Vice President - 1962 - 1965
* President Burlington County, New Jersey, Branch - 1961 - 1965
