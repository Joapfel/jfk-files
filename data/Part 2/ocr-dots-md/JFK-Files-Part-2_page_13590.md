At 11:44 a.m. on June 5, 1975, Daniel M. Armstrong, III, telephonically advised me that he is scheduled to be interviewed at 10:00 a.m. on June 6, 1975, in Room 308 of the New Senate Office Building by Mark Gitenstein. He requested a Bureau representative to be available for consultation during the interview. I told Mr. Armstrong that I would make appropriate arrangements and that the individual who would be available would contact him to make necessary arrangements prior to the interview.
