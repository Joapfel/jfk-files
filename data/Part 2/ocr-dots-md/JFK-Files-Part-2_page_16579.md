SUMMARY PUNISHMENT - CIVIL RIGHTS

Synopsis:

By letter dated 12/11/69, from AAG JERRIS LEONARD, Civil Rights Division, a preliminary investigation was requested to determine whether violations of 18, USC, Sections 242 and 241 are involved in the shooting of FRED A. HAMPTON and MARK CLARK by the Cook County States Attorney's Police raid on the Black Panther Party (BPP) apartment, 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago, on 12/1/69. SAC M. W. JOHNSON personally advised USA THOMAS A. FORAN, Superintendent of Chicago Police Department, JAMES D. CONELISK, and Cook County States Attorney EDWARD V. HANRAHAN, of the requested investigation. HANRAHAN stipulated that all 11 officers in the raid would be made available for interview provided an official from his office was present and that a copy of the interview was also available. SAC M. W. JOHNSON also contacted Attorney WARREN WOLPSON representing victim BRENDA HARRIS, who was to consult with other victims attorneys and to advise if they will make their clients available for interview. SAC M.W. JOHNSON also contacted DENNIS CUNNINGHAM, Attorney for BPP, who desired an attorney be present during FBI examination of EFP apartment. On 12/17/69, CUNNINGHAM advised SAC M.M. JOHNSON that "it is politically impossible for the EFP to voluntarily cooperate" with any FBI investigator. AAG JERRIS LEONARD in Chicago on 12/18/69, advised he would personally handle FGJ inquiry into this matter. On 12/22/69, FBI examination commenced on the premises at 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago, and FGJ commenced receiving evidence in this case.
