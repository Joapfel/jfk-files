# Demonstration Protesting United States
Policy in Vietnam,
Washington, D.C.

January 8, 1968

The aforementioned Vel-Phillips, according to public source information, in addition to being Alderman and Milwaukee Common Council Member, is a member of the Milwaukee Youth Council (MYC), National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP). The MYC has been active in daily marches and demonstrations in Milwaukee, Wisconsin since August 28, 1967 in an effort to obtain open housing legislation in Milwaukee.

The "Wisconsin State Journal", local daily newspaper, Madison, Wisconsin, in its November 17, 1967 issue described Mrs. Lee Zeldin, age 38, 5705 Forsythia Place as a "Vietnam Protestor". The "Capital Times", in an article contained in its issue October 19, 1967 identified Lee Zeldin as a registered lobbyist for the Committee for Direct Action, described as an anti-Vietnam War group. Lee Zeldin is also said in this article to have been active in distributions of anti-War pamphlets.
