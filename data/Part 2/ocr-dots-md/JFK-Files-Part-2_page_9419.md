ZAYED SHAKUR, BPP New York and JOHN HENRY BOWMAN, BPP Philadelphia, came to Detroit to join with MICHAEL D. HILL, leader Detroit NCCF, on October 6, 1970, to record a television show for "Black Talk", a weekly television show which appears on Channel 7, Detroit, every Sunday at 3:00 p.m., with issues of interest to the black community. This show was televised on October 18, 1970, and participants primarily discussed and expressed their versions of the raids on BPP Offices in Philadelphia and New Orleans.
