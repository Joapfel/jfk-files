The Attorney General

To date, Congress has refused to appropriate funds for the
operation of the Subversive Activities Control Board (SACE).

During a current study of FBI jurisdiction by this Bureau, the questions have arisen: has the authority to designate organizations been taken from the Attorney General and given to the SACE by Executive Order 11605, or is this authority concurrent; and at this time, what are the responsibilities and authority of the FBI under Executive Order 10450, as amended, and Executive Order 11605?

Would you please provide me with a statement of the Department's
current position on these questions.
