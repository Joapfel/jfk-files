19 December 1966

MEMORANDUM FOR: Mr. James S. Woods

THROUGH : Head of Career Service

SUBJECT : Notification of Designation as a Participant in the CIA Retirement and Disability System

1. I have determined that you meet the criteria outlined in HR 20-50 for designation as a participant in the CIA Retirement and Disability System. Your designation as a participant was made effective 18 December 1966.

2. You are hereby notified of your right to appeal this action to the Director of Central Intelligence as specified in HR 20-50. Such appeal must be received in the Office of the Director not later than 30 calendar days from the date of this memorandum.

Emmett D. Echols
Director of Personnel
