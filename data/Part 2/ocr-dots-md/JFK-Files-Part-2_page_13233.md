RE: INTERVIEW OF RETIRED FBI SPECIAL AGENT (SA) BY SSC STAFF MEMBER

Set out below is the verbatim text of a statement voluntarily furnished to the FBI by Mr. Alan G. Sentinella who served as an SA of the FBI from July 12, 1948, until his retirement March 22, 1971. He is presently employed as Director of Safety, Georgia State University, Atlanta, Georgia.
