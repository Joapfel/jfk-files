b. Multinational Counterinsurgency Force.

Explore the feasibility of organizing an OAS Counterinsurgency Brigade for employment throughout Latin America to combat the effects of subversion.

Such a proposal, made at this time, would exhibit the serious determination of the U.S. to support vigorously an anti-subversion program.

c. Training and Orientation.

Provide for a greatly expanded out-of-country training and orientation program for key segments of each Latin American country's population such as:

(1) Enlisted and officer military personnel to be scheduled in Panamá and/or the United States.

(2) Police personnel to be scheduled in Panamá or the United States.

(3) Newspaper personnel to be scheduled within the United States.

(4) Nurses (male and female) to be trained in the United States.

(5) Civilian technicians to be trained in United States training schools.

The thrust of such an expanded training program, taking place in the United States, is not just to enhance the skill and education level of the people involved but, even more, to expose the Latinos to US influence, to show them our country, to persuade them by close association that our view is correct, and this to stiffen them to resist subversion in any form.
