26 MAY 66

25489

1. LIRING-3 PURCHASED CUBANA TICKET FOR CUBA 26 MAY, WITH MONEY PROVIDED BY ROWTON. WILL DEPART 27 MAY. SIGNED TRUE NAME RECEIPT FOR FUNDS. PLANS STAY WEEK. HAS CONTACTED FRIENDS AMONG CUBAN ARTISTS WITH WHOM WILL LIVE WHILE ON ISLAND.

2. LIRING-3 TOLD ROWTON HE MET ARNOL RODRIGUEZ CAMPS IN 1962 WHILE LIRING-3 EMPLOYED WITH CUBAN MINISTRY EDUCATION. ACTUALLY WIFE RODRIGUEZ WORKED UNDER LIRING-3 AND FRIENDLY ASSOCIATION BEGAN THROUGH WIFE. RODRIGUEZ THEN WORKING FOR MINISTRY LABOR.

3. QUICK ACTION ON LIRING-3 VISA BROUGHT QUERIES FROM CUBAN CONSUL MEXICO CITY ABELARDO CURBELO PADRON AS TO WHOM LIRING-3 KNEW IN CUBA. CUPBEL SAID EVEN FOR CUBANS IT TAKES WEEK. LIRING-3 TOLD ROWTON HE ANSWERED ONLY THAT HE HAD FRIENDS CUBA. ROWTON GAVE LIRING-3 MONEY WITH WHICH HE BUYING GIFTS MEXICO FOR RODRIGUEZ AND FAMILY.
