### III. Resources Designed to Enhance the Capabilities of the Latin American States to Resist Communist Subversion

#### 1. Programs now in being:

In appraising the programs which are now in progress it is necessary to reflect that, extensive though they are, many have not yet accelerated to their full thrust and, taken all together, they have certainly not fortified our allies to the point where subversion can be said to be under full control.
