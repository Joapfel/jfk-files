1. FURTHER DEBRIEFING LIRING-4 PRODUCED FOLLOWING:

A. SHE LONG TIME FRIEND CLARIBEL (GUILFRIDO), NIECE PRESIDENT DORTICOS WHO WORKED AT CUBAN PRESIDENCY IN 1964 WHEN LIRING-4 LAST SAW HER. GUILFRIDO WHO BORN IN LIRING-4 HOME TOWN IN ORIENTE ABOUT 1938 IS ANTI-CASTRO. LIRING-4 FELT GUILFRIDO COULD PROBABLY HELP HER OBTAIN USEFUL JOB IF STILL IN CUBA AND MIGHT EVEN AGREE COOPERATE AGAINST REGIME. GUILFRIDO RECENTLY GOT MARRIED AND MOVED BUT LIRING-4 FELT COULD FIND HER.

B. LIRING-4KNOWS LOLO DE LA (TORRIENTE), ELDERLY CUBAN NEWSPAPER WOMAN WHO DISAFFECTED WITH CURRENT REGIME. LIRING-3 WHO ALSO KNOWS WOMAN TOLD ROWTON THAT IN EARLY 1967 MEXICAN LEFTIST PAINTER RAUL (ANGUIANO) CONFIED TO HIM HE RECEIVED LETTER FROM DE LA TORRIENTE WHO CLOSE FRIEND ASKING HIM HELP HER OBTAIN MEXICAN VISA IN ORDER LEAVE CUBA SINCE SITUATION THERE "INTOLERABLE."
