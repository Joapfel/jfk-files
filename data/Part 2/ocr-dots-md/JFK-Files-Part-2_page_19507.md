On January 15 American women will assemble in Washington, D. C. With Jeannette Rankin* leading the Brigade, they will converge on Congress as it reconvenes. They will petition for a redress of their grievances and will demand that Congress end the war in Vietnam and turn its power to healing our "sick society".

# This is "Woman Power"!

In support of these women and their demands, Southern California Women will assemble for appropriate ceremonies, marching andhofleting in Los Angeles on Monday, January 15, 1968:

9:30 A.M., on the steps of the Old State Bldg., 217 West 1st St.

11:30 A.M., City Hall, 200 North Spring St.

1:00 P.M., Board of Education, 450 North Grand

(please, wear black if possible)

# "Turn Woman Power to Peace Power"

* Jeannette Rankin, Montana, U.S.A., is the first woman elected to Congress. A consistent, dedicated voice for a world without war, she says, "...Women are the ones who must be concerned with the needs and development of the human race." Her Brigade is your chance to demonstrate your concern!
