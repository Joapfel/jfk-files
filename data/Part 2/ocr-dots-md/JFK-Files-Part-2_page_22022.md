**OCCURRENCE:** Memo: "I reported to higher authority the substance of Mr. HARVEY'S briefing of the SPECIAL GROUP AUGMENTED on 3 MAY 1962, pointing out the very limited progress which has been made and the uncertainty of the ultimate effectiveness of our program. I mentioned the DUQUET incident as indicative of the morale problem. It was agreed we would continue along the present lines until the terminal date-of-the-present-phase in August."
