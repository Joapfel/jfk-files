Activities of Russell Stetler and John Gerassi, US Citizens

1. The information below was acquired by SUGAR from a former employee of the London-based Bertrand Russell Peace Foundation (BRFF) who talked with Allan Krasa, an American leftist who is Director of the Free University in New York City and who is close to Russell Stetler and John Gerassi.

2. Stetler spends most of his time travelling between the US and London to consult with Ralph Schoenman, Director of the Bertrand Russell Peace Foundation (BRFF) regarding the Foundation's activities. There are now only three officials in the BRFF. Stetler is undecided about his political future and is considering the possibility of returning to graduate school. Schoenman does not have infeasible BRFF funds at his disposal and suggests there are great financial crises. At times Stetler is very bitter about Schoenman's methods of operation and often feels that the latter "sells down the river those who work for him".

3. Gerassi, a member of the International War Crimes Tribunal's first investigating team to North Vietnam in December 1966 and January 1967, was in Cuba the week of 5 July 1967. He is no longer on friendly terms with Ralph Schoenman, and apparently went to Cuba for a different reason than that of Schoenman, who was there in late June 1967.¹
