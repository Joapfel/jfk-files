## Conclusion

The American public should not rely on the JFK Board to settle the question of what happened in Dallas and why. That is not the [*37] Board's mandate. n208 The Board is not charged with answering the question of who murdered President Kennedy. It is not running an investigation; it is, instead, seeking to disclose documents in an age of open secrets, an age in which we have come to embrace the idea that openness is to be preferred and that accountability is the touchstone for public confidence in government. n209
