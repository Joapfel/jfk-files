Miss JOAN SOLEBECK, Employment Supervisor, Cook County School of Nursing, 1900 West Polk Street, Chicago, advised that a search of their records revealed a personnel file for ANNEE WHITE, 2237 South Koeeler, Chicago, Illinois.

Miss SOLEDECK advised that ANNEE WHITE entered on duty at the hospital as a clerical employee on November 29, 1951. She stated that the file further revealed that WHITE was terminated on June 2, 1956, and again rehired, exact date unknown. She said that WHITE again terminated her employment at County Hospital on December 29, 1959.

Miss SOLEBECK said that while employed at Cook County Hospital, WHITE was under the direct supervision of Miss MARCEL WILSON, RN, and that her specific duties were that of a clerk assigned to the Trauma Unit. Miss SOLEBECK said that the personnel file further revealed that WHITE was born October 11, 1930, at Hartwell, Florida. Her Social Security Number is 354-24-5846. Her telephone number is listed as 762-0129, which is the telephone number of her mother, Mrs. ANNIE B. GURBY, 2237 South Koeeler.
