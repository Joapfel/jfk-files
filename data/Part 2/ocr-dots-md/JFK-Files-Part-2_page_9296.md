Title: BLACK PANTHER PARTY -- DETROIT DIVISION

Character: EXTREMIST MATTERS

Synopsis: The Detroit NCCF was raised to full BPP status, 5/71. Their headquarters the Ministry of Information, is located at 157 Collingwood, Detroit, with offices at 2591 Pennsylvania, and 2435 Pennsylvania, Detroit. The office at Flint, Michigan, was closed during this period. Detroit continues to be directed from BPP headquarters, Oakland, California, and have contact with other BPP chapters. Detroit continues to sell "The Black Panther" newspaper, which is their main source of funds, although sales fell off during 9/71 and 10/71. A number of NCCF members were arrested the first part of 1971, in connection with robberies, beatings. Some members of the Detroit BPP have become disenchanted with NEWTON's policies and are switching to the faction of the BPP supporting ELDRIDGE CLEAVER.
