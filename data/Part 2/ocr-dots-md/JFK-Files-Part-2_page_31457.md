### E. "People's World" (PW)

The PW is a west coast communist newspaper published in San Francisco.

The Subject attended a dinner for the benefit of the PW held at 81 Clementina Street, March 16, 1952. At this dinner the Business Manager of the PW announced that those present should sign petitions asking the President not to enforce the McCarran Act. He also suggested a telegram be sent to the President to protest the arrest of GUS HALL and BEN DAVIS under the McCarran Act.

(SF T-4, 3/19/62)

The Subject attended a social affair for the benefit of the PW which was held at 600 2nd Avenue, San Francisco, on March 31, 1962.

(SF T-5, 4/3/62)

This social affair was sponsored by the Teamsters Club, SFCCP, for the benefit of the PW.
