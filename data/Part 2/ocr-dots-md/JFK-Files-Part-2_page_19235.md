Convert to MarkdownNovember 27, 1967

To all American Women:

I am proud of the fact that my first vote came in Congress was a vote against war. It was the first opportunity for a woman to vote, because I was the first woman to be elected to Congress.

I believed then, as I do today, that women are the ones that must be concerned with the needs and development of the human race. I have always fought for the dignity of all human beings -- for those of the present as well as those of future generations.

I will continue to struggle as long as I live. I hope all women will respond to the following call and join me in Washington on January 15, 1968, the opening day of Congress.

Jeannette Rankin
