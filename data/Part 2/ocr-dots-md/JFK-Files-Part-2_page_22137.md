OCCURRENCE:

On June 20, 1963 the Special Group approved a CIA paper dated June 1970 regarding political action in [Haiti] (The substance of the paper is not expressed). "Mr. MCCONE said he has some reservations about the quality of any possible replacements for [DUVALIER] but agreed that it is necessary to take some action in order to prevent [Haiti] eventually going very far to the left. Colonel KING... said that in his opinion, we have no feasible alternatives to cooperate with [BOSCH] in attempting to unseat [DUVALIER]....
