Name(s) as shown on Form 1040

A. Who Should File.—You will need to complete either Part I or Part II of this form if you have a capital loss to carry over to 1975.

B. How to Compute Carryover.—If you have a capital loss carryover, complete either Part I or Part II of this form, but do not complete both.

1. Complete only Part I if lines 4(a) and 12(a) on your 1974 Schedule D (Form 1040) **DO NOT SHOW A LOSS.**

You will have a capital loss to carry to 1975 if the amount on your 1974 Schedule D (Form 1040), line 16(a), or line 33, is **LARGER THAN** the loss deducted on your 1974 Form 1040, line 29.

2. Complete only Part II if either (or both) line 4(a) or line 12(a) on your 1974 Schedule D (Form 1040) shows a loss.
