## PROPOSED BIOGRAPHY

O'NEAL, Birch Dilworth.—b. Bainbridge, Ga., June 1, 1913; U. of Ga., A.B. 1935; U. of Ga., Law Sch., LL.B. 1937; atty. pvt. prac., 1937-38; atty., Dept. of Justice, 1938-42; to the Dept. of State, att., Mexico City, D. F. and Mo. ota, Colombia, 1942-45; atty., self 1946-47; to the Dept. of State, att., Caracas, 1947-49; Regal officer, Dept. of Defense 1949-51; married.
