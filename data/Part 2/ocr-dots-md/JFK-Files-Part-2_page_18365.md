ON SEPTEMBER 18, 1975, FORMER SAC, JACKSON, ROY K. MOORE WAS CONTACTED BY SAC SULLIVAN IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE INSTRUCTIONS IN RE NITEL. MR. MOORE APPRECIATED THE CONTACT. HE ADVISED THAT DUE TO THE MANY SPECIAL ASSIGNMENTS HE SERVED ON DURING THE PERTINENT PERIOD AND HIS MANY TRANSFERS HE HAS AT THE PRESENT TIME NO RECOLLECTION WITHOUT BENEFIT OF THE FILE AND WOULD CERTAINLY NOT WISH TO REFRESH HIS RECOLLECTION THROUGH REVIEW OF PERTINENT FILES FOR PURPOSE OF ANSWERING THE INQUIRY. IF, HOWEVER, THE SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE REPRESENTATIVE HAD PERTINENT DOCUMENTS AUTHORED AND SIGNED OR INITIALED BY MOORE AND UTILIZED THIS MATERIAL TO REFRESH MOORE'S RECOLLECTION, HE WOULD, OF COURSE, COOPERATE FULLY. HE POINTED OUT HE WOULD IN NO WAY BE UNCOOPERATIVE BUT SIMPLY HAS NO INDEPENDENT RECOLLECTION AT THE PRESENT TIME.

END

SJP FBIHQ CLR

SEP 18 1975
