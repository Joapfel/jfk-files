The media controversy reached a new high on December 12 when the _Sun Times_ exposed the fact that the pictures that accompanied the _Tribune_ "exclusive" had been erroneously described. After visiting the apartment the _Sun Times_ reporter claimed that the picture of bullet holes near the kitchen door were in fact nail holes and—that—the photo purporting to show the bathroom door (proving that the Panthers had fired from the front bedroom) was in fact a picture of the door to the front bedroom which had been struck by police bullets fired through the living room wall. The State's Attorney's office and the _Tribune_ both acknowledged the mistake, but no explanation of how it occurred was provided.
