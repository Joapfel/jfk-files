WASHINGTON FIELD VIA WASHINGTON -ENCODED-

DEMONSTRATION PROTESTING U.S. POLICY IN VIETNAM,

CONCERNING (INTERNAL SECURITY)

VIDEM, BUFILE 105-138315

ANZELM A.CZARNOWSKI, SEVEN FIVE ONE THREE W. SIXTYTHIRD STREET, ARGO, ILLINOIS, MEMBER OF CP OF ILLINOIS, OCTOBER, FORTH THREE - JANUARY, FIFTYFIVE, FURNISHED TODAY COPY OF PAMPHLET CAPTIONED, "A CALL TO AMERICAN WOMEN", ISSUED BY JEANNETTE RANKIN BRIGADE, FIVE TWO ZERO N. MICHIGAN AVENUE, CHICAGO. PAMPHLET DESCRIBES BRIGADE AS BROAD COALITION OF WOMEN FROM MANY AREAS OF AMERICAN LIFE WHO WILL MEET IN WASHINGTON, D.C., JANUARY FIFTEEN, ZIYHY-EIGHT FOR OPENING DECONGRESS TO DEMAND FOLLOWING:

ONE. CONGRESS, AS ITS FIRST ORDER OF BUSINESS, RESOLVE TO END THE WAR IN VIETNAM AND IMMEDIATELY ARRANGE FOR THE WITH-DRAWL OF ALL AMERICAN TROOPS.
