II. Summary of Contact:

Investigator Robert Buras met Mr. Atzenhoffer at his service station and learned that he had joined the Civil Air Patrol Cadets, Moisant Squadron in Sept. 1954 and left 1957. He was a platoon Sgt. and used to call the roll and remembered Oswald's name being on the roll. He has no recollection of ever training with him or speaking with him, and remembers him being a quiet guy. When he saw Oswald on TV in 1963 he didn't recognize him but on one of the newscasts he saw Ed Voebel talking about him (LHO). He remembered Voebel as the man that Oswald was close too and stayed with at the meetings. Atzenhoffer only knew Voebel from CAPC, not socially.

Ferrie did come to Moisant Squadron and teach some courses and Atzenhoffer took his first plane flight with Ferrie. He
