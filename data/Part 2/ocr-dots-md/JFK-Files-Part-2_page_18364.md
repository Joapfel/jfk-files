REBUNITEL SEPTEMBER 17, 1975.

RANDOLPH E. TROW AND JOHN H. WAGNER CONTACTED BY SENIOR SUPERVISOR SHERRILL W. LAW, WHO IS PERSONALLY ACQUAINTED WITH BOTH INDIVIDUALS, ON SEPTEMBER 18, 1975. CHARLES F. HEINER IS CURRENTLY IN EUROPE. WILL RETURN TO RICHMOND WEEK OF SEPTEMBER 22, 1975, AND WILL BE IMMEDIATELY CONTACTED UPON HIS RETURN BY RICHMOND. BUREAU WILL BE ADVISED OF THIS CONTACT.

END

LNG FBI HQ CLR
