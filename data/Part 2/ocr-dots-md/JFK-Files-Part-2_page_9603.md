F. Acquisition of Firearms and Explosives

On January 7, 1972, it was reported that most members of the SAO possess several firearms and frequently are armed. Furthermore, members of the SAO are endeavoring to acquire explosives and munitions of all descriptions.

(SD T-2, January 7, 1972)

II. ACTIVITIES OF LOCAL GROUPS

A. Name, Location, and Estimated Membership

On January 7, 1972, it was reported the SAO leadership considers that the organization has groups established at the following locations:

San Diego, California

The group at San Diego controls the State of California and has the same location as the national headquarters. This group has eight members and approximately the same number of prospective members and supporters.
