As stated on 18. June, a source, who has supplied multiple addresses, has sent a letter to its supporters, which stated the following:

Dear Sirs,

For many years I have been a member of the Labour Party in England. For some time now, I have also been a member of the Labour Party in Wales. In addition, for about two and a half years now, I have been a member of the Labour Party in Northern Ireland. In general, I have always supported the Labour Party in all its forms and, therefore, I support your call to form a Labour government in Britain.

I do not consider myself to be a political scientist, but my views are based on common sense. The main reason why I support the Labour Party is that it is the only party which will fight for the rights of working people in this country, not just in Wales, but also in England and in Northern Ireland. It is the only party which will defend the interests of working people in Britain, not just in Wales, but also in England and in Northern Ireland.

Thank you for your time.

Yours sincerely,
Graham Fawcett
Leader of the Labour Party in Wales
