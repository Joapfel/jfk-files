(9) Administrative data

(a) General

All references to following items, all administrative in nature, should be contained in cover pages accompanying reports:

I. Reserve index, security index, file numbers and titles of other cases, priority apprehension program, top functionary status, security index flash notices (FD-165), forms FD-122 and FD-128, and Bureau instructions for handling cases or any information contained in Bureau or field correspondence which is not investigative in nature.
