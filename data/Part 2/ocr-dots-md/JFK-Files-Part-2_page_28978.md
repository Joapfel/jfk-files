# PART II—INTEREST INCOME

Interest on bonds, debentures, loans, notes, tax refunds and all types of savings accounts including banks, credit unions and postal savings is taxable.

Interest on the following obligations is exempt from tax:

(a) Bonds and other obligations (other than tax refunds) of the United States, the District of Columbia and territories of the United States. (Interest on Philippine Islands obligations issued on or after March 24, 1934 is not exempt.)

(b) Bonds (but not other obligations) of California and its political subdivisions issued after November 4, 1902.

(c) Interest on bonds of Alaska and Hawaii issued prior to their achieving statehood.

Note: If total taxable interest income was $400 or less, do not complete this part; but enter the total amount of interest received on Form 540, page 1.
