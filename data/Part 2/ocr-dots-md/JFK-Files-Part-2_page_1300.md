**EDUCATION:**

Entered Harvard University, Sept. 1940, completed requirements for degree,
cum laude, in history, government and economics, Feb. 1943, awarded degree
while in service, Nov. 1943.

<u>History:</u> 30 hours, including, American, modern European, ancient, medieval,

Honors thesis: <u>Andrew Jackson and the Presidency</u>

<u>Government:</u> 12 hours, including, American federal, comparative, administration, political theory.

<u>Economics:</u> 12 hours, including, principles, banking, international trade.

<u>Further:</u>
Yale University, 1<sup>st</sup> year, ASTP, Far Eastern Area and Japanese,
including, Chinese and Japanese history, Pacific islands, society,
Japanese political parties and society and spoken Japanese.

University of Michigan: HHS Language School, including spoken and
written Japanese, Japanese political, social and military organiza-
tion (awarded 12 hours graduate credit) 1944-45.

<u>GRADUATE:</u>

Training: Johns Hopkins University, 1947-48, 6 hours, international
relations and political geography, including economic geography
and seminar study of Korea.

University of Pennsylvania, 1948-50, 31 hours, history and
political science.

<u>History:</u> 30 hours, including, American social, diplomatic,
development and organization of British Empire, British foreign
policy 1919-1949, American colonial, political since 1665,
United States and Latin America.

<u>Political Science:</u> 12 hours, government and politics Latin America.

<u>DIPLOMS:</u> A. B. Harvard, A.M., University of Pennsylvania (doctorates awarded in history, University of Pennsylvania 1951).

<u>MILITARY SERVICE:</u> U. S. Army, 1943-1945, Graduate corporal, unit: ARS, Japanese specialist.

<u>EXPERIENCE:</u>
Instructor in History, Dickinson College, Carlisle, Pa., 1946-48.
Assistant Professor of History, Dickinson College, Carlisle, Pa., 1949 -
Subjects taught: world history, medieval, American, Far East, International
relations, Indian geography, historical geography.
