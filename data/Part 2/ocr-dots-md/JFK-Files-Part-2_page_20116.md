FORMER MEMBER OF SUBJECT ORGANIZATION

The WONACAC Conference procedure continued uninterrupted with votes on various floor discussions until an interruption was made by Rose Weber, a WONAAC coordinator. She addressed all YSA-SWP members at the conference, asking them what was their real reason for being in attendance at the WONAAC Conference. She accused the YSA-SWP of not being sincerely interested in abortion rights, but rather being interested in what was best for the SWP-YSA. She stated that : because of the YSA-SWP influence being enforced on the conference, she was resigning as a WONAAC coordinator and staff member. She stated that she detested the intervention of the YSA-SWP in this conference; that the SWP-YSA has caused conflict among the WONAAC staff and therefore, they cannot work out constructive solutions to their problems. She also suggested that the "Zero Population" and planned parenthood have no voice in discussions at this conference.
