Director, FBI

CUBAN ACTIVITIES IN THE U.S.
IS - CURA

Re Rules 5/20/58 and 6/29/59 both captioned
"Intelligence Coverage of Diplomatic Representatives
of Friendly Foreign Nations."

State Department clearance for Bureau to insti-
tute program to develop live informants and sources within
all Cuban diplomatic establishments in the United States
was granted on 11/30/59. Bureau authority is therefore
granted to recipient offices to conduct a survey regarding
the identities of individuals who can be utilized in
this matter. On completion of this survey, the recipient
offices should submit their recommendations as to the
individuals to be investigated in separate 134 cases with
a request for Bureau authority for investigation in
each individual case. Include in your survey, the names
of sources and informants that are currently being used
in Cuban diplomatic establishments within your respective
territories together with the names of sources and
informants not assigned to an establishment but who are
in a position to furnish information concerning Cuban
Government activities in this country.

In view of the present situation in Cuba,
this matter should be given preferred attention.
