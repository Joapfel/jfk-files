Convert to MarkdownROBERT WILLIAM WILLIAMSON

As previously noted, WILLIAMSON was apprehended by Special Agents of the FBI on August 22, 1971, inside the Post Office Building, 401 Market Street, Camden, N. J. On the same date he was afforded a hearing before U. S. Magistrate CHARLES L. RUDD, Camden, N. J., and was remanded to the custody of the U. S. Marshal in lieu of $25,000 good bail. He was released on September 7, 1971, after posting ten per cent cash or security on a $10,000 bond which had been set by the Third Circuit Court of Appeals, Philadelphia, Pa., on September 3, 1971.
