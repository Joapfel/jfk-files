IN VIETNAM, PORTLAND, OREGON, JANUARY
15, 1968

A leaflet of the Portland chapter of the Women's International League for Peace and Freedom (WILPF) dated December 5, 1967, identified WILPF as follows: "The WILPF was founded by Jane Addams in 1915. Since this time, it has been steadfastly opposed to militarism and war. It is an international organization believing that peace must be based upon economic and social justice."
