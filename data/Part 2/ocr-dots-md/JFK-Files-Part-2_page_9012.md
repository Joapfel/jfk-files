September 30, 1968

TO: Members of Subcommittee on Castro-Communist Subversion.

SUBMITTER: Chairman, Subcommittee on Cuban Subversion

SUBJECT: Report for July - August on Actions Taken to Combat Castro-Communist Subversion.

Attached is a draft of the Subcommittee's report for July and August on actions taken to counteract Castro-communist subversion in the USA. The draft has been prepared on the basis of the material which you supplied with certain modifications. The CIA number will note that I have extracted from his manuscript the items of action taken and placed there in the body of the report. The Intelligence information contained in the manuscript & have made into an annex. I think it very useful to have the Intelligence data from part of the report. And I understand that in the future the CIA number prepare his manuscript in two parts.

May I have your opinion on the draft by close of business October 1, 1968.

Copies to: CIA - Mr. Michael (1)
DOD - Col. Bill (1)
CUTA - Mr. LaRae (1)
BAR - Mr. Herr (1)
Justice - Mr. Hughlan (1)
