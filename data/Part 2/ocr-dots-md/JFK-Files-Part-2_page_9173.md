Date of transcription 8/13/74

WILLIAM JUNIOR STAFFORD, residing at 1103
Claridge Lane, Lexington, Kentucky, and known to inter-
viewing agents as JAY STAFFORD, furnished the following
information.

STAFFORD advised that an individual known to
him as HINDEN appeared at Stafford Lock Shop,
Lexington, Kentucky, in the fall of 1973 and at that
time involved JAY and his brother LARRY STEVENSY STAFFORD
in a conversation concerning the STAFFORDS' knowledge
of lockpicking. His ability to pick locks and their atti-
tudes considerably involvement in such activities which
might require the unauthorized picking or opening of
locks. STAFFORD advised that neither he nor his brother
expressed any interest in HINDEN's suggestions and HINDEN
left the stafford Lock Shop and was not seen again until
his appearance at the stafford Lock Shop on or about
Monday, July 22, 1974.

JAY STAFFORD advised that on or about Saturday,
July 20, 1974, an article appeared on the front page
of the Richmond, Kentucky, newspaper and this article
referred to the fact that JAY and his brother, LARRY,
had been involved in a burglary in Frankfort, Kentucky,
on the evening of July 5, 1974. On this appearance by
BROWN at the Stafford Lock Shop on July 22, 1974,
BROWN disclosed that he had read this article concerning
JAY and his brother LARRY and advised that he felt
like the STAFFORDS were his kind of people. BROWN
suggested that the STAFFORD brothers might need some
help in getting out of this mess and that if they were
willing to "go to broke", he would disclose to them
a scheme to be concluded, wherein the STAFFORDS'
participation might mean their influence and friends
who could help the STAFFORDS with the revolution
of their problem. BROWN then advised the STAFFORDS
that if they were interested in this scheme that they
should contact him at his residence at 101 Cherry
Street, Berea, Kentucky.
