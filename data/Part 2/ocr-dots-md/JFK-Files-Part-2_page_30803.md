Piscilla Johnson, age 32, born long ago, is a foreign correspondent for North American Newspaper Alliance. Moscon, now unemployed because of Soviet refusal to renew visa, stopped coming to Bad Godesberg 9 July for several days, possibly w.r.t., or way back to United States. Purpose of Bad Godesberg visit is to discuss her future with Ed Faltermayer, Bonn correspondent of Wall Street Journal, who had acquaintance. Marie R. Cauchner, who acquainted w/ Faltermayer, will see Johnson. Although doubtless HP will doubtless wish debrief ter upon return to states, Cauchner could arrange for intro. Cauchner would arrange for interview w/ FROB officer for social and exchange of views meeting in Bad Godesberg 15-20
