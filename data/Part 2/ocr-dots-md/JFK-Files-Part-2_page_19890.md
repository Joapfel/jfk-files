ON APRIL TWENTYSEVEN LAST, A SOURCE WHO HAS FURNISHED RELIABLE INFORMATION IN THE PAST ADVISED THAT THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA CHAPTER OF WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION HAS PROPOSED A CANDLELIGHT MARCH FROM ST. MARKS EPISCOPAL CHURCH, THIRD AND A STREETS, S.E., WASHINGTON, D.C., TO THE EAST STEPS OF THE UNITED STATES CAPITOL. TO GAIN SUPPORT FOR LEGALIZED ABORTION. THE MARCH IS EXPECTED TO START AT NINE THIRTY PM AND END APPROXIMATELY ELEVEN PM ON THE NIGHT OF MAY SIX NEXT AND APPROXIMATELY TWO HUNDRED INDIVIDUALS ARE EXPECTED TO PARTICIPATE.
