Memorandum

TO : DIRECTOR, FBI

FROM : SAC CHICAGO

SUBJECT: BLACK PANTHER PARTY (BPP)
RM

DATE: 11/21/69

Enclosed for the Bureau are 11 copies of a letter-head memorandum (LHM) dated and captioned as above.

The Chicago Police Department has been furnished the contents of information set forth in this LHM as well as the Illinois State Attorney's Office.

CG T-1 is:

No matter is being opened in the Chicago Office concerning this matter inasmuch as information indicates the weapons were apparently legally purchased, under the terms of existing Firearms laws, possession of same is apparently rampart throughout BPP members and apartment rent is paid with BPP funds.

Chicago is following this matter closely and if further information developed warranting investigation, Chicago will immediately institute same.
