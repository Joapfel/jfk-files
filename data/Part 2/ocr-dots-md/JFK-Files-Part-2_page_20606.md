UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION

Indianapolis, Indiana

January 24, 1972

BLOOMINGTON ABORTION COALITION

On November 4, 1971, Sergeant John Tomlinson, U. S. Park Police, Washington, D. C., furnished a permit application for a demonstration to be held November 20, 1971, at Washington, D. C. The sponsoring organization was given as Women's National Abortion Action Coalition, 917 15th Street, Northwest, Washington, D. C. In addition to the main sponsoring organization, other sponsors were also listed and included in those listed was the Bloomington Abortion Coalition, 702 Atwater, Bloomington, Indiana, 47401.

On November 10, 1971, a confidential source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that a meeting of the Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) was held at Indiana University, Bloomington, Indiana, on November 2, 1971. At the meeting, a discussion concerning the Washington, D. C., demonstration of the Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) was held at which time Paula Savich, head of the women's faction of the YSA and Chairwoman for the Bloomington Abortion Coalition (BAC), gave a report. Savich stated that bus tickets for the November 20, 1971, demonstration to be held in Washington, D. C., would be sold in Bloomington on November 8, 1971. Savich stated that the local chapter, which is the BAC, would be asked to assume fund-raising quotas and help to pay debts incurred by the national organization. Savich encouraged all YSA members, both male and female, to travel to Washington, D. C., for the demonstration. It was also discussed that male members of the YSA would be expected to assume part of the transportation costs by YSA women who participated in the November 20, 1971, demonstration at Washington, D. C. According to this source, the BAC is backed primarily by the YSA at Indiana University and the Chairwoman, Paula Savich, is a member of the YSA at Indiana University. The organization was formed by the women's faction of the YSA principally for attendance at the Washington, D. C., demonstration.
