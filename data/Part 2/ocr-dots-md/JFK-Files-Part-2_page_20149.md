WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WONAAC).
INTERNAL SECURITY DASH COMMUNIST (TROTSKYIST).

RE WFO TELETYPE DATED OCTOBER TEN, LAST.

ON OCTOBER ELEVEN, INSTANT, A CONFIDENTIAL SOURCE, WHO HAS FURNISHED RELIABLE INFORMATION IN THE PAST, ADVISED THAT AT APPROXIMATELY NINE AM THIS DATE, A WONAAC SPONSORED PRESS CONFERENCE WAS HELD IN FRONT OF THE UNITED STATES SUPREME COURT, WASHINGTON, D. C. (WDC), BY FIVE WOMEN REPRESENTATIVES OF WONAAC, INCLUDING DOCTOR BARBARA ROBERTS, WONAAC NATIONAL COORDINATOR WHO ACTED AS SPOKESWOMAN FOR WONAAC. THE PRESS CONFERENCE LASTED APPROXIMATELY TWENTY MINUTES, AND CONCERNED THE HEARINGS BEING HELD AT THE COURT REGARDING THE CONSTITUTIONALITY OF THE RESTRICTIVE ABORTION LAWS IN TEXAS AND GEORGIA. DOCTOR ROBERTS STATED THAT WONAAC HOPES THESE LAWS WOULD BE DECLARED UNCONSTITUTIONAL.
