CIVIL RIGHTS

This refers to your memorial of December 5 and December 8, 1969 entitled "Lee's Forkher Party Activity, Chicago, Illinois."

In connection with this matter, I am enclosing here with two copies of a telephone call of December 5, 1969 to the Department from John O'Conner of the Illinois State Branch, BACAP and other officials of that organization. We have also received telegrams from Thomas Placo, Manacost Lilliac, Chicago Fully Defender, Milton H. Tosag, Jr., Executive Director, National Civil rights and from a number of individuals of Harvey, Tillinghast, all of these communications generally alleging that the shooting and killing of members of the Black Panther organization were, on or about December 4, 1969, unnecessary, unjustified and of exceptional justice. They request that an investigation be made of the matter.

In view of the circumstances, please conduct a multi-investigation so that we may determine
the outcome of the matter.
