b. Publications.
Diko is a prolific author and is at present writing an eight part work on Bulgaria to be entitled "Under the Whip of Stalin." The eight parts of the book are:

(1) The Liberation of Bulgaria

(2) In the Hands of the Militia

(3) Justice and Communism

(4) Prisons and Camps

(5) Agrarians and Communism

(6) Workers and Communism

(7) Army and Communism

(8) Church and Communism

Diko has already written parts 1, 5 and 7. Parts 1 and 5 have been published in Greece. They are enclosures No. 1 and No. 2 to this despatch. Part 7 (enclosure No. 3) is being published by the Greek General Staff and is at the printers at the present time. Diko received about 3,000,000 drachmae for each part of his book which has been published; he expects to earn about the same amount for Part 7, and for subsequent parts.

Diko states that he has written the books which have been published "for the Greek audience." He realizes that they will not meet approval in the West, largely because they are too emotional. Also, he has omitted from the Greek versions of his book certain anti-Monarchist sentiments which he did not express "for political reasons."
