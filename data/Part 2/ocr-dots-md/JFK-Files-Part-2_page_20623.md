WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WNAAC) CONVENTION

FEBRUARY ELEVEN DASH THIRTEEN, NINETEEN SEVENTY TWO, BOSTON,
MASS. IS-C (TROTSKYIST)

A SOURCE WHO HAS PROVIDED RELIABLE INFORMATION IN THE PAST
ADVISED TWO TWELVE SEVENTYTWO THAT THE TWO TWELVE SEVENTYTWO
SESSION OF THE WONAAC CONVENTION AT BOSTON UNIVERSITY,
BOSTON, MASS., BEGAN AT TEN AM WITH SERVICES AND A GENERAL
ASSEMBLY AT MARSH CHAPEL, BOSTON UNIVERSITY WHICH RAN
UNTIL ELEVEN AM.

THE REMAINDER OF THE DAY CONSISTED OF WORKSHOPS HELD IN
VARIOUS BOSTON UNIVERSITY CLASS ROOMS FOLLOWED BY ANOTHER
GENERAL ASSEMBLY IN HAYDEN HALL, BOSTON UNIVERSITY DURING THE
LATER EVENING HOURS.

THE DAYS EVENTS WERE WITHOUT INCIDENT. AN ESTIMATED EIGHT
HUNDRED TO ONE THOUSAND INDIVIDUALS WERE IN ATTENDANCE DURING
THE DAYS PROCEEDINGS. THE CONVENTION IS SCHEDULED TO
RECONVENE AM TWO THIRTEEN SEVENTYTWO AND TO RUN THROUGH LATE
EVENING THAT DATE.
