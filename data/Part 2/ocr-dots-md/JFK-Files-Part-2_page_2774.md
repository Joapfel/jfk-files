     Mr. Kent is indeed a professional in his field and an expert in propaganda operations which require area knowledge, technical know-how, and imagination. In a specialty where it is only too easy to go off the deep end, Mr. Kent has always kept his wits about him and avoided parochialism. He is a lucid writer who focuses on the essential. By nature, Mr. Kent is an impatient man who has certain biases which sometimes get into the way of his operational judgements. He is keenly aware of this and his efforts to remain as "uninvolved" as possible are commendable. In summary, Mr. Kent is a "doer" who in his field contributes much.
