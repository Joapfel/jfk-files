**OCCURRENCE:**

Zogboy sometime in Dec of 1961, described to Halpern a meeting at the director's office in which McCone had stated that Helms would henceforth "be his man on Cuba." (13) Zogboy told Halpern that Helms had no inkling that he would be appointed head of the Cuban operations before this meeting (13). Sometime after the meeting Helms came to Zogboy's office, checked into the organization of Zogboy's operations and directed that Cuba would be a separate activity and no longer part of the MI Division (14).
