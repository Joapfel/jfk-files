18 January 1957

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD

THROUGH: Staff Training Officer
SUBJECT: Completion of Operational Management Course for FE Personnel

1. Mr. Robert P. Wheeler, PE/VI, has completed a Special Management Course (Operational Management Seminar) held 7-18 January, 1957.

2. The course covered 38 hours of lectures and group discussions, together with a limited amount of reading. The course outlined the responsibilities of management at the Branch Chief level and selected problems of Headquarters management dealing with clandestine activities.
The individual named completed all course assignments but received no grade, since no evaluation was made of the student's performance in this special course, or of his ability to perform a particular job assignment.

FOR THE DIRECTOR OF TRAINING:

Robert P. Welf
ROBERT L. WELK
Chief Instructor, Basic Management
