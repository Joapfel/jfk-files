BY LIAISON

Honorable U. Alexis Johnson
Deputy Under Secretary of State
for Political Affairs
Department of State
Washington, D. C.

My dear Mr. Johnson:

Special Agent O. N. Bartlett has recently discussed with you and Mr. Francis E. Neloy our proposal to expand and improve our capacity to produce data of intelligence and investigative value regarding countries in Central and South America.

An integral part of our intelligence and internal security coverage do the developing of individual sources within the diplomatic establishments in the United States of foreign countries. We endeavor to direct our efforts in those areas where such coverage is likely to be most beneficial. For this reason we concentrate on those countries faced with increased communist subversion, countries tending toward alignment with the Soviet-Sino bloc or countries involved in actual or potential revolutionary situations with international ramifications.

We are fully cognizant of the delicacy of such a program and have set up what we feel are sensible safeguards. In order to assure coordination with foreign policy objectives we request clearance from the Department of State before designating a particular country as a target. Whether then conduct most discreet investigations of carefully selected individuals, preferably citizens of the United States, who are connected with the diplomatic establishments of the particular country.
