BECAUSE OF A NEED FOR COMMUNICATION BETWEEN THE PEOPLE INSIDE THE PRISONS AND OUTSIDE THE PRISONS (CONCENTRATION CAMPS). THE BLACK PANTHER PARTY IS SPONSORING A "FREE BUSING PROGRAM" TO JACKSON STATE PENITENTIARY. THE FIRST BUS WILL BE LEAVING ON DECEMBER 23, 1971 FROM 157 COLLINGWOOD AT 10:00 A.M. AND RETURN AT 6:00 P.M. WE WOULD LIKE TO HAVE THE BUSING PROGRAM ON A MORE CONSISTENT BASIS SUCH AS:

(1) HAVING A BUS OR BUSES GOING TO PRISONS MORE THAN JUST ONCE A MONTH.

(2) HAVING MORE THAN ONE BUS.

IN ORDER TO HAVE A SUFFICIENT BUSING PROGRAM, SUPPORT IS NEEDED, EITHER BY GETTING A BUS DONATED OR SUPPLYING SOME FINANCIAL AID TO THE PROGRAM. WITH THE UNDERSTANDING WE ALL HAVE BY BEING PART OF THE LIBERATION STRUGGLE AND KNOWING THE NECESSITY OF THE BUSING PROGRAM, WE HOPE THAT YOUR ORGANIZATION WILL COOPERATE WITH THE PROGRAM AND ASSIST US WITH IMPLEMENTING A SUCCESSFUL BUSING PROGRAM.

ALL POWER TO THE PEOPLE!

DETROIT BRANCH OF THE BLACK PANTLER PARTY
MINISTRY OF COMMUNISM 157 COLLINGWOOD
