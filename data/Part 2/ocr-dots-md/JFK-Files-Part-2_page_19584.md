UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT
Memorandum

TO The Director

FROM N. P. Callahan

SUBJECT The Congressional Record

Pages 3145-3146. Senator Thurmond, (R) South Carolina, spoke concerning the demonstration on January 15 by a group of anti-war protesters called the Jeanette Rankin Brigade. He stated "Upon close inspection, one sees that the list of organizers and sponsors of this group is pretty much the same list of tired campaigners for far-left causes." In short, the brigade was a typical united front operation--the tried and tested Communist tactic by which many well-intentioned people are led into supporting a cause organized, financed, and directed by professional militants." Mr. Thurmond set forth information on Mrs. Vivian Hallman described by the press as the leader and organizer of the Jeanette Rankin Brigade. He also included information on her family which included the following: "Michael and Terence Hallman, sons of Vice President and Vivian Hallman, were among the principal organizers of the W. E. B. DuBois Clubs, which have been described by FDL Director J. Edgar Hoover as a Communist-spawned youth organization. The Hallmans, wife and employer and son, were deeply involved in the protests at the University of California at Berkeley, both in the so-called free speech movement, and in the sit-ins at San Francisco."
