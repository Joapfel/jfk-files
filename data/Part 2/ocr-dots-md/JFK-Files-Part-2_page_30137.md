APPLICANT

1. LOOP

TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PRINTERS' INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE THE EYEBALL ON INKING SLAB.

3. PASS AND DRY FINGERS THOROUGHLY.

4. TOLL FINGERS FROM MAIL TO MAIL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. BE SURE IMPRESSIONS ARE RECORDED IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN AMOUNT OF MISTAKES MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION AS THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL CONDITION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE GR-CONSTANCES.

8. EXAMINE THE COMPLETED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERS MUST FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INCidentally, AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
