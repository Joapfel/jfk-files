30 November 1960

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Minutes of Special Group Meeting, 30 November 1960

PRESENT: Messrs. Merchant and Mann; Douglas; Gray;
Dulles and Bissell

2. Cuba

Mr. Gray solicited the Group's assistance in arriving at a
consensus of views as to what exactly had been decided as a result
of the high-level meeting of the day before. The following points were
agreed:

a. Mr. Gray's associates stand ready to deny U.S.
activity based in Guatemala, even though it may become
blown (Mr. Douglas urged that every effort be made to
make it possible to deny plausibly).

b. If the program is enlarged, additional facilities
may be obtained, but at this stage not in the U.S. No
training of Cubans will be conducted in the U.S. under
present circumstances. (Mr. Bissell told the Group of
the possibility of an additional finca in Guatemala.)
