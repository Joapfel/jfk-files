During the executive session of captioned Committee on July 16, 1975, members of that Committee requested that they be furnished answers to the following questions:

(1) How are files established on Congressmen?

(2) How long are such files maintained?

(3) Who has access to the Congressional files and "official and confidential files"?

Attached for your approval and forwarding to the Committee is an original of a memorandum in response to the above-mentioned questions. A copy of this memorandum is enclosed for your records.
