NATIONALITY GROUP COVERAGE
ISRAEL

It is recommended that we continue our efforts to develop live sources and informants in Israeli diplomatic establishments in the United States.

State clearance was obtained on 6/2/58 for the FBI to develop sources and informants at Israeli diplomatic establishments. Our request for this clearance was based upon our need to combat the activities of Israeli intelligence agents operating out of the Israeli Embassy, Washington, D. C., and the Israeli United Nations Delegation, New York City. At the same time, as a result of the Suez Crisis in 1957-1958, the possibility of war in the Middle East was prevalent and Israel occupied a central position in this sensitive area. Our needs for sources were based, therefore, upon FBI investigative needs and the need to obtain significant intelligence data for other U. S. Government agencies.

After instigating this program, we developed an extremely valuable informant who was an American citizen and who was employed at the Israeli Embassy. This informant was used for several years and furnished valuable information of assistance in our investigation of Israeli intelligence matters and significant intelligence data. She left the Israeli Embassy in 1963. At the present time we have developed several former employees of Israeli diplomatic establishments in the U. S. and persons who have operated in the past for the Israeli Intelligence Service. Some of these persons continue to have contacts with persons in Israeli establishments; however, we have no sources presently employed in Israeli diplomatic establishments in the U. S. This is primarily explained by the sensitivity of the program; the fact that the Israel Security Service is extremely thorough in briefing its diplomatic employees concerning possible recruitment efforts by U. S. intelligence services and the employment by the Israelis of a very few U. S. citizens who would be logical targets for recruitment.
