NATIONALITY GROUP COVERAGE
IS - JAPAN

(Buded: 10/30/64)

ReNYlet 7/7/64, no copy WFO, requesting authority
to initiate nationality group coverage of Japanese establish-
ments.

Through liaison channels on 7/27/64, State Department clearance was secured for this program. Accordingly, WFO and New York should ascertain identities of employees of the Japanese Embassy and UN Mission who are citizens of U.S. Any such person considered to have potential should be made the subject of an individual file and Bureau consulted prior to institution of background investigation.

The foregoing is not intended to limit the
development of sources to individuals employed in Japanese
establishments. Should you find that other areas of
Japanese activity, such as organizations of Japanese nationals,
require coverage you should not hesitate to initiate the
necessary action,

Under caption of this letter, New York and WFO
should advise Bureau by 10/30/64 and thereafter at 90-day
intervals as to over-all progress made in this matter.
