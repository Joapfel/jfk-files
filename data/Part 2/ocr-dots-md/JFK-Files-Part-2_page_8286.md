8. Since his arrival in the United States, the Subject has come to our attention on the following occasions:

On 14 May 1957, the Department of State informed CIA that the Subject had contacted their Office and tried to interest them in purchasing a feature length film script and asked if a representative of CIA would like to meet the Subject and discuss the script. The offer was turned down.

On 28 June 1960, the Subject attempted to arrange a meeting with the then Vice-President Richard M. Nixon. A member of Mr. Nixon's staff turned the matter over to Mr. F. J. Sheridan, Contact Division, Office of Operations. Mr. Sheridan interviewed the Subject at length, acting on behalf of Mr. Nixon's staff, with no CIA connection.
