On August 22, 1974, New York Office (NYO), Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) Special Agents (SAs) DREW H. CARR and COREY W. HART obtained a subpoena duces tecum from Assistant United States Attorney (AUSA) JOHN M. GROSS, Southern District of New York (SDNY), for the original registration card for one MIKE BROWN, who was registered on August 5, 1974 at the Commodore Hotel, Park Avenue at 42nd Street, New York (NY), N.Y. It is noted the subpoena duces tecum was prepared in the name of Hotel Biltmore, Madison Avenue and 43rd Street, NY, NY, since the Commodore Hotel records are maintained at the Biltmore Hotel.

On August 22, 1974, at 12:00 Noon, aforementioned agents served the subpoena duces tecum to JAMES L. BOWEN, Assistant General Credit Manager for the Biltmore Hotel. BOWEN complied with the subpoena request and released original registration card.

Interviewed on 8/22/74 of New York, New York File # NY 185-229

by S.As. DREW H. CARR & COREY W. HART/mad Date dictated 8/22/74
