CONSULATE GENERAL OF VENEZUELA,
NEW YORK CITY
INTERNAL SECURITY - VENEZUELA

Reurlet 9-26-58.

State Department clearance was previously obtained on 8-19-58 for program looking toward development of sources on carefully selected basis at Venezuelan Embassy, Washington, D. C., and other Venezuelan establishments in this country, if deemed necessary. Accordingly, you are authorized to proceed with such a program with respect to captioned establishment.

No active investigations or interviews with persons connected with the captioned establishment should be conducted without specific Bureau authority since the Bureau must obtain State Department clearance for each such investigation and interview.

① -- 65-63113 (Intelligence Coverage of Diplomatic Representatives of Friendly Foreign Nations)
