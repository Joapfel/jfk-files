August 27, 1971

MICHAEL JOSEPH DOYLE was presented an Interrogation - Advice of Rights form 1; SA JOHN R. GINGERICH and advised by SA GINGERICH that an interview was desired regarding the burglary of the Selective Service Offices at the U. S. Court- house and Post Office Building, 4th and Market Streets, Camden, N. J., the early morning of August 22, 1971. DOYLE read the Interrogation - Advice of Rights form, advised he fully understood the contents of the form, and thereby signed the form.

DOYLE advised he did not wish to answer any questions.
