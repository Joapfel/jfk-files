Subject: SAMUEL ADASOY JAFFE INTERNAL SECURITY - RUSSIA

I - Mr. Peterson

Reference is made to your letter dated August 26, 1970, "captioned "Request for Letters Exchanged with ChiCom Newsman by Samuel Adason Jaffe," your reference CSCI-316/02601-70.

This Bureau also no longer maintains contact with subject; therefore, it will not be possible for us to respond to the request in your letter of reference.

ECP:ecog (4)

NOTE: Classified "Secret" since it repeats information so classified by CIA.

Subject is former correspondent for ABC who was stationed in Moscow and while there was considered as recruited agent by Soviets, according to defector source. We conducted several interviews of him during March and April, 1969, during which he did not adequately resolve allegations to our satisfaction and we have had no further contacts with him since September, 1969, when he volunteered information concerning encounter with a Soviet at UN in New York. In relation CIA requested Bureau to ask subject for copies of letters exchanged with New China News Agency official since 1/70 since it no longer maintains contact with him.
