WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WONAAC), OOWF IS-NEW LEFT

RENYTEL THIS DATE.

ON THIS DATE SAS OF THE FBI OBSERVED A DEMONSTRATION AND MARCH SPONSORED BY WONAAC WHICH COMMENCED AT FOUR THIRTY PM AT FOUR TWO ST. AND TIMES SQUARE, NYC. APPROXIMATELY FIFTY INDIVIDUALS PARTICIPATED IN THE DEMONSTRATION WHICH MARCHED TO PROTEST ALL ABORTION LAWS.

AT FIVE THIRTY PM DEMONSTRATION PROCEEDED SOUTH ON SIXTH AVE. TO HERALD SQUARE (THREE FOUR ST. AND SIX AVE) NYC ARRIVING APPROXIMATELY SIX PM. DEMONSTRATORS OBSERVED CARRYING POSTERS READING "ABORTION IS A WOMAN'S RIGHT, WONAAC". WHILE MARCHING, DEMONSTRATORS CHANTEO A SLOGAN "NOT THE CHURCH, NOT THE STATE, WOMEN MUST DECIDE OUR FATE". MAJORITY OF DEMONSTRATORS WERE WOMEN AGED APPROXIMATELY FIFTEEN TO TWO FIVE YEARS. DEMONSTRATORS ENDED SIX PM NO INCIDENTS OBSERVED.

END PG ONE
