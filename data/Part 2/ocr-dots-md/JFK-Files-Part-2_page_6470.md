1 DEC 1963

MEMORANDUM FOR: OFFICE, SECRETARY OF THE ARMY

ATTN: MR. JOSEPH CALIFANO, GENERAL COUNSEL

SUBJECT: Training of Cuban Refugees in Nicaragua (S)

1. (U) References:

A. Intelligence Memorandum 19 Nov 63, above subject, to Office, Secretary of the Army

b. Intelligence Report #2870006563, 9 Aug 63, Subject: Prominent Cuban Exiled Leaders visit Nicaragua (U), from USARMA, Nicaragua

c. Intelligence Report #2870009363, 31 Oct 63, Subject: Puerto Cabanas and Cuban Exiles (U), from USARMA, Nicaragua

d. Summary of Information, 1 Nov 63, 112th INTC Gp, Subject: Cuban Officer Training Program (U)

e. Memorandum for Record, ACSL-CX, 19 Nov 63, Subject: Cuban Brigade Member (U)

2. (S) Reference ln, prepared at the request of your office, advised that query by USARMA, Managua within the U. S. Embassy there, revealed no indication that Col. Manuel Fernandez D. McPhail, 0-30613, USA Retired, the former Chief of US Army Mission, was in anyway involved in the training of Cuban refugees in Nicaragua.

3. (C) During the above inquiry, the USARMA, Managua called attention to two intelligence reports he had furnished (references lb. and lc above) reporting Nicaraguan press comment on the visit of exiled Cuban refugee leaders Dr. Manuel ARTUÑO Buena, Dr. Carlos PRIO Socarras, Dr. Manuel Antonio DE VÁRIONA, Dr. Francisco FERNANDEZ Pla, Dr. Laureano B&TISTA, Dr. Orlando PUENYES, Sr., Miguel DR LEON, and Roberto RODRIGUEZ to Nicaragua during July and August 1963. The USARMA's comment on the newspaper speculation and rumors concerning this visit was that he "does not have any firm information or indicators that anti-Castro forces are being trained in Nicaragua."

OSA. ASG. CONTROL NO. 0813B
