BUT WARDEN DID NOT HAVE A SYSTEMATIC THEOLOGY AND HIS ATTEMPTS TO INITIATE HIS PROGRAM CONTINUALLY PROSTRATED HIM AND THE COMMUNITY TOO. THIS PROGRAM WAS DESTINED TO SEEM AS A ROAD FOR A STRUCTURED POLITICAL VEHICLE.
