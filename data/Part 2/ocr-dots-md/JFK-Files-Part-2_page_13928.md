OTHER SPECIFIC FBI PRACTICES AND PROGRAMS

Reference is made to SSC letter to The Attorney General dated May 14, 1975, with attached appendices, requesting certain documents and other information from the FBI.

Pursuant to your request in Appendix C, Part III, Item 29, for all memoranda and other materials pertaining to the origins, implementation, and termination of Project INLET, involving preparation of an Intelligence Letter for the President, enclosed are copies of the following documents:

1) W. C. Sullivan memorandum to C. D. DeLoach captioned "FBI Intelligence Letter for the President," dated November 7, 1969, with enclosed letters to the President and The Attorney General,

2) W. C. Sullivan memorandum to C. D. DeLoach captioned "FBI Intelligence Letter for the President," dated November 20, 1969, with enclosed letter to all field offices, captioned "FBI Intelligence Letter for the President Code Name 'Inlet' Research-Satellite Matter," dated November 26, 1969.

3) R. D. Cotter memorandum to W. C. Sullivan captioned "FBI Intelligence Letter for the President," dated April 23, 1970.

4) Letter from the Acting Director to all field offices captioned "FBI Intelligence Letter for the President Code Name 'Inlet' Research Matter," dated December 26, 1972.
