WOMEN'S NATIONAL MARCH ON THE PENTAGON, APRIL TEN, NINETEEN
INTERNAL SECURITY - MISCELLANEOUS
SEVENTYONE, IS-MISC.

AT TWO THIRTYTWO PM, INSTANT DATE, A GROUP OF APPROXIMATELY SEVEN HUNDRED AND FIFTY MARCHERS CROSSED MEMORIAL BRIDGE FROM WASHINGTON, D.C., INTO ARLINGTON, VA., AND PROCEEDED TO THE MALL ENTRANCE AT THE PENTAGON VIA WASHINGTON BLVD. MARCHERS SPRAY PAINTED THE WALLS OF AN OVERPASS OVER GEORGE WASHINGTON PARKWAY WITH WRITINGS "BITCHES UNITE", "OFF THE PENTAGON", "FREE ERIKA". MARCHERS ARRIVED AT PENTAGON AT THREE O'CLOCK PM. NUMEROUS SPEECHES WERE GIVEN BY UNIDENTIFIED INDIVIDUALS ADVOCATING THE FREEING OF ANGELIA DAVIS AND BOBBY SEAL; STOPPING THE WAR AGAINST BLACK AMERICA; AND DEMANDING PRESIDENT NIXON AND WAR WAGGERS IN THE PENTAGON TO END THE WAR. THEY DISPLAYED SIGNS "FREE ERIKA AND BOBBY-STOP WAR AGAINST BLACK AMERICA," "NO JUSTICE IN AMERIKKKA", "SUPPORT SISTERS IN STRUGGLE-ASIA, AFRICA AND END PAGE ONE"
