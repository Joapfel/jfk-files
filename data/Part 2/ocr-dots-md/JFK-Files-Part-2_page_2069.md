Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO : Chief, FBT
FROM : Commo
SUBJECT: Training

DATE: 30 June 1949

Mr. James A. Noel was given the training course, "Basic Familiarization in Sound and Surveillance Equipment."

This training was given on June 28, 1949 in the Commo Demonstration Room, 1005A "L" Building and was of two hours duration.

J. Kaplan
