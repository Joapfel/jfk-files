ROLLIN GARY REID, a Detroit BPP member recently returned from California where he worked at the BPP's campaign for the election of BOBBY SEALE as Mayor of Oakland, California.

November 21, 1972

BOBBY SEALE is the National Chairman of the BPP in Oakland, California.

LEONARD MONGO, a Detroit BPP Member, flew from Detroit, Michigan, to California on April 6, 1973 to help in the BPP's campaign for the election of BOBBY SEALE.

JODY WEAVER and LOUIS TOLBERT JOHNSON, both BPP members from Detroit, remain in the San Francisco, California area where they are reportedly engaged in working on the programs of the BPP.
