SUBJECT: Senate Select Committee Request

ALL FBI INFORMATION CONTAINED HEREIN IS UNCLASSIFIED

DATE 10/17/80 BY S.P.Melij.

Reference is made to my memorandum of September 5, 1975, by which I forwarded to you for your attention and appropriate response a letter dated August 29, 1975, from the Senate Select Committee which contained a "partial list" of selected cases in which IRS files indicated that the Department had received tax returns and other tax information. You will recall that that letter requested that the Department expedite the production of its files showing the origin of each request, the use made of the information, and the names of Departmental personnel with firsthand knowledge of the request for and use of the tax information in each instance of such request.

Attached for your further attention and additional response is what is hoped to be the Committee's completed list of selected cases for which the SSC seeks the same information as requested in their letter of August 29.

Please advise me as soon as possible of any delays that may make prompt compliance with this request impossible.

cc: Michael Uhlmann, AAG, OLA
