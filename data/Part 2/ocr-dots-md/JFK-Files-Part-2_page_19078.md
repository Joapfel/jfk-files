Re NY letter, 9/19/68, which reported in part that the captioned source may be re-assigned as a correspondent in Washington, D. C. -U-

New York is requested to verify the source's current assignment to Washington and, if possible, ascertain his address and telephone number. In addition, since WFO contemplates a thorough and detailed interview of the source, it is requested that New York forward to WFO copies of all pertinent serials which would assist in evaluating his relationship with Soviets as well as with the Bureau. -U-
