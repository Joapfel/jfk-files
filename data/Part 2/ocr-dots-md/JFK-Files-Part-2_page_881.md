COURSE OBJECTIVES - CONTENT AND METHODS

The course is designed for Agency personnel requiring a sound basic understanding of contemporary China and some acquaintance with the fundamentals of the Chinese language. The aim is familiarisation, to specialisation. The course focuses on familiarised China. The language familiarization phase includes promotion of Chinese words, an introduction to the most widely accepted system of dictionary recording of Chinese characters and the telegraphic code. The area phase includes: cultural and historical development, geography and resources, economic development and problems, the political system, social change and control in contemporary China and foreign relations.
