That was a disclosure Saturday during the press conference by ROBERT CAMPBELL, 21, of Chicago, a reported section leader of the organization. The conference was at the home of JAMES STUBBS, 22, 520 South Water Street, a reported 'local Captain of Defense' with the Panthers.
