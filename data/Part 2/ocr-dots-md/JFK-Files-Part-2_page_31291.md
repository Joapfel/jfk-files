Shortly after he returned to the United States, Ribotere was debriefed in his own territory by representatives from the CIA's office of Operations and in conjunction with representatives SR/6 beginning on the Air Force. It was decided that a more intensive debriefing was in order and Ribotere was subsequently brought to the Washington, D.C. area where he was debriefed for a period of two weeks. The debriefing reports include a chronology of Ribotere's life and the CIA's assessment of him as well as a large body of information regarding life in the Soviet Union, Ribotere's work there and specific information on persons he had met during his residence there.
