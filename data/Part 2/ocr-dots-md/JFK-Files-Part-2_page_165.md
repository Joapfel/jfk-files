Draft of Dispatch

1. LICOOKY-1 has been terminated effective 1 July 1966.
The last meeting with her was held on 23 June. She was
paid her salary through 30 June, her income tax refund
for the period 1 January 1965 - 30 June 1966 and given
money for one-way air fare to New York City. She was
completely satisfied with the financial terms of the
settlement. A Termination Secrecy Oath, signed in true
name, was obtained.

2. L-1 plans to return to New York on or about 1 July. She said she will look for a job there, and is confident she will have no difficulty obtaining one. She does not yet know where she will be living.

3. L-1 is subletting the apartment she occupies here. She has purchased the KUBARK-owned household effects which had been loaned to her for that apartment. For this reason, in addition to the fact that she has acquired many close friends and interests here as a result of five years residence, the Station expects that she will return to Mexico, although she insists she has no plans to do so.
