MEMORANDUM FOR: V.B. Career Management Committee
SUBJECT: Recommendation for Promotion of Mr. Jerome Fox
from 08-12 to 03-13

1. The Vietnam Station has recommended the promotion of Mr. Jerome Fox from 08-12 to 03-13. He has been in his present grade for almost five years. He is already performing at the level normally expected of a 03-13. Mr. Fox was ranked fifth among all 08-12's currently at Vietnam Station. The Station recommendation as contained in Saigon 6925 (T2709) is quoted in the following paragraphs.

2. This employee has performed at a highly considerable level during his tour at this Station. During the first year he was assigned to a variety of jobs, all of which he accomplished with vigour and efficiency. These includedlicences responsibilities with both the Staatsli. Branch of the National Police and the Saigon Municipal Police. He has also engaged in bilateral agent activities; he has developed, recruited and managed two bilateral agents, and plans to attempt the recruitment of a third prior to his departure. During the last period of his duty, Subject has concentrated on the development and progress of a sensitive FSI/CI activity concerned with a small intercept. He initiated this project, and has since managed it in such a manner that it has evolved into a unilateral project with considerable potential. During the course of his tour, the case officer was also charged with the implementation of an activity (asglued to bring about the release of an American prisoner held by the VC). While this and are also to proceed, Subject acknowledged this difficult task with a reasonable degree of initiative and energy.

3. Subject displays the qualities of a highly intelligent, well-qualified officer. He has demonstrated his own tasks with a matter and efficient manner, and has always demonstrated an excellent understanding of his role and mission of his unit and the station in this area. He appears to be highly motivated, and I consider him an officer with a great deal of long-range potential.

Chief, Vietnam-Quodnha Branch

(11 Aug 66)
