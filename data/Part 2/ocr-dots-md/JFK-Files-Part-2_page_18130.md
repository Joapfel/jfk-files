Dear Mr. Kelley:

In accordance with instructions received from the Bureau, I am enclosing a bill in the amount of $750.00 from the law firm of Kantor, Shaw & Davidoff. Mr. Herbert Kantor is an attorney who acted as my counsel during an appearance by me before the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Operations in Washington, D. C. on August 12, 1975.

After notification by the Committee and the Bureau that my appearance had been requested, I was advised by the Legal Department of the Bureau that I had the right to have an attorney accompany me during my appearance. I was also advised by the Bureau that neither they nor the Department of Justice could provide me with counsel due to a possible conflict of interest on their part.

The matters to be discussed before the Committee involved only my prior official duties in the counter-intelligence field. At this point, I strongly believed that to best protect both my constitutional privileges and the Bureau's overriding interest, the most prudent course of action would indicate the retention of an attorney. I would like to advise you that much thought was paid to the selection of an attorney since confidential Bureau activities would have to be discussed with him and it was felt that the person selected would, in effect, be representing the Bureau in addition to myself.

The specific legal fees incurred, as itemized in the enclosed bill, are only in connection with the preparation for and attendance at my appearance before the Committee.
