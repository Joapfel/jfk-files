18-March-1965

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT: Proposal to Exploit L-I and Jose Revellon in AN Operation

1. It is proposed that at the next meeting with U-25 we pass a message as follows:

"A big commandante or captain who used to be a leader of the university students opened a numbered (secret) account in Switzerland through Colombian Suarez (my successor). He was traveling in Europe at that time, roughly December 1964-January 1965. The deposit was in the amount of $10,000."

The message is purposely so phrased that it could apply to both L-I and Jose. Revellon. Both are part-presidents of the FEJ (Federation of University Students), one is a commandante and the other a captain, and both were traveling in Europe in December 1964-January 1965.

2. Subject proposal has taken the following information into consideration.
   ~~L-I~~

a. The record: The file shows that since April 1959 and repeatedly since then J-L-I has threatened to eliminate Castro, but has not done so. He also was reportedly on the verge several times of defecting but never did. There are numerous reports of varying credibility that he has been and is a Castro agent. His character is such that he has been described in a report as "a probable thief, a possible user of narcotics, a homosexual or a friend of homosexuals," but also "an exceedingly brave man." Various reports indicate that he is a heavy drinker and a party lover. Thus a person with such traits could reasonably be believed to be involved in irregular financial transactions.
