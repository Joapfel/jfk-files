UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION

Chicago, Illinois 60604
November 7, 1969

BLACK PANTHER PARTY

See appendix for characterization of Black Panther Party (BPP).

On November 5, 1969, ________, both of whom have furnished reliable information in the past, advised that the following BPR members departed O'Hare Airport, Chicago, Illinois, at 11:40 p.m., on American Airlines Flight 151, which will arrive in San Francisco at 1:53 a.m., November 6, 1969:

Fred Hampton, Deputy Chairman,
Illinois BPR

Joan Gray, Field Lieutenant, Chicago
Branch of the Illinois BPR

Artie Seale and son, the wife and child
of Bobby Seale, National BPR Chairman

Sources advised that the purpose of Hampton and Gray's trip to San Francisco is unknown, other than to contact the National BPR Central Committee in Berkeley, California.
