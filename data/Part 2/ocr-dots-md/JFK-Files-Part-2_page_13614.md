RE: INTERVIEW OF FONDFOR ASSISTANT TO THE DIRECTOR JOHN P. MOHR BY SEC STAFF MEMBERS

Mr.ahr also recalled that during his interview of
May 9, 1975, he had advised Miss Candy had turned over to
Mr. Mark Felt of the FBI the official and confidential files
of Mr. Hoover which amounted to one-half a file drawer.
Subsequently, Mr.ahr learned from Miss Candy that the official
and confidential files turned over to Mr. Felt amounted to
one and one-half file drawers. This information was subsequently
furnished to Mr. Gitenstein by Mr. Hohr. On that occasion,
Mr. Gitenstein asked Mr. Hohr whether an itemized list of the
official and confidential files was made and if so where the
list was. Hohr told Gitenstein that he was sure a list was
made and that in all probability the list accompanied the
official and confidential files turned over to Mr. Felt by
Miss Genty.

In addition to the above, Mr. Hohr on May 12, 1975, recalled that Gitenstein asked about a requirement of all Bureau employees to turn over any confidential files in their possession ordered by Mr. Hoover following the revelation that former Assistant to the Director William C. Sullivan had turned over certain confidential files to former Assistant Attorney General Hardin, Mr. Hohr advised Gitenstein that Mr. Hoover had in fact levied such a requirement on Bureau officials and that all confidential files were to be turned over to Mr. Felt along with a memorandum explaining the nature of these files. Gitenstein asked Hohr where those memoranda would be filed. Hohr responded by saying he pressured they were confidential memoranda and would have gone with the confidential files turned over to Mr. Felt by Bureau officials maintaining such files.
