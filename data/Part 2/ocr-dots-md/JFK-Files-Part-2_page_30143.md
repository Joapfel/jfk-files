TO OBTAIN PERFECT FINGERSPRINTS:

1. USE THE LIFETIME INK.

2. DIP THE CARD ON AN INDOORS SLAD.

3. PRESS A FEEL OF PENTHOUGHLY.

4. USE NEWLY WASHED HAND, POUR OUT, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. USE THE NEW FINGERPRINTS IN a CONVENTION ORDER.

6. IF A NEW FINGER PRINT IS REQUIRED, IT IS NECESSARY TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A ROTATION THAT THE EFFICIENCY IN THE LINEAR FINISHED BLOCK.

7. USE ONLY THE NEWLY CREDITED WITHIN TWO WEEKS TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SWAP THE COPY TO THE ONE RECEIVED WITH A FINGER STARRING TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CRISTAL DIFFERENCE.

8. DRAWN UP THE CORRECTLY PRINTED TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN HEAD THAT THE PRINTS ARE ALL INTO THE PATTERN SUGGESTED ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERN OCCURS ACCORDING TO THE ACTUAL SHOW HERE).
