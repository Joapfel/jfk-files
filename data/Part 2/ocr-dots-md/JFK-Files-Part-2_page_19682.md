ALEXANDER JOYCE

On 7/7/72, advised that the Sojourner Truth Press was a printing press for hire, which prints mostly New Left material for New Left organizations, yet espouses no opinions of its own. As long as their fee is met, they print what is desired (papers, pamphlets, flyers, etc.)

All those individuals mentioned above are, individually, subjects of security investigations in Atlanta.
