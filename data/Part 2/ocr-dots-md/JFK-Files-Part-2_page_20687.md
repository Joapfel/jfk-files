MARKS EPISCOPAL CHURCH, THIRD AND A STREETS, S.E., WASHINGTON, D.C., TO THE EAST STEPS OF THE UNITED STATES CAPITOL TO GAIN SUPPORT FOR LEGALIZED ABORTION. THE MARCH IS EXPECTED TO START AT NINE THIRTY PM AND END APPROXIMATELY ELEVEN PM ON THE NIGHT OF MAY SIX NEXT AND APPROXIMATELY TWO HUNDRED INDIVIDUALS ARE EXPECTED TO PARTICIPATE.
