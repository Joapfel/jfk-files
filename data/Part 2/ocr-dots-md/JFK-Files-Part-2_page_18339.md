John:

The attached letter is self-explanatory and seeks information which you, better than I, know to be of the most inordinate sensitivity.

The letter's penultimate paragraph requests my "personal attention to this matter, including [my] own examination of the three documents in question, and hopefully, a determination by [me] that these documents should be provided to the Committee in unexcised form (except for the names of so-called "live" informants)."

I would appreciate an early meeting with you and other appropriate Bureau representatives to discuss a proposed resolution of the matters raised by the attached requests.
