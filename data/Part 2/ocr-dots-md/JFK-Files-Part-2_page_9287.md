SF T-16 advised on April 17, 1971, that KENNETH HORSTON, leader of the Detroit NCCF, was in California, and talked with Minister of Defense, HUEY NEWTON, on April 16, 1971.

DE T-1 advised on April 21, 1971, that EDGAR ROBINSON, distribution manager of "The Black Panther" newspaper for the Detroit NCCF, left Detroit en route Oakland, California, on April 20, 1971, apparently to attend the funeral of SAM NAPIER, a member of the BPP who was killed in New York.

DE T-1 advised on April 24, 1971, that LONNIE DARDEN, a member of the Detroit NCCF, went to Toledo to check on the situation there as some members apparently walked into the office wearing Cleaver buttons on April 23, 1971, but were put out. He was then to proceed to Cleveland where HUEY NEWTON, Minister of Defense for the BPP was scheduled to appear, however, he failed to show up.

DE T-3 advised on April 29, 1971, that LONNIE DARDEN, member of the Detroit NCCF, returned from Cleveland, Ohio on April 25, 1971.

DE T-3 further advised that EDGAR ROBINSON, distribution manager of "The Black Panther" newspaper, returned from California, on April 26, 1971.

DE T-1 advised on various dates in April, 1971, that Detroit received approximately 31,600 copies of "The Black Panther" newspaper during April, 1971, from BPP distribution, New York. DE T-1 advised that money for payment of the newspaper is sent to BPP headquarters, Oakland, California.

VI. FACTIONALISM

NY T-5 advised on April 12, 1971, that OLIVER MC CLURE, also known as Malik arrived in New York from Detroit over the weekend of April 9-11, 1971, for consultation with leaders of the BPP Cleaver Faction.

MC CLURE was the leader of the Detroit NCCF but was purged. Detroit continues to be loyal to NEWTON.

DE T-1 advised on April 14, 1971, that OLIVER MC CLURE returned to Detroit from New York on April 12, 1971, and brought with him an unknown number of Cleaver Faction newspapers, "Right On". MC CLURE has not made any attempts to organize a Cleaver Faction in Detroit to source's knowledge.
