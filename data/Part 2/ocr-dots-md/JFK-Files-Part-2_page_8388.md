LOS ANGELES BRANCH

Charter No. 1

Dear Penwomen,

A HAPPY NEW YEAR! Our new year of 1958 begins with our next Meeting - Tuesday, January 14th, - let's all be there. Our Program starts at 12:00 Noon, at the Hotel Knickerbocker, 1714 Ivar Street, Hollywood, California.

A General Workshop will be conducted at 11:00 A. M.

Joe Balding will lead us in the Salute To The Flag.

Dr. Edna L. Phillips, Vice President, will give the Invocation,

I. Demitroff, author, journalist, writer and poet, is our Guest Speaker.
We look forward with pleasure and anticipation to hearing him speak on
"The Role of the Bulgaria Writers Before and After the Communist Regime."

Mr. A. J. Burns, Educational Manager of the Western Division of the Readers' Digest, will be presented with great pride. His subject, "Building A Better World Through Better Reading" should strike to all our hearts as writers.

Dr. Lucia Liverette, President, with her husband, Dr. Jack Liverette, Pastor of the Community Church of Los Angeles, will present a musical program of accordion and saxophone numbers.

a

Dean Callerias of St. Sophia Greek Orthodox Church is our Honor Guest.

BOARD MEETING will follow the Program.

We wish to thank our President again for the most enjoyable time as guests in her home on December 29th.

PLEASE remember to pay 1958 Dues and help our Treasurer to start her work in our behalf.

DR. EDNA M. PHILLIPS,
Program Chairman
