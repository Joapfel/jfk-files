RE: DEMONSTRATION PROTESTING UNITED STATES POLICY IN VIETNAM

CONFIDENTIAL

On January 10, 1968, the first source reported LINDA CORR, a Negro, and THORUNZROBEL, white, are also planning to go to Washington, D.C., but their mode of travel is as yet unknown. The source said both are CP members at Seattle. In addition, four women from Tacoma, Washington, are preparing to go, but their identities have not been learned.
