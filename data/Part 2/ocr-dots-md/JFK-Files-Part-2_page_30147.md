APPLICANT

TO: OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PRINTER'S INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE INK EVENLY ON INKING SLAB.

3. WASH AND dry FINGERS THOROUGHLY.

4. ROLL FINCES FROM HAIR TO HAIR, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGEES TO SLIP.

5. IF SURE IMPRESSIONS ARE RECORDED IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN AMPUTATION OR DEFORMITY MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION AS TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL CONDITION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CIRCUMSTANCES.

8. EXAMINE THE COMPLETED PRINTS TO BE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERIMPRINTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INFREQUENTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).

THIS CARD FOR USE BY:

LEAVE THIS SPACE BLANK

1. LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES IN FINGERPRINTING APPLI- CANTS FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT PURPOSES.*

2. OFFICIALS OF STATE AND LOCAL AUTHORITIES FOR PERMITS OF IMMIGRATION, LICENSES, INSPECTION, AND DOCUMENTATION AUTHORIZED BY STATE STATUTES AND ATTACHMENTS BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE UNITED STATES, U.S. AIR and CUSTOMS, WILL BE REGULARLY INSPECTED IN ALL UNIFORMS, E.G., PERMANENT ARMS, CLEAR AGGREGATE, ETC.

3. DO NOT USE THIS CARD IF:
   a. THERE IS INSURANCE FOR THE REQUESTED SERVICE.
   b. THE REQUEST IS SO GREATLY UNJUSTIFIABLE THAT IT IS CLEARLY OUT OF COMMONsense LIMITS.
   c. THE PERSON WHO PERFORMS THE REQUEST IS CAPTURED, COERSED, OR DRUNKEN.
