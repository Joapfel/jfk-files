Reference is made to San Francisco memorandum dated January 4, 1968.

Copies of this memorandum have been furnished locally to the U. S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Secret Service, and U. S. Attorney.

A source advised on January 5, 1968, that the following individuals from the Northern California area are planning to depart San Francisco January 13, 1968, via plane for Washington, D. C. to support the demonstration on January 15, 1968. Source indicated that all of these women, excluding NIKKI BRIDGES, participate in activities of the Women's International League for Peace and Freedom (WILPF) in Marin County, California.
