MEMORANDUM FOR : CI/ON

SUBJECT : LICOOKY-1 (201-278841)

An amendment is requested to the Operational Approval of LICOOKY-1 to use her 1) to develop and report on members of the Mexican Writers' Association, 2) to assess her influence on these members and leaders, and 3) to continue her work in the AEM and to become a close confident of Pellicer, Rulfo and other influential leaders of the AEM. This is in compliance with the request set forth in HMMA 26,621, 15 July 1965, a copy of which has been made available to your office.

W. J. Kaufman
