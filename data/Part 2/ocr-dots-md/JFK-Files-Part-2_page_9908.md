Professor FISCHER-GALATI explained that in the absence of a complete denial by Mr. FRITZ that the CIA had any dealings with RIHA at any time, he, therefore, concluded and was convinced the information furnished by Mr. FRITZ did not represent a personal opinion but must have come from within the CIA. He added that inasmuch as it then appeared to him that RIHA had left voluntarily, he also concluded he had no reason to fear for the safety of RIHA. Professor FISCHER-GALATI said that if he had been subsequently quoted in the "Colorado Daily" or any other newspaper as saying that RIHA had left voluntarily and was safe, such quotation would have been correct.

Professor FISCHER-GALATI continued that he did not feel, however, that the explanation about RIHA was adequate and for that reason subsequently directed a letter to the Chief of the Foreign Agents Registration section within the Justice Department, whom he knew well. He thereafter received a reply that the Chief to whom he directed the letter was deceased.

He stated he thereafter directed a second letter to the same section mentioned above within the Justice Department in mid-December, 1969, again inquiring about RIHA, but as yet has received no reply.

He stated he did not know of anything of value ever received by TANNENBAUM as a result of any representation.
