"The racist dog policeman must withdraw immediately from our communities, cease their wanton murder and brutality and torture of Black people, or face the wrath of the armed people."

On Saturday, October 24, the Second Precinct Police Station led an assault on the new headquarters of the Detroit chapter of the National Committee to Combat Fascism, (NCCF).

The attack began about 5:00 P.M. when the Police officers accustomed NCCF members selling Black Panther Newspaper near Myrtle and 14th Street. The Stege continued until about 10 A.M. Sunday morning, when Police arrested fifteen Black youth who were in the NCCF headquarters, under the watchful eyes, and some not so watchful eyes, of citizens of the community. Eight of those arrested were Blacks.

ONE POLICE ATTACKER WAS KILLED AND TWO OTHERS WOUNDED IN THE POLICE SIEGE ON THE NCCF HEADQUARTERS. NO ONE INSIDE THE NCCF HEADQUARTERS WAS HURT DURING THE SIEGE, FEN THOUGH THE POLICE USED ALL KINDS OF POWER-KILL" WEAPONS AND CHEMICAL WARFARE AGENTS. DURING THE ASSAULT ON THE COMMUNITY AROUND MYRTLE AND 16th ST.

"THE TIME WHEN PEOPLE CAN SIT BACK AND VIEW PROCESSES OF INJUSTICE AND INEQUALITY HAS COME TO AN END."

People were forced out of their homes at point and were prevented free passage through their own Community. The Police over, with armed force, the Myrtle and Eleventh Community, and People came to understand that the only way to regain the control of their Community and their lives is to develop a Force greater than that of Police and the other agencies who were and to assist the Police-the State Police Department, and the National Guard were all on alert and had observers in the Siege.

During the assault and siege,crowds of Community people gathered to give symbolic support to the Black youth inside the house or attack. The People were there to show the Community disagreed with the actions they supposed "Protectors of Liberty".

The Crowd was unorganized and had no way to the armedPolice respond to their wishes

WHEN THE POLICE BEGAN TO ATTACK THE CROWD THE PEOPLE BECAME A FRIGHTENED HOPE AND THE ATTACKING POLICE WERE ABLE TO DISPERSE THE CROWD WITH NO RESISTANCE FROM THE PEOPLE THAT HAD GATHERED TO SUPPORT THE YOUTH IN THE HEADQUARTERS ON 16th STREET.
