On January 11, 1973, Mr. Williams submitted a new proposal to purchase SAT for a total price of $5,605,000. Mr. Williams cited a tentative commitment for a loan of $4.0 million and his offer was contingent upon an additional loan. The offer called for a total payment of $5,605,000 broken down as follows:

Acquisition of stock for Actus and SAT $2.145 million

Payment of debt to Air America 3.125 million

Credit for payments to AAM since (10 June) 1972, in liquidation of long term debt .335 million

Total payment $5.605 million

Prior to accepting Mr. Williams' offer, CIA representatives again discussed the sale of SAT to Mr. Williams with the CAB. Mr. R. Tenney Johnson of CAB indicated that the board would be interested in seeing SAT continued. Mr. Johnson stated it would not be necessary to surface the Agency's name as the true owner of SAT in the CAB proceedings, and that he did not anticipate any problems with other supplemental carriers as a result of the sale to Mr. Williams.

On January 19, 1973, the DCI approved the sale of SAT to Mr. Williams. It was noted that Mr. Williams' offer was within 5 percent of the original asking price, was above the independent evaluation for sale as a going concern and was at a figure which would not seem to give the buyer windfall profit.
