This form requests information to permit the Social Security Administration to determine whether the veteran had active military or naval service during World War II (September 16, 1940-- July 24, 1947) or post-World War II (July 25, 1947--December 31, 1956) for which military service wage credits may be granted.

The duplicate copy of this form should be kept in the veteran's military file. If there is ever a subsequent change made in the service or retirement record which affects the information furnished on this form, the Social Security Administration should be advised accordingly.

PART I—The items identifying the veteran are completed by the requesting Social Security Administration office shown in the upper right corner of the form. That office will also check block(s) after “Part II” and/or “Part III” to show the type of information being requested.
