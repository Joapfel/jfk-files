First, with respect to preventing the export of Communist subversion by or through Cuba, there is a summary of what we are doing now, and what we can do in the future.

Second, with respect to assisting the Latin American nations to resist the impact of Communist subversion, there is a summary of our current programs and a listing of specific additional things which we can do in other words, a treatment of the subversive question from two points of view -- therapy as regards Cuba; prophylaxis as regards Latin America at large.

### II. Means to Impose the Constraints of Communist Subversion By and Through Cuba

#### I. Programs Now In Motion.

The following existing programs are all having some adverse effect on the basic capabilities of the Communists to organise, train, teach, equip, underwrite and export subversive strength into Latin America from Cuba:

1. Reconciliation and Surveillance activities, which unite to provide information and to represent a continuing deterrent to subversive movement.

(a) Plague ship patrol of the Windward Passage.

This action contributes to the restriction of illicit Cuban sea traffic to Haiti and Dominican Republic area.

(b) Aircraft patrol flights out of Guatemala.

These flights, augmenting surface surveillance, keep watch for small craft, floating and merchant shipping operating in the Caribbean south of Cuba, and waters adjacent to and north of Jamaica.
