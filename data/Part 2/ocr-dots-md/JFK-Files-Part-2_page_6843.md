Enclosed is a copy of a letter dated April 7, 1953, from the Chief Counsel of the House Subcommittee on Administration of the Internal Revenue Law, requesting permission to inspect the Immigration and Naturalization Service files and the personnel files relating to Henry V. Grumwald including his service with the Office of Alien Property and the Federal Bureau of Investigation.

The Immigration and Naturalization Service files and the Alien Property files have been obtained and will be made available, upon request, to FBI reports, in accordance with policy.

Advice would be appreciated whether the Bureau has a personnel file on Grumwald, and, if so, whether the Bureau would have any objection to making it available, aims any investigative reports, for examination by a Subcommittee representative. If the Bureau has such a file and no objection to its examination, please advise whether the Bureau would prefer to make it available for examination in one of the Bureau's offices or in this office.
