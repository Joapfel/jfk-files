(1) Attacking Cuba resources

(a) Purchase the defection of personnel, aircraft, ships, boats and other valuable items.

(b) Conduct non-attributable actions against petroleum resources, upon which much of Cuba's economy depends. These actions should extend all the way from direct destruction to sabotage through chemical and biological contamination of carriers and storage.

(c) By means of intelligence indicators (aircraft flights, agents, clear radio transmission, beach jumpers) cause Cuban military forces to stand continually at high alert level and thus to consume supplies which could otherwise be dedicated to subversive training.

(2) Diminish Cuban financial resources.

(a) Consider introduction of counterfeit currency in large quantities, to distort the Cuban financial posture, and to undermine confidence.

(3) Create suspicion and disorientation between Cubans and Russians.
