SECRET

Unless sufficient reform is made to satisfy the aspirations of the laborer; and unless sufficient evidence is presented to embryonic intellectuals that Castroism is the greater of the political/economic evils facing them, Castro will continue to find a fertile bed for his seeds.

J. Non-attributable actions

(1) Through third parties, seek and destroy the communist agents, full resources, and subversive capability wherever it exists.

(2) Penetrate communist party labor and youth groups in the Latin American target countries.

Objectives would include:

(a) Publicized exposure of the apparatus.

(b) Persecution of subversive plans and approval of leaders by government authority.

(c) Counterespulsion against future subversive planning.

(3) Instigate and stage selected terrorist incidents in Latin American countries, designed to instigate Castro.

(4) Armage for caches of Soviet-Czech arms to be "discovered" in selected Latin American countries, ostensibly smuggled in from Cuba.
