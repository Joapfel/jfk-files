Re: Testimony of Retired FBI
Special Agent (SA) Robert R.
Nichols Before SSC Executive Session

John Smith of Senator Buddleston's office asked the difference between a bug and a wiretap and Nichols responded that a bug was a microphone implanted on the premises whereas a wiretap was an interception of a telephone line. Smith then asked if there was a bug on King or the SCIC in Atlanta and Nichols responded "not to my knowledge." Seidel asked about any other places in Atlanta where there may have been bugs on King and Nichols advised he had no knowledge of any. Seidel asked if Nichols was familiar with a Willard Hotel (Washington, D. C.) incident involving King and Nichols responded that he was not.

Original and one copy to the Attorney General copy for forwarding to the White House. Debriefing of Nichols, including telephonic contact from him 6/2/75, by S. F. Phillips.
