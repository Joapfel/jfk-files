Re: INTELLIGENCE COVERAGE OF DIPLOMATIC REPRESENTATIVES OF FRIENDLY FOREIGN NATIONS

65-63113 (Bufile)
ISINT
65-58700 (Bufile)

The Bureau is aware of the delicate problem involved in developing sources and informants in the Israeli field, which problem has been pointed out in previous surveys made by the recipient offices. However, in view of the valuable information that may be obtained through the development of good sources in this field, the Bureau desires that you redouble your efforts in this regard. It is important that you keep in mind in connection with this matter the necessity of carefully handling all phases of the development of this program to assure that there will be no embarrassment to the Government and to the Bureau.
