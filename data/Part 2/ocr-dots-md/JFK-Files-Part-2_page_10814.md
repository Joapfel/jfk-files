TOSI was given an Interrogation; Advice of Rights form which she read and she was also verbally advised of what that rights form meant. She stated she understood what the form said, and her rights, but she did not wish to sign this form, nor would she give any information regarding her arrest or the charges against her.

She was again advised that the basic charge against her was for conspiracy in connection with the destruction of government property. She was photographed, fingerprinted and strip searched by Bureau employee TRUDY A. CROUTHARMEL. She was allowed to rest and walk around in the room where she was being interviewed and she used the women's rest room a few times and obtained drinks or water whenever she requested. She was asked continually if she were all right and if she wanted anything and each time she would either say "no" or "I am fine."
