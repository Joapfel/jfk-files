September 30, 1963

TO: Members of Subcommittee on Castro-Communist Subversion.

FROM: Chairman, Subcommittee on Cuban Subversion

SUBMIT: Report for July - August on Actions Taken to Combat Castro-Communist Subversion.

Attached is a draft of the Subcommittee's report for July and August on actions taken to respondent Castro-Communist subversion in the hemisphere. The draft has been prepared on the basis of the material which you furnished with certain modifications. The CIA number will note that I have extracted from his submission the ideas of action taken and placed there in the body of the report. The Intelligence Information contained in the submission I have made into an annex. I think it is very useful to have the intelligence data form part of the report, and request that in the future the CIA number prepare his submission in two parts.

May I have your comments on the draft by close of business October 1, 1963.

Copies to:

CIA - Mr. Wheeler (2)
DOD - Col.usi (2)
USIA - Mr. Vagley (1)
BAR - Mr. Hott (1)
Justice - Mr. Geoghegan (1)
