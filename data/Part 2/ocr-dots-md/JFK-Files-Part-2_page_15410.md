BURMA ACTIVITIES IN THE U. S.
IS - BURMA

The Washington Field Office (WFO) advised by letter 3/1/60 that SA Frank B. Still, Jr. met Mr. Harlan C. Westover, Chancellor, Embassy of Burma, Washington, D. C., at a cocktail party during the 1959 Christmas and New Year holidays. Westover appeared friendly and indicated he did most of the work for the First Secretary at the Embassy of Burma. His work consists mostly of handling the Embassy accounts and purchase agreements with American firms. WFO believes Westover would be amenable to further contact.

We are in need of additional informants in the Burmese field and WFO desires authority to conduct an investigation of Westover leading toward his development as a possible source or informant regarding Burmese matters.

OBSERVATIONS:

In view of the strategic location of Burma, which borders China, and the fact it is in an area of constant unrest, believe Westover, if developed, as a source or informant, could furnish us information which may be of interest to the State Department and other U. S. Government agencies. The Bureau's responsibilities regarding developments affecting the internal security of the U. S. along with keeping other U. S. Government agencies informed of information received through our sources and informants warrants us at this time to secure State Department clearance for a program to develop adequate informants coverage within Burmese diplomatic establishments in the U. S.

RECOMMENDATION:

That liaison discuss orally with an appropriate official in the Bureau of Security and Consular Affairs, Department of State, the possibility of this Bureau developing live sources within Burmese diplomatic establishments in the U. S. If it is approved, also secure clearance for very discreet investigations of potential sources which we will thereafter select.
