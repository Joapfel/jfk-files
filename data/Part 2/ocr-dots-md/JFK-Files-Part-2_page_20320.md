Demonstration Protesting United
States Policy in Vietnam,
Washington, D. C.
January 15, 1968

On January 11, 1968, Mr. John Martin, Passenger Representative, Pennsylvania Railroad, Pennsylvania Station, New York City, stated he had been informed by the New York office of the Jeannette Rankin Brigade (JRB) that the overflow of women traveling to Washington, D. C., on January 15, 1968, by train would be handled by buses furnished through the All State Bus Corporation, Brooklyn, New York. These buses would depart from the vicinity of Pennsylvania Station, New York City.

On January 11, 1968, Mr. Robert Stimell, Sales Representative, All State Bus Corporation, 3163 Coney Island Avenue, Brooklyn, New York, advised that his company had contracted with JRB to furnish five buses to depart from 31st Street and Eighth Avenue (vicinity Pennsylvania Station), New York City, at 7 A.M., January 15, 1968. These buses have a total seating capacity of 245. The All State Bus Corporation has received a deposit of $250 on January 11, 1968, from Miriam Thompson of JRB. Stimell advised that the All State Bus Corporation was acting as the bus broker with JRB, and it was anticipated that the actual buses used would come from the New York and Keansburg Line, a New Jersey-based company.
