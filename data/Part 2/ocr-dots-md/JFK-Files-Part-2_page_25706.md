16 October 1965

MEMORANDUM FOR
THE
SUBJECT
°Ambassador notified:

: The Ambassador
: Lee O'SULLO/Contact with the Soviet Embassy

**1. The following information has received**

On 1 October 1965, an American made contact with the Soviet Ambassador and identified himself as La: OSWAD. This officer has informed the Soviet Ambassador that he has been at the Soviet embassy on 26 September 1955 and has told him about Valery Yelchinov, a partner of the Ambassador. In addition to being at the Soviet embassy he receives a reply from the Ambassador indicating his request. He also is supplying information with regard to this request.

**2. Our Envoys are informed so long as the OSWA above is producing until end of October 1959 to be in Russia, Belarus, a former member of the U. S. Marine Corps who defected to the Soviet Union in October 1979.

**3. This office will advise you if additional information on this matter is needed.**
