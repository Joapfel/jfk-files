In Bolivia, I was interested in particularly the Bolivian Air Force's work of setting up a public school system over in the eastern slopes of the Andes-- down in the jungles and in the very sparse settlements. They were very enthusiastically setting up one-room school houses down there, flying in teachers and bringing some education in there, which I thought was a great project, and encouraging them.
