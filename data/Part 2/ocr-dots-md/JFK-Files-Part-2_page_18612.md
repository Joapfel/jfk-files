May 17, 1965

MEMORANDUM FOR MR. TOLSON
MR. DELL'GONT
MR. DE LOACH
MR. SULLIVAN

President Lyndon B. Johnson called and asked if I would check one of his (as they want him to be the Chief of the Army in that position) affairs, which would, in effect, control two cities. The President stated he did not know his first name. I asked if he was in the Dominican Republic at the present time. The President stated he was; that they were getting pretty close to a settlement with them and he thought we had checked him out, but anyway I had the whole list and he wants to be sure they are checked and that there are none who are known communists because that is one thing both the right and left wings would attack him on, so the only way to prevent any, the President, is to be sure they are not Communists. The President stated another thing is how are they going to get rid of the Communists when we do get a government. I stated that is going to depend on the strength of the one in power.

I told the President that in connection with one of our agents who is going down to Puerto Rico with us on the next flight plane, we have an Agent who is going on the same plane, but as to whether we can get a microphone in, we do not know because he is living in a house rather than a hotel. I stated one of our Agents is driving—go ahead to the airport in his car so he will have an opportunity to talk to him. The President asked if an agent knew he was an agent and I stated he would as he had just reviewed a letter from the Agent on the plane will not be known to him. I stated what I said before. Arrive in Puerto Rico, a surveillance will be put on him but if he stays with us we anticipate, it will be difficult to cover except the telephones. I stated there are five lines operating between Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic and we have all five lines covered.
