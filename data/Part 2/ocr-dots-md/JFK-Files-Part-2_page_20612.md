TELETYPE TO DETROIT, ET AL

WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION
ACTION COMMITTEE (WONAC)
CONVENTION, 2/11-13/72,
BOSTON, MASSACHUSETTS

NOTE:

WONAAC is sponsoring national convention in Boston,
Massachusetts. Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) and Socialist
Workers Party (SWP) presently dominating and controlling many
WONAAC chapters throughout the country. Imperative that we
have appropriate coverage at this convention in view of the
large number of YSA and SWP members who are expected to attend
This is the second informant authorized to attend this
convention. The informant is a female active in SWP and
Women's Liberation activities in Detroit. She was specifically
requested to attend this convention by party leaders. Although
Detroit does not indicate estimated expenditures, it is to be
noted informant will be traveling by automobile with four other
females from the Detroit area. Her expenditures for expenses
would obviously be minimal.
