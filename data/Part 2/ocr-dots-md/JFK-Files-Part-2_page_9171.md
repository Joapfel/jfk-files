NEIL BREEN, Lieutenant, Special Services Unit,
United Nations (UN) Security and Safety Section, was inter-
viewed in the vicinity of 43rd Street and UN Plaza, New
York, New York. Lieutenant BREEN furnished the following
information:

On Wednesday, August 7, 1974, he was on duty in the UN General Assembly (UNGA) Building, when he was notified by Sergeant KITCHELL that an explosive device had been discovered in the Meditation Room. Lieutenant BREEN advised that he then participated in the clearing of the UN lobby of all visitors. He advised that the New York City Police Department (NYCPD) Bomb Squad arrived shortly thereafter and removed the explosive device through the UN main entrance.

Lieutenant BREEN advised that he then participated with other members of the Special Services Unit in the interview of UN security personnel on duty that morning. Lieutenant BREEN advised that the Meditation Room is open only from the hours of 9:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., seven days a week. He advised that the room is normally cleaned daily, during the morning hours usually. He further stated that no smoking is permitted in the Meditation Room and that no packages are allowed to be carried into the room. Lieutenant BREEN advised that the room is a small, dark enclosure frequently used for purposes of prayer and meditation by both UN delegates and visitors. He added that normally there are three UN security officers on duty in the main lobby of the UN at all times.

Lieutenant BREEN stated that the officers on duty are instructed to search incoming packages and that visitors are invited to check packages and related items at the UN main gate prior to entry into the UN Building. He advised that on occasion various ethnic and political groups during the course of demonstrations in the UN had staged sit-down strikes, but that there had never been any warning or indication regarding the use of explosives. Lieutenant BREEN advised that he could furnish no further information regarding the incident at the present time.
