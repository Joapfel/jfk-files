DEMONSTRATION PROTESTING UNITED STATES POLICY IN VIETNAM, WASHINGTON, D.C.

JANUARY 15, 1968
INFORMATION CONCERNING
(INTERNAL SECURITY)

On January 9, 1968, A.T. Nardone, General Traffic Agent, Public Service Coordinated Transport, Newark, New Jersey, advised that one Mrs. Darnell, 204 Eastbourne Terrace, Moorestown, New Jersey, has made a reservation for one Public Service Bus, capacity fifty persons, to leave Cherry Hill, New Jersey, Shopping Center Mall at 8:00 AM, on January 15, 1968, to arrive at Union Station, Washington, D.C., the same date. The company has been advised that the group plans to participate in a demonstration in Washington, D.C.

The bus is to return to Cherry Hill, New Jersey, the same day, leaving from somewhere in the Georgetown area of Washington, D.C., at about 6:00 PM.
