A second source, an agency which collects
intelligence data in the Chicago area, has advised
in the past that Russ Meek is a militant Negro who
has made a number of speeches in the Chicago area
expressing hatred for whites. Meek has claimed that
law enforcement officers are determined to exterminate
black people. Meek has also urged that Negroes
arm themselves.

According to this source, the Black Impeachment Committee (BIC) is a "paper" organization which has been created by Meek. BIC is without any active membership.

The Black Liberation Alliance (BLA) is a community action group focusing attention on local organizing through local community councils, working on a daily basis with members of the black community on such problems as housing, education, health and welfare problems.
