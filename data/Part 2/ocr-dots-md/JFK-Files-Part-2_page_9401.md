## K. Meetings and Rallies

DE T-14 advised on December 14, 1971, that BOBBY GEORGE SEALE, Minister of Defense, BPP, arrived in Detroit from Oakland, California, on December 9, 1971, spent the evening with the Detroit BPP and on December 10, 1971, proceeded to Ann Arbor, Michigan, where he spoke at the John Sinclair Freedom Rally, University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, Michigan.

DE T-14 heard SEALE comment to a small group of people that the BPP is opening free food clinics and that "if the pigs interfere with him in any way, he will open free gun clinics and distribute free guns". SEALE further commented that "we have access to all the guns we want and will kill any pig that gives us trouble". DE T-14 advised that SEALE spoke regarding plans and programs of the BPP.

JOHN SINCLAIR is chairman of the White Panther Party (WP), also known as the Rainbow Peoples Party.

The WPPI is a national white, hippie-oriented revolutionary organization which was founded around the time of the Vietnam War and received support to the BPP.
The party has added a ten-point program of its own, all of which call for the elimination of racial discrimination and freedom of the individual.
