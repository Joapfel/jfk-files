WOMEN'S NATIONAL MARCH ON THE PENTAGON, APRIL TEN
NINETEEN SEVENTYONE; INTERNAL SECURITY - INFORMATION CONCERNING.

ON APRIL EIGHT INSTANT, AT TWELVE FORTYFIVE PM, SPECIAL AGENTS OF THE FBI OBSERVED ABOUT TWELVE WHITE FEMALE INDIVIDUALS IN THEIR EARLY TWENTIES ASSEMBLE AND HOLD A PRESS CONFERENCE NEAR THE FRONT OF THE JUSTICE DEPARTMENT BUILDING, TENTH AND PENNSYLVANIA AVENUE, NW, ENTRANCE. TWO OF THE FEMALES UNFOLDED AND HELD A LARGE BANNER INSCRIBED, QUOTE WOMEN'S MARCH, APRIL TEN DASH A FRESH WIND BLOWS AGAINST THE EMPIRE UNQUOTE. TWO OTHER MEMBERS OF THE GROUP SOPKE. THE FIRST ONE STATED THEMARCH ON APRIL TEN TO THE PENTAGON WAS NOT UNDER THE SPONSORSHIP OF ANY SINGLE GROUP; THAT THE MARCH WAS CALLED TO HAVE WOME FROM ALL GROUPS JOIN IN TO PROTEST THE WAS IN VIETNAM. THE SECOND SPEAKER IDENTIFIED AS LAURIE END PAGE ONE.
