J. Non-attributable actions

(1) Investigate and stage selected terrorist incidents in Latin American countries, designed to implicate Castro.

(2) Arrange for withdrawal of Soviet-Czech arms to be "discovered" in selected Latin American countries, extensively smuggled into Cuba.

IV. Conclusion.

It is the belief of the Committee that the actions outlined in the preceding sections comprise a series of steps which in some areas will stem and in others attack the program of Cuba-based Communist underground operations in Latin America. Each individual action embodies some element of usefulness in itself, and any combination of actions may generate correspondingly favorable results. There is no profound relationship which demands the combining of any specific set of actions.

It is recommended, therefore, that an early selection be made of those actions listed above whose execution is compatible with national obligations and dedications in other areas and which are within U.S. capabilities, and that they be put quickly into effect since the longer the subversive influences in the Hemisphere are permitted to mature, the more difficult it will be their ultimate elimination.

And finally, recognizing the diverse nature of the Cold War activities involved, it is strongly recommended that a single point in the Government be designated and assigned responsibility for supervising execution of the total program.
