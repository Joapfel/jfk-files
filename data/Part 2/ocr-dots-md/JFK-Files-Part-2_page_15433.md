Memorandum

TO : W. C. Sullivan

FROM : S. B. Donahoe

DATE: October 12, 1961

1 - Liaison
1 - Mr. G.C. Moore

SUBJECT: INTELLIGENCE COVERAGE OF FRIENDLY FOREIGN COUNTRIES

In my memorandum of 10-6-61 it was pointed out that we had proposed to the Department of State the expansion of this program to fifteen Central and South American countries. The program involves the developing of live sources in diplomatic establishments in the U.S. of foreign countries with emphasis on countries faced with communist subversion, Sino-Soviet bloc activity or revolutionary situations. We felt the existing situation in Latin America warranted this expansion. In view of the fact that U. Alexis Johnson, Deputy Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs, declined State clearance for this expansion it was recommended and approved we go on record with letters to Secretary of State Rusk and the Attorney General informing them of the program and its merit and pointing out we were unable to further implement this program in view of the State Department decision.

There is an additional factor here which warrants consideration. Mr. U. Alexis Johnson, with whom we discussed this matter at State, has been extremely cooperative with the Bureau from time to time. He was particularly helpful in connection with the opening of the office of the Legal Attache in Manila. In fact, on our behalf, he exerted pressure on the U.S. Ambassador there in order to secure his approval of the establishing of the office. He undoubtedly can be helpful in the future on such matters.

For the above reasons it is believed highly desirable to send a letter to Johnson directly rather than to Rusk. Johnson is a top-level official and we will have served our purpose by putting him on notice and outlining our views.

ACTION:

Attached are letters to Deputy Under Secretary of State Johnson and the Attorney General.
