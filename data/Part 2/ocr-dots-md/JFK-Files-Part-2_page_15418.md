INDONESIAN ACTIVITIES IN THE U.S.
IS - INDONESIA

Reference is made to memorandum from Mr. Donahoe to you dated 6-15-60 in the above-captioned matter. Liaison was instructed to talk with State regarding the possibility of the Bureau's developing live sources within Indonesian diplomatic establishments in the U.S., and if approved secure clearance for very discreet investigations of potential sources which will be selected in the future.

Instant matter was discussed on 6-17-60 by Mr. Emond of
the Liaison Section with Mr. Frank Meloy, Assistant to Mr. Raymond
A. Hare, Deputy Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs.

On 7-19-60 Mr. Meloy advised Mr. Bartlett of the Liaison
Section that after considering this matter State has come to the
conclusion that the successful penetration of Indonesian diplomatic
establishments within the U.S. would be of real interest to State;
that the results would be interesting and useful and that State has
decided to grant approval to the Bureau to proceed with this matter.
This approval also carries with it, clearance for discreet investi-
gations of potential sources.
