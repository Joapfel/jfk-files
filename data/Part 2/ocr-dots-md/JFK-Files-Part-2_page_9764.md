This Bureau will continue the above policy under Executive Order 10450; however, should you determine that Security Hearing Boards may hear witnesses only in the presence of the employee and his counsel, it will be appreciated if you will immediately advise this Bureau so that all individuals interviewed who may later be requested to testify before a Security Hearing Board will clearly understand that such testimony must be in the presence of the employee and his counsel and they may be cross-examined by the employee or his counsel.

It can be anticipated that, in connection with some hearings held under Executive Order 10450, the hearing board may request agents of this Bureau to appear and testify. In this connection, this Bureau would associate it if the policy followed in this respect under Executive Order 9335 could be continued. This policy was set forth in a confidential memorandum from the Attorney General dated December 11, 1947, to all department and agency heads of the Executive Branch of the Government, which stated that Special Agents of this Bureau will testify before hearing boards where they are in a position to furnish competent evidence. They will not, however, participate otherwise in the proceedings nor will they be made available merely to interpret information appearing in their investigative reports. It is suggested you may wish to furnish such information to all departments and agencies of the Executive Branch of the Government.

Previous memoranda to you observed that as Executive Order 10450 is an Executive Order, it is assumed its coverage is limited to the Executive Branch of the Government. In this connection, the question may arise as to whether the General Accounting Office, the Government Printing Office, and the Library of Congress might be permitted to participate in the procedures affected under Executive Order 10450. You may recall that they have participated in the procedures of Executive Order 9335. You may therefore desire to ascertain from the General Accounting Office, the Government Printing Office, and the Library of Congress whether they wish to participate in the procedures prescribed by Executive Order 10450, and if they do, desire to participate, determine whether the provisions of Executive Order 10450 authorize such participation.
