WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WONAAC)

AT TWO P.M., APRIL SIXTEEN, NINETEEN SEVENTY-TWO,
APPROXIMATELY SIXTY INDIVIDUALS COUNTER DEMONSTRATED AGAINST
THE KNIGHTS OF COLUMBUS PARADE, VICINITY OF FIFTH
AVENUE AND EIGHTY SIXTH STREET NYC. THIS COUNTER-DEMON-
STRATION WAS SPONSORED BY WONAAC ADVOCATING REMOVAL OF
ALL RESTRICTIVE ANTI-ABORTION LAWS. COUNTER-DMONSTRATION.
CONCLUDED FOUR P.M., NO INCIDENTS OR ARRESTS.

CHARACTERIZATION

WHOSE AIM IS TO EFFECT THE CHANGE IN ABORTION LAWS
