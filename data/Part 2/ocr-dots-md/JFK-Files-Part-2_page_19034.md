SECRET

SAMUEL ADASON JAFFE

Recent Contacts with Soviets

In response to questions, Jaffe stated that
with the exception of the contact initiated by Boris
Sedov concerning a photograph of Jaffe taken in Red Square,
all recent contacts with Soviet nationals, have resulted
from Jaffe's initiative. He stated he has invited the
following Soviets to his residence for social functions:

Boris Georgievich Strelnikov, "Pravda" correspon-
dent, who lives in the same apartment building as Jaffe;

Mikhail Korneyevich Polonik, Counselor, Political
Division, Soviet Embassy; D.C. RUSSELY.

Valentin Mikhailovich Kamenev, Counselor, Cultural
Division, Soviet Embassy; D.C. RUSSIA.

Oleg Danilovich Kalugin, First Secretary, Press
Division, Soviet Embassy; D.C. RUSSIA.

Viktor Vasilyevich Kopytin, Tass correspondent;
Kopytin has been reported as connected
with KGB.

Leonid Yefimovich Lipovetskiy, Radio Moscow
correspondent; Russia DC

Yuriy Vladimirovich Barsukov, "Izvestia" correspon-
dent.
Barsukov has been reported as connected
with KGB.

Jaffe said he and his wife were invited out for
a drink with Kopytin and his wife, whom Jaffe described as
very attractive. Jaffe stated: "I had a couple of drinks.
They picked up the check."

Jaffe said he also had contact with a Radio Prague
correspondent. Jaffe described him as a very young, capable
individual for whom Jaffe has a high regard.
