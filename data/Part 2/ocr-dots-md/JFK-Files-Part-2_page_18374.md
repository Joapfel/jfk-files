REBUNITEL SEPTEMBER 17, 1975.

FORMER SA IRVING R. ANDERSON WAS CONTACTED THIS A.M.
AND ADVISED OF INFORMATION SET FORTH IN RETEL. SA
ANDERSON'S INITIAL REACTION WAS THAT IF CONTACTED BY THE
SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE STAFF FOR INTERVIEW HE WOULD LIKE
TO "TELL THEM TO GO TO H". HOWEVER, HE FELT THAT
HE TOOK THIS STANCE, HE WOULD HURT THE BUREAU INASMUCH
AS THE BUREAU HAS PLEDGED COOPERATION WITH THE SENATE
SELECT COMMITTEE. HE SAID HE WOULD NOT WANT TO TAKE
ANY ACTION, EVEN THOUGH NOW A FORMER EMPLOYEE, TO HURT
THE BUREAU IN THE FUTURE. HE STATED THAT HE SERVED THE
BUREAU FOR MANY YEARS, STILL FEELS HE IS PART OF THE
BUREAU, AND IS DEDICATED TO BEING USEFUL TO THE BUREAU
IN ANY MANNER WHATSOEVER.
