d. Movement of Personnel.

Through multilateral and bilateral political arrangements with Latin American states, seek to impede the movement of persons into and out of Cuba. (This is to be expanded by State).

e. Establish the Physical and Economic Capability of Castro Cuba to Train and Export Subordinates.

It is evident that ready funds, in considerable quantity, are required to conduct a substantive training program, not to speak of other costly factors — arms, demolitions, fuel, food, propaganda, etc. Specific actions aimed at reducing the Cuban's resources will thus have a direct effect upon their ability to pursue their program of subversion, will cause them to turn to these other internal problems and will give the Russians pause as to the wisdom of pursuing a program which is costly and marginally productive. Likewise, every action which contributes to instability, unrest, apprehension and disension between Russian and Cuban will reduce correspondingly both their ability and their opportunity to drive forward on a program of substantive training and exportation.
