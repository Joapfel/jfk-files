With approval of Attorney General (1/31/62), we instituted program to expand use of technical surveillances on diplomatic establishments of countries outside Soviet bloc. Following is an analysis of this program and its current status:

We advised Attorney General 1/26/62 that on selective and probing basis we would explore coverage of following countries under this program by instituting coverage, on six or eight establishments at a time:

* Argentina
* Brazil
* Republic of Congo
* Guinea
* India
* Indonesia
* Japan
* Laos
* Mali
* Mexico
* Morocco
* Panama
* Portugal
* Thailand
* Venezuela
* South Vietnam

After necessary surveys and State clearance in each instance, initial coverage was initiated early March, 1962, and to date coverage on following establishments has been instituted:

* Embassy of Argentina
* Embassy of Brazil
* Brazilian Delegation to United Nations (UN)
* Congolese Delegation to UN
* Embassy of Guinea
* Guinea Delegation to UN
* Embassy of India
* Indian Delegation to UN
* Embassy of Indonesia
* Indonesian Delegation to UN
* Embassy of Japan
* Embassy of Mali
* Embassy of Mexico
* Mexican Delegation to UN
* Embassy of Panama
* Embassy of Portugal
* Portuguese Delegation to UN
* Embassy of Venezuela
* Venezuelan Delegation to UN

Of the above-listed establishments, as of this date we have productive coverage on following (others discontinued as unproductive):

* Embassy of Argentina
* Embassy of Brazil
* Embassy of Mexico
* Embassy of Panama
* Embassy of Venezuela
