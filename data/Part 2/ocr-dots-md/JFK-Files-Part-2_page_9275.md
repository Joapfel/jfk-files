DE T-4
July 12, 1972

L. Teaching Revolutionary Programs and Policies

Political education classes are no longer regularly
held on Sundays. The classes are now held at random during
the week as the BPP is currently more concerned with its
survival programs.

DE T-2
September 14, 1972

M. Travel of Party Members

On May 6, 1972, LONNIE DARDEN and JODY WEAVER flew to Oakland, California. On May 9, 1972, DARDEN returned to Detroit. JODY WEAVER remained in California.

DE T-3
May 9, 1972

ROLAND GARY REID and WILLIAM ARTHUR ELDER are presently in California and do not intend to return to Detroit.

DE T-2
July 17, 1972

WILLIAM ARTHUR ELDER has been an active member of the BPP in Detroit.

On August 21, 1972, LOUIS TOLBERT JOHNSON travelled to California to help work in the BPP campaign to elect BOBBY SEALE as Mayor of Oakland, California.
