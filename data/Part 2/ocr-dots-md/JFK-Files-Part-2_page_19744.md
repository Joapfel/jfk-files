WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WONAC), SPONSORED DEMONSTRATION, NOVEMBER TWENTY, NINETEEN SEVENTYONE, WASHINGTON, D.C. IS-NEW LEFT (OO: WFO).

ON NOVEMBER NINETEEN, SEVENTYONE, A SOURCE RELIABLE IN THE PAST, ADVISED THAT THE WOMEN'S RIGHT TO CHOOSE OF MARYLAND THREE ZERO TWO EIGHT GREENMOUNT AVE., BALTIMORE, MARYLAND, HAD HOPED TO CHARTER A BUS TO TRANSPORT PARTICIPANTS TO CAPTIONED RALLY, BUT LACK OF INTEREST MAKES USE OF A BUS DOUBTFUL.

A SECOND SOURCE, RELIABLE IN THE PAST, STATED THAT REPRESENTATIVES OF WOMEN'S LIBERATION HAD ATTEMPTED TO SOLICIT SUPPORT FOR CAPTIONED RALLY AMONG CITY COLLECTIVES WITH LITTLE SUCCESS.

A THIRD SOURCE, RELIABLE IN THE PAST, ADVISED THAT THERE WAS LITTLE ENTHUSIASM FOR CAPTIONED RALLY AMONG BALTIMORE AREA RESIDENTS AND FEW PARTICIPANTS WERE EXPECTED TO ATTEND FROM BALTIMORE AREA.
