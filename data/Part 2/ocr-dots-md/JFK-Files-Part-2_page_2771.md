## COURSE OBJECTIVES - CONTENT AND METHODS

The Midcareer Executive Development Course, a part of the Midcareer Training Program, is designed to enable potential executive officers to develop and widen their understanding of the Agency, of the Government, and of international affairs.

The Course consists of three major segments, each of approximately two weeks duration. The topics covered through lectures, seminars, panel discussions and field trips are:

1. The functions, relationships and problems of various Agency components.

2. U.S. domestic problems and their effect on Government policy.

3. Current developments in key international affairs.

## ACHIEVEMENT RECORD

No evaluation of the student is made during the course, and no final grade is given upon course completion.
