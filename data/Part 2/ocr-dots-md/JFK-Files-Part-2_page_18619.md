Convert to Markdown# Memorandum for Messrs. Tolson, Belmont,
DeLoach, and Sullivan

August 24, 1965

I stated that I think we are going to have trouble down there but I think CIA can handle it with their force. He replied that they will be delighted to have us stay but they certainly appreciate the fact we may need our people elsewhere. I said we need them in this country where the riots are occurring; that this is not really our field; that it is CIA's field; and that the sooner we get back to our own field, the better.

I again stated it is very likely the President may speak to
Admiral Rabora about this and he thanked me for calling.

John Edgar Hoover
Director
