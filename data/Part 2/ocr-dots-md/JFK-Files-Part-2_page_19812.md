Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) and Socialist Workers Party (SWP) presently dominating and controlling many WONAAC chapters throughout the country. Imperative that we have appropriate coverage at this convention in view of the large number of YSA and SWP members who are expected to attend This is the second informant authorized to attend this convention. Although Detroit does not indicate estimated expenditures, it is to be noted informant will be traveling by automobile with four other females from the Detroit area. Her expenditures for expenses would obviously be minimal.
