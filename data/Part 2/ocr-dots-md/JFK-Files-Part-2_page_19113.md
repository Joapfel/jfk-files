ADD ABC, MOSCOW (UPI-17)

JAFFE WAS SUMMONED TO THE SOVIET MINISTRY TODAY AND GIVEN THE ORDER
TO LEAVE.

HE LATER TOLD REPORTERS IT WAS NOT CLEAR WHETHER THE RUSSIANS
HAD ORDERED THE BUREAU CLOSED OR MERELY HIS EXPULSION. BUT HE SAID
HIS EXPULSION WOULD IN EFFECT BE CLOSING DOWN THE BUREAU.

IT WAS UNDERSTOOD A VISA HAD BEEN DENIED FOR JAFFE'S SCHEDULED
SUCCESSOR AS MOSCOW CORRESPONDENT. JAFFE HAD PLANNED TO LEAVE MOSCOW
IN DECEMBER FOR REASSIGNMENT.
