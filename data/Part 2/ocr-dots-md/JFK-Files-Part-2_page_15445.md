NATIONALITY GROUP COMMISSION - CHILE
INTERNAL SECURITY - CHILE

A recent State Department report assessing the Communists' activity in Chile (CC) observes the basic objective of the CC is to weaken Chile's ties with the U. S. and moving it toward the Soviet Union and other blue countries. The same report notes the efforts of the Soviet Union and Communist China to increase trade with Chile but concludes that negotiations in this field are primarily a cover for political and intelligence operations and a means for carrying out functions of a diplomatic mission. The possibility exists that Chile could take a favorable position with regard to the Soviet Union and blue countries.

The Bureau in order to be in a position to obtain current intelligence data concerning Chile on April 4, 1962, obtained State Department clearance to discreetly institute investigation designed to develop sources at the Chilean establishments.

Accordingly, the recipient should institute a survey for the purpose of identifying persons employed at the Agency of Chile, Washington, D. C., the Chilean mission to the United Nations and the Consulate of Chile, both in New York City, particularly United States citizens, who might procure potential sources or informants. Submit your requests for investigation in this regard in separate 134 cases with a request for Bureau clearance to institute investigation in each individual case.

Submit the results of your initial survey under instant caption to reach the Bureau by May 20, 1962, and thereafter on a 90-day basis setting forth progress being made to develop confidential sources and informants until such time as adequate coverage is assured.
