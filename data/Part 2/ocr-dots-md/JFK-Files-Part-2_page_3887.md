## MCKELIBEN SPEAKS

In his talk Friday before the ALBC, McKeliben stated: "I would never betray the confidence the people have placed in me. If your faith in me has been shaken at all, I want you to know you are looking at a man who has never had anything to do with them (racists), and I will never have anything to do with them."

He said there is no doubt that came from Carlos Marceola if he did the same.

Young has claimed to receive two such telegrams, but Life says at present as far as may be known, the telegrams were a personal communication to the governor, sent down to the state by the telephone.

Addressing the group, McKeliben said the public should know we are not the only ones trying to get our way.
