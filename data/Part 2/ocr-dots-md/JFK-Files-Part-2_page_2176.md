# MEMORANDUM

TO :

FROM : James A. Noel

SUBJECT : $10,000 Optional Insurance

This memorandum is authorization to cancel the $10,000 additional optional life insurance coverage under the Federal Employees Group Life Insurance Program which I elected to take in February 1968. I request that this cancellation be effective as of 3 May 1969.

James A. Noel
