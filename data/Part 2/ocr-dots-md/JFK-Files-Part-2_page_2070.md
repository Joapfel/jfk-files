SECURITY INFORMATION

MAR 20 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR THE SECRETARY OF STATE

ATTENTION : Mr. W. Park Armstrong, Jr.

SUBJECT : Boaz, James Arthur, Request for Appointment in the Foreign Service

REFERENCE : Appendix 5-b to Memorandum of 23 November 1951 Subject, Representation in Foreign Service Missions

ENCLOSURES : a. Application Forms 57 and DP-34 b. Medical Forms 88 and 89 c. Occupational History Supplement d. Proposed Biography

1. It is requested that Mr. James Arthur Noel, U.S. D.C., 59600 be appointed in the Foreign Service with the title of Attaché, 102-3, 58481, for duty in the American Embassy at Caracas, Venezuela. Mr. Noel will replace Mr. Robert E. Dahlgren who occupies position number one as listed in above reference.

2. Mr. Noel has been a member of the Foreign Service of the United States since January 1931. He served for 10 years as vice-consul with the Department of State at various locations in Mexico. Mr. Noel joined this agency's predecessor organization in 1904 and was assigned to Spain as Chief of Station at Bilbao and Barcelona, and as Intelligence Officer at Madrid. He subsequently became an employee of this agency in 1947 and has since served as Chief of Station in Guatemala at Guatemala City, and in Argentina at Buenos Aires.

3. It is requested that subject arrive at his destination on or about 15 May 1952.

Richard Held
for
LYNN B. KINGMAN
Assistant Director
