The Women's National Abortion Action Coalition

A National WOMEN'S structure proposal was presented, which stated in part as follows:

>All women are welcome in WOMEN: Black, Latina, Chicana, Native American and Asian women, gay women, high school, college, and working women, women from political organizations, trade unions, the military, and communities across the country. We are united in uncompromising struggle for our common goal: the right of all women to control fully their own reproductive lives..."

"The National Conference shall be the highest decision-making body of WOMEN, and shall operate on the basis of one woman, one vote.

"There shall be a National Coordinating Committee (NCC) composed of one woman from each national organization, area coalition, and group supporting and building WOMEN...

"There shall also be a Working Committee (WC), which is a sub-committee of the NCC and which is composed of the project directors or coordinators, the elected national staff, and one woman from each coalition, committee, group and national organization supporting and building WOMEN in the locale of the national office..."

In addition to the above, a proposal "that the National Office of the Women's National Abortion Action Coalition establish an emergency cash fund, from which to contribute significant amounts of money to organizations and individuals who are working to achieve goals compatible with those of WOMEN."

"That the National Office of WOMEN, as its first act of financial support, pledge $2,000 to the Kennedy-Schulder suit against the tax-exempt status of the Roman Catholic Church, since the Church has violated restrictions on its political activity by lobbying for anti-abortion legislation."
