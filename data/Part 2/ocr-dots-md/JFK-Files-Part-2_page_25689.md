Convert to MarkdownSUBJECT: REQUEST OF SILVIA DURAN

THIS AGENT'S STATION IN MEXICO, WAS ADVISED ON 27 NOVEMBER THAT EXTERNAL AUTHORITIES HAD REQUESTED SILVIA DURAN, MEXICAN NATIONAL EMPLOYER OF THE COBAH SECRETARY IN MEXICO, WHO HAD TALKED TO THE OSGUIND AT THE COBAH SECRETARY IN LATE SEPTEMBER 1963. THE REASON FOR THE REQUEST WAS THAT SILVIA DURAN WAS TRYING TO LEAVE MEXICO FOR CUBA.
