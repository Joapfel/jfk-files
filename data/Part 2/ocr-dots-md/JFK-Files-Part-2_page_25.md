REPORATION PUBL: A/DC/JA

ATTENTION : Mr. Howard Hunt

SUBJECT : Trace reply - Luis FERRE

REFERENCE : A/DC/JA. Memorandum dated 7 August 1964

1. Traces have been completed on:

Luis Alberto Ferre Agüero, 201-205712
AKA: Luis A. Ferren; Luis Albert Ferre
DPOB: 17 February 1934, Ponce, Puerto Rico
CIT : U.S.A., formerly, Puerto Rico
OCC : Senator and Industrialist
MS : Reina More Street, No. 2, Ponce, Puerto Rico

Subject, a wealthy member of the Puerto Rican Legislature,
heads the Republican (Statehood) Party, and is a stockholder
in El Tiempo, Inc., located at 36 Fifth Avenue, New York,
N. Y. The Chief, DJ Division desires background information
on Subject.

3. RID Files, the Office of Security, CI/OA, and WH/2/DR had a record of Subject. The 201 file is held by WH/2/DR on permanent charge. CI/OA maintains a file on Subject which was reviewed and the results are forwarded herewith, in a separate envelope, as Attachment No. 1. Included in Attachment 1 is operational and investigative information from the Office of Security and the 201 file. Attachment No. 2 is a biographic data sheet forwarded by the Office of Security.

3. The following biographic information is from the 201 file:

**Physical Description:** 5 ft., 7½ inches tall; weight = 155;
slender build; light complexion with brown hair and eyes;
oval-shaped face with a scar on the left cheek tonsil.

**Languages:** English, Spanish, and French.
