Convert to MarkdownFEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGA

Date: 8/23/71

At approximately 1:33 a.m. on August 23, 1971, Special Agents THIXTON, BARCOCK, M.VIS, and HARRISON observed a white male and three white females standing against the wall located in "Leove Driveway", east side of St. Paul's Episcopal Church, located across from the U.S. Post Office Building at Fourth and Market Streets, Camden, New Jersey.

Unknown white male later identified as BALDRY J. MUST was observed holding a walkie-talkie, which was immediately taken from him by SA THIXTON. A pair of cheap binoculars bearing a strip of adhesive tape with the word "lawnside" written on the tape and a brown tote-bag were located near the feet of these four individuals. SA THIXTON identified himself and the other agents present as being Special Agents of the FBI, and advised these four individuals that they were being placed under arrest for conspiracy. The three white females were subsequently identified as being MARCARET MARY INNESS, ANNE GRANT DUNHAM, and JOAN REILLY.
