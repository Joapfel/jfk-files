January 12, 1952

Dear Sir:

I received your letter dated January 5, stating that
I was to inform you of any changes in my present status...

I am now a grade 33 2 with an one year increase. I
am now being considered for a grade 33 3 in the Treasury
Dept. I assure you, this does not lesson my zeal to get
in the C.I.A.

I would like to know if your Agency would transfer or
consider me for appointment at a grade 33 3.

Sincerely yours,
James Schools
