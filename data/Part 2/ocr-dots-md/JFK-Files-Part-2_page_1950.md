DO 66-197

16 AUG 1968

MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Central Intelligence
THROUGH : Deputy Director for Plans
SUBJECT : Appointment of Mr. James A. Noel as Chief of Base, San Francisco, California

1. The appointment of Mr. James A. Noel as Chief of Base, San Francisco, California effective 6 September 1966 is recommended.
Mr. Noel should replace Mr. Gerald J. Steiner.

2. Mr. Noel has been an employee of the Agency since 13 September 1947, and was previously assigned as Chief of Station, Madrid, Spain. A biographic profile, including information regarding his Agency experience and training, is attached.

C. Tracy Barnes
Chief, DO Division
