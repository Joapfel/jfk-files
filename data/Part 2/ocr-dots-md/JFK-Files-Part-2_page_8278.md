5. After unsuccessful efforts to resettle KELLY in Brazil, he was temporarily removed from the holding area to Avron, Greece. A commitment was made to the Greek authorities that RVRock would not abandon him in Greece and would take steps to have him resettled in one year. Efforts were made to effect KELLY's migration to Canada, New Zealand, Australia, and elsewhere, but to no avail. After due consideration by the authorities concerned, it was decided that the best effective solution would be to let him immigrate to the United States on his own under the Refugee Relief Act program.

Our first knowledge that KELLY was representing himself as a film producer was a 14 May 1957 notice from the Department of State advising that KELLY was trying to interest them in purchasing a feature length film script.
