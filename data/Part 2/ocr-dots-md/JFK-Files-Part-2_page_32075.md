    <td colspan="2">(U) On 1 February 1972, Peter D. Herpst, PFC, 475-54-6972, Clerk,<br/>C Team, 66th USA Artillery Detachment, Datteln (LC8424), Federal Republic of<br/>Germany, APO New York 09078, was interviewed at his place of duty concerning<br/>an incident of possible Counter-Intelligence interest, and stated substantially<br/>as follows:<br/>(G) About two or three weeks ago, just before a scheduled exchange of<br/>warheads at C Team, Source was approached in a Datteln bar, the Stadtschaenke,<br/>by a man who identified himself as John Dee Jay. John was the Disc Jockey at<br/>another bar in Datteln, the Lord Nelson. Source thought that Dee Jay was<br/>only a nickname and not John's real name. John asked Source and other Americans<br/>in the bar if they knew where some of the former members of the unit are pre-<br/>sently stationed. Source could recall only one name John had asked about,<br/>Cristobal Pimentel-Davilla. John told Source "If you see Pimentel, tell him<br/>I have the package for him." Source knew that John had called the unit that<br/>night asking for Pimentel-Davilla. John at one time asked Source to tell him<br/>who the Security Sergeant of the unit was, but Source refused to answer the<br/>question. Source described John as follows: Height: Six feet to six feet<br/>one inch tall; Weight: 200-210 pounds; Age: 22-24 years old; Build: Stocky;<br/>Hair: Dark; Dress: Wore furry coat and shoes, possibly camel hair. Source<br/>had not met John before and has not seen him since and gave negative responses<br/>to all questions posed by him.<br/>(W) REMARKS: Source felt that the package John mentioned that he<br/>had for Pimentel-Davilla was probably drugs, either hashish or marijuana.<br/>Source knew that Pimentel-Davilla used both drugs and may have been selling<br/>them to other members of the unit.<br/>(Y) AGENT'S NOTES: Full identification of Pimentel-Davilla was as<br/>follows: Pimentel-Davilla, Cristobal, SP6, 582-58-9662, departed C Team on<br/>20 January 1972 on a permanent change of station to Fort Eustis, Virginia.</td>
