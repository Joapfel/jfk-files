ADDENDUM: INTELLIGENCE DIVISION VDC:ekv 4/14/75

On 4/11/75 the Executive Committee of the Ad lloc Coordinating Group on Congressional Review for the Intelligence Community was apprised of the discussions with Mr. Robert Hough, the Deputy Sergeant at Arms of the Senate, and with Mr. Kenneth Harding, Sergeant at Arms of the House. Although the Executive Committee would have preferred the FBI to conduct audio sweeps, they interposed no objection to the sweeps being handled by the Capitol Police Department. Mr. Rogerick Hills, Counsel to the President, requested, however, that both Mr. Hough and Mr. Harding be requested to periodically advise the FBI as to the sweeps of Committee space for electronic listening devices. Specifically, Mr. Hough and Mr. Harding should be asked to furnish the FBI the following: how frequently they will conduct a counter-audio sweep, what offices will be used, what the results of such sweeps will be furnished the FBI, and will the FBI be immediately notified should the Capitol Police locate an electronic listening device?
