Informant subsequently advised on 7/17/69:

PAUL WADE and one CLAYTON KENDALL (PB) from

the door at WESLEY WADE's house.

it if he becomes angry enough.

PAUL WADE said that one THOMAS O'NEAL (PB) is
a leader of the EPP in Washington Police Department and he
is formerly from Maywood.

MICHAEL HENDERSON has been released from jail and
