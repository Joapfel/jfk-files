FOLLOWING REVIEW OF THESE LETTERS BY ROWE, IT WAS INDICATED TO HIM THAT THEY WOULD BE DELIVERED IN CARE OF INSPECTOR MC GOVERN AT MONTGOMERY, ALABAMA, AND WOULD BE HELD BY MC GOVERN UNTIL AFTER THE CONCLUSION OF ROWE'S TESTIMONY.

ROWE INDICATED THAT THIS WAS AGREEABLE TO HIM AND INDICATED THAT HE WOULD WILLINGLY APPEAR AT MONTGOMERY, ALABAMA TO TESTIFY.

ALTHOUGH BUREAU PERSONNEL WAS PRESENT THROUGHOUT THE INTERVIEWS BETWEEN AAG DOAR AND ROWE, BUREAU PERSONNEL MADE NO ASSURANCES, PROMISES, NOR COMMITMENTS TO HIM.
