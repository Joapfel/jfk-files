TOP SECRET

OCCURRENCE:

In addition to the above maneuvering, Colonel Do Mau,
(Sen. Col. General), Chief of Military Security Service,
played his role by turning false military intelligence reports
which were President Diem's. These reports started on Nov.
28th October and built up a picture of strong Viet Minh
Forces approaching Saigon for attack and permitted the Secret
General Staff to issue corresponding orders, approved by the
President, to move units favorable to the equilibrium to the
outposts of Saigon and also to move units, such as the
Special Forces, away from Saigon.

**SOURCE:** Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit one,
pages 13-14.
