1. CARLOS JURADO DELMAR TOLD VALLACE S. ROWTON A JULY 1966 HE HAD DISCUSSED CASTRO DISAPPEARANCE WITH MIGUEL LOZANO VILLALOBOS, COUNSELOR OF CUBAN EMBASSY, AND LATTER REPORTED CASTRO DROP FROM SCENE DUE OPERATION FOR DOUBLE HERNIA. LOZANO TOLD JURADO HE LEARNED THIS FROM BRITISH CITIZEN WHO CAME OUT OF CUBA IN LAST FEW DAYS AFTER SPENDING THREE YEARS THERE ASSISTING AGRICULTURE PROGRAM. LOZANO REPORTED BRITISHER VERY CLOSE TO FIDEL.

2. JURADO ALSO TOLD ROWTON CASTRO HAD OVER EXERTED HIMSELF DURING RECENT GUATANAMO INCIDENT WHICH INDUCED HERNIA. JURADO ALSO OPINED CASTRO HAD SOMEHOW INCURRED SOVIET DISPLEASURE. ROWTON DID NOT QUESTION HIM ON WHERE HE GOT THIS IDEA BUT IT MAY ALSO HAVE COME FROM LOZANO.
