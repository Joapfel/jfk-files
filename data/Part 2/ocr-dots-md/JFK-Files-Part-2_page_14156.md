UNITED STATES SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE
TO STUDY GOVERNMENTAL OPERATIONS
WITH RESPECT TO INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES

RE: LEGAL AUTHORITY

Reference is made to letter dated March 19, 1975, to the Attorney General from the Chairman of the captioned Committee, which enclosed certain requests for documents and other information from the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI).

Enclosed is a copy of a memorandum from Glen E. Pormorening, Assistant Attorney General for Administration to Clarence H. Kelley, Director FBI, dated November 1, 1974, captioned "Federal Employee Security Program Executive Order 10450."

The instructions contained in the enclosure were the subject of conversation between W. Raymond Wannall, Assistant Director, and Mr. John Elliff of the Committee, May 5, 1975, at Quantico, Virginia, and are being furnished to the Committee in order to provide further information with respect to your March 19, 1975, request.
