## BACKGROUND

The Attorney General on Saturday, 1-20-62, advised Assistant Director Evans he would like to receive this week the Director's views as to what additional technical coverage should be instituted on diplomatic establishments in Washington, D. C.

Regarding the above, the Attorney General had previously indicated on 1-11-62 he was thinking in terms of extending coverage on the smaller countries rather than the "Iron Curtain" countries. He felt coverage of the Iron Curtain countries was essential but their representatives were more alert to such coverage and he felt representatives of the smaller countries might talk more freely. He indicated he did not want coverage of our true friends, like the British. He hinted at possible coverage of the Indonesians. On 1-20-62 when Evans spoke with him he indicated he felt additional coverage of the African countries might be worthwhile.

## PRESENT COVERAGE

In analysing proposals for increased coverage we have taken a look at what we already have. We have technical coverage in Washington and/or New York on eight countries of the type being considered, as follows: Cuba, Dominican Republic, France, Ghana, Israel, Saudi Arabia (in Boston), United Arab Republic and West Germany. The Saudi Arabian coverage, on King Saud, will be discontinued this week since King Saud is leaving Boston en route to Florida and then is going home. State Department has advised it does not desire he be covered in Florida.
