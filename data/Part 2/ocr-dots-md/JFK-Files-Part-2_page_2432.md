1. ON THE AFTERNOON OF 1 OCTOBER 1983 I MET AT HIS REQUEST WITH MARTY MARTINEZ, A FORMER AGENCY STAFFER NOW EMPLOYED BY THE WACKENHUT CORPORATION. MARTINEZ SAID THAT HE HAD INFORMATION TO PASS TO US WHICH ACTUALLY ORIGINATED WITH THE DADE COUNTY OFFICER CHILE BUREAU AND WHICH WAS INITIALLY FARRIED TO WACKENHUT WITH THE UNDERSTANDING THAT WACKENHUT WOULD IN TURN CONVEY IT TO THE AGENCY. PARENTHETICALLY, FOR THE RECORD, WE MAINTAIN SOME SLIGHT CONTACT WITH A FEW WACKENHUT EMPLOYEES. SEVERAL OF WHOM ARE FORMER AGENCY STAFFERS, BUT WE HAD NOT MET MARTINEZ PRIOR TO THIS MEETING.
