NUMEROUS ATTEMPTS MADE TO CONTACT FORMER SPECIAL AGENT
ROBERT F. O'NIELL, 2551 IROQUOIS, DETROIT, MICHIGAN,
TELEPHONE NUMBER 571-2296, ON SEPTEMBER 18, 1975, WITH
NEGATIVE RESULTS.

ON SEPTEMBER 19, 1975, MR. O'NIELL WAS CONTACTED BY
ASAC JAY E. BAILEY, AT WHICH TIME HE WAS ADVISED OF THE
PARTMENT CONTENTS OF REBUTEL. HE ADVISED THAT HE ONLY
SERVED ABOUT ONE YEAR AS A SECURITY SUPERVISOR IN THE DETROIT
OFFICE AND THAT HE HAD DOUBTS AS TO WHETHER HE HAD ANY
INFORMATION OF INTEREST TO THE SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE (SSC).
HE STATED, HOWEVER, THAT SHOULD HE BE CONTACTED BY A
REPRESENTATIVE OF SSC, HE WILL PROMPTLY CONTACT THE LEGAL
COUNSEL DIVISION FOR APPROPRIATE GUIDELINES AND WOULD
CLOSELY ADHERE THERETO.

ALL INFORMATION CONTAINED
HEREIN IS UNCLASSIFIED
