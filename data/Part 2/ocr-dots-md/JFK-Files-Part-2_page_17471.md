RACIAL TRAFFIC
HAYWOOD, ILLINOIS, 1969

The information herein has been disseminated
to local authorities where appropriate.

Informant advised on 7/11/89:

PAUL WADS, ALLEN HARTWAY and BILL BURNETTE returned to Maywood on 7/11 from their trip to the East Coast which included Boston, Newark, and Washington, D.C.

PAUL WADS said he had called the RPP headquarters in California and will travel there in two or three weeks to callist their aid in a plan to get FRED KAMPTON out of jail even if they have to break him out. Those who will go to California will be PAUL WADS, GREGGORY PERKINS and WALTER ALLEN. There have been several holdups in Maywood, in the past week and there is a possibility the following individual was involved (rumors heard by informant) one PMU MUMPHERY, who was released from jail on about 7/3/89.

Informant advised on 7/17/89:

On 7/18 the Maywood Police Department was called to assist in a repossession of furniture from WILLIAM WADS's after WADS had refused to return the furniture. Then WADS bought off the Maywood Police when they kicked in the door. He was arrested and PAUL WADS was very angry about the Police breaking down the door.
