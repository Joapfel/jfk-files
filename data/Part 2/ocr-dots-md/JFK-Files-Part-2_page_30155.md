TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PRINTERS INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE IN EYEHLY ON INKING SLAB.

3. WASH AND DRY FINGERS THOROUGHLY.

4. DOLL FINNERS FROM MAIL TO MAIL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. IF SURE IMPRESSIONS ARE RECORDED IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN IMPROPER OR DEFORMITY MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL CONDITION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE FIRST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAMING THE CONSEQUENCES.

8. EXAMINE THE COMPLETED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST PINTERESTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INCORPORATED AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).

THIS CARD FOR USE BY:

1. LAY IT DOWN ON A TABLE IN THE OFFICE APPLIANT CAN SEE AND READ THAT POSITIONS.

2. OFFICE OF THIS LOCAL COORDINATES FOR PUBLIC USE BY THE OFFICE OF TRANSMITTING AND RECORDING AT ATTORNEY'S OFFICE OR LOCAL CUSTODY OF COURT IN STATE OR FEDERAL COURT.

3. IF THE NAME IS TO BE PRINTED ON COPY.

4. IF THE NAME IS TO BE PRINTED ON A CUPBOARD.

5. IF THE NAME IS TO BE PRINTED ON THE LINE.
