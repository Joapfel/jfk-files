FH 52-7165 Sub S

TERRY EDWARD BUCKALEW

As previously noted, BUCKALEW was apprehended by Special Agents of the FBI on August 22, 1971, at 21 North Fourth Street, Camden, N. J. On the same date he was afforded a hearing before U. S. Magistrate CHARLES L. RUDD, Camden, N. J., and was remanded to the custody of the U. S. Marshal in lieu of $25,000 good bail. He was released on September 7, 1971, after posting ten per cent cash or security of a $10,000 bond which had been set by the Third Circuit Court of Appeals, Philadelphia, Pa., on September 3, 1971.
