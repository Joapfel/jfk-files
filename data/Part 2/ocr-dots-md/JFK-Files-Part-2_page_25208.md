TOP SECRET

## OCCURRENCE:

General Don requested Conein to meet him at JIS Narsquarers; Don stated the general officers comp committee had decided to attempt to take advantage of the 25 October National Pay In Order to stage a coup within a week; a member of General Don's staff, Colonel Nguyen Khaong, had leaked to U.S. authorities that a coup was to occur on or about 27 October; Khaong's overtures to the Americans had reached the galaxie and as a result President Nixon had ordered the 5th and 7th Divisions, which were key elements in the Saigon area for the coup, to go on extended operations outside Hanoi;

**SOURCE:** Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit One, p. 6.
