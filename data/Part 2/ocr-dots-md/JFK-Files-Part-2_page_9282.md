# I. REVOLUTIONARY PROGRAMS AND POLICIES AS EXPRESSED BY BPP LEADERS

On April 28, 1971, DE T-1 advised that the Ministry of Information for the Detroit National Committee to Combat Fascism (NCCF), organizing bureau for the Black Panther Party (BPP), was moved to a new office at 157 Collingwood, Detroit, Michigan, by orders of KENNETH HORSTON, leader of the Detroit NCCF.

DE T-1 advised that 611 Continental, Detroit, Michigan, is Community Information Center Number One.

A characterization of the BPP is contained in the appendix pages attached hereto.

# II. ACTS IN FURTHERANCE OF THE REVOLUTIONARY PROGRAM

DE T-1 advised on April 5, 1971, that 157 Collingwood, Detroit, was being opened as NCCF Community Information Center Number Two on that date.

DE T-1 advised on April 12, 1971, that four members of the Detroit NCCF, ANTHONY NORMAN, RONNIE IRWIN, RONALD SMITH, and LARRY POWELL, held up what they believed to be a dope house and one of the victims was killed.

Detective Lieutenant WILLIAM MC COY, Security Section, Intelligence Division, Detroit Police Department, advised on April 14, 1971, that Murder One warrants were obtained on April 14, 1971, for LARRY POWELL, RONNIE IRWIN, and ANTHONY NORMAN, all NCCF members.

A search warrant executed by the Detroit Police Department on April 14, 1971, at the home of BARBARA PLISKOW, white female associate of the NCCF, resulted in her arrest as well as the arrest of the above three, RONALD SMITH, and CAROLYN SMITH, members of the NCCF, and recovery of property stolen in a robbery on April 11, 1971, in which one man was killed.
