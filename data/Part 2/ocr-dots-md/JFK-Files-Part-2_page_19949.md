Memo to Mr. Sullivan

RE: DEMONSTRATION PROTESTING UNITED STATES
POLICY IN VIETNAM, WASHINGTON, D. C.

JANUARY 8, 1968

There is attached an airtel to all offices advising of this demonstration and furnishing instructions for its coverage and instructing our Atlanta and San Francisco Offices to institute investigations to further develop information concerning the Jeannette Rankin Brigade.

## RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the attached airtel to all offices be approved and transmitted.
