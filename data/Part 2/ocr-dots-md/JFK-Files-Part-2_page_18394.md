SEN STUDY

FORMER SPECIAL AGENT SAM J. PAPICH, ALBUQUERQUE, NEW MEXICO, TODAY ADVISED THAT HE WAS INTERVIEWED SEPTEMBER 18, 1975, BY DEPARTMENTAL ATTORNEYS REGARDING HIS TESTIMONY BEFORE ROCKEFELLER COMMISSION.

PAPICH STATED THAT INTERVIEW COVERED "Z" COVERAGE, FROM SURVEY, AND DEALT LIGHTLY REGARDING CONTACTS WITH ANONYMOUS SOURCES. DEPARTMENTAL ATTORNEYS PLACED EMPHASIS ON PAPICH'S KNOWLEDGE ABOUT AUTHORITY FOR ABOVE ACTIVITY.
PAPICH STATED HE REPLIED TO BEST OF HIS KNOWLEDGE HE AND ANYONE INVOLVED IN SUCH ACTIVITY ASKED AUTHORITY CAME FROM THE HIGHEST LEVEL MEANING ATTORNEY GENERAL AND/OR PRESIDENT. PAPICH EXPLAINED TO ATTORNEYS THAT INSURANCE SURVEY EFFORT WAS MADE TO DETECT CYRILLIC WRITING ON MAIL LEAVING UNITED STATES DESTINED ABROAD AND THIS PROGRAM WAS DESIGNED TO DETECT ILLEGAL AGENTS OPERATING IN UNITED STATES.
