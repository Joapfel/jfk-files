## E. Contact with Other BFP and NCCF Chapters

OLIVER MC CLURE and CHARLES HOLT, leaders of the Detroit NCCF, left Detroit for the weekly meeting at BPP, New York, at 8:00 p.m., July 25, 1970.

DE T-1
July 25, 1970

On August 13, 1970, MICHAEL D. HILL, Detroit NCCF, took SELINA JACKSON, former BFP member, from Detroit to Kennedy Airport, New York, from where they were to drive to New Haven. JACKSON is to try and discredit a witness in the ALEX RACKLEY murder trial, New Haven.

DE T-1
August 14, 1970

OLIVER MC CLURE, leader Detroit NCCF, flew from Detroit to New York on August 28, 1970, leaving about 6:00 p.m.

The Detroit NCCF attempted to obtain a speaker from the Chicago BFP for a picnic at Belle Isle Park that Detroit was planning for August 30, 1970.

CG T-6
August 26, 1970

Three buses left the Detroit NCCF, 2219 Indiandale, along with two cars about 3:00 p.m., September 4, 1970, apparently en route to the BFP Convention, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

DE T-3
September 4, 1970

THOMAS JOLLY, BFP, New York, arrived in Detroit the weekend of September 13, 1970, and returned to New York the morning of September 13, 1970, with MICHAEL D. HILL, Detroit, NCCF, for the regular Sunday meeting at the East Coast Ministry of the BFP in New York. OLIVER MC CLURE, Detroit NCCF, left Detroit about 5:00 p.m., September 13, 1970, en route New York to appear at the meeting of the East Coast Ministry in an attempt to regain his leadership of the Detroit NCCF.
