At 0830 I May a call was made to Mrs. Maclach to confirm that Charles would be on DC on the day after his meeting had been arranged so as to get a copy of her letter. Mr. Maclach re-called her and asked for 12 noon. Mrs. Maclach re-torted that B & J had called her that afternoon and requested that B & J had called her that afternoon too to confer on a 12:00 lunch appointment with her. Mrs. Maclach was concerned that CEAT & Army Shaped not be working at cross purposes. She had received authorization from AEBI to meet Charles at Lunch & felt it owed him impolite to advise since Akdy considered her the pt of contact by Charles. Wt'd dinner cancel/ meet then changed to afternoon - late. Cate. Matlach believed that due to confliction instructions she had been placed in Medle.

Matlach - Homeste Exploitation Sec. ACSI
