WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WONAAC) SPONSORED DEMONSTRATION, NOVEMBER TWENTY, NINETEEN SEVENTYONE, WASHINGTON, D.C. IS NEW LEFT (00: WFO)

ON NOVEMBER NINETEEN, SEVENTYONE, A SOURCE RELIABLE IN THE PAST, ADVISED THAT THE WOMEN'S RIGHT TO CHOOSE OF MARYLAND THREE ZERO TWO EIGHT GREENMOUNT AVE., BALTIMORE, MARYLAND, HAD HOPED TO CHARTER A BUS TO TRANSPORT PARTICIPANTS TO CAPTIONED RALLY, BUT LACK OF INTREST MAKES USE OF A BUD DOUBTFUL.

A SECOND SOURCE, RELIABLE IN THE PAST, STATED THAT REPRESENTATIVES OF WOMEN'S LIBERATION HAD ATTEMPTED TO SOLICIT SUPPORT FOR CAPTIONED RALLY AS LONG CITY COLLECTIVES WITH LITTLE SUCCESS.

A THIRD SOURCE, RELIABLE IN THE PAST, ADVISED THAT IN A LITTLE EIGHTH INCH AND A FIVE MILE DISTANCE AREA, PARTICIPANTS WERE EXPECTED TO ATTEND FROM BALTIMORE AREA.
