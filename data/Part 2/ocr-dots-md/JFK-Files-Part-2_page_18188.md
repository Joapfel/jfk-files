SUBJECT: Materials Compiled by the Bureau in Response to the Senate Select Committee's Request Concerning Electronic Surveillance of Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr.

Attached is an original and one copy of a memorandum and enclosures (Exhibits A through X) which the Bureau prepared in partial response to Senate Select Committee requests for materials pertaining to electronic surveillance of Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Although no formal agreement has been reached concerning the procedures to be followed for handling requests for materials relating to Dr. King, we think that there is no reason not to send the attached memorandum and enclosures to the SSC at this time as long as two additional excisions are made in the enclosures.
