MEMORANDUM FOR THE SPECIAL GROUP

SUBJECT: Cuban Operations

Special Group members approved by telephone on 5 December
the following:

a. Operation No. 3711, the exfiltration of three members
of an existing resistance net.

b. Operation No. 3118, the infiltration of a five-man
team for the purpose of sabotaging a railway bridge.

c. Operation No. 31110, the establishment of a ratline
into Cuban ports.

(Signed)

Paul Eckel

Distribution

Mr. Johnson
Mr. Gilpatrick
Mr. McCone
