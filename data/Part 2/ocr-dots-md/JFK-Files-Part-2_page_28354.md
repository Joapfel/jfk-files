MEMORANDUM

TO: Thomas Howarth, Budget Officer
Elizabeth Berning, Chief Clerk

FROM: I. Charles Mathews, Special Counsel
M.C.M.

DATE: December 21, 1977

RE: Ms. Jane Downey

This memorandum is to advise you that Ms. Jane L. Downey has accepted the position of Secretary with the John F. Kennedy Task Force. Her starting date will be December 30, 1977, and her starting salary will be $15,000.00.

Your cooperation will be greatly appreciated in familiarizing Ms. Downey with staff procedures and welcoming her aboard.
