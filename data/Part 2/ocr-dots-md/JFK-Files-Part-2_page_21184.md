On May 7, 1968, (N-190), NOSENKO stated that girls working in the American House were reporting on [MORONE](S) and he was particularly friendly with SVETLANA IVANOVA. He said PIETRO CECHI also reported "pieces of information" about [MORONE](S) but NOSENKO did not know of any black market dealings between CECHI and [MORONE](S). He stated that the photographs of [MORONE](S) with the Polish girl were considered insufficient to compromise him and KGB wanted to create something more, possibly photographs with Russian girls. IVANOVA was considered as a prospect in this connection and she and her girl friend had met [MORONE](S) and a Marine in a restaurant and thereafter spent the night in the "flat" of one of the Marines. At this time he stated that the Marine with [MORONE](S) on this occasion was possibly [GARCIA](S).

It is noted that when [MORONE](S) was interviewed on October 16, 1962, he furnished information regarding his intimacy with the friend of SVETLANA IVANOVA in the apartment of [MORRIS ZWANG](S).
