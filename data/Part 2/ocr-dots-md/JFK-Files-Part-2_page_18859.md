SAC Laughlin further points out the taxi driver who was
interviewed some twenty minutes after he left the Jaffes at the Mayflower
Hotel stated that after picking up the couple at 16th and R Ets., N. W.,
They told him they were "newspaper people," had spent the evening with
a "couple of Russians" and they probably would be followed. The
cab driver was instructed to try to "lose them." The cab driver s tated
that the Jaffes were attempting to obtain license numbers but he did
not think they were successful. He stated that he, himself, had not
been able to detect any surveillance. SAC Laughlin further points out
that on one occasion when Jaffe and his wife entered the Mayflower
Hotel they looked back out of the entrance on to the street and were
not aware of the fact that the surveilling agents had preceded them
into the hotel lobby by a side entrance and were in the lobby at the
time the Jaffes were looking outside for the surveillance. WFO
subsequently identified the Jaffes through inquiries at the hotel
and by reference to material in WFO files which was located the following
day.
