This is in response to SSC letter, with attached appendices, dated May 14, 1975, requesting certain documents and other information from the FBI.

Attached for your approval and forwarding to the Committee is the original of a memorandum and enclosures which constitutes our response to one of the requests. A copy of the memorandum and enclosures is being provided for your records.

Several of the enclosures pertain to telephone conversations between former President Johnson and former FBI Director Hoover. In accordance with the request of Mr. K. William O'Connor, we are deferring to him any action necessary to obtain appropriate clearance from the White House to furnish these documents to the Committee.
