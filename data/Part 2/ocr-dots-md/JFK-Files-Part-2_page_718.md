12. SOUTH KOREA: PRESIDENT PAK CHUNG-HUI'S DOMINATION OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY INHAZURATED ON 17 DEC UNDER SOUTH KOREA'S NEW CONSTITUTIONAL REGIME MAY TRANSFER POLITICAL INFUENTING TO A NEW AREA. THE CONTROVERSIAL FORMER SECURITY CHIEF KIM CHONG-PIL CONTROLS THE PARTY APPARATUS, AND HIS MANY ENEMIES ARE LIKELY TO TRY TO USE THE LEGISLATIVE PROCESSES TO TRY TO WRESTL POWER FROM HIM. PRESIDENT PAK STILL BACKS KIM, HOWEVER, AND THEY AND THEIR COHORTS WILL CONTINUE TO MAKE THE GOVERNMENT'S MAJOR DECISIONS. THE CABINET HEADLED BY CHOI TU-SON APPEARS TO HAVE BEEN CHOSEN WITH A MIND TO BALANCING FACTORIAL AND SECTIONAL INTERESTS AND FOR AN ABILITY TO CARRY OUT RATHER THAN FORMULATE POLICY.
