H. Arrests

Detective Lieutenant WILLIAM MC COY, Detroit Police
Department (PD), advised on February 2, 1972, that LONNIE
DARDEN and GERALD CRAWFORD were stopped by officers of the Fifth
Precinct. DARDEN was released and CRAWFORD was arrested and
charged with using and displaying the license of another.

guilty, sentenced to five days in the Detroit House of Corrections
and $50.00 fine.

On February 7, 1972, Detective Lieutenant MC COY, supra, advised that WILLIT CHARLES HALL, a former BPP member, was arrested February 7, 1972, for being Absent Without Leave and was returned to military control.
