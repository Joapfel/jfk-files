TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PRINTERS'INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE THE EYEBALL ON INDOING SLAB.

3. WASH AND dry FINGERS THOROUGHLY.

4. DOLL FINNERS FROM MAIL TO MAIL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. BE SURE IMPRESSIONS ARE REGISTERED IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN AMMUNITION OR DEFECT MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF ONE PHYSICAL CONDITION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, MAY THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CONSEQUANCES.

8. EXAMINE THE COMPLETED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT BEST FINGERPRINTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INCORRECTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
