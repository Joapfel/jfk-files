UNITED STATES COMMITTEE
TO STUDY COOPERATIVE OPERATIONS
WITH RESPECT TO INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES

When asked if he knew of Bureau Agents actually being engaged in physically handling the mail in any Post Office, particularly relating to mail covers, Mr. Croscioli responded that he had no knowledge of such activities.

Mr. Croscioli pointed out that to his knowledge all mail cover requests were forwarded to proper Postal authorities for handling by Postal employees only.

In conclusion, Mr. Hallach inquired if Mr. Croscioli had knowledge of the reason for the discontinuance of mail covers by the FBI as an investigative technique for a period of time in the past. Mr. Croscioli responded that he had no knowledge of this matter.

At this point, the interview was terminated at approximately 2:50 p.m., with both Messrs. Hallach and Dick expressing their appreciation of Mr. Croscioli's appearance and responses on this occasion.
