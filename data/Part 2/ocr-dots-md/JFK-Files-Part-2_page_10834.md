On September 8, 1971, MC GOWAN was given a hearing before United States Magistrate GREGORY J. POTTER, United States Courthouse, New York City, New York. Mr. STOLAR, MC GOWAN's attorney, advised the Magistrate that MC GOWAN was indeed the same person named in the indictment (supra) and waived any right to any possible identity hearing. MC GOWAN was released in his own recognizance, with the provision that he appear in Camden, New Jersey, for arraignment on the date set for such arraignment.
