## APPLICANT

1. LOOP

TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PRINTER'S INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE INK EVENLY ON INDOING SLAB.

3. WASH AND DRY FINGERS THOROUGHLY.

4. ROLL FINGERS FROM MAIL TO MAIL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. BE SURE IMPRESSIONS ARE RECORDED IN CORRECT ORDER.

A. IF AN AMPUTATION OR DEFECTIVITY MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

7. IF SOME PHYSICAL CONDITION MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO OBTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CIRCUMSTANCES.

8. EXAMINE THE COMPLETED FINGERS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT LOST FINGERPRINTS FALL INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INCORRECTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
