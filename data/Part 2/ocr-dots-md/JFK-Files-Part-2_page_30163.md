## APPLICANT

1. LOOP

TO OBTAIN CLASSIFIABLE FINGERPRINTS:

1. USE ONLY BLACK PENNY'S INK.

2. DISTRIBUTE PAPER ONLY ON RECEIVING SLAB.

3. CASH AND BACK TO ALL AS THROUGHLY.

4. BALL FINISHES, TAPVIAL, TO ALL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. IF AS ACCOUNTANT IS INCORPORATED, IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGER BLOCK.

6. IF LINE PATTERN, COMBINE TOGETHER AS IT IMPOSSIBLE TO GOTAIN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUGGEST THE FIRST WHAT CAN BE GOTAINS WITH A WOOD STAPLED TO THE CARD EXPLAINING THE CRISTANCES.

7. CHOOSE THE BEST APPLICATED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST PUBLICLY MUST GO INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INSIGNIFICANTLY AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
