Convert to MarkdownROSEMARY ELIZABETH REILLY

As previously noted, REILLY was apprehended by Special Agents of the FBI on August 22, 1971, inside the Post Office Building, 401 Market Street, Camden, N. J. On the same date she was afforded a hearing before U. S. Magistrate CHARLES L. RUDD, Camden, N. J., and was remanded to the custody of the U. S. Marshal in lieu of $25,000 good bail. She was released on September 3, 1971, after posting ten per cent cash or security on a $10,000 bond which had been set by the Third Circuit Court of Appeals, Philadelphia, Pa., on September 3, 1971.

A search of the fingerprints of ROSEMARY ELIZABETH REILLY has failed to disclose prior arrest data contained in the files of the Identification Division, FBI, Washington, D. C.
