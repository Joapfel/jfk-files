10/22/69

TO: DIRECTOR, FBI
FROM: SAC, CHICAGO
DEMTRI, IS - M.
DAVID DELLINGER, ET AL ARL - CONSPIRACY.

BLACK PANTHER PARTY, RN.
BUAGENTS OBSERVED APPROXIMATELY THIRTY MEMBERS OF
BLACK PANTHER PARTY (BPP), CHICAGO CHAPTER, PARTICIPATING
IN DEMONSTRATION IN PLAZA OF FEDERAL BUILDING, CHICAGO,
AT ONE PM INSTANT. GROUP OF TEN BPP MEMBERS DISCONTINUED
MARCHING IN PLAZA AND ALL GATHERED AROUND BRED HAMPTON
DEPUTY CHAIRMAN, ILLINOIS CHAPTER BPP. AFTER CONFERRING
FOR APPROXIMATELY TEN MINUTES, GROUP PRESENTED THEMSELVES
AT EXTERCE OF FEDERAL BUILDING FOR THE PURPOSE OF
OBTAINING ADMISSION TO COURTROOM WHERE CONSPIRACY TRIAL
OF BOBBY GEORGE SRALE AND SEVEN OTHER DEFENDANTS IS IN
SESSION. UMs ADMITTED FIFTEEN BPP MEMBERS AFTER SEARCHING
FOR WEAPONS. REMAINDER OF GROUP, INCLUDING HAMPTON,
THEN DISPersed AND LEFT AREA OF FEDERAL BUILDING.

AS OF ONE THIRTY PM AREA OF FEDERAL BUILDING RETURNED
TO NORMAL,

RAC: DBS
