## 2. Positive Results Achieved

This program has enabled us to provide the White House and the United States Government with valuable first-hand political information, including the national and international interests of the U.S. The program was particularly productive in our coverage of recent crucial areas of concern as the Middle East crisis, the continuing conflict in Lebanon, and the current political crises created in South America through the understanding and accommodation of thousands in the various countries covered. In the area mentioned, as well as in other nine millionary areas, this program has served as a basis for obtaining information which was provided by U.S. Government with current data regarding the international political scene and has contributed otherwise invaluable information for the benefit of those responsible for policy decisions at the lowest level of our Government. An example of the importance of this information is illustrated in the constants of the U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations, Charles W. Yost, made by him in a letter dated 11/18/70 to the Director. Ambassador Yost noted that the provision given to the U.S. United Nations Mission by the Bureau was deeply appreciated by him and had afforded him privileged insight and perspective.

The quality of this program is further attested to by realization by the White House of the compilation of the FTR to present positive foreign political intelligence with valuable instructions to the Bureau to expand its compilation success in electingU.S. presidential candidates. As a result of these instructions, our capability in furnishing foreign political information was enhanced through two opening of six new Madison portals to Israel, Lebanon, Venezuela, Bolivia, Colombia, Venezuela, Hungary, Israel, and Turkey. With this expansion, our overseas Madison Avenues were increased from 38 to 42 while exporting cultural personnel using increased in number from 38 to 40. This increase has increased the number of our foreign ports from 11 to 17.
