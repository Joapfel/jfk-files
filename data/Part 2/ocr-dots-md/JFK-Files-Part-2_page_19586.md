On February 1, 1968, [Detective ROBERT BOCK], Intelligence Division, Chicago PD, advised that LUCILLE MONTGOMERY was the motivating force and almost solely responsible for organizing for the Brigade in Chicago. She was assisted as needed by the individuals listed above in arranging for participation in the January activity and held several meetings at her apartment in that regard.

On April 18, 1968, [Sergeant LOVELY and Detective BOCK] advised that all information which had been developed by that agency regarding the Brigade was concerned directly with the January march on Washington and the supporting local demonstration in Chicago January 15, 1968. They had no knowledge that any meetings or other activities have been held subsequent to that time and were of the opinion that the organization as a continuing affair did not "get off the ground" in Chicago.

Information concerning the January demonstrations was received at that time from numerous Chicago informants. These and others in a position to learn of any subsequent activity were alerted through frequent contacts and have advised they have no knowledge of continuance of the Brigade as an active organization.
