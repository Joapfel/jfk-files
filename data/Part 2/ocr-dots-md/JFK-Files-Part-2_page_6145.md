Although the Board's mission is clear, in executing the law it continually confronts the powerful tensions generated by the principled claims of openness and secrecy. To choose is to lead, and the Board, in attempting to break new ground in public disclosure, confronts some profound choices. Those choices have to be informed, moreover, by a shrewd assessment of the public's right to know, the public's need to have secrets vital to its national security protected, and the intelligence services' duty to safeguard those secrets and the sources and methods that produce them. n100
