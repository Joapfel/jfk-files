THE TWO MINUTE RINGLES ARE NUMBERED IN SEQUENCE FROM NORTH TO SOUTH, TOP TO BOTTOM. THE FIRST INDICATES THE TIME, HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR. THE SECOND INDICATES THE MINUTE, AND THE THIRD INDICATES THE DOZE (OR HOUR). IF THE DOZES OR HUNDREDS ARE NOT CLEAR, REFER TO THE TIME TABLE AT THE BEGINNING OF EACH HOUR. IF THE NUMBER INDICATED IS NOT ONE OF THE DOZES OR HUNDREDS, REFER TO THE TIME TABLE AT THE BEGINNING OF THAT HOUR. IF THE INDICATION IS NOT A NUMBER IN THE RANGE OF THE DOZES OR HUNDREDS, REFER TO THE TIME TABLE AT THE BEGINNING OF THE NEXT DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR.
EXAMPLE: THE 8th NUMBER IN THE RANGE OF THE DOZES OR HUNDREDS IS 6, WHICH REFERS TO THE 6th MINUTE OF THE 10th DOZE, OR THE 6TH HOUR. IF THE TIME IS 7:10, THE NUMBER IS 7, WHICH REFERS TO THE 7th MINUTE OF THE 10th DOZE, OR THE 7TH HOUR. IF THE TIME IS 7:55, THE NUMBER IS 6, WHICH REFERS TO THE 6TH MINUTE OF THE 11th DOZE, OR THE 6TH HOUR.
(SEE NOTE 1 ABOUT THE FIRST MINUTE.)
NOTE 1: THE FIRST MINUTE IS LESS THAN A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR.
NOTE 2: WHEN USING A TIME TABLE WITH A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, THE FIRST MINUTE IS LESS THAN A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR.
NOTE 3: WHEN USING A TIME TABLE WITH A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, THE FIRST MINUTE IS LESS THAN A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, BUT THE TIME IS CORRECT.
NOTE 4: WHEN USING A TIME TABLE WITH A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, THE FIRST MINUTE IS LESS THAN A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, AND THE TIME IS 1 MINUTE EARLIER.
NOTE 5: WHEN USING A TIME TABLE WITH A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, THE FIRST MINUTE IS LESS THAN A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, AND THE TIME IS 1 MINUTE LATER.
NOTE 6: WHEN USING A TIME TABLE WITH A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, THE FIRST MINUTE IS LESS THAN A DOZE OR HUNDREDTH OF AN HOUR, AND THE TIME IS 1 MINUTE EARLIER.
