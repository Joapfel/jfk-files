REBUTEL MAY 28, 1975.

PURSUANT TO BUREAU INSTRUCTIONS, MISS OLGA CIFSA WAS CONTACTED BY SAC, CHICAGO, ON MAY 30, 1975 REGARDING CAPTIONED MATTER. SHE STATED SHE STILL RESIDES 1949 SOUTH INDIANA AVENUE, CHICAGO, AND IS STILL EMPLOYED AS AN EXECUTIVE SECRETARY AT THE FIRM OF ERNST AND ERNST, ATTORNEYS, IN CHICAGO.

MISS CIFSA WAS ADVISED THAT THE SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE HAD MADE INQUIRY AS TO HER LAST KNOWN ADDRESS, INDICATING THE POSSIBILITY THAT THEY MAY DESIRE TO INTERVIEW HER. SHE WAS FURTHER ADVISED THAT IN THE EVENT SHE IS INTERVIEWED AND DURING THE COURSE OF SAME ANY QUESTIONS WERE ASKED RELATIVE TO SENSITIVE BURFAU OPERATIONS; FTC., SHE COULD REQUEST THE ASSISTANCE OF AN AGENT TO BE PRESENT DURING THE INTERVIEW.
