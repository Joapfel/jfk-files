7. Source explained that Subject promised to advise source where she will be staying in the Miami area. Source has been requested to remain in NY until the clearing of 10 March in case hope that this information will be forthcoming. Source was also requested to consider the feasibility of visiting the Miami area to determine Subject's wherever he wants to go. Source stated that an excuse for this visit could be to visit Dr. SNAPPER and his wife in Miami in that they are leaving NY 9 March for a long-planned 10 day holiday there. Both Subject and Source are close friends of SNAPPER so this arrangement would be plausible. In this regard, Source stated that to save all concerned from embarrassment, she advised SNAPPER not to question Subject too closely as to Subject's plans upon Subject's departure from NY in that SNAPPER was under the impression that Subject was travelling directly to Cuba. Source implied to SNAPPER that Subject has to "sear her way" to Cuba. SNAPPER was described as being in no way connected with Subject's assignment nor to be in sympathy with such business. The SNAPPER plan to stay in the Richole Hotel, Miami Beach, Collins Avenue.

8. U.S. President, and Vice President of Subject being naive as to the happenings in Cuba. Source said that when discussing some aspects of the true situation there, Che GUEVARA's name came up. When Source suggested that Che may have been done in, Subject responded that this certainly was not the case. Subject stated that about 3 March one of Che's "close aides" arrived in NY. Source understands this aide came to NY for contact with the Cuban UN group. Subject learned this from the CUBS rep.

9. The CUBS rep is described as being 5'3" to 5'4" tall, slight build, small stature, tiny waist, short, a "latin type" but not dark. He looks like Harold MARINZ, the father of Subject's first baby. Source now states that the CUBS rep is probably not a Premex Latin rep as Subject stated this assignment is furthering his diplomatic career. The CUBS rep had meetings with Subject at least three times at Subject's home.

10. Depending upon developments by the morning of 10 March, Source may be asked to transit Washington for another meeting while enroute to Mexico. It is understood that Your Office will undertake the necessary coordination with other components and other Agencies and that this Office will take no further action pending your advice and request.
