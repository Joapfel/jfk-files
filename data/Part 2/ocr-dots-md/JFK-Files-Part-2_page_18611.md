Convert to MarkdownJune 20, 1968

BY LIAISON

Honorable Idar Rimestad
Deputy Under Secretary for Administration
Department of State
Washington, D. C.

Dear Mr. Rimestad:

You have previously been advised of reductions in FBI personnel overseas which were made in response to the wishes of the President. In a further effort to comply with the desire of the President, I have decided to reduce our staff in Santo Domingo by one Special Agent and one clerk. This will eliminate one position in GS-14 and one position in GS-8.

You may be sure that I will continue to give this matter close scrutiny with a view to effecting further economies whenever such action can be taken without impairing the discharge of our responsibilities.

This document is prepared in response to your request and is not for dissemi-nation outside your Committee. Its use is limited to official proceedings by your Committee and the content may not be disclosed to unauthorized personnel without the express approval of the FBI.
