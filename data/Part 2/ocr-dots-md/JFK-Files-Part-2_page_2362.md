12 October 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR THE DIRECTOR OF CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE

SUBJECT: Appreciation of Work of CIA Personnel

1. Between 1 August and 15 August 1951, in support of a special Armed Forces Security Agency operation, Mr. Edward F. Hall, Mr. Birch O'Neill, Mr. John Salb, and Dr. Raymond S. Hall executed their additional assigned duties in an efficient and admirable manner. They consistently demonstrated initiative, interest, and a spirit of cooperation which assured the success of this operation.

2. I am gratified by this further demonstration of support of the Armed Forces Security Agency by the Central Intelligence Agency and I should be grateful if you would express to these gentlemen my appreciation of a job well done.

/s/

RALPH J. CANINE
Major General, US Army
Director, Armed Forces Security Agency
