1. LOOP

TO OBTAIN QUOTAS, MENTION FINGERS PRINTS:

1. USE BLACK PEN ORINK.

2. DETACH THE EXTRACT ON UNDER SLAB.

3. USE ONLY ONE FINGER TO PRINT.

4. USE ALL FINGERS AT LEAST TOTAL, AND AVOID ALLOWING FINGERS TO SLIP.

5. USE TWO IMPRESSIONS OF EACH HAND TO RECORD IN CORRECT ORDER.

6. IF AN AIR VENT OR DOOR LOCK MAKES IT IMPOSSIBLE TO PRINT A FINGER, MAKE A NOTATION AS TO THAT EFFECT IN THE INDIVIDUAL FINGERS BLOCK.

7. IF SOME INK IS LEFT ON THE SURFACE, MAKE IT UNTERLY TO GOTTEN PERFECT IMPRESSIONS, SUBMIT THE BEST THAT CAN BE OBTAINED WITH A MEMO STAPLED TO THIS CARD EXPLAINING THE REASONS.

8. DRAW ON THE COMPLETED PRINTS TO SEE IF THEY CAN BE CLASSIFIED, BEARING IN MIND THAT MOST FINGERS POUR INTO THE PATTERNS SHOWN ON THIS CARD (OTHER PATTERNS OCCUR INCORPORATED, AND ARE NOT SHOWN HERE).
