In Reply, Please Refer to File No. 100-29231

Re: Women's Right to Choose of Maryland  
Internal Security — Communist (Trotskyist)

On November 13, 1971, a source advised that the Women's National Abortion Coalition (WONAAC) planned to hold demonstrations in Washington, D.C., and San Francisco, California, on November 20, 1971. The purpose of the demonstrations was to call for the repeal of all abortion, contraception, and forced sterilization laws. The Women's Right to Choose of Maryland, the Baltimore, Maryland, affiliate of WONAAC (WONAC), was to organize busing and housing arrangements for out-of-state women.

On November 22, 1971, a source advised that the Women's Right to Choose of Maryland, working from 3028 Greenmount Avenue (Greenmout Avenue), Baltimore, Maryland, was unsuccessful in attempts to generate enthusiasm for the WONAAC (WOHNAC) demonstration among Baltimore-area women. Therefore, the plan to charter a bus for the purpose of transporting demonstrators to the Washington, D.C., rally was cancelled.

On November 30, 1971, a second source advised that the Women's Right to Choose of Maryland had been formed as an “ad hoc” group for the specific purpose of supporting the November 20, 1971, Washington, D.C., demonstration. The source further stated that the Women's Right to Choose of Maryland ceased to exist following this demonstration and is now defunct.
