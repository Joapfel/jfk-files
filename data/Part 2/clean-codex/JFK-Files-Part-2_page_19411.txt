Richmond, Virginia

January 12, 1968

Demonstration Protesting United States Policy in Vietnam, Washington, D. C., January 8, 1968

Detective C. F. Smith, Intelligence Squad, Richmond, Virginia, Police Department, advised that he had received information that Ichiro Hasegawa, who is known to be active in peace movement groups in the Richmond, Virginia, area, was planning to attend the demonstration sponsored by the Jeannette Rankin Brigade, originally scheduled for January 8 but now planned for January 15, 1968.

Smith stated that his information indicated that Hasegawa planned to travel to Washington, D. C., to participate in that demonstration with other unknown individuals. He stated that he had no information concerning Hasegawa's mode of transportation to Washington nor her participation in the demonstration. Smith also had no information concerning the number of individuals planning to attend the demonstration with her.

A confidential source advised on September 8, 1965, that the publication “Four Lights,” described as the official publication of the Woman's International League for Peace and Freedom (WILPF) in a supplement to its July 1965 issue, listed the nominees for the National Board of the WILPF. Included in this listing was the following name: Marit Hasegawa, also known as Ichiro Hasegawa. Within the framework of the WILPF, her activities were listed as follows:

President, New Jersey State Branch, 1956–1960  
National Membership Chairman, 1960–1965  
Member of the National Board and Vice President, 1962–1965  
President, Burlington County, New Jersey, Branch, 1961–1965
