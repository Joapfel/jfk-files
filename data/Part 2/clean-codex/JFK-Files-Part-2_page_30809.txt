Classified message.

Date: 3 May 58. To: Director. From: 16-4 Paris.

1. Subject reports FES received four-month tourist Soviet visa and plans depart with [reft] two users. Upon arrival Moscow will explore job possibilities. Plans take boat via Riga.

2. Subject agreed “Embassy” briefing prior departure. Expressed in writing press do what could during time Moscow matter not pursued since subject not yet cleared.

3. Subject has some friends whom will contact upon arrival, all from intellectual and artistic circles. Believe after careful briefing subject could act as spotter and might contact appropriate persons of interest KUBAN. Also letter railing.
