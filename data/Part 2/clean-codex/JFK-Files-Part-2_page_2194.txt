Resume

U. S. Government Service

Birch D. O'Neal

From January 10, 1938, on entering U. S. government service as a Special Agent of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, to February 28, 1970 (with the exception of a few months in the fall and winter of 1946–47), was employed by various Departments and Agencies of the U. S. Government, including the Federal Bureau of Investigation, U. S. Department of Justice, U. S. Department of State, and Central Intelligence Agency. In this period of more than 32 years, steadily advanced in salary with commensurate responsibilities from $3,200 per annum to more than $30,000 per annum while holding various titles, including Special Agent, Legal Attaché, Civil Attaché, and Foreign Affairs Officer. More than 12 years of this service was performed while stationed abroad or in travel status in many foreign countries of Latin America, Europe, and Africa.
