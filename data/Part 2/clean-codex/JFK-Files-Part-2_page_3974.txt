Department of State. Telegram.

Drafted by: SCA:SCS: AMHARTL. Approved by: SCS — Hobart Luppi; SCS — WRJOCHIMSEN. Desired distribution: EUR/GER.

R 172201Z APR 69

From: Secretary of State, Washington, D.C.  
To: U.S. Mission Berlin  
Info: American Embassies Madrid, Paris, and Rome; American Consulate Zurich.

State 059324. Rome for VA.

Reference: Berlin 668, Richard Nagell.

1. February 27 and 28, 1969, Nagell called Consulate General Zurich stating he had been enticed to return to the United States in 1968 with promises his wife and children would be located. On March 10, Nagell appeared at Consulate General Barcelona requesting information regarding his wife and children.

2. Nagell appeared at Embassy Madrid March 21 without funds. Requested his sister, Mrs. Gambert, be asked to send him $75 from his bank account for a trust fund with the Department and that his sister cash his monthly check for him and cable the money to him care of AMEXCO Madrid. April 3, visited Embassy again regarding his VA check and requested assistance obtaining admission to Torrejon Air Force Base hospital for “nervous condition.” Accused Department again of breaking its promise to help him locate his wife and children. Last called Embassy Madrid April.
