Note MOSKALEV’s scientific background and travel. He was in the U.K. in 68, 70, 71, and 72. His background is biophysics. MOSKALEV’s (GRU) contact, BUBNOV, “specialized in chemical warfare.”

MOSKALEV was allegedly imprisoned during the war because of his German descent or for black-market dealings. Could he be a double agent?
