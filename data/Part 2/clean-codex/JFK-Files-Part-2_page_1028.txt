Report of Honor and Merit Awards Board. Date: 27 March 1973.

The Honor and Merit Awards Board having considered a recommendation that:

IN ID No.: 007894. Name: SMITH, Joseph B. Birth year: 1921. Ser: M. Type employer: Staff. Office of assignment: DDO/WII. Schedule: 30 D. Grade: GS 14.

Recommended: Career Intelligence Medal. For meritorious service or achievement during the period September 1951–Present. Recommends approval. Recommends award of.

Unclassified citation: Mr. Joseph B. Smith is hereby awarded the Career Intelligence Medal in recognition of exceptional achievement throughout his Agency career. During more than 21 years of service, Mr. Smith, a dedicated intelligence officer, served in a variety of important positions at Headquarters and abroad—consistently demonstrating skill, imagination and enthusiasm. Mr. Smith is a recognized authority in his field. His overall performance and the high quality of his work have contributed significantly to the mission of the Central Intelligence Agency, reflecting credit on him and the Federal service.

Remarks: Recommendation approved by ADD/O on 3 March 1973.

Approved. 1st Vernon A. Walters (Ist Vemon A. Waher), type name of Chairman, Honor and Merit Awards Board.

Director of Central Intelligence: Harry B. Fisher. Date: 13 April 1973. Signed original.
