FBI. Date: 8/8/69. Plaintext. Teletype. Urgent. To: Director, FBI. From: SAC, Chicago.

Fred Hampton. RM-BPP.

Source, reliable, advised August seven last, that Hampton's attorney would appear local court August eight instant in attempt to have Hampton freed on appeal bond during appeal of his conviction, May twenty-six last, for armed robbery charge, Maywood, Illinois. Source advised lawyer appeared instant and Hampton's bond reduced to twenty-five hundred dollars. Source advised Black Panther Party (BPP), Chicago, anticipates Hampton will be free prior to August twelve next. Hampton is deputy chairman, Illinois BPP. CG PD, USA, Secret Service and military intelligence advised.

RMM.
