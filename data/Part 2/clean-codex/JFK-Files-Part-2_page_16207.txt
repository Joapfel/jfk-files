10/4/69

Teletype.

To: Director, FBI.  
From: SAC, Chicago.  
DENTRI, 10-M.

David Dellinger, et al., ARL—conspiracy.

Black Panther Party (BPP), Chicago, Illinois. RM.

Reliable source advised, instant, Fred Hampton, chairman, Illinois BPP, made announcement to news media today, protesting arrest of seven male Negroes at BPP headquarters, Chicago, by CG PD earlier today. Hampton accused the police of continuing harassment of BPP and called for massive rally at the Federal Building, Chicago, on Tuesday, October seven, next, at twelve noon by members of the BPP and by “everyone else” in the community. Hampton gave no indication that any violence would accompany this protest demonstration.

Bobby Seale (Meale), national chairman, BPP, is currently on trial in captured A L case; however, rally called by Hampton appears primarily directed at CG PD, rather than the trial of Seale. Trial of Seale and seven other defendants, however, will.
