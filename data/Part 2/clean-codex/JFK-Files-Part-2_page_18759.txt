SAC, New York (65-15026)

Re New York letter dated 12/15/58, and San Francisco letter dated 12/23/58.

On 12/31/58, Samuel Adason Jaffe, news reporter, Columbia Broadcasting System, NYC (PSI protect identity), was interviewed by SA as Lawrence J. Doyle and Edward F. Gamber. On this occasion, subject's interest in ascertaining information with respect to business concerns on the West Coast of the United States desirous of doing business with Red China was discussed with Jaffe. It should be noted that in conversation with subject on 12/5/58, subject suggested to Jaffe that his father, whom he met, would be able to ascertain information with respect to business concerns desirous of doing business with Red China. Jaffe told subject that his father was in the Hawaiian Islands and would not return until the middle of next year.

Jaffe advised that his father had returned to San Francisco and he had talked with him on the phone. He suggested that he could tell the subject that he asked his father about business concerns on the West Coast desirous of doing business with Red China while conversing with him on the phone.

It was agreed that Jaffe, in his next conversation of any length with the subject, would recall subject's interest in business concerns desirous of doing business.
