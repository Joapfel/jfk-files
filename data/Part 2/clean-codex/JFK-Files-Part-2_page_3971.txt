From Secretary of State, Washington, D.C. To American Consulate Frankfurt. Information: American Embassy Paris, American Embassy Rome, American Embassy Madrid, American Consulate Zurich, U.S. Mission Berlin.

Paris pass USSS.

Reference: Berlin 754; State 62744 — Richard Nagell.

1. If Nagell is still in Frankfurt or inquires at addressee posts, he may be informed the Department attempted to trace his wife but was unable to do so.

2. A search of the available passport files disclosed no record on Mitsuko Takahashi Nagell.

GP-4. Rogers.
