Harvey testified that in the spring of 1962, when he was preparing to contact Roselli, he briefed Helms on the assassination plot. (Harvey, 6/25/75, p. 65)

Harvey said:

“I briefed Helms generally on the takeover of Roselli, on the doubts about the operation, on the possible … future of it, and to the extent it had then been possible, the assessment of Roselli and the cutting out of various individuals.”

Harvey testified that when he briefed Helms on the assassination plot operation, they decided that it would not be appropriate at that point to brief John McCone.
