Subject: New case 60805 — LA Division request for contact of Jackson Corporation officer.

1. This case is responsive to Requirement Control Number GPLA-7G3449 and Objective V.

LA Division has requested DOD assistance in recontacting Jones Bergman, Jr., Director of International Operations of Marine Corporation, Coral Gables, Fla. LA Division provided the follow-up address and telephone number: 13941 Midway St., Miami, Tel. (305) 443-2200. Bergman is a former Agency non-official cover officer who was asked to resign from the Agency in 1970.

2. Through an LA Division officer, Tom Elines, Bergman has requested contact with an Agency representative, as he has information that Jones may be of interest to the Agency. The name of Tom Elines may be used to facilitate contact with Bergman, as they believe that Bergman may have information on political developments in Jamaica.

3. LA Division has emphasized that this request is only in response to Bergman’s request for contact with the Agency. LA Division has no operational interest in Bergman or his company. Office of General Counsel has concurred in contacting Bergman by DCD Miami.
