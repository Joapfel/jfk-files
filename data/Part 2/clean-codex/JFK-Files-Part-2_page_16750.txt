Federal Bureau (Dureau) of Investigation

February 18, 1970

A piece of the door jamb and wallboard behind the frame was removed from the left side of the door frame of the living room at 2337 West Nauvoo, Chicago, Illinois. The out of the piece of door jamb was made at 52 inches and 64 inches from the floor.

In addition to Special Agents and Special Deputy United States Marshals, Sergeant Erickson, Star Number 1014, and Patrolman Venneigh, Star Number 9073, of the Chicago Police Department.

The above articles were removed at the request of Mr. K. William O'Connor, Attorney, United States Department of Justice, Civil Rights Division.
