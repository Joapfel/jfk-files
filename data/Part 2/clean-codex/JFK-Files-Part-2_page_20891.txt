Universal Memorandum

Director, FBI

Date: 4/14/71

From: SAC, Indianapolis (100-22551) (RSC)

Subject: Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971, IS—Misc.

Re New York airtel to the Bureau, 3/26/71.

Linda Pirrard, secretary, Indiana Bell Telephone Company, Indianapolis, Indiana (protect), advised on 4/1/71 that telephone number 317-472-9061 is an outdoor pay phone at the Mississinewa Reservoir, Peru, Indiana.

Attempts to make pretext calls to the above number on 4/1/71, 4/5/71, and 4/6/71 met with negative results regarding any SUSAN (LNU).

For additional information, a short note regarding the captioned march was indicated in a local Indianapolis underground-type newspaper, with the notation to call NANCY, 926-9770, at Indianapolis, Indiana, for information.

On 4/1/71, PSI P 3436-S (reliable) called this number regarding a People's Peace Treaty Conference to be held that weekend. NANCY has been determined to be Nancy Pittman, who attended the People's Peace Treaty Conference at New Castle, Indiana, 4/3–4/71. Indianapolis is currently conducting an investigation regarding Pittman in connection with this conference.

On 4/9/71, Janyce Sue Anderson (PSI under development), who has been contacted an insufficient number of times to determine reliability, advised that she had no knowledge of individuals going to Washington, D.C., regarding the captioned matter from the Indianapolis area.

JHB/slr.
