United States Government

Memorandum

To: W. C. Sullivan  
From: W. R. Wannall  
Subject: Intelligence Coverage of Noncommunist Countries  
Date: 2/5/64

It is believed State Department clearance should be obtained to add Republic of Vietnam to the list of countries previously approved by the State Department under the captioned program.

The captioned program involves development by the FBI of confidential sources and informants in the diplomatic establishments of friendly foreign nations where (1) it appears that the country is leaning toward the Soviet Bloc, (2) where the situation in a particular country involves a serious political or military crisis, or (3) where there is a clear investigative need. At the present time, we are developing sources and informants in the diplomatic establishments of 44 friendly foreign nations with State Department approval.

At this time, it is felt we should obtain State clearance to institute a program to develop sources and informants in South Vietnamese diplomatic establishments in the United States.

South Vietnam is an integral part of Asia where the United States is deeply involved politically, economically, and militarily to stem the tide of Chinese Communists. South Vietnam within less than six months has had its president slain and been governed by two military juntas which gained control by coup d’état. Any information which we can obtain regarding the current political picture should prove of value.

Recommendation:

Liaison should obtain State clearance from an appropriate State Department representative for the FBI to conduct discreet investigations of Potential Security Informants in Vietnamese diplomatic establishments in the United States. Liaison should advise State that no interviews will be conducted with any potential source without first obtaining State Department clearance.
