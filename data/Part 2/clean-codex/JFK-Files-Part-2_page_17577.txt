To: Director, FBI (62-116395).

SENSTUDY 75.

Re: Bureau teletype, May 2, 1975.

Former SA John J. Quinn was contacted by me personally and advised per instructions in retel. He was appreciative of advanced notice regarding a possible interview and stated that he would desire an agent be present if such interview occurs. He stated he would personally contact me if he is contacted by staff members.

Bureau will be kept advised.

End.
