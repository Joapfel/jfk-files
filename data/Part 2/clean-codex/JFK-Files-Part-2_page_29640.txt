10. Legal Attaché Memo to Winston Scott re Elena's Allegation that She Had Stayed at the Hotel Vermont from the Day After the Assassination Until November 30, 1963

On 10/13/66, the Legal Attaché wrote a memo to Win Scott reporting that a reliable confidential information had reported that the records of the Hotel Vermont disclosed that one “Elena Paz, housewife from San Luis Potosi,” had registered at the Hotel Vermont on November 23, 1963. She left on November 30, 1963. The memo said, “The above individual may or may not be identical with Elena Garro de Paz.” The House Select Committee on Assassinations has been unable to determine why the Central Intelligence Agency and the Federal Bureau of Investigation (Federal Bureau of Investigations) waited until 1966 to investigate this aspect of Elena’s story.

Charles Thomas’ 12/24/65 memo stating “She and her daughter did not personally register at the hotel. She thinks Calvillo registered them as relatives or friends of his from San Luis Potosi,” corroborates Elena Garro’s presence at the Hotel Vermont.
