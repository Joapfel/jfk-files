Sergeant Eugene Connolly, Area Four Task Force, Chicago Police Department, advised on June 9, 1969, that 11 Panther members were arrested after leaving Panther headquarters during the evening of June 8, 1969. They were charged with obstructing police. A search of the automobile they were in revealed hypodermic needles, one marijuana cigarette, and chemicals described by the police as being ingredients for incendiary bombs.

Source advised 16 BPP members were indicted on June 9, 1969, and charged with kidnapping, conspiracy in an attempt to commit murder, and several other local charges. They are:

Fred Hampton, Deputy Chairman, Illinois BPP.

Richard Powell, member of the West Suburban Branch, Illinois BPP.

William O'Neal, Chief of Staff, Illinois BPP.

Wilmer Angrum, member of the West Suburban Branch, Illinois BPP.

James White, member of the West Suburban Branch, Illinois BPP.

David Valentine, member of the West Suburban Branch, Illinois BPP.

Billy Brooks, Deputy Minister of Education, Illinois BPP.

Harlon Walton, member of the West Suburban Branch, Illinois BPP.
