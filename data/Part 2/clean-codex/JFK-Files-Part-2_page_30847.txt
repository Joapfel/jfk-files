Biography

Born 30 October 1927 in Nikolayev, Ukraine. Defected 4 February 1964.

Father: Ivan Isidorovich Nosenko.

Prominent Soviet shipbuilding engineer. Became Soviet Minister of Shipbuilding in Moscow.

Attended naval training schools. At end of war, entered Institute of International Relations in Moscow. Married in 1947 to daughter of Soviet lieutenant general. Marriage dissolved after Stalin's purge of father-in-law.

In 1950, joined Soviet Naval Intelligence Service and served in Far East and in Baltic. In early 1953, transferred to KGB; assigned as counterintelligence officer of American Embassy section of the American Department.

In June 1953, married daughter of First Deputy Chief of State Committee for Coordination of Scientific Research Work; wife and children left in Soviet Union when he defected.

In June 1955, transferred to Tourist Section of Seventh Department of KGB Internal Counterintelligence Directorate. Joined Communist Party in 1957.
