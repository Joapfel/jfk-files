WAVE 3103 (IN 46462), dated 8 January 1963.

Reported that Brigade leaders asked the families committee for $25,000 in operational funds, which they claimed Robert Kennedy indicated to Ruiz Williams (RUIZ Williams) and Roberto San Roman (Roberto/SAN ROMAN) should be made available to them through establishment of a joint bank account between the committee and the Brigade. The families committee turned down the Brigade request but was disturbed that the request was made.

Memo for the Record, dated 24 January 1964.

Subject: Roberto Perez San Roman (PEREZ San Roman) (201-282287).

In his 23 January meeting with Robert Kennedy, Perez remarked that he had discussed Brigade problems, in general, with the writer (Office of Security), whom he apparently identified only as his Agency contact. According to Perez, the Attorney General asked for the writer's identity, which Perez, who knows the writer only as “Bob,” was unable to give. (Was Robert F. Moroney.)
