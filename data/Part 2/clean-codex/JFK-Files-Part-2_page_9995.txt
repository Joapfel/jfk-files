Date: July 2, 1953.

To: Mr. James E. Hatcher, Chief, Investigations Division, U. S. Civil Service Commission, Washington 25, D. C.

From: John Edgar Hoover, Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation.

Subject: Executive Order 10450 Security Requirements for Government Employment.

With the inception of the cautioned Executive Order it became evident to this Bureau that certain problems would arise which have been encountered in the past in this Bureau's operations under the provisions of Executive Order 8335. Several of these have been posed to the Department of Justice and the latter has now furnished this Bureau its advice regarding them.

One consideration is just what agencies in the Federal Government are to be included in the operations of Executive Order 10450. As your files will reflect, by letter dated August 21, 1947, the Honorable Arthur J. Flemming, then the Acting President of the Commission, furnished this Bureau a list of all such agencies to be included within the provisions of Executive Order 9325. Numbered among these were the General Accounting Office and the Government Printing Office, neither of which were agencies of the executive branch. “The thought occurred to me whether these agencies are to be included under the new program and if so whether your estimated cases to be handled for these agencies in the current and succeeding fiscal years which you have furnished this Bureau include the General Accounting Office and the Government Printing Office.
