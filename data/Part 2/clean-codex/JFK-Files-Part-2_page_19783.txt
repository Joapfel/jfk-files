To: Director, FBI. From: SAC, New York (100-174481). Subject: Boston Women's Abortion Action Coalition, IS-C (Trotskyist).

ReWF Oteletype, dated 11/20/71, captioned “Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) Sponsored Demonstration, 11/20/71, Washington, D.C., IS—New Left,” and BS airtel and LHM, dated 1/18/72, captioned as above.

On 1/26/72, NY 7010-S provided a flyer on the WONAAC conference to be held on 2/11, 2/12, and 2/13/72 at Boston University, Boston, Massachusetts. It is to be noted that the flyer indicated a mass rally is scheduled for 2/11/72 from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM, and that strategy sessions, limited to women only, are scheduled for 2/12 and 2/13/72.

In view of the above, all female informants of the NYO operating in the New Left area have been canvassed and at this time, none are in a position to attend.

On 2/2/72, NY 4253-S advised that from 600 to 800 individuals were expected to attend the above conference, including people from Canada and other countries. The identities of these individuals or the speakers are not known at the present time, and the NYO has no additional information other than that set forth in referenced airtel.
