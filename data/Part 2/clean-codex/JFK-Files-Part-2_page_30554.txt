Hunt

Tricker compromised on 12/2/60 and cancelled; was assigned “Terence” in Calabana.

Was COS in Montevideo.

President Nardone of Uruguay asked Eisenhower not to transfer Hunt.

Envelope: “1960 Personal Information re. Auto accident, desire to stay on in Uruguayan assignment and work related problems.”

June 27, 1960.

6/27/60 Hunt TDA to Cuba, then to Mexico on PCS under both JMARC Dute commercial cover.

50 for JMARC ads Joseph Hangan.

Hunt was political action officer, representative to FRD in El Salvador and Mexico.
