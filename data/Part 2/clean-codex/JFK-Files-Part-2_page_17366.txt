FBI

Date: 2/7/69

Transmit the following in February.

Via teletype. Priority.

To: Director; SAC, San Francisco; SAC, Springfield.

From: SAC, Chicago.

Black Panther Party (BPP), racial matters.

Source, instant, advised Eddy Perry, aka Pablo (Peblo), alleged leader in the Vice Lords (VL), Chicago West Side street gang, and four other unknown alleged VL members met with BPP February 5 and 6 last, offering to change name to BPP and join forces. Source's opinion above represents sub-gangs of VL Nation and entire nation not represented.

Fred Hampton, deputy chairman, BPP, Chicago, and other BPP members suggested VL keep own name and group form.
