201-20-8

December 5, 1979, 1:47 PM.

Right type: ANKORD.

The U.S. have secretly written letter embodying following ideas:

“KALECYCLIC”

As you may know, for several months I have been engaging in introduction to the commercial items for Mr. SIMOBAS and Mr. VEGA of your embassy. For a number of months, those purchases went very well, and I believe both your government and I were satisfied with the area of items and deliveries.

Since the departure of Dr. STEWART and Mrs. VEGA, I have not been able to carry on my business with them for a normal salary. I have come to much trouble to gain articles that they were interested in and to deliver them as instructed; but now, after making my last delivery, I have still not been paid.

I object to the offer of payment in making payment to me and request that Mr. SIMOBAS or his agregado in this city be informed that I already sent the money and cannot make further deliveries until—

And now go back to your work.
