Member of subject organization: Doctor Barbara Roberts, WONAAC project director; Marcia Coleman, Black Task Force; Beulah Sanders, National Welfare Rights Organization; Shirley Wheeler, convicted abortion law violator, State of Florida; Lana Clarke Davis, who stated she had an abortion thirty years ago in Florida; Linda Jenness, SWP presidential candidate, 1972; Marge Sloan, a Black female; Joyce Brown, not further identified; Doctor Ann Clay, Florida abortion group; Carol Henderson Evans, Boston Women's Liberation; Linda Dineen, from Canada, who averred that Canadian abortion laws are repressive; Stephanie Coontz, National Peace Action-Coalition; and Nancy Stearn, lawyer for Shirley Wheeler.
