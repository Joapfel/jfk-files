Title: Washington, D.C., Chapter Womens National Abortion Action Coalition (D.C. WONAAAC)  
Character: Internal Security-C (Trotskyist)  
Office: Washington, D.C.  
Bureau File #: 100-468406

Synopsis: D.C. WONAAAC affiliate of Womens National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC), which was founded July, 1971. WONAAAC national headquarters originally located Washington, D.C. (WDC), but moved to New York, New York, December, 1971. D.C. WONAAAC located at 1346 Connecticut Avenue, N.W., WDC, and key people involved all members of WDC locals of Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) and/or Socialist Workers Party (SWP). D.C. WONAAAC does not maintain formal membership, and funds are obtained through contributions. Does not hold regular meetings, and most activities are in conjunction with those planned by national office. Goals synonymous with those of national office, which have been defined as “working on a national scale to make abortion legal, to end restrictive contraception laws, and to end forced sterilization.”

Details: At Washington, D.C.
