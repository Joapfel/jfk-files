NR009 NY CODE

April 10, 1971, 9:20 a.m., urgent.

To: Director, Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division; Alexandria; Washington Field 100-53558; Chicago.

From: New York 100-172646.

Women’s National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971. IS-MISC.

Re New York telephone call to Washington Field Office instant date. On instant date at 7:30 a.m., SA’s observed three buses—Coast Cities Cruiser No. 511, Community Coach Lines Nos. 506 and 426—depart from Union Square, New York City, for Washington, D.C. (DMC), with approximately 135 girls under 25 years of age, 95 percent white.
