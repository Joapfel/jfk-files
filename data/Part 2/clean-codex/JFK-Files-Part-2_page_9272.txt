J. Rallies and Demonstrations

During the period May–October 1972, the Detroit Chapter of the BPP did not hold any rallies or sponsor any demonstrations.

DE T-8  
October 26, 1972

K. Programs

1. Free Breakfast for School Children

The breakfast program continues to be in operation at 2228 Bewick, Detroit.

DE T-6  
May 5, 1972

The breakfast program at 2228 Bewick, Detroit, has been suspended temporarily in lieu of a free lunch program during the summer months. The breakfast program will be reinstituted at the start of the school year in September 1972.

DE T-6  
June 16, 1972

The free lunch program has been replaced by the free breakfast program.

DE T-2  
September 6, 1972

The breakfast program is also in operation at 157 Collingwood, Detroit, and at 2228 Bewick, Detroit. Food for the programs is solicited from store owners in the area and some food is purchased by the Party.

DE T-2  
September 14, 1972
