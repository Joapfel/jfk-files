FBI.

Date: 9/17/69.

Teletypes.

To: Director, FBI; SAC, San Francisco.

From: SAC, Chicago.

Black Panther Party (BPP). Racial Matters.

A source reliable in the past advised today that he learned from Fred Hampton, chairman, Illinois chapter, BPP, that Hampton received instructions from national BPP headquarters to arrange a mass demonstration at noon Saturday, September 20, next, at the Federal Building, 219 South Dearborn, Chicago. The theme of the demonstration was said to be “Where is Bobby?” Hampton indicated he had been instructed to solicit the cooperation of Chicago peace groups; names were not given.

Bobby Seale, national chairman, BPP, scheduled to appear in Chicago September 24, next, for trial.
