LLAGOSTERA: Said, today a good man died.

CONCEPCION: Asked who.

Wife.

LLAGOSTERA: Responded, Kennedy.

CONCEPCION: Laughs.

Wife.

LLAGOSTERA: Said, poor man, may God have him in glory.

CONCEPCION: Laughs again.

Wife.

1330 hours — Call to Embassy.

CALLER (female, unidentified): Asked if LUISA knows about the news of Kennedy's death.

LUISA: Surprised, said it is a lie and asked who.

CALLER: Said in an attempt in Texas.

LUISA: Again surprised, questioned if the news was official and asked when it occurred.

CALLER: It happened at 1300 hours.

LUISA: Became joyful and said, “How great!”

CALLER: Laughs and indicates that his (Kennedy's) brother and wife were also injured.

LUISA: Laughs and says again, “How great; nevertheless, I don't believe it.”

CALLER: Says turn on the radio.
