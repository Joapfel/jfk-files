SR POINTER Information

HT LINGUAL—an DO project: opening U.S. citizens' mail going to other countries, mostly the Soviet Union, during the 1950s. Most mail was opened in New York.

Actual opening and monitoring of mail was assigned to OS, with information turned over to the DO.

SR-POINTER—OS name for HT LINGUAL.

WESTPOINTER—opening mail in San Francisco, also known as MK SOURDOUGH. Targeted the Chinese.

PROJECT INDIAN—targeted mail to and from Pakistan.

BANJO—actual letters, copies intercepted.

PROJECT SETTER—mail opened in New Orleans, targeting South America.

The project was exposed in the Rockefeller Commission report.

Project names released with JFK information.
