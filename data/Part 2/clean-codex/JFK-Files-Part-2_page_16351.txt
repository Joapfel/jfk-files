Federal Bureau of Investigation

June 6, 1969

A search of the premises located at 2350 West Madison Avenue, Chicago, Illinois, was conducted incidental to the arrest of eight Black Panther Party members. The search took place on June 4, 1969, between 6:30 a.m. and 7:39 a.m. The search yielded the following items of ammunition:

Shotgun cartridges: 1 box of 25 20-gauge shotgun shells, 25; 2 boxes of 25 16-gauge shotgun shells, 50; 4 boxes of 25 12-gauge shotgun shells, 100; 13 loose 16-gauge shotgun shells, 13; 40 loose 20-gauge shotgun shells, 40. Total: 228 shells.

Rifle and handgun cartridges: 83 .38-caliber cartridges; 188 .30-06-caliber cartridges; 233 7-62 x .33-caliber cartridges; 73 3.80 automatic-caliber cartridges; 46 .22-caliber cartridges; 30 .25 x .35-caliber cartridges; 21 .40-caliber cartridges; 9 .32 automatic-caliber cartridges; 1 .30-30 Winchester-caliber cartridge; 1 .32 Remington-caliber cartridge; 1 6.0 Remington-caliber cartridge. Total: 686.

On June 4, 1969, in Chicago, Illinois.

SA Donald W. Oxley

June 6, 1969

Date completed: June 6, 1969.
