1148 PM urgent, 12-13-69.

To Director.

From Chicago.

IP.

Black Panther Party (BPP), PM.

Tom Panush, records officer, Cook County Jail, Chicago, Illinois, advised instant that the following were released instant on bond: Brenda Harris, five thousand (five hundred cash); Lewis Trulock, two five thousand (two five hundred cash); Harold Bell, five thousand (five hundred cash); Blair Anderson, five thousand (five hundred cash).

Above arrested December four, last, by Chicago PD, after shootout at 2337 West Monroe, Chicago, which resulted in the death of Fred Allen Hampton, deputy chairman, Illinois BPP, and Mark Clark, lieutenant, Illinois BPP, at Peoria, Illinois.
