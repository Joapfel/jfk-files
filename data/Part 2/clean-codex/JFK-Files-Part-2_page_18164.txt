U.S. Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSC).

Re: Interview of former FBI Special Agent (SA) Edward F. Gamber by SSC staff member.

All information contained herein is unclassified. Date: 10/18/75. By SA Gamber (Gamberg).

Set out below is information concerning an interview of former FBI SA Edward F. Gamber by an SSC staff member.

On receipt of information by the FBI that the SSC might interview Gamber, he was contacted by the Atlanta, Georgia, Field Office of the FBI on August 15, 1975, and advised of the necessity, should he be interviewed by the SSC, to protect matters relating to sensitive Bureau operations. Such matters would relate to sources, methods, techniques, third agency consideration and ongoing investigations. He was told that if he desired, after being contacted by the SSC staff, he could contact the FBI's Legal Counsel Division by collect call for information to assist him, including his obligations as to confidentiality of the information acquired as an FBI employee. It was emphasized to him that the FBI's offer of assistance was not intended to impede the SSC work but was done as a cooperative gesture and to safeguard sensitive Bureau information.

On August 21, 1975, Gamber advised the Atlanta Field Office that he had been contacted by Paul Wallach, SSC staff member, who had arranged to interview Gamber in the Atlanta area on September 2, 1975. Gamber also advised that he intended to contact the Legal Counsel Division of the FBI by collect call on August 22, 1975.

Personnel file: Former SA Edward F. Gamber.
