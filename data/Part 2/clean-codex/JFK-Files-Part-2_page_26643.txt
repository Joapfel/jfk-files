Soviet Submarine-Launched Missiles.

Cruise missile. U.S. designation: SS-N-3. IOC: 1961. Operational range: 250 NM. Warhead weight: 1,000–2,000 lbs. Remarks: Carried by E-I and E-II-class nuclear submarines and by J-class diesel units. A few W-class diesel torpedo attack submarines were modified to carry this missile. Surface launched.

Ballistic missiles. U.S. designation: SS-N-4. IOC: 1960. Operational range: 300 NM. Warhead weight: About 2,200 lbs. Remarks: Carried by most G-class diesel submarines and by some H-class nuclear units. A few Z-class diesel torpedo attack submarines were modified to carry this missile. Surface launched.

U.S. designation: SS-N-5. IOC: 1963. Operational range: 650 NM. Warhead weight: 1,600–2,400 lbs. Remarks: Carried by most H-class nuclear submarines and by some G-class diesel units. Underwater launched.

U.S. designation: SS-N-6. IOC: Probably 1968. Operational range: 1,500 NM. Warhead weight: 700–1,700 lbs. Remarks: Probably intended for the new Y-class nuclear submarines. These 16-tube Polaris-type units first went into service in mid-1968.
