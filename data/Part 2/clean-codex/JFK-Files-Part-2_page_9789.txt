Memorandum

To: Clarence M. Kelley, Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation

From: Glen E. Pommerening, Assistant Attorney General for Administration

Subject: Federal Employee Security Program, Executive Order 10450

Date: August 23, 1974

The practice as set forth in your subject memorandum of August 14, 1974, for furnishing to the Office of Management and Finance copies of investigative reports satisfies the needs of the Department.
