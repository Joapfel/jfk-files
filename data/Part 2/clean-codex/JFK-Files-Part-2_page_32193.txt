NSC Action No. 483 NSC 2427h, 5/5/61. Substance: No separate Cuban military forces should be organized in U.S. Cuban nationals would be encouraged to enlist in U.S. Armed Forces. Status: Closed. DEF recommended to President (Pres.) draft program be terminated 6/30/62 due to modest results.

NSC Action No. 483 NSC 2422j, 5/5/61. Substance: No immediate trade embargo on Cuba. Status: Closed, with President's action of 2/3/62 declaring total embargo on trade with Cuba with exception of medicine and food on humanitarian grounds.

NSC Action No. 483 NSC 2422l, 5/5/61. Substance: Alliance for Progress to be strengthened. Status: Closed. Weekly report now from AID to White House.

NSC Action No. NASAN 194, 10/2/62. Substance: Policy toward non-bloc (non-block) ships in Cuban trade (action U.S. Government proposes to take with regard to shipping to Cuba). Status: Pending. Action transferred to EXCOM.

NSC Action No. NASAN 100, 10/3/61. Substance: Contingency planning for Cuba. Status: Pending. Action transferred to Johnson channel.
