Staff members’ print cards from the FBI were turned over to Pvt. Alford, who receipted for them, so he could take them away from Committee spaces and spend more time studying them. During the week of July 2, 1978, Pvt. Alford was able to obtain the fingerprint card of Regis Blahut, a former D.C. fireman. On Friday, July 7, 1978, at approximately 2:30 P.M., Pvt. Alford contacted William Cross of the Security Staff and stated he had made a tentative identification of Blahut’s prints on the safe door and one of the photos in Book Five, Part One of the Kennedy autopsy photos. On Monday, July 10, 1978, at 2:30 P.M., Pvt. Alford returned to the Committee offices and, in the presence of G. Robert Blakey, Robert Morrison, and William H. Cross, returned all fingerprint cards and the 13 cards of lifted prints. He stated that he had identified a certain number of points in several prints and, in his opinion, they were those of Regis Blahut. These prints were on the inside of the safe door and on photo 39E2, which had been ripped from Book Five, Part One, and removed from the cellophane cover. After returning all materials, Pvt. Alford departed at 2:55 P.M. on July 10, 1978.
