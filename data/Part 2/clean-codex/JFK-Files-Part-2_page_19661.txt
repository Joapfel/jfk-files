Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Communications Section

March 9, 1971

Teletype

NR001. Plain.

11:42 a.m. Urgent, 3/9/71, VAB.

To Director (Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division)

From Seattle (100-30902).

Committee to Defend the Right to Live, International Women’s Day march, Seattle, Washington, March 8, 1971. IS-Miscellaneous. VIDEM. OO: SE.

On March 9, instant, Seattle Police Department reported approximately sixty persons assembled at the building housing the Women’s Jail, Fourth and Cherry, Seattle, Washington, beginning about 4 p.m. March 8 last, and marched through downtown area for about thirty minutes beginning at 5 p.m. Affair was under the auspices of a new group calling themselves the Committee to Defend the Right to Live, but the core of the participants appeared to be former members of the Seattle Liberation Front, which was responsible for a violent attack on the Federal Courthouse in Seattle in February.
