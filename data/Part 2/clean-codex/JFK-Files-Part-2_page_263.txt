162125Z. DIR CITE MEXI 5254. LICOCKY. Reference: DIR 45335.

1. Station probably at fault for not forwarding LICOCKY-1 my address in MEXI 4628. However, feel in general when P3PRIME national agent asks for contact, must be for what he feels good reason.

2. L-1 relates that while visiting home of friend in MY SHE, she picked up phone and caller was Estelle “Stasha” Sokolowska (Madrigal), whom she had known in PERUMEN and in MEXI during SI, where she had come to have baby of Harold Martinez, Nicaraguan leader of Frente Sandino in Cuba. Attending physician was Mario Madrigal, young Costa Rican Communist doctor who subsequently married Sokolowska and took her to PERUMEN some time in 1962. Sokolowska had left Madrigal and arrived in New York via Montreal, 12 or 13 September. Told L-1 that “if nothing else worked out,” planned to join Jo Anne Salas, American, now teaching English in CHISA. Sokolowska’s mother is Anna Sokolowska, 563 179th St., Bronx, NY. Estelle was staying with her.
