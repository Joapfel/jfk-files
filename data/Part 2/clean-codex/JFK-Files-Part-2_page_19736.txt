Federal Communications Division.

November 20, 1971.

NR 004 SF PLAIN.

4:46 P.M., November 20, 1971. Urgent message.

To: Director.

From: San Francisco (100-71464) (P).

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (NORAAC); demonstration, November 20, San Francisco, is New Left.

Rally at Civic Center ended by 3:45 P.M. Official police estimate of crowd was approximately 800. Several hundred young people attending a ski show nearby were merely onlookers and not part of demonstration. No incidents or arrests.

Administrative.

Re San Francisco Telegram, November 20, 1971. REC-32 100-468406.

SAS observing were Edward O'Flynn and Jay Grodin. Jerry Wright, 115th MIG, advised. Tom Dickson, Intelligence Detail, source of official crowd estimate.

LHM follows.

End.
