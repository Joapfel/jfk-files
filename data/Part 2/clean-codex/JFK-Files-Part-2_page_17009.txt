SAC, Chicago. 2/20/70.

SA Roy Martin Mitchell.

Black Panther Party. RM-BPF.

Who has provided reliable information in the past, will not testify, but attests to accuracy of the following.

Advised on 2/20/70.

According to various stories circulating among BPP leaders and lawyers concerning the police raid on 12/4/69 at 2337 West Monroe Street, BRENDA HARRIS fired the first shot from a shotgun after the police announced that they were police officers and there to serve a search warrant for possession of illegal weapons.

Source stated that SKIP ANDREW, BPP lawyer, telephonically advised BPP leaders on this date that those surviving the above raid are to appear at the Federal Grand Jury hearing on 2/24/70 after 2:00 p.m. at 210 South Dearborn, and all are instructed to plead the Fifth Amendment.
