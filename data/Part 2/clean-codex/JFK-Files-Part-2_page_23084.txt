In mid-1960, the counterrevolutionary Armando Cubría Ramos and Mario Tauler Sagué, members of the “La Cruz” counterrevolutionary organization, were infiltrated through the Punta Hicacos zone in Matanzas Province.

Tauler Sagué, a gangster, together with Cubría Ramos, were instructed to carry out a plot against our Prime Minister, as well as to commit various sabotage and terrorism actions. For accomplishing this, they were given a great quantity of warlike material and equipment by the CIA, which were taken from them when arrested.
