10/22/69

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago

Demtri, IS-M.  
David Dellinger, et al., ARL—conspiracy.  
Black Panther Party, RN.

BU agents observed approximately thirty members of the Black Panther Party (BPP), Chicago Chapter, participating in a demonstration in the plaza of the Federal Building, Chicago, at 1:00 p.m. instant. A group of ten BPP members discontinued marching in the plaza and all gathered around Fred Hampton (Bred Hampton), deputy chairman, Illinois Chapter BPP. After conferring for approximately ten minutes, the group presented themselves at the entrance (EXTERCE) of the Federal Building for the purpose of obtaining admission to the courtroom where the conspiracy trial of Bobby George Seale (SRALE) and seven other defendants was in session. UMs admitted fifteen BPP members after searching for weapons. The remainder of the group, including Hampton, then dispersed and left the area of the Federal Building.

As of 1:30 p.m., the area of the Federal Building returned to normal.

RAC: DBS
