WAVE 4222 (IN 17952), dated 12 September 1963.

Immediate action: Stated that the November 1963 issue of See Magazine, already on newsstands, contained a wanted poster of Fidel Castro on the front cover. Poster read, “Wanted dead or alive, Fidel Castro for crimes against humanity, 10 million dollars reward.” Same copy highlighted these stories, with top billing given to a story titled “The CIA needs men—can you qualify?” The wanted poster was part of a story, the gist of which was that DRE offers a 10 million dollar reward “to person or persons who with the help of the DRE will assassinate Fidel Castro.” Offer alleged that reward was on deposit with Banco de Santo Domingo and was signed by Luis Fernandez-Rocha Rodriguez. Comment: Fernandez first came to the U.S. in May 1960. Was polygraphed in March 1962, suspected of being G-2. Clearance granted 18 April 1962 for use in DRE; encrypted AMHINT-53. Featured on Channel 4 TV on 26 September 1962 as having been organizing the DRE underground in Cuba since 20 May and having directed an attack on 24 August on Teatro Blanquita.
