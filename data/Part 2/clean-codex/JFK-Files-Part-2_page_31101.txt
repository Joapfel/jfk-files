DWO — 1 volume + 1 package of loose documents.

8 November 1961. Personality File Action Request from Howard Gossage /W144. Jorge Angel Nobregas Heria (sic), DOB 7/22/24, Havana.

3 June 1957 — Field Information Report. Documentary list of Cubans who left Havana for exile during the period of 7 May–1 June 1957. Jorge A. Nobregas Heria (sic), 1 June, to San Jose.

PRQ, 23 October 1961. Jorge Angel Nobregas Heren. Educated in the U.S.: Georgia Military Academy, North Georgia College, and University of Georgia. B.A. in International Relations, 1950.

Dispatch, 27 October 1961, to Chief: WHD from COS/SNWAVE. Lists Nobregas as one of the crew members of M/V Susan Ann.

29 August 1961. Memorandum, attention Bill, signed B. Information on Jorge Nobregas, also known as “Coco.” Jorge was one of three brothers: Leele, Jorge, and Cuello. He was an activist in the revolution against Batista. He worked in the Aleman group. When Castro took over, Nobregas took over Hotel Riviera in Cuba. He was accused of stealing Riviera funds but protected by Chief of Police Almejeiras, who supposedly was the one who placed him in the Ministry.
