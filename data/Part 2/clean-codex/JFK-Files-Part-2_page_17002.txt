1/2/70

Teletype.

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago

Black Panther Party. Re: BFP. OO: San Francisco. Reliable in past.

Confidential source advised that an instant rally was scheduled for 3 p.m., January 4, next, at the Afro-Arts Theatre (AFFRO-ARTS THEATRE), 3937 South Drexel Boulevard, Chicago.

Rally in memorial to Fred Hampton (FRED HALFTON), deceased deputy chairman, Illinois BFP, killed December 4, last, in a raid conducted by Cook County State’s Attorney’s Police.

Source also advised BFP was tentatively planning to announce opening of “People’s Medical Center,” with purpose of freely treating ghetto residents.

Source stated Bobby Rush, defense minister, Illinois BFP; D. B. Lucas, leader, Black Liberation Alliance, Chicago; and Alderman A. A. “Sammy” Rashan were being advertised as speakers. Entertainment will also be provided, according to source, and contributions solicited. Money obtained.
