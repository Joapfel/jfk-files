3 March 1994

Memo to: C/HRG

Subject: Mangold Litigation

Today I alerted OGC (Theresa Wilcox/Katlynn Kelly) that the JFK collection contains a significant number of documents on Nosenko (5,000–6,000 pages), which may impact the Mangold litigation. As I understand it, the Mangold Litigation is over the Angleton (Angelton) files and is several years old. According to JFK reviewers at the FBI, documents on Nosenko's defection and treatment are part of the case. I talked with Theresa Wilcox, who is the paralegal handling the litigation; however, she was not familiar with the documents involved. She will consult with the lawyer on the case and get back to me.

I consider the ball to be in OGC's court. I propose that we continue to review the Nosenko files. If there are Mangold or other considerations to address, we can do so after the HRG review is complete.

Barry
