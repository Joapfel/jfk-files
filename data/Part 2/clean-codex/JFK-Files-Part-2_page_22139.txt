Persons/Entities and dates: McCone; Fitzgerald; Hughes — 8/19/64. Bundy; Papich; Bosch, Jose — 7/30/64. Jessup; RFK. Steakley; JMWAVE. JMWAVE; Vance — 6/10/64.

Categories: SGA; MONGODB; CASTRO.

Occurrence: According to the minutes of the 303 Committee on 7/30/64, “It was agreed that Mr. Fitzgerald would contact Sam Papich of the FBI in regard to the earlier report of an alleged plot with Mafia overtones to assassinate Castro and which the AG agreed to handle as a matter of law enforcement.” (116) The reference is clearly to a 6/10/64 memorandum information report from the DDP to the Director, (116) which was disseminated to the Special Assistant to the President for National Security Affairs; Assistant Secretary for Inter-American Affairs, Department of State; Director of Intelligence and Research, State Department; Director, DIA; the AG; the Director, FBI; DDCI and the DCI. The 6/10/74 report “related a proposal for the assassination of Castro that was made to prominent Cuban exiles. The Mafia appeared to be involved in the scheme.” The asking price was $150,000. Jose Mario Pepe in Bosch, Preso of Bacardi Rum, would contribute $50,000. Bosch approached the Chief of Station, JMWAVE, and suggested the U.S. government also contribute. This was rejected. The record indicates CIA’s only involvement was to report information of its existence.

Source: Last record of incident found by IG Rept. is memo from McCone to Bundy, 8/19/64, reporting the results of FBI interviews with the alleged participants. “Obviously nothing came of the plot.” (117)
