9/18/58

PLATIN

Airtel

NY 105-18389

Advised on 9/17/58 that a female, who is apparently from the UN, mentioned to subject that a girlfriend of hers was giving a party on Saturday and asked her if she had any friends to bring along. She thought that perhaps subject could come and also bring a friend, as her girlfriend is short of men. This woman expressed the thought that it would be nice to have subject there, as they would enjoy his company. Subject gave a preliminary affirmative answer to the invitation and arranged to discuss it further with this woman at the UN on 9/16/58. He mentioned that he has had a very difficult week and that anything could happen.

This woman mentioned that, as far as subject's book is concerned, she has checked every day but it has not been received as yet. She believes that these books come in from Europe. Subject mentioned that they were having a lesson tomorrow, Thursday, and Friday they arranged to see one another before their class. This woman would like to know at that time whether subject could bring a friend, as she would like to advise her girlfriend so that she could plan on a certain number of people.

Informants at the UN Secretariat were alerted to the possible presence of subject there on 9/10/58, and it is the intention of this office to afford further coverage of subject on Saturday, 9/20/58.

Also advised on 9/17/58 that subject and an individual believed to be BAN JAME were in contact with one another and that this individual invited subject to his home for dinner on Friday, 9/16/58. He gave his address as 200 West 4th Ave., NYC (JAME's address). This individual said that he wanted to see subject on the day of the opening session but that he was called to NJ about the reason which occurred there. He chided subject about the fact that subject never watches television and would not be allowed of this. Subject mentioned that he might be in this individual on television interviewing a young hero who saved a woman.
