United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. C. D. DeLoach

From: Mr. W. Sullivan

Subject: FBI Intelligence Letter for the President

Date: November 7, 1969

Pursuant to the Director's instructions to keep the President fully informed of significant intelligence developments within the purview of the Bureau's security responsibilities, we will prepare and transmit to him, on a continuing basis, an intelligence letter of the type enclosed. To preserve the impact and selectivity of this letter, dissemination is suggested for only the President and the Attorney General.

Recommendation: That the attached letters to the President and the Attorney General be approved enclosing the proposed current intelligence letter.
