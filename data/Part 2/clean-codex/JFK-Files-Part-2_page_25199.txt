Persons/Entities: McCone, Lodge, Smith. Date: October 6, 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: In a CIA cable to Saigon (DIR7366lof, 6 October), the following appears: “... McCone directs that you withdraw recommendations from Ambassador (concerning assassination plan) under McCone instructions as we cannot be in imposition actively condoning such course of action and thereby engaging our responsibility therefore.”

Source: IG Report 1967 (CIA), p. 28. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/27/75.

Persons/Entities: Trueheart (TRUEHART), Lodge, McCone, Smith. Date: October 7, 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: In a response to the CIA cable of October 6, 1963, to Saigon, the station in Saigon responded (SAIG1463, 7 October): “Action taken as directed.” In addition, since DCM Trueheart (TRUEHART) was also present when the original recommendation was made, specific withdrawal recommendation at McCone’s instruction was also conveyed to Trueheart. Ambassador Lodge commented that he shares McCone’s opinion.

Source: IG Report 1967 (CIA), p. 29. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/27/75.
