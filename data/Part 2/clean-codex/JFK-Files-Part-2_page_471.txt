L-3 stated Curbelo on 18 August indicated Cubans very fearful for Cuban/Mexican relations due to Rico Galan case and did not want to be accused by Mexicans of granting asylum to guilty parties. It is very unusual for Cubana on Mexico-to-Havana run to carry only five passengers.

4. L-3 stated he saw Fernando Rico Galan, brother of Victor, in Cubana office 12 August attempting to arrange flight to Havana. Flight did not come to Mexico City that day, and Fernando was arrested in roundup. L-3 also saw on 12 August one Noel Barra Zenil attempting to arrange passage. Barra described by L-3 as a rabid Communist revolutionary who was close to Rico Galan and until recently a long resident in Havana.

Also recently L-3 asked Lopez why he did not do more in cultural press work for Cuba. Lopez replied that was not important, but what was important was assisting revolutionary forces in Mexico.
