11/24/69. Code. Teletype. Urgent.

To: Director. From: SAC, Chicago.

Frederick Allen Hampton. Threat against the President.

Luis Kutner, attorney at law, 105 West Adams, Chicago, Illinois, advised instant date that he was present at a meeting on November 23 last by the University of Illinois Circle Campus, Chicago, Illinois, sponsored by the Women’s International League for Peace and Freedom, Chicago Metropolitan Branch, a pacifist group wherein Frederick Allyn (Allen) Hampton spoke concerning the Black Panther Party (BPP), of which he is the chairman for the State of Illinois.

Meeting attended by approximately 40 individuals, mostly elderly white women, and Hampton spoke from approximately 3:00 p.m. to 3:50 p.m. about the BPP as a revolutionary party.

During this speech, Hampton stated on three or four occasions, “Nixon must die.” Hampton spoke concerning the possession by the BPP of guns, both at their headquarters, 2350 West Madison, Chicago, as well as at the Hampton Resort.
