June 5, 1975

Legal Division

Attached teletype advises that former Agent Richard H. Davis, Atlanta Office, was contacted by Senate Select Committee (SSC) Staff Member (FNU) Epstein in an attempt to arrange an interview concerning the Bureau investigation of Martin Luther King. On the night of June 4, 1975, Michael Epstein, Staff Member, SSC, advised SA Paul V. Daly that he had contacted former Agent Davis to arrange an interview concerning Bureau investigations of Martin Luther King, Stanley David Levison, and the Southern Christian Leadership Conference (Southern Leadership Christian Conference) (SLCC). Epstein stated Davis refused to submit to an interview since he was bound by an employment secrecy agreement and needed necessary clearance from the Bureau. He told Epstein that he would not contact the Bureau for such clearance. Epstein requested that the Bureau release Davis from the agreement for purposes of this interview, which will be a staff interview in Atlanta.

Legal Division recommends that Davis be released from the security agreement for purposes of this interview and to answer questions concerning the King, Levison, and SLCC investigations. If approved, we will separately advise Davis through the Atlanta Office of this decision and give him pertinent briefing.
