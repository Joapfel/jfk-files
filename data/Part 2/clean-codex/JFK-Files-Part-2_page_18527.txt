Teletyp[e] urgent. To SAC Atlanta. From Director, FBI (62-116395). SENSTUDY 75.

New York Office has advised that former SA John G. Willis was recently contacted by Senate Select Committee (SSC) staff member Patrick Shea, who interviewed him relative to his knowledge of former Bureau source Sam Jaffe, who allegedly has been in contact with SSC. Jaffe allegedly advised SSC that he formerly enjoyed a pleasant relationship with former SA Willis and former SA Edward F. Gamber with regard to Soviet intelligence matters. Jaffe allegedly stated to SSC that Bureau personnel became cool toward him in approximately 1966–1968 after Jaffe returned from Hong Kong and that since that time Jaffe has been unable to hold a job and believes that the FBI is being vindictive toward him and is depriving him of a means of making a livelihood.
