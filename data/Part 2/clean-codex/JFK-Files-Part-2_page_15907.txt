5/26/69

Teletype

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Chicago

Frederick Allen Hampton. RM.

Sergeant Phillip Hartigan, Cook County Sheriff's Police, advised instant Hampton sentenced two to four years on robbery conviction this PM, by Judge Sidney Jones, Maywood, Illinois.

No appeal bond and Hampton to be incarcerated Illinois State Penitentiary, Joliet, Illinois, this PM. Military advised.
