Phone memo on intercept. 15 October 1962.

“MO asks NEFYEDOV for either SARNASHEV or KOSTIKOV. Neither of them is in. NEF asks who is speaking. VSEMOCHKIN says that NEF doesn’t know him. ‘We are passing through Mexico.’ NEF tells MO to hold the line. GARM takes the phone. MO repeats his name—VSEMOCHKIN. MO says that KOSTIKOV promised to come over. KOSTIKOV takes the phone. MO asks KOSTIKOV if he will come and get us. KOSTIKOV doesn’t have an auto available right now. KOSTIKOV says that everything opens at 1000. MO says we will be waiting for KOSTIKOV. Apparently, MO and others are going to Habana. MO asks if KOSTIKOV could get 500 dollars that ‘we’ have spent on the trip. KOSTIKOV will see about it.”

Date of birth: 17 March 1933, Moscow. Employee, government embassy, Mexico City, DOI June 1961.
