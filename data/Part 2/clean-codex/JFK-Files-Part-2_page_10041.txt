United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. J. E. Adams

From: Legal Counsel Jan

Subject: SENSTUDY 75

Date: 3/8/76

Attached is a letter dated 3/5/76 addressed to Michael E. Shaheen, Jr., Esquire, Office of Professional Responsibility, U.S. Department of Justice, from Frederick A. O. Schwarz, Jr., Chief Counsel of the captioned Committee, confirming the fact that the FBI has reviewed the captioned Committee's draft report titled “CIA and FBI Mail Opening.” The FBI's review, according to this letter, was to ensure the draft report met all security problems. It is noted that the FBI's review of this report is not for purposes of accuracy but rather to ensure that there are no problems relating to classification of material or similar problems.

Recommendation:

That the Intelligence Division review the attached letter to determine if it accurately reports our review of the report in question and whether there may be additional changes desired in the draft report.
