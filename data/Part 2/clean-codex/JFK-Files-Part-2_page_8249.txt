October 1950

Early in October 1950, Dimitrov visited the Lavrion Camp, where he met with a number of his Bulgarian friends and advised them that he had been given a “charter and instructions” by certain members of the American Embassy to form a committee of five members for planning and executing a fight for the liberation of Bulgaria. This committee, according to Dimitrov, would be established in Athens and would cooperate with other similar committees already formed in France, Germany, and other countries. The committee would later expand to 20 members and establish a so-called “Democratic Agrarian Bloc.” Recruits would come from among members and sympathizers of the Bulgarian Democratic Party (Moushynov) and from the Gichev Agrarians.
