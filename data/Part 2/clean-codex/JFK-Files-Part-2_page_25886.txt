Soviet Embassy, 15-60-55.

27 September 1963.

134-M8-T128, 0900–2114 hours.

395-(1605) IN. OV: From the Cuban Consulate. Says that there is an American here who has requested a transit visa for Cuba because he is going to the USSR. OV wants to know with whom he (the American) spoke at the Soviet Embassy because she (OV) had sent him over to them, thinking that if the Soviets approved the visa, then the Cubans would give him one without any more processing, only advising the Immigration of Cuba. OV wants to know with whom the American spoke since he himself does not know either, and he was told that there were no problems. IV asks that OV wait a minute.

404–410. OV is Silvia Duran of the Cuban Consulate and explains the same to the other IV. This one tells her to leave her name and number so he can call her. Silvia gives it to him along with the telephone number, 11-28-47.

Silvia says as a personal thing that she has moved and that she has not advised them about her change of address and will not receive the Bulletin, and she can advise them now. IV asks that she call Koukharenko in order to change the address, and then asks for the name of the Cultural Attaché. Silvia says it is Teresa Proenza, whose number is 14-13-26. IV thanks her.
