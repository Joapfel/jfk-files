7. PRQ Part 2 169 C85514

Section I: Contact and Development

Woodrow C. Olien met Ray in October 1958, when Ray was head of the Civic Resistance Movement in Havana. Three clandestine meetings were held prior to 1 January 1959, attended by Olien, Wallace A. Growery, and Ignacio Mendoza (MRC 04 member), who originally introduced Olien to Ray. After 1 January 1959, Ray was given an important post in the revolutionary government, and Olien has seen him only twice since. Olien posed as a political officer of the State Department.

Section II of the PRQ states Ray is friendly to the U.S., once having received a grant for special study there. He is a dedicated, patriotic Cuban, opposed to Communism but has taken no active part in combating it.
