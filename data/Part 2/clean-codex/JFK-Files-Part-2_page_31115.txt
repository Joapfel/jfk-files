16 August 1962 Information Report #00-K-3,233,366.

Subject: Christian Democratic Movement (MDC).

Source: Cuban national presently in exile in 45.

After the failure of the Bay of Pigs (Bay of Pigeon), the MDC began to split into factions. In October 1961, Gepin Fernandez Badue also “lives” arrived from Cuba and stated he was a representative of the clandestine MDC movement in Cuba. Mrs. Cardona accepted his credentials and assigned him to the CRC council.

MDC parole consists of three groups. One, headed by Jose Ignacio Basco, has the largest following and is organized in Miami, New York, Chicago, Venezuela, and Cuba. Another group is headed by Melcher Gaston; this group broke away after the invasion but is now coming back to the original MDC. The third group is headed by Laureano Batista, who is also bringing his group back to the original MDC. Batista is presently president of the MDC, and the three groups are believed to be reunited by August 1962, rendering Batista’s leadership.
