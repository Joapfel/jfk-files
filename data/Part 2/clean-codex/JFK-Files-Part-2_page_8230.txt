Headquarters. AMCHAOS.

Ref: Headquarters.

1. In final coverage on 18 May of WPC, which ended 16 May, press has made the following additional observations regarding U.S. participants:

A. Sidney Peck (P. Eck), identified as “USA professor,” spoke at the Congress on Victory War.

B. WPC has awarded the Joliot-Curie Gold Medal posthumously to Rev. Martin Luther King. The medal was accepted by Rev. Ralph Abernathy (Abrahathy) and Rev. W. Walker, “an American peace fighter.”

A photograph (pouched) of the presentation also pictures U.S. delegates William Douthard (Dourt Hard) and Mrs. Addie Wyatt.

C. U.S. delegates Ruth Gage-Colby (Ruth Gage - Col B') and Sylvia Kuscher (Kusher) (SIO) spoke at a meeting of Indochinese and American delegates.

D. Americans Herbert Aptheker (Apt Heker) and Carlton Goodlett were reelected to the Presidential Board.
