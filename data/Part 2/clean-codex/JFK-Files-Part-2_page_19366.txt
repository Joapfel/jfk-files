7:50 PM, urgent, 1-10-68, ARK.

To: Director and Washington Field. From: Atlanta.

Demonstration protesting United States policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January 15–16 next; IS; OO: WFO.

Cynthia Yvonne Mitchell, PRT (Ghetto), being designated by Atlanta to accompany group leaving Atlanta by chartered Greyhound bus at 7 AM, January 15 next, en route to Washington to participate in presenting anti-United States protest to members of Congress. Group will return to Atlanta via Greyhound, arriving 8 AM, January 16 next. Arrangements made by informant through Mrs. Nancy Collinson, Atlanta, Georgia. Informant will be compensated up to $100 for transportation and expenses on SAC authority. Informant being used in this instance as means of acquainting herself with persons active in Vietnam activities and racial activities and for purpose of reporting pertinent information regarding activities of this group in Washington, D.C.

2 CC Washington Field.

If the intelligence contained in the above message is to be disseminated outside the Bureau, it is suggested that it be suitably paraphrased in order to protect the Bureau's cryptographic systems.
