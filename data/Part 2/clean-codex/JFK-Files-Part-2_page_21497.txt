Persons/Entities: Wainwright, William; Williamson, Earl; Lash, A. M. Dates: August 14–23, 1962. Categories: Castro.

Occurrence:

Wainwright and Earl Williamson, a Spanish-speaking case officer from Madrid, give A. M. Lash-1-S/W training and supplies.

On August 17, 1962, Williamson cables CIA headquarters that “have no intention give A. M. Lash physical elimination mission as requirement but recognized this something he could or might try to carry out on his own initiative.” (85).

CIA headquarters replied to Williamson by cable on 18 August: “Strongly concur that no physical elimination missions be given A. M. Lash.” (Emphasis added.)

Source: IG Report, 1967.

Staff: Baron.

Date: 7/2/75.
