The January 30, 1969, arrests were for aggravated assault, resisting arrest, and disorderly conduct, resulting in one year's probation and a $90 fine on the aggravated assault and disorderly conduct charges; the charge of resisting arrest was dismissed.

The March 25, 1969, arrest for unlawful use of a weapon and possession of an unregistered gun was also dismissed.

The Chicago Police Department file discloses that SATCHEL had been assigned Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) Number 756 977 G.

II. Current Activities

On March 26, 1969, I advised that SATCHEL was one of several Black Panther Party members arrested by the Chicago Police Department in possession of unregistered weapons.

On April 1, 1969, advised that SATCHEL continued to carry a pistol described as a .32 automatic.

On April 2, 1969, advised that SATCHEL was one of the Black Panther Party members who was always armed and always looking to assist fellow members with any trouble they might encounter.

On April 2, 1969, advised that RONALD SATCHEL was utilizing the alias “DOC,” inasmuch as his current Black Panther Party title was Minister of Health.

On April 15, 1969, advised that as of April 14, 1969, SATCHEL was designated permanent Minister of Health for the Black Panther Party.

On April 19, 1969, advised that SATCHEL continued to attend all Black Panther Party functions and was one of the most active members of the party.
