Q. Did you have knowledge that he was involved with Cuban operations affiliated with the CIA?

Mr. Halley. No. Again, at the time I had no knowledge of Robert Maheu. I have since read about Robert Maheu in the press, allegations and so forth. The name is familiar to me now.

Mr. Baron. The same question for Sam Giancana.

Mr. Halley. Again, I say in answer that at the time that I was involved in ’62 and ’65 I did not know anything about Sam Giancana. I have since read in the press about him. Therefore, the name is familiar to me today.

Mr. Baron. Did you know at that time that Sheffield Edwards, who was then Director of the Office of Security, was connected in any way with those efforts against Castro personally or against the Castro government?

Mr. Halley. At that time, Shef Edwards was known to me as Director of Security. That is all. I had no knowledge of his involvement in any activity against Fidel Castro in a personal sense.

Shef Edwards did help Security Officers assigned to my staff in Miami. Therefore I would periodically go to him about the performance of his officers who were on my staff. So during the time that I was involved in Cuban affairs, yes, I did see him, yes, I did talk to him about Cuban affairs in Miami, the performance of his officers on my staff, but nothing about anything
