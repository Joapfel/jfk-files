15 January 1963

Subject: Bradley, Edgar Eugene (see also Bradley, Leslie Norman)

Allegation:

Edgar Eugene Bradley, field representative for an Eastern radio evangelist, Dr. Carl McIntire, was arrested on 29 December 1967 on a charge by Garrison of being a conspirator in a plot to assassinate President Kennedy.

Source:

News media articles for 20, 24, 26, and 28 December 1967 and 3 June 1968.

OS indications:

CIA connection:

None. Personnel records reveal there was an applicant by the same name as the subject, but the file was destroyed in December 1966 as it was two years old. Subject's employer, Carl McIntire, was approved for contact use in May 1954, but according to the Contacts Office, he was never used by them as he was reported to be unreliable.

CS indicators:

Negative for subject.

His employer, Carl McIntire, was approved, with caution, for contact use on 22 November 1954. However, according to the Domestic Contacts Office, he was not used by them as he was reported to be unreliable. His file reflects that he is an “anti-American Rightist.” He spoke on “How Communism is Using the Churches” in Hollywood, California, on 14 December 1954. On 30 April 1954, an article in the New York Herald Tribune quoted him as saying, “The seriousness of Communism in the Churches is far greater than we realize.” TIME magazine for 16 May 1954 identified him as the founder and head of the small American Council of Christian Churches, which it described as “the farthest tip” of the right, while one of American Protestantism. The LUTHERAN magazine for 30 December 1954 stated that the Revised Standard Version of the Bible had been adopted as part of the incessant attack by Carl McIntire and his “Twentieth Century Reformation Hour” broadcasts.

Details:

It is alleged that the subject's arrest by Garrison is a case of mistaken identity. A New York magazine article for 8 January 1963 reported that it seemed possible indeed that Garrison had him mixed up with two other men. “One is a sensitive pilot named Bradley who was once jailed in Cuba for anti-Castro activities. The other, an ex-cmn who used the alias Bradley and who changed his name and address several times before being connected on a bad-check charge in Dallas on 22 December 1967.”
