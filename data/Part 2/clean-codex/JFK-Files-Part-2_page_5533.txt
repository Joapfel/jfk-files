Re: NOSENKO

Having reviewed Bay 45, we note several reports, not completely identified as B author.

(1) “Conclusions & Comments in the Case of Yuriy I. Nosenko” (aid x678) (The Salie Report), 264 pages. It is in Box 45, Folder 8. It has been released in JFK, JFK62-F9. It concludes Nosenko is good.

(2) “Case of Yuriy I. Nosenko,” 835-page study, dated February 1967. No indication that it has been released. Believed to have been prepared in SB, perhaps by Bagley—or perhaps written by CI/STAFF. It concludes Nosenko is bad.

(3) “The Examination of de Bona Fides of a KGB Defection,” dated February 1968, 447 pages. This is found in ORIS Box 62, JFK-FLD 9. It was released except for Annex A (ANNE A), which is found in Box 45, Folder 23. It concludes Nosenko is not bona fide. Author unknown.
