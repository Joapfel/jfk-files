Chronological Sketch—continued

1963. Contact with FBI and CIA.

1968. Travel and business (Operation Sword) in Mexico and Honduras.

1968–1972. Mechanic and salesman for the Pan-American Aluminum Corp., 185th St., Miami, Florida.

June 17, 1972. Adventure: Watergate Office Building.

June–July 1972. Moved to Washington, D.C. jail.

January 8, 1973. Watergate trial: U.S. vs. G. Gordon Liddy et al.

January 15, 1973. Plea of guilty entered.

Circa May 1973. Moved to Federal Prison Institute in Danbury, Connecticut, then moved to Eglin Air Force Base. France moved to Dade County Jail.

January 1974. Released on appeal bond.

1974. Salesman for Heavy Equipment Company, Miami, Florida.

1974–1975. Salesman for Dodge Is. Repairs, Miami, Florida.

1975. Salesman for Continental Egg Corp., Miami, Florida.

1974–1975. Salesman for Miami Book Efg. Company, 4460 N.W. 37th Avenue, Miami, Florida.
