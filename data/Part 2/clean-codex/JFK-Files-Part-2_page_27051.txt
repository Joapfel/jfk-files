President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board. Agenda for meetings of December 27–28, 1962.

December 27th

1. The Chairman's time.
2. Review of Board's draft interim report to the President.
3. Review of draft report on intelligence community activities relating to the Cuban Arms Buildup (received from DCI on December 26, 1962).
4. Depart for lunch.
5. Lunch — The Jay-Adams Hotel (guests of Dr. Langer).
6. Review of the world situation. Mr. Ray-Cline, Deputy Director/Intelligence, OAM Discussion Group.
7. Resume: review of reports on Cuban Arms Buildup.
8. Dinner at residence of Mr. John McCone (3025 Whitehaven Street, Northwest, D.C.).
(Carried by Mr. of D. R. Mc own. Accepted as read, copied.)

December 26th

9. The Chairman's time.
10. Discussion of report on the Cuban Arms Buildup with the following key intelligence community officials:
a. 9:30–10:30, Mr. John McCone.
b. 10:30–11:00, Lt. General M. Carter, Deputy Director, CIA.
c. 11:00–11:30, Lt. General G. Blake, Director, NSA.
d. 11:30–12:00, Ms. Thomas Hughes, Deputy Director, Intelligence and Research, Department of State.
e. 12:00–1:00, Lt. General Joseph Carroll, Director, DIA.
