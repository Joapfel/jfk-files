6 April 1951

Discussions held between ADAMSON and HAND of CIA and Colonels COVERDALE and CARLAN, Mr. TOD, and Captain VILLEMETTE of G-2. Points given below are those the Army wants covered in a cable to OSO in Panama.

a. Fort Clayton hospital detention was considered the best possibility.

b. Has this been discussed with G-2 USACARIB? If so, does it meet their approval? Can it be worked without advising Provost Marshal, Washington? (So leave in G-2 channels.)

c. AC of S G-2 Washington concurred in principle with proposal of assistance to CIA in this matter.

d. As soon as operational details are worked out with USACARIB, a cable advising generally that it can be effected is to be forwarded by G-2 USACARIB to ACos S G-2 (personal for BOLLING). Details will come back through CIA.

e. Identification of KELLY (DIMITROV) case.

f. Detention period maximum of one year; relieve Army sooner if CAS
