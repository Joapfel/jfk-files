FBI

Date: 6/6/89

Transmit the following in: AIRTEL

Priority

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Chicago (157-1291)

Subject: Black Panther Party

Enclosed for the Bureau are 11 copies of a LAM concerning the captioned matter and particularly the “Wongested Plan for the Complete Breakdown of the State of Illinois Power Structure,” or alleged war plans for the Illinois Chapter of the Black Panther Party (BPP).

In view of the special significance of this information, it is set forth as a separate LAM, and a more comprehensive LAM concerning all of the items seized during the FBI raid on 6/4/89 of the Illinois Chapter of the BPP, Chicago, follows.
