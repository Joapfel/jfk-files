UFGA 15233, dated 20 April 1964.

JMWAVE Station forwarded to Headquarters, Special Affairs Staff, a copy of a report on Byron Cameron (CAMERON), which it had sent locally to the FBI. A usually reliable source reported that Cameron said he was representing an unidentified group of persons who had made contact with two Cuban gangsters who were willing to kill Fidel Castro (CASTRO).

DIRECTOR 23308, dated 22 May 1964.

Headquarters, CIA, sent to the State Department, eyes only to Crimmins (CRIMMINS) and Bowdler (BOWDLER), a message which Harvey Summ (SUMM) asked the CAS Miami Station to transmit to the Office of the Coordinator of Cuban Affairs, Department of State. During a visit to Summ's office on 21 May, Tad Szulc (SZULC) mentioned conversations he had had with Manuel Ray (RAY) of JURE within the past few days. Ray expressed appreciation for help he had received thus far from the USG and said he could use more.

DBA 73544 (FBI), dated 8 June 1964.

Memo from the FBI Newark, N.J., office. Reportedly, a man or men working for H. L. Hunt, Texas millionaire, discussed the possibility of doing away with Cheddi Jagan or Castro (CASTRO), or both. It was on a private basis and was not in any way connected with the U.S. Government. Reportedly, the CIA knew about it, though.
