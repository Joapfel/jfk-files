Subject: Leibel Bergman; Herb Block.

Reference: Our memorandum of 6 February 1970, subject: Davida Fineman and Leibel Bergman.

1. A sensitive source has reported that Leibel Bergman and Herb Block share a deep detestation of the Progressive Labor Party (PLP). Block stated that he was convinced that the PLP was correctly run by the CPUSA and that he (Block) had learned that Fred Jerome’s mother, Alice Jerome (a prominent member of the PLP), had secretly attended a party school in the Soviet Union after being publicly associated with the PLP.

2. The main reason that Block and his wife wanted to live in China was to continue denunciations of the PLP. Block is convinced that his previous communications with the Chinese about PLP had had some effect and that the PLP would shortly be “derecognized” by the Chinese.

Place transmitting by: Matt Gr. S. J. Papich.

13 March 1970.
