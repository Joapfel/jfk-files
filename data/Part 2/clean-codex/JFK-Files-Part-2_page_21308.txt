Scott Holloway, head of the CIA Station, on 2-20-98, American Embassy, Managua, Nicaragua, advised on 9-5-62 that Noel Pallais, who is T-1 in the accompanying report, runs the financial enterprises of General Anastasio Somoza of Nicaragua. Holloway stated that Pallais has been in close contact with the CIA Office in regard to this case. He was made available for contact by Holloway and was subsequently interviewed at the CIA office in Managua. Holloway stated he personally has encouraged General Somoza not to have anything to do with Werbell, Smith and associates. He stated he feels the offer to sell arms and ammunition and provide mercenaries which was made to the Guatemalan Government was only of secondary interest.
