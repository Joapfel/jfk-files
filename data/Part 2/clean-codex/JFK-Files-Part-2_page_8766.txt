AMRANGE Project 19-6-81/1-4.

Memo for Chief TFW from DC/TFW/FI concerning DEGRIP Report dated 28 August 1962.

Concerns one Vasco Cecchi, proprietor of the Montecatini Restaurant in the Vedado quarter of Havana, frequented by Fidel Castro and his entourage. Employees of the restaurant reportedly were anti-Castroites. The source of the report called attention to a closet, partly under the stairs, used for storage, but which he believed could be used to hide a man comfortably. On one of the floors above the restaurant lived a certain Lieutenant Ramos who was evidently an intimate of Fidel Castro. Castro often visited him.

Comment: See File “Plots to Assassinate the President of the U.S.” 100-300-12, memo from Secret Service dated 12 December 1962, concerning one Jose Menendez Ramos who may be identifiable with Lieutenant Ramos.
