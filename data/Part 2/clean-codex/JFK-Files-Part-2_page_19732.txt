To Pennsylvania Avenue, and east on Pennsylvania Avenue to the west front of the United States (U.S.) Capitol.

During the march from the Ellipse to the Capitol the group walked approximately twenty abreast, displaying signs such as “Rhode Island Women for Abortion Reform,” “My Body Not the States,” “Every Child a Wanted Child,” “Free Abortion on Demand,” “Defend Shirley Wheeler,” “Keep the State Out of My Virginia” (sic), “Seven Gay Women,” “Boston, Repeal All Abortion Laws,” “Do Not Labor Under Restrictions,” and “Wilson High School.”

During the gathering (CATHERING) on the Ellipse there were two tables of Socialist Party (SWP) literature set up containing pamphlets reading, “Vote for SWP for Seventy-Two,” and other leaflets advocating Linda Jeness for president and Andrew Pulley for vice president. Four SWP literature tables were also set up at rally site at west front on the Capitol.
