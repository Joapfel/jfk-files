10. In discussions on terrorism and narcotics, we pointed out most of our targets are in Buenos Aires. Could we run agents from here against those targets in Argentina? Answer: Yes, but only if coordinated with and approved by HQS and Buenos Aires station. Concerning manpower, we said we are at present undertaking a review of both terrorist and narcotics targets to decide the extent of the problem and then the manpower required, so these efforts are in a state of flux. We said we were under the impression that Buenos Aires may not have narcotics in its OD but that they have a very full plate.
