Fangelle handled CIA communications in Moscow. The CIA used State Department channels of communication, but could code messages by means of one-pad encrypting so that the message was secret until it was forwarded to CIA headquarters, where it was decoded.

In 1959 the Soviets declared Fangelle persona non grata as a result of his involvement with P. S. Popov. Fangelle was the case officer of Popov, who worked for the GRU, Soviet military intelligence.
