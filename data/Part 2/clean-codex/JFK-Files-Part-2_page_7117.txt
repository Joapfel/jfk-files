Known Travel of Latin Americans to and From Cuba — January 1964.

Argentina: January to 9; from 3. Purpose: 1 UNESCO delegate, 2 well-known Communists went to Cuba.

Bolivia: January to 3; from 1. Purpose: 1 attending preparatory meeting of the Latin American Communist Youth Congress in Habana, 1 will attend a course at the Cuban equivalent of Soviet Komsomol school.

Brazil: January to 0; from 0.

Br. Guiana: January to 1; from 0. Purpose: 29 December 1963 — not previously reported — unknown.

Chile: January to 14; from 15. Purpose: Returning from Habana celebration 5th anniversary of Cuban Revolution.

Colombia: January to 4; from 0. An additional 15 Colombian travellers to Cuba should be added to the report for December 1963, which brings the total for December to 25. It is estimated that 13 of the 25 went to Cuba for the 1 January celebrations.

Costa Rica: January to 11; from 13. Purpose: 3 to Cuba to attend meeting at Habana at which Venezuelan charge against Cuba was discussed. The remainder were sent to Habana for 2 January celebration.

Dom. Rep.: January to 0; from 1. Purpose: 1 attended Architects Congress.
