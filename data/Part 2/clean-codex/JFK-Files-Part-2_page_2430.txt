TI-125-73

Chief, Domestic Collection Division  
Attention: Deputy for Field Operations (Starling)  
Chief, Miami Field Office

Contact with TTT A 120

1. In view of the considerable interest expressed from time to time in any agency involved with TTT, the following information is submitted for the record. We have learned indirectly but apparently accurately that Samuel J. Chavez, Jr., has very recently left his position with the Internal Revenue Service and is now employed as a senior executive concerned with Internal Revenue security matters for TTT. You will recall that Chavez was at one time a military intelligence specialist with the Miami Field Office and later served there briefly as a staff deputy.

2. Needless to say, this office does not maintain contact with TTT and we plan no professional contact with Mr. Chavez. Local propriety and routine courtesy are such that on occasion an unintended social encounter may occur. Should we meet Mr. Chavez again, no official business will be conducted of any kind.
