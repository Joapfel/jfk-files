This matter is being pursued intensively by the Detroit Office, and contact is maintained on a daily basis with the Intelligence Unit of the Michigan State Police and the Special Investigations Bureau of the Detroit Police Department, both having direct interest in local black nationalist activities and conducting surveillances in regard to key black nationalist figures in the Detroit area. Racial informants, who are familiar with black nationalist activity in the Detroit area, are contacted often in regard to an organized BPP in the Detroit area. Contact with the above sources has revealed that no organized BPP has been established in the Detroit area wherein the BPP is holding regular scheduled meetings.

In the event any information is received to show that a BPP is organized and meeting at a regularly scheduled time, the Bureau and other appropriate offices will be properly advised.
