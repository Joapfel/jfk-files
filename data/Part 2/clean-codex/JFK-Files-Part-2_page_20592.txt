Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

January 16, 1972.

Teletype.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC), OOWF IS-New Left.

RENYTEL this date.

On this date, SAs of the FBI observed a demonstration and march sponsored by WONAAC, which commenced at 4:30 PM at 42nd Street and Times Square, NYC. Approximately 50 individuals participated in the demonstration, which marched to protest all abortion laws.

At 5:30 PM, the demonstration proceeded south on Dixie Avenue (Sixth Avenue) to Herald Square (34th Street and Sixth Avenue), NYC, arriving approximately 6 PM. Demonstrators were observed carrying posters reading, “Abortion Is a Woman's Right, WONAAC.” While marching, demonstrators chanted a slogan: “Not the church, not the state, women must decide our fate.” The majority of demonstrators were women aged approximately 15 to 25 years. Demonstrators ended at 6 PM. No incidents observed.

End page one.
