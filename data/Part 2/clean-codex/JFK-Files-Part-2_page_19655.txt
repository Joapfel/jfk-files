United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Boston (100-42759) (C)

Subject: Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971, IS-MISC

Date: 7/8/71.

ReBestel to Bureau, 4/9/71, and Cleveland airtel and LHM, 5/18/71.

In New York LHM of 3/26/71, it was mentioned that a number to be called in Boston concerning the captioned matter was “617-666-1243 (KATHY).” On 4/1/71, personnel, New England Telephone Company, Boston, advised that 666-1243 was assigned to Henry Norr, 59 Pearson Ave., Somerville, Mass. He was scheduled to move on 4/2/71 to 91 Glen Street, Somerville. Henry Norr is a Priority II SI subject of the Boston Office.

On 7/6/71, source (mail carrier), Winter Hill Branch, Somerville Post Office, Somerville, Mass., advised that Henry Norr had lived at 59 Pearson Ave., Somerville, but recently moved to 91 Glen Street, Somerville. A girl by the name of Kathy Mc Fee had also resided in the same apartment as did Norr at 59 Pearson Ave. When she moved, however, she left no forwarding address and her current location was unknown.

The following informants have advised they had no information concerning the Women's National March or Kathy Mc Fee:

Informant on 4/2/71, 5/28/71, and 6/29/71.

On 4/2/71 and 6/29/71.

On 4/2/71, 5/25/71, and 6/28/71.

Symbol #.

Boston had developed no additional pertinent information concerning this matter and is closing its file on this communication.

Boston. KPW/pc. 20 July 1971. New Left.
