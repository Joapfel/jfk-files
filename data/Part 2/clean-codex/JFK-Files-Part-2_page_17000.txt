1/2/70

Teletype

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago

Black Panther Party. RM-BFP. OO: San Francisco.

Confidential source advised that an instant rally was scheduled for 3 p.m., January 4, next, dedicated to the Afro-Arts Theatre, 3937 South Drexel Boulevard, Chicago.

Rally in memorial to Fred Hampton (Haleton), deceased deputy chairman, Illinois DPP, killed December 4, last, in a raid conducted by Cook County State’s Attorney’s police.

Source also advises DPP tentatively planning to announce opening of “People’s Medical Center,” the purpose of which will freely be to treat ghetto (GHENITO) residents.

Source states registrar Bobby Rush, deputy minister of defense, Illinois DPP; Bob (DOB) Lucas, leader, Black Liberation Alliance, Chicago; and Alderman A. A. “Sammy” Rayner are being advertised as speakers. Entertainment will also be provided according to source, and contributions solicited. Money obtained
