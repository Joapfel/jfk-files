1922552. CITE Mexico City 8310. Director info JMWAVE.

TYPIC LIRING

1. Wallace B. Rowton met LIRING-3 18 August in last meeting prior to L-3 departure for Cuba 19 August. L-3 told Rowton he had talked with Abelardo Curbelo Padron on 18 August, from whom he learned the following:

A. Arnol (Arnold) Rodriguez Camps has been made Minister of Sugar in newly organized or reorganized Sugar Ministry. L-3 told Rowton he would contact Rodriguez Camps first upon arrival in Cuba to check his present position and latest developments.

B. Sending of old DC-4 airplane to Mexico 16 August was ostensibly special service for Josephine Baker and 16 children to transport them to Mexico. However, according to Curbelo, this plane also gave Cubans an excuse for not evacuating Mexicans implicated in Rico Galan case who were pressing Cuban Embassy for sanctuary in Cuba to avoid arrest.

2. L-3 believes Rico Galan case points up conflict in Cuban Embassy between Security/Intel and Foreign Ministry groups. On 15 August L-3 told Rowton that Julian Lopez Diaz, press and cultural attaché, warned L-3 not to take 16 August plane because there were going to be several secret.
