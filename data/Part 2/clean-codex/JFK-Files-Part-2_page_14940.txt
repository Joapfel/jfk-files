Bureau files also show that in September 1957, Alfred Burka, while Assistant United States Attorney, called upon the Director to pay his respects. He stated he had always held the Bureau in high esteem and had very kind words to say about his contacts with our local offices. In October 1957, while serving as Assistant United States Attorney, he requested that the Director autograph a copy of the book “The FBI Story” for him and also give him an autographed photograph. His request was granted. (94-4-30-12248)

Action:

The field has been instructed to bring the previous investigation of Burka up to date. Appropriate memorandums will be submitted upon completion of our current inquiries.
