M. Teaching Revolutionary Programs and Policies

Political education classes are held every Sunday at 2219 Indiandale, Detroit.

July 30, 1970: Political education classes will be held at 611 Continental, Detroit, beginning December 1970.

DE T-1. November 1970.

Political education classes are currently being held at 611 Continental, Detroit, but have been discontinued at 3595 17th Street, Detroit.

DE T-1. January 4, 1971.

N. Miscellaneous

Huey Newton, Minister of Defense, BPP, has been making strange statements, such as supporting the Gay Liberation Movement and that the fellows should not relate to the girls so much.

DE T-1. September 4, 1970.

DE T-26 advised on November 30, 1970, that one of the trucks being driven by the Detroit NCCF members headed for the Constitutional Convention in Washington, D.C., broke down on the turnpike near Pittsburgh on November 26, 1970. While stopped, they called Washington, D.C., and determined that the convention had been called off and were instructed to return to Detroit. The group was upset upon receipt of this information but decided to return to Detroit, and the truck that had broken down was left to be repaired while the rest of the convoy made a U-turn on the Pennsylvania Turnpike and headed back to Ohio. Upon reaching the end of the Pennsylvania Turnpike, all vehicles had to pay the full toll, after which they again called Washington, D.C., and determined that the convention had been rescheduled. They turned around and headed back to Washington, D.C., picking up the truck that had broken down en route.
