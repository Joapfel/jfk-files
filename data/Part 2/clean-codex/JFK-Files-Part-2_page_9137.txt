United States Department of Justice. Federal Bureau of Investigation.

Report of: Joseph A. Marion, Jr.  
Date: January 31, 1967.  
Field Office File #: 157-1558.  
Title: Frank August Drager, Jr.  
Office: Richmond.  
Character: Racial Matters (Organization).

Synopsis:

Subject born 8/4/40 at Trenton, New Jersey; received a General Discharge under honorable conditions from the U.S. Air Force 9/2/60; sent to Mercer County Workhouse, Trenton, New Jersey, on 3/6/64 for nine months for assault with a deadly weapon; made application for membership in the American Nazi Party (ANP) 7/26/65; was assigned duties of purchasing of materials and quality control on ANP presses on 10/26/66; and is presently employed on a full-time basis by the ANP, Arlington, Virginia. Background, including criminal record, previous employments and residences, marital status, and education set forth.

To: U.S. Secret Service, 1800 G Street, N.W., Washington, D.C.
