Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 8/23/71

Subsequent to the arrest of Barry James Musi, he was taken to the U.S. Federal Building, 401 Market Street, Camden, New Jersey, for processing.

MUST be fingerprinted by SA Klokowski Donovan.

He was photographed by SA William J. Adams.

Musi was searched by SAs John H. Stoddard, Jr., and Toby Marvin Harding, and the following items were confiscated:

1. One Commonwealth of Massachusetts driver's license #03236434 in the name of Barry J. Musi.

2. One pack cigarette package bearing a Massachusetts tax stamp.

3. One electrical connector.

The latter item was found in the trouser pocket of Musi.

An inventory listing the above items was prepared, and Musi was requested to sign the inventory as a certification that these were the only items taken. He declined to do so. A copy of the inventory was then given to Musi.

On 8/22/71 at Camden (Carden), New Jersey. File #: PH 52-7165 SUB S.

SA John H. Stoddard, Jr., and SA Toby (Doxy) Marvin Harding.

Date dictated: 8/23/71.
