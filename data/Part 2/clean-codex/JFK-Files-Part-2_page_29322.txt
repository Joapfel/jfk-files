On or about 9/30/63, this may have happened.

MCS receives a transcript in which Silvia Duran discusses an American and his problem with a Soviet consul. Win Scott writes “Can we identify?” on the transcript. Goodpasture notifies Flick that an American is apparently trying to get a visa and that the COS would like to identify him. The Cuban and Soviet monitors are asked to notify Flick of any conversation involving an American or in English. Etcetera, however.

10/1/63

Flick is notified by a monitor that there is an English conversation on the Soviet tap. Flick listens and hears the man identify himself as Oswald. He pulls the tape, marks it “urgent,” and notifies Goodpasture that they have an identification of an American. The tape is delivered to Tarasoff for immediate transcription. It is received late this day or early on the second by Goodpasture. Disposition of tape unknown; could “urgent” tapes have been routinely retained for a longer period? Goodpasture now has identification but does not know for sure that it is the one Scott has asked about. The guy in the 9/27 conversation sounds real interesting, but there is nothing in this conversation, or 9/2, that clearly connects. She discusses reporting it with Manell. There is some disagreement over whose responsibility it is to report. Either a decision is made, or the conflict continues until the photo is checked.

Goodpasture ties in the 9/28 Soviet conversation regarding the address.

10/7/63

Photos from 10/1, 10/2, 10/3, and 10/4 (?) are received in base from the Soviet Embassy.

10/8/63

B. Manell drafts report cable. Goodpasture adds paragraph 2.

10/10/63

In the interim, Goodpasture tries to identify the MMM photo. HQ response received. Descriptions do not match. Controversy in the station over whether Oswald is the same. The 9/27 and 9/28 conversations are tied in to LHO during this week. Definitely of cabinet and conversation and voice comparison.

10/16/63

Oswald “P” file opened.

10/18/63

By this time, a photo of MMM has been received at the station. Goodpasture picks up on it. This MMM photo is the one from Cubemb on 10/15.
