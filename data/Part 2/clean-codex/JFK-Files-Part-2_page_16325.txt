A male individual referred to as WADDELL (phonetic) is being held in the Chicago BPP “Political Jail” at Chicago BPP Headquarters, 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, Illinois. WADDELL reportedly raped a sister Panther and is to be expelled from the BPP.

6/2/69

Source identified a photograph of GEORGE SAMS, JR., also known as ROBERT WADELL SMITH, DETROIT GEORGE, and GEORGE CAMP, as a person he knows who uses the name WADDELL (phonetic) and who is now at Chicago BPP Headquarters, 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, Illinois.

Source further advised it is common knowledge among Chicago-area BPP members that WADDELL is fleeing from some serious crime and is being held as a BPP prisoner at the Chicago Chapter BPP jail at 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, Illinois.

Source stated that Chicago BPP Central Staff members are aware WADDELL is being sought for the crime of murder in another state and is actually being held in the Chicago BPP Chapter jail for raping a BPP member in Detroit, Michigan, recently.

6/3/69

WADDELL was to be released from the Chicago BPP jail on June 3, 1969.

6/3/69

At 12:30 a.m., June 4, 1969, GEORGE SAMS, JR. was then at Chicago BPP Headquarters, 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, Illinois, and expected to remain there during the remainder of the night.
