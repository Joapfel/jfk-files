Rally to repeal anti-abortion laws at Federal Office Building, Cleveland, Ohio, or Public Square, Cleveland, Ohio, sponsored by Abortion Project Coalition (APC), Cleveland, Ohio, 25 August 1972. IS-RA.

On 25 August, instant, SAS of the FBI observed approximately fifty individuals assemble at noon in Public Square, Cleveland, Ohio. Of this group, approximately seventy-five percent were females and the remaining twenty-five percent were males.
