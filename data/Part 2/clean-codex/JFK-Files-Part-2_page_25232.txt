Colonial Insights.

Dates: November 1, 1963, 1330 hours. Categories: DIEM.

1965 Maroons pour into Saigon from the direction of Dien Bien Phu; Interim indicated to be from Quang Tri Training Center.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., June 20, 1975, Conein, Exhibit One, page 14. Staff: Dawson. Date: June 30, 1975.

Persons/Entities: Colonel Le Quang Tung.

Dates: November 1, 1963, 1400 hours. Categories: DIEM.

Colonel Le Quang Tung captured; directed to order Vietnamese Special Force to cease fire; firing ceased at Village No. 7.

Fighting at palace; about 200 troops observed.

Thirty-five surrendered cars moving toward the palace.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., June 20, 1975, Conein, Exhibit One, page 14. Staff: Diem. Date: June 30, 1975.
