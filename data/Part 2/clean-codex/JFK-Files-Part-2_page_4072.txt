United States Department of Justice

Federal Bureau of Investigation

Washington, D.C.

October 2, 1962

The following information was submitted by Richard Case MacGill.

This document contains neither commands nor commissions of any kind and shall not be distributed outside your agency.

The above-named individual appeared at his own volition at the American Embassy, Mexico City, on September 23, 1962, and volunteered the following information concerning himself:

Although he declined to furnish a local residence address for himself in Mexico City, he stated he could be contacted through a friend named Armour Greenworth, Room M15, Hotel Ibero, when he would contact him every day. He also exhibited his Tourist Card Number 152337, which listed his address as 4037 Leonard Avenue, Los Angeles, California. This card showed that it had been issued at Los Angeles on August 17, 1962, and that MacGill had entered Mexico through Ciudad Juárez, Chihuahua, on August 24, 1962. The card also reflected that he established his identification by exhibiting United States Passport Number 022729.

He related that he had formerly been employed by the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control of the State of California as a Special Investigator. He stated once a week of his investigation of some large drunk groups in the Los Angeles area, one of which he investigated as the Machine Zone, and some controversy which he had heard from LAPD divers about his Los Angeles Police Department colleagues.
