June 3, 1975

Material for Senate Select Committee (SSC) Investigating Intelligence Activities.

1. Title/Subject: Legal Authorities of the FBI.

2. Originating Organization: FBI.

3. Nature of Material: Letterhead memorandum (LHM) and its enclosures described below.

4. Document Classification: Some of the enclosures to the LHM classified “Top Secret.”

5. National Security Information Stamp: Should have.

6. Summary of Contents: The LHM refers to a prior LHM furnished to the SSC enclosing a copy of “Intelligence Division Position Paper on Jurisdiction” dated 2/13/75 and noted that certain exhibits were extracted prior to dissemination due to Third Agency Rule considerations. The LHM advised that those considerations had been resolved and that the exhibits were being furnished with the LHM.

7. Requestor/Date of Request: SSC. 3/19/75. Response pertains to Part I of the SSC original letter of request dated 3/19/75.

8. Releasing Authority: FBI.

9. Date of Submission: 5/20/75.

10. Location of File Copy: FBI file 62-116395.

Assistant Director: Agency Rule clearances obtained.
