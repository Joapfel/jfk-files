Date: 10 September 1970.

To: Director, Central Intelligence Agency. Attention: Deputy Director for Plans.

From: John Edgar Hoover, Director.

Subject: Samuel Adasoy Jaffe (Adason) — Internal Security, Russia.

Reference is made to your letter dated August 26, 1970, captioned “Request for Letters Exchanged with ChiCom Newsman by Samuel Adason Jaffe,” your reference CSCI-316/02601-70.

This Bureau also no longer maintains contact with the subject; therefore, it will not be possible for us to respond to the request in your letter of reference.

ECP: ecog (4).

Note: Classified “Secret” since it repeats information so classified by CIA.

The subject is a former correspondent for ABC who was stationed in Moscow and, while there, was considered a recruited agent by the Soviets, according to a defector source. We conducted several interviews of him during March and April 1969, during which he did not adequately resolve the allegations to our satisfaction, and we have had no further contacts with him since September 1969, when he volunteered information concerning an encounter with a Soviet at the United Nations in New York. In relation, CIA requested the Bureau to ask the subject for copies of letters exchanged with a New China News Agency official since January 1970, since it no longer maintains contact with him.
