UPI-42 (China Visit).

New York—Two UPI correspondents have received permission to visit China and will be departing soon on their assignments.

Sam Jaffe, a veteran foreign correspondent, leaves later this month on special assignment for the UPI Audio Network. He will also file dispatches for the UPI newswires and provide picture coverage as well.

Donald Mackay (Nackay), UPI general manager for Canada, will accompany a Canadian trade delegation to China in August. His news and photo coverage also will be supplemented by voice reports for the UPI Audio Network.
