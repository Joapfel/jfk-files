Edwards MFR—re BATTLE. Memo prepared for RFR preloading area briefing.

“Rosselli and Mahen spent considerable time in Microni talking with the Corrieri. Giannaccone also present. Giannaccone put them in touch with a ‘corrier’ was used back. form to Hainault, Corrier said open penance.

January 1971—CIA contact with RAM cut off because Hughes Baille and unfavorable male. However, OSB note from Helms had directed fermen. Mahen related as early as 1966. ‘Flags’ ignored.
