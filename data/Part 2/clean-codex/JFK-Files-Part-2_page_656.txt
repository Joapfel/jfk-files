Reference: Mexico City (HEXICO CITY) 7549 (IN 32832).

1. Further debriefing of Carlos Jurado Delmar by Wallace. Rowton produced the following regarding Jurado trip Cuba, 27 May–3 June 66:

2. Militia force which captured landing party in launch recently was led by Cuban Minister of Education. Jurado stated Cuba literally bristling with very latest weapons, including missiles with nuclear warheads. In response to Rowton query, Jurado stated he assumed Soviets controlled nuclear weapons but that Cubans now well trained to use them if necessary.

3. Jurado stated every Cuban he talked to was sincerely convinced invasion imminent and that only alertness of militia had prevented a successful landing. Jurado entered movie one day during week, saw only three spectators, and left in embarrassment. Subject said it was opinion in Cuba that abortive invasion had some direct connection with elections in Santo Domingo. He added he was told state of war mobilization in Cuba would last about month.

8 June 65. IN 33490.
