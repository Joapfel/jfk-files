3 PM urgent, 12-10-69.

Director: JIM CHICAGO.

Black Panther Party (BPP), RM.

Tom Panush, records officer, Cook County Jail, Chicago, Illinois, advised instant that the following were released instant on bond: Brenda Harris, five thousand (five hundred cash); Lewis Trullock, two five thousand (two five hundred cash); Harold Bell, five thousand (five hundred cash); Blair Anderson, five thousand (five hundred cash).

Above arrested December 4, last, by Chicago PD after a shootout at 2337 West Monroe, Chicago, which resulted in the death of Fred Allen Hampton, deputy chairman, Illinois BPP, and Mark Clark, lieutenant, Illinois BPP, at Peoria, Illinois.
