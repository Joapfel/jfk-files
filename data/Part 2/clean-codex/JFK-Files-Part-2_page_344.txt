Type: LILING.

6 December 1966, 17:53817.

1. LILING-3 reported to Wallace B. RTWTON on 21 November 1966 that he was sent a Cuban Embassy individual from Havana who told him he was traveling under an assumed name. L-3 reported the individual told him he was in Mexico to buy coffee seeds on direct orders from Fidel Castro.

2. L-3 stated he believed the man's name to be Israel Echemendia. A check of Cuban manifests for October and November 1966 shows no arrival of Israel Echemendia, but Israel Suarez de la Paz arrived on 23 October 1966. A recent LIENVOY report shows Suarez's interest in coffee matters.

3. On 23 November, Suarez phoned the commercial office in the Embassy and told the secretary he wanted to talk to Hugo del Pino about a meeting at the Mexican Institute of Coffee. When told Hugo was not in, he asked if Rolando (Curbelo) could go with him to an interview at the Institute. He said it was very important to them and that they were much interested in the problem. He asked her to tell Hugo he was going to the Institute and that Hugo was to come too. Three minutes later, the man called the same phone and told the secretary he was now at the Institute and awaiting Hugo.

Israel Suarez — 901-257780 UMA-00017, 11 February 1969: represented by D’NE as a highly intelligent person in F.O.C. 6.
