DIER — Departamento Investigativo del Ejército Revolucionario.

14 December 1960. Cable to Director from Havana. IN 16758. Panegue reportedly under arrest at Cabana Prison, and his wife at the Women’s Prison, on charge of aiding Escambray fighters.

13 January 1961. Cable to Bell from Guatemala City. IN 1688. Alexander Roerke gives information that Frank Fiorini and Orlando Boech represent Panegue in Cuba. Fiorini and Boch recruiting and equipping force for assault on Cuba on 18 January. Receiving financial aid from Anti-Communist International Association of NYC.

14 March 1962. Dispatch to Chief, Tank Force W, from COS/SOWAVE. Panegue organized a hunger strike by Cuban Eules, who held a rally on 13 March 1962. Purpose was to show JFK that Cubans are willing to die in exile if their right to defend Cuba from Communism is negated. Motto of strike is “Hunger or War.”

C.I.U. 3414.
