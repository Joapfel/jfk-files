The Marine has no firm offer of employment. He has indicated that his former employer will entertain offering employment with a suitable salary to provide the necessary support of his mother.

3. In evaluation of all facts available, it is the opinion of the Board that Private First Class OSWALD meets the requirements of paragraph 10273.10CM for release from active duty.

4. The Board recommends that Private First Class Lee H. OSWALD be released from active duty with the Marine Corps for reasons of dependency.
