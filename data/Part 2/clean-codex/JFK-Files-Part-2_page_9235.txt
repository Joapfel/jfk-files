Intercommunal Survival Center

Detroit Branch  
Black Panther Party  
157 Collingwood

November 1972

To the concerned businessmen:

The Detroit Branch of the Black Panther Party would like to take time out to thank you for the many times you have donated to the survival programs that we have had. In the past, your contributions have enabled us to give away 1,600 free bags of groceries to the poor and mess of people in different areas throughout the city.

Since there are so many hungry people in the city of Detroit, we feel we can't just stop feeding them. So in the future, we plan to start stocking up on food to give to the people on a day-to-day basis. But we cannot do this without the beautiful help that you, the store owners, give by just donating one or two items a week. We also, in turn, inform the community which stores have donated—for when you help the community, in return they help you. So, if you are concerned about the community, then show them by giving your support.

All power to the people!

Lonnie Dee
