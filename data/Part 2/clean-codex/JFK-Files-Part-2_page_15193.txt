Attachment A

The following technical surveillances are being maintained at the specific request or with the specific approval of the Office of the Deputy Under Secretary of State.

Cuba: Embassy, D.C.—estimated monthly cost $2,250; Consul and Attachés, D.C.—$1,605; Consul, NYC—$2,294; Consul, Miami—$733.

France: Chancery, D.C.—estimated monthly cost $5,188.

Germany: Embassy, D.C.—$3,687.

United Arab Republic: Chancery, D.C.—$1,210; Attachés, D.C.—$1,369; UN, NYC—$1,529.

Total estimated monthly cost: $19,866.

During the past six months, technical coverage of the Iraqi Chancery in Washington and the Iraqi Consulate and UN office in New York City was discontinued as a part of this program.

Special technical coverage was maintained for the benefit of the State Department at the French Embassy residence from 4-15-60 to 4-29-60, which period included the time French President Charles de Gaulle visited the United States.
