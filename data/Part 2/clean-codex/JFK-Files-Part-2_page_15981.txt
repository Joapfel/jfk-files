At Chicago, JEFF FORT, leader of BR, a Negro youth gang on Chicago’s South Side, had sent him a message that he (FORT) would blow off HAMPTON’s head if HAMPTON continued going into BR territory.

HAMPTON indicated he desired to join forces with BR, but FORT was opposed to joining with BPP.

On January 29, 1969, FRED HAMPTON, Chicago BPP leader, met with JEFF FORT, leader of BR. HAMPTON told FORT that the Rangers should join forces with BPP, but FORT refused to consider it. FORT told HAMPTON to stay out of Chicago. HAMPTON told FORT that he was worse than white racists in holding back the progress of Black people. (1/28/69)

On March 12, 1969, it was learned that there was bad blood between BR and BPP in Chicago. A gang war between BPP and BR was not beyond imagination. (3/12/69)

On April 8, 1969, it was learned that Chicago BPP had been negotiating with BR regarding an alliance, but JEFF FORT, leader of BR, was proving to be a stumbling block. (4/9/69)

On April 24, 1969, Chicago BPP held a meeting at Englewood High School, Chicago, Illinois. BILLY BROOKS, Chicago BPP member, advised students that the aim of BPP was to gain control of the city through recruitment of Negro teenagers and Negro youth gangs. BROOKS stated that two Negro youth gangs, the Devil’s Disciples and the Cobrastones, had already aligned themselves with BPP. BROOKS stated that BPP was trying to convert BR to join BPP. (4/29/69)
