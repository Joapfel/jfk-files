1. Assassination?

Mr. Angleton. Relating to assassination, KGB agents, Vassall, Vassily, the British agents, and many agents in France, an alleged penetration in the U.S., et cetera.

Senator Baker. And did McCone accept your advice?

Mr. Angleton. He did, except that I understood that a lot of it has been declassified. And when he gave this interview recently with Shor, I called his executive officer Elder and I asked him, and he said, who in the Agency ever alleged to McCone that we now had re-established the bona fides?

Well, et cetera, and don't give me that stuff.

And he said, well, I will give it to you.

And I never heard from him since. But McCone was a man who would not have made this statement. He has total recall.

Senator Baker. I am sure he does.
