ReBulet to Legat Hong Kong 2/17/71 and WFOlet to the Bureau, aka IS-CH.

It is noted that captioned subject's relationship with SZETO Kung, a new Chinese news agency representative in Hong Kong, is set forth in ReWFOlet. In regards to further contact with JAFFE, WFO will, (UACB), continue the policy as outlined in ReWFOlet.
