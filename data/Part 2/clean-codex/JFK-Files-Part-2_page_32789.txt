Office of the Secretary of Defense, Washington 23, D.C.

31 May 1962

Memorandum for the Special Group (Augmented).

From: Brig. Gen. Langdale.

Subject: Operation Mongoose Progress.

Intelligence buildup by CIA includes efforts to recruit a Cuban diplomat in place and an official in an airlines office in Havana. The CIA team that landed in Pinar del Rio Province last March was successfully resupplied by maritime means on 24 May. The Navy and CIA have worked out a cooperative effort to strengthen the U.S. security and intelligence program at Guantanamo (Cuantanamo).

The CAC continues to provide word from inside Cuba covering local security moves and popular unrest, reportedly increasing. A substantial reduction in oil refining and manufacture of tires and tubes is reported from CAC sources.

Economic pressure upon the Communist regime may be increased by a Department of Commerce move denying Energia facilities at U.S. ports to vessels under Sino-Soviet bloc charter carrying cargo to Cuba. State Department reports having solicited the views of our Ambassador to Japan regarding possible approaches to the Japanese, asking them to cease sugar purchases from Cuba.

Propaganda (Prorogation) efforts of USIA and CIA continue to apply pressure upon the Cuban regime by focusing upon abuses of power and failure of key programs such as medical and food. The VNA use of refugees in interviews especially underlined this theme. I am looking into a USIA proposal that we counter Communist Cuba moves to increase Latin America by participation in the athletic North Central American and Caribbean Games set for August 11–12 in Winston (Kingston), Jamaica; a “free Cuba” team might be effective, with good publicity support.
