Number and date: (69) - 5/25/70. Name: Nelson Molina. Airline: American. From Chicago, Illinois, to Cuba at gunpoint with 66 passengers and a crew of 7. Process filed 5/27/70, New York. Still in Cuba.

Number and date: (70) - 6/4/70. Name: Arthur (Auther) Gates Barkley. Airline: TWA. From Phoenix, Arizona, to St. Louis, Missouri, with 51 passengers and 7 crew members. Demanded $100,000,000 or would destroy the aircraft. Process filed 6/4/70, Alexandria, Virginia. Presently undergoing psychiatric evaluation.
