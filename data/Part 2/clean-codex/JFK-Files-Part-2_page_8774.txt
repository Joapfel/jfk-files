AMSANTA Project 19-6-73/3.

Memo for the Record dated 10 July 1963 by Lou De Santi. Concerns FBI informant Victor Thomas VICENTE, a Puerto Rican citizen who penetrated Communist parties in New York City, left New York for Cuba via Mexico as a guest of ICAP (Cuban Peoples Friendship Institute). He was debriefed by CIA staffer Lou DeSanti upon return to the United States in July–August 1963. VICENTE stated he had had interviews with CASTRO and Che GUEVARA. VIOENTE (VICENTE) penetrated the FPCC, the Casa Cubá, and Jose Martí Club in New York City. Comment: Page 127 of the IG 1967 report mentions an unnamed Puerto Rican who claims he participated in a CASTRO-inspired assassination plot in Dallas. Do not know if VICENTE could be that individual.
