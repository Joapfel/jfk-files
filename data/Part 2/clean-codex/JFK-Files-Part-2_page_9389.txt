Free Breakfast for School Children Program

Weekly Menu

Week of Dec. 13–Dec. 17

Monday: Orange juice, bacon, jugs, snacks.

Tuesday: Oatmeal, toast, jelly, salami (fried).

Wednesday: Chocolate milk, hot dogs (fried), cream of wheat, peaches.

Thursday: S–E & orange, lettuce & tomato, Mon toast, ge.

Friday: Orange juice, grilled cheese, bacon, sliced peaches.

Food donations for the breakfast program are needed, such as breakfast meats, dry goods, milk, bread, etc. Also, money to obtain these items is acceptable.

If you wish to donate or give assistance in helping out at the breakfast program, call 867-7742 or come to the breakfast site at 2228 Bewick between Vernor and Kercheval.
