The individual then left to obtain photographs needed for his application.

The man probably returned on September 27, 1963, with the photographs and completed the applications in Ms. Duran's presence. As the amount of time required to process this document could have taken as long as twenty days or the response could have been negative, Azcue told the man that he could grant him an intransit visa to Cuba, without consulting his government, if he had a Russian visa. The individual then left to attempt to obtain his Russian visa.

After the man left the Cuban Consulate, Azcue received a telephone call from the Soviet Consulate (Consulte). The Soviet Consul explained that the man's documents were legitimate, but that the Soviet Consulate could not issue a visa until it received authorization from Moscow.

Emphasizing that the Cuban Consulate never received visitors after working hours, Mr. Azcue opined that the individual probably returned to the Consulate on September 28, 1963.
