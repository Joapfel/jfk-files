Communications Section

July 7, 1972

Teletype

NR 016 WF Coded  
5:38 PM NITEL 7-7-72 ASW  
To: Acting Director, New York  
From: Washington Field. Two pages.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) convention July fourteen through sixteen, seventy-two, at Hunter College, New York, New York. Internal Security—C (Trotskyist).

Informant, member of Washington, DC local of Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) and WONAAC, has been selected by Washington, DC YSA leadership as part of District of Columbia WONAAC (WON AAC) delegation to New York conference July fourteen through sixteen, next. Source advised that this conference is particularly important to the YSA because it is felt that anti-Trotskyist women in WONAAC will attempt to negate influence of Trotskyist women in WONAAC at captioned conference.

Bureau authority is requested to send source to captioned, and Bureau authority is further requested to advance to source. End page one.
