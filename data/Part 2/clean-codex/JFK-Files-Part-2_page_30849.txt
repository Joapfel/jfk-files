File: Examination of Bona Fides of a KGB Defective. 1968.

Note on cover:

CIA finds this study to be inaccurate in many important details and to obtain the results of faulty judgments leading to unfounded conclusions.

Interrogation periods:

12 February 1964: Arrived in Washington, D.C.

14 February 1964: Met with representatives of the Swiss and Soviet governments; renounced U.S. citizenship.

18 February: Initial debriefing completed.

24 February: Introduced to FBI for debriefing.

12–26 March: Hawaii vacation.

4 April: Placed in permanent safeguard outside Washington, D.C.; polygraph began.

24 April: Interrogation completed.

May–November: Friendly interrogation.

Interim consultation with FBI.

26 January 1965–5 March: Hostile interrogation.

February 1965: N. executed protocols.

26 July–14 August: Further interrogation.

19–25 October 1966.

May 1965: Three-week interrogation by psychiatrist and psychologist.
