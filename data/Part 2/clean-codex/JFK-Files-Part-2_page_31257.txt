AMTRUNK

Discussed to identify shelter use of denatured personnel in armed forces in Cuba.

Fall ’63, Nestor Antonio Moreno Lopez and Enrique Gallardo Robera developed Leonido Plan and breastwork Castros.

Moreno defected in April 1961 via Jorge Volsky; Cuban citizen of Polish origin. Presently USFA’s, then in Polish Air Force under British Command. Married a Cuban in Miami, May 1961.

Volsky contacted Tad Syule and Mel Burly Bordwen and Herwitch (Stet). CFT design plan to Miami station; it is known as AMTRUNK.

Never programmed for 8 July, September 6—had 3 sources exclusively, Gruin.

Moreno in October 1963 was removed from AMTRUNK due to underperformance. He was fired, appealed, and JFIC through Volsky and Syule.

Had some contacts but no real action capability; in 1965 Gruin’s project amused.

Syule and Volsky, both Polish, met in Cuba in 1959–60.

Jose Ricardo Rabel Nunez supervised agent versus deflated Dicleys, joined AMTRUNK, and returned to Cuba with him.
