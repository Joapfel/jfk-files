William Alexander Morgan

The Morgan File contains the following documents and information:

A. Information covering 1959–1973:

1. Cable dated March 24, 1959, stating that Morgan was staying at the Hotel Capri in Havana and was very close to Eloy Gutierrez Menoy. Morgan hired Tony Beacon, Philadelphia Daily Newsman, to handle publicity. POA request initiated for Morgan, 24 March.

2. Biographic Data Sheet: Alias “El Americano.” Date of birth: 4-19-28. Place of birth: Cleveland.

3. Request for Cancellation of Approval, 8 May 1959. Reason: derogatory information. Cancelled May 6, 1959.

B. Information covering 1 January 1960–14 August 1974:

1. May 19, 1960, cable from the Director reporting that he was in contact with Headquarters on March 18 regarding a plan. They were considering recruiting Morgan. The U.S. Army inquired whether CIA had any operational interest in Morgan and the group around him, mentioning Bob McCormack, Bob Brown, Tony Nichols, Morgan’s bodyguards Michael S. Colin, Jack Ossorio, Frank Emmick, Jerry Hemming, and June Cobb, Fidel’s secretary. Headquarters replied that there was no operational interest. Cobb was from New York and had been working for Castro as a translator since September 1959.

2. March 17, 1960: Source stated that on February 16, 1960, he had observed Morgan in Toledo, Ohio, in the company of Roberto Augustus Brown, Jr.; Toledo Police Department.
