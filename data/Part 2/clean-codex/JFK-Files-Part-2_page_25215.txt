Persons/Entities: Bundy, Johnson, Kilpatrick, McCone. Date: October 24, 1963. Categories: Diem, Special Group.

Occurrence:

On October 24, 1963, Mr. McCone had a Special Group meeting and expressed his fear that the top U.S. diplomatic and military officials in Saigon were becoming too involved in conversations between General Don and Conein (Coein). Such involvement could destroy Conein’s usefulness and involve Ambassador Lodge and General Harkins in an operation difficult to deny. The Special Group authorized a message to Lodge and Harkins emphasizing a secure system of contacts with General Don and the Vietnamese military, an assessment of Don’s status and future plans, and tighter control over the Don/Conein meetings. The group agreed to firm up policy thinking regarding Vietnam before Lodge returned to Washington and to hear Mr. Lodge’s views on what courses of action should be taken.

Minutes of Special Group Meeting, October 24, 1963. Staff: Notary Public in Singapore. Date: June 28, 1975.

Persons/Entities: Lodge, Bundy, General Don, Conein. Date: October 24, 1963. Categories: Diem.

Occurrence:

In a CIA cable from Saigon (SAIG1964) from Lodge to Bundy, it was noted: “CAS has been punctilious in carrying out my instructions. I have personally approved each meeting between General Don and Conein, who has carried out my orders in each instance explicitly...”

Source: IG Report 1967 (CIA), page 35. Staff: Dawson. Date: June 27, 1975.
