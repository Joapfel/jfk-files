Subject: Lee Henry Oswald (Ostald).

Reference is made to CIA out telephone No. 74873, dated 10 October 1963, regarding possible presence of subject in Mexico City.

It is requested that you forward to this office as soon as possible two copies of the most recent photographs you have of subject. US will forward them to our representative in Mexico, if needed, will attach to determining if the Lee Oswald (Ostald) in Mexico City and subject are the same individual.
