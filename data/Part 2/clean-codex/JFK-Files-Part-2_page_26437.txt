Telegram Information Report. Central Intelligence Agency.

This material contains information affecting the National Defense of the United States within the meaning of the Espionage Law, Title 18, U.S.C. Sections 793 and 794, the transmission or revelation of which in any manner to an unauthorized person is prohibited by law.

Country: South Vietnam. Report No. TDCS-3/500,097. Subject: Comments of Tran Van Ly on the desirability, problems and consequences of a coup d'etat. Date distributed: 28 January 1962. Precedence: Routine. Date of information: 25 January 1962. Place and date acquired: Vietnam, Saigon, 26 January 1962. Appraisal: 3. References: Taylor, IN 18750; Kaysen; Belk; R. Johnson. Field report No. FVS-7006.

This is unevaluated information. Source gradings are definitive. Appraisal of content is tentative.

Source: A Vietnamese National Army officer working at the General Staff Headquarters, from Tran Van Ly. Goodfall and Durham.

1. On 25 January 1962 Tran Van Ly, opposition leader imprisoned from November 1960 to February 1961 following the attempted coup of 1960, said that certain general officers have consulted with him on the possibility of attempting a coup d'etat. (Field comment. (TDCS-3/500,042) FVS-699.
