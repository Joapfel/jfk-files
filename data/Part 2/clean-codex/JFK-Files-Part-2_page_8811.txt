CSCI 3/776,625, dated 19 June 1963. Memorandum to FBI from CIA.

Subject: Ricardo CABRERA Amoedo.

Attached memorandum dated 30 April 1963 states that Ricardo CABRERA Amoedo, also known as “Cayo,” who lived in the United States, returned to Cuba when CASTRO assumed power in January 1959, returned to Miami in 1960 as an active G-2 member, and continued to work as an informer for CUIS. During 1960 he went to Cuba and had an interview with VALDES, Fidel CASTRO, NARANJO, and Juan ORTA, then secretary to Fidel. In Miami, CABRERA worked for the G-2, together with PIMENTAL (fnu) and Pepe NOVA, who were chiefs of G-2 in Miami. He was a man who would sell his mother. He became an American citizen and used to brag about being a member of CIA.

Volume II (Box I): Summary of Relations with FBI.

DINAR.

On 15 March 1963, the FBI’s ZRKNICK supervisor met with CIA personnel for discussion of the case and promised to keep CIA Headquarters informed of all developments. The FBI provided text of an SW message from the AMAPOLA letter drop indicating travel of one Evaristo FERNANDEZ to Costa Rica in connection with some kind of operation. CIA sent the information immediately to Costa Rica in view of the President’s trip.
