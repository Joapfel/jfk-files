Informer states there was publicity after the Mardi Gras incident alleging Banister was “framed” by NOPD because he was uncovering corruption within the department. The N.D. Crime Commission defended Banister, as did many other prominent groups in the area.

Banister has associated himself with Leander Perez, D.A. for Plaquemines (Plaza mine) Parish. Perez is extremely right-wing and known to have denied voting rights to blacks.

Interview with an official of NOPD indicates Banister had a good record, but began to publicly criticize his superior. His incident at Absinthe Bar in 1957 was thoroughly investigated, and 50 witnesses made statements. The majority stated Banister was drunk.

Two other interviewees indicate the bar incident was exaggerated because Banister was getting too close to the boxes of police administration.
