DIP. LFG WAVE CITE MEXI 7234

KAPOK TOPIC

1. Wallace B. Rowton has made contact with Carlos Jurado, artist husband of Villain Chichay, clerk in Ciran Consulate. Rowton has become quite friendly with Jurado, has taken him and Chichay to dinner, and in turn Jurado escorted Rowton on a tour of Rivera murals in Mexico (MEXI) City buildings.

2. Rowton, using an alias, has explained himself as being in the economic research business. Where the first meeting developed from Rowton's visit to a salon where Jurado was showing his paintings, the relationship has now swung around to a place where Rowton is interested in the general welfare of Jurado and wants to do something for him if Jurado can supply some service for which Rowton could logically afford to pay him. Jurado is snapping at this bait and appears genuinely to like Rowton, aside from the fact that Rowton appears to be a non-American businessman with money.

3. In the last meeting, Rowton queried Jurado on his contacts in Mexican commerce, and Jurado said he had none. Rowton asked what sort of contacts he had in Cuba since that subject was of interest to all, and Jurado fell over himself counting what he could do for Rowton on the Cuban subject.

9 May 1966
