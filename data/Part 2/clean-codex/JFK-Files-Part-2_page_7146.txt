To: Canberra. Info: Director. RYBAT PLMHCONGA. Congressman Milford.

Refs: A. Director 787959. B. Director 787555.

Please advise Congressman Milford that COS Wellington can arrange a 15 January, 0800 hours, breakfast meeting with New Zealand Security Intelligence Service Director, Brigadier H. E. Gilbert, at Congressman Milford's hotel. Although Embassy Wellington briefings on Operation Deepfreeze are scheduled for the morning of 15 January, Congressman Milford may prefer to meet with COS and Brigadier Gilbert. Ambassador Selden concurs with this arrangement. Transportation to the airport for the flight to Mt. Cook will be available immediately after the meeting with Gilbert. Please advise Congressman Milford's preference so COS can confirm the above arrangement with Brigadier Gilbert. E2IMPDET.
