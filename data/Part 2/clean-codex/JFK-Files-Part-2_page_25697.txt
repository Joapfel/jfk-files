Subject: Actions of Sileria Dray (Sileria DEA), Underlies Echevarria, Employed by the Cuban Consulate, Following Her First Interrogation by the Mexican Authorities

1. A female-used criminal source, well versed in political personality and events in the Cuban Embassy and Consulate in Mexico City, attempted several times on her return to the Cuban Consulate on 20 November 1963 to have Sileria Dray (Sileria DEA) tell her colleague immediately the same story about her association with Lee Harvey Oswald (Lee Barys OSAID) that was published in the Miami City press. This information, which appears to have been given to the press by certain Mexican authorities, indicated that, according to Dray, Oswald had visited the Cuban and Soviet Consulates to obtain a permit in order to visit these countries, and she had submitted his case to the Cuban Consulate.

2. The only point in the account that Sileria Dray (Sileria DEA) gave her colleague was that she was certain the Mexican authorities intended to send her back to the United States for a confrontation with Oswald. According to the source, Sileria Dray (Sileria DEA), an intelligent and quick-witted woman, did not fear such a conversation.

3. It should be noted that SLA does not know the proper origin of the suggested confrontation. The idea originated, in all probability, with the Mexican authorities involved in the interrogation of Dray.

4. According to the same source, immediately after and since the extradition, the personnel of the Cuban Embassy was stockaded and paraded. No expressions of pleasure at the extradition were heard, and there was little discussion of it. The guards and official conversations in the Cuban Embassy and Consulate tend to confirm the information that Dray visited the Embassy only to get a visa to visit or to transit the country.
