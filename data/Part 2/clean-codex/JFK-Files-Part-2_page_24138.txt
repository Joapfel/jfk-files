Single Entities

17. TOMOSAIC

A Delaware corporation based in London which provided an international news feature service; the project also supported the publication of books of propaganda value. The news feature service has been discontinued and the corporation will soon be dissolved.

18. AELEDGER

A French corporate entity located in Europe which publishes a foreign-language weekly periodical disseminated to recipients in and out of Russia. It contains articles on Soviet and Bloc matters, including a large amount of material prepared covertly in the USSR by Soviet dissidents. The periodical attempts to encourage and assist activities of moderate segments of Soviet society.

19. AEDYNAMIC

A New York corporation located in New York City which publishes periodicals and selected books for infiltration into the Soviet Union and distribution to travelers and Soviets residing outside the USSR; fosters regional nationalism within the USSR; supports a private organization of regional nationalities; and supports the AEDYNAMIC branch office in Munich, Germany.

20. QRMYSTIC

A sole proprietorship located in the Washington metropolitan area, owned ostensibly by an Agency employee whose journalistic services are utilized in support of foreign operations.

Proprietary Management

LPPANDA Complex

The three companies below support a one-man accounting and management company in Europe which provides services to other proprietary operations.
