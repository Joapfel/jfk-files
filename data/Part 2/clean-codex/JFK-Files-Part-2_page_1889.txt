Section VIII. Agency Employment History (Continue).

Inclusive dates: Aug 57–Feb 59. Location: Manila, P.I. Directorate office or division branch: FE. Title of job: Chief of Records Management Team.

Description of duties: Chief of a team of three individuals stationed PCS Manila but travelling throughout the F.E. (including Australia) to set up record systems, including registry, according to Headquarters regulation. This was to ensure that field systems would be in line with HGS procedures.

Inclusive dates: Feb 57–July 59. Location: Mexico City, Mexico. Directorate office or division branch: WH. Title of job: Team Member of Records Management Team. Grade held in job: GS-7.

Description of duties: Member of a team of 4 sent to Mexico to establish and streamline a system of records. This involved setting up a 201 system, better name trace system, charge-out system for files, and the amplicrofilming of FBI records. My main specialty was 201s.

Inclusive dates: Aug 54–Nov 56. Location: Struth, Keratin, Kyso Sula, IV. Directorate office or division branch: FE. Title of job: Admin Assistant. Grades held in job: GS-5, GS-7.

Description of duties: Name tracks and other regular registry duties.
