MM 149-425

On January 29, 1968, S advised SA Robert (C) G. Strong that the recent dynamiting attacks are the work of Orlando Bosch of M.I.R.R.

On January 31, 1968, at noon, SA Samuel W. Jones observed Orlando Bosch Avila at the corner of 18th Avenue and West Flagler Street, in company with two unidentified Cuban males. Bosch was driving a Karmann Ghia Volkswagen with 1967 Alabama license 55-9211.
