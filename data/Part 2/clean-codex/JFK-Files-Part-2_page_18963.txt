Samuel Adason Jaffe

Titov arrived in the United States at New York City on January 24, 1963, destined for the Soviet Mission to the United Nations with the rank of First Secretary. He departed on October 1, 1963, and returned to New York City on November 19, 1963. He again departed the United States on July 24, 1964.

Jaffe again indicated that he recognized photographs of Vladislav Mikhaylovich Kovshuk, aka Vladimir Komarov, particularly the younger photograph; however, he did not recognize these names. He said he believed he had contact with this individual in the United States and not in Russia.

Jaffe stated his closest contacts among Soviet nationals in Moscow were:

Mikhail (“Mike”) I. Bruk  
Yuriy Vladimirovich Fedorov  
Aleksandr “Sasha” Nezmanov, his translator  
Nikolai Sergeevich Kalinin
