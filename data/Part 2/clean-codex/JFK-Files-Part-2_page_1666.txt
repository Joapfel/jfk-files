Office Memorandum, United States Government.

To: File  
Subject: James S. Woods  
Date: 3 January 1952

1. Contacted Treasury Department this date, and the Employee Relations Officer stated that the subject had no efficiency ratings since he had not been there long enough before being granted military furlough and after his discharge. However, she stated that Mr. Woods had been a very satisfactory employee and there was nothing derogatory in his file.

L. E. Blais
