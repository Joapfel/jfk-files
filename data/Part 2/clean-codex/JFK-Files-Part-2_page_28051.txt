Memorandum

To: All Staff Employees  
From: Budget Officer  
Date: January 3, 1977  
Re: Payroll Certification

Starting with the January 1977 payroll, the certification to the House Finance Office requires, among other things, the relationship, if any, of each staff employee to any current Member of Congress (those taking office January 3, 1977).

The following are the relationships to be included in the certification: father, nephew, mother, niece, son, husband, daughter, wife, brother, father-in-law, sister, mother-in-law, uncle, son-in-law, aunt, daughter-in-law, first cousin, brother-in-law, stepfather, sister-in-law, stepmother, stepbrother, stepsister, half-brother, half-sister.

All staff employees are requested to complete this form and return it to the Budget Officer.

Approved  
Richard A. Sprague

I am not related to any Member of Congress.
