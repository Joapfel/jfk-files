26 November 1963

Memorandum No. 81 for General Earle G. Wheeler (JCS)

Mr. Frank K. Sloan, Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense, ISA (Regional Affairs); Major General J. D. Alger, USA; Rear Admiral W. F. A. Wendt, USN; Major General J. W. Carpenter III, USAF; Brigadier General C. J. Quilter, USMC.

Subject: Interdepartmental Coordinating Committee of Cuban Affairs: Report on Status of Implementation of Actions Designed to Counter Subversion (U).

Reference: DA Memo No. 64, subject as above, dated 19 September 1963.

Attached herewith is a final draft of the Report to the President of the Subcommittee on Cuban Subversion on Progress Made During September 1963 in Curbing Cuban Subversion in Latin America.

It is requested that comments, if any, be provided this office by 1200 hours, 2 December 1963. The Department of Defense portion of the subject report is based on submissions provided in response to the referenced memorandum.

Enclosure: As stated.

cc: Mr. Yarmolinsky (OSD); Lieutenant Colonel Haig; Mr. Califano; ASG.
