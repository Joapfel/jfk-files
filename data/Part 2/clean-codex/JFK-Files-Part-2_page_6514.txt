This entirety, in order that the People's Government of Cuba have the options.

a. Extend surveillance of Cuba and the Caribbean:

(1) Intensively monitor, observe, and report upon air and sea transportation—both scheduled and nonscheduled; both Far East and Communist—in the Caribbean area contiguous to Cuba, especially by a multinational politico-military program providing for intelligence-gathering cooperation with Latin American states concerning suspected carriers while maintaining their territorial waters.

(2) Exploit fully the intelligence opportunities available through the system passing through Havana, while reviewing periodically the possibility of interrupting the system for security reasons.

b. Intensify psychological actions.

(1) Create problems for the Castro regime and further programs by an intensified psychological effort involving radio, news, and airborne TV emphasizing the hardships and sufferings of the people of the Soviet Union by the campesinos (Gampesinos), while subversive trainees are given the opportunity to receive.

(3) Increase greatly the amount of information and training to Cuba, and intensify emphasis on the negative effects of Communism on the Cuban people by the Castro subversive group.

(4) Conduct airborne television by aircraft carrying people and Latin American students in Cuba, utilizing the opportunities for Castro-subversive efforts designed to affect the Hemisphere.
