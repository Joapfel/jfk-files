File: Nosenko Interrogation Transcript.

Reel No. 2: 26 July 1965 (Dely Abin). Complete translation of verbatim transcript.

P. 1–4. D. questions fact that N. did not know a second Sauchenko, who was lieutenant general in KGB.

5. Nosenko's chiefs: Fedotov, Shubnyakov, Gribanov.

9. Nosenko states he received pay of 1,700 rubles plus 500 for rank.

D. claims no pay was given for rank at time N. began in March 1953.

27. N. served as interpreter-translator of intelligence of the Seventh Naval Fleet in the Pacific Ocean.

28. Afterwards worked in Baltic in 1952 as a senior interpreter-translator of the MAH, MRP of intelligence of the Fourth Naval Fleet in the Baltic.

41. Cannot tell main safe apartment or surveillance base of 1st Department for 1953–55.

42. N. names safe apartments of surveillance of the Second Chief Directorate, Vogstanine Square area of Kropotkinskaya Bridge.
