9 December 1966.

6 December 1966.

LIEU-NG-J reported to Wallace B. Wilson (Rivilson) on 21 November 1966. He had met in the Cuban Embassy an individual from Havana who told him he was traveling under an assumed name. L-3 reported the individual told him he was in Mexico to buy coffee seeds on direct orders from Fidel Castro.

2. L-3 stated he believed the man's name to be Israel Echemendia. A check of Cuban manifests for October and November 1966 shows no arrival of Israel Echemendia. But Israel Suarez de la Paz arrived 23 October 1966. Recent LIENVOY shows Suarez interested in coffee matters.

3. On 28 November, Suarez phoned the Commercial Office in the Embassy and told the secretary he wanted to talk to Hugo del Pino about a meeting at the Mexican Institute of Coffee. When told Hugo was not in, he asked if Rolando (Correlo) could go with him to an interview at the Institute. He said it was very important to them and they were much interested in the problem. He asked her to tell Hugo he was going to the Institute and that Hugo was to come too. Three minutes later, the man called the same phone and told the secretary he was now at the Institute and that he was awaiting Hugo.
