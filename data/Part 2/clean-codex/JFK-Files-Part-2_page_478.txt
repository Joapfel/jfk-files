2 August 1966. IN 68647.

1. Estimating with Wallace B. Rowton on 1 August 1966, Carlos Jurado Delmar stated Miguel Lozano Villalobos had shown him an aforementioned (LEFOREMENT) cable from Havana which arrived Mexico City after departure of Abelardo Curbelo Padron on 25 July and summarized a list of deficiencies and inefficiencies of Curbelo during his assignment in Mexico City.

2. Cable signed by Raul Roa Garcia. Cable cited unanswered message requesting specific information and list of actions not taken. Lozano told Jurado it was unlikely Curbelo would return to Mexico City.
