CITE JAKARTA 35549 (SHIMA ACTING)

To: Director.

RYBAT PLMHCONGA Congressman

Refs: A, Director 787555; B, Director 785722.

1. Charge Mike Rives has been advised Congressman Milford's request for station briefing per Ref A. Will arrange briefing after congressman's arrival.

2. On 2 January WAFAWN/1 telephoned station to say that cable has been received by BAKIN from HACLAUSE/1. We proposed meetings with Ministry of Defense, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Indonesian Parliament and call on President Suharto. He also proposed that General Murdani brief the group on general security situation. BAKIN has sent cable to Murdani to return to Jakarta early next week to participate in briefings. Briefing of congressmen and their staff members scheduled to be held at BAKIN headquarters on 7 January. Congressman Milford will participate in this briefing. Station arranged BAKIN briefings based on instructions from charge.
