3 March 1951

DIMITROV signed a memorandum certifying that he had received three hundred dollars from Lawrence F. MacBRIDE, literary agent, representing a sum paid for a complete and unabridged option on the use and possible publication of three books written by me and brought to Mr. MacBRIDE, in Greek translation, by who acted in this instance purely informally and as a friend. The option is effective as of this date for a period of approximately six calendar months.

Memorandum dated 3 March 1951, attachment to SGAA 24772, not in file.
