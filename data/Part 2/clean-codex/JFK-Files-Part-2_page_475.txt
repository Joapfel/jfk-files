Director

PBRUMEN LIRING

Reference: Mexico City 8126 (WL8738)

9 August 1966, IN 73260

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace B. Rowton at an 8 August meeting he prepared to revisit Cuba to further develop contacts for Rowton business activities. He plans to request a visa 9 August directly from Apnol Rodriguez, who is still in the Foreign Ministry, though no longer Vice Minister. L-3 expects speedy service on visa request, allowing him to take the 13 August flight to PBRUMEN.

2. L-3 very anxious to take wife and child with him on this flight, and her visa approval several weeks ago is expected to arrive in time to accompany him. Wife and child will fly on to Santiago for a visit with her mother. Will give L-3 $1,200 U.S., half in Mexican pesos, to finance trip. L-3 to stay in Havana 13–26 August, go to Santiago 27 August, return to Mexico 29 August.

3. Rowton confident L-3 relationship progressed to point he can levy specific political, economic, and even military requirements on L-3, who will do his best to satisfy. L-3 told Rowton he realizes the necessity of keeping Rowton informed in detail on Cuban situation since secret.
