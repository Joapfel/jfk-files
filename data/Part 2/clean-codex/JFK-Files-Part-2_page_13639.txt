SENSTUDY 75

Rebutel May 2, 1975.

All information contained before this date is good. This is a sample message by Senate Select Committee (SSC).

Inquiries made of Bureau by Senate Select Committee (SSC) concerning a number of present and former FBI employees, including their current whereabouts, suggests they may be interviewed by SSC staff, exact subject matters for interviews unknown. Set out below are names and last known addresses of former Bureau employees and office of assignment of incumbents, REC-100, all of whom SSC has inquired about.

Each of these former employees is to be immediately contacted and alerted that he (she) might be approached by the 1 - Rome (by mail).

See note page four.
