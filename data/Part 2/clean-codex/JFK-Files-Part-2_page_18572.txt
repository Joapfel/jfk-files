July 21, 1975

Legal Counsel Division

Attached teletype advises former SA John Matter was requested by the Senate Select Committee to make himself available for interview at 11:30 a.m., July 25, 1975. Mr. Matter telephonically advised this will be a staff interview concerning the Bureau's investigation of Martin Luther King. He requested a Bureau representative be available for consultation during the interview.

Matter will be briefed by representatives of the Intelligence and Legal Counsel Divisions prior to his interview.

Legal Counsel Division recommends that any applicable employment agreement be waived for purposes of this interview and that an Intelligence Division representative accompany Matter to be available for consultation during the interview.
