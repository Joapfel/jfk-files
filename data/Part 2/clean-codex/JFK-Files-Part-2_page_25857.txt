United States Government

Memorandum

To: Mr. Belmont  
From: A. Rosen  
Subject: Jack Ruby; Lee Harvey Oswald (LER HARVEY OSWALD) — victim civil rights  
Date: November 25, 1963

At 9:20 A.M. this date, an individual identifying himself only as Mr. Miller, 1125 14th Street, Northwest, contacted the Bureau and advised that he was a close friend of Ruby. In addition, he identified Clint Murchison (Clinic Murchison) and Tommy Webb as being friends of Ruby. Miller declined to furnish additional information but agreed to be interviewed. Washington Field Office was immediately advised and an Agent dispatched in order to get full details concerning the above.

I — Mr. DeLoach
