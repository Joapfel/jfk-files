21 September 1970: Cruz indicated he would not be in the next day; Mirabal would take his place. He went out of town, not known where.

21 September 1970: Cruz called Tampico and Canada from his apartment again.

21 September 1970: Avila Camacho offered Cruz his projection room (probably at Banco).

21 September 1970: LIFEAT: Woman outside (Marina de Corrales) called Cruz to give him her new address, Amsterdam 302. She had something to tell Cruz.

22 September 1970: Miguel DONOSO called Cruz to see if Cruz had received a word from his daughter. Cruz said no. Cruz then told Donoso about the showing of a film at Teatro Orientacion, Thursday, 5:30, and said he would see him there.

23 September 1970: Tomeo (Domeo) in Havana called Cruz to get the price on 400 boxes of Agfa-Gevaert photographic paper, BS-1, 8.24 cmts.

22 September 1970: LIENVOY: Cruz talked with Moya about a new Bell and Howell 16mm projector.

22 September 1970: Cruz called Sr. Tomeo of COR Nacional.

23 September 1970: LIENVOY: Havana told Cruz that the U.S. $2,800 was deposited for Cruz to buy the photographic material from Gustavo Tomeo.

23 September 1970: LIENVOY: Cruz called Aurora Velasco and Antonio ROSETTE (549-49-85) to invite them to a special film showing on 24 September.

23 September 1970: Jesus and Mercedes worked at the Embassy until 19:30.

23 September 1970: LIENVOY: Cruz called Quebec regarding the purchase of tape recorders in Canada.
