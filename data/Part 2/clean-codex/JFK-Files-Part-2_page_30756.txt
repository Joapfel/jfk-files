April 29, 1959. Classified message to Director from Havana says Raúl Castro has begun a major purge of the Cuban Armed Forces. Among those to be eliminated is Víctor Panegu (Panegue), aka “Diego.”

October 18, 1960. Conversation with Major Víctor M. Panegue (Commandante Diego), October 6 at 0900.

When Panegue arrived in Miami, he got together with Alianza de Liberación through Dr. Luis Loute Agüero. He could not get immediate help from this group, so resigned. He also said he had tried to get help from the U.S. Government but was unsuccessful.

Panegue is convinced that the only thing needed to overthrow Castro is arms and supplies. He has a plane which he intends to fly into Cuba with a cargo of arms.

The author of the memorandum recommends that CIA offer assistance to Panegue independently of all other organizations, as he seems capable of becoming an important factor in the future.

November 2, 1960. Cable to Director from Maale Panegue and eight men leaving for Cuba overnight on a 40-foot yacht, Trudy Lou. The craft was purchased by Orlando Beach.
