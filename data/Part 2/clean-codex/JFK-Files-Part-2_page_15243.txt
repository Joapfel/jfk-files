United States Government Memorandum

To: W. C. Sullivan

Date: February 4, 1963

From: W. R. Wannall

Subject: Technical Surveillance Coverage of Diplomatic Establishments

With the approval of the Attorney General (1/31/62), we instituted a program to expand our use of technical surveillances on diplomatic establishments of countries outside the Soviet bloc. Following is an analysis of this program and its current status:

We advised the Attorney General on 1/26/62 that, on a selective and probing basis, we would explore coverage of the following countries under this program by instituting coverage on six or eight establishments at a time:

Argentina; Japan; Panama; Brazil; Laos; Portugal; Republic of the Congo; Mali; Thailand; Guinea; Mexico; Venezuela; India; Morocco; South Vietnam; Indonesia.

After the necessary survey and State Department clearance in each instance, we initiated initial coverage during early March 1962, and to date coverage on the following establishments has been instituted:

Embassy of Argentina

Embassy of Brazil

Brazilian Delegation to United Nations (UN)

Congolese Delegation to UN

Embassy of Guinea

Guinea Delegation to UN

Guinea Delegation to UN

Embassy of India

Indian Delegation to UN

Embassy of Indonesia

Indonesian Delegation to UN

Embassy of Japan

Embassy of Mali

Embassy of Mexico

Mexican Delegation to UN

Embassy of Portugal

Portuguese Delegation to UN
