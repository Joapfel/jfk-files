File C-102798 (old file C-70300). Delaware date: May 1958 through October 1976.

Classified message. 7 July 1960. From Bonn 15-13. Airgar FROB Cire Bonn 9-956. RedsRir.

Priscilla (Piscilla) Johnson, age 32, born long ago, is a foreign correspondent for North American Newspaper Alliance. Moscon, now unemployed because of Soviet refusal to renew visa, stopped coming to Bad Godesberg 9 July for several days, possibly w.r.t., or on way back to United States. Purpose of Bad Godesberg visit is to discuss her future with Ed Faltermayer, Bonn correspondent of Wall Street Journal, who had acquaintance. Marie R. Cauchner, who acquainted w/ Faltermayer, will see Johnson. Although doubtless HP will wish to debrief her upon return to States, Cauchner could arrange for introduction. Cauchner would arrange for interview with FROB officer for social and exchange of views meeting in Bad Godesberg 15–20.
