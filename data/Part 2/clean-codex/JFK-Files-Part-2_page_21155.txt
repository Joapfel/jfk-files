11. American Intelligence Use of Tourists

Pages 146–150 relate to Nosenko’s (NOSENKO’s) awareness of what the KGB knew concerning the use of tourists by foreign intelligence services. It was noted that he claimed to have prepared a report for SCD Chief Gribanov (GRIBANOV) in 1959, but could not remember the content, any examples included therein, or the conclusions regarding American-British tourist intelligence operations. It is stated that he claimed no agents were definitely identified among American tourists and no tourists had been caught in the act of mailing letters, servicing dead drops, or contacting agents, with one exception.
