Subject: Training of Cuban Refugees in Nicaragua (S)

4. (C) Reference 1a, from the A. C. of 8, G-1, Fourth Army, reports alleged efforts by Lt. Manuel ARTUÑA Bussa to get some of his former associates in the Bay of Pigs invasion to resign their U.S. Army commissions and go with him to a revolutionary camp in Nicaragua. A copy of this reference is enclosed for your information. COMARC, Fourth Army, and 3rd Army have been directed to continue to report on the alleged recruiting activity by ARTUÑA.

5. (C) Reference 1a reports information received from Ft. Holabird, N.Y., that a former Cuban Brigade member, 2nd Lt. Jose Haul VARONA Gonzales, C-231220B, had been invited by 2nd Lt. Enoide OLIVA, former second in command at the Bay of Pigs, to visit Washington, D.C., on 18 November 1963 to meet with Lt. Robert Kennedy, the Attorney General. VARONA is a student at Ft. Holabird and reportedly was the G-2 of Brigade 1964 at the Cuban invasion. When VARONA returned from Washington, he did not mention seeing Mr. Kennedy, but did say that he had met with Lt. OLIVA. The actual purpose of the meeting is not known. A copy of reference 1a is enclosed for your information. Mr. Robert Kennedy did confer on 17 November 1963 with Manuel ARTUÑA Bussa, Roberto SAN ROMAN, also known as Roberto Perez SAN ROMAN, Jose SAN ROMAN, also known as Jose Perez SAN ROMAN, and Enrique Jose HUIZ (HUIZ) Willim (Willim) Alfert. They were also scheduled to meet with Mr. Robert Kennedy on either 21 or 22 November 1963. There is no indication that Ft. Holabird student VARONA was present.

6. (C) This office has no information whether Mr. Kennedy is aware of ARTUÑA's alleged recruiting activity. The information in paragraphs 3 through 5 above, and the inclosures hereto, are furnished in the event that they may be of some pertinence to your inquiry regarding Colonel McPhail, reference 1a above.

R. D. Albano, Col., GS
