Typic Liring.

1. Carlos Jurado Delmar reported to Wallace 3. Rowton on 1 August that he had contacted Manolo Gonzalez in Veracruz on 30 and 31 July. Gonzalez is a Mexican functionary and local employee of the Cuban Consulate in Veracruz. He has resided in Veracruz for the past 35 years and has worked for the Consulate most of this period.

2. Jurado had been sent to Veracruz by Rowton to develop Gonzalez for use in Rowton-Jurado business deals and ultimately for intelligence purposes. Gonzalez agreed to work with Jurado on condition that his cooperation remain “between us,” since he said it would be embarrassing to him if Havana (HAVA) learned he was doing business on the side.

3. Gonzalez told Jurado he was going to Havana (HAVA) soon on his own since he was not familiar with the city since the Revolution. He plans to fly Cubana from Mexico City (MEXI CITY) on 5 August and will arrive in Mexico City (MEXI CITY) the day before. Rowton will have Jurado offer him a free overnight stay in a new apartment rented by Jurado and formerly occupied by Abelardo Curbelo Padron (ASELARDO CURBELO PADRON). There is no evidence Gonzalez has yet asked for a visa.
