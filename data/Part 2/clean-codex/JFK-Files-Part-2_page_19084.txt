United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI  
Attention: Crime Records Division  
From: Legat (Leegat), Hong Kong  
Subject: Sam A. Jaffe  
Source of Information  
Date: 8/29/66

Captioned subject is the American Broadcasting Company's China-Southeast Asia News Correspondent based in Hong Kong. Jaffe has indicated a very friendly attitude toward the Bureau and personnel of the New York Office. Unless certain facts militate to the contrary, it is expected that Legat will become better acquainted with Jaffe through mutual friends.

The Bureau is requested to advise whether there is any information in files (Buffles) which would make it inadvisable for Legat to have contact with Jaffe.
