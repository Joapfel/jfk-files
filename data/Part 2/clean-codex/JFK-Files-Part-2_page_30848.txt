1957–1958 used ALIBO. Yuray Ivanovich Nicola-Tez went to London as security escort for Soviet sports delegation.

1958 joined American-British Commonwealth Section of Seventh Department to identify and recruit foreign intelligence agents visiting the Soviet Union as tourists, including counterintelligence operations involving entrapment.

January 1960 transferred to American Embassy Section of the American Department; responsible for monitoring contacts between U.S. Embassy personnel and Soviet citizens and for collecting information on American Embassy personnel to facilitate recruitment.

March 1962 accompanied Soviet delegation to the Disarmament Conference in Geneva as security escort.

January 1964 returned to Geneva as security escort for government delegation to Disarmament Conference in Geneva.
