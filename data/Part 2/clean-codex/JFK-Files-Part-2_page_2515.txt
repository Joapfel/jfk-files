Mr. George Martin Roanek,

Reference is made to your contract with the United States Government, as represented by the Central Intelligence Agency, effective 1 March 1970, as amended.

Effective 8 April 1979, paragraph four, entitled “Compensation,” is amended by deleting the figure $37,270 as it appears therein and substituting in lieu thereof the figure $61,270. Said paragraph is also modified to provide that subsequent augmentation of your basic fee, based upon the percentage increase authorized for U.S. Government employees in the annual legislative pay increases, will be individually authorized by contract amendment at appropriate times as requested and certified by the responsible Government official, provided, however, that your total compensation under this paragraph does not exceed the equivalent of the salary rate paid at the top step of GS-15.

All other terms and conditions of the contract, as amended, remain in full force and effect.

Central Intelligence Agency
