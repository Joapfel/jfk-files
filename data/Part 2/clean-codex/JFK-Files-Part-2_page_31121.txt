Volume I:

15 December 1960 dispatch, UFGA-465. To: Chief, WHD. From: CO87 SMWAVE. Subject: AMOT-2 subsource in Havana. Gavaldo G. Mendoza uses the pseudonym “Napoleon.” Address: 866 Calle Zanja, first floor, Havana.

22 February 1961 dispatch, Chief, WHD Division, from Chief of Staff/SMWAVE. AMOT reports on Friends of Democratic Cuba, Inc. in New Orleans, including:

“Although we acknowledge the lack of understanding of some Cubans and the ambitions which grow in epide, we believe that the establishment of this company was thought up by Mr. Durhan and Mr. Banister to obtain personal benefits with the approval of Mr. Arcacha. We also doubt that this company received no donations of any type, and we believe that a thorough investigation of the funds of the stevedores union and the books of the New Orleans Exporters Company should be conducted by authorized personnel, and we suppose that interesting developments should come from this matter, maybe pointing not only to personal gain by its promoters but also to the evaporation of federal tafes of the U.S. Government.”
