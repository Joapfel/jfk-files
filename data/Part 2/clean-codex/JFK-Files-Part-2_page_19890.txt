April 28, 1972. NR002 WF coded. 5:00 p.m. NITEL, 4-28-72. To Director. From Washington Field (100-55420).

Proposed candlelight march to United States Capitol, Washington, D.C., sponsored by District of Columbia Chapter of Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAC), May 6, 1972. Internal Security—Communist (Trotskyist).

On April 27 last, a source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that the District of Columbia Chapter of Women's National Abortion Action Coalition has proposed a candlelight march from St. Mark's Episcopal Church, Third and A Streets, S.E., Washington, D.C., to the east steps of the United States Capitol to gain support for legalized abortion. The march is expected to start at 9:30 p.m. and end approximately 11:00 p.m. on the night of May 6 next, and approximately 200 individuals are expected to participate.
