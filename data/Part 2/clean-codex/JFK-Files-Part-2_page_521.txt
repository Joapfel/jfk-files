212318Z, Mexico City 8938. 22 October 1966, 23083. Director, info JMWAVE.

Type: LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 8889 (dated 20 April 1965).

1. LIRING-2 reported to ROWTON (POWTON) on 13 October 1966 that he had made contact with Cecilia Carmen Marquez de Rossie after the latter arrived on a Cubana flight from Havana on 17 October 1966.

2. LIRING-3 stated LIRING-4 had met Cecilia at the plane, but that the latter was seen to be so open in her denunciation of the Castro regime that LIRING-3 told LIRING-4 to break contact for fear of jeopardizing her position. LIRING-4 noted Cecilia was contacted at the hotel by Cuban refugees of “doubtful loyalty” and that Cecilia, after these contacts, was seen to have a large sum of U.S. dollars in her possession. LIRING-4 reported she saw one quantity amounting to five hundred.

3. In view of LIRING-3’s present status with LIRING-2, LIRING-3 confirmed with ROWTON that he should report the facts concerning Cecilia to LIRING-2. He believes that to withhold such information might work to his disadvantage were it to become known to Cuban Embassy personnel.

4. Cecilia reported to LIRING-4 that Cubans had bumped her off the plane on 9 October 1966 on the pretext that there was no room. They did not arrest or question her.
