Address official communications to the Secretary of State, Washington, D.C.

Department of State, Washington.

In reply refer to: FC-LEM.

May 17, 1948

To: Donald H. Galloway, Esquire, Assistant Director, Central Intelligence Agency.

From: Jack D. Neal, Chief, Division of Foreign Activity Correlation.

Reference is made to your memorandum dated May 7, 1948, regarding Mr. Birch D. O'Neal.

The Embassy at Caracas has been notified by telegram May 14, 1948, that Mr. O'Neal's travel from Caracas to Habana and return for a conference approximately May 19, 1948, is authorized by travel order No. 8-9649, May 4, 1948, chargeable to allotment Z-137235.
