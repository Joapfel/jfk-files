United States Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations With Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSC)

Re: Request Pertaining to Electronic Surveillance

Note:

Technical coverage information set forth in this memorandum was extracted and retrieved following an exhaustive, extensive research of Martin Luther King's "June" file, his main file, and the "June" files of the Communist Influence in Racial Matters (CIRM), the Communist Party, United States of America (CP, USA), and SCIC. In addition, sundry other files were reviewed that were directly or indirectly related to the investigation of Martin Luther King. The enclosures (see Exhibits A through X) to the memorandum were also the result of the review of the aforementioned files. These files and the eisur indices were reviewed by Special Agents George R. Ross and Robert D. Shea. In regard to Item 6(d) set forth in Appendix D of the 5/14/75 letter, an agreement was reached between SSC Staff Member Mark Gitenstein and Supervisor Elmer Larson of the Sensudy (Sensudy) 75 Project that a response to Item 6(d) is not necessary. Item 6(d) is as follows: "A list of all memorandums and other material reflecting dissemination outside the FBI of information from each surveillance, stating author, address(es), and dates together with the purpose of such dissemination."
