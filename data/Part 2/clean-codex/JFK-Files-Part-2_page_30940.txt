July 6, 1978

Betsy Palmer

Source: foreign intelligence.

Guy Banister — 428810

Banister approved on 10 November 1960 for contact use as a routine investigative report, dated 13 September 1960, conducted in the event Guy Banister & Associates might be used as a cover mechanism.

Synopsis:

General business reputation is good, but his business is not very sound. He was incorporated on 21 January 1958 and is president; his wife is vice-treasurer and secretary-treasurer, and Joseph A. Oster Leen has been placed on the company. NSF checks have been returned. Informants interviewed advised that Banister was suspended as audit superintendent of NOPD because he was involved in a Doron Town bar incident during Mardi Gras in March 1957. Suspension from NOPD was lifted, and Banister was reassigned to a position with no authority; he resigned two days later. He purchased a newspaper in Pretna, Louisiana, which he had for one and a half years, during which time he was charged with defamation of character.

Began private detective business and became associated with Kent Courtney, publisher of the right-wing publication Independent American. Checks were done on Banister, Oster, and Ferdees in the course of an investigation, including credit and police checks.
