Code cablegram. Urgent.

To Legal London. From Director, FBI. Senate Select Committee.

Mark Gitenstein, senior staff member, Senate Select Committee, which is currently examining procedures of U.S. intelligence agencies, has advised that the Committee has expressed an interest in how other countries handle their domestic security investigations; that is, those investigations analogous to our extremist and subversive investigations. Gitenstein is leaving for London August 6 next and has indicated a desire to meet with appropriate British officials any time August 11 or 12 next for a general briefing if this is possible. He has no objection to Legal sitting in on such briefing.

Gitenstein's request has been discussed with [John] (5).
