1/5/70

Teletype

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago (P)

Black Panther Party. RM-BPP.

Source, reliable in past, advised Bobby Rush, deputy minister of defense, Illinois chapter, BPP, to make a news announcement to local media that the Panthers have an independent pathologist report indicating Fred Hampton (deceased deputy chairman, Illinois BPP, as a result of the Illinois State's Attorney's police raid December 4 last) had received a drug through eating approximately eight hours before death. Indication is Panthers will not cooperate with the Illinois Cook County Coroner's Grand Jury scheduled to convene January 6 next at 2600 South California; however, will submit pathologist report. Rush also called for BPP supporters to appear for a demonstration at the above address January 6 next.

No violence anticipated or known planned by BPP. Appropriate agencies cognizant.
