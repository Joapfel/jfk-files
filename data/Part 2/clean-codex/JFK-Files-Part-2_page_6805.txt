10 April 1996

HPSCI Briefing—JFK Declassification

1. Nature of the records: Oswald 201, sequestered. Other records: minutes of DCI morning meetings; working files. Third Agency documents: FBI, SSCI, Presidential libraries.

2. Pages released: 227,000. Percentage of pages redacted: 70%.

3. Process of declassification: Former senior officers in HRG review. Coordination with OGC and DO (DO team detailed to HRG).

4. Standards for review in the JFK Assassination Records Collection Act, 1992: Records related to the assassination or investigation into the assassination. The law provides grounds for postponement of disclosure of records. “Clear and convincing evidence” must be presented to the Board. Examples include the identity of an agent currently requiring protection, a source or method currently utilized, and a foreign government relationship currently requiring protection.

5. The Board has authority to release records unless it agrees there is “clear and convincing evidence” to support a postponement. The Board then has to justify on the record each redaction with which it agrees. Once a determination is made, the Board must publish it in the Federal Register within 14 days.
