041905Z, Mexico City 9318, DCT 2/93, CC53, C9PS, AADP. Director info: JMWAVE, Guatemala City. 5 January 1993.

Topic: Liring. Reference: Mexico City 9608 15.

1. All are highpointers.

2. Liring-3 met at Huehuetenango bus station by two men who identified themselves as “Octavio” and “Falcon” and local Fuerzas Armadas Revolucionarias (FAR) representatives. Since Liring-3 was only able to obtain a 48-hour Guatemala visa, the FAR representatives asked him to recross into Mexico and meet them again the night of 22 December at a point on the Mexican side of the border on the Pan-Am Highway near La Mesilla, Guatemala. Liring-3 did as told, was led across the border blind and driven to Guatemala City by a succession of vehicles. During all legs of the trip, a second vehicle was used well ahead of the one driving Liring-3 to guard against roadblocks.

3. In Guatemala City, Liring-3 was taken to a house on 29th Avenue occupied by a woman introduced as Vicky (will obtain exact address). Told to stay there until contacted.
