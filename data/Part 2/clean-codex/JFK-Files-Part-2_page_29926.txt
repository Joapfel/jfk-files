Subject Case Reference: CARRAFA, Eugene Michael

31 March 1977. A gangland enforcer involved in the assassination attempt on Fidel (Fidele) Castro, Charles Nicoletti was found shot to death in a burning car near the Golden Horns Restaurant in Northlake, Illinois. Nicoletti was once a capo in the Chicago family and was once Sam Giancana's right-hand man.

On 29 March 1977, George de Mohrenschildt (de Mohrenscchildt), former friend of Oswald, allegedly committed suicide in Manalapan, Florida, at the home of Mrs. Charles Tilton, III.
