Liaison with Other U.S. Agencies 19-2-4.

Director 03964, dated 8 December 1962.

Headquarters CIA cabled Secret Service, FBI, and State Department Security, giving the text of a letter from Havana, dated 27 November 1962, concerning a plot to assassinate President Kennedy. The letter also stated that Fidel was very anxious to know how plans were progressing. The letter was addressed to a post office box in Miami belonging to Radio Libertad La Voz Anti-Comunista de America. Since the addressee was unknown to members of Radio Libertad, one of their Miami (Maimi) representatives passed the letter to a contact of JMWAVE Station. The addressee was Bernardo Adalberto MORALES-Rivero. Comment: See Cuban Foreign Relations 19-9-2, DBF 47612, 27 April 1960.

Director 04013, dated 8 December 1962.

Headquarters CIA provided White House Secret Service, FBI, and State Department Security with traces on Bernardo MORALES, above, who entered the U.S. in May 1962 on a fraudulent visa.

DBA 28980 (FBI), dated 12 December 1962.

Advised the Agency that the FBI had no information identified with MORALES-Rivero, intended recipient of the above letter, or Jose MENENDEZ, the sender.
