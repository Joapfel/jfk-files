To: Director, FBI (105-28333)  
Date: 10/6/58  
From: SAC, Philadelphia (134-756)  
Subject: Samuel Adason Jaffe  
PSI

Re NY letter to BU 9/22/58.

On 9/29/58, Miss [REDACTED], Mail and Files Clerk, Philadelphia District Office, INS, advised S. E. Davis Porter that there was no record in the files of that office of any Abram S. Jaffe, born in Russia about 1893, having received derivative citizenship through the naturalization of his father, who entered the United States about 1894 and was naturalized at Philadelphia.

On 9/29/58, [REDACTED] checked the naturalization records of the USDC, E.D. Pa., Philadelphia, for all persons named Jaffe who were naturalized during the period from 1890 through 1920. Twenty-nine petitions for naturalization by persons named Jaffe were reviewed, and none was found in which the petitioner listed any child named Abram, born about 1893.

There is no information in the files of the Philadelphia Office which is identifiable with Samuel Adason Jaffe or Abram S. Jaffe.
