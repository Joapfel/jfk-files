Vice-Intelligence Division, Montgomery County Police Department.

Continuing Investigations Reports.

Subject/Case Reference: Eugene Michael Carrafa.

08-24-76: Sharon Kohler, of USF & G Insurance Company, contacted the investigator and advised him that their investigators had contacted Steve Segal of Kruze Auctions in Ft. Wayne, Indiana, regarding Carrafa's (Caraffa's) alleged purchase of the three automobiles reported stolen by him from a Kruze auction. Steve Segal advised the USF & G (USP & G) investigators that they keep records on all automobiles handled by their agency; however, they do not have any records of the three automobiles in question ever being offered for sale by their agency or of them ever being purchased by Carrafa (Caraffa). In addition, Carrafa owes Kruze Auctions $1,822.00. Sharon Kohler is forwarding documentation to the investigator concerning the above. Cpl. R. W. Swain, Jr.
