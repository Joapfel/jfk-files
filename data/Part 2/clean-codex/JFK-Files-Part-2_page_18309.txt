Dear Mike:

Contained in an FBI internal memorandum dated 8/23/56 from Belmont to Boardman, captioned “CP, USA—Counterintelligence Program Internal Security-C,” supplied to this committee by the Bureau 7/25/75, is a reference on page 4 to a memorandum “covering the first phase of the IRS angle.” We request copies of this memorandum and any others relating to the use of the IRS in the disruptive program described in the memo cited above.

Delivery of the declassified version of these memoranda and the 8/28/56 memo cited above is requested by Thursday 9/25/75.

We also request that the attached transcript of J. Edgar Hoover testimony be excised for public disclosure along the agreed upon declassification procedure.
