Office of the Soviet Military Attaché — Closed-Circuit Television

Rebuttal, 10/25/66, reflecting installation authorization.

This letter is for information and record purposes.

In response to your request, a new closed-circuit television system was recently installed in connection with the captioned case. The system consists of a remotely controlled video camera, zoom lens, and pan-tilt head mounted in a special disguise. A 17-inch monitor is located nearby and provides a view of the pertinent scene. Monitor personnel can remotely change the focal length, focus, apertures, and field of view of the lens. Certain compensation for changing light conditions can also be made at the monitoring site.

Attached is a technical addendum listing the equipment installed together with special circuit and cable data to assist WFO personnel in the maintenance of the equipment. Appropriate inventory changes should be made.
