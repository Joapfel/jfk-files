Miniature Transmitters

Ref: MIM-160-70 of 21 September 1970

1. Referenced name reported on a miniature transmitter developed by Electro-Finders, Inc., a subsidiary of Mackenbut Protection Systems, Coral Gables, Florida.

2. Manuel J. Chavez has advised me that Electro-Finders, Inc., operations have been moved from California to Coral Gables and, in case we have any interest in the equipment, full particulars can be obtained through this office.
