Dr. IEG WAVE CITE MEXI 7234. KAPAK TYPIC.

1. Wallace B. Rowton has made contact with Carlos Jurado, artist husband of Miriam (Niriam) Chichay, clerk in Cuban Consulate. Rowton has become quite friendly with Jurado, has taken him and Chichay to dinner, and in turn Jurado escorted Rowton on tour of Rivera murals in Mexico (Mexi) City buildings.

2. Rowton, using alias, has explained self as in economic research business. Where first meeting developed from Rowton visit to salon where Jurado was showing his paintings, relationship has now swung around to place where Rowton, interested in general welfare of Jurado and wanting to do something for him, could pay him logically if Jurado can supply some service. Jurado is snapping (skapping) at this bait and appears genuinely to like Rowton, aside from the fact Rowton appears to be a non-American businessman with money.

3. In last meeting, Rowton queried Jurado on his contacts in Mexican commerce and Jurado said he had none. Rowton asked what sort contacts he had in Cuba, since that subject of interest to all (DT ALL), and Jurado fell over himself counting what he could do for Rowton on Cuban subject.

9 May 1985. 5 May 66. IN10462.
