For the representative of Holland to the United Nations.

This agent's file was transferred to the 1st Department of FCD, which was planning to work with him in the U.S.

On April 10, 1968 (N-178), Nosenko stated that this Italian agent may have been recruited on a homosexual basis and had gone to New York to work as a cook for either the Belgian or Dutch representative to the United Nations. Boris Ivanov, KGB resident in New York, was in Moscow about this time and was very interested in this cook. Nosenko believed that he might recognize the name of this agent.

Investigation disclosed that the individual referred to by Nosenko was most likely identical with Domenico Canala (S), Italian, born in 1920, who arrived in New York August 31, 1960, and thereafter served as an employee in the household of the Belgian Ambassador to the United Nations, Walter Loridan (S). Subsequently, in 1961, he began employment as a cook in the Argentine Embassy in Washington, D.C.

On August 1, 1968 (N-213), Nosekno (Nosenko) was shown the photographs and names of Pietro Cecchi and Domenico Canala (S), at which time he stated that he recognized Canala's name.
