United States Government Memorandum

To: Acting Director, FBI (100-468406)  
From: SAC, New York (100-174481)  
Date: 3/30/73  
(C)

Subject: Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC)—Miscellaneous—Information Concerning  
(OO: NY)

Re New York letter to the Bureau, 6/6/72; Bureau 0-7 to New York, 7/12/72; and Philadelphia letter to the Bureau, 2/28/73.

As outlined in referenced New York letter, the New York Office noted, as far back as June 1972, it recognized that, although several leaders of WONAAC were known to be SWP members, their activities, as pertaining to WONAAC, had been directed toward the legal repeal of all restrictive abortion laws, and investigation had disclosed no indication that WONAAC, as an organization, had engaged in any subversive or violence-prone activity.

Referenced Bureau 0-7, referencing the aforementioned communication, nevertheless instructed that the New York Office, as office of origin, had overall responsibility for captioned organization, and directed the New York Office to reopen its case and submit semi-annual reports.

A review of the results of the continuing investigation of WONAAC, together with all indices and references pertaining thereto, since June 1972, reflects no apparent change in the activities or the organization or its membership from that outlined above.

The New York Office is in accord with the view of the Philadelphia Division, as set forth in its referenced letter, that WONAAC represents basically a social change vehicle which, despite disparate political views of its leaders and membership, deals with the single issue of abortion rights.
