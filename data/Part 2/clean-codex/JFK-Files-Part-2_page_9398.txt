Detroit Black Panther Party.

We would like to take this opportunity to inform you of “Survival Day” in the Black and oppressed community. The date set aside for this event is May 20, 1972. Survival Day is designed to help eliminate some of the oppression in our community. One of the greatest concerns of poor and oppressed people is survival through the basic necessity of eating. During the Survival Day ceremonies, which will be sponsored by the Black Panther Party (Black Partner Party) and supported by the businessmen of the Black community, we have planned a free food giveaway. Understanding the need for food combined with the role of the businessmen in the community, we are asking the concerned businessmen to help support the community through your donations of goods necessary to furnish the people with a full bag of groceries.

We would like to notify you that on Friday, April 21, and Saturday, April 22, Comrades Gerald Crawford and William Elder, representatives of the Party, will come to your place of business to give you further information on the event.

Listed below are some of the items needed:

1. Chickens — 1,000  
2. Sugar — 1,000 lbs.  
3. Canned peas — 2,000  
4. Canned corn — 2,000  
5. Rice — 1,000 lbs.  
6. Fruit — apples, oranges, and bananas.  
7. Bread — 1,000
