External Asset Report—undated. Salvat is National Coordinator, clandestine operations. POA extended 8–16 July 1961 (sic). FRG Part II submitted 1 November 1961 (sic); OA pending. Salary: $275 per month. Subject has extensive training and should have highest priority for infiltration operations.

6 April 1960 cable. To: Director. From: Havana. Salvat’s first course in hiding in Havana; think he may be picked up by DIER in connection with his outspoken support of Confe Aguero. Ampelm/11 Franco is Elena Jalla, a local embassy employee.

3 September 1960 cable. To: Director. From: MASH. IN47347. Directorio Revolucionario Estudiantil (DRE) to be formed 5 September 1960. DRE Committee to travel to a Latin American country to make public announcement of formation of group. Propaganda Head of DRE is Manuel Salvat.

13 September 1960 cable. To: Director Jr. From: MASH. DRE to be affiliate, not member, AMRASP. Mueller and Salvat also members of AMRASP student group.
