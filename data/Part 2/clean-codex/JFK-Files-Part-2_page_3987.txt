Department of State. Telegram HCM869.

Berlin 02538, 22 October 1968, 1750Z. From US Mission Berlin to Department of State, Washington, D.C., priority 3311. Info: American Embassy Bonn, priority. Reference: State 258355.

Subject: U.S. prisoners in East Germany—Richard Case Nagell and Leon Burris James—file.

1. Ricey New called at Mission October 21 and outlined both cases in sense referenced in reftel.

2. Vogel invited Ricey New and Mission officer to call at his East Berlin office October 22. Stange not in action due to injuries suffered in automobile accident last week.

3. At conference in his office, Vogel stated Nagell’s release was scheduled by East German authorities for October 23 at 0930, to take place at Sandkrugbruecke crossing point. “The release will be unconditional as an expression of goodwill,” according to Vogel.

4. Vogel described Nagell’s condition as “not good at all, frequently quite bad, according to physician who will be with him when he is delivered by ambulance.” Vogel will deliver Nagell to Ricey New, who will be accompanied by Mission officer and chief psychiatrist, U.S. Army Hospital, Berlin.

5. When delivery is made, Department will be informed in brief flash telegram. No press statement will be issued by Mission.
