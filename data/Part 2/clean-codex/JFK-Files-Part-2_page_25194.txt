Persons/Entities: McNamara (McNamarra); Lodge; McCone; Richardson; Smith. Dates: 3–4 October 1963. Person/Entity: Diem.

Occurrence:

On October 3, Lodge wrote a personal letter to McCone stating that “I... agree with what I understand McNamara said to you. The time has come for a new face, but I also think of Richardson as a devoted and a patriotic American...” On October 4, 1963, in a CIA cable from Saigon (SAIG1397), the following report appeared: “...it is clear that Lodge wants a change in chief of station....departing Saigon 18–20 hours...”. In a comment in the IG report, it is noted that Richardson’s departure was based on a headquarters request that he return to headquarters on TDY as soon as possible. Upon Richardson’s departure, his deputy David R. Smith became acting chief of station.

Source: IG Report 1967 (CIA), page 26.

Staff. Date: Dawson.
