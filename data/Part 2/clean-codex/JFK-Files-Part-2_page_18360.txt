Personal Attention

SENSTUDY 75

Re Butels May 2, 1975, and September 4, 1975, to all offices and Butels September 3, 1975, to selected offices informing latter that Senate Select Committee (SSC) had requested identities of all supervisors and coordinators for Cuintelpros in selected offices for (1) New Left and Black Extremist, 1967 through 1971, and (2) White Hate, 1964 through 1971.

SSC also requested locations of persons named in field responses to referenced September 3, 1975, teletypes, and latest information in FBI headquarters files has been furnished to SSC. SSC staff may contact current and/or former employees named, to interview them concerning their knowledge of Cuintelpros in which they had supervisory or coordinating responsibilities.

Leroy W. Sheets, former employee, is to be contacted immediately and alerted that he might be approached by the SSC staff for interview. Mr. Sheets (MER SHEETS) may, after being contacted by SSC staff, contact Bureau's end page one.
