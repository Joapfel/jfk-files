Handwritten Notes from the Original “Notes on War” Series. This handwritten document includes notes from the original “Notes on War” series. The series was developed by a German military officer and is believed to have been used by German soldiers during World War I to communicate their thoughts and experiences.

I. Problem:

A. Depression from loss.

B. Fear and frustration in a given situation.

C. Theory:

A. Effect of functioning under pressure, as well as an overall impact of the somatic body.

D. Practice:

A. Commander.

B. Intelligence: 1. Internal. 2. External.

C. Political.

D. Medical.

2. Communications. See below.
