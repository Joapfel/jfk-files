2/26/69

Plaintext teletype, deferred.

To: Director  
From: SAC, Chicago

Racial tensions, Maywood, Illinois, 1969.

Captain Wilbert Samuels, Maywood, Illinois Police Department, reported unidentified person or persons splashed unidentified flammable liquid on the loading dock and nearby empty trailer of Madison Chemical Company, Maywood, early this a.m. and ignited same. Maywood Fire Department responded at 2:48 a.m., extinguishing fire. Truck and trailer charred. Damages estimated at one thousand dollars. No specific suspects. Investigation continuing. Maywood Police Department aware of previous reports over last year from Chicago source, reliable in past, to effect Fred Hampton and associates discussed possibility of torching Madison Chemical Company. Same source has identified Hampton as one of current leaders in Black Panther Party, Chicago. Military advised.

DME
