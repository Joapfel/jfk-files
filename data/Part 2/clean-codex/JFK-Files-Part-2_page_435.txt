Director, info JMWAVE.

TYPIC LIRING

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace B. Rowton on 26 September that LIRING-4 was now officially employed as secretary to LIRING-1. According to LIRING-3, LIRING-1 and LIRING-2 will go home PCS in December.

2. The new consul who will replace LIRING-1 is an employee who was assigned to the Embassy two years ago. LIRING-3 could not remember his name but will query LIRING-4 and obtain it.

3. LIRING-3 told Rowton he had been asked about cultural activities at the Embassy by Arnol (Arnold) Rodriguez Camps on his last trip to Havana.

LIRING-3 said he replied there was none. When he returned to Mexico City, LIRING-3 carried a letter to Julian Lopez, cultural attaché, and after Lopez read it he appeared cool toward LIRING-3. More recently, Lopez appeared to warm up again with LIRING-3 and had now asked LIRING-3 to direct Cuban art activities in Mexico, particularly plastic arts. LIRING-3 accepted. LIRING-3 tags Lopez as CUIS type.
