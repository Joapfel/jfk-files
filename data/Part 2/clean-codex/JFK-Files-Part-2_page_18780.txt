11/7/58

Plain Text

Airtel

To: Director, FBI (100-361868)

From: SAC, New York (65-15026)

Subject:

On 11/5/58, SKULL ADASON JAFFE (SKULL ADISON JAFFE), news reporter, CBS, telephonically contacted SA EDWARD P. GALLER and said that he possessed some information which he would like to furnish to the Bureau.

On 11/6/58, SA LAWRENCE J. DOXL (SA LAWRENCE J. DOYLE) and EDWARD P. GALLER interviewed JAFFE. He furnished information which is set forth in a letterhead memorandum, five copies of which are enclosed.

JAFFE advised that he has been trying to obtain permission to visit Red China as a journalist, but he has not received any encouragement from the Red Chinese authorities. He talked to the subject about getting some assistance from the Soviets. The subject told him that there was nothing which he could do, nor was there anyone at the Bureau.

Enclosure.

Bureau (100-361868); New York (13-911), SKULL ADASON JAFFE (SKULL ADISON JAFFE); New York (65-15026).
