At approximately 5:00 a.m. on June 4, 1969, Special Agents (SA) of the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), Chicago Office, surrounded the premises of Chicago BPP Headquarters, 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, Illinois. SA R. B. Yadon, Jr., telephonically informed a person answering the telephone at Chicago BPP Headquarters that a warrant was outstanding for the arrest of George Sams, Jr., and three other individuals. SA Yadon attempted to elicit the cooperation of those within BPP Headquarters, without success.

After approximately 30 minutes, the occupants of Chicago BPP Headquarters were ordered out onto the street and placed under arrest. Eight individuals from BPP Headquarters were arrested.

On June 4, 1969, Assistant United States Attorney Nicholas J. Etten, United States Attorney's Office, Chicago, Illinois, authorized the filing of a complaint charging the eight individuals arrested with harboring and concealing George Sams, Jr., in violation of Title 18, Section 1071, United States Code.
