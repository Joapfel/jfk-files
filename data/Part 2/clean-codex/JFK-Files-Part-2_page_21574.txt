Mr. Wallach. Going back just a step, I take it that you had no knowledge of any mail opening projects while you were with the Inspector General's Office in 1954?

Mr. Osborn. To the best of my recollection, I don't believe I was briefed on what was then HT LINGUAL, and which subsequently became SR POINTER.

Mr. Wallach. I think those just may have been different names for the same operation, different divisions.

Mr. Osborn. I think they were, yes, different code names. They changed them frequently.

Mr. Wallach. While you were in Berlin—you said you were Station Chief?

Mr. Osborn. Deputy Chief of Base.

Mr. Wallach. Did you have knowledge of the military's mail operation over there, mail intercept operation?

Mr. Osborn. I may have had, but I can't recall it specifically.

Mr. Wallach. Did you subsequently gain knowledge of that mail intercept operation?

Mr. Osborn. I have heard of it somewhere.

Mr. Wallach. What is the nature of the operation that you have heard of?

Mr. Osborn. That the military was intercepting mail.
