2. Nucleus: Such a base would be drawn from three to four thousand exiles who, according to Romani, have previously received military training from members of the U.S. Armed Forces. Romani claimed U.S. policy is to ferment internal resistance in Cuba and strengthen the resistance by infiltration of trained commando groups of exiles.

For this reason, the U.S. continues to give tacit support for training of exile military organizations.

3. Romani made the following review of the policies of principal exile parties:

(A) CULC is concentrating on dissemination of anti-Castro propaganda in Cuba to prepare the ground for insurrection. It favors infiltration by small military groups but rejects a general invasion.

(B) JURE (Junta Revolucionaria), led by Manuel Ray, considers the principal requirement to be securing the support of discontented elements in the Cuban militia and armed forces.

(C) Alliance of MRP (Movimiento Revolucionario Popular), Second Front of Escambray, and Alpha 66 supports continuation of hit-and-run raids on the Cuban mainland in order to maintain the morale of resistance forces in Cuba.
