SENSTUDY 75.

Re Bureau NITEL, May 28, 1975.

On June 4, 1975, Michael J. Rozamus, 650 Gulf Drive, Holmes Beach (Holmes Fach), Florida, was contacted. Mr. Rozamus had not been available for contact until this date because of travel. Mr. Rozamus advised that he has not been contacted by any representatives of the Senate Select Committee but he was alerted that he may be approached by a member of the committee staff. Mr. Rozamus was advised of the contents of REIUNITEL concerning the fact that he may request that an FBI agent be present during interview in the event questions are asked relating to sensitive Bureau operations. Mr. Rozamus was also advised that as a prelude (prelide) to the interview he may contact the Bureau's Legal Counsel Division by collect call. He was advised that the Bureau's offer of assistance was not intended.
