Cuban Intelligence Services 19-5-1

OOA 3/215,189, dated 27 March 1962.

Concerns Fidel Castro's intelligence service that operated out of the 13th floor of the INRA Building in Havana. It appears that its mission was to infiltrate counter-revolutionary groups operating in Cuba and the United States. Juan Orta was head of it before he defected to the United States.

OAA 3/215,192, dated 27 March 1962.

Concerns agents of Fidel Castro's intelligence service. Luis Tacornal made several trips to the United States on missions for Fidel. Tacornal told Juan Orta he was recruited by Rolando Masferrer to form a resistance unit in Havana. Orta told Tacornal to carry out a mission to see how many counterrevolutionaries he could get to join the resistance unit.

DBA 67028 (FBI), dated 24 March 1964.

Concerns interview of Harry Dean in California on 10 December 1963. Dean said he was connected with the July 26th Movement as early as 1958; visited Havana and saw Juan Orta personally in June 1960; and on returning to Chicago joined the FPCC (Fair Play for Cuba Committee) at the instigation of the Cuban Consul in Chicago.
