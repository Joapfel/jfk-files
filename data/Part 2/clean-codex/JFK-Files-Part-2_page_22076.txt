Persons/Entities: McCone, Helms, Bundy, R. Kennedy, Hoover. Dates: March 2, 1964. Categories: Special Group, Mongoose.

Occurrence: Helms states that agency officers have reported plans of Cuban exiles to assassinate selected Cuban government leaders. Agency officers told Cubans that the U.S. would not condone the planned actions. Plans included: Cuban exile approach to March 2, 1964, by a businessman to discuss a plan which was “still in the embryonic state to assassinate Fidel Castro.” The businessman has underworld connections with Cosa Nostra. The latter group has offered to assassinate Castro for $150,000. Proposes to use five men. March 3, 1964, meeting in which Byron Cameron says he met an unidentified group which would be willing to assassinate selected Cuban officials for cash. Specifically, the Cameron group is interested in assassinating Fidel Castro, Raul Castro, and Ernesto Guevara (Guevera). Cameron knows that he can be indicted for conspiracy.

Source: Memorandum for DCI McCone from Richard Helms re plans of Cuban exiles to assassinate selected Cuban government leaders, June 10, 1964 (03105-031110).

Staff: diGenova.
