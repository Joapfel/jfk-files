The following technical surveillances are being maintained at the specific request or with the specific approval of the Office of the Deputy Under Secretary of State:

Cuba: Delegation to Organization of American States, D.C., estimated monthly cost $1,674; Mission to United Nations, N.Y., estimated monthly cost $551. France: Chancery, D.C., estimated monthly cost $4,563. United Arab Republic: Chancery, D.C., estimated monthly cost $3,145; Delegation to United Nations, N.Y., estimated monthly cost $1,653. Total estimated monthly cost: $11,586.

During the past six months, technical coverage of four official Cuban establishments was discontinued when the establishments were closed by virtue of severance of diplomatic relations between the U.S. and Cuba. Coverage of the attaché offices in Washington of the United Arab Republic was also dropped because of nonproductivity.
