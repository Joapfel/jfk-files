IV. Changes, additions, and deletions of data appearing in Section A of reserve index cards.

(A) Changes in data appearing on Section A reserve index cards should be submitted to the Bureau by Form FD-122a.

(B) Deletions from Section A of the reserve index should be recommended by letter. The letter should set forth the basis for the recommendation.

(C) Changes in office of origin are to be handled by submission of Form FD-128a. Forward Section A cards to the new office of origin.

(B) Section B.

I. This section is to contain the names of all other individuals included in the reserve index. This section is to be maintained only in the field office. Cases falling within this category should be thoroughly reviewed and analyzed from an investigative viewpoint at least once every two years on a continuing basis. As a minimum, this review should include verification of residence and employment and contact with logical sources and informants. Be alert to selecting logical subjects for interview as a means of developing potential sources, informants, and witnesses. Also be alert for recommending individuals for inclusion in Section A of the reserve index or the security index if warranted. Form FD-154a may be used for this review.

II. Changes in office of origin are to be handled by submission of Form FD-128a. Forward Section B cards to the new office of origin.
