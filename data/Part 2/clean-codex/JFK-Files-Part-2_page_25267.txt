Persons/entities: Thich Tri Quang; McNamara; Diem. Dates: Spring, 1965. Categories: Diem.

Occurrence:

Higgins, in her book, quotes McNamara as stating in Spring of 1965 that: “Thich Tri Quang has made serious trouble for us before, and he will again. Perhaps the greatest mistake we made was when we gave Thich Tri Quang Embassy.” (from September through November 1963) Page 103 at the U. S.

Source: Our Vietnam Nightmare by Marguerite Higgins, Harper and Rowe.

Staff: Dawson.

Date: July 3, 1975.
