Addition: Intelligence Division VOC:ekv, 4/14/75.

On 4/11/75, the Executive Committee of the Ad Hoc (Ad lloc) Coordinating Group on Congressional Review for the Intelligence Community was apprised of the discussions with Mr. Robert Hough, the Deputy Sergeant at Arms of the Senate, and with Mr. Kenneth Harding, Sergeant at Arms of the House. Although the Executive Committee would have preferred the FBI to conduct audio sweeps, they interposed no objection to the sweeps being handled by the Capitol Police Department. Mr. Rogerick Hills, Counsel to the President, requested, however, that both Mr. Hough and Mr. Harding be requested to periodically advise the FBI as to the sweeps of Committee space for electronic listening devices. Specifically, Mr. Hough and Mr. Harding should be asked to furnish the FBI the following: How frequently they will conduct a counter-audio sweep, whether offices will be open, whether the results of such sweeps will be furnished to the FBI, and whether the FBI will be immediately notified should the Capitol Police locate an electronic listening device.
