Classified message.

IN 17407, page 2.

2. LICOOKY reports on 15 March tried to return the phone call which Estelle Sokolowska Madrigal made her in New York on 10 March. Left her phone number with S Hotel switchboard. Says S will surely call her and probably stay with her when she comes through Mexico (MEXI). Advise what action Mexico (MEXI) is to take.

CFN 6574 A, DIR 88211 B, Mexico (MEXI) 6419, Venezuela 4, HMMW-9800, June 61, February; SI Janet no longer Hotel Osegueda Arevalo Osegueda, June 61, headquarters Janet; 15 March, Estelle Sokolowska Madrigal, New York, 10 March, S Hotel, S.
