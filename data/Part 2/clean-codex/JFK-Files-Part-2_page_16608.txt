Sergeant Groth furnished the following identifying data concerning the seven victims in this case:

Brenda Harris, age 16, born August 16, 1951, 1848 South Hamlin, Chicago, Illinois.

Verlina A. Brewer, age 17, born January 11, 1952, 175 West 107th Street, Chicago, Illinois, or 2139 Yorktown Drive, Ann Arbor, Michigan.

Sergeant Groth advised that Brewer was a mental patient in Ann Arbor, Michigan, prior to her coming to Chicago and that her parents had reported her as a missing person.

Blair J. Anderson, age 16, born November 22, 1951, 6943 South Justine, Chicago, Illinois.

Sergeant Groth advised that Anderson had previously been arrested on September 10, 1969, in connection with a Black Panther Party “shoot-out.”

Ronald Satchmel, age 19, born June 27, 1950, 6046 South Clyde, Chicago, Illinois.

The following victims were taken to the Cook County Jail:

Deborah Johnson, age 19, born November 26, 1929, 6750 South Parnell, Chicago, Illinois.

Louis Trulock, age 39, born May 6, 1930, 1932 West Jackson, Chicago, Illinois.

Harold Bell, age 23, born November 29, 1916, 1317 Howard Court, Rockford, Illinois.

Two of the State Attorney’s police officers who were hurt are John Ciczewski, who was shot in the leg, and Edward Capreody, who had injuries to his hand as a result of flying glass.
