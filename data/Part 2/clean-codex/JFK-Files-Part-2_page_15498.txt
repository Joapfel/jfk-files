Department of State, Bureau of Intelligence and Research

November 25, 1964

Memorandum for: Mr. Bartlett, FBI

Subject: Investigation of Cambodian UN Mission

The Department of State has no objection to FBI undertaking a discreet investigation or survey of the Cambodian Mission to the UN and its employees. However, great caution should be exercised to see that the Cambodian representatives remain unaware of it. Our relations with Cambodia at this moment are extremely delicate, and we could not afford any operation involving any risk of disclosure.
