On January 11, 1968, (N-92), Nosenko (Hosenko) gave a written summary of the MORONE case, which was basically the same as his previous information but added that MORONE and a U.S. Marine in 1961 met in the city of Moscow with a female agent who worked at American House and her girlfriend. They went to a restaurant and about one or two o'clock in the morning returned to the American Embassy (or American House), where the girls remained until morning. He also related that MORONE was not approached by KGB primarily because STORSBERG was considered more important and KGB did not want to prejudice that case.

On April 6, 1968, (N-177), Nosenko (Hosenko) stated that he believed the Marine who went to the restaurant with MORONE was BEGGS. He further stated that SARWAT EL SHAZLY also reported something about MORONE, but he could not recall what it was.
