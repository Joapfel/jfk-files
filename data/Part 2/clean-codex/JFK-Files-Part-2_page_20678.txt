Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (NORAM) Convention

February 11–13, 1972, Boston, Massachusetts

That the coalition continue to organize black, Puerto Rican, Chicano, Native American, and Asian-American participation in their activities.

That members of local coalitions confront candidates for office and force them to take a stand on abortion, and that they publicize those who oppose abortion so that the voters can see where these politicians stand.

That the coalition make a special effort to reach out to and involve gay women in their activities.
