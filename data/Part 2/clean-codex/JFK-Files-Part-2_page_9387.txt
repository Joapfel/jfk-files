Free Breakfast for School Children

There will be a free breakfast for school children program, sponsored by the Black Panther Party, starting Monday, November 15, 1971, at 228 Bewick (downstairs), between Vernor and Kercheval.

Hot meals (such as grits, oatmeal, waffles, bacon, eggs, etc.) will be served to the youth in the community and those that attend Scripps Elementary School at 2100 Hurlbut (Hulburt). Breakfast will be served between 7:00 a.m. and 8:30 a.m., Monday through Friday.

Parents in the community are welcome to come to the breakfast program and can volunteer to give assistance in serving the children if they desire to do so, or they can inform their friends and neighbors of the program. If there are any questions concerning the program, please feel free to call the telephone numbers listed below.
