NR 009 CE CODED

5:33 PM NITEL, March 25, 1975, KCL

To Director

From Charlotte (100-00)

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Rebulet—March 24, 1975.

Charlotte Division has one supervisor assigned full time to security matters; no other supervisory personnel handle security.

There are 3 headquarters agents assigned full time to security matters and one additional headquarters agent assigned 75 percent to security investigations. One resident agent is assigned 75 percent to security matters, 4 additional resident agents are assigned 50 percent to security matters, and 9 additional resident agents are assigned from 5 to 15 percent of their work in security classifications.

No supervisory or agent personnel assigned to counterintelligence program, which was discontinued per Bureau instructions in 1971.

End
