FBI resol 105-126109. 3/31/64.

Subject reported by sources to have attended gatherings of FPCC in a private residence, Tampa, Fla., 11/20/63, and was expecting to get a phone call from Cuba in order to get word on his departure for Cuba.

Obtained ref. tourist card at Torino—many Consulate in Mexico in Tampa 11/20/63, entered ref. by private auto 11/23/63, and departed Mexico City en route to Havana 11/27/63.

U.S. State Department contains no record of subject applying for or being granted permission for travel to Cuba.

Further disc.: Promenade Rose, High Bridge.

Tampa FPCC met 11/20/63 at Torre P. Many students to show color studies taken in Cuba. Lopez identified by photo as having been present at the meeting.
