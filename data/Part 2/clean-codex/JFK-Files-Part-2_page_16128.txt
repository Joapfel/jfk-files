Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 2/25/70

1. Miss Joan Solebeck (Solebeck), Employment Supervisor, Cook County School of Nursing, 1900 West Polk Street, Chicago, advised that a search of their records revealed a personnel file for Annie White (Anne E. White), 2237 South Koeler, Chicago, Illinois.

Miss Solebeck (Solbeck) advised that Annie White (Annee White) entered on duty at the hospital as a clerical employee on November 29, 1951. She stated that the file further revealed that White (Whits) was terminated on June 2, 1956, and again rehired, exact date unknown. She said that White (Whits) again terminated her employment at County Hospital on December 29, 1959.

Miss Solebeck (Solbecx) said that while employed at Cook County Hospital, White was under the direct supervision of Miss Mabel Wilson, RN, and that her specific duties were those of a clerk assigned to the Trauma Unit. Miss Solebeck (Solbecx) said that the personnel file further revealed that White was born October 11, 1930, at Hartwell, Florida. Her Social Security number is 354-24-5846. Her telephone number is listed as 762-0129, which is the telephone number of her mother, Mrs. Annie B. Gunby (Annib B. Gunby), 2237 South Koeler (Koeier).

Miss Solebeck advised that from a review of the County payroll records, the following is to be noted regarding White's employment during the week of December 1, 1969:

December 3: 3:00 to 11:00 shift

December 5: Sick

Miss Solebeck (Soldeck) stated that White has recently filled out an application for reinstatement and that this application is presently being considered.
