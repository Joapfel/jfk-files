The source added that Al Evanoff was chairman of the morning session on March 8, 1970, and in that connection introduced David Faigen, who reported on the Community Workshop. Mr. Faigen stated that his group recommended a mass demonstration to be held in New Haven, Connecticut, on July 4 through 6, 1970. In addition, the following recommendations were made:

1. A national clearinghouse for audio-visual material for mass education.

2. A national newsletter.

3. A center for literature and information furnishing articles and reprints.

4. A speakers bureau.

5. A continuation committee from the Emergency Conference.
