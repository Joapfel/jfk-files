Source advised the Illinois Chapter of the BPP had announced a rally for October 25, 1969, at the Federal Building, Chicago. The purpose of the rally was to protest the “racist trial and miscarriage of justice” perpetrated on Bobby Seale by Federal Judge Julius Hoffman at the Federal Anti-Riot Conspiracy trial currently being held at the Federal Building.

October 21, 1969.

On October 25, 1969, Special Agents of the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) observed at 12:25 p.m. approximately 25 White persons and 35 Negroes gathered in the Federal Building plaza. These individuals had apparently gathered for the previously announced rally to protest the above-described trial.

Protestors were observed carrying signs reading “Get Out of Vietnam Now,” “Free Huey,” “Free Bobby,” “Stop Fascist Raids,” “Free Panthers Now,” “Youth against Fascism,” and “Racist Pig Daley.”

At 1:00 p.m., FBI agents observed a group of 35 Negroes carrying 10 BPP flags commence marching back and forth in the Federal Building plaza area. The group chanted, “Your left, your right, now back up the gun. Free Bobby.” At 1:05 p.m., this group commenced marching around the Federal Building and circled the building five times. About 1:20 p.m., speeches commenced on the southeast corner of the Federal Building plaza. Ronald Satchel, Minister of Health, Illinois BPP, spoke regarding the west side medical center project of the BPP. He said the project was to be “people controlled” and “community controlled.” He demanded that the political prisoners Huey Newton and Bobby Seale should be freed. He also blamed the problems of the poor Negro on the capitalistic system in this country. He concluded by saying that members of the BPP were not opportunists but revolutionists.
