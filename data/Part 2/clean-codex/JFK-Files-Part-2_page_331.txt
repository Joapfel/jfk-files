39285Z. CITE: Mexico City 9560.

Director info: JMAAVE, Guatemala City.

TYPIC LIRING.

Reference: A. Mexico City '95 12 (IN5386). B. Director 56923.

1. LIRING-2 saw Jacobo Rodriguez Padilla the night of 1 December 1966 and asked for contacts in the Guatemalan Communist Party. Jacobo gave him the name and phone number of Señora Hernandez (FNU), in Guatemala City, and said he would cable her that LIRING-3 would call. KROWTON will get the phone number. LIRING-3 proceeded by rail to the Mexican-Guatemalan border 2–3 December and called Hernandez from the Mexican side. She promised to put him in contact with an “important person from company” and asked him to call back the evening of 3 December or the next morning, since the company official was not easy to locate on short notice.

2. LIRING-3 phoned Hernandez the morning of 4 December and was informed that a meeting could not be arranged prior to 18 or 20 December, since the official was away on business. Hernandez said the meeting would be held in Quezaltenango, Guatemala, and instructed him to check in at any three hotels in that city, and they would locate him.
