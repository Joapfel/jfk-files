Black Man, listen to the voices of the people.

Our history in this country has been written in blood. Our blood. The most tragic scenes in that history are those of Black blood being killed by Black men. It is now time to change those scenes. It is time to regain that honor that was taken from our people by those individuals that have allowed themselves to be used as pawns and cannon batterers by the oppressors of the people.

Just as our Black men are used in Vietnam to kill the poor people of Vietnam for our own oppressors, who also employ people with weapons of war to patrol, brutalize, and kill Black people here in the United States, here in Detroit.

Just as the Black G.I. must salvage his honor, our honor, by refusing to become a part of the monstrous war machine abroad, you must refuse to become a part of the monstrous war machine here in the other country of war—the American Empire.

We have to pull away from that flunkeyism, especially when it's dealing with the well-being of our people and the honor of your own soul. We must not allow ourselves to fight for the private interests of a few individuals or a narrow clique, but for the interests of all our people. We must fight for freedom and sanity.
