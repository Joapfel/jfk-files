231655Z, Mexico City 8026. Director.

Reference: Mexico City 7994 (9/10/97).

I. Carlos Jurado Delmar told Wallace B. Rowton the afternoon of 22 July 1966 that he had discussed the matter of airmen prisoners in North Viet Nam with Miguel Lozano earlier that day. Jurado said Lozano remarked that in his operation airmen would not be executed and referred to the recent reply of the North Viet Nam president to the International Red Cross that prisoners were being treated humanely. Lozano added there was no such thing as execution of prisoners was foreseen.

S. Believe this is the best knowledge the Cubans at the Embassy (ENSASSY) here have of the situation and can only conclude the original statement by Adelardo Cursello was intended to spark an otherwise ordinary conversation and perhaps attempt to get a rise out of Ramiro Abreu and Jurado.

CS comment: Advised that Rowton (I) would meet Jurado and attempt to assess Cuban knowledge of NVN intentions regarding the airmen prisoners.
