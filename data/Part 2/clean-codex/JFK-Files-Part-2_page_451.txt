KAPOK PERUMEN LIRING.

References: A. Mexico City 8458 (IN 8/3/7). B. FARROR 33487.

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace S. Rowton on 19 September 1966 that, on passing through Havana from Santiago on his way home to Mexico City, LIRING-4 had visited Aurelio Alonzo, a mutual friend in the Ministry of Foreign Trade.

2. Alonzo (Alonso) told LIRING-4 that their friend Ramon Rossie had absconded with $300,000 of MINCEX funds, which had been entrusted to him to make purchases in Syria. This had led DSE, which at first believed Rossie defected for political reasons, to believe that his motives for flight were purely personal and for financial gain. LIRING-4 also contacted Rossie’s Venezuelan wife in Havana. The latter told LIRING-4 she had been offered a good job by the Cuban government but had turned it down because she desired to return to Venezuela with her two children.

3. According to LIRING-4, from Alonzo (Alonso), this refusal by the wife caused DSE to reassess the case, and it now inclined to believe Rossie was secretly in touch with his wife and would probably return to her once she was out of Cuba. LIRING-3 told Rowton that if the wife comes through Mexico, she will most probably contact LIRING-3 and LIRING-4.
