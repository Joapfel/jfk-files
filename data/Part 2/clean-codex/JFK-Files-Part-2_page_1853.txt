29 MAR 1966

Memorandum for: Mr. James S. Woods  
Subject: Quality Step Increase

1. I was pleased to learn that you have been granted a quality step increase. Such a recognition is proof of the high esteem in which you are held by your supervisors in the Clandestine Services.

2. Please accept my personal best wishes. I am confident that your future performance will be of a continuing high quality.
