Persons/entities: CONEIN; Gen. Duong Van Minh; Gen. Tran Van Don; Tran Thien Khiem; Tran Van Minh (cont'd). Date: Nov. 1, 1963. Category: DIEM.

Occurrence: Conein was contacted by Captains Eon, Alde de Caray to Conewal Eon, at 1818 hours, stating that Conein was wanted immediately at General Staff Headquarters. Since this was not within previous security arrangements for the next meeting, Conein questioned Eon, who said he was only carrying out instructions and stated that Conein (Consein) should come in uniform to JCS. He then departed. While getting into uniform, the dentist whose office had been used for meetings appeared at Conein's home with contradiction of the message that Conein (Consein) was to proceed immediately to JCS headquarters. No further statement was made about the matter in progress. Conein alerted JCS.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit one. Staff: Dawson. File: pages 8–9. Date: 6/30/75.

Occurrence: Baiyan via radio net using a pre-arranged voice code and proceeded to the Wiet Room at JCS headquarters. Present were Generals Duong Van Minh, Tran Van Don (Tran Van Ron), Phan Khanh Chien, Tran Thien Khiem (Tran Thaen Khaliem), Tran Van Minh, Nguyen Ngoc Le, Mai Dau Xuan, Le Van Khu, Tran Tu Dat, Nguyen Van La, Nguyen Giac Ngo, Trinh Nguyen Tam, Le Van Nguyen; Colonels Le Hau, Nguyen Kwong, Duong Nguyen Lam; Majors Le Nguyen Khaing, Do Khaue Hai, and unidentified junior officers.
