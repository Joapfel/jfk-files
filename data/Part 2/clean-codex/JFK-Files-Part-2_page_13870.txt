To: Intelligence Community Staff, Central Index. From: Federal Bureau of Investigation. Subject: Abstract of Information Provided to Select Committees. Date provided: 6/12/74.

To whom provided: SSC. Memorandum containing results of interview provided to Attorney General and to Mr. James A. Wilderotter, Associate Counsel to the President.

Identification: Miss Helen W. Gandy, former Executive Assistant to the Director of the FBI.

Classification of information: U. Key words: Management; Operating Procedures.

Summary: Miss Gandy was questioned by staff members of SSC concerning the organizational and operational procedures followed in former Director Hoover's office, as well as maintenance of and disposition of the files of the former Director marked “Official and Confidential.”
