4:00–6:00 p.m. Composing General Assembly workshop.

1:00–4:00 p.m. Roberts advised geographic and implementational proposals.

The source advised that the following women are members of SWP-YSA and were appointed to the presiding committee for the purpose of chairing various sessions: Carol Lipman, Nancy Rosenstock, Delphine Welch, and Matilde Zimmerman.

She will be composers represented, if any, in this program.

The source advised that a general workshop was being chaired by Matilde Zimmerman from 11:00 to 11:30 a.m., in which proposals were discussed that would be submitted to the conference for adoption by WONAC, and it consisted of a group made up of approximately 30 women, each meeting in various classes.
