V. Activities

Captain Wilbert Samuels, Maywood, Illinois, Police Department, advised on May 26, 1969, that a mock trial was held at Maywood City Hall on May 24 and 25, 1969, with the permission of Maywood Mayor Leonard A. Chabala. Approximately 140 individuals participated, with about 70 Negros, 55 whites, and 15 Mexicans noted. The purpose of the mock trial concerned the recent trial of Fred Hampton, who was convicted and sentenced from two to five years on a charge of robbery. The mock trial found Hampton innocent.

Source advised that a meeting of the BPP was held at Charles Knap's Restaurant, 5th Avenue, Maywood, Illinois, on May 28, 1969. Approximately 20 BPP members were present, along with the Latin Brown Berets, a youth gang. The purpose of this meeting was to raise money for bond regarding Fred Hampton's recent conviction.

On June 5, 1969, source advised that a meeting was held at 1423 South 11th Avenue, Maywood, Illinois. Members of the BPP discussed the recent raid on Panther Headquarters in Chicago by the FBI.

On June 6, 1969, source advised that the BPP was sponsoring a “Breakfast for Children Program” at several locations in Chicago, and the officers of the local Chicago chapter decided to continue this program as long as food continued.
