FBI

Date: 11/20/71.

Transmit the following in plaintext or code via airtel. Priority.

To: Director, FBI. Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division.

From: SAC, WFO (674-0).

Subject: UNSUB; threat to bomb United States Capitol, Washington, D.C., 11/20/71.

Re WFO airtel and LHM, 11/12/71, captioned “Woman's National Abortion Action Coalition (NONAC) Sponsored Demonstration, 11/20/71, Washington, D.C., IS-New Left” (WFO file 100-54927).

EID. Bomb threats.

Summary of Complaint:

Enclosed are original and 6 copies of LHM dated and captioned as above and original and one copy of FD-376.

LHM is being furnished to local agencies by WFO.

Telephone call received at WFO by SA James P. Wooten.

Information regarding receipt of bomb threat and results of search was telephonically furnished to Bureau.

At 12:20 p.m., 11/20/71, an unidentified individual, possibly a white female, called Communications Dispatch Service, LHM, and told Officer Horn: “Members of the B Tribe and the Woman's Liberation March, while posing as tourists going through the Capitol, are going to plant a bomb between 1500 hours and 1530 hours. This will be done during the day.”

Action: UACB. No further action being taken. LHM enclosed. Copy furnished to USA, Washington, D.C. FD-376 enclosed to LHM.
