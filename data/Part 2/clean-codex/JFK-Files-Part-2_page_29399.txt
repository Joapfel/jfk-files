There are some indications that the photographic coverage was more extensive than just office hours. Even though the Embassy was not open past 6:00 p.m., visitors could still gain entrance by ringing the gate bell. “Russian speakers can get in any time.” One CIA officer who was in Mexico City remembers “that the photo surveillance was constant except for instances where it would be down for security reasons or equipment malfunction.” A blind memorandum, dated 11/27/63, entitled “Memo passed to Mr. Papich of FBI with info on photo coverage of embassies and info on Kostikov,” says, in part:

We have photographic coverage during daylight hours on the USSR, Cuba, Czechoslovak and Polish Embassies. Their consulates are located in the embassies and therefore the coverage of the embassies would include coverage of the consulates. The photographic coverage is of a continuous nature during daylight hours. However, weather conditions and other factors affecting any photographic efforts require that the coverage not be considered as total or complete.
