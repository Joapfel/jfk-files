To: Director (Attn: DID). Boston (100-43809).  
From: Detroit (100-NEW) IP.

National Women's Abortion Action Coalition Convention at Boston, Massachusetts, February 12–13, 1972. IS-C (Trotskyist).

Bureau authority is requested to send DE 2278-PSI to captioned convention. A member of the Detroit Branch SWP (DBSWP), as source, has been specifically requested to attend by party leaders. Source will be traveling to Boston by POA in accompaniment of four other female members of the DBSWP. REC-52 100-468406-59.

No request for advance of funds being made at this time.

Source will maintain contact with the Boston office and will furnish pertinent information to Boston as indicated.

Upon return to Detroit, source will be thoroughly debriefed and FD-306 reflecting source's report of captioned will be submitted to Boston for inclusion in their LHM.

End.
