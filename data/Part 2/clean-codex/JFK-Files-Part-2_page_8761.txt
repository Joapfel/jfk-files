JMWAVE forwarded the 29 November 1962 letter from Jose MENENDEZ to Bernardo MORALES in Miami and suggested that Headquarters pass it to the Treasury Department for technical handling. JMWAVE was anxious to receive results of both letters on the grounds that they may be part of a planned G-2 PW operation in the U.S. or against U.S. targets.

Director 04538 dated 11 December 1962.

Headquarters CIA cabled Secret Service, FBI, and State Security for traces on Olga DUQUE de Heredia and Aida MAYO, Miami representatives of the organization Radio Libertad La Voz Anti-Comunista de America, the address to which the letter to MORALES was addressed. DUQUE and MAYO turned the letter over to a contact of CIA, since MORALES was reportedly unknown to them. DUQUE was married to Humberto LOPEZ Perez, operator of Radio Libertad, Caracas, Venezuela; Aida MAYO was a close friend of Fidel CASTRO and was believed to act occasionally on behalf of the Cuban Government.

Director 04501 dated 12 December 1962.

Headquarters advised JMWAVE Station that Headquarters, in disseminating information, did not reveal Cesar Augusto GAJATE Puig as intermediary between WAVE and DUQUE and MAYO. Suggested WAVE advise Secret Service of GAJATE's role since Secret Service.
