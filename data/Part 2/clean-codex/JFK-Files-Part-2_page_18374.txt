SENSTUDY 75

REBUNITEL, September 17, 1975.

Former SA Irving R. Anderson was contacted this a.m. and advised of information set forth in RETEL. SA Anderson's initial reaction was that if contacted by the Senate Select Committee staff for interview he would like to “tell them to go to H.” However, he felt that if he took this stance, he would hurt the Bureau inasmuch as the Bureau has pledged cooperation with the Senate Select Committee. He said he would not want to take any action, even though now a former employee, to hurt the Bureau in the future. He stated that he served the Bureau for many years, still feels he is part of the Bureau, and is dedicated to being useful to the Bureau in any manner whatsoever.
