2. Arriaga suggests various specific areas covered by the Mexican Army. He suggests Station Mexico City, through appropriate assets, may be able to determine Mexican Army strength and location of units in Guatemalan frontiers. Station Guatemala City will undertake to provide true information with respect to Guatemalan Army frontier strength.

3. As a result of conversations with Cols. Sosa and Arriaga, it appears that Guatemalan Armed Forces are moving forward on their own initiative, and neither officer has as yet made any specific request for WOFACI assistance, although as operations develop, WOFACI may be able to provide assistance, possibly in the form of transportation, communications, or technical aids. For the moment, Station will provide assistance which has been requested for Treasury Guard.
