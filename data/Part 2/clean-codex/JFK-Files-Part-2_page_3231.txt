Following receipt of this call from the Deputy Director, Esterline passed the word on to Dave Phillips with the comment that he thought perhaps such broadcasts might be used as filler during the intervals when Swan was not doing its regular broadcasts.* 16/

By early August 1960, however, the official policy for the Swan propaganda broadcasts aimed at Cuba was to be "the Revolution Betrayed." This theme was formalized by the Department of State; and at the direction of the Secretary of State, copies of a 105-page document addressed to this theme were presented to members of the National Security Council, to members of the Inter-American Peace Committee, and to U.S. Embassies in the Latin American countries.
