A BLACK PANTHER FREE BUSING PROGRAM TO PRISONERS

With the majority of this country's cars and other motor vehicles being manufactured in Detroit, many people in this state of Michigan still can't get to the prisons to visit their loved ones.

Realizing [end-less for felony formation], the Black Panther Party entered a free busing program for families and friends of prisoners. In many of our chapters and branches, such programs are underway, serving the community.

We (the Black Panther Party) would like to continue to serve you on a consecutive basis to the prison. On Sunday, June 4, 1972, the bus will be leaving for those who are visiting regular inmates, and on Sunday, June 18, 1972, the next trip is for those who are visiting trustees only.

The bus will be leaving at 9:00 a.m. and will return by 6:00 p.m. It is important that you arrive at 157 Collingwood at 8:00 a.m. for early seating arrangements.

Necessary items or information needed to visit inmates in Jackson 8113 (Jackson 8113) Penitentiary are:

(1) Inmate's name and I.D. number.

(2) Whether he is a trustee or not.

(3) Your name, address, zipcode, and phone number.

(4) You must be on inmate's visiting list. If not, write inmate or call 867-7742 or 43 to obtain methods of getting on inmate's visiting list.

(5) Your relationship to the inmate.

Make your reservation today, as seats are limited. For further information, contact:
