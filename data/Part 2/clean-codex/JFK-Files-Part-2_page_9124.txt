Phoenix, Arizona

October 22, 1971

Secret Army Organization.

On October 16 and 17, 1971, a coterie of five men met clandestinely in a motel in Yuma, Arizona, and oriented themselves with the plans and process of reorganizing the Minutemen under the same basic tenets but under the new name “Secret Army Organization.” PX T-1 advises that the five known individuals who attended this meeting used first names only and traveled to this meeting place from San Diego, California; Seattle, Washington; Las Vegas, Nevada; and Phoenix, Arizona. The five are identified briefly as follows:

Jerry Davis, also known as Sam Davis. Address: San Diego. Race: White. Sex: Male. Nationality: American. Height: 5'8". Weight: 150 pounds. Age: 33 (approx.).

(FNU) Barry. Address: San Diego. Race: White. Sex: Male. Nationality: American. Height: 5'10". Weight: 175 pounds. Hair: Brown. Miscellaneous: Wears glasses.
