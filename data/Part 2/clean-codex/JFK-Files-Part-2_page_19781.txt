Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAC) sponsored demonstration, November 20, 1971, Washington, D.C., and San Francisco, California.

On September 22, 1971, a source advised that Helen Meyers, Socialist Workers Party (SWP) organizer in Los Angeles, California, transferred to the SWP in Berkeley, California, effective August 31, 1971.

The SWP has been designated pursuant to Executive Order 10450.
