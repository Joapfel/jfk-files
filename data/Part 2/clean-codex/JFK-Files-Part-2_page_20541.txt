To Director, FBI (Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division)

WFO (100-54927)

From Indianapolis (100-23792)

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) sponsored demonstration, November 20, next, Washington, D.C.

IS—New Left. OO: WFO.

A confidential informant, who has provided reliable information in the past, advised November 18, instant, approximately fifteen individuals will be attending captioned demonstration from Indiana University, Bloomington, Indiana. Individuals will represent Bloomington Abortion Action Committee. Included in those traveling will be approximately five members of the Young Socialist Alliance from Indiana University who are supporting demonstration. Individuals.
