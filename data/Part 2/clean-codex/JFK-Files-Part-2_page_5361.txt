Records Control Schedule. Continuation Sheet.

Special Studies: Record copies of reports and evaluations following investigations and studies of various Agency programs, such as Career Service and CT Program. Filed by subject. Permanent. Transfer to the Agency Records Center at the end of each year or when no longer needed.

Working Files: Duplicates and working drafts of surveys and material related to investigation or survey. Each staff member maintains own files. Non-record. Temporary. Destroy 6 months after survey completed.

Classified Information Accounting and Control Records.

Top Secret Log: Temporary. Destroy 5 years after documents are downgraded, transferred out of the control point, or destroyed. GRS 18-6a.

Log maintained chronologically on Secret and below documents: Temporary. Destroy after two years. GRS 18-5.

Log used for recording receipt and disposition of official personnel files referred to staff for review: Temporary. Destroy quarterly after folders are returned to sender.

Document Receipts: Retain signed copies of material transmitted outside the office. Temporary. Destroy after 2 years. GRS 18-3.

Courier Receipts: Office copy of receipts signed by courier when material is picked up for delivery. OL is the Office of Record. Non-record. Temporary. Destroy when 3 months old.
