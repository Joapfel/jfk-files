Persons/Entities: Merchant, Gray, Irwin, Bissell. Date: December 9, 1959. Categories: Special Group; MONGODB.

Occurrence:

On December 9, 1959, the Special Group continued discussion of the clandestine broadcasting to Cuba. Mr. Bissell explained that it would not be possible to place the transmitter in the Canal Zone, and there was discussion of alternate sites including three U.S. islands in the general area, the Canal Zone, and B Quick's or soil.

It was recognized that any radio broadcasting from the site would undoubtedly be used by Trujillo to further his fight with Trujillo, but the group agreed that this was not necessarily a drawback.

Source: Minutes of Special Group Meeting, December 9, 1959.

Staff: Kelley. Date: June 27, 1975.
