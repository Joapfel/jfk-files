United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. J. B. Adams  
Date: 7-16-75  
From: Legal Counsel  
Subject: Status of Responses to Congressional Committee Requests

The purpose of this memorandum is to set forth in summary form the requests we have received from various Committees in Congress and the status of our replies.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (Church's Committee)

Letter, 5-14-75. Nature of request: Includes among other things information concerning wire-taps, including their reconstruction of Bureau files, as well as Inspection surveys for ten offices. Status: Responses to most questions prepared, approved, and delivered.

Letter, 5-28-75. Nature of request: Requests certain Senators on Committee be given access to any information concerning them in FBI files. Status: Being handled under same guidelines as Freedom of Information Act requests. Letter to Committee from Department requesting discussion be held.

Letter, 6-13-75. Nature of request: Requests complete information concerning mail openings and mail covers. Status: Material being gathered for response.

Letter, 6-27-75. REC-102. Nature of request: Request for information concerning alleged abuses.

Distribution: Mr. Wannall, attention Mr. Cregar; Mrs. Metcalf; Mr. Mintz; Mr. Hotis; Mr. Daly.
