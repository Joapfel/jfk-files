5 March 1977. Cable IV. 207098.

To: Director. Cite: Caracas 16–17.

Response to a 4 March news article in “El Mundo,” Caracas, naming CIA as Orlando Bosch, Ricardo Morales Navarrete, Orlando Garcia Vasquez, Luis Posada Carriles, etc. Cable states, “Fortunately, none are currently active assets.”

8 April 1977. Memo. Chief, Security Analysis Group. From: John F. Sullivan Jr. and Michael W. Reneck. Subject: “El Mundo” exposure; Office of Security indices check on names, some of which are outlined above.

“In those cases above where a security file exists, there is no evidence that any of the individuals are current, active assets.”
