Agent Report.

Name of subject or title of incident: Fromer, David Reed. SSvc registrant, SSN: 564 58 8137. Date of birth: 21 July 1942. Place of birth: Chicago, Illinois.

Date submitted: 9 December 1966.

Report of findings.

(Local agency—subject) On 8 December 1966, files of Casual Source I-U-10 were examined regarding the subject and revealed the following:

On 28 December 1959, it was reported that the subject, who was residing at 1932 Cabrillo, San Francisco, California, was the son of Irving Rhodes and Katherine Fromer. It was subsequently reported that the subject was residing at 2452 Bancroft, Berkeley, California.

On 12 April 1961, it was reported that the subject was on the mailing list of SLATE, a student rights organization at the University of California, Berkeley (UCB).

In 1960 and 1962, the subject was a freshman at UCB and resided at 2462 Bancroft, Berkeley.

On 18 June 1963, it was reported that he attended the Arnautoff Bon Voyage Party, which was under the auspices of the American Russian Institute, San Francisco, on 26 May 1963.

On 26 June 1963, it was reported that the subject attended a meeting under the auspices of the Continuations Committee of World Congress and General Disarmament & Peace, San Francisco, on 7 May 1963.

In an article in the San Francisco Chronicle, a local newspaper, on 22 September 1966, it was reported that the subject, residing at 13 Rhonda Way, Mill Valley, California, had won a federal court stay in his battle against the draft board. He was attending the University of Wisconsin and is a conscientious objector.

(Local agency—subject) On 8 December 1966, files of Casual Source San Francisco A-14 were examined regarding the subject and revealed no record.
