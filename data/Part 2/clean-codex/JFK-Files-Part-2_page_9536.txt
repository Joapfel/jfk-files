The Press Release

Participants: Ben W. Fondron; Erone Desaussure; David M. Johnson; Shanti Joneson; Cassandra Parker; Jerome Lee; William Cunningham; Victor Grayson; Mrs. Beverly Fleming; Kim Gardner; Carol E. Smith; Sylvia Robinson; Diana Brown; Linda Warnsley; Pat Duncan.

On the night of October 24, 1970, at 5:30 p.m., the reactionary Gestapo forces, under the command of Commissioner John F. Nichols, staged an attack on the H.C.C.F. office located at 16th and Myrtle.

In the course of the attack, the office was bombarded with a barrage of bullets and tear gas bombs, resulting in the arrest of 15 members on trumped-up charges of conspiracy to commit murder and murder. Their ransom (so-called bonds) ranged from $100 to $25,000.

We see this overt activity as another attempt to destroy the Black Panther Party and its efforts in combating fascism here in this oppressive society and the world over.
