Interrogated Nosenko (INTERPRODATED NOSENKO) during 26 July–13 August 1965.

27 November 1963

Memo for: Chief, SR/CI

Subject: Peter Deryabin comments on President Kennedy’s assassination.

Benefits to Khrushchev (KRUSCHEV) of JFK’s (JK’s) death:

1. Less Western pressure on Khrushchev (KRUSCHEV); conciliatory U.S. attitude.

2. Diversion for internal Soviet problems (problems of 1963 harvest, Chicom arguments).

3. Because of Castro allegations, Johnson would restrain from any planned intervention in Cuba.

4. Khrushchev’s (KRUSCHEV’s) hand would be strengthened in dealing with Chicoms.

5. Any plans by JFK to get tough with Soviets during election year would not be sabotaged.

6. De Gaulle would emerge as strong Western leader; he was supportive of Soviets.

7. Rising intellectual dissension (DISSENGION) in USSR would be thwarted (THIARTED).

8. The assassination (AGASSINATION) would fit right and secret.
