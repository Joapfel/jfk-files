7:48 p.m., Nitel, 31 March 1971. From Washington Field to Director, attention Domestic Intelligence Division. New York (100-172435). Alexandria. From Washington Field (100-53558).

Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971; internal security information concerning.

On March 31, 1971, a representative of the United States Park Police, Washington, D.C. (WDC), made available a copy of an application for a proposed demonstration in park area(s) under administration by National Capital Parks, National Park Service, United States Department of the Interior, dated March 24, 1971. The application was executed by one Abigail X. Kaplan, 4009 East-West Highway, Chevy Chase, Maryland, which stipulated the sponsoring.
