CIA Specifications.

4 May 1992.

Tabulation of pages in the Oswald pre-assassination file:

1 page, 6 Mar 64: Note-slip on classified version held in NARA.
4 pages, 30 Jan 76: CIA transmittal sheet, with NARA's query of 15 Jan 75 on deleting #s.
1 page, 6 Mar 64: DD/P transmittal sheet, regarding NARA's holding.
4 pages, 6 Mar 64: Helms memo to Rankin, describing file contents.
5 pages, 6 Mar 64: Copy of above, with Helms note to Rankin.
1 page, 31 Oct 59: State cable from Moscow to Secretary of State.
1 page, same date: Redacted copy of same.
1 page, 1 Nov 59: Press clipping.
1 page, 2 & 4 Nov: Notes on Oswald and Papich (FBI) query.
3 pages, 2 Nov 59: Foreign Service dispatch from Moscow to Department.
1 page, 9 Nov 59: State cable, Moscow to Secretary of State.
1 page, same date: Redacted copy of same.
1 page, 9 Nov 59: State cable, Tokyo to Secretary of State.
1 page, same date: Redacted copy of same.
1 page, 16 Nov 59: Press clipping.
1 page, 26 Nov 59: Press clipping.
1 page, 25 May 60: Cover memo from Director Hoover, FBI, to Helms.
7 pages, 12 May 60: Attachment to above.
2 pages, 12 May 60: FBI report from Dallas.
2 pages, 25 Oct 60: State (Cumming) to Helms, listing U.S. defectors.
2 pages, same date: Redacted copy of same.
1 page, 3 Nov 60: DD/P Bissell to State (Cumming).
1 page, same date: Redacted copy of same.
1 page, 18 Nov 60: Internal cover memo to DD/P, to accompany draft reply to Cumming.
1 page, undated: Handwritten description of letter cited above.
2 pages, 21 Nov 60: Cover letter, Bissell to Cumming, on U.S. defectors.
1 page, same date: Declassified version of Oswald information from file.
7 pages, same date: List of U.S. defectors attached to Bissell letter.
1 page, 18 Nov 60: Memo from Horton, acting C/CIS, to Bissell.
9 pages, 21 Nov 60: Second copy of Bissell letter and list, above.
2 pages, 9 Dec 60: Two copies of redacted request to set up Oswald 201.
3 pages, 11 Jul 61: Foreign Service dispatch, Moscow to Department.
4 pages, 26 May 61: Two copies of Foreign Service dispatch, Moscow to Department.
1 page, 13 Apr 61: Department of State instruction on Oswald citizenship and passport, signed Rusk.
1 page, 26 Jan 61: State memorandum of conversation regarding Oswald.
1 page, same date: Redacted copy of same.
14 pages, 13 Jul 61: Cover letter to Helms from Hoover, plus several FBI reports, much of which is illegible.
1 page, 28 Sep 61: Short biography of Marina, redacted, for inclusion in Oswald 201.
4 pages, 13 Oct 61: Foreign Service dispatch, Moscow to Department, covering copies of four Oswald letters to Embassy Moscow.
1 page, 7 Dec 61: Form from INS to DD/P, asking for any derogatory information on Oswald.

Not a part. Added later. Deleted.
