At approximately 4:33 a.m. on August 22, 1971, two white males and three white females standing against the wall located in Cleove Driveway, east side of St. Paul's Episcopal Church, located across from the U.S. Post Office building at Fourth and Market Streets, Garden, New Jersey. One white male, later identified as Fruitly J. HSC, was observed holding a walkie-talkie, which was immediately taken from him by SA THIXTER. A pair of cheap binoculars were found on the tape, and a brown tote bag was located near the feet of these four individuals. SA THIXTER identified himself and the other agents present as Special Agents of the FBI and advised these four individuals that they were being placed under arrest for conspiracy. The three white females were subsequently identified as MARGARET MARY INNESS, ANNE CRANEY DUVILLY, and JOHN REILLY.

On 8/22/71, Camden, New Jersey. File No. Philadelphia 52-7165-SUB S. SA HOWARD G. THIXTER, SA RICHARD G. HARRISON, SA ROGER B. FARCOCK.
