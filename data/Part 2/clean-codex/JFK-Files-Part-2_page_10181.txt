March 9, 1976.

Mail Room. Teletype Unit.

Note: SAC, SF advised by teletype 2/23/76 that a Robert Lee Lewis, alleged investigator of the Church Committee, had attempted to get someone to testify before the committee concerning the mishandling of the Hearst case. Inquiry has determined that Lewis is not employed by the Church Committee. This instructs LA to conduct appropriate impersonation investigation.
