July 26, 1971.

NRCDI PH code. 0125 AM. Urgent. 7-26-71. JEW.

To: Director.  
From: Philadelphia (52-7165).

MEDBURG (Camden action).

Informant has been attending nightly meetings regarding Camden Action with John Peter Grady, the prime MEDBURG suspect. Source debriefed at length on a daily basis and advised as follows:

On July 20, source advised in detail as to present plans utilized in forthcoming Camden “rip off” of draft board, Camden Federal Building. Source furnished positions of lookouts, outriders, and cutoff cars, as well as established procedures. Source furnished a partial list of personnel to be utilized by Grady.

During the period July 20–24, Grady and the group conducted nightly surveillance of Camden Federal Building for the purpose of obtaining additional information regarding police patrol patterns, night watchmen’s rounds, and general vehicular and pedestrian traffic in the vicinity of Camden Federal Building.

Enclosure: 2387.
