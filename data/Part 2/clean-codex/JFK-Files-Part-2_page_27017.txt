III. Miscellaneous “histories” which were not requested but which, considering the Senate Select Committee’s Charter and stated areas of interest, are in the nature of “glaring omissions.”

1. Staff relations with the Department of State and with Ambassadors, 1957–1967.

2. War Planning in the Clandestine Services, 1948–1965.

3. The Evolution of Ground Paramilitary Activities at the Staff Level, October 1949–September 1955, and a supplemental history covering 1955–1971, three volumes.

4. Development of Paramilitary Contingency Cadres, 1948–1971, two volumes.

5. Operations in Mozambique, 1942–1969.

6. The Secret War in Korea, June 1950–June 1952.

7. CIA and Korea, 1946–1965.

8. The Psychological Services Staff of the Office of Medical Services, 1951–1966.

9. Military Secrets in an Open Society: The Yale Report.

10. History of the Defector Program, April 1946–December 1966.

11. History of Operation LINCOLN, 13 February 1959–October 1964, apparently involving the Cubans.

12. Record of Paramilitary Action Against the Castro Government of Cuba, 17 March 1960–May 1961.
