Name: Giovanni Roselli

Aliases: John Roselli, John Russell, John Kasselle, John Passelli.

Description: Born 7-4-04, Chicago, Illinois; 5'8", 171 lbs.; brown-grey hair, blue eyes.

Localities: Resides at 1251 No. Crescent Hts., Hollywood, California. Frequents gambling casinos at Las Vegas, where he has a room at the Tropicana Hotel. Travels frequently throughout the United States.

Family background: Divorced from movie actress June Lang; father: Vincenzo; mother: Maria Russo, both deceased; cousin: Joseph Evangelista.

Criminal associates: Dragna family, Philip Kastel, Harold Meltzer, Francisco Costiglia, Charles Fischetti, Louis Piscopo.

Criminal history: FBI No. 3339986. L.A. Sheriff's Office No. 51247. Record dating from 1926 includes arrests for suspicion of robbery, vagrancy, interfering with trade, mail fraud, and violation of parole.

Business: Vice-president, Monte Prosser Productions, and varied other interests.

Modus operandi: A top Mafia figure in Los Angeles and Las Vegas. Active in labor racketeering and control of gambling.
