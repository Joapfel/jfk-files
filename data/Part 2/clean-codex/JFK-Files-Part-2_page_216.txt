Message Form. Origin: C. Bustos-Videla. Unit: 17/1. Extension: 4506. Date: 22 March 1966. No index. File in CS file no. 201-278841.

Routing. To: MEXI. From: Director. Info: VR, file. RD copy. MEXI. LICOOKY. Ref MEXI 6574 (IN 77407).

1. Headquarters has located what appears to be AREVALO and OSEGUEDA reports prepared by LICOOKY-1 (L-1) in 1961. Pouching copies HMMW-14432. For your information, “JANET” resigned early 1963.

2. ODENNY reports SOKOLOWSKA under surveillance in Miami now and, according to current information, probably will return to New York for a meeting with her case officer prior to traveling to MEXI and onward to HABANA. ODENNY will keep KUBARK advised of her travel plans. Possibility ODACID will lift her passport.

3. If SOKOLOWSKA sees L-1 in MEXI, only requirement is for L-1 to elicit any information she can regarding SOKOLOWSKA’s activity in Miami, particularly regarding fulfillment of her case officer’s instructions and her plans for HABANA. In case SOKOLOWSKA exits Cuba via MEXI, L-1 should elicit what she can and obtain travel plans.

4. ODENNY wishes to interview LICOOKY on her next trip to the States. At time of next trip, please arrange contact instructions we can pass to ODENNY.

End of message.

WH comment: Request Headquarters look again for LICOOKY reports on AREVALO and OSEGUEDA. Request requirements for LICOOKY against SOKOLOWSKA.

W/C/Mr. John A. Castano (ID draft)

William V. Broe, C/WHD.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
