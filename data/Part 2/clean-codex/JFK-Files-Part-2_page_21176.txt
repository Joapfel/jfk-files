Comment:

The fact that KEYZERS was trained for a period of time by STORSBERG in the military code room could well suggest to the KGB that he was to be the replacement for STORSBERG. The U.S. Army intelligence file was reviewed by FBI representatives on July 10, 1968, and contained no indication that KEYZERS's homosexual activities were discussed with him before he left Moscow on June 16, 1961. On the contrary, a memorandum prepared by his superior, Colonel Peter L. Urban, the U.S. Army attaché, dated June 16, 1961, which summarized KEYZERS's activities, mentioned derogatory comments that had been received about his relations with members of the American Ice Capades who were known or suspected homosexuals and noted that KEYZERS had been informed of the reasons for his transfer from Moscow, “except that he was not informed of the allegations of homosexuality in order not to aggravate the possibility of a rash act by him while within the Soviet Union.”
