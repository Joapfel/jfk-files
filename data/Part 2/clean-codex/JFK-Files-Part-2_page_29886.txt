Subject/Case Reference.

A check of the Maryland State Medical Examiner's Office in Baltimore regarding the 29.0 mg% ethanol in Piccerelli's liver reveals that this is the normal amount of ethanol produced by a decomposing liver; if translated to a blood-alcohol percentage, it would only be .03%.

In essence, Janice Mae Piccerelli did not die of an overdose of alcohol and/or tranquilizers.
