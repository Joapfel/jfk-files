Report of: SA Donald V. Ritenour, Jr. Office: Detroit, Michigan. Date: 5/18/71. Field Office File No.: 157-3075. Bureau File No.: 105-165706-15.

Title: Black Panther Party (BPP), National Committee to Combat Fascism (NCCF), Detroit Division.

Character: Racial matters; Smith Act of 1940; seditious conspiracy, rebellion and insurrection.

Synopsis: The Detroit NCCF continues to operate under the direction of BPP National Headquarters, Oakland, California. Detroit continues to sell “The Black Panther” newspaper and operate a Free Breakfast for School Children Program. Edgar Robinson and Kenneth Horston, both Detroit NCCF members, visited BPP National Headquarters in 4/71. Four NCCF members were arrested by the Detroit Police Department for Murder I as a result of a holdup in which a man was killed. A purged Detroit NCCF member has been contacting the Cleaver Faction of the BPP in New York; however, no attempts have been made to start a Cleaver group in Detroit.
