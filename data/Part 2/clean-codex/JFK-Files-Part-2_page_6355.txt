Subject: Comments regarding the JFK Assassination Records Collection Act of 1992

1. In general, there is not much in the regulation to cause the DA undue concern except for Sec. 1400.2, Interpretation of additional records and information; specifically (e), (1), (2), and (3). The schedules are classified in the aggregate, filing systems and organization structures are classified in toto, and the storage facility is an Agency Records Center at a classified location. I know this may not be a lot of help, but I have little or no argument or comment concerning the other sections.
