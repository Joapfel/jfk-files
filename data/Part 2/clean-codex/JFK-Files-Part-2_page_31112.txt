Nov. 4, 1960. Cable to Director Jr.: MASH.

MASH planning to infiltrate first DRE team in Havana Province by 10 Nov. 1960 in order to be present when student general strike occurs on 14 Nov. Long-range objective of team is to organize anti-Castro student harassment force within student circle. Selva and Mueller on this team.

POA granted 3 Nov. 1960 for Selva.

May 9, 1961. Cable to Bell Jr.: JMWAVE.

Selva arrested in Cuba about 18 April. His true identity did not become known to authorities, and following interrogation and investigation he was released.

PRG Part II, 1 Nov. 1961.

Case Affair—Ross Crozier (Noemayr).

Selva has met Oliver H. Corbston on one or two occasions and knew him as Douglas Guzman. Selva's PM duties will be limited, but involve some sabotage and demolition, as well as distribution and organization of clandestine guerrilla groups within Cuba.

When DRE leader Alberto Mullar was in Cuba Mastra with 400 guerrillas, C/4 failed to support effort with material air drops.
