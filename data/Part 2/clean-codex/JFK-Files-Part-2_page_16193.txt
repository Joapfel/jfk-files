Peter Dedich, Special Agent, Alcohol, Tobacco, and Firearms, 36 East Wacker Drive, Chicago, Illinois, advised that their investigation concerning the firearms recovered by the States Attorney's Office at 2337 West Monroe Street reflected that their department succeeded in tracing only four of the 19 guns recovered. The four traceable firearms are as follows:

1. .32-caliber Smith & Wesson (S and W) Model 30-1, serial number 730073, was sold on October 29, 1963, to Brown Supply Company, 416 South Adams Street, Peoria, Illinois. On September 19, 1964, Brown Supply Company sold the firearm to H. Mayo, 501 Hamilton Boulevard, Peoria, Illinois. Mayo owns and operates a restaurant and is described as a white male with blond hair and blue eyes, 5 feet 9 inches tall, weighing 150 pounds.

2. Colt .45-caliber automatic pistol, Model 1927, serial number 7111. This weapon was sold by Casanova's (Casavanova's), Milwaukee, Wisconsin, to M and M Sporting Goods Store, 3823 North Richards, Milwaukee, Wisconsin. The firearm was sold to M and M Sporting Goods on June 9, 1969. The store's books and records report the firearm in their inventory but have no record of its sale, and it is their belief that the gun has been stolen.

3. .30-caliber M1 carbine, serial number 16573, was purchased on August 21, 1969, by Darkin M. Gentry, head of the Black Panther Party, Milwaukee, Wisconsin.
