Departments and Agencies Included in the Operations of Executive Order 10450.

Executive Office of the President:

White House Office; Bureau of the Budget; Council of Economic Advisers; Executive Mansion and Grounds; National Security Council; National Security Resources Board; Office of Defense Mobilization; Office of the Director for Mutual Security; President's Advisory Committee on Government Organization.

Executive Departments:

State; Treasury; Department of Defense, including the Office of the Secretary of Defense, Department of the Army, Department of the Navy, and Department of the Air Force; Justice; Post Office; Interior; Agriculture; Commerce; Labor; Health, Education and Welfare.

Independent Agencies:

American Battle Monuments Commission (American Battle Comments Commission); Atomic Energy Commission; Board of Governors, Federal Reserve System; Canal Zone Government; Central Intelligence Agency; Civil Aeronautics Board; Civil Service Commission, including the Federal Personnel Council; Committee on Government Contract Compliance; Committee on Retirement Policy for Federal Personnel.
