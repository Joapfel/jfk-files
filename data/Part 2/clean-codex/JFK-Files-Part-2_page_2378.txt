Medical Clearance (Civilian)

11 February 1947

Certificate No. 1

This is to certify that Birch D. O'Neal was given a physical examination on this date and found fit for service outside the United States.

John R. Tietjen, 1st Lt. MC

16 March 1947

Certificate No. 2

This is to certify that Birch D. O'Neal was given a physical inspection on this date and found free of any communicable diseases.

John R. Tietjen, 1st Lt. MC

Explanatory Note: Certificate No. 1 is issued after a complete physical examination has been made to determine the individual's physical fitness for foreign service. Certificate No. 2 is issued after a physical inspection. It should be done within 48 hours prior to departure.
