United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. Tolson  
From: W. C. Sullivan  
Subject: Burglary of Media, Pennsylvania, Resident Agency (Camden Action)  
Date: August 21, 1971

SAC Roy K. Moore, in charge of the captioned investigation, indicated today that information received from an informant indicated a strong possibility that an attempt will be made by John Peter Grady and his group to break into the Federal Building at Camden, New Jersey, and thereafter destroy and carry off records of the draft board and military intelligence.

The Department has indicated that should the break-in be consummated, those involved should be arrested. In the event Grady and his group are on the scene with apparent intent to break in but stop short of such action, the Department has indicated they should be taken into custody.

SAC Moore advised that Philadelphia and Newark Agents are being deployed in a position to take whatever action is appropriate.

Action: Information.
