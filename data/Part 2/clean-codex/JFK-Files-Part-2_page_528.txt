2722182. Cite Mexico City 8551.

Director info: JMWAVE.

29 September 1966.

TYPIC: LIRING-3.

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace by Rowton on 26 September that LIRING-4 was now officially employed as secretary to LIRING-1. According to LIRING-3, LIRING-1 and LIRING-2 would go home PCS in December.

2. The new consul who would replace LIRING-1 was an employee who had been assigned to the embassy two years ago. LIRING-3 could not remember his name but would query LIRING-4 and obtain it.

3. LIRING-3 told Rowton he had been asked about cultural activities at the embassy by Arnol Rodriguez Camps on his last trip to Havana (HAVNA). LIRING-3 said he replied there were none. When he returned to Mexico City, LIRING-3 carried a letter to Julian Lopez, cultural attaché, and after Lopez read it, he appeared cool toward LIRING-3. More recently, Lopez appeared to warm up again with LIRING-3 and had now asked LIRING-3 to direct Cuban art activities in Mexico, particularly plastic arts. LIRING-3 accepted. LIRING-3 tags Lopez as CUIS type.

27 September 1966.
