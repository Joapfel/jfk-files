April 30, 1968. Pineda Gonzalez. Pineda came over on boat with DI. AD visited Pineda in June 1968 and asked Pineda to receive three 57mm recoilless rifles in February somewhere outside Miami and deliver them to unspecified SFN people in Miami. They would be used to attack Sonet Slings. AR dislodged [illegible] U.S. policy from Pineda to organize demonstrations and armed attacks to embroil the U.S. and create [illegible]. AT convinced U.S. [was] protecting Castle. AR would fund Pineda at $300 to $5,000 per month. Pineda to call Será when organized. She would provide the money.
