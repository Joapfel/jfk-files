September 18, 1975

U. S. Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSG)

Reference is made to the letter from the Chairman of the SSC to the Attorney General dated August 28, 1975, requesting delivery of previously-requested FBI materials pertaining to Martin Luther King, Jr. Reference is also made to prior FBI communications which have, on a continuing basis, effected delivery to the SSC of materials concerning King and responsive to SSC requests dated July 8 and 14, 1975.

The purpose of this memorandum is to effect delivery to the SSC of information and materials relating to the July 8 and 14, 1975, SSC requests, and specifically to the following items in those requests:

July 8, 1975: Items 2, 6, 8, 15, and 20.

July 14, 1975: Items 19 and 20.

In respect to Item 2, a through f, of the July 8, 1975, request, effort has been made to divide the material being delivered into specific categories a through f. However, in some instances material overlaps and information in one communication may actually apply to more than one category.

In response to your request, we are delivering to the SSC a communication dated April 10, 1967, which relates to the 1967 revision of an FBI monograph concerning Martin Luther King, Jr. FBI records indicate this revision was authored by Special Agents Robert L. Shackelford and Dwight M. Wells.

This document is prepared in response to your request and is not for dissemination outside your Committee. Its use is limited to official proceedings by your Committee and the content may not be disclosed to unauthorized personnel without the express approval of the FBI.

Enclosure.

Original and one copy to AG.
