Since source had worked all day Saturday, August seven last, he was released from surveillance duties at approximately twelve forty-eight a.m. Sunday, August eight, by McGowan (MC GOWAN) and Murphy and told to get some sleep—that they had sufficient personnel to complete surveillance during the early a.m. hours of Sunday, August eight.

SAC (MEDBURG) conferred with Bureau. Since Grady was not on scene, no break-in attempts were made, and in view of plans formulated for a break-in next weekend, the decision was made to make arrests next weekend.

Bureau will be kept advised of developments.
