4. View Gonzalez’s general acceptance of Jurado’s pitch. Rowton will pass 100 dollars U.S. to Gonzalez through Jurado to help him meet expenses. Rowton will have Jurado pass Gonzalez’s requirements prior to his departure. Jurado was told by Rowton not to surface Rowton to Gonzalez. Jurado is also planning to make a second expedition to Havana for Rowton soon and expects to see Gonzalez there. He will take his wife with him, but is delayed by difficulties obtaining a visa for her.
