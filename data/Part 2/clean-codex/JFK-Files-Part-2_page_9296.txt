United States Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Investigation.

Office: Detroit, Michigan. Bureau File No. 105-165706 Sub 15. Report of: SA Donald V. Ritenour, Jr. Field Office File No. 157-3075. Title: Black Panther Party—Detroit Division. Character: Extremist matters.

Synopsis: The Detroit NCCF was raised to full BPP status in May 1971. Its headquarters, the Ministry of Information, is located at 157 Collingwood, Detroit, with offices at 2591 Pennsylvania and 2435 Pennsylvania, Detroit. The office at Flint, Michigan, was closed during this period. Detroit continues to be directed from BPP headquarters, Oakland, California, and has contact with other BPP chapters. Detroit continues to sell “The Black Panther” newspaper, which is its main source of funds, although sales fell off during September and October 1971. A number of NCCF members were arrested during the first part of 1971 in connection with robberies and beatings. Some members of the Detroit BPP have become disenchanted with NEWTON’s policies and are switching to the faction of the BPP supporting ELDRIDGE CLEAVER.
