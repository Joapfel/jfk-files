Howard I. Felchlin

Pages 90–93 of the paper relate to Assistant Army Attaché Howard I. Felchlin. It was indicated that Nosenko correctly identified Felchlin but knew nothing of his background, did not recall names of agents specifically targeted against him, did not recall a newspaper article in March 1954 falsely accusing Felchlin and his companion, did not recall any unusual circumstances involving the expulsion of Felchlin and McKinney in 1954, and when told that the expulsion took place in that year stated this was not the correct date. It was also indicated Nosenko did not recognize the name of Felchlin’s maid, Federova.
