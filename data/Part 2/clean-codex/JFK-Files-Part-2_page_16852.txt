Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 6/6/69

Larry Maurice Robertson, 3326 West Fulton Avenue, Chicago, Illinois, was interviewed by Special Agents (SA) who identified themselves to him as SAs of the FBI. Prior to the commencement of the interview, Robertson furnished Form FD-395, “Interrogation; Advice of Rights,” which he carefully read and refused to sign. He stated that he understood what his rights were but that he did not desire to sign any legal document. Robertson furnished the following information:

Robertson furnished SAs with his name, address, and basic descriptive information. He refused to make any further statements to the Agents regarding the Black Panther Party or his involvement with the Party. He denied any knowledge of George Sams, Jr.
