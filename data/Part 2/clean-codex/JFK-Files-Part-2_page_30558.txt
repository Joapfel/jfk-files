Memorandum for the Record.

Subject: Procurement of intelligence by Mrs. Dorothy L. Hunt.

1. On 25 February 1963, I delivered to Mr. W. Lloyd George, Chief, FI, thermofax copies of a memorandum and a letter of transmittal dated 18 and 20 February, respectively. Both were prepared by Ambassador Antonio Garrigues (Garriques) for Prince Stanislas Radzilovitch and taken to the latter in New York by Alfonso de Borbon, second secretary of the Spanish Embassy in Washington.
