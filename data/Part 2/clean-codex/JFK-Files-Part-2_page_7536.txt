27 September 63: OSWALD went to Cuban Embassy, Mexico City, and made an appointment to get a visa to Cuba on the way to the USSR. OSWALD went to the Soviet Embassy to get a letter of recommendation. They would not give it to him because they did not know who he was.

OSWALD went to the Soviet Embassy on 27 September.

He then went back to the Cuban Embassy on 27 September.

Valery KOSTIKOV talked with Sylvia Duran and said the Soviets had never heard of OSWALD.
