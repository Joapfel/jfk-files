To obtain classified fingerprints:

1. Use black paper or pink.

2. Distribute in secret on invoice slip.

3. Do not use for personal purposes.

4. Do not use throughout.

5. Dull finished or chewed to bear and avoid all grinding to slip.

6. If an ink or letter is improperly made, it impossible to print a furger, make a notation of the error on the furger, and return the document as is.

7. If some principal position makes it impossible to obtain perfect impressions, submit the best that can be obtained with a LIFO stacked to the card explaining the circumstances.

8. Examine the completed prints to see if they can be classified, bearing in mind that most fingerprints call into the patterns shown on this card. Other patterns occur incorrectly and are not shown here.
