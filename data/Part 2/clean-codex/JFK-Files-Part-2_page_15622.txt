Black Panther Party

The above Maywood PD source advised that following the services, a few shots were heard and several unidentified gang members purporting to be Blackstone Rangers told police that individuals, whom they could or would not identify, allegedly belonging to the Disciples, another South Side youth gang, and whose members were in attendance at the services, had fired a shotgun at the vehicle in which the Rangers were riding. No injuries were reported, nor were any arrests made, and the area was normal shortly after the services ended.

The body, according to the Maywood PD source, was then moved to O'Hare Airport and placed into the care of Delta Airlines without further incidents.

The Chicago PD source furnished the description of the following list of firearms confiscated during the raid on the above Panther apartment on December 4, 1969:

One Colt .45 caliber automatic, serial no. 7111, Model 1927.

One Sears Roebuck 20-gauge shotgun, Model 21, no serial number.

One Springfield 12-gauge shotgun, Model 67H, no serial number.

One High Standard 12-gauge shotgun, marked “Riot 20-6,” Model K1200.

One Browning 12-gauge shotgun, serial no. H15423.

One High Standard 12-gauge shotgun, marked “Flite King Brush,” Model K1200.
