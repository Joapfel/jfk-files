United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. J. B. Adams  
From: Legal Counsel  
Subject: SENSTUDY 75  
Date: June 2, 1975

On 5-30-75, John Elliff, Task Force Director of the captioned Committee's Task Force on Domestic Intelligence Activities, and SA Paul V. Daly of this Division discussed the possibility of Elliff and members of his task force conducting the inquiry into the FBI having lunch with the Director. Elliff stated he felt that such an arrangement would be very beneficial and indicated that he and four or five staff members would attend such a luncheon.

It would appear that such an arrangement might be beneficial, and consideration should be given to arranging a luncheon if the Director's schedule permits.

Recommendation: That the luncheon be arranged in line with the above at a time convenient to the Director.
