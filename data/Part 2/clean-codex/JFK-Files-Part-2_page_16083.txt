United States Government Memorandum

To: SAC, Chicago

From: SA Roy Martin Mitchell

Subject: Black Panther Party  
RM-BPP

Date: March 18, 1970

Who has provided reliable information in the past, will not testify, but attests to the accuracy of the following information and which appropriate has been disseminated as well as orally furnished to the proper local or federal agency, advised as follows on the indicated dates:

3/2/70: Willie Calvin, Raymond Hewitt, Clarence Terry, travel BPP leaders.

Calvin was assigned to be Hewitt and companion known only as “T.C.,” security guard when they came through.
