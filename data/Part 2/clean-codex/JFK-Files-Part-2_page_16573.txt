Noting: Summary Punishment. Civil Rights.

This refers to your memorials of December 5 and December 8, 1969, entitled “Lee's Forkher Party Activity, Chicago, Illinois.”

In connection with this matter, I am enclosing herewith two copies of a telephone call of December 5, 1969, to the Department from John O'Connor (O'Conner) of the Illinois State Branch, BACAP, and other officials of that organization. We have also received telegrams from Thomas Placo, Manacost Lilliac, Chicago Daily Defender (Chicago Fully Defender), Milton H. Tosag, Jr., Executive Director, National Civil Rights, and from a number of individuals of Harvey, Tillinghast. All of these communications generally allege that the shooting and killing of members of the Black Panther organization were, on or about December 4, 1969, unnecessary, unjustified, and of exceptional justice. They request that an investigation be made of the matter.

In view of the circumstances, please conduct a multi-investigation so that we may determine the outcome of the matter.
