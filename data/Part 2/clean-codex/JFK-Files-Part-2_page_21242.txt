On April 12, 1968 (N-179), a list of names including those of Eryik Kehaya (Eryik Kehaya) and his son Ery Whitaker Kehaya was displayed to Nosenko. He selected the name Kehaya and stated he believed this was the name of the father and son previously mentioned by him. After seeing the name, he recalled that the name of the young man was Ery rather than “Eric.” He did not know if a recruitment approach had been made to these individuals.

Investigation disclosed that the Kehayas have been in the tobacco business for many years and have traveled throughout the world, including trips to the USSR.
