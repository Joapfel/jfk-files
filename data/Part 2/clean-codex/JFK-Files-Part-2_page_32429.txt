3. Field comment: Terrorist activities have been increasing in Venezuela. Several members of the Fuerzas Armadas de Liberación Nacional (FALN, Armed Forces of National Liberation) took temporary possession of the office of the prefect in the town of San Diego de los Altos, about 20 miles from Caracas, on 4 February. They tied up the policemen on guard, stole a few rifles, and painted the walls with anti-government slogans. On 5 February an attack was made on the home of Colonel José Antonio González, commander of the military tribunal, located in a well-to-do residential area of Caracas. This was followed by another attack on 12 February, which resulted in the government establishing roadblocks and sending troops and police into the area. On the morning of 9 February an armed attack was made on the American [portion garbled will forward], where offices were set on fire and equipment destroyed, and on the night of 9 February Sears warehouse in Antimano was completely destroyed by members of the FALN. Three bridges on the Pan American Highway, Barquisimeto area, State of Lara, were damaged by bombs. On 12 February an attempt was made on that day to blow up a Creole oil line in Catia La Mar (Catia La Mag), Federal District, but only one small bomb exploded, causing minor damage.
