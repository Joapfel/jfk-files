On April 3, 1971, an anti-abortion law march was held at St. Paul, Minnesota, from the residence of the Governor to the steps of the State Capitol Building. A large crowd, predominantly women, participated in this peaceful march.

At a meeting of the TCB-SWP held in April 1971, Mary Hillery reported that there were approximately 600 participants in the anti-abortion law march held on April 3, 1971 location. Rebecca Ann Finch in November 1971 was an active member of the YSA-TCG.

On November 16, 1971, at a meeting of the YSA-TCG held in Minneapolis, Minnesota, discussion centered around a mass action demonstration to be held on November 20, 1971, in Washington, D.C., sponsored by WONAC. It was announced that the MWAAC had conducted a telephone fund drive campaign in order to raise money to send two busloads of individuals from this area to participate in the Washington, D.C., demonstration. It was announced that buses would depart cities from Minneapolis, Minnesota, at 9 a.m. on November 19, 1971; among those individuals planning to participate were approximately ten persons active in the YSA-TCG or the TCB-SWP.
