April 13, confidential source who furnished reliable information in the past advised WONAAC would conduct a demonstration at Governor Rockefeller's office, New York City, to protest the ban on Medicaid payments for abortions.

April 13, instant, special agents of the Federal Bureau of Investigation observed four women enter the office of the New York governor, where they presented the governor's aide a petition relative to the above-mentioned ban. No demonstration occurred; women departed.

The above-mentioned source advised that on April 16 WONAAC would conduct a counter-demonstration to action by the Knights of Columbus on April 17, 1972, demanding repeal of abortion laws in New York State. Demonstration scheduled for 1:30 p.m. in the vicinity of Fifth Avenue.
