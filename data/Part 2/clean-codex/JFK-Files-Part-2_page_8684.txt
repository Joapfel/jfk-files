MASH 1470 (IN 38669), 6 November 1960.

Cable to CIA Headquarters and Havana stating that RECAREY (fnu), cousin of a Guat trainee, arrived in MASH from Havana on 5 November to check the bona fides of Eduardo OJEDA Camaraza. OJEDA had made contact with AMYUM/AMRUNG Havana and provided details of MASH recruitment, training, names of trainees, and the mission in Cuba. MASH feared AMRUNG/AMYUM might try to kill OJEDA as a Castro penetration/provocateur if his bona fides were not established.

RIMM 5846 (IN 2708), 4 June 1961.

Radio message 84 from agent in Cuba advised: “Functionary of Italian Embassy by name of MORATORI says he works for one intelligence organization of yours, says he is in contact with Martin Elena and others and that you have plans for invasion within 30 days after killing Fidel. Advise if we can confide in MORATORI.”

BELL 0987 (OUT 9804), 6 June 1961.

Radio message 72 to agent: “MORATORI unknown to us. Do not confide in him. MORATORI’s information of pending invasion within 30 days after killing Fidel not true. Beware of rumor mongers and provocateurs.”
