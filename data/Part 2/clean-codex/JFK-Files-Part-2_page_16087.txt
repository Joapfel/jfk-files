Black Panther Party

Subpoenas Have Been Issued by the Will of the People

Fred Hampton (ed Hampton) and Mark Clark were murdered by State's Attorney Hanrahan and his cohorts. These gangsters and murderous hoodlums used the old Ku Klux Klan (Klu Klux Klan) tactic of killing a few niggers to keep the rest in line. When the pigs saw the people were concerned about the deaths they tried to justify their actions by holding a rigged inquest. In the inquest you could see the pigs were lying because each one had a different story. But still the old gangster and picked, upper class, racist jury found the verdict to be JUSTIFIABLE HOMICIDE. Come to the people's quest to find out the truth. Then it will be up to the people to see and decide that justice is done.

We welcome all people to witness this inquest.
