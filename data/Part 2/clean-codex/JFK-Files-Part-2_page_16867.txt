United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation

Copy to: USA, Chicago; USA, New Haven.

Date: June 12, 1969.  
Office: Chicago, Illinois.

Title: George Sams, Jr.; Lockett Philip Bibbs; Bruce Allen Dickson; Andrea Beverly Graves; Harvey Dwight Holt; Patrick Keen; Larry Maurice Robinson; Jessie Ward; Georgia Washington.

Character: Unlawful flight to avoid prosecution—murder; conspiracy to commit murder; kidnapping; conspiracy to commit kidnapping; binding; harboring.

Synopsis: On 6/3 and 4/69, information was received that subject George Sams, Jr. was being secreted at the headquarters of the Black Panther Party (BPP), Chicago. During the early morning hours of 6/4/69, Chicago agents attempted to gain access to this office in an attempt to locate the subject. After repeated attempts to gain cooperation from members of the BPP, all occupants were ordered out of the premises. Arrested and charged with harboring subject George Sams, Jr., in violation of Title 18, Section 1071, United States Code, were subjects Lockett Philip Bibbs, Bruce Allen Dickson, Andrea Beverly Graves, Harvey Dwight Holt, Patrick Keen, Larry Maurice Robinson, Jessie Ward, and Georgia Washington. AUSA, Chicago authorized filing of these charges before the United States Commissioner, Chicago, on 6/4/69. All subjects appeared before the United States Commissioner that day and were released on bond for reappearance on 6/20/69. A search incidental to the subjects' arrests and in connection with the search for the fugitive resulted in the seizure by agents of 13 mm., large quantities of ammunition, swords, a gas mask, office machines, books, and quantities of records, posters, and literature. Subject Sams reportedly enforced PUR BPP and should be considered armed and extremely dangerous.
