The source stated that the Illinois BPP was paid solely from the admissions charged and received a total of approximately $500.00.

The source stated that Cook was arrested for a traffic violation during the evening.

The source advised that one Mark Clark, BPP leader, Peoria, Illinois, made arrangements for this speech.

The source stated that one Leon Harps, BPP leader, Peoria, Illinois, is responsible for distributing the Black Panther Paper, and one Edward McChriston appears to be responsible for the Illinois BPP branch in Peoria, Illinois.

The source stated that the Chicago group arrived back in Chicago about 6:00 a.m. on October 30, 1969.
