Mexico City 9810.

Occurred 23 December when Octavio and Falcon returned and escorted him by car to Gualan on Trans-Oceanic Highway beyond Zacapam. There LURING-3 was introduced to “Pascual” and “Salvador,” who identified themselves as members of the “Edgar Ibarra” front. These two representatives stated the following:

A. Tricontinental committee agreements were not carried out because of the practical problems involved in their implementation.

B. For the above reason, Cuban assistance has been inconsequential, and therefore guerrillas have resorted to more direct methods to obtain assistance, including bank and train robberies. The most direct source of outside assistance has been the Soviets via their embassy in Mexico.

C. Guerrillas also unable to count on Cuba for assistance, as Cuba too preoccupied with its own problems of revolutionary consolidation. Pascual and Salvador evidenced sarcasm in this statement.

D. “Edgar Ibarra” guerrillas control a front more than 120 kilometers long and require more and more supplies to expand membership and increase their area (not further described).

E. In the past, Guatemala guerrillas themselves arranged.
