Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division

From New York (100-171481)

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC)

At two p.m., April sixteen, nineteen seventy-two, approximately sixty individuals counter-demonstrated against the Knights of Columbus parade, vicinity of Fifth Avenue and Eighty-Sixth Street, NYC. This counter-demonstration was sponsored by WONAAC, advocating removal of all restrictive anti-abortion laws. Counter-demonstration concluded four p.m., no incidents or arrests.

Characterization

WONAAC has been described as an organization of groups whose aim is to effect the change in abortion laws.
