Persons/Entities: Gen. Lansdale; Bruce Cheever (CIA); Harvey Patewell. Dates: 25 October 1962. Categories: Cuba; Mongoose; Special Group Augmented.

Occurrence:

Cheever suggested to Patchell that Gen. Lansdale call Bill Harvey since Bill had briefed Gen. Lansdale on what the CIA was doing. Patchell indicates that Gen. Lansdale was informed of Cheever's comments.

Source: Memorandum for record re Item for Meeting of Special Group Augmented on 26 October 1962, prepared by Lt. Col. Patchell on 10/26 (00737).

Staff. Date: 6/29 PGW.
