Addendum to Memo Papich to Belmont (continued).

Re: Coverage of Noncommunist Diplomatic Establishments.

On military, air, and naval attachés of friendly countries, and the liaison and freedom of action existing between them and counterparts at the Pentagon. The Bureau is not in a position to effect control of their actions and information-gathering ability by investigation on a general basis. What we are going to have to do is attempt to build up substantial data against those attachés which Soviets and satellites are known to utilize and either attempt to compromise or use them, or consider possible presentation of the problem for State Department action.
