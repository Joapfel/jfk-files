Note:

By Bureau teletype, 2/8/72, we authorized attendance of DE 2278-PSI at captioned. At that time DP made no request for payment to informant. Informant has returned to Detroit and furnished exact accounting of expenses amounting to $35.55, and Detroit has requested Bureau to reimburse informant for these expenses. Detroit has also requested Bureau to authorize payment of $100 for services rendered, but Detroit furnished no details whatsoever of information furnished. Payment for expenses is warranted and is being authorized; however, no payment for services will be authorized until Bureau furnishes results of information obtained by informant during captioned.
