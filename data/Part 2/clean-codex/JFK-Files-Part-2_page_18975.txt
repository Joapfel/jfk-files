5/1/69

To: Director, FBI (FUI) (105-14704)

From: SAC, WFO (105-1871) (P)

Re NY airtel to Bureau, 4/23/69, and WFO letter to the Bureau, 4/23/69, captioned “Bureau, 4/23/69.”

Enclosed for the Bureau are five copies, and for New York, two copies of a memorandum of contact concerning former NY 2524-S’s contract with MILLIE MARARE, NYC. This memorandum was obtained on a confidential basis from JOHN N. SMITH, Domestic Operations Division, CIA.

JOHN SMITH, Domestic Operations Division, CIA (convert), in exploring the agency’s urgency in attempting to place an individual on the China Mainland, recently requested that WFO the names of individuals outside the United States who might possibly be receptive to an approach by former NY 2524-S, with whom SMITH maintained official and social contact while both were recently assigned to Hong Kong.

LJForder
