E. Publications

1. Distribution of the BPP Newspaper

DE T-1, DE T-2, and DE T-7 advised that from May through October 1972, the BPP received the following copies of “The Black Panther” newspaper from BPP national distribution, New York City, New York:

May 1972: 6,000 copies  
June 1972: 10,000 copies  
July 1972: 8,000 copies  
August 1972: 11,000 copies  
September 1972: 9,000 copies  
October 1972: 8,000 copies
