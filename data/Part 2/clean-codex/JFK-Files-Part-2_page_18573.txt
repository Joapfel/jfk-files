8:58 a.m., urgent, July 21, 1975, JFD.

To Director (62-116395). From Tampa (105-5390). SENSTUDY 75.

The Tampa NITEL, July 11, 1975.

On the evening of July 20, 1975, former SA John Matter, telephone 813-936-1691, was telephonically contacted at Fort Myers by Mike Epstein of Senate Select Committee staff. Epstein requested a meeting at Fort Myers the night of July 22 or the morning of July 23.

REC-26.

About one hour after the initial call, Epstein called Matter again and finally set an appointment for Friday, July 25, 1975, at 11:30 a.m., Room G308, New Senate Office Building.

Matter will travel to Washington for this meeting and requests Supervisor Paul Daly, Legal Counsel Division, to telephonically contact him in Fort Myers. Matter desires a Bureau representative at the meeting July 25, 1975.

End.
