301930Z, CITE CARACAS 7262.

Mexico City info Director.

FORUMEN.

Ref: Mexico City 1954 (to 1455).

1. No station traces.

2. Would appreciate that station exercise every effort to avoid establishment of this cover business, particularly important that LIRING-3 not take any initiative. Have no objection to MEXI monitoring developments, but do not desire to become engaged operationally.

3. PCV obtaining substantial financial support from abroad at present time and station able to monitor this through existing assets. While a Cuban-sponsored funding mechanism would probably support the Douglas Bravo dissident PCV group and the MIR, believe that tangible advantages to be derived by the extremists would outweigh the value of any intelligence produced.

4. Has LIRING-3 been sufficiently tested in operations vis-à-vis Guat guerrillas to establish his bona fides? Is he still unwitting and, if so, what is the degree of our control over him?

6 July 1957. 30 June 1957. M 02328.
