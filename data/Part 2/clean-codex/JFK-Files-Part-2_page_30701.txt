Volume I – Manuel Ray

7. PRQ Part 2 (169 C85514)

Section I (Contact and Development)

Woodrow C. Olien met Ray in October 1958 when Ray was head of the Civic Resistance Movement in Havana. Three clandestine meetings were held prior to 1 January 1959, attended by Olien, Wallace A. Growery, and Ignacio Mendoza (MRC member), who originally introduced Olien to Ray. After 1 January 1959, Ray was given an important post in the revolutionary government, and Olien has seen him only twice since, Olien posing as political officer to the State Department.

Section II of PRQ states Ray is friendly to the U.S., once having received a grant for special study there. A dedicated, patriotic Cuban, opposed to Communism but has taken no active part in combatting it.

9. PRQ Part 2 (continued)

Section VI—Subject has definite political action potential, is a pro-democratic official of the government who is not in sympathy with the aims of international Communism and who is interested in strengthening ties between Cuba and the U.S. Probably would never consent to agent status, but could by persuasion and argument be encouraged to influence the policies of his government along lines compatible with U.S. government policies.

Section XIV—Information in PRQ Parts I and II was obtained from file research and personal contact with subject.
