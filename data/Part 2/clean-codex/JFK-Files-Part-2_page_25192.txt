Persons/Entities: Conein; General Don; General Duong Van Minh; General Ton That Dinh; Ngo Dinh Nhu. Date: October 2, 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence:

Conein/General Don meeting at Mhatoang with the following significant points: (a) General Duong Van Minh desired a private conversation with Conein; (b) General Ton That Dinh (Don That Dinh), Commanding General, III Corps, realized he had been duped by Dinh, Governor of Saigon, between 21 August and 16 September 1963; (c) during September 1963, the generals submitted a request to President Dinh to accord them positions in different ministries, which they did not expect Dinh to honor; (d) Don stated that the generals now had a plan; (e) prior to General Dinh's press conference of 29 August, an unidentified Vietnamese civilian told Counsellor Ngo Dinh Nhu that the Americans were contacting various Vietnamese in order to exchange information on a coup. Don was called by Nhu and shown a list prepared by Nhu stating that Americans were engaged in coup plotting; Canada's name was not on the list.

Source: Hearings, SSCI, 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit One. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.
