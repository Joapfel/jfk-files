Jeannette Rankin Brigade  
Information Concerning  
(Internal Security)

The January 2, 1968, issue of “The Atlanta Journal,” a daily newspaper published in Atlanta, Georgia, carried an article captioned “Miss Rankin Plans Peace Talk Here” which stated that Jeannette Rankin would address a group of Atlanta women interested in peace activities at the Ebenezer Baptist Church in Atlanta, Georgia, on January 4, 1968, and Mrs. Martin Luther King, Jr., a national sponsor of the Jeannette Rankin Brigade, would also speak at this meeting. The article stated further that Isobel Cerney, founder of the Southern Coordinating Committee to End the War in Vietnam, would be on the program at the Ebenezer Baptist Church.

Isobel Forbes Cerney
