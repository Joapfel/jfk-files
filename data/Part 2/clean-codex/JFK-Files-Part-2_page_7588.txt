DF-440

9 July 1963

Information Report

Subject: Colonel Yurly Ivanovich Moskalevsky.

Source: AEDAZZIE-1.

Date of information: 1961.

1. Source identified a photograph of Moskalevsky (Moskalevsky) not by name, nor was the name supplied as that of an Air Force colonel and GRU officer in the Information Directorate of the GRU who attended a meeting in London in May or June 1961.

Comment:

2. No SR/6/Bio traces.

3. Informal contacts report that Yurly Ivanovich Moskalevsky, born 17 January 1920, a radio engineer, attended a Conference on Electronics in London from 12 to 17 June 1961.

9 July 1963.

N.B. Birthdate also recorded as 23 July 1920.
