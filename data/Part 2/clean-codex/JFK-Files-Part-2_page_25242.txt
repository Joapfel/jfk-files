General Don Conein. Ambassador Lodge.

General Don stated to Conein that Don had tried to alert Conein at the latter's residence since before noon and no one answered. A later week revealed that Conein's telephone was out of order. Conein was told that he was to keep a telephone line open to Ambassador Lodge; Don informed Conein that Colonels Le Gung Tung (Army Special Forces Commander), Phynh Eun Hien (Air Force Commander), and Colonel Cho Van Vien (Commander of the Airborne Brigade) were now absent; that Colonel Njong Ngoc Luan (Commander of the Civil Guard) and Lieutenant Colonel Le Nguyen Khuong (Commander of the Marine Brigade) were under guard and not yet declared and considered neutral.

Colonel Tung was brought in under armed guard and forced at gunpoint to order a cease-fire between the Special Forces units adjacent to JGS headquarters and two platoons of the 8th Division. Once the cease-fire had been declared between Special Forces and JGS, thirty-five armored personnel carriers loaded with troops in combat gear started moving toward Saigon proper.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit One. Source: p. 9, cont'd. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.
