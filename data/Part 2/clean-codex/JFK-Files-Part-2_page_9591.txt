Title: Secret Army Organization (SAO).

Character: Extremist matters—Secret Army Organization.

Synopsis:

Secret Army Organization (SAO) formed at San Diego, California, in late September 1971, by Jerry Lynn Davis to replace the Minutemen (MM) organization, considered defunct in the San Diego area. The organization was formed to provide military-type training for self-defense, to oppose communism and totalitarian government, and to take over political power in the United States if the present government fails. Jerry Lynn Davis was considered “Commander” of the SAO at San Diego, and other officers are set forth. Members of the SAO were active in acquiring and possessing firearms and explosives. Tentative arrangements were made for extension of the SAO with branches at Las Vegas, Nevada; Phoenix, Arizona; and Seattle, Washington. Additional SAO contacts were claimed at Portland, Oregon; El Paso, Texas; Los Angeles, California; and in the states of Kansas and Oklahoma. Contact was made with Jesse Clayburn Stevens, alleged leader of the East Coast portion of the MM organization, and tentative arrangements were made for an alliance between Stevens’ group and the SAO. The SAO had a total of eight members at San Diego, California, with approximately the same number of supporters and sympathizers.
