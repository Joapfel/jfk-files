16. Night clubs, cafes, pubs:

There is little indication Cruz and his wife go to night clubs or cafes. On 15 August 1968 they did go to CARDINI's, but this was probably because a Cuban singer was playing there.

17. Vacations:

Cruz and Mercedes do not appear to take vacations in Mexico or to travel much in Mexico on weekends. Mercedes does apparently think it is important to see the tourist sights, as she has told visiting Cubans.

They also seldom go to Cuba. The whole family, including the maid, returned to Cuba for one month of home leave in January 1969, after having been in Mexico for two years. Mercedes had not returned to Cuba during this time, nor had the children.

She returned again with the children at the end of September 1969 to have her third baby. They all returned to Mexico at the end of January 1970.

18. Hobbies, clubs, lodges:

Cruz's sister indicated Cruz has no hobbies.

From information available to the Mexico City Station, neither Cruz nor Mercedes has been noted to have hobbies.
