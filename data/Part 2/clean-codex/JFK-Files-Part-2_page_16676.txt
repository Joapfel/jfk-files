Federal Bureau (Dureau) of Investigation

Date: January 16, 1970

On January 9, 1970, photographs were taken of the killed door, rear entrance, and porch of the building at 2335 West Monroe, Chicago, Illinois. Present during filming were Special Agent Jesse H. Queen, U.S. Department of Justice attorney, and a resident of this address.
