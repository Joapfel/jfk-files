The Attorney General

June 4, 1975

Director, FBI

United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC)

By letter dated May 14, 1975, with attached appendices, the SSC requested certain documents and other information from the FBI.

Item number one a. of Part I, “Policies and Procedures,” Appendix B of the referenced letter requested Committee Staff access to all indices and control files pertaining to SAC Memoranda and SAC Letters for 1960 to the present.

Enclosed for your approval and forwarding to the Committee is an original of a memorandum advising the Committee that the above-requested material is presently available for examination at FBI Headquarters by appropriately cleared personnel of the Committee.

A copy of this memorandum is being furnished for your records.
