Cable. 5AEPF:36253.

6:28:30Z, 6 April 1968. Mexico City 5141. Guatemala City; information to Director, Tegucigalpa.

Reference: Mexico City 5111 (N85161).

1. On 5 April 1968, the station received photographic copies of a Guatemalan passport which LIRING-3 said Francisco Lara would use to enter Honduras from Guatemala. Copies indicate there was a discrepancy (DSPECENCY) in the name originally reported in the reference and the name on the passport. Correct name is Jose Maria Oliva Carranza, ordinary Guatemalan passport number 154825, series D, registry number 22743, issued 27 May 1963 in Guatemala City. Oliva was born in San Felipe, Retalhuleu (RETALEULEU), on 9 February 1935.

2. Other documents photographed include military inscription in the Guatemalan Army, card number 49859, birth certificate number 2825671, cedula de vecindad 287155, and electron card 127817.

3. These documents were photographed by LIRING-3 when they were passed to him by Francisco Lara for use in making false Mexican documentation. Pouching copies of documents to addressees.
