Teletype to SACs, New York and WFO.

Re: Women's National Abortion Action Coalition. 100-463106.

Detailed FD-306 to NY within five days in accordance with SAC Letter 72-3(C), re reporting of results of subversive conferences.

Note:

WONAAC was founded in NYC in 7/71 for the purpose of forming a national movement of groups throughout the U.S. who favor legalized abortions. The organization is dominated by Socialist Workers Party (SWP) and Young Socialist Alliance (YSA). WONAAC is scheduled to hold an International Conference at NYC on 3/9–11/73. INFORMANT is a member of the YSA and SWP, and INFORMANT is a member of the YSA; both have been selected by SWP leadership to attend the conference in New York and to remain over for political activity at the YSA-SWP National Office on 3/11/73. Expenses involve $38 each for round-trip travel by Metro Coach from D.C. to NYC and $50 each for food, lodging, contributions, and miscellaneous expenses for two days. Outlay would be $88 for each informant, making a total of $176. Based on the fact that the sources were selected by SWP, YSA/SWP leadership to be members of the delegation to attend the International Tribunal, WFO is being authorized to advance each source $38.
