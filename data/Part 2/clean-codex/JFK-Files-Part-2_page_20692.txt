Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

May 6, 1972. Teletyping.

From: Washington Field (100-55966) (C) 3P.

Candlelight (CANDLERLIGHT) march to United States Capitol, Washington, D.C., sponsored by District of Columbia chapter of Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (D.C. NONAAC), May 6, seventh TWC. Internal security—Communist (Trotskyist).

The WFO teletype dated April 20th, 74 last.

On May 6th, instant, a confidential source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that the District of Columbia Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (D.C. NONAAC) held a forum meeting at St. Mark's Episcopal Church, Third and A Street, Southeast, Washington (WACHENTON), D.C. This information was attended (ATTACHED) by approximately one minute. Separate interviews with last name on May 6 instant.

The source stated (STARTED) that following the meeting, the group left the church and proceeded (RECODED) in a candlelight (CANDLERLIGHT) procession to the east steps of the Capitol Building.
