151615Z April 1968. Cite: Mexico City 5205. 15 April 9129B.

Guatemala City. Info: Director, Tegucigalpa.

Refs: A. Mexico City 5111 (N 85161). B. Mexico City 5141 (N 86263).

1. Upon return from short vacation, Wallace B. Rowton was given sealed letter from LIRING-3 by LIRING-4. Letter indicated:

A. LIRING-3's (LERING-3's) departure for Guatemala and Honduras delayed when Francisco Lara accidentally shot himself in the ankle while cleaning a gun. Lara anxious news of accident not reach ears of FAR members, forbidden to carry weapons in Mexico.

B. LIRING-3 departed 10 April with Lara and another Guatemalan who was a member of Yon Sosa's faction. LIRING-3 stated in letter this Guatemalan was insistent he talk directly with Yon Sosa.

C. LIRING-3 and Lara will probably visit San Salvador in addition to Guatemala and Honduras.

2. Per LIRING-4, another Guatemalan who was introduced to LIRING-3 by Lara and is probably Rodolfo Sandoval called at her apartment 9 April looking for LIRING-3, who was out.
