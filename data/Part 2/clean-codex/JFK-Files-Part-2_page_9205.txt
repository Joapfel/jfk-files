Reporting office: Detroit. Office of origin: San Francisco. Date: 7/9/74. Investigative period: 5/14/73–6/20/74. Title of case: Black Panther Party. Report made by: SA Robert W. Meyers, Jr. Typed by: dmz. Character of case: EM.

Reference: Detroit report of SA Lawrence W. Levine, 5/9/73.

RUC.

Administrative.

Detroit chapter of BPP has closed all its offices, terminated all of its activities, and all but two members have relocated in Oakland, California. In view of this fact, Detroit is considering this matter RUC.

Detroit is maintaining cases on all active members until their location in Oakland, California, is verified by San Francisco.

Accomplishments claimed. Convictions. Fugitives. Fines. Savings. Recoveries. Acquittals. Case has been pending over one year. Pending prosecution over six months. Special agent in charge. Approved (signature).
