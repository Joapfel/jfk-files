7. Cable to Director from JMWAVE, dated 11 October 1963.

On October 1, Cisneros tells AMTAUP-1 that Ray will visit Betancourt later in the week to report on the status of JURE negotiations with the U.S. and promises made to Ray by RFK during recent interviews.

8. Cable 172301Z, 17 October 1963, to Director from JMWAVE.

On 15 October 1963, Jose (Gomez) Rodriguez, also known as Cabeza, JURE member, tells AMTAUP-2 that CIA officials from Washington, D.C., are coming to Miami to finalize agreements with JURE. Also said JURE would never tell CIA exact landing sites on the Cuban coast.

9. Cable to Director from Mexico City, 26 October 1963.

260035Z. Dir WAVE Cite Mexi 6677. Reference A: Mexi 6455, not sent WAVE, IN 36021. Reference B: HMMA 22285, also received 2285.

JURE representative Julio Lanier pressing LITAMILF2 to obtain Mexican visas for Luis Giun Figueredo and Rogelio Cisneros Diaz, who want to inspect the plane of Reference A.

Fast action needed or L-2 will not be able to meet Cisneros and have an “in” with the JURE group for the purpose of coverage of JURE PM activities in Mexico. Lanier will set up a meeting in Miami or San Juan.

10. Dispatch to Chief, Special Affairs from CS/JMWAVE, dated 21 October 1963.

Information received from AMTAUP-2.

Ray to arrive in Miami 26 July, in secret, because he wanted to hold private meetings to discuss military affairs with action groups in Miami. Met at the home of Rogelio Cisneros Diaz on 28 July. Met with Armando Alanis Angulo and asked him to join JURE. Alanis was former Chief of the Fiscal District of the Treasury in Camaguey Province. Ray says he has an organization inside Cuba but needs personnel on the outside with which to operate. Claimed to have 300 weapons in Miami.

On 18 August, Cisneros meets with Alanis and Luis Aguiar, who belongs to the Alanis group. Talked of plans for Aguiar’s men to infiltrate Las Villas Province and open a JURE organization.

Alanis officially joins JURE on 20 August 1963 and received Cisneros’ assurances that they would get financial help from JURE.

11. Cable to Chief, Special Affairs Staff from JMWAVE/CS, dated 22 October 1963.

On 30 September, Ray departed for San Juan from Miami, after having visited Venezuela, Costa Rica, Honduras, and Panama to gain support for operations against Cuba.

12. Cable 79065 on 29 October 1963.

Willard R. Nauman will arrive in San Juan on October 31, 1963, to meet Ray in St. Thomas. Do not plan contact unless need arises.
