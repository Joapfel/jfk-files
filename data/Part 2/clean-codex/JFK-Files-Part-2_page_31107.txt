Personality File Action Request — 23 December 1961. Rodriguez, a member of FRD in 1961. 201-293114. DPOB: 1935, Cuba.

Cable — 6 December 1960.

To: JMGOLD. From: Director. OUT 99988.

FBI report from Francisco Bustard, former G-2 agent, revealed Rodriguez, aka El Mexicano, and four colleagues working with the Diaz Long group volunteered to provide the following information on Cuban G-2:

1. Who was financing the Diaz Long group in Cuba.
2. Location of arms in Cuba.
3. Names of the Diaz Long group in Cuba.
4. Locations outside Cuba where materials are stored.
5. Place, date, and time of plane departure for Cuba.
6. General plans of the counter-revolution.

FRT stated reports would furnish sufficient proof to put subjects in prison. Wanted $5,000 for a report on who was the person in Cuba financing the counter-revolution. FRT stated the only reason he was in the U.S. was because he faced a 30-year prison sentence in Cuba.

Another report indicates a female friend of Diaz Long, working at Miami airport, was also a G-2 agent. Suggest Diaz Long be cautioned.
