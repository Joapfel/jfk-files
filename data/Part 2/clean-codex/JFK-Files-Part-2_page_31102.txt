Cable dated 15 November 1961, to Director from JMWAVE. IN 22200.

JMWAVE received derogatory information on Nobregas from reliable sources. In August, Leslie Nobregas, captain of Susan Ann, was instructed to get rid of his brother, Jorge, due to many reports of his homosexual tendencies. Leslie refused. Because of the reasons listed below, JMWAVE must never completely terminate all operational relationships with the Nobregas brothers and two of Jorge's special friends who are crewmen of Susan Ann.

a) On 1 November, the crew entered and mutinied due to unfair treatment and favoritism shown to Jorge's cliques.

b) The crew lacks confidence in Leslie's fortitude.

c) The crew says Leslie has more than once been forced to accomplish a mission because crewmen held a pistol to his back.

d) Jondreis says they will not “operate with us” if the Nobregas group remains.

e) Other crews recently supported the Nobregas group.
