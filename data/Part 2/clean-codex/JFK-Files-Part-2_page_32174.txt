DAN: 296-010208

Subject: White, Joseph Timothy. PFC, 492-60-2196. DPOB: 5 November 1961, St. Louis, Missouri.

1. On 1 September 1982, the Reserve Officers Training Corps (ROTC) military personnel file of Joseph Timothy White, PFC 492-60-2196, located at the Kemper Military College, Boonville, Missouri 65233, was furnished by John E. Parker, LTC, INF, Professor of Military Science, and disclosed the following:

A. White enrolled in the Kemper Military College (KMC) on 9 June 1980 with the ROTC program.

B. White attended basic camp from 9 June 1980 to 14 July 1980 at Fort Knox (FTNOX), Kentucky 40121. On 16 June 1980, White was apprehended by the Fort Knox (FT KNOX) military police (MP) and charged with a uniform violation and disobeying a lawful general order. An MP report with a handwritten statement by White was forwarded under separate cover. White was held at the MP station for approximately 2 hours, at which time he was released to his unit, Company B, 4th Brigade, 15th Battalion, Fort Knox (FT KNOX), Kentucky.

C. On 12 February 1981, White enlisted in the U.S. Army National Guard for a period of six (6) years. White was assigned to Battery C, 1st Battalion, 128th Field Artillery, Marshall, Missouri 65340.
