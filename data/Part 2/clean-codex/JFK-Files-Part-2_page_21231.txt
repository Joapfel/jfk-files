21. WILLIAM TARASKA

On page 430 it is stated that NOSENKO did not indicate his source of information on WILLIAM TARASKA. This comment is considered confidential.

Comment: It is noted that NOSENKO'S notes pertaining to TARASKA have been destroyed; however, on November 16, 1967, (N-25), NOSENKO stated that this case occurred in 1959 when he was Deputy Chief of the 1st Section, 7th Department. The case officer for TARASKA was MICHAEL VINOGRADOV. On March 29, 1968, (N-174), NOSENKO stated that the Chief of 7th Department, DUBAS, had one or two meetings with him. He was considered to be recruited.
