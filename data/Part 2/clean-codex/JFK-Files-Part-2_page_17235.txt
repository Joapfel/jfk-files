Re: Donnette Verlina Brewer

On February 13, 1970, confidential source number three advised that Donnette Brewer and three Black males departed Detroit on that date en route to the Bronx, New York, chapter of the BPP. At the time of their departure, Brewer commented that she was wanted in Chicago, Illinois, in connection with the resistance offered to police officers on December 4, 1969, when Fred Hampton was killed, and that she was going “underground” in New York City to avoid prosecution.
