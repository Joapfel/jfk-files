November 19, 1971.

Wire type.

NR 002 HO code. 10:40 AM urgent, 11-19-71, IRV.

To Director, attention: Domestic Intelligence. WFO 100-54927. From Houston 100-12404.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) sponsored demonstration, November twenty, nineteen seventy-one, Washington, D.C., is New Left.

On November seventeen, last, a source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised approximately fifteen persons from Houston Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) group in Houston plan to join chartered bus from Austin, Texas, and proceed to demonstration in Washington, D.C., scheduled for November twenty, next, pertaining to abortion. Source advised in addition to persons riding chartered bus, three members of Houston YSA will be traveling to Washington in their personal cars.

The Houston contingent is scheduled to return to.
