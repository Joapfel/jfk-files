Persons/Entities: Conein (CONEIN), Ambassador Lodge. Date: 10/23/63. Category: Diem.

Occurrence:

Conein (CONEIN) asked why the generals had not spoken to Ambassador Lodge at the 19 October reception; Don stated that security would not permit an approach to the Ambassador since other members of the GVN were present; Don promised he would seek permission of the coup committee to give Conein (CONEIN) their political plan for the Ambassador's eyes only and requested a meeting in downtown Saigon on 24 October.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein (CONEIN), Exhibit One, page 6.

File: Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.

Persons/Entities: General Don (NERAL DON), R74228, Lodge, Henry Cabot (DGE, HENRY CABOT). Date: October 23, 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence:

A October 23, 1963 entry into the Saigon stations, “History of the Vietnamese Generals up,” noted that a meeting with Conein (CONEIN) was requested by General Don (NERAL DON). Don (NERAL DON) said the generals had decided to try to stage a coup within a week following 26 October. Conein (CONEIN), per instructions, passed the substance of DIR74228 to Don (NERAL DON). Don (NERAL DON) promised he would seek permission of the coup committee to give Conein (CONEIN) their political plan for the Ambassador's eyes only.
