On April 12, 1969, it was learned that the Chicago chapter of SDS had furnished money to Chicago BPP leaders whenever the BPP needed money.

On April 15, 1969, Chicago BPP leaders met with Chicago SDS leaders at SDS Chicago headquarters for the purpose of leaders of both groups becoming more familiar with each other.

On April 29, 1969, it was learned that Chicago BPP had formed a coalition with the Chicago chapter of SDS and the Young Lords (Youth Lords).

The Young Lords is a Chicago youth gang consisting of approximately 55 males of Puerto Rican origin between the ages of 14 and 20 years, located on Chicago’s Near North Side.

On May 6, 1969, SDS members, BPP members, and members of the Latin Kings and Latin Queens, Puerto Rican street gangs on Chicago’s Near North Side, joined the Young Lords in demonstrating against Chicago police in connection with the shooting of a Puerto Rican youth on May 4, 1969, by an off-duty Chicago police officer.

B. Chicago BPP and Blackstone Rangers (BR)

Blackstone Rangers (BR) is a Negro youth gang located on Chicago’s South Side.
