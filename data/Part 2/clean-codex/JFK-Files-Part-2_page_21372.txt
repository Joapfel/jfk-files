Memorandum

To: Director, FBI (100-398547). Date: March 1, 1963.

From: SAC, New York (100-112920)(P).

Subject: Angela Prince Moutsos, aka SM-C.

(00: New York)

Rerep of SA John H. Sheehan, dated June 29, 1962, at New York.

Subject, who is on the SI of the NYO, departed from New York on December 31, 1960, for Havana, Cuba. At that time she indicated she was to join her husband, Pedros Moutsos, in Cuba and remain there for two years. It is noted that the subject's husband, Pedros Moutsos (Budfile 105-30972), was a source utilized by the CIA as of June 1960.

The Bureau is requested to alert CIA sources for any information concerning subject's activities and residence in Cuba.
