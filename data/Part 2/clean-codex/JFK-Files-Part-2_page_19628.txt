From: Baltimore (100-28415)

Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971. IS-MISC.

Instant date, Sergeant Harwood Burritt, Inspectional Services Division, Baltimore City Police Department, advised on April 9 next at 8:00 p.m. individuals associated with the Baltimore Women's Liberation Movement, a publicly described women's rights organization, plan to hold a meeting at 3028 Greenmount Avenue, Baltimore. Purpose of this meeting is to discuss possibility of individuals from Baltimore traveling to Washington, D.C., on April 10 next to take part in Women's March on Pentagon. Sergeant Burritt advised to date, it is not known if individuals from Baltimore will actually take part in the march, and if so, the number involved or mode of travel to Washington, D.C.
