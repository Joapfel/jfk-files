Biographical Information.

Name: Mr. Robert Prontiss Wheeler. Grade: GS-15. Service designation: DI. Date and place of birth: 4 October 1915, Fitzgerald, Georgia. Marital status: Married; six children.

Education and career outside of Agency: 1934–1937, Mercer University; 1941–1942, University of California; 1942–1943, University of Colorado; 1944–1946, George Washington University, M.A., International Relations; 1941–1942, University of California, Rector in Political Science (part-time); 1942–1946, U.S. Navy, Language Officer, Intelligence Specialist, Lt.; 1946–1952, War Dept., GRI, FRC, Tokyo, Intelligence Specialist.

Language proficiency: Japanese—Fair.

CIA experience: EOD CIA Sep 1952, Acting Chief, FI, concurrently Chief of Liaison, Tokyo Station; Jun 1956, Deputy Chief, FE/1; Mar 1957, Acting Chief, FE/1; Dec 1957, Chief, FE/P0/FI, FE Division.

CIA training: Operations Course, Clandestine Methods & Techniques, Staff Indoctrination, Secret Writing, Documentation, Clandestine Services Review, CIA Review, Special Management Course, Reading Techniques.
