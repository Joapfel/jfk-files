Persons/entities: Lansdale, Gilpatric, Bundy, McCone, Johnson, Taylor (Taylord), RFK (RFk), Parrot. Date: 14 Nov 62. Category: MONGODB.

Occurrence:

Lansdale states, “On October 30–31 for an ‘Eyes Only’ background memorandum requested by Roswell Gilpatric, information was requested from CIA on the current status of all operations. CIA reported all militant operations have been ordered held at a stop, although the volatile Cubans were frustrated and not under complete control. The Matahambre operation was not mentioned by the CIA in reporting on operational teams inside Cuba, although I asked for specifics on militant teams inside. Two action teams CIA reported as being inside Cuba were described as in place only collecting intelligence, not actions (sabotage, etc.).”

Note: A handwritten note next to the above statement by Lansdale reads, “Harry Chever say he did not ask.”

Source: 14 Nov 62 special memo for the Special Group Augmented, prepared by Brig. Gen. Lansdale (00761).

Staff: PGW 6/30.
