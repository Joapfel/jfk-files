Termination of the CFA—the make was grouped slow and undramatic. Rovelli, in May 1967, reported that the groups which had been delivered to Cuba in May had arrived in Cuba. In April, an unclassified three-man team had then departed in June to influence the plan. In future reports, p. 845.

During September 1962, Ronzelli (Rovelli) related to Harry that de l’Unive was now readying a second team who were supposedly military men, whose assignment was to penetrate Castro’s bodyguard. During this period, the “medicine” was introduced and still in place, and the three men of the first team were safe. L1967 Des. Report, p. 57.

However, the second team never made Cuba, and a series of alibis were constructed during December and July 1963. Nevertheless, de l’Unive supposedly collected $370 for the maintenance of these individuals, Ronzelli (Rovelli) having received the payment from Harry. L1967 F.O. Report, p. 5-2.
