Date: June 6, 1969.

On June 4, 1969, SAS JOHN H. HOODSON, III (Sas Jon W. Hodson, III), SCOTT, and SHELDON ROBINSON, JR., arrested LOCKERT R. DILLON on the sidewalk in front of 2350 West Madison. This address houses the offices of the Chicago Chapter of the Black Panther Party (BPP), and DILLON was about to enter same at the time he was arrested.

DILLON was subsequently transported by Bureau automobile to the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) office at 219 South Dearborn, where he was photographed, fingerprinted, and provided with a printed warning and waiver form, which he read but declined to sign.

DILLON was interviewed by SAS JOHN H. HOODSON, III (HOODLEY), JESUITE T. COLLINS (JESUIT T. COLLINS), and Joseph F. STURTEVAN regarding his association with the BPP, and he advised as follows:

DILLON stated he had been a member of the BPP for about four or five months. He then amended his statement regarding his membership with the BPP and said, “I am merely a friend of the party and work for them.” He stated, however, that he had never formally joined the BPP. DILLON stated that at the time of his arrest he was about to enter the offices of the BPP to help supervise their “breakfast for children program.” He stated that his duties were to set tables and help serve the children as they were being fed. He stated that the BPP tried to help the people in the neighborhood and he felt it was a very good and worthwhile organization. He advised that he had never seen any guns or other weapons in the BPP headquarters and that he had never seen a member of the BPP carrying a gun on his person.

DILLON was shown a photograph of GEORGE SAMS, Jr., and stated he did not know SAM by name or photograph. DILLON advised he had been in the BPP headquarters several times during the previous evening, but again denied having seen SAM on the premises.

On June 4, 1969, at Chicago, Illinois.

File No. CG.

SAS JOHN H. HOODSON, III, SCOTT, JOSEPH R. ROBINSON, JR. (SHELDON ROBINSON, JR.), JESUITE T. COLLINS, by Joseph F. STURTEVAN, Jr.
