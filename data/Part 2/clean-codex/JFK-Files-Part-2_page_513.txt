30 November 1966. 49724. Date distributed: 8 December 1966.

1. On 21 November, LILING-3 reported one Fundora or Fundidor (FN and matronymic unknown), who is official in Havana in charge of foreign relations for the Cuban Communist Party, stated in mid-November that Cuban-Mexican relations were hanging by a thread. According to LILING-3, this person performs liaison with all foreign Communist parties. Defer any dissemination headquarters in view of lack of detail and identity of Cuban official.

2. Ambassador only informed of Cuban's statement.
