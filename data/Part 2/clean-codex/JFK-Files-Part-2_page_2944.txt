29 June 1962 McCone meets with the Bureau of the Budget to review the projected intelligence community budget.

10 July 1962 McCone meets with Frank G. Wisner on the latter's future.

12 July 1962 McCone discusses consultant status with Allen W. Dulles.

20 July 1962 President Kennedy is briefed in detail on information derived from Anatole Golitsyn (Golitsen).

27 July 1962 McCone becomes immersed in the nuclear testing issue and the role of intelligence in the event of a treaty banning nuclear testing in the atmosphere.

28 July 1962 McCone meets with Prince Souvanna Phouma of Laos.

10 August 1962 At a Cuban meeting, McCone makes the first explicit statement outside the Agency on the possibility of offensive missiles in Cuba.

10 August 1962 Najeeb Halaby (Halaky) proposes covert development of supersonic transport.

21 August 1962 McCone warns senior government officials about offensive missiles in Cuba.
