Reference Washington Field Office letter to the Bureau dated 5/31/72.

A review of the Denver indices revealed the following individuals had files pertaining to them:

Rita Mae Brown  
Lee Schwing

The following information was furnished to Denver (31-4480) from the Sacramento Police Department in a letter dated 10/26/70:

Rita Mae Brown, born 9/24/47 at Pine Ridge, South Dakota, 5 feet tall, 100 pounds, dark brown hair, brown eyes, listed as Sioux Indian and part Negro, was arrested in Sacramento, California, by the Sacramento Police Department on 6/19/70 at 1910 5th Street, Apartment 9, engaged in an act of prostitution for which she received $10.00. She was fined $125.00 or ten days in the county jail. Jail sentence suspended for three years. Brown’s former address is listed as 940 Hays Street, San Francisco, California.

FBI Identification Number 379078F reflects Brown was arrested by the Denver Police Department, Denver, Colorado, on 9/2/65, 9/28/65, and 10/12/66, for investigation of prostitution. The FBI Identification Record further indicates that Brown was also arrested on 9/28/68 in Denver, Colorado, for investigation of prostitution and on 6/27/70 by the Sacramento, California, Police Department for investigation of prostitution.
