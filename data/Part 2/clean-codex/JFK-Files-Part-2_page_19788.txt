Re: Abortion Project Coalition  
Cleveland, Ohio

On December 3, 1971, a second reliable source advised that during a meeting of the Cleveland Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) on November 21, 1971, Robbie Scheer stated that about 5,000 women attended the Women's Liberation March in Washington, D.C., on November 20, 1971, including a bus of approximately 40 women from Cleveland. The Cleveland YSA, according to the source, considered 40 a good number for the first women's liberation march.

Young Socialist Alliance (YSA)  
Cleveland, Ohio

The Cleveland branch of the YSA is the youth group of the SWP in Cleveland.

On December 22, 1971, the above source advised that on December 21, 1971, Robbie Scheer had stated that there were some intersession classes being held at CWRU, Cleveland, Ohio, by the APC. These are three classes in Women's Liberation: one on Abortion, one on Women's Literature, and one on Psychological Aspects of the Women's Liberation Movement.

On December 22, 1971, a reliable source advised that about 2,000–2,500 persons attended the march and rally for abortion law reform on November 20, 1971, in Washington, D.C., of which approximately 80% were female and 20% were male. The source stated that the march was fairly orderly and noted only one minor incident, which occurred at a theater along the route that was showing the movie “The Sex Machine.” There was no destruction and no arrests were made.
