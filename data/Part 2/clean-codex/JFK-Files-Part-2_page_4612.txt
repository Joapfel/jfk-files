Dispatch. To: Chief, Division. From: Chief of State, JANE. Processing: Proposed action accomplished; MARPC for indexing; no indexing required. Only qualified headquarters desk can do indexing. Abstract. Subject: Operational/JANE/ANCT Status Report. Microfilm.

Action required: References. Action required: For information and files.

1. Following is a brief summary of JMAV/KUDSK current assets in the Target Area (Cuba):

AMCT-34: Subject is a source of operational intelligence in Habana. We are maintaining contact via S.O. Subject had a W/T set, cached it and no longer has access to it. A.R.Y-1 has been instructed to deliver another one to him.

AMCT-168: Subject is a source of operational intelligence in Oriente Province. We are in contact with Subject via a cutout at the Guantanamo Naval Base.

AMCT-106, 107 and 108: These three Subjects are sisters. They provide AMCT-34 with operational intelligence in Habana.

Holando MANGUARTE Julia: Subject is an AMCT sub source in Pinar del Rio (Minar del Rio). Subject is currently reorganising his assets in that Province.

José Manuel FERNÁNDEZ (FERNANDÉZ) Quintana: Subject is an AMCT sub source in the Port of Habana.

Violeta MACIAS is an AMCT live drop in Habana and is still active.

Norberto MARTINEZ is an AMCT sub source and is active in Habana and Pinar del Rio (Minar del Rio) Provinces.

2. JMAV/KUDSK current and future plans are to continue to support and direct the existing assets it has in Cuba as outlined in paragraph 1 above. Moreover, plans are now in motion for the following:

Exfiltrate, either legally or black, MANGUARTE and MARTINEZ for further training and return to the target area.

Distribution: 3- WND. Date typed: 22 May 61. Dispatch symbol and number: UFGA-1639. Headquarters file number: 19-5-3.

E. Originating.
