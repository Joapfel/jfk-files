A second source who has provided reliable information in the past on 2/12/72 identified over thirty members of the Socialist Workers Party (SWP), Boston, and Carol Lipman, a leader in the SWP, and WONAAC, formerly of the SWP, Boston, and now believed located in NYC, as having been in attendance at the opening rally of the 2/11–13/72 WONAAC convention at Boston University, which rally was held in Hayden Hall, Boston University, the evening of 2/11 last.

The Boston SWP is the local affiliate and followed the aims and purposes of the national SWP. The SWP has been designated pursuant to Executive Order 10450.

Re Boston tel 2/12/72.

Sources are James Cunningham, Boston University Campus Police, and BS 808-S.

Other sources scheduled to attend the above convention have not made contact this date with Boston Office, probably due to security reasons.

Bureau will be kept cognizant of any additional information developed. Boston (BOSTM) following.
