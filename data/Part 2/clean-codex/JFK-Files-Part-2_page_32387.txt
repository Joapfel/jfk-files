Early January 1962

Habana (Nabana), c/a 7 January 62

BUJ 316/36-6-62

19 January 1962

BUJ-43-62

1. Source reports an armament or stockpiling of war materiel at Casablanca, a Habana (Hombna) District, northeast shore of Habana (Hombna) Bay, Habana (Hombna) Province.

2. Source has heard this materiel is targeted for an invasion of South America.
