September 26, 1975

To: John A. Mintz, Assistant Director, Legal Counsel Division, Federal Bureau of Investigation

From: Michael E. Shaheen, Jr., Special Counsel for Intelligence Coordination

Subject: Senate Select Committee Request

Attached for your attention and prompt response is another letter of request from the Senate Select Committee.

You will note that the Committee asks that response to the attached request be readied for Monday, September 29, 1975.

cc: Paul Daly
