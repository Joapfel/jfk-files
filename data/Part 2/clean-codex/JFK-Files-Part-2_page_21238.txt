3. DOMENICO CANALA S

On January 13 and 30, April 10, and August 1, 1968, NOSENKO furnished information pertaining to this case. On January 13 (N-97), he stated that ALEKSANDR STEPANOVICH MALYugin was working with agent “AMICO” (PIETRO CECCHI), an Italian cook working in Spaso House. He said that MALYugin had another Italian agent who was working in the Argentine Embassy. On January 30 (N-114), NOSENKO stated that MALYugin recruited an Italian cook of the Argentine Embassy, a friend of PIETRO CECCHI, in the late 1950s. About 1960 or the beginning of 1961 this agent left Moscow for New York to work as a cook.
