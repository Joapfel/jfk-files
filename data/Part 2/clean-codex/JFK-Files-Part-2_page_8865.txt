Confidential. Info.

Cite DIR 85387.

To multi-addressee cable info.

Perpetual ZRPRIMA.

Reference: DIR 22218.

1. Recently concluded headquarters review of ZRPRIMA program shows that KUBARK efforts have not achieved any significant results and continuation of program as such is no longer warranted. Main reason for KUBARK failure is that Fidel's Cuban technician recruiting program has been a miserable failure when measured against original objective of 2,000 techs from Western Europe alone. Though our figures are not complete, we estimate probably less than fifty candidates have received contract approval and we are able to confirm about ten who have actually made it to the island. KUBARK succeeded in inserting about thirty recruited techs into the various hiring channels, but so far only two have received contract approval from HAVA.

2. Cuban failure to recruit in significant numbers appears due largely to reluctance of Foreign Ministry and Foreign Service officers to pursue tech recruiting mission aggressively, bureaucratic incompetence in Havana, and fear of infiltration by hostile agents.
