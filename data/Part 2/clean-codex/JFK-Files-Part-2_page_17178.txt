12/14/69

Teletype.

To: Director and SACs, Washington Field and San Francisco.

From: SAC, Chicago.

New National Mobilization Committee Against the War in Viet Nam, demonstrations in Washington, D.C., November thirteen-fifteen, nineteen sixty-nine.

Black Panther Party (BPP). RM-BPP.

Source, reliable, advised instant, Fred Hampton, deputy chairman, Illinois chapter, BPP, informed Richard Levy, from Washington, D.C., not further identified, that he, Hampton, would not be in Washington, D.C., on November fifteen next.

Hampton advised Levy, BPP Central Committee, National Headquarters, did not approve BPP participation in any Washington, D.C., moratorium activities November fifteen next. Levy reportedly surprised at Hampton's attitude and was referred by Hampton to David Hilliard.
