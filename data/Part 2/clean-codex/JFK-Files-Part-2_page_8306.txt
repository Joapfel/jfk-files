Athens 588, October 13, 1950, 769.00/10-1350.

Activities of Dimiter Adamov Dimitrov (“Diko”), a Bulgarian refugee presently in Greece.

Dimitar Adamov Dimitrov, a young Bulgarian refugee known as “Diko,” seems to be the most active Bulgarian now in Greece. His youth, energy and capabilities indicate that he might become a figure of importance in Bulgarian émigré circles. This report on him is divided into the following sections:

1. Biography

The following biographic details were supplied by Dimitrev (Dimitrov) himself and cannot be checked in Athens.

Dimitar Adamov Dimitrov was born on May 7, 1924, in the village of Medkovets, district of Lom, Bulgaria. His father was a farmer. His primary education was completed in Lom. He finished his high school training in 1941–1942 in Sofia, and studied philosophy for one year at Sofia University (1942–1943).

In 1942 Diko joined a secret branch of the Bulgarian National Agrarian Union. In 1943, he claims to have begun underground activities, due to persecution by “Fascists.” He claims to have worked closely with Dr. G. M. Dimitrov during that time. This claim is partially substantiated by letters written to Diko by Dr. G. M. Dimitrov in 1947.

On September 2, 1944, Diko emerged from hiding to begin work with the Mashanov, Guitchev, Moraviev Agrarians. After the events of September 9, 1944, however, he again went into hiding and began activities aimed against Communists.
