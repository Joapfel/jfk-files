INS File Reviews

A. Benny Binion

File No. A8 952 077  
Date of birth: 11/20/04  
Place of birth: Pilot Grove, Texas  
FBI No. 206 216

General Information:

Took over policy in Dallas after Freiden was killed in 1936—former boss of Dallas million-dollar policy racket; had 40% of Herbert Noble’s take. In protection racket, he had trouble with Noble; they were bitter enemies. There were seven attempts to kill Noble. Noble’s wife was killed 11/29/49 when she started her car.

A DPD lieutenant reportedly tapped phone calls and miked a room. BB supposedly knew who killed Noble’s wife and would have him killed in jail as a goodwill gesture if Noble let BB back into Dallas rackets. BB fled Dallas in 1946.

Record: Charged in Freiden killing but released 12/28/36. Charged with concealed weapon 9/30/32. Convicted of homicide of bootlegger 5/25/32; received suspended sentence.

12/6/53 news clip: BB pays $516,541.93 in back taxes. Still received a five-year sentence on tax evasion charge.

1956 investigation satisfies INS that BB is a U.S. citizen through birth, as he claimed, and will not be deported.

7/8/55 INS memo: BB currently a prisoner at Leavenworth.

BB’s file also contains reference to investigation of Harry Remer Urban. Date of birth: 9/9/06; Dallas.
