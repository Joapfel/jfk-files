Attachment

23 May 1973

4 February 1972: Call from Jim Taylor, OMB, regarding “Bud” Krogh and his deputy, Walter Minnick, of the Domestic Council, who planned foreign travel in connection with their narcotics interests. Jim was alerting us to their intention to ask us for funds for the travel.

7 February 1972: Follow-up from Taylor: plan 3–5 trips for 3 individuals, all in FY 72. Cost probably $10K. Minnick ready to make first trip. Iden 32 is plugged in. ExDir said okay regarding Tehran visit.

7 February 1972: See attached memo from Egill Krogh, Jr., to Bill Colby and follow-on memos from Seymour Bolton (21 July 1972) and Colby (2 August 1972).

12(?) May 1972: John Hurley, OMB, called, mentioning possible Krogh/Minnick attendance at a Latin American Iden 102 conference on narcotics. He also said Jeff Shepherd, White House, was laying on a trip with Iden 32 for himself, Shepherd, and Mark Alger, OMB, to Europe.

23 June 1972: Iden 3 commented on Krogh/Minnick interest in getting CA activities, including large-scale PM, on narcotics front. Iden 3 pushing small-scale PP.

6 July 1972: Iden 3 called. Notes say only: “Minnick-Hurley film. We will send.”

2 August 1972: Iden 3 called regarding Colby letter (attached). Said travel orders ready; need money this PM. No record in my notes as to who was traveling when, but have faint recollection of its being to Mexico City. Sam Hines and Iden 73 contacted by Iden 3.

1 November 1972: Related? Iden 3 call. Again notes are cryptic: “WH/GAO-Grace (Iden 32): Survey ENDD problems/Mexico.”
