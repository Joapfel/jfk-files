April 20, 1972.

Teletype Section.

MRG19 WF coded. 6:37 p.m. Nitel, April 20, 1972, ALM.

To Director.

Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division, New York.

From Washington Field (100-55420), 2P.

Proposed demonstration at the residence of the Apostolic Delegate to the United States, Washington, D.C. (WDC), sponsored by Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (CONAAC), May 3, 1972; internal security. Communist. (Trotskyist.)

On April 19, a source, reliable in the past, advised that Trotskyist members of WOMANC (WONAAC) have proposed to demonstrate on May 3 at the “Vatican Embassy” in WDC, presumably in reference to the residence of the Apostolic Delegate to the United States, 3339 Massachusetts Avenue, Northwest, WDC. Time of the demonstration and number of participants were not obtained.

EX-112. REC-33.

May 2, 1972.

Source stated the “Trotskyists” referred to are members of the Socialist Workers Party (WSP).
