Stockholm Conference on Vietnam (SCV). IS-MISC; VIDEM.

On 11/10/70, (WF 1777-S) received via mail an official invitation to the SCV-sponsored World Conference on Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia, which will be held at Eriksdalsskolan, Ringvagen 66, Stockholm, Sweden, 11/28–30/70. A preparatory meeting will be held at 6 p.m., Friday, November 27th. The opening plenary session will take place on Saturday morning, 11/28/70.

Bureau authority is requested to send (WF 1777-S) to the World Conference and to advance the source the sum of $775 for anticipated expenses, a strict accounting of which would be made upon the source's return. Anticipated expenses are: round-trip airfare at $596.60; registration fee, $20.00; incidental expenses, including board and room, at $158.40.

If granted, the source would depart Kennedy International Airport, New York City, at 8:20 p.m., 11/26/70, via Scandinavian Airlines System, Inc., and arrive in Stockholm, Sweden, at 11:10 a.m., 11/27/70. Departure from Stockholm would be on 12/1/70, consistent with SAS schedules.
