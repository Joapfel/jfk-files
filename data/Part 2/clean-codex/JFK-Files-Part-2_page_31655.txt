Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 5/19/66

Mr. Robert F. Leveroni, Chief Clerk, Exchange Bank, Main Office, 4th and Mendocino (Mendecino) Avenue, Santa Rosa, California, advised that he personally checked the open and closed accounts of the main Exchange Bank's master indices under the name Richard Clayton Ivey and could not locate this name in the records.

Mr. Leveroni also checked the following branches of the Exchange Bank for an open or closed account in the name of Ivey, without results:

Montgomery Village Branch, Santa Rosa  
Roseland Branch, Santa Rosa  
College Avenue Branch, Santa Rosa

Mr. Leveroni advised that when he checked the Rohnert Park Branch, Rohnert Park, California, he was advised by James W. Harris, Manager, that he personally opened a savings account in the name of Richard Clayton Ivey and Dorinda D. Ivey. The account number is 28-04474. Harris further advised that he was personally acquainted with Richard Clayton Ivey and was the person who witnessed Ivey's signature.

On 5/13/66 at Santa Rosa, California. File No. SF 14-207.

SA Merle R. Adams/p&e. Date dictated: 5/13/66.
