Persons/entities: Lodge; Department of State; Monaharra; Taylor. Date: 5 October 1963. Category: Diem.

On 5 October 1963, in a states cable to Saigon (DEPTEL534), it was stated as a fourth instruction that the Ambassador should continue his policy of “cool correctness” toward GPN.

Source: IG Report 1967 (CIA), p. 30.

Persons/entities: Kennedy, John F.; Lodge, Henry Cabot; White House (WHITEHOUSE). Date: October 5, 1963. Category: Diem.

In a White House cable to Saigon (CAP63560) of 5 October 1963, the following message appears:

“...President today approved recommendation that no initiative should be taken to give any active, covert encouragement to a coup. It should be urgent covert effort with closest security under broad guidance of Ambassador to identify and build contacts with possible alternative leadership as it appears....”

Source: IG Report 1967 (CIA), p. 30.
