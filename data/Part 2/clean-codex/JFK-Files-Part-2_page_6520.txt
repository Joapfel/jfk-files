(a) Existing officer military personnel to be schooled in Panama and/or the United States.

(b) Police personnel to be schooled in Panama or the United States.

(c) Newspaper personnel to be schooled within the United States.

(d) Nurses (male and female) to be trained in the United States.

(e) Civilian technicians to be trained in United States training schools.

The thrust of such an expanded training program, taking place in the United States, is not just to enhance the skill and education level of the people involved but, even more, to expose the Latin to U.S. influence, to show them our country, to persuade them by close association that our view is correct, and thus to stiffen their resolve to combat subversion in any form.

d. Troop Information Program

Expand greatly the current Latin American troop information program in order to educate the Latin American servicemen (and their civilian contemporaries) to the subversive danger they face.

e. News and Propaganda

(a) Underwrite, as necessary, and conduct an information program within each country in Latin America using these themes:

(a) An exposition of facts on subversive training, movement of subversives, and general subversive programs.

(b) Objectives of the Communists within each country.

(c) Communist methods of operation.
