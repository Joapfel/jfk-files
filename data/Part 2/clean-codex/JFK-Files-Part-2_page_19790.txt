Memorandum

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Indianapolis (100-24006) (C)

Subject: Bloomington (Boomington) Abortion Coalition  
IS-C (Trotskyist)

Date: 1/24/72

ReIPtel 11/18/71 captioned “Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (Women’s National Abortion Coalition) (WONAAC) Sponsored Demonstration 11/20/71 Washington, D.C., IS-New Left. OO: WFO.”

Enclosed for the Bureau are six copies of LHM re captioned Bloomington (Boomington) Abortion Coalition (BAC).

First confidential source is IP 3274-S; second confidential source is IP 3828-PSI.

For the information of the Bureau, captioned organization was formed primarily for the purpose of getting people to travel to Washington, D.C., for the demonstration on 11/20/71 sponsored by the Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (Women’s National Abortion Coalition) (WONAAC). The main organization for the BAC was on the part of two female YSA members at Indiana University, Bloomington, Indiana. The organization does not meet regularly and is not presently a continuing organization and has not met since the demonstration in Washington, D.C. In view of the fact that the organization is not a continuing organization, no further investigation is being conducted by the Indianapolis (Indiam polis) Division into this matter and it is being placed in a closed status.
