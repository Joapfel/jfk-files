Request for Information & Review, 5 November 1964.

POA granted, 13 November 1964.

8 October 1964.

Report of Cover Assessment from Assessment & Evaluation Staff, Medical Staff. Panegue, one of six members of the “radio operator” trainee group (three radio operators), was given psychological assessments in August 1964. Panegue is the oldest (date of birth: 18 September 1920) and least intelligent, but most experienced, of the group. Likely he would try to dominate the group because of his vast experience. His shortcomings are thought to outweigh his assets.

17 November 1964. Classified Message 1622582.

Panegue assigned AMRUG-5.

19 December 1964.

Memorandum of Oral Commitment. Panegue engaged under oral contract in December 1964. To receive $275.28 per month following completion of satisfactory release. Will receive a raise of $50 per month upon completion of training and a $50 raise upon infiltration. Possibly an infiltration of Oriente Province; see item 13.
