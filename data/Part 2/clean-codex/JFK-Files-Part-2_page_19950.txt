Demonstration by Jeannette Rankin Brigade on January Eight next. Information concerning—internal security.

Rew FOTEL dated November Twenty-Eight, past captioned as above. WFO 1777-S advised this date Jeannette Rankin Brigade is a “one shot” organization formed by women in various cities to hold demonstrations “all over” the United States on January Eight next. Brigade founded in response to statement by former Congresswoman Jeannette Rankin to effect that if large numbers of women were arrested for protesting war it would “bring the war machine to a halt.” Above source states much bickering between New York and WDC groups of Women Strike for Peace concerning this organization. Source states civil disobedience being planned for the AERAIR. Source attempting to obtain further details. LHM follows.

Approved: Special Agent in Charge
