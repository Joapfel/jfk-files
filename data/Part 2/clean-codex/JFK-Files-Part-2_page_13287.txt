June 4, 1975.

Teletype. NR 025 AT CODED. 8:08 PM NITEL, June 4, 1975 JWS.

To: Director, FBI (62-116395).

From: Atlanta (62-2854).

Attention: INTD W. D. Cregar.

SENSTUDY ’75.

On 6/4/75, former agent Richard H. Davis, 1147 Wild Creek Trail, N.E., Atlanta, Ga., advised he was contacted this date by (FNU) Epstein, whom he believed was calling from Washington, D.C. Davis stated he feels Epstein is a member of the Senate Select Committee since inquiry concerned Davis’ knowledge of Martin Luther King case. Epstein stated he wished to interview Davis either in Atlanta or Washington, D.C.; however, Davis said that no definite arrangements were made during conversation other than he would contact Epstein as to an appropriate date for interview in Atlanta.

Davis was informed he could contact the Office of Legal Counsel collect for assistance in this matter. Davis stated he would do so.

End.
