022135Z. CITE MEXICO CITY 9097. Director info JMWAVE.

KAPOK TYPIC LIRING.

Ref: MEXICO CITY 90!! (IN 27211).

2 Nov 65, 31465.

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace B. Rowton on the evening of 1 Nov 66 that the only significant information Reynaldo Cepeda Hernandez had given him in the way of briefing for the forthcoming trip was that LIRING-3 would be met at Havana (HAVA) Airport by one Herrera (FNU), who would put him in contact with the proper people.

2. The flight originally scheduled for 2 Nov was first postponed to 3 Nov, then set back to 4 Nov. A strike of Mexicana airline employees may cause further delay, since Mexicana services Cubana.

3. LIRING-2 and Cepeda met with LIRING-3 on 26 Oct, as reported in the reference, but no significant information resulted. LIRING-3 said it appeared the meeting was called to impress him with the importance of Cepeda and LIRING-2. Cepeda did all the talking. The ambassador dropped in, greeted LIRING-3 cordially, but left. Cepeda prefaced the meeting with a lengthy lecture on Cuba vis-à-vis the Tricontinental. The point made was that Cuba was independent of the activities of this organization but directly involved in some aspects due to its geographic location.
