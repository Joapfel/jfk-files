Demonstrating U.S. Policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C.

January 15, 1968

We March

To support the thousands of American women assembled today in Washington, D.C.

Led by former Congressman Jeannette Rankin, they are petitioning Congress:

- To end the war in Vietnam now.
- To arrange for complete withdrawal of American troops.

We Wear Black

- To protest the destruction of life—American and Vietnamese.
- To protest the devastation of Vietnamese country and culture.
- To protest the atmosphere of violence in which our children are being raised.

We Pledge

The strength of our collective political power to nominate and elect candidates who will work for peace in 1968.

Notify your present Congressional Representatives and Senators of your agreement with these aims by signing this page and mailing to them in Washington, D.C.
