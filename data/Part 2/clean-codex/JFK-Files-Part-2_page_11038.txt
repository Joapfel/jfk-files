M. AMERICAN LEGION CONTACTS

The SAC or ASAC shall contact at least once each year all national officers, departmental (state) officers, and members of the Americanism Commission of The American Legion. A letter shall be submitted on March 15 of each year showing that the above American Legion officials have been contacted.
