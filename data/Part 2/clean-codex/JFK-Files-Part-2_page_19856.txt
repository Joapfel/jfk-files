April 20, 1972.

MR019 WF coded, 6:37 p.m., NITEL 4-20-72 ALM.

To Director.

Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division, New York.

From Washington Field (100-55429) 2F.

Proposed demonstration at the residence of the Apostolic Delegate to the United States, Washington, D.C. (WDC), sponsored by Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WOMAC), May 3, 1972; Internal Security—C (Trotskyist).

Communist.

On April 19, a source, reliable in the past, advised that Trotskyist members of WOMAC have proposed to demonstrate on May 3 at the “Vatican Embassy” in WDC, presumably in reference to the residence of the Apostolic Delegate to the United States, 3339 Massachusetts Avenue, Northwest, WDC. Time of the demonstration and number of participants was not obtained.

Source stated the “Trotskyists” referred to are members of the Socialist Workers Party (WSP).
