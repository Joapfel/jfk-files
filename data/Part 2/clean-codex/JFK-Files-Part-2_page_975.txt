TSS/PB/Training Division Evaluation. Darkroom G. Basic Photography No. 1.

Name: Jesus P.M. Division: CRJ BR FA. Dates trained: from 23 June to 17 July ’79.

This course is primarily designed to develop the following skills: use of 35 mm cameras with accessories, document copy, processing and printing.

I. Manipulation of camera: Good.  
a. Leica: Good.  
b. Retina II C: None.  
c. Recordak: Good.

II. Processing and printing: Good.  
a. Film loading: Good.  
b. Film processing: Good.  
c. Enlarging: Good.  
d. Reflex and contact printing: Excellent.

III. Use of accessory equipment.  
a. Exposure meter: Good.  
b. Filters: Unsatisfactory.  
c. Telephoto and wide angle lenses: Good.

IV. Document copy and small objects.  
a. Available light: Unsatisfactory.  
b. Accessory illumination: Good.  
c. HOOWU, Portra lens, focus slide: Good.

V. Ground photography.  
a. Coverage.  
b. Report: Unsatisfactory.

VI. Casing.  
a. Coverage.  
b. Report.

VII. Surveillance.
