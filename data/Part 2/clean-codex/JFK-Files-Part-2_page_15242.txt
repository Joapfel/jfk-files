Office Memorandum, United States Government

To: Mr. J. Edgar Hoover, Director, FBI

From: Andrew F. Oehmann, Executive Assistant to the Attorney General

Subject: Technical Surveillance Coverage of Diplomatic Establishments

Date: January 31, 1962

Please refer to your memorandum of January 26, 1962, captioned as above.

The Attorney General has approved your proposal to test the potential of the increased technical surveillance referred to in your memorandum as set forth in the last two paragraphs thereof.
