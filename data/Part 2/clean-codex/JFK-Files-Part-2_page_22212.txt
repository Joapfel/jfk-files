Department of State.

Persons/entities: Lansdale; McCone; Rusk; Taylor; RFK; McNamara; Johnson; Lemnitzer; Harvey; Gilpatrick; Bundy; V.O.H. Categories: SGA MONGODB.

Occurrence: Lansdale sets forth the psychological activities to be used to carry out objective A (i.e., the discreditation and isolation of the Cuban regime).

The psychological activities include statements beamed to Cuba, Voice of America short-wave broadcasts, propaganda, cartoon books, TV outlets to Cuba, propaganda exhibits, developing specific proposals for use of balloons to deliver propaganda to Cuba, directing propaganda at Soviet and other Bloc personnel in Cuba, Voice of Free Cuba broadcasts from some submarines, making available to the International Narcotics Commission documented evidence of Cuban exportation/importation of narcotics, arranging for caches of limited Soviet Czech (check) arms to be discovered in selected Latin American countries, ostensibly smuggled in from Cuba.

Source: 8/31/62 memo from Gen. Lansdale to SGA (00669-00672).

Staff: Wallach. Date: July 3, 1975.
