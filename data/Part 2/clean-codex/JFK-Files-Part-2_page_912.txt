Section II: Specific Characteristics of the COC-052

This course lasts 10 weeks. The class meets 4 times per week—5 days a week. Students are required to do a minimum of 10 hours per week or else fail in the course. Laboratory classes of about 40 hours. The instructor is able to meet at any time by appointment with each student individually. Section III: Ceramics

The general aim of this course is to make this a throughgoing curriculum for the student who has already acquired a reading ability in the language of an insufficient introduction to the language. It is written as a text for further advancement either by independent learning in the area or by further study.

The specific objectives are as follows:

Objective 4. Ability to produce and distinguish all the sounds of the language.

Objective 5. Auditory skills, especially a series of connected sentences.

Objective 6. Accuracy in analysis of complex sentences into their components.

Objective 7. Ability to understand and remember a sketched dialogue in a wide variety of contexts.

Objective 8. Applied to the field of communication using limited factor of speed and accuracy, not for the purpose of communication.
