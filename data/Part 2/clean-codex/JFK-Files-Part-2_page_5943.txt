11 February 1993

Memorandum for: J. Barry Harrelson, JFK Project Officer, Associate Information Review Officer, DCA

Subject: CIA-IR-Ii — ARRB Request for Search on Warren Commission Critics (DA/ITC No. 21)

1. This is with reference to your 2 February 1993 memorandum (Tab A) and our subsequent telephone conversation on 6 February, wherein you requested that the Directorate of Administration (DA) search for files or records on Warren Commission critics Edward J. Epstein, Josiah Thompson, Mark Lane, Harold Weisberg, or Sylvia Meagher.

2. Based upon the limited biographic information provided on the subjects, the Office of Personnel Security (OPS), the Office of Human Resource Management (ERM), and the Office of the Publication Review Board conducted thorough searches. No records were located on Josiah Thompson or Mark Lane. As you know, OPS located a file on Harold Weisberg as a result of its search in response to CIA-IR-27 (DA/ITC No. 14). At your request, on 6 February 1993 (Tab 5), we requested that OPS make arrangements to have this file reviewed by the ARRB to determine which documents are Assassination Records. Accordingly, OPS did not address the Weisberg file in this CIA-IR-II response.

3. As a result of DA’s searches (Tab C), we located the following files and/or information:

Edward J. Epstein — ATN 0 488 768, found in OPS; file found in PAB.

Harold Weisberg — computer printout attached, found in ERM.

Sylvia Meagher — CPS advised that there was an impersonal folder regarding the subject and a 1969 Ramparts Magazine article, which were destroyed in accordance with the Records Control Schedule on 1 January 1994.
