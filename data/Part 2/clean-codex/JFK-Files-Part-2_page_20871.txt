NR023 BA PLAIN  
629 RPM NITEL 4-7-71 PWM

To Director, Alexandria; Washington Field.  
From Baltimore (100-28415).

Women's National March on the Pentagon, April Ten, Seventy One, IS-MISC.

Instant date, Sergeant Harwood Burritt, Inspectional Services Division, Baltimore City Police Department, advised on April ninth next at eight PM individuals associated with the Baltimore Women's Liberation Movement, a publicly described women's rights organization, plan to hold a meeting at 3028 Greenmount Avenue, Baltimore. Purpose of this meeting is to discuss possibility of individuals from Baltimore traveling to Washington, DC, on April tenth next to take part in Women's March on Pentagon. Sgt. Burritt advised to date, it is not known if individuals from Baltimore will actually take part in the march, and if so, the number involved or mode of travel to Washington, DC.

CC to IDIU.
