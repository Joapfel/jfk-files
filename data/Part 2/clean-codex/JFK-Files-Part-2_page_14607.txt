Memo Donahoe to Belmont

Re: Intelligence Coverage of Friendly Foreign Countries (Semiannual Evaluation)

Details

Re memo 6-25-59 evaluating captioned program and analyzing the preceding six months' results. This is a re-evaluation and report on results achieved since that date.

Origin and Purpose

Basic program inaugurated in 1954 and expanded in 1955 and 1958 to provide for acquisition of positive intelligence and investigative information through three principal facets:

1. Receipt of notification from six other agencies of arrival in the U.S. of intelligence personnel of friendly foreign governments.

2. At specific request or with specific approval of the Deputy Under Secretary of State or official designated by him, maintenance of technical surveillances on official establishments in the United States of countries located in trouble areas.

3. With prior State Department approval, development of live sources within establishments of friendly countries, as Bureau's intelligence and investigative requirements demand extension of coverage.

Scope and Cost

1. Under the first category, the Bureau receives notification from other intelligence agencies of the arrival of intelligence personnel in the U.S. Handling of these notifications is confined to the Seat of Government and principally involves review of any file references and indexing procedures at nominal costs. In appropriate instances, the field is alerted to the presence of intelligence personnel, but active investigation is not instituted in the absence of information which would form the basis for a security-type case.
