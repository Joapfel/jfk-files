2. Luis Villoro, editor of Revista de la Universidad de Mexico, also one of the organizing committee in Genoa and attending Congreso de la Comunidad Cultural de Latino America in Arica, Chile. Expects to meet a number of Genoa attendees who he thinks will want a progress report on plans for CLE organizing meeting. Villoro’s plans rather vague. Realizes Genoa meeting attended mainly by leftists and that if CLE were launched under same auspices it would be heavily weighted on the left. At the same time realizes that the only writers organization in Mexico with enough cohesion to get a meeting going would be AEMAC, which covers the whole political spectrum and which so far has successfully avoided taking political stands.

3. Although not clear to what extent plans for formation of CLE may come up during Arica meeting, a number of those in attendance likely to be the same as attended Genoa meeting and may try to steamroller commitment from Mexico (HEXI) delegation to host organizational meeting. Mexican invitees to Arica besides Villoro are Hector Azar, head of Theater Department of National Fine Arts Institute; Padre Angel Maria Garibay, leftist and winner of the National Literary Award; Hector Mendoza, unidentified; Juan Rulfo, who is not going; and Mario Monteforte Toledo, Guatemalan resident in Mexico (HEXI), who is a Marxist writer for the leftist magazine Siempre.
