Department of State. Telegram.

Page 01, Berlin 00668, 16 April 1969. Action: SCS 04. Information: EUR 17, SCA 01, VA 01, L 03, INR 07, CIAE 00, NSAE 00, P 04, USIA 12, DODE 00, PPT 02. R 161100Z April 1969.

From US Mission Berlin. To Secretary of State, Washington, 4104. Information: American Embassy Madrid; American Embassy Paris; American Embassy Rome; American Consulate Zurich.

Rome for VA.

Subject: Welfare: Richard Case Nagell. Reference: Berlin 2567.

1. Nagell has returned to Berlin. He called at the Mission April 10 and again April 14. Since he was obviously irrational, the consular officer urged him to call on an Army psychiatrist in Berlin familiar with his case, which he did April 15. The psychiatrist says he is mentally ill and should be hospitalized.

2. Nagell said he returned to Berlin because he had “connections” with local press who would publicize his “complaints” against the U.S. Government (lack of medical and dental treatment, lack of assistance in locating family in the U.S., “true” role of Washington lawyer Ricey New, etc.).

3. In view of Nagell’s history of imprisonment in East Germany, former intelligence connections, and mental condition (for which he is apparently entitled to full VA benefits), we consider Nagell’s presence in Berlin undesirable (both for himself and for the U.S. Government) and hope we can persuade him to leave of his own accord. Any information on his activities since leaving.
