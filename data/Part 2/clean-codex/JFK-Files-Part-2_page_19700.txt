Re: Chicago letter dated 8/21/72

On September 6, 1972, Lieutenant Phil Cancell[i] (PHIL CANCELLI), Mundelein, Illinois Police Department, advised that his records are negative regarding Olie Lyall Ryan.

On September 15, 1972, Sergeant Bernard Leonard, Lake County Sheriff’s Office, Waukegan, Illinois, advised his records are negative regarding Ryan.

On August 21, 1972, SC G. Pach reviewed the records of the Bureau of Records and Communications, Chicago Police Department, and found no record for Ryan.
