10:10 p.m., NITEL, 5/21/75, VAN.

To Director (62-116395).

From Alexandria (62-217) (RUC). One page.

SENSTUDY 75.

Re: Alexandria teletype to Bureau, May 3, 1975.

Former Bureau agent Harold P. Leinbaugh, 1160 Wimbledon Drive, McLean (MC CLEAN), Virginia, contacted on May 20, 1975, in accordance with Bureau instructions. Leinbaugh stated his feeling at present is that he will refuse to be interviewed by a staff member of the congressional committee, but in the event he consents to an interview and sensitive matters arise, he will request the presence of a Bureau agent or contact legal counsel at FBIHQ.
