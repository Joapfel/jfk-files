Executive Protective Service Incident Report.

Serial (Case) Number: FM-72-398. Date occurs: June 13, 1972. Home address: 405 E. Oak St., Springfield, Va. Location (Nation): Chile. Street address: 1736 Massachusetts Avenue, N.W. Nature of incident: Burglary If. Reported by: Herman Navarro. Address: 1706 Hanlet Street, Springfield, Va. Office phone: 787-274-6.

Sergeant Stevens E. Butler. Sergeant Riley M. Richardson. Witness: N/A. Address: N/A. Home phone: N/A. Office phone: N/A.

Details of incident:

At approximately 1330 hours, Monday, May 13, 1972, Foreign Missions Division-Control Center was advised by U.S.D.C., 3D, that a burglary had taken place at the Chancery of Chile, 1736 Massachusetts Avenue, N.W., sometime between 1600 hours on Friday, May 13, 1972, and 0355 hours this date.

Sergeant Riley M. Richardson and the undersigned were dispatched at approximately 1335 hours in Cruiser #23.

The undersigned was advised by Mr. Herman (M.T.) Navarro, Administrative Counselor of the Embassy of Chile, that the Chancery of Chile was entered and all the offices were ransacked. At this time the method of entry or items taken is unknown.

Mr. Navarro advises that he will contact the Foreign Missions Division-Match
