REFUTEL, 28 May 1975.

Pursuant to Bureau instructions, Miss Olga CIFSA was contacted by SAC, Chicago, on 30 May 1975 regarding captioned matter. She stated she still resides at 1949 South Indiana Avenue, Chicago, and is still employed as an executive secretary at the firm of Ernst and Ernst, attorneys, in Chicago.

She was further advised that in the event she is interviewed and during the course of same any questions were asked relative to sensitive Bureau operations, etc., she could request the assistance of an agent to be present during the interview.
