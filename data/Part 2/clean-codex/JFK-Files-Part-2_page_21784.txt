Mr. Wides. Is it your sworn testimony that you never knew of any surveillances or efforts to penetrate groups for counterintelligence purposes by Cubans under your supervision?

Mr. Sarno. No, sir, no. You're speaking of penetrating groups, yes. They penetrated groups. Surveillance—I never sent them out on a mission to surveil anybody concerning whether they were suspect or whatever.

Mr. Wides. Was there any surveillance of Cubans in Miami conducted by—whether it was in cooperation with the Bureau or not—conducted by any of the AMOTs (AMOUNTS) Cubans that you were aware of?

Mr. Sarno. I cannot recall if there ever was.

Mr. Wides. I don't mean a specific one.

Mr. Sarno. I don't recall ever giving them any assignments.

Mr. Wides. I'm not talking about assignments, I'm talking about whether you're aware of their conducting—

Mr. Sarno. As I say, I do not recall them conducting any surveillance on anybody.
