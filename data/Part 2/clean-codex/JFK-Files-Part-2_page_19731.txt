November 20, 1971.

NR 204 WF Code.

8:50 PM NITEL, 11-20-71 ALM.

To Director.

Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division.

Boston; Chicago; Cleveland; New Haven; New York.

From Washington Field (100-54927) 5P.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) sponsored demonstration, November 20, 1971, Washington, D.C. IS—New Left.

On instant date, special agents of the FBI observed the following:

At approximately 10:00 A.M., women representing various women's groups from Boston, New Haven, New York, Cleveland, Chicago, and other cities began gathering on the Ellipse in Washington, D.C. (WDC). The assembled group departed the Ellipse at approximately 12:10 P.M., at which time the group numbered about 1,500.

From the Ellipse, the group proceeded north on Seventeenth Street, Northwest, east on R Street, south on Fifteenth Street.
