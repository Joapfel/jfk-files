1/8/68

Code XDDAMNET. Teletype deferred. Sent by coded teletype.

To SAC Seattle, Reg 13 ST 119. From Director FBI (100-448475).

Demonstration protesting U.S. policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January fifteen next.

Reurtel January five last.

Authority granted for informant to attend captioned demonstration. Up to one hundred fifty dollars may be paid source for expenses incurred in making trip.

Instruct source to maintain contact with WFO and promptly furnish pertinent data developed. Seattle advise WFO code name used by informant. WFO advise Seattle name of agent to contact and phone number to be used.

Promptly debrief source on return your area and submit LHB.

Note:
