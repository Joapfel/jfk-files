SGT. DANIEL GROTH; JAMES DAVIS; JOHN CISZEWSKI; EDWARD CARMODY, et al., Chicago, Illinois police officers—subjects; FRED HAMPTON; MARK CLARK; BRENDA HARRIS; VERLINA BREWER; BLAIR ANDERSON; RONALD SATCHIEL, et al.—victims.

Summary punishment—civil rights.

Enclosed are two copies of a self-explanatory Departmental letter dated and two copies of its enclosure.

Complete the requested investigation in accordance with the provisions of Section 27, Volume II, Manual of Instructions, and submit within ten days of the receipt of this communication.

State in the first paragraph of the details of your report that it contains the results of a limited investigation and underscore the word “preliminary.”

Advise all persons interviewed.

Advise appropriate officials at the outset that this investigation is being conducted at the specific request of the Assistant Attorney General in charge of the Civil Rights Division, U.S. Department of Justice, referring to him by name as well as title.

Remarks: If victims or other persons on the premises at the time of the raid decline to be interviewed, you should immediately advise the Bureau.
