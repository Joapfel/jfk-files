14 December 1960

Cable to Director from Havana. IN 16758. Panegue reportedly under arrest at Cabana Prison and his wife at Women's Prison, on charge of aiding Escambray (Ecombray) fighters.

13 January 1961

Cable to Bell from Guatemala City. IN 1688. Alexanderande Rourke gives information that Frank Fiorini and Orlando Bosch (Boech) represent Panegue in Cuba. Fiorini and Bosch recruiting and equipping force for assault on Cuba 18 January. Receiving financial aid from Anti-Communist International Association of NYC.

14 March 1962

Dispatch to Chief, Task Force W from COS/MWAVE. Panegue organized a hunger strike by Cuban exiles (Epiles), held a rally 13 March 1962. Purpose was to show JFK that Cubans (Cebane) are willing to die in episode (exile) if their right to defend Cuba from Communism is negated. Motto of strike is “Hunger or War.”
