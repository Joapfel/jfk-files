8 October 1997

Name Information Sheet

Name: Mankel, Jose Marie Andre, also known as Mankel, Andre.

Date of birth: 1 October 1917.

EOD: Contract agent at least by March 1961.

Present residence: Unknown.

Wishes regarding cover/no cover: Unknown.

Sensitive assignments/activities:

Mankel was recruited as a ZRRIFLE agent in late 1960. The objective of the ZRRIFLE project was to procure foreign code and cipher materials and information concerning such matters. Except for a two-month temporary duty assignment (November–December 1960) to Africa, Mankel apparently operated in Europe, establishing his cover while trying to spot and assess individuals that the CIA could use in safe-cracking operations, in line with one of the principal missions of FI/D.

Agency contract terminated in February 1964.

Current activities: Unknown.

Other notes:

First Agency contact with Mankel was in 1958, through the Chief of the Luxembourg Intelligence Service, in connection with an illegal narcotics operation into the United States. For 18 months, he was contacted sporadically on behalf of the Bureau of Narcotics. The Bureau's files reflect an excellent performance by the contact.

Virtually all data on Mankel, except his true name, have been released.
