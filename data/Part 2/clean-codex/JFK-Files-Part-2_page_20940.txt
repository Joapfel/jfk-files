United States Government Memorandum.

To: Acting Director, FBI.

From: SAC, WFO (100-55290) (P).

Subject: “Furies” newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement publication, Washington, D.C. — Revolutionary Activities (00:WFO).

Date: 7/31/72.

Re: WFO letter to the Bureau, 5/31/72; Jacksonville letter to the Bureau, 6/20/72; Springfield letter to the Bureau, 7/6/72; and Atlanta letter to WFO, 7/20/72.

For information of all receiving offices, a review of WFO files indicates that two of the staff members of the captioned publication, Susan Hathaway and Natasha Byrd, have known and associated with a few of the Weatherman fugitives. Also, the subscriber to the telephone at the “Furies” address, 219 11th Street, S.E., Washington, D.C. (WDC), is Nancy Myron. Myron is employed by the Institute for Policy Studies (IPS), a self-described “New Left Think Tank.”

100-55500 (Myron). 100-55497 (Reid). 100-55498 (Schwinc).

Rev. Act./Sect.
