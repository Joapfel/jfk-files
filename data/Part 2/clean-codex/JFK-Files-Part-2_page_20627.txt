United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI

Date: 2/9/72

From: SAC, Minneapolis (100-16058) (C)

Subject: Minnesota Women's Abortion Action Coalition, aka Minneapolis Abortion Action Coalition; Minnesota Abortion Action Coalition IS-C (Trotskyist)

OO: Minneapolis

Re Bureau O-1 Form captioned “Minneapolis Abortion Action Coalition, aka Minnesota Abortion Action Coalition; IS-C (Trotskyist),” received at Minneapolis on 1/10/72.

Enclosed for the Bureau is the original and five copies of a letterhead memorandum prepared under the title “Minnesota Women's Abortion Action Coalition.”

The enclosed LHM is classified confidential because it contains information from MP T-1 through MP T-4, the unauthorized disclosure of which could reasonably result in compromising the sources' identities and impair the national defense interests of the United States.

Individuals named in the enclosed LHM are either on the ADEX or are informants or sources of the Minneapolis Office. An investigative summary report has been submitted regarding Rebecca Ani Finch with the recommendation she be placed on the ADEX. Investigation is currently being conducted concerning Jane Van Deusen, looking toward the submission of a summary report with the recommendation that she be placed on ADEX.
