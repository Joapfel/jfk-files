FBI.

Date: 3/16/71.

Airtel.

To: Director, FBI. From: SAC, New York. Subject: Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971, IS-MISC.

Enclosed for the Bureau are 6 copies and 2 copies for WFO of an LHM concerning the captioned activity.

Source used in attached LHM is informant.

This LHM is being classified “Confidential” because the unauthorized disclosure of information furnished by NY 6474-S could reveal the identity thereof and could be injurious to the national security of the United States.

The NYO will furnish any additional information regarding this matter to the Bureau and WFO.

Distribution: 2 Bureau (6 enclosures); 2 Washington Field (2 enclosures); 1 New York (100-172435), The Committee to Defend the Right to Live; 1 New York (100-164665), Women's Liberation Movement (WLM) (42); 1 New York (134-15562A) (31), REC-103; 1 New York (100-171548), Women's Conference in Canada, 3/24–4/7/71 (42).
