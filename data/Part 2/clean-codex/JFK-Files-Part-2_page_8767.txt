MEXI.6844 (IN 57745), dated 8 November 1963. Cable to headquarters. DEGRIP reported that CASTRO visited the restaurant at least once a month and was “a sitting duck.” CECCHI (CEOCHI) was reported to be a cowardly type.

MEXI 0880 (IN 73754), dated 14 September 1964. Cable to headquarters reported that CECCHI was arrested and imprisoned in La Cabana Fortress and was to be tried for plotting against the life of Fidel CASTRO. Prosecutor was to ask for a 30-year sentence. Employees of the restaurant in mid-August 1964 told the source that CECCHI was arrested for illegal currency transactions. Comment: No 201 file on CECCHI. Preliminary traces do not indicate a connection with CIA.

WAVE 0662 (IN 44282), dated 13 April 1965. In April 1965, Francisco CHAO Hermida revealed to a WAVE case officer his “grand plan,” for which he requested CIA support. The plan was to recruit trusted men in Miami, infiltrate Cuba, surveil CASTRO, and, at an opportune time, assassinate him. The case officer told CHAO that CIA was not interested and could not do business with him.
