BS 100-43809

During early March, 1972, TOBA FINGER reported that at an increasing rate abortion work was becoming a more important area of work than FL and that a number of SWP and YSA female members were being transferred into the abortion work.

BS T-1

March 26, 1972

The stress of the SWP/YSA female members in the general Boston area who are active in female matters continues to be away from FL and into BOWAAC with only two or three remaining on the fringes to follow FL activities.

BS T-1

May 10, 1972

Recent reports concerning SWP/YSA faction work within BOWAAC disclose that, as previously occurred with the Party's work within FL, those in the Party faction have apparently alienated themselves from the other females active in BOWAAC and for the most part have lost any support from others not in the Party faction. Any suggestions now offered by those of the Party faction in BOWWAC (BOWAAC), which comprises approximately half of the activists in the group, are no longer accepted, cause considerable argument and there is evidence of “red baiting”. As a result, the Party faction does not now believe that they can build BOWAAC into any successful supporter type organization as had resulted through Party work in the Student Mobilization Committee (SMC).
