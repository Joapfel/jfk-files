Letter to SAC, New York et al.

Re: Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC). 100-468406.

Note:

WONAAC has been the subject of separate investigation by numerous offices. In relet dated 2/28/73, Philadelphia stated that WONAAC represents basic social change dealing with the abortion issue and, considering the recent U.S. Supreme Court upholding the right of abortion, investigation of WONAAC should be discontinued, but activities followed through investigation of SWP and YSA. New York, office of origin in this matter, as well as other offices handling WONAAC investigations, including Philadelphia, are being advised that the Bureau agrees with observations of Philadelphia concerning the controversial abortion issue and, in the future, will report activities of WONAAC in a subheading of SWP semiannual organizational reports.
