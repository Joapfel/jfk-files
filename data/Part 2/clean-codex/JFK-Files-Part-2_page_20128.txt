7/14/72

To SACS, Detroit (100-40426); New York (100-174481).

From Acting Director, FBI (100-468406).

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) convention, July fourteen through sixteen, seventy-two, at Hunter College, New York, New York; IS-C (Trotskyist).

Redetel July thirteen, last.

Authority granted to advance DE two eight three one-S one hundred thirty-five dollars for expenses, including round-trip transportation to New York, to attend captioned conference. Obtain exact accounting of expenses from informant.

Prior to source traveling to conference, Detroit and New York coordinate identity of special agent and contact telephone number through which informant can maintain contact with New York Office. Detroit instruct informant contact New York Office to report significant developments.

REC-28.

Detroit contact informant immediately upon return and submit detailed FD-306 to New York within five days and 1-134-22865.

6 July 1972.
