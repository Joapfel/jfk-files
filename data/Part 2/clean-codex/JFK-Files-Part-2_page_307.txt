Guatemala City 6831.

Director, Mexico City.

Reference: Guatemala City 5347 (IN 6/29).

1. On the evening of 28 December, COS discussed with MINDEF Col. Arriaga the objective of three-way collaboration (COLLASRATION) on the problem of arms smuggling (AMIS SMUGGLINS) from Mexico. COS gave (DANE) an account of the results of conversations by COS and Roddy with Cols. Sosa and Chinchilla and informed Arriaga of immediate assistance to Chinchilla. Arriaga welcomed the idea of collaboration and said that, at the time of his visit to Mexico in September, Mexican defense officials had promised to strengthen military coverage on the Guatemalan border. He said that the Mexicans had complied fully with the requirement and had effective control of the whole Mexican frontier with Guatemala. When asked if he had anything additional to add after COS’s departure for Mexico, Arriaga said he would like to make it known to interested parties that the Guatemalan Army, after 1 January, would increase its forces on the border to provide equally complete coverage on this side of the frontier.

31 December 1985.
