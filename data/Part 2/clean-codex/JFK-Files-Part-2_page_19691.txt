United States Government Memorandum

To: Acting Director, FBI

From: SAC, Charlotte (100-11664) (P)

Date: 9/25/72

Subject: “Furies” Newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement Publication, Washington, D.C.; IS—Revolutionary Activity

(00: WFO)

Re WFO letter to Bureau dated 7/31/72.

On 9/1/72, a well-placed source advised that Blowing Rock, N.C., telephone number 295-3321, had been discontinued effective 5/23/72. This source stated the number was formerly listed to James W. Hathaway, Laurel Lane, Blowing Rock, N.C. Also, Hathaway was a teacher at Appalachian State University, Boone, N.C.

On 9/5/72, Douglas Townsend, Chief of Police, Blowing Rock, N.C., advised that James Hathaway, who resided on Laurel Lane (Laural Lane) in Blowing Rock, was associated with Appalachian State University in Boone as a college professor and his wife was a former movie actress. Chief Townsend stated that the Hathaways have some sort of “connection” or relatives in Italy and that they had left Blowing Rock some months ago, but just recently returned and are planning on opening a restaurant in the Blowing Rock area in the future.

Chief Townsend stated that he had no record of the Hathaways as having been arrested for any crime in the Blowing Rock area.

Bureau (RH); WFO (100-55290) (RM); Charlotte.
