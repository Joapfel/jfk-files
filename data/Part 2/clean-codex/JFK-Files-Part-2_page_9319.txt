Free Breakfast Program for Children

Huey P. Newton (ey P. Newton),

The National Committee to Combat Fascism, which is a political organizing bureau of the Black Panther Party, has set up in Flint a free breakfast program for children in the community. The breakfast is set up to bring the children and the community together and feed the children well-balanced hot meals every morning. The children are being educated into the awareness of Black revolutionary art, revolutionary songs, and the Ten Point Platform (Linen Point Platform) of the Black Panther Party, which is what Black people need, want, and must have. People who are interested in what their children are doing should come through the office with their child, or when they have the spare time, to find out how they could be of help and find out what their children are learning.

But the People's Free Breakfast Program is being sabotaged by a few individuals who don't want these children exposed to the truth. These people are the lackey teachers and their hired bodyguards, the local policemen (pigs). Some of these schools are Dort, Doyle, Cook, Kennedy, and all instill fear in these children's minds with threatening failure notices for being late or for coming through our office. Among the other foul (DOWL) things going down, the Panther Papers are being taken from the children. But even this level of repression will not stop the People's Free Breakfast Program from building that face-to-face relationship with the people of the community, nor our concern for the welfare of the people in meeting their basic needs.

We, the N.C.C.F. and the people, will not let this breakfast program be sabotaged because of fascist pigs, or any of the people's programs, because together as a whole, we will have community control.
