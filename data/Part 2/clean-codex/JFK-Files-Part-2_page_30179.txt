To obtain classifiable fingerprints:

1. Use good, printer’s ink.
2. Distribute in even, thin film on the inking slab.
3. Roll and dip margin thoroughly.
4. Roll finger from nail to nail, avoiding altering the fingers to slip.
5. If carried inside, and received in correct order.
6. If a clear physical condition makes it impossible to obtain perfect impressions, submit the best impressions that can be obtained, with a wire stapled to the card explaining the condition.
7. Examine the completed prints to assure that they can be classified, bearing in mind that most fingerprints fall into the patterns shown on this card. Other patterns occur frequently and are not shown here.

This card is for use by:

1. Law enforcement agencies in fingerprinting applicants for law enforcement positions.
2. Officials of state and local governments for purposes of official bidding, licensing and fire writing, as authorized by state statutes and approved by the Attorney General of the United States; local and county courts, judges, magistrates, and other jurisdictional agents unless specifically banned by a state election law or elected by a local government.
3. Certify if applicant has been interpreted as an unauthorized banker. If so, express the intention to do this by the credit bureau of the state. Do not talk about it without clear consent from the bureau.

Please note: If any of these requirements was not met, please indicate why. If none, see if there is an error in the information provided by you.
