Dates of employment: October 1933 to 7 August 1944. Exact title: Vice Consul. Salary or earnings: 2,250 per month.

Place of employment: (Mexico) Mazatlan (Masatlan) and Guadalajara. Immediate supervisors: Rufus H. Lene, Jr., and Maurice L. Stafford. Salary: 3,000.

Employer: State Department, Foreign Service. Business or organization: government. Employees supervised: consular clerks. Two months at Mazatlan (Masatlan), four at Guadalajara; transfer to Bilbao.

Description of work: Consistent officer in charge of all immigration work at these two consulates, as well as shipping, notarial services, passport and citizenship work, welfare and protection activities; also engaged in economic and political reporting at these posts. In charge of both consulates for prolonged periods during absence of principal officer.

Military training. Location: BONE.

Education. Last high school attended: San Diego Senior High School, San Diego, California. Highest grade completed: senior high school. Subjects studied: History, Economics, Commercial Law, Spanish.
