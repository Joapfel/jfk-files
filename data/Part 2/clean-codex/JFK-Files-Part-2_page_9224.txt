Copy to: Secret Service, Detroit.

Report of: SA Lawrence W. Levine. Date: May 9, 1973. Office: Detroit, Michigan. Field Office File No.: 157-3075. Bureau File No.: 105-165706 Sub 15. Title: Black Panther Party. Character: Extremist Matter.

Synopsis: BPP headquarters is located at 2228 Bewick, and a BPP “pad” is located at 4200 Buena Vista, Detroit, Michigan. Detroit BPP chapter leader is Philanders Lonnie Darden, also known as Lonnie Darden, the Minister of Information. The BPP operates a Free Breakfast Program and a Free Busing to Prison Program and continues to sell “The Black Panther” newspaper. Identities of leaders and members, as well as BPP activities and financial summary, set out.
