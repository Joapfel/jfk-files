II. Communist Party (CP) and Related Activities

A. San Francisco County CP (SFCCP)

SF T-2, a self-admitted former member of the CP in the San Francisco area during the period from 1952 to 1957, advised on November 5, 1962, that she knows Irving Fromer and his wife as members of the CP in San Francisco during that period.

At a meeting of the Sunset Club, SFCCP, which was held at 1235 42nd Avenue, San Francisco, on May 16, 1962, a discussion was held regarding Irving Fromer and his wife. It was noted that the Fromers (Promers) had recently moved to the area covered by the Sunset Club. Herb Nugent, Chairman of the SFCCP and a member of the Sunset Club, remarked that the Fromers (Promers) are in the Lincoln Club, SFCCP, and that they are needed in the Lincoln Club. He said, however, that the Fromers (Promers) do not contribute much to Club life and this is one of the problems of the Lincoln Club. He said that the Lincoln Club had agreed to meet at the Fromers' (Promers') house, and the Fromers (Promers) didn't even arrive to attend meetings held at their own home. Nugent said that both of the Fromers (Promers) are active in other mass organizations.

(SF T-3, 5/18/62)

Irving Fromer is a member of the Lincoln Club, SFCCP.

(SF T-3, 3/1/63)
