JAY SMITH advised that he and his brother proceeded to the residence in Peoria, Illinois, on July 21, 1974, arriving there at approximately 6:00 p.m. Upon arrival, Jay and Alc (Alec) were invited into the Jay residence. Alc was introduced and, at that time, observed a white female visitor, approximately 115 pounds, who was being introduced by “J.J.” JAY SMITH provided that he announced this female to be Jay’s, as he said.

SMITH stated that while at the BROWN residence, Alc M. declared to his brother that he, LEE H., was a member of the Iron Cross Motorcycle Gang, an organization which originated in the state of Illinois during the 1930s. Alc advised him that Iron Cross Motorcyclists drove their motorcycles in the state of Texas and did not have insurance on their motorcycles. The gang had gone to Kentucky on a previous occasion. DRO N talked about the fact that he had a Harley-Davidson (Farley Davidson) motorcycle, which DRO N had described as a “charger,” and that this motorcycle was currently loaned to a friend of his in the state of Florida.

DRO N exhibited to JAY SMITH and his brother a number of newspaper articles and clippings (clemmings), including photographs, which Lee M. related were the result of having been a member of an organization around the country. DRO N disclosed to the SMITH brothers that he had been involved in relations with the government before he was fired and made to transfer to a secret department or area for the purpose of etching, disfigurement, and starting lights during the course of the investigations.

DRO N further disclosed to JAY SMITH and his brother a number of photographs which SMITH recalled as “tolling alive.” Those photographs, according to Lee H., were of the United Nations facilities in New York City. DRO N pointed out particulars of those photographs to JAY SMITH, noting on some of them.
