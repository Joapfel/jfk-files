Reference John Roselli—real name Filippo Sacco, AKA Don Giovanni, arrived in the U.S. 09/16/11.

In 1951, during the Kefauver Hearings, Roselli testified that he knew Jack Dragna, Momo Adamo, Tony Accardo, Frank Costello, Joe Sica, Bugsy Siegel, Joe Adonis, Anthony “Little Augie Pisano” Carfano, Joe Massei, Meyer Lansky, Louis “Little New York” Campagna, Frank Milano, Joe Profaci, Sam Maceo, and Lucky Luciano.

Roselli answered to Louis Tom Dragna and Harold Meltzer, and he was sponsored for membership in the Beverly Hills Friars Club by Frank Sinatra and Dean Martin.

On 10/20/67 Roselli took the Fifth before U.S. District Court Judge Warren Ferguson in Los Angeles during a hearing in reference to an immigration charge of failing to register, and he was fingerprinted. During this hearing, Mrs. Barbara Crosby, wife of singer Gary Crosby, testified that she knew Roselli and he was like a father to her. It is noted that Roselli’s tolls reflect that he called Gary Crosby this year.
