III. Teaching of Revolutionary Program

Advised on December 15, 1969, that he had attended in the past a few political orientation classes conducted by Chicago BPP leaders. EC T-. stated that Bobby Rush normally discussed the BPP Ten Point Program to determine whether those in attendance had committed the Ten Points to memory as required.

Also advised that Fred Hampton usually discussed “Quotations From Chairman Mao’s Red Book” and had those in attendance give their views. In conclusion, stated that “Essays of Huey” and military discipline were discussed by the Panther leaders.

Advised on December 1, 1969, he had attended a political orientation class of the BPP at the Precious Blood Church, South Western Avenue and West Congress Expressway. The class was taught by Fred Hampton, and the material used was from the latest issue of “The Black Panther,” the official newspaper of the BPP. Hampton stated that those in attendance should not get involved with the police unless they intended to win. Hampton stated every time a Panther dies, a policeman should die. He said a Panther without a gun should not be called a Panther and would probably be purged from the party. Hampton admonished the members present not to wear Panther buttons unless they were prepared to die. He concluded by saying the masses would have a revolution even if they had to be persuaded by the barrel of a gun.
