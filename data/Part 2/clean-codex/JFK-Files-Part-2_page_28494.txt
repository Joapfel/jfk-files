June 6, 1977

Thomas M. Gannon, S.J.  
Select Committee on Assassinations  
U.S. House of Representatives  
Washington, D.C. 20515

Dear Tom:

This is to acknowledge receipt and acceptance of your letter of June 3, 1977, resigning from the staff of the Select Committee on Assassinations effective at the close of business on Friday, June 17, 1977.

I regret that we will be losing you, but wish you the best of luck in your new position.
