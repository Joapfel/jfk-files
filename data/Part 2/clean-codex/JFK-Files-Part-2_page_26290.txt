File. Telegram Information Report.

Country: South Vietnam, Cambodia. Report No. TDCS-3/522,837. Subject: Possible severance of Cambodian-South Vietnamese diplomatic relations. Date of info: mid-September 1962. Place and date acquired: Vietnam, Saigon, 14 September 1962. Field report No. FVS-7943. Source: a senior official (B) in the General Confederation of Vietnamese Labor.

1. Le Van Dong, former Minister of Agriculture, said on 14 September 1962 that he has been advised by a reliable source in Cambodia that the Royal Cambodian Government (RKG) is planning to sever relations with South Vietnam. The decision is to be formalized at a cabinet meeting on either 19 or 20 September, at which time the RKG Minister of National Defense will request the severance of diplomatic relations.

2. Field dissemination: State, Army, Navy, Air, USMACV, MAAG, USOM, CINCPAC, PACFLT, ARPAC, PACAF, 9th Field Station.

End of message.
