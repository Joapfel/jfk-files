Demonstrations protesting the trials of individuals indicted for their activity during the Democratic National Convention (DNC) of 1968 in Chicago, Illinois.

“This city hasn't seen anything yet. We're going to destroy all symbols of the police system.” On this occasion, SAs of the FBI observed Fred Hampton, Deputy Chairman, Illinois BPP Chapter, Chicago, who called for everyone to meet at the Federal Building, Chicago, every Tuesday at noon, until the trial ends. He observed, “Don't worry about going to school, working or anything else, just meet here every Tuesday.”

At 2:15 p.m. on October 7, 1969, the demonstrators departed in small groups and the Federal Building plaza area returned to a normal appearance. No incidents occurred or arrests were made during this activity.
