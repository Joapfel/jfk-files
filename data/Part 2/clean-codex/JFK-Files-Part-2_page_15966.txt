Expenses incurred by Chicago BPP Chapter would appear to be limited at this time to maintaining headquarters space, air travel, and bail bonds. Chicago BPP Chapter is not known to have any salaried employees. Financial aspects of Chicago BPP Chapter appear to be handled by several BPP leaders in Chicago, with general Chicago BPP members unaware of the financial condition of Chicago BPP Chapter.

February 28, 1969.

It was learned that the Chicago Chapter of the BPP now pays a salary of $80.00 a month to Central Staff members of the Chicago BPP Chapter.

March 26, 1969.

Fred Hampton, Deputy Chairman of Illinois BPP and leader of the Chicago BPP Chapter (Chicago PBB Chapter), has been receiving from $300.00 to $600.00 for his appearances at various universities and college campuses.

When members of the Chicago BPP Chapter appear at a location where one of their members is to speak, they usually charge an admission fee. If the proceeds from the admission fee are not considered sufficient, BPP members will take up a collection before the affair is over.

February 21, 1969.

Fred Hampton, Chicago BPP leader, has been receiving $800.00 to $1,000.00 for his appearances at various universities and college campuses.

January 16, 1969.

Student groups at Northeastern Illinois State College, 5500 North St. Louis Street, Chicago, will pay $200.00 or more for BPP members to speak to their groups.
