To Director. Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division. Alexandria, Washington Field 100-53558, Chicago. From New York 100-172646.

Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971. IS-MISC.

Re New York telephone call to Washington Field Office instant date.

On instant date at seven thirty AM, SA's observed three buses—Coast Cities Cruiser number 511, Community Coach Lines number 506, and number 426—depart from Union Square, New York City, for Washington, D.C. (DMC), with approximately 135 girls under 25 years of age, 95 percent white.
