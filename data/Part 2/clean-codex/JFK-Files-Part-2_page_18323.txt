From: Legat Buenos Aires (62-37).

SENSTUDY-75.

Re: U.S. CAB, September 19, 1975.

For information, Buenos Aires Embassy received instructions from the U.S. Department of State via the Roger channel with regard to the visit of David Aaron, a former U.S. Department of State Foreign Service officer, and William Truehart, a former U.S. ambassador, who are staff members of the Church Committee.

These State Department Roger channel messages were directed to U.S. embassies at Brasilia, Buenos Aires, and Montevideo, and the U.S. Consul General in Rio de Janeiro.

The State Department instructed each recipient in a September 17, 1975, message that “primary objective is to discuss with ambassador, deputy chief of mission, chief of station (CIA) and defense attaché, the relationships and exercise of authority by the ambassador over intelligence activities in the field.”
