The purpose of the march was to gain support for legalized abortion and to protest existing abortion laws.

The source advised that leaders of the march included Eloise Chase, Sharon Mallinor and Barbara Chic, all members of the Washington, D.C. branch of the Socialist Worker's Party (SWP).

SWP has been designated by Executive Order 10450.

VOMARC is influenced by SWP and Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) members. YSA is the youth affiliate of SWP.

On May 6 instant, representatives of the Federal Bureau of Investigation observed approximately fifty to seventy-five individuals, mostly females, proceed to the United States Capitol in a candlelight procession. The procession lasted from approximately one hour (beginning and ending at the church) to 10:15 p.m. and was highlighted by the placing of a cardboard coffin on the steps of the east side of the Capitol Building to symbolize the death of ten thousand women from illegal abortions performed in the United States during 1970.
