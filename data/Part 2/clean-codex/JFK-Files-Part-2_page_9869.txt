The following notes were taken by James Dick, Senate Select Committee Staff, on February 18, 1976, based on a review of the file on the seventeen wiretaps which occurred between May 1969 and February 1971. These notes contain two types of summaries: (a) essentially political or personal information which was obtained from the wiretaps and disseminated to Henry Kissinger, John Ehrlichman, H.R. Haldeman, and/or President Nixon, and (b) a list of the targets and the dates the coverage was in operation for each.
