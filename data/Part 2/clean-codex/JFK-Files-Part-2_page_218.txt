DIR CITE MEXI 6574.

LICOOKY.

References: A DIR 2231. B MEXI 6419 (NCG-6243) SI.

1. LICOOKY still insists on completion of her first trip to Venezuela, about February 1961, after which she returned directly to ZRMETAL. She met “JANET” and an unknown case officer, apparently Peruvian (PERUMEN) specialist, in a no-longer-remembered hotel and delivered to JANET extensive profiles of OSEGUEDA and AREVALO. The station is particularly interested in recovering the OSEGUEDA write-up to be used as the basis for an updated report. Note these occurrences prior to the station’s connection with LICOOKY. Our files begin June 1961. Appreciate headquarters’ effort to locate and query “JANET.”
