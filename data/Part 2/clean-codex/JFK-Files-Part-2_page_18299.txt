September 25, 1975

To: John A. Mintz, Assistant Director, Legal Counsel Division, Federal Bureau of Investigation

From: Michael E. Shaheen, Jr., Special Counsel for Intelligence Coordination

Subject: Senate Select Committee letter dated September 23, revising SSC letter dated September 4, requesting information relating to FBI use of IRS information.

Attached is a letter dated September 23 from the Senate Select Committee which revises a September 4 letter from the SSC requesting certain materials pertaining to FBI use of IRS information. Please prepare an appropriate response to the September 23 letter.

cc: Paul Daly

All test information contained maybe is unclassified.
