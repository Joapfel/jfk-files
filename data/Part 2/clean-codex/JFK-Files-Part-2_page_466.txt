7. Aurelio of MINCEX told LERING/3 their mutual friend Ramon Rosie, who had been sent to Syria by MINCEX, had done an excellent job but had finally defected and married a local Syrian telephone operator. Rosie has wife and child in Havana. Aurelio implied Rosie had defected with a considerable amount of Cuban funds. LERING/3 commented to Rowton that Rosie could be useful to Rowton's business interests because of his many contacts in the Middle East and Europe. LERING/3 believes he knows Rosie well enough to recruit him for Rowton.

8. As to political maneuvers, LIRING/3 reported Arnol Rodriguez told him the most powerful elements in Cuba at present are Raul Castro and Ramiro Valdez, who carry the weight of the military and security forces despite the fact neither has popular support. Their opposing faction is headed by Dorticos and Raul Roa Garcia (CARCIA), who appear to hold relatively good popular support. Fidel Castro is in the middle, attempting to conciliate the two factions but without success to date.

9. According to Arnol Rodriguez, LIRING/2 is no longer consul, Mexico City.
