Annex A

Statements of Soviet Officials About Nosenko

Pages 361–398 of the previous summary contain statements by Soviet officials, including several sources, in regard to Nosenko.

These remarks cover the period from February 1964 to June 1967.

None of the quoted information, with the exception of a statement by a source (a self-professed former KGB Second Chief Directorate agent who stated Nosenko was not a KGB officer but a “civilian” connected somehow with the KGB), raises a question concerning the identity of Nosenko or whether he was actually a KGB officer. It is not considered that the remark by the source can be given any particular weight or that it represents any material evidence he was not a KGB officer.

Source #1, Source #3, and Source #2 had no personal knowledge of Nosenko, and most of their comments were based on information from third parties or obtained through conversation.
