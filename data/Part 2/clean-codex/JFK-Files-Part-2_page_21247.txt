4. Nikolai Skvortsov

On June 29, 1968, (N-206), a photograph containing the name of Aleksandr Mikhalylovich Chechevitsyn was displayed to Nosenko, who identified the photograph as an old photograph of Skvortsov. It is noted that Chechevitsyn had accompanied Aleksey Nikolayevich Solovyev to Bangkok in 1960, where Solovyev was in contact with a State Department communications technician, Anthony Lapka. This contact was the first step of an attempted recruitment of Lapka, who went to Italy later in 1960. It is noted also that Nosenko advised that Skvortsov during 1960–61 was trying to get a visa to go to Italy.

Recruitment approaches were made to Lapka by the Soviets in Italy in 1960 and in France in 1962. He was prominently mentioned in information furnished by Shamrock, which undoubtedly emanated from John Discoe Smith, who was well known to Lapka.
