TYPIC LIRING.

Ref: Director 39039.

22 October 1965.

1. LIRING-2 called LIRING-3 to the Embassy on 19 October. During the meeting, L-2:

A. Thanked L-3 for his cooperation during the Lopez affair and complimented him on the accuracy of his information.

B. Said he had given much thought to L-3’s suggestions and criticisms and added, “We would like you to assist us further in specific projects here in Mexico.”

2. Per previous directives from ROWTON, L-3 told L-2 he would be glad to help the revolution, provided:

A. Projects assigned to him were of importance to the revolution.

B. Embassy personnel were not involved in them for mutual protection.

3. L-3’s reaction to L-2 was very favorable. He complimented him on his serious approach, said he had anticipated L-3’s conditions, and felt L-3 should go to Havana to discuss projects directly with responsible persons rather than work through the Embassy. L-2 did not think it wise to keep this secret.

This is necessary for amend step action. ZB.
