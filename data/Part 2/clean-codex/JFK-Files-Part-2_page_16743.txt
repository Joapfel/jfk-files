Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 2/17/70

Tom Hett, State's Attorney's Office, 26th and California, Chicago, Illinois, furnished the following list of guns seized on the Black Panther raid at 2337 Westaine on December 4, 1969:

1. Colt .45 caliber automatic, 5-inch blue, Model 1927. Argentina, serial number 7111.

Shipped June 9, 1969, to M&M Sporting Goods, 3823 N. Richards, Milwaukee, by Casanova's (Casavanova's) Milwaukee.

They have the gun on their books but do not have it in their possession and have never reported it stolen. They say they will report it now.

2. Sears Roebuck 20-gauge pump, 28-inch blue steel, Model 2i. No serial number, so it cannot be traced.

3. Springfield 12-gauge pump, 28-inch blue steel, Model 67H. No serial number, so it cannot be traced.

4. High Standard 12-gauge pump, Model K-1200. No serial number, so it cannot be traced.

5. Browning High Standard 12-gauge autoloading model, Standard H15423.

Sold by Browning Manufacturing to Sears Roebuck on October 13, 1954. Sears is located at 403 S. State, Chicago, Illinois. Sears does not keep records that far back, so unable to trace further.

6. High Standard 12-gauge pump, Model K-1200. No serial number, so unable to trace.

7. Remington 12-gauge pump, Model 870, serial number 20240V. Unable to trace because Remington threw out its records for guns made in 1950.
