k. Dissemination at Bureau

There are situations in which dissemination is made only at the Bureau. Do not disseminate information to any field branch of another Government agency where security measures are inadequate. In such instances, bring data to the immediate attention of the Bureau for FBIHQ dissemination. In addition, the Atomic Energy Commission receives reports only through the Bureau. Information relating to foreign travel plans of security subjects is disseminated to interested agencies, including the State Department, only at FBIHQ.
