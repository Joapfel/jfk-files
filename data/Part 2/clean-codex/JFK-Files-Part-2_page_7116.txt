Known Travel of Latin Americans to and from Cuba — January 1964.

Argentina: To Cuba, 9; from Cuba, 3. Purpose: 1 UNESCO delegate and 2 well-known Communists went to Cuba.

Bolivia: To Cuba, 3; from Cuba, 1. Purpose: 1 attending a preparatory meeting of the Latin American Communist Youth Congress in Habana, and 1 will attend a course at the Cuban equivalent of the Soviet Komsomol school.

Brazil: To Cuba, 0; from Cuba, 0.

Br. Guiana: To Cuba, 1; from Cuba, 0. Purpose: 29 December 1963—not previously reported—unknown.

Chile: To Cuba, 14; from Cuba, 15. Purpose: returning from Habana celebration of the 5th anniversary of the Cuban Revolution.

Colombia: To Cuba, 4; from Cuba, 0. An additional 15 Colombian travellers to Cuba should be added to the report for December 1963, which brings the total for December to 25. It is estimated that 13 of the 25 went to Cuba for the 1 January celebrations.

Costa Rica: To Cuba, 11; from Cuba, 13. Purpose: 3 went to Cuba to attend a meeting at Habana at which the Venezuelan charge against Cuba was discussed. The remainder were sent to Habana for the 2 January celebration.

Dom. Rep.: To Cuba, 0; from Cuba, 1. Purpose: 1 attended the Architects Congress.

Ecuador: To Cuba, 8; from Cuba, 0. Purpose: 5 children and 3 adults went from Mexico.
