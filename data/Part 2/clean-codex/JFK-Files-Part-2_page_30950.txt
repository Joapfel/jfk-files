13 December 1967.

Report—Subject LBF. OS Indices Results:

Copy of 22 November 1963 letter in Subjects file, signed as Sec. for Military Affairs, Spec. Committee, MDC, addressed to Major General Bela K. Kiraly, President, Hungarian Freedom Fighters Federation, Inc., offering services of MDC and arranging meetings and discussions with other anti-Castro organizations and the Hungarian Freedom Fighters. The Hungarian Freedom Fighters were agency-sponsored and covert action, requesting information on MDC before granting approval for contact between the two organizations.

CIA Connection: LBF was granted Provisional Operational Approval on 30 December 1959 and Operational Approval on 20 February 1960 for use as a PP Agent, Havana, Cuba. Political and Psychological.

2. Deletion: Extract from investigation of Justin McCarthy, dated April 1961. Limited information included regarding one Laureno Batista.

3. Memo to files, undated, indicates A/1 will give approval for Batista to meet with Kiraly.
