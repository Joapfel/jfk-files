Sergeant Roland S. Leczkowski, Chicago Police Department, advised on June 9, 1969, that the following four weapons, included among thirteen weapons obtained at Chicago Black Panther Party (BPP) Headquarters, were listed in Chicago Police Department records as having been stolen.

Smith and Wesson (Smith and Wessen), .38 Special, serial number 573764. Reported stolen on April 11, 1969, from Louisa McGeorge, 1353 South Karlov, Chicago, Illinois.

12-gauge shotgun, Model 10, serial number 010728. Reported stolen from United States Mail in August 1968.

Remington rifle, Model 514, .22 caliber. Reported stolen on November 23, 1968, by Virginia State Police, Salem, Virginia.

Sears Roebuck Model 300, 12-gauge shotgun. Reported stolen on June 7, 1968, by New York Police Department.
