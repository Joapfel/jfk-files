Persons/Entities: Halpern, Gen. Lansdale; Bissell; Hopky. Date: October 1961. Category: Castro.

Occurrence:

Shortly after the meeting with Bissell, Halpern and Zeeberg were introduced to Gen. Lansdale, who was the new Chief of Operations for an effort to remove Castro from the Castro regime. The initial meeting was concerned with only organizational matters.

Source: Halpern, 6/18/75. Staff: Phen. Date: 7/1/75.
