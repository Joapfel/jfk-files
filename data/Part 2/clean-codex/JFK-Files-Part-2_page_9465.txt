Community News

Ever since the Black Liberation Movement started in 1965, greedy individuals have been pimping off the people. One such band of individuals exist in Flint, going under the guise of Bro-Ham, Brothers of Ham.

Willy Noland, John (Big Word) Coleman, and Will (Run the Show) Frank-in (Franklin) started this organization supposedly to serve the people. Through trickery and demagogic strategy, they went on to deceive the people into thinking that this organization was a righteous Black militant organization, intent on serving the people and bringing justice to the people.

Run the Show Franklin and Loudmouthed Coleman used Will Noland as a hand tool. They also tricked a lot of unknowing brothers into doing their little deeds, like getting money from their conference, the pigs. From there, they moved on to a higher level, Ombudsman, which is no more than an organization that is built around telling the people one thing and then telling their conference another. Getting money and saying that they are going to help the people in the Black community, while the pigs are still brutalizing, beating and killing niggers.

Black people are still living in roach- and rat-infested houses. Black people are being herded into urban renewal projects, which are in essence zigger removal projects. Black children are still hungry, while pigs tell, patrol, and control the community, and apart from this, 17 new pigs have been added to the Black community to harass the community.

Even the students in school are being vamped on while the boot-licking Hackett(s), Run the Show and Big Word, loudmouthed lying Coleman, are running around with .25s in their pockets, sitting around on the board of the Frank Coalition with pig prosecutor, two-faced Robert Leonard, greedy, various businessman Sommerfield, and other pig businessmen, who ain't gonna give a dike to any Black people at all. They drink plenty of liquor.
