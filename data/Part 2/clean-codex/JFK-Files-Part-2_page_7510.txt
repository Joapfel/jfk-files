Subject: Juriy Ivanovich Moskalev

26 March 1977

Date of birth: 23 July 1920

Place of birth: Iguamnovo, USSR

Height: 172 centimeters

Hair: Brown (medium light)

Eyes: Gray

Wife: Married to Vera Nikolayevna Streltsova

Children: None listed.

Education: Unknown

Miscellaneous:

Became a Communist Party member in 1951.

Was allegedly imprisoned during the war because of his German descent or for black-market dealings.

Occupation: As of May 1973, was with the Institute of Biophysics of the Soviet Ministry of Health. Doctor of Medical Sciences. Professor.

1922: Family moved to Leningrad.

1948–1970: Lived in Moscow.

1958: Became head of a division of the Institute of Biophysics, Soviet Ministry of Public Health.

1951–1953: Head of the Pathophysiology Laboratory, Institute of Biophysics.

1952: During the last half of the year, had a month’s TDY at the Biophysical Laboratory in Moscow.

1953: When the source, Dr. Wilhelm Menke, left STIB as chief of the Pathophysiological Laboratory at Sungol in October 1953, Moskalev took that position. He reportedly was not well qualified.
