Outgoing Telegram, Department of State.

Origin: Action: AmConGen Georgetown 293 EUR.

Info: USIA, SCA, INR, CIA, NSA, OSD, Army, Navy (CAVY), Air.

Urgent intelligence.

Washington investigation agencies have no information to confirm alleged munitions shipments from Cuba to Georgetown.

Story originated with caption in current edition of Life magazine to the effect that two Russian ships were carrying munitions from Mariel, Cuba, to BG. O'Leary of the Washington Evening Star apparently seized on the Life story as the basis for an Evening Star article. It is unlikely that any responsible U.S. official would confirm the story as O'Leary alleged in the article.

If repeated, if the Department is asked by the press, we plan to say we have no information but are making inquiries.

End.

Rusk.

Drafted by: EUR:BNA:R. J. Tepper:ldd. February 8, 1963.

Telegraphic transmission and classification approved by: EUR/BNA — M. Gordon Knox.
