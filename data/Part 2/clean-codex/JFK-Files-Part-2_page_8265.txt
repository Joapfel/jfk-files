19 April 1951

According to Yani Kolomonos, Dimitrov's friend, the latter agreed to meet Piray at the French Embassy between 11 and 12 o'clock on Thursday, 19 April. At this meeting Dimitrov will (1) give the French detailed accounts of all United States operations of which he is aware, with special emphasis on Radio Gorianin, and (2) receive for this information the sum of 10 to 15 million drachmae.

Kolomonos suggests that this effort of the French might be a communist-inspired attempt to penetrate United States intelligence activities.
