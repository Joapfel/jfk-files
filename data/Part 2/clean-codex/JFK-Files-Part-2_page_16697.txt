Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 2/25/70

Mrs. Anne White, 2237 South Keeler, Chicago, Illinois, was located at the above address and served with a Federal Grand Jury Subpoena calling for her appearance before the Federal Grand Jury of the United States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois at 11:00 AM, February 25, 1970.

Mrs. White advised that she had been employed at Cook County Hospital since approximately 1951 until December 1, 1969. She said that her supervisor while employed at the County Hospital was Miss Karl Wilson. She stated that while employed at Cook County Hospital, she had been a clerk assigned to the Trauma Unit. She further advised that during the week of December 1, 1969, she recalls being off one day during this week or two days perhaps, and that if she did work during that week, it was the 8:00 to 11:00 shift.

Mrs. White advised that she is presently employed at Sears Roebuck as a sales clerk at Herman Avenue.
