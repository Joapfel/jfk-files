Persons/entities: Ambassador Lodge; Secretary McNamara (MAC NAMARA). Date: October 12, 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: Captain Nguyen Cong Nguyen, Chief of Operations, Senior Command, stated he was contacted by two different groups during the week of 5–12 October; the second group was headed by Lt. Colonel Dang Trog Toc who claimed to be related with Wharton Lodge and Secretary McNamara who had previously spoken with Captain Nguyen as non-official. On October 18, McNamara (Mac Namara) offered money; Nguyen to still be undeclared which group he will support.

Staff: Dawson.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein (Corein), Exhibit One.
