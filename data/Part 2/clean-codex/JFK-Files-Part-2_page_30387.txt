6. Mr. Goldsmith. The response that was provided to the Committee indicates that the CIA had no standard procedure for debriefing returning US defectors. What was the basis for that policy, or actually for the absence of the policy?

7. Mr. Tovar. I don't know. I can speculate only—not having been there and not having any involvement—I don't know what the mentality of those in charge was at that time.

8. Mr. Goldsmith. What would your speculation be?

9. Mr. Tovar. I would speculate that the contact with and debriefing, in effect the supervision of Americans in that category, would be the FBI's prerogative, and the Agency as a rule would not have had any basic interest or reason to be involved unless there were a specific counterintelligence concern.
