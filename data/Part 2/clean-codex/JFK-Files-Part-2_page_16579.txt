Copy to: 1 - USA, Chicago (Attn: AUSA Robert J. Briarstone).

Report of: SA C. Leonard Treviranus. Date: 12/22/69. Field Office File No.: Officer Chicago. Title: Fred A. Hampton (Deceased); et al. - Victims. Chairman: Summary Punishment - Civil Rights.

Synopsis:

By letter dated 12/11/69 from AAG Jerris Leonard, Civil Rights Division, a preliminary investigation was requested to determine whether violations of 18 USC, Sections 242 and 241, are involved in the shooting of Fred A. Hampton and Mark Clark by the Cook County State's Attorney's Police raid on the Black Panther Party (BPP) apartment, 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago, on 12/1/69. SAC M. W. Johnson personally advised USA Thomas A. Foran, Superintendent of the Chicago Police Department, James D. Conelisk, and Cook County State's Attorney Edward V. Hanrahan of the requested investigation. Hanrahan stipulated that all 11 officers in the raid would be made available for interview, provided an official from his office was present and that a copy of the interview was also available. SAC M. W. Johnson also contacted Attorney Warren Wolpson, representing victim Brenda Harris, who was to consult with other victims' attorneys and advise if they would make their clients available for interview. SAC M. W. Johnson also contacted Dennis Cunningham, attorney for BPP, who desired that an attorney be present during FBI examination of the BPP apartment. On 12/17/69, Cunningham advised SAC M. M. Johnson that “it is politically impossible for the BPP to voluntarily cooperate” with any FBI investigator. AAG Jerris Leonard, in Chicago on 12/18/69, advised he would personally handle the FGJ inquiry into this matter. On 12/22/69, FBI examination commenced on the premises at 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago, and FGJ commenced receiving evidence in this case.
