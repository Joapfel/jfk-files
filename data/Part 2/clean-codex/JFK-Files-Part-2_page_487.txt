20 July 1965, 200352Z. Mexico City. 7971. Priority: Director. TYPIC LILING. 59838.

1. Wallace B. Rayton met with Carlos Jurado on 19 July and obtained the following information, which undoubtedly came from LILING-1, who returned to Mexico City from Havana on 15 July.

2. The situation in Vietnam is grave because the North Vietnamese are going to execute a U.S. airman recently captured.

3. Rayton (Rowton) elicited this statement from Jurado after Jurado told him LILING-1 had brought Jurado a message from Arnol Rodriguez Camps, who recently was replaced as Vice Minister of Foreign Affairs. The message to Jurado from Rodriguez via LILING-1 was to the effect that he had been replaced and was awaiting reassignment, but the effect of the replacement was not serious. Jurado told Rayton that LILING-1 had said there was much organization going on in Cuba now, but it was not serious. However, LILING-1 said the situation in Vietnam was serious because the North Vietnamese were going to shoot down American aircraft, and as a consequence this would escalate the war there.

4. Rayton has scheduled a meeting with Jurado on the evening of 20 July. He will attend to learn more details and establish CAS for swap.
