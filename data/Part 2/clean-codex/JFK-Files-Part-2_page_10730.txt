ANNE GRANT DUNHAM

As previously noted, DUNHAM was apprehended by Special Agents of the FBI on August 22, 1971, in the courtyard of St. Paul's Church, 422 Market Street, Camden, N.J. On the same date, she was afforded a hearing before U.S. Magistrate Charles L. Rudd, Camden, N.J., and was remanded to the custody of the U.S. Marshal in lieu of $20,000 good bail. She was released on September 3, 1971, after posting ten percent cash or security on a $7,500 bond which had been set by the Third Circuit Court of Appeals, Philadelphia, Pa., on September 3, 1971.

A search of the fingerprints of ANNE GRANT DUNHAM (GRAND) has failed to disclose prior arrest data in the files of the Identification Division, FBI, Washington, D.C.
