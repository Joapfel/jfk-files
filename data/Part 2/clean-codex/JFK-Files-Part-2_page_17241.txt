Roosevelt Hilliard resides at 1608 Chestnut Street, Oakland, California, and is described as follows:

Race: Negro. Sex: Male. Date of birth: January 16, 1937. Place of birth: Jackson, Alabama. Height: 5'7" tall. Weight: 145 pounds. Hair: Black, semi-natural hair which is receding at the forehead. Complexion: Dark. Characteristics: Frequently has a mustache.

The second source advised on February 25, 1969, that Roosevelt Hilliard was to a BPP class in Berkeley, California, on the evening of February 24, 1969.
