For Schools Children.

The free breakfast program sponsored by the Detroit branch (FRANCH) of the Black Panther Party was opened at its second “breakfast center.” The newest center is located at 157 Collingwood, between Second and Wood. Ward, formerly the Westside, is serving approximately 25 to 35 kids.

The other location is at 2228 Beaty, between Vernon and Merchétal. On Tuesdays, it is first-arrived; more than 85 children [attend] every school morning.

Hot meals (such as crisps, catmears, waffles, bacon, dodgs, etc.) are served each day from 7:00 a.m. through 9:00 a.m., Monday through Friday.

The people in the community are welcome to come to the breakfast program and can volunteer to give assistance in serving the children. If they desire to do so, they can inform others of the program. If there are any questions concerning the breakfast program, please feel free to contact us. The numbers are listed below.
