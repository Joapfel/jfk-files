Subject: Request by U. S. Senator Walter F. Mondale, Senate Select Committee Member, to Review His FBI Files

Adams, Assistant to the Director, Deputy Associate Director, in his office from 8:05 a.m. to 10:50 a.m. During this time Senator Mondale reviewed the following FBI material pertaining to himself.
