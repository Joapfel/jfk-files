C. April 7, 1964

State Department Cable No. 1775 (control 133) informs Embassy that Coleman, Slawson, and Willens plan to travel to Mexico on 8 April for the purpose of:

a. Determining the nature and extent of the effort of U.S. investigating agencies abroad.

b. Investigating the effort of Mexican authorities.

c. Consulting U.S. and Mexican authorities regarding further investigation, if any is determined necessary.
