The source utilized in the enclosed LHM to characterize ISOBEL FORBES CERNEY is informally.

The enclosed LHM is being classified since unauthorized disclosure of information furnished by the informant reflected therein could reasonably result in identifying this source of continuing value and jeopardize the future effectiveness thereof.

Reference Bureau airtel to Albany, 12/5/67, instructed San Francisco and Atlanta to institute investigation under the caption “JEANNETTE RANKIN BRIGADE.” The only individual with any known subversive background determined to have had any connection with the Jeannette Rankin Brigade in the Atlanta Division is ISOBEL FORBES CERNEY, who was last known to have paid CP dues in California in 1961, and apparently subsequently was “kicked out of the Communist Party because of her present religious jag” in 1961.

The only activity of the Jeannette Rankin Brigade with which ISOBEL FORBES CERNEY was known to be connected was on one occasion when she participated in a public meeting at the Emmanuel Baptist Church in Atlanta on July 18, 1961, where discussions were held regarding plans for the Jeannette Rankin Brigade trip to Washington.
