Federal Bureau of Investigations, Communications Section.

NR002 SV Code. 7:15 PM NITEL, 3/25/75 PNR.

To: Director. From: Savannah. March 25, 1975. Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

REBUNITEL all SACs, March 24, 1975.

In response to RENITEL, the following breakdown in personnel assigned to internal security and counterintelligence matters is being set forth:

Internal Security and Counterintelligence personnel: 1 SAC—0; 1 ASAC—0; 1 supervisor—40 percent internal security, 10 percent counterintelligence; 1 agent—90 percent internal security, 0 counterintelligence; 1 agent—50 percent internal security, 10 percent counterintelligence; 1 agent—10 percent internal security, 0 counterintelligence; 1 agent—0 internal security, 30 percent counterintelligence.

All other agents' accumulative total of time would equal 10 percent for internal security and 0 for counterintelligence for one agent.
