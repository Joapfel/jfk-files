Richard Gibson in London contacts Detroit concerning departure of Robert Williams from London.

a. From: London: Richard Gibson.

b. To: Detroit: 1685 Murland, Brother Imari.

c and d. Rob and Milton took off without incident at 0945 London time and should be arriving in Detroit around noon your time. Yours in the struggle.

e. U.S. citizen who was an organizer for the Fair Play for Cuba Committee and an editor for the English-language section of Revolutionary Africa, Algiers, in 1963. He is a frequent contact of Robert Williams.

f. Richard Henry, brother of Milton Henry. Both Henrys and Williams gave a press conference in Dar-es-Salaam on 19 May 68, announcing the separatist Negro nation, the “Republic of New Africa,” of which Williams is the President.

g. Robert Williams, Negro militant who fled to the U.S. in 1967 and resides in Cuba. Williams and his lawyer, Milton Henry, arrived in Detroit on 12 Sep 69 on a special flight from London.

h. Milton Henry, a Pontiac, Michigan, lawyer. On 11 Sep 69, Tony Smythe (N/I) in London informed Conrad Lynn, long-time associate of Williams and a militant civil rights lawyer, in New York that he (Smythe) wanted to know what was wrong with Milton Henry’s non-cooperation.
