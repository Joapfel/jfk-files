2. Report deleted, dated 7/19/61 — third-party information.

3. Sergio visits survivors of Cuban exile infiltration of the U.S. who landed in New Orleans and were hospitalized because of their weakened condition. He tried to see them on 3 May 1961, but entrance to the hospital was denied until his bona fides could be established. Visited them on 10 May 1961. Arcacha was then head of the New Orleans chapter of CRC.

4. Washington Star (Washington Start) article, dated 1 April 1967, on Garrison problem — warrant (warrent) issued for Arcacha's arrest on charges of conspiring to burglarize a munitions bunker in 1961 with Gordon Novel.

5. Bio memo — undated. Sergio Arcacha Smith was one of the promoters of Friends of Democratic Cuba in New Orleans, 6 January 1961. Investigation conducted by a reliable Miami Station asset concluded that FRC was organized for personal gain.

6. FBI memo on FRD, 1/18/61, New Orleans, deleted.

7. Memo dated 31 May 1967 by Raymond G. Rocca (Ramond G. Rocca).

Subject: Garrison and the Kennedy Assassination — Gordon Dwane Novel

A. Summary — Novel has both claimed and denied CIA affiliation; a thorough check of CIA records shows no affiliation. It is possible that NBC is supporting Novel to get a large audience for an exposé of Garrison. Novel may have exaggerated his presumed relationship with Sergio Arcacha Smith, who also is not CIA-affiliated.

B. Novel is mysterious — little information — 29-year-old former owner of a bar in the French Quarter and an electronics expert.

C. In Columbus, Ohio, Novel is free on $10,000 bond, living in a rent-free apartment, and drives a car with dealer tags. Rocca questions where the money is coming from.

D. Novel in McLenon, Virginia, 25–26 March 1967. Given a polygraph by Lloyd Furr. Furr and Richard L. Bast operate the firm Redex, which sells audio and counter-audio devices.

E. CIA finds no evidence of ever having had an Evergreen Advertising Agency, which Novel claims he worked for and which was a CIA front.

F. Records show CIA never recruited or paid Arcacha directly, but since he was associated with FRD in New Orleans, which was created with CIA assistance, guidance, and financial support may have been given to him through the Miami FRD circa May 1960.
