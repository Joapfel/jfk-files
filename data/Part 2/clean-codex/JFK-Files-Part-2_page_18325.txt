Instructions that he desires to personally confer with Truehart prior to the latter's departure on September 27, 1975. Ambassador Hill is scheduled to return to Argentina during the afternoon of September 26, 1975.

Another communication dated September 18, 1975 was directed to the above listed posts via the State Department Roger channel. This message states verbatim: “Aaron and Truehart have told us they are especially interested in kinds of reporting done by the Embassy officers, attachés, SRF (CIA) and FBI, with particular emphasis on the division of effort among them. In this connection, they would also like to talk to the FBI officer at your post and have so informed FBI here.”

From the foregoing, it would appear that the main purpose of Aaron and Truehart's visit is to attempt to develop information indicating that duplication exists in intelligence collection overseas among the various intelligence agencies. There appears to be a basic misconception by Aaron and Truehart.
