Persons/Entities: McCone; Castro. Categories: MONGODB; S&A.

Occurrence: McCone testified that the SGA approved a program to contaminate with carbon dust cylinders in trucks and locomotives which Castro had purchased from Europe. This was to be done at the time of shipment. The program met with some success. McCone could not recall where the actual sabotage of the purchased equipment took place (22–23).

Source: McCone, Testimony, 6/6/75. Staff: Rhea. Date: 6/27/75.
