UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

Memorandum

To: Mr. Adams  
Date: 8-29-75  
From: Legal Counsel  
Subject: SENSTUDY

On 8-29-75 former Assistant Director Joseph Ponder called Inspector John B. Hotis to advise that he had been contacted by Michael Epstein, staff member, Senate Select Committee. Mr. Epstein said he was going to be in the Los Angeles area this coming weekend and would like to interview Mr. Ponder on Sunday 8:31-75 concerning his involvement in the Martin Luther King investigation.

Ponder was told that we are waiving his secrecy agreement for purposes of this interview, but he was not to discuss matters other than the Martin Luther King investigation. In addition he was advised that he should not furnish information which would identify confidential sources, disclose sensitive methods or investigative techniques, jeopardize ongoing investigations, or violate the Third Agency rules. Ponder was told that if he had any questions regarding the proper scope of the interview, he should call Bureau Headquarters collect and discuss the matter with SA Elmer Larson.

Recommendation: That Mr. Ponder be released from existing employment agreement for purposes of the forthcoming interview by Mr. Epstein concerning the Martin Luther King investigation.

Mr. Wannall  
Mr. Mintz  
Mr. Cregar  
Mr. Hotis
