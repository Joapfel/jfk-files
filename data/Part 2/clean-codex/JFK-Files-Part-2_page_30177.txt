Applicant

1. Loop

To obtain fingerprints, mention fingers.

1. Use black pen or ink.

2. Detach the extract on underside.

3. Use only one finger to print.

4. Use all fingers at least once, and avoid allowing fingers to slip.

5. Use two impressions of each hand to record in correct order.

6. If an air vent or door lock makes it impossible to print a finger, make a notation to that effect in the individual fingers block.

7. If some ink is left on the surface, make every attempt to get perfect impressions. Submit the best that can be obtained, with a memo stapled to this card explaining the reasons.

8. Draw on the completed prints to see if they can be classified, bearing in mind that most fingers fall into the patterns shown on this card. Other patterns occur occasionally and are not shown here.

The lines determine center of loop and delta; puff show.

2. Whorl

This card is for use by law enforcement agencies in fingerprinting applicants for law enforcement positions.

Officials of state and local governments may distribute this card for purposes of documentary identification, licensing, and documentation, as authorized by the Attorney General. Local and state governmental officials, including village officers, may use it for certificates or certifications for release from service. Please note: If this card is different from the approved form, use this new form.

3. Arch

Arches have no deltas.
