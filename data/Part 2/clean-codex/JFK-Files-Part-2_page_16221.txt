Chicago, Illinois

August 29, 1969

Black Panther Party (BPP)

The BPP and the Students for a Democratic Society (SDS) are described on the appendix page attached hereto. All sources utilized in this memorandum have furnished reliable information in the past.

On August 27, 1969, officials of the BPP provided the following:

Officials of the BPP decided that organization should publicly disavow any connection with SDS and SDS National Action Day in Chicago, Illinois, on October 11, 1969.
