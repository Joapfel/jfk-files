Experience

Boe, James Arthur (BOE., James Arthur)—born New York, N.Y., March 19, 1911; San Diego, Calif., High School graduate; bookkeeper, paints and varnishes firms, 1929–30; assistant to traffic agr., Chamber of Commerce, 1930; appointment clerk in consulate at Mexico City, January 26, 1931; V.C. at Mexico City, September 25, 1935; at Manhattan, October 6, 1938; temporary at Houston, Tex., from October 1939 to May 1940; at Guadalajara, July 17, 1941; at Bilbao, August 15, 1941; at Madrid, December 23, 1945; at Barcelona, May 1, 1946; appointment writer, service staff officer of class six, and assigned to the Sept., October 27, 1947; assistant at Státscitán (Státscitán) Mur., 20, 1927; class four, May 2, 1949; assistant at Houston Airs (Airs), June 17, 1949; married.
