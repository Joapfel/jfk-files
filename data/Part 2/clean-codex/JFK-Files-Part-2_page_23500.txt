Interview Questions for Gordon Gray

Trujillo

1. Memo of July 1, 1960, from J.C. King (Chief, WHD) to Acting DCI recommending the delivery of arms (high-powered rifles with telescopic scopes) to the opposition of Trujillo. The memo says that “Approval for delivery of these arms has been given by Assistant Secretary of State Roy Rubottom.”

Were there any proceedings of the Special Group at this time which authorized delivery of arms? We don't have record of any authorization until January 12, 1961, Special Group minutes.

If not, did the DCI and Assistant Secretary of State exceed their authority?

Generally, what was the procedure in the Eisenhower administration for handling the approval of such a project?

Could the DCI approve it under his own authority?

Did the Assistant Secretary of State exceed his authority by not requesting and receiving approval from the Secretary of State?

Shouldn't the Special Group have been consulted?

2. Special Group minutes of 12 January 1967. Gray was present. The Group approves shipment of arms to “dissidents inside the Dominican Republic.”

Do you remember this event? Are the minutes accurate?

What was the policy behind the approval?

Weren't the weapons intended for use in assassination?
