5. Aurelio confided to LIRING/3 that Cuba's most urgent requirement was charter of 24 medium towage ships for use in 1967. Aurelio added this was classified secret and that Sergio Martinez knew nothing of this and that LIRING/3 should not mention it. Aurelio told us he would be in Mexico City about 15 September, en route to Canada, and could appreciate any leads LIRING/3 could give at that time as to the best and cheapest channel for renting ships. Aurelio commented Cuba could not publicize this need since past experience indicated chartering was more difficult and expensive for Cuba once the requirement became known to certain governments. Possibility exists Russians may have cut down their assistance in transporting Cuban sugar, forcing Cubans to charter bottoms for this purpose.
