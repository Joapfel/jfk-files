During his interview, Casarin stated that his recollection of the discussion mentioned in the 25 November 1963 memo was that it took place during the last days of his tenure with the 6 Branch. This would have placed the discussion sometime in July 1962. He stated that the date assigned to the discussion in the memos must have been erroneous, as he was traveling with his family between assignments in the summer of 1960 and did not begin this assignment with the 6 Branch until September of that year. (Ibid., pp. 4–5)
