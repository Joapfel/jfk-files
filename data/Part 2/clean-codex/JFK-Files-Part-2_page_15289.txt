Memorandum from R. D. Cotter to W. C. Sullivan  
Subject: Intelligence Coverage of Non-Soviet-Bloc Countries  
65-63113

The Service is training fifty “deep cover” intelligence agents to be dispatched to the United States to collect intelligence, as France is no longer closely associated with NATO. CIA has also furnished information indicating that two French diplomats attached to the French Materiel Military Mission are suspected intelligence agents.

Action:

It is recommended that Bureau Liaison contact appropriate officials at the Department of State to obtain State clearance for the FBI to conduct discreet investigations of potential security informants in French diplomatic establishments in the United States. Liaison should advise State that no interviews will be conducted with any potential sources without first obtaining State Department clearance.

L.H. OK H/P V SS

Discussed with A.S. Beford (Bedford), 12/5/66.

Will advise CGM.

12/2/66. Approved by A.S. Beford (Bedford), State.

Attention: B.
