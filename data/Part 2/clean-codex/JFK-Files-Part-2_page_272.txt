Dispatch. To: Chief of Station, Mexico City. Info from: Chief, WH Division.

Subject: LICOONY-1 participation in Mexican Writers' Association.

Reference: EDEA 26,621, 15 July 1965. Removed: CS Project and EDEA. CS classification: 50-6-129/3. Job No. 4787077. Box: 27. Fold: 3.

1. LICOONY-1's efforts as described in reference have substantial merit and some potential, particularly if the Station can find a way to tie them in with the LIREAM effort in the intellectual field.

2. LICOONY-1 certainly is to be commended for her effort in getting writers both of the right and left, such as Carlos PELLICER Camara, to join the AEM. From a KUBARK standpoint, the most interesting contact is the one with PELLICER because he has been the reported force behind the COMES/CLE effort in Mexico.

3. Although her assigned tasks as reported in paragraphs 7a, b, and d of the LILINK memo do not entail making her aware of other Station assets engaged in MHSPAWN operations among the intellectuals, an amendment to her OA is required, and one has been requested. If her efforts progress to the point where she might be able to influence leftist writers in their efforts to establish the CLE in Mexico (paragraph 7c of the LILINK memo), Headquarters should be forwarded your ideas on why and how LICOONY-1 should be used as an active MHSPAWN agent, and another amendment will be required for this role.

4. ANDRIETTE will discuss this operation with the Station during his TDY.

5. In view of the restricted OA which confines KUBARK's association with LICOONY-1, it is important that Headquarters remain aware of any unusual activity in her personal life which might affect her security or the granting of amendments to her OA for individual operations. Therefore, we would appreciate your keeping us informed on a more timely basis of any undertakings such as the recruitments for membership in the AEM described in reference.

Distribution: Original and 1 to COS, Mexico City. CS copy filed in 50-6-129/3. Ernest A. LUSBY.
