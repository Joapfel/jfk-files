5 January 1965

Memorandum for the Record

Subject: Luis Albert Ferro (201-205712)

1. WH/2/Dominican Republic holds Subject's file, which has been loaned to DO/SA. Mr. Allen on 5 January 1965 telephoned the DR Desk Officer, Mr. David McGrath, and explained that DO/SA would like to make Subject witting of KUBARK interest in WUBRINY D. Mr. McGrath said that the Desk is not now using Subject and that it has no objection to DO/SA briefing Subject. He asked that a memorandum covering the contact be prepared for inclusion in the Subject's file.

2. Mr. Allen on 5 January 1965 informed DO/Security that DO/SA desires to make Subject witting of KUBARK interest in WUBRINY D. A PCSA had previously been granted on 8 December 1964 with the understanding that a CSA can be quickly obtained because of the existing O/A on him. DO/Security said that necessary action for a CSA would be undertaken at once and that he foresees no problem.
