Betsy Palmer

Review of Manuel Ray y Rivero Files, Volumes I–VIII

March 29, 1978

Volume II  
October 1, 1960–November 22, 1960

As of 21 November 1960, MRP was organized as follows:

Manuel Ray — Chief.  
Ray Chibas — Special Assistant to Ray.  
Juan Esteves Ramirez (Ramires) — Secretary.  
Ramon Barquin — Military and intelligence matters.  
Cisneros — Chief of MRP in Cuba.

1. 10/4/60 cable from London claims that a new Cuban resistance group was organized under Ray and has 400–500 men in Las Villas Province. It is not part of FRD but is in touch with it.

2. 10/7/60 cable from Havana. AURELIANO SANCHEZ ARANGO has withdrawn from FRD. JUSTO CARRILLO and FELIPE PAZOS will be integrated into Ray’s MRP. VARONA wishes to join also, but Ray insists VARONA return to Cuba to do so.

3. 10/12/60 cable to Director. RAMON BARQUIN claims CARRILLO and ARANGO are going to boycott the FRD Executive Committee and sabotage its work. Barquin is planning to join with Pazos, Ray, Chibas, MIRO, and QUEVEDO to form a new frente and then request union with FRD. If accepted, Barquin would become military head. If Barquin does not get U.S. government support for this plan, he will return to Cuba to try to overthrow Castro and win election on an anti-Yankee platform.

4. 10/17/60 contact report from Wallace A. Parlett. Subject: Meeting with JUAN ESTEVEZ and JORGE BERUFF, 10/13/60. Cubans said Ray’s group will not lower themselves to answer the “Fidelismo without Fidel” charge and will let their actions speak for themselves. MRP is highly disciplined and feels the majority of Cuban people favor its platform. Its leaders are interchangeable. If Ray comes out of Cuba, Chibas or Estevez could go in and take his place.

5. 10/24/60 memorandum for files. Subject: Representative of Manuel Ray to Professional Groups.

ENRIQUE HIRAM GUTIERREZ Y RODRIGUEZ (date of birth 5/20/31, Havana) travels frequently to Puerto Rico on architectural business. He also frequently enters the U.S. at Miami. He represents Ray’s organization to various professional groups in Havana and serves as a courier to Miami.

6. DRE information. 10/24/60 cable states that [MULLER] reported receiving a letter from ANTONIO GARCIA, DRE representative in Cuba, stating that the organization is going well but needs funds and material aid. MASH asks Mueller (Muller) to get details of Garcia’s “groupos de accion” and details of the organization. MASH gave Muller $500 for Garcia. [Muller] is concerned about Manolo Ray.
