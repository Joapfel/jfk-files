Mr. Sarno. There may have been. I really can't state which one he was used on if he was ever used for photographic surveillance.

Mr. Wides. Which what he was used on?

Mr. Sarno. I mean if he was used on photographic surveillance. I don't recall ever ordering him out on photographic surveillance.

Mr. Wides. Do you recall if there ever was any photographic surveillance carried out by any of the AMOT's?

Mr. Sarno. I have no recollection. I do not recall.
