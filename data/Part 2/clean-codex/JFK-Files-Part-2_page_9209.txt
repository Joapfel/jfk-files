Title: Black Panther Party

Character: Extremist Matter

Synopsis: BPP headquarters formerly located at 2228 Bewick and 10438 McNichols, Detroit. The office on Bewick closed during the month of November 1973, and the office at 10438 McNichols closed in February 1974; both facilities were vacated. BPP leader Philander Lonnie Darden and the majority of active members relocated to Oakland, California, to assist the National Headquarters in that city. All community programs and related activities terminated. Identities of leaders, active members, and relocating members set out. Activities conducted and financial status set out.
