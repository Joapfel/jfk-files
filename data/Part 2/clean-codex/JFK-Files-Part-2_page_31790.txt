“From Berkeley to D.C.

“At last summer’s Communist Party convention in New York City, Bloice was elected to the CP’s National Committee, and the requirements for serving on that policy board make it clear Bloice has been a CP member since at least January 1964, although intelligence agencies place it many years earlier.

“Just two months ago the newsletter of the Robert Scheer campaign—he is keeping his organization intact under the name of Community for New Politics (CNP)—carried a social note: ‘Carl Bloice, heretofore coordinator for CNP, has left Berkeley for the larger battlefield in Washington, D.C., where he will be a correspondent for People’s World and The Worker. At a farewell party December 23 at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Robert Scheer, Carl was presented with several going-away gifts, including a larger-than-life poster of Mao Tse-tung.… Carl has indicated he will be an occasional correspondent [for the Scheer newsletter].… We all wish him the best of luck in Washington and we feel fully comforted by the fact that we are not losing a coordinator, but gaining a spy.’

“The portrait of Mao probably brought a gust of laughter when it was unveiled and presented to the Moscow-line Bloice, and the ‘gaining a spy’ phrase was simply New Left humor. “Scheer has told Human Events that he hired Bloice ‘for his technical competence’; that he likes him as a person, but violently disagrees with his politics.
