FREE THE DETROIT “16”

The Detroit “16” are 16 brothers and sisters who are being held in the medical dungeons better known as Wayne County Jail, on charges of murder and conspiracy to commit murder.

On October 24, these brothers and sisters were taken out of the Black (16th & Myrtle) community for feeding hungry kids in the morning, for trying to establish a free medical clinic which would serve all the people, and other survival programs. But the pigs’ justification for taking the people’s warriors off the street was for the murder of Pig Glenn Smith and conspiring to murder Pig Glenn Smith.

Five of the eight sisters are out on $5,000 bonds each. Only one of the eight brothers, Lorrie Darden, has bond, which is $20,000 and two substitutes. The remaining seven are without bonds.
