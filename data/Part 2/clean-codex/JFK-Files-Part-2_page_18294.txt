United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. J. B. Adams

From: Legal Counsel

Subject: SENSTUDY 75

Mr. Mintz; Mr. Adams; Mr. Wannall; Mr. Cregar; Mr. Hotis; Mr. Daly.

Date: 9/2/75

All information contained herein is unclassified by spammel.

On 8/22/75, John T. Elliff, Task Force Director for the Domestic Intelligence Task Force of the Senate Select Committee, advised that regarding proposed plans for testimony by Bureau officials before the Senate Select Committee that the Committee tentatively scheduled hearings for mid-October. He cited October 16th as being the approximate date. Initially, according to Elliff, hearings concerning the Bureau were to have been initiated in the first week of October but the Staff Members did not have the necessary background information to conduct the hearings at that time and instead scheduled hearings concerning IRS for early October.

Elliff indicated that the tentative format for these hearings would be to furnish the Bureau an outline of or a series of questions pertaining to the area to be covered in testimony prior to our appearance. He indicated that the hearings would cover a narrow topic on any given day so that a Bureau official could be briefed and respond to questions raised concerning the particular subject matter.

Elliff indicated that typical subjects of hearings concerning the FBI would be a one-day hearing on political abuse or a series of hearings on COINTELPRO.

Elliff indicated that it was the Committee's intent to continue hearings regarding the FBI into mid-December once they are initiated with the Bureau testifying at least once a week during that period. He advised they also envisioned the initial hearing as setting forth what the scope of the Senate Select Committee inquiry had been into the FBI and of topics to be covered by hearings. Elliff felt this would inform the House Select Committee of what areas the Senate Select Committee had delved into.

Recommendation: For information.
