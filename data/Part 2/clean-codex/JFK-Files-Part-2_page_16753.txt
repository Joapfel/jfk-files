Date: 2/26/70.

At 6:00 PM, an autopsy was performed on the body of Frederick Allen Hampton by Dr. Charles Petty, pathologist from Dallas, Texas. The autopsy was performed at the Veterans Administration Hospital, Shreveport, Louisiana. Those in attendance, according to a list provided by the U.S. Marshal, Shreveport, Louisiana, were as follows:

For the State of Illinois: Thomas Bett, Assistant State Attorney; Nick Motherway, Assistant State Attorney; Dr. Jerry Kearns, Cook County Coroner's Office; Dr. William Hevrells, Pathologist.

For the Hampton family: Francis E. Andrew, Attorney; Dr. David Spiegel, Pathologist; Dr. Earl Caldarell, Pathologist.

For the U.S. Government: Dr. Charles Petty; Jerris Leonard, Assistant Attorney General, Civil Rights Division, U.S. Department of Justice; James Turner, Deputy Assistant Attorney General, U.S. Department of Justice; Donald E. Walter, U.S. Attorney, Shreveport, Louisiana; Deputy U.S. Marshal.
