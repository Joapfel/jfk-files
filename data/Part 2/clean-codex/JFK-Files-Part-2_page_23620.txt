The White House Office

1. The President has now approved the contingency plans discussed during your stay here, with one major exception: you are not to request a pre-signed request for help from Dominican dissident groups.

2. We consider it essential that you continue to work to strengthen sentiment among dissident groups. Your work along this line had been very积极; however, we also must not permit any danger of disclosing U.S. interests with political assassination or intervention in the Dominican Republic. This principle is overriding and must prevail in doubtful situations. However much you can do to protect U.S. interests within this framework, use most of the measures in which you are not.

If an overthrow of Trujillo—whether or not in the case of assassination or by a pro-U.S. group—the pro-U.S. group should promptly make every effort to constitute itself as a reasonable and plausible provisional government. It should immediately declare itself such and request assistance from the U.S. Venezuela, Colombia, and the OAS are favorable to this procedure, and it will be followed up.
