Department of State Telegram.

To U.S. Mission Berlin. Info: American Embassy Bonn. Berlin, Germany, and Sweden.

HCM582. State 25B355.

CIMDIS.

Subject: U.S. prisoners in East Germany—Richard Case Nagell and Leon Burris James.

1. J. Ricey New informed Department October 18 that Nagell will be released October 23; will require immediate medical attention due to mental condition. Ricey New arriving Berlin afternoon October 21, and will coordinate arrangements with mission.

2. Ricey New also informed by Vogel that another American citizen, Leon Burris James, being detained in East Germany on unspecified charges. As mission aware from previous experiences with him, James has long history of psychiatric problems and has been in frequent difficulties in many countries over last five years or so. He was convicted of passport fraud in January 1966 and is currently under indictment for violation of Title 18 USC Section 1542. A warrant has been issued for his arrest. Most recent episode of which Department aware was his deportation from Czechoslovakia to FRG in April this year. A summary of James' adventures and arrests is being airouched. We have informed Ricey New in a general way of this background.

3. Ricey New wished to inform us herself of status of these.
