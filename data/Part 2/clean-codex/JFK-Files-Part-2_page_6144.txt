Congress intended for the Board to oversee the opening to the public of a substantial amount of material—perhaps in the millions of pages. n96 Congress, therefore, clothed the Board with broad subpoena and other powers. n97 The Board is without precedent in American history, with powers that reach far beyond, for example, the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA). n98 The Board's only task is to make the public record of one epic historical event as complete as possible. n99
