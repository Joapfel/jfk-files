United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. D. J. Brennan, Jr.  
From: S. J. Papich  
Subject: Stokely Carmichael, Eldridge Cleaver racial matter  
Date: October 28, 1969

All information contained herein is unclassified except where shown otherwise.

Reference is made to memorandum dated 10/20/69, received from CIA setting forth information from a sensitive source to the effect that “Carmichael remained in Africa because he feared that President Nixon would try to arrest him for the assistance he had given Eldridge Cleaver in fleeing to Cuba.” The Liaison Agent has been endeavoring to obtain an evaluation of this information.

On 10/27/69, Richard Ober, CIA, advised that the source of the information was “SUGAR” (Richard Gibson). He stated “SUGAR” did not furnish any additional details. He further confided that because of past experiences, the reliability of the information could definitely be questioned. In the past, “SUGAR” has furnished information of a general or nonspecific nature.

Action:

The above information is being directed to the attention of the Racial Intelligence Section.
