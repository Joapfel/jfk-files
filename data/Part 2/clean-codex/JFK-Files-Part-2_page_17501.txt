Chicago, Illinois

January 23, 1970

Fredrick Allen Hampton

Hampton, Deputy Chairman of the Illinois Chapter of the Black Panther Party (BPP), was identified as one person killed in the first-floor apartment at 2337 West Monroe, Chicago, Illinois, on December 4, 1969, as the result of a gun battle between alleged BPP members occupying this apartment and officers of the Illinois State's Attorney's Police who were attempting to serve a search warrant for illegal possession of weapons on the occupants of this apartment.

See appendix for characterization of BPP.

The State of Illinois Coroner's Certificate of Death lists the cause of Hampton's death as a gunshot wound of the head and brain, and the date of death as December 4, 1969. The coroner's physician's signature is shown as Edward J. Shalgos, M.D.

Hampton was buried at Haynesville, Louisiana, in the Bethel Mission Baptist Cemetery.
