WFO (100-54927)

From: Philadelphia (100-53818) (P)

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WOMAAC)-sponsored demonstration, November 20 next, Washington, D.C., is New Left. (OO: WFO)

On November 17 last, a confidential source who has provided reliable information in the past advised that no charter has been placed with Greyhound Lines by the Women United for Abortion Rights (WUFAR) or related groups.

On November 17 last, a second confidential source who has provided reliable information in the past advised that a request for ten buses was made with the Public Service Coordinated Transportation by WUFAR, but the company could not provide the desired number of buses, so the order was not taken.

On November 17 last, a third confidential source who has provided reliable information in the past advised that WUFAR placed a deposit with the Trenton Philadelphia Coach.
