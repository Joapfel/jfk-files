2. Robert Armstrong

On page 402 it is indicated that Nosenko provided no source for his information regarding Robert Armstrong.

Comment:

On November 14, 1967, (N-22c), Nosenko stated he had heard from Gryaznov that the 1st Department suspected Armstrong was a homosexual and planned “active” work against him.

3. Harold Berman

On page 403 it is stated that Nosenko did not indicate the source of his information on Harold Berman.

Comment:

On August 1, 1968, (N-213), Nosenko stated that Berman was in the USSR several times. He was first of interest to the 7th Department in 1957 or 58 but later in 1960, when he came in connection with the Powers case, he was of interest to 1st Department.
