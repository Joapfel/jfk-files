Persons/entities: Richard Helms; Mr. Harvey. Categories: Castro.

Occurrence: Helms testified that he does not recall whether he selected Harvey to head the Cuban operation, but that he did certainly approve Harvey's selection. Prior to receiving the commitment, Harvey had been in charge of a staff in the foreign intelligence staff whose function was to mount operations against Kissies and installations of foreign powers overseas to acquire codes and ciphers by surreptitious entry (49–50).

Source: Helms, 6/13/75. Staff: Rhea. Date: 6/28/75.
