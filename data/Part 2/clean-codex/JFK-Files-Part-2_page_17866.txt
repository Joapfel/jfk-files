To: Director, FBI.

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

From: Columbia (105-NEW).

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re: Bureau NITEL to all SACs, 24 March 1975.

Columbia presently has zero agents assigned to counterintelligence matters. In internal security matters Columbia has the following number of agents and supervisors assigned the following percentages of time:

Agents: 1 at 75 percent; 1 at 30 percent; 3 at 20 percent; 1 at 10 percent; 3 at 5 percent or less.

Supervisors: 1 at 20 percent.

End.
