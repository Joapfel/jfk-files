In discussing BKHERALD approach to Soviet target, AARON asked if it really made sense to try to recruit Soviets in “out of way places” where a Soviet, even if recruited, would not likely have access to information of value to USG. We explained that our interests are not primarily in local Soviet activities but in internal Soviet matters, pointed out access Soviets have to this information even while abroad, said some Soviets recruited abroad are selected for internal handling, and also pointed out the CI value of certain types of information provided by recruited RIS officers. None of this seemed to impress AARON, who continued to question the value of BKHERALD Soviet operations in many, but undefined, areas. He said he had worked in the White House where he saw all intelligence, and found it hard to justify BKHERALD Soviet program input on the basis of intelligence production. We argued this is one program where a large amount of case officer time is required on a worldwide basis in order to acquire the few good sources who can be picked up and who are picked up. TRUEHEART asked for examples, and we referred them to headquarters for details. TRUEHEART said it was his impression that BKHERALD is now in a “Soviet phase,” whereas in earlier years it had been in covert action or other phases. We said Soviets have always been a major target, and our approach to them has been refined over the years.
