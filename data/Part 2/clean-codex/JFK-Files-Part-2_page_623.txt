Classified message.

B. LIRENO-1, former Mexican immigration officer, will be queried on his ability to recontact former colleagues along the border area who have access to information on arms smuggling for possible unilateral recruitment. This is a difficult operation, however, because of combo problems created by distance from Mexico City. Also, anyone who is not from the border area is easily spotted by locals and would create suspicion.

C. On 5 or 6 January 1967, Mexican and Guatemalan authorities plan to meet to discuss smuggling activities and what these governments can do to jointly smash arms traffic. COS Guatemala will find out who attended the meeting and what was agreed upon to give Mexico City Station a clearer idea of follow-up possibilities.

D. In view of the fact LIREBEL-1's trip to Cuba has not panned out to date and the fact he could be of immediate use to Guatemala City Station in working with the Guatemalan government on guerrillas and arms traffic, plan to cancel Cuba travel and have him re-enter.

9 January 1967
