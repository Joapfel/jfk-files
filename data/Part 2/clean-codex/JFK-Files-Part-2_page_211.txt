TYPIC LICOOKY AMROD.

Reference: DIR 92845.

21 April 1966.

C0988.

1. STASHA (S) now in town staying in boarding house. LICOOKY spending considerable time with her and hopes to get answers to reference questions by end 20 April session.

2. S did great deal of writing during Miami trip, mostly on communal formations of Miami exiles, which she gave to NY CUIS case officer. Arrived in MEXI with no travel funds and after near hysteria found husband had sent tickets to CUBANA office here. Plans to take 22 April CUBANA flight, though does not know how long will remain in Cuba. Definitely hopes to go to Poland to improve Polish and while there make up mind whether wants continue Cuban association. Whole attitude here is one of distrust of validity of her own decisions and constantly asks L for advice. L has so far avoided giving any.

3. REYNANDER sees LICOOKY at 1008 local time 21 April and can relay any further questions HOS wishes put.

LICOOKY, 201
