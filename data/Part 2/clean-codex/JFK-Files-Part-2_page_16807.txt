Report of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, Washington, D.C. 20535.

To: F&I, Chicago.

Re: Sgt. Daniel R. Groth, et al.; Chicago police officers; Fred A. Hampton (deceased), et al.—victims; summary punishment—CR.

Date: March 3, 1970.

Specimen received: 3/2/70.

Q283: .45 Auto bullet from knee of Verlina Brewer.

Results of examination: Specimen Q283 is a .45 Auto bullet that was identified as having been fired from the .45 Thompson submachine gun, serial number 5954, previously submitted and referred to as specimen K21.
