16 October 1965

Memorandum for the Ambassador

Subject: Lee O'SULLO/Contact with the Soviet Embassy

Ambassador notified.

1. The following information has been received.

On 1 October 1965, an American made contact with the Soviet Ambassador and identified himself as La: OSWAD. This officer informed the Soviet Ambassador that he had been at the Soviet Embassy on 26 September 1955 and told him about Valery Yelchinov, a partner of the Ambassador. In addition to being at the Soviet Embassy, he received a reply from the Ambassador indicating his request. He is also supplying information with regard to this request.

2. Our envoys are informed so long as the OSWA above is producing until the end of October 1959 to be in Russia, Belarus, a former member of the U.S. Marine Corps who defected to the Soviet Union in October 1979.

3. This office will advise you if additional information on this matter is needed.

The Ambassador  
The Minister  
Consular for Political Affairs  
Regional Security Officer
