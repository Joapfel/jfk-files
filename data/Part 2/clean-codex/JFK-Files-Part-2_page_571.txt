270045Z January 1968. Cite: Mexico City 4277.

Director info JMWAVE.

PERUMEN LIRING-4.

References: A. Mexico City 4123 (IN 29420). B. Director 67665. C. Mexico City 4271 (IN 38363).

1. In further conversations with LIRING-4, ROWTON reached the following tentative agreement:

A. ROWTON will provide 608 dollars for three months for maintenance of LIRING-4 and her daughter (child now going with MIXER) in Cuba, plus 150 dollars to cover travel expenses.

B. LIRING-4 will inform ROWTON via S/W of detectable (DEFECTABLE) targets and of progress made in obtaining a position of interest in MINREX, Interior Ministry, or Presidency.

C. If ROWTON instructs her to remain in Havana beyond the three-month period (maximum amount of time she is allowed to stay out of Mexico without jeopardizing her immigrant status in that country), ROWTON will arrange reinstatement of the full time already accumulated in Mexico and will deposit to a New York account in LIRING-4's name “reasonable” monthly compensation commensurate with the position she obtained.
