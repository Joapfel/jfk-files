Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

REBUNITEL to all SACs, March 24, 1975.

The following is a breakdown of field agent personnel assigned to internal security and counterintelligence matters in the St. Louis Division as requested by re communication.

Neither the SAC nor the ASAC are assigned to internal security or counterintelligence matters. Only one supervisor is assigned to such matters; he spends approximately 55 percent of his time on internal security and two percent on counterintelligence matters.

Six agents spend approximately 75 percent of their time on internal security and one percent on counterintelligence matters.

The remaining agents spend no time on counterintelligence matters but spend the listed percentage of time on internal security: three agents, 50 percent; three agents, 25 percent; one agent, 10 percent; two agents, 5 percent; two agents, 2 percent.
