Friday 26 May

To: Robert C. Morrison  
From: William Webb  
Re: Access to Autopsy Photographs

At 3:50 p.m. on 5/27/78, Ida Dox, the medical illustrator, requested permission to view the Kennedy autopsy photographs along with the medical panel. After trying to contact Blakey and Matthews (Mathews) without success, I, William Webb, authorized Oliver Champion to allow the panel and Mrs. Dox access.
