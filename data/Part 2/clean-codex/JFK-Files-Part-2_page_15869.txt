Source advised the National BPP, Berkeley, California, sent 23 cartons of newspapers to Chicago on November 15, 1969. The charges were $158.60.

November 14, 1969: Source stated the Illinois BPP sent $2,000 to the National BPP. $1,700 was in payment for newspapers, $250 for records, and $50 for buttons. The Chicago chapter also requested that 27,000 papers be sent to Chicago.

December 17, 1969: Source advised that Lucy Montgomery, former wife of a prominent Chicago attorney and minority-group sympathizer, was alleged to have donated $2,000 to the BPP for a medical center.

November 19, 1969: Source also stated the BPP had approximately $3,000 in cash, the money being received from the return of various bonds.

November 19, 1969: Source stated that allegedly the BPP had received approximately $40,000 in total donations since the death of Hampton, the largest donation being received from the University of Wisconsin during the week of January 12, 1970, in the amount of $4,000.

January 9, 1970.

VIII. Contacts with Communist Party and Other Groups

Source stated that a CP of Illinois meeting was held on October 18, 1969, and attended by National Officers Claude Lightfoot and Charlene Mitchell. At this meeting it was stated that the CP lacks influence in black communities and that there was no direct relationship between Communists working in the BPP.

October 22, 1969.
