Memorandum

January 11, 1967

To: Louis Ivon, Investigator  
From: Lynn Loisel, Investigator  
Re: Phone call from Jimmy Johnson regarding Ferrie and the Smith case (January 10, 1967, 4:00 p.m.)

It is to be noted that Jimmy Johnson, who was once a close associate of Ferrie but broke free from Ferrie, renewed his friendship with Ferrie as a favor for this office and for information purposes.

Jimmy stated that Ferrie had contacted Pershing the other day regarding the Cuban woman's name that was mentioned in the Saturday Evening Post article.

Ferrie had called a lawyer (name unknown) last night, January 9, 1967, and spoke with the lawyer about his being investigated by this office. Details of the conversation are unknown.

Ferrie has a lot of old army paraphernalia in his apartment, such as walkie-talkies, incendiary bombs, etc.

Ferrie was showing nude pictures (moving films) to two young boys when a district police car shined a light on his front porch. He became frightened and uncontrollable, telling Jimmy that the police must suspect him.

Jimmy stated that one Steve Littleton parked a twin-engine Beechcraft in Ferrie's spot at the airport and that their plane was flown in from out of town. It is at Ferrie's disposal. He also heard Ferrie and Littleton talking about a little island somewhere and expressing interest in knowing if they could land the Beechcraft on this island.
