SENSTUDY, 1975.

On July 24, 1975, John F. Kehoe, Jr., former SA, Commissioner of Public Safety, Commonwealth of Massachusetts, advised that on July 23, 1975, he had received a telephone call from Paul Wallach, who stated he was a staff member of the United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities. Wallach gave his telephone number as 202-224-1700. Wallach asked Kehoe if he was a former Special Agent of the FBI, and Kehoe replied in the affirmative. Wallach then inquired whether Kehoe had been involved in electronic surveillance or other covert intelligence-gathering activities in New York City in the 1960s.
