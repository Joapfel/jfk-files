RESCATE (Movimiento de Rescate Constitucional Democrático) (Cuban Revolutionary Democratic Rescue Committee), 19-120-39.

Anti-Castro revolutionary group; one of the groups forming FRD (Democratic Revolutionary Front) and subsequent CRC (Cuban Revolutionary Council). It was controlled by Antonio de Varona, with headquarters in Miami.

RIMM 4359 (IN 2330), dated 8 April 1961.

Radio message from an on-island agent. Concerns the fact that a Cuban by the name of Hilly Patten, who had been in exile in Miami a year previously, belonged to the Rescate group and apparently would be bringing arms in from Venezuela soon. The agent said his personal opinion was that Tony Varona wanted to produce armed action before other groups and for that reason had purchased arms on his own.

DBA 5681, dated 28 March 1962 (FBI).

Stated that Sergio Aparicio, Secretary General of Rescate in New York City, advised a State Department representative that Rescate had purchased about 30 weapons, which were delivered to Miami and then by boat to the underground in Cuba.
