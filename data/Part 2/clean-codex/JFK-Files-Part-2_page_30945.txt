Carlos Zarraga Martinez. Cuban Revolutionary Council and Norte Revolucionario Democrats.

30 October 1967.

Subject: FRD—created by Agency in May 1960.

Allegation: Horuma bunker alleged to be CIA cache for use during the Bay of Pigs invasion. Sergio Arcache Smith accused by Garrison (Garisson) of robbing this bunker.

Details: JRD (FRD) created with Agency assistance, guidance, and financial support, and consisted of several anti-Castro groups. Formed as a political action, propaganda, and military unit. Helped recruit and carried on a program of social assistance for the 2506 Brigade and their families.

Circa January 1961, the CRC formed to coordinate and direct FRD activities with Agency and White House approval and support.

FRD completely absorbed by CRC in September 1964.

CRC had direct access to President Kennedy and top White House aides.
