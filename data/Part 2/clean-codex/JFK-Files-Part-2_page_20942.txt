WFO 100-52290

On 5/18/72, a source in a position to know advised that the following individuals were connected with the Sojourner Truth Press:

Sally Gabb, 1190 Mansfield Ave., N.E., Atlanta, Ga.

Nancy Jones, 348 Mell Ave., N.E., Atlanta, Ga.

Jennie Hamilton, 193 Elizabeth Ave., N.E., Atlanta, Ga.

Alexander Joyce, 348 Mell Ave., N.E., Atlanta, Ga.

On 7/7/72, OK 405-B advised that the Sojourner Truth Press was a printing press for hire, which prints mostly New Left material for New Left organizations, yet espouses no opinions of its own. As long as their fee is met, they print what is desired (papers, pamphlets, flyers, etc.).

All those individuals mentioned above are, individually, subjects of security investigations in Atlanta.
