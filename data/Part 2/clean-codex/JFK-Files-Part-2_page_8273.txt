Military attaché PIRAY.

[He was] picked up by Greek Alien Directorate (over 2). On 19 April 1951, [he] was to meet PIRAY at the French Embassy to give detailed accounts of U.S. operations of which he was aware, with special emphasis on Radio Bonanin, and receive 10–15,000,000 drachmae for this information. Apparently did not make this who, as he had been arrested; see above.
