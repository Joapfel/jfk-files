FBI. Date: 11/12/71.

Transmit the following in plaintext or code. Via AIRTEL (Priority).

To: Director, FBI (Attn: DID). From: SAC, New York (100-174481).

Subject: Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) sponsored demonstration, November 20, 1971, Washington, D. C. — New Left (OO:WFO).

Re WFO airtel, dated 11/5/71.

(NY 4253-S), who has furnished reliable information in the past, advised on 11/12/71 that a New York planning meeting of the captioned group was held at the Loeb Student Center, New York University, New York, NY, on 11/11/71.

For the information of the Bureau and WFO, plans formulated during this meeting called for the renting of 20 buses which can transport approximately 1,000 individuals. The cost for a round-trip ticket from New York, NY, to Washington, D. C. (WDC) will be $10.00. The buses are scheduled to depart Union Square, New York, NY, at 6:00 AM on 11/20/71, and return the same day.

NY 4253-S advised that the above meeting was completely dominated and controlled by members of the Socialist Workers Party (SWP).

Special Agent in Charge.
