DE T-1

February 8, 1971

On January 4, 1971, Edgar Robinson, distribution manager for the Detroit NCCF, visited the office at Flint, Michigan, and told a woman from the neighborhood who dropped in that there are eight million Panthers in the country who will take up the gun and take over the country, giving it a blood bath if two BPP members on trial in New Haven, Connecticut, are convicted.

On Sunday, January 10, 1971, members of the NCCF were marching in the street in front of 611-13 Continental after the political education meeting, and were made to move by the police.

On Sunday, January 17, 1971, members of the NCCF again marched in front of 611-13 Continental and had observers from the community patrolling the streets, hoping to get some favorable publicity by causing an incident when the police arrived. However, no police cars were seen, and no more march—

Wayne Theodore Robertson, leader of the Flint branch of the Detroit NCCF, at their regular political education meeting on January 31, 1971, placed emphasis on the brothers arming themselves and recommended that they get a pistol to carry as soon as possible.
