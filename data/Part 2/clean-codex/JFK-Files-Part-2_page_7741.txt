Dear Sir,

I wrote a letter on February 15, 1978, to Mr. James Callaghan, Prime Minister, enclosed registered mail No. 251067.

On July 18, 1963, I was in London, England, at the airport. Immigration Officer No. 175 found that I had no visa and placed me under house arrest. Mr. Smith, in police uniform, Secret Service, brought me a steak dinner, and a police officer interrogated me. He asked me what the Embassy of the U.S.S.R. said and what I had heard in Vienna, Austria. I said I had heard that one American defector, Mr. Lee Harvey Oswald, had defected to Russia and returned to the United States of America. This man was preparing to kill, to assassinate, President John F. Kennedy. Mr. Smith, the police officer, asked me, “Do you know his name?” I said his name started with “O.” His name was published one time in a newspaper, the Washington Post. Mr. Smith, the police officer, said it was easy to find.
