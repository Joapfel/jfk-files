Several days later, while warning a key Congolese leader about coup plots led by Lumumba and two of his supporters, the Chief of Station urged arrest or other more permanent disposal of Lumumba, Gizenga, and Mulele (CIA Cable, IN 15643, Leopoldville to Director, 9/20/61).

Gizenga and Mulele were Lumumba's lieutenants who were leading his supporters while Lumumba was in UN custody.
