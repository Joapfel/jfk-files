Orlando Bosch Avila — Office of Special Counsel 257 921. Volume dated 1962–78. Deletions: 1 sealed envelope.

5 March 1977 cable IV. 207098. To: Director CITE Caracas. Response to a 4 March news article in “El Mundo,” Caracas, which named CIA after Orlando Bosch, Ricardo Morales, Navarette, Orlando Garcia Vasquez, Luis Posada Carriles, etc. Cable states, “Fortunately, none are currently active assets.”

8 April 1977 memorandum. Chief, Security Analysis Group. From: John F. Sullivan Jr.; Michael W. Rameck. Subject: El Mundo Exposure. Office of Security indicated a check on names, some of which were outlined above: “In those cases above where a security file exists, there is no evidence that any of the individuals are current, active assets.”
