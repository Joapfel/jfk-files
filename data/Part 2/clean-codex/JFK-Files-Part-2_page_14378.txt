Teletype to SACs, Alexandria et al. Re: SENSTUDY 75. 62-116395.

On FBI's activities at Democratic Convention, Atlantic City, August 22–28, 1964.

Set out below are names and last known addresses of former Bureau agents assigned to special squad at Atlantic City, August 1964. Each of these former agents is to be immediately contacted and alerted that they might be approached by the SSC staff. They are not, repeat not, to be advised of the area which may be covered in any interview of them by the SSC. They should, however, be told that in the event they are interviewed and during the course of same, questions are asked which relate to sensitive Bureau operations, they can request that an FBI agent be present. Contacts with these former agents to be handled personally by SAC or ASAC. In the event this not feasible for just cause, to be handled by a senior supervisor.

Immediately after contact, results should be furnished Bureau by teletype in above caption. If a former agent no longer in your territory or temporarily away, set out lead to other office immediately with copy to FBHQ.

Newark should include recontact with Devlin for purpose indicated above and also furnish Bureau any information Devlin may have furnished your office in addition to that in your teletype.
