1. Richard Case Nagell, also known as Joe Cramer, Joe Krane, and Robert C. Nolan, 201-746537, well known to LNERGO. Subject entered the U.S. Army in 1948 and was honorably discharged as a captain in 1959. During the Korean conflict he received many campaign awards. He was the sole survivor of an airplane crash in November 1954; several suffered facial disfigurement and were given psychiatric examinations. Nagell's mother, brother, former wife, and friend all consider him to be mentally disturbed. There is no indication Nagell was involved in espionage, the Communist Party, or Fair Play for Cuba Committee activity.

2. In a 1964 letter to LNERGO, Nagell referred to another letter concerning Oswald, which he allegedly wrote to LNERGO in 1963. LNERGO files contain no record of the 1963 letter. Nagell was imprisoned on a bank robbery charge and has attempted suicide.
