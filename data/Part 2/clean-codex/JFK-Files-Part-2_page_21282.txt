Country: Costa Rica/Central America. DOI: 19–25 September 1971. Subject: Christian Democratic Regional Seminar for Central American Christian Democratic Leaders.

Acquired: El Salvador, San Salvador, 29 September 1971.

Source: A deputy of the Christian Democratic Party in the National Legislative Assembly. He is a usually reliable reporter. He obtained the information from a person who attended the seminar.

1.
