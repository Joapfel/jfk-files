VII. Hijackings of U.S. Planes

The following are summaries of all hijackings since January 1, 1968. There has been a total of 70 incidents of hijackings or attempts to hijack aircraft during this period. There were 56 hijackings and 14 aborted hijackings.

During 1968, there were 17 hijackings to Cuba and 3 aborted hijackings.

During 1969, there were 31 hijackings to Cuba, 1 to Damascus, 1 to Rome, and 6 aborted hijackings.

During 1970, thus far there have been 6 hijackings, 5 of which went to Cuba, and 1 to Lebanon, along with 5 aborted hijackings.

1968. Number and date: (1), February 17, 1968. Name: Thomas James Boynton. Airline: privately owned. From Marathon, Florida, to Cuba at gunpoint with pilot aboard. Process filed February 20, 1968, Florida. Returned to United States through Canada, November 2, 1969, pleaded guilty May 12, 1970, and sentenced to 20 years June 4, 1970.

Number and date: (2), February 21, 1968. Name: Lawrence Mahlon Rhodes, Jr. Airline: Delta. From Tampa, Florida, to Cuba at gunpoint with 102 passengers and crew of 7 aboard. Fleeing felon at time of hijacking. Process filed December 22, 1968, Miami, Florida. Surrendered himself at Madrid, Spain, February 11, 1970. Entered plea of not guilty and is currently undergoing psychiatric examination.
