CP-9212, dated 10 March 1967.

Concerns Jaime CARDONA-Londono, Colombian, arrested in New Orleans in May 1964 for trespassing. He had in his possession a typewritten document that purported to be a proposed constitution for a new super world government, with provisions to condemn to death various personages, including L. Johnson, L. Johnson’s daughter, and Mrs. J. Kennedy. When interviewed on 22 December 1964, he did not indicate any interest in the death of the President.
