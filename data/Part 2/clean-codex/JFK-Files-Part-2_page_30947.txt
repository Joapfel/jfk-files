27 Aug 1965

Memo & file from Laurence G. Parr

From: Alien Affairs Staff

Subject: Ricardo Anibal Morales (Navarrote)

# 380858

On 8/26/65, Morales had returned to the U.S. having completed his assigned duties for SOD, was debriefed, and is no longer of interest to SOD. Hence, no I-512 is to be issued for him to replace the one which expires 9/25/65.

10 Dec. 1964

Memo & file from W. M. Knott

Morales (AAC-1142) - #380858(C) and Alberto Perez Martinez (AAC-1143) - #223124(C) returning to the U.S. on a PAA flight into Kennedy International on 11 Dec. 1964. Braden, I + NS, advised and requested to have his N.Y. office keep the I-94s from showing a N.Y. entry and instead use the following:

Paroled Section 212(d)(5), Dec. 11, 1964, indefinitely.

Regarding assignment in Congo, which began circa 9/25/64. Morales stayed behind to escort Perez; the rest of the group arrived at Andrews AFB on 5 Dec. 1964 on an Air Force plane.
