Central Intelligence Agency.

Country: Ecuador/Cuba/Latin America. Report No. CS-3/535,791. Subject: Request by Cuban Diplomat in Chile to Member of the Revolutionary Union of Ecuadorian Youth for Information on Leftist Activity in Ecuador. Date distributed: 1 February 1963. No. pages: 1. References: RD-W-2107. Date of info: Mid-January 1963. Place and date acquired: Ecuador, Guayaquil (22 January 1963). Field report No. HEG-2108.

Source: Member (C) of the Revolutionary Union of Ecuadorian Youth. Appraisal of content: 3.

Alfredo Vera Arrata, a member of the National Executive Council of the Revolutionary Union of Ecuadorian Youth (URJE) until his expulsion from URJE during its National Convention held in early January 1963, has received a letter from Pedro Martinez Perez, First Secretary of the Cuban Embassy in Santiago, Chile, in which he asks Vera to send a person to Chile to inform him on current leftist activity in Ecuador. Vera plans to send Jaime Galarza Zavala, also expelled from URJE, if funds can be raised for the trip.

Field Comment. Martinez is in charge of Cuban subversive activities in Chile, Peru, and Ecuador.
