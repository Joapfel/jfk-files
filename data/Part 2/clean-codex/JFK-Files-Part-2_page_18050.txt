Teletype. NRWD1 AQ Code. 10:00 AM, urgent, 9/11/75.

To: Director, FBI. From: Albuquerque.

Attention: Section Chief W. U. Cregar, INTD. SESTUDY-75.

On September 10, 1975, Sam J. Papich, former special agent, now residing in Albuquerque, New Mexico, advised he had been contacted by a Mr. Wallach, Senate Select Committee, Washington, D.C., and was advised by Wallach that he had been subpoenaed to testify before the above committee in Washington, D.C., on September 22, 1975. Papich advised he was told to be prepared to testify for approximately eight hours under oath concerning “the mail problem.” Papich expects to arrive in Washington, D.C., September 21, 1975, and will probably stay at Statler.

Papich also advised that he was contacted by a representative of the Department and arrangements were made for Papich to be interviewed in Albuquerque on September 18, 1975, by Departmental attorneys Paul Bouscher and (FNU) Malone. ST. 114 REC-7, 62-116 395-799. According to Papich, this interview concerns his testimony before the Rockefeller Committee.

Legal Counsel: AJH.
