16285CZ. Cite Mexico City 9334. Director info JNJAVE. TYPIC LIRING.

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace S. R outon 14 Dec 66 he had heard in Havana (HAVA) on recent trip (he returned next 5 Dec) that Fidel Castro had recently caused the arrest of eleven members of the editorial staff of Granma (GRAMA) newspaper.

2. L-3 labeled those arrested as members of the “Red Guard,” which had criticized Castro for his recent speech regarding Cuban militia irregularities and difficulties. The criticism, reflected in columns of Granma (GRAMA), brought about the arrest of eleven.

3. Routon asked L-3 for identities of those arrested, but the latter could not supply them except to say one was the husband of his friend Velkis Cusa, not further identified. Routon has asked L-3 to discuss the arrests with Ismael Suarez de la Paz in order to obtain more details.

4. L-3 stated he had discussed internal Cuban affairs with Suarez, and the latter told him Fidel Castro was experiencing difficulties accomplishing objectives because of bureaucratic clumsiness of the Party, underlings, and unbelievable inefficiency of the Cuban diplomatic service. Suarez told L-3 Castro was required to delegate important and sensitive tasks to his close confidants in order to assure their accomplishment.
