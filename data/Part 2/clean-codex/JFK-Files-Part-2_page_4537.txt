Classified message. Routing.

Direction: C/EE 3 (EE notified and copy issued at 1145, 30 October 63 OJ).

Info: DOP, AARP, C/SOD 4, C/CI, C/FI, 2, C/WE 2, C/SAS 5, RF, VR.

30 October 63 N 5047.

Propriety DIP info FRAN, LOND, BRUS, HAGU, MADR, WAVE. Cite HAMB 3880.

Right to be ZPHAGON (CASA ILOR) MHAPRON.

RES: HA 3879 ("5036").

Identity A — Isidro Contreras, commanding officer of the Pinar del Rio naval district with headquarters in Bahia Honda. Classname and very close to Chief of Naval Operations Azierain, who appealed to him to take present position.

Identity B — FNU Ignarra Matarran, commanding officer of the Eastern Naval District (Oriente).

Identity C — Jose Ponce, former commander of the Army of the Center, is in Santa Clara. Began a six-month guided missile course in Port Press in July 63, which is being conducted by high Soviet Army officers. Ponce took part in the Granma landing and is brother-in-law of Ramiro Valdez (Ramimo Valdez), Minister of Interior.

Identity D — Orlando Fernandez, referred to as "Saborit" by friends, commander in the Navy, now employed as executive in Mambisas headquarters in Havana (Hawana).

Identity E — FNU Jomaron, born circa 31, captain in the Navy employed as executive officer to Identity B. Wife, whose brother was killed with rebels at Bay of Pigs, now in the U.S. with two children.
