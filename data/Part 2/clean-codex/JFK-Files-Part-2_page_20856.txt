NY 100-172646

March on the Pentagon. For the information of AX and WFO, these individuals are identified as follows:

Dana Biberman

On 2/3/70, MI-295-S (former NY 6476-S) advised that Dana Biberman was then connected with the Weatherman faction of SDS. (NY file 100-165434; BU file 100-457986.)

Sylvia Warren

On 10/14/69, NY-6909-S advised that Sylvia Warren was the mistress of Brian Flanagan, a member of the Weatherman faction of SDS, at the time he was arrested on 10/11/69 by the Chicago, Ill., Police Department on charges of attempted murder, following a battle between an SDS group and the police in which Richard Elrod, Assistant Corporation Counsel for the City of Chicago, was seriously injured. (NY file 100-167933; BU file 100-456940.)

Lead: Philadelphia will furnish information regarding this matter to the Bureau, AX, and WFO.
