To Acting Director 100-468406. Attention Domestic Intelligence, New York 100-174481.

From Washington Field 100-55420. Four pages.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC).

Internal Security—Communist (Trotskyist).

Re WFO teletype dated October 10, last.

On October 11, instant, a confidential source, who has furnished reliable information in the past, advised that at approximately 9 a.m. this date, a WONAAC-sponsored press conference was held in front of the United States Supreme Court, Washington, D.C. (WDC), by five women representatives of WONAAC, including Doctor Barbara Roberts, WONAAC national coordinator, who acted as spokesperson for WONAAC. The press conference lasted approximately 20 minutes and concerned the hearings being held at the Court regarding the constitutionality of the restrictive abortion laws in Texas and Georgia. Doctor Roberts stated that WONAAC hopes these laws would be declared unconstitutional.
