Carlos Jose Bringuier also gave the following information relating to himself.

Possesses Immigrant Resident card No. A 12 546 223, dated February 8, 1961.

Bringuier further stated that he is a local delegate of the Cuban Student Directorate in the city of New Orleans, appointed by Jose Antonio Lanuza, Secretary, American Affairs, Cuban Student Directorate, P.O. Box 805, Main Post Office, Miami, Florida, telephone Franklin 3-1476.

Bringuier stated that his first job in New Orleans was with the California Redwood Produce Co. on S. Peters Street, owned by Mr. L. C. Levy. Bringuier stated he worked there two days.

Bringuier then worked at Mac's Discount House, located at 714 Canal Street, for about one year, beginning April 1, 1961.

Bringuier then became a self-employed peddler in April 1962 with his brother-in-law, Rolando Pelaez. They bought clothing and radios wholesale and went aboard ships to sell them. Bringuier stated that he had a Port Security Card.

Bringuier then stated that on October 1, 1962, he opened the Casa Roca clothing store at 107 Decatur Street and has been working there until this date.

Bringuier stated that he keeps the sign, in the Cuban's possession at the time of arrest this date, in his store. This sign measures about 3 feet by 4 feet, with a picture of the Statue of Liberty, a hand with a dagger at the end of a long chain, and Cuba ringed by two links of the chain. The sign reads: “Danger! Only 90 miles from U.S.A., Cuba lies in chains!”

Bringuier stated that the sign was given to him by Ernesto (Arnesto) Rodriguez, in his 30s, who resided at 1208 St. Charles Street in New Orleans, at the time Arturo Frondizi came to New Orleans in January or February 1962. Bringuier believes Frondizi is a Communist, and that he has two brothers in Argentina who are Communists.

Bringuier further stated that he came to New Orleans directly from Miami, Florida, by Greyhound bus, arriving in New Orleans on February 18, 1961.
