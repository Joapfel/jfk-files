United States Government Memorandum

To: Acting Director, FBI

Date: 12/26/72

From: SAC, New Haven (100-21771) (RUC)

Subject: “FURIES” newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement publication, Washington, D.C., revolutionary activities

OO: WFO

Re: WFO letters to the Bureau, 7/31/72 and 9/29/72.

On 9/7/72, GS-NH-604-G advised that Hartford, Conn., telephone number 232-7678 has been listed since prior to 3/29/72 to Hyman Berson, 5 King Edward Rd., West Hartford, Conn.

Mr. Ted Munson, West Hartford PD, Records Division, advised on 10/24/72 that there is no record for this individual in their files. Miss Joann Curran, Connecticut State Police Bureau of Identification, advised there is no record for one Hyman Berson.

The 1971 City Directory for West Hartford lists Hyman (Sylvia A.) Berson as employed as a buyer for Youth Center Stores, Hartford, and residing at 5 King Edward Rd., West Hartford.

Detective Joseph Hammick, CID, Hartford, Conn., PD, advised on 10/24/72 that there is no record for Berson and that the names of Hyman Berson and his wife, Sylvia, are unknown to the Intelligence Division.

On 11/9/72, NH-839-PST advised that Hyman and Sylvia Berson are unknown to source.

On 11/20/72, NH-648-PST advised that Hyman and Sylvia Berson are unknown to source. NH-839-PST was contacted on 12/13/72, also with negative results.

NHO indices are negative regarding both Hyman and Sylvia Berson.
