Subject/Case Reference: CARRAFA, Eugene Michael

09-09-76: Major Brown received a call from Mr. James W. Shoemaker, 19515 Darnestown Road, Bealsville, Maryland, 972-8325. Mr. Shoemaker related that he found some papers belonging to Eugene Michael Carrafa on his property located at 14825 Dufief Mill Road, Gaithersburg, Maryland. Writer, Cpls. Swain and Cleveland responded to this address—a farm—and were led to a private dumping area about one mile from the road and in the woods.
