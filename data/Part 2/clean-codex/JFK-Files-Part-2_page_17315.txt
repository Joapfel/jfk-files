Emergency Conference to Defend the Rights of the Black Panther Party to Exist (ECDBPP)

On March 10, 1970, a third source advised that on Sunday, March 8, 1970, the second session of the ECDBPP was held at Malcolm X College, Newberry Street and 14th Place, Chicago. The session began at 11:00 a.m. The following members of the CP of Illinois were in attendance at this session:

In addition, Ed Johnson and Bessie Choll from Chicago were in attendance.

A fourth source advised during December 1969 that Ed Johnson and Bessie Choll regularly participated in weekly vigils sponsored by Women for Peace held on Saturdays in the Loop area of Chicago. The source added that the Women for Peace, Chicago area, was founded in Chicago on November 1, 1961, along with other Women for Peace groups throughout the country for the purpose of petitioning all governments “to end the arms race, not the human race,” conducting peace vigils for the purpose of ending the Vietnam War, abolishing the draft, and abolishing the “U.S. military foreign policy.”
