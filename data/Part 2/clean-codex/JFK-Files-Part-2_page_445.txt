TYPIC LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 8608.

13 October. 19737.

1. Wife and two children of Ramon Rossie listed on Cubana manifest of October 1966 to Mexico City; however, manifest showed they did not travel. Source LIFIRE.

2. LIRING-3 told Wallace B. Rowton 11 October 1966 he had heard Cecilia Marquez Fernandez de Rossie and two children arrested as they were boarding Cubana for Mexico City 9 October. Source of LIRING-3 information not disclosed but probably came from other passengers who made flight.

3. LIRING-3 also told Rowton he had heard Rossie now in Brazil where he made derogatory statements against Cuban regime. LIRING-3 suspected this may have been cause for wife's arrest at airport.

LIRING-3.
