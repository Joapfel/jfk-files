Betsy Palmer

Review of Manuel Ray y Rivero Files, Volumes 1–XVIII (201-239298)

April 6, 1978

Volume IV — February 1, 1961–March 31, 1961

1. Operation Approval granted for Ray's use as a political asset on 7 February 1961.

2. Memo to WH/4 Security, from WH/4 CI. Subject: Eloy Gutierrez Menoyo Group. Date: 23 February 1961.

Ray pressuring Mr. Droller to have four individuals freed from McAllen Detention Camp who belong to the Gutierrez group. Ray claims they are members of his group and have been most active against the Castro regime in Cuba. They are as follows:

Asenio Suarez, Lazaro  
Lesnik Menendez, Max  
DPOB 8 September 1930  
Vueltas, Las Villas  
Porta Bolanos, Gustavo  
Redondo Gonzalez, Roger

3. Cable to Bell, from JMWAVE. Date: March 1, 1961.

Artime advises that Ray's group is opposed to banning the Communist Party and in favor of “nationalizing everything.” Ray favors maintaining trade with the USSR and wants no change in the present agrarian reform program.

4. Cable to JMWAVE from Bell. Date: March 10, 1961.

INS is concerned over the increased news coverage of Ray's arrival in the U.S. and activities here. Afraid they will be confronted about Ray's arrival, which was described as “black” in another communication, JMWAVE asked to re-advise Ray that his story is that on 10 November 1960, he landed somewhere on the Keys in a fishing boat, made his way to a road where he was picked up by an INS patrol car, taken to the WAVE area, examined, declared a refugee, and released with admonition to always keep INS advised of his address.

5. Memo to S — Dr. Berle, from ARA — Philip W. Bonsal. Subject: Recent Cuban Developments. Date: March 16, 1961.

Conversation with Jose Miro Cardona and Berle reveals that Cardona has misgivings about Ray and Pazos (Pazoz) and the MRP. Says Ray is bitterly anti-American as well as probably totalitarian in his thinking. He no longer believes Ray has any considerable support inside Cuba. Thinks Ray's group is endeavoring to exercise influence in our government so as to be the major recipient of whatever assistance can be obtained here. He is particularly concerned about the role played by Felipe Pazos.
