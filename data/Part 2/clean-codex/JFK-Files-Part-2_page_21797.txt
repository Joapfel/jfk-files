The period when Mr. Harvey was setting up the staff Task Force W, is that correct?

What contact, if any, did you have with Task Force W personnel as distinct from the station itself?

Mr. Sarno. None whatsoever.

Mr. Wides. They never came down and spoke to you?

Mr. Sarno. When I came out of Cuba, when I escaped from Cuba, I was brought up here to Headquarters and debriefed, so I imagine that the person that debriefed me, this is in July of ’61, I would imagine that individual was part of the Task Force.

Mr. Wides. I think that was still in the Division. They had not split the Task Force until the winter of ’61, ’62.

Mr. Sarno. I see. Well—

Mr. Wides. But after that when you were in Florida, you had no contact with anyone, to your knowledge, other than the Station personnel?

Mr. Sarno. Well, I don’t know. Occasionally they would call me to Washington to discuss something.

Mr. Wides. And did you discuss something with people in the Task Force, the Cuban Task Force?

Mr. Sarno. The Cuban Task Force.

Mr. Wides. Mr. Cheevers?

Mr. Sarno. I know Mr. Cheevers, yes.
