170103Z, CITE MEXICO CITY 7347.

Director.

KAPOK DYVOUR PBRUMEN AMROD LICOOKY.

Reference: Mexico City 7076.

1. STASHA arrived in Mexico 17 April by Eastern from New York. Station not yet able to confirm from airlists. Departed for Cuba 24 April.

2. S reticent about her Miami sojourn. Mentioned Jerry Heming and his friend “Howie,” who helped her get around in exile group. In what LICOOKY interpreted as a slip, S identified her CUIS case officer in New York as GUTIERREZ. Said she had given him several rolls of tape covering Miami visit.

3. Future contact to be via travelers between Habana and Mexico who will mail letters in country of destination. S wants American magazines sent her via Prensa Latina. Precise employment in Cuba not certain. Allegedly can have old job with Prensa Latina, but LICOOKY suggested she try Casa de las Americas.

4. LICOOKY feels S drawn back to Cuba more by practical than ideological or emotional considerations, since government would supply housing and husband would have to help support children.
