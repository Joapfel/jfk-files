Plaintext. Teletype. Urgent.

To: SAC New York  
From: Director FBI  
SENSTUDY 75

The Senate Select Committee has requested that SAC Robert Charles Denz and SA John C. Sullivan be made available in Washington, D.C., for interview by that committee on 9/24/75. The interview will involve their knowledge of the Bureau's investigation of Martin Luther King, Jr., the Southern Christian Leadership Conference, Stanley David Levison (Levision), and Communist influence in the racial movement. SAC Denz and SA Sullivan should arrange to arrive in Washington in time to be briefed by a representative of the Legal Counsel Division and Intelligence Division at 9 a.m., 9/24/75, in Room 4509 of the Justice Building.

EX-106.

Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section. SEP 19 1975.
