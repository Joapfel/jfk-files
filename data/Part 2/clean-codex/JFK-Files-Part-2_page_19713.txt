United States Government Memorandum

To: Acting Director, FBI

From: SAC, New Haven (100-21771) (RUC)

Subject: “Furies” newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement publication, Washington, D.C.; revolutionary activities

Date: 12/26/72

Re: WFO letters to the Bureau, 7/31/72 and 9/29/72.

On 9/7/72, informant advised that Hartford, Conn., telephone number 232-7678 has been listed since prior to 3/29/72 to Hyman Berson, 5 King Edward Rd., West Hartford, Conn.

West Hartford PD, Records Division, advised on 10/24/72 that there is no record for this individual in their files. Miss Joann Curran, Conn. State Police Bureau of Identification, advised there is no record for one Hyman Berson.

The 1971 City Directory for West Hartford lists Hyman (Sylvia A.) Berson as employed as a buyer for Youth Center Stores, Hartford, and residing at 5 King Edward Rd., West Hartford.

CID, Hartford, Conn., PD, advised on 10/24/72 that there is no record for Berson and that the names of Hyman Berson and his wife, Sylvia, are unknown to the Intelligence Division.

On 11/9/72, informant advised that Hyman and Sylvia Berson are unknown to source.

On 11/20/72, informant advised that Hyman and Sylvia Berson are unknown to source. Informant was contacted on 12/13/72, also with negative results.

NHO indices are negative regarding both Hyman and Sylvia Berson.
