Free Lunch Program and Liberation School

The People of Detroit:

The Detroit Branch of the Black Panther Party is having a Liberation School and Free Lunch program to better serve the needs of our youth here in the community of Detroit.

Liberation School provides for our youth a further education while helping to improve their learning ability. Classes taught at Liberation School will be math, projects, history, ideology of the Black Panther Party, and primary skills, for those who are between the ages of 3 to 1½ years old.

If you would like for your children to attend Liberation School, bring or send them to 2435 Pennsylvania.

Donations of food, supplies, and money will be appreciated.
