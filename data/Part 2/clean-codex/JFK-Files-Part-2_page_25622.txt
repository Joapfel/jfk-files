June 24, 1976. Testimony of John Rosselli before Committee. Senator Church presiding.

Los Angeles. Pages 7–8.

Rosselli first met Maheu in approximately July 1960, pursuant to a telephone call from Maheu. At their first meeting, Maheu told Rosselli that a high government official had requested that Maheu recruit Rosselli to “fight Communism 90 miles away from Florida”—to recruit Cubans to assassinate Castro.

New York. Pages 10–11.

Rosselli had a second meeting with Maheu and went to New York in September under the alias of John Rawlston [Castro was in New York at this time]. Rosselli met Jim O’Connell of CIA, plus “three others.”

Florida. Pages 12–15.

O’Connell, Maheu, and Rosselli met in a Florida hotel approximately one week after New York. Rosselli introduced Giancana to Maheu. He also introduced X (Trafficante) to Maheu. He obtained permission from Maheu to bring Giancana into the project.

Page 17.

Rosselli recruits Cuban #1. Rosselli’s cover was as a Wall Street businessman. Rosselli and Maheu agreed on a $250,000 price tag for the job.

Page 20.

O’Connell never met Cubans. Trafficante, Cubans, and Maheu met. Maheu brought $10,000 and poison capsules.

Page 26.

Rosselli told Giancana about CIA involvement to keep his mouth shut. Rosselli only had one discussion with Giancana.

Page 27.

O’Connell called Rosselli after the Bay of Pigs. He met in Florida not long after the Bay of Pigs with William Harvey, O’Connell, and Rosselli. Harvey told Rosselli that he wanted Rosselli to have nothing to do with Maheu and not to use Giancana connections.

Page 35.

Rosselli indicates that pro-Castro people in Florida probably had an inkling of assassination attempts.

Page 44.

During the Missile Crisis, Harvey asked Rosselli to see if “they” had any intelligence on us, on the missiles.
