Partly as a result of the efforts of the CIA station in Santiago, the Government of Chile prohibited the entry into Chile of organizers of the Cuban-sponsored Second Latin American Youth Congress, which had been scheduled to take place in Santiago in August. This was one of the factors that led to postponement of the Congress.

f. After considerable prodding by the CIA station and the Embassy, the Minister of Public Security of Costa Rica presented legislative proposals to the Costa Rican Assembly that will require all persons intending to travel to Communist countries to have their travel approved by a newly created Costa Rican security agency.

g. The CIA station in Mexico City developed information concerning the clandestine arrival of passengers in Vera Cruz on Cuban merchant ships. Efforts are being made to obtain more information and to encourage stricter controls by the Mexican authorities.
