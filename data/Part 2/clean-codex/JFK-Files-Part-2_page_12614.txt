k. Dissemination at Bureau

There are situations in which dissemination is made only at Bureau. Do not disseminate information to any field branch of another Government agency where security measures are inadequate. In such instances, bring data to immediate attention of Bureau for SOG dissemination. In addition, Atomic Energy Commission receives reports only through Bureau. Information relating to foreign travel plans of security subjects is disseminated to interested agencies, including State Department, only at SOG.
