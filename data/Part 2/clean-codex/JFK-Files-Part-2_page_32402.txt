Paris AFP in English to the Far East, 1905, 7 February 1963.

(Excerpt) Georgetown—British Guiana in a state of near panic today after newspapers and radio stations put out a Washington newspaper report that Cuba was sending two ships full of Soviet arms to this country. Premier Cheddi Jagan (Cheddi Jagan) called the report from the Washington Evening Star fantastic and unfounded. A spokesman in the Home Affairs Ministry said the government expected no arms from the Soviet Union or Cuba.

7 February, 749 p.m., FG/OP.
