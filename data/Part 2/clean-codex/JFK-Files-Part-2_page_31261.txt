AMLASH

Rejects notion that AMLASH is a Cuban agent. No contact between August 1962 and September 1963.

7 September and 9 September cables indicate that AMLASH is a “spotted brat”; hopes as initial plan form, but approach in Chile interpreter allowed him to recruit his own cohorts.

Port Velleve conference with Castro’s speech to Dan Hartner on AMLASH leads to—leave at Castro’s Summit/Fernberg hit. C/A had not proposed any idea at the time. June 8—in meet until 30 October 63.

19 November 63: AMLASH told C/A officer he was returning to Cuba. On 20 November, he was reported as delayed until Federation of something, “ункль,” INKLESK.
