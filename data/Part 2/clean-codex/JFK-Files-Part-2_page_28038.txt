MEMORANDUM

To: Professor Blakey

From: Edward M. Evans, Chief Investigator, MLK

Date: September 22, 1977

Subject: Louis Parisi, Investigator, MLK

Mr. Parisi has agreed to take a position with us as Staff Investigator assigned to the Martin Luther King, Jr. Task Force.

He will start September 26, 1977 at $28,000.

EME:ek

9/23/77

Hold until we know the amount we have to reimburse the New Jersey State Police—somewhere around $20,000. The balance will be his Congressional salary.
