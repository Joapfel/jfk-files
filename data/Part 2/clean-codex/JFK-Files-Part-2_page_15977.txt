II. Chicago BPP Relations with Organizations and Other Groups

A. Chicago BPP and SDS

On January 10, 1969, Chicago BPP leaders spoke to a group at the University of Illinois, Urbana, Illinois, which was sponsored by the SDS chapter at the University of Illinois.

On February 10, 1969, leaders of SDS and BPP at Chicago discussed plans to hold rallies on February 16, 1969, and February 17, 1969, throughout the United States in honor of the birthday of Huey P. Newton, National BPP leader, who is presently serving a 2-15 year prison sentence.

On April 9, 1969, a rally was held at Chicago, Illinois, which was jointly sponsored by SDS and BPP members from Chicago. The purpose was to raise money for individuals under indictment as a result of disturbances at the Democratic Convention, which was held at Chicago in August 1968.

Bobby Seale, National BPP leader, spoke to the group, as did Fred Hampton and Bobby Rush, Chicago BPP leaders.

It was learned on April 6, 1969, that the Chicago chapter of SDS was going to do mass leafletting on April 8, 1969, in Chicago universities and colleges. The leaflets were in support of the Black Panther Party and especially in support of Bobby Lee Rush, leader of the Chicago BPP, who was to be tried on a murder charge.

The SDS show of support was also for 19 Chicago BPP members who were recently arrested, as well as for Bobby Seale, National BPP leader, who had been indicted for crossing state lines to incite a riot.
