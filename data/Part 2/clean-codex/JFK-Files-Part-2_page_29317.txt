Anthony Atzenhoffer (Atzenoffer) never got to know Ferrie very well. He went to one social party that was given at either Ferrie's house or Col. Jim Salmon's. James Salmon was killed in a plane accident on 10-15-78. The party was all guys, and when they started drinking he left. He was about 16, and his father didn't allow him to drink. Salmon and some others were seniors, and Atzenhoffer didn't get to know them. He remembers that Ferrie flew a blue Stinson that he kept at Moisant (MoSSant) Airport, now New Orleans International, where private planes are no longer allowed.

Ferrie never indicated that he was homosexual in any way. The only thing that he can remember that was not of CAPC was Ferrie wanting the kids to participate in some kind of experiment for Tulane University. They didn't do it.

Atzenhoffer was shown the photo book and related the following:

#5. Clay Shaw, knew from newspapers.

#27. Looked familiar, but the name didn't mean anything.

#62. Melvin “Pinky” Seeling. Knew him from either CAPC or other friends. He remembers that Seeling's father owned a large dairy farm in Kenner, Louisiana. Last saw him a few years ago. Seeling is in the Air Force and is stationed in Corpus Christi, Texas. Garrison's (Garrion's) men showed him a mug shot of Seeling, and he was surprised to learn that he had been arrested.

A check of New Orleans Police Department files indicates that he was arrested on August 30, 1961, and booked with vagrancy, investigation of burglary, and hold for the FBI under item number H-13903-61. His date of birth is given as 8-1-37. The report will have to be ordered from the microfilm section.

Subject not identified in the photo book except by last name.
