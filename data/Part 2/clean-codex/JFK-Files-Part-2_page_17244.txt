3/20/69

Teletype

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago, and SAC, Detroit

Black Panther Party (BPP), RI

Chairman Cleveland Cook, Field Secretary (Marshall), and Robert Satchel, Minister of Health, all Chicago BPP members, departed Chicago, instant, en route to Detroit, Michigan. May attempt contact with Robert White, Field Secretary (Marshall) and Detroit chapter, BPP, who is in prison. Group traveling in Cook's 1964 maroon Pontiac, bearing unknown 1969 Illinois plates. Allegedly Hampton and Satchel are armed.

Yvonne King, acting Minister of Labor, Chicago BPP, and Satchel allegedly were in Detroit over the weekend, March 15 to 17 last, and stayed with White's parents. White's uncle is allegedly Ted Williams, wealthy ex-husband of Aretha Franklin, nationally known Negro vocalist. He allegedly provided money for King and Satchel's flight back to Chicago.
