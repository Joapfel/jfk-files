3. Sam Bonacoury.

On 16 June 1967, Bonacoury got M. Klein and took the trip as a result of the CPAP lots.

In early 1967, intervention with FNL Farrell of FNS regarding possible deportation. See Tab C, Ressell CPA file, stating that my Etwas OS Bom, Agnon Rocca, and Housten were attending to ward off FSN surveillance in 1963–1967. D.C. Report, page 129.

Conversation with Sam Pugor on 3 May 1967. Sam commented that Ressell and Bonacoury saw C.F.T. over a barrel because of that operation. He said that he doubted that the FBI would be able to do anything about Ressell or Bonacoury because of their previous activities with first-year people. 1967 F.G. Report, page 131.
