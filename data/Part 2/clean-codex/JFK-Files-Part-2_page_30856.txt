File: Donald J. Darick.

Converted to pseudonym per request from Paul Kartman, 2/5/74.

Organizational designations: DDP Directorate. Soviet Bloc. OZ. AEGPHINIX (AEGPHINIX), formerly AETONER.

Biographic Data.

May 1944: Transferred to GUKR/MGB, Moscow, and completed course at Higher Counter-Intelligence School in May 1945.

May 1945: Transferred to Counter-Intelligence Directorate for Navy, serving as its Komsomol (KONGDOML) secretary.

May 1946: Transferred back to home area with MGB.

April 1947–June 1951: Assigned to Chief Directorate of the Coast Guard (GOAT GUARD), promoted to major in 1949.

June 1951–defection 1954: MGB operations officer at Moscow (MOSCOU) headquarters and abroad.

By Whtyng.

Memo in lieu of fitness report, 26 September 1973.

Has prepared major analyses on two persons who appear to have been Soviet penetration pages, 425 pages, secret.
