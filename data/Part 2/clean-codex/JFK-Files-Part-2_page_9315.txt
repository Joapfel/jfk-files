The National Committee's to Combat Fascism is moving to meet the basic needs of the poor underprivilege of our community. One of the ways in which we do this is by the implementation of a free breakfast for children program.

The breakfast programs are not only to feed the children in our community, but also to bring the children, parents and all people of the community together to work and see to it that every child is fed a well-balanced breakfast each morning.

This being the people's breakfast, our goal is to spread this program throughout the communities of Michigan. This is the people's program and without the people's support, we have nothing. Since this is a basic necessity, it has to be had.
