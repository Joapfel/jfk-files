Office Memorandum, United States Government.

To: Chief, Research Section/CB/PSD  
From: L. J. Dunn  
Subject: DUGAN, Emmet E. — GF-6-371, page 22  
Date: 18 April 1958

As matter of information you may be interested in knowing that the DDP initiated a request through the CIA Library to obtain all newspaper clippings which pertain to the death of Subject. Miss Barbara Wade (X 2457) advised on 18 April 1958 that the Library has articles from the Washington Post on March 14 and 16; New York Times on March 16; New York Mirror on March 17; and Christian Science Monitor on March 15.

L. J. Dunn
