A usually reliable CIA source reported in Santiago during 1971 that Chilean Naval Captain Jaime Rojas Brugues was an instructor of cryptology in the Chilean Army G-2 intelligence training group. The CIA source obtained this information from a close friend who was a student in this intelligence group. (S)

Records of the Defense Attaché Office, Santiago, indicate that Jaime Rojas Brugues in January 1974 was serving as the security chief of the Chilean MFR and that his primary area of responsibility was asylee-refugee affairs. Defense Attaché Office records in Santiago indicate that the commander of the Chilean naval training ship, “Esmeralda,” is Jorge J. Davanzo Cintolesi.
