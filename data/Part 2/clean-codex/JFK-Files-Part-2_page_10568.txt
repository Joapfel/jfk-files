July 21, 1975

United States Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SEC)

During the appearance before the SEC on May 16, 1975, the following questions were asked by members of that Subcommittee:

(1) How are files established on Congressmen?

(2) How long are such files maintained?

(3) Who has access to the Congressional files and “official and confidential files”?

Attached is a response to questions one and two and, in part, question three. With regard to that portion of question three concerning access to the official and confidential files, be advised that these files are maintained in the Special File Room of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, Files and Communications Division. Access to these files is strictly limited, and only those having a necessity for access may review these files. A log is maintained as to who has access to the official and confidential files.
