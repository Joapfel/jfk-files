FBI.

Date: 12/4/69.

Teletype.

To: Director and SAC, San Francisco.  
From: SAC, Chicago.

Black Panther Party, RM-BPP.

A confidential source, reliable in the past, advised that in connection with the Monroe morning raid on the Black Panther Party, 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago, by Cook County State's Attorney's police, which resulted in the killing of Fred Hampton, deputy chairman, Illinois BPP, numerous unidentified individuals have contacted Panther headquarters, 2350 West Monroe Street. Individuals contacting Panther headquarters requested information regarding funeral arrangements and all were instructed they should not be “sympathetic” but should furnish money for bail fund.

Source also advised Chicago Panthers are in contact with numerous Chicago radio stations in an effort to secure air time on a “talk program” to discuss the Panther version of Hampton's death. To date, no commitments have been made by local radio stations.
