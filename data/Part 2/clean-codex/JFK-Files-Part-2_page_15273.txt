United States Government Memorandum

To: W. C. Sullivan  
From: W. R. Wannall  
Subject: Intelligence Coverage of Non-Soviet-Bloc Countries  
Date: September 30, 1964

W. R. Wannall and

State has previously granted clearance for FBI programs to develop sources and informants in the diplomatic establishments in the United States of several African countries for the purpose of developing needed intelligence information. State clearance for these programs is limited to our investigating logical persons who may be so developed. Prior to actually contacting such persons to determine their potential, State clearance is again obtained for each person being considered. In the past, State has indicated it feels that because of the risks involved, we should only consider for development as sources, American citizens employed at African diplomatic establishments. However, State has indicated that where there is an investigative need for such a source, State would consider granting clearance for development of other than American citizens.

Action:

It is recommended Bureau Liaison obtain State clearance for the FBI to institute a program to develop sources and informants in Algerian diplomatic establishments in the United States indicating we will, of course, obtain State clearance prior to interviewing any potential informant or source.
