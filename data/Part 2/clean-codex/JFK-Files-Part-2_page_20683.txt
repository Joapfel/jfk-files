WOMAC (WOMAAC) was founded during July 1971 for the purpose of forming a national movement of groups scattered throughout the United States who favored and worked to legalize abortion. During the founding conference, WOMAC's goals were defined as “working on a national scale to make abortion legal, to end restrictive contraception laws, and to end forced sterilization.” WOMAC is headquartered in New York City.
