FW 88613. FBI. Date: 5-24-69. Transmit the following in teletype via: Black Panther Party (BPP) RM.

Advised May 23 last that an LNU, Negro female, contacted Fred Hampton (fRED HAMPTON), deputy chairman, Illinois BPP (illinoIs BPP), and related she was being flunked in a course at Crane Junior College (crane junior college), Chicago, scene of racial disturbances in the past, by an instructor, first name unknown, Novar. She stated she would approach Charles Hurst, president, Crane College, Monday next, and if she could not get her grade changed to a passing mark, she would kill Novar.

Hampton finally recalled the LNU and attempted to discourage her regarding the above threat. Despite warning by Hampton that the conversation might be overheard, the LNU related the above story. She related upon Hampton’s request that she could be reached at 61 East 46th Street, Chicago, through telephone numbers 373-4827 and DA 6-9686. Indicated latter number is employer where she will be Saturday instant.
