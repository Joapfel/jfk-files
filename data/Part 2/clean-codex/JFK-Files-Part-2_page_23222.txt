Mr. Baron. Did Richard Bissell ever mention to you that lethal biological agents had been developed in the CIA laboratories for possible use in the Congo to assassinate Lumumba? Or they were available for such use?

Mr. Tweedy. I think that it would be closer that TSD probably had the capability or had the agent or whatever it was. When I say agent I mean chemical agent. I suspect I heard that, yes.

Mr. Baron. Would this have been under Sidney Gottlieb's auspices or direction in TSD?

Mr. Tweedy. If he was Chief of TSD at the time it would have been. I don't recall. Was he? I think he was.

Mr. Baron. Did Richard Bissell ever indicate to you directly that Sidney Gottlieb was involved in developing poisons that might be used for assassination?

Mr. Tweedy. I don't recall that at all.
