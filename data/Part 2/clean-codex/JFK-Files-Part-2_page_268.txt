Origin: WH/1. Unit: 4506. Extension: 23 Sept 65. To: Mexico City. From: Director. CONF: WH8, VR, File. File copy: No.

24 Sep 65 18 46E

To: Mexi. Info. Cite DIR. 45335.

1. HDQS received phone report from NYDO 23 Sept that LICOOKY-1 had contacted KUJUMP through Igor Gordenvitch of Vision magazine. LICOOKY had told Gordenvitch she had been recruited for KUBARK by Mr. Hermsdorf in Cuba, was now Mexi, and wanted a KUBARK contact in New York. She only planned be there a couple of days prior returning Mexi and gave no indication why she wished contact.

2. Although Gordenvitch is cleared KUNOLF contact, prefer not use him as intermediary to LICOOKY. In view her plan return Mexi shortly HDQS making no contact. Request station debrief LICOOKY on her actions, particularly why she revealed her KUBARK relationship.

End of message.

William V. Broe, Chief, WH D.

N.J. Kaufman, C/WH/1.

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
