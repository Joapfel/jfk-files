Cable Secretariat Dissemination. Classified message.

File: VR. WNOCOG 8, CAS, CALPROP, C.A.P./C.H.I.C.2, C.OA. FI, FLINTZ.

Guatemala City cable 8996, 6 May 1968, 1621BZ. Mexico City info Director. Reference: Mexico City 5540 (IN 08570).

Liaison reports that personal and LIRING is being held Pavon Prison Farm pending sentencing by Fourth Juzgado de Primera Constancia (CNSTANCIA). Only charge is distributing Communist propaganda.

Liaison speculates they will receive light sentence, if any.

17 May 1968.
