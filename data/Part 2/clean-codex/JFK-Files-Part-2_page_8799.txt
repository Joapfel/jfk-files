Memo for Chief, Special Affairs Staff, by Henry Hecksher, dated 3 September 1963. File No. 19-124-27/3 (Box 28).

A Tad Szulc article in a special to The New York Times, datelined Key West, 1 September 1963, quotes “the judgment of authoritative quarters in Cuba” that Washington tolerance of small refugee groups identified with political factions objectionable to most Cubans—specifically, groups that apparently operate from Nicaragua and are identified with ARTIME—works against the emergence of meaningful opposition. Hecksher comment: “I wonder who is actually behind what looks to me like a concerted denigration campaign?”

DBA 42843, dated 3 June 1963 (FBI). File No. 19-124-27 (Box 29).

From an interview with Santiago Alvarez Rodriguez on 23 May 1963 and information from his contact in April 1963 with attorney Paulino A. Sierra. Sierra wanted Alvarez to meet an American arriving in Miami from Chicago, name unknown, who claimed to have $30,000,000 available to donate to Cuban exiles to establish unity and a provisional government in exile.
