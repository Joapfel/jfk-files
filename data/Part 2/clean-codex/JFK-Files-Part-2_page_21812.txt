1. Origins of the picketing? How would it come about?

Mr. Sarno. Well, I don't believe it was started—I know it wasn't started in AMUTS because one individual cannot start it, but the groups, the Cuban exile groups who were located in New York, decided to picket it because, I believe, of Mexico's relations with Cuba.

Mr. Wides. At this time, wasn't the Agency carrying on a wide variety of operations to try and isolate Cuba economically and commercially?

Mr. Sarno. I believe they were, yes. I was never involved in any of those operations.

Mr. Wides. And including air traffic people, airlines going to Cuba. That was one aspect of the efforts to isolate Cuba.

Mr. Sarno. Well, the airlines going to Cuba, if I can recall at that time, were not Mexican airlines.

Mr. Wides. You didn't have any idea at the time as to what—

Mr. Sarno. Mexican airlines do not fly to Cuba. The Cubans always flew their airplanes over. The Mexicans were not flying.

Mr. Wides. So you understood this to be harassment of a Mexican company or a Mexican national company, because of Mexico's general relations with Cuba?

Mr. Sarno. General relations with Cuba, that is what I…
