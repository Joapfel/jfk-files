Persons/Entities: WAINWRIGHT, William; LASH, A. M.; TEPEDINO, Carlos (TEREDINO); McCONE. Dates: August 10–11, 1962. Categories: CASTRO.

Occurrence:

CIA officer WAINWRIGHT and A. M. LASH-1 meet, along with Carlos TEPEDINO (TEREDINO), a Cuban exile jeweler in New York City, in Copenhagen (85). WAINWRIGHT reported that the term “assassinate” was used in the discussion of A. M. LASH’s future role in Cuba and it visibly upset A. M. LASH, who preferred the term “eliminate.” (85).

Discovery Leads: Note: This discussion of assassination is taking place at the same time that DCI McCONE lodges his objection, after the Special Group (Augmented) meeting, to McNAMARA’s suggestion that the technique of assassination could be used against CASTRO.

Source: IG Report, 1967.

Staff: Baron. Date: 7/2/75.
