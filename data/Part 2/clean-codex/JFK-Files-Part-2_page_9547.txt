Murders of the Detroit NOC[P], incarcerated in the Wayne County Jail on charges of murder, conspiracy to commit murder and malicious destruction of property, have become victims of a plot of total annihilation of the Black Panther Party and its organizing units.

Inside the jail, the Detroit 15 have been subjected to inhuman acts of harassment. The food received by the 15 has been drugged and leaves the comrades queasy. The sisters receive threats of beatings and one sister was locked up in a room, from where she awakened with “crabs” all over her. FBI agents have been interrogating them and the matrons harassing them. These sadistic acts are to break the comrades’ spirit and to show the super-image of power and authority by those in control.
