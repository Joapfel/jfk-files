File Search on President Kennedy’s Assassination — OCI Front Office

1. The Office of D/OCI has a file labeled “Material regarding President Kennedy’s Assassination.” It contains:

a. Memo entitled “Rumours About Lee Harvey Oswald,” discussing rumor-mongering in the world public regarding the presumed assassin, dated 23 March 1964.

b. Listing of government leaders expected to attend Kennedy’s funeral.

c. Memo entitled “Fidel Castro’s Speech,” containing an “objective analysis” of the implications of Kennedy’s assassination, dated 25 November 1963.

d. FBIS (FIBS) study, never disseminated, entitled “The Past Soviet Propaganda Assessment of President Johnson,” dated 25 November 1963.

e. FBIS Daily Report — World Reaction Series — on foreign radio and press reaction to Kennedy’s death and Johnson’s accession, dated 26 November 1963.

f. Memo entitled “Castro’s Speech of 27 November on the President’s Assassination.” The speech says Oswald was the innocent dupe of a complex plot. Memo dated 29 November 1963.

g. Unclassified copy of Washington Post (Wash. Post) article on Castro’s reaction to Kennedy’s death, dated 29 November 1963.

h. Three copies of a memo entitled “World Reaction to the Assassination of President Kennedy,” dealing with the idea of a possible cover-up in the killing of Oswald—to seal his lips and protect the extreme right, whose tool he was. Memos dated 25 November 1963.

Copy passed to Michael, 28 October 1975.

Carla Scopelletti.

28 October 1975.
