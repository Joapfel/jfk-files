Authority. Rank: Corporal.

II. Sub-Section Leader

No Panther member who is a sub-section leader will have this authority unless duly appointed by a Captain or coordinator in conjunction with his section leader.

1. The Sub-Section Leader must build and construct a squad of Panther members who are dedicated and have successfully completed their six weeks of training.

2. He must maintain daily contact with his squad and also with his section leader or Captain.

3. He must know the names of all members of his squad, 24 hours a day, and know how to contact them. He must also know how to contact his section leader and report to him daily.

4. He must spend at least two hours a day studying and keep up with all daily news so as to keep politically aware.

5. He should work in the community where he lives, or is assigned to conduct propaganda, know the community problems, and know the existing business establishments by categories in his sub-section and the general number of residents.

6. He must keep a check on all the Panther members of his squad in the areas of:

a. How well they do their propaganda work.

b. Firing practice in private sessions known only to them, and field stripping of weapons.

c. How well they know the rules and other materials of the party line.
