Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971; Internal Security—Information Concerning.

On April 8, at 12:45 p.m., special agents of the FBI observed about twelve white female individuals in their early twenties assemble and hold a press conference near the front entrance of the Justice Department Building, Tenth and Pennsylvania Avenue, NW. Two of the females unfolded and held a large banner inscribed, “Women's March, April 10—A fresh wind blows against the empire.” Two other members of the group spoke. The first one stated the march on April 10 to the Pentagon was not under the sponsorship of any single group; that the march was called to have women from all groups join in to protest the war in Vietnam. The second speaker identified as Laurie. End page one.
