DIRECTOR 34099, dated 20 April 1963. Cable from headquarters to HAMB and FRAN, stating sabotage program against Cuban shipping was of sufficiently high priority to require utilization of any appropriate asset.

HAGU 3698 (IN 22804), dated 22 April 1963. Proposed that AMLEG-29 place a four-hour-delay explosive, “CLAM,” in one of 34 drums of “TETRAHYDROFURAN” which were to be loaded on the deck of the HAVANA.

DIRECTOR 35006, dated 24 April 1963. Cable from headquarters to HAGU, BRUS, HAMB, FRAN, CASB, and RABA, stating U.S. government policy calls for all feasible forms of pressure on the CASTRO regime to inhibit its success and culminate in disorder leading to its downfall. Realize motivation of assets will be a major key to success of operations, and that difficulties will be encountered due to their interpretation of USG policy. Burden of positive motivation thus rests on case officers involved, who must decide each case on its own merits and use considerable judgment to induce cooperation.
