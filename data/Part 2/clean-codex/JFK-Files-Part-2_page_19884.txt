Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Communications Section

May 6, 1972

Teletype

From: Washington Field (100-55966) (C) 3P

Cablelight march to United States Capitol, Washington, D.C., sponsored by District of Columbia Chapter of Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (D.O. NOMAAC), N.Y. six, seventy-two. Internal Security—Communist (Trotskyist).

Re: Info teletype dated April twenty-eight, last.

On May sixth, instant, a confidential source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that the District of Columbia Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (C.O. NOMACO) held a forum meeting at St. Mark's Episcopal Church, Three and A Street, Southeast, Washington, D.C. This meeting was attended by approximately one thousand interviewed and last REC-110 seven Whirey from the third PM on May sixth instant. The source stated that following the meeting, the group left the church and proceeded in a Cablelight procession to the east steps of the Capitol Building.

Included in summary to White House and Attorney General. Date 5/8/72 am
