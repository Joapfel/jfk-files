Source commented that he did not recognize the individual depicted in the photographs of Shveitser and he never heard of this individual; however, he expressed the opinion that the large number of visits Shveitser has made to the United States with a variety of Soviet groups suggests he may be a KGB agent.

Source was asked if he knew of any connection of Soviet poet Yevgeniy Yevtushenko (Yektushenko) with KGB. (Referenced memorandum dated September 30, 1970 indicates that during interview of Samuel Adason Jaffe on September 24, 1970 concerning his contacts with members of the Soviet group attending the Pugwash Conference in 1970, Jaffe indicated he first met Shveitser a number of years ago when Jaffe interviewed Soviet poet Yevgeniy Yevtushenko (Yektushenko) with the assistance of Shveitser during the Youth Festival in Helsinki, Finland.)
