HDCA 3405, dated 29 November 1963 (Vol. III)

Dispatch from Santo Domingo to Hqs. concerning Eduardo TAVARES Justo. TAVARES queried EMGLUE-1 on 2 October 1963 about his experiences in the U.S. but did not give any indication that he was going there. On 9 October, five days after TAVARES’s departure, EMGLUE-1 reported that TAVARES had not gone to Canada as planned, but to New York. On 22 November 1963, EMGLUE-1 reported that as of 17 November TAVARES was still in New York. E-1 believed TAVARES was a Communist, or at least had Communist ideas.

HMMA 22632, dated 18 December 1963 (Vol. III)

Dispatch from Mexico City to Hqs.: “On 21 November 1963 LICOOKY-1 obtained the following information regarding Francisco AMADO. For the past year AMADO has continued to receive visits from emissaries of Fidel CASTRO and to make mysterious trips. On each occasion AMADO receives money for various expenditures.”
