Control of Cuban Travelers 200-9-49.

KNGS 0948 (IN 54974), dated 5 November 1963.

On 15 October 1963, Dominican student Carlos Manuel Cabral (CABRAL) Lofontaine attempted to enter Cuba via Kingston, ostensibly to attend an architects’ conference. Jamaican authorities, suspecting the trip to be a pretext, returned Cabral to the Dominican Republic.

The First Secretary of the Cuban Embassy in Ottawa, Osvaldo Relova (RELOVA), visited Kingston from 14 to 21 October 1963; the purpose was unexplained. While there, he lived in the Consulate for the entire stay.

HABA 12,838, dated 5 November 1963.

Report concerning unofficial irregular air service established between Cuba and Brazil for the transportation of political rather than commercial travelers.

POSN 0340 (IN 54917), dated 5 November 1963.

On 25 October 1963, a Russian plane arrived in Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe, with 60 passengers. Forty-one transferred to Panama for Rio; the remainder left on the “Russian” plane. Comment: Note on cable states that the Cubans were returning from an architects’ congress.
