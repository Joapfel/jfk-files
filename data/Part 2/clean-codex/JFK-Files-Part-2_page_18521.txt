United States Government

Memorandum

To: Mr. J. B. Adams

From: Legal Counsel

Subject: Status of Responses to Congressional Committee Requests

Date: 7-24-75

The purpose of this memorandum is to set forth in summary form the requests we have received from various Committees in Congress and the status of our replies.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (Church's Committee)

Date of Request: 5-14-75. Nature of Request: Includes, among other things, information concerning wiretaps, including their reconstruction of Bureau files, as well as inspection surveys for ten offices. Status: Responses to most questions prepared, approved, and delivered.

Date of Request: 6-27-75. Nature of Request: Request for information concerning alleged abuses. Status: Department seeking clarification.

Date of Request: 6-30-75, received by Bureau 7-8-75. Nature of Request: Request pertaining to COINTELPRO. Status: Response being prepared.

Date of Request: 7-3-75. Nature of Request: Requests documents pertaining to COINTELPRO be furnished to the Committee in Committee space. Status: Response being prepared.
