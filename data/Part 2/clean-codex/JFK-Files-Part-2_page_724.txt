Classified message.

To: Director  
From: Mexico City  
Action: WH 8  
LCO: DDP, DODS/L, DODS 4, SPOG 2, CI/OPS, FI, SAS 8, RF, JR

151758Z. DIR WAVE CITE MEXI 6536. PERIMEN. HTBASTE. 15 OCT 03. IN 40401.

1. While in New York 7 October contacting Cuban (CURAN) exiles with possible links to Mexico, LITAMIL-3 had a chance encounter and chatted briefly with Antonio de Souza Carbajal, member of Cuban (CURAN) delegation.

On his own initiative, L-3 suggested defection, to which de Souza replied he was merely performing his duty and was very frightened. De Souza did state he would talk with L-3 again in the future under secure circumstances. L-3 describes de Souza as “a key man.”

2. Request traces, comments, and advice on another trip by L-3 to N.Y. for the purpose of talking with de Souza.
