Memorandum for the Record. Date: 13 November 1968. Subject: Gordon Duane Novel. File Number: 526 113.

According to an FBI liaison check through Mr. Cregar, 11/13/68, Gordon Duane Novel (fugitive witness in Garrison's probe of the assassination of President Kennedy) was born 2/7/38, New Orleans, Louisiana.

Office and title: Appraisal Section. Signature: Maxine Casebolt.
