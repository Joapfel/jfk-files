New work—hand in and look at Letter King, but I lack material: Dr. King's article in “Ebony”; my trip to the land of Gondar. It would of course be necessary for me to know some articles on the Very High Price, how it was...

My long essay outline, “Earthly Letter,” King's road to the Cross, if still at the publishers. I hope it will be published at least, and it will have the possibility to send it to you. If somebody in your country will place an international symposium with the late thought and meaning of Martin Luther King, please let me know. It would be my deepest wish to contribute; I hope you will not consider this wish immediate—it is very serious. Of course, I could not be the judge of my own work.

Thank you again, dear Mrs. King, for your letter.

Yours very sincerely,  
Raissa Orlova
