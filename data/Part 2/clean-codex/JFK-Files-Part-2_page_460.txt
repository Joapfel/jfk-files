Bossie of reference, paragraph 7: While visiting Secret Army July 1968, was recruited by BOFACI and trained to become on-island assembly. Bossie was left Syria for fracture 14 July, arrived Cuba but therefore stayed Syria with new Syrian wife. Early July Cuban representative insisted, began talking locate Bossie. We undertook his requests from 14 July until 13 August, when he walked into BOFACI cover office Barcelona, Spain. He subsequently gave Madrid where station had little luck desiring him due to his agitation and nervousness. 20 August was signed QUT claim. He and new wife hope resurrect Venezuela, where he is a naturalized citizen. Bossie did not defect with any Cuban funds. We broke when arrived Barcelona, and reliable sensitive source indicated Cuban initially began looking for him when forced to because he had limited funds.
