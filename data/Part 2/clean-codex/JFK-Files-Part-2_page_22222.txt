Persons/Entities: Gen. Lansdale; John McCone; Gen. Carter; Bruce Creevey (CIA). Date: 30 October 1962. Category: Operation Mongoose.

Occurrence:

After Lansdale's conversation with McCone, he called Bruce Creevey, who assured him that he had received similar orders from Carter and that all militant actions under CIA were being held at a stop.

Source: Memo from the record by Gen. Lansdale dated 30 October 1962.

Staff. Date: PGW, 6/29.
