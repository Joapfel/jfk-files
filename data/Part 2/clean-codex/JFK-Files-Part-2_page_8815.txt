SJOS 6429 (IN 68164), dated 26 November 1963.

Cable from San Jose to Headquarters: “Costa Rican Deputy Julio Sunol Leal (ZRKNICK suspect, Communist fellow traveller and pro-Castro propagandist) planned to depart 24 November to attend Peace Conference in Poland.

Delayed departure and now planning to leave San Jose by air for Poland 26 November. Plans stop en route in Texas, repeat Texas. Then will travel on to Poland. Station suspects Sunol will attempt to gather data in Texas for use in pro-Communist Castro propaganda regarding assassination of Kennedy. Sunol travelling with diplomatic passport.”

MEXI 7085 (IN 68561), dated 27 November 1963.

Cable to Headquarters from Mexico City: “Subject arrived in Mexico on TACA Flight 772 at 1600, 26 November. Leaving on KLM Flight 684 for Amsterdam 27 November. Flight stops in Houston and Montreal en route.”

HZPA 13,960, dated 3 December 1963.

Dispatch from Panama City to Headquarters. At the 22 November 1963 Esterline-HYSAGE-1 (Thelma King) meeting, news was received of President Kennedy’s death. This had a profound effect on H-1. Her first reaction was that it must have been done by racial extremists, and she spent considerable time extolling the virtues of President Kennedy and what he had been trying to do.
