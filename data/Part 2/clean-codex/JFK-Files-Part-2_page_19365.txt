1/11/68

Code. Urgent. Sent by coded teletype.

EX-109.

To SAC Atlanta.

From Director, FBI.

Demonstrations protesting U.S. policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January fifteen, nineteen sixty-eight.

Reurtel January ten last.

Authority granted for Cynthia Yvonne Mitchell, PRI, to attend captioned demonstration. Instruct source to promptly apprise WFO of any pertinent data developed during trip to Washington. Special Agent Garnett Tunstall, phone number Executive three-seven one zero zero, extensions three nine one or four six zero, should be contacted for this purpose.

Atlanta advise WFO code name used by this source.

Note: Mitchell is a 20-year-old colored female who is employed as a bank clerk in Atlanta. She was approved as a probationary racial informant in November 1967, as she was familiar with some of the racial issues and close to dissident racial groups in Atlanta. She is paid under SAC authority. Atlanta, by retel, advised that they were sending informant, UACB, to participate in demonstration as a method of acquainting her with persons active in Vietnam and racial activities. Teletype being used in order to instruct Atlanta in the method source should contact WFO while in Washington area.
