On August 24, 1968, (n-217), NOSENKO stated that he had seen the name PARSONS, which reminded him of a 2nd Department, SCD operation against a man by that name. He believed that TAMARA KUNGAROVA worked against this man. This happened prior to the time KUNGAROVA worked for him (NOSENKO) and probably while she was working against the British; however, NOSENKO was not sure that PARSONS was of British nationality.

4. YEVGENIYA DANKO

On page 109 the paper refers to YEVGENIYA DANKO, who worked for ARSENE FRIPPEL at the American Express Company S office in Moscow and later for FRIPPEL's successor, MICHAEL JELISAVCIC. It was indicated NOSENKO stated that he took DANKO over from another 7th Department case officer during the period 1955–57, and she was used to report on both FRIPPEL and JELISAVCIC. The paper noted that FRIPPEL left Moscow in 1961, by which time NOSENKO had transferred from the 7th Department.
