Classified message.

4 August 1987. 040003Z. Cite Mexico City 2372. Guatemala City info Director.

Photo Leonardo (Castillo) Hoffman, chief, San Marco guerrilla area, Guatemala, showing LIRING-3, who did not recognize. LERING-3 stated, however, that Motifs (Orozco) told him 31 July that Castillo had been killed. Orozco also implied David (Casasola) Mejia was also dead.
