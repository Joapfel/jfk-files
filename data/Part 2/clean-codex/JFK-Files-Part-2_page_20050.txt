Via teletype. January 4, 1968. Enciphered.

FBI New York. 7:25 p.m., urgent, January 4, 1968.

To Director, Washington Field 100-47173, Newark and Philadelphia. Attention: Domestic Intelligence Division. WFO via Washington.

From New York 100-161601.

Demonstration protesting United States policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January fifteenth next; information concerning internal security; coordinating office: WFO.

On January third last, informant reliable-conceal advised at New York County Communist Party Committee meeting December twenty-eighth, sixty-seven, those present were urged to attend January fifteenth next demonstration in Washington, sponsored by Jeannette Rankin Brigade (JRB).

On January third last advertisement appeared in “New York Times,” page fifty-seven, captioned “Dagmar Wilson Says Join Me in Washington January Fifteenth.” Advertisement included comment: “Thousands of American women, led by Jeannette Rankin, first U.S. Congresswoman, will demand that Congress end the war in Vietnam and our social crisis at home.”

Two copies to WFO.

A. M. Inglurch for the Director. L. E. Wray.
