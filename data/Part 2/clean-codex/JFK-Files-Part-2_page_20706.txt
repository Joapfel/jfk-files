Teletype to New York and WFO.

Re: Women's National Abortion Action Coalition convention July fourteen through sixteen, seventy-two, at Hunter College, New York, New York.

100-468406.

WFO contact informant immediately upon return and submit detailed FD-306 to New York within five days in accordance with SAC letter 72-3(c), re reporting of results of subversive conferences.

Note:

WOMAAC was founded in New York City in July 1971 for the purpose of forming a national movement of groups throughout the United States that favor legalized abortion. This organization is dominated by the Socialist Workers Party (SWP) and Young Socialist Alliance (YSA). WOMAAC scheduled a conference at Hunter College, New York, New York, July 14–16, 1972. WFO source informant advised that anti-Trotskyist women in WOMAAC will attempt to negate the influence of YSA control of the organization at the captioned conference. In a teletype dated July 7, 1972, WFO requested authorization to advance informant $113 for expenses in connection with attendance at this conference. Expenses involve a bus for round trip by Metro Coach from WTC to New York City and three days' food and lodging at a rate of $25 per day. WFO furnished a code name to New York for use by the source. The source is currently authorized $250 per month for services and $45 per month for expenses. Based on the fact that the source was selected by WFC TLA leadership to be a member of the delegation attending this conference, WFO is being authorized to advance the source $113. WFO and New York are instructed to coordinate regarding the source maintaining contact with New York and to ensure that FD-306 is submitted within five days after conclusion of the conference.
