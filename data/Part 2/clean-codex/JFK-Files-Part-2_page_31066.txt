6. Intelligence Information Cable, dtd 28 June 1966. Source: qualified American observer.

On 15 June 1966, Rafael Garcia-Bango, Cuban exile in Spain, received a call in Madrid from Ray in P.R. regarding Ray's plans to assume power in Cuba. Garcia-Bango told Ray it was essential he come to Madrid before 25 June and return to San Juan in order to talk to a member of the Cuban delegation for the 10th Caribbean and CenAm games in P.R., prior to the return of the delegation to Cuba. Ray agreed to do so.

Ray was enthusiastic over the possibility of overthrowing Castro in the next several months. He knew Garcia-Bango had a bad reputation but felt he could prove useful. Also useful would be Garcia-Bango's brother, Jorge Garcia-Bango Dirube, who had a more respectable reputation. Ray also stated that he had received indirectly a message from Jose Llanusa Gobel and considered him a potential ally.

7. Harry Mahoney debriefed Ray, as described in Memo for the Record dtd 8 July 1966. The memo mentions Julian Gallo at this time, and Ray indicated he knew and respected him.

8. Memo No. 771, 1 Sept 1966, for Chief, OPS Cuba/WH Division/DDP, Attn: Mr. Harry Mahoney. Subject: MIG-21. Attaching communication from DIA authorizing $100,000 should the MIG-21 operation succeed. Funds to come from Air Force escrow fund now held by CIA.

Note: Other memos refer to MIG, which Ray said he knew a Cuban pilot would fly out of Cuba. Ray never came through with any information that was usable, and finally said he could not contact the pilot.

9. Cable dtd 4 Jan 69, CITE WH/Miami 2233, Ref: Caracas 1490 16-17.

AMOT reports on 9 Aug 68 indicating Ray planned to dissolve JURE and hold a Congress of Cuban exiles in 1969 to create a new organization to fight Castro. Ray was working with Haitian groups and hoped that, if the new invasion of Haiti was successful, he would be granted operational bases and a radio station in Haiti.

10. Memo dtd Feb. 8, 1971, to Director, FBI, from DD/P indicates that CIA had no current operational interest in Manuel Ray Rivero.
