25 May 68, IN 18245.

1. Hector Rodolfo (Sandoval) Alegria is Guatemalan rebel who, together with Francisco (Lara), engineered Liring-3 and Francisco Lara's trip to Guatemala, where he is now incarcerated. Sandoval saw Liring-4 several times after Liring-3's arrest, at one point saying he might have to hold up a bank to come to Liring-4's assistance. According to Manuel Marcue Pardinas, Sandoval was arrested 5 May shortly after his wife returned from a trip to Guatemala. Sandoval's wife is now staying at a house owned by Marcue together with Francisco Lara's family.

2. In an attempt to counter the eventuality that Mexican police, on the basis of information provided by Sandoval, might be looking for Liring-4 (Lering-4) for interrogation, the station asked Liring-4 to give up both apartments she and Liring-3 had been renting. Liring-4 is now living with her girlfriend. Before moving, she received a visit from a man who described himself as a lawyer of the Mexican Embassy, Guatemala City.
