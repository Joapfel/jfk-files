8/21/78

Joaquin (Joagun) Godoy y Solis (continued from 8/4/78)

DCD documents — no notes.

Godoy y Solis, Joaquin (Joagun), C-94894. CI/OA.

1. Request for Appraisal or Investigative Action. 17 October 1961: Godoy, AM.P.AZZ/1. To be used as contact between WAVE and MRP.

2. PRQ — Part II — 16 October 1961: Godoy, aka Oscar Acosta. Date of birth: March 13, 1927, Havana, Cuba (DPDB). No political affiliations — works for MRP.

3. Professional Operational Approval granted 17 December 1961.

4. PRQ — Part II — 3 May 1963: Clarence E. Imeryage developed Godoy in NMWAVE area for PM operational leads after his arrival from Cuba in summer of 1961. As an official of MRP, Godoy to influence its policies and guide its anti-communist propaganda effort. To be offered approximately $300 monthly — half in cash and half in escrow, the latter for both lover and control purposes.

5. Request for cancellation of Godoy on 27 July 1967.
