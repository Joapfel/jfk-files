United States Government Memorandum.

To: Acting Director, FBI.  
Date: 9/25/72.  
From: SAC, Charlotte (100-11664) (P).  
Subject: “FURIES” newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement publication, Washington, D.C.; IS—revolutionary activity.  
(00: WFO)

Re WFO letter to Bureau dated 7/31/72.

On 9/1/72, a well-placed source advised that Blowing Rock, N.C., telephone number 295-3321 had been discontinued effective 5/23/72. This source stated the number was formerly listed to James W. Hathaway, Laurel Lane, Blowing Rock, N.C. Hathaway was also a teacher at Appalachian State University, Boone, N.C.

On 9/5/72, Douglas Townsend, Chief of Police, Blowing Rock, N.C., advised that James Hathaway, who resided on Laurel (Laural) Lane in Blowing Rock, was associated with Appalachian State University in Boone as a college professor and his wife was a former movie actress. Chief Townsend stated that the Hathaways have some sort of “connection” or relatives in Italy and that they had left Blowing Rock some months ago, but had just recently returned and were planning on opening a restaurant in the Blowing Rock area in the future.

Chief Townsend stated that he had no record of the Hathaways having been arrested for any crime in the Blowing Rock area.

On 9/14/72, a check of the Charlotte Office indices revealed no record for James W. Hathaway, mentioned above, or Susan Hathaway, mentioned in the referenced WFO letter.

Bureau (RM).  
WFO (100-55290) (RM).  
Charlotte.
