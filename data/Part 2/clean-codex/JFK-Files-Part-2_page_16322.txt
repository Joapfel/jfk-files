Title: Black Panther Party (BPP).

Racial Matters.

Smith Act of 1940.

Seditious Conspiracy, Rebellion and Insurrection.

Character: Rebellion and Insurrection.

Synopsis:

At 12:30 a.m., 6/4/69, a reliable source advised that George Sams, Jr., a federal fugitive, was at Chicago BPP headquarters, 2250 West Michigan Street, Chicago, Illinois. Shortly after 5:00 a.m., 6/4/69, a Chicago FBI agent telephonically advised the Chicago BPP O.D. (officer of day) that warrants were outstanding for four BPP members, one or more of whom were believed to be at BPP headquarters. The agent heard statements, “I’ll kill you,” “Give me that shotgun,” and other similar statements in the background. After repeated attempts to obtain cooperation from BPP members at BPP headquarters, all BPP members were ordered out, and eight individuals were arrested by FBI agents and charged with harboring. A statement such as “in the revolution one wins or dies” was made by a BPP member. A handwritten twelve-page memorandum captioned “Suggested Plan for the Complete Breakdown of the State of Illinois Power Structure,” labeled “Top Secret,” as well as other BPP pamphlets and literature, were obtained in a search of BPP headquarters for Sams. Weapons, ammunition, tear gas bombs, gas masks, knives, and other items were obtained.
