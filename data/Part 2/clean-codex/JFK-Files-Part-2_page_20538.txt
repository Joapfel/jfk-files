Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

NR 010 WF coded. November 17, 1971, 5:07 PM, NITEL 11-17-71 ASW.

Teletype.

To Director, attention: Domestic Intelligence.

Cleveland (100-27031). New York (100-174481).

From Washington Field (100-54927).

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAC) sponsored demonstration, November 20, 1971, Washington, D.C., is New Left.

Cleveland and New York are advised to have informants attending the captioned demonstration report information to WFO by calling 628-2837 or 628-2826 and speaking with the person answering.

Cleveland and New York will advise WFO of code names to be used by respective informants.

Administrative.

Re New York teletype to Bureau November 17, last, and Bureau airtel to Cleveland November 16, last, captioned Students for a Democratic Society (SDS), is New Left.

End.

MRF FBI Washington, D.C.
