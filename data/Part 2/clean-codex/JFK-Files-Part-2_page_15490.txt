Nationality Group Coverage—Republic of Vietnam. 13—Vietnam.

DUBXD: 6-23-84.

Effective immediately, instant recipients are instructed to open new files bearing instant caption and submit letter to Bureau under this caption every 90 days setting forth progress made to develop confidential sources and informants at Vietnamese diplomatic establishments. Your initial letter should be forwarded to reach Bureau on or before 6-23-84. These 90-day letters should also indicate progress being made to develop sources and informants outside diplomatic establishments.

Be guided by instructions and observations set forth in Rulet to New York with copies for WFO dated 2-18-64, “Intelligence Coverage of Non-Soviet-Bloc Countries.”

WFO. File 65-63113 (Intelligence Coverage of Noncommunist Countries).

Note: Bureau has program involving development of sources and informants in diplomatic establishments of friendly foreign nations when such would be in the best interest of U.S. Inasmuch as Vietnam is such a sensitive area, it was recommended Liaison obtain State clearance for Bureau to include Vietnam in this program. State approval was obtained 3-19-64.

Both New York and WFO are familiar with Bureau instructions and policy with regard to this type of coverage.
