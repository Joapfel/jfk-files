Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971. Internal Security—Miscellaneous, IS-MISC.

At 2:32 PM, instant date, a group of approximately 750 marchers crossed Memorial Bridge from Washington, D.C., into Arlington, Va., and proceeded to the Mall entrance at the Pentagon via Washington Blvd. Marchers spray-painted the walls of an overpass over George Washington Parkway with the writings “Bitches Unite,” “Off the Pentagon,” and “Free Erika.” Marchers arrived at the Pentagon at 3:00 PM. Numerous speeches were given by unidentified individuals advocating the freeing of Angela (Angel ia) Davis and Bobby Seal; stopping the war against Black America; and demanding President Nixon and war wagers in the Pentagon end the war. They displayed signs reading “Free Erika and Bobby—Stop War Against Black America,” “No Justice in Amerikkka,” and “Support Sisters in Struggle—Asia, Africa.”
