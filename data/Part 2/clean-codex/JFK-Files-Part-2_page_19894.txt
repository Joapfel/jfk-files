United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI (100-468406)

Date: 4/27/72

From: SAC, Los Angeles (100-79014)(c)

Subject: Womens National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC), IS-C

Los Angeles Womens Abortion Action Committee, IS-New Left

Re Los Angeles letter and letterhead memorandum (LHM) dated 12/9/71, and Los Angeles report of SA Richard J. Kaysa dated 12/23/71, captioned “Womens Liberation Movement.”

For information of the Bureau and New York, referenced communications report activities sponsored by the Los Angeles Womens Abortion Action Committee (LAWAAC), a Los Angeles-based affiliate of WONAAC, which is headquartered in New York City.

Although LAWAAC continues to exist in name, this group has not been active in sponsoring activities since its participation in the 11/20/71 WONAAC-sponsored demonstration in San Francisco, California.

It is anticipated that LAWAAC (LAW AAC) will sponsor or participate in future demonstrations, and Los Angeles will report any future activity by this group.
