FBI

Date: 4/7/69.

Teletyped urgent message.

To: Director. From: SAC, Chicago.

Frederick Allen Hampton. RM.

Hampton found guilty jury trial instant before Cook County Circuit Judge Sidney A. Jones, Jr., Chicago. Was charged with July ten last robbery, Maywood, Illinois. Hampton released on two thousand dollars bond pending May thirteen next sentencing date. Military and Secret Service advised.
