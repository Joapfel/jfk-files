United States Government Memorandum.

To: Acting Director, FBI.

From: SAC, Charlotte (100-11664) (RUC).

Subject: “Furies” newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement publication, Washington, D.C.

Date: 11/7/72.

Re Charlotte letter to Bureau, 9/25/72.

On 10/5/72, Barbara Ragan, Credit Bureau, Boone, N.C., advised that their records reveal James Hathaway is a professor at Appalachian State University in Boone and that his wife’s name is Mary and they reside in care of General Delivery, Blowing Rock, N.C.

On 10/26/72, Douglas Townsend, Chief of Police, Blowing Rock, N.C., advised that both James Hathaway and his wife are residing in Blowing Rock, N.C., and that they intend to open a restaurant in Blowing Rock sometime in the future.

ST-103.
