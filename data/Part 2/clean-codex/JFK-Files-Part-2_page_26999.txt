Use of Cuban (Cutan) pilots would involve a delay of some months due to the precise training required. General Carter pointed out that we are accused of overflights in any case and that the possibility of Cuban (Cusan) protests should not dissuade us from making these flights if they are necessary. It was pointed out that other types of photography, especially useful in pinpointing critical targets, do not give sufficient detail for precise identification of certain types of equipment. After some discussion, the Group agreed to take cognizance of this matter and requested that it be reported at an appropriate time when specific targets and information needs could be identified.
