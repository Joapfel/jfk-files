CIA Information Report.

Report No. CS-3/532,138.

Country: Cuba.

Subject: Identification of Cuban vessels allegedly prepared to invade a Caribbean country.

Date of info: 28 November 1962.

Source: Traveling Salesman (F).

Appraisal of content: 3.

Headquarters comment: According to TDCS-3/530.509, dated 6 December 1962, as of mid-November two Cuban ships loaded with personnel and equipment were saved said to be prepared for an invasion of Haiti. Source said he would provide the names of the ships and their departure date.

The Frucuba and the Fundador, Cuban-flag vessels, were docked at the Arsenal docks in Casablanca (Habana) in late November. These Yeche vessels were scheduled to participate in an invasion of some Caribbean country.
