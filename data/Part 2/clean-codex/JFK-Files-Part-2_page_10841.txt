Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date of transcription: 9/2/71

Dale S. Cunningham, 130 North 4th Street, Camden, New Jersey, after being arrested by Special Agents of the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), was brought to the Camden, New Jersey, Resident Agency for processing and interview by Special Agents Eugene S. Debbaudt and Don G. Owens. He was immediately shown the “Interrogation; Advice of Rights” form by Special Agent Debbaudt, which he read and advised he understood, but refused to sign. He would provide no information relating to his arrest. He would not provide any personal data.

The following description was obtained from observation: Sex: male. Race: white. Eyes: brown. Hair: dark brown, balding. Height: approximately 6'1". Weight: approximately 200. Scars and marks: none noted. Characteristics: wears glasses.

Interviewed on 8/27/71 at Camden, New Jersey. File No. NEWARK 89-56.

By SAS Don G. Owens and Eugene S. Debbaudt. Date dictated: 8/31/71.
