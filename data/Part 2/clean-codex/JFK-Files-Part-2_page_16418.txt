A 16-page pamphlet entitled “Huey Newton Talks to the Movement” was observed at Chicago BPP headquarters, 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, Illinois, on June 4, 1969.

Hundreds, perhaps thousands, of copies of this pamphlet were observed. The quantity of pamphlets would indicate the likelihood of plans for distribution.

The pamphlet sets forth Newton’s (NEWTON’s) comments, observations, and philosophy regarding the BPP movement in the United States.

Newton (NEWTON) also discusses cultural nationalism, the Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee, as well as his observations concerning the “liberals” and white revolutionaries. The entire pamphlet is being set forth in the appendix section of this report.
