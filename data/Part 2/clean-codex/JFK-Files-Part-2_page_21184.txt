On May 7, 1968, (N-190), Nosenko (Hosenko) stated that girls working in the American House were reporting on MORONE(S) and he was particularly friendly with Svetlana Ivanova. He said Pietro Cechi also reported “pieces of information” about MORONE(S), but Nosenko did not know of any black market dealings between Cechi and MORONE(S). He stated that the photographs of MORONE(S) with the Polish girl were considered insufficient to compromise him and KGB wanted to create something more, possibly photographs with Russian girls. Ivanova was considered as a prospect in this connection, and she and her girlfriend had met MORONE(S) and a Marine in a restaurant and thereafter spent the night in the “flat” of one of the Marines. At this time he stated that the Marine with MORONE(S) on this occasion was possibly GARCIA(S).

It is noted that when MORONE(S) was interviewed on October 16, 1962, he furnished information regarding his intimacy with the friend of Svetlana Ivanova in the apartment of MORRIS ZWANG(S).
