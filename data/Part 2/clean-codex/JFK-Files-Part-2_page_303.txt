1. Fuller (FULL ARE) gives highpoints Liring-3 debriefing by Rowton on 2 January on his visit to Guatemala and his discussions in village near Zacapa with alleged representatives of Guatemalan guerrilla group.

2. Liring-3 met at Huehuetenango bus station by two men who identified themselves as “Octavio” and “Falcon” and local Fuerzas Armadas Revolucionarias (FAR) representatives. Since Liring-3 only able to obtain 48-hour Guatemala visa, FAR representatives asked him recross into Mexico and meet them again night 23 December at point on Mexican side of border on Pan-American Highway near La Mesilla, Guatemala. Liring-3 did as told, was led across border blind and driven to Guatemala City by succession of vehicles. During all legs of trip, second vehicle was used well ahead of one driving Liring-3 to guard against roadblocks.
