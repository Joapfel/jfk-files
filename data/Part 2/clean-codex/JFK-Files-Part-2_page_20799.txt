Title: Washington, D.C., Chapter Womens National Abortion Action Coalition (D.C. WONAAAC)

Character: Internal Security-C (Trotskyist)

Synopsis: D.C. WONAAAC, an affiliate of Womens National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAAC), which was founded in July 1971. WONAAAC national headquarters was originally located in Washington, D.C. (WDC), but moved to New York, New York, in December 1971. D.C. WONAAAC was located at 1346 Connecticut Avenue, N.W., WDC, and the key people involved were all members of WDC locals of Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) and/or Socialist Workers Party (SWP). D.C. WONAAAC did not maintain formal membership, and funds were obtained through contributions. It did not hold regular meetings, and most activities were conducted in conjunction with those planned by the national office. Its goals were synonymous with those of the national office, which have been defined as “working on a national scale to make abortion legal, to end restrictive contraception laws, and to end forced sterilization.”

Details: At Washington, D.C.
