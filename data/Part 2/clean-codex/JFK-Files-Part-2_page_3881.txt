Meanwhile, Gov. John J. McKeever has signed a request for extradition of D’Alonzo Smith, a New Orleans contractor arrested in Texas. The grand jury indicted Smith on a charge of perjury.

The indictment accuses Smith of paying $5,000 to Alonzo Young, a former aide to McKeever, to introduce him to him a month between Smith and Edward D. Pieter, a Baton Rouge passenger official.
