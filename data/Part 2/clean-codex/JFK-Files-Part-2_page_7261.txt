4. Curiously, Aaron asked DCOS' secretary for date evening 26 Sep. Although it turned out primarily social event and he did not attempt to elicit information from secretary, Aaron did make following comments of interest:

(A) He said he had begun trip with certain amount anti-BKHERALD bias, but this had been muted somewhat by favorable impressions he had obtained of various BKHERALD officers in the field with whom he had spoken.

(B) He said he thought Buenos Aires' Embassy political section was “jealous” of BKHERALD.

(C) He implied his ego had been bruised on trip because people frequently assumed Ambassador Trueheart, not he, was senior officer. He said he next plans trips to Europe and elsewhere. Headquarters, therefore, might wish to alert future stations he will visit of pecking order.
