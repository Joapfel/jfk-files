G. Membership of Chicago Chapter of BPP

The Chicago Chapter of BPP has an estimated membership of 100 to 130 members, which includes BPP members in BPP branches under the Chicago BPP Chapter. Approximately 30 members are considered to be active in BPP matters in the Chicago area.
