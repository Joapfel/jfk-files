2nd December, 1963

Dear J.

Subject: Jack RUBENSTEIN

Your RJ 2995 of Nov. 29, 63, and further to our telecom of Dec. 1st.

The only Jack RUBENSTEIN known to have arrived in ISRAEL during 1962 stated that he is a native of POLAND, born 1906 and a resident of NEW YORK.

He arrived on May 17 and departed on June 7, accompanied by his wife Mimi.

K.
