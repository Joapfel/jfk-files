10 October 1959. From: Director. Copy 1. Confidential: R. Routine.

To: Cite DIR 7-483. Info. Ref: IN 36017.

L. Lee Oswald (Osfald). Lee Henry Oswald (Osfald).

1. Probably identical. Born 18 October 1939, New Orleans, Louisiana, former radar operator in United States Marines who defected to USSR (US SS) in October 1959. Oswald (Osfald) is five feet ten inches, one hundred sixty-five pounds, light brown wavy hair, blue eyes.

2. On 31 October 1959 he attempted to renounce his United States citizenship to the United States FBI (FIB) in Moscow (Moscor), indicating he had applied for Soviet citizenship. On 13 February the U.S. FBI in Moscow received an undated letter from Oswald (Osfald), postmarked Minsk (Hinsk) on 5 February 1961, in which subject indicated he desired return of his U.S. passport and wished to return to USA if “they could come to some agreement concerning the dropping of any legal proceedings against me.” On 8 July, on his own initiative, he appeared at the FBI with his wife to see about his return to the States. Subject stated that he actually had never applied for Soviet citizenship and that his application at 10 October [continued].

Releasing officer. Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer.
