Manuel Ray — Volume XIV

B. Rogelio Cisneros calls Rodriguez (Halpern) on 20 May to advise him certain arms are being manufactured to JURE specifications in El Monte and are being investigated by the U.S. Treasury. Cisneros says JURE has invested $50,000 in these weapons being manufactured by Knuson (sic) Manufacturing Co.

C. Rodriguez (the undersea) tells Cisneros this is a pretty sad situation, that JURE must make use of Costa Rican, Venezuelan, and other Latin American connections or they were going to get “clobbered” in the U.S.

Halpern and Rodriguez (Rodrigaez) advised Mr. FitzGerald of the above. He agreed the Agency could not assist JURE in this matter. On 20 May, Mr. Bowdler of the State Department was notified of the matter, and the Deputy Chief, WH/SA, was advised.

E. Joseph O'Neill of OGC called on 20 May and informed [them] that JURE was involved, but the Agency did not want Revenue to call off its investigation because of the “autonomous” nature of our relationship with JURE.

6. Cable to DIR from JM-WAVE, dated 22 May 1964. Sender states that the manner of Tad Szulc in an office visit on May 21 and the new confident tone expressed by Cisneros conveyed a feeling to the cable sender that Ray has made it into Cuba.

7. Cable to JMWAVE from DIR, dated 23 May 1964. Request Reuteman ([Reuteman]) advise AMCARBON/1 ([AMCARBON/1]) that he has checked with headquarters and that Ray is not receiving CIA support. Reuteman ([Reuteman]) should trace the history of the intimate relationship of AMCAPE/1 ([AMCAPE/1]) with Ray, noting that since the Bay of Pigs, AMCAPE/1 ([AMCAPE/1]) has been serving, in effect, as a public relations man and lobbyist for Ray. Further explain to AMCARBON/1 ([AMCARBON/1]) that AMCAPE/1 ([AMCAPE/1]) must be credited with single-handedly placing the entire Cuban nation on alert and causing the withdrawal of masses of conscripted workers from cane fields merely by articles playing on the theme of the threat of infiltration by Ray and a few men. Tell AMCARBON/1 ([AMCARBON/1]) that CIA would not become party to a Ray operation, knowing the close relationship with AMCAPE/1 ([AMCAPE/1]), particularly since the latter is well known to be less than friendly to CIA.

8. Intelligence Information Cable, dated 26 May 1964. Cisneros says that as of 23 May 1964, no contact has been established with Ray, but Cisneros believes he has reached Cuba.

9. Intelligence Information Cable, dated 28 May 1964. Cisneros says Ray has not yet been able to infiltrate Cuba and will make another attempt the night of 28 May 1964. Cisneros says vigilance by U.S. authorities over the activities of JURE representatives in Florida is making support of Ray's effort extremely difficult.
