Miscellaneous

ABC-TV series “The FBI,” produced by QM Productions in conjunction with Warner Brothers–Seven Arts, Inc., is shown on the American Broadcasting Company Television Network. This program is completing its fourth year on the air, and production plans are going forward for a fifth season. The series, based on actual FBI cases, has consistently occupied a strong position in television ratings; the latest 30-market Nielsen (Nielson) rating reflects that it is the second most popular show on the air. It is estimated that the program has 26 million viewers each week.

In connection with “The FBI,” plans are being made to present a two-hour program based on the Barbara Jean Mackle kidnapping case next season. This program will not be televised until all prosecuting action is completed.

Spot announcements are made periodically following the program, highlighting individuals sought by the FBI who are on the “Ten Most Wanted Fugitives” list.
