KAPOK PERUMEN LIRING.

7 June 1966, 13:2041.

Ref: MEXICO CITY 7454 G2466D.

1. Carlos Jurado Delmar returned to Mexico City on 3 June from a trip to Havana sponsored by Wallace L. Rowton (3. Rowton). He did little to promote Rowton’s business deals except reestablish relations with Arnol Rodriguez Camps, who asked Jurado to send him some spark plugs for a car.

2. Jurado said the atmosphere was extremely tense in Havana, but people do not believe soldiers penetrated Guantanamo, where they were shot and then managed to get out. People generally believe this Cuban provocation was to cover the sorry economic plight of the country and bolster sagging morale.

3. Subject reports Cuba calling on other countries for volunteers to augment the armed forces, and Miriam Chichay told Rowton Cuban Ambassador to Mexico Joaquin Armas had attended a meeting of officials in Havana at which the call for volunteers was discussed.

4. Further debriefing by Rowton in progress. Jurado still asking no prying questions of Rowton, as might be expected if Jurado had blown Rowton to the Cubans. Chichay told LITAINT-2 last week Jurado was in Havana, no other details. LITAINT-2 told Station C/O he thought Jurado was on a cultural mission.
