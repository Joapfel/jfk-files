4. Cable to Director from JMWAVE, dated May 27, 1962, discusses a front-page article in the Miami Herald of this date by James Buchanan entitled “Miro Fades as Exile Chief: Three Latin Leaders Behind Ray.” It states that Ray has the backing of Gov. Muñoz Marín (Monoz Marin), Pres. Rómulo Betancourt, and former President José Figueres (Jose Figueras), and that Ray has taken over the post with the blessings of the U.S.

It further states Ray’s appointment as “Chief in Exile” came from underground in Cuba, according to Joaquín Godoy, Provisional MRP head in Miami. The article credits Cubans in Miami with saying the “new man in the field” is Adolf A. Berle, Jr., who has been special assistant to the State Department and is also a member of President Kennedy’s special task force on Latin America. The implication is that Berle is running a new U.S.-sponsored Cuban effort. The release caused a furor in the local Cuban colony.

Cable to JMWAVE from the Director, 28 May 1962, states that a State Department spokesman will state that the story is without foundation at a noon press briefing this date.

6. On 9 June 1961, Ray transmits a detailed study of a general program designed to attain the liberation of Cuba. The general thesis states that the MRP believes that the strongest position for representation and canalization of the aspirations of the large majority of the Cuban people is offered by the set of ideas and aims of the democratic left.
