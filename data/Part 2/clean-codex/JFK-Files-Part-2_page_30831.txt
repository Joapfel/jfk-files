Geo. de Mohrenschildt (GEO. DE MARBENCHILD). Office of Security.

Memo, 12/30/74, by Jerry G. Brown, SAS: Left Dallas after 19 April 1963 for a trip to New York, Philadelphia, and Washington, D.C., then traveling to Hawaii.

No information in testimony on whom they were doing [sic] 19 April–late May 1963.

On 29 April 1963, Gale Allen, Joe Cage officer, had requested an expedited check on DEM. “Exact reason unknown.”

“It is interesting that Allen’s interest in DEM coincided with the earlier portion of this trip, and the information would suggest that possibly Allen and DEM were possibly in the same environment in Washington, D.C., circa 26 April 1963.”
