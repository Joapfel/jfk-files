U.S. Department of the Navy copy. Central Intelligence Agency. 24 October 1963. CONF: OEF. INFO: Routine. Date processing: 23 October 1973. Info: CITE DIR. Subject: Lee Henry Oswald (Ostald). E7978.

Reference is made to CIA out telephone No. 74673, dated 10 October 1963, regarding possible presence of subject in Mexico City. It is requested that you forward to this office as soon as possible two copies of the most recent photographs you have of subject. (I'll forward them to our representative-in-Mexico, who will attach them to determining if the Lee Oswald in Mexico City and subject are the same individuals.)

Based on: End of message (IF 40357).
