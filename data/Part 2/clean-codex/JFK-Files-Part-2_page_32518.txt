The Agency's press service during the reporting period transmitted 12 articles, commentaries, and backgrounders on its wireless file service to USIA posts throughout Latin America for placement in the local newspapers, and the Voice of America Spanish broadcasts to Latin America carried a total of 20 commentaries and features on the subject of Cuban-based subversion in addition to the regular reporting of developments in the hourly newscasts.
