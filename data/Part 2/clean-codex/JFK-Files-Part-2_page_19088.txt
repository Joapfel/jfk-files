Date: 1/31/66

SAC, New York (134-3315)

From: Samuel Adason Joffe

Subject: Re NY letter, 12/9/65.

[The informant] was not in contact with this office after 12/2/65. As the Bureau has been advised, this informant intended to travel to the West Coast of the U.S. and return to NYC on 12/20/65. He apparently did return to NYC but was involved in participation on the American Broadcasting Corp.'s year-end news show, which involved travel on the part of the informant in a group of ABC correspondents throughout the U.S. Several phone calls have been made to the American Broadcasting Corp. in an effort to contact this former informant, but on each occasion he was found not to be in NYC. It is known that he was in Washington, D.C. over the weekend of 1/15–16/66 and presumably he is now en route to his new assignment in Hong Kong. When last contacted, he advised that he would have to proceed to Hong Kong during the latter part of January 1966 and presumably would not be back in the NYC area for approximately one year.

In view of his assignment overseas and his past association with Soviet nationals, as well as his assignment in Moscow, the Security Flash Notice currently placed in the Identification Division will be continued for one more year. After the passage of another year, the wisdom of continuing this Security Flash Notice will be evaluated.
