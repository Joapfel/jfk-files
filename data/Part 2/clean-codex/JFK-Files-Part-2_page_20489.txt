United States Government

Memorandum

To: Director, FBI (100-448475). Date: 3/19/68.

From: SAC, San Francisco (100-59802) (RUC).

Subject: Demonstration protesting U.S. policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January 15–16, 1968—information concerning (internal security).

Office of Origin: Washington Field.

Jeannette Rankin Brigade—information concerning (internal security).

Re San Francisco airtel and letterhead memorandum to the Bureau, dated 1/16/68.

This matter was opened by the San Francisco Office on November 17, 1967, on receipt of information that an organization known as the Jeannette Rankin Brigade had sent out a call for a demonstration to be held at the White House, Washington, D.C., on January 8, 1968, in protest of the war in Vietnam. As noted in referenced San Francisco communication, the above efforts culminated in a demonstration at San Francisco, California, on January 15, 1968, and a demonstration held in Washington, D.C., on January 15, 1968.

A review of the San Francisco files in the above matter reflects that Nikki Bridges (NIKKT BRIDGES), San Francisco Chapter of, listed on March 2, 1967, as a recipient of a new yearly subscription to the “People’s World” (PW), expiration date March 4, 1968. There is no known Communist Party (CP) membership on the part of Bridges. Vivian Hallinan, Executive Secretary of the Brigade, is the only other individual noted as an officer of the Brigade according to San Francisco files. San Francisco files do not reflect any known CP membership on the part of Hallinan.
