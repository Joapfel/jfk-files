FBI

Date: 11/28/67

Plaintext.

Transmit the following in via teletype. Teletype deferred (priority).

To: Director, FBI; SAC, New York.

From: SAC, WFO.

Demonstration by Jeannette Rankin Brigade on January eight next. Information concerning internal security.

Inspector Thomas I. Herlihy, Intelligence Division, Metropolitan Police Department, advised this date an official of the Pennsylvania Railroad advised him that two Pennsylvania Railroad trains had been reserved by the Jeannette Rankin Brigade in New York City for travel to Washington, D.C., on January eight next. No further information available to Inspector Herlihy.

New York requested to ascertain details concerning above.

Teletype.

Approved: D. F. Li.

Special Agent in Charge.
