STP. COMMO. Chrono (Dummy).

Date: 23 November 1963. File: P-3593. Silvia Duran P-. LITEMPO/Prod.

To: Immediate DIR. Info.

Cite: MEXI 7020. Number: KAPOK A. Reference: DIR 84888.

1. Saw photos of Lee Oswald on television night of 22 November and it obvious photos sent to Dallas were not identified with Lee Oswald held Dallas. Dates are as given on photos.

2. MEXI station reviewing all available photos persons entering Soviet and Cuban Embassies.

3. MEXI suggesting to LITEMPO 2 that Silvia Duran, who [was a] Mexican employee at Cuban Embassy and who put Lee Oswald on telephone from Cuban Embassy to talk to Soviet Embassy on 26 September, be arrested immediately and held incommunicado until she gives all details of Oswald known to her. (See Rep F.)

4. LITEMPO 2 can say D.F.S. coverage revealed call to him if he needs to explain.

End of message.

Coordinating officers. Authenticating officer. Outgoing. W.C.C. Releasing officer.
