241655Z, CITE MEXICO CITY 7422. Priority. Director info: JMWAVE.

KAPOK, PBRUMEN, LIRING.

Reference: Mexico 7234. 24 March 1980. Serial No. 23654.

1. Wallace B. Rowton had a luncheon meeting with LIRING-3 on 22 May, with the result that LIRING-3 enthusiastically urged Rowton to let him make a trip to Cuba to learn what items the Cubans want to buy from Western firms.

2. LIRING-3 believes he is dealing with an international Greek businessman with extensive contacts who wants economic research done on Cuba for unnamed clients wishing to sell to Cuba. LIRING-3 said he had many high-level contacts inside Cuba developed before the Castro takeover.

3. LIRING-3's best friend is (FNU) Rodriguez, who is high in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and who might be Arnol Rodriguez Camps, Deputy Foreign Minister. Will check to be certain. LIRING-3 also claims personal friendship with Raul Roa Kuri and even Fidel Castro.

4. LIRING-3 knows Rowton only as Nicolas Harris. He has asked no questions to better establish Rowton's bona fides. Apparently accepts him without question. Due to LIRING-3's close secret.
