December 18, 1964

Dr. Leland (Ieland) J. Haworth  
Director  
National Science Foundation  
1951 Constitution Avenue, N. W.  
Washington, D. C.

Dear Dr. Haworth:

A confidential source who has furnished reliable information in the past has advised that the National Science Foundation contemplated utilizing the Southern Christian Leadership Conference to locate qualified applicants to enter the University of Mississippi and other southern schools where the National Science Foundation has a program in progress.

There is enclosed for your information a summary entitled “Communism and the Negro Movement—A Current Analysis” which sets forth information regarding the Southern Christian Leadership Conference and its President, Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr.

Upon removal of the classified enclosure, this transmittal letter may be downgraded to “Secret.”
