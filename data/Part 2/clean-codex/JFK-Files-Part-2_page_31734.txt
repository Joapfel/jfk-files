Source advised that the “SCHEER for Congress Committee” was an organization formed in early 1966 for the purpose of supporting Robert Scheer as a candidate for a seat in the United States House of Representatives, United States Congress, from the 7th Congressional District of California in the primary election of June 1966.

This committee was a group of diverse people formed for the specific purpose of supporting and working for Robert Scheer in his bid for a seat in the United States Congress.

The primary plank in this committee’s platform was opposition to U.S. involvement in the Vietnam War and to further and continuing involvement by the U.S. Government in the War on Poverty and civil rights.

SF T-24  
July 31, 1967

A closed membership meeting of the Socialist Workers Party (SWP) was held at 873 Broadway, New York, New York, on June 30, 1966. During this meeting, one of the members said he had attended a National Conference for New Politics and that this organization had a $100,000 fund. Contributions from this fund were described as having been made to different organizations and individuals, including $1,000 to Robert Scheer for his election campaign.
