b. UFGA 3926 dated 1 May 1962: 19-124-25

Dispatch from JMWAVE to Headquarters, which forwarded (not attached) a paper prepared within the Station on the subject of the restructuring and revitalization of the C.R.C. Noted that the Station had major concern with regard to the extent and scope of non-CIA support, in the clandestine field, to the Cuban exiles and to the Cuban underground. It would appreciate information furnished by Headquarters or other field stations.
