1. Senator Schweiker. And this was called JIMMAYB. Who was it?

2. Mr. Halley. That is the cryptonym that the Agency used for the Miami station.

3. Senator Schweiker. And to whom in that chain of command at the CIA did you report when you were the operating head?

4. Mr. Halley. There were different people at different times. When I first went to Miami, the task force commander was Mr. William Harvey. He reported then to the ODP, who reported to the Director of Central Intelligence. At a later point in the operation, Mr. Harvey moved on to other tasks, and Mr. Fitzgerald became the chief of that particular unit, and thus after Mr. Fitzgerald, Mr. Hart was the head of that particular unit.

5. And between Harvey and Fitzgerald, I think there was a short period of time when a fellow by the name of Bruce Cheever was the acting chief of that particular unit, or the chief. I have forgotten the exact arrangement there.

6. Senator Schweiker. Sometime during this period did I change its title from Task Force W to SAS, is that correct; or not correct, or do I have it mixed up?

7. Mr. Halley. My recollection of that chronology was it first called SAS and then became Task Force W, and so became integrated back into the whole division, which was the Western Hemisphere Division.
