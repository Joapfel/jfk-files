Title: Irving Rhodes Fromer

Character: Security Matter - C

Synopsis:

Subject still resides at 881 Clayton Street, San Francisco, and is employed as an architect-draftsman through the American Institute of Architects, 254 Sutter Street, San Francisco. No information has been received reflecting that subject has been active in the CP during the past year. Subject was present at a May Day meeting of the CP held in May 1965, and he has supported activities of the San Francisco CCPAF and Women for Peace. He has participated in a number of demonstrations sponsored by the VDC and acted as chairman of a meeting of the Haight-Ashbury VNC.
