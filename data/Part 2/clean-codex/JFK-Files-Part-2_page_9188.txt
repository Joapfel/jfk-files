Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date of transcription: 9/23/74.

George Menegus, 37 Schenck Avenue, Brooklyn, New York, telephone (212) 647-8368, was interviewed at the Commodore Hotel, 42nd Street and Park Avenue, Manhattan, New York City, where he is employed as Chief of Security.

He advised that a hotel registration card indicates that Mike Brown registered at the hotel at 7:15 p.m., August 5, 1974, and was assigned Room 1092. Brown indicated that his address was Rt. 7, Box 236, Little Rock, Arkansas. Brown paid $29.08 cash in advance for the room and checked out sometime on August 6, 1974. Menegus stated that there is no check-out time stamped on the registration card and Brown presumably checked out sometime before the hotel's 1 p.m. check-out time. Menegus stated that the registration card indicates that Brown checked in alone and that no additional charges, such as telephone calls, were billed to him. The registration indicates that Commodore Hotel clerk #16, Mr. Bill Foster, handled the transaction with Brown.

Menegus stated that the original registration card is maintained by Mr. James J. Shevlin, General Credit Manager, Hotel Biltmore, Madison Avenue at 43rd Street, Manhattan. Menegus explained that the Commodore Hotel and Biltmore Hotel are part of the same chain and records of both hotels are maintained at the Biltmore.

Interviewed on 8/21/74 at New York, New York. File # NY 185-229.
