193402. Cite Mexico City 9934.

19 January. 81261.

Director info JMWAVE, Guatemala City.

Topic: LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 9339 (IN72194).

1. At meeting with Wallace B. Rowton 16 January 67, LIRING-3 reported that Cuban A13 on 11 January called LIRING-3 aside during reception at EMB and stated he had received cable from Arnol Rodriguez Camps asking LIRING-3 return to Cuba for additional discussions.

2. LIRING-3 told Rowton he believes this is answer to his first letter to Rodriguez following last trip to Cuba mid-December, at which Rodriguez introduced him to Osmani Cienfuegos and at which he discussed LIRING-3 proposal to assist Tricontinental Committee in Guatemala.

3. LIRING-3 and station believe it best L-3 not jump at minute Cubans call, so he telephoned Rodriguez night 17 January, informed him he would go to Cuba but not until after he had made another trip south, which he must do in next few days. LIRING-3 plans to go to Guatemala (GLAT) about 27 January after receiving supplies Rowton ordered while at headquarters, which are due to arrive in Mexico 25 January. Possibility exists that Rodriguez will ask LIRING-3 to come to Cuba prior to trip to Guatemala, but LIRING-3 intends to beg off if possible. Results of phone talk will be obtained at Rowton-LIRING-3 meeting night 18 January.
