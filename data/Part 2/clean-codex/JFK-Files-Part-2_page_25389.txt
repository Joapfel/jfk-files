More on Hunt Castro project.

Assassination alleged to have been planned as part of a smaller invasion effort, and preparations were allegedly made in Costa Rica. Source: Szulc, supra.

May 1965: Hunt Castro scheme “aborted when Dominican Republic strife broke out” (scheme also allegedly involved McCord, Barker, and Sturgis).

May 1965 (or later): As the Katzenbach/Helms/LBJ meeting on the Dominican Republic ended, LBJ asked Helms if CIA had ever been involved in assassination of a leader, and Helms said “no.” CIA poisoning plot at Haitian Palace in ’68. Rosselli indicted for alien law and interstate gambling crimes (Friars Club card game rigging). Source: Katzenbach testimony to Senate Select Committee on CIA.

March 3, 1967: First Anderson column on Rosselli. In response to Anderson story, RFK tells Walinsky and Edelman he shut it off. CIA bombing Haitian palace. Rosselli sentenced to 5 years. Sources: Anderson column, 4/2/75; Government Opposition to sentence reduction, supra.

Early 1969: Assistant Attorney General Wilson asks for Justice Department Rosselli file. Source: Keeney Watergate memorandum of conversation.

1970: Rosselli goes before grand jury under immunity regarding Giancana. Source: Government Opposition, supra.

January 18, 1971: First of three Anderson columns.

January 19: Maheu, scheduled to go before grand jury on Las Vegas skimming, calls Attorney General John Mitchell. Same day: Haldeman asks Dean to inquire into Hughes/O’Brien/Maheu ties. Source: Lenzner memo to Senator Ervin on Rosselli and phone records.

January 27: Maheu interviewed by Wilson and Peterson on Rosselli matter.

February 1: Caulfield to Dean memo that Maheu was involved in covert activity for CIA in early 1960s. Rosselli files motion for reduction in sentence, citing Anderson stories and presenting memorandum to court.
