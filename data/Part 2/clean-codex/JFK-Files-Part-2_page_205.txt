172183Z, CITE Mexico City 7347. Director. KAPOK DYVOUR PSRUMEN AMROD LICOOKY. Reference: Mexico City 7875.

1. STASHA arrived in Mexico on 17 April by Eastern from New York. Station not yet able to confirm from air lists. Departed for Cuba on 24 April.

2. S reticent about her Miami sojourn. Mentioned Jerry Heming and his friend “Howie,” who helped her get around in exile group. In what LICOOKY interpreted as a slip, S identified her Cuban case officer in New York as Gutierrez. Said she had given him several rolls of tape covering her Miami visit.

3. Future contact to be via travelers between Habana and Mexico who will mail letters in country of destination. S wants American magazines sent her via Prensa Latina. Precise employment in Cuba not certain. Allegedly can have old job with Prensa Latina, but LICOOKY suggested she try Casa de las Americas.

4. LICOOKY feels S drawn back to Cuba more by practical than ideological or emotional considerations, since the government would supply housing and her husband would have to help support the children.
