1936–1946: Tried in Vratsa Court for having engaged in subversive groups all over Bulgaria. Sentenced on four counts, to death, 15 years, 6 years, and 9 years; jailed (jaded) in solitary confinement.

26 June 1947: Tried and sentenced to death by Sofia Military Court. Tried and sentenced to death by the court in Lou.

June 1947: Taken to a quarry in Vratsa.

8 July 1947: Escaped from quarry, went to Western Europe. From there to Zverova, then Sofia, where he received help from British and Americans. Slept on one occasion in Sofia house of Jack Horner.

23 July 1947: Crossed Greek frontier south of Zagoria.

24 July 1947: Gave himself up at Shahim.

2 August 1947: Admitted to British hospital in Karlsruhe.

Circa August 1947: Eighteen days later transferred to British hospital in Salonika (Salanika).

Circa mid-September 1947: Sent to Athens. Greeks sent him to refugee camp at Lavonia. Stayed 9 months.

Circa June 1948: Athens, where he obtained employment broadcasting in Bulgarian on Radio Athens.

See newsstand dated 4 January 1952.

1949: Worked for Pierre Florimond, Salonika (Salanika), French vice consul.
