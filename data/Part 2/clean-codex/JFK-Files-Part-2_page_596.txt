Mexico City Station asset LIRING-3 recently had a series of meetings with young Mexican leftists Sergio Gabriel (Valdez) and Raul (Moreno) Wonchee, who were interested in helping leftist elements in Venezuela and other Latin American countries outside the Communist Party framework. Valdez, who would take care of the commercial venture used as cover for this activity, is currently in contact with Maryed with Garcia Mendez, Avenida Los Geranios, Res. Mapara 4, La Florida, Caracas. Garcia, who is divorced or obtaining a divorce, is a healthy woman who travels extensively and took an apartment course in Mexico with Valdez. She is willing to finance a decorative glass factory run by Valdez and is unwitting of the latter’s plan to use such facility as cover for support of subversive activities. Moreno, who claims to have leftist contacts in Venezuela, would handle clandestine work. The two men went to the Cuban Embassy, Mexico, for help but were given the runaround and finally told to contact LIRING-3, as their project was “too sensitive for Cubans to support at this time.”
