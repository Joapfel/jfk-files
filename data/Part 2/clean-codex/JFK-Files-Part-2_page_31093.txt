18 October 1965.

Panegue participated in Cuban exile activities in the New York area in 1962 and later registered in ISR and the Department of the Army in August 1962 (dropped without prejudice 18 November 1963). In 1964, Panegue was a member of an infiltration team. Recruited by the Miami station as a radio operator/infiltrator and favorably screened on 5 August 1964. P.O.A. granted 13 November 1964. Paid $275 monthly beginning 1 September 1964. Trained by Cuban agents in Miami but never used operationally. Amicably terminated 1 October 1965 and recommended for re-use. Reported in 1969 as conducting clandestine trips to Cuba on behalf of Junta Militar de Cuba.
