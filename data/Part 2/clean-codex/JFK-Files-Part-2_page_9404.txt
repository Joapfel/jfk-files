DE T-2

April 12, 1972

M. Teaching Revolutionary Programs and Policies

A political education class was held at 157 Collingwood, Detroit, on October 24, 1971. The class was led by Edgar Robinson and consisted of reviewing the “Red Book.” Kenneth Horston and Lonnie Darden, leaders of the Detroit Chapter, were present.

DE T-1

October 26, 1971

Political education classes are no longer regularly held on Sundays. The classes are now held at random during the week as the BPP is currently more concerned with the survival of its members rather than the community.

DE T-3

November 3, 1971

Kenneth Horston, leader of the Detroit BPP, currently in BPP National Headquarters, Oakland, California, advised Detroit to get involved in the church, stay away from ideological debates, and try to bring the church people around to their thinking.
