September 19, 1975.

Teletype. NR 018 LA Code, 537 PM NITEL 9-19-75 RLD.

To Director. From Los Angeles (157-5089). Attention: Supervisor Keith Mendenhall, Room 4427, JEH Building. Attention: INTD.

Re Bureau teletype call September 19, 1975, and Los Angeles teletype April 23, 1975.

As set forth in referenced Los Angeles teletype, Captain Mervin King, Los Angeles Police Department, advised that his department had no objection to divulging that the letter from Jane Fonda to Leo M. Atarasso, dated March 25, 1970, had been obtained by the Los Angeles Police Department through a legal search, in connection with a homicide investigation involving Saundra Pratt, wife of Elmer Pratt, an acknowledged leader of the Cleaver faction (LCEAVER faction), Black Panther Party (BPP), classified by 5411, XGDS Two, indefinite.
