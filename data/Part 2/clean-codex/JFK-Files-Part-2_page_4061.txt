The following has been added to the record: that all statements, theories and assumptions made by or on behalf of each service are correct; and that each service will act in good faith so long as it is satisfied that it is not causing any harm to the others.

The following has been added to the record: that no person has any right whatever against any of the persons so named, or any of their friends or others, for any reason whatever, whether civil or criminal; and that if any such person has any cause of complaint against any of the persons so named, he will not seek redress against them, nor will he make any claim therefor. He will not try to get any money from them, nor will he give them anything. He will not use their names in any way which may tend to bring them into disrepute or otherwise harm them. He will not use their names in any way which may tend to harm or injure the reputation of any of the persons so named, whether public or private, nor will he give them anything which may tend to harm or injure the reputation of any of the persons so named, whether public or private.
