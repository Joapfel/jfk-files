24 August 1971. U.S. Embassy Moscow Airgram. 26 September 1971. Science Berkeley Lab, 600254.

Four Soviet scientists, MOSKALEV, MOISEEV, NENOT, and MASSE, participated in the same Transuranium Biology Symposium at Hanford during the week of September 26. They were in San Francisco by chance at the same time and desired to visit Berkeley on the same day. They did not travel to the conference together. It all seemed to be coincidental. Drs. MOSKALEV and MASSE reportedly understood more English than they could speak. Dr. MOSKALEV was the USSR's representative on the ICRP internal dose committee.
