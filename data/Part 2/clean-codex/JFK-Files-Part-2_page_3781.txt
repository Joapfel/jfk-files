United States Government Memorandum

Date: 18 April 1967

To: Chief, Security Research Staff, OS  
From: Mr. Bruce Solie  
From: Director, Domestic Contact Service (Services Division)  
Subject: District Attorney Garrison's Investigation Into Alleged Conspiracy to Assassinate President Kennedy — NO-112-67  
Reference: DCS memoranda dated 6 and 11 April 1967, same subject
