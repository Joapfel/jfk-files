Subject interview. On 25 February 1963, Epifanio Romero-Delgado, Cuban pre-inductee, who resides at 135 SW 18th Court, Miami, Florida; permanent mailing address c/o Gustavo Acosta, 1140 SW 9th Street, Miami, Florida; employed in a shoe factory in Hialeah, Florida, the name and address of which are unknown to the subject; social security number 463-74-2051, was interviewed in Spanish at the U.S. Armed Forces Main Induction Station, Coral Gables, Florida, concerning his arrests, and he stated substantively as follows:

On 20 April 1961, he was arrested by the G-2 in Finca de la Mar, San Cristobal, Pinar del Rio, on suspicion of working with the anti-Castro elements there. He was suspected because of the company he kept, people who were anti-Castro, and because he did not socialize with those who were in favor of Castro. He was held five days by the G-2, during which time he was interrogated a number of times and held without food or water.

He was then sent to the jail in Consolidación del Sur, Pinar del Río, Cuba, where he was held for 13 days. He was then released without trial for lack of evidence.

The subject stated this was the only time he has ever been arrested and that, other than what was mentioned herein, he has never been held, arrested, detained, or imprisoned for civil, political, or military reasons.
