NR 1019 B S CODE.

9:54 PM. Urgent. 4-9-71. WFY.

To: Director, Washington Field, Alexandria.

From: Boston (100-42759).

Liberation. Women's National March on the Pentagon, April ten next, is miscellaneous.

On April nine, seventy-one, a source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that forty-five people will leave on a bus at ten PM tonight, April nine, instant, en route Washington, D.C. They will depart aboard a Brush Hill bus from 68 St. Stephen St., Boston, destined for the United States Department of Justice at Washington, D.C., and will be in that city to participate in the Women's March on the Pentagon.

According to the above source, this trip to Washington, D.C., is being sponsored by the Student Activities Unit.
