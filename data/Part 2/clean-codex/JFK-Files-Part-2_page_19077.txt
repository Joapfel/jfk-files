NY 134-3315

This is the Soviet informant described as “VYACHSLAV KISLOV” who was in contact with him in Moscow. It is noted that KUSKOV was one of a number of Soviet “tourists,” who, according to the U.S. Department of State, was to attend a Symposium on Results of International Geophysical Year at Los Angeles, 1963 (NY file 105-62912).

It is noted that there is no indication that KUSKOV did come to the U.S. for the above symposium. NYO cannot locate a photograph of this individual.
