UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT Memorandum

To: SAC, Chicago

Date: 3/10/69

SA James W. Gerblick

Racial Tensions  
Maywood, Illinois – 1969

Where appropriate, the information has been disseminated to local authorities.

Fred Hampton spoke before a group of about two hundred Negro youths and adults at the Irving School, 16th and Madison, Maywood, on Friday night, 2/28/69. This was sponsored by a sorority, and was about Negro history.

JWG.
