EKSTAIR.

1. On 18 April we had Lyle O. Kelly picked up by BGKANTAR and put under protective arrest. He is told life is threatened and does not know of our role. Reasons we found it necessary: Glantz-like (GLANTZ-like) activities including megalomania, lack of security, and misappropriation of funds. Most serious was that he was about to sell knowledge of our operation to the local fairies (FAIRYS) for an initial sum of local equivalent of $1,000, price—we considered insulting low.

2. Unfortunately Kelly is so hot and prepared that our normal flake disposal system is not safe. Run of will rejects manage (RUN OF WILL REJECTS MANAGE) to mail letters, so certainly Kelly should be once.

3. Can ZRMETAL or BGMIDDY take him off our hands. Repeat, he still thinks we [are] his protector, so he can be persuaded to leave here voluntarily. He must, however, be denied mail facilities. Request utmost serious consideration aid. Whole DREAMER ops in security jeopardy. Three months should be sufficient to put him out of date and therefore.
