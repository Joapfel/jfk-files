g. Revolutionary New Left underground-type publications.

(1) Conduct no investigation without prior Bureau approval.

(2) Review all New Left underground-type publications coming to your attention to determine if pertinent material therein advocates civil disobedience, rioting, preparation or use of explosives, and other extremist activity. Advise the Bureau of the results of the review and request Bureau approval for investigation. Submit your request using a caption containing the title of the publication with the characters “IS - New Left.”

(3) Upon receipt of Bureau authority for investigation, obtain information concerning the nature of the publication, its location, circulation, sources of income, and principal organizers.

(4) Handle the investigation discreetly through established sources and submit results in a form suitable for dissemination.
