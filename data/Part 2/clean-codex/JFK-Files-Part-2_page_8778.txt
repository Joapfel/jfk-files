Director 8214 to WAVE, dated 2 January 1963.

HURWITCH (State Department Cuban Coordinator) replying briefly to AMSPELL letter to President Kennedy by referring DRE organization to President Kennedy's 29 December Miami speech as latest official statement of U.S. policy.

“Using DRE 26 December coded broadcast over WMIE as opening wedge, NEWBY reprimanded FERNANDEZ for failure to report unusual substance of text. Also pointed out this flagrant violation of FCC regulations, thus placing CIA in embarrassing position. FERNANDEZ apologized but said it was urgently necessary to alert Cuba DRE to penetration danger. This was first time existence of penetration was reported by FERNANDEZ or other DRE member. To inquiry why FERNANDEZ withheld such information from CIA, although known to DRE for months, FERNANDEZ replied DRE merely suspected penetration before but only now certain. Avoided giving specifics.

Comment: After four years as chief of CIA-subsidized DRE group, FERNANDEZ resigned July 64 to pursue medical studies.”

UFGA 7383 from JMWAVE to Headquarters, dated 28 January 1963.

Stated that Jorge MEDINA Bringuier, Secretary General of the DRE in Cuba, was a G-2 agent. That MEDINA was in contact with FERNANDEZ while in Cuba and planned to use FERNANDEZ to establish his bona fides with CIA.
