Barney Kilbride served a subpoena directing Gov. John J. McKeithen to appear at 2 p.m. Thursday on William V. Redmann's executive counsel to the governor in Baton Rouge.

Previously Jefferson Parish racket figure Carlos Marcello and two of his brothers, Samuel and Peter, were also subpoenaed to appear Thursday.

Meanwhile Wednesday, East Baton Rouge District Attorney Sargent (Sargens) Pitcher, conducting his own crime investigation, said in Chicago that he has met with “tremendous success and accomplishments” on a trip to gather information about organized crime.

Young Accompanies

Pitcher, who said details will be made public at the proper time, was accompanied to Chicago by Audrey Young, a former aide to McKeithen.

Young's testimony before the East Baton Rouge Grand Jury has led to an indictment already in that jury's investigation of organized crime.

McKeithen was attending the Washington Parish Fair in Franklinton when his subpoena was served. Redmann said the governor would have appeared on invitation without the subpoena.

District Attorney Jim Garrison Tuesday first had invited the governor to appear in New Orleans, then subpoenaed him.

Garrison, when asked why he issued the subpoena, said, “Originally, I was following protocol and then I decided this is no time for protocol.”

“We want to find out what these allegations of organized crime are all about and, so far, nobody seems to know except what they read in Life Magazine.”

“We are going to call everybody we think can shed some light on this thing if we have to tell the ambassador to the United Nations.”
