Hunt, 1960.

Was in Cuba 12/7–15/56. Mexico 12/5-0 to 3/3-5. On 9/27/60, Hunt was given the alias “Edward J. Hamilton.” In 6/63, a post office box was opened in D.C. for Hunt (Woodridge) under the alias. In 10/60, assigned JMWAUE; box cancelled 6/65. Retired in 1970. From 7/60 to 10/60, assigned MCS. From 1/57 to 6/60, Montevideo. From 7/60 to 3/61, was at JMATE in the MC and Coral Islands.
