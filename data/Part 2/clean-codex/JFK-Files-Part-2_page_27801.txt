MEMORANDUM

To: Tom Howarth, Budget Officer  
From: Charlie Mathews, Special Counsel  
Date: October 5, 1977  
Re: Ms. Beth Anne Lichtenfels

Ms. Beth Anne Lichtenfels has accepted the position of Document Control Clerk with the John F. Kennedy Task Force. Her effective starting date will be October 11, 1977, and her starting salary will be $12,000.00.

Your full co-operation will be appreciated in familiarizing Ms. Lichtenfels with staff procedures and welcoming her aboard.
