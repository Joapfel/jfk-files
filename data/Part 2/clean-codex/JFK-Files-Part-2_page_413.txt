Director info JANWAVE.

For personal use only.

Ref: Mexico City 8977 (M 3535-2).

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace B. Rowton on the evening of 26 October 1966 that he had been asked by LERING-2 to come to the Embassy at 1900 hours that night for a meeting with LERING-2 and Reynaldo Cepeda Hernandez.

2. LIRING-3 pressed LIRING-2 for a general idea of the meeting agenda. LIRING-3 replied, “When you go to Havana you will be seeing certain Central Americans and we would like to talk this over with you before you go.”

3. LIRING-3 will make a detailed report to Rowton on the evening of 27 October, which will be taped. According to present plans, LIRING-3 will leave for Havana on a flight scheduled for 1 November 1966.

Li Ring/3 reported he had again seen Liring/2 on 24 October.
