On 2/18/72, Mr. Marvin Glickman, rental agent, 150 5th Avenue, New York, New York, advised that the Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) rented room number 843 at this address on 12/14/71. Glickman stated that a Joanna Misnik, Treasurer, WONAAC (WONACC), signed the rental agreement. The rent was $5,700.00 per year or $475.00 per month. The phone at WONAAC headquarters is 212-741-0450.
