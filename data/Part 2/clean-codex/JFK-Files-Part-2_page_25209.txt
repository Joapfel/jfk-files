Persons/Entities: General Minh (EN. MINH), Don (DON EN.), General Harkins (EN. HARKINS), Conein (ONEIN). Date: 10/18/63. Category: Diem.

Both Generals Minh and Don presented the following report by General Harkins (Markins) as well as most other AAW generals; though Ambassador talked with all generals, none mentioned Conein (Granein).

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit One, page 16. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.

Dates: 11/1/63; 10/21/63. Category: Diem.

Occurrence:

In addition to the above maneuvering, Colonel Do Minh, who was in command of the unit, called up Major General Nguyen Van Thuan, Chief of Military Security Service, to lend his men a hand by advising him on military intelligence reports which went to President Diem. These reports stated on about 24 October that he had built up a picture of strong Viet Cong forces approaching Saigon for attack and promised the Joint General Staff to issue appropriate orders, approved by the President, to move units favourable to the convenience of the outskirts of Saigon and also to move units, such as the Special Forces, away from Saigon.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit One, pages 13–14. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.
