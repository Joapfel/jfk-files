The elements of the Presidential Guard had been contracted and then were early depositions during the fighting. The Ranger Command was not contracted since Parachute Units were assigned to individual corps and divisions. Those assigned to Corps (Corp) units were not named to be loyal to that unit. The Air Force was 100 percent with the Corps (Corp) plotters with the exception of its commanding officer, Colonel Ikyub Kim Eim. The medical service, a non-commissioned officer, was with the Corps (Corp) plotters. The man always considered faithful to President Eim was to fall in line with the category of the Vietnam Special Forces, which its command [was] issuing the necessary orders under duress.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit One, page 10; page 11. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.
