The next cable that Mexico City sent to Headquarters said, in part, “Other than info already sent re Oswald’s connection with Sov and Cuban Embassies, no other info available.” The next cable reports a literal transcription of the 9/28 and 10/1/10:45 conversations. The next cable that refers to the transcripts of Oswald’s conversations reports the 9/27/10:30 call, the 9/27/4:05 call, the 9/27/4:26 call, the 10/1/10:31 call, and the 10/3 call. With this cable Mexico City informed Headquarters of all the substantive information available from the Soviet Embassy electronic surveillance.

Mexico City also informed Headquarters on 11/24/64 that the tapes from the period in which Oswald had visited the Soviet and Cuban Embassies had been erased. A cable on the previous day had informed Headquarters that it was “probable” that the Oswald tapes had been erased. An earlier cable that same day reported that “Station unable compare voice as first tape erased.
