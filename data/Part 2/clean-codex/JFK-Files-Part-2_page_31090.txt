April 29, 1959. Classified message. Director from Havana: Raúl Castro has begun a major purge of Cuban Armed Forces. Among those to be eliminated are Victor Panegu (Panegue), aka “Diego.”

October 18, 1960.

Conversation with Major Victor M. Panegue (Commandante Diego), 6 October at 9:00.

When Panegue arrived in Miami, he got together with Alianza de Liberación through Dr. Luis Conde Aguero. He could not get immediate help from this group, so resigned. Clev said he had tried to get help from the U.S. Government but was unsuccessful.

Panegue is convinced that the only thing needed to overthrow Castro is arms and supplies. He has a plane which he intends to fly into Cuba with a cargo of arms.

Author of memo recommends that CIA offer assistance to Panegue independent of all other organizations, as he seems to be capable of becoming an important factor in the future.

CUU3443.

November 2, 1960. Cable to Director from Maale: Panegue, 48 men leaving for Cuba the night of November 1 on a 40-foot yacht, Trudy Lou. Craft purchased by Orlando Rosel.
