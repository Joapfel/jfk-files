Applicant.

1. Loop: The lines between the center of the loop and delta must be clear.

2. Whorl: These lines do not go through deltas and must be clear.

3. Arch: Arches have no deltas.

To obtain classifiable fingerprints:

1. Use black printer's ink.
2. Distribute ink evenly on inking slab.
3. Wash and dry fingers thoroughly.
4. Roll fingers from nail to nail, and avoid allowing fingers to slip.
5. If smudged impressions are recorded in the correct order.
6. If an improper or deformity makes it impossible to print a finger, make a notation to that effect in the individual finger block.
7. If some physical condition makes it impossible to obtain perfect impressions, submit the best that can be obtained with a memo stapled to the card explaining the consequences.
8. Examine the completed prints to see if they can be classified, bearing in mind that most fingerprints fall into the patterns shown on this card. Other patterns occur incorporated and are not shown here.
