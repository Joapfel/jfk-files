Persons/Entities: Conein (Conine), Huan, General Harkins, Ambassador Lodge. Date: October 17, 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: Conein (Conine) and Huan held a meeting during which Huan confirmed the fact that he was attempting to contact and had a message from the general. At a reception to be given 18 October by General Harkins for Ambassador Lodge, one of the generals would approach the Ambassador and ask the latter to vouch for Conein (Conine); if the Ambassador conditions that Conein (Conine) to speaking with the Ambassador's authority, he should be prepared for a future meeting outside Ba Meng. Huan was only providing a message and did not elaborate further.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Corein (Conine), Exhibit One. File: Page 5-6. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.

Persons/Entities: Lam Ngoc Huan, Ambassador Lodge. Date: October 16, 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: Mr. Lam Ngoc Huan, Manager of Caravelle Hotel and former RVW officer who was retired due to specifications of involvement in November 1970 coup plot, approached an Embassy officer after a social event and stated he was trying to establish contact with a military official designated by Ambassador Lodge to take part in confidential discussions. Huan claimed he was representing some Vietnamese officials and other officers who wanted to talk to an American military officer about the situation in South Vietnam.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Corein (Conine), Exhibit One.
