ReBUAIRTEL, December 5 last.

Seattle 76's has been asked to accompany a delegation to subject VIDEM from Seattle area by Taimi Halonen, CP member.

Effort has been made to work informant back into CP activity in Seattle, and going with above delegation would considerably enhance her possible success in this connection in addition to providing coverage. Informant in past was CP member in Everett, Snohomish County, Washington.
