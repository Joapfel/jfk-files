Applicant

1. Loop

The lines between the center of the loop and delta must be clear.

2. Whorl

Where lines intersect between delta and circles must be clear.

3. Arch

Arches have no deltas.

To obtain classifiable fingerprints:

1. Use black printer's ink.
2. Distribute ink evenly on inking slab.
3. Wash and dry fingers thoroughly.
4. Roll fingers from nail to nail, and avoid allowing fingers to slip.
5. If due impressions are recorded in correct order.
6. If an amputation or deformity makes it impossible to print a finger, make a notation to that effect in the individual finger block.
7. If some physical condition makes it impossible to obtain perfect impressions, submit the best that can be obtained with a memo stapled to the card explaining the cause.
8. Use only completed prints to see if they can be classified, bearing in mind that completed prints fall into the patterns shown on this card. Other patterns occur in inadequacy and are not shown here.
