On January 6, 1969, the Central Committee of the BPP at Chicago met and decided to conduct no activity or attempt to recruit members from BR territory. BPP leaders do not plan, as of this time, to meet with JEFF FORT or any members of BR.

On January 14, 1969, FRED HAMPTON and BOBBY RUSH, leaders of the BPP at Chicago, Illinois, appeared on the radio program of Radio Station WVON, Chicago. HAMPTON stated that the BPP was currently in the process of educating BR at Chicago. JEFF FORT, leader of BR, immediately telephonically contacted WVON and stated that HAMPTON was confused as far as educating BR. FORT stated that BR was educating the BPP, and this was the understanding reached when leaders of BR and BPP met at a Chicago church several weeks ago.

On January 15, 1969, FRED HAMPTON, BPP leader in Chicago, accused JEFF FORT, leader of BR, of being responsible for the deaths of more Negroes than the Ku Klux Klan or police at a meeting in Maywood, Illinois, a Chicago suburb. HAMPTON said FORT is “in” with Chicago’s Mayor DALEY, because when he is arrested he is always released.

On January 15, 1969, HAMPTON said he is the new MALCOLM X.

The friction and hatred between well-organized Negro youth gangs and the BPP at Chicago is so great that it seems unlikely the BPP will be able to obtain any unity among substantial numbers of black individuals at Chicago in the near future or possibly at any time.

On January 17, 1969, FRED HAMPTON, Chicago BPP leader, advised at a BPP rally held on January 17, 1969.
