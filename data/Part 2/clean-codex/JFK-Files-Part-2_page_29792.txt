Continuing Investigations Reports

Subject/Case Reference: Carrafa, Eugene Michael

On 08-05-76, Det. Fred Elflein, NYCPD, O/C, contacted writer regarding their recovery, at 1915 hours on 08-04-76, of the body of a W/M, shot numerous times in the head and strangely (STRANGL) wrapped in chains. Said body had identification papers of Carrafa and Knol (ph.); the body rose to the surface near buoy #4, Great Kills Harbor, off Staten Island. It is believed that the body is that of Carrafa.

F.B.I. was contacted by writer and is forwarding prints on Carrafa to writer. Carrafa has no record, served in the Marine Corps, and was born in Waterbury, Conn.

A check of Gino's Classical Cars through the State Department of Assessments and Taxation reveals that it was incorporated on 11-18-74 by Stanley N. Tashoff, Esq.
