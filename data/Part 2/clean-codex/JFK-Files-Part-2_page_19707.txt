Report of: Date: JERRY M. GRAVES. Field Office File No.: 12/13/72. Title: 100-55290. Office: Washington, D.C. Bureau File No.: 100-472144.

“Furies” Newspaper. Women’s Liberation Movement publication, Washington, D.C.

Character: Internal Security—Revolutionary Activities.

Synopsis:

“Furies” Newspaper, self-described as a lesbian/feminist publication, was published by a group of individuals located at 219 11th Street, S.E., WDC. The reported purpose of the newspaper was to espouse the cause of radical lesbianism and to align itself politically with the views of the “New Left.” The paper was published from January through August 1972 and did not espouse any radical revolutionary or anti-government themes. Background and activities set out.

Details: At Washington, D.C.

I. Predication and Background

Investigation to determine the extent of activities was initiated regarding the “Furies” Newspaper in January 1972, after the following information was received:

On January 4, 1972, WF T-1 advised that a new publication known as the “Furies” Newspaper was being formed in Washington, D.C., to espouse the cause of radical lesbianism and to align itself politically with the views of the “New Left.” The source advised that a trial issue was scheduled to be published and distributed during January 1972; no specific date was given.
