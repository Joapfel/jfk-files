With regard to meetings in 1962 and 1963, FRIPPEL (S) said he visited the USSR in February 1962, and went to Odessa for one day where he was met by “NIKOLAEV” and “GRIBOV.” At that time they asked if any American Naval personnel were aboard the “Olympia,” Greek vessel on which he was traveling. Again in the summer of 1962 he accompanied a group of newspaper editors to the USSR, at which time he was contacted by “NIKOLAEV” at FRIPPEL's (S) Moscow hotel room. He next visited the USSR in February 1963, and again was approached by “NIKOLAEV” in Odessa.
