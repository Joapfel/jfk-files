Outgoing Telegram, Department of State.

February 6, 10:16 a.m., 1963.

Origin.

Action: Embassy (Arambassy), Georgetown 286.

Info: EUR, SS, G, SAL, ARA, P, USIA, SCA, NSC, INR, CIA, NSA, OSD, Army, Navy, Air, RMR.

Unclassified message referring to this message by cite number will be designated EFTO.

The February 8 edition of LIFE contains a story on Cuba with the following caption under a photo of a Russian freighter:

“But refugees aboard the Shirley Lykes said two ships put out from Mariel (Cuba) only last week with holds loaded with weapons sent by Castro to British Guiana.”

Would appreciate any available comment.

Rusk.

Action: State (Army ACSI). Info: DCSOP, OSA, JCS. DA IN 827722.

Drafted by: EUR, DNA:RTenner.cjk. Telegraphic transmission and classification approved by: TNA V. Clearances.
