On 7/13/69, a person who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that, to date, the following Chicago Panthers either have departed or plan to depart for the above conference:

Jewel Cook, field secretary and acting deputy chairman, State Offices, Illinois Black Panther Party (BPP).

Willie James Clavin, aka Jew Man, captain of defense, Chicago Chapter, Illinois BPP, who will temporarily occupy deputy minister of defense, State Office, if Bobby Rush is incarcerated regarding pending local appeals.

Eugene Charles, Jr., lieutenant of information, Chicago Chapter, Illinois BPP.

Michael McCarthy, lieutenant of education, Chicago Chapter, Illinois BPP, approximately 19 years old, 5'11", 155 pounds, dark complexion, black natural-style hair, and brown eyes.

Joan Gray, field lieutenant, Chicago Chapter, Illinois BPP, described as about 19 years old, 5'6", 135 pounds, with black hair and brown eyes; disposition very arrogant.

Rufus Charles Walls, aka Chaka, deputy minister of information, State Office, Illinois BPP.

Hank Gaddis, aka Poison, field lieutenant, Chicago Chapter, Illinois BPP, described as approximately 18 years old, 5'9", 160 pounds, with natural-style black hair and brown eyes, which appears to have been pushed in; complexion light skin.

Stephannie Fisher, secretary on information staff, Chicago Chapter, Illinois BPP, described as approximately 20 years old, 5'11", 130 pounds, looks similar to a bear.
