Memorandum for: Mr. Colby.

Attached is the material we requested of Dick Ober:

A. Ten reports, subject: Foreign support for activities planned to disrupt or harass the Republican National Convention.

B. Five reports, subject: Foreign support for activities planned to disrupt or harass the Democratic National Convention.

C. Two memoranda regarding Agency support to Secret Service for Democratic and Republican Conventions.

Ober advises that the only American we reported on to the IEC is Rennie Davis. BE (1–1 May 73).
