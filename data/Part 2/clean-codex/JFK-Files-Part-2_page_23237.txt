Mr. Baron. Did the Chief of Station in the Congo attend the meeting in Rome?

Mr. Tweedy. I am sure he would not. In other words, if he saw Bissell, he would have merely come to Rome and seen him, but he would have had no reason whatsoever. In fact, it would have been ridiculous for Bissell to invite a man who didn't belong in the North African meetings, and that merely raised the question whether Hedgman was there.
