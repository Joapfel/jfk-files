Jannastur Noel. 511 Alban Towers, 3700 Massachusetts Avenue, N.W., Washington, D.C. Birth: March 19, 1911.

Application for Federal Employment.

Date of employment: January 1931 to October 1938. Exact title of position: Vice Consul. Salary: starting, $1,600 per annum; final, $2,000 per annum.

Place of employment: Ensenada, Mexico. Immediate supervisor: Mr. A. Smale (Mm. A. Smale), Consul.

Name and address of employer: State Department, Foreign Service. Reason for leaving: transfer to Mazatlan (Hazatlan).

Number and kind of employees supervised: Two consular clerks.

Description of work: General consular work, including shipping services, visas, passports, welfare and protection, agricultural reporting, and drafting replies to trade inquiries.
