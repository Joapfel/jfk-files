Women's National March on the Pentagon, April 10, 1971; IS-MISC.

On April 5, instant, a source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that, concerning the captioned (CAPTINED) march, fourteen buses will depart (PART) NYC for WDC at about 6:00 AM on April 10, next, with about 630 passengers for the march. The buses chartered are Crown Coach, Coast Cities Cruisers, Inc., Community Coach Lines, and Garden State (GARDEN STATED) Transit Lines. The buses will proceed directly to the Justice Department, WDC, where they will unload. The empty buses will then drive to the Pentagon, where they will wait for the end of the march. All will return to New York with their passengers on the night of April 10, next. ST-109 REC-66.
