WFO (100-54927).

From Baltimore (100-29231) RUC.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAC), sponsored demonstration, November 20, 1971, Washington, D.C. IS-New Left (OO: WFO).

On November 19, 1971, a source reliable in the past advised that the Women's Right to Choose of Maryland, 3028 Greenmount Avenue, Baltimore, Maryland, had hoped to charter a bus to transport participants to the captioned rally, but lack of interest makes use of a bus doubtful.

A second source, reliable in the past, stated that representatives of Women's Liberation had attempted to solicit support for the captioned rally among city collectives with little success.

A third source, reliable in the past, advised that there was little enthusiasm for the captioned rally among Baltimore-area residents, and few participants were expected to attend from the Baltimore area.

Administrative: First source is BA 1235-S. Second source is BA 2036-PS. Third source is CS BA 1820-S. No LHM follows. REC-36 100-46 8906.
