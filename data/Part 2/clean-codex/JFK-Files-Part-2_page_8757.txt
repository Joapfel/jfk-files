Operational Guidance 19-126-4.

Memo to C/WH/4 from Dave Phillips, dated 2 July 1960.

Concerns propaganda action to be taken against the visit of Nikita Khrushchev (KHRUSHCHEV) to Cuba regarding the 25th of July celebration, including a rumor of an attempted assassination against Khrushchev. Speculation on the assassination attempts was to be surfaced on the WRUL program, in AMPACA's paper, etc.

Memo to the Director of Central Intelligence from C/WHD, dated 5 July 1960.

In an effort to prevent or counteract Khrushchev's (KHRUSCHEV's) visit to Cuba, headquarters proposed propaganda action to instigate a rumor or rumors that Khrushchev's life would be in danger while in Cuba, from a well-planned assassination attempt. This would be propagated from other Latin American countries.

Director 84642, dated 22 November 1963.

Propaganda guidance to WAVE Station for media assets. Requested they use tape excerpts of appropriate portions of JNC Directive.
