II. Communist Party (CP) and Related Activities

A. Communist Party

When contacted in March 1965, confidential informants conversant with various phases of CP activities in the area of San Francisco in which Subject resides were unable to furnish any information reflecting that Subject had been active in the CP during the past year.

Subject, however, attended the annual May Day meeting sponsored by the San Francisco Free Speech Forum, held at 150 Golden Gate Avenue, San Francisco, on May 1, 1965.

The San Francisco Free Speech Forum is a name used by the San Francisco County CP to sponsor public meetings.

The principal speaker at this meeting was Gus Hall, General Secretary of the CP, USA. Hall accused the administration of President Johnson of being dangerous and trigger-happy, stating that our foreign policy is a cold-blooded war machine which would turn back history. Hall stated that Marines had been sent to the Dominican Republic on the pretext of protecting American lives. He alluded to U.S. foreign policy as being imperialistic and said that the President was not receiving full support for his invasion of other countries and that the U.S. would never be successful in these undertakings.
