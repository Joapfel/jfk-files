United States Government

Memorandum

To: J. B. Adams

Date: April 23, 1975

From: Legal Counsel

Subject: United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities

By memorandum dated April 18, 1975, we advised the Attorney General that one document, a memorandum dated May 20, 1954, captioned “Microphone Surveillance,” requested by the captioned Committee originated with the Department, and the determination as to whether or not it should be released to the Committee should be made by the Department. It was also pointed out to the Department that this particular document, while it is classified, had been printed publicly by the United States Government Printing Office.

On April 22, 1975, Jack Fuller, Assistant to the Attorney General, telephonically advised that the Department was declassifying the document and requested that the Bureau declassify its copy.

Document declassified 4/28/75 by T. E. Burns per instruction.

Action:

Intelligence Division declassify Departmental Memorandum captioned “Microphone Surveillance,” dated May 20, 1954.
