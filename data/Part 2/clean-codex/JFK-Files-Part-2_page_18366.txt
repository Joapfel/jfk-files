To: Director (62-116395). Atlanta (62-2854).

Attention: INTD, W. O. Gregar. From: Knoxville (62-1109).

SENSTUDY 75.

Re: Bureau NITEL, September 17, 1975, and Atlanta TEL, September 18, 1975.

Former SA Carl E. Claiborne (Clairborne) was contacted this afternoon and advised of information set forth in referenced TEL. Mr. Claiborne's initial reaction was quite strong and he indicated that if contacted by the Senate Select Committee staff for interview he would hesitate to tell them anything. He stated that his posture in this regard was based on the fact that he did not want to tell them anything that would hurt the Bureau. He indicated that his feeling at this time was that he would not submit to interview unless subpoenaed; however, Mr. Claiborne stated that if he was contacted by the Committee staff he would immediately advise me and thereafter contact the Legal Counsel Division of the Bureau for further advice. End.

Federal Bureau of Investigation. Communications Section. Personnel Records Unit.
