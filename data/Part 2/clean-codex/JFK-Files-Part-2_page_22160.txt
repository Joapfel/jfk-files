Persons/Entities: Carter, Marshall; Kamchatka; UFORELY. Dates: 9/20/62. Categories: Special Group. Occurrence: Special Group meeting on 20 September, the proposed use of FORELY against Kamchatka was disapproved. The A-DCI raised the possibility of using this vessel against Cuba, but no decision was made, although the Group appeared favorably inclined.

Source: Memorandum by Carter, 9/21/62 (02637).

Staff: Wallach. Date: 7/2/75.
