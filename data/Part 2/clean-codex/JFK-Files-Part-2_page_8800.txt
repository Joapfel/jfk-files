The American was described as white, male, about 45 years old, 5 ft. 10 inches, 180 lbs., well-dressed, businessman type, who spoke a little Spanish and obviously had been in Havana at one time. The American indicated that he was from Chicago.

WAVE 1013 (IN 78067), dated 11 July 1963. Concerns activities of the Cuban Committee for Liberation (CCL), sparked by Guillermo ALONSO Pujol on 21 May 1963. The Agency learned from DGI defector SLIPSTREAM-1 in November 1971 that ALONSO was a Cuban intelligence agent. The cable goes on to state that Enrique RUIZ Williams had apparently been inactive, at least in Miami circles, since denying via the local press on 22 May that he was hand-picked by Attorney General Kennedy to unify anti-Castro exiles.

CSDB 3/656,611, dated 13 September 1963. Indicates that SIERRA’s actions relating to the Cuban Committee for Liberation would lead to chaos and anarchy.
