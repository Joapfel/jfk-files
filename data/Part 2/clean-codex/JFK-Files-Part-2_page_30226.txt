About 4:05 p.m. on June 23, 1978, Mark Flanagan, upon returning some autopsy material to the security room along with Lance Svendson, noted that several autopsy photos were lying loosely on top of the other books. The photos removed were 39E1, 39E2, 40E, and 41E. This consisted of two pages containing four photos. Photos 39E1 and 39E2 had been ripped from the book. One photo, 39E2, was out of the cellophane enclosure and lying loose.

A quick check showed that these photos were from Book Five, Part One. An immediate check of all the books was begun by Flanagan and Svendson to ascertain if any other photos might be missing. This check is still in progress.

Book Five has not been signed out recently.

Check completed by Mark and Lance. Nothing found missing.

The persons who were in this room on June 23, 1978, were Edwin Lopez, Dan Hardway, Mitch Mars, and Regis Blahut, CIA.

Both Mark and Lance feel there is no way this could have been done accidentally. Someone had to go into the safe and do this.
