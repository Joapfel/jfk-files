Memorandum for: Office, Secretary of the Army

Attention: Mr. Joseph Califano, General Counsel

Subject: Training of Cuban Refugees in Nicaragua

1. References:

A. Intelligence Memorandum, 19 November 1963, above subject, to Office, Secretary of the Army.

B. Intelligence Report No. 2870006563, 9 August 1963, subject: Prominent Cuban Exiled Leaders Visit Nicaragua, from USARMA, Nicaragua.

C. Intelligence Report No. 2870009363, 31 October 1963, subject: Puerto Cabanas and Cuban Exiles, from USARMA, Nicaragua.

D. Summary of Information, 1 November 1963, 112th INTC Group, subject: Cuban Officer Training Program.

E. Memorandum for Record, ACSL-CX, 19 November 1963, subject: Cuban Brigade Member.

2. Reference 1n, prepared at the request of your office, advised that a query by USARMA, Managua, within the U.S. Embassy there, revealed no indication that Colonel Manuel Fernandez D. McPhail, 0-30613, U.S. Army retired, the former Chief of the U.S. Army Mission, was in any way involved in the training of Cuban refugees in Nicaragua.

3. During the above inquiry, USARMA, Managua, called attention to two intelligence reports he had furnished (references 1b and 1c above), reporting Nicaraguan press comment on the visit of exiled Cuban refugee leaders Dr. Manuel ARTUÑO Buena, Dr. Carlos PRIO Socarras, Dr. Manuel Antonio DE VÁRIONA, Dr. Francisco FERNANDEZ Pla, Dr. Laureano BATISTA (B&TISTA), Dr. Orlando PUENYES, Sr., Miguel DR LEON, and Roberto RODRIGUEZ to Nicaragua during July and August 1963. USARMA’s comment on the newspaper speculation and rumors concerning this visit was that he “does not have any firm information or indicators that anti-Castro forces are being trained in Nicaragua.”
