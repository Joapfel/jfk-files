CUMER discussed the unemployment situation in the United States and said unemployment was increasing because of automation. He said the unions are worried and noted that recent strikes were for job security rather than increased benefits. He said that if the United States had a planned economy and traded with Soviet nations, most of the ills of unemployment could be cured. He also spoke briefly on repressive government acts and union-busting legislation. He said the government is applying antitrust provisions to unions and there is a vicious prosecution of labor leaders. He said these acts are the products of the Cold War and reaction.

A resolution to the President and the Attorney General was passed at this meeting, requesting that the government take action against the Birmingham Police Department for police brutality. At the suggestion of IRVING FROMER, they included a condemnation of the government for past inactivity regarding the resolution.
