File: April 3, 1964–May 20, 1964

(19) April 7, 1964: Meeting of DCI, President, and others on Cuba covert action. Here DCI advocates strong program to obtain overall objective set forth in a paper of June 8, 1963: “The ultimate objective of this policy would be to encourage dissident elements in the military and other power centers of the regime to bring about the eventual liquidation of the Castro/communist entourage and the elimination of the Soviet presence from Cuba.” (Emphasis added.)
