Dispatch. To: Chief, Finance Division; Chief, P. A20328. From: Chief of Station, Leopoldville (Leopolandille). Dated 27 July 1961. Reference: VIROQUE; Finance/VIROQUE; Reference of Accountability to Dept. 8, Kayseri. Marked for incoming. No incoming received. Dropping can be added by qualified No. deck only. Action due: Credit account amount.

1. On 7 July 1961 VIROQUE was advanced the sum of U.S. $1,100.00 for living expenses in Greenville while auditing current transportation to PHILIP. NIB-4928 requested VIROQUE return to PHILIP. This is his only outstanding advance with Greenville Station. Receipt attached hereto.

2. NIB-4928 authorized Station to withhold money received from sale of personal car against outstanding advances. However, before his departure VIROQUE accounted for 11,500 BCP over what he paid the Station. The sum of 82,500 BCP from the sale of his enterranno plus 11,500 BCP accounted at $0/$1.00 (LIEAP-1599 and NIB-4928) gave VIROQUE a credit of $1,361.60. In January 1961 VIROQUE was cabled to Greenville by the Station and was authorized $10.10 per mile for use of his personal car; therefore, 12.5 miles times $0.12 equals $31.99, leaving VIROQUE a credit of U.S. $1,112.88 (see attachment).

3. VIROQUE was paid his June salary of $500 on 26 June and also his housing for July ($300.00). The Station was notified on 1 July, NIB-4928, that VIROQUE was to return to PHILIP. However, July rental was used in the final settlement of the lease for the usual charges claimed by local landlords and in lieu of 30 days’ termination notice. Receipt forwarded under Vouchers 1159.

4. Accountability in the amount of U.S. $100.00 is herewith transferred to Headquarters plus a credit of $1,112.85. As VIROQUE was artificially told he would have to account for the above $100.00 for living/traveling expenses, Station did not subtract that amount from his credits.

Angela F. Deane

18 July 1961

Attachments: Receipt for $100, h/w; Greenville certification by Guthrie, h/w; Conulhatville estimate, h/w; Breakdown of advances and accounting, h/w.

Distribution: Eas-B, w/a n/c.
