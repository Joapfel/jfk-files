The rally concluded at approximately four p.m., at which time the crowd had diminished to about three hundred.

A source, reliable in the past, advised there were representatives of the New York Spartacist League, the Progressive Labor Party, and Kalamazoo Women's Liberation in attendance at the rally. Source added that an announcement from the speakers' platform proclaimed WONAAC was fifteen thousand dollars in debt and asked for contributions.

Shortly after one-thirty p.m., an anti-abortion group of about one hundred fifty to two hundred persons arrived at the east side of the U.S. Capitol carrying placards reading, “The right to happiness for unborn babies,” “Love life, same babies,” and “To mell with Della.”

This group opposing the captioned rally demonstrated peacefully and departed the area at about two-ten p.m.
