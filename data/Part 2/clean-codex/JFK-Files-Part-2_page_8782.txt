GNVA 5006 (OUT 54584), 28 Mar 1963 (TDCSDB 3/653,939).

Filed 68-6-147/4.

At the height of the October 1962 Cuban crisis, CASTRO harangued a Soviet commander about the “impossibility” of permitting U.S. planes to photograph Cuba. The Soviet succumbed to CASTRO’s persuasiveness and took action which resulted in shooting down a U-2 plane. The Soviet command was furious, and the Soviet officer was returned to the USSR under arrest.

GNVA 6022 (IN 79271), 12 Dec 1963. Filed 68-6-147/3.

Report that there was no speculation among orbit or Soviet officials regarding implications of Johnson’s accession. There was a special alert status ordered for Cuban missions as a result of the assassination of President Kennedy. The alert was still in effect 9 December 1963.
