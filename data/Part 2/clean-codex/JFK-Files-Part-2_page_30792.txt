Kos: Yes.

Mir: Here it was arriving or the other trip?

Kos: Yes.

Mir: I called to tell you the following: regarding that matter that we had talked about, to see if we would spend Saturday in Chapultepec Park because my wife is preparing some food to eat there.

Kos: I'm sorry, but I've just made plans for another trip and am leaving this very day. So please forgive me for not being able to go with you. (At this point Kostilov is erroneously addressed by his name as agent, and Mir corrects him.)

Kos: Have you already talked with Nectiporenko?

Mir: I'd like to talk with Pover gov.

Kos: Wait a minute, he'll come now.

Mir: If he's busy I'll call you later, but I'll be available for the next.
