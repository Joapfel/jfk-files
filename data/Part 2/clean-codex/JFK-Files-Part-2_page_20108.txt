Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) conference, 15 and 16 July 1972, Hunter College, NYC. IS-C (Trotskyist).

RENYTEL, 15 July 1972.

A confidential source advised that the late second session of the third WNAAC conference convened at 9:00 a.m. at Hunter College, NYC. Source stated morning session, 9:00–11:00 a.m., included organizational workshops not covered at the 15 July session, such as “press work,” “fund raising,” and “strengthening of local coalitions.” Agenda from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., termination of conference, was scheduled as a plenary session whereby all work performed at the two-day conference was expected to culminate in a unified national women's campaign set of proposals favorable to WNAAC. REC-71.

Source stated, however, that a split developed in the ranks of WNAAC between the Socialist Workers Party (SWP) faction, which had predominated in controlling the conference, and Independence—all others representing the non-SWP faction. Source stated at the end of page one.

56, 21 July 1972.
