Free Busing Program to Prison

With the majority of the country's cars and other motor vehicles being manufactured in Detroit, many people in this state of Michigan still can't get to the prisons to visit their loved ones.

Realizing the need for transportation, the Black Panther Party initiated a “Free Busing Program” for families and friends of prisoners. In many of our chapters and branches, such programs are already underway, serving the community.

We, the Black Panther Party, would like to continue to serve you on a consistent basis to the prison. On Sunday, April 16, 1972, the bus will be leaving for those who are visiting regular inmates, and the following Sunday, April 23, 1972, will be the next trip for those who are visiting trustee (only).

The bus will be leaving at 9:00 a.m. and will return by 6:00 p.m. It is important that you arrive at 157 Collinswood at 8:00 a.m. for early seating arrangements.

Necessary items of information needed to visit inmates in Jackson State Penitentiary are:

(1) Inmate's name and I.D. number.

(2) Whether he is a trustee or not.

(3) Your name, address, autocode, and phone number.

(4) You must be on inmate's visiting list. If not, write inmate or call 867-7742 or 43, to obtain methods of getting on inmate's visiting list.

(5) Your relationship to the inmate (innate).
