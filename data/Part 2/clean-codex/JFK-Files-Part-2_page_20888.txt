Re New York telephone call to Washington Field Office, instant date.

Re New York teletype dated 4-8-71 and New York airtel and LHM dated 4-5-71.

Washington Field Office telephonically advised of above. New York 7395 PSI advised 4-8-71 that no buses would depart from Bronx, New York, for captioned march.

SAS observing departure of buses were Albert E. Faller, Reno F. Walker, Alan J. Ouimet, and William H. Batts.

No LHM being submitted.
