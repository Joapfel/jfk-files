Job description: Chief of Station at Barcelona. Administration of office. Preparation of all administrative and intelligence reports emanating from Barcelona office. Direction of Barcelona chains. Direction of Bilbao chains with contact maintained by occasional visit to that area. Processing of Bilbao material. Preparation on behalf of the Consulate General, Barcelona, of political reports; of reports on military activity on behalf of the Military Attaché, Madrid, and of weekly report on movement of naval vessels on behalf of the Naval Attaché, Madrid (through the Consulate General). Cooperation with the Joint British-American Trusteeship (ACC), Madrid, on “safe haven” cases. Liaison with British I.S.

Approved: William L. Shipp (Chief of Mission). Madrid Office (Field), date 20.10.46 (Administrative Officer). U.S. Office, date 13 Nov (Branch Chief). (Security Officer). (Special Funds Officer). Personnel Review Committee, date 19 Mar (Special Funds Officer).
