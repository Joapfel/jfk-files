Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC)-sponsored demonstration, November 20, 1971, Washington, D.C.; New Left (WFO).

On November 19, 1971, a source reliable in the past advised that the Women's Right to Choose of Maryland, 3028 Greenmount Ave., Baltimore, Maryland, had hoped to charter a bus to transport participants to the captioned rally, but lack of interest made use of a bus doubtful.

A second source, reliable in the past, stated that representatives of Women's Liberation had attempted to solicit support for the captioned rally among city collectives with little success.

A third source, reliable in the past, advised that in an eight-inch and five-mile distance area, participants were expected to attend from the Baltimore area.
