To: Acting Director, FBI (100-472144)

Date: 1/31/73

From: SAC, WFO (100-55290) (P)

Subject: “Furies” Newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement Publication, Washington, D.C.

IS-RA  
(00: WFO)

Re WFO report of SA Jerry M. Graves, dated 12/13/72.

On 12/27/72, informant advised that some of the internal conflicts within the “Furies” organization had been settled, but there are no plans to reinstitute publication of the paper. Source stated that most remaining members are writing articles for other lesbian-oriented publications and periodicals.

Mrs. Jewel Johnson, 1117 Anacostia Avenue, S.E., Washington, D.C. (D.G.) (WDC), (protect identity), owner of property at 219 11th Street, S.E., WDC, advised on 1/16/73 that apparently this dwelling is being occupied by the same group of women who rented it, since the rent continues to be paid.

She added, however, that about 6 weeks ago, she attempted to inspect the premises and found no one present at that time.

She has driven by the residence several times at night since then, and has not noted any lights on in the house, nor have several attempted phone calls been answered.

Baltimore and New York furnish results of previously requested investigation.

WFO will continue to follow activities of “Furies” members.
