Five buses returned empty to garage for lack of passengers.

Observed among group was Linda Sue Evans, Weatherman, wearing a short dark-brown leather jacket, multicolored dress over blue dungarees with red slash at bottom. Evans has short blond hair and is wearing large earrings.

On instant date at 7:25 a.m., SAS observed six buses departing Ninetieth Street and Amsterdam Avenue, New York City, with approximately 250 passengers, the majority being white and under twenty-five. Buses included those numbered 666, 676, and 675 of Coach Cities Cruisers, Inc.; number 74 of Transit Charter Service; and numbers 43 and 82 of Garden State Transit Lines, all New Jersey charter buses.

All Weatherman are to be considered armed and dangerous.
