a. Missing Materials

The documentation and elaboration of the first reason can be found in Sections II.A. and B. preceding. Simply put, this Committee has not seen all of the photographs produced by the photosurveillance operations in Mexico City. Hence, it cannot conclude that a photograph of Oswald does not exist among those photographs it has not seen.
