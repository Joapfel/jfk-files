8 June 1966

Excerpt from HMM 29157.

Paragraph 32: “ROWTON is off and running on his development of LIRINGS 3 and 4. He is doing this under false flag as a Greek businessman. Again, the ubiquitous LIAR-1 is in a position to monitor this operation as he is close to both LIRINGS and has no connection with ROWTON. On 27 May LIRING-3, who is well connected in PERUMEN, flew to PERUMEN on a trip to scout out business opportunities for ROWTON. LIRING-3 continues to work at LIRING.”
