The purpose of the march was to gain support for legalized abortion and to protest existing abortion laws.

The source advised that leaders of the march included Eloise Chase, Sharon Hahn, and Barbara Chis, all members of the Washington, D.C., branch of the Socialist Workers Party (SWP).

SWP has been designated by Executive Order 10450.

Woman is influenced by SWP and Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) members. YSA is the youth affiliate of SWP.

On May 6, representatives of the Federal Bureau of Investigation observed approximately 50 to 75 individuals, mostly females, proceed to the United States Capitol in a candlelight procession. The procession lasted from approximately 9:13 p.m. (beginning and ending at the place of a resolution) to 10:15 p.m. and was highlighted by the placing of a cardboard coffin on the staircase of the east side of the Capitol Building to symbolize the death of 10,000 women from illegal abortions performed in the United States during 1970.
