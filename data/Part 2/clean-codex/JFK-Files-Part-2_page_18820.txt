FBI. Date: 8/28/58.

To: Director, FBI. From: SAC, New York (134-3315).

Subject: Changed. Samuel Adason Jaffe, also known as Sam A. Jaffe. PSI (DESECO).

Re New York letter, 8/19/58, captioned “Sam A. Jaffe; PSI (DESECO).”

The title of this case has been changed to reflect subject's full name as obtained in a review of case entitled “Samuel Adason Jaffe; IS-R,” file 105-28333, subject of which is identical with subject in instant matter. File review reflects that Jaffe has been known to the Bureau as a journalist in contact with Soviets since 1954, both in Washington, D.C., and New York, N.Y. Contacts appeared to be legitimate. Jaffe travels extensively and has traveled to Russia.

Jaffe voluntarily contacted SF on 12/29/55, and was interviewed there regarding his association with Soviets, and proved cooperative.

This office still desires to conduct a “sounding out” interview with Jaffe based on his telephone call to this office on 7/28/58, regarding his dinner engagement with two Soviet nationals. Permission for this interview requested in reletter 8/19/58.

Foster.

Approved: Special Agent in Charge.
