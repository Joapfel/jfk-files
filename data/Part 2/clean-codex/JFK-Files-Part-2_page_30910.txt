Review of Manuel Ray y Rivero Files  
Volume VI of XVIII

4. Cable to Director from JMWAVE, dated May 27, 1962, discusses front-page article in the Miami Herald of this date by James Buchanan entitled “Miro Fades as Exile Chief: Three Latin Leaders Behind Ray.” States that Ray has the backing of Gov. Muñoz (Monoz) Marín, Pres. Rómulo (Romulo) Betancourt, and former President José Figueres (Figueras), and that Ray has taken over the post with the blessings of the U.S. Further states Ray’s appointment as “Chief in Exile” came from underground in Cuba, according to Joaquín Godoy, Provisional MRP head in Miami. Article credits Cubans in Miami with saying the “new man in the field” is Adolf A. Berle, Jr., who has been special assistant to the State Department and is also a member of Pres. Kennedy’s special task force on Latin (Lantin) America. Implication being that Berle is running a new U.S.-sponsored Cuban effort. Release causing furor in local Cuban colony.

Cable to JMWAVE from Director, 28 May 1962, states State Department spokesman will state story is without foundation at a noon press briefing this date.

5. Listing of principal persons in Puerto Rico with the MRP: Manuel Ray; J. Miguel (Migues) Portela; Felipe Pazos; Claudio Carmona; (Col.) Ramón (Ramon) Barquín; Jerónimo (Jeromino) Estevez; Juan Meléndez; Bergnus; Gabriel Gelabert Alvarez; Rodolfo Nodal; Sara Portela.

6. On 9 June 1961, Ray transmits (transmitts) a detailed study of a general program designed to attain the liberation of Cuba. General thesis states that MRP believes that the strongest position for representation and canalization of the aspirations of the large majority of the Cuban people is offered by the set of ideas and aims of the democratic left.
