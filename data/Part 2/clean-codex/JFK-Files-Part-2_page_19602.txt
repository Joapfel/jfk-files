Organization is known as “April Ten Women’s Committee, Six Two Seven Amsterdam Avenue, New York, New York”; Kaplan is a representative of the organization, and Sylvia Warren, Three Seven Five Riverside Drive, Thirteen E, New York, New York, was the New York representative.

Application further reflects the demonstration will take place on April Ten, Nineteen Seventy One, with a five thousand maximum number of participants. Women participants in the demonstration will assemble on the grassy area between Seventh and Ninth Streets and Constitution and Madison Drive, Southwest, WDC, from eleven AM to twelve noon, during which one unnamed person will speak. The group, from noon to three PM, will march west along Constitution Avenue to Twenty-Third Street, south on Twenty-Third to Lincoln Memorial and across Memorial Bridge.
