Chicago, Illinois

January 24, 1969

Berry Lewin.

During September and October 1968, furnished numerous three by five cards bearing names and addresses. Source advised that these cards were in the possession of Michael Kenneth Klonsky, self-admitted National Secretary of the Students for a Democratic Society (SDS). One such card sets forth the following information:

“Barry Lewin, 275-6937, Med. relief group, Mayor’s office—filmed interview to show (marked out) our side of demos last week. Is straight and doesn’t want to mess up if he does it. Wants to know best stuff to say.”

A characterization of the SDS is attached.

On January 16, 1969, Lewin, a white male, is planning to depart Chicago at 3:00 P.M. on January 17, 1969, in a 1968 Ford, green Galaxie 500, license unknown, en route to Washington, D.C., for the Inauguration ceremonies on January 20, 1969. Lewin may be accompanied by three or four unidentified individuals.

This source stated that Lewin is a chemist employed by the Hoffman La Roche Pharmaceutical.
