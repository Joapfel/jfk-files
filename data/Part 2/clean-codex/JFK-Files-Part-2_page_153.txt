1. Reference is made to your informal request dated 26 April 1967, 11:38, (AA) A03-1004200, subject as above, and to this Office's CBCI 316/01366-66 dated 25 March 1966.

2. This is to advise you that this Office no longer has contact with the source who originally furnished the information concerning Estelle SOKOLOWSKA de MADRIGAL in March 1966. The source is currently residing in the United States, New York City, and we have no objection to your contacting the source and debriefing her concerning any questions you may have concerning this case. The source has been alerted to the fact that you might contact her regarding this matter. The source is Viola June COBB, usually referred to as June COBB, who is mentioned in your informal memorandum to this office dated 1 June 1964, subject: Lyle Stuart.

3. We would appreciate being apprised of any new information concerning the SOKOLOWSKA case that is surfaced as a result of your debriefing COBB.
