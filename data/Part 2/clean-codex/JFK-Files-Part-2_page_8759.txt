These referenced documents (UFGA 15954 and WAVE 7129, IN 93714) had to do with a plot to assassinate Castro. This information was disseminated to the Special Group and other key officials. The Special Group was concerned about the matter and referred it to the Department of Justice as a matter of law enforcement.

WAVE 9168, IN 26320, dated 12 July 1964. Cable to Headquarters. AMWAX-1 (Oscar Fernandez Viega) and UC AMBER-2 (Miguel Guillermo Truyol-Gimeno) were independently interviewed by local FBI representatives concerning a plot to assassinate Castro. Both denied any knowledge of or participation in any plan to assassinate Castro. For Headquarters’ information: “Regarding AMWAX-1 and UC AMBER-2, WAVE wishes to emphasize that both sources clearly understand they have no sanctions from WAVE to directly or indirectly promote, sponsor, or support such plan or any similar plans regardless of person against whom they are directed.”
