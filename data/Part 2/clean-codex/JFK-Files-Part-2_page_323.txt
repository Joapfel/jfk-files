I 143328Z. Cite: Mexico City 9638. Director info: Guatemala City, JMWAVE. PBRUMEN, LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 9560.

1. Further debriefing of LIRING-3 by Wallace B. Rowton produced additional information regarding LIRING-3’s impending trip to Guatemala to meet Communist Party members.

2. The phone number for Agustine Hernandez is Guatemala City 20 057. Jacobo Rodriguez Padilla gave LIRING-3 this number, which was used for first contact with Agustina. After LIRING-3 returned from the Mexico (MEXI) border, Jacobo told LIRING-3 not to ask for Agustina at that number but for Juan Antonio Franco. Jacobo told LIRING-3 that Agustina worked at the Pedro Santo Printing Office but could be reached at Franco’s number when not at work.

3. No Mexico (MEXI) station traces Agustina. HMM 4340, 26 June 1959, reports that Juan Antonio Franco attended a meeting of PGT on 29 May 1959 at Manuel Maria Contreras (CONTReras) No. 43-J, Mexico City. Seventy persons attended the meeting. LIESCORT reported on 29 January 1959 that Juan Antonio Franco, a painter, was a new applicant for PGI membership.

4. LIRING-3 told Rowton that he would leave on 15 December 1966 in order to arrive in Huehuetenango in time to be in place on 18 December, per Agustina’s instructions. LRNG-3 JST.
