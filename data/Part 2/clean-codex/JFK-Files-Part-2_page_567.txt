PER UEN.

Reference A: Mexico City 4371 (0.45349). B: Director 70322. C: JMWAVE (JNVAVE) 1115 (N.42146).

1. Via LIRING-3, Rowton met Xochitl Vivo de la Torriente on 16 February 1968, daughter of Lola de la Torriente mentioned in references. Xochitl, ECRN Mexico CA 1932, is a Mexican citizen. She lived in Cuba from 1958 to 1963, where she was active as a ballerina in the modern dance group of Cuba, which traveled to many places in Europe, including Moscow. She left Cuba in 1963 for the U.S., where she worked in New York as a showgirl and secretary for several years. She is single but now living with an American and works as a theatrical artist.

2. Xochitl is thoroughly anti-Communist, is dissatisfied with her situation, and is anxious to drop her American boyfriend. She would like to get into some sort of business giving her financial independence. She is in regular mail contact with her mother in Cuba and feels her mother, who very much would like to leave Cuba, is completely anti-regime.

3. After several meetings with Xochitl, Rowton obtained her.
