260035Z. DIR WAVE cite Mexi 6677.

Fast action needed on L-2. He will not be able to meet Cisnero and have an “in” with the JURE group for the purpose of coverage of JURE PM activities in Mexico. Lanier will set up a meeting in Miami or San Juan.

10. Dispatch to Chief, Special Affairs from CS/JMWAVE, dated 21 October 1963.

Information received from AMTAUP-2.

Ray was to arrive in Miami on 26 July, in secret, because he wanted to hold private meetings to discuss military affairs with action groups in Miami. He met at the home of Rogelio Cisneros Diaz on 28 July. He met with Armando Alanis Angulo and asked him to join JURE. Alanis was former Chief of the Fiscal District of the Treasury in Camagüey Province. Ray said he had an organization inside Cuba but needed personnel on the outside with which to operate. He claimed to have 300 weapons in Miami.

On 18 August, Cisneros met with Alanis and Luis Aguiar, who belongs to the Alanis group. They talked of plans for Aguiar’s men to infiltrate Las Villas Province and open a JURE organization.

Alanis officially joined JURE on 20 August 1963 and received Cisnero’s assurances that they would get financial help from JURE.
