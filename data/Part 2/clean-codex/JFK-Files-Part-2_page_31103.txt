18 January 1963. Cos/JMWAUE. UFGA-7219.

Subject: Operational / GYROSE/AMCONCERT/1. Report on Jorge Nobregas (Nobregae) Heria.

Nobregas was jailed in La Cabanas Prison after jewel theft in 1959. Jose Aleman Jr. made arrangements through Jose Llanusa Golol. We have Jorge released.

Information Report, November 1963. TDCS DB 3/65-8,111.

Source: reliable local law enforcement officer, Miami.

As of 11/20/63, Nobregas, member of Montecristi group, had 14 M-3 submachine guns which he was offering for sale in Miami. Believed weapons represent some which were stolen at Big Pine Key at time of April 1961 invasion.

Deletions: sealed envelope.

FBI, 2 April 1963, no fed file, Washington, D.C. Subject: Anti-Castro Activity.

FBI, 13 April 1963, MM 105-1360 (Miami). Subject: DRE.
