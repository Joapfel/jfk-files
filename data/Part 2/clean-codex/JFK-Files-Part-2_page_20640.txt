The source advised that the following women are members of SWP-YSA and were appointed to the presiding committee for the purpose of chairing various sessions: Carol Lipman, Nancy Rosenstock, Delphine Welch, and Matilde Zimmerman.

The source advised that a general workshop was chaired by Matilde Zimmerman from 11:00 to 11:30 a.m., in which proposals were discussed that would be submitted to the conference for adoption by WONAC. It consisted of a group made up of approximately 50 women, each meeting in various classrooms.

The source advised that the afternoon session between 2:30 and 5:00 p.m. consisted of small groups meeting in various classrooms at Boston University, including the following workshops:

Forced Sterilization

Forced Sterilization: “Aids, Credits, Loans, etc.” is one way the U.S. medical community is dealing with the problem of birth control, especially for black women who want to remain married. The government should support this program so that more people can benefit from it. The medical authorities have to make sure that such programs are well run. This includes providing good data concerning effectiveness, side effects, and other problems.
