TYPIC LIPING

Ref A: Director 21425

B: Mexico City 7971 (IN 59535)

1. Wallace B. Rowton, in a meeting on 20 July with Carlos Jurado, determined that the statement reported in Ref B regarding the execution of prisoners was made by Abelardo Curbelo Padron in the presence of Ramiro Azreu Quintana and Jurado. The statement was made positively by Curbelo in the context of a brief discussion regarding the gravity of the situation in Viet Nam.

2. Though the fact that Curbelo made the statement rather than Abreu, who recently returned to Mexico City from Havana, tends to detract from the impact of the statement, Rowton has instructed Jurado to continue checking out the statement in the Embassy to determine the questions raised in paragraphs 2 A and B of Ref A. Jurado told Rowton he would talk with Miguel Lozano Villalobos, Embassy counselor, who, according to Jurado, is a much more reliable source and more aware of the situation in Havana than either Curbelo or Abreu.
