Results of examination:

The bathroom door of the first-level apartment at 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago, Illinois, exhibited a natural or varnish finish and bears many paint spatters deposited as a result of painting of surrounding areas.

The door and door frames are very old and show physical signs of having been salvaged from other use. Some of the individual pieces of the door frame appear to have originated from other sources, based upon the paint colors and layer structures present or absent on each part.

An examination of specimens K53, K59, K60, and Q153, Q154, Q155, Q156, and Q157 from the hinge components and door.
