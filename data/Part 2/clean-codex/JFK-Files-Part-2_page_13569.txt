4-0-76

Legal Control

Select Committee of Intelligence Activities

During various contacts with William Miller, acting Director of the State Select Committee, and Chairman Leona Reis (D-district), Chairman of the State Select Committee, the question has come up regarding the area in a case of the private office of individual and committee members, to ensure the absence of any electronic listening devices. The Committee personnel have been told that we consider this absolutely essential and they agree.

In exploring this matter, it was determined that the Capitol Police Department recently established a special unit to handle all types of security problems within the Capitol and its complex, including checks for electronic listening devices. Personnel of this unit have been selected from outside training. The operations of the unit are controlled through the persons of the Army and the Senate.

This matter has been discussed with Robert Song, Deputy Sheriff at Arms of the Exchange, and with everyone at Arms of the House. House advised that the special unit already has conducted security surveys of the new office space of the Select Committee and certainly will perform any other tasks of this type needed by the Committee or its individual members. He said, in fact, that since they now have this copier by itself, it would cause any outside agency coming on to the Hill to perform the task. While this is already being done in contract data auditing regarding the newspaper security checkers, this is still not far from where our duties of the State Select Committee will be next year's checkers. He said their duties require that the new Select Committee meet again in writing.
