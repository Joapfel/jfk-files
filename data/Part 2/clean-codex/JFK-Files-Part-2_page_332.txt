3. LIRING-3 returned to Mexico City on 6 December and recontacted JACOBO, reporting what transpired. JACOBO explained the delay in meeting as natural in view of the security precautions necessary under present conditions and surmised that the Communist official would have to travel to Quetzaltenango (QUEZALTENANGO) from the mountains. JACOBO said he had no idea who the representative would be but hoped he would be from the TURCIO guerrilla band. JACOBO said TURCIO had been replaced by CESAR MONTES, an old friend of JACOBO. JACOBO promised to give LIRING-3 a letter to MONTES, which LIRING-3 could present to the person sent by HERNANDEZ. The letter would ask that LIRING-3 be taken to MONTES.

4. LIRING-3 plans to proceed to Quetzaltenango (QUEZALTENANGO) on 15 December. Per ROWTON instructions, he will inform the Communist representative or MONTES that he has developed excellent commercial contacts in Guatemala City, has discussed general plans of aid with OSMANI CIENFUEGOS, and wants to help the revolution by exploiting unwitting commercial contacts to pass arms, etc. ROWTON intends to place LIRING-3 in the ARVANITIS Metalo-Plastics industry in Guatemala for cover purposes through STYLIADOS PREVOLIS, an employee of the Greek (CREEK) Embassy in Mexico City, who is a close friend of ARVANITIS and could give LIRING-3 a letter of introduction if required.
