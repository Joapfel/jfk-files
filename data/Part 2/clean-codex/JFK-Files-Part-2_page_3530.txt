Cuban Economic Development — continued

Opposed the Second Agrarian Reform Law and is willing to use “doctrinally unwelcome” measures to increase production.

Cuba is taking further steps to improve its fishing industry, including several construction projects in the Chuyima shipyards in the Almendares River, the launching of 18 fishing boats, and the sending of an official mission to Spain in connection with the fishing industry.

Insurgency and Civil Unrest:

Counterrevolutionary elements apparently attacked an observation post on Cayo de Cristo on a recent undetermined date.

A suspected counterrevolutionary was arrested on 3 December in Guantanamo.

Cuban Foreign Service:

The Cuban ambassador to the USSR is in Havana for a short “vacation.”

Cuban Air Flight Connections:

The Soviet-made AN-12 transport plane being ferried to Cuba reached Recife, Brazil, on 5 December, and left there for its last stop before it is scheduled to arrive in Cuba on 7 December.

Shipping Support for Cuba:

The US Embassy in Beirut believes that Lebanon may act to curtail the use of its ships in the Cuban trade. This would result in a significant decline in Cuban shipping in free world bottoms.

Cuban–Chinese Communist Relations:

Wang Yu-ping has been named the new Chinese Communist ambassador to Cuba. He is a senior diplomat, but not as high-ranking an individual as the recently appointed Chinese Communist ambassadors to Algeria and the UAR.

Bloc Economic Relations:

A Bulgarian arms negotiator has returned from Havana to Sofia.
