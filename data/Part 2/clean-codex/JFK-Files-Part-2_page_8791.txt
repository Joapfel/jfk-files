Memo for the Record dated 28 January 1963 by Robert Moroney, Office of Security. Subject: Roberto Perez San Roman (201-282287).

Perez was to speak with Hurwitch of the State Department.

Perez had a dinner appointment with Robert Kennedy at Kennedy’s Palm Beach home on 26 January 1963. Kennedy was to explain in detail plans for future use of the Brigade, which emerged from a high-level meeting on Friday, 25 January 1963.

WAVE 3068 (IN 45935), dated 8 January 1963.

Concerned the 5 January 1963 meeting between Zamka (Dave Morales), CIA, and Manuel Artime for the purpose of re-recruiting Artime, debriefing him on Brigade activities, and providing guidance. Ruiz-Williams reportedly told Artime that Robert Kennedy instructed Ruiz, Oliva, and Jose Perez not to accept any money from the CRC or CIA; that Kennedy was setting aside $25,000 from Families Committee funds for Brigade leadership use for trips, expenses, etc. WAVE Station had no other information to confirm or deny this statement by Robert Kennedy to Ruiz. WAVE Station commented that within the present policy framework the CRC could engage in full-spectrum propaganda and political action activities; intelligence collection; military training of officers and enlisted men via the current DOD program; and counterinsurgency training.
