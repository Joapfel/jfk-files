United States Government

Memorandum

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Chicago (100-44775) (C)

Subject: Jeannette Rankin Brigade

Date: 4/26/68

Re my airtels and LHMs 1/4/68 and 1/17/68 captioned “Demonstration Protesting U.S. Policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January 15-16, 1968; Information Concerning (IS).”

As detailed in communications, the January demonstration was a function of the Jeannette Rankin Brigade, self-described in its literature as a broad coalition of women from many areas of American life. Chicago headquarters of the Brigade was given as c/o Georgina Gronner, 520 North Michigan Avenue, Chicago. In developing information regarding the January activity, it was determined that the Chicago coordinating committee was composed of Lucille Montgomery, Marjorie Kinsella, Carol Travis and Rosalind Durham, with travel arrangements being made by Sylvia Kushner.

Lucille Montgomery was chairman of the host committee of the convention of the National Conference for New Politics (NCNP) in September, 1967.

Carol Travis, who was secretary of a steering committee which arranged for the NCNP convention, attended a meeting of a Communist Party (CP) of Illinois Club held in Chicago February 14, 1968, according to investigation concerning Travis is pending.

Kinsella, Durham and Kushner are known CP members. No subversive information concerning Montgomery is identifiable in Chicago indices.

Captioned case was initiated to determine the extent of activity and degree of communist influence in the organization in the Chicago area.

On January 12, 1968, Intelligence Division, Chicago PD, identified Georgina Gronner as the wife of Dr. Robert Gronner, H.D., 3162 North Pine Grove Avenue, with offices at 670 North Michigan Avenue, Chicago.
