DCI/IC 75-2663

United States Senate

Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (Pursuant to S. Res. 21, 94th Congress)

Washington, D.C. 20510

July 25, 1975

Mr. Walter Elder  
Central Intelligence Agency  
Langley, Virginia

Dear Walt:

This is to confirm our telephone conversation of July 25, 1975, in which I conveyed to you the requests of the Senate Select Committee for full and complete information on the following topics:

1. Any financing by CIA, direct or indirect, of U.S.-owned, including proprietaries, domestic or foreign media, including but not limited to books, articles, publishing houses, films, TV programs, scholarly journals, magazines, and news services.

2. Full and complete information, direct or indirect, on CIA use of United States citizens who are newspaper men, journalists, movie, TV, or any other persons connected with the media in any way.

3. Full and complete information on the direct or indirect involvement of the CIA with U.S. citizens who were connected directly with religious groups, including churches and missionaries, with activities or relationships in foreign countries.

With kind regards,
