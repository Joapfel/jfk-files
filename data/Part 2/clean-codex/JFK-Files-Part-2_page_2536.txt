7 October 1977

As of this date, a full search has been made of CPD for the whereabouts of the subject's file. Our records indicate that it was charged out to Jim Hutchinson in July, probably in reference to a question raised by Terry Hutchinson concerning dependent travel. He has searched, and his secretary has searched OGC, with no success.

On 6 October, Chuck Sandman, John Rizzo, and the undersigned met with Brad Abernathy concerning Terry's questions. We all agreed that any sums expended on Bertotally's life travel should be considered taxable additional income. In regard to his question concerning approval authority for travel: Chief, CI. We also covered pay and travel of our independent contractors in conformance with government rules and regulations, and Abernathy and John R. indicated acceptance of the situation.
