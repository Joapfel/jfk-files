United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC)

Reference is made to the SSC letter, and appendices thereto, dated May 14, 1975, requesting certain documents and other information from the FBI.

Enclosed is an original and one copy of a memorandum and enclosures (Exhibits A through X) responsive to some of the requests which concern electronic coverage of Martin Luther King, Jr. by the FBI. This material was compiled prior to the receipt of the July 15, 1975, letter from Mr. K. William O'Connor of the Department to Mr. John T. Elliff of the Committee. In view of the information in paragraph four, page two of the July 15, 1975, letter concerning special considerations in the King case, we are making no recommendations concerning the furnishing of the enclosed memorandum to the Committee as this appears to be a decision for the Department to make.
