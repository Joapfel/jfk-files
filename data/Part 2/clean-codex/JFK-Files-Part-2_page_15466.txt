United States Government

Memorandum

To: W. C. Sullivan  
Date: February 4, 1963  
From: W. R. Wannall

Subject: Technical Surveillance Coverage of Diplomatic Establishments

With the approval of Attorney General (1/31/62), we instituted a program to expand our use of technical surveillances on diplomatic establishments of countries outside the Soviet bloc. Following is an analysis of this program and its current status:

We advised Attorney General on 1/26/62 that, on a selective and probing basis, we would explore coverage of the following countries under this program by instituting coverage on six or eight establishments at a time: Argentina, Brazil, Republic of the Congo, Guinea, India, Indonesia, Japan, Laos, Mali, Mexico, Morocco, Panama, Portugal, Thailand, Venezuela, and South Vietnam.

After the necessary survey and State Department clearance in each instance, we initiated initial coverage during early March 1962, and to date coverage on the following establishments has been instituted:

Embassy of Argentina.  
Embassy of Brazil.  
Brazilian Delegation to United Nations (UN).  
Congolese Delegation to UN.  
Embassy of Guinea.  
Guinea Delegation to UN.  
Embassy of India.  
Indian Delegation to UN.  
Embassy of Indonesia.  
Indonesian Delegation to UN.  
Embassy of Japan.  
Embassy of Mali.  
Embassy of Mexico.  
Mexican Delegation to UN.  
Embassy of Portugal.  
Portuguese Delegation to UN.
