April 1954 to present — Central Intelligence Agency, 2430 E Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. I have been employed with this Agency since April 1954 as a Foreign Affairs Officer. In that capacity, I have had regular, almost daily contact and liaison with representatives of the Department of Justice and Department of State on matters of a legal and political nature. My duties involve the application of knowledge and experience in the legal field, both foreign and domestic.
