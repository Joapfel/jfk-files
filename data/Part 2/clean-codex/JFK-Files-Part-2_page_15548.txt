(9/30/69)

Officer Tink, Chicago, Illinois PD, advised on October 3, 1969, that seven Negro males were arrested at BPP headquarters, 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, during the early morning hours on October 3, 1969, following a shooting incident. Those arrested were:

Jerel Hawkins (Hawins)
Bradley Green
Wade Steinney
Billy Brooks (Broks)
Morris Brown
Harry Moon
Terrance Watson

Officer Waner, Chicago PD, advised on October 1, 1969, that Officer Chanley, Chicago PD, was injured in front of BPP Headquarters by a shotgun blast fired by an unknown individual. Officer Waner stated the shotgun blast was believed to have originated from Panther Headquarters. Officer Chanley was taken to Illinois Research Hospital.
