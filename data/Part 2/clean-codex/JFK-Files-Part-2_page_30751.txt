State Cables — November–December 1977 — Visas for wife and child of Orlando Bosch.

State Cables — bombings — July 12–13, 1976.

Reminder: sealed envelopes — FBI documents.

12 February 1976 cable IN 824723. CITE: Caracas 29063 16-23 16-29.

To: Priority director, Santiago, Buenos Aires, LA/Miami. WKSARLET-3 (former unilateral asset) advises he is investigating the bombing of the Cuban ambassador in Caracas and is travelling during the week of February 15. D Santiago, Buenos Aires, and Miami.

Cuban Ambassador Norberto Hernandez Arbelo reported receiving a bomb in a large Manila envelope bearing a return address. Dr. Gustavo A. Vollmer, WKSARLET/1, former WKSARLET chief, is a security consultant. Vollmer has retained WKSARLET/3, who is operating a commercial investigation service, to determine who used Vollmer’s name. WKSARLET/3 maintains contacts with Orlando Bosch.

18 February 1976 cable IN 830506. CITE: Caracas 29129.

Frank Castro had knowledge of an attempt to assassinate Kennedy and told WKSARLET/3. In order to use the information, the Agency is afraid they will compromise WKSARLET/3.
