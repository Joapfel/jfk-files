Intelligence Information Cable. IN-60597.

16 22 02 Z. TDCS-314/15974-66. Distributed 16 December 1966. Country: Cuba/Mexico. Date of information: 14 December 1966. Subject: Cuban purchase of Mexican coffee seed.

Acquired in Mexico City, Mexico, on 14 December 1966. Field report. Source: A Mexican intellectual who is a new unwitting reporting asset. Source is in close contact and on friendly terms with Cuban officials traveling to Mexico City.

1. Ismael Suarez de la Paz has spent more than a month in Mexico on a special coffee seed buying mission for Cuban Premier Fidel Castro Ruz. Headquarters comment: Suarez arrived in Mexico on 23 October 1966, assigned as an attaché in the commercial section of the Cuban Embassy. When Suarez finally concluded negotiations for the purchase of ten tons of seed, he found upon delivery that the Mexicans had wet the seed and that moisture accounted for one ton of the weight of the shipment. This deception represented a loss of $10,000.00 in U.S. dollars to the Cubans.
