FBI, Washington, D.C. FBI, Cincinnati (CINCINTI). 11:26 p.m., urgent, January 14, 1968. To Director and WFO. From Cincinnati, file no. 100-17137.

Demonstration protesting United States policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January 5, 1968, information concerning.

An instant date, a confidential source who has furnished reliable information in the past advised that approximately forty women departed Yellow Springs, Ohio, by bus at 9 p.m. this date for the captioned demonstration, Washington, D.C., January 15 next. The bus, which resembles a Greyhound-type bus, was chartered from the Southeastern Bus Lines, Inc., Jamestown, Ohio.

Source advised that although it was snowing and road conditions were hazardous, the women were determined to reach Washington, D.C., for the captioned demonstration.

Administrative. Recitel January 13, last.

Source is Chief James A. McKee, Police Department, Yellow Springs, Ohio.
