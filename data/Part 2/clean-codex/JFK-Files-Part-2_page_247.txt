HMMA-26269 (not to SAGO).

Subject is formation of Comunidad Latinoamericana de Escritores (CLE), which was discussed during the January 1965 meeting of Communauté Européenne d’Escrivains (COMES) in Genoa. Carlos (Caplos) Pellicer (P), prominent Mexican poet and communist-liner (COMMY-LINER), one of the MEXI delegates to that meeting, agreed to push for organizing a meeting in Mexico during 1965 to form a sister organization to be called CLE. This meeting has never been called.

However, early in 1965 MEXI station agent LICOOKY-1 was an active cooperator (COOPERATCR) in organizing Asociación de Escritores Mexicanos (Mexicansos), A.C. (AEMAC), which is a service organization for writers to get better royalties, publicity, distribution, and other benefits. This organization, whose constitution specifically forbids political activities and international affiliation, elected Carlos (Caplos) Pellicer president. P has been trying numerous devices to bring the CLE organizing meeting under AEMAC auspices, but so far frustrated (FRUSTPATED) by membership.
