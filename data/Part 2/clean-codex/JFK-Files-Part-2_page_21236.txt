A subsequent passport application filed by WILL BURTIN indicated that he was in Moscow from May 20–29, 1961, for “USIA.” Inquiry at USIA disclosed that WILL BURTIN was given a contract on April 13, 1961, to design, produce and install the exhibit, “Medicine, USA,” in the Soviet Union. He was instructed, however, on October 20, 1961, to stop work on the exhibit, and travel expenses of $1600 submitted by him were disallowed.

On July 19, 1968, the Director of Security, CIA, advised that in May 1961, a component of CIA was in contact with BURTIN to discuss problems in the medical science field; however, it was not known whether his services were utilized. The Director of Security of CIA has also advised that GEORGE LUBIN was of interest to a component of CIA in 1959 when he represented his company at a fair in the USSR and in 1960 when he was again scheduled to visit the Soviet Union.
