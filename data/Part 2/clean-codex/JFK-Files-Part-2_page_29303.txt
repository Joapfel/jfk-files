Information Report. Office of Naval Intelligence. Basic-60. Date of report: 30 July 1960. Evaluation: P-3. Date of arrival: 10–21 July 1960. Personal contact. From: U.S. Naval Attaché, Cuban. Subject: Venezuela/Cuba—Request for assistance by former Cuban army officer.

This report covers the visit of two Cuban nationals to the office of the Naval Attaché.

1. Background. On 18 July, the Chief of the U.S. Naval Mission to Venezuela called the Naval Attaché and said that two Cubans had approached him in an effort to obtain assistance in getting visas to the U.S. He stated that he had directed them to the office of the Naval Attaché.

2. Interview. Shortly thereafter the two men arrived at the CNL. They identified themselves as:

Sergio Acil Smith, Cuban—Y.M.N. Address: Avenida Urbaneta #11, edificio La Pelota, N.Y. Telephone: 32 07 92.

He possessed a valid Cuban passport and stated that he was married to a Spanish subject who had a valid Spanish passport. He claimed his grandfather was an American and that he was a graduate of Columbia University. He stated he had been in the Cuban diplomatic service briefly and that he presently represented several U.S. firms in Venezuela, primarily in textiles. He was acquainted with CHAVIS from business calls he had made to Venezuelan Naval Headquarters selling uniform equipment. In showing his papers he exposed a police-type shield. When queried, he stated that it was an honorary police badge of the New York Police, given to him by Paul Taylor, whom he said was Deputy Police Commissioner, NYC, and Rolando ZUBIZARRA. Taft-Hartley REG 22.
