DE 157-3075

Edgar Robinson, Detroit BPP, advised BPP National Headquarters that he had removed himself from the staff of the Detroit Chapter.

DE T-4. October 30, 1971. Kenneth Horston is leader of the Detroit Chapter of the BPP.

DE T-1. October 27, 1971. Since the departure of Edgar Robinson, who was distribution manager for the BPP, Lonnie Darden is second in command to Kenneth Horston.

DE T-1. November 4, 1971. Lorene Banks is in charge of the Breakfast Program.

DE T-1. November 17, 1971. Lonnie Darden is referring to himself as the Minister of Information while Kenneth Horston is in California.

DE T-1. January 19, 1972. Leonard Mongo is currently in charge of finances.
