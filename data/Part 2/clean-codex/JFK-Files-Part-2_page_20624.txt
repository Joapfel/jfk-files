A second source who has provided reliable information in the past on 12/12/72 identified over thirty members of the Socialist Workers Party (SWP), Boston, and Carol Lipman, a leader in the SWP, and HONAAP, formerly of the SWP, Boston, and now believed located in NYC, as having been in attendance at the opening rally of the 2/11–13/72 HONAAP convention at Boston University, which rally was held in Hayden Hall, Boston University, the evening of the 11th.

The Boston SWP is a local affiliate and followed the mission and purpose of the national SWP. The SWP has been designated pursuant to Executive Order 10450.
