Photographs.

The first picture shown was a three-quarter full shot of Oswald, exposing a left profile as Oswald and that he looked downward. The second photograph which Mr. Piccolo remembered seeing was a back-of-the-head view of Oswald. Mr. Piccolo remembered that both of these photographs were taken from above Oswald and to his left. Mr. Piccolo was shown a copy of Warren Commission Exhibit #237, the famous Mexico Mystery Man photograph. He stated that this was definitely not the man in the photographs exhibited to him. Mr. Piccolo correctly identified an unlabeled frontal photograph of Lee Harvey Oswald shown to him by the HSCA.

Mr. Piccolo was also asked whether he knew anything about the circumstances surrounding the Agency's initial discovery of the photographs he claims to have seen. He stated that he did not have any first-hand knowledge of their discovery, but that it was the type of thing, “a coup,” that would have traveled through the Agency “grapevine.”
