Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

November 19, 1971. Teletype.

NR 006 PH Code. 1:50 a.m., NITEL, 11-19-71, JJR.

To: Director, attention DOMINTEL.

WFO (100-54927).

From: Philadelphia (100-53818) (P).

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WOMAAC)-sponsored demonstration, November twenty next, Washington, D.C., is New Left (OO: WFO).

On November seventeen last, a confidential source who has provided reliable information in the past advised that no charter has been placed with Greyhound Lines by the Women United for Abortion Rights (WUFAR) or related groups.

On November seventeen last, a second confidential source who has provided reliable information in the past advised that a request for ten buses was made with the Public Service Coordinated Transportation by WUFAR, but the company could not provide the desired number of buses, so the order was not taken.

On November seventeen last, a third confidential source who has provided reliable information in the past advised that WUFAR placed a deposit with the Trenton Philadelphia Coach.
