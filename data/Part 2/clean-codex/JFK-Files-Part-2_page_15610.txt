FBI

Date: 12/5/69

Transmit the following via teletype.

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago

Black Panther Party. RM—BPP. OO: San Francisco.

Chicago PD source advised that approximately one hundred ten Black organizations met at Woodlawn Mennonite Church, 4612 South Woodlawn Avenue, afternoon instant, to formulate plans for presenting “case for Black people” before United Nations. Groups represented at meeting circulated petition supporting BPP attorney Charles Gary’s plan, carried by news services December 4 last, that Gary intended to appear before United Nations and charge United States with “genocide of militant Blacks.”

Meeting dispersed without any incidents being reported or arrests made.
