This group arrived and assembled at this point at approximately twelve forty-five p.m., where they heard speakers deliver speeches principally on repealing all existing anti-abortion laws, eliminating forced sterilization, and repealing all contraceptive laws.

Member of subject organization: Gale Haygold, a member of the APC as well as a member of both the Cleveland branch of the Socialist Workers Party (CESUP) and the Young Socialist Alliance (YSA); and Carylo Lee, a member of APC as well as the CBSWP.

Some of the statements shouted by the assembly were, “Abortion should be a woman’s decision,” “Abortion is not murder,” and “A woman should have control over her own body.” The rally terminated at approximately one forty-five p.m. Cleveland, Ohio, PD advised no arrests, injuries, or incidents of violence occurred during the rally. U.S. Secret Service, Cleveland, cognizant.
