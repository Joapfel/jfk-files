1. HQS files indicate Arturo Serrano in 1966 was director, PCM publishing house Fondo de Cultura, in Mexico City, but there are no details regarding his contacts with third-country exiles or officials. We are therefore unable to comment on the validity of his claim to assist LIRING/3.

2. Concur Guatemala City comment Ref B. Feel LIRING/4 should continue to press Serrano for more details on his channel to and identity of Guatemalan officials involved. Believe LIRING/4 and station will then be in better position to assess validity of Serrano claim. Suggest LIRING/4 also check Serrano story with leftist circle of friends to attempt to verify it and explore possible sources of funds if feasible. Station relaying office. Coordinating offices.
