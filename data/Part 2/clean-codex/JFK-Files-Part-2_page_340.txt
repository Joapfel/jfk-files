Classified message. From: Diss. Ryz. Action: JWH-D. Advance copy. Time: 6DEC66. Info: FILE UK C/WH/COG-8 f/c, C/FI-2.

06125 M. Cite Mexico City 9512.

Director info JUAVE. KAPOK PERUMEN LIRING. Ref: Director 56923.

6DEC66 53867.

1. Re para one ref, IGG account correct. LIRING-3 took son by former marriage with him and both returned. LIRING-3 told ROWTON that when he delayed by embassy he told CEPEDA he was taking son along as hostage to prove good intentions. CEPEDA protested this was not necessary but LIRING-3 took son anyway.

2. Re para four ref, LIRING-3 departed for Chiapas 1 or 2 December 66 for identification and preliminary assessment of candidates as possible smuggling channels. Had talk with JACOBO RODRIGUEZ PADILIA prior to departure, but illness of ROWTON did not allow him to get results of meeting. LIRING-3 will return about ten December.

3. Re para seven ref, LIRING-4 not working but states intention of entering UNAM for a course in anthropology next semester. Other questions posed in para seven will be answered upon return of L-3 from Chiapas.

4. Pouching additional details of ROWTON's debriefing of L-3.

Rep has interest in information concerning LIRING-4 current status.
