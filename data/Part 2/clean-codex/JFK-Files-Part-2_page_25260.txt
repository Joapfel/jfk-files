Persons/Entities: Diem. Dates: November 2, 1963, 1130 hours. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: 1130. Lieutenant Colonel Phong Pgeo said that he and his forces suffered six long Peace during the early morning hours to report him and his men to the JCS after their surrender. A search revealed they were not at the palace (paleo) and had not been there during the coup. Two reported back to the JCS. Villas in Saigon (Sanigon) and Cholon known to be owned by the Ngo family were searched.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., June 20, 1975, Conein, Exhibit One, page 24. File. Staff: Dawson. Date: June 30, 1975.

Persons/Entities: Diem (Dima). Dates: November 2, 1963, 1330 hours. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: Officer in J-2, JCS, reported that Diem (Dima) and I was fed an orderly were caught and killed by Nhu's (mya's) personnel at a church in Cho Quua, Cholon. Coprocured with the rest. Captain Do Mad, a nephew of La Mau, Mrs Chief.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., June 20, 1975, Conein, Exhibit One, page 25. File. Staff: Dawson. Date: June 30, 1975.
