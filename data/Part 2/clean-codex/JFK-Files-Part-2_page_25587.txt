Name: Sam Giancana.

Aliases: Sam Mooney, Sam Malone.

Description: Born 7-16-08, Chicago, Illinois; 5'9", 175 lbs., hazel eyes, dark chestnut hair, fair complexion, and medium build.

Localities: Resides at 1147 Wenonah Avenue. Frequents Oak Park, Illinois, and the Army Lounge, Norwood House, and Villa Venice, all of Chicago, Illinois.

Family background: Wife, Angeline DeTolve, died in 1954. Has three daughters, Annette, Bonita Lou, and Francine. Mother deceased; father is Tony Giancana, and sisters are Mary, Josephine, and Victoria. Sam's consort is Ladana Collins, 20 E. Delaware, Chicago, Illinois.

Criminal associates: Tony Accardo, Paul Ricco, Willie Baddone, Nicholas D'Amico, Sam Battaglia, and Leonard Gianola, all of Chicago, Illinois.

Criminal history: FBI #58437, Chicago PD #E-27465. Subject has a record of 13 arrests dating from September 1925, on such charges as murder, grand larceny, auto theft, burglary, and liquor law violations, with two felony convictions.

Business: Owns the Forest Lounge, the R & S Liquor Co., and the Lohmar Dist. Co., Chicago, Illinois, and has gambling interests and an interest in the shrimp business in Cuba.

Modus operandi: A top-ranking member of the Mafia in the State of Illinois and a director of the organization's activities in Chicago and vicinity.
