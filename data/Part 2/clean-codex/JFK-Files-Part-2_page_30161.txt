To obtain classifiable fingerprints:

1. Use black printers' ink.
2. Distribute the ink on inking slab.
3. Wash and dry fingers thoroughly.
4. Roll fingers from nail to nail, and avoid allowing fingers to slip.
5. Be sure impressions are registered in correct order.
6. If an amputation or defect makes it impossible to print a finger, make a notation to that effect in the individual finger block.
7. If one physical condition makes it impossible to obtain perfect impressions, make the best that can be obtained, with a memo stapled to the card explaining the consequences.
8. Examine the completed prints to see if they can be classified, bearing in mind that the best fingerprints fall into the patterns shown on this card. Other patterns occur infrequently and are not shown here.
