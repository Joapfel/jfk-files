94th Congress, 2d Session

H. Res. 1073

In the House of Representatives

March 4, 1976

Mr. Edgar submitted the following resolution, which was referred to the Committee on House Administration.

Resolution

Resolved, That the report of the Select Committee on Intelligence filed on January 29, 1976, and entitled “Report of the Investigations of the United States Intelligence Agencies and Activities” shall be printed as a House document.
