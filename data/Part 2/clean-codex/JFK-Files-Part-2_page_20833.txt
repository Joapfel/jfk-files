United States Government Memorandum.

To: Director, FBI (100-468406). From: SAC, New York (100-174481) (C). Subject: Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC), IS-C (Trotskyist). Date: 7/30/73.

For the information of receiving offices, the Bureau has authorized discontinuance of active investigation of captioned organization.

Receiving offices are, nevertheless, requested to remain alert for any pertinent activity on the part of this organization which would be of interest to this Bureau.

Distribution: Bureau, Albany, Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, Cleveland, Detroit, Philadelphia, Washington Field, and New York.
