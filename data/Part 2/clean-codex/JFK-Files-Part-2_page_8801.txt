WAVE 8562 (IN 74653), dated 5 December 1963.

Reports that PRIO's declarations, wherein he accused the U.S. Government of an agreement with Russia to replace CASTRO with a Tito-type government, originated with Guillermo ALONSO Pujol in Washington. It was received by PRIO through Guillermo BELT. Pujol has friendly relations with certain members of the diplomatic circle in Washington and thinks he is well informed, although he lives in Europe. Guillermo MARQUEZ, publisher of the New York weekly Ahora, and Carlos MARQUEZ Sterling are both part of PRIO's plan and allegedly have become associated with Richard NIXON in accordance with a Republican Party plan to bring up the Cuban case before the elections.
