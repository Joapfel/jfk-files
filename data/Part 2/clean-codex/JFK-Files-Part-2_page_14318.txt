United States Government Memorandum.

To: Mr. W. R. Wannall. From: Mr. W. O. Cregar. Subject: SENSTUDY 75. Date: May 9, 1975.

Memorandum Legal Counsel to Mr. J. B. Adams dated 5/5/75 recommended that a Bureau representative be available for consultation by Mr. Mohr during the time Mr. Mohr is being interviewed by Staff Members of the SSC 5/9/75. REC-100.
