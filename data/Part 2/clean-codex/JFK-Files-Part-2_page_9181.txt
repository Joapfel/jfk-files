Date of transcription: 8/13/74.

Larry Sweeney Stafford, 3676 Crosby Drive, Lexington, Kentucky, known to the interviewing agent by previous association, furnished the following information:

Stafford advised that an individual known to him as Mike Brown appeared at the Stafford Lock Shop of Richmond, Kentucky, in August 1973. The Stafford Lock Shop is a business owned and managed by Larry Sweeney Stafford and William Junior (Jay) Stafford from the fall of 1973 until approximately August 1, 1974. During this visit by Brown in the fall of 1973, Brown involved Larry Stafford and his brother Jay in a conversation which was interpreted by Larry Stafford as designed to produce Larry's and Jay's feelings or attitudes concerning the possibility of the Stafford brothers' involvement in burglary or unauthorized lock-picking activities. Larry Stafford advised that neither he nor his brother Jay gave the impression that they would be interested in those type of activities, and Brown subsequently left the Stafford Lock Shop and was not heard of nor seen again until July 22, 1974.

On July 22, 1974, Brown again appeared at the Stafford Lock Shop and disclosed the fact that he had read an article in the Richmond, Kentucky, newspaper wherein Jay and Larry Stafford were described as two individuals arrested on July 4, 1974, burglarizing Grant's Department Store in Frankfort, Kentucky. Brown made reference to the fact that he had read this article and disclosed to the Stafford brothers that he felt that they were his kind of people.
