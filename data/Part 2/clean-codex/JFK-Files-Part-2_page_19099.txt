LEONID ZAMAYATIN, Chief of the Press Office of the Soviet Foreign Ministry, he was treated most cordially. When this former informant's exit visa was finally issued, he found that it was good for a period in excess of a week after the seven-day period in which he was to depart from the Soviet Union.

While furnished some information about Soviet nationals of interest to the Bureau, none of this information was of such a nature as to warrant dissemination and it will be appropriately channeled to files in the NYO.

At such time as is available for interview, information furnished by him will be forwarded to the Bureau.

Submitted for information.
