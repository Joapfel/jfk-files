Consuelo Esperon's (.-ITAMIL 7) personnel record in Mexico could not be located. Carlos Jurado J. Delmar (LIRING 3) was born in Cuba on November 3, 1927. He entered Mexico July 1, 1965, and taught Graphics and Art at U.N.A.M. until January 1, 1968, when he disappeared. The Mexican officials assumed that he returned to Cuba.
