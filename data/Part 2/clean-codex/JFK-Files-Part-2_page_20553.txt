To: Director (Attn: DOMINTEL), WFO. From: Boston 100-43809.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) sponsored demonstration November twenty, seventy-one, Washington, D.C., is—New Left (OO: WFO).

A source who has provided reliable information in the past, on November sixteen last, advised that Matilde Zimmerman, phone number five four seven dash one eight one eight of Boston Women's Abortion Action, 552 Mass. Ave., Cambridge, Mass., Room 9, had ordered five buses with a forty-five-person capacity each from the Brush Hill Transportation Company, Dorchester, Boston, Mass., for travel to Washington, D.C., November nineteen next. A fifteen-hundred-dollar cash deposit against a total cost of thirty-five hundred dollars was made November fifteen last. The buses are scheduled to depart for Washington, D.C., approximately 9 PM, November.
