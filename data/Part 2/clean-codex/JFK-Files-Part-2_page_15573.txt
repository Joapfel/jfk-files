On September 18, 1969, the Chicago Tribune, Chicago, Illinois, newspaper, page 5, column 1, contained the following article:

“Find Panthers Feed Only a Few Children”

The Black Panther party’s program to feed breakfast to school children is almost non-existent in Chicago, a Tribune investigation disclosed yesterday.

Although Fred Hampton, the party’s Illinois chairman, claims that 3,000 to 3,500 children are being fed at three locations each week, it was found that on some days the centers were never opened and on others only a handful of children showed up.

Solicit Food and Cash

Local and federal law enforcement agencies, which have been closely watching the Panthers’ activities, have also discovered that the Panthers continue to solicit food, milk, and cash donations for the program from merchants and other businessmen throughout the city.

One merchant, who regularly donates large amounts of food for the program, recently told investigators that when he visited one of the centers, the few children present were being read excerpts from what he said was the Communist Manifesto before the breakfast was served.

List 3 Locations

The Panthers list their breakfast centers in the Trinity Lutheran Church, 4837 State Street; the Jackson Boulevard Christian Church, 2412 Jackson Boulevard; and the Better Boys Foundation Club, 1512 S. Pulaski Road (Pulaskiird).

All three of the feeding centers were visited Tuesday, and not one of them was open to feed children.
