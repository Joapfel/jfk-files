Ramiro Policarpo Lopez sent to Russia by Cuban government to study.

Left Key here in late 1963 to go to Tampa to attempt to return to Cuba. Had returned to U.S. in 1960 or 1961 because he was afraid of being drafted into the Cuban militia.

Married to Branche Andrea Leon on 8/11/62 before a Notary (Rotary) Public in Key West.

Wife lived at Porter Place Housing Project in 1964, employed at Newsreis, Inc., Key West.

Wife stated subject did not have future to travel to Mexico and Cuba; he said he had proper (potter) financial help from an “unrecognized organization,” presumably able in the Tampa area.

Subject had apparently had no prior history of epilepsy before coming to the U.S.

Treated by Dr. James D. Malgrat, 3207.
