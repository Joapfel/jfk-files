LIBERATION SCHOOL AND FREE LUNCHEON PROGRAM

Dear Parent(s):

All power to the people.

After careful study and analyses of the Children’s Liberation School and Free Lunch Program, we have found that the overwhelming majority of the youth attending the program come for just the free lunch portion. No question about it, brothers and sisters, food is lacking in the community, and the free lunch program helps to quell the hunger in our stomachs. We also must remember, brothers and sisters, that another need lacking in our poor and oppressed community is education that teaches us our true role in this decadent society, a society that refuses to meet the basic human needs and desires of those responsible for making America the richest country in the world today. Remember, it was our blood and sweat that has been and is exploited. Our youth must be educated to the trickery that has kept us divided for too long. So the Black Panther Party urges you as a parent to send or bring your children to the Liberation School!

Save the babies!

All power to the youth!

Intercommunal Survival Center  
Detroit Branch  
Black Panther Party
