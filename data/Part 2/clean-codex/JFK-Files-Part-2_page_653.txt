Mexico City 7613, 14 June 1966, 37364. Director info JMWAVE.

KAPOK DYVOUR LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 7558 (N33490).

1. At lunch 13 June 1966, Carlos Jurado del Mar told Wallace S. Rowton that for their business purposes he planned cultivating contacts he has in Mexico with a Central American group authorized by the Tricontinental Committee to purchase arms and supply them to Latin American revolutionaries.

2. In response to Rowton's queries, Jurado said he was almost certain one of the group was a Salvadoran he met in Havana during the revolution. Jurado said this person visits Mexico more or less regularly and he and Jurado have mutual friends here. Jurado indicated he hoped to make contact with this group within the next 60 days. Because of the present Jurado-Rowton relationship, Rowton did not ask for the name of the Salvadoran at this meeting but is certain he can obtain it shortly.

3. In passing, Jurado mentioned Antonio del Conde Pontones (201-728065), who helped supply Fidel with the Granma and arms. Commented del Conde probably not being used by the Tricontinental Committee because semi-business and how arms he to operate.
