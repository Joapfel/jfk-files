United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Alexandria (100-669) (RUC)

Subject: Women's National March on Pentagon, 4/10/71  
IS—Miscellaneous

Date: 5/28/71

ReAXteletype to Bureau, 4/10/71.

On 5/7/71 and 5/19/71, Gordon W. Crossman, Naval Investigative Service (NIS), telephone OX 3-3525, advised that Jeanette Ann Varner, a lieutenant in the U.S. Navy Nurse Corps on active duty, spoke at the Pentagon during the demonstration there on 4/10/71. He advised that her date and place of birth is 6/1/46, at Creston, Iowa. Her Social Security number is 482-54-1520 and her Navy service number is 726633W. She resides with a doctor, Sidney N. Wolfe, employed by U.S. Naval Hospital, Bethesda, Maryland, and his wife at 2506 Cliffbourn Place, N.W., Washington, D.C. He advised that her permanent residence is 310 East Terry Street, Centerville, Iowa, and that she is currently stationed at the Bethesda Naval Hospital. He further advised that she attends classes at George Washington University, Washington, D.C., and that she is active in the “Women's Lib.”

Alexandria indices negative.

No further investigation remains outstanding and this case is considered RUC.
