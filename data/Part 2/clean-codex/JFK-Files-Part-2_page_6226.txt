9. On 7 March 1967, Pravda published an article called “Clay Shaw of the CIA,” dated 6 March 1967 and written by V. YERMAKOV. The article charged that the CIA made use of the services of Clay Shaw for its own interests in Italy. The Pravda piece noted that the DA in New Orleans was accusing Shaw of participation in the assassination conspiracy. It said SHAW spent “several years” in Poms as a World Trade Center loader. It claimed that the Center was a cover for financing anti-Communist activity. The CIA gave directives to SHAW. The World Trade Center was a branch of “Pomiden” (arc), “which the newspapers in Switzerland openly accused of ‘criminal operations’....” (Comment). It appears that all of the Pravda charges are untrue, except that there was a CIA-SHAW relationship. So far as is known, SHAW was never asked to use his relationship with the World Trade Center for [氯qingstina] purposes and, in fact, he has not been in Italy.
