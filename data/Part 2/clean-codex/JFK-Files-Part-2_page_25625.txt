Howard—

In re your “battle plan” memo to Hart and Garn, I think you should consider the possible wisdom of our Committee making some kind of very low-key announcement that we’re doing something. The purpose would be two-fold—to prevent getting wholly co-opted by the new House Committee (and agencies making a claim that since the House is doing it, they can ignore us), and also to put the House on notice that there is a specific area we have carved and will continue to carve out for ourselves. Most important, we must do something affirmative to ensure that the sources out there come to us. If we say nothing, potentials will just go to the House under the assumption that that’s the only thing going on.

Attached is the type of low-key statement someone could say in response to an inquiry (which I assume Spencer gets lots of).

Mike
