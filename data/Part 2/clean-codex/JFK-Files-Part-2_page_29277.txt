E. Joseph Civello

File #All 302 676. Date of birth: 2/3/02, Port Allen, Louisiana.

9/9/59 memo: Original family name was Shivera; changed to Civello in 1932. Few news clips on Civello and the Appalachin (Appalachia) meeting.

5/22/59 memo: Says Civello and Frank Desimone claim to be cousins. INS unable to verify this.

3/13/59 news clip: JC will not testify before the L.A. grand jury. His attorney said he is uneducated and confused. The grand jury will give him another chance before a contempt hearing.

9/11/58 report: Ross Musso is JC's brother-in-law.

Civello's file contains testimony given by John Cusach, narcotics agent, on 1/19/58 before the N.Y. Joint Legislative Committee on Government Operations.

This text runs down a list of top Mafia men in 1958 with brief biographical sketches.

Civello is mentioned; says he was convicted in Dallas in 1937, possibly on a narcotics charge, and received a 15-year sentence, which was commuted. He was released from prison on 11/6/48. He now has a pardon application pending.

Gabriel Mannarino is mentioned: date of birth, 10/31/16, Pennsylvania. Says he runs numbers and slot machines with his brother Sammy in Westmoreland County, Pennsylvania.

In 1950, word was that Gabriel Mannarino was going to move in on the Pittsburgh numbers racket and let the “Cuban mob” take over in New Kensington.
