Further details UNTIL-17, 11 Sept 59: On 1 Sept 59, Renigro Diaz, former senior pilot for Cuban Air Force and deemed senior pilot for Cuban Mapping Institute (ICCI), Angel Ros, ICCC co-pilot, and Ismael Suarez arrived Miami in connection with alteration of an aircraft to accommodate photo equipment. Suarez represented that Diaz and himself were highly influential persons in the Castro government, and was obviously the senior person in the group and clearly the one given orders. Suarez said that at one time during the revolution he was coordinator of the several revolutionary fronts. Suarez invited Source QUALIM to visit him in Havana and gave QUALIM a key to apartment 65, corner of Calls D and 23rd. Suarez told QUALIM that Suarez’s code name was “GUAMINDIA” and that use of this name would resolve any QUALIM problems in Cuba. Traces on Ros and Diaz indicate both left Cuba in 1960 for political reasons.
