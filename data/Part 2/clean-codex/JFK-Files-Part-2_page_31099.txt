PRQ Part II, 3 May 1963. Subject will be CIA penetration of a Cuban offshore group and will be paid a monthly stipend depending on the amount and type of his production.

Cable, 8 July 1963, IN 7543. To: Director. From: JMWAVE. Indicates NRP, Alpha 66, and SFNE have combined.

1 July 1965. Dispatch. Chief/WH from COS/MOS. Transmitted memorandum of oral commitment on Today, who began employment with CIA in a PW capacity in 1961, subsequently transferred to CT activities, and up until now paid from ODA funds. To receive a salary of $50 per month.

6 July 1967. Dispatch. Chief/WH. From: COS, JMWAVE. Today terminated 30 June 1967. His overall reporting has been marginal at best during the past year, partly due to his lengthy absence from the area and a decreased interest on the part of JMWAVE in the offshore groups he is reporting on. Recommend for reuse if situation arises. Has been lured as consultant for Hudson Institute.
