Copies of the important photographs were given to the Manells for them to retain for routine use in the course of their work.

The Manells' recollection of their role in this operation is very different from that of Ms. Goodpasture. Mrs. Manell testified that Ann Goodpasture held the photographic production very tightly. Her recollection was confirmed by her husband. The Manells stated that they did not routinely review or see all of the production from the Soviet Embassy photographic surveillance operation.

They claim they only saw the photographs that Ms. Goodpasture thought were important enough to bring to their attention. According to the Manells, access to this file was tightly controlled by Ms. Goodpasture.
