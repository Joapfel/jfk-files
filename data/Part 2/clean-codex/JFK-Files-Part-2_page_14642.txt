United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. Bishop  
From: M. A. Jones  
Date: 12/29/70  
Subject: Brief for Director's Use in Connection with Attorney General's Staff Conference Thursdays, 5:00 p.m.

You will recall that the captioned brief was discontinued during the holiday season. It is presumed these conferences will resume on Thursday, January 7, 1971. Accordingly, it is suggested that all divisions revise and update their sections of the brief and submit such revisions to be received on or before noontime Tuesday, January 5th.

Recommendation: None. For information.

Mr. Tolson; Mr. Mohr; Mr. Sullivan; Mr. Bishop; Mr. Brennan; Mr. Callahan; Mr. Casper; Mr. Conrad; Mr. Felt; Mr. Tavel; Mr. Gale; Mr. Rosen; Mr. Walters; Mr. Beaver; Miss Gandy; Miss Holmes; M. A. Jones.
