1/11/68

Code teletyped message.

To SAC Atlanta. From Director FBI.

Demonstrations protesting U.S. policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., January fifteen, nineteen sixty-eight.

Reurtel January ten last.

Authority granted for informant to attend captioned demonstration. Instruct source to promptly apprise WFO of any pertinent data developed during trip to Washington. Special Agent Garnett Tunstall, phone number Executive three-seven one-zero-zero, extensions three-nine-one or four-six-zero, should be contacted for this purpose. Atlanta advice WFO code illustrated by other sources. Copy mailed WFO.

(Cynthia Yvonne Mitchell)

Note:

Informant was approved as a probationary racial informant in November 1967, as she was familiar with some of the racial issues and close to dissident racial groups in Atlanta. She is paid under SAC authority. Atlanta, by relEI, advised that they were sending informant, UACB, to participate in demonstration as a method of acquainting her with persons active in Vietnam and racial activities. Teletype being used in order to instruct Atlanta in the method source should contact WFO while in Washington area.
