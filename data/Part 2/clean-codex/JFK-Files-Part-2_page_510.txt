142020 Z, cite Mexico City 9633. Director info: Guatemala City, JMWAVE.

PBRUMEN LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 9560.

IN 58952.

1. Further debriefing LIRING-3 by Wallace B. Rowton produced additional information concerning LIRING-3's impending trip to Guatemala to meet Communist Party members.

2. The phone number for Agustina (Agustine) Hernandez is Guatemala City 20 057. Jacobo Rodriguez Padilla gave LIRING-3 this number, which he used for his first contact with Agustina. After LIRING-3 returned from the Mexican border, Jacobo told LIRING-3 not to ask for Agustina at that number but for Juan Antonio Franco. Jacobo told L-3 that Agustina worked at the Pedro (Piedro) Santo Printing Office, but could be reached at Franco's number when not at work.

3. No Mexico Station traces Agustina. HMM 4348, 26 June 59, reports Juan Antonio Franco attended a meeting of PGT on 29 May 59 at Manuel Maria Contreras No. 43-J, Mexico City. Seventy persons attended the meeting. LIESCORT on 29 January 59 reported Juan Antonio Franco, painter, was a new applicant for PGT membership.

4. LIRING-3 told Rowton he would leave 15 December 66 in order to arrive in Huehuetenango in time to be in place on 18 December, per instructions from Agustina.
