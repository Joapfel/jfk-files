4. Cable to DIR from JMWAVE, dated 28 March 1964. [AM] AUF/2 spoke with Blas Enrique Comas Castro, captain of JURE boat VENUS, which is in Puerto Rico. Said Cisneros and Ray recently arrived in P.R. from Caracas and said VENUS would be used to infiltrate Ray into Cuba in a few days.

[AM] AUJ/2 said Armando Alanis Angulo said he was leaving Miami on 30 March for a new JURE base within six hours of Miami. Said base was under the responsibility of Dunney Perez Alamo and himself. Also said Ray had chosen VENUS to make the trip to Cuba and VENUS was armed with a 20MM gun, four .50-caliber and one .30-caliber machine guns.

5. Cable to DIR from JMWAVE, dated 28 March 1964. [AM] AI/2 reports that Alanis, Perez, and Antonio Arias Gonzalez, JURE members, are dissatisfied with Ray’s organization because they had now been forbidden to criticize U.S. government policies, and the fact that they were allowed to have a training camp near Miami indicated that Ray was accepting CIA aid. Cisneros claimed the main objective of JURE was to keep the U.S. government entertained with the training base set up in the Miami area and that the departure of JURE members to infiltrate Cuba would be outside the U.S.

6. Accounting of funds received by Ray, from 21 June 1963 to 8 April 1964, shows a total expenditure of $217,924.28 by CIA.

7. Memorandum for the Chief, WH Division, dated 14 April 1964, from Alfonso Rodriguez. Subject: My Meeting with Ray on 13 April 1964.

Essence of Ray’s plans:

1. Two teams expected to go into Oriente Province this week.
2. Ray will turn over JURE contact with us to Cisneros, effective 23 April.

Ray’s immediate objective once in Cuba will be to initiate systematic, continuous bombings of strategic and non-strategic targets. Power plant in Havana, major refinery, and aqueduct in Santa Clara are immediate objectives.

8. Cable to DIR from JMWAVE, dated 26 April 1964. [AM]/2 reported that the JURE training base opened near Tampa, Florida, for 30 men and that plans were being made to set up a base in the Dominican Republic.
