DBF 95904, dated 8 December 1961 (FBI). Sent to Secret Service, I&NS, State Department Security, and CIA. Stated that the I&NS Office in Honolulu was requested by the I&NS Regional Office in San Pedro, California, on 4 December 1961 to place a lookout notice on Aldo Pedro MARGOLLES Duenas. MARGOLLES was reported to be the son of the Chief of National Police in Cuba and attempting to enter the United States to meet with CASTRO agents to assassinate President John Kennedy.

WAVE 0793 (IN 13094), dated 18 January 1962. WAVE advised it had photographs of MARGOLLES and Emilio ARAGONES, also involved in the plot, and would attempt to develop further information on the assassination plot.

UFGA 3134, dated 23 January 1962. JMWAVE Station forwarded to Headquarters photographs and biographical data on MARGOLLES and ARAGONES.

DIRECTOR 38056 (OUT 94877), dated 27 January 1962. Cable to Latin American Stations and Bases concerning the plot by MARGOLLES and ARAGONES. Requested any information on their current whereabouts, on a priority basis.
