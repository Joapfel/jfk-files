Russian Embassy (RUSSIAN ENB:SUU), 10-10-65.

1 October 1965.

45.

MO: In at 1031. MO talks to MI in broken Russian. Hello. I was at your place last Saturday and I talked to your consul. They said that they'd send a telegram to Washington, and I wanted to ask you if there is anything new?

MI: I'd like to ask you to call another phone number.

MO: Please.

MI: Please write it down: 15-60-55, and ask for a consul.

MO: Thank you.

MI: Please.

Attachment B:

A telephone call to the Soviet Military Attaché at 1031 hours on 1 October 1965 by an unidentified man speaking broken Russian who asked about a telegram which they were to send to Washington. The unidentified man said he was at the Soviet Embassy “last Saturday.” The Soviet told him to call 15-60-55.
