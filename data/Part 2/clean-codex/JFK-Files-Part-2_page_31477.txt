Title: Irving Rhodes Promer  
Character: Security Matter - C

Synopsis:

Subject resides at 881 Clayton Street, San Francisco, and is employed as an architectural draftsman through the American Institute of Architects, 47 Kearny Street, San Francisco. Subject attended CP-sponsored May Day meeting and ARI affairs. He taught a class on Marxism and Art at San Francisco SS. He attended PW bazaar, National Guardian affair, and a film sponsored by SFCCPAP. Attended a mass meeting in San Francisco sponsored by CURT and took part in a peace walk in San Francisco. Also attended meetings sponsored by the Continuations Committee of World Congress on General Disarmament and Peace.
