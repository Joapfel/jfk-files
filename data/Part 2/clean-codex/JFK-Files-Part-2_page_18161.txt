To: Intelligence Community Staff. From: FBI. Attn: Central Index.

Subject: Abstract of Information Provided to Select Committees.

Date provided: 9/18/75.

Identification: Memorandum and enclosures.

In response to: SSC letters 8/28/75; 7/8/75, Items 2, 6, 8, 15 and 20; 7/14/75, Items 19 and 20.

Classification of information: S.

Key words: Surveillance, electronic intelligence collection. All information contained herein is unclassified. Date 10/18/75 by S. P. Almore.

Summary: Materials on Martin Luther King, Jr., relating to meetings between FBI and Dr. King; Nobel Prize awarded to Dr. King; meeting between President and King on 2/9/65; materials for an interview of Director by reporters for Washington Evening Star 6/19/69; memoranda by Internal Security Section and by former Assistant Director William Sullivan concerning the August, 1963, March on Washington; monographs of 1967.
