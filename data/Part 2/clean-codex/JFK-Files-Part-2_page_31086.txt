12 February 1976 cable, IN 824723. Cite: Caracas 29063. To: Priority Director, Santiago, Buenos Aires, LA/Miami.

WK SCARLET-3, former unilateral asset, advises he is investigating the bombing of the Cuban ambassador in Caracas and is traveling during the week of 15 February to Santiago, Buenos Aires, and Miami.

Cuban Ambassador Norberto Hernandez Carbelo reported receiving a bomb in a large manila envelope bearing the return address of Dr. Gustavo A. Vollmer. WK SCARLET/1, former WK SCARLET chief, is a security consultant to Vollmer and has retained WK SCARLET/3, who is operating a commercial investigation service, to determine who used Vollmer’s name. WK SCARLET/3 maintains contacts with Orlando Bosch.

Frank Castro had knowledge of an attempt to assassinate Henry Kissinger and told WK SCARLET/3. In order to obtain information, the Agency is afraid they will compromise WK SCARLET/3.
