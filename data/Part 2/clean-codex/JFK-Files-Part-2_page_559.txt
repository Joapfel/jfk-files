1. Following cabled in answer to questions raised in paragraph 3, reference B:

A. FAR knows LIRING-3 has capability to obtain false documentation. LIRING-3, for example, obtained a false birth certificate and military registration card for Jacobo Padilla.

B. Leftist contacts of LIRING-3, including Guatemalans, know he is familiar with the Guatemala border area in Chiapas. LIRING-3 worked for a number of years in Chiapas for the Mexican equivalent of the Indian Affairs Bureau. It is also well known to FAR that LIRING-3 has a business located in the border area in San Cristobal de las Casas, since this business was to be used as cover for arms supply to Guatemalan rebels.

C. As Guatemala City station will remember, LIRING-3 was taken by FAR to a hideout in Guatemala, where he held talks with FAR members. LIRING-3 even supplied FAR with some medicine and cognac, for which, incidentally, he never paid.
