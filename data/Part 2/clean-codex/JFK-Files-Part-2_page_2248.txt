Caracas. Special Operations.

Out 81388. Wash 3578. To: CARA. Cite: WASHF.

Re: CARA 546 (IN 31421) and CARA 547 (IN 31483).

Vincent B. Ogden.

1. Gage not considered suitable to act as station chief for prolonged period.

2. Time will not permit Ogden taking home leave in addition to necessary 30 days TDY here prior to Gage's departure.

3. For these reasons desire Ogden postpone home leave until after Crimmins has become well indoctrinated.

W. G. Tharp. RSW JSR DDEB. A. M. Wheeler.

0119Z, 14 May 1949.
