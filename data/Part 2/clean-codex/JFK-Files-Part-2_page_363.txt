3. Embassy allowed LERING-3 to depart 6 November, but without enthusiasm. Upon arrival in Havana, LERING-3 was met at the airport not by Herrera, as previously mentioned by Cepeda, but by four uniformed security officers. They chatted amicably with him on insignificant matters and tried four hotels in an attempt to find his place of stay. Efforts were unsuccessful. Finally, LERING-3 suggested he stay with Honduran friend Rene Ochoa Funez. The security officers agreed, and he settled there.

4. The next morning, four officers returned and took LERING-3 to a finca in Pinar del Rio. The place was filled with American arms and men and women dressed in combat clothes undergoing paramilitary training. He was shown other similar fincas, one to which he was lifted by helicopter in Camagüey and another to which he hiked 20 kilometers. He was given no explanation for the tour, and on 10 or 11 November he returned to Ochoa's home.

5. On 14 November, LERING-3 contacted ARTOL RODRIGUEZ, who had been on a week-long trip to Camagüey. LERING-3 said he spent three hours telling Rodriguez details of the Lopez case and explaining that he had a plan for his Mexican and Central American contacts into a network which would assist in performing clandestine subversive missions.
