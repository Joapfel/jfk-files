Translator. Biographical data: Personnel employed by the Office of the Prime Minister.

Cobb, June. Sex: female. U.S. citizen.

Since September 1959, she has been employed in the office of Fidel Castro, Prime Minister of Cuba. Miss Cobb was in New York City for medical treatment and intended to return to Cuba on December 3, 1959.

Coeb (Cobb), Viola June. U.S. citizen. Translator of “The Shark & the Sardines”—pre-Castro. Expelled from Materials for political activities (November 1961). Employee, Security Office of Prime Minister (June 1961).

List of people connected with intelligence, subversive, and sabotage activities in behalf of the Cuban government, as requested in working files of CI Branch, Security Division, ACSI, on 10 February 1962.

10 February 1962.
