Support the Free Breakfast for School Children™

The Free Breakfast Program sponsored by the Detroit Branch of the Black Panther Party has opened its second “Breakfast Center.” The newest center is located at 157 Collingwood between Second and Woodward. The opening date of the second center is April 12, 1972.

The other location is at 2228 Bewick between Vernon and Kercheval on the Eastside. It is presently serving more than 95 children every school morning.

Hot meals (such as grits, oatmeal, waffles, bacon, eggs, etc.) are being served daily at 7:00 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., Monday through Friday.

The people in the community are welcome to come to the breakfast program and can volunteer to give assistance in serving the children if they desire to do so, and can inform others of the program.
