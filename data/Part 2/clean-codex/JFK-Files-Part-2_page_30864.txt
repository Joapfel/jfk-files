CIA Briefing.

Files are records.

Basis for opening 201 file: operational interest in individual (mostly foreigners); potential recruitment. Would trace files in the field or system for any information on the person. Tracking an individual; e.g., foreign specialist, counterintelligence.

Sometimes, after there are several files on a person, it is more convenient to put it all in one file: 201.

Tracing means running through all variations of a person's name and obtaining a computer printout. Case officer could initiate a 201 file; he is usually most knowledgeable.

Type: reliable tip that a person is disclosing military secrets. Case officer's judgment call as to whether a 201 file is opened, but the information would be passed or indexed, even if a 201 file is not opened.
