Secret 282320Z. CITE Mexico City 1930. Guatemala City. Info: Director, JNWAVE.

29 June 1957 (N), Q0593.

1. LIRING-4, who now works with LIRING, talked to Carlos Enrique Salazar at LIRING on 22 June. The latter said:

A. He fled Guatemala on 7 June because he was connected with the rebel movement and the police were looking for him.

B. He is staying with Angel Campiraran Sanchez (Angel Campiran), who owns a battery shop at Calle de Amores 806, Letracx, Mexico City.

Angel Campiran is the father of Enice Campiran, a young Mexican student reportedly assassinated with her husband by police in Guatemala City.

C. He could give Marco Vinicio Gonzalez, president of the “Sociedad de Alumnos Universitarios de Guatemala,” as a reference in Guatemala.

D. The rebel movement in Guatemala is crumbling as a result of intensified army and police counterinsurgency activities.

2. LIRING staff apparently is in no hurry to help the Guatemalan rebel, who is destitute (DESTILE) without papers and anxious to go to the government (PBRUMENT). Will try to have LIRING-3 contact him, who studied painting in Guadalajara, and debrief him regarding rebel contacts in Guatemala.
