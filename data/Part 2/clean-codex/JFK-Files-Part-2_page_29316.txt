Kennedy

Outside Contact Report

Date: 10-16-78. Time: 3 PM.

I. Identifying Information:

Telephone: 504-393-6899. Name: Anthony William Atzenhoffer, Jr. Address: 740 Goodson Dr., New Orleans (Algiers), La. Type of contact: Telephone person.

II. Summary of Contact:

Investigator Robert Buras met Mr. Atzenhoffer at his service station and learned that he had joined the Civil Air Patrol Cadets, Moisant Squadron, in September 1954 and left in 1957. He was a platoon sergeant and used to call the roll and remembered Oswald's name being on the roll. He has no recollection of ever training with him or speaking with him, and remembers him being a quiet guy. When he saw Oswald on TV in 1963, he didn't recognize him, but on one of the newscasts he saw Ed Voebel talking about him (LHO). He remembered Voebel as the man that Oswald was close to and stayed with at the meetings. Atzenhoffer only knew Voebel from CAPC, not socially.

Ferrie did come to Moisant Squadron and teach some courses, and Atzenhoffer took his first plane flight with Ferrie.

III. Recommended Follow-up (if any):

None. He doesn't have any photos or records that might help.

Signature: Robert Buras.
