2. Programs Which May Be Initiated.

As in the case of Section II, the programs outlined below are within reasonable technical and practical competence. However, as they cover the complete spectrum of actions, it is realized that some may be found unsuitable, on political or other grounds.

B. Surveillance

(1) Establish a Caribbean sea and coastal surveillance program which provides for multilateral exchange of information and multilateral participation in an alerting system; each country to be responsible for patrolling its own segment.

Requirement of intelligence input into the Caribbean alerting system and provision for exchanging intelligence between subscriber nations.
