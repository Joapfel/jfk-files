Cite Mexico City 2075. Caracas info Director. FBRUMEN.

Ref: A, Mexico City 1954 (MO1455). B, Caracas 7262 (NO2528).

1. According to Sergio Valdez, Yatter's contact with Venezuelan rebels in Caracas is Ramiro Ortega, whom he originally met in Mexico. Ortega instructed Valdez, when the latter comes to Caracas, to insert an ad in a local newspaper saying: “La persona interesada en artesanía mexicana favor comunicarse con Sergio Valdez,” followed by an address. Either Ortega or another member of the Venezuelan resistance will make contact. Valdez is also in contact with Venezuelans in Mexico.

2. The station believes Valdez and Raul Moreno are determined to help the Venezuelan resistance, with or without the advice of LIRING-3. Since it appears desirable to monitor their activities, LIRING-3 formed a loose commercial association with them in Mexico City by the name “Colorluz.” This will also help LIRING-3 in future dealings with his revolutionary friends in Cuba when he next visits Havana, which he is being urged to do soon, as he could point to personal secret.

12 July 1957. Classified message. Total copies: 38.
