At this point, Ms. Tirado informed Consul Eusebio Azcue of the situation. Azcue had been in his private office, which he shared with his upcoming replacement, Alfredo Mirabal. Azcue politely explained the requisites for an intransit visa to Oswald. When he noticed that Oswald was a stubborn man, he told Oswald that he was obviously not a friend of the Cuban Revolution because he would otherwise understand that Cuba had to be extremely careful with the people it allowed in the country. Azcue and Oswald yelled at each other. Then Azcue went to the door, opened it, and asked Oswald to leave. Oswald did not revisit or telephone the Consulate. Ms. Tirado described Lee Harvey Oswald as approximately five feet six, with sparse blonde hair, weighing about 125 pounds.
