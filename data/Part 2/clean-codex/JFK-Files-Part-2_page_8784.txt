AMSWEEP Operation, 19 June 1969.

SW Message No. 45 from agent, undated.

Expressed deepest sympathy for death of President Kennedy. Added that death of Kennedy produced great nervousness and concern in CASTRO and his close associates. Further, that there was a great mobilization of troops throughout the country.

SW Message No. 14 from AMSWEEP-12, dated 19 November 1964.
