Annie Goodpasture was recalled from retirement by the IG Staff. She served with the LAD Task Force from mid-March to mid-May 1977. See following paragraph on Ms. Goodpasture.

Based on her long tenure at the Mexico City Station, which spanned the critical period from September to November 1963, Ms. Goodpasture was recalled by the IG Staff in February 1977 to answer numerous questions focused primarily on Mexico City Station activities. The IG Staff has written a statement regarding Ms. Goodpasture's review of the Mexico City Station files. After assisting the IG Staff, Ms. Goodpasture joined the LAD Task Force from mid-March to mid-May 1977. Her contribution during that period consisted primarily of the following:

a) Recall, review, and translation from Spanish to English, when not provided, of microfilm of Mexico City Station.
