They are not conducting a specific investigation of any nature and do not desire to meet with host country personnel and wish to avoid all publicity. (S)

In the September 17, 1975, message, the State Department indicated that “Aaron and Truehart are knowledgeable staff members and that it is believed that recipients could be forthcoming in responding to their inquiries.” The September 17, 1975, message instructed, however, that operational details, sources and methods were not construed to be legitimate avenues of inquiry. The September 17, 1975, message noted that both Aaron and Truehart had been briefed by State Department and CIA officials for their visit. (S)

Aaron’s and Truehart’s schedule called for them to be in Brasilia from September 21, 1975, until September 23, 1975, when they would leave for Montevideo. They would arrive in Buenos Aires on September 24, 1975, with Aaron departing on September 26 and Truehart on September 27. It is noted that Ambassador Hill is presently in the U.S. and has sent.
