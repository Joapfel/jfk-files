11 July 1962. Dispatch UFG-1223 (poor copy). Report from Cuban businessman on planned formation of a new group by Manuel Ray Rivero. Manuel Ray, Felipe Pazos, Raúl Chibie, and Ramon Barquin, among others, planning to form a new, yet unnamed organization. Ray has sent Rogelio Caneyos D’Manie to contact various exile personalities for this purpose.

Ray is hoping to get financial assistance from CRC for his new group. He feels CRC is a worn-out organization but a good place to be while the new group gains momentum.

25 July 1962. Cable to Director from San Juan.

23 July 1962. Press interview—Ray announces formation of SURE to include all “popular” factions outside Cuba in an effort to restore splintered movements into an effective working organization.
