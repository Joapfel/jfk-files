Personality File Action Request, 19 October 1960. Manuel Salvat Roque, 201-28605. DBAB: 27 March 1940, Sagua, Cuba.

News article, Miami News, Hal Hendrix, no date. “Red Terrorism Rules at Havana University.”

Three university students escaped after hiding in Havana for two months: Alberto Muller, Ernesto Fernandez Travieso, and Manuel Salvat. Escape was possible under the protection of the Brazilian Embassy. All three blamed Major Rolando Cubela (Cubela Cubela), Castro’s personal choice to head the University Student Federation, and his aide of “murdering” the autonomy of the university. The three, on arrival in Miami, offered their services to the Cuban Democratic Revolutionary Front and wanted to organize an anti-communist student organization within the front.

18 August 1978.
