“Roberto Folder” Periodic Activity Report. Dated (presumably) December 1963.

On 25 November 1963, the agent was asked to report on the reaction to the assassination of President Kennedy by governmental right-wing and left-wing circles. The agent, Roberto, was also asked to report on the status of plans of aggression against Cuba.

Memo to Chief of Station, JMWAVE, from C/SAS/CI. Dated 6 December 1963.

Cites memos of 22 and 29 November 1963 attached, which are not attached to the document. The 22 November 1963 memo reportedly included an intercepted SW message that mentioned Ricardo IRURETA Goyena, who worked at the Hotel Fontainebleau in Miami. The 29 November 1963 memo reportedly contained an intercepted SW message, a letter addressed to Irma SUAREZ in Cuba in both open text and secret writing, and another letter apparently intended for Manolo GARCIA, also in both open text and secret writing.

PACY 1073 (IN 48464), dated 26 October 1963.

Cable from Panama City to Headquarters: “Hope you understand that PONCHAY (Jake Esterline) and Station CI group are dissatisfied with unresolved aspects of HYSAGE Ops., as is Headquarters. Do not, however, at this point, see spectre.”
