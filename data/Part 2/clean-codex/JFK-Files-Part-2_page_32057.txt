NDA 511.

P 20.4.72 MAR 72.

From CO 6th MI Group, Munich, Federal Republic of Germany, to DCSI, USAREUR, Heidelberg, Federal Republic of Germany.

The following message is retransmitted for your information:

09113E FEB 72.

From CO, 6th MI Company, Frankfurt, Federal Republic of Germany, to CO, 6th MI Group, Munich, Federal Republic of Germany.

Subject: Incident of possible CI interest.

1. At 11 hours, 1 February 1972, Lieutenant Haygood, headquarters, 6th USA Artillery Detachment, Soest (MC 3013), Federal Republic of Germany, contacted the Düsseldorf field office to report an incident involving an unidentified person attempting to elicit official information from a member of C Team, 6th USA Artillery Detachment, DA ELN (LC 8424), Federal Republic of Germany.

Action: CI.
