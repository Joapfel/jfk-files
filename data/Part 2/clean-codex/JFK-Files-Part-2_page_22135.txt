Persons/Entities: Edwards Gomez; date: 11/62; category: Castro. King Holcomb; date: 11/62; category: Castro. Bannerman O'Connell.

Occurrence:

O'Connell testified that when Edwards was on leave, once, King spoke to Robert Bannerman (who was not witting of Castro project), and O'Connell had to tell Bannerman that B. was not “privy” to project. O'Connell was embarrassed. (Tr. 36, 99–100.) No one but King, not Gomez or Holcomb, knew (100).

Source: Testimony of James P. O'Connell, 5/30/75.

Staff: John Bayly.

Date: 6/30/75.
