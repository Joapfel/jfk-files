Code. January 11, 1968. Teletype. Urgent.

To SAC New York (100-161801). From: Director, FBI.

Demonstration protesting U.S. policy in Vietnam.

Washington, D.C., January fifteen next. Information concerning internal security.

Bristol, January eleven.

Authority granted for New York 3164-S to accompany demonstrators from New York City for captioned demonstration. Advise informant to immediately contact Washington Field Office, extensions 3904 or 60, promptly upon receipt of important data.

REC-68. 100-4/48-11-35.

Promptly debrief source on return to your area and submit communication suitable for dissemination.

134-9703. RWH:jal (5).

Notes:

NY 2164 S 4S = 4-year-old white male resident of New York who is employed as a photographer reporting on the news in Washington, D.C., since February 1961, and has been an informant since February 1961, and is currently paid up to $300 for services and up to $80 for expenses incurred. Source has furnished information on the oppressive Labor Movement, Casa de La Americas, the Cuban Mission to the UN, and the Student Committee for Travel to Cuba. Demonstration in Washington on January 15 is sponsored by the Jeanette Rankin Brigade with the support of various women's pacifist groups. The demonstrators expect to petition Congress when they reconvene on January 15.

January 12, 1968. 62JAN18 1968.

Sent by coded teletype. For immediate release to the press.
