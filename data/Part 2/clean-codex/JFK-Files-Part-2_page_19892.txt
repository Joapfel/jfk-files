May 1, 1972

NR 010 CV Code  
9:35 PM, urgent, 5/1/72, RJB

To: Director

From: Cleveland (100-NEW) 2P

Women, halting abortion activation continuing.

March and rally to repeal all anti-abortion laws, sponsored by Adoption Project Coalition (APC), Cleveland, Ohio, May sixth, next. Internal Security—New Left.

On instant date, reliable source furnished leaflet announcing captioned march and rally. According to leaflet, supporters of APC are to assemble at twelve noon at the New Federal Building, East Ninth Street and Lakeside. The marchers will then proceed south on East Ninth Street to Euclid Avenue, west on Euclid Avenue to Public Square, and then to the Old Federal Office Building at the corner of Superior Avenue and Public Square, where the rally will be held. Indication march and rally will be other than peaceful.

Cleveland, Ohio PD, local Secret Service, and General Services Administration building management advised.

Included in letter to White House and Attorney General. Date 5/2/72.
