F.B.I. Criminal Division, Bureau of Investigation.

August 27, 1971.

Date: August 27, 1971.

Michael Joseph Doyle was presented an Interrogation—Advice of Rights form by SA John R. Gingerich and advised by SA Gingerich that an interview was desired regarding the burglary of the Selective Service Offices at the U.S. Courthouse and Post Office Building, 4th and Market Streets, Camden, N.J., in the early morning of August 22, 1971. Doyle read the Interrogation—Advice of Rights form, advised he fully understood the contents of the form, and thereby signed the form.

Doyle advised he did not wish to answer any questions.

The following description was obtained from observation and interview:

Name: Michael Joseph Doyle  
Aliases: None  
Date of Birth: November 3, 1934  
Place of Birth: Longford County, Ireland  
Height: 5'7"  
Weight: 136 pounds  
Build: Slender  
Hair: Auburn  
Eyes: Blue  
Scars and Marks: None  
Address: 2907 Federal Street, Camden, N.J.  
Occupation: Catholic priest—12 years  
Employment: Catholic priest—St. Joseph's Pro-Cathedral, Camden, N.J.  
Father: John Doyle, born May 14, 1898, at Longford County, Ireland. Deceased 1967.  
Mother: Rose Creegan Doyle—Longford County, Ireland.  
Brothers: Patrick Doyle—Longford County, Ireland; John Doyle—Catholic priest, Brigantine, New Jersey.
