The individuals interviewed stated they never saw any documents nor heard any information that LHO was a CIA agent while stationed in Tokyo. Only after being given the cable publication and the Serrison investigation in the late 60s were they aware of the alleged allegation.

Subjects 2, 7, and 8 expressed belief that LHO may have been recruited by the KGB during his tour of duty in Japan. They based this belief on the fact that during the period 1933–35 of their stay in Japan, the EIP (Station) in Tokyo had identified a KGB program specifically addressing cleared cases to recruit U.S. military personnel in Tokyo. However, they had no information that the recruitment of LHO by the KGB did not take place.
