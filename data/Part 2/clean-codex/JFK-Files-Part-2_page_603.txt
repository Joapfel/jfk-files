17 June 1967. M 92302. Director JMWAVE. PBRUMEN.

1. LILING-4 received a phone call 4 June from a certain “DELVA” asking for LILING-9. When told LILING-9 was not at LILING, “DELVA” left a message to have L-S contact her in room 909 of the Maria Isabel Hotel. This information was confirmed by LIENVOY the same day.

2. Discreet check at the Maria Isabel identified the occupant of room 909 as Sandra Linda Contreras, U.S. citizen giving home address as New York, no other details. Contreras checked out of the hotel early afternoon 14 June. Station checking air manifests that date to New York. Would appreciate traces including LNERGO.

LILING-3. 19 June 1967.
