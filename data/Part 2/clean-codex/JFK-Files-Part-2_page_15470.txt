United States Government

Memorandum

To: W. C. Sullivan

From: W. R. Wannall

Date: April 30, 1963

Subject: Technical Surveillance Coverage of Diplomatic Establishments

With approval of the Attorney General (1/31/62), we instituted a program to expand use of technical surveillances on diplomatic establishments of countries outside the Soviet bloc. Following is an analysis of this program and its current status:

We advised the Attorney General on 1/26/62 that on a selective and probing basis we would explore coverage of the following countries under this program by instituting coverage, on six or eight establishments at a time:

Argentina; Brazil; Republic of Congo; Guinea; India; Indonesia; Japan; Laos; Mali; Mexico; Morocco; Panama; Portugal; Thailand; Venezuela; South Vietnam.

After necessary surveys and State clearance in each instance, initial coverage was initiated in early March 1962, and to date coverage on the following establishments has been instituted:

Embassy of Argentina; Embassy of Brazil; Brazilian Delegation to the United Nations (UN); Congolese Delegation to UN; Embassy of Guinea; Guinea Delegation to UN; Embassy of India; Indian Delegation to UN; Embassy of Indonesia; Indonesian Delegation to UN; Embassy of Japan; Embassy of Mali; Embassy of Mexico; Mexican Delegation to UN; Embassy of Panama; Embassy of Portugal; Portuguese Delegation to UN; Embassy of Venezuela; Venezuelan Delegation to UN.

Of the above-listed establishments, as of this date we have productive coverage on the following (others discontinued as unproductive):

Embassy of Argentina; Embassy of Brazil; Embassy of Mexico; Embassy of Panama; Embassy of Venezuela. REC-52.
