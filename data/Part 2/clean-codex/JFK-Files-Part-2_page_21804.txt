1. Mr. Wides. Chicago?

2. Mr. Sarno. No, sir.

3. Mr. Wides. Anyplace on the West Coast or the Southwest?

4. Mr. Sarno. No, sir.

5. Mr. Wides. Boston?

6. Mr. Sarno. No, sir.

7. Mr. Wides. So you only recall persons who had been in AMUTS telling—why don't we refer to him as San Jenis?

8. That they were going to another city as being New York and San Juan?

9. Mr. Sarno. New York and San Juan are the only two I ever recall they ever had any AMUTS or ex-AMUTS or people that had been in the organization went.

10. Mr. Wides. Now when they went and they recruited other people, was that San Jenis' suggestion or under his supervision?

11. Mr. Sarno. They usually collected a group around people that they trusted.

12. Mr. Wides. Was that up to San Jenis' supervision and direction?

13. Mr. Sarno. He directed them to do it, yes. I didn't even know the individual, I never met him. It's one individual.
