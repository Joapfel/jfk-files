NR001 NY Plain. 1:15 AM, NITEL, 07-17-72, JPM. To Acting Director. From New York (100-174481).

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) conference, 7/15 and 16/72, Hunter College, NYC. IS-C (Trotskyist).

RENYTEL, 7/15/72.

A confidential source advised that, in the late second session of the third WNAAC conference, convened at 9:00 AM at Hunter College, NYC, the morning session from 9:00 to 11:00 AM included organizational workshops not covered at the 7/15 session, such as “press work,” “fund raising,” and “strengthening (STRINGTHINING) of local coalitions.” The agenda from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM, termination of the conference, was scheduled as a plenary session whereby all work performed at the two-day conference was expected to culminate in a unified national women's campaign set of proposals favorable to WNAAC.

REC-71.

Source stated, however, that a split (SPLID) developed in the ranks of WNAAC between the Socialist Workers Party (SWP) faction, which had predominated in controlling the conference, and Independence—all others representing the non-SWP faction. Source stated at the end of page one.

July 21, 1972.
