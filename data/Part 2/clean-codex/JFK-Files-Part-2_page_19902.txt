6/19/72

To SAC, Detroit (134-3030)

From Acting Director, FBI (134-22865)

DE TWO EIGHT THREE ONE DASH S; SM - DWAAC.

Redet el June fifteen, last.

Authority granted advance informant amount not to exceed one hundred thirty-five dollars for expenses, including round-trip transportation, to cover meeting of Women's Political Caucus (WPC), Washington, D.C., June twenty-four to twenty-five, next. Obtain exact accounting of expenses from informant.

WFO and Detroit coordinate code name of informant and identity of special agent and contact telephone number through which informant can maintain contact while in Washington, D.C. Detroit instruct informant to maintain contact with WFO in event particularly significant information developed which should be submitted by teletype. Detroit instruct informant be particularly alert for extent of Socialist Workers Party (SWP) influence or control over meeting and identities of SWP members in attendance.

Detroit contact informant immediately upon return and submit detailed LMH with airtel cover.
