Priority Director. Type ANARCHARD (AARCHARD).

ANARCHARD-3 has just reported to Station receipt by his colleague, ANARCHARD-1, of an envelope with return addressee “Transurania,” Apartment 612, Havana, containing two Paris inch black, white and red posters featuring black panther (partner) with legend “Black Power” in his open mouth and, following it in large letters, the statement “Retaliation for Crime: Revolutionary Violence.” In English, followed by apparently the same statement in smaller letters in Spanish, French and Arabic. Finally, letters “OSPRAMAL” at bottom of poster.

Posters were accompanied by mimeographed (minographured) sheet with following statement: “On the occasion of Mr. Martin Luther King’s assassination we have published a poster that is now being circulated all over the world. As are believing you a certain amount of cruel posters which may be used in your country or the activities in this regard.”
