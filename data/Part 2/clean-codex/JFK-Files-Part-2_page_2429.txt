Cite DCU/MIAI 15377.

To DCU/Headquarters.

Attn: (08 (French).

Subject: Case 65695.

Ref: DCU/Headquarters 15695.

1. I met Bruce Berckmans at his Jackknife office this morning as scheduled. Berckmans began the interview by requesting that I read a multi-space letter which he is thinking of sending to the Inspector General. It is a detailed statement of grievances stemming from his forced resignation and indicates that he is considering a suit against the Agency. He has in fact retained counsel, apparently another former Agency staffer, but they have not yet decided whether to proceed with legal action. Other elements should probably be included.

2. The previously unknown Berckmans, who is fond of saying that he resigned from the Agency but not the United States, offered to serve as a fairly typical DCD contact with respect to the information he derives in his regular Jackknife duties. Berckmans reportedly has many useful contacts, especially related to Latin America and soon in the Far East. Under normal circumstances this might be a useful informational relationship.

3. I told Berckmans, however, that in view of the circumstances described in paragraph 1 above I felt it impossible for us to maintain any systematic relationship at this time. Should the decision not to proceed with his suit and other things fall into place, perhaps a future useful contact could be established.

4. In describing his duties Berckmans did drop one minor piece of information which might be useful against the current Jamaican situation. Through a subcontractor his company is consulting a contract in Jamaica which would provide customs services for Revere Bauxite Holdings, which the company is apparently planning to not release.
