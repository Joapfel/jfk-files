Memorandum for: Special Contracting Officer

Subject: Project termination of Ernest G. Maycrink (p) as independent contractor for SR Division

Reference: SR/DOB/59-1735 dated 17 September 1959; SR/DOB/59-1660 dated 24 July 1959

1. Because of the indefinite postponement of Project AESENTINEL, under which the subject individual was engaged, the subject has been terminated by this office on 20 October 1960.

2. The subject individual will be picked up on contract on 21 October 1960 by Africa Division. The subject was paid on 20 October 1960 by this office all monies remaining due him as per the Engagement of Independent Contractor Agreement for Project AESENTINEL. The subject has no further remunerative claim against the U.S. Government.
