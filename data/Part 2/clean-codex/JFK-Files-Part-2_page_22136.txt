Persons/Entities: Houston. Dates: ’50–’60; ’60s; assume new date. Categories: Castro.

Occurrence:

Houston testified that he cannot remember that his legal advice as to not continuing a project was ever rejected by CIA (47), although Houston recalls vetoing two propositions: one, to train personnel for foreign election work by training in domestic political parties; and, two, to monitor long-distance telephone calls from Latin America to Canada and to New York (47-7).

Source: Testimony of Lawrence Houston, 6/2/75.

Staff: John Bayly.

Date: 6/30/75.
