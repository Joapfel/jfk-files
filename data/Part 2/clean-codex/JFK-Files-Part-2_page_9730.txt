The following is basically the program of the National Revolutionary Army EXACO, as it was released to T.W.B.I. and news services in early April 1974. It is only a “beef” version of some of our goals, and at a much later date a version will be released that will totally and thoroughly explain our political platform.

1. All non-Whites lose citizenship and prepare for repatriation, i.e., separated society until transportation can be arranged.

2. Federal Reserve abolished and all illegal tender returned and replaced with legal notes to those persons who are legal U.S. citizens.

3. Israel immediately declared an illegal enemy state.

4. Immediate deportation of all Jews—racial or religious.

5. Income tax abolished. Product taxation and industrial support for the national budget.

6. Immediate cancellation of the national debt.

7. Immediate elimination of foreign, Jewish, and enemy control of banking and financial institutions.

8. Immediate seizure of all properties and interests of all Jewish people and/or enemies of America.

9. Immediate reinstatement of a healthy racial standard.

10. Abolition of foreign aid until necessary determination can be investigated.

11. Immediate release of all political prisoners presently being held by the enemy government.

12. Immediate and total revision of the judiciary system.

13. Immediate reorganization and/or elimination of all useless government agencies.

14. Immediate reorganization and/or elimination of the welfare and all other federal “giveaway” government agencies.

15. Immediate arrests and rapid trials of America’s enemies and those persons who have willfully conspired to “sell out” America’s working masses. Harsh punishments to those convicted.

16. Protection by the government of citizens against illegal seizure of personal property and monies by alien users.

17. Right to work and receive a fair wage to healthfully maintain all citizens and their families.
