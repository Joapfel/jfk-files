Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

May 1, 1972.

Teletype. NR 010 CV Code. 9:35 p.m., urgent, 5/1/72, RJB.

To Director.

From Cleveland (100-NEW) 2P.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition, Cleveland, Ohio, May sixth, next. Internal Security—New Left.

On instant date, reliable source furnished leaflet announcing captioned march and rally. According to leaflet, supporters of APC are to assemble at twelve noon at the New Federal Building, East Ninth Street and Lakeside. The marchers will then proceed south on East Ninth Street to Euclid Avenue, west on Euclid Avenue to Public Square, and then to the old Federal Office Building at the corner of Superior Avenue and Public Square, where the rally will be held. SOR REC. 21. EX-116 indicates the march and rally will be other than peaceful.

Cleveland, Ohio Police Department, local Secret Service, and General Services Administration building management advised.

May 4, 1972.
