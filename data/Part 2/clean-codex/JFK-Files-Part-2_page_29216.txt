Kennedy

Outside Contact Report.

Date: 6-1-78. Time: 3:00 PM.

I. Identifying Information:

Phone: 504-835-4860. Name: Juanita Brown. Address: 561 Andrews St., Metairie, La. Type of contact: Telephone; person.

II. Summary of Contact:

Mrs. Brown works in the payroll department of the William B. Rielly (Reilly) Coffee Co. She worked there when Lee Harvey Oswald worked for the company. Mrs. Brown and other ladies used to go to Adrain (Adrian) Alba's garage next to the company to buy a cold drink because the company did not have a drink machine. Occasionally several of the ladies would go together on their break. Mrs. Brown remembers that on a couple of occasions Oswald was in the garage office. She did not talk to him, nor does she remember him ever saying anything in her presence. She couldn't remember any of the other ladies at the time of this contact, but she will call back if she recalls any of the names.

End of Memo.

III. Recommended Follow-up (if any):

None.

Signature: Robert Brown.
