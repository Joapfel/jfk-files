(12) 10 October 1965. Dispatch from COS/JMWAVE.

Panegue terminated as of 31 October 1965. Agent service record recommends him for re-use on the basis of his knowledge of interwar contacts in Northern Orient.

(13) 15 September 1977. W Managua #095-686.

Panegue participated in Cuban episode activities in the New York area in 1962 and later registered in ISR, Department, and Army in August 1962. Dropped without prejudice 18 November 1963. In 1964 Panegue was a member of the MDC X infiltration team. Recruited by Miami station as radio operator/enfantree and favorably swirled on 5 August 1964. POA granted 13 November 1964. Paid $275 monthly beginning 1 September 1964. Trained by Cuban agents in Miami but never used operationally. Amicably terminated 1 October 1965 and recommended for re-use. Reported in 1969 as conducting clandestine trips made Cuba on behalf of Junta Militar de Cuba.
