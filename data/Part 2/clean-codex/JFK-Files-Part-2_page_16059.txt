Federal Bureau of Investigation

June 10, 1969.

After being advised of the identities and official capacities of the arresting officers, Bruce Allan Dickson was arrested for harboring a Federal fugitive at 5:58 A.M., June 4, 1969, at 2350 West Madison, Chicago, Illinois. He was immediately orally advised of his rights as contained in the “Interrogation; Advice of Rights; Your Rights” Form. He declined to make a statement and refused to give his identity. He was then transported in Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) Radio Car Number 180 to FBI Headquarters, 219 South Dearborn Street, Chicago, for processing by Special Agents Kully V. England and Richard S. Swensen.

At FBI Headquarters, Dickson was again advised of his rights as contained in the “Your Rights” Form, and he stated he understood his rights, but refused to be interviewed other than giving his name and his home address, which he listed as 2350 West Madison, Chicago, Illinois.

The following is a description of the subject obtained through interview and observation:

Name: Bruce Allan Dickson. Date of birth: Refused to give. Place of birth: Refused to give. Former employment: Janitor. Residence: 2350 West Madison, Chicago, Illinois. Race: Negro. Sex: Male. Height: 6 feet, 1 inch. Weight: 165–170 pounds. Eyes: Brown. Hair: Black. Build: Medium. Complexion: Light. Scars: 9-inch scar on upper left arm with stitching scars on either side; 1-inch scar on left shoulder; ½-inch circular scar inside elbows; ½-inch circular scar on back of elbows.

On 6/4/69 at Chicago, Illinois. File number.

SA Kully V. England and SA Richard S. Swensen/RSS. Date dictated: 6/10/69.
