Persons/entities: General Don, Ambassador Nolting, Conein. Date: July 1963. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: On July 4, 1963, Conein received a message from Don, Commander of I Corps at the time, to meet with him in the Caravelle Hotel for a public meeting, and the hotel was jam-packed. Conein states: “It was at that time on the 4th of July that I had the first indication that something serious was being contemplated, when General Don told me that if the Buddhist situation does not get settled, that the military are planning. I reported this fact, and I believe it was around the 8th or 9th of July, that more indications...” p. 20.

Source: Hearings, SSCI, June 20, 1975, Conein. Staff: R. Dawson. Date: 30 June 1975.

Persons/entities: General Duong Van Minh, General Tran Van Don, Major General Le Van Kim, General Tran Thien Khiem. Date: 7/63. Category: Diem.

Occurrence: The initial coup decision and agreement among the coup principals occurred later. General Vu Van Thanh has been the identifiable leader of the coup principals from the outset. General Tran Van Don has been General Mau’s deputy and principal officer at this point, to route his subordinates and unit civil soldiers. Major Hsu Nam was General Le Van Kim’s, probably the most intelligent of the competitors, who was responsible for aligning himself with Nguyen Thi Psylla’s parties and developing political planning. General Tran Nguyen Thien’s role was to issue necessary orders to support him and to act as Chief of Staff to General Mau during the plotting after the coup.

Source: Hearings, S.S.C.I., 6/20/75, Conein, Exhibit one, page 10. Staff: Dawson. Date: 6/30/75.
