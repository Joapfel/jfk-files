Following is a resume of points covered during more than six hours of off-the-record discussions with Congressman Dale Milford (D-Texas) on 25 October 1975. Because of fever, COS had not been released from hospital and discussions were therefore held at the hospital sitting room.

1. Congressman Milford opened the discussion by reiterating concerns surfaced the previous afternoon. In addition to his referent A assessment, he stated that he believed that the first phase of the committee report will state that:

A. Duplication of intel missions resulted in multichannel forwarding of information.

B. Bureaucratic inefficiency resulted in delays.
