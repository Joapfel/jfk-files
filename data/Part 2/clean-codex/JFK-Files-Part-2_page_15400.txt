November 30, 1959

Mr. A. H. Belmont

Mr. J. A. Frombosch

Foreign Political Matter — Cuba. Internal Security — Cuba.

Reference is made to the memorandum from Mr. Donahoe to you dated November 23, 1959. In connection with the Bureau's control program aimed in developing live sources in diplomatic establishments of countries outside the Soviet bloc with a view to developing investigative and intelligence data in “trouble-spot areas,” the Director approved a recommendation that Liaison discuss with State the problem of developing live informants in Cuban establishments in the U.S.

On November 30, 1959, Mr. Bartlett of the Liaison Section discussed instant matter with Mr. J. P. McDonnell, Bureau of Security and Consular Affairs, Department of State. Mr. McDonnell advised that the Department of State had no objection to the Bureau attempting to develop live sources within the Cuban establishments in the U.S. Mr. McDonnell was advised that the Bureau would check with State on individual cases as to clearance for specific interviews. Mr. McDonnell said this would be completely in accord with the State Department thinking.

Action: For information. Appropriate instructions will be issued to the field.
