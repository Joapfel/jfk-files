Date: 23 November 1963. File: P-3593, Silvia Duran P-. LITEMPO/Prod.

To: Immediate DIR. Info: KAPOK. Ref: DIR 84888. Cite: MEXI 7020.

1. Saw photos of Lee Oswald on television night of 22 November and it was obvious photos sent to Dallas were not identified with Lee Oswald held Dallas. Dates are as given on photos.

2. MEXI station reviewing all available photos of persons entering Soviet and Cuban embassies.

3. MEXI suggesting to LITEMPO 2 that Silvia Duran, who was a Mexican employee at Cuban Embassy and who put Lee Oswald on telephone from Cuban Embassy to talk to Soviet Embassy on 28 September, be arrested immediately and held incommunicado until she gives all details of Oswald known to her. (See report.)

4. LITEMPO 2 can say D.F.S. coverage revealed call to him if he needs to explain.

End of message.

Coordinating officers. Releasing officer. Authenticating officer.
