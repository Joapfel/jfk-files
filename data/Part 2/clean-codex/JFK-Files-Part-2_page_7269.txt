252234Z September 74. Staff. CITE Buenos Aires 34503, final section of 2. To: Immediate Director. Info: Montevideo, Brasilia, Rio de Janeiro. RYBAT PLVWCADET.

10. Considerable interest shown in subject of terrorism. We described two principal terrorist groups, Montoneros and ERP, and stressed difficulties of penetrating these groups. AARON said that Chargé d’Affaires had mentioned to him the case of a source from another area who had provided coverage in this area. (AMB aware of coverage by PJTENNIS-3 from time of his first visit and had mentioned it to MONTLLOR.) We explained how a source such as this can establish good contacts at higher level and provide good information.

We stressed that our interest was in collecting information bearing on security of U.S. personnel and property. AARON asked whether we would provide to liaison information from our agent which would enable them to capture terrorists during a meeting with our agent. Answer: no, for several obvious reasons, which we spelled out.

11. Evening 25 September AARON appeared to have recovered from illness, went shopping with TRUEHEART. Because Buenos Aires stores will be closed 26 September and because AARON not leaving.
