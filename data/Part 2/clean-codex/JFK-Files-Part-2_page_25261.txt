Persons/entities: Gen. Mai Huu Xuan Nhu. Date: 11/2/63, 1130 hours. Category: Diem.

General Mai Huu Kuan (Xuan Nhu) led the detail which located Diem and Hsu at a villa on Pung Fung Street in Cholon. Kuan returned to the JCC villa after their bodies. Cause of death at this time is unknown.
