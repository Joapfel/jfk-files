Report of SW and 7 1/2 S Training.

Date: 27 October 1955.

1. Pseudonym: Joseph B. Smith.
2. Office, Division, Branch.
3. Project assignment: Malaya Station.
4. Dates trained: 10 October 1955. Location: Singapore.
5. Training received: Basic SW: briefing in use of low-level systems for drop-drop was only. P.S.: No restoration. Other.
6. Evaluation: Smith was able to spend only a couple of hours in the briefing session. During this time, he was able to use all wave systems assigned for station use and, since he will be able to get help from Samuel J. THEYNH (g.v.) if necessary, he should have no difficulty with these. Further training would be required if mail channels or higher-level systems were to be used.
7. Instructor: Ulysses A. CROOKS/COOK.

Chief, KURIOT Laboratory.
