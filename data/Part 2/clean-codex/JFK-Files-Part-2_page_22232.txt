Persons/entities: Gen. Lansdale, Gilpatrick, Don Wilson. Dates: October 31, 1962. Categories: MONGODB, SGA, CUBA.

Occurrence:

Gilpatrick asked Lansdale to give him a brief outline of Operation MONGOOSE in light of current U.S./USSR negotiations regarding Cuba. Lansdale talked to Bruce Cheever and “sketched in the purpose, which was to get a fix for Gilpatrick as one of the U.S. negotiators with the Soviets on whether or not all actions (sabotage, etc.) had come to a stop—didn't want the U.S. embarrassed and Gilpatrick wanted to be sure of this.”

Source: October 31, 1962 memo for the record by Gen. Lansdale (00744).

Staff: Wallach. Date: July 3, 1975.
