22 Oct 85: 23083.

TYPIC LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 8839 (N 20149).

1. LIRING-2 reported to ROWTON 19 Oct 66 he had made contact with CECILIA CARMEN MARQUEZ DE ROSSIE after the latter arrived on a CUBANA flight from Havana 17 Oct 66.

2. LIRING-3 stated LIRING-4 had met CECILIA at the plane but that the latter had been so open in her denigration (DENUGATION) of the CASTRO regime that LIRING-3 told LIRING-4 to break contact for fear of jeopardizing her position. LIRING-4 noted CECILIA was contacted at the hotel by Cuban refugees of “doubtful loyalty” and that CECILIA, after these contacts, was seen to have a large sum of U.S. dollars in her possession. LIRING-4 reported she saw one quantity amounting to five hundred.

3. In view of LIRING-3’s present status with LIRING-2, LIRING-3 confirmed with ROWTON that he should report the facts about CECILIA to LIRING-2. He believes that to withhold such information might work to his disadvantage were it to become known to Cuban Embassy personnel.

4. CECILIA reported to LIRING-4 that Cubans had bumped her off the plane 9 Oct 66 on the pretext that there was no room. They did not arrest or question her.
