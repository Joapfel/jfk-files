Incoming Telegram, Department of State.

Control: 13542. Received: August 17, 1964, 6:19 a.m. From: Tokyo. Action: SECSTATE 526. Date: August 17, 2 p.m.

Roger Channel.

Personal for McCone from Reischauer.

We will be reporting in another form our analysis of the case of the two Soviet musicians. I just want to tell you, however, that we would not repeat not have been able to get the results we did if it had not been for Pete Wheeler. He was on the job within minutes after the men walked in, got his Russian-speaking people working quickly, and helped to handle the whole affair with efficiency and dispatch. His experience in Japan and the confidence which the Japanese authorities showed in working with him were a decisive factor in the definite change which took place over the weekend in the Japanese attitude on how to handle cases like this.

His contribution has been major and the results will be long-lived. This is only another example of the service Wheeler has performed here over the years. We are sorry to see him leave and will miss him very much.

GP-1.

BA

Reischauer
