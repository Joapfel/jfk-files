Cuba.

Central "Non" Radio Transmitting Station, Rancho Boyeros Airport, Habana Provincia (O).

19–20 January 1962. Rancho Boyeros, January 1962. O-3. MUJ 316/37-1-62. 30 January 1962. Kone. MUJ. MUJ. MUJ-39-62.

1. Sub-source reports that the Government of Cuba had planned to inaugurate a new seatour radio transmitting station at the Rancho Boyeros Airport, south of Matanzas City, on 19 or 20 January 1962.

2. The purpose of the station was to be communication between Cuba and Tezacoalpa, Honduras (Houduras), on the 9077 frequency.
