SE Study 75.

Teletype.

REBUTEL to Alexandria et al., September 17, 1975, and Memphis teletype to the Bureau, September 18, 1975.

Numerous attempts made to contact former Special Agent Robert F. O'Niell, 2551 Iroquois, Detroit, Michigan, telephone number 571-2296, on September 18, 1975, with negative results.

On September 19, 1975, Mr. O'Niell was contacted by ASAC Jay E. Bailey, at which time he was advised of the pertinent contents of REBUTEL. He advised that he only served about one year as a security supervisor in the Detroit office and that he had doubts as to whether he had any information of interest to the Senate Select Committee (SSC). He stated, however, that should he be contacted by a representative of SSC, he will promptly contact the Legal Counsel Division for appropriate guidelines and would closely adhere thereto.
