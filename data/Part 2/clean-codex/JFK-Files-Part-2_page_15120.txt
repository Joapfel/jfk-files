III. Statistics re Hijackings Since January 1, 1968

A. Hijackings

To Cuba: 1968—17; 1969—31; 1970—5; total—53. Other destination: 1969—2; 1970—1; total—3. Aborted: 1968—3; 1969—6; 1970—5; total—14. Total: 1968—20; 1969—39; 1970—11; total—70.

A firearm was used in 53 of the 70 incidents, and in 6 instances there was gunfire. In 1 of the incidents, 1 death and 2 injuries resulted. In the 2nd, 2 individuals were wounded. In the remaining 4, no one was injured.

B. Individuals Involved

Latin: 1968—17; 1969—21; 1970—2; total—40. Fleeing felons: 1968—3; 1969—7; total—10. Black militants: 1968—3; 1969—3; total—6. Others: 1968—8; 1969—26; 1970—10; total—44. Total: 1968—31; 1969—57; 1970—12; total—100.

Includes one black militant.
