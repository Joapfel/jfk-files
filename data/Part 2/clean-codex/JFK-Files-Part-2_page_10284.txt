FR 73-183

8 May 1973

Memorandum for: Deputy Director for Operations

Subject: Foreign Resources Division operational activities with possible flap potential

Reference: FR Memorandum, 73-180, dated 7 May 1973, same subject

1. The answers to your questions are as follows:

a. Question: Do we recruit Americans?

Answer: Yes, we recruit Americans to be used as academic support assets and access agents. These Americans are used for spotting and assessment purposes only and do not perform any recruitments.

Paragraph 1. b. and 1. c. are deleted because they contain sensitive agency operational methods and techniques.
