DYVOUR 4

Meeting 1 Aug with Luis Ferre produced foll:

A. Ferre had conversation 25 July with Frank Chavez, AFL-CIO leader in Puerto Rico. Chavez proposed Ferre go Dom Rep, act as catalyst between conflicting forces, and usher in democratic government; argued that a man of Ferre's stature could serve in place of OAS commission. Ferre favorably inclined in principle. Ferre in contact with Carlos Chardon, well-known Puerto Rican agronomist who now in DomRep at invitation of Balaguer in connection with agrarian reforms. Chardon favors idea of Ferre mediation. (Comment: Ferre requested nothing; Yauger volunteered nothing. FOR has info Yauger expects talk next with Ferre & Aug).

B. Chavez had just returned from seeing Arturo Espaillat in Canada. As long-time friend of Espaillat, Chavez claims credit for getting Ramfis Trujillo release Espaillat. Chavez also friendly with Ramfis who, according to Chavez, favors democratization of DomRep but wants to leave country, liquidating family assets at ten cents on the dollar. Assets in DomRep estimated at 800 million dollars. Ferre commented that in the long run it would be cheaper for Dominicans
