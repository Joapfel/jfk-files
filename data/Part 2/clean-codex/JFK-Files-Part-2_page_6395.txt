18 July 1996

Memorandum for: Chief/VAB/OS

From: Karen Gilbert, Public Communication Branch, Public Affairs Staff

Subject: Headquarters Visitors—Presidential JFK Assassination Records Review Board

1. On Monday, July 22, 1996, seven members of the Presidential JFK Assassination Records Review Board (see attached sheet) will be visiting the Agency from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., along with two members of the Center for the Study of Intelligence.

2. They will arrive in three personal cars. They have been directed to arrive at the VCC at 9:45 a.m. for badging and parking directions. They will be met at the OHB lobby by Karen Gilbert, Public Liaison Officer, Public Communications Office. They will be given a tour of the first floor of the OHB and the Exhibit Center and then taken to the EAA Store. Following this, they will depart.

3. For Security/Access: Please notify the Headquarters Receptionists and the Security Officers at the Main Gate of the date and time of arrival. Please badge our visitors with Visitor Escort Required badges at the Visitor Control Center and direct them to the Old Headquarters Building lobby entrance and to VIP parking.

4. For Office of Medical Services: Please add them to your visitors list for 22 July 1996.

5. For Protective Programs Group: Please inform the Security Officers at the 123 Entrance that the cars will be dropping off the group at the OHB lobby entrance and direct them to parking in the VIP lot.

6. If you have any further questions prior to the day of the visit, please contact Karen Gilbert at x51744. On the date of the visit, please direct any questions or problems to the Public Communications Branch at 482-0623.

Karen Gilbert
