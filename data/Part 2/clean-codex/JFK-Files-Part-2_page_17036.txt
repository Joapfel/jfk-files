United States Government Memorandum

To: SAC, Chicago  
Date: March 10, 1970  
From: SA Roy Martin Mitchell  
Subject: Black Panther Party — RM-BPP

Who has provided reliable information in the past, will not testify, but attests to the accuracy of the following information.

Advised as follows on the indicated dates:

March 2, 1970

Willie Calvin, Raymond Hewitt, Clarence Terry, travel BPP leaders.

Calvin was assigned to be Hewitt and companion known only as “T.C.,” security guard when they came through.
