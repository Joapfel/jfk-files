3 September 1966. N89335.

Reference: Mexico City 23108, ALK 79989.

1. Wallace B. Rowton and Liring/3 held brief meetings 2 September 1966 following Liring/3's return from Havana 29 August. Below are some highlights of Liring/3's ten-day trip.

2. Arnold (Arnol) Rodriguez Camps holds a key position in the Ministry of Sugar, although he is not actually minister. He expects to travel to socialist countries, probably Poland and Czechoslovakia, in the near future in order to arrange future purchases of sugar.

3. On his own initiative, Rodriguez told Liring/3 that Liring/4 should be employed at the Cuban Embassy in Mexico City. Apparently, the fact that she is already working there is not known in Havana. Rodriguez said Liring/4 should not be given diplomatic status since this would jeopardize her status as Liring/3. Rather, she will work ostensibly as a volunteer. Liring/3 has never admitted to Rowton that Liring/4 is working in the embassy.
