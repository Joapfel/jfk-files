Dear Friend:

Let's paraphrase Pastor Martin Niemöller (Pastro Martin Niemoller's) description of Nazi Germany:

First they came for the Panthers. But I was not a Panther, so I did not defend them.

Then they came for the student activists.

Then they came for the black people. But I was not black, so I did not defend them.

Then they came for trade unions. But I was not a trade unionist, so I did not defend them.

Then they came for the teachers. But I was not a teacher, so I did not defend them.
