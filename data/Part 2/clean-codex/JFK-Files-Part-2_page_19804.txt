February 11–13, 1972, Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (WONACLAC) Convention, Boston, Massachusetts. IS-C (Trotskyist).

A source who has provided reliable information in the past advised on 2-12-72 that the 2-12-72 session of the WONAAC convention at Boston University, Boston, Massachusetts, began at 10:00 AM with services and a general assembly at Marsh Chapel, Boston University, which ran until 11:00 AM.

EX-103 REC-35 100-468406.

The remainder of the day consisted of workshops held in various Boston University classrooms, followed by another general assembly in Hayden Hall, Boston University, during the later evening hours.

The day’s events were without incident. An estimated 800 to 1,000 individuals were in attendance during the day’s proceedings. The convention is scheduled to reconvene on 2-13-72 and to run through late evening that date.
