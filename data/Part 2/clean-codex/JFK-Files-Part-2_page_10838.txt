United States Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Investigation.

Copy to: 1-USA, Newark.

Report of: Robert H. Waller (A). Date: September 2, 1971. Office: Newark, New Jersey. Field Office File No. 89-56.

Title: Dale Speers Cunningham; Special Agent Robert H. Waller—victim.

Character: Assault on a federal officer and obstruction of justice.

Synopsis: Dale Speers Cunningham, FBI No. 49-200-G, arrested by Bureau agents, Camden, New Jersey, on August 27, 1971. Cunningham charged in complaint authorized by AUSA D. William Subin and filed before U.S. Magistrate Charles L. Rudd, Camden, New Jersey, with violation of Title 18, U.S.C., Sections 11 and 1505. Cunningham afforded hearing before U.S. Magistrate and remanded to custody of U.S. Marshal, Camden, New Jersey, in lieu of $50,000 bail. Cunningham appeared before U.S. District Judge Mitchell H. Cohen, Camden, New Jersey, on August 30, 1971; bail reduced to $10,000 and remanded to custody of U.S. Marshal. FBI record reflects previous arrest in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, on May 29, 1967, for resisting arrest and disorderly conduct.
