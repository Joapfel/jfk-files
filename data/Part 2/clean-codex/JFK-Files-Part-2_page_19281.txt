No other stops between Newark and Washington. Train will depart Washington seven ten PM, January fifteenth next, arrive Newark ten forty-five PM, arrive New York City eleven PM.

Martin stated a group from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, organized by one Ethel Taylor, telephone number L O four five five seven zero, has reserved three coaches on regular train departing Philadelphia eight ten AM, January fifteenth next, arriving Washington, DC ten twenty AM.
