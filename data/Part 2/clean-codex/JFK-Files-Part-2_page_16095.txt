Teletypе.

To: Director, FBI.  
From: SAC, Chicago (P).

Black Panther Party. RM-BPP.

Source, reliable in past, advised instant Bobby Rush, Deputy Minister of Defense, Illinois chapter, BFP (BPP), to make news announcement instant to local media that Panthers have independent pathologist report indicating Fred Hampton (deceased Deputy Chairman, Illinois BFP (BPP), as result of Illinois State's Attorney's police raid December four last) had received a drug through eating approximately eight hours before death. Indication is Panthers will not cooperate with Illinois Cook County Coroner's Grand Jury scheduled to convene January six next, twenty-six hundred South California; however, will submit pathologist report. Rush also called for BFP (BPP) supporters to appear for demonstration at above address January six next.

No violence anticipated or known planned by BFP (BPP).

Appropriate agencies cognizant.

RMM
