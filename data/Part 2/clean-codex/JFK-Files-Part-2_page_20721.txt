Member of subject organization.

The YSA and SWP in the Boston area have approximately 15 of their female members active in FL work, with approximately six to eight in and the controlling forces of BOWAAC. As of January 1972, Jane Roland, who joined the Boston SWP in late 1971, was heading SWP/YSA work in FL and BOWAAC, as Matilde Miles had not returned to Boston, but rather transferred to New York.

BS T-1. January 12, 1972.

In late January 1972, Toba Singer and Jane Roland, leaders of the local SWP/YSA FL faction, reported that there was continuing trouble within FL with respect to the control of that group by the SWP and YSA forces and also the direction of FL into any kind of abortion work.
