Said he knew everyone at Cuban Embassy, MEXI, and could go to Cuba for ROWTON.

4. ROWTON slowed matters down by saying he had to go to Europe for about three weeks but that they would talk more about it upon his return. In turning conversation to wife, ROWTON learned she has a father in New York who left Cuba to get away from revolution. Father and daughter on good terms, and he writes her every week. His name is Chinese for peach blossom, and CHICHAY is Cuban attempt at transliteration. JURADO spoke Chinese name, but ROWTON unable to remember phonetic pronunciation and could not ask for spelling. CHICHAY's mother still in Cuba with brothers and sisters.

5. LITAINT-2 has been cultivating CHICHAY several months. Saw letter on her desk one day in consulate and asked if it bore new Cuban stamps. CHICHAY said no, it was from her father in New York. She is unsalaried at consulate, and CURBELO, who, according to LIENVOY, reported he is a friend of JURADO, has delayed sending her PHS to Cuba for employment on salary basis. Reason for delay not known. She is friendly with CURBELO but not with LIRING-1. When their daughter was sick, LITAINT-2 gave CHICHAY money to take child to doctor.
