DE 157-3075

Beverlina Powell, financial secretary, Illinois Chapter BPP, arrived in Detroit on November 19, 1970, where she conferred with Charles Holt, second in command, Detroit NCCF, regarding the possibility of borrowing buses for the use of Chicago in going to the Constitutional Convention in Washington, D.C.; however, none were available.

DE T-1. November 20, 1970.

John Henry Bowman, BPP Philadelphia, arrived in Detroit Friday, December 11, 1970, and left Sunday, December 13, 1970, returning to Philadelphia. He apparently came to Detroit to see Linda Warnsley, one of the Detroit 16 recently released from jail, as he did not do anything else while in Detroit.

DE T-1. December 14, 1970.

F. Publications

1. Distribution of “The Black Panther” newspaper

The following copies of “The Black Panther” newspaper were received by the Detroit NCCF from National Distribution, 1336 Fillmore Street, San Francisco, California:

Number: 10,000. Issue date: July 4, 1970.
Number: 7,000. Issue date: July 11, 1970.
Number: 7,000. Issue date: July 18, 1970.
Number: 12,000. Issue date: July 25, 1970.

DE T-1. July 20, 1970.

NCCF members are required to sell 150 copies of “The Black Panther” each day.

DE T-1. August 6, 1970.

The newspapers dated August 1, 1970, were refused by the NCCF in view of the fact that the airline was unable to locate them until it was time for the NCCF to pick up the papers dated August 6, 1970.
