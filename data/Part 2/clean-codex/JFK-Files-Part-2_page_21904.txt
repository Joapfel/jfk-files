On 19 September 1960, two members of Africa Division met with him to discuss “an operational assignment in Africa Division.” In connection with this assignment, WIROGUE/1 was to be trained in demolitions, small arms, and medical immunization. In October 1960, a cable to Leopoldville stated that Headquarters had the intent to use him as a utility agent in order to “(a) organize and conduct a surveillance team; (b) intercept packages; (c) blow up bridges; and (d) execute other assignments requiring positive action. His utilization is not to be restricted to Leopoldville.” (I.G. Memorandum, 3/14/75.)

WIROGUE made his initial contact with Hedgman in Leopoldville on December 2, 1960 (CIA Cable IN 18739, 12/17/60). He was given two instructions by Hedgman: (1) to “build cover during initial period”; and (2) to “spot persons for a surveillance team” of intelligence assets in the province where Lumumba’s support was strongest (CIA Cable IN 18739, 12/17/60).

Soon after receiving these instructions, agent WIROGUE approached QJWIN and asked him to join an “execution squad.” This incident is described by Leopoldville Chief of Station Hedgman in a cable to CIA headquarters (Hedgman, 8/21/75, p. 99):

QJWIN, WHO RESIDES SAME HOTEL AS WIROGUE, REPORTED LATTER TOLD HIM HE HAD LIVED IN ALASKA, JAPAN, SOUTH AMERICA, GERMANY AND OTHER PARTS OF EUROPE. QJWIN SAID WIROGUE SMELLED AS THOUGH HE WAS IN INTEL BUSINESS. STATION DENIED ANY INFO ON WIROGUE. 14 DEC QJWIN REPORTED WIROGUE HAD OFFERED HIM THREE HUNDRED DOLLARS PER MONTH TO PARTICIPATE IN INTEL NET AND BE MEMBER OF “EXECUTION SQUAD.” WHEN QJWIN SAID HE WAS NOT INTERESTED, WIROGUE ADDED THERE WOULD BE BONUSES FOR SPECIAL JOBS. UNDER QJWIN QUESTIONING, WIROGUE LATER SAID HE WORKED FOR AMERICAN SERVICE.

In discussing local contacts, WIROGUE mentioned QJWIN but did not admit to having tried to recruit him. Then Chief of Station tried to learn whether WIROGUE had made the approach, but the latter claimed he had taken no steps. Chief of Station was unable to contradict him, as he did not wish to reveal QJWIN’s CIA connection. (CIA Cable, Leopoldville to Director, 12/17/60.)
