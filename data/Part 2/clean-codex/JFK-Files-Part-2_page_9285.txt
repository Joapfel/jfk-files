For Children

The National Committee to Combat Fascism is moving to meet the basic needs of the poor and underprivileged of our community. One way in which we do this is by the implementation of a free breakfast for children program.

The breakfast programs are not only to feed the children in the community, but also to bring the children, parents, and all people of the community together to work and see to it that every kid is fed a well-balanced breakfast each morning.

This being our people's breakfast, our goal is to spread this program throughout the communities in Michigan. This is the people's program, and without the people's support, we have nothing since it is a basic necessity. It has to be met.
