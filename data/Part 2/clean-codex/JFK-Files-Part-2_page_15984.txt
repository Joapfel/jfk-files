2/10/69.

G. Chicago BPP and Communist Party (CP)

On April 9, 1969, at a meeting of the Communist Party of Illinois (CP), held in Chicago, Illinois, a CP leader stated he objected to BPP because it is a group apparently asking to become black capitalists. Any group that seeks to join the capitalist system, in one form or another, will lose the support of CP. The CP leader stated he recently heard a BPP spokesman express satisfaction that the Viet Nam war was continuing because the United States may be defeated, which is wrong because it is the sons of working men who are doing the fighting.

4/10/69.
