Date: 2/17/70.

William J. Davis, registered pharmacist, Walgreens Drug Store, 2300 West Madison, advised that their prescriptions are filed in number order only and no records are kept concerning prescriptions in name only.

Mr. Davis advised that he has seen Fred A. Hampton, deceased, and another member of the Black Panther Party in Walgreens purchasing cigarettes and so forth, but does not recall the following people ever obtaining prescriptions at Walgreens:

Fred A. Hampton  
Mark Clark  
Brenda Harris  
Verlina A. Brewer  
Blair J. Anderson  
Ronald Satchel  
Deborah Johnson  
Louis Trulock  
Harold Bell

Date: 2/10/70. Chicago, Illinois.

SA Frederick R. Stall and SA C. Leonard Treviranus FFS.

Date dictated: 2/13/70.
