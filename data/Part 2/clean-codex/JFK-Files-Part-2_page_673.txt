Contacts with high officials. Rowton told him the first thing he should do was find out what these officials personally need on the outside. LIRING-3 acknowledged this was probably a smart move, as he had become somewhat disillusioned seeing high officials buying all types of Western conveniences and products for themselves.

5. LIRING-3 was officially invited to the 26 July celebration. Rowton told LIRING-3 he appeared to be a good candidate for Rowton’s needs, since he placed personal acquaintance and confidence in these matters above practical or theoretical knowledge of economics. Subject commented Cubans had learned they could not buy essential commodities directly and openly on the world market; hence, they would welcome LIRING-3’s role as an intermediary whom they could trust. Rowton hinted he had contacts in the munitions trade in Italy and Greece and would be interested in Cuban military requirements. LIRING-3 said he could handle this also through Rodriguez with the Cuban General Staff.

6. At the end of lunch, LIRING-3 assured Rowton he could visit Cuba for 10 days, ostensibly to see his wife’s family. He also assured Rowton he would not have to explain to anyone his relations with Rowton. Next meeting set for 25 May.
