Further in connection with the above-mentioned cancellation of WATSON's visa, it is noted that the Washington Post for 10/3/65 contained a radio-television column by LAURENCE LAURENT captioned "Getting Boots from Soviets is Fashionable for Networks," in which LAURENT stated that SAM JAFFA of ABC had been told that he was no longer welcome in Moscow. LAURENT's column stated in part, "What did JAFFE do? He was adjudged guilty of a report delivered September 15, from Washington, by AEC News Diplomatic Correspondent, JOHN SCALI. JOHN had reported that a shake-up was expected in the Soviet hierarchy." -U-
