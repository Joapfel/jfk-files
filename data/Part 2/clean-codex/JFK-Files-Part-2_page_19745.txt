Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) sponsored demonstration November twenty next, Washington, D.C. — New Left.

Reliable source advised November eighteen last that Illinois Women's Abortion Coalition (IWAC) has sold approximately twenty seats on chartered Trailways bus, which will depart Chicago late afternoon November nineteen instant, for captioned matter at Washington, D.C. IWAC hopes to fill one bus and possibly another, but need for second bus at this time is doubtful. Tentative plans are for bus to return to Chicago late afternoon November twenty next.

Re Washington Field airtel November five last.

Chicago source is CG 7707-S.
