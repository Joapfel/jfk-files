21 June 1967, 195240.

1. Subject of reference B returned to Cuba 19 June.

LIRING-3 told them they hand-carried letters from Havana friends, had several meetings with them. Information obtained during these meetings was cabled to Mexico City 1319, (IN'93728) 1942 and 1943. (GN 94380)

2. During private conversation with LIRING-3 shortly before returning to Cuba, Dr. Jose Aguilera Maceiras told L-3 he was senior vice minister and number two in the Cuban Ministry of Education. Unlike Dr. Max Figueira Araujo (APAUJO), education vice minister in charge of higher university education, he was a member of the Communist Party and, as such, claimed he could do a few things for L-3 if and when the latter comes to Cuba. Aguilera added Figueira was a typical old-style teacher and still bourgeois at heart who had a chance to apply for commitmentship but did not take it.

3. Have asked LIRING-3 for any biography of vice ministers and his personal assessment of both. It already appears he is not overly impressed with personality: Figueira, who he feels is opportunistic.
