Date: June 5, 1969.

The following United States (US) currency and coin was recovered from the second-floor headquarters of the Illinois Black Panther Party (BPP) at 2350 West Madison Avenue on June 4, 1969:

Twelve twenty-dollar bills: $240.00. Thirty-six ten-dollar bills: $360.00. Thirty-one five-dollar bills: $155.00. One hundred and fifty-seven one-dollar bills: $157.00. Three 50¢ pieces: $1.50. One hundred and forty-nine quarters: $37.25. One hundred and nineteen dimes: $11.90. One hundred and seven nickels: $5.35. One hundred and thirty-eight pennies: $1.38. Total US currency: $969.38.

Also recovered was one Canadian five-cent piece: $0.05. Total: $969.43.

The above money was scattered throughout the office and was taken for safekeeping.

On 6/4/69 at Chicago, Illinois.

File #.

SA David M. Frahm.
