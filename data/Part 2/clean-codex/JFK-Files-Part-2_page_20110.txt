Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) conference, July 15–16, 1972, at Hunter College, New York, New York. IS-C (TROUSKYISB).

Re WFO night tel to Bureau 7-7-72, Bureau night tel to New York 7-11-72, New York tels to Bureau 7-10-72 and WFO 7-12-72, and Detroit night tel to Bureau 7-14-72.

A confidential source advised instant date a two-day conference of the third Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) commenced 9:00 AM instant date at Hunter College, New York, N.Y., principal announced purpose of which is to map out a national campaign to defend and extend the right to abortion in New York and help win repeal of all restrictions against abortion in states of the U.S.
