IV. Changes, additions, and deletions of data appearing in Section II: Reserve index cards.

(A) Changes in data appearing in Section A reserve index cards should be submitted to the Bureau on Form FD-122a.

(B) Deletions from Section A of the reserve index should be recommended by letter. The letter shall set forth the basis for the recommendation.

(C) Changes in office of origin are to be handled by submission of Form FD-128a. Forward Section A cards to the new office of origin.

(B) Section B.

I. This section is to contain the names of all other individuals included in the reserve index. This section is to be maintained only in the field office. The file of a reserve index B subject should be reviewed whenever relevant. Informant information or other information is channeled to the file. A determination should be made at that time as to whether additional investigation is warranted.

II. Changes in office of origin are to be handled by submission of Form FD-128a. Forward Section B cards to the new office of origin.
