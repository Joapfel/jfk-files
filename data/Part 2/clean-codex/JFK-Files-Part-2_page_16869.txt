Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: June 6, 1969

On June 4, 1969, SRS and Lockett R. Bibbs were arrested on the sidewalk in front of 2350 West Madison. This address houses the offices of the Chicago Chapter of the Black Panther Party (BPP), and Bibbs was about to enter at the time he was arrested.

Bibbs was subsequently transported by Bureau automobile to the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) Office at 219 South Dearborn, where he was photographed, fingerprinted, and provided with a printed warning and waiver form, which he read but declined to sign.

Bibbs was interviewed by SRS regarding his association with the BPP, and he advised as follows:

Bibbs stated he had been a member of the BPP for about four or five months. He then amended his statement regarding his membership with the BPP and advised he was merely a friend of the party and worked for them. He stated, however, that he had never formally joined the BPP. Bibbs stated that at the time of his arrest he was about to enter the offices of the BPP to help supervise their “Breakfast for Children” program. He stated that his duties were to set tables and help serve the children as they were being fed. He stated that the BPP tries to help the people in the neighborhood and he feels it is a very good and worthwhile organization. He advised that he had never seen any guns or other weapons in the BPP headquarters and that he had never seen a member of the BPP carrying a gun on his person.

Bibbs was shown a photograph of George Sams, Jr., and stated he did not know Sams by name or photograph. Bibbs advised he had been in the BPP headquarters several times during the previous evening, but again denied having seen Sams on the premises.

Chicago, Illinois. File date dictated: June 6, 1969.
