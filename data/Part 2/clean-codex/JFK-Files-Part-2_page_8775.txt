AMSLOUCH Project 28-6-33.

RIOD 2944 (IN 83322), dated 18 December 1963.

Mario Rogerio MESTRINHO de Mello, official in the Brazilian (Brazillian) Embassy, Havana, reported the following to Rio Station contact in Rio on 13 December 1963:

a) Wide rumor of assassination attempt against Fidel CASTRO after his TV appearance on 6 December, resulting in the killing of the man next to him. CASTRO uninjured. Would-be killer at large.

b) Wide rumor Cuban José “Pepe” LLANUZA met Lee OSWALD in Mexico before the Kennedy assassination.

AMSOUR Project 19-6-70.

SW Message No. 27 from agent, dated 11 October 1964:

“On the assassination attempt against Fidel, we know they took two civilians wounded by shooting to the Calixto Garcia Hospital. Moments later, two soldiers from Fidel’s guards presented themselves in said hospital, taking away the wounded. We do not have more details.”
