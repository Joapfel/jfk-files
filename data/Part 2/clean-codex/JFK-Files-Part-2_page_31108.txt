22 March 1963. Memo to Director, FBI, from DDP. Subject: Francisco Rodriguez Tamayo, as “El Meneano.”

An untested source told CIA representative in December 1962 that FRT was responsible for the imprisonment of Roberto Perez Cruz. The source was imprisoned with Perez in Principe prison. Perez said Major Efigenia Armejeiras told him FRT was a paid agent of COC in Miami. Perez was executed a few days later.

Cable, 30 May 1968. To Director from Caracas. Man PIRASH/4 advised that FRT was arrested using a false passport—Nicolas Diez—but was actually FRT, “a gangster long sought by the FBI.” Source said he had come to Venezuela to assassinate an unknown target for $15,000.

DCD: 1 document, no notes. Some information as in item 3 above.

Love documents: 1 paragraph. Telegrams: 1 sealed envelope.
