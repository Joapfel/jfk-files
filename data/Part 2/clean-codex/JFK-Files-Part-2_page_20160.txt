United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation

Report of: William R. Clough. Office: New York, New York.  
Date: November 30, 1972.  
Field Office File No.: 100-174481. Bureau File No.: 100-468406.  
Title: Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC).  
Character: Internal Security-C (Trotskyist).

Synopsis: WONAAC founded as result of conference held in New York City, New York, during July 1971, for purpose of forming National Movement of Independent Groups throughout the United States who favored legalized abortions and ending restrictive contraception laws and forced sterilization. National and New York State staff offices located at 150 Fifth Avenue, New York City, New York. WONAAC does not maintain formal membership, and funds are obtained through contributions. YSA and SWP influence and control over WONAAC reported since founding conference. Source advised in February 1972 that, as a result of de-escalation of the Vietnam War, YSA-NYL plans to concentrate in the area of the Women’s Liberation Movement through influence in WONAAC. Activities of WONAAC dutifully reported at regular membership meetings of YSA-NYL during the period March 1972 through October 1972. Carol Eaton, National Staff Member, advised under pretext in November 1972 that SWP/YSA influence is not now significant, and WONAAC has always accepted help from whatever source offered. WONAAC International Tribunal scheduled to be held in New York City in March 1972.

Details:

Investigation in this case is predicated upon receipt of information in July 1971 from NY T-1, alleging the Young Socialist Alliance (YSA) and Socialist Workers Party (SWP) had taken over and were influencing the First National Women’s Abortion Conference, held during that month.
