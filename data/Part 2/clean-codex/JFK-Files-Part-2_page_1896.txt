Section XIII. Children and Other Defendents.

1. Number of children (including stepchildren and adopted children) who are unmarried, under 21 years of age, and are not self-supporting.

2. Number of other defendents (including spouse, parents, stepparents, sister, etc.) who depend on you for at least 50% of their support, or children over 21 years of age who are not self-supporting.

Provide the following information for all children and defendents: Name; relationship; year of birth; sex (M/F); citizenship; address.

Additional comment and/or continuation of preceding items.
