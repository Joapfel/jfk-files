16005CZ, Mexico City 9534. Director info JMWAVE. TYPIC LIRING.

10 December 1956.

1. LIRING-3 told Wallace E. Rowton 14 Dec 66 he had heard in Havana (HAVA) on recent trip (he returned next (NEXI) 5 Dec) that Fidel Castro had recently caused the arrest of eleven members of the editorial staff of Granma newspaper.

2. L-3 labelled those arrested as members of the “Red Guard,” which had criticized Castro for his recent speech regarding Cuban militia irregularities and difficulties. Criticism reflected in columns of Granma and brought about arrest of eleven.

3. Rowton asked L-3 for identities of those arrested, but the latter could not supply them except to say one was husband of his friend Velkis Cusa, not further identified. Rowton has asked L-3 to discuss arrests with Ismael (ISNAEL) Suarez de la Paz in order to obtain more details.

4. L-3 stated he had discussed internal Cuban affairs with Suarez, and the latter told him Fidel Castro was experiencing difficulties accomplishing objectives because of bureaucratic (SUREAUCRATIC) clumsiness of party underlings and unbelievable inefficiency of the Cuban diplomatic service. Suarez told L-3 Castro required delegating important and sensitive tasks to his close confidants in order to assure accomplishments.
