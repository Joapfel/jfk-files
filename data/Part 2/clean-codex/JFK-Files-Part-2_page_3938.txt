Zurich 1629. 15 March 1969.

Director info Bern.

Ref: Director 83209.

17 March 1969. IN 15450.

1. Nagell last appeared at Zurich Consulate the morning of 7 March and stated that if he received no satisfaction by five o'clock that afternoon he would carry out his threats and expose the U.S. Government on radio, television, and in the press.

2. He then disappeared, nothing further was heard from him, and no publicity was given. On the morning of Monday, 10 March, the Zurich Consulate was advised by telephone from the U.S. Consulate in Barcelona that Nagell had approached them and made similar threats.
