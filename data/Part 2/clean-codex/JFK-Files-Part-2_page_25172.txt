Dates: 31 Aug. 1963. Categories: DIEM.

Occurrence:

August 31, 1963, in the Saigon Station's chronology of August 1963 coup activities, the following report appears:

General Harkins was authorized to meet with Khiem to give Khiem assurances of USG backing. Khiem showed a reluctance to talk and suggested that Harkins meet with General Minh. Khiem commented that Minh had called off the planning and was working on other methods which Khiem could not describe. Khiem further stated that the generals were not ready as they did not have enough forces under their control. Harkins decided not to give Khiem assurances which he had been authorized to impart. Lodge approved Harkins' decision on 31 August after Harkins reported. (13)

Source: I.G. Report 1967, Part C, p. 13.
