Re New York letter to the Bureau 1/10/73.

On this date, Lieutenant John Nabors, New York State Capitol (Capital) Police, Albany, New York, stated that the demonstration mentioned in the referenced communication failed to take place on 1/30/73, and no demonstrators were in evidence at the Capitol (Capital) Building.

Appropriate local officials were advised on 1/22/73 of the proposed demonstration.
