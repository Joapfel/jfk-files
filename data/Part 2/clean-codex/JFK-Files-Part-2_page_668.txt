260005Z CITE MEXICO CITY 7454 EASBY ACTING DIRECTOR

DYVOUR PBRUMEN LIRING

Ref: Director 27441

26 May 66 24650

1. LIRING-3 informed ROWTON 25 May he obtained one-day visa service 24 May. Meeting held: 1300 hours 25 May prior arrival ref. LIRING-3 showed apparent disappointment in ROWTON not being able to confirm trip. ROWTON's next meeting with LIRING-3 26 May, lunch, at which he will give subject go sign.

2. Quick service on visa appears to confirm LIRING-3 boasts he is confidant of RODRIGUEZ. ROWTON has impressed on LIRING-3 that he, ROWTON's agent and middle man in this transaction, must not reveal ROWTON. If ROWTON revealed, then Cubans would want to deal directly with ROWTON, cutting out LIRING-3. LIRING-3 states he understands this clearly. LIRING-3 assures ROWTON he need not inform Cubans anything of arrangement since they trust him.

3. CAN has supply ROWTON with Greek passport, New York driver's license, and any other logical pocket litter indicating Greek native in international commercial operations. Material would not be used for travel, but as operation develops may be handy. Flash Greek secret!
