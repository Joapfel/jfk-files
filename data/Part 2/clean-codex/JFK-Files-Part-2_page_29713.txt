In the 1960s Elena Garro (Garra) became a significant writer. Hogar Sólido, El Rey Mago, La Señora en su Balcón, Ventura Allende, Andarse por las Ramas (Andaise por las Rimas), Parada Empresa, and El Viaje are plays that have had appreciative audiences in Europe, where they were translated into German, as well as in Mexico. Ms. Garro's short stories are collected in a volume called La Semana de Colores. The Literacy Supplement of the London Times has called her novel Los Recuerdos del Porvenir (Porenir), “a splendid success.” Critics have said of her: “For Elena Garro, there is no frontier between reality and fantasy; in any case, the latter is a second reality—perhaps more intense—to which one may penetrate without passport or forewarning, thanks to the effectiveness of a literature fired with passion, flavor and life.” Many people who knew Elena have asserted that the frontier between reality and fantasy is also difficult for her to distinguish in real life. (Biography Data Form on Elena Garro (Garra) de Paz (Paza) prepared by Charles Thomas)

Ms. Garro, for many years, was an active worker in the Confederación Nacional Campesina (CNC), the agrarian.
