Mr. Hotis; Mr. Daly; Mr. E. W. Walsh; Mr. W. R. Wannall; Mr. W. O. Cregar; Mr. S. F. Phillips.

REBUTEL May 2, 1975.

Senate Select Committee (SSC) staff member Lester Seidel has furnished the following tentative schedule for interviews in your office: San Diego, 7-30-75: SAS Earl M. Petersen, Lawrence F. Wirick; Los Angeles, 8-5-75: Wallace E. Ward, Richard A. Bloeser; 8-7-75: Assistant Director Robert E. Gebhardt; San Francisco, 8-11-75: Leo S. Breneisen; 8/11-12/75: SAC Charles W. Bates. Purpose of interview is to be COINTELPRO and Bureau investigation of the Black Panther Party. Additionally, SAC Bates will be interviewed concerning knowledge of “Houston Plan” by SSC staff member Loch Johnson.

I have waived your employment agreements for purposes of these interviews. Each should note that he has the right to counsel; however, the FBI is unable to provide private counsel. There are certain privileged areas concerning which SAS would.
