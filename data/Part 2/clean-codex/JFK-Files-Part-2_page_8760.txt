Plots to Assassinate the President of the U.S. 100-300-12

Director 03964 dated 8 December 1962

Cable from Agency to Secret Service, FBI and State Department Security forwarding text of a letter from Havana, dated 27 November 1962, to Miami, concerning plot to assassinate President Kennedy. Letter was addressed to a post office box in Miami belonging to the Radio Libertad La Voz, Anti-Comunista de America. Since the addressee was unknown to members of Radio Libertad, one of their Miami representatives passed the letter to a contact of JMWAVE Station. Addressee was Bernardo Adalberto Morales-Rivero.

Director 4013 dated 8 December 1962

Cable from Agency to addressees cited above. Provided traces on Bernardo Adalberto Morales-Rivero who entered the U.S. in May 1962 on a fraudulent visa.
