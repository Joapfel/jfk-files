17 October 1977

Memorandum for: Chief PCS/CSS/B&F  
From: DC/CPD  
Subject: Contract amended, Bruce A. BERTOTALLY  
Reference: Your memorandum, subject as above, dated 29 June 1977.

Your referenced memorandum raised two basic questions in regard to an amendment effected to the contract of Bruce Bertotally. To determine the answers, a meeting was held 6 October 1977, with discussion and decision by those present: Brad Abernathy, SSA/DDA; John Rizzo for the General Counsel; and Charles Sandman with Rogers C. Brooks for CPD.

In subparagraph (a) of your memorandum, you requested identification of the level of the responsible Government official who must approve the operational necessity travel of the subject’s wife. The Chief, CI Staff, was so identified.

Subparagraph (b) basically related to the taxability or nontaxability of funds made available for and relating to the dependent wife’s travel. It was the consensus and agreement of all present that all such funds should be considered additional compensation and taxable.

Rogers C. Brooks
