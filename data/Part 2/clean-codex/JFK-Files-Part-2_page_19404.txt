United States Department of Justice

Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

January 12, 1968

Demonstration protest against United States policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C.

January 15, 1968

On January 5, 1968, Officer James McGovern (MC. GOVERN), Civil Disobedience Unit, Philadelphia Police Department, made available two flyers pertaining to the scheduled January 15, 1968, Jeannette Rankin Brigade (JRB) demonstration in Washington, D.C. The first carried the heading “Join the Jeannette Rankin Brigade” and listed the address and phone number for the JRB as 20 South 12th Street, Philadelphia, Pa. (LO 4-5570). The second was a message “To All American Women” from Jeannette Rankin and listed a partial list of sponsors. Copies of these flyers are attached to this memorandum.

The information service of the Bell Telephone Company of Pennsylvania reflected that the listing for LO 4-5570 was Women Strike for Peace, 20 South 12th Street, Philadelphia, Pa.

On January 5, 1968, Johanne T/Science of the Women Strikers for Peace (WSP) Office was contacted under suitable pretense. She stated that the Jeannette Rankin Brigade was an ad hoc committee formed of various peace groups and church women. It was being coordinated in Philadelphia out of the WSP office only for this one occasion and for the group of women going to Washington, D.C., on January 15, 1968. A march to the Capitol to petition Congress was planned. Thereafter, the civil strike would then be held indoors at Georgetown University, which had provided facilities for the convening.
