To: Director, FBI. From: SAC, WFO.

Demonstration by Jeannette Rankin Brigade on January 8, 1967, in Washington, D.C. Information concerning internal security. (00: WFO.)

Re WFO teletypes dated 11/28/67 and 11/29/67.

Transmitted herewith are 10 copies of an LHM for the Bureau describing possible anti-Vietnam War protest activity in Washington, D.C. Two copies are enclosed for the New York Office.

LHM is being disseminated locally to Secret Service, United States Attorney, and military intelligence agencies.

Source used in LHM is WF 1777-S.

LHM is classified confidential in view of the fact that information reported by the source could lead to the identification of that source and compromise future effectiveness.

WFO continuing to follow and will report further pertinent details.

Bureau, 10 enclosures, 1-105-138315 (VIDEM). New York, 2 enclosures (KM). WFO, 1-100-44061 (VIDEM).
