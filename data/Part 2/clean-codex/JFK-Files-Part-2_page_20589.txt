WOMEN'S NATIONAL ABORTION ACTION COALITION (WONAAC)

On November 28, 1971, and November 29, 1971, sources one and two advised that LAWAAC, an affiliate of WONAAC, is dominated and controlled by members of the LAL-SWP and the LAB-YSA.
