Distribution: U.S. Secret Service, Chicago (via courier); Region I, 113th Military Intelligence Group, Evanston, Illinois (via courier); OSI, Chicago (via courier); NISO, Chicago (RM).

Report of: SA Alan R. Stephens  
Office: Chicago, Illinois  
Date: November 7, 1969  
Title: Black Panther Party (BPP) Chicago Division  
Character: Racial Matters—Organization

Synopsis: Headquarters of the Chicago Chapter, BPP, continues to be located at 2350 West Madison Street, Chicago, Illinois. Sources report West Suburban Chapter as being inactive at this time. During Antiriot Law Conspiracy trial currently being tried in Chicago, the BPP has conducted several demonstrations at the Federal Building alleging constitutional rights of Bobby Seale, National BPP and one of eight defendants, being violated. All members of the BPP must be considered possibly armed and dangerous.
