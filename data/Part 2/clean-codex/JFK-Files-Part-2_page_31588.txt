G. Three-day Protest Rally and Planned Strike, UCB, February 7, 8 and 9, 1966

On February 4, 1966, a Special Agent of the FBI received a copy of a printed leaflet captioned “Why Strike?”, issued by the VDC. The leaflet commented that if the JOHNSON administration escalates the war in Vietnam, so must “we” intensify “our” commitment to stop it. The leaflet informed the VDC was planning a work-transfer walk-out for Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday (February 7, 8 and 9, 1966).

The leaflet commented the strike was not aimed directly at the University, but at a political system which influenced it. The leaflet remarked the protest was a political protest and would be successful if a sizeable minority of students and faculty committed themselves to building a peace movement.

“For 3 days we plan to transfer the work we would normally do in class to a discussion of the war and of the development of programs to stop it.” The leaflet went on to say, “On Wednesday, a student walk-out is planned. Everybody transfer your work during the demonstration. We urge the entire university community to join together in this action.”

Officer HULL, UCB Campus Police, informed on February 7, 1966, that on February 7, 1966, the VDC sponsored a rally at Sproul Hall Plaza, UCB. Officer HULL related the rally commenced at 12 noon and terminated at 1:00 PM. According to Officer HULL, the speakers included SUSAN STEIN, JAMIE ALLEN, JACK WEINBERG, MORRIS HIRSCH and ROBERT HURWITT, all of whom spoke against U.S. foreign policy in Vietnam. Officer HULL stated the rally was MC’d by HURWITT, who commented there would be a march to Congressman CONELAN’s offices later in the day.
