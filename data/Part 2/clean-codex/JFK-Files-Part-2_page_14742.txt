SAC, Washington Field (134-6831)

October 15, 1968

Director, FBI (134-15133)

Re WFairtel 10/10/68, captioned “Counterintelligence and Special Operations (Nationalities Intelligence).”

Authority granted to pay (WF 1777-S) $548 for airfare so she may travel to the Soviet Union in 11/68 as head of Women Strike for Peace delegation, invited by Soviet Women’s Committee. Brief informant to develop information relating to the nature of Soviet influence of domestic New Left and alert her to possibility she might be approached abroad for Soviet intelligence use.

Keep Bureau advised of all pertinent developments.

See memo R. D. Cotter to Mr. W. C. Sullivan, dated 10/14/68, captioned same as above, and prepared by DR:sib.
