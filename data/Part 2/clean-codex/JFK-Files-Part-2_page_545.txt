1. Hector Rodolfo (Sandoval) Alegria is a Guatemalan rebel who, together with Francisco Lara, engineered LIRING-3 and Francisco Lara's trip to Guatemala, where he is now incarcerated. Sandoval saw 8188442 several times after LIRING-3's arrest, at one point saying he might have to hold up a bank to come to LIRING-3's assistance. According to Manuel (MARCUE) Pardinas, Sandoval was arrested 5 May shortly after his wife returned from a trip to Guatemala. Sandoval's wife is now staying at a house owned by MARCUE together with Francisco Lara's family.
