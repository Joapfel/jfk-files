h. On the question of intervention—Mexican papers stating this is a Mexican victory on the Cuban issue—this is being replayed by U.S. papers—leaves a poor impression. State said this is being discussed with the President and will be covered by Lincoln White at a press conference today. CIA and USIA agreed that more should be done. State could not answer on the Cuban talks—unknown how deeply Cuba was covered and whether the question of diversion of U.S. goods or movement of people was discussed. Any hint of a Mexican victory will be demoralizing to people in Cuba, the exile community, and Latin America in general.
