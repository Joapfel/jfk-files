FBI. Date: 2/5/72. Airtel, priority.

To: Director, FBI. From: SAC, New York (100-174481). Subject: Boston Women's Abortion Action Coalition is-C (Trotskyist).

Ref: F01 teletype, dated 11/20/71, captioned “Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WOMANC) sponsored by Boston Women's Abortion Action Coalition, 11/26/71, acct. to D.C., is-C, in lit,” and BSairtel and ILM, dated 1/18/72, captioned as above.

On 1/26/72, NY 7010-S provided a flyer on the WOVAAC conference to be held on 2/11, 2/12, and 2/13/72 at Boston University, Boston, Massachusetts. It is to be noted that the flyer indicated a mass rally is scheduled for 2/11/72 from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM, and that strategy sessions, limited to women only, are scheduled for 2/12 and 2/13/72.

In view of the above, all female informers are invited to attend the NY 7010-S convention on Sunday, November 2, 1972, from 8:00 to 12:00.

Individuals were expected to attend the above conference, including people from Canada and other countries. The identities of these individuals or the speakers are not known at the present time, and the NYC has no additional information other than that set forth as referenced earlier.
