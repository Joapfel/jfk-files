1. Further debriefing LIRING-4 produced the following:

A. She is a longtime friend of Claribel (Guilfrido), niece of President Dorticos, who worked at the Cuban presidency in 1964 when LIRING-4 last saw her. Guilfrido, who was born in LIRING-4's hometown in Oriente about 1938, is anti-Castro. LIRING-4 felt Guilfrido could probably help her obtain a useful job if still in Cuba and might even agree to cooperate against the regime. Guilfrido recently got married and moved, but LIRING-4 felt she could find her.

B. LIRING-4 knows Lolo de la (Torriiente), an elderly Cuban newspaper woman who is disaffected with the current regime. LIRING-3, who also knows the woman, told ROWTON that in early 1967 Mexican leftist painter Raul (Anguiano) confided to him that he received a letter from de la Torriiente, a close friend, asking him to help her obtain a Mexican visa in order to leave Cuba since the situation there was “intolerable.” Anguiano confessed to LIRING-3 that he was in a quandary.
