15 APR 91298

Guatemala City info Director, Tegucigalpa.

Refs: A. Mexico City 5111 (11 85161). B. Mexico City 5141 (11 86263), NR 16 Apr 83.

1. Upon return from short vacation, Wallace B. Rowton was given sealed letter from LIRING-3 by LIRING-4. Letter indicated:

A. LERING-3's departure for Guatemala and Honduras delayed when Francisco Lara accidentally shot self in ankle while cleaning gun. Lara anxious news of accident not reach ears of FAR members forbidden carry weapons in Mexico.

B. LERING-3 departed 10 April with Lara and other Guat who member Yon Sosa faction. LERING-3 stated in letter this Guat insistent he talk directly with Yon Sosa.

C. LERING-3 and Lara will probably visit San Salvador in addition to Guatemala and Honduras.

2. Per LIRING-4, other Guat who introduced to LERING-3 by Lara and is probably Rodolfo Sandoval called at her apartment 9 April looking for LIRING-3, who was out.
