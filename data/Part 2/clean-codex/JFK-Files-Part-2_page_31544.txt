Nanya J. Corn, also known as Nancy J. Corn

A review of the records of the Berkeley Police Department revealed that Nancy J. Corn, also known as Nanya Corn, was arrested by the Berkeley Police Department for failure to have in her possession an automobile driver's permit. She was released on $56.00 bail.

On January 7, 1966, Nanya Corn pled not guilty to the charge; however, she was found guilty and the judgment was suspended.

The following is a description of Nanya Corn as set forth in the records of the Berkeley Police Department:

Race: White. Sex: Female. Date and place of birth: January 30, 1943, New York. Height: 5'3".
