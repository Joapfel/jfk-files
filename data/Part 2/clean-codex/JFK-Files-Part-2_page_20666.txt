Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

April 13, 1972. NR 056 NY Code. 8:38 PM, Nitel, 4-13-72, BGW.

To Director (100-468406). Attention: DID. From New York (100-174481), 2P.

Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC), IS-C (Trotskyist).

On April 13, a confidential source who furnished reliable information in the past advised that WONAAC would conduct a demonstration at Governor Rockefeller's office, New York City, to protest the ban on Medicaid payments for abortions.

On April 13, instant, special agents of the Federal Bureau of Investigation observed four women enter the office of the New York Governor, where they presented the Governor's aide a petition relative to the above-mentioned ban. No demonstration occurred. The women departed the Governor's office without incident.

The above-mentioned source advised that on April 16 next, WONAAC will conduct a counter-demonstration to action by the Knights of Columbus on April 17, 1972, demanding repeal of abortion laws in New York State. Demonstration scheduled for 1:30 PM, vicinity of Fifth Avenue and

New York 88321.
