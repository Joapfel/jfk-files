Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 2/25/70

1. Employment Supervisor, Cook County School of Nursing, 1800 West Polk Street, Chicago, advised that a search of their records revealed a personnel file for Annie White, 2237 South Keeler, Chicago, Illinois.

Annie White entered on duty at the hospital as a clerical employee on November 29, 1951. She stated that the file further revealed that White was terminated on June 2, 1956, and again rehired, exact date unknown. She said that White again terminated her employment at County Hospital on December 29, 1960. White said that while employed at Cook County Hospital, she was under the direct supervision of an RN, and that her specific duties were those of a clerk assigned to the Trauma Unit. White said that the personnel file further revealed that she was born October 11, 1930, at Hartwell, Florida. Her Social Security Number is 354-24-5845. Her telephone number is listed as 762-0129, which is the telephone number of her mother, Mrs. Anne E. Gunsby, 2237 South Keeler.

From a review of the County payroll records, the following is to be noted regarding White's employment during the week of December 1, 1969:

December 1: Sick. December 2: 3:00 to 11:00 shift. December 3: 3:00 to 11:00 shift. December 4: Sick. December 5: Sick. December 6: Day off. December 7: Day off. December 8: Vacation day. December 9: Vacation day. December 10: Vacation day.

White has recently filled out an application for reinstatement, and this application is presently being considered.

On 2/24/70 at Chicago, Illinois.
