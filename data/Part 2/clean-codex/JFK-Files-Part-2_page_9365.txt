On July 21, 1971, Erone C. Dessausure, Benjamin W. Fondren, and David M. Johnson were sentenced to three to four years in State Prison of Southern Michigan, Jackson, Michigan, by Detroit Recorders Court Judge John R. Murphy, on a charge of felonious assault.

DE T-13  
July 21, 1971

The above three individuals were members of the Detroit 15, 15 members and associates of the Detroit NCCF arrested on October 24, 1970, at the NCCF office, 3376 16th Street, Detroit, in connection with the shooting of two Detroit police officers on that date. Of the 12 who were tried regarding this matter, all were found not guilty except the above three.

DE T-13 advised on July 7, 1971, that Ronald Smith pled guilty to second-degree murder, Ronnie Irwin, Larry Powell, and Anthony Norman pled guilty to armed robbery, before Detroit Recorders Court Judge John R. Murphy on July 7, 1971.

On August 23, 1971, Detective Sergeant William Henahan, Detroit Police Department, advised that Powell was sentenced to 20 to 40 years, Norman to 15 to 30 years, and Smith to life in prison. Irwin changed his plea.

DE T-3 advised on September 23, 1971, that Irwin was found guilty and sentenced to life in prison on September 23, 1971.
