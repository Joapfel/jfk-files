United States Government. Memorandum.

To: Director, FBI (105-28333).

From: SAC, WFO (105-8906)(P).

Date: 4/29/69.

Re WFO letter of 2/28/69 requesting Bureau authority to interview Samuel Adason Jaffe and Bureau letter of 3/12/69 authorizing interview.

Enclosed are the original and four copies for the Bureau and one copy for information of the New York Office of the letterhead memorandum captioned “Samuel Adason Jaffe.”

Jaffe was interviewed by Special Agents Turner and James P. Wooten on 3/20, 21, and 27/69. The sources mentioned in the letterhead memorandum are WFT-1, WFT-2, WFT-3, and WFT-4.

As is indicated in the enclosed letterhead memorandum, Jaffe identified through photographs a large number of Soviet nationals with whom he has been connected in the United States and in Russia, many.
