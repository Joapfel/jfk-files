Persons/Entities: Walt Elder, RFK, General Lansdale, McGeorge Bundy, Bruce Cheever (CIA). Date: 9/14/62. Categories: Mongoose; Special Group Augmented.

Occurrence:

CIA Headquarters and all WH stations are to be especially alert for any shipments of arms or other subversive material from Cuba to other Latin American countries—Chief, Task Force W says this alert has been laid on and is in force.

Source: Memorandum for the record on Mongoose (MONGODB) meeting, 14 September 1962—Walt Elder.
