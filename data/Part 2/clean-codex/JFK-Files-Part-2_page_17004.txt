1/5/70

Teletype.

To: Director, FBI.  
From: SAC, Chicago.

Black Panther Party. RM-BPP.

Source, reliable in past, advised Bobby Rush (Ruse), deputy minister of defense, Illinois chapter, BPP (EPP), to make news announcement to local media that Panthers have independent pathologist report indicating Fred Hampton (deceased deputy chairman, Illinois BPP as result of Illinois State's Attorney's police raid December four last) had received a drug through eating approximately eight hours before death. Indication is Panthers (Painters) will not cooperate with Illinois Cook County Coroner's Grand Jury scheduled to convene January six next, 2600 South California; however, will submit pathologist report. Rush also called for BPP supporters to appear for demonstration at above address January six next.

No violence anticipated or known planned by BPP. Appropriate agencies cognizant.
