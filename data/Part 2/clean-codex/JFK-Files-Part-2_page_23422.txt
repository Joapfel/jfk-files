C. LEOP 0972 (IN 23052)

Would appreciate your views following:

WILFLAT 1, 01

A. Formation may be realtioned by giving extra pay, perhaps 25–50 percent more than now being received, to corps of officers and enlisted men up to 200. Do not feel that National Military School group can be used for this. Raise question whether loyalty of enlisted men could be so held for long. Suggest this best handled by giving funds to WILFLAT 4 for him to give to WILFLAT 2, or use WILFLAT 4 to keep its spine strength end.

B. Ammunition: What reserves of ammunition to be held under [signature's line drawn]? Control would be required. Assume 10,000 rounds for small arms might suffice. We would require info calibre and registrars.

Coordinating officer
