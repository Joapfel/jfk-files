SF T-49  
April 1, 1953

Jessica Treuhaft was identified in 1956, at hearings of the Subversive Activities Control Board, as Financial Director of the California Labor School and as a CP functionary, by witnesses William Foard, Dorothy Jeffers, Timothy Evans and others.

The California Labor School has been designated by the Attorney General of the United States pursuant to Executive Order 10450.
