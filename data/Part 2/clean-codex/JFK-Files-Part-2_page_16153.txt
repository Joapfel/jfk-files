Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 1/9/70.

On December 21, 1969, Officers August Blue, Badge 200, and John Deneen, Badge 8, State Attorneys Police of Cook County, Chicago, Illinois, brought two doors to the address 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago. One door was for the front bedroom, and one door with one panel missing was for the entrance to the first-floor apartment.

Officers Blue and Deneen stayed with the doors and did not turn them over to the FBI.

On 12/31/69 at Chicago, Illinois. File No. SA William J. Downey, Jr. Date dictated: 1/6/70.
