Chronology (Allegations and Reports of Any U.S. Involvement in Assassinations)

1959: Richard Bissell (Bissel) becomes DDP (head of Plans Directorate).

October 1960: Tap on Dan Rowan’s phone in Las Vegas, allegedly (a) as a favor to Giancana and (b) done by Rosselli. Date and Rosselli’s role from Government Opposition paper to Rosselli motion for reduction in sentence, 1971.

February 1961: Lumumba mysteriously killed during Congolese strife. Source: Washington Post and Star contemporaneous accounts.

1961: News reports of CIA consideration of a Lumumba plot, but claim it was done by others, e.g., Belgians? Source: Washington Post, 2/6/75 story by Lardner (Lardener) and Greider, Tab A.

Early 1961: Attempts to kill Castro before Bay of Pigs landing by Rosselli team. Sources: Jack Anderson 1967, 1971, and 1975 columns; Watergate Helms memorandum of conversation.

April 1961: Bay of Pigs landing.

May 31, 1961: Trujillo assassinated; charge of U.S. involvement. Sources: Jack Anderson column, 1/19/71, on Smathers’ conversation with JFK; Agee book, obviously suspect in some respects.

Rest of 1961 and 1962: Possibly ongoing efforts to kill Castro by Rosselli team. Last attempt by Rosselli team in 1963, infra.

Sometime in the early 1960s: RFK quashes prosecution of Giancana for Rowan Las Vegas tap. Source: Watergate memoranda of conversation on Keeney and Wilson interviews.

October 1962: Cuban Missile Crisis, date for background reference.

Early 1963: Rosselli training a team in the Miami area. Washington Post Greider/Lardner (Lardener) story, supra, states Army Ranger Captain Ayres saw him.

March 1963: Rosselli’s last effort to kill Castro. Source: J. Anderson 1971 columns.

Sometime in 1963: RFK stopped further efforts. Source: J. Anderson 1971 columns.

November 1963: Diem killed in Saigon coup. Source: J. Anderson, 1/19/71 column.

JFK told Smathers later he thought CIA was involved.

Colby was head of the DDP Far East Division at the time. Source: articles on Colby.

Early 1964: Howard Hunt and others from the Bay of Pigs effort begin a plot for another Castro assassination. Source: reported by Tad Szulc in a 1974 book on Howard Hunt.
