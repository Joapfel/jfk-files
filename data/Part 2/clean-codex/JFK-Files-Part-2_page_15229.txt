International Security—Chile

A recent State Department report assesses the Communists’ efforts to weaken Chile’s ties with the U.S. and align it with the Soviet Union and other bloc countries. The same report notes that trade with Chile is being used as a cover for political and intelligence operations and primarily as a means for carrying out the functions of a diplomatic mission. The possibility exists that Chile could take a favorable position with regard to the Soviet Union and bloc countries.

In order to be in a position to obtain current intelligence data concerning Chile, the Bureau obtained State Department clearance on July 4, 1962, to immediately initiate an investigation designed to develop sources at the Chilean embassies.

Accordingly, the recipients should institute a survey for the purpose of identifying persons employed at the American Consul General of Chile, Washington, D.C.; the Chilean Mission to the United Nations; and the Consulate of Chile, both in New York City—particularly United States citizens who might possess potential as sources or informants. Indicate your request for investigation in this regard in accordance with a request for Bureau clearance to institute an investigation in each individual case.

Submit the results of your initial survey under this caption to reach the Bureau by May 10, 1962, and thereafter on a biweekly basis, setting forth progress being made to develop confidential sources and informants until such time as adequate coverage is assured.
