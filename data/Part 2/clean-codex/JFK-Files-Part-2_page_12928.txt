To: Intelligence Community Staff, Attention: Central Index. From.

Subject: Abstract of Information Provided to Select Committees.

How provided: Document; briefing; interview; testimony; other. Date provided.

To whom provided: SSC — The Attorney General with a copy for forwarding to the White House. HSC.

Identification: Memorandum reporting results of interview by SSC Staff Members of FBI's representatives at the U.S. Embassies, Brasilia, Brazil, and Buenos Aires, Argentina.

In response to: HA. Classification of information: S.

Key words: Information handling; Lisbon, foreign.

All information contained herein is unclassified. Date 10/30/00 by S. P. Zalm Jehl.

Summary: In Brasilia the interview was directed mainly toward attempts to elicit examples whereby CIA had withheld dissemination of information of interest to other agencies. In the Buenos Aires office, questions concerning the mechanics of legal reporting on intelligence information developed locally to FDIHQ and the local coordination of this information with the Agency components, the general nature of intelligence developed and reported. Inquiry was also made into the reason for FBI expansion of foreign liaison activities in 1970.
