Persons/Entities: Bissell, Richard; Gottlieb, Sidney; Harvey; White House; Siever, Arnold.

Date: February 1961 (Harvey's rough estimate). Categories: Executive Action / ZRRIFLE; Castro; Lindo + Congo.

Occurrence: Bissell assigns to Harvey the developing of “an executive action capability,” i.e., a general stand-by capability to carry out assassinations when required.” (37)

“Harvey's notes quote Bissell as saying, ‘The White House has twice urged me to create such a capability.’ Bissell recalls discussing the question of developing a general capability with Harvey.” (37)

“Harvey says that Bissell had already discussed certain aspects of the problem with Arnold (Siever and with Sidney Gottlieb.” (37–38)

Source: I.G. Report 1967, pp. 37–38.

Staff: Baron. Date: 28.6.75.
