14 September 1970: LIENVOY: Antonio Rosette asked for Cruz and whether the material had arrived on Monday's flight. It had not.

14 September 1970: LIENVOY: Francisco Mora (541-2852), artist, wanted to introduce Cruz to Raul Kampfner, Director of Cine.

15 September 1970: LIENVOY: Antonio Rosette called Cruz to see if the material he had filmed, presumably in Cuba, had arrived. Cruz said he would query Cuba and would go to Rosette's house on Thursday, the 17th, alone, to discuss this. Rosette's phones: 549-49-55; 516-01-58.

15 September 1970: Alberto Lopez called Cruz at the Embassy. Cruz was not in.

15 September 1970: Maria Eugenia Saul called Cruz at the Embassy. Cruz was not in.

15 September 1970: LIENVOY: Cruz called Prensa Latina. Roger Menendez was there, so Cruz talked to him. Roger said Cruz was a hard worker, as he could never find him. They agreed to talk next week to make a date to get together.

15 September 1970: LIFEAT: At 8:49, Mercedes called Cruz at the office; he was not in. She left word that people were in the apartment waiting for Cruz. He had a supper engagement, presumably at home.

17 September 1970: LIENVOY: Professor Edelberto Torres was at the Embassy looking for Cruz at noon. Comment: Possibly connected with the series of talks Torres sponsored at UNAM on Chile, pro-Cuba and pro-Communist.

17 September 1970: LIFEAT: Avila Camacho took an envelope to Cruz's apartment. Cruz was at Prensa Latina.

17 September 1970: LIFEAT: Carlos Carrillo called Cruz at home. Cruz was not in.
