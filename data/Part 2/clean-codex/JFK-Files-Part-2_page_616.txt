082253Z CITE MEXICO CITY 3306

Director info: JMWAVE, Guatemala City. PBRUMEN LIRING.

Ref: Director 73089.

1. Re questions asked ref, Wallace B. Rowton saw LIRING-3 off on plane which traveled nonstop from Mexico City to Guatemala City. L-3 said he stayed at Florida Hotel as planned and received phone calls there from FAR representatives. L-3 said he met several times and had lunch with Nicolas Batsakis, director of Metaloplastica, and brought back samples of plastic flowers made at the factory, which were seen by Rowton.

2. Rowton planning to query L-3 for more details of trip and descriptions of individuals he met. Next Rowton-L-3 meeting night of 8 February 1967. FAR representative, Mexico City, has not yet contacted L-3 to pick up medicine, radio tubes, and cognac.

3. HGS, please index Nicolas Batsakis and Metaloplastica to Mexico Station file P-12729.
