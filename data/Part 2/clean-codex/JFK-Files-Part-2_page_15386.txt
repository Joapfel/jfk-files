Memorandum: Donahoe to Belmont

Re: Intelligence Coverage of Friendly Foreign Countries

(2) Recommend our liaison representative at the same time discuss the proposed system with Mr. Murphy to be certain it will meet his needs and, if he is agreeable, recommend we institute this procedure and eliminate contact with the Bureau of Security and Consular Affairs on this program.

(3) At the same time, recommend liaison discuss with Mr. Murphy our existing target countries of United Arab Republic, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran, Lebanon, Jordan, Yemen, Sudan, India, Israel, Venezuela, Brazil, and Nationalist China; determine if he agrees to these targets; and obtain clearance for discreet investigations of selected individuals as potential sources, recognizing we will give him written data and seek specific individual clearance before we interview a potential source.

Detailed

6/18/59: Above target countries discussed with Murphy. He advised OK to investigate proposed source list; he will want to take up interviews on a case basis; that a U.S. citizen working for these countries will present much less of a problem than a national gone through this country. He said the facts surrounding these nationals will determine if approved before going on interview.

OJSBZ
