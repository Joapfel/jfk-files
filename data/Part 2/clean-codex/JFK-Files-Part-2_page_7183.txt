10. The Congressman was concerned that there was a duplication of effort and intently questioned the multichannel nature of reporting when only one decision could be taken. Col. Long, the Deputy J-2, was particularly eloquent in defense of multiple reporting channels, pointing out the speed and assurance of coverage when such were employed. He added that placing a unifying layer of bureaucracy on top would tend to delay the more rapid transmission of intelligence; to stifle dissenting views; and to create a monstrous-sized super-agency which might prove less susceptible to control and review. The Congressman was particularly secret.
