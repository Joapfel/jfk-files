Cable Traffic, Mexico City/Director

October 1963

72527 to MEXI from DIR, dated 10/1/63

1. Basic headquarters position—assist any responsible anti-Castro group. JURE qualifies. L/2 can give covert assistance but not jeopardize his operational utility. Recommend Lanier contact be maintained.

2. Julio Lanier Hevia was one of the original members of Pro-Unidad Cubana en Exilio, formed in February 1962.

3. Manolo Ray and Ernesto Betancourt (MEXI 6255) of ELC have had dealings in the past and jointly held discussions with the U.S. government on Cuban problems. Possible, therefore, that Lanier and Betancourt approaches are related.

79557 to MEXI from DIR, dated 31 October 1963

1. Do not wish MEXI station to intervene with the Mexican foreign minister on behalf of JURE representatives, as this would obviously establish official U.S. support for JURE. Headquarters not aware Litamil/2 has foreign minister contact, but if he can assist on the visa problem, no objection. It might be useful for L/2 to make a pretense of helping in order to appear cooperative to Lanier.

2. Angel Luis Guiu Figueredo was chief of military operations for MRP in Cuba until he took asylum in May 1961. WAVE asset from mid-1962 to mid-1963. Rogelio Cisneros Diaz is a member of the JURE Executive Board and former national coordinator of MRP in Cuba.

6455 to DIR from MEXI, dated 9 October 1963

1. JURE representative in Mexico, Julio Lanier Hevia, told Litamil that a long-range seaplane was lined up for use against Cuban soil. It is currently used commercially to haul shellfish. Based near Tampico. Two JURE leaders are coming to Mexico soon to inspect it. L/2 will try to elicit more details.

C/S comment: Requested timely reporting regarding any air strikes being planned or mounted against Cuba by anyone.

Cuban Ambassador to Mexico, 1963—Joaquin Hernandez Armas
