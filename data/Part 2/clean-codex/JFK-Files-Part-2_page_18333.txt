Dear Mr. Daly:

Enclosed is a list of documents which the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities may decide to declassify for its public hearings on the Huston Plan. We invite your opinion on any sections which you think ought to be sanitized before declassification. Of course, the great majority of key documents on the Huston Plan are already a matter of public record.

Since these hearings may be held as early as September 23rd and certainly by early October, your quick response would be most helpful.
