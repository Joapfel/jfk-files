On March 28, 1969, five Chicago BPP members were arrested in Chicago and charged with unlawful use of weapons.

On April 2, 1969, four Chicago BPP members were arrested in Robbins, Illinois (Chicago suburb) on charges of unlawful use of weapons and conspiracy to commit murder.

On April 12, 1969, four members of Chicago BPP were arrested. The charge was attempting to purchase machine guns.

On April 27, 1969, five members of Chicago BPP were arrested in Summit, Illinois (Chicago suburb) and charged with unlawful use of weapons, conspiracy to commit murder, robbery, and aggravated assault.

In regard to the above arrests, all BPP members’ cases are still in litigation. All above arrestees were leaders in BPP at Chicago, Illinois.
