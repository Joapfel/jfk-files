Ref: Mexico City 8944.

260355. BGL 09/05.

1. LIRING-3 reported to Wallace B. Rowton on 24 October 1966 that LIRING-4 passed a message to him the morning of 23 October that Reynaldo (Cepeda) Hernandez, First Secretary, Cuban Embassy, and identified DGI officer, wished to see him the same day. LIRING-3 met Cepeda the same morning in the embassy reception room. The meeting lasted about 30 minutes.

2. Cepeda made immediate reference to a recent conversation with LIRING-3, and LIRING-2 mentioned in the reference. LIRING-3, feeling he should play it close to the chest, replied he had talked with LIRING-2 and was planning a trip to Havana in early November for certain discussions. Cepeda replied he was aware of everything and had called LIRING-3 to state he was very interested in the whole affair and wanted to work closely with LIRING-3 in the future. Cepeda said he was planning to go to Havana on 12 November 1966 and suggested they both go together in order to keep close contact during discussions.

3. LIRING-3 replied he would try to revise his plans to arrange for the trip to coincide with that of Cepeda.

4. Rowton told LIRING-3 this clever plan of Cepeda to insinuate.

LIRING-1 — Abraham.
