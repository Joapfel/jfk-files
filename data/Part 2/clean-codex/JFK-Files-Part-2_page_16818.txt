Results of examination:

Caliber .45 Auto bullets

The specimens referred to above as Q190, Q198, Q207, Q205, Q210, Q218, Q222, Q235, Q251, Q275, and Q278 are all .45 Auto lead bullets that were identified as having been fired in the .45 Thompson submachine gun, serial number 5954, previously referred to as specimen K21.

Specimen Q193 is a mutilated .45 Auto bullet with the same general rifling characteristics as those referred to in the preceding paragraph. However, specimen Q193 did not have sufficient individual characteristics to permit identification.
