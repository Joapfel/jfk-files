Classified message. Date: 5/27/62. To: Director. From: JNWAVE. Action: TFW 10 (Mr. Charles Matt, new, notified and copy slotted). Info: DCI, DDP, ADDP, CA 22, GA/PRG, CA/PROP, S/C 2. Marked: at 10:30, 27 May. OPV DIF CITE PAVE 4777. GYMOSE.

1. Miami Herald, 27 May, carries front-page item by James Buchanan entitled “MIRO Fades as Exile Chief: Three Latin Leaders Behind Ray.” Article flatly states that MIRO and Council have been replaced by Manuel Ray, with backing from Governor Muñoz Marín (Monoz Marin), President Rómulo Betancourt (Rosario Betancourt), and former President José Figueras; that Ray has taken over the post with the blessing of the United States. Further, articles state: “From various sources Herald learned that MIRO was notified of change only this week,” and that Ray’s appointment as “chief in exile” came from “underground in Cuba,” according to Joaquín Godoy, provisional MRP head in Miami. After furnishing a résumé of Cuban activities since the origin of AMBUD, the article credits Cubans in Miami with saying the “New Man in Field” is Adolf A. Berle Jr., who has been special assistant to the State Department and is also a “Member of President Kennedy’s Special Task Force on Latin America.” The implication is that Berle is running a new United States-sponsored Cuban effort.

2. This release is causing some furor in the local Cuban colony, to the extent that at least one CPS agent destined for isolation training has asked to be relieved if there is any truth in this story. Meanwhile, WAVE.
