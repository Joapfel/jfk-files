1 February 1967 despatch (poor copy) containing JMWAVE and Chief, WHD—re: cancellation of Provincial Operational Approval. Among those listed is Ernesto (Arnesto) Napoleon Rodriguez Gonzales.

17 April 1961. Provincial Operational Approval granted 8 April 1960 and extended to 8 October 1961.

24 August 1960 dispatch from COS/Havana to Chief, WHD; info: COB, JMASH—#HKHA-6718. Conversation with AMIUTE/1 (Rodriguez), signed by Gerald L. Gardyne. Rodriguez gives information as case officer on information regarding anti-Castro military with which he is in contact. Says old military groups, about 10,000 strong, have organized to oppose Castro. Sent a representative to Miami to offer support to Tony Visona of the FRD.

6 April 1960—Request for Approval or Investigative Action report. Rodriguez will be used in audio surveillance operations.
