Chronological Schedule of Residences and Employments of: Frank Sturgis

Present address: 2515 N.W. 122nd Street, Miami, Florida 33167.

Also known as:

Frank Anthony Sturgis  
Frank A. Sturgis  
Frank Angelo Fiorini  
Frank A. Fiorini  
Frank Fiorini  
Frank Angelo Fiorino

1924, December 9: Born in Norfolk, Va.

1930–1942: Resided at 510 High Street, Germantown, Philadelphia, Pa.

1942–1945: U.S. Marine Corps Reserve (Parris Island, S.C.; Pacific theatre).

1945: Honorable discharge, Klamath (Klamouth) Falls, Ore.

1945: Miami, Florida.

Circa 1945–1948: Norfolk, Va. (drs: Bank St., etc.); joined Norfolk Police Department.

Attended William and Mary College; also attended Virginia Polytechnic Institute.

Circa 1946: Employed as manager of the Virginia Tavern on East Main Street.

Circa 1948: Enlisted in the U.S. Naval Reserve at the Norfolk Naval Air Station.

Circa 1949: Employed as owner and manager of the Whitehorse Tavern on East Main Street.

Circa 1949: Honorable discharge.

Circa 1949–1950: U.S. Merchant Marine, travelling to and from Europe.

Circa 1950–1952: U.S. Army, stationed in Germany.

Circa 1952: Honorable discharge.

Circa 1952–1954: Owner-manager of Tophat Nightclub in Virginia Beach, Va.

Circa 1956–1961: Cuba (Sierra Maestra); Miami, Florida; Mexico; Venezuela; Costa Rica; Guatemala; Panama; Honduras; Bahama Islands.

1958: Contact with CIA at U.S. Consulate, Santiago, Cuba.

1959: Contact with CIA at U.S. Embassy, Havana, Cuba.

1959: Contact with FBI Headquarters, Washington, D.C.

1961–1970: Bay of Pigs.

CIA control officer: Sam Jenis.
