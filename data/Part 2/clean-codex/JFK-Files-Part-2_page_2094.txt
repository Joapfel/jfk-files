James L. Arthur Noel (Jam L. Arthur NOEL), 511 Alban Towers, 3700 Massachusetts Ave., N.W., Washington, D.C.

Birth: March 19, 1911.

Application for Federal Employment.

Date of employment: January 1931 to October 1938. Exact title of position: Vice Consul. Salary: starting $1,800 per annum; final $2,000 per annum. Place of employment: Ensenada, Mexico. Immediate supervisor: Mr. A. Smale, Consul. Employer: State Department, Foreign Service. Reason for leaving: transfer to Mazatlan. Number and kind of employees supervised: two consular clerks.

Description of work: General consular work, including shipping services, visas, passports, welfare and protection, agricultural reporting, and drafting of replies to trade inquiries.
