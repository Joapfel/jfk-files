Classified message.

10 May 1953, 2335Z. Cite: Mexico City 5540. Guatemala City info Director.

Ref: Mexico City 5375 (No. 98384).

1. Manuel H. Marcue Pardinas (MARCUE) informed LIRING-4, 5 May, that his sister and daughter-in-law had succeeded in obtaining Guatemalan visas and traveled to Guatemala City in an effort to see Carlos Miguel (MINUEL) Marcue (PERSAVAL) in jail. Marcue said his sister and daughter-in-law, who returned during the week of 12 May, learned that PERSAVAL and LIRING-3 were sentenced to a minimum term of six months.

2. LIRING-4 is currently exploring the possibility of traveling to Guatemala to talk to LIRING-3. The station is in favor of such travel if she can get a visa, as LIRING-3 can be expected to brief LIRING-4 on Cuban matters during his term of imprisonment.

3. Can Guatemala City station confirm LIRING-3’s sentence and provide details?

BT
