Memorandum

To: Acting Director, FBI

From: SAC, Cincinnati (100-20519) (RUC)

Subject: “FURIES” Newspaper, Women’s Liberation Movement Publication, Washington, D.C.; Revolutionary Activities (OO: WFO)
