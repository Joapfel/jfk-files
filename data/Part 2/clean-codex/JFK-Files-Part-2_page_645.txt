6 July 1966. No. 51413.

Director info: JUNAVE.

DYVCUR BRUMEN LIRING.

Ref: Mexico City 77:(43725).

1. Carlos Jurado Delmar told Wallace S. Rowton (Vallace S. Rowton) in July 1966 he had discussed Castro’s disappearance with Miguel Lozano Villalobos, counselor of the Cuban Embassy, and the latter reported Castro’s drop from the scene was due to an operation for double hernia. Lozano told Jurado he learned this from a British citizen who came out of Cuba in the last few days after spending three years there assisting the agriculture program. Lozano reported the Britisher was very close to Fidel.

2. Jurado also told Rowton Castro had overexerted himself during the recent Guantanamo incident, which induced hernia. Jurado also opined Castro had somehow incurred Soviet displeasure. Rowton did not question him on where he got this idea, but it may also have come from Lozano.

Ref: R.Y.BAT.
