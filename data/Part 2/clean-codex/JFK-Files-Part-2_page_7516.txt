1 February 1970. 1 July 1970. OOB 321/17309-70. Fnu Moskalev and P.V. Ramzayev were scheduled to attend the Biology & Ecology of Polonium and Radiolead Conference, Sutton, U.K., 30 April to 1 May 1970, but apparently were not permitted by the USSR to attend.

24 August 1970. OOB 321/20573-70. Moskalev was among the Soviet delegation to the Second International Congress on Radiation Protection, Brighton, U.K., 3–8 May 1970. Moskalev personally attended virtually every session and was very busy asking many questions. He asked all questions through an interpreter, but when answers were given in English, he always seemed to understand without benefit of his interpreter.

13 November 1970. OOA(S) 322/33488-70. In September 1970, Moskalev visited a U.S. facility devoted to research in the field of nuclear medicine. “It is obvious that he is a careful and trustworthy worker.” Visited Brookhaven in October 1963; Sun Valley in 1967; and the Hanford Biology Symposium in 1969.
