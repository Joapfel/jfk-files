6, 06:05 a.m., urgent, 8 August 1971, JEW. To Director (52-94527). Newark (52-6817). From Philadelphia (52-7135 SUB-S).

MEDBURG; Camden action. O.O.: Philadelphia.

Principal source, Camden action reported meeting held 8:00 p.m. Saturday, 7 August, at apartment of Michael Giocondo, 229 Cooper Street, Camden, New Jersey. John Peter Grady not present, as he was visiting family at New Jersey village seashore area. Active surveillances from roof of townhouse lots and on-street surveillances commenced 9:55 p.m. Saturday, 7 August, and continued to 4:50 a.m. Sunday, 8 August. Following persons present and participated: Father Edward J. McGowan (MC Gowan), Father Edward J. Murphy, Father Michael J. Doyle, Robert Hardy, Robert W. Williamson, Kathleen M. Rodolfi, Anita A. Ricci, Michael J. Giocondo, Robert G. Good, Ann Dunham, identified white female.

SAC MEDBURG instituted full coverage of post office building, area Camden, New Jersey. Rooftop and on-street surveillances observed, subjects’ communications monitored, and nine subjects photographed at their surveillance poses. At approximately 12:30 a.m., McGowan (MC Gowan) and Murphy told source the plans to break-in appear.
