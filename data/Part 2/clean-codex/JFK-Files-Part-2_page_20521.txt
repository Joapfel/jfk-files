FBI.

Date: 11/12/71.

To: Benedict, E.E.; Anderson, R.D.  
From: SAC, New York (100-174481).  
Subject: National Democrat Action Coalition (WONACO) sponsored demonstration, November 3, 1971, Washington, D.C. — IS - New Left (OC:NFO).

NEWFO airtel, dated 11/5/71. Informant who has furnished reliable information in the past advised on 11/2/71. MAC & NY planning meeting of captioned group was held at the Loeb Student Center, New York University, New York, NY, on 11/11/71.

For the information of the Bureau and WFO, plans are being made to call a national convention of 1,000 individuals.

If this is not done by November 15, 1971, I will require the same day.

The above meeting was completely dominated and controlled by members of the Communist Workers Union.

(C) — Pymon (PM), November 15, 1971.
