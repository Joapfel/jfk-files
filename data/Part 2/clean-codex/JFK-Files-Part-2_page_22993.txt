Mr. Baron. Can we turn now to the structure of the JMWAVE Station and explain that a bit? How large was the Station? What was the command structure within the Station?

Mr. Halley. The Station started expanding in whatever timeframe it was, somewhere after the survey was completed. It must have started expanding sometime in March of 1962.
