1. Action required: None, for station background only. Response to reference questions regarding the sharing of information with liaison will be provided in a separate message.

2. Headquarters believes subject to be identical to William Leonard Oltmans, (DPOB 10 June 1925, Huizen, the Netherlands), who was assigned 201-0155221 on 27 February 1957, on the occasion of a name check request against PNINFINITE files from NVGIFT. At the time, Oltmans was ostensibly a correspondent for an obscure Dutch weekly publication at the United Nations in New York. His reputation at the UN was such that he was ostracized by the Dutch delegation and fellow Dutch newsmen for his openly pro-Indonesian public stand on the Dutch Guinea question. He also claimed to work for an unknown news syndicate in the United States, but none of his colleagues could claim to have seen any of his writings published. His main income seemed to have been derived from public lectures in the United States on international politics, especially matters touching on Asia in general, and Indonesia specifically. He claimed he grew up in Indonesia, and therefore knew the area and its “conditions” well. In addition, he was constantly seen with what a Western source would call “the wrong crowd,” and he had excellent contacts with all the communist-dominated countries, including Cuba, and was on intimate terms with elements such as the Guineans, the Indonesians, and the Egyptians.
