1/25/66

Re: Mylet to Bureau, 12/9/65, captioned as above.

Enclosed are six copies for the Bureau and two copies for New York of a LIM, dated and captioned as above, which sets forth information concerning contact between Sam Jaffe and subject.

It is noted that Sam Jaffe is the subject of closed WFO file 105-8906 (Buffer 105-28333; W7 file 134-3315), and information has been previously furnished to the Bureau.

The report set forth the results of an interview on 12/2/65, and therein it was noted he was told by Amzoli Kanlev, Number 5 man in the Press Department of the Soviet Foreign Ministry, that if there was anything the Soviets could do for the informant, Oleg Kalugin was in Washington, D.C. The informant then went on to say that he was acquainted with Kalugin.

Bureau: 105-28333 (Jaffe); 105-40630; 134-5315 (Jaffe). New York. WFO: 105-8906 (Jaffe).
