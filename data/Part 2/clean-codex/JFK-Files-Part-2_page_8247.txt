30 April 1950 (date of information)

The Bulgarians at Lavrion Camp established a new political organization known as the “P. D. Petkov Organization.” The members, who are listed in the protocol attached to the report, have deserted the Bulgarian Agrarian Party in an effort to retain their “pure” agrarian character. In the opinion of the Petkov Organization, the Agrarian Party has been penetrated by the leftists. Protocol signed on 30 April 1950.

DIMITROV’s name appears as a member of the Board of Control.

SO 48993, 16 September 1950
