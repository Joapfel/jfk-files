April 21, 1975

United States Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations Within Respect to Intelligence Activities

Reference is made to letter dated March 19, 1975, to the Attorney General from the Chairman of the captioned Committee, which enclosed certain requests for documents and other information from the Federal Bureau of Investigation.

One of the requests, under V., A., was the Special Report of the Interagency Committee on Intelligence (Ad Hoc) dated June 1970. This is the so-called “Huston Report” and was prepared for the White House. Under the circumstances, this request has been referred to the White House.

Under V., B., a request was made for the original report on COINTELPRO activities prepared by the Committee headed by Assistant Attorney General Henry Petersen (Petorsen). Since that report is the property of the Department of Justice, the request has been referred to that Department.
