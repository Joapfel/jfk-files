Bobby Lee Rush

31 August 1971

1 — Chicago Guidance  
1 — Mr. D. A. Brumble

Compliance Division, Internal Revenue Service (IRS), U.S. Department of the Treasury, Washington, D.C., has advised that their master files show no record of tax returns filed by the subject for 1969. They have initiated an investigation to secure any returns which may be due, and the Bureau will be advised upon completion of the investigation. Upon receipt of the same by the Bureau, Chicago will be advised.

Under no circumstances is the information set forth above to be disseminated outside the Bureau, and it is not to be included in any item to be disseminated at a future date.

DAB:mcm

Note:

Rush is a top leader and national functionary in the Black Panther Party (BPP). As Minister of Defense, Chicago Chapter of BPP, he administers Midwestern U.S. BPP activities, which in the past included attacks on police, bombings, and stockpiling of weapons. The Bureau has previously furnished IRS with his complete background. Information from IRS was in response to a request made of them by the Bureau on March 9, 1971, through the Attorney General.
