First Assistant United States Attorney Schmetterer (SCHMetterER) advised at 4:05 p.m. on December 19, 1969, that he had been in contact with Cook County Coroner Anthony J. Toman. Toman advised Mr. Schmetterer that, as the premises at 2337 West Monroe Street, Chicago, had now been sealed and were under his control, he would grant access to the FBI at any time for the purpose of examination.

It is noted that an FBI examination of these premises commenced on the morning of December 22, 1969.

Mr. Schmetterer advised on December 22, 1969, that the December 1969 Federal Grand Jury was extended and commenced receiving into evidence the official reports of the Cook County Coroner's Office concerning the deaths of Fred A. Hampton and Mark Clark on December 4, 1969. This evidence was made available to the Federal Grand Jury by Kyram Philan from the coroner's office. It is noted that on December 22, 1969, a Special Agent of the Chicago Office was sworn in and made an agent of this Federal Grand Jury.
