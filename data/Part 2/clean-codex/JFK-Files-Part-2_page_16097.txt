2/2/70

Teletype

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago

Black Panther Party (BPP). RM—BPP. Co: San Francisco. Reliable in past.

Confidential source advised instant rally scheduled for three p.m., January four, next, at Afro-Arts Theater, 3937 South Drexel Boulevard, Chicago. Rally in memorial to Fred Dalton, deceased deputy chairman, Illinois BPP (RPP), killed December four, last, in raid conducted by Cook County State’s Attorney’s Police.

Source also advises BPP tentatively planning to announce opening of “People’s Medical Center,” the purpose of which will be to treat indigent residents.

Source states Presby Kosey Rush, deputy minister of defense, Illinois BPP; Bob Lucas, leader, Black Liberation Alliance, Chicago; and Alderman A. A. “Sammy” Rather being advertised as speakers. Entertainment will also be provided according to source, and contributions solicited. Money obtained.
