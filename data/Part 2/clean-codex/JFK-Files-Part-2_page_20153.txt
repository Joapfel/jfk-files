Attachment dated.

From: New York 100-174481, 2 pages.

Demonstration in front of the White House, October eleven next, sponsored by Women’s Abortion Action Coalition, IS-C (Trotskyist (Rotskyist)).

On October three last, confidential source, who has furnished reliable information in the past, made available a flyer captioned “Attention, all women. Demonstrate to demand the Supreme Court rule in favor of a women’s right to choose.” Published by Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (WONACO), 150 Fifth Avenue, New York City.

Flyer encouraged all women to appear October eleven next, at twelve noon, in front of White House, Washington, D.C., to present demands for the right to the abortion law. SF-113 indicates that on that date, United States Supreme Court will reject suits (shits) challenging constitutionality of restrictive Texas and Georgia abortion laws. Flyer states it is presently October 31, 1972.

Mayor to demonstrate in front of Supreme Court and woman.
