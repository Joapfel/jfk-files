INS File Reviews, page 3.

C. Joe Campisi.

File No. A12 531 109.

8/5/69 letter: Says JC, Peter J. Piccola, and Jimmy I. Maggiota are of interest to the O.C. and Racket Section of the Justice Department.

3/7/62 report: Verifies JC's citizenship through his father and school records. Carlo Campisi says he came to the U.S. with his wife in 1907 to Louisiana and moved to Dallas in 1917. Lists JC as a gambler, making bets in excess of $1,000 on baseball games, and says he knows many influential people in Dallas city government and DPD.

11/7/61 memo: INS investigator lists Dallas residents who are involved in gambling rackets and/or liquor rackets: JC; S. Campisi; Jimmy Maggiotta; Joe L. Tinnerrello; Joseph J. Colletta; John Michael Carcelli; P. Piccola; Johnny Patrona.
