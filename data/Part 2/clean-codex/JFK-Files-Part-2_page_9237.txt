A Program for Survival

Free Breakfast Program: Provides children a free hot breakfast every school morning.

Free Busiest to Release Progress: Provides free transportation to private for families and friends of incarcerated men and women.

David Hillard People's Free Shoe Program: Provides free shoes to the people made at the David Hillard Free Shoe Factory and elsewhere.

People's Free Food Program: Provides free food to Black and other oppressed people.

This is the list of the programs that the Detroit branch is sponsoring, but they can't be successful without the help from you, the people!
