The cable reported that Contreras was a reporter for El Sol in Tampico; was circa thirty years old; married, with three children; studied law at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) from 1960 to 1964; belonged to a pro-Castro group at UNAM; was persecuted by the Mexican police for this affiliation and moved to Tampico to escape the persecution.

Contreras told the Mexico City Station official that he and four other individuals had met Oswald as they exited a roundtable discussion held at the School of Philosophy at UNAM. Contreras stated that Oswald had made inquiries on the UNAM campus about pro-Cuban groups and had been directed to his group. Contreras reported that though the group initially mistrusted Oswald, fearing he was a "CIA provocation," they allowed Oswald to remain with them that day and night and part of the following day.
