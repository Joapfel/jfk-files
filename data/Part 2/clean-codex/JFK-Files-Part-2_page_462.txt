NAPOK PERUNEN LIRING.

Reference: Mexico City 8458 (13 8/30/65).

All LIRINGS told Wallace B. Foston that in Havana airport, 29 August, as he left ENARK on CLEANA to Mexico City, he met Lino Salazar, former employee, Clean Embassy Mexico. Salazar told LIRING/S he was at the airport to escort Virginia A. Gomez Cuevas de Lizalde to the plane and see her, S.P.R., if on way back to Mexico City.

A. Virginia is sister of Gumersindo Gomez Cuevas, arrested in Mexico SALAN roundup. Gomez, according to police statement, was reportedly in charge of drawing up plans on essential industrial centers in Mexico for Mexico City 82410. 1EN 16854-).

S. LIRING talked with Virginia on flight. She appeared very depressed over arrest of her brother and friends in Mexico. She said she had been unable to do any sightseeing in Havana. LIRING/S inferred from the fact she was escorted to the airport that she must have been in contact with CLEAR officials in Havana. On return flight she acknowledged having no money, and LIRING/S pressed 150 AD on her as a friendly loan. LIRING/S also concluded from her sad appearance that CUEAS officials had given her UCO.
