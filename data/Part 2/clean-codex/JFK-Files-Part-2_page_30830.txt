Angleton, Office of Security. Teletype, November 1974. From FBIS London to RUDC/AMEMB London.

Angleton Interview / London Domestic T.V.

A. Well, I can say that mail coverage was very successful on USWALD...

Corr. What do you mean successful?

A. Well, successful in the sense that when he appeared as the assassin of the FRES. and when I went back in the fields there was correspondence from Moscow that gave an insight that no agents could possibly have given to the man.
