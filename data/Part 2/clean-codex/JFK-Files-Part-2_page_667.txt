MELI. 27 May 1966, 0105Z. Cite Mexico City 7457 (Easby acting). Director info JMWAVE. KAPOK PBRUMEN LIRING. Reference: Mexico City 7454 (W 24660).

26 May 1966

1. LIRING-3 purchased Cubana ticket for Cuba 26 May, with money provided by ROWTON. Will depart 27 May. Signed true-name receipt for funds. Plans stay week. Has contacted friends among Cuban artists with whom will live while on island.

2. LIRING-3 told ROWTON he met ARNOL RODRIGUEZ CAMPS in 1962 while LIRING-3 employed with Cuban Ministry of Education. Actually, wife Rodriguez worked under LIRING-3 and friendly association began through wife. Rodriguez then working for Ministry of Labor.

3. Quick action on LIRING-3 visa brought queries from Cuban Consul, Mexico City, ABELARDO CURBELO PADRON, as to whom LIRING-3 knew in Cuba. CURBELO (CUPBEL) said even for Cubans it takes a week. LIRING-3 told ROWTON he answered only that he had friends in Cuba. ROWTON gave LIRING-3 money with which he was buying gifts in Mexico for RODRIGUEZ and family.
