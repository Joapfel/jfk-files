Cristina Aron, Clearwater, Fla., also had FCC meetings at her home. Her husband, Manuel Aron, had been chairman of the Tampa FPCC. On 19 August 1963, they moved to Tampa.

Cristina Aron announced at the 8 December 1963 meeting that Lopez had arrived safely in Cuba.

Carried courtesy Cuban visa at time of issuance of pass. Tourist card. Lopez had said his destination was Mexico, valid for 15 days.

Louis Frierze, Tampa Art and Music Co., 205 East Tyler St., Tampa, placed an ad for FPCC in the Tampa Tribune on or about 15 May 1963. Tampa FPCC did not exist at that time.
