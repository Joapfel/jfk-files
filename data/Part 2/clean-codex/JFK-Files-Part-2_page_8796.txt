WAVE 8316 (IN 70529), dated 30 November 1963. 19-6-68/3, Folder No. 4.

Paragraph 1 gave translation of WAVE 8279 (IN 69695), Message 45, from AMWEE-1 to case officer:

“Our condolences on death of President. MAURI (Mario MAURI, Italian diplomat) reports on 22 November that the Cubana aircraft delayed its departure five hours, from 1700 to 2200, waiting for an important passenger who arrived at Mexico City Airport in a private twin-motor aircraft at 2130 hours Mexico (Mexi) time, transferring directly from his plane to the Cubana plane without going through customs or immigration. Travelled in pilot’s cabin. MAURI returned on same aircraft to Cuba. I shall come out 29 November but do not know whether I can stay. Best that you come to the airport that day. Here there is great movement of troops toward Pinar del Rio.”

Comment: See AOCOVE Operation Memo, AMCOVE-1 SW Message No. 71, dated 10 December 1963. [A-1] reported that Mario MAURI flew from Mexico to Havana on 23 November.
