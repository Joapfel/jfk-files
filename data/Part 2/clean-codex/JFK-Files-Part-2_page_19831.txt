United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Detroit (100-40622)

Subject: Women’s National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAC) Convention, February 11–13, 1972, Boston, Massachusetts, IS-C (Trotskyist)

Date: 3/1/72

Re: Detroit teletypes dated 2/8 and 2/16/72.

Enclosed for the Bureau are eight copies of a letterhead memorandum dated and captioned as above. No handouts are being furnished the Bureau, as it is anticipated Boston will provide all miscellaneous paraphernalia Boston deems necessary for effective coverage.

Enclosed for Boston, Chicago, Cleveland, Philadelphia, and New York are two copies each of the captioned LHM, as persons identified in the LHM reside within each respective division.

This LHM is being classified confidential in order to protect the source, who is of continuing value, the disclosure of which might be prejudicial to the national defense interests of the nation.

The source is DE 2278-PSI. Location is this LHM.

Each receiving office is requested to make additional copies of this LHM as needed to disseminate to individual case files.

Bureau (100-43809). Boston, Chicago, Cleveland, Philadelphia, New York, and Detroit.

Agency: Army, ONI, OSI, Secret Service, State, DIA.
