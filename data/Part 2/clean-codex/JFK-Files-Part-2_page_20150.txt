According to the source, following the press conference, Doctor Roberts departed and the other four women proceeded inside the Supreme Court building, where they attended the hearings from 9:20 a.m. to 11:45 a.m.

This source advised that a WONAA-sponsored demonstration was held in front of the White House, WDC, from approximately 12 noon until 1:30 p.m., with approximately 25 to 30 women participating in the demonstration, the purpose of which was to express sentiment for legalized abortion. During the course of the demonstration, participants chanted, “Sisters unite, stand up and fight, abortion is our right,” and carried various signs, some of which read, “Abortion is my right,” “My body, my life, my right to decide,” and “Women must decide our fate, not church or state.”

According to the source, there were no incidents or arrests during the press conference or during the demonstration.
