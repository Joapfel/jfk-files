II. Identity of Leaders and Membership Information

A. Officers of Chicago Chapter BPP

As of July 1969, the following individuals, listed in alphabetical order, were reported by sources as officers of the Central Staff of the Chicago Chapter BPP:

Brooks, Billy Lamar, born July 18, 1948. Brooks, commonly known as “Che,” is the Deputy Minister of Education, Illinois BPP, and acts as Field Secretary.
