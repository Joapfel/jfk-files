Subject: DDO Coordination on DDS&T's Review of Deletions from The CIA and the Cult of Intelligence by Marchetti and Marks

Item 25, manuscript page 47. Item 159, manuscript page 181. Item 160, manuscript page 182. Item 161, manuscript page 183. Item 162, manuscript page 184. Item 163, manuscript page 184. Item 164, manuscript page 185. Item 205, manuscript page 234. Item 208, manuscript page 235. Item 209, manuscript page 236. Item 295, manuscript page 424. Item 297, manuscript page 425. Item 299, manuscript page 426. Item 301, manuscript page 427.

2. In Tab D of referent, the DDS&T deferred to DDO or OS the decision on the classification of Item 190, manuscript page 214, relating to Soviet capabilities to make intelligible the sounds picked up by audio devices discovered in the code room of the American Embassy in Moscow. The appropriate DDO Component finds that this item must remain classified since it relates to cryptography and is exempt from the General Declassification Schedule of.
