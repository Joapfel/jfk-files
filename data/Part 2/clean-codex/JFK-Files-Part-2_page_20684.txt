United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI (100-468406)

From: SAC, Los Angeles (100-79014)(c)

Subject: Women's National Abortion Action Coalition (WONAAO) IS-C; Los Angeles Women's Abortion Action Committee IS—New Left

Date: 4/27/72

Re Los Angeles letter and letterhead memorandum (LHM) dated 12/9/71, and Los Angeles report of SA Richard J. Raysa dated 12/23/71, captioned “Women's Liberation Movement.”

For information of the Bureau and New York, referenced communications report activities sponsored by the Los Angeles Women's Abortion Action Committee (LAWAAC), a Los Angeles-based affiliate of WONAAO, which is headquartered in New York City.

Although LAWAAC continues to exist in name, this group has not been active in sponsoring activities since its participation in the 11/20/71 WONAAO-sponsored demonstration at San Francisco, California.

It is anticipated that LAWAAC will sponsor or participate in future demonstrations, and Los Angeles will report any future activity by this group.

Bureau. New York. Los Angeles.
