1. Request traces and POA Jorge Antonio Abasolo Garcia, Cuban, DOB 1927, industrial engineer, graduate Georgia Tech, address Calle 96 Oxaber 308, telephone 2-6301, Miramar, Havana. Subject divorced from Marie Paz Someillan, who now reported living with two children ages 5 and 7 in MNTY. Subject chief of division in Ministry Industries. Works closely with Ernesto “Che” Guevara. Controls wood furniture industry, portion of paper industry including paper sacks, toy industry, one other unspecified industry. Has had some contact with Soviets in connection with cement procurement; recently reported belief Soviets constructing submarine base in Cuba. Came to Mexico on special passport 2 August ’63 to see children, but former wife managed to prevent contact. Returned Cuba 21 August.

2. Brother of subject is Gustavo Abasolo, Cuban, about 42, left Cuba about 1959 or 1960, going first to Bermuda, later to Mexico. Employed continuously 22 years by Compania Interamericana de Seguros. Now assistant manager of sales for Mexico. Strongly anti-Castro, friend of AMSEVER-2. Corresponds with subject through Netherlands Embassy diplomatic pouch. At station request through A-2 Gustavo.
