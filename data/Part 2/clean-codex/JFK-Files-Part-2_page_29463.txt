(3) Joseph Burkholder Smith Allegation

Mr. Joseph Smith, a retired CIA officer, was interviewed by the House Select Committee on Assassinations on 19 October 1977. At this interview Mr. Smith mentioned that the Mexico City Station had been very proud of finding “the picture of Oswald.”

He mentioned this in connection with a woman who worked for Winston Scott in Mexico. Mr. Smith was reluctant to speak about this at the time and the topic was not pursued.
