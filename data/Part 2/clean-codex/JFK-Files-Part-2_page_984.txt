Important.—The filing of this form will completely cancel any designation of beneficiary under the Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance Act you may have previously filed. Be sure to name in this form all persons you wish to designate as beneficiaries of any group life and accidental death insurance payable under that act at your death.

Examples of Designations

How to Designate One Beneficiary

Mary E. Brown: 214 Central Avenue, Muncie, Ind.; niece; share: all.

How to Designate More Than One Beneficiary

Alice M. Long: 809 Canal Street, Red Bank, N. J.; aunt; share: one-fourth. Joseph P. Brady: 360 Williams Street, Red Bank, N. J.; nephew; share: one-fourth. Catherine L. Rowe: 792 Broadway, Whiting, Ind.; mother; share: one-half.

How to Designate a Contingent Beneficiary

John M. Parrish, if living: 810 West 180th Street, New York, N. Y.; father; share: all. Otherwise to: Susan A. Parrish: 810 West 180th Street, New York, N. Y.; sister; share: all.
