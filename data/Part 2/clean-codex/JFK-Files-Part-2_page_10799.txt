At approximately 4:35 a.m., August 29, 1971, the Selective Service space and surrounding area located on the fifth floor, U.S. Post Office, 401 Market Street, Camden, N.J., was entered by Special Agents of the Federal Bureau of Investigation. Special Agents Lugeli (Lugene), S. Debbaudt, and Tong, E. Tillerly entered the area and surrounded the Selective Service Office by way of the building fire escape. Special Agents Robert H. Waller and Joe R. Gingerich entered the Selective Service Office through the front door, which opened onto the fifth-floor corridor.

Upon entrance into this space, Special Agents Debbaudt, Tillerly, Waller, and Gingerich announced the FBI's presence and instructed everyone to remain as they were until told to do otherwise.

Special Agents Debbaudt and Waller immediately placed under arrest three individuals who were located within the Selective Service space. A male, later identified as Robert G. Good, was standing in the area of Local Board #9. A female, later identified as Kathleen Mary Ridolfi, was standing next to Good. A male, later identified as Michael John Giocondo, was in the area of Local Board #7. Special Agents Debbaudt and Waller moved these persons to the center of the Selective Service space and had them lie on the floor. No questions were asked of these persons other than their names, which they refused to furnish.

On 8/22/71 at Camden, N.J.
