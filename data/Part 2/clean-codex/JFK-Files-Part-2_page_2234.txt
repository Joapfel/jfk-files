Proposed Biography

O'Neal, Birch Dilworth—born Bainbridge, Georgia, June 1, 1913; University of Georgia, A.B. 1935; University of Georgia Law School, LL.B. 1937; attorney, private practice, 1937–38; attorney, Department of Justice, 1938–42; to the Department of State, attorney, Mexico City, D.F., and Bogotá (Mo. ota), Colombia, 1942–45; attorney, self-employed, 1946–47; to the Department of State, attorney, Caracas, 1947–49; legal officer, Department of Defense, 1949–51; married.
