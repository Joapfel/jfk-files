REBUAIRTEL to Albany, December 5, 1967.

Kinley Jones, reporter, WSB Television Station, Atlanta, Ga., advised on December 28, 1967, that during an interview with Jeannette Rankin at Watkinsville, Ga., on December 26, 1967, she stated she intended to lead the proposed March of Women in Washington, D.C., on January 15, 1968, at which time they would march to the Capitol Building and present a petition for redress of grievances to John McCormack, Speaker of the House of Representatives. Rankin stated she had received a telegram from McCormack informing her he would be glad to greet them.

Rankin explained that Mrs. Vivian Hallinan of San Francisco, Calif., informed her sometime ago that a group of women had formed an organization called the Jeannette Rankin Brigade and asked her if she would be the honorary president of the organization, at which time she informed them she would be delighted to serve.

Rankin stated she was asking women to express ideas against the institution of war and that she was opposed to the use of violence to settle any dispute.

Atlanta continuing attempts to obtain additional details on January 4, 1968.
