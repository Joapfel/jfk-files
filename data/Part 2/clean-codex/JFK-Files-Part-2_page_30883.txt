The above individuals were selected for interview because of the positions they held at the time with the CIA at the Tokyo Station during the period from 1959 to 1964.

James Wiley gave testimony in a closed hearing before the SCA that a CIA case officer told him that LHO was a CIA agent and also mentioned LHO’s cryptographic specialist.

This took place at the Tokyo Station shortly after word of the JFK (SFK) assassination was received on 29 November 1963. Wiley stated that he could not recall the name of the case officer or the encryptionist. Wiley also advised that considerable conversation took place among CIA employees in Tokyo at the time concerning the Oswald-CIA agent issue.

The basic questions asked of the CIA employees selected were as follows:

(a) Have you ever seen written documents that could indicate that LHO was a CIA (EIS) agent or a source of information?

(b) Were you ever advised orally that LHO was an agent?

(c) Have you ever heard conversation or heard, speculatively, that LHO was a CIA agent or intelligence source for any government service?

(d) Were you in a position to know if LHO was recruited as an agent during his tour of duty with the military in Japan?
