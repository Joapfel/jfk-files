Country: South Vietnam. Report No. TDCSDB-3/655,558. Subject: Status of coup d'etat plans of Tran Kim Tuyen's group. Date distributed: 16 July 1963. Date of info: 15 July 1963. References: IN 80366. Place and date acquired: Vietnam, Saigon (15 July 1963). Field report No. FVS: 9309. Appraisal: 3.

This is unevaluated information. Source gradings are definitive; appraisal of content is tentative.

Source: A high-level Vietnamese official (C) with extensive contacts among government leaders.

On 15 July 1963 an individual associated with the coup d'etat group headed by Dr. Tran Kim Tuyen, currently an advisor at the presidency, said that their nationalist, anti-Communist coup would occur “soon.” However, as the Viet Cong were reportedly planning to intensify their activities during the week prior to the anniversary of the Geneva Accords, on 20 July, the coup would not be staged until after that date. The coup would be directed against President Diem (Demas) as well as the Ngo Dinh Nhus (Ngo Dinh Nhus), and the group planned to establish a constitutional.
