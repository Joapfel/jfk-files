NSC Action No.

483 NSC 2422a, 5/5/61. Substance: Agreed that US policy toward Cuba should aim at the downfall of Castro regime. Status: According to files, out of this NSC directive grew a policy paper, to be handled differently from regular guidelines papers on each country.

As of 9/4/62, White House was informed that action on this NSC directive would be reported in compliance with NSAM 181 (NASAM 181), which transferred certain NSC & NASA actions to special group (Alexis Johnson).

483 NSC 2422d, 5/5/61. Substance: Importance President attaches to obtaining adequate intelligence on Cuban military capabilities. Status: Pending. Action to be reported in compliance with NSAM 181 (Johnson channel).

483 NSC 2422e, 5/5/61. Substance: Importance President attaches to publication in free world press of terrorist actions of Castro regimes, etc. and to possible political action to end current terror. Status: Pending. Action transferred to EXCOM.

483 NSC 2422g, 5/5/61. Substance: Relations with Cuban Revolutionary Council should be improved. Status: Pending. Action transferred to EXCOM.

483 NSC 2422i, 5/5/61. Substance: Cuban refugees to be given refugee status; refugees in Miami to be relocated; refugees to be eligible to apply for travel privileges. Status: Pending. Action transferred to EXCOM.

483 NSC 2422k, 5/5/61. Substance: Initiation of negotiations to enlarge willingness of other American states to join in action against Castro. Status: Pending. Action transferred to EXCOM.
