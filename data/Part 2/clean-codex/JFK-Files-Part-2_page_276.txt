LICOOKY Project Progress Report

March 1965

1. Production

The LICOOKY Project has not produced any information reports during this reporting period. During this period LICOOY-1 has been reporting on her activities and working as follows:

a. L-1 learned that the Guatemalan PGF have approached Bartolomé Costa-Amic (Costs-Amic) about printing a “newsletter” to be distributed in Guatemala. When this occurs she will be able to obtain copies and identify the authors and other pertinent details.

b. Continuing reports on the activities of Miguel Angel Toralla Burgos, Guatemalan guerrilla now in exile in Mexico. L-1 has established that Toralla meets Victor Manuel Gutiérrez once a week at some undisclosed place and time.

c. L-1 is attempting to identify and obtain the address and telephone number of one “Juanita.” “Juanita” acts as a courier to Guatemala for Gutiérrez and also acts as a cutout between Gutiérrez and Dr. Raúl Osegueda Pelalá (Oseguida Pelalá). It has been established that Gutiérrez always uses “Juanita’s” phone to make his telephone calls. “Juanita” also takes telephone calls for Gutiérrez’s (Gutierrezs).
