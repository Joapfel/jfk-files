OCC: Marvin Francis:lq. UN: WH/1/3E04. EXT: 6924. Date: 11 July 1968.

To Mexico City.

Should not advance LIRING/4 any funds unless LIRING/4 able securely explain their source.

End of message.

Index: SERRANO.

WH comments:

LIRING/4 approached by PCM member Arturo SERRANO who claimed he has contact with Guatemalan exile who could arrange bribe through relatives in Guatemala to assist LIRING/3 who imprisoned that country.

Guatemala City requested more information prior to commenting on feasibility of bribe offer by SERRANO.
