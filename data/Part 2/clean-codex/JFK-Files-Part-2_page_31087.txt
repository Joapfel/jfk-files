08 November 1976. Cable 943114.

To: Priority Santrajo. Info: San Jose, Paracas.

Morales was source of numerous Secret Service reports during Kissinger’s visit to Caracas in February 1976 concerning a reported attempt by Bozek to kill Kissinger. One Secret Service report indicated Morales had provided the Caracas legal attaché with reliable information in the past.
