Classified message.

Director. Rio de Janeiro. C/SAS 3. DDP, VR.

Secreto 181909Z.

DIR JOO WAVE CITE RIOD 2944.

BRYANT TYPIC YOBITE AMSTRUT.

1. AMSTRUT visited 13 December by Nario (Mario) Rogero Mestrinho de Mello, official in Brazilian Embassy Havana, now on leave (RID). (0)

(Nestrinho) reported as follows:

A. Brazilian [end of war] Pinto intoxicated not frequently and for extended periods of time.

B. Wide rumor assassination attempt [on] Fidel Castro after his appearance on 6 December resulted [in] killing [of the man] next to him. Castro uninjured. Would-be killer still at large.

C. Recent Brazilian foreign travel restrictions resulting [in] difficulties [for] Brazil commies desiring [to] visit Cuba. No details given.

D. Wide rumor Cuban Jose “Pope” Glanizakmet [met] Lee Oswald in Mexico before Kennedy assassination. (Also received LL-20.)

E. (Nestrinho) returning [to] Cuba late January 1964. Agreed [to] take personal items requested by A-1’s friends.

2. (Nestrinho) also delivered to A-1 [a] letter from Mariaanna Arguelles, who wrote:

A. Conde Eligero Alemana, formed cult of top in Havana, was not yet returned from Algiers with group [of] Cubans [who] will be there.

19 April 1964
