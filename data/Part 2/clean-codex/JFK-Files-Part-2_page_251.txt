PRIORITY DIR CITE MEXI 6043. LICOOKY. REF DTR 74752.

Miss Jone (Joan) COBS returning from New York to fight libel suit concerning ARVIVA stock, 26 January 1966, R. 42076.

1. Subject returned 22 January by plane to New York to consult lawyers Brown (PROWN), Cross and Hamilton regarding a 600,000-dollar libel suit being brought against her by Lyle Stuart, publisher of English version of book, for her charges that he altered original text. Visit revolves around lawyers’ desire to obtain release of her Senate testimony of March 30, 1962, for use in defense. She is unwilling to release because of delicacy of some revelations about LINLUCK, AREVALO and others. Stuart’s lawyers are Scheiman (SCHEIMAN), Albert and MacLean. Feels confident that through personal visit to lawyer she can prevent case coming to trial.

2. She also representing self in commercial arbitration case 8 February against Stuart for his failure to pay full royalties due her on English edition book.

3. Easby had long session 28 January attempting to persuade her.
