Memorandum

To: T. Howarth, Budget Officer  
From: G. Robert Blakey, Chief Counsel and Director  
Date: August 25, 1977  
Re: Mr. Jan Schlichtmann

This is to inform you that Mr. Jan Schlichtmann has accepted the position of Law Assistant/Legal Unit with the Committee staff. Mr. Schlichtmann's effective starting date will be August 25, 1977, and starting salary will be $19,500.

Your cooperation in familiarizing him with the operation of the staff will be greatly appreciated.
