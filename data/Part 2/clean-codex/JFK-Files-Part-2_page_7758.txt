Via teletype, 123800, April 11, 1968. Enciphered.

To Legat, Santo Domingo. From Director, FBI.

Assassination of Martin Luther King, Jr. Retrials numbers two and four dated April eight last. Pertinent data in retels was incorporated into LMH and furnished to the White House, Attorney General, and interested government agencies by letter April ten instant.

In view of nature and notoriety of allegations made by Hernan Henriquez Lora concerning this matter, Legat must endeavor to verify or disprove such allegations.

Photograph of one Hernan Henriquez Lora, also known as Hernan Henriquez Urena Lora, born October twenty-four, fifteen, at San Diego, Cuba, or Santiago de Cuba, Cuba, being forwarded separately.

REC-A 44-38261-498.

Bureau files reveal above individual is Dominican citizen and his parents were Max and Guarina Henriquez.
