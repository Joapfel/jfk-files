Samuel Adason Jaffe

Attitude of Vyacheslav Kiselov, aka “Slava” (Vyacheslav Aleksandrovich Kuskov).

Jaffe said he and “Slava” discussed the way the Soviet Union was changing, and “Slava” admitted changes were occurring and that “this was bad.” Jaffe tried to convince “Slava” that many of these changes were desirable; however, “Kiselov couldn’t see this at all (saying), ‘We have to bring it about through Marxism-Leninism.’ I disagreed. Even China will come around. Even you (Russia) are coming around. He (‘Slava’) refused to accept this.”
