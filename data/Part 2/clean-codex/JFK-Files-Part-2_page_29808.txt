Continuing Investigations Reports.

Carrafa, Eugene Michael.

Subject/Case Reference: Carrafa, Eugene Michael.

08-09-76: Interview with Michael J. Rinaldi of Rinaldi-Hines Funeral Home regarding the services held for Janice Mae Piccerelli, W/F, 38 years. Mr. Rinaldi stated that two of the visitors were “storybook” O/C types, W/M, 40s, dark suits, sunglasses, and hats. Certificate of Cremation No. 10679 reveals that the subject was cremated on July 23, 1976, and her date of death was given as July 21, 1976, the date found. She was cremated at the Oakridge Abbey, Hillside, Illinois; the funeral directors were Mrazek & Russ, Chicago, Illinois.

Mr. Rinaldi stated that no death certificate accompanied the ashes, and he found it unusual that the subject would be cremated without the certificate. Mr. Rinaldi further stated that Piccerelli’s brother, Conrad Joe Bliffin, paid $864.00 cash for the service, and no one has bothered to apply for the $250.00 Social Security death benefit.
