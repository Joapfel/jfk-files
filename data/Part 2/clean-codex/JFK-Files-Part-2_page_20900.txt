Committee to Defend the Right to Live, International Women’s Day March, Seattle, Washington, March 8, 1971. Is miscellaneous, VIDEM. OO: Seattle.

On instant date, a source who has furnished reliable information in the past reported two unidentified individuals went to a meeting of the Freedom Socialist Party, a schismatic group of the Socialist Workers Party which has been designated by the Attorney General of the U.S. pursuant to Executive Order 10450, held in Seattle during the evening of March 4 last, and distributed leaflets advertising an International Women’s Day March beginning 4:30 PM at the Women’s Jail, Fourth and Cherry, Seattle. The affair is under the sponsorship of a new group, the Committee to Defend the Right to Live.
