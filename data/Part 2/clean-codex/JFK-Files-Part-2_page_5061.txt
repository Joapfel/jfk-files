Nonetheless, as SLANK/1 provided information which indicated that the government of Cuba played no role in the Kennedy assassination, we need to tie up all loose ends as we approach the thirty-year declassification time limit on files covering that era. We would appreciate, therefore, having you ask VZTOUCHDOWN if he recognizes the name and photo and what he knows about SLANK/1.
