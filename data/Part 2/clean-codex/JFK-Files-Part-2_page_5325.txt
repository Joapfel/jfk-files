Other issues discussed

Crypts other than LIs and AMs—there was a discussion about deleting the digraph and letting the rest of the crypt remain; everyone seemed receptive, but no decision.

Bill mentioned the Iranian takeover as a good example of the damage caused by release of documents with crypts, file numbers, etc. Gunn asked if there was an internal “damage” study prepared. Bill said he would check.

Marwell rejected the approach of the Agency having the opportunity to discuss Board actions before official notification. If we have evidence, we need to present it upfront, not come back with additional evidence after the decision.

The balancing of public interest versus Agency needs came up several times; Marwell commented that even if a document or item is not important to the Oswald story, the Agency must still defend it.

Bill stated several times during the meeting, “If important to the story, we can live with release.”

Barry  
12 October 1995
