H. Arrests

Detective Lieutenant William McCoy, Detroit Police Department (PD), advised on February 2, 1972, that Lonnie Darden and Gerald Crawford were stopped by officers of the Fifth Precinct. Darden was released and Crawford was arrested and charged with using and displaying the license of another. Crawford appeared in traffic court on February 2, 1972, was found guilty, sentenced to five days in the Detroit House of Corrections and a $50.00 fine.

On February 7, 1972, Detective Lieutenant McCoy, supra, advised that Willit Charles Hall, a former BPP member, was arrested February 7, 1972, for being Absent Without Leave and was returned to military control.

Leonard Mongo and Walter I. Rozier were arrested on February 18, 1972, by the Highland Park, Michigan, PD, for disorderly person and loitering in connection with their soliciting money and interfering with customers at a store in connection with the BPP.
