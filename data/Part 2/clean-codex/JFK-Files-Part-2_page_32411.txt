Electronic Communications.

There are numerous reports of a highly classified nature concerning electronic communications between Cuba and the bloc, within Cuba, and between Cuba and Latin America. Some reports infer that Prensa Latina (Pressa Latina) facilities may be used for other than news purposes, and there are frequent reports of clandestine (ciuadecatine) radio instructions to Latin America from Cuba and between clandestine (ciaandnetine) stations in Latin America.
