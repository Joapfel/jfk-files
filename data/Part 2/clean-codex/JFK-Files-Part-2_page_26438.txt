A coup with the support of military elements. However, these groups were too divided to be successful. Ly believed the only successful coup would be one mounted by the Army itself. U.S. Speaking in general terms, Ly said that in setting up such a coup two things were important: to avoid bloodshed, and to organize as small a force as possible in order not to allow the plan of action to be exploited by the VC. Ly’s solution would be to have the general officers declare the whole of South Vietnam before it was to be in a state of siege.

H. 10/3/20.

The troops would then be forbidden to advance until 1500 hours A.E. or to move without an express order from the Revolutionary Committee (Revol.) to any other place where all roads leading to Saigon would be charged.

In case the coup were successfully brought about, three general officers would direct everyday government, business, and media, with the help of technical advisors and all the secretaries of state, who would have remained at their posts. During this period, the people would be able to choose the head of state.
