Persons/entities: Murphy; Kilpatrick; Lyman; Castro; Castro's sister; Fitzgerald. Date: 4/23/63. Categories: Cuba; Special Group.

Occurrence: Murphy asked Fitzgerald whether Castro's immediate entourage had been penetrated. Fitzgerald replied that Castro's sister worked for us, but that she didn't always have inside information.

Source: Memorandum, dated 4/23/63, prepared by Lyman and Kilpatrick. Date: Wallach 7/2/75.
