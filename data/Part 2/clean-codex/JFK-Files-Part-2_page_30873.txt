Dobrinin Materials. Translations of material rejo from Soviet Embassy, Washington, D.C., relative to Embassy file on LHO and Marina.

Marina's application onto register residence permit: last place of registration in USSR (USAR): city of Minsk (MIINSK), Ul. Kommunisticheskaya, house, apt. 25.

LHO letter to Russian Embassy, Washington, D.C., asks for information on how to subscribe to “Pravda” (“PROVA”) or “Izvestia” (“IAVESTIA”) Russian-language newspapers, or some other Russian-language magazines such as “Ogonyok” (“IGONYOK”). “I would like for the Embassy to send us any periodicals or bulletins which may put out for the benefit of your citizens living, for a time, in the USA.”
