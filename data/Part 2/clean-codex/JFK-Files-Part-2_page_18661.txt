To: Intelligence Community Staff, attention: Central Index. From: FBI.

Subject: Abstract of Information Provided to Select Committees.

How provided: Document. Date provided: 7/16/75.

To whom provided: SSC.

Identification: Memorandum.

In response to: SSC letter 5/14/75, Appendix A, Item 5J.

Classification of information: U.

Key words: COINTELPRO.

Summary: SSC requested all memoranda of FBI and Justice Department discussing the legal authority of the FBI to use so-called “COINTELPRO” techniques and methods, including all forms of organizational “Disruption.” A review of all logical files of the FBI and a canvass of appropriate personnel knowledgeable of the counterintelligence programs has failed to locate any documents discussing the legal authority of the FBI to use COINTELPRO techniques and methods.

62-116395. FMK: fmk. (4).

Original via liaison to the Central Community Index in connection with MENSTUDY 75.
