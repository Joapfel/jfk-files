Political, in the hands of the party, we have allowed various elements of the non-partisan Black and radical white communities to conduct aspects of our defense on the theory that involvement on such a basis would promote wider mobilization into the movement in general from our substructural level; that is, exposure in this particular area of anti-establishment would serve to move them into actions later on at higher levels. Insoluble contradictions have resulted...

We condemn no one. We thank all those sweet, earnest people who tried to act in our behalf. But, we do now recognize that all aspects of the movement through the two stages, revolution and socialization, must proceed under the purposeful guidance of the People's Vanguard Party.

George Jackson  
San Quentin Prison

June 21, 1971
