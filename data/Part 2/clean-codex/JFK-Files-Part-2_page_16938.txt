United States Government Memorandum

To: SAC, Chicago

From: SA Roy Martin Mitchell

Subject: Black Panther Party (BPP)

Date: October 8, 1969

Who has furnished reliable information in the past, will not testify but attests to the accuracy of the following information, advised on the dates indicated:

October 8, 1969

Lynn French, Manuel Dozier.

These persons will more or less represent the BPP at an alleged rally at the International Harvester Plant, 2600 South California, October 9, 1969.
