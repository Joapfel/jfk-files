File: Nosenko Interrogation Transcript

Reel #1, 26 July 1965 (Delyabin). Complete translation of verbatim transcript.

Q: Whence and how did you show up in city of Moscow?

A: At end of 1952 arrived in Moscow from city of Sovetsk, Primorskayi Key (10–15 kilo. from city of Baltimsk, formerly Pilau—base of Fourth Naval Fleet). Baltic Navy intelligence point is in Sovetsk. Worked there for several months in 1952, went to Moscow to work in Navy intelligence and then something else. Was on one-month leave when he went to Moscow, did not have to report to headquarters. (D. disputes that it would be possible to go on leave and relocate without reporting.)

While on leave in Moscow, “the business arose that I would be taken to work in the MGB” (Ministry of State Security). Question of being transferred to MGB came up at Kobalov’s dacha at about 11/1/53, parents present. Later contacted by Ministry of State Security and told to report.
