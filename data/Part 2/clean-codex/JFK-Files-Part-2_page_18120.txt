United States Government

Memorandum

To: Mr. J. B. Adams  
From: Legal Counsel  
Subject: SENSTUDY 75  
Date: 9/9/75

Mr. Mintz; Mr. Wannall; Mr. Cregar; Mr. Hotis; Mr. Daly.

On 9/9/75, John Elliff, Domestic Task Force Head of the Senate Select Committee, telephonically contacted SA Paul V. Daly and requested that SA Douglas MacDougall be released for purposes of submitting to a Staff interview concerning mail openings.

Elliff stated that he was currently being interviewed by Senate Select Committee Staff Members on mail covers; however, he indicated there is an apparent misunderstanding as the Senate Select Committee also requested that he be made available for Staff interview on mail openings at the same time. Elliff was advised that this was not true and that James Dick of the Senate Select Committee had requested only a release insofar as mail covers was concerned for purposes of an interview with MacDougall and that this particular topic had been confirmed with Dick on a separate occasion to insure that their intent insofar as MacDougall was concerned was to interview him on mail covers. Elliff then requested that MacDougall be released for mail openings and inquired as to how long this particular procedure might take. He was informed that prior to MacDougall's being made available for such an interview the Bureau would brief MacDougall concerning his Fifth Amendment rights and would want MacDougall to have sufficient time to consider whether he might want to retain private counsel or whatever other steps he feels necessary to protect his rights.
