SAC, Chicago

Date: February 25, 1968

From: SA James W. Gerblick

Subject: Racial Tensions, Maywood, Illinois, 1968–1969

Where appropriate, the information has been disseminated to local authorities.

One Leroy Jones was with Fred Hampton and Paul Wade at the college campus over the past weekend and helped them to start trouble. Informant learned this from Paul Wade, who also advised they (Wade and Hampton) went to the college after Wade had received a telephone call from a girl, not identified, who asked Hampton to come and speak at the campus.

JWG
