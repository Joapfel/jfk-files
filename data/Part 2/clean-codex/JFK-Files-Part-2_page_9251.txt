DE T-3  
May 1, 1973

VI. Connections with Other Black Extremist Groups

BPP members are not allowed to hold membership in any other group or organization. The BPP operates independently and has no affiliation with other extremist groups. BPP members do not officially come into contact or associate with members of other militant organizations.

DE T-1  
February 14, 1973

DE T-1, DE T-2, DE T-9, and DE T-10 all advised in January 1973 that the BPP faction headed by Huey P. Newton remains diametrically opposed to the BPP faction headed by Eldridge Cleaver.

Huey P. Newton is the leader of the BPP-Newton Faction (NF), headquartered in Oakland, California.

Eldridge Cleaver is a leader in the BPP and early in 1971 was expelled from that organization. Those members of the BPP who continue to support him and advocate the use of guns and guerrilla tactics became known as the BPP-Cleaver Faction (CF).
