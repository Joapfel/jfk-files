The Hearst case. Montgomery told Lewis he had nothing in this regard he could testify to, that the FBI was doing a fine job, that he, Montgomery, did not approve of the way the Church Committee had handled itself in the past and that they should get off the backs of the CIA and the FBI and let them get on with the job and then hung up.

Montgomery made the comment that he thought this was a terrible way to attempt to get facts. SAC Bates suggested to him that if he felt so strongly about it, he should consider writing an article for the Examiner as to the approach made to him by the Committee. Montgomery retired several months ago, but is still close to the Examiner.
