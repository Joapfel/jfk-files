Plans, Programs, and Projects

HR 230-8d(3)

(3) Periodic Review and Reaffirmation. The Operating Official responsible for the project will review each approved administrative plan or fiscal annex at least once each year, coincident with consideration of renewal of the project and will either:

(a) affirm in writing to the responsible Deputy Director that the previously approved provisions remain adequate and valid; or

(b) initiate appropriate revision when changing circumstances dictate the need.
