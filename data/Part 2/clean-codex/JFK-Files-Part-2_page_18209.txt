Memorandum for the Attorney General

Re: Martin Luther King, Jr.

This Bureau's investigation of the communist influence in racial matters has developed considerable information indicating the influence upon Martin Luther King, Jr., head of the Southern Christian Leadership Conference, by individuals with subversive backgrounds such as Stanley David Levison, Clarence Jones, Bayard Rustin and others.
