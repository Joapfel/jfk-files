Memorandum

To: Director, FBI (105-28333)

From: Legat, Hong Kong (105-6699)

Subject: Sam Jaffe

Date: 10/25/72

Reference: NYlet & LHM 8/7/72.

Further information relating to the subject is set forth below.

Office of origin should review indices to determine whether further investigation is warranted under guidelines set forth in Section 105-G, Manual of Instructions.

Remarks: Subject born 6/19/29. Listed as traveling on U.S. passport Z752999, issued 4/8/68, Hong Kong.

For the information of the Bureau and recipient offices, the above-captioned subject contacted Legat telephonically to enlist his assistance, as he had been prevented from departing Hong Kong with his family on 10/22/72 by the Hong Kong authorities for non-payment of resident taxes. His U.S. passport was in fact picked up by the Hong Kong authorities. Subject was advised that this was apparently a matter between himself and the Hong Kong Government and that there was absolutely nothing Legat could do to assist.

Bureau. Foreign Liaison Desk. New York. WFO.

17 October 1972.

S.W. Ng/H — Hong Kong.
