To London. Info Mexico City, JMWAVE.

Typio Liring.

Ref: Mexico City 8487 (IN 91995), not sent London.

1. Mexico City source reported information on Cuban need for 24 ships of approximately 10,000 tons. Ships possibly to be used to transport 1967 sugar crop, which reportedly to reach large proportions. Foregoing may indicate Russian shipping aid to Cuba being cut.

2. Please query Stanley A. Cooke (P) to see whether he can discreetly confirm Cuban need for ships.

End of message.
