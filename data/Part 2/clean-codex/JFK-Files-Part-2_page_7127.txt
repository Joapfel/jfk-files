RYBAT PLMHCONGA. Congressman Milford.

Ref: Wellington 15837 (IN 787572).

1. Congressman Milford desires referenced meeting at 0800 and will expect contact for details upon his arrival.

2. He is very easy to talk to and am sure you will enjoy the meeting. He met here with heads of liaison services, about 45 minutes with each. His principal questions are what they think of the current investigations, what impact have they had on foreign intelligence relations, etc.

3. He has a minor hearing problem, so should arrange to have him seated as close as possible to Brigadier Gilbert.

4. Best regards. E2, IMPDET.
