Abortion Project Coalition (APC)

This is a self-described group of women in Cleveland, Ohio, dedicated to repeal of all abortion laws so that women have the right to choose whether or not to bear children. It is also actively working for no forced sterilization and no restrictive contraceptives law.

Socialist Workers Party (SWP), Cleveland, Ohio Branch

The Cleveland branch of the SWP is affiliated with the national SWP and follows its directives.

The SWP has been designated pursuant to Executive Order 10450.
