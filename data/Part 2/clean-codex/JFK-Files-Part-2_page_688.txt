Dispatch. To: Chief, MID. For release immediately. To: Chief of Station, JIJAVE; Chief of Station, Mexico City. Processing action: only available IFN; can be reevaluated. From: Chief of Station, San Salvador.

Subject: JIJERAS. Identification of Jorge ARTIAS Genus as Salvadoran delinquent to Tri-Cultural Conference.

Action required—reference:

1. CICID forwarded to FEDS a copy of the 15 February 1966 edition of Politics, a Mexican publication that contained photos of and speeches delivered by the chiefs of the various delegations to the Tri-Cultural Conference held in El Salvador in January.

2. “Jorge ‘Fiz’” (“Jorge ‘Fيز’”), described himself as the chief of the Salvadoran delegation, and was identified from his photo by JIJAVE and JIJAVE-2 as Jorge ARTIAS Genus, a member of the Central Committee of the FED. According to JIJAVE-2, ARTIAS is probably the number one on the FED Monarchy, based on CIVILIZACION Figuren, Jorge Shafieh EHTZI, and Salvador Cyotetano CUIPO. It is rather strange that the FED said “Jij” on this list, since he was JIJAVE-2 and JIJAVE-2 reported that, while in Cuba in 1951–52, ARTIAS had an affair with a girl that resulted in her pregnancy, and the Cubans sent a strong complaint to the FED concerning his conduct.

3. A LIVACATE main intercept (Mell-81291) indicated that ARTIAS was expected in Mexico in January. It apparently has not yet returned to El Salvador, and thus he may also be representing the FED at the 23rd CEESDR Congress. Any available photos of Salvadorans at that affair would be appreciated.

4. R.I. please index. Jorge ARTIAS Genus (as single P.M.). CIT: El Salvador. In photo of chiefs of various delegations to Tri-Continental Conference in El Salvador in January 1966 (Politics, 15 February 1966), “Jorge Fiz,” head of Salvadoran delegation, was identified as Jorge ARTIAS Genus.

Distribution: 3—COS; 1—COS, JIJAVE; 1—COS, Mexico City.

Mark R. Cumby. Dispatch symbol and number: HSG.-5557. Date: 1 April 1966. MCIS file number: 201-111045; FHL 63-1-117.9.
