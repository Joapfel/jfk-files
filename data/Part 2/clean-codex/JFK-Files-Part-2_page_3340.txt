Thank you again for sending me a copy of your memorandum. As you know, the EEC is keeping the Cuba problem under constant scrutiny, having discussed it in some detail at its January 14 meeting and again this morning. At the first of the meetings, the Secretary of State informed the policy toward Cuba which had been approved by the President, and the conclusion of this morning's meeting was that this policy is essentially correct. We anticipate that, at future meetings of the EEC, there will be further discussions concerning Cuba to determine whether there should be a modification of policy toward that country. In view of the fact that today's EEC meeting indicated continued agreement on the validity of the policy discussed in January, it would be very remiss to develop new policy documentation at this time, especially since the proposals in your earlier memorandum closely parallel policies which are already underway. Should you, or your colleagues on the Joint Chiefs, desire a further briefing on our actions and operational plans to carry out the policy, I should be more than glad to arrange it.

Livingston F. Preclearant  
Under Secretary for Political Affairs
