12855Z March 68, Mexico City 479D. Guatemala City, info Director.

1. On 5 March 68, LIRING-3 reported he had dinner with Carlos Persaval Marque, name of Marcela Marque Pardoes, director of the now-defunct magazine “Politica,” corruptist bimonthly publication. Persaval asked LIRING-3 if he could obtain a false passport for wife of important Guatemalan guerrilla (Gunnilla) leader in Mexico who must travel Guatemala. LIRING-3 said he would do what he could to obtain documents.

2. On 6 March, Persaval said two important Guatemalan revolutionaries had to travel to Guatemala from Mexico by mid-March and asked if LIRING-3 was still willing to help. LIRING-3 answered in the affirmative but said there was limited chance of obtaining false documentation. He could, however, brief these revolutionaries on the Guat/Mexi border in order to make illegal crossing easier. If they desired, he could personally guide them across border.

3. On 9 March, Persaval continued discussions with LIRING-3 on Guatemalan revolutionaries who had to travel to Guatemala urgently. He said one of them had legal documentation but the other did not.
