Reference: Buenos Aires 34511 (IN 69826S).

1. Visitors met with Ambassador Hill for one hour and a half the evening of 27 September at his residence and then went directly to the airport. After introducing the visitors to the ambassador, COS withdrew and waited in another room. Upon departing, COS heard the ambassador ask the visitors for their credentials. The ambassador later told COS he suggested to the visitors that COS be present during the discussion, but they preferred to talk with him privately. COS was called in, however, to participate in the last few minutes of the discussion. In COS's presence, Mr. Aaron asked the ambassador whether he thought he should have the right to see all information produced within his mission, to which the ambassador replied affirmatively. Aaron then asked the ambassador whether he in fact saw all information. The ambassador replied he did not know, but had confidence in COS in this regard. Aaron then asked COS whether he was authorized to show the ambassador all station reporting. Answer: Operational reporting identifying agents or clandestine methods of operation could not be shown to the ambassador without Secret Staff.
