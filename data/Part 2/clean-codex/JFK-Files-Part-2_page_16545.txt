Date: June 5, 1969.

Andrea Leverly Graves was interviewed at the Federal Bureau of Investigation Office, 219 South Dearborn Street, Chicago, Illinois, on June 4, 1969, by Special Agents Richard A. Carr and John J. Loughiney, Jr. Prior to being interviewed, Graves was advised of the identity of the interviewing agents and was furnished with a form entitled “Interrogation. Advice of Rights,” which she read. After reading this form, Graves stated she understood her rights and declined to sign the form.

Graves admitted she is a member of the Black Panther Party; however, she declined to state how long she had been a member.

Graves was shown photographs of George Sams, Jr., and Kory L. Hill and stated she did not know either of them and had never seen them.

Graves refused to answer any further questions, with the exception of some questions regarding her background and description.

An examination of Graves’ personal effects disclosed the following items:

One aerosol spray can of “Protect-J” defensive spray, which was retained.

A piece of paper bearing telephone number 875-1423.

Card listing person to notify in case of emergency as Grandmother Burton, 8440 Vernon, telephone number 7AC-4223, and ST3-9523.

Card indicating Graves had completed a course in Red Cross Home Nursing at Peace Memorial Hospital, June 26, 1968.

Card indicating Graves is a registered practical nurse.

On June 4, 1969, at Chicago, Illinois.

SAs John J. Loughiney, Jr. and Richard A. Carr.

The document contains neither the name nor the telephone number of the FBI agent in charge. The reference to a “Practical Nurse” is unclear.
