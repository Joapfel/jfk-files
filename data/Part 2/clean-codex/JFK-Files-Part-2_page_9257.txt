Title: Black Panther Party

Office: Detroit, Michigan

Bureau File #: 105-165706 Sub 15

Character: Extremist Matters

Synopsis:

Detroit BPP headquarters located at 157 Collingwood, with an office at 2228 Bewick and a pad at 4200 Buena Vista, all Detroit, Mich. They continue to operate the free breakfast program for school children and sell “The Black Panther” newspaper. Additional programs and activity set forth. The Detroit BPP operates under the leadership of Lonnie Darden, Minister of Information.
