Mr. Schwarz. Just one detail that Mr. Smothers suggested to me. Is NSAM 100 what the White House showed you, or is it another document?

Mr. Bundy. No, 181. But let me be precise. I asked to see the documents relating to—from the National Security files relating to August 1962. And they gave me the NSAM file identified as the significant memorandum, NSAM 181.

Mr. Schwarz. Now, the contingency to be examined, therefore, was what would happen—would it help the U.S., that is the question, if Castro were to disappear?

Mr. Bundy. What would it be like if Castro were to disappear? The question is open ended.

Mr. Schwarz. What was the conclusion?

Mr. Bundy. I don't remember. My unaided sense of the matter is that intelligent political analysis would have suggested in 1961 or at any time later that the removal of a single individual in a revolution complex and general as this would have quite unpredictable and not necessarily helpful effects.
