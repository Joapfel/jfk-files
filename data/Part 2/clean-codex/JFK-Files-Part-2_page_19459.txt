Women to take first step to end the war

Are you tired of paying all your taxes for bullets and bombs? Fed up with fighting senseless wars? Shocked at the burning of innocent women and children?

Women are emotional—yes, because we're still human. We care about our loved ones and are outraged at the killing!

Practical—yes, because we're used to making ends meet, putting our shoulders to the wheel and getting things done!

Powerful—yes, because we have 51% of the vote. We control the “purse strings”; why not the “political strings”?

Join us to start the Peace Wheel rolling, January 15 at noon on the Polk Street steps of City Hall, in support of the Jeannette Rankin Brigade going to Washington to petition Congress to (1) end the war, (2) heal the sick society at home and (3) repair the damages done in Vietnam. We will give a copy of the petition to Mayor Alioto. We will telephone our Congressmen from the Federal Building to let them know women will be calling on them.
