Free Busing to Prison Program

One of the purposes of the National Committee's to Combat Fascism is to implement programs and services that are free and beneficial to the Black community. At the present time we are implementing a free busing to prisons program for people who have friends, sons, and relatives at Jackson State Penitentiary. The inmates there are just allowed three visits a month and are subjected to dehumanizing living conditions.

Number eight of our ten-point party platform states: We want freedom for all Black men held in federal, state, county, city, prisons and jails. We know that they haven't received a fair trial in racist Babyland. Black people are being judged by a jury of racist whites and Blacks of a different peer group that has no understanding of the average man from the Black community. We feel that Black people should be retrained in the Black community by people from the same social and economic background.

The free prison busing program will provide a means of transportation for the relatives and a boost for the inmates' morale. We're asking all concerned organizations, groups, social clubs, churches, and individuals to contribute to this program.

We need: buses or vans; drivers; money.

The free prison busing program will start Saturday, October 3, 1970. Visiting days are Wednesdays and Saturdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
