5. L-3 gave Rowton copy letter he sent Arnold Rodriguez Camps after he returned from last frustrating trip Cuba. Also told Arnold he planning trip Central America soon to contact and assess business contacts. Also relayed what Rowton learned re prices of weed killer Mexi and asked for specifications and quantities desired. Also stated was somewhat disturbed and resentful at way Reynaldo Cepeca and DSE treated him.
