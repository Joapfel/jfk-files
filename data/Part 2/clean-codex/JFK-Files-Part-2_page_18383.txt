Dear Mike:

As Mark and you discussed today on the phone, it was the Committee's intent in Fritz Schwarz' letter to you of September 4, 1975, that the FBI, as well as the Internal Security Division, provide information pertaining to the individuals and organizations listed in that letter. Of course, this letter and the August 29 letter are modified by my letter of September 10. Furthermore, this request is of the highest priority, and therefore we want to be kept informed of its progress on a daily basis. Perhaps the best approach would be to have Bureau representatives contact either Mark Gitenstein or Art Harrigan immediately so that we might prioritize the names listed in the September 4 letter.
