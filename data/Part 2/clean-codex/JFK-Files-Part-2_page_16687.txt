Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 2/17/70

Registered pharmacist, Walgreens Drug Store, 2300 West Madison, advised that their prescriptions are filed in number order only and no records are kept concerning prescriptions in name only.

He advised that he has seen Fred A. Hampton (FRED A. HAMPTON), deceased, and another member of the Black Panther Party in Walgreens purchasing cigarettes and so forth, but does not recall the following people ever obtaining prescriptions from Walgreens:

Fred A. Hampton; Mark Clark; Brenda Harris; Verlina A. Brewer; Blair J. Anderson; Ronald Satchiel; Devorah Johnson; Louis Truluck; Harold Bell.

Date: 2/10/70. Chicago, Illinois. On SA.

Date dictated: 2/13/70.
