Re: New York letter dated 9/22/58.

On 10/2/58, the Clerk, Greater Kansas City Credit Bureau, Kansas City, Missouri, and Mrs. Clerk, Sheriff’s Office, Olathe, Kansas, whose office covers Mission, Kansas, advised they have no record of Jaffe’s wife’s mother, Josephine A. Cave.

On 10/2/58, the Clerk, Greater Kansas City Credit Bureau, advised that the records of this Bureau have on file a report dated 11/10/52 concerning L. Clark Winter, 7108 Mission Road, Mission, Kansas. His wife is listed as Lois Jameson Winter, and two children, not otherwise identified, were reported living at the home. His prior residences were listed as Hoosier Court, Bloomington, Indiana, and 316 Frontenac Place, Worthington, Ohio. At the time of this report, Winter was employed at the Kansas City Art Institute as a sculptural instructor and had reportedly been there since September 1949. He was previously reported as employed by Ohio State University as a professor of sculpture. According to his credit record, Winter’s credit was good.

Mrs. Clerk, Sheriff’s Office, Olathe, whose office covers Mission, Kansas, advised on 10/2/58 that the records of that department reflect that Lester Clark Winter was fined $2.00 and costs on 10/3/50 for running a stop sign. No further information was noted.

The 1953 Northeast Johnson County Directory lists Lester Clark Winter and Lois J. as residents at 7108 Mission Road, Mission, Kansas; his occupation is listed as instructor, Kansas City Art Institute. Succeeding directories do not list him.
