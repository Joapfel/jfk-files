Federal Bureau of Investigation

Date: 2/26/70

At 6:00 PM, an autopsy was performed on the body of Frederick Allen Hampton by Dr. Charles Petty, pathologist from Dallas, Texas. The autopsy was performed at the Veterans Administration Hospital, Shreveport, Louisiana. Those in attendance, according to a list provided by Joseph Keane, U. S. Marshal, Shreveport, Louisiana, were as follows:

For the Hampton family:

Francis E. Andrich, Attorney  
Dr. David Spear, Pathologist  
Dr. Earl Caldhew, Pathologist

For the State of Illinois:

Thomas Bett, Assistant State Attorney  
Nick Motherman, Assistant State Attorney  
Dr. Jerry Kearris, Cook County Coroner's Office  
Dr. William Navrella, Pathologist

For the U. S. Government:

Dr. Charles Petty  
Jerris Leonard, Assistant Attorney General, Civil Rights Division, U. S. Department of Justice  
James Turner, Deputy Assistant Attorney General, U. S. Department of Justice  
Donald E. Walter, U. S. Attorney, Shreveport, Louisiana  
James Gardner, Deputy U. S. Marshal

Shreveport, Louisiana. 2/16/70.
