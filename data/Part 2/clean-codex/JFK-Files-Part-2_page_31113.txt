With their leader abandoned and in jeopardy, Salvat wrote to friends in the U.S. that he would kill P/14 personnel involved if anything happened to Miller.

3 February 1962 dispatch UFGW-796. Operational approval granted Salvat effective 23 January 1962.

8 March 1962 Station Asset Report. Salvat is action type, considered one of the bulwarks of the free Cuban student world. Has infiltrated Cuba and worked underground, undergone arrest and interrogation, been freed and returned to Miami, and infiltrated again on three attempts that failed in the latter part of 1961. He is almost legendary among Cuban high school and university youth.

13 March 1968 — Request for cancellation. Salvat no longer of operational interest and c14.
