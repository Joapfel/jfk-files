1/2/70

Teletype

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Chicago

Black Panther Party (LPP), RU-BPP. CO: San Francisco. Reliable in past.

Confidential source advised an instant rally scheduled for three p.m., January four, next, at New Afro-Arts Theater, 3937 South Drexel Boulevard, Chicago.

Rally in memorial to Fred Hampton (Dawton), deceased deputy chairman, Illinois Black Panther Party (LPP), killed December four, last, in raid conducted by Cook County State’s Attorney’s Police.

Source also advises LPP tentatively planning to announce opening of “People’s Medical Center,” the purpose of which will freely be to treat residents.

Source states Mr. Bob Rush, Department Minister of Defense, Illinois LPP; Bob Lucas, leader, Black Liberation Alliance, Chicago; and Alderman A. A. “Sammy” Rayner (Rawner) being advertised as speakers. Entertainment will also be provided according to source and contributions solicited. Money obtained.
