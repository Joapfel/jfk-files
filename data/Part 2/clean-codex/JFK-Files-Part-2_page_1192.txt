Training Evaluation. Countries Title, Operations Control Group 16.

Name: MART, Joseph B. Sex: Male. Date of course: 25 November–13 December 1957. Number enlisted: 15. Date of enlistment: 16 June 1921. End date: 17 August 1951. Closure on date: GS-13. Office: DDP PR. Resident assignment or employment status: Deputy Chief of PR/CS.

List Descriptions: To familiarize the student with the various types of U.S. operations and with methods and procedures peculiar to CE in the field, not at headquarters; to outline CE objectives; and to increase proficiency in the recognition and exploration of leads.

List Characteristics: The Conference Operations Course is of three weeks’ duration. The curriculum material is presented by means of lectures, Zooms, and directed reading. It is divided into sections as follows:

A. Introduction (definition of terms, the CI staff, operational security and risk assessment, sources of CI leads, CI reporting and records, documentation)

B. Interruption

C. Foreign Intelligence Services (with emphasis on currently hostile services)

D. Penetration and Sabotage

E. Double Agent Operations

During the course each student is required to present a written case and to submit a written variant of the same. Evaluations are based on problems, written exercises requiring analyses of actual case studies, the case analysis presented by the students, and an objective test.
