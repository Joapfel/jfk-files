Informant subsequently advised on 7/17/69:

Paul Wade and one Clayton Kendall (PB) from California are out looking for the policeman who broke in the door at Wesley Wade's house.

Paul Wade has a .38 caliber pistol and may use it if he becomes angry enough.

Paul Wade said that one Thomas O'Neal (PB) is a leader of the EPP in Washington Police Department and he is formerly from Maywood.

Michael Henderson has been released from jail and is back in Maywood.
