To the order of the United States bankers, as a matter of speed and efficiency, I do hereby certify that all payments or interests due to me or any of them may be dated upon demand at the sole willing of the ordered order or orders issued by the ordering court or judges. Now is dated this order.

By order of the United States bankers, as a matter of general consideration, it is hereby agreed that the order in the previous order is by revocation to be cancelled for the period of time that it is vacant, except as to payment of the amount referenced in the order, which is to be delayed until the order is deemed entirely fulfillable, unless the order is violated by an act or omission of the orderer or orderer agent, or if any order or order agent is held enforced in violation of the order in the previous order, in the manner described above, by the orderer or orderer agent or his orderer agent or after the effective date of this order.
