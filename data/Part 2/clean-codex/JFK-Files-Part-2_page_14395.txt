May 6, 1975.

Oral Information Provided to the Senate Select Committee (SSC) Investigating Intelligence Activities.

1. Subject: Martin Luther King, Jr.

2. Witness’ organization: Mr. Daniel J. Brennan, Jr., retired Special Agent, FBI.

3. Classification of information: Unclassified.

4. Summary of content:

Mr. Brennan was questioned concerning the former FBI investigation of Martin Luther King, Jr., specifically during 1964. However, he was unable to furnish any of the requested information concerning the King investigation as he could not recall any information of the nature desired.

5. Date of interview and before whom:

Interview conducted by Mr. Michael Epstein, Staff Member, SSC, 5/2/75.

6. Organization reference no.: FBI file 62-116395.

7. Relation to intelligence community problems: None.
