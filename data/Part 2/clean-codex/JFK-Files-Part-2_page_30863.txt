Shirakova, Rima. 201-803914.

Zmitryuk, married name. Husband Valeriy Zmitryuk, born 29 October 1937, engineering student.

Memo.

Department of State, Bureau of Intelligence and Research, to Deputy Director of Plans, CIA (date not legible). Attention: Mr. Calvin Tenney. Subject: Zmitrook (Zmitryuk), Rima—Lee Harvey Oswald’s Intourist guide in Moscow.

“Although complete files at our Office of Special Consular Services no longer are available on persons who might have sought financial assistance at Embassy Moscow between 1958 and 1963.”

Dispatch, 15 August 1966.

After birth of her daughter in October 1963, Shirakova left Intourist and was employed as a teacher of English in a Moscow teacher’s training college.

Born: 19 January 1939. Date of marriage: 1923.
