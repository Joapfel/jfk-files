191342 MAY 71 CITE

Headquarters CHAOS.

Reference: Headquarters.

1. In final coverage on 18 May of WPC, which ended 16 May, local press made the following additional observations regarding U.S. participants:

A. Sidney Peck (Sidney P. Eck), identified as “USA professor,” spoke at Congress on the Vietnam War.

B. WPC awarded the Joliot-Curie Gold Medal posthumously (EXHUMELY) to Reverend Martin Luther King. The medal was accepted by Reverend Ralph Alfred Mathy and Reverend W. Walker, “an American peace fighter.”

C. A photograph (pouched) of the presentation also pictures U.S. delegates William Douthard (Dourthard) and Mrs. Addie W. Wyatt.

D. U.S. delegates Ruth Gage-Colby and Sylvia Kusher (Kuscher) spoke at a meeting of Indochinese and American delegates.

E. Americans Herbert Aptheker (Apt Heker) and Carlton Goodlett were reelected to the presidential board.
