PD 100-10983

Enclosed for the Bureau are twelve copies, for San Francisco two copies, and WJO two copies of a Letterhead Memorandum (LMM) suitable for dissemination concerning a proposed anti-war demonstration on Monday, 1/15/68, in Portland, Oregon, in support of a demonstration protesting U.S. policy in Vietnam, Washington, D.C., 1/15/88. Sources utilized in this LMM, who have furnished reliable information in the past, are as follows:

First source: PD 395-S  
Second source: AT. 2882-S  
Third source: PD 303-S

The LHM is classified “confidential” because data reported from informants could reasonably result in identification of confidential informants of continuing value and compromise future effectiveness thereof.

Copies of the LHM disseminated to USA, Secret Service, MI, OSI, and NIC, all Portland.

The summary of information contained in the LHM was not submitted to the Purseau by teletype inasmuch as a lengthy AORW notice was received concerning the demonstration.

Portland will continue to follow this matter until Bureau instructions.
