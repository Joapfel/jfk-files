United States Government Memorandum.

To: Director, FBI (105-28333). Date: 8/14/69.

From: SAC, WFO (105-8906) (C).

Subject: IS-R.

Re WFOlet dated 6/17/69.

Since relet the subject has contacted WFO on only one occasion, 8/8/69. At that time he stated that there had been no follow-up by Boris Sedov and, in fact, he had not had any contact with Soviet officials except casual contact with Boris Strelnikov at the swimming pool at their place of residence. — C.

In view of the above, there appears to be no basis for keeping this case in a pending status, and it is therefore being closed subject to being reopened in the event of additional developments. — C.
