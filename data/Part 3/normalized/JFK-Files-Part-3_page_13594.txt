MR. DOING IT: By the provisions of SEC-403 which is the basic CIA statute. Those letters would go in, in the last year to Mr. McClellan and Mr. Mahon, and they would be responded to at the completion of the appropriations process by Mr. Mahon and Mr. McClellan, Senator McClellan, and Congressman Mahon to the Director of OMB identifying the amount they had appropriated.
