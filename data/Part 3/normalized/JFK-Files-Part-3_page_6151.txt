United States Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSC)

5) "Ramparts"

documents - "Ramparts" was the subject of Hunter Nail request number 105 (Exhibit E-1).

date - This request was also most likely made between January 16, 1969, and August 28, 1969.

basis - The first item in the Bureau file concerning "Ramparts" is dated October 22, 1964; however, no active investigation was authorized at that time. Investigation was authorized in May, 1967, based on a request received from the Department of Justice (Exhibits E-2 and 3). No request to include "Ramparts" on the Hunter Nail watch list was located; however, a document dated October 17, 1968, does indicate that "Ramparts" furnished a list of names, probably their mailing list, to the Soviets (Exhibit E-4).
