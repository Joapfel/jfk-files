DECEMBER 1, 1975

Mr. RANGEL introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Public Works and Transportation

A BILL

To change the name of the J. Edgar Hoover F.B.I. Building.

1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

2. That section 8 of the Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial Bi-centennial Civic Center Act (86 Stat. 1022) is amended

5. by striking out "J. Edgar Hoover F.B.I. Building" both

6. times it appears and inserting in lieu thereof "Federal

Bureau of Investigation Building".
