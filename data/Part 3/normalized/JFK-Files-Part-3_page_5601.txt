SHELTON stated that he was authorized by the KLAN to draw $15,000.00 per year salary but 1966 was the first year the KLAN was financially able to pay him the full $15,000.00. Prior to 1966, his salary was determined at the end of each year by aggregating his withdrawals from the KLAN, reduced by the amount of documentation for travel and other KLAN expenses he had turned in during the year.

SHELTON converted his carport into an office and moved the KLAN office from the Alston Building in Tuscaloosa into his space May 1, 1966. The KLAN pays SHELTON $35.00 per month rent and $15.00 per month on his utilities.

SHELTON maintains that four of the eight $500.00 checks he received from DIXIE ENGINEERING COMPANY bounced. The reason the four $500.00 items do not show on his bank statements as a debit to the account was the balances at the time the checks were returned were less than $500.00 and he picked up the checks with cash or with another check from DIXIE ENGINEERING.
