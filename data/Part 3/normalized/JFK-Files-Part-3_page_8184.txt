INFORMATIVE NOTE

Date 5/5/75

Attached teletype reports results of contacts by Newark Office with three former Bureau Agents to alert them they might be contacted by staff of Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC). Former SA Leo Clark (e.o.d. 1/11/43; retired 2/1/65; Sr. RA, Atlantic City, New Jersey, 1944 to retirement) was belligerent concerning our contact of him. He had previously testified before Watergate Committee regarding 1964 Democratic Convention in Atlantic City and gave same information to SSC Staff Members 4/17/75. (Clark was one of Agents on FBI special squad at Atlantic City during convention).

ACTION:

Copy of this teletype being placed in personnel file of former SA Clark for due consideration should interview of him be entertained in the future.
