1. NAME OF CASE OR IDENTIFICATION OF SITUATION Robert M. Shelton Lake Sherwood Northport, Alabama

2. PRINCIPAL ISSUES INVOLVED Diverting Klan money to personal use.

3. REASONS FOR SENSITIVITY Taxpayer is the Imperial Wizard of the United Klans of America, K.K.K., Inc. He is presently being investigated by the House Un-American Activities Committee of Congress. He and the Klan have had national publicity as a result of the Congressional hearings and civil rights activities in the South.

ACTION TAKEN OR PROPOSED

The taxpayer has been contacted by Revenue Agent John H. Murrell on two occasions prior to referral to the Intelligence Division. He has indicated he will cooperate with the examining agents as soon as his records are released by the House Un-American Activities Committee.

This case was referred to the Intelligence Division only a few days ago; therefore, a realistic estimate of the completion date will not be practicable until at least next month.

It is prepared in response to intelligence requests and is not for dissemination outside your Committee. Its use is limited to official proceedings by your Committee and the content may not be disclosed to unauthorized personnel without the express approval of the FBI.
