In that file drawer was one copy of 11 Murphy's Tourist Handbook to India and Ceylon which had been 12 stamped Confidential because it was the only copy in town, and 13 they wanted to keep track of it; one 1925 military attache 14 report from London on the British Army in India; and then a 15 considerable number of clippings from the New York Times that 16 this old lady, Mrs. North, had been clipping since World War I, 17 and that was it.
