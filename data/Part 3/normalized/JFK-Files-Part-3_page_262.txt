Counterintelligence Program

During the Presidential Inauguration activities in Washington, D. C., January, 1969, the National Mobilization Committee to End the War in Vietnam (NMC), was utilizing walkie-talkies on the "citizen band" to coordinate movements and activities.

NMC communications were disrupted and confusing orders were issued when the Washington Field Office of the FBI, utilizing similar communications methods, identified with the NMC network and countermanded orders or supplied misinformation to NMC marshals.
