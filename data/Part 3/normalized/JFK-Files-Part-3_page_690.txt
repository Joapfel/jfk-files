SUBJECT: SAMUEL ADASON JAFFE INFORMATION CONCERNING

CLASSIFIED BY SP2AUK DECLASSIFY ON: 25X

Attached New York teletype advises of information from former Bureau Agent, who is a current informative asset, that the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Operations (SSCIO) has become aware of Jaffe's allegations that the FBI has taken actions which have prevented him from obtaining or keeping a job in the news media field. Jaffe claims that his association with Bureau Agents with regard to Soviet intelligence matters cooled off after he was identified by a Soviet defector as cooperating with Soviet Intelligence and that the FBI has since deprived him from making a living in his profession. The SSCIO is looking into Jaffe's situation to determine if his allegations are true and if the FBI can be prevented from taking such actions in other similar situations.

BACKGROUND:

Jaffe is a former correspondent for ABC and CBS who was a symbol number informant of the New York Office from 1958 to 1961. During this period, he furnished information on his numerous contacts with Soviet nationals whom he met through his assignment as a CBS correspondent at the United Nations. In 1961, he joined ABC as its correspondent in Moscow where he served until he was expelled in 1965 following the publication of an article unfavorable to the Soviet Union. He was then transferred to Hong Kong where he remained until 1968. Since 1968, he has worked for various news media and has acted as a free lance journalist. Following his return from Hong Kong, he took up residence in Washington, D. C., and was in contact with the Washington Field Office, furnishing information on Soviets and Chinese nationals whom he met in the course of his employment.
