In Our Opinion

Assign Congress To Hunt Bombers

Here we go again. The bombing of a State Department restroom and followup phone call from someone calling itself the "Weather Underground" is reminiscent of times past. The bombers are apparently protesting any economic aid to non-Communists in South Vietnam and Cambodia. There's a certain irony in the timing. At the very moment the CIA and FBI are being raked over the coals by self-righteous members of Congress, a radical group like the ones under surveillance in the late '60s and early '70s pulls off a terrorist act. We wouldn't blame the FBI for turning the investigation of the latest bombing over to the congressional committee investigating government surveillance. Let the legislators handle it. Some measure of watchfulness must be maintained over agencies like the FBI and CIA. The repugnant aspect of it all is congressmen leading the media bandwagon to roast the agencies.
