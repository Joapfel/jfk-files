SENSTUDY 75. INFORMATION CONCERNING.

RE OMAHA TELCALLS TO BUREAU AND MINNEAPOLIS, JUNE-19, 1975.

IN THE JUNE-19, 1975 AFTERNOON ISSUE OF THE OMAHA WORLD HERALD, A DAILY NEWSPAPER, OMAHA, NEBRASKA, APPEARED THE FOLLOWING ARTICLE CAPTIONED "SENATE COMMITTEE SENDING INVESTIGATOR TO GORDON, NEBRASKA". THE ARTICLE APPEARED UNDER A WORLD HERALD WASHINGTON BUREAU DATELINE.

CONTACT WITH OMAHA WORLD HERALD AUTHORITIES AT OMAHA FAILED TO DEVELOP THE NATURE OF THE SENATE COMMITTEE'S INVESTIGATION AT GORDON, NEBRASKA. WORLD HERALD AUTHORITIES END PAGE ONE
