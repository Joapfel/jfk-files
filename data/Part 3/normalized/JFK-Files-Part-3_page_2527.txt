JUL 16 1975

TO: Harold R. Tyler, Jr. Deputy Attorney General

FROM: K. William O'Connor Special Counsel for Intelligence Coordination

SUBJECT: Elliff Response to My Letter of July 15, 1975

The attached response to the July 15, letter may indicate some intention to amplify access by interpretation.

Elliff told me that this letter was prepared and sent before the SSC considered it. No has discussed my July 15, letter with Schwarzs and Brothers and believes that this letter closely reflects their, and SSC members, view as to how to proceed.

I told Elliff we would study the letter and be back in touch; in the meanwhile, I said that the provision of access of the 7/15 letter stood as written. I will consult with the Bureau before preparing a substantive response.

cc: Doug Marvin John Hintz
