On January 27, 1969, Captain JOHN EDDY, Providence, Rhode Island Police Department, Detective Division, advised that the Crown Import Corporation at 6711⁄2 Broadway, is probably identical with the company which formerly occupied 667 Broadway, known as the Capital Manufacturing Company. He stated this company went out of business approximately one year ago and was formerly the company that dealt with "Gaffer" watches, which are cheap, inexpensive Swiss-made watches, bearing names similar to those of well-known watches.

He stated in one instance, watches were sold which bore the name Hamilton and were confused by unknowing individuals with Hamilton watches.

He stated this company was never known to deal in any stolen jewelry or other type of merchandise and dealt primarily with the "Gaffer" watches.
