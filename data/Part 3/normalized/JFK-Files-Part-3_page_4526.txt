TWENTY FIVE LAST, A PCI ADVISED THAT GAMBLING OF ALL TYPES HAS BEEN SUPPRESSED IN NEW KENSINGTON, PA., TO A POINT BEYOND ANYTHING IN HIS RECOLLECTION FOR LAST TWENTY FIVE YEARS. NUMBERS, SPORTS BETS AND HORSE BETS ARE DOWN TO ABOUT TWENTY FIVE PERCENT OF THE NORMAL OF ONE MONTH AGO AND CARD, CRAPS AND BARBOUT GAMES ARE EXTINCT.
