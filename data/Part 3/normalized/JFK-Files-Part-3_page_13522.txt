MR. BELIN: Any other questions by members of the Commission concerning the Castro situation?

CHAIRMAN ROCKEFELLER: Yes. Was there evidence to your knowledge that Castro was aware of this attempt or became aware of it?

MR. BISSELL: I don't remember any evidence to that effect. But I don't remember ever seeing any report to that effect.
