6 MR. GRAY: Yes, I do. My own strong feeling is that if the President does not have this board, he must have something like it. It is the only agency in government that I know of which on behalf of the President can oversee and interpret and understand the intelligence activities of all of the agencies of the government engaged in intelligence, which includes not only the CIA, but the DIA, and to some extent the AEC, to some extent the FBI, and other government agencies from time to time who find themselves involved purposefully or not in foreign intelligence activities. This board, I feel, should have the confidence of the President. He should be, feel comfortable with its membership. If any President inherits such a board and is not comfortable, then he ought to make his own board.
