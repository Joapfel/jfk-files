House Select Committee on Assassinations

In response to Part B, Item 4, it should be noted that HSCA request of November 30, 1977, asked for all FBI material pertaining to Martin Luther King, Jr., and the Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) in the FBI's Counter- intelligence Program (COINTELPRO) file. By letter dated January 11, 1978, you were advised that representatives of the HSCA agreed to limit its request to COINTELPRO files involving the Communist Party USA and Black Nationalist-Kale Groups and to limit the period of HSCA interest from January 1, 1967, to May 1, 1968. On January 23, 1978, Staff Attorney Michael Eberhardt reiterated that the HSCA's interest is that period rather than the years 1965-1968 as set forth in the request of December 13, 1977. The above-mentioned COINTELPRO files and files of the King and SCLC security investigations from January 1, 1967, to May 1, 1968, have been prepared for HSCA access. Therefore, based on knowledge of personnel of the Congressional Inquiry Unit processing HSCA requests regarding King, all material at FBI Headquarters, which may contain COINTELPRO-type activities against King and SCLC during the period January 1, 1967, to May 1, 1968, has been made available to the HSCA.

NOTE: See Director, FBI, letter to Assistant Attorney General, Criminal Division, Attention: Mr. Robert L. Keuch, dated 1/25/78, captioned, "House Select Committee on Assassinations, U. S. House of Representatives (HSCA)."
