HOUSE SELECT COMMITTEE ON ASSASSINATIONS U. S. HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES (HSCA)

RE: OCTOBER-5, 1977, REQUEST OF HSCA FOR INFORMATION IN POSSESSION OF THE FBI RELATIVE TO CLIFTON BAIRD, BRENT HARDIN AND ARLIE BLAIR.

This constitutes a complete response to the October 5, 1977, letter of G. Robert Blakey, Chief Counsel and Director, HSCA.

Chief Counsel Blakey requested all FBI information relative to Clifton Baird, Brent Hardin and Arlie Blair.

Utilizing identifying data for Baird, Hardin and Blair furnished by the HSCA, retrievable information in FBI Headquarters files identifiable with Baird, Hardin and Blair has been prepared for the Committee's access subject to the usual excisions and deletions.

Also available for the Committee's access are documents relating to Brent Hardin which were previously pre- pared on May 18, 1977, pursuant to the receipt of the Committee's request dated April 27, 1977, for these documents.

Where information is not provided, it is either not retrievable from Headquarters files or is not being produced pursuant to the Memorandum of Understanding.
