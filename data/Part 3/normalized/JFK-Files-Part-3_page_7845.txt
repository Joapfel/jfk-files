Memorandum V. V. Kolombatovic to Mr. W. R. Wannall

Re: John D. Tarpey Assistant Chief Inspector, Office of Criminal Investigations, U. S. Postal Service, Appearance Before Senate Select Committee (SSC) 6/10/75

Representatives of the SSC have recently requested the Inspection Service of the USPS to make available for interview the Inspector in Charge of the Boston Post Office. According to Jackson, the Inspector in Charge of Boston has been there for many years and is most knowledgeable about past. and present activities in that office. In the opinion of Jackson, the representatives of the SSC will soon travel to Miami, Florida, to inquire into the activities of the Postal Service in that area.

The above matter has been coordinated with SA P. V. Daly of the Legal Counsel Division.

ACTION:

For the information of the Director.
