Letter to Birmingham

RN: counterintelligence program 107-9-4

8. The gross income of Sholten and his wife for the year 1963 totals $1,075. This amount does not appear to be sufficient to support a family of five. All possible efforts should be made to develop evidence indicating Sholten is withholding or concealing income.

This investigation should be afforded prompt and continuing attention and the Drexel must be advised of all pertinent developments.

When all possible leads have been developed a blind memorandum should be prepared setting forth Sholten's back-ground and current position with the plan and all data probative of a possible violation of Internal Revenue laws. The Bureau will make this information available to appropriate Internal Revenue Service officials at the request of Government.
