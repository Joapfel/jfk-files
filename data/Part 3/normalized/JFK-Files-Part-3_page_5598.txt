ST-103 REC-67

This report relates to the alleged evasion of income taxes for the years 1963 and 1964 by ROBERT M. SHELTON and the alleged wilful failure to file 1961 and 1963 corporate returns by the UNITED KLAN'S OF AMERICA, K.K.K., INC. It was also alleged that SHELTON diverted KLAN funds to his personal use. SHELTON has been Imperial Wizard of the UNITED KLAN'S OF AMERICA, K.K.K., INC. since 1961. He maintains an office for himself and the KLAN in his residence at 18 Lake Sherwood, Northport, Alabama 35476. No received mail for himself, the KLAN, and ALABAMA RESCUE SERVICE at P. O. Box 2369, Tuscaloosa, Alabama 35401. ALABAMA RESCUE SERVICE is a cover name for the KLAN organization in Alabama. He filed his individual income tax returns involved with the District Director, Birmingham, Alabama.

AUG 9 1967 Federal Agent Frank Hutchinson, Tuscaloosa, Alabama, cooperated in the joint investigation. This case has been classified as confidential.
