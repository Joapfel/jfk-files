After weighing the foregoing and considering the unique role of and regard for the Director of the FBI, it is my conclusion that the Nation would feel comfortable with tenure for the Director of the FBI, and tenure would contribute toward countering any construction that appointment of any Director was political in the sense that the Directorship would not necessarily change hands with each administration. I feel the incumbent senses a greater independence through tenure.

I feel that tenure should be for a period such as nine years to minimize the occasions when appointive consideration would coincide with a change in administrations. Such a period would also provide the incumbent a sufficient feeling of independence. However, this Bureau defers to the Department on the subject of length of time.
