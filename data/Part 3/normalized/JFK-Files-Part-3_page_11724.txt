Productivity

During fiscal 1971, 14 persons wanted in the United States were located by the office and one informal deportation was arranged. TOKYO

In addition to Japan, Tokyo covers South Korea and Formosa. The latter presents a particularly delicate situation as the United States moves toward recognition of Communist China. Our Nationalist Chinese contacts, however, have promised to continue their cooperation despite what they view as an unsympathetic move by the United States Government. Despite the language barrier in both South Korea and Formosa, our representatives have been consistently successful in getting the work of the Bureau handled in adequate fashion. As the United States military commitment in Japan is reduced and as the economic goals of the United States and Japan draw farther apart, it is increasingly important that we have a liaison capability in Tokyo.
