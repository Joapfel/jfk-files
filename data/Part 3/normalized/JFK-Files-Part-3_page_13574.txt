Bureaucracy is so big, even in the case of CIA, that I am skeptical that ten good people can ensure that there would not be any examples or cases of operations outside of the law. I think that has to start at the top with a tone, and direction, and a sense of responsibility that the key officials bring to their job. So I guess I am not saying that you can cut off problems by having ten more people.
