Counterintelligence Programs

Miami Division alerted "Fort Lauderdale News" of Klan and National States Rights Party rally at Vero Beach-Winter Park, Florida, August 29, 1968, and participation of Charles Baker Riddlehoover. Newspaper articles of a negative nature were written regarding the Klan and Riddlehoover.

As a result of unfavorable publicity in newspaper articles, Riddlehoover's organization failed to gain a following and rally at Winter Beach was a failure.
