This memorandum is to advise of testimony by Postal Inspector John D. Tarpey on 6/10/75 before the SSC.

On 6/13/75, Inspector John D. Tarpey confidentially furnished Liaison Officer L. A. Crescioli the following information:

Tarpey testified before the SSC on 6/10/75 for a period of approximately two hours. At the outset of his testimony, Tarpey read a prepared statement in which he informed the Committee that although he wished to cooperate fully he was producing, upon advice of the U. S. Department of Justice, only six internal U. S. Postal Service documents which predate Tarpey's investigative files and which concerned U. S. mail openings in New York City. The six documents turned over to the Committee do not contain any information relating to the FBI; instead, they pertain to mail openings by CIA in New York City. The SSC accepted Tarpey's statement and did not press him for any additional documents. However, a SSC staff representative, (FNU) Wallach, inquired of Tarpey as to whether or not he knew of mail openings in Los Angeles by FBI Agents Ron Mascar (phonetic) and (FNU, but possibly William) Miller.* Tarpey replied that he was not knowledgeable on this matter and never heard of the above two Agents. (Tarpey requested that the above information concerning the two Los Angeles Agents be carefully handled by the FBI since he fears that Wallach may have posed this as a test question for Tarpey).
