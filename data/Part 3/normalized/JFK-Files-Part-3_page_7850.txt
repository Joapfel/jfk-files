SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES (SSC)

RE: INTERVIEW OF FBI SA BY SSC

A: I recall the registration desk, therefore, perhaps I did, but I am not sure.

Q: Did you use old or new tapes?

A: I do not recall.

Q: What time of day was it?

A: I seem to recall that it was at night. I feel a good guess might be between 8:00 p.m. and midnight, or thereabouts.

Q: Did you monitor for only one night?

A: That is my recollection.

Q: Did you and Bill Campbell relieve each other?

A: I do not recall this. It seems we both monitored at the same time.

Q: Was Dr. King in the room being monitored?

A: I associated voices I heard in the room to be that of Dr. King.

Q: Were there other people in the room being monitored?

A: I again associated voices heard with Mr. Abernathy, another male from Baltimore whose name I do not recall, and I believe the voices of two women.

Q: Was there any visual monitoring?

A: Not to my knowledge.
