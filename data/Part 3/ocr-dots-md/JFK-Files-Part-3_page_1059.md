Counterintelligence Programs

During 1988, the Students For a Democratic Society (SDS) Chapter at California State College was factionalized. The Los Angeles Office directed informants to attempt to widen the breach between the groups by implying that Robert Ray, head of the SDS Chapter, was using SDS funds to support his drug habit and that Delfin Lugo, an active SDS member, had used SDS funds for his own use while he had been treasurer of the Chapter. Although Ray, according to one informant, was a heavy drug user, we are unable to verify whether he or Lugo actually misappropriated SDS funds.

At subsequent SDS meetings, there were fist fights between the groups, thus disrupting the meetings. Additionally, members of one faction made threatening early morning phone calls to members of the other faction if they showed up at the meetings. Robert Ray was eventually discredited and replaced as Chairman of the SDS Unit.
