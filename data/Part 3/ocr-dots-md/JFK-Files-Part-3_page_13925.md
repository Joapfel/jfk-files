NSA Director Blake had informed Mr. Coyne that the document was from NSA to overseas stations on steps to be taken in tests of procedures with respect to intercepts of Soviet scrambler traffic.

Dr. Baker noted that this is serious, because our only hope for exploiting Soviet scrambler communications is to take advantage of operator and encryption mistakes, so any reminder to the Soviets that NSA personnel are looking for "busts" will be very bad.
