Reference is made to a telephone conversation between Associate Deputy Attorney General James A. Wilderotter and Inspector John B. Hotis on March 25, 1975.

This will confirm the authorization provided by Mr. Wilderotter to Mr. Hotis with regard to permitting CIA to furnish a memorandum dated February 7, 1960, entitled "CIA Operations in the United States," to the staff of the Senate Select Committee.

It is our understanding that Mr. William G. Miller, Staff Director, Senate Select Committee, telephonically contacted Mr. John Clarke, Associate Deputy to the Director of Central Intelligence, and requested a copy of the above-described memorandum.
