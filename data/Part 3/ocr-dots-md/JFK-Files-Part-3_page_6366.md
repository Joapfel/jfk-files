4. Revenue Agent HUTCHINSON and I interviewed SHELTON on seven different occasions between April 5, 1967 and May 17, 1967. He instructed his wife, BETTY LOU SHELTON, and KLAN BOOKKEEPER, MRS. MELVIN SEXTON, to cooperate with us and make available all records of financial transactions of the KLAN. SHELTON stated that:

a. He did not furnish books and records of the KLAN to the House Un- American Activities Committee because his attorney advised him he could not waive his immunity on part of his records or on part of his testimony. He either had to take the "Fifth Amendment" all the way or disclose confidential records of KLAN membership which he had taken an oath not to disclose.

b. The books and records of the KLAN are audited at least once a year by KLAN EXAMINERS. The results of this examination is reported on the annual corporate income tax return.

c. The KLAN is a ten exempt organization and should not be required to file tax returns. SHELTON exhibited a letter from the State of Alabama Revenue Department which stated that, "The UNITED KLANS OF AMERICA, INC., is exempt from Alabama income tax and as long as it is operated not for profit and not for the gain of any individual it will not be necessary that corporate income tax returns be filed."

SHELTON acknowledged he had never applied for exempt status for the KLAN for Federal tax purposes because he would have to disclose information regarding KLAN membership which he was not at liberty to disclose.
