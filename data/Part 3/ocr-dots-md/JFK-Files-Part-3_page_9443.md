Wilderotter told O'Conner it was the White House position that these girls should not be made available for interview unless the Committee allows Bureau personnel to be present. Wilderotter stated these two employees had been cited as examples wherein the White House felt interviews should not be conducted without representatives of the particular agency or Bureau being present as they are service personnel. According to Wilderotter, Shwartz agreed to this position.

O'Conner telephonically contacted Special Agent Daly on June 4, 1975, at which time he advised that he had discussed the interview with Shwartz, Epstein, and staff member Mark Gitenstein, and they would hold in abeyance the request to interview the employees pending a review of documents the Bureau is making available concerning 1964 Atlantic City Convention as stated in the Committee's request of June 14, 1975.
