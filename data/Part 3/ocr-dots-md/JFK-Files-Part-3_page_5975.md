The following comments and investigative instructions are noted in connection with these tax returns:

1. The individual returns filled jointly by Shelton and his wife during the period 1959 through 1963 indicate Shelton's occupation to be "liquorist" and "barber" and in no instance do they indicate any employment or association with the Firm. This is in conflict with the corporate returns filed by Shelton for the USA where he indicated his position as "Imperial Wizard" or "President."

2. Shelton's return for 1959 indicates that it was prepared by E. R. Allen, Jr., Certified Public Accountant, Post Office Box 808, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. The form and typing on the other tax returns, both corporate and individual, indicate a strong likelihood that he too was prepared by Allen. You should review your files and conduct necessary investigation to determine if Allen is active in connection with financial matters relating to the Firm.
