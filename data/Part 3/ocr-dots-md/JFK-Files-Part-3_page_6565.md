A confidential source, who has furnished reliable information in the past, advised on October 15, 1968 that Georgy I. Isachenko had been informed that "Ramparts" alleged had not received the list of names and addresses from the Soviets in return for the list that had been furnished by "Ramparts" to the Soviets. [Isachenko] was to check into this situation in an attempt to clear up this misunderstanding. The source was of the opinion that the list referred to pertained to mailing lists maintained by "Ramparts", and the Soviet Information Department, Washington, D.C.

The current "Diplomatic List," published by the United States Department of State reflects that Georgy I. Isachenko is the Information Counselor of the Embassy of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics, Washington, D.C.
