Dear Senator Mansfield:

You will recall that by letter dated January 27, 1975, you and Senate Majority Leader Hugh Scott requested that the FBI not destroy or otherwise dispose of material relevant to the subjects of inquiry by the Senate Select Committee.

Please find enclosed a copy of a letter which I have addressed to Senator Frank Church, Chairman of the Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations With Respect to Intelligence Activities, suggesting what, I hope, is a reasonable modification of our total moratorium on file destruction.

Please accept my assurance of continued cooperation in all matters of mutual interest.

JAN-20-73

Clarence L. Kelley
