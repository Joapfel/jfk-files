Reference is made to the December 18, 1975, letter of the SSC as modified at a conference on December 30, 1975, between Messrs. W. O. Cregar and P. V. Daly of this Bureau and Committee Staff Members Michael Epstein and Mark Gitenstein.

This memorandum effects delivery of material in response to Items 1, l(a), l(b), and l(e). The matters referred to are four FBI investigations about which the SSC was previously furnished materials relating to FBI preventive actions.

Concerning Item 1, which requested reports or letter-head memoranda (LHMs) disseminated outside the Bureau by FBI Headquarters (FBIHQ), for the six-month period preceding the act of violence prevented, the following is noted. In regard to the incidents involving the proposed assassination of a narcotics agent in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and the planned racial violence in Cleveland, Ohio, there were no reports or LHMs submitted by either the Minneapolis Field Office or Cleveland Field Office during the six-month period preceding the incident.
