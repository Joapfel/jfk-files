# Counterintelligence Programs

The San Francisco Office advised on September 12, 1960, that through its continuing informant coverage of the Communist Party, USA (CPUSA), it was aware of new subscribers to the CPUSA newspaper, "Peoples World" (PW). San Francisco instituted policy of interviewing each new subscriber to PW.

Archie Brown, a Northern California CPUSA leader, complained that a major problem in trying to increase the membership of the CPUSA is that new subscribers to the PW are interviewed by the FBI. This has caused these subscribers to drop their subscriptions and to disassociate themselves from CP members.
