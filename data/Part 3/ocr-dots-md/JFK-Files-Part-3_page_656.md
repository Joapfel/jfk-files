U. S. SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE
ON INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES (SSC)

INTERVIEW OF SA EARL M. PETERSEN
BY SSC STAFF MEMBER

Mr. Seidel:

Did you ever supervise any techni-cal installation or electronic surveillance of the BPP in San Diego?

SA Petersen:

No. I don't recall anything like that being done here.

Mr. Seidel:

How would you feel about writing a letter to a wife saying her husband was sleeping with various other women if the husband was a BPP leader?

SA Petersen:

I would hesitate to do this because it might break up a family. If the facts were true and this might cause the BPP leader to be less effective, it would probably be a good counter-intelligence move.
