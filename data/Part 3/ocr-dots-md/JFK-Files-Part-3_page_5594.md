Letter to Birmingham
RN: counterintelligence program
107-9-4

8. The gross income of Sholton and his wife for the year 1983 totals $1.076 million. This amount does not appear to be sufficient to support a family of five. All possible efforts should be made to develop evidence indicating Sholton is withholding or concealing income.

This investigation should be afforded prompt and
continuing attention and the Duxbury must be advised of all
pertinent developments.

When all possible leads have been developed a blind
investigation should be undertaken setting forth Sholton's back-
ground and current position with the help and all data probative of
a possible violation of Internal Revenue laws. The Bureau
will make this information available to appropriate Internal
Revenue Service officials at the seat of Government.
