NAAACP officials are aware that the NAAACP is a communist target. As a result, the NAACCP has taken a firm stand against communism and has warned its branches to be constantly alert against communist efforts to infiltrate them. The constitution of the NAACCP contains an anticommunist clause widely prohibited membership in the organization by known Communist Party members.

There is no indication that any of the key figures of the NAAACP are or have been members of the Communist Party. Loy Wilkins, Executive Secretary of the NAAACP, has been strongly anticommunist and has encouraged to keep the NAAACP free of communists.

The following national officers of the NAAACP had communist front affiliations--in no instance extensive--a number of years ago, but none is known to have been a Communist Party member: Artur Pilingra, President; Bishop Stephen G.
