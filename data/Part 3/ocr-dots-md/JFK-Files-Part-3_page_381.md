SUBJECT: Synopsis of Responses: Congressional Access to Personal
Files Maintained by the FBI

With the exception of the Solicitor General's Office, all Departmental components whose views were solicited have provided their views on the question of Congressional Access to Personal FBI Files.

OLC, OLA, and Criminal Division favor a combination of options 1 and 3. Civil Rights Division favors option 1. The Bureau chooses option 3.

This office recommends the adoption of the position articulated by OLC and concurred in by OLA and Criminal.
