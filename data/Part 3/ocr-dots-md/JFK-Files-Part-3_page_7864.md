RE: REQUESTS PERTAINING TO "OFFICIAL AND CONFIDENTIAL FILES"

Reference is made to your memorandum from captioned Committee dated May 14, 1975, and the appendices thereto, which contained a request relating to the "official and confidential files." In response to material requested in Appendices C, Part III, Items 21, 22, and 23, of the aforementioned letter, we have available for review by representatives of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities, summaries of the contents of the "official and confidential files."

Arrangements for the review of these documents may be made in accordance with existing procedures. These documents are located in the Intelligence Division of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
