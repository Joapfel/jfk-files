MEMORANDUM FOR THE ACTING ATTORNEY GENERAL

RE: PROGRESSIVE LABOR MOVEMENT
INTERNAL SECURITY - PLM

Marxist-Leninist
Group Functionary

The Progressive Labor Movement is an ultraleft Marxist-Leninist organization which follows the Chinese Communist Party line. Mortimer Harvey Scheer is the National Vice Chairman of the Progressive Labor Movement. He has been sent by the Progressive Labor Movement to the west coast to organize the Progressive Labor Movement in that area. He is one of the two principal founders of the Progressive Labor Movement. Technical coverage of Scheer's residence could materially assist in the investigation of the Progressive Labor Movement, organization and its program. In view of the demonstrated radical nature of the Progressive Labor Movement and its open disdain of lawful authority, it is believed this coverage will be of value in the determination of impending trouble areas in time for preventive measures to be taken.

I recommend that authority be granted to install a technical surveillance at the residence of Mortimer Harvey Scheer, 2629 Acton Street, Berkeley, California, or at any address to which Scheer may move in the future.
