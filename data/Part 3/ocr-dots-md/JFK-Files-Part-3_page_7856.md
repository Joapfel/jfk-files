Q: Were you told of the purpose of monitoring Dr. KING?

A: I am not sure. So much of this is now a gray area to me. I do seem to recall asking for the reason and being advised that this was an internal security matter and that there was some indication that Dr. KING was being influenced by persons who leaned toward the left.

Q: Were you told that such people might be with Dr. KING?

A: I do not recall. I simply have the recollection that our purpose was to determine whether he was being so influenced. I hasten to admit, however, that I am very hazy on this and it may very well be that I learned of these indications through conversations even after the occurrence of our monitoring. I can not be more specific in this regard.

Q: Who was with you at the time of the monitoring?

A: BILL CAMPBELL.

Q: Was anyone else involved?

A: I believe I did meet BILL BERGERON (phonetic) in the lobby of the hotel. No one else to my recollection.

Q: DID Would you sign out from the office to go to the hotel?

A: This would be the customary procedure, but whether it was done in this instance I do not recall.
