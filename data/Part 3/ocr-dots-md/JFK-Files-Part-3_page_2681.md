VISIT OF SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE (SSCI) STAFF
TO MIAMI FIELD OFFICE AND LEGAL MEXICO CITY REGARDING DRUG
ENFORCEMENT AGENCY (DEA).

THE SSCI IS CONDUCTING A STUDY OF THE DEA IN CONNECTION
WITH THE COMMITTEE'S ROLE IN OVERSIGHT OF CERTAIN PORTIONS OF
DEA BUDGET. PRIMARILY, THE COMMITTEE IS INTERESTED IN,
DETERMINING THE ROLE OF THE DEA IN THE INTELLIGENCE COMMUNITY.

SSCI CHAIRMAN BIRCH BAYN HAS REQUESTED THE FBI TO ASSIST IN
THE COMMITTEE'S STUDY BY PROVIDING BRIEFINGS TO STAFF MEMBERS
REGARDING FBI/DEA RELATIONSHIPS. I HAVE AGREED TO ASSIST THE
COMMITTEE IN ITS STUDY. AS SUCH, YOU ARE TO PROVIDE BRIEFINGS
TO THE COMMITTEE STAFF REGARDING OUR RELATIONSHIPS WITH DEA.
