UNITED STATES SENATE SELECT COMMITTEE
TO STUDY GOVERNMENTAL OPERATIONS WITH
RESPECT TO INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES

Reference is made to letter dated March 19, 1975, to the Attorney General from the Chairman of captioned Committee, which enclosed certain requests for documents and other information from the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI).

Under IV. A. (Policies and Procedures) the table of contents of the FBI Manual of Instructions and certain portions of this Manual were requested. In accordance with this request the following material is enclosed:

1) Table of contents; 2) Preface. Guidelines for FBI Investigations; 3) Section 36. Espionage (classified "Secret"); 4) Section 84. Registration Act; 5) Section 80. Sabotage; 6) Section 87. Investigations of Subversive Organizations and Individuals; 7) Section 88. Sedition; 8) Section 95. Treason; 9) Section 97. Voorhis Act; 10) Section 122. Extremist Matters and Civil Unrest.

Not included as enclosures are Section 105, Internal Security — Nationalistic Tendency (Foreign Intelligence), classified "Secret"; Section 107, Part I, Internal Security Informants and Confidential Sources; Section 107, Part II, Foreign Counterintelligence Assets; or Section 130, Extremist Informants and Sources. These Sections are deemed to contain extremely sensitive information and for that reason are not being transmitted; however, they are available for review by appropriately cleared personnel of the committee staff at FBI Headquarters.

Under IV. B. the table of contents of the FBI Manual of Rules and Regulations and pertinent portions of this Manual were requested. There is enclosed a copy of the table of contents of the FBI Manual of Rules and Regulations.
