letter dated May 14, 1975, with attached appendices, requesting
certain documents and other information from the FBI. With
regard to material requested in Appendices A, III, Items

This is in accordance with the agreement made by Bureau officials and Deputy Attorney General Tyler, on June 23, 1975. The excisions were made to protect personal privacy.
