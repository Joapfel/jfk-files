Concerning Item 1(a), which requested predications for each investigation, it should be noted that in the incident involving the proposed assassination of a narcotics agent, there was no predication submitted by the Minneapolis Field Office. This was due to the fact that there was no FBI jurisdiction concerning this matter and consequently no investigation by the FBI.

Concerning Item 1(b), which requested the first investigative report or LMI disseminated by FBIQ outside the FBI in each case, and a list of recipients of that report or LMI, the following should be noted. Regarding the incident involving the proposed assassination of a narcotics agent, no report or LMI was submitted by the Minneapolis Field Office as there was no FBI investigation. On the administrative pages accompanying reports or LMI's involving the other incidents, there are notations which identify those agencies which received copies of these documents.

Item 1(e), requested annual reports or LMI's from the Office of Origin to FBIQ for the last five years during which each case was an open (pending) investigation. Regarding the incidents involving the Manafi Muslim sect in Washington, D. C., the proposed assassination of a narcotics agent in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and the planned racial violence in Cleveland, Ohio, no annual reports or LMI's were submitted by either the Washington, Cleveland, or Minneapolis Field Office.
