Memorandum for the Attorney General

Although the BPP-CF is not under the total control or direction of Al Fatah, the above-named member of the BPP-CF has been used by Al Fatah as an agent to support Al Fatah operations. It is therefore requested that the President of the United States through the Attorney General, in the interests of national security, consider this request in conjunction with all other intelligence information available to the Executive concerning this subject, and that you authorize the surveillance requested until January 19, 1973.
