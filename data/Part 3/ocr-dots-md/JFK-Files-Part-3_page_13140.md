109-584-3911

TP 52-S advised that Rodrigo Calderon of Miami, Florida, a former high ranking officer in Cuba under former dictator Fulgencio Batista, came to Tampa on 4/25/64 and met secretly with other Batista followers. This group met for the purpose of formulating plans of their own in the event Fidel Castro was overthrown in the near future by anti-Castro forces that were not followers of Batista. It was felt that this group feared Manuel Artime and desired to formulate plans to be able to re-enter Cuba because it was common knowledge that some Batista supporters would be regarded as undesirables in Cuba when Castro was thrown out. It was believed that this meeting was held at the home of a woman doctor by the name of Dr. Miranda. Others present were Dr. Jose Mijares and a Dr. Santos, who worked for Hillsborough County in Tampa.
