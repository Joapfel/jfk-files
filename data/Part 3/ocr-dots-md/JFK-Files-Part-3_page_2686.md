      INFORMATION IS DESIRED OF HIM IN ORDER THAT HE CAN DECIDE<br/>
      WHETHER HE WILL NEED PRIVATE LEGAL COUNSEL. QUIGLEY SAID HE<br/>
      IS RELUCTANT TO TAKE ANY INITIATIVE WHATSOEVER IN THIS MATTER<br/>
