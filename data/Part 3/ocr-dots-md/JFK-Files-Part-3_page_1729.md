HOUSE SELECT COMMITTEE ON ASSASSINATIONS
U. S. HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES (HSCA)

Reference is made to letter to the Attorney General from G. Robert Blakey, Chief Counsel and Director, HSCA, dated October 18, 1977, requesting access to material in the files of the FBI pertaining to:

Harvey Gene Love
George G. Pittman
Stephanie Helene Sheehan
Jonathon Henry Shea
Keith Albert Wright
Frank Quenqui
Randolph Erwin Rosenson

Retrievable information in FBI Headquarters files concerning the above individuals, with the exception of Rosenson, has been prepared for the Committee's access subject to the usual excisions or deletions. Where information is not provided, it is either not retrievable from Headquarters files or is not being produced pursuant to the Memorandum of Understanding.

By FBI memorandum dated October 13, 1977, documents containing information concerning Rosenson were provided the HSCA.
