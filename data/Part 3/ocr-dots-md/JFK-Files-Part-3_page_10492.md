HOLDERS’ INFORMATION

We have enclosed the following notice and attached it herewith a copy of the letter issued by the NYSE to you on October 23, 1987. As you may see, we have received an attachment to our letter which is attached to this notice.

The letter was issued as a result of our concern that the NYSE had failed to follow through with the implementation of the requirements of the NYSE’s stock market listing standards. We are sorry to hear that you have not been able to complete your plan for the implementation of the NYSE’s new standards within the time frame prescribed by the NYSE. It would be wonderful if we could receive your progress report by return mail. In the mean time, please let us know if you have made any progress toward implementing the standards. If you have completed the process of developing and implementing a plan for the adoption of the NYSE’s new standards, please let us know how far along you have come so that we can better understand what more we can do for you. In the mean time, please let us know if you have any questions or concerns about this matter. Thank you for your time and attention to this matter. We look forward to hearing from you soon.

Sincerely,
