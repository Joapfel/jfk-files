MEMORANDUM FOR THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

RE: WORKERS WORLD PARTY
INTERNAL SECURITY - WWP

The Workers World Party (WNP) consists of former Socialist Workers Party (SWP) members who split from the SWP in February, 1959. The WNP favors a stricter interpretation of Marxism-Leninism based upon the writings of Leon Trotsky than does the SWP.

This Bureau is currently investigating the WNP and the SWP. The latter organization has been designated pursuant to Executive Order 10450.

Milton Neidenberg was appointed organizer of the Buffalo Branch of the WNP on May 26, 1960, and it is anticipated that he will conduct WNP business in his home. Current informants reporting on the WNP are only able to report on weekly meetings. Technical coverage would materially aid the Buffalo Office in its investigation of Neidenberg and the WNP, and would provide immediate intelligence information regarding the organizational efforts of the WNP both locally and nationally. It is noted that most of the WNP membership is located in Buffalo, New York.

I recommend, therefore, authorization of a surveillance on the residence of Milton Neidenberg, 95 Busti Avenue, Buffalo, New York, or at any other residence to which Neidenberg might move.

Approved:

Acting Attorney General
