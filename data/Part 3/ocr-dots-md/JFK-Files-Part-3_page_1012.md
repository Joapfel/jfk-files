Counterintelligence Programs

On October 18, 1960, the San Francisco Office directed a letter to the San Francisco Branch Headquarters of the Socialist Workers Party (SWP) inviting attendance at an address to be given by Daniel Rubin, National Youth Secretary of the Communist Party, USA (CPUSA), on October 19, 1960, at the Peter Wright School of Dance in San Francisco.

Trotskyist SWP members attended meeting, as did a reporter. Trotskyists caused considerable disruption and exposed Rubin as being a communist, which he was attempting to conceal by claiming to be merely the editor of "New Horizons." The SWP Trotskyists identified "New Horizons" as a communist paper being published by the Communist Party attempting to influence youth.
