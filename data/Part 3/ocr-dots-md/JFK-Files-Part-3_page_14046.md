MEMORANDUM FOR THE FILE

SUBJECT: Meeting of the President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board, April 23, 1963

The Board met at 9:00 a.m., April 23, 1963, in the Board's offices, Room 297, Executive Office Building, at the call of the Chairman, Dr. Killian. Present were Board members Doolittle, Murphy, Clifford, Gray, Baker, Pace, Langer and Land, and Messrs. Coyne and Ash.

Dr. Killian opened the meeting with the observation that when the DCI joined the Board meeting later in the day, Board members should be prepared to discuss with Mr. McCone the latter's April 15, 1963, submission to the President of comments in response to the 14 recommendations made in the Board's March 8 report on Cuba.

The Chairman asked Mr. Coyne to give the Board a brief oral summary of the DCI's April 15 submission to the President. Mr. Coyne did so by repeating for the information of the Board members the comments which he had provided to Mr. Bundy in a memorandum dated April 22, 1963, in response to Mr. Bundy's request that he be furnished Mr. Coyne's own reaction (not that of the Board) to the DCI's communication to the President.
