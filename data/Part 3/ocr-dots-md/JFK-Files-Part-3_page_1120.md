Counterintelligence Programs

On May 24, 1965, the Cleveland Office made available copies of Socialist Workers Party (SWP) literature relating to Rod Holt to Mr. George Dobres, member, Cleveland Board of Education, to alert him to the association of Holt's wife, Marilyn Holt, a Cleveland schoolteacher with the SWP.

On July 2, 1965, Dobres advised the Cleveland Office that based on this information, it did not appear that Marilyn Holt's contract would be renewed. Subsequently, her contract was not renewed.
