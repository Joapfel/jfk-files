Conrad was asked whether it was necessary to account by inventory for all tapes used in making copies of tapes. He answered that it was not so necessary because the volume was so small. He was asked if he knew whether any record or memorandum was made of such a request in connection with the King case and responded that he did not know; that he had no recollection. He was asked if it was possible that such a written record might exist and if so, would it not be in FBI files? He answered that he did not think he could contribute to this question as almost anything is possible. He added, however, that he had no knowledge or recollection of such a record or any knowledge of its existence in FBI files.

He was then asked if he had ever heard of mailings
of tapes involving King. He replied that he had seen something
in a newspaper within the past year or two indicating such
an allegation. His recollection was that the news account
was of a copy of a tape being mailed to Mrs. King, and that
Mrs. King had denied receiving same.
