# BASIS FOR INVESTIGATION:

The investigation originated as a referral from Audit Division. It was alleged that SHELTON received $4,000.00 from DIXIE ENGINEERING COMPANY of Mobile, Alabama, during the years 1963 and 1964 and reported only $2,000.00 from this source during the aforementioned years. It was further alleged that SHELTON travels in a late model Cadillac, drives van and recently built a new house while reporting very nominal amounts of income on his tax returns.

In respect to the KLAN, it was alleged that no corporate returns were filed for the years 1961 and 1963 and that corporate funds were being diverted to the personal use of corporate officers.

KU KLUX KLAN organizations were the subject of an investigation by the House Un-American Activities Committee during the years 1965 and 1966. SHELTON was tried and convicted on a Contempt of Congress charge for refusing to testify and produce KLAN records before this committee. He was given the maximum sentence, one year in prison and $1,000 fine, on October 16, 1966. SHELTON's conviction is presently on appeal.

Evidence was offered before the House Un-American Activities Committee alleging SHELTON signed checks on the KLAN bank account in Tallahassee, payable to cash, aggregating $5,600.00, which were cashed at grocery stores and service stations; and that he allegedly paid for automobiles and a diamond ring with KLAN funds. SHELTON refused to confirm or deny these allegations when testifying before the Congressional Committee.
