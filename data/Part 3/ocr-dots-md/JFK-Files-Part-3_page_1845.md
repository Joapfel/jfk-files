U. S. House of Representatives (RSCA)

The Savannah Office of the FBI was Office of Origin in both the NSRP and Stoner investigation during the period of RSCA interest. The following agents of that office were case agents in the Stoner investigation: Carlton Dillard, January, 1966 - April 1967; William P. Friday, April, 1967 - October, 1967; John H. Murphy, October 1967 - September, 1969. With variation of only a few days, these same Agents were case agents in the investigation of the NSRP. Dillard is now retired. Friday and Murphy are currently assigned to the Savannah Office and are scheduled to retire December 31, 1977.
