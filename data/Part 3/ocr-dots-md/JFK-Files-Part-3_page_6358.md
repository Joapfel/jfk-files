Attached are Xerox copies of Federal income tax return filled by Robert H. Allen and his wife for the years 1959-63 inclusive. Also attached are Xerox copies of United States corporation tax returns filled by Robert H. Shelton and a corporate officer of the United States Bank of America, Inc. (USA), for the years 1961 (year ending 6/30/62) and 1963 (year ending 6/30/64) no null or a statement of no tax liability for the year ending 6/30/63. Information not forth in these tax returns is for lead purposes only and should not be used forth in the details of any investigative report.

The following comments and investigative instructions are noted in connection with these tax returns:

1. The individual returns filled jointly by Shelton and his wife during the period 1959 through 1963 indicate Shelton's occupation to be "brokerage" and "management" and in no instance do they indicate any employment or association with the Firm. This is in conflict with the corporate returns filled by Shelton for the USA where he indicated his position as "Imperial Wizard" or "President."

2. Shelton's return for 1959 indicates it was prepared by Ed F. Allen, Jr., Certified Public Accountant, Post Office Box 303, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. The form and typing on the other tax returns, both corporate and individual, indicate a strong likelihood that too were prepared by Allen. You should review your files and conduct necessary investigation to determine if Allen is active in connection with financial matters relating to the Firm.
