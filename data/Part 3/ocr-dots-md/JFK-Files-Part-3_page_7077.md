Dear Mr. Kelley:

Thank you for forwarding me a copy of your letter to Bill Cotter covering postal-related matters which might come up in the Select Committees' review of U.S. intelligence. I have passed this communication to John Clarke and can reaffirm that John will be in touch with Bill as these matters evolve. I want to assure that subjects dealing with any postal relationships are properly coordinated.

I am furnishing Mr. Cotter with a copy of this letter.
