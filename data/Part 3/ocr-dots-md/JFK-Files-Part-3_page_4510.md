INFORMANT WILL ATTEMPT TO OBTAIN FURTHER INFORMATION CONCERNING LOCATION OF FARM AND IDENTITIES OF PERSONS INVOLVED. BOTH SUBJECTS SHOULD BE CONSIDERED ARMED AND DANGEROUS.
