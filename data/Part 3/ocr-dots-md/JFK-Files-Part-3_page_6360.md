RN: COUNTY INTELLIGENCE PROGRAM

8. The gross income of Sholton and his wife for the year 1983 totals $1,076. This amount does not appear to be sufficient to support a family of five. All possible efforts should be made to develop evidence indicating Sholton is withholding or concealing income.

This investigation should be afforded prompt and continuing attention and the Durenau must be advised of all pertinent developments.

When all possible leads have been developed a blind memorandum should be prepared setting forth Sholton's background and current position with the King and all data probative of a possible violation of Internal Revenue laws. The Bureau will make this information available to appropriate Internal Revenue Service officials at the seat of Government.
