INTERVIEW OF SA EARL M. PETERSEN
BY SSC STAFF MEMBER

Mr. Seidel: Did he retire early?

SA Petersen: No. He was in a position to retire and retired because he wanted to take advantage of a cost of living increase by retiring prior to a certain date, such as June 30.

Mr. Seidel: Were the BPP and US, Inc. both violence oriented groups?

SA Petersen: Very definitely. There was extreme friction between the BPP and US, Inc. at all times.

Mr. Seidel: Do you recall some caricatures used in connection with COINTELPRO?

SA Petersen: Yes.

Mr. Seidel: Who had the program at that time?

SA Petersen: I believe SA Burns had it then.

Mr. Seidel: What were the cartoons?

SA Petersen: I recall seeing them but do not recall any specific cartoon.

Mr. Seidel: Do you think anything done in COINTELPRO could have had a reaction causing someone to kill an individual in the other group?

SA Petersen: Because of the extreme antagonism between the two groups, anything we did would have had about the same effect as a gnat biting an elephant. I do not think COINTELPRO had that kind of impact.
