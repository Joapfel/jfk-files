INSPECTOR D. E. MOORE

DEM:mjt
1/25/71

I certainly agree with the Inspector that one field division is no different than another in connection with the topic as important as Soviet Espionage. The point that Mr. Branigan and I were attempting to make is that we have to recognize the difference in methods of operation in Soviet intelligence officers in New York, and WFO. The Inspector may be assured that I will follow the operations of Soviet-bloc intelligence officers in New York, WFO and also in San Francisco in view of the establishment of a consulate there. I will continue to be most alert to any successful procedures utilized in one office that may be productive in another and will certainly see that the procedures are applied where feasible.

ASSISTANT DIRECTOR C. D. BRENNAN

DEM:mjt
1/25/71

I concur with Inspector Moore.
