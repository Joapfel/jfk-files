HOSEA WILLIAMS

During October, 1964, a confidential source advised that on October 3, 1964, RUTH BATLEY, then an employee of the Southern Christian Leadership Conference, (SCLC), Harlem, New York Office, while discussing several SCLC personalities with one COXKIE CAMPBELL, referred to WILLIAMS. BATLEY said WILLIAMS had mentioned to her (date not specified) that on a prior occasion (date not specified) HONorer PNETTS O'DELL had told him more about politics than he could read.
