REBUTEL MARCH 24,1975.

ONE SUPERVISORY AGENT DEVOTES ONE-THIRD OF HIS TIME TO COUNTERINTELLIGENCE AND ONE THIRD TO INTERNAL SECURITY.

SEVENTEEN AGENTS ARE REGULARLY ASSIGNED EITHER COUNTERINTELLIGENCE OR INTERNAL SECURITY MATTERS IN ADDITION TO OTHER CLASSIFICATIONS. OF THESE 17 AGENTS THE EQUIVALENT OF FIVE AGENTS WORK COUNTERINTELLIGENCE MATTERS FULL TIMW AND FIVE AND ONE THIRD AGENTS WORK INTERNAL SECURITY.
