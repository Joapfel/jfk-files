It is also reported SSC investigator Arthur Jefferson departed San Diego for Los Angeles on 1/27/76. He is going to probe FBI activities pertaining to two rival black extremist groups. This involved killing of two Black Panther Party (BPP) members during shootout with US members on UCLA campus in 1/69.
