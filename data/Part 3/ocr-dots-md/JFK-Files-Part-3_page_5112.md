# PROPOSED CHANGE IN MANUAL OF RULES AND REGULATIONS

## Part I, Section 10, Appeals and Grievances

### G. NONVETERANS' APPEAL RIGHTS

Recommendations for administrative action following inquiry by a field office, headquarters division, and possibly including the FBI Inspection Division staff, where serious charges may result, are forwarded to the Personnel Section of the Administrative Division. The matter is reviewed and a decision recommended. The Assistant Director of the Administrative Division, following his review of the matter, determines what final action is to be taken up to and including dismissal. The employee may appeal this final action to the Director.
