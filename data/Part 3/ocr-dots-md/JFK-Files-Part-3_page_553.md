Counterintelligence Programs

In order to cause an increase in factionalism among the Communist Party (CP) membership in Philadelphia, in March, 1965, an informant was directed to suggest to Aaron Libson, youth leader of the CP in Philadelphia, that Francis (sic) Gabow, District CP Board member, be forced to take a six-month leave of absence for health reasons. Gabow was critical of Libson (and vice-versa) but had been previously ordered by her doctor to have bed rest because of a nervous condition. It was hoped that Gabow would be forced from CP work because of this and/or recent Libson's effort.

Gabow tendered her resignation from all positions of leadership in the CP Eastern Pennsylvania District on May 4, 1965.
