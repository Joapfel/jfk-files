In the event of sick or annual leave, one man could not possibly keep mail current and work loads in other units would, as a practical matter, preclude use of other personnel to carry out the work. The supervisor retained would reduce the case load in the Subversive Individuals Unit to a more reasonable and practical 533 case load average and also allow for a limited degree of flexibility in the informant matters from time to time as necessary.
