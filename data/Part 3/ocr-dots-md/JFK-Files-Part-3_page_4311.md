MEMORANDUM FOR THE ATTORNEY GENERAL:

RE: /COMMUNIST PARTY, USA\
INTERNAL SECURITY - C

Headquarters Basic
Revolutionary Group

In April, 1957, two technical installations were instituted on the building located at 23 West 26th Street, New York, New York, which is occupied by the Communist Party, USA. One installation covers the national headquarters of the Party and the other covers the office of the Party's New York District. These sources furnish extremely valuable and up-to-date coverage of the activities of top level/national and state functionaries of the Communist Party, USA. These sources also provided information concerning Political Affairs Publishing, Incorporated, which publishes "Political Affairs," monthly theoretical organ of the Communist Party.

During the past six months, these sources have continued to provide timely and valuable data relating to the activities of the Communist Party. For example, valuable information has been received concerning the location of important Party meetings, the Party's plans for demonstrations while officials of the United States Government are in New York City, travel plans of Party leaders and planned public appearances of Party leaders on college campuses.

Unless you instruct to the contrary, these technical surveillances will be continued for an additional six months.
