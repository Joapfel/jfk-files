MEMORANDUM FOR THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

Recent information indicates chapters are being formed in Vallejo and Fresno, California, and there is a strong likelihood that instructions obtained from national headquarters will be relayed by the Sacramento chapter to the Vallejo and Fresno chapters by telephone.

It is requested that authority be granted for
a telephone surveillance on the Black Panther Party head-
quarters at 2941 35th Street, Sacramento, California, or
on any other place to which the headquarters may be moved.
