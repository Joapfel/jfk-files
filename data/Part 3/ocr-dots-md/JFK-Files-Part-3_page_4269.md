# MEMORANDUM FOR THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

3. As set forth previously in this memorandum, Silveria who has been extremely active in Students for Democratic Society affairs and who may be in contact with Humanists, will utilize the phone at 31 Pierrepont Street. It is anticipated other individuals in the group will also use this phone; however, their constitutives are unknown at this time.

4. It can be expected that the interceptions on the phone at 31 Pierrepont Street will furnish us with information concerning the whereabouts of Weatherman members and possibly the whereabouts of Weatherman members who are fugitives. It could possibly furnish us with plans of this group to violate Federal statutes as noted above.

I, therefore, recommend the installation of a telephone surveillance at 31 Pierrepont Street, Brooklyn, New York, for a period of thirty days. If approved, you will be advised when this installation has been made.

Respectfully,

John Edgar Hoover
Director
