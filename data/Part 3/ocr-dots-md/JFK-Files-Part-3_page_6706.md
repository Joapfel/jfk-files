Memorandum to Mr. W. C. Sullivan

RE: [SAM SURVEY] 65-65884

OBSERVATIONS:

It is agreed that the problem to locate (Soviet) illegals - has assumed serious proportions and that the resumption of this program could be of inestimable value to the Bureau in meeting its responsibilities in that regard. However, present Bureau policy precludes the use of this technique. For this reason, it is not believed that we consider reinstitution of this program directly or through CIA or postal authorities.

ACTION:

New York is being advised that the reinstitution of Sam Survey Program either directly or through CIA or postal authorities is not advisable at this time.
