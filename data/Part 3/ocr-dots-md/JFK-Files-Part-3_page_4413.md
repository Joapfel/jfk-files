MEMORANDUM FOR THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

RE: PROGRESSIVE LABOR PARTY
INTERNAL SECURITY
PROGRESSIVE LABOR PARTY

Marxist-Leninist Group
Affiliate

The Progressive Labor Party is an ultraleft Marxist-Leninist organization which follows the Chinese Communist Party line. Clayton Van Lydegraf is a member of the National Committee of the Progressive Labor Party and is the principal organizer of the Progressive Labor Party in the State of Washington. Technical coverage of the residence of Van Lydegraf could materially assist in the investigation of the Progressive Labor Party organization and its programs. In view of the demonstrated radical nature of the Progressive Labor Party and its open disdain of lawful authority, it is believed this coverage will be of value in the determination of impending trouble areas in time for preventive measures to be taken.

I recommend that authority be granted to install a technical surveillance at the residence of Clayton Van Lydegraf, 3058 Northeast Blakeley, Seattle, Washington, or at any address to which Van Lydegraf may move in the future.

APPROVED Noleff
