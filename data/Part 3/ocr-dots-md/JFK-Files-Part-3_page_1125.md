Counterintelligence Programs

On September 5, 1963, the Newark Office confidentially furnished Dr. R. T. Jacobsen, Assistant Superintendent of Schools, Union County High School System, Berkeley Heights, New Jersey, public source information concerning a school teacher, Joseph Edelman. This information revealed Edelman's association with the People's Rights Party, a communist influenced group.

On December 10, 1963, Dr. Jacobsen informed Newark Office that Edelman's teaching contract would not be renewed next year.
