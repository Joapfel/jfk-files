Title: ANGELA YVONNE DAVIS✓

Character: SECURITY MATTER - C

Synopsis: ANGELA YVONNE DAVIS, also known as "Tamu", resides at 1107-1/2 West 87th Street, Los Angeles, California. DAVIS is presently unemployed. Background data is being set out. On 6/21/68, DAVIS was elected as a staff member of the Los Angeles Chapter of SNCC. DAVIS dropped out of SNCC and joined the Communist Party. DAVIS is a member of the Chc Lumumba Club, Horace Smith Section, SCDCP. DAVIS' activities in connection with the SCDCP are set out. Description data set forth.
