# ACCOMPLISHMENTS
## NEW LEFT SECTION

SECTION CHIEF - R. L. SHACKELFORD Attached are comments from each unit setting out the most noteworthy accomplishments of the New Left Section since the last Inspection. This Section is responsible for the supervision of investigations of the New Left movement, including terrorist groups and individuals engaged in bombings, arson and assassinations, and other New Left groups and individuals both on college campuses and off campus. Investigations concerning New Left publications are also supervised in this Section, as are sabotage investigations, and Protest and Trotskyist groups.
