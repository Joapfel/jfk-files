This is to consider recommendation of the New York Office that the Sam Survey Program be reinstituted.

BACKGROUND:

Sam Survey was a successful program operated between 1959 and 1966, which involved screening of air mail destined for selected European countries and cities by our Agent personnel in an effort to detect (Soviet) illegal agent mail. RECOMMENDATION OF NEW YORK:

New York points out that although ample evidence indicate the presence of a substantial number of Soviet illegals, the Bureau has been unsuccessful in locating any in recent years; that various programs and projects put into operation have been unsuccessful. Further, it is its contention that the illegal's most vulnerable area is still his letter writing and that this is the area we have the best chance by far to locate and identify illegals. Therefore, New York wishes to submit for the Bureau's consideration the reinstitution of Sam Survey. If this is not possible, it recommends that the Bureau consider requesting CIA for postal authorities to take over this program.
