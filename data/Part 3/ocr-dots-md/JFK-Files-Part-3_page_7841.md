RE: INTERVIEW OF FORMER FBI AD BY SSC

Conrad was asked what would be done in the Laboratory if someone was asked to make an examination and the examiner thought it was improper or had other questions about the matter. Specifically, Conrad was asked if there was anyone that the examiner could go to in such an event. Conrad responded that there, of course, was a recourse for the examiner. He could take the matter up with his Section Chief, or with his AD, or, if he chose to, he could address the matter to Director Hoover. Conrad was asked whether he had been consulted by a Laboratory employee as to the propriety of making copies of King tapes. He responded that he absolutely was not so consulted.

Conrad was then asked whether a John Matter was an employee of the Laboratory when Conrad was there. Response was "Yes." Conrad was asked what Matter's expertise was and he responded that it was basically telephone security. When asked whether that included wire tapping, that is, whether Matter was qualified to perform wire tapping, the answer was "Yes." A similar answer was given to the question as to whether Matter was qualified to listen to tape recordings and attempt to filter them.

**NOTE:**

Briefing of Conrad, prior to interview, was conducted by AD Mintz, Legal Counsel Division; and S. F. Phillips of INTD, who accompanied Conrad to the SSC offices. It was not necessary for Conrad to consult with Phillips at any time during the interview. Debriefing of Conrad as reported herein was by Phillips.
