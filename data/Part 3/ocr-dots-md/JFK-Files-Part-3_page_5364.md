IN ABEYANCE ANY RECORDS DESTRUCTION PROGRAM PREVIOUSLY
APPROVED BY STATUTE OR REGULATIONS. FURTHER INSTRUCTIONS
IN THIS MATTER WILL BE ISSUED UPON RECEIPT.
