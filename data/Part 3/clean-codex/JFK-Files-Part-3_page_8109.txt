Memorandum

To: Mr. W. R. Wannall

From: W. O. Cregar

Subject: SENSTUDY - 75

Date: 5/21/75

On 5/15/75, Senate Select Committee Staff Members Mark Gitenstein and Thomas Dawson reviewed Manual of Instructions Sections 107, Part I, relating to security informants and Section 130 relating to extremist informants. The review took place from 3:50 p.m. to 5:40 p.m. in the conference room at INTD. SA Elmer W. Larson of SENSTUDY - 75 project was present during the review. Gitenstein and Dawson asked several questions concerning administrative and technical clarifications but raised no substantive questions concerning the sections under review.

Action: None. For record purposes.
