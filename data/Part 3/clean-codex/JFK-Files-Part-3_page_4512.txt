Urgent, 8 August 1961, 4:25 PM.

To: Director, FBI (FEI) 92-2914–92-2918 and SAC, Miami 92-155.  
From: SAC, Pittsburgh 92-236–92-228.

Gabriel (Garbiel) Mannarino, also known as AR, Samuel Mannarino, also known as AR, PG 615-C, advised that on the evening of August 5 last, Victor Stanziano and Willie Sams, both of New Kensington, Pa., agreed that Sams would go to Greater Pittsburgh Airport that evening to pick up Sam Mannarino, who was arriving from Miami, Florida, accompanied by an unidentified man. Stanziano stated that the Mannarinos are controlled in some respects by “some big guys upstairs.” For example, he pointed out that in 1960, the Mannarinos became angry at a New Kensington contractor named Martin Murin, who had fronted some of their legitimate business holdings and who broke away from them after a dispute.
