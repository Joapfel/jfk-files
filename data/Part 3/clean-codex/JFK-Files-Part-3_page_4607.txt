United States Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Investigation

Report of: Herbert L. Blom  
Date: April 25, 1961

Field Office File No.: 105-9440  
Title: Evelyn Eleanore Archer  
Office: Newark, New Jersey  
Bureau File No.: 105-95507  
Character: IS—Cuba

Synopsis:

In January 1961, subject was praising Castro and, in conversation with NK T-1, asked if he was interested in making money and, if so, she would have him meet a friend from either Cuba or Miami. NK T-1, in April 1961, advised subject had made no further mention concerning this matter.

Previous investigation shows subject has been associated with Cubans involved in Cuban affairs. Probation records show subject and others were arrested during February 1958, in Texas, charged with conspiracy and unlawful possession of firearms. On July 11, 1958, subject was sentenced in U.S.D.C., Houston, Texas, to two years and fined $500.00, but was placed on 22 months’ probation. Background information and association with Manuel Arques set forth. The March 26, 1961, issue of the Asbury Park Sunday Press contains an interview by a reporter showing subject’s present attitude concerning Cuba.

Details:

On January 24, 1961, NK T-1 advised that several months previously, while talking to the subject at a bar, she was praising Fidel Castro and what he was doing for Cuba. Later in the conversation, she asked the informant how he felt about making a “buck” or if he cared how he made it. NK T-1, in going along with the conversation, stated to her that he was interested in making money, to which she replied that she was expecting a friend from either Cuba or Miami, not recalled by NK T-1, and that when he arrived she would have them get together.
