Dear Mr. Elder:

Further to our conversation regarding formal requests for documents and records, I attach a list of supplemental document requests.

What I would propose is that you and I discuss this list to insure that we have properly identified the requested documents.

In addition to the attached list I would like to review studies in the historical series: (1) “Maritime Operations in Indonesian Operations—1957-1958;” “Overthrow of a Government, November 1952-1953;” and any studies prepared that trace the CIA's activities in relation to the overthrow of the government of Guatemala in 1954.
