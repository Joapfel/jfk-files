AT-T-4, 5/68

In early May, 1968, Stanley Levision complained to William A. Rutherford it was imperative that Abernathy operate SCLC with a firm hand and not spend all his time making public appearances. Levision cautioned that if Abernathy did not do so, he would find Harry Delafonte and himself reluctant to assist him and SCLC.

AT-T-4, 5/68

On November 17, 1964, Dora E. McDonald, Secretary to the President, SCLC, made necessary travel arrangements for Rev. King and other persons to travel to Oslo, Norway, in connection with the presentation of the Nobel Prize to King. According to the arrangements made by McDonald, Abernathy was to travel in a group of persons which included Bayard Rustin, Lawrence Reddick, Harry Wachtel, and Leonora Wachtel.
