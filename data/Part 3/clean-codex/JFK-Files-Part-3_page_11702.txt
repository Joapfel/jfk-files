American Repatriate Program

The American Repatriate Program, which was instituted in February 1962 to specify the procedure to be followed upon receipt of information that U.S. citizens residing in Cuba had been issued Swiss Protection Passports by the Swiss Embassy in Havana, Cuba, for travel to the U.S., was discontinued by memorandum from W. R. Wannall to Mr. C. D. Brennan, dated March 25, 1971.

Domestic Intelligence Division Inspection. January 18, 1971.
