President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board

Agenda for Meeting of November 9, 1962

1. 9:00–9:30 — The Chairman's Time — Executive Session.

2. 9:30–10:15 — Preliminary Review by Board Staff of Recent Cuban Developments. Mr. Patrick Coyne.

3. 10:15–10:30 — Recent Discussions of Cuban Developments with the Defense Intelligence Agency and the National Reconnaissance Office. Mr. Gordon Gray.

4. 10:30–11:00 — Recent Photographic Intelligence Acquisitions. Mr. Arthur Lundahl, NPIC.

5. 11:00–12:00; 11:30–12:30 — Defense Intelligence Agency Activities Including DIA's Role in the Cuban Situation. Lt. Gen. Joseph Carroll, USAF, Director, DIA.

6. 12:00–12:30 — State Department Views on Recent Intelligence Developments Relating to Cuba. Mr. Thomas Hughes, Acting Director, Intelligence and Research, Department of State.

12:30–1:05; 1:05–1:30 — Lunch, Room No. 21, Executive Office Building.

7. 1:30–1:45 — Status Report on CIA's Covert Operations. Mr. Robert Murphy.

9. 1:45–2:15 — Review of the Recent Series of Soviet Nuclear Tests. Not taken up. D/C = 11/100. D/A = 11/30. Dr. Albert Wheelon, Office of Scientific Intelligence, CIA. Dr. Donald Chamberlain, Office of Scientific Intelligence, CIA.

10. 2:15–2:30 — Executive Session.

DCI = 11:00. D/A = 11:30.
