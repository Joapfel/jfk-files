Counterintelligence Programs

In July 1968, the Pittsburgh Office confidentially alerted a staff worker for the Mellon Foundation of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, that Unity Incorporated, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, had a store that was utilized as a Black Power center, and one of the leaders of Unity Incorporated said the basement of the store would be remodeled as a target range. The leader of the group was in possession of a suitcase containing several pistols and ammunition. The purpose of this contact was to prevent Unity Incorporated from receiving a grant of $150,000 that the organization had requested from the Mellon Foundation.

Unity Incorporated did not get the $150,000 grant.
