Washington Field Division. Miscellaneous Information.

First communication dated 6/5/58. Last communication dated 10/29/65. Approximate pages: 67. Derogatory information: Yes. Category: Congressional—information. Originals and copies.

Contains almost entirely letters to Hoover from SACs in the Washington Field Office containing general immoral or criminal activities on the part of diplomats, government employees, politicians, sport figures, socially prominent persons, Senators, and Congressmen.
