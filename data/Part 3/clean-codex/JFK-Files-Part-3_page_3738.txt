Date 10/30/68 by Spalny.

Sanitation workers strike, Memphis, Tenn., racial matter.

On April 2, 1968, Lt. E. H. Arkin, Memphis Police Department, Inspectional Bureau, advised that the funeral of Larry Payne, a seventeen-year-old Negro who was shot by a Memphis Police Department officer after store looting growing out of the March 28 racial disturbance in Memphis, was held without incident at Clayborn Temple, Memphis. About five hundred attended, despite the fact that the church can seat twenty-five hundred. The body was interred immediately after the funeral. Arkin advised that another sanitation workers sympathy march was scheduled for downtown Memphis on the afternoon of April 2.

Not recorded:

Eastern Airlines advised on April 2 that Rev. Martin Luther King, Jr., and Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) aides Ralph D. Abernathy, Bernard Lee, and Andrew M. Young were due to arrive in Memphis at 10:15 a.m. on April 3 from Atlanta.

April 5, 1968.

Included: letter to White House and Attorney General, dated 4/26/68, and express approval of the FBI, dated 4/26/68.
