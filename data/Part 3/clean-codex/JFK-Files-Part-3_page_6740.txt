Further information by SP-2 ALM 1176. Date: 10/13/04.

Robert Karvin Shelton is a full-time paid employee of the United Klans of America, Incorporated (UKA), Knights of the Ku Klux Klan, with the title Imperial Wizard. He is probably the most influential and well-known Klansman in the United States. We are encouraging, as a counterintelligence technique, the development of information discrediting Shelton or embarrassing him to the UKA.

Through analysis of the income tax returns, we may be able to determine the secret source of Shelton's income or depository used by him for the Klan organization. There is a possibility that he is misappropriating funds. We also have information that Shelton has been on the payroll of a Kloblo, Alabama, engineering firm as a public relations man.

In the event we determine such information, we will consider exposing Shelton within the Klan organization, publicly or by furnishing information to the Internal Revenue Service.

Recommendation:

It is recommended that the Lindon Section obtain from the Internal Revenue Service two uncertified copies each of the federal tax returns filed by Shelton and the USA for the past five years. Attached is a memorandum for the use of the Lindon Section.
