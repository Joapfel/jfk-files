SENSTUDY - 7-. TELETYP.

Remytelcal, July 15, 1975.

On July 15, 1975, Sam Papich, Director, New Mexico Governors Commission on Organized Crime (former Special Agent, FBI), advised he had been telephonically contacted on this date by Dave Martin, Associated Press, Washington, D.C., who was inquiring concerning Papich's knowledge of events which led to statements made by Director Kelley on July 14, 1975. Specifically, Martin wanted Papich to identify those agents who participated in burglaries of foreign embassies and also wanted the identities of the embassies.
