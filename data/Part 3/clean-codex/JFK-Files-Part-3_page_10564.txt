March 25, 1975.

NR002 SV Code. 715 PM NITEL 3/25/75 PNR. To Director. From Savannah.

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

REBUNITEL all SACs March 24, 1975.

In response to RENITEL, the following breakdown in personnel assigned to internal security and counterintelligence matters is being set forth:

Internal Security: 1 SAC, 0; 1 ASAC, 0; 1 supervisor, 10 percent; 1 agent, 0; 1 agent, 10 percent; 1 agent, 0; 1 agent, 30 percent.

Counterintelligence: 1 SAC, 0; 1 ASAC, 0; 1 supervisor, 40 percent; 1 agent, 90 percent; 1 agent, 50 percent; 1 agent, 10 percent; 1 agent, 0.

All other agents' accumulative total of time would equal 10 percent for internal security and 0 for counterintelligence for one agent.

End.
