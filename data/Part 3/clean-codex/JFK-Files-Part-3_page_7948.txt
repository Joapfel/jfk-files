First communication dated: 1/25/63. Last communication dated: 1/26/63. Approximate pages: 5. Derogatory information: Yes. Category: Congressional. Originals only.

Contains letter dated 1/25/63 from Coded to Hoover advising that, prior to being robbed on 1/25/63, Coded had picked up a Negro female at a low-class night spot and tried to take her to a tourist home. Coded and the female were followed by two Negro males who assaulted him.

Contains newspaper clippings about robbery.
