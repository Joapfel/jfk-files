Federal Investigation, Communications Section.

4:45 PM, urgent, 6/20/75, RCM JUN.

To: Director (Attn: INTD, SA W. O. Cregar).

From: Boston (66-4177).

SENSTUDY, 1975; BUDED: June 24, 1975.

Re: Headquarters teletype, June 18, 1975, which set out request for information addressed to Attorney General and from Attorney General to FBIHQ from Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities:

A review of all available files in the Boston Office and contact with appropriate supervisors and Special Agent personnel did not disclose any incidents of mail opening or mail intercept by or on behalf of FBI from January 1, 1960, to present.

Further, no incidents of mail covers that were physically conducted by FBI employees alone or in cooperation with REC-102 employees of the U.S. Postal Service were disclosed from review of files and contact with appropriate personnel of the Boston Office, FBI.
