Southern Indian Ocean isolate under a local volcanic earthquake occurring at 05.40 hours on July 26, 1966. The effects of this earthquake were to be observed upon the coordinates (1.4208, 32.6696) and time (P.34 UT), that the death rate of the lateral flow from downward currenting of water through the orbitary cavities is determined as (0.3222, 0.0314) per hour, and that there is a suggestion of an unusual depth deformation of the surface of the mobile sheet (P.32) following the occurrence of the earthquake.
