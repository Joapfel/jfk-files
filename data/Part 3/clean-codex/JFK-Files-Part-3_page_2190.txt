Communications Section.

January 23, 1976. Teletype. No. 008 SA code. 4:40 p.m. Nitel, January 23, 1976.

To Director. From FBI San Antonio.

Attention: INTD, Mr. W. C. Cregar. Senior, 1975.

Telecall of Mr. Seymour Phillips to SAC, San Antonio, January 22, 1976, concerning former Special Agent Edwin Dalrymple (Dalrympale).

On January 22, 1976, I notified Mr. Edwin Dalrymple (Dalrympale), 4211 Prickley Peak Drive, Austin, Texas 78751, telephone 212-340-1479, that a representative of the Senate Committee on Intelligence had requested the FBI identify a red-headed agent assigned to the Houston FBI office around 1963 or 1964. The Bureau has advised two agents assigned to that office during this period with red hair, one being former Special Agent Edwin Dalrymple (Dalrympale).

Mr. Dalrymple (Dalrympale) was advised of the inquiry by this representative of the Senate Committee on Intelligence, and the FBI was cooperating with the committee. He was retired 4-6-72.

April 1972. Copy retained in Personnel Records Unit.
