United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. Cochran  
From: T. F. Kelleher  
Subject: SENSTUDY 75  
Date: 7/28/75

The purpose of this memorandum is to provide an assessment of the accuracy and possible damage that might be suffered by the Bureau by the release of information to the Senate Select Committee on CIA as set forth in Cregar to Wannall memorandum dated 7/24/75 captioned as above.

Attached are separate commentaries relating to items appearing on corresponding page numbers in the CIA document.

It is to be noted that no written record or personal recollection could be located concerning item 7, page 00118 relating to the purchase of cameras.

Attached are nine pages containing the results of the requested assessment. Copies of these assessments have also been made for each designated copy of this memorandum.

Action: For information.

Enclosures.

REC-102.

Copies: 1—Mr. Cochran; 1—Mr. Wannall; 1—Mr. McNiff.
