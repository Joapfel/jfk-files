2. Angleton's "I am not privy to who struck John."

Angleton.

The whole quotation makes it clear that he was being asked about C14 activities occurring during the period of anti-war tramplings in this country, and that he was responding to that question.

The slightly reasonable meaning of his answer was: "There were a lot of things going on in the Agency during that period of anti-war tramplings, and I wasn't aware of everything that was going on."

The period of anti-war tramplings didn't begin until 1967 or 1968, some five years or more after Pres. K's death.

The line "I am not privy to who struck John" is drawn from the Bible, and its literary meaning is "I don't know."
