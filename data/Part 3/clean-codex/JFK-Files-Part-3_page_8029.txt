First communication dated 3/2/42 (only communication). Approximate pages: 2. Derogatory information: Yes. Category: Prominent persons. Originals only.

Folder contains memo of Mr. Hoover regarding duty status with the U.S. Navy. Report that receives $5,000.00 per week for his weekly broadcast and he is receiving this money while on duty status with U.S. Navy. Hoover recommends that see President in order to get his duty status cleared up. Hoover obviously feels services valuable to country and wanted this problem resolved.
