January 13, 1976

United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC)

This document is not for distribution in response to your request and is not for dissemination outside of the intended use limited to official proceedings by your Committee and its staff. It may not be disclosed to unauthorized personnel without the express approval of the FBI.

This memorandum effects delivery of material in response to the December 23, 1975, request. Excepted from material being delivered is that furnished to the FBI by the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), the Department of State Office of Security, and certain foreign intelligence agencies. Information from CIA and the Department of State Office of Security has not been included under the third agency rule and a request for this information should be directed to them separately.

References to Mr. Jaffe contained in other FBI files of which he is not the subject are not included in the above material, in accordance with the procedure agreed to by the House Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (HSC) on October 22, 1975, when it requested similar material on Mr. Jaffe. On December 31, 1975, Mr. Robert Kolley, Staff Member, SSC, advised a representative of the FBI that receipt of copies of only that material furnished to the HSC would satisfy the SSC's request.
