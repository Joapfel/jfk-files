In Reply, Please Refer to File No.

Federal Bureau of Investigations, Washington, D.C. 20525

October 17, 1968

Georgiy I. Isachenko

Internal Security-3

All information contained herein is unclassified except where shown otherwise.

A confidential source, who has furnished reliable information in the past, advised on October 15, 1968, that Georgiy I. Isachenko had been informed that “Ramparts” had not received the list of names and addresses from the Soviets in return for the list that he had furnished to the Soviets. Isachenko was to check into this situation in an attempt to clear up this misunderstanding. The source was of the opinion that the list referred to pertained to mailing lists maintained by “Ramparts” and the Soviet Information Department, Washington, D.C.

The current “Diplomatic List,” published by the United States Department of State, reflects that Georgiy I. Isachenko is the Information Counselor of the Embassy of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics, Washington, D.C.
