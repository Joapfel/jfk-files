NRO01 AQ CODE

10:55 AM, urgent, 3-36-75.

To: Director. From: Albuquerque. Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities. Rebutel, March 24, 1975.

Albuquerque has one supervisor who spends 25 percent of his time in the supervision of internal security matters and three agents who work these matters on a part-time basis, with 20 percent, 40 percent, and 40 percent of their time devoted to these matters.

One of the above agents spends 10 percent of his time on counterintelligence matters.

There are 15 resident agents, each of whom works approximately five percent of their time on internal security matters.

End.
