MM 92-1870

In December 1957, MM T-13 advised that NORMAN ROTHMAN controlled all of the slot machines in Cuba, with the exception of those in Havana.

The Brockville Trust and Savings Company, located at Brockville, Ontario, Canada, was burglarized between 7:30 PM on May 3, 1958, and 8:30 AM on May 4, 1958. Taken in the theft were bearer bonds, registered bonds, jewelry, and cash. A Federal Grand Jury at Chicago returned a suppressed indictment against ROTHMAN on June 30, 1959, charging him with aiding and abetting on one count of Title 18, Section 2315, U.S. Code, and all counts of Title 18, Section 1343, U.S. Code. A warrant was issued by the U.S. District Judge, Chicago, and bond was set at $5,000. ROTHMAN was apprehended by Bureau Agents, Miami, Florida, on July 2, 1959, and released on $5,000 bond. He was not convicted on these charges.

On May 14, 1958, MM T-14 advised that PATSY ERRA had an interest in the Sans Souci Nightclub along with ROTHMAN and SAM MANNARINO.

On September 2, 1959, MM T-15 advised that NORMAN ROTHMAN appeared to be the individual who arranged all illegal deals at the Biltmore Terrace Hotel, Miami Beach, Florida, and that this hotel was headquarters for narcotics and call-girl operations.

On October 26, 1959, MM T-16 advised that ROTHMAN appeared to be working with PASQUALE MICHAEL ERRATA and possibly with SAM and GABRIEL MANNARINO.

In 1960, the subject, along with others, was convicted of attempting to smuggle guns stolen from a National Guard armory in Ohio to Cuba and received a five-year prison term and a $10,000 fine.
