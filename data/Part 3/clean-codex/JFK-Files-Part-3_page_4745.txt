Re Los Angeles letters to New York dated 8/7 and 30/63.

The referenced communications requested the Richmond and various other Divisions to ascertain the identity of subscribers to numerous telephone numbers which have been called from a telephone of Robert A. Maheu and Associates, 10889 Wilshire Boulevard, Los Angeles, California.

The results of the investigation by Richmond are being forwarded to the Bureau because two former Special Agents of the FBI are among the subscribers to telephones called from this Los Angeles office.

The following investigation was conducted by SA Howard E. Richardson.
