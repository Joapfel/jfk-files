Memorandum for the Attorney General

Re: Progressive Labor Movement  
Internal Security – C

Marxist-Leninist Group Affiliate

The Progressive Labor Movement is an extreme left grouping of communists who have split from the Communist Party, USA in criticism of the Party's revisionistic interpretation of Marxism-Leninism. The Progressive Labor Movement supports the Red Chinese position against that of the Soviet Union and is an extremely militant organization. This organization is acting, by means of agitation and propaganda, to continue racial demonstrations in New York City. This activity is being directed from its Harlem headquarters at 336 Lenox Avenue, New York, New York. Technical coverage of this headquarters will materially assist in the investigation of the Progressive Labor Movement's part in the New York City racial situation and will be of value in determining impending trouble areas in time for preventive measures to be taken.

I recommend that authority be granted to install a technical surveillance at the Harlem Progressive Labor Movement headquarters, 336 Lenox Avenue, New York, New York, or at any address to which this organization may move in the future.

Approved: ony leB Knykel
