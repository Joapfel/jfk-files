Memorandum for Mr. Wannall

Subject: Select Committee of the Senate

Action:

If approved, Bureau Supervisor Fred B. Griffith, Document Classification and Security Coordinator, be designated as the Bureau representative to meet with the Chairman of the USIB Committee and representatives of NSA and CIA.

Arrangements with USIB, NSA, and CIA are appropriate as recommended here; however, contacts with the Senate Committee and staff should be made by liaison established with Congress. Cooperation of Intelligence Division and Legal Counsel would serve best. JAM.
