M. A. Jones to Mr. Bishop Memo

Subject: Suggested Name Change, Crime Records Division

More accurately describes our responsibilities, it is felt, than any other title suggested. It is noted the more likely alternatives discussed were “Research and Information Division” and “Research and Correspondence Division.”

Recommendations:

1. That Mr. Gray approve the renaming of the Crime Records Division to Crime Research Division.

2. That in line with the foregoing, the attached letter to Mr. Thompson be approved and sent.
