Levison recommended Hunter Pitts O'Dell (U'Dell), who had been lent to the National Committee on the CPUSA in December 1963 under the pseudonym Cornelia Junck. He accepted Levison's suggestion and employed O'Dell (U'Dell) in the organization, the Southern Christian Fellowship Conference (S.C.F.C.). In 1963, Levison identified O'Dell (U'Dell) as a Christian (基督) fundamentalist of the type of T. P. Palmyra, Florida, with whom he was associated with King, to whom he sent a message from the pulpit.
