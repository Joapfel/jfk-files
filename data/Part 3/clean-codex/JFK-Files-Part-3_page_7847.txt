Seventh Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC)

Re: Interview of FBI SA by SSC

Q: At WFO?

A: I am a recruiter for the FBI.

Q: How long have you been so engaged?

A: About 3½ years.

Q: Were you on Mr. Belter's squad at the time you monitored Dr. King?

A: Yes.

Q: Where did that occur?

A: At the Willard Hotel.

Q: Have you done other monitoring before?

A: Yes.

Q: Is this the only monitoring work you have done outside of the Washington Field Office?

A: Yes.

Q: Do you recall when this occurred?

A: I seem to think it was shortly before President Kennedy's assassination in 1963.
