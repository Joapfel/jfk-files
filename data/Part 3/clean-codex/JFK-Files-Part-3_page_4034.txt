Drive to force all grocers in Negro area to cease selling Hart's Bread, Wonder Bread, Coca-Cola, and Sealtest dairy products, as these firms do not hire a sufficient number of Negroes.

On April 2, Lt. Arkin advised that Memphis city officials are considering seeking a court injunction prohibiting any mass march led by King, or an injunction specifying conditions under which a march could take place. They are also considering resumption of a curfew and placing strike-support leaders under a peace and financial responsibility bond.

Arkin advised that the remainder of the Tennessee National Guard, with the exception of one battery consisting of 293 officers and men of the Memphis guard unit, have left Memphis.

At 3:05 p.m., 60 sanitation workers and sympathizers departed Clayborn Temple carrying placards, following their usual parade march to Beale Street, north on Main to City Hall. No incidents reported.
