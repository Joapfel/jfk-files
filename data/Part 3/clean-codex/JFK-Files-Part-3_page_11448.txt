By letter dated February 11, 1976, the Senate Select Committee (SSC) requested that arrangements be made for SSC staff members to interview FBI personnel who authored certain Counterintelligence Program (COINTELPRO) documents relating to alleged FBI involvement in “gang war” between the Black Panther Party (BPP) and the US Organization.

These persons are:

Butte Division—Special Agent (SA) Roy L. Burns, assigned to Billings, Montana, Resident Agency (documents authored while assigned San Diego).

Minneapolis Division—Assistant Special Agent in Charge (ASAC) William D. Neumann (documents authored while assigned FBI Headquarters (FBIHQ)).
