SENSTUDY 75. Information concerning.

Re Omaha telcalls to Bureau and Minneapolis, June 19, 1975.

In the June 19, 1975 afternoon issue of the Omaha World-Herald, a daily newspaper in Omaha, Nebraska, appeared the following article captioned “Senate Committee Sending Investigator to Gordon, Nebraska.” The article appeared under a World-Herald Washington bureau dateline.

Contact with Omaha World-Herald authorities at Omaha failed to develop the nature of the Senate Committee's investigation at Gordon, Nebraska.
