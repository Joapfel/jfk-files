Item I.

Q: What about Julian (JUNAN) Bond's campaign? Any support to influence state?

A: No. As I recall, Bond was already a member of the Georgia legislature by July 1964 or 1965.

Q: What election was conducted by Bond?

A: None to my knowledge beyond such courage of SNC as may have resulted from courage of SNC with which Bond was affiliated.

Q: In what manner a question was asked of other members of the Georgia legislature. My answer was state—yes, only concern is Hall-Hain Governor.
