Federal Bureau of Investigation. U.S. Department of Justice (Jurisprudence). Communications Section.

November 16, 1962.

Teletype. Urgent, 11/15/62, 9:38 PM.

To Director, FBI 92-3267.

From SAC, Los Angeles 92-113C 1P.

John Roselli, AKA AR.

Neighborhood sources could furnish no information that Roselli was at the Los Angeles apartment on November fifteen instant, and the car of Herman Spitzel, which Roselli has been using, was not parked at the apartment. Roselli may have gone to Santa Barbara for a physical check-up, as he stated to LA 4268-C he planned to do this week.

LA 4268-C did not see Roselli at Friar's Club, Beverly Hills, on the afternoon or evening of November fourteen last.
