Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Communications Section

NITEL, 25 March 1975, sent 9:25 a.m., 26 March 1975.

26 March 1975

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, New York  
Attention: Budget and Accounting Section

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re: Bureau teletype, 24 March 1975.

New York has one SAC assigned to counterintelligence matters (Division III) and one SAC assigned to internal security matters (Division IV). There are no ASACs in the New York Office.

SAC, Counterintelligence Division (III) spends full-time on counterintelligence matters.

SAC, Internal Security Division (IV) spends 40 percent of his time on counterintelligence matters, and 60 percent is spent on internal security matters.

Six supervisors handle internal security matters, and 14 supervisors handle counterintelligence activities.

114 agents are assigned to internal security matters, and 331 agents are assigned to counterintelligence matters.

End.
