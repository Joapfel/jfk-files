8:25 PM NITEL 3/25/75 RJB.

To: Director.  
From: Cleveland (66-5641).

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re: Bureau teletype to all SACs, March 24, 1975.

Cleveland has the following number of agents assigned to internal security work: SAC none, ASAC none, supervisors two, agents 20. The following numbers are assigned to counterintelligence work: SAC none, ASAC none, supervisors one, agents 16. The three supervisors handle security-type matters exclusively.

Cleveland has utilized percentages of agents' time spent in these categories where applicable, especially in the resident agencies.

Classified by 6020, XGDS category 2, indefinite.

End.

March 25, 1975. Teletype.
