KHRUSHCHEV VISIT AND SOVIET INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES IN THE U.S.

First communication dated 12/15/59 (original—copy only). Approximate pages: 12. Derogatory information: Yes. Category: Prominent persons. Copies: Both.

General content: Memo deals with briefing of President by Mr. Hoover and AG regarding Soviet intelligence activities in the U.S. Briefing to prepare President for U.S. visit by Khrushchev.

Memo is accompanied by three loose-leaf folders giving examples of intelligence activities in the U.S. by Soviets and physical surveillance photographs.
