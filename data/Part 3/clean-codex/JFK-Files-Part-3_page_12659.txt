Director, FBI

U.S. State Select Committee with respect to Intelligence Activities.

Enclosed is the original of a memorandum concerning testimony subsequently given by Mr. Sentinella before an executive session of the Committee. A copy of the memorandum is also enclosed for forwarding to Mr. James A. Wilderotter, Associate Counsel to the President.
