Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Communications Section

March 25, 1975

Teletype

NR 004 OM Code

4:38 p.m. NITEL 3/25/75 BJP

To: Director

From: Omaha

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities

Re: Bureau teletype to all offices, March 24, 1975.

Omaha currently has seven agents assigned full time to internal security matters, with two agents spending 60 percent and two agents spending 25 percent of their time on IS matters. One supervisor spends approximately 50 percent of his time on IS matters.

Two agents and one supervisor spend approximately 10 percent of their time on counterintelligence matters.

End

MSE FBIHQ
