Caption on folder or file: White House Employees.

First communication dated 4/17/57. Last communication dated 5/10/57. Approximate pages: 14. Derogatory information: Yes. Category: Investigative. Ticklers only: No. Originals only: No. Both: Yes.

General content of file or folder: Folder captioned “White House Employees.” Folder contains memoranda and report regarding an FBI investigation of two employees at the White House who are homosexuals. This investigation was referred to the FBI by the White House in view of the possibility of a breach of security concerning these individuals.
