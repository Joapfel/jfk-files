Title: Norman Rothman  
Character: Anti-Racketeering

Synopsis:

Subject continues to reside in Surfside, Florida. Reportedly now employed organizing junket flights to New Frontier Hotel and Casino, Las Vegas, Nevada. Also still owns Albion Lounge, Miami Beach, Florida. Subject continues to associate with Patty Erra and reportedly with Santo Trafficante.

Details:

Residence

On December 14, 1967, it was determined that Rothman continues to reside at 425 Surfside Boulevard, Surfside, Florida.

Hangouts

On June 6, 1967, MM T-1 advised that Rothman occasionally visits the Fontainebleau Hotel and is a good friend of Max Eder. He occasionally cashes checks at the Fontainebleau Hotel.

Employment

On August 24, 1967, MM T-2 advised that Rothman was no longer connected with the International Lithographers Company.
