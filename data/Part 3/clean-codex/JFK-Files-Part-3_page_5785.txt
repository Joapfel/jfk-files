Date: July 25, 1968

To: Office of Security, Department of State

From: John Ligerar Hoover (J. Edgar Hoover), Director

Subject: MR. NAR HUDD A. JOLLY MARY A. C.

Documents of the Passport Office, Department of State, indicate that Park Home was issued passport number 962053 on April 18, 1968, removed April 3, 1969. In his application for removal of the passport, People indicated that he intended to depart from the United States in late July 1969. If he intended to visit England, Holland, France, the Soviet Union, Czechoslovakia, and Japan, he would indicate that this was a holiday to the International Orient-East Congress to be held in Moscow, Russia, August 17–18, 1969.

Reports containing the results of a security investigation of Police have previously been forwarded to your office. Copies of these reports are being furnished further to the Central Intelligence Agency under separate cover.

The Legal Attachments in London, Paris, and New York have been requested to arrange for the receipt of any information concerning the subject's activities that may relate to the acquisition of the Security Services in the area covered by these Legal Attachments. I would appreciate the receipt of any information that may come to your attention regarding Policy's activities in the Soviet Union and in Czechoslovakia.

EX-105 REC-82

Attention: Deputy Director, Plans
