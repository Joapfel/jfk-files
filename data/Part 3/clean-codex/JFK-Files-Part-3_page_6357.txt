Robert Karvin Shelton is a full-time paid employee of the United Klans of America, Incorporated (UKA), Knights of the Ku Klux Klan, with the title Imperial Wizard. He is probably the most influential and well-known Manman in the United States. We are encouraging, as a counterintelligence technique, the development of information discrediting Shelton or embarrassing to the UKA.

Through analysis of the income tax returns, we may be able to determine secret sources of Shelton's income or depository used by him for the Klan organization. There is a possibility that he is misappropriating funds. We also have information Shelton has been on the payroll of a mob hit, Alabama, engineering firm as a public relations man.

In the event we determine such information, we will consider exposing Shelton within the Klan organization, publicly or by furnishing information to the Internal Revenue Service.

Recommendation

It is recommended the Lindon Section obtain from the Internal Revenue Service two uncertified copies each of the federal tax returns filed by Shelton and the USA for the past five years. Attached is a memorandum for the use of the Lindon Section.
