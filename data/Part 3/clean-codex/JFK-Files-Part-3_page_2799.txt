SENSTUDY 75, BUDED September 8, 1975

Re: Bureau teletype, September 5, 1975.

On September 8, 1975, Supervisor Joseph C. Ball and SA Baymoo'Kelly met with Walter J. Laugheed, Assistant Director, and Robert Wennerholm, Legal Counsel, Dade County, Florida Public Safety Department (DCPSD), at the DCPSD.

These two officials were advised of the request to the FBI by the Senate Select Committee (SSC) for access to all memoranda and other materials which relate to electronic surveillance of Dr. Martin Luther King by state and local agencies.

Information in Miami airtel May 23, 1966, captioned “Martin Luther King, Jr., Security Matter-C,” was orally furnished, and they were asked whether the DCPSD has any objection to the FBI releasing to SSC the material emanating from DCPSD and contained in this airtel, which would disclose that the DCPSD used electronic surveillance in their coverage of King.
