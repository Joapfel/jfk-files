Mr. Connor: Mr. Secretary, would you comment on the obedience of a Cabinet official or highly placed Federal official who was ordered by the President to do something that violates the law clearly or is unconstitutional?

Mr. Rusk: No, no. I was once instructed by President Kennedy to make an expenditure which my lawyers told me was unlawful, that the money was not for that kind of purpose, and I spoke to the Attorney General about it and said, what do I do. And I think his reply was, well, if you go to prison, your salary will continue while you are in prison.

Well, that was not very helpful. But anyhow, I refused to make the expenditure and told the President why, and he did not pursue it.

No, I think that there can come a time when if a President persisted then the Cabinet officer would have to resign, if that is what the President wanted to do.
