U.S. Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (OCG).

Re: Review of killing FBI Special Agent (SA) by SSC staff member.

Epstein told Whitson (Whitcon) that in the late summer of 1964, former FBI Assistant to the Director William C. Sullivan had telephoned Whitson at his home one Saturday and told him that late FBI Director Hoover wanted Whitson to deliver a package to Miami, Florida, by plane and, on arrival at the Miami airport, to telephone Sullivan for further instructions. Whitson did as directed and, upon calling Sullivan, was instructed to address the package to Mr. Martin Luther King. Accordingly, Whitson had the package weighed, put postage stamps on it, and addressed it to Martin Luther King (not Mrs. Martin Luther King, as Epstein had indicated). The following day, Sunday, Whitson flew back to Washington, and on the next day, Monday, he informed Sullivan that his instructions had been carried out. Sullivan commented to Whitson, “Someday I will tell you about that.”

Epstein asked Whitson who had paid for the stamps to mail the package, and Whitson said that he had probably done so utilizing per diem money. Epstein asked whether he had put a return address on the package, and Whitson replied in the negative. Epstein asked if Whitson had gone to the Miami FBI Office, whether any agent had met him at the Miami airport, and whether Whitson had discussed this matter with anyone. Whitson told Epstein that he had not gone to the Miami Office, that no agent had met him, and that he had discussed the matter only with Sullivan. At this point, Whitson asked Epstein how he got the story. Epstein merely laughed and did not reply to the question.

Epstein also asked Whitson whether he had ever written a memorandum regarding this matter, to which Whitson replied in the negative. Epstein asked if Whitson had ever seen a memorandum on this matter, and Whitson again replied in the negative.
