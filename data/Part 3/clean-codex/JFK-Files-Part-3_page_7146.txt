The FBI, of course, utilizes Form FD-291, “Employment Agreement,” which has been executed by all current employees and must be signed by all new personnel entering on duty, to cover the foregoing situation. While we do not have an exit secrecy agreement signed by the terminating employee, Form FD-193, “Report of Exit and Separation,” requires (under Paragraph D, Page 2) that the exiting employee be cautioned regarding divulging confidential information and reminded that such improper action may violate Department of Justice regulations as well as Federal statutes providing for substantial fines and lengthy imprisonment or both.
