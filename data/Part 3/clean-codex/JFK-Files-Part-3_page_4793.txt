United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation

Copy to: U. S. Attorney, Miami (Attention: Departmental Attorney William Earle).

Report of: Allan M. Trankley. Office: Miami, Florida. Date: 5/28/68.

Field Office File #: 92-1870. Bureau File #: 92-9844. Title: Norman Rothman.

Character: Anti-Racketeering.

Synopsis: Subject still residing Surfside, Florida. Continues to frequent Albion Lounge, Miami, Florida. Contacts of subject set out.

Details

Residence: Subject continues to reside at 425 Surfside Boulevard, Surfside, Florida.

Criminal Activities: On April 5, 1968, MM T-1 advised that Rothman is earning his money in volume and in a quick manner which would indicate it comes from narcotics.

Travel: On April 15, 1968, MM T-2 advised he received information that Norman Rothman had traveled to Las Vegas, Nevada.

On April 18, 1968, MM T-2 advised that Rothman had returned to Miami on April 15, 1968, and for some unknown reason, is avoiding contacts and telephone calls.
