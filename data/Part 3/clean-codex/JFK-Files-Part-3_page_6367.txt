e. SHELTON stated that he was authorized by the KLAN to draw $15,000.00 per year salary, but 1966 was the first year the KLAN was financially able to pay him the full $15,000.00. Prior to 1966, his salary was determined at the end of each year by aggregating his withdrawals from the KLAN, reduced by the amount of documentation for travel and other KLAN expenses he had turned in during the year.

f. SHELTON converted his carport into an office and moved the KLAN office from the Alton Building in Tuscaloosa into his space May 1, 1966. The KLAN pays SHELTON $35.00 per month rent and $15.00 per month on his utilities.

g. SHELTON maintains that four of the eight $500.00 checks he received from DIXIE ENGINEERING COMPANY bounced. The reason the four $500.00 items do not show on his bank statements is a debit to the account; the balances at the time the checks were returned were less than $500.00, and he picked up the checks with cash or with another check from DIXIE ENGINEERING.

h. KLAN checks were made payable to “Cash” because just anyone would not cash a KLAN check. When he left Tuscaloosa on KLAN business, he never knew where he would be able to cash a check, so he would make the check payable to cash and cash the check at whatever grocery store or service station where he was known. The amounts were charged to him on the KLAN checks, and he brought back gas tickets, mutual bills, invoices, etc., to credit them to the account.
