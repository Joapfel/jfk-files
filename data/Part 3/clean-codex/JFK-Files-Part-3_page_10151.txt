The Number of FBI Field Office Agents Assigned to Internal Security and Counterintelligence Matters

Based on a survey of all FBI field offices, it has been determined that matters relating to internal security and counterintelligence require the following commitment of manpower in FBI field offices, stated in terms of equivalent full-year employees.

Internal Security: Special Agents in Charge, 2.80; Assistant Special Agents in Charge, 1.75; Special Agent Supervisors, 47.06; Special Agents, 671.07; total equivalent full-year agent employees, 722.68.

Counterintelligence: Special Agents in Charge, 2.34; Assistant Special Agents in Charge, 2.16; Special Agent Supervisors, 46.18; Special Agents, 904.29; total equivalent full-year agent employees, 954.97.

These equivalent full-year employees represent the following percentages of total field office agent personnel assigned as of 2/28/75.

Internal Security: Special Agents in Charge, 4.2%; Assistant Special Agents in Charge, 2.9%; Special Agent Supervisors, 11.9%; Special Agents, 9.5%; percent of total agent personnel, 9.5%.

Counterintelligence: Special Agents in Charge, 3.5%; Assistant Special Agents in Charge, 3.6%; Special Agent Supervisors, 11.7%; Special Agents, 12.8%; percent of total agent personnel, 12.6%.
