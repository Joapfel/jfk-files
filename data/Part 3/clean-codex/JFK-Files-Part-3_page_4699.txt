U. S. Department of Justice, Communications Section.

October 30, 1962. Teletype. Urgent, October 29, 1962, 9:18 p.m.

To Director, FBI, 92-3267.

From SAC, Los Angeles, 92-113C 1P.

John Roselli, aka AR.

Neighborhood sources advise Roselli residing in a Los Angeles apartment on the morning of October 29. A late-model Chevrolet bearing California license XZX 880 was parked in Roselli’s stall at the apartment garage.

Hertz Corporation advised that their car with license XZX 880 was rented at 2:13 a.m. on October 29 by John Roselli from the Hertz station at the Beverly Hilton Hotel, Beverly Hills, California. Rental was made by telephone, and the car was delivered to Roselli at Friar’s Club, Beverly Hills. Carte Blanche credit card was used by Roselli to rent the car, and he stated he would return it November 2.

REC-30.

LA 4268-C was at Friar’s Club October 22 but did not see Roselli. Roselli was paged, and an acquaintance took the call and told the caller Roselli was not there.
