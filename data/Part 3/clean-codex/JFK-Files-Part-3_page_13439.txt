Mr. Connor: Or a member of the family of the President, say?

Mr. Rusk: I would think almost any other Cabinet job would be more appropriate for a member of the family of the President than the Attorney General or the Secretary of State and Secretary of Defense.

Mr. Kirkland: How do you feel, Mr. Secretary, about the question of whether the DCI should be a career intelligence officer, perhaps?

Mr. Rusk: My feeling on that is a little bit like my attitude to what extent Ambassadors should be drawn from career members of the foreign service, that you ought to try and find the best man that you can. There may be times when that person would be a career man, there may be other times when he would come from the outside, and I would a little bit rather not try to generalize on that particular point, because you really ought to find the best man you can get for the job.
