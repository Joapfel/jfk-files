Office of Security – William D. Pawley.

Two volumes. Deletions: 10 sealed envelopes.

Volume I.

24 January 1952 memo to W. A. Osborne from George O'Rourke. Subject: Pawley, William Douglas, EE-7144-A.

00/e requested a security check on Pawley as a potential source of foreign intelligence information. No derogatory information on his loyalty; due to his promotion in A.S., it was suggested that he be placed in the Approved/Caution Category.

During the investigation, several persons described him as a shady, slick business operator. General Chennault stated that his record in India and China was questionable and that he was involved in attempted bribery.

Volume II.

Deletions: Three sealed envelopes.

13 July 1954 memo to Director from Sheffield Edwards, Director of Security. Subject: William W. Pawley.

State Department security file indicated it contained derogatory information alleging black-market activities, income-tax difficulties, possible misuse of Lend-Lease material, and questionable money transactions.
