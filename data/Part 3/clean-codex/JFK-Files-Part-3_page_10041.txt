25 March 1975

Teletype.

NR 001 HN CODE.

5:08 PM HST, NITEL, 25 March 1975, JVS.

To: Director.

From: Honolulu (66-) IP.

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re: Bureau NITEL to all SACs, 24 March 1975.

Honolulu currently has four special agents assigned primarily to security matters, broken down by percentage in counterintelligence and internal security as follows: two agents, 90 percent on counterintelligence and 10 percent on other matters; one agent, 75 percent counterintelligence and 25 percent internal security; one agent, 30 percent counterintelligence, 40 percent internal security, and 30 percent other matters. Three additional agents spend approximately 15 percent of their time on internal security.

The ASAC, who supervises security matters, spends 35 percent of the time on security, broken down as follows: 25 percent counterintelligence and 10 percent internal security.

End.
