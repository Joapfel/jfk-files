Counterintelligence Programs

In June 1968, an informant in the San Diego, California, area, who was a Communist Party (CP) member, claimed to have been offended by Ben Dobbs, executive secretary of the Southern California CP, who was running for statewide office. The informant, a black, claimed to have been ignored by Dobbs and raised the issue that he engaged in discrimination against blacks in the Southern California District CP. The claim was made at a Southern California District CP Negro Commission meeting, and a tape recording was made of the meeting.

Dobbs apologized to the informant. The above tape recording was played to the CP District Board members and caused internal dissension between Board members. Some maintained the taped meeting was designed to discredit Dobbs and was staged by Board member Bill Taylor. Taylor, in turn, was angry at the other Board members.
