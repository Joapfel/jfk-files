Attention: Budget and Accounting Section

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Rebutel March 24, 1975.

The SAC and the ASAC of the Oklahoma City office are not assigned internal security or counterintelligence matters. The Oklahoma City office has no supervisor or special agent assigned full time to internal security and/or counterintelligence matters.

Following is a breakdown in percentages of supervisors’ and agents’ time spent on internal security and counterintelligence matters:

Supervisor: number 1; internal security 75; counterintelligence 5.  
SAs: number 31; internal security 615; counterintelligence 65.

Of the thirty-one special agents, it is noted that 8 are assigned 50 percent or more of their time on internal security and counterintelligence matters but account for 550 percent of the 615 percent internal security time and 62 percent of the 65 percent of counterintelligence time.
