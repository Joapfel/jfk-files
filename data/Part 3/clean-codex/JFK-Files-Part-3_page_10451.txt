Johnny Carpenter

The American Musician Who Taught Medicine for decades and then became America's leading authority on the health care of ARP patients. In his influential books, including Medicine for the Mad and The Singing Patient, John Carpenter brought thousands of people back from the edge of death. In his articles, which appeared in such magazines as The New Republic and The Saturday Evening Post, he helped shape the way that doctors thought about and treated their patients. In his clinical experience, he realized that much of the traditional medicine used to treat mental illness was actually harmful. He wrote a number of ground-breaking books, including Medicine for the Mad, which has sold more than one million copies, changing the way that physicians think about and treat mental illness. Other books have become bestsellers. His most recent book, Medicine for the Mad: How to Stop Being a Patient, has sold more than one million copies.
