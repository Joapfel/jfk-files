This memo, as you know, received the following comment from Mr. Hoover: “I am glad to see that light, though it has been delayed, has come to the Domestic Intelligence Division,” and so on.

I would just quote part of the language and maybe you can help us understand the psychology that led to it.

The first part of the memo says: “We have got to remove King from his pedestal.” Then it says, “The negroes will be left without a national leader of sufficiently compelling personality to steer it in a proper direction. This is what could happen but need not happen if the right kind of national negro leader could at this time be gradually developed so as to overthrow Dr. King and be in the position to assume the role of leadership of the negro people when King has been completely discredited.”
