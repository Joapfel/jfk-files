11:17 PM, urgent, 28 March 1968, ARK.

To Director (102-106670) and Memphis  
From Atlanta (100-5586)

Martin Luther King, Jr., SM-C.

Sanitation workers strike, Memphis, Tennessee. RM.

At 1387-R furnished the following information at 4:30 PM, 28 March 1968.

Informant's information was obtained from discussion shortly before with Hosea Williams, Director of Voter Registration and Political Education, SCLC, currently in Atlanta, who during late afternoon, 28 March 1968, conferred telephonically with Martin Luther King, Jr., President, SCLC; Ralph D. Abernathy, Vice President and Treasurer, SCLC; and Bernard Lee, Special Aide to the President, SCLC.

King, Abernathy, and Lee are currently in Room 801, Holiday Inn, Memphis.

With reference to racial disturbance in Memphis, 28 March 1968, King is extremely dejected. He and Abernathy strongly feel disturbance on this date deliberately planned.
