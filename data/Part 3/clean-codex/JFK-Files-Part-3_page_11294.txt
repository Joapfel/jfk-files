In resident agencies (RA), the following situation exists:

One agent in Colorado Springs spends 50% and another agent spends 25% of their time on IS and CI matters. In Boulder RA, one full-time agent and one agent spending 60% of his time handle IS and CI matters. In Fort Collins RA, one agent spends 50% and the other 40% of their time on these matters. In Cheyenne RA, one agent spends 25% of his time on these matters. In the other RAs, only a minor fraction of the time of each RA is devoted to IS and CI matters. Total time would possibly equal that of one SA full time. In all instances, about three-fourths of time spent on security investigations is on IS matters, with the remaining one-fourth on CI matters.
