United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI

From: SAC, Miami

Subject: Counterintelligence Program, Internal Security, Disruption of Hate Groups

Date: 10/14/64

Re Bureau letter to Atlanta, 9/2/64.

Newspapers

“Miami Herald.” A morning Miami, Florida, daily newspaper. George J. Bezier, Managing Editor (SAC Contact).

“The Miami News.” An evening local Miami, Florida, newspaper. William C. Baggs, Editor (SAC Contact).

“The Ft. Lauderdale News.” A daily Broward County newspaper. Milton Kelly, Editor.

TV Stations

WCKT-TV, Channel 7, NBC. Gene Struhl, News Director.

WLEW-TV, Channel 10, ABC. Charles H. Stopphiller, General Manager (SAC Contact). Charles Matthews, National Sales Director.
