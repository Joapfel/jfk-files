MIAMI AIRTEL TO BUREAU, DECEMBER 5, 1963, recommending screening of mail from Miami to Mexico; approximately 5,000 letters a day would be involved.

MIAMI AIRTEL TO BUREAU, NOVEMBER 21, 1963, advising a Manila envelope mailed from New York City was intercepted and believed to contain a book and money for FOXTROT.

NEW YORK AIRTEL TO BUREAU, NOVEMBER 26, 1963, indicating that a portion of the money furnished to FOXTROT found in the aforementioned book originated from Soviet funds used in their clandestine operation.

Memorandum of SA William E. Dowling dated December 2, 1963, containing compilation of agent time spent on FOXTROT case.

Bureau letter to Miami, December 5, 1963, requesting appropriate coverage by Miami of a post office box used as a mail drop by FOXTROT.
