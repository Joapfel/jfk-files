Adequacy of Personnel

Section Chief R. D. Cotter: As of close of business 1/8/71, the complement of this Section was as follows: Special Agents 13, secretaries 5, and clerks 15, for a total of 33. This is broken down by unit as follows:

Section Front Office: 2 Special Agents, 1 secretary (stenography), 1 secretary (typing).

Central Research Unit: 5 Special Agents, 1 secretary, 2 supervisory clerks (technical publications writers), 1 clerk (publications coordinator), 1 clerk (typing).

Racial Reporting Unit: 2 Special Agents, 1 secretary, 1 supervisory clerk (technical publications writer), 2 clerks.

New Left Reporting Unit: 2 Special Agents, 1 secretary, 1 supervisory clerk (technical publications writer), 1 clerk.

Special Records Unit: 2 Special Agents, 1 secretary (assigned but not authorized, see number 3 below), 6 clerks.

The following responses are numbered to correspond with Inspector's specific questions:

1. There are no vacant positions.

2. There are no personnel on transfer into or out of Section at this time and no pending resignations.

3. The only shortage or overage of authorized personnel is in Special Records Unit. The position of secretary in this unit has been approved by the Director contingent upon final action by the Position Classification Office, which is now pending.

4. Current clerical personnel complement is considered adequate but not excessive.
