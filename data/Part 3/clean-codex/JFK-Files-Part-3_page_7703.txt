This is to advise you that pursuant to request from the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC) I have released you and former SA David E. Todd from applicable employee secrecy agreements for the purpose of a staff interview by SSC concerning COINTELPRO and the investigation at San Francisco of the BPP. Lester B. Seidel, staff member of SSC, will travel to San Francisco to conduct the interviews in approximately two weeks.

An agent will be available during interview to assist in making a determination as to whether or not a particular question should be responded to. This agent is not to be considered private counsel and will not be present during interview.
