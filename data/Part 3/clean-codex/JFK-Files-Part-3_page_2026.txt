United States Government

Memorandum

To: The Associate Director  
Date: 12/22/77  
From: Legal Counsel  
Subject: House Select Committee on Assassinations (HSCA)

Purpose: The purpose of this memorandum is to recommend that the Director release from their secrecy agreements SAs Harry L. Lee, Richard D. Rackleff, James R. Riorden, Warren L. Walsh, John B. Simpson, Richard H. Davis, Jack T. Beverstein, Robert Nichols, and former Assistant Director Alex Rosen.

Details: By letter dated 12/20/77, HSCA (HSAC) requested to interview SAs Harry L. Lee (former), Richard D. Rackleff, James R. Riorden, and former Assistant Director Alex Rosen. By letter dated 11/8/77, the Committee requested permission to interview SA John B. Simpson, Richard A. Davis (former), and SA Jack T. Beverstein (former).

By letter dated 11/3/77, the Committee requested the identity of the case agent most familiar with white hate activities in the period from 1965 to 1966. By letter dated 11/17/77, the Committee was advised that this agent is SA Warren L. Walsh (former). By letter dated 12/9/77, the Committee requested permission to interview SA Robert Nichols (former).

Recommendation:

That the Director release from their employment agreement for purposes of interview by the captioned Committee the following individuals: Harry L. Lee, Richard D. Rappleff (Rackleff), James R. Riorden, Warren L. Walsh, John B. Simpson, Richard H. Davis, Jack T. Beverstein, Robert Nichols, and former Assistant Director Alex Rosen.
