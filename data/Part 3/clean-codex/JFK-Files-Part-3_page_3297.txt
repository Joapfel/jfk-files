Sanitation Workers Strike, Memphis, Tenn.

On April 2, 1968, Lt. E. H. Arkin, Memphis Police Department, Inspectional Bureau, advised that the funeral of Larry Payne, seventeen-year-old Negro who was shot by a Memphis police officer after store looting growing out of the March 28 racial disturbance, Memphis, was held without incident at Clayborn Temple, Memphis. About five hundred were in attendance despite the fact the church can seat twenty-five hundred. Body interred immediately after funeral. Arkin advised that another sanitation workers sympathy march is scheduled for downtown Memphis the afternoon of April 2.

Not recorded: Eastern Airlines advised on April 2 that Rev. Martin Luther King, Jr. (gMartin Luther King, Jr.), and Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) aides Ralph D. Abernathy, Bernard Lee, and Andrew M. Young were due to arrive in Memphis at 10:15 a.m., April 3, from Atlanta.

April 5, 1968.
