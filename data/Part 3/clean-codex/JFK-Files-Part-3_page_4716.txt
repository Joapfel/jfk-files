U. S. Department of Justice, Communications Section.

November 10, 1962. Teletype.

Urgent 11/9/62, 10:54 p.m. AH. To Director, FBI 92-3267. From SAC, Los Angeles 92-113C 1P.

John Roselli, aka AR.

LA 42-68-C observed Roselli at Friar's Club, Beverly Hills, evening November 8 last, playing cards with Alexander Rudolph (not further identified). About 8:30 p.m., Roselli had dinner at club with Jonie Taps and “Doc” Merman, who were described by source as officials of Twentieth Century Fox Studios.

Physical surveillance.

Neighborhood source and disclosed Roselli at apartment until after 3 p.m. November 9 instant, and Ford T-Bird registered to Herman Spitzel parked in Roselli's stall at apartment garage. Spitzel is close friend and former employee of Roselli.

End and acknowledgment.
