Memorandum

To: Director, FBI (157-9-Main)

From: SAC, Atlanta (157-826) (P)

Subject: Counterintelligence Program—Internal Security—Disruption of Hate Groups (United Klans of America, Inc., Knights of the Ku Klux Klan)

Date: 9/16/64

ReBulet 9/2/64.

In 1960, a comparatively small group of Klansmen pulled out of U. S. Klans, Knights of the Ku Klux Klan, Inc., and formed a new Klan group called Knights of the Ku Klux Klan, Inc.

The Imperial Wizard of this new group was Robert L. Bing of Jonesboro, Georgia. In the summer of 1961, following a meeting between Bing and officials of United Klans of America, Inc., Knights of the Ku Klux Klan (United Klans), the Knights of the Ku Klux Klan, Inc., were merged into United Klans and Bing was given the position of Imperial Klokard of United Klans.

A meeting was held at Birmingham, Alabama, 9/5–6/64. At this meeting, Bing was opposed for election as Imperial Klokard by J. D. Johnson (Johnwon), a Georgia State Klan Officer.
