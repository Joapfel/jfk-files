Sabotage

Special Investigations—East Unit

Incidents of sabotage have been on the rise since the mid-1960s, occasioned primarily by growing opposition throughout the country to the Selective Service System, the Armed Forces, and in particular to the war in Vietnam.

There are under investigation at the present time 7 sabotage cases with prosecution pending involving 29 subjects who are charged with 43 separate violations, including attempted sabotage, destruction of government property, Anti-Riot Law violation, civil rights violations, violation of the National Firearms Act, interstate transportation of stolen motor vehicles, and conspiracy. In connection with one of the above investigations, one subject in October 1970 was found guilty and sentenced to five years, which sentence is being appealed, and another subject in November 1970 pleaded guilty and is scheduled to be sentenced sometime in January 1971.
