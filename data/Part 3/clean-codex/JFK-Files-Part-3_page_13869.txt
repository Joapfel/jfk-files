A regular CIA clandestine asset in Peru with close contacts in Peruvian left-wing circles reports that Cuba has given, and is still giving, training to Peruvians in Cuba—including demolition instructions, manufacture of explosives, and other aspects of guerrilla warfare.

A 50-point questionnaire issued by the Cuban Government is said to “clearly pinpoint” Cuban interest in information which could be used for sabotage operations in Peru, subversion of the Peruvian police, and for illegal entry and travel in Peru. Some 150 Peruvians are reported receiving such training in Cuba. (CIA, Lima, III 46954, 9 January.)
