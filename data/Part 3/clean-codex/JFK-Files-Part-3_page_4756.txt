United States Department of Justice

Federal Bureau of Investigation

Report of: Allan M. Trankley  
Office: Miami, Florida  
Date: January 31, 1969  
Field Office File No.: 92-1870  
Bureau File No.: 92-9844  
Title: Norman Rothman

Character: Anti-Racketeering

Synopsis:

Subject reportedly in Atlanta, Georgia, in October 1968 to arrange for payment of gambling debts owed by an Atlanta individual. Reportedly has lost Albion Lounge, Miami Beach, Florida, for failure to pay bills. Subject presently in New York, New York; purpose not known.

Details:

Travel

On November 25, 1968, Mr. A. Newman, National Airlines, Miami International Airport, advised that subject had taken Flight No. 6 to New York, New York, and had departed at 1:20 p.m.

On December 9, 1968, Joe Falcone, station manager, National Airlines, Miami International Airport, advised that subject was to take Flight No. 90 to LaGuardia Field, New York, New York, departing Miami at 5:00 p.m. At 4:48 p.m., subject was observed to board the plane. He was alone.
