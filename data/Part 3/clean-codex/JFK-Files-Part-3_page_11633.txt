Security Informant Program. 8/19/71.

At the time of last inspection in January 1971, a total of 1,159 security informants was being operated. As of July 31, 1971, the number had increased to 1,182, or an increase of 23. During the same period, the number of potential security informants increased from 765 to 841, or an increase of 76.
