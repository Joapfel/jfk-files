Re: Superseded Sections of the Manual of Instructions

Note:

Pursuant to a prior SSC request, we furnished them current MOI Sections 36, 84, 86, 87, 88, 95, 97 and 122. Due to the sensitive nature of Sections 105, 107 and 130, these were made available for review only at FBIHQ.

Listed below are the Intelligence Division representatives and the MOI sections reviewed for this project:

Section 36—Espionage. Representatives: James W. Johnson; Dennis R. Dickson. Authors: CI-1; page CI-3.

Section 84—Registration Act. Representative: Louis J. Brune. Author: IS-2.

Section 86—Sabotage. Representative: William N. Preusse. Author: IS-2.

Section 87—Investigations of Subversive Organizations and Individuals. Representative: Thomas J. McNiff. Author: IS-2.

Section 88—Sedition. Representative: George J. Lex. Author: IS-2.

Section 105—Internal Security—Nationalistic Tendency. Representatives: James W. Johnson; Howard W. Dare, Jr.; Dennis R. Dickson. Authors: CI-1; CI-2; CI-3.

Section 107—Internal Security Informants and Confidential Sources; and Foreign Counter-intelligence Assets. Representatives: Edward P. Grigalus; James W. Johnson; Howard W. Dare, Jr.; Dennis R. Dickson. Authors: IS-2; CI-1; CI-2; CI-3.

Section 122—Extremist Matters and Civil Unrest. Representative: Robert D. Shea. Author: IS-1.

Section 130—Extremist Informants and Sources. Representative: Robert D. Shea. Author: IS-1.

As previously noted, there were no MOI revisions during the period of interest to Section 95—Treason and Section 97—Voorhis Act.

The revisions of the MOI requested by SSC consists of approximately 2,200 pages and is approximately 9" in height. Due to the bulkiness of these revisions, they are not being attached to this memorandum.
