Office of the Deputy Attorney General

Washington, D.C. 20530

October 17, 1975

To: John A. Mintz, Assistant Director, Legal Counsel Division, Federal Bureau of Investigation

From: Michael E. Shaheen, Jr., Special Counsel for Intelligence Coordination

Subject: Senate Select Committee Request

Attached for your immediate attention and comments is a letter from the Senate Select Committee which is self-explanatory and which raises anew an issue previously addressed by the Bureau, with the Attorney General, the Deputy Attorney General, and this Office. Please note that Chairman Church asks the Attorney General to advise him of our position by Tuesday, October 21, 1975.

cc: Paul Daly
