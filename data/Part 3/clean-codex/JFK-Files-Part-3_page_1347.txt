Dear Mr. Attorney General:

In connection with the investigation by this Committee into the death of Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., it is requested that the FBI provide the Committee with photographs of all Atlanta agents assigned to the security investigation of Dr. King during the period 1967–68. To the extent possible, these photographs should depict the agents involved as they appeared in 1967–1968 or as close thereto as possible.

Your prompt attention to this matter will be greatly appreciated.
