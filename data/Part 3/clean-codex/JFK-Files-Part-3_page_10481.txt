Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

March 25, 1975.

Teletype.

NR 001 HN CODE.

5:08 PM HST NITEL March 25, 1975 JVS.

To: Director.

From: Honolulu (66-) IP.

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re: Bureau NITEL to all SACs, March 24, 1975.

Honolulu currently has four special agents assigned primarily to security matters, broken down by percentage counterintelligence and internal security as follows: 2 agents, 90 percent on counterintelligence and 10 percent each on other matters; 1 agent, 75 percent counterintelligence and 25 percent internal security; 1 agent, 30 percent counterintelligence, 40 percent internal security, and 30 percent other matters. Three additional agents spend approximately 15 percent of their time on internal security.

ASAC supervises security matters and spends 35 percent of time on security, broken down as: 25 percent counterintelligence and 10 percent internal security.

End.

Hold.
