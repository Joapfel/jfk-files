Title: Angela Yvonne Davis

Character: Security Matter — C

Synopsis: Angela Yvonne Davis, also known as “Tamu,” resides at 1107-1/2 West 87th Street, Los Angeles, California. Davis is presently unemployed. Background data is being set out. On 6/21/68, Davis was elected as a staff member of the Los Angeles Chapter of SNCC. Davis dropped out of SNCC and joined the Communist Party. Davis is a member of the Che Lumumba Club (Chc Lumumba Club), Horace Smith Section, SCDCP. Davis’ activities in connection with the SCDCP are set out. Description data set forth.

Details:

Investigation in this matter was predicated upon receipt of information from LA T-3 that Angela Yvonne Davis was a member of the Communist Party in 1969.

Birth Data and Citizenship

On January 30, 1969, records of the Jefferson County Board of Health, Bureau of Records and Vital Statistics, 1912 Eighth Avenue South, Birmingham, Alabama, reflected that under Birth Certificate number 3737026 for the year 1944, Angela Yvonne Davis was born January 26, 1944, the daughter of Benjamin Frank Davis and Sally Marguritte Bell.
