United States Government Memorandum

To: Director, FBI (65-67003)

From: SAC, San Francisco (105-11581)

Subject: GUS Survey Espionage

Date: 1/31/62

Re: San Francisco letter to Director 1/11/62 advising San Francisco survey would run through February 9, 1962.

As of 1/28/62, a total of about 1,764,440 letters has been surveyed in 46 days.

Fifty-one cases have been opened to determine whether recipient might be Soviet illegal agent. Thirty-two cases have been closed when subjects appeared to be legitimate residents.

San Francisco does not at this time recommend continuance of this survey beyond February 9, 1962. Upon completion of authorized period and evaluation of investigations resulting therefrom, a further recommendation will be made as to reinstituting this program.
