Civil Rights and Domestic Violence.

Folder. First communication dated 3/10/47. Last communication dated 3/24/47. Approximate pages: 100.

Derogatory information: No. Category: Administrative—Policy. Originals only.

General content: Booklet summary regarding Civil Rights and Domestic Violence for use by Hoover in addressing the President's Committee on Civil Rights, setting forth FBI policy and position; and correspondence from Hoover to the Committee concerning his views on spelling out the rights of individuals rather than leaving it up to judicial interpretation.
