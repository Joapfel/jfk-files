President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board. Tentative Agenda for Meeting of December 7, 1962.

1. 9:00–9:30 — Status of implementation of prior Board recommendations, Mr. Ash.
2. 9:30–10:30 — Oral report by Board's Cuban Panel.
3. 10:30–1:00 — Discussions with DCI's Cuban Task Force (Messrs. McCone, Carroll, Blake, and Captain) with Kirkpatrick-Helms.
4. 1:00–2:00 — Lunch.
5. 2:00–2:30 — Executive session.
6. 2:30–3:00 — Discussion with Dr. Charyk (Capt. Sollerman).
7. 3:00–4:00 — Status report on the organization, programs and plans of NRO (Messrs. Charyk, Scoville and Carroll joined by the DCI).
8. 4:00–5:30 — Discussion of Board's preliminary conclusions and recommendations on the Cuba problem.
9. 5:30–end — Discussion of future Board business.
