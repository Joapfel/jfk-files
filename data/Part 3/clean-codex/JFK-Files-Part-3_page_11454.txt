SD 66-1714

Reviewing FBI attempts to disrupt Black nationalist groups.

Williams allegedly told Jefferson of his attempts to mediate a feud between the Black Panthers and US in San Diego in 1969. Williams states he was informed by two city officials, former Police Chief O.J. Roed and former City Manager Walter Hahn, that the Black Panthers planned to assassinate him and information about the plot had been furnished by the FBI. Roed and Hahn are reported in the article as not recalling the meeting or telling Williams of a death plot against him.

Williams indicates that upon being so advised by Roed and Hahn, he notified Councilman Floyd Morrow, and Morrow has stated he recalls being so advised.
