SENSTUDY 1975.

Re Seattle telephone, 8 July 1975.

On 16 July 1975, U.S. Attorney Stan Pitkin, Western District of Washington, Seattle, advised he recently received a letter from Barbara Ann Banoff on U.S. Senate letterhead with the caption “Select Committee to Study Government Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities, Frank Church, Chairman.” In the letter, Banoff requested Pitkin to furnish the following:

1. Any available information on the county grand jury investigation regarding the Larry Ward shooting, including the identity of witnesses. Also, information regarding the inquest and civil trial in the Ward matter.

2. Information on the federal grand jury which indicted “Seattle 7.” Wanted copy of indictment and, if available, copy of transcript of trial. Also names of four FBI informants in this case.
