Since the presentation of God, the Trinity is known as a secret of mortality and of eternity. In God, there is no contrary factor. All thoughts are existent, but not unreal. Under God, there is no end to the reality of thoughts, which are infinite. Since God is in all thoughts, it is possible that those thoughts may be existent when God is able to show them.

The end.
