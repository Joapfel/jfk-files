“All America Women’s Congress,” held Havana, Cuba, January 11, 12, 13, 1963.

Attendance: Number of countries participating: 19. Number of guest countries represented: 15. Number of delegates: 425. Number of guests: 95. Total number of women attending: 820. Cuban women attending: 177. Net total foreign women attending: 543.

Breakdown of delegates, by occupations: Workers—factory workers, peasants and employees: 110; professionals: 49; housewives: 127; teaching profession: 95; students: 20; writers: 4; journalists: 8; congresswomen: 8; city councillors: 2; businesswomen: 2. Total: 425.

Number of delegates, by countries: Argentina: 19; Bolivia: 1; Brazil: 8; Canada: 17; Colombia: 13; Costa Rica: 8; Cuba: 177; Chile: 31; Ecuador: 4; El Salvador: 3; Guatemala: 10; Honduras: 3; Mexico: 93; Nicaragua: 2; Panama: 2; Paraguay: 3; United States of America: 14; Uruguay: 15; Venezuela: 2.
