On April two, a source advised that at a press conference at Clayborn Temple, April two, Rev. Jesse Jackson of SCLC's (SLC's) “Operation Breadbasket,” Rev. S. B. Kyles of Metropolitan Baptist Church, Memphis, and Rev. Edward L. Brown, Mt. Pisgah CME Church, advised that with SCLC personnel in Memphis, nightly mass strike support meetings will be resumed, the first at Mason Temple on the night of April three, with King as featured speaker. They said a mass march will be held Friday, April five, and that they will have schoolchildren participate since marching with “Martin Luther King, Jr., the greatest living American,” would be more educational than being in school.

When asked by the press if King would personally march, Jackson said King might not march. He did not elaborate. When asked if the SCLC group would violate a possible injunction against their marching, Jackson said, “The white man has already enjoined the black man to the ghetto and the white man doesn't need to validate it, by sending us a piece of paper.” Kyles said the problem facing Memphis is not militant black youth but is “white racism.” Jackson described Operation Breadbasket as massive.
