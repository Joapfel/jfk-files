1 - Mr. J. Cochran, Jr.

United States Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSC)

Re: Further Documents Pertaining to the FBI and Department of Justice Policies and Procedures

Reference is made to SSC letter dated May 14, 1975, with attached appendices, requesting certain documents and other information from the FBI.

Pursuant to your request in Appendix B, Part I, Item 7, for guidelines used by the FBI Inspection Division for inspecting field offices and for inspecting divisions of the FBI, we currently have such material available for review at FBI Headquarters by appropriately cleared personnel of the Committee.

1 - The Attorney General

TEB:dmt

Note:

See H. N. Bassett memorandum to Mr. Wannall dated June 2, 1975, captioned “Senstudy 75,” wherein the Inspection Division advised it has no objection to a review of the field and headquarters inspection manuals by members of the Committee staff, provided the review is conducted in FBI space with an Inspection Division representative present. When SSC review is requested, we will have the Inspection Division representative make the manuals available.
