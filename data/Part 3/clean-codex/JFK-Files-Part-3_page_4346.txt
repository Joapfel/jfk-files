Office of the Director

United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Washington, D.C. 20535

January 26, 1970

Memorandum for the Attorney General

Re: Black Panther Party  
Los Angeles Division

Approval was granted by you on March 20, 1969, for a telephone surveillance on the office of the Black Panther Party, 4115 South Central Avenue, Los Angeles, California. On December 2, 1969, you granted approval to continue that telephone surveillance for an additional three months.

The above address is no longer being used as the Los Angeles Black Panther Party office. The office has been transferred to 9818 Anzac, Los Angeles, California.

The Black Panther Party continues to be the most violence-prone and dangerous black extremist organization in the United States. The Los Angeles Chapter, in turn, is one of its most active groups. The December 8, 1969 shooting encounter of members of this group with the Los Angeles Police Department, of which you are aware, attests to the group's violence, defiance of law and order, and internal security threat.

You are also aware that the installation described above furnished extremely valuable information concerning many facets of the operations of that organization. It is expected that an installation at the new location will also furnish extremely valuable information not otherwise obtainable.

This document is prepared in response to your request and is not for dissemination outside your Committee. Its use is limited to official proceedings by your Committee, and the content may not be disclosed to unauthorized personnel without the express approval of the FBI.
