J. C. Farrington to Mr. McDermott Memo

Request by U. S. Senator Walter D. Huddleston, Senate Select Committee member, to review his FBI files.

Portions of file 166-5980 were masked out. As these masks will be removed, a Xerox of the pertinent portion of the respective documents has been made for record purposes and attached to this memorandum.
