June 9, 1975

The Attorney General

Director, FBI

U.S. Senate Select Committee with Respect to Intelligence Activities

Reference is made to my letter dated May 22, 1975, forwarding a memorandum concerning an interview by a staff member of the captioned Committee of retired FBI Special Agent Alan G. Sentinella.

Enclosed is the original of a memorandum concerning testimony subsequently given by Mr. Sentinella before an executive session of the Committee. A copy of the memorandum is also enclosed for forwarding to Mr. James A. Wilderotter, Associate Counsel to the President.
