Mr. Adams. Let me take the last one first, provocateurs. Our policy has not—or our policy has been to discourage any activities which in any way might involve an informant doing something that an agent cannot do, which would be in the area of being a provocateur, which basically is entrapment. And we have had some allegations of entrapment come up. We feel we have satisfactorily answered them. This is a very technical legal field which boils down, of course, to the fact that if a person is willing to do something, and the government merely provides the opportunity, that is not legally entrapment. So if a person comes to us and says I have been asked to participate in a break-in of a Federal building, I would like to help you, then the law basically would indicate we have the authority to continue to let him operate.
