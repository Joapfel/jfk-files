Federal Bureau of Investigation  
U.S. Department of Justice  
Communications Section

November 9, 1962

Urgent, 11/8/62, 10:55 p.m.

To Director, FBI 92-3267

From SAC, Los Angeles 92-113C 2P

John Roselli, AKA, AR. LA 4268-C, advised he observed Roselli at Friar's Club, B.H., California, about 4 p.m., November 7. Roselli was talking with Irving J. Leff, part owner, New Frontier Hotel, Las Vegas.

Roselli was still at the club at 6:30 p.m. when informant left.

Neighborhood source advised that a 1962 Ford T-Bird, California license XBU 842, was parked in Roselli's apartment garage stall the morning of November 8. Bureau previously advised this car was registered to Herman Spitzel, former employer and close friend of Roselli.

In March 1961, Roselli contacted private detective Fred Otash regarding an investigation Otash was conducting for Peter Fairchild, guardian of Robert Machris.
