The Attorney General

February 20, 1975

Director, FBI

SMOOTH SELECT COMMITTEE

Reference is made to a telephone conversation between Deputy Attorney General Laurence Silberman (Siltarman) and Mr. James Adams, Deputy Associate Director, FBI, on February 14, 1975.

This will confirm the authorization provided by Mr. Silberman (Siltarman) to Mr. Adams with regard to permitting CIA to furnish a memorandum dated February 7, 1968, entitled “Central Intelligence Agency Operations in the United States,” to Mr. Samuel F. Preston, Senior Staff Member, House Appropriations Committee. For your information, attached is a copy of this memorandum.

It is our understanding that Mr. Preston telephonically contacted Mr. John Clarke, Associate Deputy to the Director of Central Intelligence, and requested all written agreements between the FBI and CIA to be furnished him for the use of the House Appropriations Committee.
