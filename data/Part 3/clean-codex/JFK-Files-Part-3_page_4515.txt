U. S. Department of Justice, Communications Section.

August 8, 1961. Teletype. Urgent, 8-8-61, 10:45 PM EDST WRJ.

To: Director, FBI. From: SAC, Pittsburgh.

Gabriel Mannarino, also known as AR Samuel Mannarino, also known as AR PG 621-C, advises Mannarino’s negotiating to purchase or lease Anchorage Road house, Allegheny River Boulevard, and Sandy Creek Road, Verona, Pennsylvania, in anticipation of possible administration change in City of New Kensington, Pennsylvania, this coming fall. Plans are to move entire gambling operation to this new spot if necessary. Subjects should be considered armed and dangerous.

End and ack pls.
