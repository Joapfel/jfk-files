Counterintelligence Programs

In April 1969, the New York Office sent spurious postcards, which duplicated the type used by Rochell Kendrick, 792 Columbus Avenue, New York, New York, Chief of the New York Consulate of the Republic of New Africa (RNA), a black extremist organization, to advise members of RNA meetings. The spurious postcards were sent on short notice to reschedule or cancel RNA meetings.

The above-mentioned postcards caused disruption and suspicion among RNA members in the New York City area.
