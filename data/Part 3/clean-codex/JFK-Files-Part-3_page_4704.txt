U. S. Department of Justice. Communications Section.

November 1, 1962.

Teletype.

Urgent, 10/31/62, 10:14 PM AH.

To Director, FBI 92-3267.

From SAC, Los Angeles (Los Angelex), 92-113C 2P.

John Roselli, AKA AR.

LA FOUR TWO SIX EIGHT C observed Ros­selli at Friar's (Friar-S) Club, Beverly Hills, Calif., about 4 P.M. October thirty last in company Irving J. Leff, a part owner, New Frontier Hotel, Las Vegas, and an individual called “Ross.” Leff related that during Cuban crisis last week people rushed to buy firearms and ammunition and Ros­selli said that he was in Miami during this period and people there were doing the same thing.

Leff asked Ros­selli why he didn't call and tell Leff what to do. Ros­selli said he was in Las Vegas and Leff could have called him but Leff was “too cheap” to make the call. Informant said that apparently Ros­selli returned to Los Angeles Friday, October twenty-six last and then the same night or the following morning departed.
