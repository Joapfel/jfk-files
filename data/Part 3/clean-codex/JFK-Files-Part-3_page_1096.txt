Counterintelligence Programs

In December 1967, an anonymous letter was mailed to officials of Carrsville Community Center, Carrsville, Isle of Wight (Wright) County, Virginia, stating that the Ku Klux Klan (KKK) was using the community center for holding dances. The Klan was using the cover of Southampton (Southhampton) Activities Club. The group was part of United Klans of America, Inc., KKK, and was raising funds through the dances.

After February 1968, the lease to the Klan was terminated. The officials considered the dances had become rowdy, and they did not know previously that the Klan was involved.
