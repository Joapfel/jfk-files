Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Communications Section

NR 09 PG PLAIN

7:35 p.m., NITEL, March 25, 1975, RGM

To: Director, FBI

From: Pittsburgh (62-NEW)

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

REBUNITEL, March 24, 1975.

There are 110 special agents assigned to the Pittsburgh office: 75 headquarters city agents and 35 resident agents (RAs).

One supervisor is assigned to Counterintelligence (CI) full time. One supervisor is assigned to Internal Security (IS) full time.

Eleven headquarters agents are assigned to CI full time, and seven headquarters agents are assigned to IS full time.

Resident agent assignments are as follows:

6 RAs, 2 percent CI; 5 RAs, 2 percent IS.  
3 RAs, 3 percent CI; 2 RAs, 3 percent IS.  
3 RAs, 5 percent CI; 1 RA, 4 percent IS.  
2 RAs, 6 percent CI; 2 RAs, 5 percent IS.  
3 RAs, 7 percent CI; 1 RA, 7 percent IS.  
2 RAs, 8 percent CI; 2 RAs, 11 percent IS.  
1 RA, 25 percent CI; 1 RA, 12 percent IS.
