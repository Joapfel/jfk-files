From Stockholm to Helsinki via SAS, flight No. 728, date 7/31.  
From Helsinki to Moscow via Aeroflot, flight No. 108, date 7/31.  
From Moscow to Kiev via Aeroflot, carrier open, date 8/17.  
From Kiev to Prague via Aeroflot, carrier 211, date 8/20.  
From Prague to Copenhagen via Malev (Hungarian Airlines), flight No. 380, date 8/20.  
From Copenhagen to Idlewild via SAS, flight No. 919, date 8/20.
