Assistant Attorney General, Criminal Division

Attention: Mr. Robert L. Keuch

November 10, 1977

Director, FBI

House Select Committee on Assassinations, U.S. House of Representatives (HSCA)

Reference is made to a letter to the Attorney General from G. Robert Blakey, Chief Counsel and Director, HSCA, dated October 18, 1977, requesting access to material in the files of the FBI pertinent to seven listed individuals.

Enclosed are two copies of a memorandum which responds to the above request and advises that material on six of these individuals is available for HSCA access at FBI Headquarters. Information on the seventh individual, Randolph Erwin Rosenson, was previously furnished to the HSCA by FBI memorandum dated October 13, 1977.

Enclosures: 2.
