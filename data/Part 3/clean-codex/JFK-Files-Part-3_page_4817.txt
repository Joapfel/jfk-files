Title: Norman Rothman  
Character: Anti-Racketeering

Synopsis:

Subject continues to reside in Surfside, Fla. Continues to associate with LCN figures in Miami, Fla., and has been described as carrier of funds and errand boy for LCN. Subject reportedly has been backing the Bolita operation of Erwin Brenner, using hoodlum money.

Details

Residence

Rothman was observed at his residence, 425 Surfside Boulevard, Surfside, Florida, on January 31, 1968; February 1, 8, 15, 1968; March 4, 7, 11 and 13, 1968.

On February 1, 1968, MM T-1 advised that there is no unusual activity at Rothman's house during the evening hours.

Associates

On December 20, 1967, MM T-2 advised that Patty Erra, Norman Rothman, Anthony Salerno, Louis (LNU), John Tronalone, “Chink,” and Joe Indelicato were at “Joe the Bartender's Room,” Hilton Plaza Hotel, Miami Beach, Florida.
