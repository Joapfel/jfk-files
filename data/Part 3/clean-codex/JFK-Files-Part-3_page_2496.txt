Re: SAM survey

The SAM survey was concerned with the examination by Bureau agents (BUAGENTS) of airmail destined for certain European cities or countries prior to its being placed in bags and placed aboard the planes for the purpose of identifying persons corresponding with known Soviet mail drops in Europe and to identify and locate individuals in the U.S. sending letters to Europe which may be part of a Soviet illegal network.
