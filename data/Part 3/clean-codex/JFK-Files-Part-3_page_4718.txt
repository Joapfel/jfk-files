Federal Bureau of Investigation  
U.S. Department of Justice

Communications Section

November 15, 1962

Teletype

Urgent, 11/14/62, 10:30 p.m. AH

To Director, FBI, 92-3267

From SAC, Los Angeles, 92-113 2P

John Roselli, AR.

Roselli had a reservation to Las Vegas, Nevada, at six-thirty p.m., November 14, instant, but cancelled and made no other reservation.

LA 4268C went to dinner on November 13, last, with Roselli and Jonie Taps, who informant says is an official at Twentieth Century-Fox Studio. During dinner Roselli said about six years ago he received seven hundred and ninety thousand dollars from Bryan Foy, movie producer, to purchase an unidentified hotel and casino in downtown Las Vegas. That deal went through and Roselli received points for handling. Identity of hotel not presently known.

Roselli said he was going to Santa Barbara this week for a physical checkup and to Palm Springs, California, on November 16, next.
