Attached are Xerox copies of federal income tax returns filed by Robert H. and his family (UVA). Also attached are Xerox copies of United States corporation tax returns filed by Robert H. Sholton (Shelton) as a corporate officer of the United States of America, Inc. (USA), for the years 1962 (year ending 6/30/62) and 1963 (year ending 6/30/63). No return is due on a statement of no tax liability for the year ending 6/30/63. Information not set forth in these tax returns is for legal purposes only and should not be used without written permission from any investigative report.

The following comments and investigative instructions are noted in connection with these tax returns:

1. The individual returns filed jointly by Shelton and his wife during the period 1959 through 1963 indicate Shelton's occupation to be “elecianian” and “bannergor,” and in no instance do they indicate any employment or association with the firm. This is in conflict with the corporate returns filed by Shelton for the USA, where he indicated his position as “Electrical Wizard” or “President.”

2. Shelton's return for 1959 indicates it was prepared by Ed F. Allen, Jr., Certified Public Accountant, Post Office Box 300, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. The form and typing on the other tax returns, both corporate and individual, indicate a strong likelihood that two were prepared by Allen. You should review your files and conduct necessary investigation to determine if Allen is active in connection with financial matters relating to the firm.
