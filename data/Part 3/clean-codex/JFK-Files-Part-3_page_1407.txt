Subject: House Select Committee on Assassinations (HSCA) Bureau deadline — 1/13/78.

Re FBIHQ teletype to numerous offices, including Atlanta, 12/27/77.

Referenced Bureau teletype indicated that the HSCA was currently conducting an investigation into the assassination of Martin Luther King, Jr. (MURKIN); and in connection with this investigation, the HSCA had requested the FBI provide the following:

(1) The identification of field office case agent(s) assigned to the MURKIN investigation in recipient offices, including the division, section or squad assignment, and the nature of the agent's assignment immediately prior to his involvement in the MURKIN investigation.

(2) Identification of the field office case agent(s) assigned to the counterintelligence program (COINTELPRO), efforts directed against King and the Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) during the period 1965–1968 in recipient offices, including the division, section or squad assignment, and the nature of the agent's assignment immediately prior to his involvement in the COINTELPRO, King, and SCLC investigations.

EX-130 REG-36 62-117290-394.

In addition, recipients were to identify the case agent(s) involved in the security investigation of King and SCLC, as well as COINTELPRO.
