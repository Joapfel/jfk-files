Federal Bureau of Investigation. U.S. Department of Justice. Communications Section.

October 25, 1962. Teletype. Urgent, 10-24-62, 9:26 p.m. DRS.

To Director, FBI, 92-3267. From SAC Los Angeles, 92-113 C.

John Roselli, aka AR-LA-4268-C, was advised by an employee at Friars Club, Beverly Hills, that Roselli was going to return to Las Vegas either October 24 or 25. Source at Hal Roach Studios, Culver City, California, stated Johnny Mashio, who has been working in Las Vegas on contracts with hotels regarding shooting movies of floor shows in casinos, is no longer on good terms with Hal Roach, Jr., for whom he has been working, and is contemplating working again with Roselli in efforts to film the movie. Source said he learned that Frank Sinatra, well-known entertainer, told Roselli to “throw strikes” at Hal Roach, Jr., and Carroll Case so they would be unable to make the movie in Las Vegas. Source did not know what, if any, action Roselli has taken in Las Vegas.

REC-91 92-3267-359.
