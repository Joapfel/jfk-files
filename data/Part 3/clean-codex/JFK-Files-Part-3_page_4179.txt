Racial Matter  
Sanitation Workers Strike, Memphis, Tennessee

On April 2, 1968, Lt. E. H. Arkin, Memphis Police Department, Inspectional Bureau, advised that the funeral of Larry Payne, a seventeen-year-old Negro who was shot by a Memphis police officer after store looting growing out of the March 28 racial disturbance in Memphis, was held without incident at Clayborn Temple, Memphis. About five hundred were in attendance despite the fact that the church can seat twenty-five hundred. The body was interred immediately after the funeral. Arkin advised that another sanitation workers’ sympathy march is scheduled for downtown Memphis on the afternoon of April 2.

Not recorded:

Eastern Airlines advised on April 2 that Rev. Martin Luther King, Jr., and Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) aides Ralph D. Abernathy, Bernard Lee, and Andrew M. Young were due to arrive in Memphis at 10:15 a.m. on April 3 from Atlanta.

J. E. D. Haydon
