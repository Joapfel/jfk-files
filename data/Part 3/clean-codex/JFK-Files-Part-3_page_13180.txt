Title: David Yaras  
Character: Anti-Racketeering

Synopsis:

Yaras was last observed in Miami in early August 1967, in company with Anthony Ricci and others. Yaras has not been known to be in Miami since August. A Miami informant reports that Yaras maintains a degree of control over the activities of Miami and Miami Beach gamblers and bookmakers for the Chicago organization. Yaras was last observed in Chicago on 10/24/67.
