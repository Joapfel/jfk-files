SENSTUDY.

Re Philadelphia NITEL to Director, July 29, 1975, Bureau phone call to Philadelphia and Philadelphia phone call to the Bureau, July 30, 1975.

On July 30, 1975, Robert Thweat, U.S. Navy commander, retired, advised he had no personal objection to his name being divulged. However, Thweat advised that his source is a relative and that once Thweat's name is divulged it would immediately identify his source of information.

Thweat advised his source had related to him that one Daniel O'Flaherty, who is employed with the Church Committee, had removed from his work area by hiding underneath his shirt a CIA memorandum containing derogatory information on Senator Henry Jackson. O'Flaherty then xeroxed the memorandum. The date for this occurrence is not known to Thweat.
