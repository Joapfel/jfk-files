Attached are Xerox copies of Federal income tax returns filed by Robert M. and his family. Also attached are Xerox copies of United States Income Corporation tax returns filed by Robert M. Shelton and a corporate officer of the United States of America, Inc. (USA), for the years 1964 (year ending 6/30/62) and 1963 (year ending 6/30/63). No null or a statement of no taxability for the year ending 6/30/63. Information not forth in these tax returns is for legal purposes only and should not be used in the determination of any investigative report.

The following comments and investigative instructions are noted in connection with these tax returns:

1. The individual returns filed jointly by Shelton and his wife during the period 1959 through 1963 indicate Shelton's occupation to be "liquorist" and "barber," and in no instance do they indicate any employment or association with the Firm. This is in conflict with the corporate returns filed by Shelton for the USA, where he indicated his position as "Imperial Wizard" or "President."

2. Shelton's return for 1959 indicates that it was prepared by E. R. Allen, Jr., Certified Public Accountant, Post Office Box 808, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. The form and typing on the other tax returns, both corporate and individual, indicate a strong likelihood that they too were prepared by Allen. You should review your files and conduct necessary investigation to determine if Allen is active in connection with financial matters relating to the Firm.
