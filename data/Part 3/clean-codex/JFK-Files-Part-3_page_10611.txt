4. Will participate with the governor in press releases on the arrests of Klansmen when appropriate. This has been done in the recent arrests in McComb, Mississippi, in connection with recent bombings in that area.

5. Information has been developed through an informant that a faction of the Klan is interested in putting another man in office rather than the present person. More information is being obtained on this so as to exploit all possibilities and create dissension among the leaders, and by doing so the opportunity may present itself to have the informant put in a high office.

The above is some of the potential counterintelligence action contemplated by this office. It is being studied on a day-to-day basis. The Bureau will be furnished with complete details on all the above plans as the opportunities are made and present themselves, prior to any specific action being taken by this office.
