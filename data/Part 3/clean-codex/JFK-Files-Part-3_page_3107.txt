Should any former employee contact your office and have any question regarding his obligation not to divulge information obtained by virtue of his past FBI employment, he should be instructed to contact legal counsel, FBIHQ, by collect call. Your conversations with former employees must be in keeping with our pledge. It is believed such a procedure would insure proper protection and also facilitate the work of the SSC.

The above procedure also applies to current employees of your office. However, contact with the legal counsel should be handled through the SAC.
