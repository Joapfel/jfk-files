FBI. Date: 1/4/78.

To: Director, FBI (62-117296), FOSFIRE. Attention: Congressional Inquiry Unit, Special Investigative Division.

From: SAC, Atlanta (66-2242) (RUC).

Subject: House Select Committee on Assassinations (HSCA). Bureau deadline: 1/13/78.

Re FBIHQ teletype to numerous offices, including Atlanta, 12/27/77.

The referenced Bureau teletype indicated that the HSCA was currently conducting an investigation into the assassination of Martin Luther King, Jr. (MURKIN). In connection with this investigation, the HSCA had requested that the FBI provide the following:

(1) Identification of field office case agent(s) assigned to the MURKIN investigation in recipient offices, including the division, section, or squad assignment and the nature of the agent's assignment immediately prior to involvement in the MURKIN investigation.

(2) Identification of field office case agent(s) assigned to Counterintelligence Program (COINTELPRO) efforts directed against King and the Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) during the period 1965–1968 in recipient offices, including the division, section, or squad assignment and the nature of the agent's assignment immediately prior to involvement in the COINTELPRO, King, and SCLC investigations.

In addition, recipients were to identify the case agent(s) involved in the security investigation of King and SCLC, as well as COINTELPRO.
