SENSTUDY 75.

Re: San Diego telephone call to Bureau, 12/4/75.

Enclosed for the Bureau are eight copies of a letterhead memorandum reflecting information received in the San Diego Office concerning Gary Thomas Rowe, Jr.

Deputy Scott Boies of the San Diego County Sheriff's Office was contacted and advised that if he felt he recognized Rowe, he should not discuss the matter openly with anyone, in that Rowe was obviously attempting to protect his identity. Deputy Boies was not advised that Rowe was identical with Thomas Neil Moore.
