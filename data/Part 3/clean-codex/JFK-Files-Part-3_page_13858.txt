Ecuador, Guayaquil (December 1962–January 1963).

HEG-2104.

This is unevaluated information. Source gradings are definitive. Appraisal of content is tentative.

Source: Ecuadorian (Ecuadorean) official (B) with access to travel information. Appraisal of content: 2.

1. During late December 1962, Ketty Romo Lereux Giron, a member of the Communist Party of Ecuador (PCE), returned to Guayaquil from the Soviet Union.

2. During early January 1963, Antonio Ruiz Flores, a member of the POE who had been in the Soviet Union for six months, returned to Milagro.

3. On 11 January 1963, Cesar Muñoz Vicuña, a member of the POE, was to travel to Moscow to undertake studies in civil engineering.

4. On 12 or 15 January 1963, Ecuador Padilla and Alfredo Almeida Vargas, members of the POE from Milagro, were scheduled to travel to Cuba.

5. Julio Olmedo Muñoz Gonzalez, Antonio Mosquera Anchundia, Jose Luis Cardenas Pinto, and Carlos Baste Castillo, members of the POE, were selected by the POE to travel to Cuba during the last half of January 1963.
