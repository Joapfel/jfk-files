United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. J. B. Adams

From: Legal Counsel

Subject: Senate Select Committee Testimony by the Director, December 9, 1975

Date: 12/4/75

Legislative Proposal to Change the Name of the J. Edgar Hoover FBI Building

In connection with the Director's appearance on December 9, 1975, to testify before the captioned committee, it is possible that he may be asked concerning recent proposals in Congress to change the name of the building. Three bills were introduced in the House of Representatives on December 1, 1975, for that purpose. H.R. 10905, introduced by Congressman Gude of Maryland, and H.R. 10918, introduced by Congresswoman Schroeder of Colorado, would change the name of the building to “FBI Building.” H.R. 10916, introduced by Congressman Rangel of New York, would change the name to “Federal Bureau of Investigation Building.” All three bills were referred to the House Committee on Public Works and Transportation, whose chairman is Congressman Jones of Alabama.

Recommendation:

For information of the Director in connection with his preparation for testimony.
