U.S. Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSC)

Reference is made to the December 18, 1975, letter of the SSC as modified at a conference on December 30, 1975, between Messrs. W. O. Cregar and P. V. Daly of this Bureau and Committee Staff Members Michael Epstein and Mark Gitenstein.

This memorandum effects delivery of material in response to Items 1(a), 1(b), and 1(e). The matters referred to are six FBI investigations about which the SSC was previously furnished materials relating to FBI preventive actions.

Concerning Item 1(b), which requested the first investigative report or letterhead memorandum (LMI) disseminated by FBI Headquarters (FBHQ) outside the FBI in each case, and a list of recipients of that report or LMI, the following should be noted. On the administrative pages accompanying each of these reports or LMI's there are notations which identify those agencies which received copies of these documents.

Item 1(e) requested annual reports or LMI's from the office of origin to FBHQ for the last five years during which each case was an open (pending) investigation. In lieu of annual reports from San Francisco Field Office, which is office of origin for the overall investigation of the Black Panther Party (BPP) nationally, reports are being furnished from our Detroit Field Office in whose territory the activity of interest actually took place.
