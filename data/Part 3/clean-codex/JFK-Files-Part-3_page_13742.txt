PRC Part I – DPOB 20 August 1964, Havana.

Request for Investigation – 23 March 1964. Soto to be used as PM trainee.

15 October 1963. Memo for Chief, Alien Affairs Staff as Jr. Robert Gambino, Security Officer, Special Operations Division. Soto to be employed under Project WITHRUSH in the Congo.

4 May 1966. Memo for File from Alien Affairs Office. Subject: Antonio Soto Vasquez, No. 242369 (c). Soto returned from Congo 8-9 on 20 December 1965. No longer of interest, and 500. File closed.

30 August 1966. Cable to Director from SWAHL. Soto requests return to Congo. Possibility exists that he had problems with polygraph test. Request additional decision regarding reuse in Congo if opportunity arises, and retest him.

Request for release denied in Cable 32999 and J. B. Scanlon from Director.
