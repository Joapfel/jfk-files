MR. GRISWOLD: I was not thinking of publications, but of letters. Did copies of such letters ever come to you in intelligence reports?

MR. RUSK: No. It wouldn't come to me in that form, sir. Normally it would come to me as a piece of information, and it would normally have some introductory phrase, and this was for the purpose, I suppose, of protecting sources, some introductory phrase saying usually reliable sources, or something of that sort.

Then if I wanted to prick up my ears and ask about the source for a particular item, I could do so and I would be told. I don't recall any instance, any information that came out of this mail that ever caused me to ask for a source, and it turned out to be opening mail.
