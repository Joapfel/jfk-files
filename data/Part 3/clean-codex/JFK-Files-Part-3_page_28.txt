OCI promptly set about adopting Smith’s suggestions as to content, and Smith commented that he had noticed the change. 11/

A major alteration in the PDB system occurred at the end of February 1967. Seemingly all that was involved was that President Johnson decided that he would rather get the Brief in the morning than in the evening.
