Presidential Authority. Cabinet 6, Drawer 2. Folder. First communication dated 5/19/54. Approximate pages: 1. Derogatory information: No. Category: Administrative. Ticklers only.

General content: Memorandum from Hoover advising that the Attorney General had stated in conference that a resolution for introduction in Congress was being considered which would give the President full authority to initiate military action in Indochina (Indo China). Hoover notes that this would impose heavy responsibility upon the FBI.
