United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation

Report of: Allan M. Trankley. Office: Miami, Florida. Date: 9/27/68. Field Office File No.: 92-1870. Bureau File No.: 92-9844. Title: Norman Rothman. Character: Anti-Racketeering.

Synopsis: Rothman still resides at Surfside, Florida. He is reportedly now sole owner of the Albion Lounge, although not observed at this place of business. Informant advised subject continues to travel around the country for the hoodlum element.

Details.

Residence: Subject continues to reside at 425 Surfside Boulevard, Surfside, Florida.

On August 29, 1968, Rothman's car was observed at his residence at 10:05 A.M.

On September 12, 1968, Rothman's car was observed at his residence at 1:15 P.M. His residence was observed from 1:15 P.M. to 2:15 P.M., and no activity was noted.
