1961: Edward J. Hamilton pseudonym used by Hunt in connection with the Cubon project in Florida.

Hunt traveled to India one or two times per year, two or three times to Paris, one or two times per year to London, and one or fewer times per year to Rome. His only domestic travel in 1963 would have been to New York. All travel was done in his own name. A. B. Edgell.

On 22 November 1963, Hunt and his wife were driving on H Street and 9th Street in Washington, D.C., when they heard the news on the car radio. They had been in a Chinese grocery store. On the way home to 5215 Walton Road, Summer, Maryland, they stopped at Sidwell School and picked up the upper Wisconsin Avenue. Hunt’s younger daughter, Kevin. Robert and Courtney Kevan remember that Robert Kennedy’s children, who attended the same school, had been picked up from school by the Secret Service, and the children knew it too.
