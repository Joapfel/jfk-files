The Foreign Service of the United States of America.

The American Embassy, Rome, Italy.

Date: July 30, 1963.

To: Director, FBI (92-3267).

From: Legat, Rome (92-77) (RUC).

Subject: John Roselli, aka Anti-Racketeering.

Re Los Angeles airtel 6/3/63, which requested Legat check birth records in Caserta, Italy.

Dr. Aristide Manopulo, Central Bureau, Criminal Division, Italian National Police, Rome, Italy, advised on July 26, 1963, that a check of the birth records in Caserta, Italy, and in the surrounding communities failed to reveal a record for Vincenzo Roselli, Maria Russo Roselli, or Giovanni Roselli. The records were also searched under the name Rosselli.

Dr. Manopulo stated all of the above individuals are unknown in Caserta and in the surrounding communities.

Bureau. Liaison Section. Los Angeles (92-113). Rome (92-77).
