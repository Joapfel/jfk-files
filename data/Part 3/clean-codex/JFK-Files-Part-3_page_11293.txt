NR007 DN PLAIN.

5:35 PM NITEL, March 26, 1975. JSGMAR, March 26, 1975.

To: Director, FBI.

Teletype.

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

From: Denver (66-DEAD).

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re Bureau tel to all offices, March 24, 1975.

Personnel assigned to Internal Security (IS) and Counterintelligence matters (CI):

SAC: 0. ASAC: 0. Supervisor: one full time, three-fourths of time to IS and one-fourth to CI. Agents: 12 and one-half, three-fourths time to IS and one-fourth time to CI.

In Denver office, IS and CI matters are handled by field supervisor on a full-time basis. Deserter fugitive cases are also handled on the squad, but only a fraction of the supervisor's and squad's time is used on these cases. SAC and ASAC are kept abreast of important developments and, of course, have ultimate authority, but have no IS or CI matters assigned to them. Besides the supervisor, there are eight special agents (SA) in headquarters city assigned full time to IS and CI matters, with only a small fraction of this time given to deserter fugitive cases. All bombing matters.
