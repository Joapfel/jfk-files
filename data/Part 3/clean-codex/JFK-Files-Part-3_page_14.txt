AD/CI R. J. Smith responded in a memorandum of 5 November 1964. He felt that the Agency would have a graceful way of dropping some of its publication if the publication were to undergo a transformation in which it would acquire a new name, a new cover, and a new format. It would become President Johnson's publication, specifically designed to meet his needs. To increase the chances of its being read by the Chief Executive, it would be delivered to conform to the President's working habits by being delivered in the late afternoon so that it could be part of his evening reading.

Smith noted that the Review, which Mr. Johnson “usually reads,” was for his evening perusal. Smith also noted the importance of finding a way to establish direct contact with the President so that OCI could get feedback.
