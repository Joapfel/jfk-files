Counterintelligence Programs

On July 26, 1968, FBIHQ approved a plan by the Tampa Office to have informants attend a State Officers' meeting of the Knights of the Invisible Empire (KOIE) on August 2, 1968, to vote for removal of Bill Richardson, Grand Dragon, KOIE. Action was suggested following a recent arrest of Richardson for arson.

This action isolated Richardson's influence and reduced his effectiveness.
