Teleype. Routine. January 19, 1978. From Director to Springfield.

The House Select Committee on Assassinations (HSCA) is currently conducting an investigation into the assassination of Martin Luther King, Jr. By letter dated December 30, 1977, to the Attorney General, the HSCA requested the FBI provide all files, documents, and other materials concerning Richard Geppert, born July 3], [94], residing at 340 Niedringhaus Avenue, Granite City, Illinois.

By letter dated January 6, 1978, the HSCA extended its request to include field office files. The HSCA also furnished a signed statement of Geppert, dated December 28, 1977, authorizing HSCA review of all material concerning him in possession of the FBI. In this statement Geppert also disclosed that he volunteered to HSCA staff members the extent and nature of his informant relationship with the FBI.
