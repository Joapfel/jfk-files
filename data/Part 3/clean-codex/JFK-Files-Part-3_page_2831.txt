FOXTROT, espionage—Cuba:

FOXTROT was the Bureau code name for a Cuban intelligence agent in Miami. Investigation was initiated on the basis of information from Bureau Source 6. All of the following documents were disseminated under the above caption:

Bureau airtel to New York, December 12, 1962, on new names and addresses in Cuba in the event Miami instructed to institute coverage of mail from Puerto Rico to Cuba.
