Central Intelligence Agency.

Country: Costa Rica/Cuba. Report No. CS-3/535,911. Subject: Inclusion of Militia Training in Communist Party Program. Date distributed: 4 February 1963. No. pages: 1. References: RD. Date of information: Mid-January 1963. Place and date acquired: Costa Rica, San José (17 January 63). Field report No. HRS-3009.

Source evaluations are definitive. Appraisal of content is tentative.

Source: Costa Rican (B) with good contacts in Communist Party circles, who talked with Lopez's mother. Appraisal of content: 3.

Militia training will be included in new courses of instruction soon to be started by the Partido Vanguardia Popular (PVP—Costa Rican Communist Party). Alvaro Lopez Vargas, a PVP member who was formerly an employee of Public Works, was sent to Cuba in June 1962 to be trained in militia organization. Upon his return to Costa Rica, Lopez will help conduct similar courses in Costa Rica.
