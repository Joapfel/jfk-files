Title: Ralph David Abernathy.

Character: Racial Matters.

Synopsis: Subject born 3/11/26, Linden, Alabama, resides at 690 Laverne Drive, NW, Atlanta, Georgia, serves as pastor of West Hunter Street Baptist Church and serves as President of the Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC). Served in the U.S. Army from 8/12/44 to 10/27/45, honorable discharge, ASN 349 94 505. Credit satisfactory. Atlanta Police Department records reflect three traffic violations. In connection with SCLC matters, subject has been in contact with individuals having prior CP membership or affiliation. Description set forth.

Details:

I. Background

A. Birth

The 1964 “yearbook” published by the Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC), 334 Auburn Avenue, NE, Atlanta, reflected Ralph D. Abernathy was born March 11, 1926, at Linden, Alabama.

B. Citizenship

By virtue of his birth, Abernathy is an American citizen.
