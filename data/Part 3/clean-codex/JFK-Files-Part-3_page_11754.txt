Dividing Domestic Intelligence Division into Two Divisions

Inspector E.S. Miller: Assistant to the Director Sullivan has suggested that Domestic Intelligence Division (DID) be divided into two separate divisions. In order that this matter can be analyzed during this inspection, you are requested to furnish:

1. Complete table of organization for each of two new divisions as you would organize them if the suggestion were adopted. Do not use names of supervisors, section chiefs, or Front Office administrators. Use only position designations, section and unit designations, and the number of agents and clerks designated for each unit and section.

2. In the event you feel work now being handled by another division should be realigned if the above suggestion is adopted, so indicate under the appropriate new division.

3. Following each of the two tables of organization for the above new divisions, set out section by section and, where appropriate, unit by unit, your reasoning for placement in the division. In this regard, it will be necessary to take into account and balance both volume of work and nature of work being handled.

4. Indicate advantages in adoption of the above suggestion.

5. Set out disadvantages you feel apparent in dividing DID. Also indicate how such can be overcome and resolved.

6. Since adoption of the above would involve possible realignment of space in some instances, you are requested to advise what new space would be necessary and what other movement of personnel and equipment would be involved.

Comments of Assistant Director requested.

See attached CB.
