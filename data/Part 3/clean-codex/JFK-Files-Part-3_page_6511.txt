October 15, 1975

U. S. Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSG)

Reference is made to the letter from the Domestic Intelligence Task Force Director, SSC, to the Special Counsel for Intelligence Coordination, Office of the Deputy Attorney General, dated October 7, 1975, wherein certain documents relating to mail intercept programs were enclosed. The referenced letter requested these documents be appropriately excised for future use at public hearings of the SSC.

This letterhead memorandum transmits to the SSC copies of these documents appropriately excised and, where necessary, declassified. The documents are being transmitted to the SSC for whatever use the Committee deems appropriate.

The Attorney General.

Enclosure.

Mr. J. A. Mintz. Mr. J. B. Hotis. Mr. W. R. Wannall. Mr. W. O. Cregar.
