FBI. Date: 8/16/63.

To: Director, FBI (92-3267). From: SAC, Los Angeles (92-113C). Re: John Roselli, aka AR.

LA 4396-C* advised Roselli not at his Los Angeles apartment from afternoon 8/9/63 until evening 8/12/63. Whereabouts during this period unknown.

On evening of 8/13/63 neighborhood source observed Roselli leaving his apartment carrying extra clothing. He left in his 1963 T-Bird, which was being driven by a dark-haired girl who was possibly his current girlfriend, Natalie V. Loughran, aka Vickie Lockwood. LA 4396-C* advised Roselli did not return to apartment until evening of 8/14/63. Roselli and girl may have driven to San Diego on evening 8/13/63. San Diego Office advised that Maurice H. Friedman registered for apartment #146 at Half Moon Inn, San Diego, California, a $65-per-day suite, on 8/13/63 and that there are “a number of hangers-on” with Friedman. San Diego has been requested to conduct investigation to determine if Roselli was also there.

LA 4268-C advised that Roselli was at the Friar's Club in Beverly Hills, California, on afternoon of 8/15/63. While there, he called an individual named Harry and discussed a check and denied that he had endorsed any check. Vern Lynch, IRS, advised that on the morning of 8/15/63 an IRS representative interviewed.

IRS [JFK Law 11(a)]
