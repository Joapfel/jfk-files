Memorandum to Mr. Tolson

Re: The Long Committee

I called Mr. DeLoach and briefed him on this problem in order that he might contact Senator Eastman in an effort to warn the Long Committee away from those areas which would be injurious to the national defense. (Of course, I made no mention of such a contact to the Attorney General.) Mr. DeLoach advised that Senator Eastman is in Mississippi and he will contact him upon his return Monday.

I didn't see what all the excitement was about. I thought there was no reason for discontinuing all traffic. There are technical discussions going on with some outside experts. We think it's reasonable to assume that Indiana and all other states will accept Indiana as well; believe it or not, we won't have any problems finding a justice.
