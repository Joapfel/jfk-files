The Honorable Griffin B. Bell, Attorney General, U.S. Department of Justice, Washington, D.C. 20530

Attention: Mr. Robert L. Keuch

Dear Mr. Attorney General:

In connection with the Select Committee's investigation into the circumstances surrounding the death of Martin Luther King, it would be appreciated if you could make available to the Committee staff materials in the files of the Federal Bureau of Investigation relating to the following individuals for the period from January 1, 1967, to December 31, 1968:

Love, Harvey Gene. Date of birth: 4/1/35. FBI number: 592-813A.

Pittman, George G. Date of birth: 6/20/38. FBI number: 919-933C.

Sheehan, Stephanie Helene. Date of birth: 7/26/53. FBI number: 951-133N4.

Shea, Jonathon Henry. Date of birth: 6/12/47. FBI number: 688-958J2.

Wright, Keith Albert. Date of birth: 7/27/44. FBI number: 122-750H.

Quenqui, Frank. Date of birth: 12/9/08. FBI number: 160-8374.

Rosenson, Randolph Erwin. Date of birth: 3/20/34. FBI number: 34-711E.

In addition, it would be appreciated if you could make available to the staff the criminal records for these individuals. This written request confirms an oral request made by myself to Mr. Dan Coulson on October 18, 1977, in the offices of the Committee.

Thank you.

GRB:DM

Copy to: Dan Coulson
