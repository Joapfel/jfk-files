Central Intelligence Agency.

Material contains information affecting the national defense of the United States within the meaning of the Espionage Laws, Title 18, U.S.C. Sections 793 and 794, the transmission or revelation of which in any manner to an unauthorized person is prohibited by law.

Country: Dominican Republic/Cuba. Subject: Travel of Dominican-Communist defendants to Cuba. Date of information: 14 December 1962. Place and date acquired: Ireland, Dublin (17 and 20 December 1962). Report No. TDCS-3/531,991. Date distributed: 21 December 1962. Precedence: Routine. References: IN 361.80; 3861.8. Appraisal: 2 (that the Dominicans were actually absent). This is unevaluated information. Source gradings are definitive. Appraisal of content is tentative. Source: An official Irish service.

1. On 14 December 1962, a Czech Airlines (CSA) plane transited Honolulu Airport, Ireland, en route to Habana, Cuba, from Prague, Czechoslovakia. Among the passengers were the following eight Dominican Communists:

A. Maximo Lopez Molina

B. Islander Selig Delmonte

C. Tomas Pamiento Erickson Alvarez

D. Jerry Estrella Jacob O'Connor (ALEC reported as Jerry Estvilla Jacob)

E. Juan Jose López Fernández

F. Ramon (RANOM) Aguirren Finido Mujia

G. Hugo Francisco Cabrera Garcia

H. Gustavo Federico Ricart Ricart.
