U. S. Department of Justice, Communications Section.

August 3, 1961. Teletyping.

Urgent, 8-3-61, 4:54 PM TGH.

To Director, FBI, 92-2914, 92-2918. From SAC, Pittsburgh, 92-236, 92-228.

Gabriel Mannarino, AKA, AR; Samuel Mannarino, AKA, AR. On August third, sixty-one, PG 545-C advised that Gabriel Mannarino has bought or leased a farm in Westmoreland County, believed near Camp Joan, PA. He is using the barn to hold gambling games in anticipation of a move by a new city administration in New Kensington, PA, to suppress gambling activities in town. Informant will attempt to obtain further information concerning location of farm and identities of persons involved. Both subjects should be considered armed and dangerous.
