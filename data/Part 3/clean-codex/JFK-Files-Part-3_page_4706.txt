Federal Bureau of Investigation. U.S. Department of Justice. Communications Section.

November 2, 1962. Teletype. Urgent, 11-2-62, 8:29 PM MB.

To Director, FBI, 92-3267. From SAC, Los Angeles, 92-1023C. John Roselli, aka AR.

LA-4268-C advised Roselli at the Friar's Club afternoon November 1. During the afternoon, the name of Pat O'Malley, who is allegedly affiliated with vending machine operations in the Chicago area, was mentioned, and Roselli stated that he knew everyone in the rackets in Chicago but that he had never heard of O'Malley. Roselli also said that in Las Vegas quoted odds are eight to five that Pat Brown will win over Nixon in the race for governor of California. Roselli then added that he was betting on Brown to win but was going to vote for Nixon. In this regard, it is noted that Roselli is not a registered voter in Los Angeles County and, because of his conviction in 1943, is not entitled to vote.

Neighborhood sources advise Roselli is still in Los Angeles.

Hertz Corporation, Beverly Hilton Hotel station, advised Roselli still has possession of rental car, which he stated he would return.
