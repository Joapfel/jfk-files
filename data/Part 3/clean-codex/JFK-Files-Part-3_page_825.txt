Senator Church's Committee Investigating CIA Activities.

SENSTUDY 75. Information concerning.

Re Omaha telecalls to Bureau and Minneapolis, June 19, 1975.

In the June 19, 1975 afternoon issue of the Omaha World-Herald, a daily newspaper, Omaha, Nebraska, appeared the following article captioned “Senate Committee Sending Investigation to Gordon, Nebraska.” The article appeared under a World-Herald Washington bureau dateline.

Contact with Omaha World-Herald authorities at Omaha failed to develop the nature of the Senate Committee's investigation at Gordon, Nebraska. World-Herald authorities.
