January 19, 1976

U. S. Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (SSC)

Reference is made to the SSC request dated January 7, 1976.

On January 7, 1976, copies of the documents to be used as exhibits to the public hearings on FBI mail openings were delivered to the FBI for review of their sanitization in terms of their public release in published form.

These documents were reviewed, marked for some additional excisions, and returned to the SSC on January 9, 1976, for whatever use the Committee deems appropriate.
