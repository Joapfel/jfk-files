1. Did the CIA intercept a letter and money order from Marguerite Oswald (Osuall) to Lee Harvey Oswald shortly after Oswald defected to the Soviet Union?

CIA says no.

Mail intercept program discussed in News Comm report.

No determination was made as to how the FBI learned of the letter and money order because FBI functioning was not within our charter.

Even if the CIA had intercepted the letter and reported it to the FBI, it would not seem distinguishable from other mail interceptions or justify a separate investigation.

The mail intercept program of the CIA was not directed against Oswald individually.

The Warren Commission received detailed evidence from the FBI with respect to the letter and money order.

Certainly, if Oswald was a CIA agent, as some have alleged, the CIA would not have been reporting to the FBI on such mail received by Oswald.
