Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re Bureau teletype dated March 24, 1975, captioned as above.

Internal security and counterintelligence matters are supervised exclusively on the ASAC's desk in the Knoxville Division. It is estimated that the ASAC spends approximately 25 percent of his time on such matters, divided 15 percent to internal security and 10 percent to counterintelligence.

In addition, there are five agents within the division working primarily on security matters. Two of these agents spend 80 percent of their time on internal security work and 5 percent of their time on counterintelligence; one agent spends 75 percent on internal security and 20 percent on counterintelligence; one agent spends 60 percent on internal security and 35 percent on counterintelligence; and one agent spends 20 percent on internal security and 75 percent on
