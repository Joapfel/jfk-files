Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

NRO02 SV Code.

7:15 PM NITEL, 3/25/75 PNR.

To: Director. From: Savannah. Date: March 25, 1975. Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

REBUNITEL all SACs, March 24, 1975.

In response to RENITEL, the following breakdown in personnel assigned to internal security and counterintelligence matters is being set forth:

1 SAC: Internal Security 0; Counterintelligence 0.  
1 ASAC: Internal Security 0; Counterintelligence 0.  
1 Supervisor: Internal Security 40 percent; Counterintelligence 10 percent.  
1 Agent: Internal Security 90 percent; Counterintelligence 0.  
1 Agent: Internal Security 50 percent; Counterintelligence 10 percent.  
1 Agent: Internal Security 10 percent; Counterintelligence 0.  
1 Agent: Internal Security 0; Counterintelligence 30 percent.

All other agents' accumulative total of time would equal 10 percent for internal security and 0 for counterintelligence for one agent.

End.
