Adequacy of Personnel  
Internal Security Section

Section Chief A. W. Gray: As of close of business 8/17/71, the authorized personnel of the Internal Security Section was 17 Special Agents, 7 Secretaries, and 13 Clerks. A breakdown by units follows.

Front Office. Authorized personnel as of close of business 8/17/71: Section Chief, 1; Secretary, 1; Number One Man, 1; Secretary, 1.

Communist Party, USA, Unit: Supervisors, 1; Secretary, 1; Clerks, 1.

Foreign Influence and Pro-Chicom Unit: Supervisors, 3 (SA Benedict assigned temporarily to Nationalities Intelligence); Secretary, 1; Clerks, 2.

Klan and White Hate Groups Unit: Supervisors, 2; Secretary, 1; Clerks, 2.

Security Informant Unit: Supervisors, 4 (SA Seabaugh assigned temporarily to Nationalities Intelligence); Secretary, 1; Clerks, 5.

Subversive Individuals Unit: Supervisors, 5 (SA W. L. Smith extended sick leave beginning 6/25/71); Secretary, 1; Clerks, 3.
