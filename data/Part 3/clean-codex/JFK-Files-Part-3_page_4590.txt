G. If cooperative and if her confidence can be gained, it is believed that she could furnish valuable information concerning current Cuban matters based on her association with persons who have been so involved.

Bureau note:

Current inquiries of subject are based on a report that subject was praising Castro in her conversations. Since she has apparently changed her attitude toward Castro, an interview of her would be a logical method to determine if she possesses any information of value regarding the Cuban situation and to appraise her informant potential.
