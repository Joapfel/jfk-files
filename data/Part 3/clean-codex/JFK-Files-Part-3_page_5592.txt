Information not set forth in these tax returns is for lead purposes only and should not be used without the details of any investigative report.

The following comments and investigatory instructions are noted in connection with these tax returns:

1. The individual returns filed jointly by Shelton and his wife during the period 1959 through 1963 indicate Shelton's occupation to be “astronomer” and “manager,” and in no instance do they indicate any employment or association with the Klan (Ellen). This is in conflict with the corporate returns filed by Shelton for the USA, where he indicated his position as “Imperial Wizard” or “President.”

2. Shelton's return for 1959 indicates it was prepared by E. F. Allen, Jr., Certified Public Accountant, Post Office Box 301, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. The form and typing on these other tax returns, both corporate and individual, indicate a strong likelihood that they too were prepared by Allen. You should review your files and conduct the necessary investigation to determine if Allen is active in connection with financial matters relating to the firm.
