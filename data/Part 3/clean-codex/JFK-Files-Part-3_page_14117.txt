President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board

Revised Agenda for Meeting of January 26, 1963

1. 9:00–9:30 — Executive Session.
2. 10:45 — Depart Executive Office Building for Pentagon.
3. 11:00 — Review of Results Obtained from Reconnaissance Relating to the Military Buildup in Cuba. Lt. General Joseph Carroll, Director, Defense Intelligence Agency, Room 2 C 468. Members will be met at the Mall Entrance.
4. 12:25 — Depart 2 C 468.
5. 12:30 — Meeting with the Chairman, Joint Chiefs of Staff, Room 2 E 873.
6. 1:00 — Meeting with the Secretary of Defense, Room 3 E 880.
7. 1:30 — Return to Washington — Lunch.
8. 2:45 to end of day — Completion of Final Report to the President on Board's Cuba Review and Discussion of Future Board Business.
