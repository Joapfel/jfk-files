Dear Al:

This will confirm our conversation on November 26, 1975, wherein you authorized the Bureau to use a videotape recording of your “live” telecast concerning the appearance of Deputy Associate Director James B. Adams before the Church Committee. You indicated that this was authorized for the exclusive viewing by FBI personnel.

This is to advise you that the videotape of Mr. Adams’ testimony is being restricted solely to FBI personnel. We certainly appreciate your consideration in this regard. Thank you for this authority.
