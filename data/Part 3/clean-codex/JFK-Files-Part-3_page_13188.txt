In conclusion, MART stated that he was sure that if MIKE COPPOLA was alive there would have been no violence in the area. He related that COPPOLA had silent but firm control over gambling matters, and he did not believe in rough stuff. It was MART's opinion that a new young group of individuals are attempting a takeover in the area and are resorting to any means available to prove their position. He indicated that FRANK ROSENTHAL, DAVE YARAS and BENNY HUSICK would certainly fall under the category he had just mentioned.
