(2) In response to questions regarding improper conduct by FBI employees, I stated that alleged violations of law by FBI personnel should be investigated by the FBI or other appropriate agency; that the Inspection Division has conducted inquiries regarding allegations of misconduct; that an Office of Professional Responsibility has just been established in the Justice Department, and we will advise that office of our major investigations of departmental personnel, including FBI employees, for alleged violations of law, regulations, or standards of conduct; that I would reserve comment regarding possible creation of a national inspector general to consider matters of misconduct by employees of any federal agency.
