Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Communications Section

March 27, 1975

Teletype

NR015. No code. 7:15 p.m., urgent, March 27, 1975. JMF.

To Director, FBI.

From SAC, New Orleans (66-2832).

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section, Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (INTELIGENCE ACTIVITIES).

Re Bureau NITEL March 24, 1975; New Orleans teletype March 26, 1975; BUTEL call March 27, 1975.

The following are corrections to referenced New Orleans teletype. Number of supervisors assigned to Internal Security (IS) and counterintelligence matters, one; number of agents assigned to IS matters, twelve, of which seven are also assigned to counterintelligence matters. Percentage of agent time spent on IS matters, 25 percent, and percentage of agent time spent on counterintelligence matters, 35 percent. Percentage of supervisory time spent on IS matters, 25 percent, and 35 percent spent on counterintelligence matters.

End message.

DBS FBIHQ CLR
