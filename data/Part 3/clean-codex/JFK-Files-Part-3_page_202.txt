Counterintelligence Programs

In August 1964, Mrs. C. P. Kandahl, Seattle Ski Club, was contacted in order to have her deny use of the Seattle Ski Club site at Snoqualmie Pass, Washington, to members of the Young Political Explorers League (YPEL), a Communist Party (CP) front, for use as a summer camp. YPEL had arranged camp rental under the guise of using the camp for underprivileged children. Mary Sutherland, wife of District CP Organizational Secretary Milford Sutherland, was advised that the camp's water system had broken down; therefore, the camp could not be held.
