Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

May 2, 1975. Teletype. Urgent, 7:00 PM CST. SENSTUDY 75.

Date: 1/19/61.

To: Director, FBI (62-116395). From: Omaha (62-NEW) (P).

Re: Bureau teletype, May 2, 1975.

Former SA John J. Quinn was contacted by me personally and advised per instructions in retelegram. He was appreciative of advance notice regarding a possible interview and stated that he would desire an agent be present if such interview occurs. Stated he would personally contact me if he is contacted by staff members.

Bureau will be kept advised.

End.
