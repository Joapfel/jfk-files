Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

27 March 1975.

NR002 SC PLAIN. 9:23 a.m., urgent, 3/27/75. To Director. From Sacramento (66-511). Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re Bureau NITEL, 24 March 1975.

In headquarters, Sacramento has one supervisor assigned full time to internal security and counterintelligence matters. Seven agents are assigned full time to internal security matters; three agents are assigned full time to counterintelligence matters; one agent expends 75 percent on counterintelligence matters and 25 percent on internal security.

In resident agencies, one agent spends 90 percent on internal security and 10 percent on counterintelligence; one agent spends 50 percent on internal security and 50 percent on counterintelligence. Seven RAs handle a variety of classifications and also handle internal security and counterintelligence matters. These seven agents spend approximately 25 percent of their time on internal security and counterintelligence matters: 20 percent on internal security and 5 percent on counterintelligence.

End.
