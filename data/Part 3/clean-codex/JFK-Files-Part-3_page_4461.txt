Federal Bureau of Investigation. Reporting office: Philadelphia. Office of origin: Philadelphia. Date: 11/24/59. Investigative period: 10/7/59–11/17/59. Title of case: Angelo Bruno, aka. Report made by: J. Robert Pearce. Typed by: MMR. Character of case: Anti-racketeering.

Reference: Report of SA J. Robert Pearce dated 10/8/59 at Philadelphia.

Leads.

Miami. At Miami, Florida.

1. Will remain in contact with Miami 655-PC who, according to Miami letter to Philadelphia, 12/31/58, dined at the Luau Restaurant, North Bay Village, Florida, on 11/19/58 with Angelo Bruno. It should be learned from this informant if Bruno has liquidated his interest in Cuba; if Bruno has other financial interests in the Dominican Republic; if Bruno intends to return to the Miami, Florida, vicinity; and the identity of all individuals with whom Bruno had interests in the Hotel Plaza Gambling Casino in Havana, Cuba.

Approved. Special Agent in Charge.
