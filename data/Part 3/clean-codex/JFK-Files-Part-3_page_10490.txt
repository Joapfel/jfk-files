March 25, 1975, 11:00 a.m.

New York, March 25, 1975

The President of the Senate to the Governor of the State of New York, by telephone, within the presence of the House of Representatives, to the Chief Judges, Counsel, etc.

By Joe Gordon

Joseph L. Gordon, Jr., President of the Senate

By John M. Carmine, Jr., Governor of the State of New York

By George R. Harris, Jr., Chief Judge, etc.

The President of the Senate in his captainship is hereby certified that the proposed amendments do not comply with Article XIX, paragraph (a), or paragraph (b) of the bill before it is subject to consideration by the House of Representatives.

The President of the Senate has been requested by the Chief Judge to provide an additional hour for consideration of the proposed amendments. The Senate will continue to reconvene at 10:00 a.m. on March 26th, and resume its session thereafter.

Joseph L. Gordon, Jr., President of the Senate

John M. Carmine, Jr., Governor of the State of New York

By George R. Harris, Jr., Chief Judge, etc.

The President of the Senate undertakes to transmit a copy of this notice by facsimile to each Republican House member and to each of the Senate chiefs of staff. The original document will be submitted within four hours thereof.

Lori B. Spartan, Secretary of the Senate

Joshua S. Davis, Secretary of the Governor

James M. Smith, Interim Governor
