United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. J. B. Adams  
From: Legal Counsel  
Subject: SENSTUDY 75  
Date: 10/22/75

On 10/22/75, Barbara Banoff, Senate Select Committee Staff Member, requested that former SAs George C. Moore and Fred H. Baumgardner be made available for deposition concerning their knowledge of COINTELPRO.

Recommendation: That former SAs Moore and Baumgardner be released from their existing employment agreements and the provisions of the Code of Federal Regulations for purposes of deposition.

Personnel File — George C. Moore, ST-111.  
Personnel File — Fred H. Baumgardner.
