Title: Norman Rothman  
Character: Anti-racketeering

Synopsis:

Subject, a resident of Miami Beach, Fla., was born 12/26/14 in New York City; was active in gambling in Cuba in the Sans Souci Nightclub and reportedly controlled all slot machines in Cuba except those in Havana. Subject's gambling activity in Cuba was reportedly backed financially by the Mannarino brothers from New Kensington, Pa., and Patsy Erra, all LCN figures. Subject was convicted in 1960 with others attempting to smuggle guns, stolen from a National Guard armory in Ohio, to Cuba and received a 5-year prison term and $10,000 fine. Subject was reportedly an associate of Santo Trafficante (SANTO' XTRAFFICANTE), Jr., an LCN figure from Tampa, Fla. Subject is currently reported to have an undisclosed interest in Albion Lounge, Miami Beach, and to be employed with International Lithographers. Also reportedly deeply involved in the shylock business with Patsy Erra. Physical description set out.
