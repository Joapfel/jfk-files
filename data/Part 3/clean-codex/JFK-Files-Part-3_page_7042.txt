Office of Management and Budget. Route slip.

To: Mr. Hoobler.

From: Jim Purcell. Date: 3-7-75.

Remarks:

Mr. Hoobler:

Will you please provide the requested information indicated by Mr. Scantleburg's memo of February 24 pertaining to the FBI. Item “8,” which refers to the FBI Internal Security Division, should be interpreted as the FBI's Intelligence Division.

I would appreciate having this information, if possible, by close of business today.
