Dear Sir:

Enclosed is our 1967 income tax return. Salary for which there are no W-2 forms comes from several short- or part-time jobs that did not withhold.

Just as last year, we cannot, of course, voluntarily pay our tax so long as the war in Vietnam continues. Should the government run short of funds in the meantime, we suggest repeal of the oil depletion allowance or any other of the tax breaks for the wealthy.

Enclosure.
