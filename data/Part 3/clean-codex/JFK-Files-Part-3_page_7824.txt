July 16, 1975

Mr. John T. Elliff, Director  
Domestic Intelligence Task Force  
United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities  
Washington, D.C. 20510

Dear Mr. Elliff:

Your letter of July 16, responding to mine of July 15, was, as you may recall, hand-delivered to me by Mr. Dick at 12:45 today. I understand that your interpretive amplification is generally shared by Mr. Schwarz.

We are studying the suggestions you have made; in the meantime, of course, we are prepared to proceed in accordance with the provisions of the July 15 letter.

cc: Deputy Attorney General  
Doug Marvin  
John Mintz
