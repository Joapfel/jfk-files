NR 09 PG PLAIN.

March 25, 1975. Teletype.

7:35 PM NITEL, March 25, 1975, RGM.

To: Director, FBI.

From: Pittsburgh (62-NEW).

Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

REBUNITEL, March 24, 1975.

There are 110 special agents assigned to the Pittsburgh Office (75 headquarters city agents, 35 resident agents (RAs)).

One supervisor assigned counterintelligence (CI) full time.

One supervisor assigned internal security (IS) full time.

11 headquarters agents assigned CI full time and 7 headquarters agents assigned IS full time.

Resident agent assignments as follows:

6 RAs 2 percent CI; 5 RAs 2 percent IS.
3 RAs 3 percent CI; 2 RAs 3 percent IS.
3 RAs 5 percent CI; 1 RA 4 percent IS.
2 RAs 6 percent CI; 2 RAs 5 percent IS.
3 RAs 7 percent CI; 1 RA 7 percent IS.
2 RAs 8 percent CI; 2 RAs 11 percent IS.
1 RA 25 percent CI; 1 RA 12 percent IS.
