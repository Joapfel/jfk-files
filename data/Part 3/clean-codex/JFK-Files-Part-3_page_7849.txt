Re: Interview of FBI SA by SSC

Matter and that there was some indication that Dr. King was being influenced by persons who leaned toward the left.

Q: Were you told that such people might be with Dr. King?

A: I do not recall. I simply have the recollection that our purpose was to determine whether he was being so influenced. I hasten to admit, however, that I am very hazy on this and it may very well be that I learned of these indications through conversations even after the occurrence of our monitoring. I cannot be more specific in this regard.

Q: Who was with you at the time of the monitoring?

A: Bill Campbell.

Q: Was anyone else involved?

A: I believe I did meet Bill Bergeron in the lobby of the hotel. No one else to my recollection.

Q: Did you sign out from the office to go to the hotel?

A: This would be the customary procedure, but whether it was done in this instance I do not recall.

Q: Did you handle any equipment, i.e., take any to the hotel?

A: No. My recollection is that the equipment was already set up in the room we occupied.
