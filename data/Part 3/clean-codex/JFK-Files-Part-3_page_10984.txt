April 30, 1975

Material for Senate Select Committee (SSC) Investigating Intelligence Activities.

2. Originating organization: FBI.

3. Nature of material: Letterhead memorandum.

4. Document classification: Unclassified.

5. National security information stamp: NA.

6. Summary of contents:

LIM refers to the request of the SSC for a list of names and titles of certain FBI Intelligence Division (INTD) personnel at FBHQ and numbers only of certain other INTD personnel, and relates the agreement reached between FBI officials and Mr. William Miller, Staff Director of the SSC, that the original request be modified as follows. The FBI will prepare the necessary list and make it available for review at FBHQ by appropriately cleared personnel of the SSC staff.

7. Requester/date of request: SSC, 3/19/75.

8. Releasing authority: FBI REC-106 6/2-1/6 3/23-100.

9. Date of submission: 4/8/75.

10. Location of file copy: FBI file 62-116395-53.
