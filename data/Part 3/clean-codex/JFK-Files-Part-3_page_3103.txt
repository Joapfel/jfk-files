SENSTUDY-75.

Rebutel May 2, 1975.

In connection with the work of the Senate and House Select Committees, their representatives may contact your office for information.

In one recent instance, a representative of the Senate Select Committee telephonically inquired as to the identity of the SAC in a particular office during 1973.

In handling such inquiries, insure establishing bona fides of the representative by showing credentials on personal contact or, if telephonic contact, by telephoning back to the committee.

Unless information is of a public nature, as in the instance cited above, obtain FEIN clearance prior to supplying any information. FEIN must be expeditiously advised of all information furnished.

End.
