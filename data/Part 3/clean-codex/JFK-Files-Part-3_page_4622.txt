FBI.

Manuel Adalberto Arques Diaz, also known as Manuel Arce, Mario Arce, “Manny,” “Manolo,” and “Manolito,” IS-Cuba; Registration Act—Cuba, is subject pending Bufile 105-83117, Miami file 105-2221, in which case Miami is origin.

Arques, a self-admitted supporter of Prio and naturalized U.S. citizen of Cuban birth, formerly was publicity director of the Cuban Tourist Commission at Miami until he resigned during November 1960.

Arques has been identified by several sources as having been an intelligence agent in the U.S. for Juan Orta, who until recently was the Secretary of Fidel Castro.

Upon interview by BuAgents on 12/1/60, Arques denied ever having been a Cuban intelligence agent. However, he admitted sending written and verbal messages to Orta during 1959 concerning activities in the U.S. of “Batistianos.” Arques stated he is not in accord with policies of the Castro government and considers Communism to be dominant in the Castro regime.

The following description of Arques was obtained through observation and interview by SAs William P. Kelly and William B. Holloman on 12/1/60:

Name: Manuel Adalberto Arques Diaz. Also known as: Manolo Arques, Manolito Arques, “Manny.”

Special Agent in Charge.
