Legal Control

Select Committee on Intelligence Activities

All information contained herein is to be classified. Date 1-19-81 by S.P.A. U/R.

During various contacts with William Miller, a staff director of the State Select Committee, and Chairman Lesley Valdez (D-Jack), chairman of the House Select Committee, the question comes up regarding the private offices of individual and corporate members and the presence of electronic listening devices. Committee personnel have been told that we consider such activity essential, and they agree.

In exploring this matter, it was determined that the Capitol Police Department recently established a special unit to handle all types of security problems within the Capitol, including checks for electronic listening devices. Personnel of this sort have received extra training from the IJCA. The operations of the unit are controlled by the persons at the Army of the House and Senate.

This matter has been discussed with Robert Song, Deputy Sergeant at Arms of the Senate, and with General Durkee, Sergeant at Arms of the House. Usega advised that the special unit has already conducted photo security surveys of the new office space of the Senate Select Committee and certainly will perform any other tasks of this type needed by the Committee or its individual members. He said, in fact, that since they now have this capability, it may well become an outside agency wishing to call on them to perform the task. We must make no commitment to do so until after hearing regarding the new method security changes. After that, if still felt necessary, write them a notice of certain electronic listening devices, see check. I also said their scale requires prior approval before they can be installed. I understand.
