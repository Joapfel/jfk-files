OLSEN: Did the whole operation get started again, then?

STURGIS: No, sir. I believe the operation was aborted. That is one reason why, after two hours in port, we were picked up and charges were put against us. The boat and the crew were sent back to Mexico. My men and I were then deported after a trial under a judge there and sent back to Miami, and that was the end of the operation.
