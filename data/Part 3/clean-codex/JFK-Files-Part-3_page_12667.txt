Re: Interview of Retired FBI SA by SSC

“Q. What was the gist of such communications?

“Q. Advisement that such installation had been authorized, instructions to initiate the coverage.

“Q. Who installed the surveillances, Al Miller; was he not a technician, a viretan?

“Q. Your answer to that question is not entirely correct. Alden Miller was an Atlanta Agent who had certain technical ability. It is my recollection the surveillance was installed by an Agent who was not from the Atlanta Office. I do not remember his name.

“Q. Did you monitor any of these surveillances?

“Q. No.

“Q. Did you monitor any such surveillance during this period of time?

“Q. Yes. I recall on two occasions filling in for another employee in connection with telephone surveillance of the headquarters of the Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee. I did not monitor the King or SCIG telephone surveillance.

“Q. What was the nature of the investigation of SKCC (SNCC)?

“Q. To determine communist influence of that organization.

“Q. What is your recollection of the Bureau’s efforts to influence any political campaign?

“Q. I have no such recollection.
