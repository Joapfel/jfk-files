(5) That when intelligence information is reported that later proves erroneous, appropriate corrective notification be given to recipients of the erroneous reports.

(6) That the DCI and the Chairman, Joint Chiefs of Staff, ensure the validity of intelligence collection requirements levied upon the CIA for support of the military in wartime.

(7) That the Defense Department review the Attaché Systems of the three Military Services to determine the advisability of establishing a single Military Attaché System.

(8) That the Defense Department ensure adequate foreign language training of Military Attachés prior to their assignment to overseas posts.

(9) That the Defense Department staff Military Attaché posts on the basis of specific intelligence needs and on the basis of linguistic and other specialized talents.

(10) That the Defense Department, for intelligence-related purposes, ensure efficient allocation of aircraft to Military Attachés at overseas posts.

(11) That the Defense Department equip field elements of the Critical Communications (CRITICOMM) Network with capability for rapid communication of critical messages among major and subordinate military commands in the field.

(12) That the DCI review NSC 5412/2 and related National Security Council Intelligence Directives, and recommend any necessary revisions to ensure adequate guidance to U.S. Ambassadors and CIA Station Chiefs overseas, with regard to (1) covert actions directed at targets within foreign countries, and (2) covert actions directed against third country targets.
