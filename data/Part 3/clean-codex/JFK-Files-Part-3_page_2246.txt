Note: The attached LHM is a response to that portion of the SSC request of December 18, 1975, dealing with the GAO review and the September 24, 1975, GAO interim report on FBI domestic intelligence investigations. GAO's final report, expected originally late in 1975, will probably be issued in January 1976.

Item 2 of the SSC letter asked for “any information which you have refuting the suggestion...of the General Accounting Office's September 24 report to the House Judiciary Committee, that cases in which violence can be predicted probably represent less than 2 percent of all FBI domestic intelligence investigations.”
