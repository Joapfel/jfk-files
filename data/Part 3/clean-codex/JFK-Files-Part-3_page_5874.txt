Stanley Levison

A confidential source advised in April 1964 that Stanley Levison was a secret member of the Communist Party (CP), USA, in July 1963. In late 1963 and early 1964, he criticized the CP for not being sufficiently militant in the civil rights struggle. Levison was described by certain CP leaders as being to the “left” of the CP in his position on civil rights. Levison’s differences with the CP, however, are merely tactical, and he continues his ideological adherence to Communism.
