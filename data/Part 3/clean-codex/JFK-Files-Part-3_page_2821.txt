The files of the Miami Office contain the following information on “mail surveillance” by or on behalf of the FBI from January 1, 1960 to the present time.

(1) Mail opening or mail intercept.

On January 2, 1963, Miami began screening airmail to Cuba emanating from Puerto Rico in connection with the case captioned “Penetrate, Espionage—Cuba,” BUFILE 65-67842, Office of Origin San Juan 65-398, Miami 65-2940.
