Federal Bureau of Investigation  
U.S. Department of Justice  
Communications Section

November 14, 1962

Teletype

Urgent, 11/13/62, 8:54 PM AH

To Director, FBI 92-3267  
From SAC, Los Angeles 92-113C 2P

John Roselli, AKA AR.

LA 4268-C advised he observed Roselli at Friars Club, Beverly Hills, the evening of November 9 last and the late afternoon and early evening of November 12 last. Neighborhood source learned Roselli was at his Los Angeles apartment the morning of November 13 instant.

According to LA 4268-C, on November 12 last, Roselli was at Friars Club with “Commander” Herman Spitzel, close friend and former employer of Roselli. Roselli remarked, “The dirty SOBs are digging into two of my corporations, but they are not going to find anything because I have a guy working for me that has it so screwed up they will never find anything.”

IRS [JFK Law 11(a)]
