Connections with the Communist Party.

Membership in the Communist Party.

LA T-3 (3/29/69). On March 29, 1959, source advised that Angela Yvonne Davis is a member in good standing of the Che Lumumba Club, Moranda Smith Section (MSS) SCDOP, Los Angeles, California.

Davis' attendance at SCDOP meetings.

LA T-8 (8/1/68); LA T-9 (8/1/68). On July 21, 1968, Angela Yvonne Davis attended a general membership meeting of the SCDOP at 7213 Beverly Boulevard, Los Angeles. Topic of discussion centered around the need for political reorganization of the working class.

LA T-10 (8/9/68). On July 23, 1968, Angela Yvonne Davis attended a staff meeting of the SCDOP at 1254 East Manchester, Los Angeles, California.
