AT-T-3, 8/61

ABERNATHY on June 9, 1963, conferred with Rev. KING, CLARENCE JONES, and STANLEY LEVISON (LEVISION) as to methods of dramatizing a proposed march on Washington, D.C., intending to pressure Congress into enacting Civil Rights legislation.

AT-T-3, 6/63

During the early part of May, 1968, ABERNATHY informed STANLEY LEVISON (LEVISION) he was scheduled to speak on May 31, 1968, at commencement exercises of Alabama State College. ABERNATHY requested LEVISON (LEVISION) prepare a speech for him to utilize on this occasion and LEVISON (LEVISION) agreed to do so.

AT-T-4, 5/68

On May 5, 1968, ABERNATHY discussed with STANLEY LEVISON (LEVISION) a proposal that SCLC become a membership organization and conduct a membership drive. They agreed the time was appropriate for implementation of a membership drive.

AT-T-4, 5/68

STANLEY LEVISON (LEVISION) on May 7, 1968, advised WILLIAM A. RUTHERFORD, Executive Director, SCLC, he would be unable to meet with him and ABERNATHY on May 8, 1968, as previously arranged for the purpose of discussing how operations of SCLC could be made more efficient.
