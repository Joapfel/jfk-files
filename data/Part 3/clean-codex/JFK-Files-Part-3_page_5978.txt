Sensitive Case Report.

To: District Director; ARCO; Southeast Regional Office Division; Director; National Office. Instructions: Through channels to ARC concerned in an original and three copies. For detailed instructions, see RC SE Memorandum No. 12-18.

1. Name of case or identification of situation: Robert M. Shelton, Lake Sherwood, Northport, Alabama. Class of tax: Income Tax. Taxable period: 1963, 1964. Amount of tax: Unknown.

All FBI information contained hereby is unclassified. Date: 10-13-66. By: SP ALM 1116.

2. Principal issues involved: Diverting Klan money to personal use.

3. Reasons for sensitivity: Taxpayer is the Imperial Wizard of the United Klans of America, K.K.K., Inc. He is presently being investigated by the House Un-American Activities Committee of Congress. He and the Klan have had national publicity as a result of the Congressional hearings and civil rights activities in the South.

4. Action taken or proposed: The taxpayer has been contacted by Revenue Agent John H. Murrell on two occasions prior to referral to the Intelligence Division. He has indicated he will cooperate with the examining agents as soon as his records are released by the House Un-American Activities Committee.

This case was referred to the Intelligence Division only a few days ago; therefore, a realistic estimate of the completion date will not be practicable until at least next month.

It is prepared in response to request and is not for dissemination outside your Committee. Its use is limited to official proceedings by your Committee and the content may not be disclosed to unauthorized personnel without the express approval of the FBI.

Submitted by (Originator's Signature). Title: Special Agent. Post of duty: Birmingham. Date: 1-17-66.

Reviewed (District Director's Signature). District: Birmingham. Date: January 18, 1966.

Reviewed (ARC's Signature).
