f. SHELTON converted his carport into an office and moved the KLAN office from the Alston Building in Tuscaloosa into his space May 1, 1966. The KLAN pays SHELTON $35.00 per month rent and $15.00 per month on his utilities.

g. SHELTON maintains that four of the eight $500.00 checks he received from DIXIE ENGINEERING COMPANY bounced. The reason the four $500.00 items do not show on his bank statements is a debit to the account; the balances at the time the checks were returned were less than $500.00, and he picked up the checks with cash or with another check from DIXIE ENGINEERING.

h. KLAN checks were made payable to “Cash” because just anyone would not cash a KLAN check. When he left Tuscaloosa on KLAN business, he never knew where he would be able to cash a check, so he would make the check payable to cash and cash the check at a grocery store or service station where he was known. The amounts were charged to him on the KLAN checks, and he brought back gas tickets, motel bills, invoices, etc., to account for the expenditures.
