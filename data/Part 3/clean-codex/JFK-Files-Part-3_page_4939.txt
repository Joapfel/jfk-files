On 6/1/61, (CG 5824-S*) advised that Hunter Pitts O'Dell, who was a secret member of the National Committee, CP, USA, had quit the CP and was officially considered by the CP to be out. However, there were no hard feelings between O'Dell and the Party, and he was still on good terms with the CP and would work with them.

Extreme caution must be exercised with respect to dissemination of the information herein, since by its nature it tends to identify (NY 694-S*), a valuable informant, as the source thereof. Unless this information is obtained from a source other than (NY 694-S*), it should not be incorporated, even in paraphrased form, in the investigative section of a report.

On 7/23/62, (NY 694-S*) stated during a personal contact with Gus Hall, General Secretary of the CP, USA, on that date, Hall advised that Hunter Pitts O'Dell was still representing the CP, USA, and that he was still on the National Committee of the CP, USA. Hall further remarked that O'Dell was a “brain-trust” for Luther King and was doing an excellent job.

Current investigation in NYC reflects that O'Dell was spending most of his time in the Atlanta area working with the SCLC. He was last reported in the New York area in June 1962.
