U.S. Bureau of Justice  
Department of Justice  
Communications Section

October 23, 1962

Teletype. Urgent, 10/23/62, 7:35 PM AH.

To Director, FBI 92-3267 and SAC, Las Vegas 92-467. From SAC, Los Angeles 92-113C 2P.

Re: John Roselli, AKA AR.

LA 4268-C advised he learned yesterday at Friars Club, Beverly Hills, that Roselli is in Las Vegas, Nevada.

Neighborhood source advised someone, identity not known, at Roselli's Los Angeles apartment night of October 22–23 last and on afternoon October 23 instant.

Records Fidelity Bank, Beverly Hills, California, reveal on October 17 last, Roselli deposited check of seven thousand one hundred forty-one dollars and sixty-six cents drawn on Union Bank, Los Angeles. Source at Union Bank stated this check dated October 16 last, drawn on Emanuel Rothman's client trust account, payable to John Roselli, and bore notation: “Distribution of assets, B and R Enterprises.” Information previously reported that end page one.
