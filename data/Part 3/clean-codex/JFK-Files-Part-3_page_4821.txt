CROGHAN determined that the files of the Greater Miami Credit Bureau reflect that GERALD SCHWARTZ is a vice president of the County National Bank and has held this position for three years. He was formerly a factory representative for Lullaby Products, Inc., and has a satisfactory credit record.

Examination of telephone directories reflects that telephone number 455-7158 is a Pensacola, Florida, telephone number.
