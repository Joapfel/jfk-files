Caption on folder or file: Microphone Surveillances.

Cabinet 6, drawer 2. Folder or file: File. First communication dated 7/59. Last communication dated 7/66. Approximate pages: 150.

Derogatory information: No. Category: Administrative, policy. Ticklers and originals: Both.

General content: Correspondence consists of memos to the Attorney General from Mr. Hoover; in-house memos and blind memos, all of which deal with microphone surveillance policy and identify subjects surveilled in the blind memos. Subjects are intelligence service, racial, and anti-racketeering.
