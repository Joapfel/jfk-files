Made one organizational recommendation. It is just one way of doing it. It was to propose that there be a small internal staff in the Agency, in the clandestine service, but with a direct line to the DCI, that should scrutinize every proposed new operation before it is initiated, and at least once a year should scrutinize every ongoing operation from one standpoint solely: the prospects of compromise and their change from year to year. My feeling is that in the years that I was in the DDP in particular, lacking this or any similar mechanism, a number of our mistakes occurred because there was not a strong enough voice raised internally in the councils of the Agency in favor of the precautions that would be required to maintain secrets.
