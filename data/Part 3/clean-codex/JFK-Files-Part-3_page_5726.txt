e. The semiannual letter should not be disseminated outside the Bureau, should be given proper security in the field office, and should contain the following information. Where there is no pertinent activity or information, a negative statement should be made.

(1) Klan Organizations, Hate Organizations, and Informant Coverage

(a) Names of Klan and hate organizations active in your territory.

(b) Coverage of each Klan broken down by Klaverns and each hate organization broken down into local chapters, showing identities of informants and sources reporting on each.

(c) Estimated membership by Klavern or chapter and basis for estimate.

(2) Racial Informants by Geographic Location

List the following under headquarters city and each resident agency within your division:

(a) Symbol numbers of all racial informants, with indications as to which are regularly paid.

(b) Names or symbol numbers of all approved potential racial informants, indicating those regularly paid.

(c) Name or symbol number of each confidential source, indicating those which are regularly paid.

(3) Individuals Under Pending Investigation

List of individuals being investigated under provisions of section 23, volume II, and section 122, volume IV, of this Manual, other than potential bombing suspects.

(4) Potential Bombing Suspects

List of potential bombing suspects of your office, including description, basis of selection, and other areas in which the individual should be considered as suspect.
