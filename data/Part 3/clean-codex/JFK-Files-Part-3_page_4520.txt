Federal Bureau of Criminal Investigation  
U. S. Department of Justice  
Communications Section

September 5, 1961

Teletype

To: Director, FBI  
From: SAC, Pittsburgh

Gabriel Mannarino, AKA; R. Samuel Mannarino, AKA. With reference to IRS, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, raid on Mannarino gambling casino and horse room in New Kensington on September 24, last, SA of IRS arrested William Sams on September 1, last, at Ken Iron and Steel Company, New Kensington, Pennsylvania, based on warrants charging violation of federal wagering tax laws. Note that Sams is included under the Top Echelon CIP. Informant advised above gambling casino and horse room are now back to almost normal operations. Both subjects should be considered armed and dangerous.
