Counterintelligence Programs

In April 1959, a flyer revealing the Communist Party, USA (CPUSA), as the true sponsors of the May First Celebration scheduled to be held at the United Plant Guard Workers of America Local 114 Hall at 13722 Linwood, Detroit, Michigan, was sent to Mr. James C. McGahey, president of the union.

The CPUSA was locked out of the union hall and had to hold the affair elsewhere. Mr. McGahey refunded the rental fee to the CPUSA.
