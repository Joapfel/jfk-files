U.S. Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC)

Interview of Former Special Agent Robert S. Baker by SSC Staff Member

Mr. Seidel asked Mr. Baker if he was acquainted with Richard Held.

Mr. Baker stated that Richard Held was assigned as Assistant Special Agent in Charge in San Diego, and he knew him at that time. He stated that he was aware that Richard Held was then the Special Agent in Charge of the Chicago Office of the FBI.

Mr. Seidel stated to Mr. Baker that he was going to interview Assistant Director Robert Gebhardt and Special Agent in Charge Charles W. Bates.

This concluded the interview of former Special Agent Robert S. Baker by SSC Staff Member Lester Seidel.

The Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) was an organization founded by Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., with the theme of new life for poor people. Dr. King, a leader in civil rights, was assassinated on April 4, 1969.
