NR 004 SF CODE

Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

12:28 PM, urgent, 3/26/75, MCC.

To: Director.  
From: San Francisco.  
Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

Re: BUNITEL, March 24, 1975.

The following breakdown excludes, on a percentage basis, man-days spent on criminal matters by agents assigned to internal security and counterintelligence cases.

San Francisco has 70 agents and 5 supervisors assigned full-time to internal security. It has 73 agents and 3 supervisors full-time and one additional supervisor 25 percent time assigned to counterintelligence. One SAC and one ASAC are 25 percent each on internal security and counterintelligence.

End.

IEWTZ

JTG, FBIHQ

March 26, 1975.

Teletype.
