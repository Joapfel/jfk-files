United States Government

Memorandum

To: Mr. Gallagher  
From: L. E. Rhyne  
Subject: SENSTUDY 75  
Date: 7/28/75

Reference is made to memorandum of Mr. Cregar to Mr. Wannall dated 7/24/75, relating to certain pages of a CIA document. Pages 107, 108, 111, 149–151, 155, 156, 158–161, 178, 180, 181, 296, 298, 301, 353, and 354 of this document were reviewed, and disclosure of this information to the Senate Select Committee will not compromise or otherwise damage an ongoing investigation presently being conducted by the FBI.

Existence of the 693-page CIA document and the information contained therein should be closely guarded and disclosed only on a need-to-know basis.
