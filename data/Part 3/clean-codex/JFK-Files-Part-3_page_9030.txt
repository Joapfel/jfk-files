Item I.

Q: What about Julian (JUNAN) Bond's campaign? Any effort to influence state?

Q: What litigation was conducted by Bond?

A: None to my knowledge beyond such courage of those who, as may have resulted from courage of SNCI with which Bond was affiliated.
