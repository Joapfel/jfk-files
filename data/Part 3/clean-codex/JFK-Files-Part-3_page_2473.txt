United States Government Memorandum

To: SAC, WFO (65-8300)

From: SA H. Dudley Payne

Subject: SAM Survey  
Espionage - R

Date: 10/11/62

On 9/26/62, the writer contacted Mr. P. A. Shorb, Superintendent of National Airport Post Office, to ascertain if our present schedules were adequate to promptly handle our responsibilities at that location. Mr. Shorb advised that at present the mail was being handled in a very satisfactory manner, and it was not anticipated that any schedules would be changed until daylight saving time ended at the end of October. He would advise us of any changes to be made at that time.

He was asked concerning the procedure for handling mail when Dulles Airport opens. He stated that at this time it was anticipated that the processing of mail insofar as our operation is concerned would be handled at National.
