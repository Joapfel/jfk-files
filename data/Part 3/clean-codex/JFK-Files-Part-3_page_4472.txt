United States Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Investigation.

Date: 11/24/59  
Office: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania  
File Number: Philadelphia 92-444  
Title: Angelo Bruno  
Bureau: 92-2717  
Character: Anti-Racketeering

Synopsis:

Angelo Bruno continues negotiations for contract to remove garbage from the city of Philadelphia to the vicinity of Salem, N.J., via water. One informant says no toll calls originated from Bruno's residence from 7/59 to 10/59. Bruno observed riding a Pontiac sedan registered to J. C. Vending Machines, Inc., 947 Southard Street, Trenton, N.J. Phil Testa, lieutenant of Bruno, driving a black sedan, Pennsylvania registration F 2562. Bruno's crap game in Mt. Holly, N.J., no longer in operation, according to one informant.
