STURGIS: I received these blanks in the early part of the 1960s from Sam Jenis, who asked me if I wanted to fill out the applications and become an employee of the Central Intelligence Agency. I told him I did not; he asked me why. I told him that I did not trust some of the agents that CIA had working for them, per se, as agents, because I felt that some of them were double agents. I felt that many of the agents had too much power in their hands, where there was a good possibility that their case officer or their station chief did not know totally what some of the agents were doing or what orders the agents were giving to underlings under them, people who were closely associated with them on a contract basis or whatever.
