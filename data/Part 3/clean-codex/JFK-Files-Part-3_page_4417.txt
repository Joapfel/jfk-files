United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Washington, D.C. 20535

September 28, 1967

Memorandum for the Attorney General

Re: Middle East Situation

The Department of State has requested urgent action to institute telephone surveillance coverage on the residence of Mahmoud Riad, Foreign Minister of the United Arab Republic, Waldorf Towers, Suite 32-F, New York City; the Iraqi Delegation to the United Nations, 14 East 79th Street, New York City; and the Office of Arab League, 757 Third Avenue, New York City. This coverage is desired because the current session of the United Nations General Assembly will undoubtedly take up the Middle East situation and it is expected this coverage will produce information of critical importance to the United States Government.

This Bureau is in a position to institute the requested coverage. In view of State Department's request for urgent action, it is requested that you advise of your decision as soon as possible.

EX 106

Approved: Ramsey Black, 5 OCT 9 1967. Date: 28 Sept 1967.
