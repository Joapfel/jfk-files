Re: “National Guardian,” Weekly Guardian Associates, Inc.; Internal Security—C. Internal Security Act of 1950

Reference is made to memorandum at New York, entitled as above, dated May 22, 1961.

A source, who has furnished reliable information in the past, furnished on July 17, 1961, a revised schedule of the “National Guardian” European tour, July 28–August 20, 1961.

The schedule of this tour is as follows:

From: Idlewild. To: Copenhagen. Via: Scandinavian Airlines (SAS). Flight No.: 912. Date: 7/28.

From: Copenhagen. To: Stockholm. Via: SAS. Flight No.: 508. Date: 7/31.

From: Stockholm. To: Helsinki. Via: SAS. Flight No.: 728. Date: 7/31.

From: Helsinki. To: Moscow. Via: Aeroflot. Flight No.: 108. Date: 7/31.

From: Moscow. To: Kiev. Via: Aeroflot. Carrier: open.

From: Kiev. To: Prague. Via: Aeroflot. Carrier: 211. Date: 8/17.

From: Prague. To: Copenhagen. Via: Malev (Hungarian Airlines). Carrier: 380. Date: 8/20.

From: Copenhagen. To: Idlewild. Via: SAS Airlines. Carrier: 919. Date: 8/20.
