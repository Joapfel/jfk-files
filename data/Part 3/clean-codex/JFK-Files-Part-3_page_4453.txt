Memo Donahoe to Belmont

Re: United Arab Republic reaction to speech of Senator John Kennedy.

[UAR Ambassador] was of the opinion that the purpose of the speech was solely to obtain Jewish votes rather than to formulate party or governmental policy. He believed such speeches are consistent with Zionist's efforts to create a breach between UAR and other governments.

It was the opinion of the UAR Ambassador that Senator Fulbright's visit with him not be disclosed.

Recommendation: For information.
