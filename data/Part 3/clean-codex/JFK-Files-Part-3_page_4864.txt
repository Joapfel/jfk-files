The following investigation was conducted by SA Michael E. Dill at Eglin Air Force Base, Florida.

On September 20, 1967, Joseph Summers, Records Office, Eglin Federal Prison Camp, furnished the prison file for Norman Rothman, number 389EF. This file reflected the following background and descriptive data concerning the subject:

Rothman was convicted in the Western District of Pennsylvania, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, on February 4, 1960, for conspiring, aiding and abetting to transport stolen property in interstate and foreign commerce, and using interstate and foreign wires to defraud. Rothman was sentenced to five years and a $10,000 fine on this charge. Rothman began serving his sentence at the Federal Penitentiary, Atlanta, Georgia, on August 28, 1951. On July 10, 1962, he was transferred from Atlanta to Federal Correctional Institution, Tallahassee, Florida. On September 12, 1963, the subject was transferred to the Eglin Federal Prison Camp, Eglin Air Force Base, Florida, where he remained until his release on February 18, 1965. The following individuals were listed as co-defendants with Rothman:

Joseph Merola  
Stuart Sutor  
Victor Carlucci  
Joseph Giordano  
Daniel Hanna

All FIA.

Rothman’s file contained a copy of a letter, dated September 22, 1961, from the Director, Alcohol and Tobacco Tax Division, which reflected that on November 4, 1958, inspectors of the Immigration and Naturalization Service and state officers detained an airplane which had landed at the Morgantown Airport, Morgantown, West Virginia. The pilot of this aircraft was determined to be Stuart Sutor.
