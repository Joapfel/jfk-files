Visit of Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (SSCI) staff to Miami Field Office and Legal Mexico City regarding Drug Enforcement Agency (DEA).

The SSCI is conducting a study of the DEA in connection with the committee's role in oversight of certain portions of DEA budget. Primarily, the committee is interested in determining the role of the DEA in the intelligence community.

SSCI Chairman Birch Bayh (BAYN) has requested the FBI to assist in the committee's study by providing briefings to staff members regarding FBI/DEA relationships. I have agreed to assist the committee in its study. As such, you are to provide briefings to the committee staff regarding our relationships with DEA.
