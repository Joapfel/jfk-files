This mail consisted primarily of letters containing personal correspondence, with some 12,000 to 20,000 letters being reviewed daily.

The JOE survey ended on July 21, 1966. The number of agents utilized ranged from four to twenty depending on the amount of mail and whether a special search was needed based on information from Bureau Source Six. Agent time spent was approximately 60 man-hours a week.
