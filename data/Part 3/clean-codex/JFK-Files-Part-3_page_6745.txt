Robert Marvin Shelton, born June 13, 1920, married, with two children, resides at 1700 Tenth Street East, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. He is presently employed with the United Klans of America, Inc., and has a reported annual salary of eight thousand dollars. The United Klans of America, Inc., is sometimes known as the Alabama Rescue Service.

It is requested that two uncertified copies each of the federal tax returns filed by Shelton and the United Klans of America, Inc., for the past five years be made available.
