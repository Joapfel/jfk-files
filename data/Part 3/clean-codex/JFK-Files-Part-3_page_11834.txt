Addendum.

Inspector E. S. Miller: During the course of instant inspection, an in-depth survey (ESM:wmj 9/3/71) was made relative to the adequacy of personnel, both agent and clerical, within the DID. The primary purpose of this survey was to determine the best possible allocation of manpower, bearing in mind the desirability of reallocating manpower to top-priority assignments.

Adequacy of Agent Personnel.
