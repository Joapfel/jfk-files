Robert Marvin Shelton

United Klans of America, Inc. (United States of America, Inc.).

Robert Marvin Shelton, born June 13, 1920, married, two children, resident of 1700 Tenth Street East, Tuscaloosa, Alabama. He is presently employed with the State Department. Imperial Wizard of the United Klans of America, Inc. (United Nations of America, Inc.), head of the Ku Klux Klan (Mu Mu Klans), Suite 400, Alvin Acland Building, Tuscaloosa, Alabama, with a reported annual salary of over $200,000. The United Klans of America, Inc. is sometimes known as the Alabama Rescue Service.

It is requested that two uncertified copies each of the federal tax returns filed by Shelton and the United Klans of America, Inc. for the past five years be made available.
