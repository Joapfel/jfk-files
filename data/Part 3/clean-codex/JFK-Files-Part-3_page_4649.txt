TP 92-1

At Tampa, Florida.

Re: 278-2574.

This number was called on July 24, 1962, apparently to Joseph Garcia. The 1962 Tampa, Florida City Directory lists Joseph A. Garcia, wife Rose M., as residing at 507 Ellicott, as at Garcia's Instant Service, and as a vice president of Central Bank of Tampa.

The directory lists Garcia's Instant Service to Joe A. Garcia, East Broadway, corner of 24th, with telephones 248-2113 and 248-2114.
