United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities (SSC)

By letter dated May 14, 1975, with attached appendices, the SSC requested certain information and documents from the FBI.

Enclosed for your approval and forwarding to the Committee is the original of a memorandum and enclosures which serve as a response to one of the SSC requests.

There does not appear to be any relation between the elimination of the FBI Crime Records Division and the mandate of the Committee contained in Senate Resolution 21. You may wish to give specific consideration to this observation before approving the forwarding of the enclosed documents to the Committee.

A copy of the memorandum with enclosures is being furnished for your records.
