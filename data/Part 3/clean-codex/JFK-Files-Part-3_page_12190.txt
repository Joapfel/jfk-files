(Addition questions)

ITEM I.

Q: What about JUVAN (JULIAN) Bond's campaign? Any effort to influence state?

A: No. As I recall, Bond was already a member of the Georgia legislature. July 1964 or 1965.

Q: What election was conducted by Bond?

A: None to my knowledge beyond such courage of a state as may have resulted from Convoy of SNCI, with which Bond was affiliated.

Q: In some manner a question was asked of other members of the Georgia legislature. My answer was stated—yes, only concern is Hall—3/25—June andufft forcing Governor.
