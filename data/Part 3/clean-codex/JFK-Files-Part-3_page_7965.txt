Kansas City Election Fraud Case.

Cabinet 6, Drawer 1.

First communication dated 6/5/47. Last communication dated 6/10/48. Approximate pages: 600.

Derogatory information: No.

Category: Administrative and information.

Ticklers only. Originals only.

General content of file or folder: Internal study of affairs of U.S. Senate Judiciary Committee hearings and an investigation concerning the failure of the Attorney General and Department of Justice to act with respect to alleged irregularities in the Missouri primary election. Includes testimony of Mr. Hoover and Bureau officials before the Committee, included as a photostat copy.
