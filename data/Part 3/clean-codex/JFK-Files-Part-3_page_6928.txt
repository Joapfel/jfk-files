Re: “National Guardian,” Weekly Guardian Associates, Inc., Internal Security—C: Internal Security Act of 1950

Reference is made to memorandum at New York, entitled as above, dated May 22, 1961.

A source, who has furnished reliable information in the past, furnished on July 17, 1961, a revised schedule of the “National Guardian” European tour, July 28–August 20, 1961.

The schedule of this tour is as follows:

From Idlewild to Copenhagen via Scandinavian Airlines (SAS), flight 912, July 28. From Copenhagen to Stockholm via SAS, flight 508, July 31. From Stockholm to Helsinki via SAS, flight 728, July 31. From Helsinki to Moscow via Aeroflot, flight 108, July 31. From Moscow to Kiev via Aeroflot, open. From Kiev to Prague via Aeroflot, flight 211, August 17. From Prague to Copenhagen via Malev (Hungarian Airlines), flight 380, August 20. From Copenhagen to Idlewild via SAS, flight 919, August 20.
