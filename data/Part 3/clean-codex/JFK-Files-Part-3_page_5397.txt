July 25, 1975

United States Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities

Re: “Official and Confidential” Files

Reference is made to a letter from John T. Elliff, dated July 23, 1975, requesting that a list of knowledgeable individuals who were contacted by the Inspection Division of this Bureau during its inquiry into the “official and confidential” files be forwarded to the Senate Select Committee.

In response to the aforementioned request, we are attaching a copy of the list of knowledgeable individuals who were contacted during the Inspection Division inquiry concerning the “official and confidential” files.
