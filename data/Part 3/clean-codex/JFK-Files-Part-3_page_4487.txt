United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation

Report of: SA Delbert W. Hahn. Office: New Orleans. Date: 4/18/69. Field Office File No.: NO 87-14835. Bureau File No.: 87-92096.

Title: David Lynn Mitchell, theft of $54,170.00 in jewelry, August 16, 1967, West Hollywood, California; Addie Katherine Earle—victim; Margaret Elaine Adams; Anthony Rossi.

Character: Interstate transportation of stolen property—major theft; interstate transportation of stolen property—receiving and concealing; unlawful flight to avoid prosecution—burglary and grand theft; harboring.

Synopsis: Rossi, as Ovidio Limone, obtained Coast Guard Z card No. 1281740 on 7/31/68 at New Orleans. Rossi cancelled insurance on Cadillac and by letter requested premium refund be forwarded to New York address. FBI Laboratory identified handwriting on hotel safe deposit box receipt as that of Anthony Rossi. David Lynn Mitchell identified a photograph of Carlos Marcello and furnished a signed statement that he sold an emerald ring to Marcello in Canal Jewelry Store for $3,000. Rossi has prior arrest for assault and robbery and should be approached cautiously.
