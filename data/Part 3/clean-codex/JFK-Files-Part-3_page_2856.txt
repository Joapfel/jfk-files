Organized Crime, Regional Training Conference, September 25–27, 1984, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania.

Rebutel invited various East Coast field divisions to send organized crime supervisors and agents assigned to organized crime matters to the above training conference. A primary thrust of this conference will be the LCN and narcotics violations.
