United States Department of Justice  
Federal Bureau of Investigation  
Washington 25, D. C.

June 29, 1954

Memorandum for the Attorney General

Interim Security - IS (5)

In several communications to you during the past year concerning Israeli intelligence operations in the United States, information has been forwarded recording an espionage ring operated by the [x] Israel in New York City. This ring was headed by Youval Tal who until March 29, 1954, when he departed from the United States, was an adviser to the [x] Secretary of State to the United Nations. Also included in this ring were [x] and [x] employees of the [x] New York City.

The Department of State has informed this Bureau that on April 14, 1954, Assistant Secretary of State Henry A. Byroade conferred with [x] and expressed to him the disquiet of our Government at the activities of [x]. Mr. Byroade noted the fact that Tal had recently left the United States and told the [x] that our Government considered it desirable that [x] also be withdrawn.

It is anticipated that the departure of these three men from the United States probably will not result in the discontinuance of [x] activities in the New York area. Since the activities have centered about the [x] there in the past it is possible that they will continue to be conducted from that establishment. Accordingly, it is recommended that you authorize technical coverage at [x].
