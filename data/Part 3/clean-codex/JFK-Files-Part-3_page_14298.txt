CIA Briefing of the Board's Covert Action Panel

September 6, 1963

The Board's Covert Action Panel met on September 6, 1963. Present were Mr. Robert Murphy, Chairman; Mr. Gordon Gray; Dr. William Langer; and Messrs. Coyne and Ash.

During the course of the meeting the Board Panel was briefed by Mr. Richard Helms (Deputy DCI/Plans, CIA) and CIA representatives Cord Meyer and Desmond Fitzgerald. The briefings were on the subject of covert action programs with respect to British Guiana, Haiti, the Dominican Republic, Vietnam, Bolivia, Brazil and Cuba.
