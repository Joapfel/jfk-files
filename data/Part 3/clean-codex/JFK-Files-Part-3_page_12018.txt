Congress of African Peoples (CAP) seeks to establish black political power through four basic activities:

1. Influencing black political candidates.
2. Organization of the black community.
3. Formation of coalitions with all dissident and extremist groups, whether white or black, so long as CAP benefits from them.
4. The massive use of disruptive tactics.

CAP has emerged as yet another deadly threat to racial harmony and, as a result of its programs and ties with foreign extremist elements and well-known revolutionary Stokely Carmichael, must also be considered an imposing threat to national security.

Investigations of CAP, its leaders, and extremist affiliates are receiving close supervision. Pertinent data developed regarding their activities is being disseminated to the Department and to interested government agencies and officials.
