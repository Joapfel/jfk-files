Task Force Concept

The Task Force concept is designed to identify various abuses which the SSC desires to investigate in-depth. A member of the intelligence community would be identified to the Executive Branch as a Task Force leader. The purpose would be to permit that Task Force leader to be completely knowledgeable on all available information throughout the intelligence community for a particular abuse. In other words, the White House could look to the Task Force leader as the one person in the intelligence community who would be privy to all information regarding a particular abuse. The Deputy Attorney General had no problem with this concept but did insist that the Task Force leaders should not negotiate directly with their counterparts on the SSC on behalf of the intelligence community for any particular abuse (the White House has insisted on this). The Deputy Attorney General instructed Mr. O'Connor to make this quite clear to Mr. James A. Wilderotter, Associate Counsel to the President, and if Wilderotter insisted on unilateral negotiations between Task Force leaders and the SSC, Wilderotter should be referred to the Deputy Attorney General in order to resolve this issue.
