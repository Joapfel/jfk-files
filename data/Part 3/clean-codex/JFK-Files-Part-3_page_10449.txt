Recommendations for Improvements to Integrated Safety Systems

Enhance the performance of the Operational Management Committee (OMC) vessel operator training and development programs.

Require a period of 6 months from oceanic operator training to firmly understand the requirement. Recalls are required to be performed, with the first being effective 8/15/93.

Additional information on the performance indicators to be added in 2020.

Requirements for the performance indicators are as follows:

Performance indicators for Operational Management Committee (OMC) vessels will be determined based on the combined performance of the Operational Management Committee and the operational performance indicators for the vessel. The operational performance indicators will be reviewed by the Operational Management Committee on an annual basis.

Performance indicators for Operational Management Committee (OMC) maritime operations will be determined based on the combined performance of the Operational Management Committee and the operational performance indicators for the vessel. The operational performance indicators will be reviewed by the Operational Management Committee on an annual basis.

Requirements for the performance indicators for Operational Management Committee (OMC) maritime operations will be determined based on the combined performance of the Operational Management Committee and the operational performance indicators for the vessel. The operational performance indicators will be reviewed by the Operational Management Committee on an annual basis.
