Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities

REBUTEL March 24, 1975.

Memphis office uses approximately 25 percent of one supervisor's time in the security field, 20 percent on internal security, and 5 percent on counterintelligence.

In addition, six agents are utilized. The following table shows the percentage of total time spent by these agents in this field:

Agent A: 100 percent security, 100 percent internal security, 0 percent counterintelligence.  
Agent B: 75 percent security, 50 percent internal security, 25 percent counterintelligence.  
Agent C: 50 percent security, 50 percent internal security, 0 percent counterintelligence.  
Agent D: 50 percent security, 25 percent internal security, 25 percent counterintelligence.  
Agent E: 25 percent security, 25 percent internal security, 0 percent counterintelligence.  
Agent F: 25 percent security, 0 percent internal security, 25 percent counterintelligence.

In addition to the one-fourth supervisor time, this office uses the equivalent of three and one-fourth agents in the security.
