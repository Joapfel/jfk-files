To: Intelligence Community Staff. Attention: Central Index. From: FBI.

Subject: Abstract of Information Provided to Select Committees.

Date provided: 6/17/75.

How provided: Document; briefing; interview; testimony; other.

To whom provided: SSC; the Attorney General with a copy for forwarding to the White House; HSC.

Classification of information: U.

Key words: Intelligence collection; surveillance; intelligence activities, domestic; organization and staffing.

Summary: Interview covered the following topics: former FBI investigation of Martin Luther King, Jr.; organization of the FBI Washington, D.C., Field Office; administrative procedures of the FBI regarding electronic surveillance.
