Phoenix Division has the following agent personnel assigned to internal security and counterintelligence matters:

A. Internal Security: One supervisor—40 percent; two agents—100 percent; two agents—75 percent; three agents—50 percent.

B. Counterintelligence:

1. Domestic—none.

2. Foreign: One supervisor—30 percent; one agent—100 percent; two agents—25 percent.
