Letter to Birmingham

RN: COUNTY INVESTIGATION PROGRAM  
107-9-4

8. The gross income of Molton and his wife for the year 1963 totals $1,076. This amount does not appear to be sufficient to support a family of five. All possible efforts should be made to develop evidence indicating Molton is withholding or concealing income.

This investigation should be afforded prompt and continuing attention and the Duxbury must be advised of all particular developments.

When all possible leads have been developed, a blind investigation should be started, getting further Molton's background and current position with the man and all data probative of a possible violation of Internal Revenue laws. The Bureau will make this information available to appropriate Internal Revenue Service officials at the end of Government.
