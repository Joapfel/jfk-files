Code. Teletype. Immediate. 1-29-75.

To all SACs and all Legats. From Director, FBI.

Destruction of field files and records.

In accordance with proposed investigation and study of government operations with respect to intelligence activities, congressional leaders have requested the Bureau not to destroy, remove from our possession or control, or otherwise dispose or permit disposal of any records or documents which might have a bearing on subjects under investigation. Accordingly, upon receipt of this communication, recipients are instructed to hold in abeyance any records destruction program previously approved by statute or regulations. Further instructions in this matter will be issued upon receipt.

Date: 6/1/000.

MOR-16.
