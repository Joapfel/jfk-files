Format for abstract of material submitted to the Senate (House) Select Committee investigating intelligence activities.

1. Subject.
2. Originating organization.
3. Nature of material: report, briefing, chart, etc.
4. Title.
5. Summary of contents: a brief narrative statement describing the content of the material provided, problems addressed, and any conclusions or recommendations.
6. Requestor.
7. Date of request.
8. Date of submission.
9. Location of file copy or copies.
10. Does material relate to an intelligence community problem? If so, identify.

All information contained herein is unclassified. Herein is a sample document. Date: 6-17-66. By: SP-2 ALP. SP-2 ALM-66.
