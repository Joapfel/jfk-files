United States Government Memorandum

To: Mr. W. C. Sullivan  
From: G. C. Moore  
Subject: Counter-Intelligence Program—Disruption of Hate Groups—RM  
Date: 6/15/70

The Birmingham Office has requested the Bureau to secure copies of the 1969 income tax returns for United Klans of America, Inc. (UKA), the Alabama Rescue Service, a name by which the UKA is sometimes known, and Robert Marvin Shelton, Imperial Wizard of UKA. Birmingham desires to review these returns for information concerning the financial standing of UKA and Shelton and any information that might be of assistance in instituting counterintelligence measures.

Recommendation:

That the Liaison Section obtain from the Internal Revenue Service two certified copies each of the 1969 income tax returns filed by Shelton, the UKA, and/or Alabama Rescue Service. Attached is a memorandum for use of the Liaison Section.
