From USARMA San Salvador to ACSI, Department of the Army, Washington, D.C.

REUR DA 923579. Since Castro regime seized power, an estimated 300–350 Salvadoran students have visited Cuba.

Currently 8–12 are there, and an estimated 6 are reportedly behind the Iron Curtain.

Visits are made generally by way of Mexico. Some students reportedly have received military training and indoctrination in Cuba to be applied on return to El Salvador. No factual information is available on which students have received training and indoctrination.

An estimated 5 or 6 students each are currently working at coffee fincas near Ahuachapan, Sonsonate, and Berlin for the purpose of subverting coffee pickers. No evidence of direct subversion efforts against armed forces. SCP-3.

Action: ACSI.

Additional distribution: DIA; DIA(AQ).
