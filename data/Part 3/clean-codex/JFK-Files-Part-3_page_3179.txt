Of information that may be supplied to you if it happens to be of a sensational nature or of a surprising nature, do you ever question the informer on how he obtained it?

Mr. Adams. I am sure this takes place. In any handling of an informant over a sustained period of time, you do have a rapport which they don't just come in and say Joe Blow said this, Joe Blow did that. There is a conversation that goes through which I feel certain would, if something looked like, if it looked like he had something that came from some improper source, I think the agent would say, “Where did you get this?”
