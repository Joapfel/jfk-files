He noticed the following described employees in the store.

1. A young man who worked behind the “wire cage” where merchandise was pounced, six feet, light-colored hair, mid-20s.

2. A woman in her early 40s with grayish-black hair who worked behind the cashier’s desk.

3. A salesman, very short, about five feet five inches, with a mustache.

He could furnish no additional descriptive data concerning the above individuals, advising that he only saw them in the store and did not talk to them.

HITCHILL stated that the safe in which the jewelry was placed by WILLS was a large, double-door safe located in the office of Canal Jewelry and Loan. He advised that the office was located at the rear of the store; that upon entering the store, you walk straight back, up two or three stairs, and turn right to enter the office.

HITCHILL stated that WILLS went with his mother, BARRY HITCHILL, in about 1951 or 1952, when MITCHELL was six or seven years of age. HITCHILL said that at that time, he and his mother were living in an apartment in Calvaster, Texas, adjacent to a beauty parlor. The owner-operator of the beauty parlor, name not recalled, resided in an upstairs apartment; she was their landlady. This apartment and beauty parlor were still in existence and, as of about one year ago, were still owned and operated by the same woman.
