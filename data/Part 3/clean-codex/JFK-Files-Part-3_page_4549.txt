FBI

Date: 6-17-59

To: Director, FBI (92-2781)  
From: SAC, Miami (92-88)

Santo Traff icante, Jr., aka. Anti-Racketeering.

MM 416-C advised 6-16-59 that the wedding of the subject’s daughter was being held as scheduled, Sunday, 6-21-59, at Havana Hilton Hotel, Havana, Cuba. Ninety-eight R.S.V.P. invitations were tendered to associates and friends in Tampa and Miami, Fla. Air transportation, hotel accommodations, and all expenses included.

Jimmie Longo, the subject’s “bodyguard,” advised that the informant’s source said deportation proceedings against the subject by Cuban authorities were “off.” Informant advised he learned from other sources that Luis Orlando Rodriguez, removed as cabinet minister by Fidel Castro, had been pressing the case against the subject and the American underworld element.

Jose Navarre now holding office. Will “cooperate.”

Extra copies furnished for Legat, Havana.
