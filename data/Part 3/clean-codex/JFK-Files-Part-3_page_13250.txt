MR. ROETHE: Were there any shots ever fired?

MR. STURGIS: No sir. As a matter of fact, in order to avoid that, because my people wanted to do it so badly, I had to take the ammunition away from them just to contain them.

MR. ROETHE: Was this an exercise that was going to be—was this going to be a shooting?

MR. STURGIS: Oh, yes.

MR. ROETHE: So it was not a bombing or anything of this sort, strictly a shooting?

MR. STURGIS: A shooting.

MR. OLSEN: How soon before you left Cuba did this setting up of the exercises take place?

MR. STURGIS: It was within, I would say, four to six weeks.
