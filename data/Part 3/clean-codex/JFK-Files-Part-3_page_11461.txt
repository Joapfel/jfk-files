Plaintext. Teletype.

To: ADIC, New York

From: Director, FBI

SENSTUDY 75

2/12/76

MDR 16

SA Daniel A. Flynn gave deposition on September 10, 1975, to the Senate Select Committee. James Dick, committee staff member, desires to have SA Flynn attest to the accuracy of deposition before notary public. Mr. Dick will contact SA Flynn in the New York office and make arrangements in this regard. Bureau approval is granted for SA Flynn to attest to the accuracy of the deposition.

Federal Bureau of Investigation, Communications Section.

FEB 12 1976

Personnel File — SA Daniel A. Flynn.

Teletype.

FEB 18 1976

Note: On 2/11/76, James Dick, staff member of the Senate Select Committee, advised SA Flynn (NYO) gave deposition on 9/10/75 on the subject of mail openings. The deposition was not under oath. Dick requested SA Flynn attest to accuracy of his deposition before a notary public. SA Flynn furnished a summary of his deposition to the Bureau which was furnished to the Department and the White House by memorandum dated 10/1/75.
