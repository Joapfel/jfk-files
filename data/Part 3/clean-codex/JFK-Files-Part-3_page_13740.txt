State of Security — William G. Gardner.

Third agency delivery — one sealed envelope. Investigative Reda Ltd., 4 June 1953 (source). State/ACSI/ONI.

Secret Service — State Department, 1940–42.

23 January 1950 memo and last Dir. 100 from Chey, Inspection and Security Staff. Re: William G. Gardner.

Proposed series and special reports on Latin America regarding Gardner. Subject was cleared as a contact in January 1948. State Department says that when he was employed by them, he had a reputation for boastfulness and was outspoken. This should not raise any security questions.
