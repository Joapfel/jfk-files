Item I.

Q: What about Julian (JUNAN) Bond's campaign? Any help to influence that?

A: No. As I recall, Bond was already a member of the Georgia legislature July 1964 or 1965.

Q: What petition was conducted by Bond?

A: None to my knowledge beyond such courage on the part of Senator C.A. Katzman as may have resulted from the courage of SNCC, with which Bond was affiliated.

Q: In what manner a question was asked regarding other members of the Georgia legislature? My assumption was that Mr. Huey Concerned Hall only concerned Bill Lee, intending to form a Governor.

Date: 17 July 1964.
