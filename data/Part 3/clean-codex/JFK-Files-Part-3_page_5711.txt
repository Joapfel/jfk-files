Office Memorandum, United States Government

To: The Director

From: A. Rosen

Subject: Bombing Matters or Racial Matters (New Classification)

Date: January 23, 1959

The attached SAC Letter is to inform the field of the new classification to be used in connection with correspondence carrying the character racial matters or bombing matters. This classification will be used in opening new cases carrying racial matters or bombing matters. It will not be necessary for either the field or the Bureau to change the classification number of existing cases or correspondence.

The Records Branch has been consulted and is agreeable to the new classification “157.” New sections and changes for manuals and handbooks are being prepared and will be forwarded to the field.

Recommendation: That the attached SAC Letter be approved and forwarded.

1 - Inspector H. L. Edwards
