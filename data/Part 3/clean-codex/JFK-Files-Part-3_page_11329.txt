7:08 PM NITEL 3/25/75 LSK. To: Director.

From: Milwaukee (66-NEW). Attention: Budget and Accounting Section.

Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Activities.

REBUNITEL to all SACs, March 24, 1975.

Milwaukee has 2 security supervisors, #5 and #6, neither of whom is assigned full time to security work. 23 agents are assigned to these 2 security squads, including headquarters and resident agents (RAs). 4 of the latter are engaged full time in security work. 15 additional resident agents not assigned to either security squad also handle some security work in their various areas. Percentage breakdown is as follows:

#5 Supervisor: Internal Security 70; Cointel 5; Other 25.

#6 Supervisor: Internal Security 30; Cointel 55; Other 15.

#5 Squad, 12 agents: Internal Security 59; Cointel 8; Other 33.

#6 Squad, 11 agents: Internal Security 44; Cointel 44; Other 12.

RAs, 15 agents: Internal Security 2.5; Cointel 4.5; Other 93.

End.

All information contained herein is unclassified. Date 1-17-75 by SP-ALM/75.
