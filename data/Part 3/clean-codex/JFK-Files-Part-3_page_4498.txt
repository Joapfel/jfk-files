Federal Bureau of Investigation.

Date: 3/10/89.

David Lyn Mickell was interviewed at the Muir Conservation Center, Mammoth, California. Mickell was sworn of his rights by Special Agent Donald R. Hos and he waived them, as shown on a documented handwritten waiver form. Mickell provided the alleged statement as follows:

February 28, 1969.

Muir Conservation Center, Jamestown, California.

“I, David Lyn Mickell, acknowledge the following voluntary statement to Wilde N. Ios and Dated A. Thomas, who have identified themselves to me as Special Agents with the Federal Bureau of Investigation. Asst. INS has told me that this inquiry relates to the identification of an individual or individuals to whom jewelry was sold. This jewelry had previously been taken by her from the house of Ann E. English Barba. Mr. Ios exhibited five photographs of five different persons to me. From this group of photographs, I selected a photograph which I identified as a photograph of an individual known to be Garland Mickell. This is the person to whom I sold an emerald ring while in the office of the Canal Jewelry and Loan Company in New Orleans, Louisiana.

“The office was located at the store of the Canal Jewelry and Loan Company and could not be viewed from the store itself. The office contained a large, double-doored safe, a desk, and one or two chairs. To gain entrance to the office required going up two or three staircases at the rear of the store.”

On 9/29/89 at ... File No. AC 87-174. SA Garland N. Ios and E. A. Davio G. Thomas. Date dictated: 3/10/89.
